## Statement

Let $A,D\subset\mathbb R^d$ be equal-volume convex bodies with volume $V$ and centroids zero. Assume a $C^1$ convex-gradient diffeomorphism $T:A^\circ\to D^\circ$ with $DT>0$ and $\det DT=1$. Put $L=(A+D)/2$, $Y(x)=(x+T(x))/2$, $\Omega=Y(A^\circ)$, $W(Y(x))=(T(x)-x)/2$ and $B=DW$. The midpoint pushforward of ordinary volume has density $\eta=\det(I-B)=\det(I+B)=\sqrt{\det(I-B^2)}\le1$ on $\Omega\subset L$. Its mass is $V$ and $\int_\Omega Y\eta=\int_\Omega W\eta=0$. The volume defect satisfies
$$
|L|-V=|L\setminus\Omega|+\int_\Omega(1-\eta),\qquad
\int_\Omega\|DW\|_F^2\le d(|L|-V)\quad(d\ge2).
$$
In dimension two $1-\eta=\|DW\|_F^2/2$ pointwise. For normalized source tails in any direction $u\ne0$ and any real shift $h$, one has
$$
V[F_A(h)+F_D(-h)]
=\int_\Omega \eta(Y)\left[\mathbf 1_{\{u\cdot Y\ge h+u\cdot W(Y)\}}+\mathbf 1_{\{u\cdot Y\ge-h-u\cdot W(Y)\}}\right]\,dY .
$$

## Proof

For distinct $x,x'$ in the convex interior of $A$, integrate the positive-definite derivative of $Y$ along their segment to obtain $(Y(x)-Y(x'))\cdot(x-x')>0$. Thus $Y$ is injective and the inverse function theorem gives a $C^1$ inverse on the open image. Differentiation gives $B=(DT-I)(DT+I)^{-1}$, which is symmetric with eigenvalues $\beta_j\in(-1,1)$ by diagonalizing the positive-definite $DT$. The two inverse source maps on the midpoint image are $Y\mapsto Y-W(Y)$ and $Y\mapsto Y+W(Y)$. Their Jacobians are $I-B,I+B$ and have the same positive determinant by $\det DT=1$. Their determinant product is $\det(I-B^2)$. Ordinary change of variables gives the midpoint density $\eta$ and shows $\int_\Omega\eta=V$. The two first moments follow by taking the half-sum and half-difference of the original first moments, since $T$ transports volume.

The decomposition of $|L|-V$ follows by splitting $L$ at $\Omega$. For $d\ge2$,
$$
\eta=\prod_j(1-\beta_j^2)^{1/2}
\le (1-\|B\|_F^2/d)^{d/2}\le1-\|B\|_F^2/d.
$$
The first inequality is arithmetic–geometric mean and the second uses $d/2\ge1$. In dimension two, expanding the equality of the two determinants gives $\operatorname{tr}B=0$, hence the eigenvalues are $b,-b$ and $\eta=1-b^2$. This gives the pointwise equality. Finally change variables separately in the two source caps using $x=Y-W(Y)$ and $T(x)=Y+W(Y)$. The source boundary has volume zero and its closed inequality becomes the corresponding closed indicator in the formula.
