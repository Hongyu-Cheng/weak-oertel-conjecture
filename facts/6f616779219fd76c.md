## Statement

Let $n\ge0$, $d\ge1$ be integers, $P\subset\mathbb R^n$ be nonempty and finite, $j\in P$, and $r_p>0$ for $p\in P$. Put $T_d=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_d)$, $q=r_j/(d+1)$, and $\Delta=\{w\in\mathbb R^d:w_i\ge0,\ \sum_i w_i=1\}$. Put $y_j=q(1,\ldots,1)$ and let $\mu$ be the sum of ordinary $d$-volume on the fibers $\{p\}\times r_pT_d$. Assume there is $b\in\mathbb R^n$ such that $r_p\le r_j+b\cdot(p-j)$ for every $p\in P$. For every closed ambient halfspace $H$ containing $(j,y_j)$ there are $a\in\mathbb R^n$ and $w\in\Delta$ such that
$$
\left|r_pT_d\cap\{X:a\cdot(p-j)+w\cdot X\ge q\}\right|
\le \left|\{X\in r_pT_d:(p,X)\in H\}\right|
\qquad(p\in P).
$$
In particular the infimum $h_\mu(j,y_j)$ over all closed ambient halfspaces containing $(j,y_j)$ equals
$$
\min_{a\in\mathbb R^n,\ w\in\Delta}
\sum_{p\in P}\left|r_pT_d\cap\{X:a\cdot(p-j)+w\cdot X\ge q\}\right|.
$$
The same formula holds when the tilt is restricted to the compact set of the bounded shared tilt lemma. The touching-majorant hypothesis holds if the displayed simplices are the actual fibers of one convex set at the bases in $P$.

## Proof

Translate the boundary of $H$ parallel to itself until it passes through $(j,y_j)$. This only decreases its mass on every fiber. Write its new margin as $L(p,X)=a_0\cdot(p-j)+u\cdot(X-y_j)$. If $u\ge0$ and $u\ne0$, divide both coefficients by $\sum_i u_i>0$ and use $w\cdot y_j=q$.

If some coordinate is negative, choose $i$ with $u_i=\min_\ell u_\ell<0$. On the simplex $r_pT_d$, let $\sigma_p$ interchange $X_i$ with the slack $r_p-\sum_\ell X_\ell$, fixing the other coordinates. It is an affine involution that permutes the vertices of the same simplex, so it preserves $d$-volume; at $p=j$ it fixes $y_j$. Set $\widetilde u_i=-u_i$ and $\widetilde u_\ell=u_\ell-u_i$ for $\ell\ne i$. Then $\widetilde u\ge0$ and $\widetilde u_i>0$. Since $(d+1)q=r_j$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
L(p,\sigma_pX)
&=a_0\cdot(p-j)+\widetilde u\cdot(X-y_j)+u_i(r_p-r_j)\\
&\ge (a_0+u_i b)\cdot(p-j)+\widetilde u\cdot(X-y_j).
\end{aligned}
$$
The inequality uses the touching majorant with the sign reversed by $u_i<0$. The last line is one ambient affine margin, through $(j,y_j)$, with the same base coefficient on all fibers. Its closed trace is contained in the trace of the transformed original margin, whose mass on each fiber equals the original through-point cap mass. Dividing the last line by $\sum_\ell\widetilde u_\ell$ produces the required cap.

If $u=0$, use $R a_0\cdot(p-j)+X_1-q\ge0$ for any $R>0$ strictly exceeding $(q+r_p)/|a_0\cdot(p-j)|$ at all bases with nonzero $a_0\cdot(p-j)$. This finite choice leaves every strictly included fiber full and every strictly excluded fiber empty. At $a_0\cdot(p-j)=0$ the original closed cap captured the whole fiber, and the replacement takes a subset of it. Here $w=e_1$. This proves the per-fiber comparison in all cases.

Every cap in the restricted class is a closed ambient halfspace through $(j,y_j)$, so the two infima agree. The bounded shared tilt lemma applies with the same positive radii, $j$, $q$ and $\Delta$, preserving all the cap masses under restriction and giving a minimum.

If the simplices are actual fibers of a convex set, every convex combination of the fibers whose base is $j$ lies in $r_jT_d$ by convexity. Thus the anchored Minkowski inclusion in accepted fact 155691fc3768c06f holds for the nonempty compact convex sets $K_p=r_pT_d$. Applying that fact at $u=e_1$ gives the required $b\in\operatorname{span}(P-j)$, since $h_{K_p}(e_1)=r_p$. No relative-interior or lattice hypothesis is needed.
