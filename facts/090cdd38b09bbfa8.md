## Statement

Take a finite set $P\subset\mathbb R^n$, positive prior $\pi_z$ and fixed uniform conditional laws on positive compact convex fibers. Let $A_t$ be progressively measurable symmetric positive semidefinite, with $\int_0^T\|A_t\|\,dt<\infty$ almost surely for every finite $T$. Put $Q_t=\int_0^tA_s\,ds$, and assume a finite continuous solution
$$
p_z(t)\propto\pi_z e^{a_t\cdot z-z^{\mathsf T}Q_tz/2},\qquad
m_t=\sum_zp_z(t)z,\qquad da_t=A_tm_t\,dt+A_t^{1/2}dW_t,\quad a_0=0
$$
exists for Brownian motion $W$. For actual fixed closed caps $H_j$, let $g_j(z)=|K_z\cap H_j|/|K_z|$, $G_j=\sum_zp_zg_j(z)$, and $c_j=\operatorname{Cov}_{p}(g_j,z)$. Then $dp_z=p_z(z-m)^{\mathsf T}A^{1/2}dW$ and $dG_j=c_j^{\mathsf T}A^{1/2}dW$, with $d\langle G_i,G_j\rangle=c_i^{\mathsf T}Ac_j\,dt$. These are bounded martingales. All $G_j$ in a finite menu are pathwise constant if and only if $\operatorname{range}(A_t)\subseteq\operatorname{span}\{c_j(p_t)\}^{\perp}$ almost everywhere for $dt\,d\mathbb P$. The same condition is equivalent to preserving the initially active bounds $G_j(t)\le G_j(0)$ almost surely for all $t$.

## Proof

Let $w_z=\pi_z\exp(a_t\cdot z-z^{\mathsf T}Q_tz/2)$ and $Z=\sum_z w_z$. Itô's formula gives $dw_z/w_z=z^{\mathsf T}A m\,dt+z^{\mathsf T}A^{1/2}dW$: the exponent's $-z^{\mathsf T}Az/2$ drift cancels the exponential correction, also when $A$ is singular. The finite-variation $Q$ has no quadratic covariations. Thus $dZ/Z=m^{\mathsf T}A m\,dt+m^{\mathsf T}A^{1/2}dW$. The quotient rule for $w_z/Z$ gives stochastic coefficient $p_z(z-m)^{\mathsf T}A^{1/2}$ and the two drift contributions $p_z(z-m)^{\mathsf T}A m$ and its negative. Summing over $z$ with fixed coefficients $g_j$ gives the cap dynamics and their brackets, including closed pure-base caps without any smoothness of traces. Bounded local martingales are martingales on each finite interval.

A continuous local martingale is indistinguishable from its initial value exactly when its quadratic variation is identically zero. Here this means $c_j^{\mathsf T}A c_j=0$ almost everywhere. Positive semidefiniteness gives the equivalent $Ac_j=0$, which for symmetric $A$ means $\operatorname{range}A$ is orthogonal to $c_j$. The finite menu allows the joint almost-everywhere assertion. If instead the initially tight upper bounds hold, at each deterministic time the nonnegative random variable $G_j(0)-G_j(t)$ has expectation zero. Apply this at rational times, then use continuity to conclude pathwise constancy simultaneously. The converse is immediate. In particular full span forces $A=0$; rank deficiency only provides instantaneous choices and does not ensure a feedback solution that preserves rank.
