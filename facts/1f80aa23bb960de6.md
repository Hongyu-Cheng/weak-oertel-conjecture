## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and, for \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\), put
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
\qquad
I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
\qquad
W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(G\) be the global complete metronoid-core capacity used in accepted fact c81f02314cb34f8b. For each parity class \(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\), put
\[
A_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi\pmod 2}a_z.
\tag{2}
\]
Call a nonempty parity class \(\xi\) good when
\[
\max_{z\equiv\xi\pmod2}a_z\le\frac{A_\xi}{4}.
\]
Use the half-strength canonical product reservoir \(r\) of accepted facts c81f02314cb34f8b and fc18c3c4832f0bfb: on every good parity class,
\[
r_{\{u,v\}}=\frac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi}
\qquad
(u\ne v,\ u\equiv v\equiv\xi\pmod2),
\tag{3}
\]
and \(r_e=0\) otherwise. Put \(Q:=|r|>0\).

Retain the load-minimal pooled system of accepted fact fc18c3c4832f0bfb. Thus each positive-reservoir edge \(e=\{u,v\}\) has a load \(0\le\omega_e\le r_e\), target
\[
m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2}\in\mathbb Z^n,
\tag{4}
\]
positive root mass
\[
\vartheta_e:=2^{-(d+1)}
\bigl(a_u^{1/d}+a_v^{1/d}\bigr)^d,
\]
and, when \(\omega_e>0\), a point \(\ell_e\in\mathsf M_{\vartheta_e}(C_{m_e})\). All edge sums below run over these reservoir edges. For each loaded target \(m\), define
\[
\Omega_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}\omega_e,
\qquad
\kappa_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{\omega_e}{\vartheta_e},
\qquad
\chi_m:=\frac1{\max\{1,\kappa_m\}},
\tag{5}
\]
and let \(k_m\) be the number of parity colors represented by positive-load edges at \(m\). Put
\[
\widehat E:=\{m:\Omega_m>0\},
\qquad
\Omega:=\sum_m\Omega_m,
\qquad
\widehat\Omega:=\sum_m\chi_m\Omega_m,
\tag{6}
\]
\[
x_m:=\frac1{\Omega_m}\sum_{e:m_e=m}\omega_e\ell_e
\qquad(m\in\widehat E),
\tag{7}
\]
and
\[
\rho_J:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E),
\qquad
r_J:=
\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
\dim\operatorname{span}\{x_m-s(m):m\in\widehat E\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Write
\[
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\tag{9}
\]
Assume the canonical hard nonterminal inequalities
\[
G<\frac Q{100},
\qquad
\Omega>\frac{99Q}{100},
\qquad
\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}\frac{\widehat\Omega}{r_J+1}<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{10}
\]
Then every loaded target satisfies
\[
\boxed{\Omega_m\le\frac G4<\frac Q{400},}
\tag{11}
\]
the number of loaded targets and the base rank satisfy
\[
\boxed{|\widehat E|\ge397,}
\qquad
\boxed{\rho_J\ge3,}
\tag{12}
\]
and the pooled mass satisfies
\[
\boxed{\widehat\Omega\ge2^{2-n}\Omega.}
\tag{13}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\boxed{r_J+1>99\gamma_d\,2^{2-\rho_J}.}
\tag{14}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_J=3&\Longrightarrow r_J\ge18,\\
\rho_J=4&\Longrightarrow r_J\ge9,\\
\rho_J=5&\Longrightarrow r_J\ge4,\\
\rho_J=6&\Longrightarrow r_J\ge2,\\
\rho_J=7&\Longrightarrow r_J\ge1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]

## Proof

For one good parity class \(\xi\), equation (3) gives
\[
\sum_{\substack{u<v\\u,v\equiv\xi\pmod2}}r_{\{u,v\}}
=\frac{A_\xi^2-\sum_{u\equiv\xi}a_u^2}{8A_\xi}
\le\frac{A_\xi}{8}.
\tag{16}
\]
Summing over the good classes and discarding the nonnegative masses of all other physical fibers yields
\[
Q\le\frac W8.
\tag{17}
\]
In particular, (10) and (17) give
\[
G<\frac Q{100}\le\frac W{800}<\frac W2.
\tag{18}
\]
The positive-fiber cap in accepted fact c81f02314cb34f8b therefore applies and gives
\[
a_z\le2^{-n}G
\qquad(z\in I_+).
\tag{19}
\]

Fix \(m\in\widehat E\). If \(m_e=m\) and \(\omega_e>0\), accepted fact fc18c3c4832f0bfb gives \(\ell_e\in\mathsf M_{\vartheta_e}(C_m)\). Hence
\[
0<\vartheta_e\le\operatorname{vol}_d(C_m)=a_m,
\tag{20}
\]
so in particular \(m\in I_+\). Therefore
\[
\Omega_m
=\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{\omega_e}{\vartheta_e}\vartheta_e
\le a_m\kappa_m.
\tag{21}
\]
There are only \(2^n\) parity colors, and the colorwise pooling estimate in accepted fact fc18c3c4832f0bfb gives
\[
\kappa_m\le\frac{k_m}{4}\le2^{n-2}.
\tag{22}
\]
Combining (19), (21), and (22) proves
\[
\Omega_m\le2^{n-2}a_m\le\frac G4<\frac Q{400},
\tag{23}
\]
which is (11). Since \(\widehat E\) is finite and \(\Omega=\sum_{m\in\widehat E}\Omega_m\), equations (10) and (23) imply
\[
\frac{99Q}{100}<\Omega<|\widehat E|\frac Q{400}.
\tag{24}
\]
Thus \(|\widehat E|>396\), and its integrality proves the first assertion in (12).

Equation (21), together with (22), also gives
\[
\sum_{m\in\widehat E}a_m
\ge2^{2-n}\sum_{m\in\widehat E}\Omega_m
=2^{2-n}\Omega.
\tag{25}
\]
Suppose for contradiction that \(\rho_J\le2\), and put
\[
A:=\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E),
\qquad
D_A:=I_+\cap A,
\qquad
B:=\operatorname{aff}(D_A),
\qquad
q:=\dim B,
\qquad
W_A:=\sum_{z\in D_A}a_z.
\tag{26}
\]
By (20), \(\widehat E\subseteq D_A\). Since \(D_A\subseteq A\) and \(A=\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E)\), it follows that \(B=A\), and hence
\[
q=\rho_J\le2,
\qquad
W_A\ge2^{2-n}\Omega.
\tag{27}
\]
The rank-at-most-two relative-core theorem in accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c now gives
\[
G\ge\frac1{64}\min\{W,2^{n-q}W_A\}.
\tag{28}
\]
Also \(\Omega\le Q\), because \(0\le\omega_e\le r_e\) for every edge. Hence (17) gives \(W\ge8Q\ge\Omega\), while (27) gives
\[
2^{n-q}W_A
\ge2^{n-\rho_J}2^{2-n}\Omega
=2^{2-\rho_J}\Omega
\ge\Omega.
\tag{29}
\]
Equations (10), (28), and (29) therefore imply
\[
G\ge\frac\Omega{64}
>\frac{99Q}{6400}
>\frac Q{100},
\tag{30}
\]
a contradiction. This proves \(\rho_J\ge3\).

Because \(\widehat E\subseteq\mathbb R^n\), one always has \(\rho_J\le n\). The just-proved inequality \(\rho_J\ge3\) therefore implies
\[
n\ge3.
\tag{31}
\]
For every loaded target, \(1\le k_m\le2^n\). Accepted fact fc18c3c4832f0bfb gives
\[
\chi_m\ge\min\left\{1,\frac4{k_m}\right\}.
\tag{32}
\]
By (31), \(1\ge2^{2-n}\), and \(k_m\le2^n\) gives \(4/k_m\ge2^{2-n}\). Thus (32) yields
\[
\chi_m\ge2^{2-n}
\qquad(m\in\widehat E).
\tag{33}
\]
Multiplication by \(\Omega_m\) and summation prove (13).

Using (13) and the strict load lower bound in (10) in the last inequality of (10) gives
\[
\frac Q{100}
>\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}\frac{\widehat\Omega}{r_J+1}
>\frac{99Q}{100}\,
\frac{\gamma_d2^{2-\rho_J}}{r_J+1}.
\tag{34}
\]
Since \(Q>0\), rearranging (34) proves (14).

Finally, accepted fact 2fe46f47d19112ea proves
\[
\gamma_d>\frac4{11}.
\tag{35}
\]
The quantity \(r_J\) is a nonnegative integer. For \(\rho_J=3,4,5,6,7\), equations (14) and (35), respectively, give
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\rho_J & r_J+1\text{ is strictly greater than} & \text{integer consequence}\\
\hline
3 & 18 & r_J+1\ge19,\\
4 & 9 & r_J+1\ge10,\\
5 & 9/2 & r_J+1\ge5,\\
6 & 9/4 & r_J+1\ge3,\\
7 & 9/8 & r_J+1\ge2.
\end{array}
\tag{36}
\]
Subtracting one in each row proves (15).
