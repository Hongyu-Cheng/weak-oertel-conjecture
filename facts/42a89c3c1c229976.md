## Statement

Let $(K_z)_{z\in P}$ be actual positive compact convex $d$-fibers, $d\ge1$, compatible on a finite complete lattice base. Use summed volume $\mu$, mass $M$, lifted fiber surface measure $dS$, and pooled within-fiber covariance norm $\Gamma$. In the unbuffered uniform outward-smoothed problem fix width parameter $h>0$, with ramp $\Psi(q)=\min(1,\max(0,1+q))$. At any own-interior source $(p_i,y_i)$ take a finite fixed balanced response menu $\nu_{ij}\ge0$, $\sum_j\nu_{ij}=1$. For each cap let $\Gamma(u_{ij})=1$, its through-mark margin be $\ell_{ij}(z,X)=a_{ij}\cdot(z-p_i)+u_{ij}\cdot(X-y_i)$, with $y_i\in\operatorname{int}K_{p_i}$, and write $W_{ij}=\Psi(\ell_{ij}/h)$, $W_i=\sum_j\nu_{ij}W_{ij}$. Set $S_{ij}=\int\Psi'(\ell_{ij}/h)\,d\mu/h$ and $A_{ij}=\int(\ell_{ij}/h)\Psi'(\ell_{ij}/h)\,d\mu$, so $\sum_j\nu_{ij} S_{ij}u_{ij}=0$ and assume $S_{ij}\le S_{\max}$.

For any one menu atom of weight $w_i>0$, the field $(\ell_{ij})_+$ divided by its total surface integral is an actual nonnegative convex inward field $\Phi_i$ with unit total loss and $\int W_i\Phi_i\,dS\ge w_i$. Now suppose finitely many groups, numbered $1,\dots,k$, are deepest source groups, and choose one such atom in each group and put $\Phi=\sum_i\Phi_i$, $D=\int\Phi\,dS=k$. For $J_{ij}=\sum_z\int_{\partial K_z}\Phi(z,X)[u_{ij}\cdot(X-g_z)]^2\,dS$, with $g_z$ the old fiber centroid, the group loss
$$
L_i=\int W_i\Phi\,dS+\sum_j\nu_{ij}A_{ij}(D-J_{ij})/(2M)
$$
satisfies $L_i-\beta_h D\ge w_i-k[\beta_h+hS_{\max}/(2M)]$, where $\beta_h$ is the old normalized smoothed maximum.

Assume the fixed-menu actual upper-cover property for this combined positive-width deformation, namely that along its normal cuts the normalized global maximum is at most $\beta_h-(t/M)\min_i(L_i-\beta_h D)+o(t)$ as $t\downarrow0$. If $\min_i w_i\ge\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$ and $\beta_h+hS_{\max}/(2M)<\gamma_d/k$, that maximum strictly decreases along this actual path. Thus an actual local minimum with all these premises is impossible.

## Proof

A through-mark unshifted cap with $u_{ij}\ne0$ at an own-interior mark has strictly positive margin on a relatively open part of the own boundary: the maximum margin on the own body is positive and attained on its boundary. Every such nonempty relatively open part has positive surface measure, by the radial parametrization from an inball, or directly by counting for an interval. The positive part of the affine margin is continuous, nonnegative and convex in all physical coordinates. Its surface integral is finite and strictly positive. Divide by it to obtain $\Phi_i$. By 8820984cf6a6942e the normal cuts with this normal-independent field are an actual positive compatible inward family, with this speed almost everywhere on the original surface. The radial volume formula gives total first loss $\int\Phi_i\,dS=1$. On the positive support of the hinge the chosen cap profile is exactly one. The other menu profiles are nonnegative, giving $\int W_i\Phi_i\,dS\ge w_i$. This single-atom assertion needs no other source group.

The sum of the fields belongs to the same cone, so one actual deformation realizes their combined speed. Its total first loss is $D=k$. The cross captures are nonnegative, hence $\int W_i\Phi\,dS\ge w_i$ for every $i$. Also $\Psi'$ is supported on $[-1,0]$ and nonnegative, giving $-hS_{\max}\le-hS_{ij}\le A_{ij}\le0$, with the two kink levels irrelevant to volume. Since $J_{ij}\ge0$, each covariance correction is at least $-hS_{\max}D/(2M)$ after averaging the probability menu. This proves the loss bound. The assumed upper-cover estimate then implies strict descent when its common lower bound is positive. This estimate is a premise about the actual maximum under the chosen whole menu, not a consequence of properness of one own cap.
