## Statement

Fix \(c_0\in(0,1/e)\) and \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\). Let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body with nonempty mixed-integer section and positive total mixed mass. Write
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
\qquad
W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}a_z>0,
\]
and put \(G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)\).

Retain one common instantiation of the canonical half-strength product reservoir and the zero-fresh self-saturated switch transport in accepted facts 6b09aae189415c70 and cb96640c07ffd5fa. Thus their reservoir mass \(Q>0\) is the same quantity, every reservoir edge is an unordered off-diagonal same-parity pair \(e=\{u_e,v_e\}\), its integer target is
\[
m_e:=\frac{u_e+v_e}{2},
\]
and the self-saturated capacity \(q^{\mathrm{sat}}\), extended by zero off its predecessor support, has total mass
\[
\Omega_b:=\sum_e q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}>\frac{97}{100}Q.
\tag{1}
\]
Assume that this common instantiation lies on the canonical hard small-capacity side of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70, namely
\[
\mathcal A(C)
:=R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
+R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+2^nh_{\max}(C)
<\frac{Q}{100},
\tag{2}
\]
with exactly the nonnegative capacity normalizations of that accepted fact.

Define the canonical source endpoint loads
\[
\beta_z:=d_z(q^{\mathrm{sat}})
=\sum_{e\ni z}q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}
\qquad(z\in\mathbb Z^n)
\tag{3}
\]
and the canonical physical-target loads
\[
\eta_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}
\qquad(m\in\mathbb Z^n).
\tag{4}
\]
Accepted fact cb96640c07ffd5fa gives
\[
0\leq\beta_z\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
\qquad
\sum_z\beta_z=2\Omega_b,
\qquad
\sum_m\eta_m=\Omega_b.
\tag{5}
\]

Assume explicitly that \(C\) is a counterexample at the least positive base dimension \(n\) for the fixed constant \(c_0\), in the exact normalization of accepted fact 1e3269a0e9b5695a. Equivalently for the present argument, the lower-dimensional induction hypothesis in that fact holds and every mixed-integer point of \(C\) has depth strictly less than \(c_0 2^{-n}W\).

For a nonempty set \(E\subseteq\mathbb Z^n\), choose \(z_0\in E\) and define its primitive affine lattice hull and rank by
\[
\Gamma(E):=
\bigl(\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}(E-z_0)\bigr)\cap\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad
L(E):=z_0+\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\Gamma(E),
\qquad
\rho(E):=\operatorname{rank}\Gamma(E).
\tag{6}
\]
These definitions are independent of the chosen \(z_0\). Put
\[
E_{\mathrm{src}}:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:\beta_z>0\},
\qquad
E_{\mathrm{tar}}:=\{m\in\mathbb Z^n:\eta_m>0\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Both sets are nonempty. Then:

1. The source endpoint support has full primitive affine lattice rank:
\[
\boxed{\rho(E_{\mathrm{src}})=n.}
\tag{8}
\]

2. For every primitive affine lattice subspace \(L\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) of codimension \(c\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\),
\[
\boxed{
\frac{\sum_{z\in L\cap\mathbb Z^n}\beta_z}
{\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\beta_z}
<\frac{803}{582}\,2^{-c}.}
\tag{9}
\]

3. For every affine hyperplane \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^n\), with no rationality or primitivity hypothesis,
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n\setminus H}\beta_z
>\frac{361}{600}Q.}
\tag{10}
\]

4. The physical-target support has primitive affine lattice rank
\[
\boxed{\rho(E_{\mathrm{tar}})\geq n-3.}
\tag{11}
\]
In particular,
\[
2^{n-\rho(E_{\mathrm{tar}})}\leq8.
\tag{12}
\]
No assertion is made about any continuous residual rank, affine synchronization of switch centers, absorption of the stationary or transport energy, closure of the zero-fresh branch, or the locked final theorem.

## Proof

The reservoir inequality in accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 is
\[
Q\geq\frac{3}{32}(W-4G).
\tag{13}
\]
All six summands in (2) are nonnegative, so (2) gives \(G<Q/100\). Rearranging (13) therefore yields the strict global estimate
\[
W\leq\frac{32}{3}Q+4G
<\left(\frac{32}{3}+\frac1{25}\right)Q
=\frac{803}{75}Q.
\tag{14}
\]

Because \(C\) is a counterexample at the least positive base dimension for the same fixed \(c_0\), accepted fact 1e3269a0e9b5695a applies. Hence every proper primitive affine lattice subspace \(L\) of rank \(q<n\) satisfies
\[
W_L:=\sum_{z\in L\cap\mathbb Z^n}a_z
<2^{q-n}W.
\tag{15}
\]

We first prove (8). Equations (1) and (5) give
\[
\sum_z\beta_z=2\Omega_b>\frac{97}{50}Q.
\tag{16}
\]
Let \(L_{\mathrm{src}}:=L(E_{\mathrm{src}})\). From the endpoint ledger in (5),
\[
\begin{aligned}
W_{L_{\mathrm{src}}}
&\geq\sum_{z\in E_{\mathrm{src}}}a_z\\
&\geq4\sum_z\beta_z\\
&=8\Omega_b\\
&>\frac{194}{25}Q.
\end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]
If \(\rho(E_{\mathrm{src}})\leq n-1\), then (15) and (14) give
\[
W_{L_{\mathrm{src}}}
<2^{-1}W
<\frac{803}{150}Q,
\tag{18}
\]
contradicting (17), since \(194/25=1164/150>803/150\). Therefore \(\rho(E_{\mathrm{src}})=n\).

Next let \(L\) be a primitive affine lattice subspace of codimension \(c\geq1\), so its rank is \(n-c<n\). Equations (5), (15), and (14) imply
\[
\sum_{z\in L\cap\mathbb Z^n}\beta_z
\leq\frac14W_L
<2^{-c-2}W
<\frac{803}{75}\,2^{-c-2}Q.
\tag{19}
\]
Divide (19) by (16). Since
\[
\frac{(803/75)2^{-c-2}}{97/50}
=\frac{803}{582}2^{-c},
\tag{20}
\]
equation (9) follows.

We now treat an arbitrary affine hyperplane \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^n\). This includes irrational hyperplanes and rational hyperplanes whose displayed normal is nonprimitive. Let
\[
E_H:=\{z\in H\cap\mathbb Z^n:\beta_z>0\}.
\tag{21}
\]
If \(E_H=\varnothing\), then (16) immediately gives (10). Suppose \(E_H\ne\varnothing\), choose \(z_H\in E_H\), and put
\[
A_H:=\operatorname{aff}_{\mathbb R}E_H,
\qquad
\Gamma_H:=(A_H-z_H)\cap\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad
L_H:=z_H+\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\Gamma_H.
\tag{22}
\]
The integer differences of points of \(E_H\) span \(A_H-z_H\). Thus \(\Gamma_H\) has rank \(\dim A_H\), is saturated in \(\mathbb Z^n\), and \(L_H=A_H\subseteq H\). Consequently \(L_H\) is a primitive affine lattice subspace of rank at most \(n-1\). Moreover every loaded lattice point of \(H\) lies in \(L_H\), while \(L_H\subseteq H\), so
\[
\sum_{z\in H\cap\mathbb Z^n}\beta_z
=\sum_{z\in L_H\cap\mathbb Z^n}\beta_z.
\tag{23}
\]
Using (5), (15), and (14), now only with \(\operatorname{rank}L_H\leq n-1\), gives
\[
\sum_{z\in H\cap\mathbb Z^n}\beta_z
\leq\frac14W_{L_H}
<\frac18W
<\frac{803}{600}Q.
\tag{24}
\]
Subtract (24) from (16):
\[
\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n\setminus H}\beta_z
>\left(\frac{97}{50}-\frac{803}{600}\right)Q
=\frac{361}{600}Q,
\tag{25}
\]
which proves (10) for every affine hyperplane without any arithmetic hypothesis on \(H\).

It remains to prove (11). For every \(m\in E_{\mathrm{tar}}\), accepted fact cb96640c07ffd5fa gives positive-root masses \(\vartheta_e\leq a_m\) for the edges with target \(m\), together with the exact physical-target budget
\[
\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\vartheta_e}\leq1.
\tag{26}
\]
Therefore
\[
\eta_m
=\sum_{e:m_e=m}
\frac{q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\vartheta_e}\vartheta_e
\leq a_m
\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\vartheta_e}
\leq a_m.
\tag{27}
\]
Let \(L_{\mathrm{tar}}:=L(E_{\mathrm{tar}})\). Equations (1), (5), and (27) give
\[
W_{L_{\mathrm{tar}}}
\geq\sum_{m\in E_{\mathrm{tar}}}a_m
\geq\sum_m\eta_m
=\Omega_b
>\frac{97}{100}Q.
\tag{28}
\]
If \(n\leq3\), then the nonemptiness of \(E_{\mathrm{tar}}\) gives \(\rho(E_{\mathrm{tar}})\geq0\geq n-3\). Suppose \(n\geq4\) and, for contradiction, \(\rho(E_{\mathrm{tar}})\leq n-4\). The primitive affine lattice subspace \(L_{\mathrm{tar}}\) is then proper, and (15) and (14) yield
\[
W_{L_{\mathrm{tar}}}
<2^{-4}W
<\frac{803}{1200}Q
<\frac{97}{100}Q,
\tag{29}
\]
contradicting (28), since \(803<1164\). This proves (11), and (12) follows by exponentiating \(n-\rho(E_{\mathrm{tar}})\leq3\). The argument used only lattice support, load, and fiber-capacity ledgers, so it supplies none of the continuous-rank or terminal conclusions expressly excluded in the statement.
