## Statement

Suppose actual proper sections of a tetrahedron share barycentric section centroid $b$. Let $C=27$ times the product of its three smallest coordinates. Each corresponding oriented cap fraction $F$ satisfies $F(1-F)\le C$, strictly for a strict quadrilateral section. If exactly one coordinate $b_h>1/3$, every strict quadrilateral cap capturing $h$ satisfies
$$
F>C,\qquad F(1-F)<C.
$$
For $C<1/4$ such a strict cap has $F$ outside the closed interval with endpoints $(1\pm\sqrt{1-4C})/2$.

## Proof

Apply actual-tetrahedral-cap-variance-all-omissions to each section and omit a largest coordinate. If $b_h>1/3$ is unique then $h$ is the largest coordinate. For a strict quadrilateral cap containing $h$, its other captured vertex has centroid coordinate at most $1/3$. The strict quadrilateral case of tetrahedral-specified-captured-cap-product therefore applies to the specified captured index $h$ and gives $F>27\prod_{j\ne h}b_j=C$. Solving $F(1-F)<C$ gives the stated interval exclusion.
