## Statement

Use the joint actual cap-record and allocation limit on fixed finite positive fibers. In a retained group at index $j$, write $H_{jk}$ for its caps, $\omega_{jk}$ for their actual law weights, $T_{jk}$ and $\bar w_{jk}$ for their allocated coareas and full physical section means, and $u_{jk}$ for their continuous normals. Let the group's finitely many labeled contact marks $Y_{ja}=(p_a,y_{ja})$ converge with fixed bases and a uniform own inball radius $r_s>0$. Suppose every cap has margin $\delta_{ja}\ge0$ at every group contact, with $\max_a\delta_{ja}\to0$. Let $\Sigma_j$ be the converging covariance matrices from the cap normalization and assume the actual same-coefficient identities
$$
\begin{gathered}
\kappa_j=\sum_a\kappa_{ja},\qquad
e_{ja}=\kappa_{ja}\delta_{ja},\qquad e_j=\sum_a e_{ja},\qquad
\bar Y_j=\kappa_j^{-1}\sum_a\kappa_{ja}Y_{ja},\\
\omega_{jk}T_{jk}=\kappa_j\rho_{jk},\qquad \sum_k\rho_{jk}=1,\qquad
\bar w_{jk}=\bar Y_j-(e_j/\kappa_j)(0,\Sigma_j u_{jk}),\\
\kappa_j\sum_k\rho_{jk}u_{jk}=\sum_a e_{ja}b_{ja},
\qquad |b_{ja}|\le(1+1/N)/r_s,\quad N=d+1,
\end{gathered}
$$
with nonnegative weights. For a positive limiting group weight, after retaining the limits of the finitely many $\kappa_{ja}$ the limiting $\kappa>0$ and the coarea-weighted law is the probability $d\rho(H)=T_Hd\Lambda(H)/\kappa$. For $\rho$-almost every cap its allocated full section mean is $\bar Y=\kappa^{-1}\sum_a\kappa_aY_a$, and $\int u\,d\rho=0$. Every support cap passes through all of the group's limiting contacts and has a proper section there. The support caps cover the whole continuous space at every one of those bases. If $Q$ is separately identified with ordinary maximal depth and the marks are ordinary deepest, those through-mark caps are critical.

## Proof

Because the continuous normals have lengths bounded away from zero, the Euclidean distance of the current cap plane from each mark in its own fiber is $\delta_{ja}/|u_{jk}|$. Eventually it is at most $r_s/2$. The own section therefore contains a $(d-1)$-ball of radius at least $r_s/2$ and is proper. Endpoint counting handles $d=1$. Full proper coarea on that section gives a uniform positive lower bound for $T_{jk}$. The common outer bound and the lower normal bound give a uniform upper bound. Summing $\omega_{jk}T_{jk}=\kappa_j\rho_{jk}$ now bounds $\kappa_j$ above and bounds it away from zero whenever the group weight has a positive limit. Pass to a subsequence for the finitely many $\kappa_{ja}$. The allocation compactification and the continuity of coordinate tests give the joint limits of $\omega_{jk}T_{jk}$ and $\omega_{jk}T_{jk}\bar w_{jk}$ as measures of the record. The first has total mass $\kappa$ and disintegrates as $T_H\,d\Lambda(H)$. In the second identity, the error $e_j/\kappa_j$ is at most $\max_a\delta_{ja}\to0$. Testing against arbitrary continuous record functions therefore gives the common allocated mean $\bar Y$ almost everywhere for $\rho$. The bound on $b_{ja}$ in the balance equation gives $\left|\sum_k\rho_{jk}u_{jk}\right|\le (1+1/N)\max_a\delta_{ja}/r_s$. Testing the coarea-weighted limit with the continuous normal yields $\int u\,d\rho=0$.

Every own-proper threshold was unchanged by the record map. On the compact record space the margin at $Y_{ja}$ converges uniformly to the margin at $Y_a$. The vanishing offsets imply that the support of the limiting group law is carried by records passing through every $Y_a$. These points retain their own inballs, so the own sections remain proper. At any given contact base, if a point $X$ were outside all the support caps, then $u_H\cdot(X-y_a)<0$ for every support record. The continuous integrand has strictly negative maximum on the compact support, contradicting zero average. This proves the asserted cover. The extra depth and mark identification makes each mass-$Q$ through-mark cap attaining by the definition of ordinary depth.
