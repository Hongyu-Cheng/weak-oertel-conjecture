## Statement

Let $T$ be a regular $4$-simplex of volume $V$ with barycentric coordinates $\lambda_0,\ldots,\lambda_4$. Set $D_\delta=T\cap\{\lambda_2+\lambda_3+\lambda_4\le\delta\}$ for $0<\delta<1/10$, and $C_\delta=\operatorname{conv}((\{-1,1\}\times D_\delta)\cup(\{0\}\times T))$. The actual product $C_\delta\times[-2,2]$ in one additional integer coordinate has exactly fifteen positive $4$-dimensional integer fibers, the five repeats of $D_\delta,T,D_\delta$. If $v=(4\delta^3-3\delta^4)V$, its total mass is $\widehat M=5(V+2v)$ and every fiber has mass strictly less than $\widehat M/4$. Its pooled within-fiber covariance and resulting smoothing norm are exactly those of $C_\delta$.

## Proof

The old projection is $[-1,1]$ and the endpoint fibers are exposed. Because $D_\delta\subset T$, every combination of its points and points of $T$ landing at base zero lies in $T$; the full $T$ was already prescribed there. This gives the three old integer fibers. The new product repeats each at exactly $t=-2,-1,0,1,2$.

For uniform volume on $T$, the sum $r=\lambda_2+\lambda_3+\lambda_4$ has density proportional to $r^2(1-r)$: at fixed $r$ the three coordinates have a two-dimensional simplex of scale $r$ and the other two have a one-dimensional simplex of scale $1-r$, with a constant Jacobian in these linear coordinates. Normalization gives density $12r^2(1-r)$, so integration up to $\delta$ gives $v/V=4\delta^3-3\delta^4>0$. Every fiber mass is at most $V$ and the total $5(V+2v)>5V$ makes $V<\widehat M/4$. Finally the pooled covariance is the sum of centered within-fiber covariances weighted by fiber volume and divided by total mass. Its numerator and denominator are both multiplied by five, giving the same covariance and norm.
