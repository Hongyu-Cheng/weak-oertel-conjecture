## Statement

Let $\Lambda=c+L$ be an affine lattice and $P=\operatorname{conv}(P)\cap\Lambda$ be finite and nonempty. Work in its relative affine span and let $k(T)$ count the occupied classes of $T$ modulo $2L$, with $k=k(P)$. Fix $d\ge1$. For each $j$ let $K_{j,z}$, $z\in P$, be positive convex $d$-bodies with canonical hull $C_j=\operatorname{conv}\bigcup_z(\{z\}\times K_{j,z})$ whose actual sampled fibers are exactly $K_{j,z}$. Assume $K_{j,z}\to K_z$ in Hausdorff distance with every $K_z$ a convex body of positive volume. Set $V(T)=\sum_{z\in T}|K_z|$ and $M_j=\sum_z|K_{j,z}|$. Suppose $V(T)\le k(T)V(P)/k$ for every nonempty proper closed minor $T=P\cap\operatorname{conv}(T)$.

Take positive continuous concave $h_j$ on $C_j$, and norms $\Gamma_j$ on continuous covectors converging uniformly on the Euclidean unit sphere to a norm. For a sampled mark $(p,y)$, use the infimum of summed fiber masses of the closed caps
$$
a\cdot(z-p)+u\cdot(X-y)+\left(\frac{h_j(p,y)E_{K_{j,p}}(y)}{M_j}\right)^{1/(d+1)}\Gamma_j(u)\ge0.
$$
Here $(a,u)\ne(0,0)$, and $E$ is the marked projective energy of d90ba05577427854, with value zero on own boundaries. Pure-base caps are unchanged, with the whole-base trace allowed in rank zero. If the maximum raw buffered depth $Q_j$ satisfies $Q_j/M_j\le b<1/k$ for every $j$, then
$$
\sup_j\max_{z\in P,\ X\in K_{j,z}}h_j(z,X)<\infty .
$$
In particular this applies to $h_j=\varepsilon_j^{d+1}L_j$ for positive concave parameters $L_j$.

## Proof

Put $m_{j,z}=\max_{X\in K_{j,z}}h_j(z,X)$. These arrays are sampled-concave: for an anchored combination choose maximizers in its input fibers and combine them in the actual target fiber. If $A_j=\max_z m_{j,z}$ is unbounded, pass to a subsequence with $A_j\to\infty$ and $m_{j,z}/A_j\to a_z$. The limit array is nonnegative, midpoint-concave at sampled midpoints, and has maximum one. Apply dba5cdade881e9ee with weights $|K_z|$ and the assumed minor quotas to find $p$ with $a_p>0$ whose limiting closed pure-base depth is at least $V(P)/k$.

For a nonnegative concave function with maximum $m$ attained at $X_0$ on a convex $d$-body $K$, the superlevel at $tm$ contains $tX_0+(1-t)K$ for $0\le t\le1$. Integration gives average at least $m/(d+1)$, and Jensen gives the same lower bound at the centroid. Thus $h_j(p,g_{j,p})\to\infty$ at the current centroids. Their energy is $|K_{j,p}|$ by d90ba05577427854. Volumes converge, the limiting own volume is positive, and the norms have a common positive lower bound on the unit sphere for large $j$. The cap displacement there tends to infinity uniformly for unit $u$.

The current fiber coordinates have a common bound. Once the displacement exceeds their common diameter, every nonvertical cap through this centroid with displacement contains every whole fiber in its nonnegative base-drift trace. Each such closed trace has weight at least the current pure-base depth at $p$. The pure-base cuts themselves are allowed, so depth at the centroid equals that pure-base depth.
