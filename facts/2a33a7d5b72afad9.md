## Statement

Let a regular equal-budget minimal positive circuit have unit continuous normals $u_j$ and positive kernel generator $\rho$ with $\sum_j\rho_j u_j=0$. Let $S_j>0$ be the full proper section coarea and $\theta_j=(\rho_j/S_j)/\sum_h(\rho_h/S_h)$. Assume there are no positive-area supporting coincidences and the circuit's normalized first response is neutral under every translation of one independently movable positive-volume fiber $K_z$. Then $S_j(z)/S_j$ is independent of $j$, where $S_j(z)$ is the proper section area on that fiber, taken as zero for a nonproper trace. In particular either all cuts are proper there or none is.

## Proof

Translating $K_z$ by $tv$ changes its cap threshold in the old fiber by $-t u_j\cdot v$. Its cap mass derivative is $S_j(z)u_j\cdot v$. At a proper cut this follows from integration of the parallel section areas and proper-section continuity, and at a nonproper cut the derivative is zero because the limiting supporting trace is empty or surface-null. Indeed the projected nearby sections lie in shrinking neighborhoods of the projected limiting trace by compactness. Total mixed volume is unchanged. Equal-budget response weights therefore make the derivative proportional to $\sum_j\theta_j S_j(z)u_j\cdot v$. Neutrality for every $v$ puts the vector $(\theta_jS_j(z))_j$ in the one-dimensional kernel spanned by $\rho$. Substituting the formula for $\theta_j$ proves the ratio identity. Positivity of every $S_j$ gives the status consequence.
