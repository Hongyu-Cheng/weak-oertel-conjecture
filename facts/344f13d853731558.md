## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and assume the
canonical hypotheses and notation of accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`.
Thus
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
\]
\[
 Q>0,
 \qquad D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\rm sel}(C)+M_{\rm off}(C)+R_{\rm cloud}(C)+D.
\tag{1}
\]
Assume the exact canonical hard inequality
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{2}
\]
The load-minimal optimizer then has positive load \(\Omega\), source loads
\(\beta_z\), source points \(p_z^\flat\), and a full-dimensional
origin-symmetric body \(D_*\) satisfying
\[
 \sum_z\beta_z=2\Omega,
 \qquad \Omega\ge Q-R,
 \qquad 0<\beta_z\le\frac{a_z}{4}\quad(\beta_z>0),
\tag{3}
\]
\[
 p_z^\flat+\frac12D_z
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
 \qquad
 p_z^\flat+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z),
\tag{4}
\]
where \(D_z=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)-c_z\).  Put
\[
 P:=\{z:\beta_z>0\}.
\tag{5}
\]

For every nonempty \(E\subseteq P\), define
\[
 A_E:=\sum_{z\in E}a_z,
 \qquad
 \rho_E:=\dim\operatorname{aff}E,
\tag{6}
\]
\[
 r_E:=
 \min_{b:\operatorname{aff}E\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{p_z^\flat-b(z):z\in E\},
\tag{7}
\]
and
\[
 \Lambda_E:=
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_E}A_E}{2(r_E+1)}.
\tag{8}
\]
Then the same literal source points and weights give two distinct capacity
bounds:
\[
 \boxed{R_{\rm sel}(C)\ge\Lambda_E,\qquad D\ge\Lambda_E.}
\tag{9}
\]
The second inequality comes from the genuinely halfspace-dominated measure
\[
 \boxed{
 \sigma_E^\flat:=
 \frac{\gamma_d}{2}\sum_{z\in E}a_z
 \delta_{(z,p_z^\flat)}.}
\tag{10}
\]
In particular, the Pro measure
\[
 \gamma_d\sum_{z\in E}\beta_z\delta_{(z,p_z^\flat)}
\tag{11}
\]
is a positive submeasure of (10), so its proposed domination is valid as a
strictly weaker consequence.

For the full loaded source support, write
\(A_P,\rho_P,r_P,\Lambda_P\) as above.  Then
\[
 \boxed{2^{\rho_P}(r_P+1)>800\gamma_d2^n.}
\tag{12}
\]
Moreover, using the intrinsic residual-rank identity,
\[
 \boxed{
 \dim\operatorname{aff}
 \{(z,p_z^\flat):z\in P\}
 =\rho_P+r_P\ge n+9.}
\tag{13}
\]

Now let \(s=(s_e)\) be the retained product matching of accepted fact
`6b09aae189415c70`.  Let \(w=(w_e)\) be any nonzero retained submatching:
\[
 0\le w_e\le s_e,
 \qquad B_w:=\sum_e w_e>0.
\tag{14}
\]
Put
\[
 d_z(w):=\sum_{e\ni z}w_e,
 \qquad E_w:=\{z:d_z(w)>0\},
\tag{15}
\]
and let \(\rho_w,r_w\) be (6)--(7) for \(E=E_w\).  For every affine
\(b:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\), define
\[
 T_w(b):=
 \sum_{z\in E_w}d_z(w)
 \mathbf1_{\{\|p_z^\flat-b(z)\|_{D_*}\le1/2\}},
 \qquad T_w^*:=\sup_bT_w(b).
\tag{16}
\]
No goodness condition on an edge is imposed in (14).  Then
\[
 \boxed{G\ge T_w^*,}
\tag{17}
\]
and the three distinct summands \(G,R_{\rm sel}(C),D\) give the joint hard
screen
\[
 \boxed{
 T_w^*+
 8\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_w}\frac{B_w}{r_w+1}
 <\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{18}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{
 r_w+1>
 800\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_w}\frac{B_w}{Q}.}
\tag{19}
\]
If affine maps \(b_1,\ldots,b_K\) cover the source endpoints in the sense
\[
 z\in E_w\Longrightarrow
 \min_{1\le j\le K}\|p_z^\flat-b_j(z)\|_{D_*}\le\frac12,
\tag{20}
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{K>200\frac{B_w}{Q}.}
\tag{21}

For the full choice \(w=s\), write
\(\rho_{\rm src}(s),r_{\rm src}(s),T_s^*\) for these source quantities.
Then

 |s|>\frac{17}{25}Q,

\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\rm src}(s)+1>
 544\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\rm src}(s)},}
\tag{24}

and every cover (20) of its positive-degree source support has
\[
 \boxed{K\ge137.}
\tag{25}

Its source graph has affine dimension at least \(n+8\).

 I\ge O.

\tag{30}

The source graph over \(E_J\) has affine dimension at least \(n+7\).

Finally, source base independence has the following exact packet meaning.

\(z_0,\ldots,z_q\in P\) be affinely independent in the base space.  There is

## Proof

The hard inequality (2) implies \(R<Q\).  Hence the zero-load clause of
accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`, which would give \(R=Q\), is impossible.
Thus \(\Omega>0\), and all objects in (3)--(5) are defined.  That accepted
fact proves
\[
 \beta_z\le d_z(r)\le\frac{a_z}{4},
\tag{35}
\]
which gives the last inequality in (3).

Fix nonempty \(E\subseteq P\).  Equation (4) gives
\[
 p_z^\flat\in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)
 \qquad(z\in E).
\tag{36}
\]
The metronoid halfspace theorem `6769574fc1bd9f8d` therefore gives
\[
 \delta_C(z,p_z^\flat)\ge\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2}.
\tag{37}
\]
Use these points and weights \(\gamma_da_z/2\) in the defining supremum of
the residual-selector capacity `be359f8a6c628483`.  Their total weight is
\(\gamma_dA_E/2\), and their intrinsic ranks are exactly (6)--(7).  This
proves the first inequality in (9).

We next prove the second inequality independently.  Let
\(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be a closed ambient halfspace.  For every
\(z\in E\) whose atom \((z,p_z^\flat)\) lies in \(H\), the fiber section
\(H_z\) contains \(p_z^\flat\).  If it is a proper halfspace, (37) gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z)
 \ge\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2};
\]
the same inequality is immediate if \(H_z=\mathbb R^d\), and an empty
section cannot contain the atom.  Summing over the pairwise distinct source
fibers proves
\[
 \sigma_E^\flat(H)\le\mu_C(H).
\tag{38}
\]
Thus (10) is genuinely halfspace-dominated.  Its mass is
\(\gamma_dA_E/2\), and its ranks are (6)--(7).  The dominated-measure
residual theorem `be359f8a6c628483` gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)
 \ge\frac{\gamma_dA_E}
 {2^{\rho_E+1}(r_E+1)}.
\]
Multiplication by \(2^n\) proves the second inequality in (9).  Finally,
\(\gamma_d\beta_z\le\gamma_da_z/4\le\gamma_da_z/2\), so (11) is a positive
submeasure of (10), proving the stated audit of the Pro measure.

Take \(E=P\).  Equations (3) and (35) give
\[
 A_P=\sum_{z\in P}a_z
 \ge4\sum_{z\in P}\beta_z
 =8\Omega.
\tag{39}
\]
Consequently each bound in (9) is at least
\[
 4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}
 \frac{\Omega}{r_P+1}.
\tag{40}
\]
The two bounds charge the distinct summands \(R_{\rm sel}(C)\) and \(D\),
each exactly once.  Hence, with
\[
 \chi_P:=
 \frac{8\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}}{r_P+1},
\tag{41}
\]
equations (1), (3), and (40) give
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge R+R_{\rm sel}(C)+D
 \ge R+\chi_P\Omega
 \ge R+\chi_P(Q-R)
 \ge\min\{1,\chi_P\}Q.
\tag{42}
\]
The last inequality uses \(0\le R\le Q\).  Equation (2) forces
\(\chi_P<1/100\), which is exactly (12).

The intrinsic rank identity in `be359f8a6c628483` gives
\[
 \dim\operatorname{aff}\{(z,p_z^\flat):z\in P\}=\rho_P+r_P.
\tag{43}
\]
For every integer \(r\ge0\), one has \(r+1\le2^r\).  Also
\[
 \left(1+\frac1d\right)^d
 \le\sum_{k=0}^d\frac1{k!}<3,
\]
so \(\gamma_d>1/3\).  Equation (12) therefore gives
\[
 2^{\rho_P+r_P}
 \ge2^{\rho_P}(r_P+1)
 >\frac{800}{3}2^n
 >2^{n+8}.
\]
The left exponent is an integer, proving (13).

We prove the arbitrary-submatching assertions.  From (14)--(15) and the
canonical endpoint bounds,
\[
 0<d_z(w)\le d_z(s)\le\beta_z\le\frac{a_z}{4}
 \qquad(z\in E_w).
\tag{44}
\]
Double-counting edge incidences gives
\[
 A_{E_w}:=\sum_{z\in E_w}a_z
 \ge4\sum_zd_z(w)=8B_w.
\tag{45}
\]
Apply (9) with \(E=E_w\).  Equation (45) gives, separately,
\[
 R_{\rm sel}(C),D
 \ge4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_w}\frac{B_w}{r_w+1}.
\tag{46}
\]

It remains to prove the complete-core term.  Fix an affine map \(b\), and
let
\[
 E_b:=\{z\in E_w:\|p_z^\flat-b(z)\|_{D_*}\le1/2\}.
\tag{47}
\]
If \(E_b=\varnothing\), then \(T_w(b)=0\le G\).  Otherwise (4), symmetry
of \(D_*\), and (47) give
\[
 b(z)\in\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z)
 \qquad(z\in E_b).
\]
Scale one representing density by \(d_z(w)/\beta_z\le1\).  Its normalized
barycenter is unchanged, so
\[
 b(z)\in\mathsf M_{d_z(w)}(C_z)
 \qquad(z\in E_b).
\tag{48}
\]
Apply accepted fact `6d6fcf377e68c79c`, with continuous dimension zero, to
the positive base measure
\[
 \sum_{z\in E_b}d_z(w)\delta_z.
\]
It gives \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E_b\) such that every
closed base halfspace containing \(m\) carries at least
\(2^{-n}T_w(b)\).  For every barycentric representation
\(m=\sum_{z\in E_b}\alpha_zz\), affinity and (48) give
\[
 b(m)=\sum_{z\in E_b}\alpha_zb(z)
 \in\sum_{z\in E_b}\alpha_z
 \mathsf M_{d_z(w)}(C_z).
\]
Thus the masses \(d_z(w)\) and common point \(b(m)\) satisfy the literal
complete-core definition in `fbeb0a501b268d12`, and
\[
 G\ge\sum_{z\in E_b}d_z(w)=T_w(b).
\]
Taking the supremum over \(b\) proves (17).  Add (17) and the two distinct
bounds (46), then use (1)--(2), to obtain (18).  Dropping \(T_w^*\) and
rearranging proves (19).

If (20) holds, then every positive source degree is counted by at least one
tube, so
\[
 2B_w=\sum_zd_z(w)
 \le\sum_{j=1}^KT_w(b_j)
 \le KG<K\frac Q{100}.
\tag{49}
\]
This proves (21).  Notice that overlaps only increase the middle sum.

For completeness, we derive the full retained-mass estimate without adding
a predecessor.  Accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` gives
\[
 |s|\ge\frac38\left(2\Omega-G-\frac W{64}\right),
 \qquad
 \Omega\ge Q-R,
 \qquad
 Q\ge\frac3{32}(W-4G).
\tag{50}
\]
The last inequality gives \(W\le32Q/3+4G\).  Substitution in (50) yields
\[
 |s|\ge\frac{11}{16}Q-\frac34R-\frac{51}{128}G.
\tag{51}
\]
Since (2) gives \(R+G<Q/100\) and \(51/128<3/4\),
\[
 |s|>\left(\frac{11}{16}-\frac3{400}\right)Q
 =\frac{17}{25}Q,
\]
which proves (22).  Equations (18)--(19) and (21), with \(B_w=|s|\), now
give (23)--(25).  Since \(544\gamma_d>544/3>2^7\), the same argument as in
(43) gives source-graph affine dimension at least \(n+8\).

Assume (26).  The hard alternative in `6b09aae189415c70` gives
\(I>Q/4\).  By (27)--(28),
\[
 S\ge I-\sum_{e\in\mathcal B}s_e
 \ge I-G>\frac Q4-\frac Q{100}=\frac6{25}Q,
\]
proving (29).  Apply (18)--(19) and (21) to the literal submatching in
(28).  Substitution of (29) gives (30)--(32).  Finally,
\(192\gamma_d>64=2^6\), so the residual-rank identity gives graph affine
dimension at least \(n+7\).

We finish with the exact source-basis packet.  Let
\(z_0,\ldots,z_q\) be as in the statement, take the standard lattice
simplex
\[
 \Delta:=\operatorname{conv}\{0,e_1,\ldots,e_q\}\subseteq\mathbb R^q,
\]
and the integral affine injection
\[
 \Phi(t):=z_0+\sum_{i=1}^q(z_i-z_0)t_i.
\tag{52}
\]
Its rank is \(q\), and its only lattice points are its \(q+1\) vertices.
At vertex \(i\), use \(p_{z_i}^\flat\).  Equation (37) gives depth at least
\(\gamma_da_{z_i}/2\).  Apply the one-factor case of
`5b6523682250e7a5` with vertex roots
\[
 \left(\frac{\gamma_da_{z_i}}2\right)^{1/d}.
\]
Because the standard simplex has no other lattice points, its packet energy
is exactly the sum in (33).  The one-factor dominance theorem
`fbeb0a501b268d12` gives \(\mathcal E(\Pi_B)\le G\), and (2) gives the
strict final inequality in (34).  If \(q=n\), (52) has full rank, proving
the full-rank clause.

FRONTIER AUDIT
The preceding theorem does not turn source graph complexity into a
macroscopic multifactor packet.  The obstruction is exact.

1.  The ranks \(\rho_w,r_w\) concern the affine graph of the endpoint
points \((z,p_z^\flat)\).  Large \(r_w\) may be entirely continuous; it
does not force \(\rho_w=n\), a single source base with \(n\) independent
incident half-directions, or even two adjacent edges.  In particular an
endpoint-disjoint family is not a star.

2.  A base-affine basis does give the literal packet (33), but (34) shows
that every such one-factor basis is already weight-thin in the hard branch.
Support rank supplies no lower bound on the individual \(a_z\), \(\beta_z\),
or incidence loads of the selected basis.

3.  The accepted one-star and transverse-atlas interfaces require one
common anchor with jointly independent actual half-directions and compatible
completion cells, or a complete integral product grid with a positive
rank-one mass minorant.  Neither (12), (18), nor the cover bounds supply
those hypotheses.  For the \(I\)-bad submatching \(J\), high endpoint-graph
rank does not imply that the cross edges of one independent two-block star
all remain in \(J\).

4.  The reflected-cloud capacity is built from centroid averages
\(\bar c_e\) on many reservoir edges with one fixed midpoint.  Source
complexity of \(p_z^\flat=(c_z+p_z)/2\) forces neither concentration at one
midpoint nor residual rank of the centroid averages.  No cloud scores from
different midpoints may be summed.

Thus the exact missing source-basis-to-packet bridge is a weighted theorem
showing that the hard source field contains either (i) one common-anchor,
full-base-rank star whose compatible completion-cell mass gives packet
energy at least \(Q/100\), (ii) one integral multifactor product grid with a
rank-one minorant of that scale, or (iii) one fixed-midpoint centroid cloud
of score at least \(Q/100\).  The current source rank, tube-cover, product
matching, one-factor basis, and atlas statements do not imply any of these
three alternatives.
