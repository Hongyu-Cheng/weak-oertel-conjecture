## Statement

Use the three-fiber support-ratio erosion of be265e22b9ce0ce6 with its specified positive bodies and interior input centers $y_A,y_B$. Suppose their middle mark $y=(y_A+y_B)/2$ is the ordinary centroid of $K$. In original coordinates the deformation is precisely the ambient affine map
$$
F_\varepsilon(z,x)=(z,(1-\varepsilon)x+\varepsilon[y+z(y_B-y_A)/2]).
$$
It preserves every normalized mixed depth under the point correspondence, without a regularity hypothesis. For a fixed minimizing ambient cap $H$ through $(0,y)$ with old mass $Q$, suppose $\varepsilon\mapsto\mu_0(F_\varepsilon^{-1}H)$ is differentiable at zero. The first loss of this fixed cap is $dQ$, while total first loss is $dM$ for old total mass $M$. Thus the first loss ratio is $Q/M$. The cap differentiability condition holds for a pure-base cap and for a cap whose nonvertical traces are proper or strictly full or strictly empty.

## Proof

By the strict convexity and moment identity in d90ba05577427854, the unique projective parameter at the centroid is zero. The tangent-extended function is then evaluated only at $1$ and equals one. Each input cut shrinks its centered support by the factor $1-\varepsilon$, and the gauge cut does the same at the middle. The displayed map has precisely those restrictions at $-1,0,1$. It bijects the ambient halfspaces and keeps the integer base. On each fiber its absolute Jacobian is $(1-\varepsilon)^d$, so every cap mass and the total mass acquire the same factor when the cap is transported. Taking infima over containing caps proves pointwise normalized invariance.

The pullback of the fixed cap through $(0,y)$ contains that same mark for both signs of small $\varepsilon$, because the map fixes the mark. Its mass therefore has a two-sided minimum at zero. Under the stated differentiability condition it has derivative zero. Pure-base pullbacks are constant. For a nonvertical proper trace, in each fiber the pullback keeps the continuous normal fixed and varies only its threshold smoothly. The section $(d-1)$-volume is continuous at an interior threshold, by concavity of its positive $(d-1)$st root for $d\ge2$, and is locally one for interval sections when $d=1$. Integrating these parallel sections gives differentiability. Strictly full and empty traces remain constant for small parameters. Finally the deformed mass of the original $H$ is $(1-\varepsilon)^d\mu_0(F_\varepsilon^{-1}H)$, yielding cap derivative $-dQ$ and total derivative $-dM$.
