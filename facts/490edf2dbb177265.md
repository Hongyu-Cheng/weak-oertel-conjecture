## Proof

Fact a7b09eaaf3adb06e gives these actual fibers, their nonaffine centroid graph, deepest middle mark, exact mass, fixed critical caps, coarea weights and projective root. The midpoint body equals $K_1$ by linear interpolation of the four vertices, so the endpoint support distances sum to $2H(n)$. Since $h<2t<99/100$, all support distances from $y$ are positive. Every field used here is continuous, nonnegative and convex in the physical point. Use 84cd8a85417b0d7a on each singleton with its old support ceiling to obtain nested positive fibers and the actual total and fixed-cap first losses.

For compatibility of the support-ratio path, apply Jensen with weights $H_{1-e_0}(n),H_{1+e_0}(n)$ to the two field arguments. Their weighted mean at an old input pair is the middle field argument at the pair midpoint. For the one-input paths, an old partner minimizing $x_1+x_2$ has sum $h$. Since $f$ decreases, the chosen endpoint field is at least twice the middle field at every actual midpoint with that endpoint. In either case the mean of the two endpoint fields is at least the middle field at every old pair. Adding the two support inequalities and dividing by two gives the middle constraint at each normal. Thus the hull preserves the exact integer fibers and their midpoint relation.

Let $u=x_1+x_2$. On the used facet arguments the tangent continuation is unneeded. Define the functions $F(u)=[1+c(u-2t)]^{-2}$ and $G(u)=[1+c((u+h)/2-2t)]^{-2}$. Their integrals over $[l,r]$ are
$$
I(l,r)=\frac{r-l}{[1+c(l-2t)][1+c(r-2t)]},\qquad
J(l,r)=\frac{r-l}{[1+c((l+h)/2-2t)][1+c((r+h)/2-2t)]}.
$$
This follows by integrating the reciprocal square of an affine function. The old field on either coordinate edge is $tF(u)$. On the inner diagonal its integral is $h(2t-h)F(h)$. On the outer diagonal the ratio $H/H_r$ changes $r-2t$ to $1-2t$ in the argument, so its integral is $r(r-2t)F(1)$. Here the diagonal support factor has a divisor $\sqrt2$, canceled by the diagonal arclength factor $\sqrt2$. For the one-input field the two coordinate-edge densities are $2tG(u)$, and the inner and outer total diagonal integrals are $2h(2t-h)G(h)$ and $2r(1-2t)G(r)$.

Cap $H_1$ includes the inner diagonal and the coordinate-edge ranges $h\le u\le 2t$, and misses the outer diagonal. At base $z=(r-1)/e_0$, $H_2$ requires $tx_1+(3t-1)x_2\ge t(r-2+4t)$. It misses the inner diagonal and the edge $x_1=0$, takes the edge $x_2=0$ for $r-2+4t\le u\le r$, and on the outer diagonal requires $x_1\ge r-2t$, a coordinate length of $2t$. Indeed its maximum on the inner diagonal is $th<t(r-2+4t)$, and the margin at the top of the $x_1=0$ edge is $(1-2t)(2t-r)<0$. The inequalities $h<2t<r$ and $h<r-2+4t<r$ hold at all three radii. Thus all traces are proper, and there are no positive-length cutting boundaries on any facet. The third cap follows by symmetry.
