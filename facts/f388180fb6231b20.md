## Statement

Let $P\subset\mathbb R^n$ be finite and nonempty, and let $K\subset\mathbb R^d$ be a full-dimensional compact convex body, $d\ge1$, with centroid zero and volume $V$. Suppose $K_p=c_p+r_pK$ with $r_p>0$ are compatible at all sampled bases: $\sum_p\lambda_p K_p\subset K_k$ whenever $k=\sum_p\lambda_p p$, $\lambda_p\ge0$ and $\sum_p\lambda_p=1$. Fix $j\in P$, $u\ne0$, and any minimizer $v$ of $u\cdot x$ on $K$. Put $a=-u\cdot v>0$ and $L_p=r_jv+r_p(K-v)$. There is a slope $b\in\mathbb R^n$ such that for every tilt $\beta\in\mathbb R^n$ and every $p\in P$ the trace of $\beta\cdot(p-j)+u\cdot(x-c_j)\ge0$ on $K_p$ has $d$-volume at least that of $(\beta+b)\cdot(p-j)+u\cdot x\ge0$ on $L_p$. In particular the raw summed volume of the original closed cap is at least
$$
 V\sum_{p\in P}r_p^d\,\Pr\!\left(r_pY\ge r_j-\frac{\beta+b}{a}\cdot(p-j)\right),
 \qquad Y=1+\frac{u\cdot X}{a},\quad X\text{ uniform on }K,\quad \mathbb E Y=1.
$$
The $L_p$ are compatible at all sampled bases; their lifted convex hull has exactly these integer fibers if $P=\operatorname{conv}(P)\cap\mathbb Z^n$. Pure-base caps have identical volumes on corresponding fibers.

## Proof

The centroid is interior, so $a>0$. Apply 155691fc3768c06f to the compatible fibers at $j$ with unit direction $-u/\lVert u\rVert$. Multiply its touching upper bound by $-\lVert u\rVert$ to obtain the touching lower bound for $u\cdot c_p-ar_p$, namely
$$u\cdot(c_p-c_j)\ge a(r_p-r_j)+b\cdot(p-j).$$
Parameterize $K_p$ by $c_p+r_pX$ and $L_p$ by $r_jv+r_p(X-v)$ using the same $X\in K$. The original margin is at least $(\beta+b)\cdot(p-j)+a(r_p-r_j)+r_pu\cdot X$, exactly the comparison margin. Both parameterizations have volume Jacobian $r_p^d$, so inclusion of the nonnegative-margin sets gives the fiberwise inequality with closed caps. Dividing the comparison margin by $a$ gives the scalar formula and the centroid of $K$ gives $\mathbb EY=1$. A pure-base cap sees only whole-fiber volumes, which agree.

For every convex representation of $k$ as in the hypothesis, the Minkowski sum of the original fibers is a translate of $(\sum_p\lambda_p r_p)K$. Taking its width and the width of $K_k$ in any fixed nonzero direction gives $\sum_p\lambda_p r_p\le r_k$, since the common width of $K$ is positive. As $0\in K-v$, the corresponding Minkowski sum of $L_p$ is $r_jv+(\sum_p\lambda_p r_p)(K-v)\subset L_k$. The section of the lifted convex hull at any $k\in P$ is $L_k$: any convex combination may be grouped by base, and the preceding inclusion handles the resulting representation; the reverse inclusion is immediate. Its projection is $\operatorname{conv}(P)$, giving the exact integer-fiber conclusion.
