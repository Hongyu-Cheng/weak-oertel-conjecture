## Statement

Use the actual planar three-fiber critical family of the marked-depth theorem, with arbitrary middle body $K$, total mass $M$, marked raw depth $Q$, and canonical normals $v_i$. Let $\overline K=\{x:v_i\cdot x\ge-h_K(-v_i)\ \forall i\}$, let $m=\sum_i h_B(-v_i)/3$, and set $f(m)=|T\cap(-mT)|/|T|$ for $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$. If $Q\le2M/9$, then
$$
\frac{|K|}{|\overline K|}<\frac{36f(m)}{7(m+1)^2}\le\frac67.
$$

## Proof

The marked-depth theorem gives $B\subseteq T$ and $|K|\le9|B|/7$ for this actual bad target. By definition $\overline K$ contains $K$ and hence contains the midpoint of the endpoint bodies. It has nonempty interior and is bounded, since a recession vector would have all three evaluations $v_i\cdot x$ nonnegative, forcing all to vanish by balance and then the vector to vanish by spanning. It is an aligned triangle with the three proper cuts of $A$ unchanged. Apply the aligned area credit with middle body $\overline K$ to get $|\overline K|/|B|>(m+1)^2/[4f(m)]\ge3/2$. Divide the previous bound on $|K|$ by this strict lower bound.
