## Statement

Retain the exact canonical objects and normalizations of accepted facts `036a276c54ec5e66`, `54f87dbfba03bb81`, `6b09aae189415c70`, `be359f8a6c628483`, `c81f02314cb34f8b`, and `e482e1c83a1dc247`. Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body, and
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 Z:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
\]
\[
 W:=\sum_{z\in Z}a_z>0,
 \qquad
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C).
\tag{1}
\]
For every parity class \(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\), put
\[
 A_\xi:=\sum_{z\in Z,\ z\equiv\xi}a_z,
 \qquad
 M_\xi:=\max_{z\in Z,\ z\equiv\xi}a_z,
\tag{2}
\]
with \(M_\xi=0\) when \(A_\xi=0\). A nonempty class is good when
\[
 M_\xi\leq\frac{A_\xi}{4},
\tag{3}
\]
and bad otherwise. The accepted physical parity estimate is
\[
 \sum_\xi M_\xi\leq G.
\tag{4}
\]

Let \(Q>0\) be the half-strength canonical product-reservoir mass. Retain the canonical hard inequality
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D
 <\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\tag{5}
\]
where all six displayed summands are nonnegative. The symbol \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\) has exactly the normalization in accepted facts `6b09aae189415c70` and `be359f8a6c628483`: its hosts are exact minimum-unmatched loopless same-parity centroid matchings with source weights \(\gamma_da_z\), and a nonzero positive submeasure \(\sigma\) of such a host contributes
\[
 2^{n-\rho_\sigma}\frac{|\sigma|}{r_\sigma+1}.
\tag{6}
\]

Retain the exact minimizing centroid-bad whole-label family \(\mathscr D_c\) fixed in accepted fact `54f87dbfba03bb81`, with the alias to the family and mass lower bound in accepted fact `e482e1c83a1dc247`. Every \(\sigma\in\mathscr D_c\) has weight \(\eta_\sigma>0\) and two distinct same-parity physical opposite endpoints
\[
 a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\in Z.
\tag{7}
\]
Its mass and accepted endpoint budgets are
\[
 M_c:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c}\eta_\sigma
 >\frac{141}{12800}Q,
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c\\
 z\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma
 \leq\frac{a_z}{4}
 \qquad(z\in Z).
\tag{9}
\]
Here \(\mathscr D_c\) and \(M_c\) are the aliases attached to the minimizing affine extension in `54f87dbfba03bb81`, not a partition obtained from another extension.

Aggregate every family of parallel direct labels before taking degrees. On unordered pairs of distinct same-parity bases, define
\[
 \ell_{\{u,v\}}:=
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c\\
 \{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}=\{u,v\}}}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \delta_z:=\sum_{v\ne z}\ell_{\{z,v\}}.
\tag{10}
\]
The scalar \(\delta_z\) is the direct-label graph degree. It is not the absolute fiber-depth notation \(\delta_C(z,x)\). For a nonempty parity class put
\[
 P_\xi:=
 \sum_{\substack{\{u,v\}\subseteq Z\\u\equiv v\equiv\xi}}
 \ell_{\{u,v\}},
 \qquad
 P_{\mathrm{bad}}:=\sum_{\xi\ \mathrm{bad}}P_\xi,
 \qquad
 P_{\mathrm g}:=M_c-P_{\mathrm{bad}}.
\tag{11}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{P_{\mathrm{bad}}<\frac G2},
 \qquad
 \boxed{P_{\mathrm g}>\frac{77}{12800}Q}.
\tag{12}
\]

For every good class and every \(z\equiv\xi\), define the residual degree
\[
 r_z^{\mathrm{res}}:=
 \gamma_da_z-2\gamma_d\delta_z.
\tag{13}
\]
There is a loopless fractional matching \(q^\xi\) on the distinct bases of that class whose degree at every \(z\) is exactly \(r_z^{\mathrm{res}}\). Consequently
\[
 \lambda^\xi_{\{u,v\}}
 :=2\gamma_d\ell_{\{u,v\}}+q^\xi_{\{u,v\}}
\tag{14}
\]
has exact source degree
\[
 \sum_{v\ne z}\lambda^\xi_{\{z,v\}}=\gamma_da_z
 \qquad(z\equiv\xi)
\tag{15}
\]
and zero unmatched mass on the good class. Choosing an exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching on every bad class and taking the classwise union with (14) gives a global exact minimum-unmatched matching \(\lambda\) in the normalization of (6). Thus the phrase zero unmatched refers to every good class in the constructed global host; no zero-unmatched assertion is made for a bad class.

Let \(\mathscr D_c^{\mathrm g}\) be the labels whose endpoint parity class is good, and put
\[
 m_\sigma:=\frac{a(\sigma)+b(\sigma)}2,
 \qquad
 q_\sigma^c:=\frac{c_{a(\sigma)}+c_{b(\sigma)}}2.
\tag{16}
\]
Define the direct centroid measure
\[
 \boxed{
 \sigma_c:=
 2\gamma_d
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c^{\mathrm g}}
 \eta_\sigma\delta_{(m_\sigma,q_\sigma^c)}.}
\tag{17}
\]
Let the centroid midpoint measure of the global host be
\[
 \nu_{\mathrm{off}}^\lambda:=
 \sum_{\substack{\{u,v\}\subseteq Z\\
 u\ne v,\ u\equiv v\pmod 2}}
 \lambda_{\{u,v\}}
 \delta_{\left((u+v)/2,(c_u+c_v)/2\right)}.
\tag{18}
\]
Then \(\sigma_c\) is nonzero, is carried by
\(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), and satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\sigma_c\leq\nu_{\mathrm{off}}^\lambda},
 \qquad
 \boxed{\sigma_c(H)\leq\mu_C(H)}
\tag{19}
\]
for every closed ambient halfspace \(H\). Its exact mass and strict lower bound are
\[
 \boxed{
 |\sigma_c|=2\gamma_dP_{\mathrm g}
 >\frac{77\gamma_d}{6400}Q.}
\tag{20}
\]

Put
\[
 P_c:=\operatorname{supp}\sigma_c,
 \qquad
 A_c:=\operatorname{aff}\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbb R^n}(P_c),
 \qquad
 \rho_c:=\dim A_c,
\tag{21}
\]
\[
 r_c:=
 \min_{s:A_c\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{x-s(z):(z,x)\in P_c\}.
\tag{22}
\]
The ranks are taken after coincident atoms have been aggregated as a measure. The hard branch forces
\[
 \boxed{
 r_c+1>
 \frac{77\gamma_d}{64}\,2^{n-\rho_c}.}
\tag{23}
\]

All direct and residual edges above are unordered and loopless. Parallel direct labels are aggregated in (10), while distinct endpoint pairs that produce the same centroid atom are allowed and are aggregated automatically in (17)--(18). The construction permits equality in the good-class condition, equality in the fractional degree criterion, and the boundary value \(2\gamma_d=1\) when \(d=1\). It makes no assertion that \(q_\sigma^c\) equals a reflected actual atom, and it gives no positive-mass interpretation or absorption of a reflected signed score.

## Proof

The hard inequality (5) and nonnegativity of its summands give
\[
 G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{24}
\]
The canonical setup in accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` has \(G<W/4\), so the physical parity estimate of accepted fact `c81f02314cb34f8b` applies and gives (4). Accepted facts `e482e1c83a1dc247` and `54f87dbfba03bb81` give the exact alias, endpoint budget, and strict mass lower bound (8)--(9). Accepted fact `036a276c54ec5e66` supplies the same-parity, distinct-opposite-endpoint ancestry of these direct labels.

Because each label has two distinct endpoints and parallel labels were aggregated, every edge contributes once to each of two graph degrees. Hence
\[
 2P_\xi=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\delta_z.
\tag{25}
\]
Equation (9) gives \(\delta_z\leq a_z/4\), and therefore
\[
 2P_\xi
 \leq\frac14\sum_{z\equiv\xi}a_z
 =\frac{A_\xi}{4}.
\tag{26}
\]
If \(\xi\) is bad, then \(A_\xi/4<M_\xi\), including when \(P_\xi=0\). Thus
\[
 P_\xi<\frac{M_\xi}{2}.
\tag{27}
\]
We split the bad-mass argument according to whether the family of bad classes is empty. If at least one bad class exists, the family is finite and nonempty, so summing the strict class inequalities (27) preserves strictness. Equation (4) then gives
\[
 P_{\mathrm{bad}}
 =\sum_{\xi\ \mathrm{bad}}P_\xi
 <\frac12\sum_{\xi\ \mathrm{bad}}M_\xi
 \leq\frac G2.
\tag{28a}
\]
If there is no bad class, then by definition
\[
 P_{\mathrm{bad}}=0.
\tag{28b}
\]
Since \(W>0\), at least one physical fiber has positive mass. Its parity class is nonempty and has \(M_\xi>0\), so
\[
 \sum_\xi M_\xi>0.
\tag{28c}
\]
Combining (28c) with (4) gives \(G>0\), and hence in this second case
\[
 P_{\mathrm{bad}}=0<\frac G2.
\tag{28d}
\]
Thus in both cases
\[
 \boxed{P_{\mathrm{bad}}<\frac G2.}
\tag{28}
\]
Now (8), (24), and (28) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P_{\mathrm g}
 &=M_c-P_{\mathrm{bad}}\\
 &>\frac{141}{12800}Q-\frac G2\\
 &>\left(\frac{141}{12800}-\frac1{200}\right)Q
 =\frac{77}{12800}Q.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{29}
\]
This proves (12), and in particular at least one good direct label remains.

We next verify the strengthened scale and the exact residual completion. The binomial theorem gives
\[
 \left(1+\frac1d\right)^d\geq2,
\]
with equality exactly when \(d=1\). Hence
\[
 0<2\gamma_d\leq1.
\tag{30}
\]
Thus scaling the direct matching by \(2\gamma_d\) is legal even as a positive submeasure of the unscaled direct centroid measure, including the equality case \(d=1\).

Fix a good class \(\xi\). By (9), every residual degree in (13) satisfies
\[
 r_z^{\mathrm{res}}
 =\gamma_d(a_z-2\delta_z)
 \geq\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2}>0.
\tag{31}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \max_{z\equiv\xi}r_z^{\mathrm{res}}
 \leq\gamma_dM_\xi
 \leq\frac{\gamma_dA_\xi}{4},
\tag{32}
\]
while (26) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{z\equiv\xi}r_z^{\mathrm{res}}
 &=\gamma_dA_\xi
 -2\gamma_d\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\delta_z\\
 &\geq\gamma_dA_\xi-\frac{\gamma_dA_\xi}{2}
 =\frac{\gamma_dA_\xi}{2}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{33}
\]
Consequently
\[
 2\max_{z\equiv\xi}r_z^{\mathrm{res}}
 \leq\frac{\gamma_dA_\xi}{2}
 \leq\sum_{z\equiv\xi}r_z^{\mathrm{res}}.
\tag{34}
\]
The exact loopless fractional degree-sequence criterion proved in accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` now supplies \(q^\xi\) with precisely these degrees. Its circle construction covers equality in (34) and introduces no diagonal edge. Adding the scaled direct edges gives (14), and direct calculation using (13) gives
\[
 \sum_{v\ne z}\lambda^\xi_{\{z,v\}}
 =2\gamma_d\delta_z+r_z^{\mathrm{res}}
 =\gamma_da_z.
\tag{35}
\]
Thus each good class has zero unmatched mass and hence attains the classwise minimum.

On every bad class choose any exact classwise minimum-unmatched centroid matching supplied by `be359f8a6c628483`. Matching constraints, source degrees, and unmatched mass decompose over parity classes. The union with the good-class matchings from (14) is therefore a global exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching \(\lambda\) for the fixed source weights \(\gamma_da_z\). This proves the host assertion without claiming zero unmatched mass on a bad class.

For a direct label \(\sigma\), both endpoint centroid points
\((a(\sigma),c_{a(\sigma)})\) and
\((b(\sigma),c_{b(\sigma)})\) belong to \(C\). Their endpoints have the same parity, so \(m_\sigma\in\mathbb Z^n\), and convexity gives
\[
 (m_\sigma,q_\sigma^c)
 =\frac12(a(\sigma),c_{a(\sigma)})
 +\frac12(b(\sigma),c_{b(\sigma)})
 \in C.
\tag{36}
\]
Hence every atom in (17) lies in the mixed-integer part of \(C\).

For fixed distinct endpoints \(u,v\), all parallel direct labels have the same atom. Therefore aggregation (10) gives the exact measure identity
\[
 \sigma_c
 =\sum_{\substack{\{u,v\}\ \mathrm{in\ good\ classes}}}
 2\gamma_d\ell_{\{u,v\}}
 \delta_{\left((u+v)/2,(c_u+c_v)/2\right)}.
\tag{37}
\]
Equation (14) gives
\(\lambda_{\{u,v\}}\geq2\gamma_d\ell_{\{u,v\}}\)
on every such edge. Thus (37) proves the positive-measure inequality
\(\sigma_c\leq\nu_{\mathrm{off}}^\lambda\). If distinct endpoint pairs have the same atom, both sides simply add their nonnegative coefficients at that atom, so the inequality is unchanged. The accepted centroid matching theorem `be359f8a6c628483` gives
\[
 \nu_{\mathrm{off}}^\lambda(H)\leq\mu_C(H)
\]
for every closed halfspace, including boundary atoms. This proves (19). Positivity of all coefficients and (29) give
\[
 |\sigma_c|=2\gamma_dP_{\mathrm g}
 >2\gamma_d\frac{77}{12800}Q
 =\frac{77\gamma_d}{6400}Q,
\tag{38}
\]
which proves (20).

The host \(\lambda\) is one of the exact minimum-unmatched hosts quantified in the definition of \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\) in `6b09aae189415c70`, and \(\sigma_c\) is a nonzero positive submeasure of its centroid midpoint measure. The ranks (21)--(22) are exactly the accepted intrinsic ranks, after all coincident atoms are aggregated. Hence (6) and (38) give
\[
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 \geq
 2^{n-\rho_c}\frac{|\sigma_c|}{r_c+1}
 >
 \frac{77\gamma_d}{6400}
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_c}Q}{r_c+1}.
\tag{39}
\]
Combining (39) with the strict hard bound in (24), cancelling \(Q>0\), and rearranging gives
\[
 r_c+1>
 \frac{77\gamma_d}{64}\,2^{n-\rho_c},
\]
which is (23).

The proof used only centroid midpoint atoms to repair the accepted host normalization. It did not equate them with the reflected actual atoms and did not convert any signed reflected score into positive matching mass. Those two interfaces remain unresolved.
