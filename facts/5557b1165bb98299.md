## Statement

Let \(n\ge1\) be an integer. Let \(A\subseteq\{0,1,2\}^n\) be a nonempty coordinatewise downset: if \(z\in A\) and \(y\in\mathbb Z^n\) satisfy \(0\le y_i\le z_i\) for every \(i\), then \(y\in A\). Let \(\widetilde w_z\ge0\), for \(z\in A\), be coordinatewise nonincreasing, meaning \(\widetilde w_y\ge\widetilde w_z\) whenever \(y,z\in A\) and \(y_i\le z_i\) for all \(i\). Extend these weights by zero to \(\mathbb Z^n\setminus A\), and assume \(\widetilde W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\widetilde w_z>0\). Define \(\widetilde S_j:=\sum_{v\in\mathbb Z^n}\min\{\widetilde w_{j+v},\widetilde w_{j-v}\}\), with the displacement sum oriented and including zero. Then there exists \(j\in A\cap\{0,1\}^n\) with \(\widetilde w_j>0\) such that \(\widetilde S_j\ge2^{-n}\widetilde W\).

In particular, let \(G\) be any finite simple graph on \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\), let \(a_i\ge0\), and put \(A_G:=\{z\in\{0,1,2\}^n:z_i+z_k\le2\text{ for every edge }\{i,k\}\text{ of }G\}\). Taking \(A=A_G\) and \(\widetilde w_z=e^{-a\cdot z}\) on \(A_G\), with zero weights outside, gives \(\max_{j\in A_G}\widetilde S_j\ge2^{-n}\widetilde W\). The tilded scalar quantities here are the registered graph subgoal's \(w_z,W,S_j\), respectively; tildes distinguish them from the actual-body objects below.

For the actual-body consequence, let \(A\) be any nonempty coordinatewise downset in \(\{0,1,2\}^n\), let \(a_i\ge0\), and define \(\widetilde w_z=e^{-a\cdot z}\) on \(A\), zero outside. Let \(d\ge1\), let \(C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, put \(C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\}\), and let \(w_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)\), with zero volume for empty or lower-dimensional fibers. Suppose \(\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:w_z>0\}=A\), and suppose constants \(K>0\) and \(M\ge1\) satisfy \(KM^{-1}e^{-a\cdot z}\le w_z\le KM e^{-a\cdot z}\) for every \(z\in A\). Put \(W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}w_z\), \(S:=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), and \(h_S(p):=\inf_{H\ni p}\mathcal H_d(S\cap H)\), where \(H\) ranges over closed ambient halfspaces. These untilded objects have exactly their meanings in accepted fact `280b299bc1e87c14`. With \(\ell:=-\log(1-e^{-1})\), there exists one point \(p^\star\in S\) satisfying

\[
h_S(p^\star)\ge\frac{\ell}{16M^2(1+4\log M)}\,2^{-n}W.
\]

For each fixed \(M\), this positive constant is independent of \(n,d,A,a,K\), and hence independent of \(G\) and its number of edges when \(A=A_G\). The point is chosen before the halfspace, so the same point obeys the corresponding lower bound for every closed ambient halfspace containing it. The actual-body conclusion is restricted to the stated log-affine comparison hypothesis; it is not asserted for all convex bodies or for arbitrary monotone scalar profiles. All logarithms are natural.

## Proof

All scalar sums are finite because \(A\subseteq\{0,1,2\}^n\). For each subset \(T\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}\), let \(A_T:=\{z\in A:\{i:z_i>0\}=T\}\), and let \(\widetilde W_T:=\sum_{z\in A_T}\widetilde w_z\). These at most \(2^n\) disjoint slices partition \(A\). Choose \(T\) with greatest slice mass. Then \(\widetilde W_T\ge2^{-n}\widetilde W>0\). Define \(j_i=1\) for \(i\in T\), and \(j_i=0\) otherwise. For every \(z\in A_T\), one has \(0\le j\le z\) coordinatewise, so downward closure gives \(j\in A\). Since some \(z\in A_T\) has positive weight, monotonicity also gives \(\widetilde w_j>0\).

For each \(z\in A_T\), set \(v=z-j\) and \(y=2j-z=j-v\). If \(z_i=0\), then \(j_i=y_i=0\). If \(z_i=1\), then \(j_i=y_i=1\). If \(z_i=2\), then \(j_i=1\) and \(y_i=0\). Thus \(0\le y\le z\) coordinatewise, which implies \(y\in A\) and \(\widetilde w_y\ge\widetilde w_z\). Consequently the oriented displacement \(v=z-j\) contributes exactly \(\widetilde w_z\) to \(\widetilde S_j\). Distinct \(z\) give distinct \(v\), since \(z=j+v\). Restricting the displacement sum to these displacements therefore gives

\[
\widetilde S_j
\ge\sum_{z\in A_T}\min\{\widetilde w_z,\widetilde w_{2j-z}\}
=\widetilde W_T
\ge2^{-n}\widetilde W.
\]

This also covers \(T=\varnothing\), for which the slice consists of zero, the center is zero, and the displacement is zero. Other reflected displacements can only add nonnegative terms. In particular, the proof never requires a product rectangle to carry a fixed fraction of the total mass.

For the graph specialization, zero belongs to \(A_G\), so \(A_G\) is nonempty. If \(z\in A_G\) and \(0\le y\le z\) coordinatewise, then \(y\in\{0,1,2\}^n\) and every edge satisfies \(y_i+y_k\le z_i+z_k\le2\). Thus \(A_G\) is a downset. Nonnegativity of the coefficients \(a_i\) implies \(a\cdot y\le a\cdot z\) whenever \(y\le z\), and hence \(e^{-a\cdot y}\ge e^{-a\cdot z}\). The general scalar assertion therefore applies with \(\widetilde W\ge\widetilde w_0=1\), proving the graph bound for any number of edges.

For the actual-body consequence, apply the scalar assertion with the log-affine profile on the stated downset \(A\), and retain its chosen center \(j\in A\). All actual fiber volumes and depth objects in the rest of the proof are those of accepted fact `280b299bc1e87c14`; the scalar profile is only used for comparison. In particular, define \(\alpha(v):=\min\{w_{j+v},w_{j-v}\}\) and \(\mathcal U_j:=\{v\in\mathbb Z^n:\alpha(v)>0\}\), exactly as in that accepted fact. The common support and the multiplicative comparison give \(\alpha(v)\ge(K/M)\min\{\widetilde w_{j+v},\widetilde w_{j-v}\}\) for every integer displacement, including those with a zero endpoint weight. Also \(W\le KM\widetilde W\). It follows that

\[
\sum_v\alpha(v)
\ge\frac K M\widetilde S_j
\ge\frac K M\,2^{-n}\widetilde W
\ge\frac1{M^2}\,2^{-n}W.
\]

The actual central weight is positive because \(j\in A\). For \(v\in\mathcal U_j\), let \(\Delta_j(v):=\log(w_j^2/(w_{j+v}w_{j-v}))\), precisely the curvature in accepted fact `280b299bc1e87c14`. Its nonnegativity follows from that accepted fact. Both endpoints belong to \(A\), and applying the profile bounds at the center and the two endpoints gives

\[
\frac{w_j^2}{w_{j+v}w_{j-v}}
\le\frac{(KM e^{-a\cdot j})^2}
{(KM^{-1}e^{-a\cdot(j+v)})(KM^{-1}e^{-a\cdot(j-v)})}
=M^4.
\]

Thus \(0\le\Delta_j(v)\le4\log M\) for every positive reflected pair. Put \(\tau=4\log M\ge0\). With the alias \(L=\tau\) for the accepted fact's cutoff parameter, its quantity \(B_j^\Delta(\tau):=\sum_{v\in\mathcal U_j:\Delta_j(v)\le\tau}\alpha(v)\) therefore equals \(\sum_v\alpha(v)\).

All hypotheses of the cutoff depth conclusion in `280b299bc1e87c14` hold: \(n,d\ge1\), \(C\) is a convex body, and \(A\ne\varnothing\) with the positive lower profile bound implies \(W>0\). Its maximum over centers and cutoffs is at least the value at this \(j,\tau\). Consequently it supplies one point \(p^\star\in S\) with

\[
h_S(p^\star)
\ge\frac\ell{16}\frac{B_j^\Delta(4\log M)}{1+4\log M}
\ge\frac{\ell}{16M^2(1+4\log M)}\,2^{-n}W.
\]

That accepted conclusion chooses the point before any ambient halfspace. It therefore gives the claimed order of quantifiers. The scale \(K\) cancels. The scalar support-partition argument uses only monotonicity, whereas the curvature bound in this last part uses the explicit log-affine multiplicative comparison; no curvature conclusion for general monotone weights has been used.
