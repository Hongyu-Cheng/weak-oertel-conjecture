## Statement

Let $F$ be a compact convex $(d-1)$-dimensional face in a continuous $d$-space, with apex $y$ at perpendicular distance $H>0$ below it in direction $-u$, where $u$ is the unit face normal. In coordinates $v=x-y=\xi+Hu$, $\xi\perp u$, write the affine denominator on the face as $L=s+a_T\cdot\xi$ with $a_T\perp u$. Suppose the affine denominator is positive on $F$ and at the apex. Under $v\mapsto v'=v/L$, the image face is in $N\cdot v'=H$ with $N=su+Ha_T$, and its tangential Jacobian is $\lVert N\rVert L^{-d}$. The measure $H L^{-d}\,d\mathcal H_{d-1}$ pushes forward to $(H/\lVert N\rVert)\,d\mathcal H_{d-1}$ there. For every measurable face patch its weighted mass is $d$ times the $d$-volume of the radial cone from the image apex to the image patch. For the full face this is $d$ times the volume of the bounded convex image pyramid.

## Proof

Translate the apex to the origin. The same affine function $L$ is positive on the entire pyramid by convexity. Since $u\cdot v=H$ on the original face,
$$
N\cdot v=(su+Ha_T)\cdot(\xi+Hu)=H L(v),
$$
so the image lies in $N\cdot v'=H$. In particular $N\ne0$ and the distance from the image apex $0$ to the image plane is $H/\lVert N\rVert$.

Take an orthonormal basis $e_1,\dots,e_{d-1}$ in the tangent plane. The derivative in direction $e_j$ is
$$
D(v/L)e_j=L^{-1}\bigl(e_j-(a_T\cdot e_j)v'\bigr).
$$
The absolute determinant of these $d-1$ tangent columns with last column $v'=v/L$ is $L^{-d}|\det(e_1,\dots,e_{d-1},v)|=H L^{-d}$, since the terms proportional to the last column drop out. It is also the tangential Jacobian times $|N\cdot v'|/\lVert N\rVert=H/\lVert N\rVert$. The same determinant convention uses only the last column when $d=1$. Thus the tangential Jacobian is $\lVert N\rVert L^{-d}$.

The map is injective and maps segments to segments. In fact at a convex combination of two points with positive denominators, the image is the convex combination of the two images with weights proportional to the old weights times those denominators. Varying the old weights fills the image segment, and a local inverse exists since the affine denominator has positive constant term $L(0)$. More explicitly, if $L(v)=L(0)+a\cdot v$, then $v=L(0)v'/(1-a\cdot v')$ on the image, with $1-a\cdot v'=L(0)/L(v)>0$. The image of the compact pyramid is thus the compact pyramid with the transformed apex and face. The area change gives the stated pushed-forward measure. Parameterize each cone by $t v'$ over its face patch for $0\le t\le1$. Its volume element is $t^{d-1}(H/\lVert N\rVert)\,dt\,d\mathcal H_{d-1}(v')$. Integration in $t$ gives the required factor $1/d$.
