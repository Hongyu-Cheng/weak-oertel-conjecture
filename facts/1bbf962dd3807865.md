## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and retain the canonical positive-fresh hard setting, notation, and actual collision pairing of accepted fact ac55cb178557f351. In particular,
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
 \qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z,
\]
the half-strength canonical product reservoir is
\[
 r_{\{u,v\}}=\frac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi}
 \quad
 (u\ne v,\ u\equiv v\equiv\xi\pmod2)
\]
on every good parity class, where \(A_\xi=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}a_z\) and every member of a good class has mass at most \(A_\xi/4\). Put
\[
 Q:=\sum_er_e>0,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\]
The actual edge-no-reuse collision pairing consists of weights
\[
 \lambda_L>0,
 \qquad
 L=\{e,f\},
 \qquad
 m_L:=m_e=m_f\in\mathbb Z^n,
 \qquad
 \Pi:=\sum_L\lambda_L>\frac{373Q}{19200},
\]
where \(e\ne f\), the four endpoints of \(e,f\) are distinct, and
\[
 A_L:=\operatorname{aff}(e\cup f),
 \qquad
 \dim A_L\leq2.
\]
For every reservoir edge, write
\[
 d_e(\lambda):=\sum_{L\ni e}\lambda_L.
\]
The predecessor budget is
\[
 d_e(\lambda)\leq\frac{r_e}{8}.
\tag{1}
\]

For every minimal slice \(A\) and midpoint parity
\(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\), put
\[
 \Lambda_{\xi,A}:=
 \sum_{\substack{L:A_L=A\\m_L\equiv\xi\ ({\rm mod}\ 2)}}\lambda_L,
 \qquad
 \Lambda_A:=\sum_\xi\Lambda_{\xi,A}.
\tag{2}
\]
Then every minimal slice obeys the absolute atom cap
\[
 \boxed{\Lambda_A\leq2^{1-n}G.}
\tag{3}
\]

There are nonnegative symmetric cross-slice weights
\[
 \theta_{\{L,L'\}}=\theta_{\{L',L\}}\geq0
\tag{4}
\]
supported only when
\[
 A_L\ne A_{L'},
 \qquad
 m_L\equiv m_{L'}\pmod2,
\tag{5}
\]
such that every label is used within its original collision weight,
\[
 \sum_{L'\ne L}\theta_{\{L,L'\}}\leq\lambda_L,
\tag{6}
\]
and, with
\[
 P_1:=\sum_{\{L,L'\}}\theta_{\{L,L'\}},
\tag{7}
\]
one has
\[
 \boxed{2P_1\geq\Pi-2G.}
\tag{8}
\]
Consequently, exactly one of the following two alternatives holds:
\[
 \boxed{G\geq\frac\Pi4,}
\tag{9}
\]
or
\[
 \boxed{G<\frac\Pi4,
 \qquad
 P_1>\frac\Pi4,
 \qquad
 2P_1>\frac\Pi2.}
\tag{10}
\]

Every positive fused cell
\[
 \Gamma=\{L,L'\},
 \qquad
 \theta_\Gamma:=\theta_{\{L,L'\}}>0,
\]
has the integer fused center and source hull
\[
 M_\Gamma:=\frac{m_L+m_{L'}}2\in\mathbb Z^n,
 \qquad
 B_\Gamma:=\operatorname{aff}(A_L\cup A_{L'}),
\tag{11}
\]
and satisfies
\[
 \boxed{M_\Gamma\in B_\Gamma,
 \qquad
 \dim B_\Gamma\leq5.}
\tag{12}
\]
Moreover, if one charges \(\theta_\Gamma\) to each endpoint occurrence of both labels in \(\Gamma\), then for every physical source \(z\),
\[
 \operatorname{mult}_\Gamma(z):=
 \mathbf1_{\{z\text{ is an endpoint of }L\}}
 +\mathbf1_{\{z\text{ is an endpoint of }L'\}},
 \qquad
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\Gamma=\{L,L'\}}
 \operatorname{mult}_\Gamma(z)\theta_\Gamma
 \leq\frac{a_z}{32}.}
\tag{13}
\]
Thus (4)--(13) are one edge-no-reuse and source-no-reuse rank-five fusion ledger, not a sum of independently chosen slice witnesses.

The same fusion can be performed after making the predecessor contact and centroid fields physically compatible. For a label \(L=\{e,f\}\), use the predecessor points
\[
 p_L^\ell:=\frac{\ell_e+\ell_f}{2},
 \qquad
 p_L^c:=\frac{\bar c_e+\bar c_f}{2},
\tag{14}
\]
and the predecessor witnesses
\[
 0\leq\varphi_e,\varphi_f,\psi_e,\psi_f\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int\varphi_g=\vartheta_g,
 \qquad
 \int\psi_g=b_g^{\mathrm{BM}}=2\vartheta_g
 \quad(g\in\{e,f\}).
\]
Define the actual label densities on \(C_{m_L}\) by
\[
 F_L^\ell:=
 \frac{\lambda_L}{2\vartheta_e}\varphi_e+
 \frac{\lambda_L}{2\vartheta_f}\varphi_f,
 \qquad
 F_L^c:=
 \frac{\lambda_L}{2b_e^{\mathrm{BM}}}\psi_e+
 \frac{\lambda_L}{2b_f^{\mathrm{BM}}}\psi_f.
\]
They have mass \(\lambda_L\), barycenters \(p_L^\ell,p_L^c\), respectively, and satisfy the simultaneous physical-fiber estimates
\[
 \sum_{L:m_L=m}F_L^\ell\leq2^{n-6},
 \qquad
 \sum_{L:m_L=m}F_L^c\leq2^{n-7}.
\tag{15}
\]
Put
\[
 \alpha_\ell:=\min\{1,2^{6-n}\},
 \qquad
 \alpha_c:=\min\{1,2^{7-n}\},
 \qquad
 \bar\alpha:=\frac{\alpha_\ell+\alpha_c}{2},
\tag{16}
\]
\[
 F_L^*:=\frac12\bigl(\alpha_\ell F_L^\ell+\alpha_cF_L^c\bigr),
 \qquad
 p_L^*:=
 \frac{\alpha_\ell p_L^\ell+\alpha_cp_L^c}
 {\alpha_\ell+\alpha_c}.
\tag{17}
\]
Then \(F_L^*\) has mass \(\bar\alpha\lambda_L\), barycenter \(p_L^*\), and
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{L:m_L=m}F_L^*\leq1}
\tag{18}
\]
on every physical precursor fiber.

For every positive fused cell \(\Gamma=\{L,L'\}\), put
\[
 y_\Gamma^*:=\frac{p_L^*+p_{L'}^*}{2}.
\tag{19}
\]
Convexity gives \((M_\Gamma,y_\Gamma^*)\in C\). The single fused measure
\[
 \boxed{
 \nu_1^*:=
 \gamma_d\bar\alpha
 \sum_\Gamma\theta_\Gamma
 \delta_{(M_\Gamma,y_\Gamma^*)},
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,}
\tag{20}
\]
is carried by \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), is halfspace-dominated by the mixed-volume measure \(\mu_C\), and has exact mass
\[
 \boxed{|\nu_1^*|=\gamma_d\bar\alpha P_1.}
\tag{21}
\]
This is one compatible density construction: the contact and centroid fields were combined pointwise in (17) before any capacity was invoked.

Let
\[
 \rho_1:=\dim\operatorname{aff}\{M_\Gamma:\theta_\Gamma>0\},
\]
and define the intrinsic residual rank after coincident full atoms are aggregated by
\[
 r_1^*:=
 \min_{a:\operatorname{aff}\{M_\Gamma\}\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{y-a(M):(M,y)\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_1^*\}.
\tag{22}
\]
In the second alternative (10),
\[
 \boxed{
 D\geq
 \gamma_d\bar\alpha\,2^{n-\rho_1}
 \frac{P_1}{r_1^*+1}
 >
 \frac{\gamma_d\bar\alpha}{4}
 2^{n-\rho_1}\frac{\Pi}{r_1^*+1}.}
\tag{23}
\]
Using the hard inequality \(D<Q/100\) and the collision lower bound for \(\Pi\), this forces the explicit remaining screen
\[
 \boxed{
 r_1^*+1>
 \frac{373\gamma_d\bar\alpha}{768}
 2^{n-\rho_1}.}
\tag{24}
\]
No assertion is made that (24) alone is impossible. The new content is the distinct-slice rank-five fusion, the mass dichotomy, and the single compatible physical field.

## Proof

We first prove the slice atom cap. For a source \(z\) in a good parity class, direct summation of the product formula gives
\[
 d_z(r):=\sum_{e\ni z}r_e
 =\frac{a_z}{4A_\xi}(A_\xi-a_z)
 \leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{25}
\]
For a fixed minimal slice \(A\), let
\[
 \eta_{A,z}:=
 \sum_{\substack{L:A_L=A\\z\text{ is an endpoint of }L}}
 \lambda_L.
\]
Every label has four distinct endpoints, so
\[
 \sum_z\eta_{A,z}=4\Lambda_A.
\tag{26}
\]
Each endpoint occurrence belongs to exactly one of the label's two reservoir edges. Therefore (1) and (25) give
\[
 \eta_{A,z}
 \leq\sum_{e\ni z}d_e(\lambda)
 \leq\frac18d_z(r)
 \leq\frac{a_z}{32}.
\tag{27}
\]
All endpoints counted in (26) belong to \(I_+\cap A\), and they span \(A\). Hence
\[
 \operatorname{aff}(I_+\cap A)=A,
 \qquad
 q_A:=\dim A\leq2,
\]
and (26)--(27) give
\[
 W_A:=\sum_{z\in I_+\cap A}a_z
 \geq32\sum_z\eta_{A,z}
 =128\Lambda_A.
\tag{28}
\]

The reservoir formula also gives \(Q\leq W/8\), exactly as in accepted fact ac55cb178557f351. Accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c applied to the actual affine slice \(A\) gives
\[
 G\geq\frac1{64}
 \min\{W,2^{n-q_A}W_A\}.
\tag{29}
\]
Since \(W/64\geq Q/8>G\), the first entry of the minimum cannot be the active one. Equations (28)--(29) and \(q_A\leq2\) therefore give
\[
 G\geq\frac1{64}2^{n-q_A}W_A
 \geq\frac1{64}2^{n-2}\,128\Lambda_A
 =2^{n-1}\Lambda_A.
\tag{30}
\]
This proves (3).

We next match distinct slices within each midpoint parity. Fix \(\xi\), regard the positive numbers \(\Lambda_{\xi,A}\) as vertex capacities indexed by the distinct minimal slices, and put
\[
 B_\xi:=\sum_A\Lambda_{\xi,A},
 \qquad
 \beta_\xi:=\max_A\Lambda_{\xi,A},
 \qquad
 U_\xi:=\max\{2\beta_\xi-B_\xi,0\},
\tag{31}
\]
with all three values zero for an empty class. There is an off-diagonal fractional matching of the slice vertices that leaves exactly \(U_\xi\) unmatched. Indeed, if \(2\beta_\xi>B_\xi\), match every nonmaximal capacity completely to a maximal one. If \(2\beta_\xi\leq B_\xi\), place consecutive arcs of lengths \(\Lambda_{\xi,A}\) on a circle of circumference \(B_\xi\) and match each arc with the antipodal translate of the others; no arc meets its own antipodal translate in positive length. In both cases the matching uses only distinct slices, its degree at a slice is at most its capacity, and its mass is
\[
 \frac{B_\xi-U_\xi}{2}.
\tag{32}
\]

Disaggregate every positive slice-to-slice matching edge into label-to-label cells. This can be done simultaneously without changing any degree. Explicitly, if the slice edge \(A,A'\) has weight \(t_{A,A'}>0\), allocate to it from a label \(L\) in node \(A\) the supply
\[
 \lambda_L\frac{t_{A,A'}}{\Lambda_{\xi,A}},
\]
and analogously on the \(A'\) side. The total supplies on both sides are \(t_{A,A'}\); couple them by their product divided by \(t_{A,A'}\). Since the total slice degree is at most \(\Lambda_{\xi,A}\), the total degree of each label is at most \(\lambda_L\). This produces (4)--(6).

By (3),
\[
 \beta_\xi\leq2^{1-n}G.
\]
Since there are \(2^n\) midpoint parity classes and \(U_\xi\leq\beta_\xi\),
\[
 U:=\sum_\xi U_\xi\leq2G.
\tag{33}
\]
Summing (32) over the parity classes gives
\[
 P_1=\frac{\Pi-U}{2},
\]
which proves (8). If (9) fails, then \(G<\Pi/4\), so (33) gives \(U<\Pi/2\) and hence (10).

For a positive label cell \(\Gamma=\{L,L'\}\), condition (5) makes \(M_\Gamma\) integral. Since \(m_L\in A_L\) and \(m_{L'}\in A_{L'}\), it also gives \(M_\Gamma\in B_\Gamma\). For any two nonempty affine spaces,
\[
 \dim\operatorname{aff}(A_L\cup A_{L'})
 \leq\dim A_L+\dim A_{L'}+1\leq5,
\]
proving (12).

For the source budget, sum a cell weight at \(z\) through the label containing that endpoint. Equation (6) shows that all cells incident to one label use at most its weight. Therefore the total left side of (13) is at most
\[
 \sum_{\substack{L:\ z\text{ is an endpoint of }L}}\lambda_L,
\]
which is at most \(a_z/32\) by (27) with the restriction to one slice removed. This proves (13), including shared endpoints between different slices.

It remains to construct and audit the compatible physical measure. Accepted fact ac55cb178557f351 supplies the densities and bounds in (14)--(15). Equations (16)--(17) show directly that \(F_L^*\) has mass \(\bar\alpha\lambda_L\) and barycenter \(p_L^*\). Moreover,
\[
 \sum_{L:m_L=m}F_L^*
 \leq\frac12\left(
 \alpha_\ell2^{n-6}+\alpha_c2^{n-7}
 \right)\leq1,
\]
which proves (18).

For a positive fused cell \(\Gamma=\{L,L'\}\), the two subdensities
\[
 \frac{\theta_\Gamma}{\lambda_L}F_L^*,
 \qquad
 \frac{\theta_\Gamma}{\lambda_{L'}}F_{L'}^*
\tag{34}
\]
have equal mass \(\bar\alpha\theta_\Gamma\) and barycenters \(p_L^*,p_{L'}^*\). In particular \(p_L^*\in C_{m_L}\) and \(p_{L'}^*\in C_{m_{L'}}\). Convexity of \(C\) therefore puts their full midpoint \((M_\Gamma,y_\Gamma^*)\) in \(C\).

Fix a closed ambient halfspace \(H\). If the fused midpoint atom of a cell lies in \(H\), at least one of the two precursor points
\[
 (m_L,p_L^*),
 \qquad
 (m_{L'},p_{L'}^*)
\]
lies in \(H\). Orient the cell to one such precursor. At each physical precursor base \(m\), sum the corresponding oriented subdensities from (34), and call the sum \(K_{m,H}\). Equations (6) and (18) give
\[
 0\leq K_{m,H}\leq1.
\tag{35}
\]
Its mass is the sum of \(\bar\alpha\theta_\Gamma\) over the cells oriented to \(m\), and, when positive, its normalized first moment is a convex combination of precursor points in the closed fiber halfspace \(H_m\). Thus that first moment belongs both to \(H_m\) and to the metronoid represented by \(K_{m,H}\). Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_m\cap H_m)
 \geq\gamma_d\int_{C_m}K_{m,H}
\tag{36}
\]
when \(H_m\) is proper; if \(H_m=\mathbb R^d\), the same inequality follows from (35), and an empty section receives no orientation. Summing (36) over the distinct physical precursor fibers gives
\[
 \nu_1^*(H)
 =\gamma_d\bar\alpha
 \sum_{\Gamma:(M_\Gamma,y_\Gamma^*)\in H}\theta_\Gamma
 \leq\mu_C(H).
\tag{37}
\]
This proves the halfspace domination in (20), including repeated precursor bases, repeated fused bases, coincident continuous points, and boundary atoms. Equation (21) follows from (7).

Apply the dominated-measure residual theorem of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 to \(\nu_1^*\), and multiply its depth inequality by \(2^n\). Its mass is (21), its base rank is \(\rho_1\), and its continuous residual rank is (22). This gives the first inequality in (23); the second follows from (10). Finally, \(D<Q/100\), \(P_1>\Pi/4\), and \(\Pi>373Q/19200\) give
\[
 r_1^*+1
 >100\gamma_d\bar\alpha\,2^{n-\rho_1}\frac{P_1}{Q}
 >\frac{373\gamma_d\bar\alpha}{768}2^{n-\rho_1},
\]
which is (24). Every fusion coefficient is bounded by one original label degree, every label degree is bounded by its original collision weight, every edge degree is bounded by (1), and every physical density is aggregated before (36) is invoked. This completes the proof.
