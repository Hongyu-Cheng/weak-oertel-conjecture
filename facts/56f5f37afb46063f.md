## Statement

For ordinary planar area and three concurrent balanced spanning caps through an interior point, let $S_i$ denote the area captured only by cap $i$ and $T_i$ the double-sector area captured by both other caps. The proposed inequality $\prod_i S_i\le 8\prod_i T_i$ fails for each of the following actual triangles.

For $K=\operatorname{conv}\{(-19,-18),(19,-3),(-4,7)\}$ and the caps $x\ge0,\ y\ge0,\ x+y\le0$, the ratio $\prod S_i/\prod T_i$ is $214705407501/22913326051$, and $\prod S_i-8\prod T_i=282589191837/7801600$. The cap areas $A_i$ in the same order satisfy $\sum_i T_i/\min_i A_i=310599/238924>1$.

For $K_0=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(3,0),(0,3)\}$, its centroid $g=(1,1)$ and the fixed centroid caps $C_0=\{x+y\le2\}$, $C_1=\{x\ge1\}$, $C_2=\{y\ge1\}$, the triangle $Q=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(3,0),(9/10,6/5)\}$ lies in $K_0$ with $g$ in its interior. In $Q$, the single-sector areas are $(5/8,5/8,1/70)$ and the double areas $D_{ij}=|Q\cap C_i\cap C_j|$, in order $01,02,12$, are $(1/2,1/56,1/56)$. The product ratio is $35$. The three signed differences $S_i-2\sum_{j\ne i}D_{ij}$ are $(-23/56,-23/56,-2/35)$.

## Proof

For the first triangle, the consecutive vertex determinants are $399,121,205$, so the origin is in its interior and its area is $725/2$. Each cap strictly isolates a different vertex. In counterclockwise ray order $(1,0),(0,1),(-1,1),(-1,0),(0,-1),(1,-1)$, intersecting the ray lines with the triangle edges gives radii $(121/10,121/23,41/8,41/5,21/2,399/53)$. Specifically, for ray vector $e$ and edge $[U,V]$ the radius of the intersection is $\det(U,V-U)/\det(e,V-U)$. A sector with endpoint pair $P,P'$ and no vertex has area $\det(P,P')/2$. A sector containing vertex $X$ has area $(\det(P,X)+\det(X,P'))/2$, by dividing the piece into two triangles from $0$. These formulas give
$$
\begin{aligned}
(T_1,T_2,T_3)&=(1681/80,8379/212,14641/460),\\
(S_1,S_2,S_3)&=(83079/1060,6701/368,3471/20),\\
(A_1,A_2,A_3)&=(13775/92,1421/20,198505/848).
\end{aligned}
$$
For the last row use $A_i=S_i+\sum_{j\ne i}T_j$, since triple intersection has area zero. Multiplying and dividing the displayed fractions gives the ratio, positive gap and overlap comparison.

For the centroid construction, it is convenient to put $Q_\epsilon=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(3,0),(1-\epsilon,1+2\epsilon)\}$ for $0<\epsilon\le1$. It lies in $K_0$ since all coordinates are nonnegative and the last vertex has coordinate sum $2+\epsilon\le3$. The barycentric coefficients of $g$ are $(\epsilon,\epsilon,1)/(1+2\epsilon)$, which are strictly positive for every $\epsilon>0$. Translating by $-g$ puts the caps in the same three canonical directions, with the first cap in the centroid list corresponding to $-x-y\ge0$. The translated vertices are $(-1,-1),(2,-1),(-\epsilon,2\epsilon)$. Intersecting with the six rays gives radii $(r,s,s,r,1,1)$, where $r=3\epsilon/(1+2\epsilon)$ and $s=3\epsilon/(2+\epsilon)$. Using the two sector area formulas above, the single areas in centroid-cap order are $((1+r)/2,(1+r)/2,\epsilon s)$ and the double areas in order $01,02,12$ are $(1/2,rs/2,rs/2)$. Substituting $\epsilon=1/10$ gives all displayed areas and signed differences. The same formulas give ratios $245/27$ at $\epsilon=1/2$ and $3283/2$ at $\epsilon=1/100$.
