## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, let $T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_d)$ with centroid $g_T$ and volume $V$, and write $\Gamma=(d/(d+1))^d$. Suppose the only positive-volume integer fibers of an actual convex body in $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^d$ are translations of $aT,bT,T,cT$ at bases $0,1,2,3$ respectively, where $0<a\le2b-1$ and $1/2<b\le c\le1$. Write $h=[b-2d(1-c)/(d+1)]_+$ and $M=(a^d+b^d+1+c^d)V$. The raw mixed depth at the actual centroid of the fiber at base $2$ is at least $(\Gamma+h^d/2)V\ge\Gamma M/2$. Depth tests every closed ambient halfspace, with the original summed fiber volumes.

## Proof

First take the common-vertex fibers $aT,bT,T,cT$. In the triple at bases $1,2,3$ write $\zeta=z-2$ and $K=T-g_T$. The shear $x'=x-g_T-\zeta(c-1)g_T$ sends the retained triple to $A'=bK+(b+c-2)g_T$, $K$, $cK$. The selected mark becomes $(0,0)$ in the local coordinates. Translating $A'$ and $cK$ by $cg_T$ yields $bT-\eta\mathbf1$ and $cT$, where $\eta=2(1-c)/(d+1)$. Points in their intersection have nonnegative coordinates and coordinate sum at most $b-d\eta\le c$. The converse holds by the defining inequalities of $T$. Thus the intersection, if positive-volume, is $hT$ after the translation. Use $E=hT-cg_T$ as a subset of both $A'$ and $cK$ when $h>0$, and $E=\varnothing$ otherwise.

A nonvertical through-mark cap has the same continuous normal and thresholds $t,0,-t$ on these three bases. Apply fact 7ad3d72ee6061cf3 to the centered body $cK$ and its measurable subset $E$; the sum on $E,cK,cK$ is at least $\Gamma c^d V+h^dV/2$. In the same halfspace the unused middle part pays $(1-c^d)|K\cap\{u\cdot x\ge0\}|\ge\Gamma(1-c^d)V$. The centroid bound here also follows from the empty-subset case of 7ad3d72ee6061cf3, choosing its threshold to make the off-center $K$ cap empty. The omitted base $0$ and any unused endpoint mass contribute nonnegatively. A pure-base through-mark cap contains the middle and at least one whole neighboring fiber, giving $(1+b^d)V\ge(\Gamma+h^d/2)V$. Every containing cap contains its parallel through-mark cap.

The scalar inequality for the ordered radii gives
$$
1+[(1+1/d)b+2c-2]_+^d\ge(2b-1)^d+b^d+c^d.
$$
The positive part on the left is $(1+1/d)h$. Multiply by $\Gamma/2$, add $\Gamma/2$, and use $a\le2b-1$ to obtain $(\Gamma+h^d/2)V\ge\Gamma M/2$.

$$
u\cdot(m_z-m_j)\ge L(r_z-r_j)+b_0(z-j).
$$
For an original through-mark margin $u\cdot(x-m_j)+\beta(z-j)$, write $x=m_z+r_z X$ with $X\in K$. The margin dominates that of the cap $u\cdot x'+(\beta+b_0)(z-j)\ge0$ on the comparison point $x'=r_jv+r_z(X-v)$.
