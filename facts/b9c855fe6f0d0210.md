## Statement

Let $v_1=(1,0)$, $v_2=(0,1)$, $v_3=(-1,-1)$, $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$, and $\eta_*=2-\sqrt{33}/3$. The only nonempty integer fibers of a convex body are full planar fibers $A,K,B$ at $-1,0,1$, so $(A+B)/2\subset K$. Assume $0\in\operatorname{int}K$, all three lines $v_i\cdot x=-1$ cut the interior of $A$, and each ambient closed cap $H_i=\{(z,x):v_i\cdot x\ge z\}$ attains the actual raw mixed depth $Q$ at $(0,0)$. Assume $(1-\eta)T\subseteq B\subseteq T$ for $0\le\eta\le\eta_*$. Write $a=|A|$, $k=|K|$, $b=|B|$, and $M=a+k+b$. If $b\le a+k$ then $Q>2M/9$. If $b>a+k$, the point at base $1$ with continuous coordinate the ordinary centroid of $B$ has raw mixed depth at least $4b/9>2M/9$.

## Proof

The triangle $T$ has vertices $(-1,-1),(2,-1),(-1,2)$, area $9/2$ and centroid zero. Each $B$ trace has zero area because $B\subseteq T$. Let $u\le v\le w$ be the sorted three $A$ cap areas and put $F=u+v+w/4$. The hypotheses match the original marked-source cap profiles of 30309d81a867776b. Their inequalities are
$$
a\le w+v+u/4,\qquad k\le 9Q/4-F.
$$
In particular $\delta=a-u-v\le w$. Suppose $b\le a+k$ and $Q\le2(a+k+b)/9$. Substituting the latter into the bound on $k$ gives $k\le a+b-2F$. Conversely substituting that profile bound on $k$ into the assumed bound on $Q$ gives $Q\le4(a+b-F)/9$. Nonheaviness and the first inequality give $F\le a$, so $\delta\ge w/4>0$. Therefore the union of the two smaller closed $A$ caps misses an actual point of $A$. Its two corresponding normal values are strictly below $-1$, so the remaining value is above $2$ since the three normals sum to zero. Write $v_\ell$ for that largest-cap normal, $h=h_A(v_\ell)>2$ and $H=h+1>3$. The same upper bound on $Q$ gives
$$
Q\le 4(b+\delta-w/4)/9\le 2+w/3.
$$

Let $D=A\cap\{v_\ell\cdot x\ge-1\}$ and $A_\eta=A\cap\{v_\ell\cdot x\ge-1+\eta\}$. Choose $p\in A$ with $v_\ell\cdot p=h$. The homothety of $D$ about $p$ with ratio $1-\eta/H$ has minimum $v_\ell$ value $-1+\eta$ and lies in $A$. Thus $w_\eta=|A_\eta|\ge (1-\eta/3)^2w$. The facet segment $F_\eta$ of $(1-\eta)T$ at level $1-\eta$ has length $3(1-\eta)|v_\ell|$ and lies in $B$. By actual midpoint inclusion, $(A_\eta+F_\eta)/2$ lies in the original middle cap at level zero. Addition of the segment increases each nonempty line section of $A_\eta$ parallel to it by its length, on the perpendicular projection of width $(H-\eta)/|v_\ell|$. Hence
$$
Q\ge w+\tfrac14|A_\eta+F_\eta|
=w+\tfrac14[w_\eta+3(1-\eta)(H-\eta)]
> \left[1+\tfrac14(1-\eta/3)^2\right]w+\tfrac34(1-\eta)(3-\eta).
$$
The difference between this last bound and $2+w/3$ is
$$
\left[\tfrac23+\tfrac14(1-\eta/3)^2\right]w+
\tfrac14-3\eta+\tfrac34\eta^2.
$$
The constant polynomial is nonnegative through its smaller root $\eta_*$ and the coefficient of $w>0$ is positive. This contradicts the upper bound. At $0\le\eta\le1/12$ the constant term is at least $1/192$, by monotonicity there. This proof uses actual portions of $A,K,B$ at each step.

If $b>a+k$, apply 6769574fc1bd9f8d at full mass to the centroid of $B$. Every ambient containing halfspace has a trace containing that centroid, which either is a continuous halfspace or includes the whole fiber, so the total raw cap mass is at least $4b/9>2M/9$.
