## Statement

Let $A,K,B$ be planar convex bodies at levels $-1,0,1$ with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$, where $K$ is a triangle. Let $y\in\operatorname{int}K$ and let $v_1,v_2,v_3$ be nonzero spanning normals with $\sum_i v_i=0$. Set $b_i=2v_i\cdot y-h_B(v_i)$, and assume the caps
$$
v_i\cdot(x-y)+(v_i\cdot y-h_B(v_i))z\ge0
$$
have proper $A$ cuts and attain the true raw mixed depth $Q$ at $(0,y)$. Put $a=|A|$, $k=|K|$, $b=|B|$, and $M=a+k+b$. Suppose $Q\le2M/9$, $b\le a+k$, and $a<b/32$. Suppose $Q$ is the global raw maximum and that the raw maxima on both endpoint fibers are strictly below $Q$.

For every $p\in A$, the compatible contraction $A_t=p+(1-t)(A-p)$ with $K,B$ fixed strictly reduces the normalized global maximum depth for all sufficiently small $t>0$. The decrease is certified by rebalancing the same three cap normals at a new meeting point while keeping the $B$ support thresholds fixed. In particular the original configuration fails to be an admissible local minimum of normalized maximum depth.

## Proof

The three original $A$ traces $H_i^A=\{x:v_i\cdot x\ge b_i\}$ cover the plane because $\sum_i b_i<0$ by the positive sum of the support gaps of an interior point of $B$. The middle cuts are proper since $y$ is interior. The actual cap hypotheses give a common area $Q$. The present-singleton weight theorem applies to these same caps under the displayed area bounds. Writing $p_i$ for the normalized reciprocals of $\sigma_{K,i}+2\sigma_{A,i}$, it gives $p_i>2/9$ whenever $A\setminus\bigcup_{j\ne i}H_j^A$ is nonempty.

The genuine depth attainment, midpoint containment, proper cuts and balanced spanning normals also give the hypotheses of ba445d671279810a for these same $p_i$. It yields $\max_i p_i\le3/4$, so any two weights sum to at least $1/4$. A point of $A$ captured only by cap $i$ belongs to a present singleton region, hence has load $p_i>2/9$. Every other capture pattern has load at least $1/4$. Since there are finitely many patterns, some $\tau>2/9$ bounds $\sum_i p_i\mathbf1_{H_i^A}$ below throughout $A$.

Apply fact 25cb17da7fc8ba32 in dimension two with the proper covering halfplanes, weights $p_i$, and any $p\in A$. With $U_i=|A\cap H_i^A|$, the weighted shell loss $\overline L(p)=\sum_i p_i[2U_i+(b_i-v_i\cdot p)\sigma_{A,i}]$ satisfies $\overline L(p)\ge2a\tau>4a/9\ge2aQ/M$.

Fact 3e7f0a31e608bd79 applies to the original common-budget caps and the contraction, since the $A,K$ cuts are proper, the normals balance and span, midpoint inclusion holds, and the endpoint maxima have strict gaps below the original global maximum $Q$. The endpoint raw depths here equal the ordinary ones because a prescribed ordinary endpoint cap can be extended with enough tilt to exclude the other two compact fibers. That fact yields an actual admissible deformation and a rebalanced common budget $q(t)$ bounding the new global raw maximum for small positive $t$. For $M(t)=|A_t|+k+b$, its normalized upper bound starts at $q(0)/M(0)=Q/M$ and has derivative $[-M\overline L(p)+2aQ]/M^2<0$. This proves strict descent for every fixed contraction center.
