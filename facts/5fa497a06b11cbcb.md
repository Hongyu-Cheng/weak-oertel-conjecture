## Statement

Let finitely many compatible positive convex $d$-fibers at fixed bases have canonical hull $C$, sampled mixed volume $dm$ of mass $M$, fiber centroids $g_z$, and pooled within-fiber covariance $\Sigma=M^{-1}\sum_z\int_{K_z}(X-g_z)(X-g_z)^{\mathsf T}\,dX$. Set $N=d+1$ and $\Gamma(u)=(u^{\mathsf T}\Sigma u)^{1/2}$. On a neighborhood of $C$ let $L>0$ be the minimum of finitely many affine functions with $\int\log L\,dm=0$. Use the actual profile $f(z,y)=(L(z,y)E_{K_z}(y)/M)^{1/N}$, where $E_K(y)$ is the infimum of $\int_K[1+a\cdot(X-y)]^{-N}\,dX$ over affine denominators strictly positive on $K$.

In one old covering group $i$, take finitely many mass-$Q$ caps $N_{ij}\cdot w\ge c_{ij}$ with continuous normal components $u_{ij}$ satisfying $\Gamma(u_{ij})=1$, proper total coareas $S_{ij}>0$, and positive weights $\rho_{ij}$ summing to one. Put $b_i=\sum_j\rho_{ij}c_{ij}$. At fixed $\varepsilon>0$ assume that the old expression $\sum_j\rho_{ij}N_{ij}\cdot w-b_i-\varepsilon f(w)$ is nonnegative on the assigned fibers and has precisely the finitely many own-interior zeros $Y_{ia}=(p_{ia},y_{ia})$. Consider a compatible signed radial path with uniform $O(t)$ radial displacement and bounded inward first speeds $v$, a continuous raw direction $h$ with $h+cL$ concave on the common neighborhood for some $c\ge0$, and independent cap-normal speeds $\nu_{ij}$ (continuous components $\dot u_{ij}$) shared across all contacts of the group. The current positive profile uses the raw $L+th$, geometrically normalized on the current fibers. Assume the actual current contact minimum localizes to the old zeros, all old zeros remain available, and the equal-budget continued caps and strict depth gaps outside the complete old cover are available. These are geometric hypotheses on the application.

Use the old data $L_a=L(Y_{ia})$, $E_a=E_{K_{p_{ia}}}(y_{ia})$, $f_a=f(Y_{ia})$, and $P_a(X)=1+a_a\cdot(X-y_{ia})$ for the optimal energy denominator. Define $D=\int_{\partial S}v\,dS$, $C_a=\int_{\partial K_{p_{ia}}}P_a^{-N}v\,dS$, $J_i=\int_{\partial S}\sum_j\rho_{ij}[u_{ij}\cdot(X-g_z)]^2v\,dS$, $s(v)=M^{-1}\int_{\partial S}\log L\,v\,dS$, and $H(h)=M^{-1}\int_S h/L\,dm$. Then the first derivative of the exact assigned minimum with moving normals and current profile and covariance is $\min_a A_{ia}$, where
$$
A_{ia}=\sum_j\rho_{ij}\nu_{ij}\cdot Y_{ia}
-\varepsilon f_a\sum_j\rho_{ij}\nabla\Gamma(u_{ij})\cdot\dot u_{ij}
+\varepsilon f_a\left[\frac{C_a}{NE_a}+\frac{J_i}{2M}-\frac{(N+2)D}{2NM}
-\frac{s(v)}N-\frac{h(Y_{ia})}{NL_a}+\frac{H(h)}N\right].
$$
For threshold speed $r$, the conservative loss of cap $j$ is
$$
R_{ij}^{0}=B_{ij}(v)+S_{ij}r-m_{ij}\cdot\nu_{ij}
-\int_{\rm out}\left[\frac{\nu_{ij}\cdot w-r}{\|u_{ij}\|}-v(w)\right]_+dS .
$$
Here $m_{ij}$ is the full proper coarea first moment (coarea is section area divided by $\|u_{ij}\|$), $B_{ij}(v)$ keeps the captured boundary speed on proper fibers and the entire speed on strict-full and inside-supporting fibers, and the integral is over the outside supporting faces. The equalized conservative loss with budget $\min_a A_{ia}$ is the minimum over $a$ of the conservative equalized losses with budget $A_{ia}$. It is concave in the joint direction and no larger than the actual equalized loss.

## Proof

For small positive $t$, $L+th=(1-tc)L+t(h+cL)$ is positive on the current hull and concave. Let $M_t$ be its current sampled mass. The logarithm of the raw normalization factor is $-M_t^{-1}\int_{S(t)}\log(L+th)\,dm_t$. Its derivative is $s(v)-H(h)$: the old integral of $\log L$ is zero, the shape derivative is $-\int_{\partial S}\log L\,v\,dS$, and the uniform raw logarithm expansion contributes $\int_S h/L\,dm$. The shape derivative uses the oriented radial shell calculation of da49875a9737b172, applicable to the continuous integrand $\log L$ on a common compact neighborhood. Uniformity of the raw expansion follows from positivity and continuity there, and the current mixed volumes converge to the old ones. Thus $\dot{\log L}(w)=s(v)+h(w)/L(w)-H(h)$ at each fixed interior point.

The uniform signed energy and covariance lemma da49875a9737b172 gives $\dot M=-D$, $\dot E_a=-C_a$, and the weighted fixed-normal contribution $\sum_j\rho_{ij}\dot\Gamma(u_{ij})=(D-J_i)/(2M)$. Differentiating the moving norm contributes the additional $\sum_j\rho_{ij}\nabla\Gamma(u_{ij})\cdot\dot u_{ij}$. The energy expansion is uniform on compact own-interior neighborhoods of the finitely many old marks, and the $L$ expansion there is uniform with continuous coefficient. Differentiating the term $-\varepsilon f_t(w)\sum_j\rho_{ij}\Gamma_t(u_{ij}+t\dot u_{ij})$, together with the moving affine part, gives exactly the displayed $A_{ia}$. Evaluation at the old contacts bounds the first upper difference quotient by the least coefficient. A minimizing sequence approaches an old contact by hypothesis, hence lies in a common interior neighborhood where the old expression is nonnegative and the first expansion is uniform. This yields the matching lower derivative bound. No motion derivative of the optimizing mark is needed.

The moving-cap shell and coarea calculation in the proof of 9ee0d9a1e9f65259 uses only the above radial hypotheses, the old caps and proper coarea, and the independent affine speeds $(\nu_{ij},r)$. In particular its proper-plane loss is $S_{ij}r-m_{ij}\cdot\nu_{ij}$, the inside-support hinge is $[(r-\nu_{ij}\cdot w)/\|u_{ij}\|-v]_+$, and the outside-support hinge is the negative term displayed here. This use of the shell calculation is independent of the profile in that fact's application. Deleting only the inside hinge proves $R^0_{ij}\le R_{ij}$. All these threshold responses are continuous and strictly increasing onto $\mathbb R$, with increments at least $S_{ij}$ times the threshold increment. Weighted inversion therefore preserves ordering of the two losses and is increasing in the budget, giving the minimum over contacts. As in the branch formula of 9ee0d9a1e9f65259, replacing the negative outside hinge by its infimum of linear terms gives each equalized loss as the infimum of the corresponding linear equalized branches. Equality is attained using the same outside sign selector in each term at the solved thresholds. The resulting infimum over branches and contacts is concave. Uniformity of the moving-shell expansion for bounded threshold speeds, and the proper inverse bound in that calculation, identify these first losses with the continued thresholds; the nonnegative weighted current margins guarantee the cover on each assigned current fiber.
