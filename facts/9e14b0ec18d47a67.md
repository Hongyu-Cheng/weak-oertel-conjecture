## Source

- [8a1fb011e3d0549f](../candidates/8a1fb011e3d0549f.json), 8a1fb011e3d0549f:05 §6, lines 183–229, two allocations make the OC-level region empty.

## Statement

Let $T=\{(x_1,x_2)\in\mathbb R^2:x_1,x_2\ge0,\ x_1+x_2\le1\}$, $V=|T|=1/2$, and take $A=T/10$, $K=B=T$ at bases $-1,0,1$. For their actual total mass $M=(201/100)V$ and $r=(4/9)M/2$, the allocation intersection
$
C_r=\bigcap_{\substack{p_A,p_K,p_B\ge0\\ p_A+p_K+p_B=r}}\operatorname{conv}\!\left(D_K(p_K)\cup\frac{D_A(p_A)+D_B(p_B)}2\right)
$
is empty, where $D_F(p)$ is the ordinary raw halfspace depth region and $D_F(0)=F$. It remains empty if the ordinary depth fields are replaced by centroidal homothetic inner cores.

## Proof

The convex hull of the three labeled bodies has exactly these integer slices, since the endpoint average $(11/20)T$ lies in $T$. To check this, any mixture at base zero uses equal weights on the endpoints, and groups their contributions into a convex mixture of that midpoint set and the given middle body. There are no other integer bases. A triangle of area $W$ has ordinary depth at most $4W/9$ at every point: some barycentric coordinate is at least $1/3$, and the closed cap toward that vertex with threshold equal to the coordinate has area at most $W(1-1/3)^2$. Put $\gamma=4/9$.

With allocation $(0,r,0)$ the middle region is empty since $r=\gamma(201/200)V>\gamma V$, so $C_r\subset(11/20)T$. With allocation $(2\gamma V/100,\gamma(197/200)V,0)$ the endpoint $A$ region is empty by the same triangle bound with $W=V/100$. Thus $C_r$ must also lie in $D_K(197V/450)$. At each $x\in(11/20)T$ the lower cap of $K$ for $x_1+x_2$ through $x$ has area at most $121V/400<197V/450$. Hence the two allocated hulls are disjoint.

For the core variant, for any planar fiber $F$ of area $W$ and centroid $g_F$, the core at level $p\le\gamma W$ is $g_F+(1-\sqrt{p/(\gamma W)})(F-g_F)$, and is empty for larger $p$. At $p>0$ write a core point as $(1-t)x+tg_F$ with $t=\sqrt{p/(\gamma W)}$ and $x\in F$. Given an ordinary halfspace through that point, map the parallel centroid half-cap by $X\mapsto(1-t)x+tX$. Its image lies in $F$ and in the point's halfspace, and has area at least $t^2\gamma W=p$ by the ordinary full-mass centroid bound of 6769574fc1bd9f8d. Thus all the core sets are contained in their ordinary depth fields, also at $p=0$, and cannot restore nonemptiness.
