## Statement

Retain one common actual canonical hard instance of accepted facts 6b09aae189415c70, d5d5de35c3f25af9, and f83d08a46c19ca2f. Thus
\[
 C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d,
 \qquad
 C_z:=\{y:(z,y)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}a_z,
\tag{1}
\]
the common hard normalization has \(Q>0\), its complete-core capacity is \(G\), and
\[
 Q\geq\frac3{32}(W-4G),
 \qquad
 G<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{2}
\]
Use the degree tag of d5d5de35c3f25af9 and write
\[
 A:=A_d,
 \qquad
 w_z:=w_z^d=\frac{d_z}{2}
 \quad(z\in A).
\tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
 0<w_z\leq\frac{a_z}{4}
 \qquad(z\in A).
\tag{4}
\]

Let \(\mathcal E\) be any nonempty finite set of unordered pairs
\[
 e=\{u_e,v_e\}\subseteq A,
 \qquad
 u_e\ne v_e,
 \qquad
 u_e\equiv v_e\pmod2.
\tag{5}
\]
Put
\[
 m_e:=\frac{u_e+v_e}{2}\in\mathbb Z^n,
 \qquad
 N_t:=|\{e\in\mathcal E:m_e=t\}|.
\tag{6}
\]
Then the actual physical midpoint fibers obey the exact collision-sensitive root budget
\[
 \boxed{
 4\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}
 \frac{\sqrt{w_{u_e}w_{v_e}}}{N_{m_e}}
 \leq
 \sum_{t:N_t>0}a_t
 \leq W
 <\frac{803}{75}Q.}
\tag{7}
\]
In particular, if the midpoint map \(e\mapsto m_e\) is injective, then
\[
 \boxed{
 4\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\sqrt{w_{u_e}w_{v_e}}
 <\frac{803}{75}Q.}
\tag{8}
\]
More generally, with \(N_{\max}:=\max_tN_t\),
\[
 \boxed{
 N_{\max}>
 \frac{300}{803Q}
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\sqrt{w_{u_e}w_{v_e}}.}
\tag{8a}
\]
Every other integer fiber created by the convex hull of the same sources contributes a nonnegative summand to \(W\), so (7) already accounts for the complete convex-body completion and cannot be weakened by additional intermediate fibers.

Apply this, in the special case \(n=2\), to the dense hub-by-four-leaf pattern used to calibrate the retained-mass alternative of f83d08a46c19ca2f. Let
\[
 z(t):=(2t,2t^2),
 \qquad
 r_i:=z(H_i),
 \qquad
 \ell_{b,k}:=z(T_b+3k)
 \quad
 (1\leq i,b\leq m,\ 0\leq k\leq3),
\tag{9}
\]
where the \(H_i\) are distinct, all \(T_b+3k\) are distinct, and the two parameter families are disjoint. Suppose all \(4m^2\) hub-leaf pairs belong to \(\mathcal E\). Give the sources the canonical degree-tag weights
\[
 w_{r_i}:=\frac{Q}{4m},
 \qquad
 w_{\ell_{b,k}}:=\frac{|\kappa_k|Q}{32m},
 \qquad
 (\kappa_0,\kappa_1,\kappa_2,\kappa_3):=(1,-3,3,-1).
\tag{10}
\]
Then all \(4m^2\) midpoint bases are distinct, and (8) forces
\[
 m\frac{1+\sqrt3}{\sqrt2}<\frac{803}{75},
 \qquad
 \boxed{m\leq5.}
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently the limit \(m\to\infty\) used in that calibration cannot be realized in an actual canonical hard instance with the same \(Q\) and source budgets. The lemma does not turn an arbitrary retained affine defect or the pairing affine dual of f83d08a46c19ca2f into a \(Q/100\) terminal; it isolates midpoint collision, rather than unrecorded intermediate lattice content, as the first remaining global completion issue.

## Proof

Accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives the first inequality in (2), and its canonical hard branch has a nonnegative ledger containing \(G\), so it gives the second inequality in (2). Rearranging yields
\[
 W\leq\frac{32}{3}Q+4G
 <\left(\frac{32}{3}+\frac1{25}\right)Q
 =\frac{803}{75}Q.
\tag{12}
\]
Accepted fact d5d5de35c3f25af9 gives (3)-(4) in the same hard instance and with the same \(Q\).

Fix \(e=\{u,v\}\in\mathcal E\) and put \(t=(u+v)/2\). Same parity gives \(t\in\mathbb Z^n\). Convexity of \(C\) gives
\[
 \frac12(C_u+C_v)\subseteq C_t.
\tag{13}
\]
The exact Brunn--Minkowski statement audited in accepted fact 11e347c295e9430b applies to the positive-volume compact convex fibers \(C_u,C_v\subseteq\mathbb R^d\). Hence
\[
 a_t^{1/d}
 \geq\frac{a_u^{1/d}+a_v^{1/d}}2
 \geq(a_ua_v)^{1/(2d)},
\tag{14}
\]
where the second inequality is arithmetic-geometric mean. Raising to the positive integer power \(d\) and using (4) gives
\[
 a_t\geq\sqrt{a_ua_v}
 \geq4\sqrt{w_uw_v}.
\tag{15}
\]

For a fixed target \(t\) with \(N_t>0\), sum (15) over its \(N_t\) preimages after division by \(N_t\):
\[
 4\sum_{\substack{e\in\mathcal E\\m_e=t}}
 \frac{\sqrt{w_{u_e}w_{v_e}}}{N_t}
 \leq a_t.
\tag{16}
\]
Now sum (16) over the distinct physical midpoint bases. Each fiber volume \(a_t\) is counted once, irrespective of source reuse or the number of pairs colliding at \(t\), so
\[
 4\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}
 \frac{\sqrt{w_{u_e}w_{v_e}}}{N_{m_e}}
 \leq\sum_{t:N_t>0}a_t
 \leq W.
\tag{17}
\]
Combining (17) with (12) proves (7). If every \(N_t=1\), this is (8). Fibers at any other integer bases have nonnegative volume and appear only in the final total \(W\), proving the completion assertion after (8).

Since \(N_{m_e}\leq N_{\max}\) for every edge, (7) also gives
\[
 \frac4{N_{\max}}
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\sqrt{w_{u_e}w_{v_e}}
 <\frac{803}{75}Q.
\]
Rearranging proves (8a).

It remains to audit the dense block. Suppose
\[
 \frac{z(H_i)+z(T_b+3k)}2
 =
 \frac{z(H_{i'})+z(T_{b'}+3k')}2.
\tag{18}
\]
Equality of the two coordinates gives equality both of the sums and of the sums of squares of the corresponding two integer parameters. Their products are therefore equal as well, so the two unordered parameter pairs coincide. The hub parameters are disjoint from all leaf parameters, and all displayed parameters within each family are distinct. Thus \(i=i'\), \(b=b'\), and \(k=k'\). Hence the midpoint map is injective on the \(4m^2\) hub-leaf pairs.

For one fixed pair \((i,b)\), (10) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{k=0}^3\sqrt{w_{r_i}w_{\ell_{b,k}}}
 &=\frac Qm\sum_{k=0}^3\sqrt{\frac{|\kappa_k|}{128}}\\
 &=\frac Qm\frac{1+\sqrt3}{4\sqrt2}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{19}
\]
There are \(m^2\) choices of \((i,b)\). Substituting (19) into (8) and cancelling \(Q>0\) gives the first inequality in (11).

Finally,
\[
 \frac{803\sqrt2}{75(1+\sqrt3)}<6.
\tag{20}
\]
For an exact check, \(803\sqrt2>450\), and squaring the equivalent inequality
\(803\sqrt2<450(1+\sqrt3)\) twice reduces it to
\[
 (803^2-450^2)^2
 =195637251481
 <261147645000
 =2(803\cdot450)^2.
\tag{21}
\]
Thus (11) implies the positive integer \(m\) is at most five. This proves every assertion.
