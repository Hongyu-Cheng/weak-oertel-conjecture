## Statement

Let $A,K,B\subset\mathbb R^2$ be convex bodies at the only integer bases $-1,0,1$, respectively, with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$. Use planar area on each fiber. Fix $y\in\operatorname{int}K$ and nonzero spanning vectors $v_1,v_2,v_3$ with $\sum_i v_i=0$. Set $h_i=h_B(v_i)$, where $h_B(v)=\max_{x\in B}v\cdot x$, and $b_i=2v_i\cdot y-h_i$. Assume each of the three closed ambient caps
$$
v_i\cdot(x-y)+(v_i\cdot y-h_i)z\ge0
$$
attains the actual mixed depth at $(0,y)$, and cuts both $A$ and $K$ properly. The trace on $B$ is the supporting face $F_i=B\cap\{v_i\cdot x=h_i\}$. Let $a_i,k_i$ be the lengths of the $A,K$ cutting chords, respectively, divided by $\lVert v_i\rVert$. Put $\lambda_i=1/(2a_i+k_i)$ and $\Lambda=\sum_i\lambda_i$.

Let $J$ be rotation through a right angle. Give the three cutting lines compatible coordinates by writing $x=y+r v_i/\lVert v_i\rVert^2+uJv_i$, using the appropriate constant $r$ on each line. Let $c_{A,i}$ and $c_i$ be the $u$-centers of the $A,K$ chords. Then $F_i$ contains a $u$-interval of length $a_i$ centered at $-c_{A,i}-(k_i/a_i)c_i$. In particular,
$$
k_i\ge a_i,\qquad
|c_i|\le \frac{a_i(k_i-a_i)}{k_i+2a_i}.
$$
For the single centrally symmetric body $(K-y)\cap(y-K)$, its Minkowski gauge $N$ satisfies
$$
2\lambda_i\le N(Jv_i)\le 6\lambda_i,\qquad
\lambda_i\le3\sum_{j\ne i}\lambda_j,\qquad
\frac{\lambda_i}{\Lambda}\le\frac34.
$$

## Proof

Fix $i$ and suppress its subscript. In the stated coordinates the planar area element is $dr\,du$, since the coordinate basis has determinant of absolute value one. The original cut thresholds in $r$ are $v\cdot y-h,0,h-v\cdot y$ on $A,K,B$, respectively. The origins on the endpoint lines average to $y$. Let $I_B$ be the $u$-interval of the $B$ support face.

Add $\varepsilon(\eta u+\beta z)$ to the cap margin for arbitrary real $\eta,\beta$ and take $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. The function $u$ is the linear coordinate of $x-y$, so the perturbed affine cap passes through $(0,y)$ and its mass is at least the original mass. On each line with $u$ fixed, the $r$-threshold moves by $-\varepsilon(\eta u+\beta z)$. The retained length on a nonempty $r$-slice $[l,m]$ with cutoff $s$ is $[m-\max(l,s)]_+$. At a proper cut its derivative is the signed margin velocity wherever the original cutoff lies in the interior of the slice. At the upper endpoint of a nondegenerate slice it is the positive part of the velocity. For almost every $u$ on a proper section the cutoff is interior, since the relative interior of a proper chord lies in the body interior. For almost every $u$ on a positive-length support face of $B$ the normal slice is nondegenerate: the interior of the projection of a convex body is the projection of its interior, as follows by projecting a segment from an interior point past the projected point in question. Outside the original section the derivative is zero. The bounded difference quotients on the compact $u$-projections allow integration. Thus
$$
0\le a(\eta c_A-\beta)+k\eta c+\int_{I_B}(\eta u+\beta)_+\,du.
$$
Taking $\eta=0,\beta=1$ gives $|I_B|\ge a>0$. For each $\eta\in\{1,-1\}$ choose $\beta$ so that the top length-$a$ subinterval of $I_B$ in the direction $\eta$ is $\{\eta u+\beta\ge0\}\cap I_B$. If $|I_B|=a$ use the whole interval. The $\beta$ terms cancel, giving
$$
0\le \eta\left(ac_A+kc+\int_{\{\eta u+\beta\ge0\}\cap I_B}u\,du\right).
$$
The two signs put $-c_A-(k/a)c$ between the centers of the leftmost and rightmost length-$a$ subintervals. Every center between them is attained by translating a length-$a$ subinterval inside $I_B$. Its midpoint sum with the actual $A$ chord is an interval of length $a$ with center $-(k/(2a))c$. By compatibility this interval lies in the actual $K$ chord, giving $(1+k/(2a))|c|\le(k-a)/2$ and $k\ge a$.

Since $y$ is interior, the symmetric core is a convex body containing $0$ in its interior, and its gauge is a norm by symmetry and convexity. Along $Jv_i$ its positive radial parameter is the nearer of the two endpoint distances in the $u$-coordinate:
$$
\frac{k_i+2a_i}{6}\le
\frac{k_i^2+2a_i^2}{2(k_i+2a_i)}
\le \frac{k_i}{2}-|c_i|
\le \frac{k_i+2a_i}{2}.
$$
The first inequality is $3(k_i^2+2a_i^2)-(k_i+2a_i)^2=2(k_i-a_i)^2\ge0$, and the second is the chord bound. Taking reciprocals gives the two gauge comparisons. Finally $Jv_i=-\sum_{j\ne i}Jv_j$, so $2\lambda_i\le N(Jv_i)\le\sum_{j\ne i}N(Jv_j)\le6\sum_{j\ne i}\lambda_j$. This gives the remaining bounds.
