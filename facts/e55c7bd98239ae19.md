## Statement

Let $C$ be a compact convex mixed body of positive mixed $d$-volume, $d\ge1$, and $y=(p,x)\in C$ with $p\in\mathbb Z^n$. Write $\mu_r,M_r,g_r$ for the original restriction, mass and centroid in residue class $r$ modulo $2$, defining $g_r$ when $M_r>0$. Under $T(w)=y+(w-y)/2$, the measures $\eta_r=2^{-d}T_\#\mu_r$ occupy the distinct base lattices $(p+r)/2+\mathbb Z^n$. Exactly the class $p\bmod2$ lands on $\mathbb Z^n$. Their sum is mixed $d$-volume of $T(C)$ on $(\tfrac12\mathbb Z)^n$. For every through-$y$ closed halfspace $H$, $\eta_r(H)=2^{-d}\mu_r(H)$, and the positive-mass class centroids become $(y+g_r)/2$. A positive-total nonnegative class mixture with weights $c_r$ has centroid $y$ exactly when $\sum_r c_rM_r(g_r-y)=0$. Such weights exist precisely when $y$ lies in the convex hull of the occupied class centroids, and with all occupied weights positive precisely when $y$ is in its relative interior. Equal weights work precisely at the original centroid.

If $r=p+s\bmod2$ with $s\in\{0,1\}^n$, relabeling that contracted coset by $-s/2$ in the base changes the margin $a\cdot(z-p)+u\cdot(v-x)$ to $[a\cdot(z-p)+u\cdot(v-x)-a\cdot s]/2$. The relabeled fiber can fail to lie in $C$.

## Proof

The affine base map is $(p+z)/2$, which maps each original coset bijectively onto its stated image lattice. At every half-lattice output its inverse is integral. The continuous Jacobian is $2^{-d}$, so summing the reindexed class measures gives exactly the half-lattice measure. The through-anchor margins scale by $1/2$ and the centroid of each pushforward is the pushforward of its centroid. Thus mixture coefficients in the centroid hull are exactly $c_rM_r/\sum_s c_sM_s$. The image of the relative interior of the coefficient simplex under the affine centroid map is the relative interior of the hull. Directly, strictly positive coefficients cannot lie on a proper supporting face. Conversely, from a relative-interior point move a small distance away from the average of all the listed centroids while remaining in the hull, then express the original point as a convex combination of that new point and a positive amount of the average. Equal class weights make the original mixture the full original measure.

After the stated base shift the map becomes $((p+z-s)/2,(x+v)/2)$, giving the margin formula. Take $n=1$ and $C=\operatorname{conv}((\{0\}\times\epsilon B_d)\cup(\{1\}\times B_d))$ for the Euclidean unit ball and $0<\epsilon<1/2$. At $y=(0,0)$ the contracted class at base $1/2$ is $(1/2)B_d$. Shifting it to $0$ puts points outside the actual fiber $\epsilon B_d$.
