## Statement

Retain one common canonical ratio-cut instance of accepted facts 27024aed67e77532, 3e918c586cba7e00, 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, and 6bfe50fed9a5540b.  Thus \(r=(r_e)\) is the full half-strength canonical product reservoir,
\[
 Q:=\sum_e r_e>0,
 \qquad
 d_y(r):=\sum_{e\ni y}r_e,
\tag{1}
\]
\(b=(b_e)\) is the completed-midpoint submatching of accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b, and \(\omega^b=(\omega_e^b)\) is the gauge-load-minimal fresh KKT load for capacity \(b\).  Put
\[
 0\leq\omega_e^b\leq b_e\leq r_e,
 \qquad
 \Omega_b:=\sum_e\omega_e^b,
 \qquad
 \beta_y^b:=\sum_{e\ni y}\omega_e^b.
\tag{2}
\]
Let \(s_b:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) be a primal optimizer paired with this fresh KKT load.  Write \(R:=R_C(r)\), let \(G\) be the canonical complete-core capacity, and let \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}\) be the original congestion loss.  Then accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b gives
\[
 |r-b|\leq R+B_{\mathrm{cong}}+G,
 \qquad
 B_{\mathrm{cong}}\leq R,
 \qquad
 R_C(b)\leq R.
\tag{3}
\]

Retain the physical endpoint set \(Y\), endpoint weights \(w_y>0\), and whole labels \(\sigma\) from accepted facts 27024aed67e77532 and 3e918c586cba7e00.  Thus every label has distinct physical opposite endpoints \(a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\), weight \(\widehat\theta_\sigma>0\), and
\[
 w_y=
 \sum_{\sigma:\,y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}
 \widehat\theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 0<w_y\leq d_y(r).
\tag{4}
\]
Put
\[
 q_y:=\min\left\{1,\frac{\beta_y^b}{w_y}\right\},
 \qquad
 \delta_\sigma:=\widehat\theta_\sigma
 |q_{a(\sigma)}-q_{b(\sigma)}|,
\tag{5}
\]
and assume the ratio-cut alternative
\[
 I_\cap:=\sum_\sigma\delta_\sigma>\frac{3Q}{400}.
\tag{6}
\]
For every \(\sigma\) with \(\delta_\sigma>0\), let \(\ell(\sigma)\) be its unique lower-\(q\) endpoint.  Define the two nonnegative completion-slack arrays
\[
 s_e^{\mathrm{del}}:=r_e-b_e,
 \qquad
 s_e^{\mathrm{hit}}:=b_e-\omega_e^b.
\tag{7}
\]

There are nonnegative numbers
\[
 \lambda_{\sigma,e}^{\mathrm{del}},
 \quad
 \lambda_{\sigma,e}^{\mathrm{hit}}
 \qquad
 (\delta_\sigma>0,\ e\ni\ell(\sigma))
\tag{8}
\]
such that every positive difference label is charged exactly once,
\[
 \boxed{
 \delta_\sigma
 =\sum_{e\ni\ell(\sigma)}
 \left(
 \lambda_{\sigma,e}^{\mathrm{del}}
 +\lambda_{\sigma,e}^{\mathrm{hit}}
 \right),}
\tag{9}
\]
and, for every physical endpoint \(y\) and every edge \(e\ni y\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\sigma:\,\ell(\sigma)=y}
 \lambda_{\sigma,e}^{\mathrm{del}}
 \leq s_e^{\mathrm{del}},
 \qquad
 \sum_{\sigma:\,\ell(\sigma)=y}
 \lambda_{\sigma,e}^{\mathrm{hit}}
 \leq s_e^{\mathrm{hit}}.}
\tag{10}
\]
The capacities in (10) are edge-end capacities: the two endpoints of one edge are distinct and may each use one copy of the displayed bound.

Put
\[
 J_{\mathrm{del}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma,e}\lambda_{\sigma,e}^{\mathrm{del}},
 \qquad
 J_{\mathrm{hit}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma,e}\lambda_{\sigma,e}^{\mathrm{hit}}.
\tag{11}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 I_\cap=J_{\mathrm{del}}+J_{\mathrm{hit}},
 \qquad
 J_{\mathrm{del}}\leq2|r-b|,
 \qquad
 J_{\mathrm{hit}}\leq2|b-\omega^b|.}
\tag{12}
\]
Every edge with \(s_e^{\mathrm{hit}}>0\) is hit by the same affine map \(s_b\):
\[
 \boxed{s_b(m_e)\in L_e
 \quad\text{whenever }b_e-\omega_e^b>0.}
\tag{13}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{|b-\omega^b|\leq R_C(b)\leq R,}
\tag{14}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 I_\cap\leq2|r-b|+2|b-\omega^b|
 \leq6R+2G.}
\tag{15}
\]
In particular, the ratio-cut branch forces
\[
 \boxed{
 3R+G>\frac{3Q}{800},
 \qquad
 \max\{R,G\}>\frac{3Q}{3200}.}
\tag{16}
\]
It also has the sharper alternative
\[
 \boxed{
 |b-\omega^b|>\frac{3Q}{1600}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 |r-b|>\frac{3Q}{1600}.}
\tag{17}
\]
In the first case \(R>3Q/1600\); in the second case \(2R+G>3Q/1600\).

## Proof

All objects above are the same physical, collision-aggregated objects as in accepted facts 27024aed67e77532 and 3e918c586cba7e00.  Fix \(y\in Y\), and put
\[
 L_y:=\sum_{\sigma:\,\ell(\sigma)=y}\delta_\sigma.
\tag{18}
\]
If \(\ell(\sigma)=y\), then \(q_y<1\), and
\[
 \delta_\sigma
 =\widehat\theta_\sigma
 \bigl(q_{h(\sigma)}-q_y\bigr)
 \leq\widehat\theta_\sigma(1-q_y),
\tag{19}
\]
where \(h(\sigma)\) is the higher-\(q\) endpoint.  Sum (19) over labels whose lower endpoint is \(y\), and use (4).  Since \(q_y=\beta_y^b/w_y\) when \(q_y<1\), this gives
\[
 L_y
 \leq(1-q_y)w_y
 =w_y-\beta_y^b.
\tag{20}
\]
Accepted fact 27024aed67e77532 gives \(w_y\leq d_y(r)\) and \(\beta_y^b\leq d_y(r)\).  Hence
\[
 L_y\leq d_y(r)-\beta_y^b
 =\sum_{e\ni y}
 \left(s_e^{\mathrm{del}}+s_e^{\mathrm{hit}}\right).
\tag{21}
\]

For each fixed \(y\), regard the two quantities \(s_e^{\mathrm{del}}\) and \(s_e^{\mathrm{hit}}\), over all \(e\ni y\), as finitely many separate bins.  Their total capacity is at least the total demand \(L_y\) by (21).  Distribute the finitely many demands \(\delta_\sigma\), \(\ell(\sigma)=y\), among those bins, stopping when each demand is filled.  This elementary greedy allocation produces (8)--(10).  It is performed independently at each physical endpoint, so every label is charged only at its unique lower endpoint and every physical collision has already been aggregated in \(w_y\) and \(\beta_y^b\).

Summing (9) proves the first identity in (12).  Summing either inequality in (10) first over the two distinct endpoints of an edge and then over edges gives the two upper bounds in (12).  Equivalently, direct summation of (20)--(21) also gives the coarser identity
\[
 I_\cap
 \leq\sum_{y\in Y}(w_y-\beta_y^b)_+
 \leq\sum_y\bigl(d_y(r)-\beta_y^b\bigr)
 =2(Q-\Omega_b).
\tag{22}
\]
Because \(0\leq\omega^b\leq b\leq r\),
\[
 Q-\Omega_b=|r-b|+|b-\omega^b|,
\tag{23}
\]
so (22) agrees with (12).

Apply the complementary saturation statement of accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d to the fresh capacity \(b\), its paired primal optimizer \(s_b\), and its gauge-load-minimal dual load \(\omega^b\).  If \(s_b(m_e)\notin L_e\), then \(\omega_e^b=b_e\).  Its contrapositive is exactly (13).  Therefore the one affine map \(s_b\) hits every edge on which \(s_e^{\mathrm{hit}}>0\), and the definition of \(R_C(b)\) gives
\[
 |b-\omega^b|
 \leq\sum_{e:\,s_b(m_e)\in L_e}b_e
 \leq R_C(b).
\tag{24}
\]
Accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b gives \(R_C(b)\leq R\), proving (14).

In the notation of accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b, one has \(|b|=\Lambda\) and
\[
 \Lambda\geq Q-R-B_{\mathrm{cong}}-G.
\tag{25}
\]
Thus
\[
 |r-b|=Q-|b|
 \leq R+B_{\mathrm{cong}}+G
 \leq2R+G,
\tag{26}
\]
where the last inequality uses \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}\leq R\) from the same accepted fact.  Combine (12), (14), and (26) to obtain
\[
 I_\cap
 \leq2(2R+G)+2R
 =6R+2G,
\]
which proves (15).

Equations (6) and (15) give
\[
 3R+G>\frac{3Q}{800}.
\tag{27}
\]
Since \(3R+G\leq4\max\{R,G\}\), equation (16) follows.  Finally, (12) and (6) imply
\[
 \max\{J_{\mathrm{del}},J_{\mathrm{hit}}\}
 \geq\frac{I_\cap}{2}
 >\frac{3Q}{800}.
\tag{28}
\]
If the maximum in (28) is \(J_{\mathrm{hit}}\), then (12) gives \(|b-\omega^b|>3Q/1600\), and (14) gives the stated lower bound on \(R\).  If it is \(J_{\mathrm{del}}\), then \(|r-b|>3Q/1600\), and (26) gives the stated lower bound on \(2R+G\).  This proves (17) and completes the proof.
