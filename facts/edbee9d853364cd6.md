## Statement

Use $v_1=(1,0)$, $v_2=(0,1)$, $v_3=(-1,-1)$, $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$, and
$$
A=\operatorname{conv}\{(-3/2,-101/100),\,(-3/2,-101/100-1/100000),\,(2,-9999/10000)\}.
$$
All three lines $v_i\cdot x=-1$ cut the interior of $A$. Their normalized section lengths $\mathcal H^1(A\cap\{v_i\cdot x=-1\})/\lVert v_i\rVert$ are below $1/1000$, and the corresponding normalized facet capacities of $T$ are $3$. Let $U_{(1)}\le U_{(2)}\le U_{(3)}$ be the sorted areas of $A\cap\{v_i\cdot x\ge-1\}$, put $F=U_{(1)}+U_{(2)}+U_{(3)}/4$ and $S=\sum_i\max_A v_i\cdot x$. Then $F<\min(|A|,|T|)$ but $S+(8/3)F<|A|+|T|$.

## Proof

Denote the displayed vertices by $P,Q,R$ in order and put $e=1/100000$, $\tau=1/10000$, $\eta_0=1/100$, $W=7/2$. The vertical side $PQ$ has length $e$, with horizontal altitude $W$, so $a=|A|=We/2=7/400000$. In each of the coordinates $x$, $y$, and $x+y$, vertex $R$ lies strictly above the respective threshold $-1,-1,1$, and $P,Q$ lie strictly below it. These strict inequalities make each cut proper.

In the $x$ cut the intersections on $RP,RQ$ lie at common edge fraction $6/7$ from $R$. Thus the section length is $(6/7)e$, and the fraction of area captured is $36/49$. In the other two cuts the value at $R$ exceeds the threshold by $\tau$. For $y$ the drops from $R$ to $P,Q$ are $s,s+e$ with $s=\eta_0+\tau$. For $x+y$ they are $s,s+e$ with $s=35101/10000$. The two edge fractions are therefore $\tau/s,\tau/(s+e)$. The horizontal distance between the two section endpoints is $W\tau e/[s(s+e)]$. On a horizontal line this equals the section length. On the diagonal line $x+y=1$ it equals the section length divided by $\sqrt2=\lVert v_3\rVert$. For the smaller $s=\eta_0+\tau>1/100$ the expression is less than $7/200000$, hence all three normalized lengths are below $1/1000$. The vertices of $T$ are $(1,1),(1,-2),(-2,1)$, so its area is $9/2$ and its facet lengths are $3\lVert v_i\rVert$.

The relative area of a triangle at $R$ formed by these edge fractions is their product, by the determinant formula. Hence the caps in the three normal directions have fractions
$$
\frac{U_x}{a}=\frac{36}{49},\qquad
\frac{U_y}{a}=\frac{\tau^2}{(\eta_0+\tau)(\eta_0+\tau+e)}<\frac1{10000},\qquad
\frac{U_3}{a}=1-\frac{\tau^2}{(35101/10000)(35101/10000+e)}.
$$
The last fraction is greater than $1-1/10000$, so $U_y<U_x<U_3$. In particular $F/a<1/10000+36/49+1/4<1$, and $a<|T|$. Finally the support sum is
$$
S=2-\frac{9999}{10000}+\frac{251001}{100000}=\frac{351011}{100000}.
$$
Using $F<a$, $S<3511/1000$, and $a<1/10000$ gives
$$
S+(8/3)F-a-9/2
<S+(5/3)a-9/2
<3511/1000+1/6000-9/2<0.
$$
