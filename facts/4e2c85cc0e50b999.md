## Statement

For every $d\ge1$, take $K=[-1,1]^d$, $C=[-2,2]\times K$ and anchor $(0,0)$. On either strict side, summing the pushforwards of unnormalized mixed volume under $(\pm k,x)\mapsto x/k$ gives $f=\mathbf1_K+2^d\mathbf1_{K/2}$. It has convex superlevels and fails every finite power-mean concavity inequality on its positive support, including the logarithmic case. The failure persists for every density representative.

## Proof

The two strict layers have distances one and two and both have fiber $K$. The change of variables $x=ka$ multiplies the layer density by $k^d$, giving the stated expression and its nested superlevels. At $(49/100,0,\ldots,0)$ and $(53/100,0,\ldots,0)$ the densities are $1+2^d$ and $1$, whereas the midpoint density is $1$. At weight one half every finite power mean of two positive unequal numbers is strictly larger than their minimum, including the geometric mean at power zero. These three values persist on sufficiently small neighborhoods of the two endpoints and of their midpoints. If a density is changed on a null set, choose endpoints from those neighborhoods outside the null set with midpoint also outside it. Such pairs have full product measure by Fubini and the invertible affine midpoint map in either endpoint. The same strict violation follows.
