## Statement

For $d\ge1$, let $U$ be uniform on $\Delta_d=\{u\in\mathbb R_+^{d+1}:\sum_j u_j=1\}$, and for $x\ge0$ put $F_x(t)=\Pr(x\cdot U\le t)$ and $\phi_d(x)=\inf_{t>0:F_x(t)>0} t/F_x(t)^{1/d}$. Assume $d\ge2$. Let $X$ be a local maximizer of $H(X)=\sum_i c_i\phi_d(X_i)$ over nonnegative column-stochastic $(d+1)\times(d+1)$ matrices, where $X_i$ is row $i$ and every $c_i>0$. Suppose the capacity vector $(\phi_d(X_i))_i$ differs from every standard unit vector and at least one row is strictly positive. Then all strictly positive rows have a unique proper minimizing threshold $t_i$, with $\min_j X_{ij}<t_i<\max_j X_{ij}$. Let $F_i=F_{X_i}(t_i)$ and let $b_i$ be the barycentric centroid of the actual section $\{u\in\Delta_d:X_i\cdot u=t_i\}$ under normalized coarea measure. There is a common vector $\lambda>0$ such that, for each strictly positive row $i$ and $S=\sum_j\lambda_j$,
$$
\lambda=c_i\nabla\phi_d(X_i)=c_i F_i^{-1/d}b_i,\qquad
c_i F_i^{-1/d}=S,\qquad b_i=\frac{\lambda}{S},
\qquad t_i=X_i\cdot b_i,\qquad c_i\phi_d(X_i)=St_i .
$$
The same equations hold between any pair of strictly positive rows with proper minimizing thresholds at a local maximum, by comparing those rows directly.

## Proof

Fact 10916437d7f6301d applies with $d\ge2$, positive objective weights, and exactly the nonnegative column-stochastic local constraints. The nonunit capacity assumption rules out a full minimizing cut at a strictly positive row. Fact d2ba4e3cbd6eb730 now gives uniqueness, local differentiability, and $\nabla\phi_d(X_i)=F_i^{-1/d}b_i$ for each such row. The section meets the relative interior of $\Delta_d$: there are interior points with affine heights strictly below and above any proper threshold, and their connecting segment crosses the threshold. Thus the section has a relative open patch of strictly positive barycentric coordinates, and each centroid coordinate is positive. Coarea uses the constant factor $1/\|\operatorname{proj}_{\{\sum_j u_j=0\}}X_i\|$ on this nonconstant section, so the normalized measure agrees with normalized section volume. It follows that $\sum_j(b_i)_j=1$ and $X_i\cdot b_i=t_i$.

Given strictly positive rows $i,k$ and column $j$, add a signed small amount to $X_{ij}$ and subtract it from $X_{kj}$. The line is feasible in both signs, and the derivative of the objective vanishes at zero. Therefore $c_i\partial_j\phi_d(X_i)=c_k\partial_j\phi_d(X_k)$. Take $\lambda$ to be any strictly positive row's weighted gradient. Summing its components yields $S=c_i F_i^{-1/d}$, and multiplication by the minimizing threshold gives $c_i\phi_d(X_i)=St_i$. If only the two rows being compared are assumed to have proper thresholds, the same differentiability and transfer argument applies to them.
