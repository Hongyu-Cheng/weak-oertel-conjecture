## Statement

Let $P=\operatorname{conv}(P)\cap\Lambda$ be nonempty and finite in an affine lattice $\Lambda$, and let $(K_z)_{z\in P}$ be positive compact convex $d$-fibers, $d\ge1$, satisfying $\sum_z\lambda_z K_z\subseteq K_p$ whenever $\sum_z\lambda_z z=p\in P$ for convex weights. Set $M=\sum_z|K_z|$. Let $G_1,\ldots,G_m$, $m\ge1$, be closed base halfspaces, each of the same mass $Q\in(0,M)$ for the weights $|K_z|$. There is a nonnegative finite convex base rate $h$ for which the inner parallel fibers $K_z(t)=K_z\ominus t h(z)B_d$ are positive, compatible and converge to $K_z$ for all small $t\ge0$. Writing $M(t)=\sum_z|K_z(t)|$ and $M_{G_i}(t)=\sum_{z\in P\cap G_i}|K_z(t)|$, their first losses satisfy
$$
M(t)=M-tm+o(t),\qquad M_{G_i}(t)=Q-tD_i+o(t),\qquad D_i\ge1.
$$
For $\beta=Q/M$, each ratio $M_{G_i}(t)/M(t)$ has right derivative $(\beta m-D_i)/M\le-(1-\beta m)/M$. In particular, if $\beta m<1$ all these ratios decrease strictly from $\beta$ for every sufficiently small $t>0$.

## Proof

Write $G_i=\{z:a_i(z)\ge c_i\}$ with $a_i$ affine. There is a sampled point in $G_i$ and one outside because $0<Q<M$ and every sampled weight is positive. Choose $0<\delta_i<\min_{z\in P\setminus G_i}(c_i-a_i(z))$. Then $h_i=(a_i-c_i+\delta_i)_+$ is convex, positive on $P\cap G_i$ (including its boundary) and zero on $P\setminus G_i$. Put $s_z=\mathcal H^{d-1}(\partial K_z)>0$, with counting measure for $d=1$, $c_i'=\sum_zs_zh_i(z)>0$, and $h=\sum_i h_i/c_i'$.

For every unit $u$, the constraints for $K_z(t)$ are $u\cdot X+t h(z)\le h_{K_z}(u)$. At any anchored relation, summing these constraints on selected $X_z\in K_z(t)$ and using convexity of $h$ and the old Minkowski inclusion gives the constraint at the new anchored convex combination. Thus the new fibers are compatible. An old inball of radius $r_z$ gives an inner homothet of factor $1-th(z)/r_z$ about its center, proving positivity and Hausdorff convergence for small $t$. The new canonical hull has these exact sampled fibers by compatibility and the same lattice sites by completeness.

For the volume derivative, take the continuous affine support bounds from 155691fc3768c06f, one indexed by each sampled base and touching there in every continuous direction. At $z$, the minimum of these bounds is $h_{K_z}(u)$ because the $z$-indexed bound touches. Applying 84cd8a85417b0d7a with the common field $V_i(u,z,X)=h(z)$ therefore gives exactly the displayed inner parallel family at the sampled bases and the derivative $|K_z(t)|=|K_z|-ts_zh(z)+o(t)$. All closed pure-base boundary fibers count in full. The total coefficient is $\sum_zs_zh(z)=m$. The contribution of $h_i/c_i'$ is supported on $G_i$ and gives exactly one there. The other terms are nonnegative, so $D_i=\sum_{z\in P\cap G_i}s_zh(z)\ge1$. Finite sums and the quotient expansion give the claimed derivative and simultaneous strict inequalities.
