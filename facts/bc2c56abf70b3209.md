## Statement

Let $d\ge1$ and let $K\subseteq\mathbb R^d$ be a full-dimensional compact convex body with volume centroid zero and volume $V>0$. Fix $0<r\le s$ and translations $c_-,c_+\in\mathbb R^d$. Suppose the only nonempty integer fibers of a convex body in $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^d$ are $c_-+rK$, $B$, and $c_++sK$ at $-1,0,1$, respectively. Set $m=(c_-+c_+)/2$ and $\rho=(r+s)/2$. The middle body $B$ is arbitrary subject to $m+\rho K\subseteq B$.

Write $\Gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$ and $c_d=(2\Gamma_d)^{1/d}$. For $0<r\le s$ put
$$
\Phi_d(r,s)=
\begin{cases}
r,&d=1,\\
\left[r c_d+\dfrac{s-r}{d-1}(1-c_d)\right]^d,&d\ge2,\ r\le s\le dr,\\
r^d,&d\ge2,\ s\ge dr,
\end{cases}
$$
and extend $\Phi_d$ symmetrically to $r>s$. The branches agree at $s=dr$.

Let $a=r^dV$, $b=s^dV$, $U=|B|$, and let $g_B$ be the ordinary volume centroid of $B$. If $S$ is the actual mixed-integer part of the body and $h_S$ is its raw depth, then
$$
h_S((0,m))\ge V[\Gamma_d\rho^d+\Phi_d(r,s)],
\qquad
h_S((0,g_B))\ge\Gamma_d U.
$$
In particular, under the scalar condition
$$
\rho^d+\frac{\Phi_d(r,s)}{\Gamma_d}\ge r^d+s^d,
$$
choosing $(0,m)$ for $U\le a+b$ and $(0,g_B)$ for $U\ge a+b$, with either choice at equality, gives raw depth at least $\Gamma_d(a+b+U)/2$ and normalized depth at least $\Gamma_d/2$. The same conclusion holds under the simpler sufficient condition $b/a\le (3+\sqrt5)/2$.

## Proof

Convexity gives $m+\rho K\subseteq B$ because $(rK+sK)/2=\rho K$. Conversely, the convex hull of the three displayed lifted fibers has exactly those integer fibers whenever this inclusion holds. Its base projection is $[-1,1]$, and at $-1$ or $1$ every generator in a convex combination has that same base. At zero the total weights of the two endpoints are equal, so the continuous coordinate is a convex combination of a point of $B$ and a point of $m+\rho K$ if both types occur, and otherwise belongs to one of those sets. The hull is compact, convex, and full-dimensional since the middle fiber is full-dimensional and an endpoint has nonzero base coordinate. For the stipulated census the total mixed mass is exactly $a+b+U$.

Apply 706ce1dc5b5b8d67 with bases $-1,0,1$, endpoint translations $c_-,c_+$ and scales $r,s$. The mark is $(0,m)$, the centroid of the retained middle copy $m+\rho K$. The fact pays $V[\Phi_d(r,s)+\Gamma_d\rho^d]$ on three distinct bases in every closed halfspace containing this mark. At the other mark $(0,g_B)$, a containing ambient halfspace restricts on its own base to a containing ordinary halfspace or to the whole continuous space. The full-volume case of 6769574fc1bd9f8d with $f=1$ on $B$ gives at least $\Gamma_d U$ there.

The first scalar condition gives $V[\Gamma_d\rho^d+\Phi_d(r,s)]\ge\Gamma_d(a+b)$. For $U\le a+b$ this is at least $\Gamma_d(a+b+U)/2$. For $U\ge a+b$ the second mark's budget is at least that target. Dividing by the actual total mass gives the normalized conclusion.

For the simpler condition, the larger endpoint contains $c_++rK$ because $0\in K$. The invertible shear $(z,x)\mapsto(z,x-m-z(c_+-c_-)/2)$ centers both retained endpoint copies and sends $(0,m)$ to $(0,0)$, preserving halfspaces and all fiber volumes. The equal-scale raw two-source bound of e561372a5640eef8 with scales $r,r$ pays $V\Phi_d(r,r)=2\Gamma_d a$ from those endpoints at this mark. The copy $m+\rho K$ on the middle base independently pays $\Gamma_d V\rho^d$ at the same mark by 6769574fc1bd9f8d. The two payments are on distinct bases. The arithmetic–geometric mean inequality gives $V\rho^d\ge\sqrt{ab}$. Setting $t=\sqrt{b/a}\ge1$, the stated ratio bound is equivalent to $t^2-t-1\le0$, so $2a+\sqrt{ab}\ge a+b$. Thus the same two-case choice proves the claim. Equivalently, this simpler condition implies the first scalar condition, since the formula for $\Phi_d$ gives $\Phi_d(r,s)\ge 2\Gamma_d r^d$ for all $s\ge r$. Both scalar conditions are sufficient tests for this certificate.
