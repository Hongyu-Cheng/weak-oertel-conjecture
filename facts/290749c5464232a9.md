## Statement

For $0<L\le3$ let $K_L=\operatorname{conv}\{(2,-1),(-1,(1+L)/2),(-1,(1-L)/2)\}$, $k=3L/2$, and $y=(x,-x/2)\in\operatorname{int}K_L$. Use the three normals $(1,0),(0,1),(-1,-1)$ and let $q_i$ be the areas of their through-$y$ upper caps. If every $q_i\le(128/279)k$ and $q_{\min}+q_{\rm mid}+q_{\max}/4\le k$, then $L>8/5$.

## Proof

Set $R=(2-x)/3\in(0,1)$. The first cap is homothetic to the triangle with scale $R$, so $q_1/k=R^2$. Since $128/279<(17/25)^2$, $R<17/25$. Reflection $(x_1,x_2)\mapsto(x_1,-x_1-x_2)$ preserves the triangle and source and interchanges caps two and three.

The horizontal cut is at $-1+3R/2$. If $R\le1-L/3$, the cut misses only a triangle at the bottom vertex $(2,-1)$, with relative area $9R^2/(9-L^2)$ from its two edge ratios. This case implies $L\le3(1-R)<3$. Its cap fraction is at least the fraction at $L=3(1-R)$, namely $2(1-R)/(2-R)>16/33>128/279$, a contradiction. Hence the cut lies strictly above the lower base vertex and below the upper one, and the upper tip formula gives
$$
z:=q_2/k=q_3/k=\frac{(L+3-3R)^2}{2L(L+3)}.
$$
Assume $L\le8/5$. If $z\ge R^2$, the profile divided by $k$ is $R^2+5z/4$. Minimizing this quadratic in $R$ over the whole line gives $5(L+3)^2/[8L(L+3)+45]=1+3L(2-L)/[8L(L+3)+45]>1$.

If $z\le R^2$, the normalized profile is $2z+R^2/4$. Its derivative in $R$ is $-6(L+3-3R)/[L(L+3)]+R/2<0$ throughout $0<R\le17/25$, $0<L\le8/5$: use $L+3-3R\ge24/25$, $L(L+3)\le184/25$, and $R/2\le17/50$. The derivative of $z$ in $L$ has the sign of $R(2L+3)-(L+3)$, which is at most $(9/25)L-24/25<0$ on the same rectangle. The profile is therefore bounded below by its value at $(L,R)=(8/5,17/25)$, namely $2(256/575)+289/2500=57847/57500>1$. Both orders contradict the stated profile bound.
