## Statement

Let $d\ge1$, $T=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:x_i\ge0,\ \sum_i x_i\le1\}$, $c=|T|$, $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$, and $s>0$. If $D_{sT}(p)$ denotes the unnormalized closed halfspace depth of $p\in sT$ for ordinary $d$-volume, then
$$
\gamma_d s^d c-D_{sT}(p)\ \ge\
\frac{c\gamma_d(d+1)}{d^2}s^{d-1}
\left|\sum_i p_i-\frac{ds}{d+1}\right|.
$$

## Proof

Let $m$ be the largest barycentric coordinate of $p$ in $sT$. It is at least $1/(d+1)$. The cap in which a coordinate attaining $m$ is at least $m$ is a copy of $sT$ contracted about its corresponding vertex by $1-m$, and contains $p$. Its volume is $s^dc(1-m)^d$, also when $m=1$. The concave function $m\mapsto\gamma_d-(1-m)^d$ on $[1/(d+1),1]$ lies above its endpoint chord, so it is at least $\gamma_d(d+1)(m-1/(d+1))/d$.

Write $E=\sum_i p_i-ds/(d+1)$. If $E\ge0$, the mean of the $d$ barycentric coordinates $p_i/s$ is $1/(d+1)+E/(ds)$, hence one is at least this large. If $E<0$, the remaining coordinate $1-\sum_i p_i/s$ is $1/(d+1)+|E|/s$. Thus $m-1/(d+1)\ge |E|/(ds)$ in both cases. Multiplication by $s^dc$ gives the claimed bound.
