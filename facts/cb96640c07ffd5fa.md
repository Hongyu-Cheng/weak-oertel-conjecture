## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and write
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
\]
\[
\mu_C(H):=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\operatorname{vol}_d\{x\in C_z:(z,x)\in H\},
\qquad
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\]
For a positive-volume convex body \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) and \(0<s\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(K)\), use
\[
\mathsf M_s(K):=
\left\{
\frac1s\int_Kx\rho(x)\,dx:
0\leq\rho\leq1\ \text{a.e.},\ \int_K\rho=s
\right\}.
\]

Retain one common instantiation of the canonical zero-fresh system of accepted fact `6bfe50fed9a5540b`. Its finite reservoir edge set consists of unordered off-diagonal same-parity pairs \(e=\{u_e,v_e\}\) of positive-volume fibers. It has reservoir weights \(r_e\), total reservoir mass and endpoint degrees
\[
Q:=\sum_er_e>0,
\qquad
d_z(r):=\sum_{e\ni z}r_e\leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{1}
\]
Writing \(c_z\) for the centroid of \(C_z\), put
\[
\mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
\qquad
D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z,
\]
and, for every reservoir edge,
\[
m_e:=\frac{u_e+v_e}{2}\in\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad
D_e:=\frac12(D_{u_e}+D_{v_e}),
\]
\[
L_e:=\frac12(\mathcal K_{u_e}+\mathcal K_{v_e}),
\qquad
\vartheta_e:=2^{-(d+1)}
\left(a_{u_e}^{1/d}+a_{v_e}^{1/d}\right)^d.
\tag{2}
\]
Each \(D_e\) is an origin-symmetric convex body with \(0\) in its interior, so \(h_{D_e}\) is a norm.
In particular,
\[
L_e\subseteq\mathsf M_{\vartheta_e}(C_{m_e}).
\tag{3}
\]

The same accepted fact constructs a nonzero matching capacity \(b=(b_e)\), with \(0\leq b_e\leq r_e\), and chooses a gauge-load-minimal optimizer \(v^b=(v_e^b)\) for its affine-defect dual. On \(E_b:=\{e:b_e>0\}\), define for every subcapacity \(c=(c_e)\) with \(0\leq c_e\leq b_e\)
\[
\mathcal F(c):=
\left\{
v:\ \sum_ev_e=0,\quad
\sum_ev_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,\quad
h_{D_e}(v_e)\leq c_e\ \text{for all }e
\right\},
\]
\[
\Delta_C(c):=\max_{v\in\mathcal F(c)}
\left(-\sum_eh_{L_e}(v_e)\right),
\qquad
\operatorname{Opt}(c):=
\left\{v\in\mathcal F(c):-\sum_eh_{L_e}(v_e)=\Delta_C(c)\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Here and below sums in (4) are over \(E_b\), with zero-capacity coordinates retained as zero if convenient. Define the self-saturated capacity by the exact alias
\[
\boxed{q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}:=\omega_e^b:=h_{D_e}(v_e^b)}
\qquad(e\in E_b),
\tag{5}
\]
and retain the predecessor notation
\[
\boxed{\Omega_b:=\sum_eq_e^{\mathrm{sat}}.}
\tag{6}
\]
Then
\[
0\leq q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}\leq b_e\leq r_e,
\qquad
\boxed{\Omega_b>\frac{97}{100}Q},
\tag{7}
\]
\[
d_z(q^{\mathrm{sat}}):=\sum_{e\ni z}q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}
\leq d_z(r)\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
\tag{8}
\]
and, for every physical target \(m\),
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\vartheta_e}\leq1.}
\tag{9}
\]
With
\[
\delta_*:=\frac{3\log(4/3)}{\log2}-1>0,
\]
one has
\[
\boxed{
\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}
:=\Delta_C(q^{\mathrm{sat}})
=\Delta_C(b)
>\frac{97}{100}\delta_*Q.}
\tag{10}
\]
Every \(\widetilde v\in\operatorname{Opt}(q^{\mathrm{sat}})\) saturates every coordinate:
\[
h_{D_e}(\widetilde v_e)=q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}
\qquad(e\in E_b).
\tag{11}
\]

Let
\[
I:=\{e\in E_b:q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}>0\}.
\]
Then \(I\neq\varnothing\), \(v_e^b=0\) off \(I\), and the normalization
\[
\boxed{v_i^b=q_i^{\mathrm{sat}}u_i}
\qquad(i\in I)
\tag{12}
\]
is well-defined and satisfies
\[
h_{D_i}(u_i)=1,
\qquad
\sum_{i\in I}q_i^{\mathrm{sat}}u_i=0,
\qquad
\sum_{i\in I}q_i^{\mathrm{sat}}u_im_i^{\mathsf T}=0,
\tag{13}
\]
\[
\boxed{
-\sum_{i\in I}q_i^{\mathrm{sat}}h_{L_i}(u_i)
=\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}.}
\tag{14}
\]

Choose \(\ell_i\in F_{L_i}(u_i)\), where
\[
F_K(u):=\{x\in K:\langle u,x\rangle=h_K(u)\}.
\]
Put
\[
\pi_i:=\frac{q_i^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\Omega_b},
\qquad
\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}
:=\sum_{j\in I}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}
\sum_{i\in I}\pi_i h_{L_j}(u_i),
\]
\[
t:=\frac{\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}}
{\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}+\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}},
\qquad
a_{ij}:=(1-t)q_i^{\mathrm{sat}}\mathbf1_{\{i=j\}}
+tq_i^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_j.
\tag{15}
\]
Then \(\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}\geq0\), \(0<t\leq1\), and
\[
\sum_ja_{ij}=q_i^{\mathrm{sat}},
\qquad
\sum_ia_{ij}=q_j^{\mathrm{sat}},
\tag{16}
\]
\[
\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}u_i=0,
\qquad
\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}u_im_j^{\mathsf T}=0,
\qquad
\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}h_{L_j}(u_i)=0.
\tag{17}
\]
Define the stationary and transport terms
\[
\mathcal O:=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}
\left(h_{L_j}(u_i)-\langle u_i,\ell_j\rangle\right),
\]
\[
\mathcal T:=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}
\langle u_i,\ell_j-\ell_i\rangle.
\tag{18}
\]
They obey the exact split
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal O=(1-t)\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}
=t\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}\geq0,
\qquad
\mathcal T=t\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}>0,
\qquad
\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}=\mathcal O+\mathcal T.}
\tag{19}
\]
Consequently
\[
\boxed{
\max\{\mathcal O,\mathcal T\}
\geq\frac12\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}
>\frac{97}{200}\delta_*Q.}
\tag{20}
\]
The quantity called \(\Omega\) in accepted fact `487ffbb5b46832e9` is, under this application, \(\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}\) in (10) and (14). It is not the mass \(\Omega_b\) in (6); the latter is the quantity called \(\Sigma\) in that fact.

For every ordered pair \((i,j)\in I^2\), choose an arbitrary contact
\[
p_{ij}\in F_{L_j}(u_i).
\tag{21}
\]
Then all \((m_j,p_{ij})\) belong to
\(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), and the single labelled contact measure
\[
\nu_{\mathrm{tr}}:=\sum_{i,j\in I}a_{ij}\delta_{(m_j,p_{ij})}
\tag{22}
\]
satisfies the joint halfspace domination
\[
\boxed{2\gamma_d\nu_{\mathrm{tr}}\preceq_{\mathrm{hs}}\mu_C.}
\tag{23}
\]
Its total atomic mass and the total mass of the dominated measure are
\[
|\nu_{\mathrm{tr}}|=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}=\Omega_b>\frac{97}{100}Q,
\qquad
2\gamma_d|\nu_{\mathrm{tr}}|>\frac{97}{50}\gamma_dQ.
\tag{24}
\]
This is one domination statement for all labels, including labels with the same physical target or the same contact point.

For each target label \(j\in I\), put
\[
b_{ik}^{(j)}:=\frac{tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{2}\pi_i\pi_k.
\tag{25}
\]
If \(\mathcal O_j\) is the contribution of target label \(j\) to \(\mathcal O\), then
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal O_j
=\sum_{i,k\in I}b_{ik}^{(j)}
\langle u_i-u_k,p_{ij}-p_{kj}\rangle,}
\tag{26}
\]
every summand in (26) is nonnegative, and
\[
\sum_kb_{ik}^{(j)}+\sum_kb_{ki}^{(j)}
=tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_i,
\qquad
\sum_i\left(\sum_kb_{ik}^{(j)}+\sum_kb_{ki}^{(j)}\right)
=tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}.
\tag{27}
\]

For every physical target \(m\) used by the self-saturated support, define
\[
I_m:=\{j\in I:m_j=m\},
\qquad
\eta_m:=\sum_{j\in I_m}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}>0,
\tag{28}
\]
\[
c_j^{\mathrm{sw}}:=\sum_{i\in I}\pi_ip_{ij},
\qquad
c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
:=\frac1{\eta_m}\sum_{j\in I_m}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}c_j^{\mathrm{sw}},
\tag{29}
\]
and the normalized switch generators
\[
g_{ik}^{(j,m)}
:=\frac{b_{ik}^{(j)}}{\eta_m}(p_{ij}-p_{kj})
=\frac{tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{2\eta_m}\pi_i\pi_k
(p_{ij}-p_{kj}).
\tag{30}
\]
Then the exact same-physical-target switch zonotope is genuine:
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Z_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
:=c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
+\sum_{j\in I_m}\sum_{i,k\in I}
[-g_{ik}^{(j,m)},g_{ik}^{(j,m)}]
\subseteq\mathsf M_{\eta_m/2}(C_m).}
\tag{31}
\]
All blocks with target \(m\) in (31) are realized by one pointwise density budget in \(C_m\), not by separately incompatible metronoid choices. The mass-weighted generator-normal energy is exactly the stationary term:
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{sw}}
:=\sum_{m:\eta_m>0}\eta_m
\sum_{j\in I_m}\sum_{i,k\in I}
\langle u_i-u_k,g_{ik}^{(j,m)}\rangle
=\mathcal O.}
\tag{32}
\]
Every summand in (32) is nonnegative. Equations (31)-(32) assert a physical switch atlas and its exact energy only. They imply no energy absorption, no residual-rank reduction, and no closure of the zero-fresh branch.

## Proof

Apply accepted fact `6bfe50fed9a5540b` to the retained zero-fresh system. It gives \(b\leq r\), the chosen gauge-load-minimal optimizer \(v^b\), the loads \(\omega_e^b=h_{D_e}(v_e^b)\), and all of the estimates in (7)-(10) except initially the equality \(\Delta_C(q^{\mathrm{sat}})=\Delta_C(b)\) and the full optimal-face saturation (11). In particular, its exact conclusions give
\[
\sum_e\omega_e^b=\Omega_b>\frac{97}{100}Q,
\qquad
\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{\omega_e^b}{\vartheta_e}\leq1,
\]
\[
\Delta_C(\omega^b)>\frac{97}{100}\delta_*Q.
\tag{33}
\]
The inequalities \(q^{\mathrm{sat}}\leq b\leq r\) also give (8).

Apply accepted self-saturation fact `d2eaf07a0b7e82f2` with capacity \(c=b\) and with exactly the selected gauge-load-minimal optimizer \(v^b\). Its reduced capacity \(w\) has coordinates
\[
w_e=h_{D_e}(v_e^b)=\omega_e^b=q_e^{\mathrm{sat}}.
\]
That fact gives
\[
\Delta_C(q^{\mathrm{sat}})=\Delta_C(b)
\]
and says that every \(q^{\mathrm{sat}}\)-optimizer saturates every coordinate, including a zero coordinate as \(v_e=0\). It also leaves \(v^b\) optimal for the reduced problem. Together with (33), this proves (10)-(11). Since \(\Omega_b>0\), the positive support \(I\) is nonempty. On \(I\), define \(u_i=v_i^b/q_i^{\mathrm{sat}}\); off \(I\), saturation gives \(v_e^b=0\). Positive homogeneity of \(h_{D_i}\) and \(h_{L_i}\), and the defining constraints and objective in (4), now give (12)-(14).

We next apply accepted synchronous-transport fact `487ffbb5b46832e9`. Use its data map
\[
\sigma_i=q_i^{\mathrm{sat}},
\qquad
\mathbf m_i=m_i,
\qquad
K_i=L_i,
\qquad
\mathbf v_i=u_i,
\qquad
\mathbf q_i=\ell_i.
\tag{34}
\]
Every \(L_i\) is a nonempty compact convex set, and its exposed face in direction \(u_i\) is nonempty. Equations (13)-(14) verify the two balance hypotheses and the strict objective hypothesis. Under (34), the synchronous fact's total mass \(\Sigma\) is \(\Omega_b\), while its objective \(\Omega\) is
\[
-\sum_iq_i^{\mathrm{sat}}h_{L_i}(u_i)
=\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}>0.
\]
Its definitions of \(\Psi,t,a_{ij}\) are exactly (15). Therefore it proves \(\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}\geq0\), (16)-(19), including the zero transported support score in (17). Equation (20) follows from (10) and the elementary bound
\(\max\{\mathcal O,\mathcal T\}\geq(\mathcal O+\mathcal T)/2\).

For the contact atlas, give the label \((i,j)\) weight \(a_{ij}\), give it the two endpoint occurrences \(u_j,v_j\) of edge \(j\), and give it the selected point \(p_{ij}\in L_j\). At an original endpoint fiber \(z\), the total labelled occurrence load is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}
\left(\mathbf1_{\{u_j=z\}}+\mathbf1_{\{v_j=z\}}\right)
&=\sum_{j\ni z}\sum_i a_{ij}\\
&=\sum_{j\ni z}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}
=d_z(q^{\mathrm{sat}})
\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
\end{aligned}
\tag{35}
\]
where the column marginal in (16) is essential. The label body in accepted whole-label domination fact `ff5bc0ba02cc86cd` is
\[
\frac12(\mathcal K_{u_j}+\mathcal K_{v_j})=L_j,
\]
and the label midpoint is \(m_j\). Thus all hypotheses of that fact hold simultaneously for the entire labelled family. It gives support in \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\) and (23), including collisions of target labels or atoms. Summing either marginal in (16) gives (24).

For each fixed target label \(j\), apply the exact pairization part of accepted fact `487ffbb5b46832e9` to the arbitrary contacts in (21). It gives (25)-(27) and the nonnegativity of every integrand in (26). Summing (26) over \(j\) gives \(\mathcal O=\sum_j\mathcal O_j\).

It remains to prove the common-density assertion (31), especially when several labels \(j\) have the same physical target. Fix such a physical target \(m\). By (3) and (21), for every \(j\in I_m\) and \(i\in I\) choose a representing metronoid density
\[
\rho_{ij}:C_m\longrightarrow[0,1]
\]
such that
\[
\int_{C_m}\rho_{ij}(x)\,dx=\vartheta_j,
\qquad
\frac1{\vartheta_j}\int_{C_m}x\rho_{ij}(x)\,dx=p_{ij}.
\tag{36}
\]
Scale it by both the self-saturated target capacity and the source probability:
\[
f_{ij}(x):=\frac{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\vartheta_j}\pi_i\rho_{ij}(x).
\tag{37}
\]
For a fixed \(j\), put \(F_j:=\sum_if_{ij}\). Then
\[
\int_{C_m}f_{ij}=q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_i,
\qquad
\frac1{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_i}
\int_{C_m}xf_{ij}(x)\,dx=p_{ij},
\tag{38}
\]
and, pointwise almost everywhere,
\[
F_j(x)
=\frac{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\vartheta_j}
\sum_i\pi_i\rho_{ij}(x)
\leq\frac{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\vartheta_j}.
\tag{39}
\]
The capacity (9) now supplies the one shared physical-target budget
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j\in I_m}F_j(x)
\leq\sum_{j\in I_m}
\frac{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\vartheta_j}
\leq1
\quad\text{for almost every }x\in C_m.}
\tag{40}
\]
In particular, the density on the left has mass \(\eta_m\), so \(0<\eta_m\leq a_m\), and the metronoid parameter \(\eta_m/2\) in (31) is admissible.

For completeness, we instantiate the density formula from the product-box part of accepted fact `c0f08e8df1893e54`, rather than using its set inclusion separately for each colliding label. Use the measure \(\mathcal H_d|_{C_m}\). For a fixed \(j\), use two identical families \((f_{ij})_{i\in I}\) and \((f_{kj})_{k\in I}\). Their individual masses are
\[
a_i=q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_i,
\qquad
b_k=q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_k,
\]
their total masses are both \(q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\), and take the product-box parameter \(\beta=q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\). For independent numbers
\[
\theta_{ik}^{(j)}\in[-t/2,t/2]\subseteq[-1/2,1/2],
\qquad
\lambda_{ik}^{(j)}:=\frac12+\theta_{ik}^{(j)},
\]
the product-box construction uses the explicit density
\[
h_j^{\boldsymbol\theta}
:=\sum_{i,k}
\left[
(1-\lambda_{ik}^{(j)})\frac{\pi_k}{2}f_{ij}
+\lambda_{ik}^{(j)}\frac{\pi_i}{2}f_{kj}
\right].
\tag{41}
\]
It is nonnegative, and since \(0\leq\lambda_{ik}^{(j)}\leq1\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
h_j^{\boldsymbol\theta}
&\leq\sum_{i,k}
\left(\frac{\pi_k}{2}f_{ij}
+\frac{\pi_i}{2}f_{kj}\right)\\
&=\frac12F_j+\frac12F_j=F_j.
\end{aligned}
\tag{42}
\]
Direct integration of (41), equivalently the exact product-box calculation, gives
\[
\int_{C_m}h_j^{\boldsymbol\theta}=\frac{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{2},
\tag{43}
\]
\[
\frac{2}{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}
\int_{C_m}x h_j^{\boldsymbol\theta}(x)\,dx
=c_j^{\mathrm{sw}}
+\sum_{i,k}\pi_i\pi_k\theta_{ik}^{(j)}(p_{kj}-p_{ij}).
\tag{44}
\]

Now sum (41) over every target label with the same physical target:
\[
h_m^{\boldsymbol\theta}:=
\sum_{j\in I_m}h_j^{\boldsymbol\theta}.
\]
Equations (40) and (42) give \(0\leq h_m^{\boldsymbol\theta}\leq1\) pointwise almost everywhere. Equation (43) gives total mass \(\eta_m/2\), and (44) gives normalized first moment
\[
c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
+\frac1{\eta_m}\sum_{j\in I_m}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}
\sum_{i,k}\pi_i\pi_k\theta_{ik}^{(j)}(p_{kj}-p_{ij}).
\tag{45}
\]
All parameters \(\theta_{ik}^{(j)}\) are independent. As each ranges over \([-t/2,t/2]\), the set of points in (45) is exactly the zonotope in (31), because changing the sign of a symmetric segment changes \(p_{kj}-p_{ij}\) to \(p_{ij}-p_{kj}\), and (25) gives
\[
\frac{tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{2\eta_m}\pi_i\pi_k
(p_{ij}-p_{kj})=g_{ik}^{(j,m)}.
\]
Thus one admissible aggregate density realizes every point of \(\mathcal Z_m^{\mathrm{sw}}\), proving (31) without pooling incompatible witnesses.

Finally, (30) gives, term by term,
\[
\eta_m\langle u_i-u_k,g_{ik}^{(j,m)}\rangle
=b_{ik}^{(j)}
\langle u_i-u_k,p_{ij}-p_{kj}\rangle.
\tag{46}
\]
The right side is nonnegative by (26). Sum (46) first over \(i,k\), then over \(j\in I_m\), and finally over all physical targets. Equation (26) yields
\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{sw}}
=\sum_j\mathcal O_j=\mathcal O,
\]
which proves (32). No step converts \(\mathcal O\) or \(\mathcal T\) into an accepted terminal capacity, controls an affine residual rank, or produces another branch-closing state.
