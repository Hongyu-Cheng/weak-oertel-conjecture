## Statement

Use the actual three-fiber body with regular $4$-simplex $T$ at base zero and $D_\delta=T\cap\lbrace \lambda_2+\lambda_3+\lambda_4\le\delta\rbrace $ at both bases $\pm1$, in the buffer-free covariance-scaled uniform outward model. There is $\delta_0>0$ such that for each $0<\delta<\delta_0$ and every sufficiently small $h>0$, at its unique deepest mark, every balanced actual active menu has boundary ramp profile $W_h$ satisfying
$$
N_\Phi:=\sum_p\int_{\partial K_p} W_h\Phi\,dS
\le(\gamma-\zeta_\delta)D_\Phi,\qquad D_\Phi:=\sum_p\int_{\partial K_p}\Phi\,dS,\qquad
\zeta_\delta=\min\lbrace (14/3125)2^{-25}\delta^4,3/1250\rbrace >0,
$$
where $\gamma=(4/5)^4$ and $\Phi(n,p,X)$ is any nonnegative finite continuous field convex in $(p,X)$ for each unit normal $n$, evaluated at the actual outward fiber normal. The ordinary mixed depth at the middle centroid is at least $\gamma/(1+2\alpha)>\gamma/2$ after normalization, where $\alpha=4\delta^3-3\delta^4$.

## Proof

Use the active cap and limiting facet moment theorems. Each endpoint boundary has $W_h\le\gamma-g_0$, $g_0=3/625$. On a middle facet $F_i$ use normalized area and the canonical profile $W_0=\tfrac15\sum_{j\ne i}\mathbf1_{\lbrace \lambda_j\ge1/5\rbrace }$. At facet dimension three, f566248002918e0f with $d=4$ gives $\int W_0 f\le(\gamma-28/3125)\int f$ for every continuous nonnegative convex $f$ vanishing at the facet centroid. For any of the four nonconstant barycentric coordinates on the facet, 275b3b7cccde92d4 with $k=3,b=1/5,r=1/20$ gives
$$
\int_{\lbrace |\lambda_j-1/5|\le\eta\rbrace } f\le40\eta\int f,\qquad 0\le\eta\le1/20.
$$
The limiting cap margins and weights differ from the canonical ones by $O(\alpha)$, by the coordinate, intercept and weight formulas and their smooth dependence near the uniform mark. The active cap theorem and the limiting menu theorem give uniform cone-wise convergence of all actual margins and weights to their limits at fixed $\delta$; the ramp transition also has width tending to zero. After positive normalization of each margin near $\lambda_j-1/5$, its ramp on the facet equals the canonical indicator outside a strip $|\lambda_j-1/5|\le O(\alpha)+o_\delta(1)$; for $j=i$ both vanish on the facet for small parameters. The two inequalities and the weight bound imply, for all menus and such $f$,
$$
\int W_h f\le(\gamma-\kappa)\int f,\qquad \kappa=14/3125,
$$
by first choosing $\delta$ small and then $h$ small.

For an arbitrary continuous convex $\phi\ge0$ on a facet, let $\ell$ support it at the facet centroid, put $f=\phi-\ell$, $I=\int\phi$ and $\varepsilon=\int f$. Its affine mean is $\phi(g_i)\ge0$. If its largest vertex value is $L>0$, the others are at least $-3L$. On the corner where the corresponding coordinate is at least $7/8$, of area fraction $1/512$, one has $\ell\ge L/2$. Since $\phi\ge\ell_+$, it follows that $\lVert \ell\rVert _\infty\le3L\le3072I$. If $L=0$ all vertex values are zero. The uniform $L^1$ convergence of $W_h$ and the limiting affine identity then give
$$
\int(W_h-\gamma)\phi\le [C_1\alpha^2+e_\delta(h)]I-\kappa\varepsilon,
$$
where $e_\delta(h)\to0$ uniformly over all menus and fields: the affine contribution is bounded by $C_1\alpha^2\int\ell+3072I\lVert W_h-W_\delta\rVert _1$, and $\int\ell$ is between zero and $I$.

Let $\phi_i(X)=\Phi(n_i,0,X)$, $n_i$ the outward normal of $F_i$, and use $I_i,\varepsilon_i$ from this estimate. The shared patch $A_i=D_\delta\cap F_i$ has normal $n_i$ almost everywhere on each of the two endpoint bodies: on its relative interior away from the truncation plane it is exactly part of the same supporting facet. The truncation-plane intersection and facet edges have zero facet area. At each shared point the convexity in $(p,X)$ gives $2\phi_i(X)\le\Phi(n_i,-1,X)+\Phi(n_i,1,X)$. The full endpoint boundary cost $E$ therefore obeys $E\ge2\sum_i |F_i|\int_{A_i}\phi_i$ with normalized patch integrals. Summing the facet inequalities and the endpoint ceiling, and splitting the endpoint loss in half, gives
$$
N_\Phi-\gamma D_\Phi\le
[C_1\alpha^2+e_\delta(h)-\kappa\,2^{-24}\delta^4]\sum_i |F_i|I_i-(g_0/2)E.
$$
Here $\kappa<g_0$ and the patch lemma gives $\varepsilon_i+\int_{A_i}\phi_i\ge2^{-24}\delta^4 I_i$. Since $\alpha\le4\delta^3$, take $\delta$ small enough that $C_1\alpha^2\le(\kappa2^{-24}\delta^4)/4$, then $h$ so small that $e_\delta(h)\le(\kappa2^{-24}\delta^4)/4$. Because $D_\Phi=\sum_i |F_i|I_i+E$, the desired constant follows. At the middle centroid every ordinary oblique cap pays at least $\gamma V$ from $T$ and the pure-base caps pay at least $V$. Divide by $V(1+2\alpha)$ and use $\alpha<1/2$.
