## Statement

Use the full-parity witness orbits with $A=\pi(C)$ for compact convex $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge1$. Let $\mu$ be the sum of original $d$-volume on its integer fibers. For a witness $p_s$ let $H_{sj}=\{(z,x):\ell_{sj}(z,x)>0\}$ be open ambient caps, with $1\le j\le k_s\le d+1$, $\ell_{sj}$ affine, and $\lambda_{sj}>0$ satisfying
$$
\sum_j\lambda_{sj}\ell_{sj}(z,x)=a_s\cdot z-h_s,\qquad h_s<\beta_s.
$$
If all $\mu(H_{sj})<T$ for $T>0$, then $\bigcup_jH_{sj}$ contains the whole continuous plane over every base in the basin of $p_s$, including the root. If $B_s$ is the set of basin bases in $P$, then
$$
\mu(B_s\times\mathbb R^d)\le\sum_j\mu(H_{sj})<k_s T\le(d+1)T.
$$

## Proof

For each basin base, the full-parity orbit lemma gives $a_s\cdot z\ge\beta_s$. Thus the weighted sum of the constituent margins is strictly positive for every $x$, so at least one margin is strictly positive. The entire plane belongs to the union of the open caps, and subadditivity of $\mu$ gives the stated strict mass bound.
