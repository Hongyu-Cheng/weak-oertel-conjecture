## Statement

Let $d\ge1$ and let $A,B$ be $d$-dimensional convex bodies of the same volume $a>0$. Let $M=(A+B)/2$ and let $g_M$ be its ordinary centroid. For $u\ne0$, put $w_D=h_D(u)+h_D(-u)$ for each body $D$, with $h_D$ its support function. For $t\in\mathbb R$ let $P$ denote the sum of the ordinary volumes captured on $A,B$ by $u\cdot x\ge u\cdot g_M+t$ and $u\cdot x\ge u\cdot g_M-t$, respectively. Then
$$
 h_M(u)-u\cdot g_M\ge\frac{w_A+w_B}{2(d+1)},
 \qquad P/a\ge\frac1{(d+1)^d}.
$$
If $d\ge2$, then for $R=w_A/w_B$,
$$
 P/a\ge W_d(R):=\frac{(R+1)^d}{(d+1)^d(R^{d/(d-1)}+1)^{d-1}} .
$$
The bounds hold for every such $u,t$, including supporting, full and empty endpoint traces.

## Proof

Normalize the $u$-projection of $M$ to $[0,1]$. For $s\in[0,1]$, the homothet of $M$ of ratio $s$ about a point at its minimum support is contained in the lower projection region with coordinate at most $s$. The probability of that region under uniform volume is therefore at least $s^d$. Integrating the complementary probability gives normalized centroid coordinate at most $\int_0^1(1-s^d)\,ds=d/(d+1)$. Thus the upper support distance is at least $w_M/(d+1)$, and the Minkowski support identity gives $w_M=(w_A+w_B)/2$.

Let $\Delta_A=h_A(u)-u\cdot g_M-t$ and $\Delta_B=h_B(u)-u\cdot g_M+t$ be the two cap heights. Their sum is at least $(w_A+w_B)/(d+1)$, so at least one height-to-width ratio is at least $1/(d+1)$. A homothet of that body about a point at its upper support, with linear ratio $1/(d+1)$, lies in the captured cap. Its volume is $a/(d+1)^d$. This proves the direction-free bound for every $d$.

For the sharper bound take $d\ge2$ and $p=d/(d-1)$. Hölder's inequality gives $(w_A+w_B)^d/(w_A^p+w_B^p)^{d-1}\le2$, so a full endpoint already supplies at least the desired bound. If neither height-to-width ratio is at least one, put $x_A=(\Delta_A/w_A)_+$ and $x_B=(\Delta_B/w_B)_+$. The respective upper-support homothets, or the trivial bound zero when a height is negative, give $P/a\ge x_A^d+x_B^d$. Their weighted sum satisfies $w_Ax_A+w_Bx_B\ge(w_A+w_B)/(d+1)$. Hölder gives
$$
 (w_Ax_A+w_Bx_B)^d\le (w_A^p+w_B^p)^{d-1}(x_A^d+x_B^d).
$$
Combining and dividing by $w_B^d$ in the width expression proves the stated result. The homothets and full-cap comparison include the threshold equality cases.
