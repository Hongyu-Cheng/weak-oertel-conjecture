## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and use the
exact canonical objects and normalizations of accepted facts
6b09aae189415c70, 7ec63df101c30343, and c57ba4b9d987a404. In particular,
put
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D,
\]
where \(Q>0\) is the half-strength canonical product-reservoir mass,
\(R\) is its realizable-submatching capacity, and
\(G=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)\). Assume the canonical hard
\(I\)-dominant inequalities
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad I\ge O.
\tag{1}
\]

Let \(s=(s_e)\) be the retained product matching. For a retained parity
class \(\xi\), write
\[
 L_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\beta_z,
 \qquad
 s_{\{u,v\}}=\frac{\beta_u\beta_v}{L_\xi}
 \quad(u<v,\ u,v\equiv\xi).
\tag{2}
\]
Let \(\mathcal E_I\) be the canonical family of \(I\)-bad opposite edges,
let \(t_e\) be the actual opposite-edge triangle mass, and let
\(\mathcal B\) be the canonical exceptional family, so
\[
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal B}s_e\le G,
 \qquad
 t_e\ge\frac18s_e\quad(e\notin\mathcal B),
 \qquad
 0\le t_e\le\frac12s_e.
\tag{3}
\]
Put
\[
 J:=\{e\in\mathcal E_I\setminus\mathcal B:s_e>0\},
 \qquad
 S:=\sum_{e\in J}s_e.
\tag{4}
\]

For every physical target \(c\in\mathbb Z^n\), define the complete actual
\(I\)-triangle target masses
\[
 \Theta_{\xi,c}:=
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi,\ m_e=c\\e\in\mathcal E_I}}t_e,
 \qquad
 T_c:=\sum_\xi\Theta_{\xi,c},
\]
\[
 E:=\{c\in\mathbb Z^n:T_c>0\}.
\tag{5}
\]
At each \(c\in E\), choose one retained parity label \(\xi(c)\) maximizing
\(\Theta_{\xi,c}\), and put
\[
 q_c:=q_{\xi(c),c}^{\circ},
 \qquad
 U_c:=S_{\xi(c),c}
 =\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi(c)\\m_e=c}}s_e.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus \(q_c\) is the canonical \(s\)-weighted output point for that one
label:
\[
 q_c=\frac1{U_c}
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi(c)\\m_e=c}}s_eF_e^\flat.
\tag{7}
\]
Define
\[
 \rho_E:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E),
\]
\[
 r_E:=
 \min_{a:\operatorname{aff}(E)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{q_c-a(c):c\in E\},
\tag{8}
\]
and put
\[
 k:=2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1).
\tag{9}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{k\ge56}.
\tag{10}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \rho_E=3\Longrightarrow r_E\ge6,
 \qquad
 \rho_E=4\Longrightarrow r_E\ge3,
 \qquad
 \rho_E=5\Longrightarrow r_E\ge1.
\tag{11}
\]
No assertion excluding any selected complexity \(k\ge56\) is made.

## Proof

The hard alternative in accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70, together with
\(I\ge O\), gives
\[
 I>\frac Q4.
\tag{12}
\]
Its exact defect inequality gives
\[
 I\ge\frac{I+O}{2}
 \ge\frac{11}{32}Q-\frac{67}{8}R-\frac{51}{256}G.
\tag{13}
\]
Since \(I=\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_I}s_e\), equations (3)-(4) imply
\[
 S\ge I-G
\tag{14}
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{
 S+\frac{67}{8}R+\frac{307}{256}G
 \ge\frac{11}{32}Q.}
\tag{15}
\]
All summands of \(\mathcal A(C)\) are nonnegative, so \(G<Q/100\).
Equations (12) and (14) therefore give
\[
 S>\frac Q4-\frac Q{100}=\frac{6Q}{25}>0.
\tag{16}
\]
Thus \(J\ne\varnothing\).

We first prove a parametric amplification of the actual opposite-triangle
mass. For an affine source line \(\ell\) containing two positive-load
atoms, put
\[
 W_\ell:=\sum_{z\in Z\cap\ell}a_z,
 \qquad
 B_{\xi,\ell}:=
 \sum_{\substack{z\in\ell\\z\equiv\xi}}\beta_z,
\tag{17}
\]
where \(Z=\{z:a_z>0\}\). Such a line has occupied-fiber rank one.
Accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c gives
\[
 G\ge
 \min\left\{
 \frac{W_\ell}{4}+\frac{W-W_\ell}{2},
 \ 2^{n-3}W_\ell
 \right\}.
\tag{18}
\]
The first entry is at least \(W/4\), while the canonical hypotheses of
accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 include \(G<W/4\). Hence
\[
 W_\ell\le2^{3-n}G.
\tag{19}
\]
The same accepted fact gives \(\beta_z\le a_z/4\), and therefore
\[
 B_{\xi,\ell}\le\frac{W_\ell}{4}\le2^{1-n}G.
\tag{20}
\]
Because \(J\ne\varnothing\), a line through the endpoints of an edge in
\(J\) has positive \(W_\ell\), so (18)-(19) also show that \(G>0\).

Fix any
\[
 0<\alpha<\frac34.
\tag{21}
\]
Call a retained parity class \(\xi\) \(\alpha\)-heavy if some affine line
through two positive \(\beta\)-atoms in that class satisfies
\[
 B_{\xi,\ell}>\alpha L_\xi.
\tag{22}
\]
Equations (20) and (22) give, for every \(\alpha\)-heavy class,
\[
 L_\xi<\frac{2^{1-n}}{\alpha}G.
\tag{23}
\]
The full product-matching mass in one class is
\[
 |s|_\xi
 =\frac{L_\xi^2-\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\beta_z^2}{2L_\xi}
 \le\frac{L_\xi}{2}.
\tag{24}
\]
Let \(S_{\mathrm H}(\alpha)\) be the \(J\)-mass in
\(\alpha\)-heavy classes. There are at most \(2^n\) parity classes, so
\[
 S_{\mathrm H}(\alpha)
 \le\sum_{\xi\ \alpha\text{-heavy}}|s|_\xi
 <\frac G\alpha.
\tag{25}
\]
If there is no \(\alpha\)-heavy class, then
\(S_{\mathrm H}(\alpha)=0<G/\alpha\) because \(G>0\), so the same strict
inequality holds.

For a positive edge \(e=\{a,b\}\) in class \(\xi\), accepted fact
7ec63df101c30343 proves the exact noncollinear-triangle identity
\[
 t_e=\frac{s_e}{2}
 \left(1-\frac{B_{\xi,\ell(e)}}{L_\xi}\right),
\tag{26}
\]
where \(\ell(e)\) is the affine line through \(a,b\). Hence, in an
\(\alpha\)-nonheavy class,
\[
 t_e\ge\frac{1-\alpha}{2}s_e.
\tag{27}
\]
In an \(\alpha\)-heavy class, every edge of \(J\) is nonexceptional and
(3) gives \(t_e\ge s_e/8\). Writing
\[
 T_J:=\sum_{e\in J}t_e,
\tag{28}
\]
and using \((1-\alpha)/2>1/8\), equations (25) and (27) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T_J
 &\ge
 \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\bigl(S-S_{\mathrm H}(\alpha)\bigr)
 +\frac18S_{\mathrm H}(\alpha)\\
 &=\frac{1-\alpha}{2}S
 -\frac{3-4\alpha}{8}S_{\mathrm H}(\alpha)\\
 &>
 \frac{1-\alpha}{2}S
 -\frac{3-4\alpha}{8\alpha}G.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{29}
\]

We next convert (29) into one-label-per-target residual-selector mass. At
a fixed \(c\in E\), there are at most \(2^n\) retained parity labels.
Maximality in (6), followed by \(J\subseteq\mathcal E_I\), gives
\[
 \Theta_{\xi(c),c}
 \ge2^{-n}\sum_\xi\Theta_{\xi,c}
 \ge2^{-n}
 \sum_{\substack{e\in J\\m_e=c}}t_e.
\tag{30}
\]
The inequality \(t_e\le s_e/2\) in (3), applied only inside the selected
label, gives
\[
 \Theta_{\xi(c),c}\le\frac{U_c}{2}.
\tag{31}
\]
Thus
\[
 U_c\ge2^{1-n}
 \sum_{\substack{e\in J\\m_e=c}}t_e.
\tag{32}
\]
Every edge of \(J\) has \(t_e\ge s_e/8>0\), so its midpoint belongs to
the complete physical support \(E\). Summing (32) over the distinct
physical targets and putting \(U:=\sum_{c\in E}U_c\) yields
\[
 U\ge2^{1-n}T_J>0.
\tag{33}
\]

The equality (7) is exactly the canonical definition of
\(q_{\xi(c),c}^{\circ}\) in accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70; it is an
\(s\)-weighted average, not a \(t\)-weighted one. That accepted fact also
gives the full common-body inclusion
\[
 q_c+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{2U_c}(C_c).
\tag{34}
\]
In particular, \(q_c\in\mathsf M_{2U_c}(C_c)\). Accepted fact
6769574fc1bd9f8d therefore shows that every closed fiber halfspace
containing \(q_c\) has \(C_c\)-mass at least
\[
 w_c:=2\gamma_dU_c.
\tag{35}
\]
Thus \(0<w_c\le\delta_C(c,q_c)\) in the exact notation of accepted fact
be359f8a6c628483. Choose an affine map attaining the minimum in (8).
The residual-selector definition in that accepted fact gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge
 2^{n-\rho_E}\frac{\sum_{c\in E}w_c}{r_E+1}
 =2^{n-\rho_E}\frac{2\gamma_dU}{r_E+1}.
\tag{36}
\]
Accepted facts 6b09aae189415c70 and be359f8a6c628483 give
\(D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\). Since \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\)
are distinct summands of \(\mathcal A(C)\), equations (9), (29), and
(33)-(36) give, for every \(\alpha\) satisfying (21),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+D
 &\ge
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_E+2}\gamma_d}{r_E+1}U\\
 &\ge\frac{8\gamma_d}{k}T_J\\
 &>
 \frac{4\gamma_d(1-\alpha)}{k}S
 -\frac{\gamma_d(3-4\alpha)}{\alpha k}G.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{37}
\]
No other lower bound is charged to either of these two summands.

Accepted fact c57ba4b9d987a404 gives \(k>12\), while accepted fact
d43b9ff596fd01aa gives \(\rho_E\ge3\). Hence \(k\) is a positive
multiple of \(8\) and
\[
 k\ge16.
\tag{38}
\]
We first exclude every \(k\le40\). In (37), take
\[
 \alpha=\frac15.
\tag{39}
\]
Then
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+D
 >
 \frac{16\gamma_d}{5k}S-\frac{11\gamma_d}{k}G.
\tag{40}
\]
Put
\[
 \lambda_1:=\frac{16\gamma_d}{5k}.
\tag{41}
\]
After discarding the nonnegative \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\) and
\(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\) summands, (40) gives
\[
 \mathcal A(C)>
 R+\left(1-\frac{11\gamma_d}{k}\right)G+\lambda_1S.
\tag{42}
\]
The binomial theorem gives \(0<\gamma_d\le1/2\). Using (38), the
coefficients left after subtracting \(\lambda_1\) times the left side of
(15) obey
\[
 1-\lambda_1\frac{67}{8}
 =1-\frac{134\gamma_d}{5k}
 \ge1-\frac{67}{80}
 =\frac{13}{80}>0,
\tag{43}
\]
and
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 1-\frac{11\gamma_d}{k}
 -\lambda_1\frac{307}{256}
 &=1-\frac{1187\gamma_d}{80k}\\
 &\ge1-\frac{1187}{2560}
 =\frac{1373}{2560}>0.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{44}
\]
Accepted fact c57ba4b9d987a404 proves
\(\gamma_d>25/68>4/11\). If \(k\le40\), then
\[
 \lambda_1
 >\frac{64}{55k}
 \ge\frac{64}{2200}
 =\frac8{275}.
\tag{45}
\]
Equations (15) and (42)-(45) would give
\[
 \mathcal A(C)>
 \lambda_1
 \left(S+\frac{67}{8}R+\frac{307}{256}G\right)
 >\frac8{275}\frac{11}{32}Q
 =\frac Q{100},
\tag{46}
\]
contradicting (1). Therefore
\[
 k>40.
\tag{47}
\]
Together with the multiple-of-eight conclusion in (38), this leaves
\(k=48\) as the only value below \(56\).

Suppose for contradiction that
\[
 k=48.
\tag{48}
\]
Since \(\rho_E\ge3\) and \(2^{\rho_E}\) divides \(48\), either
\[
 (\rho_E,r_E)=(3,5)
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 (\rho_E,r_E)=(4,2).
\tag{49}
\]
In both cases \(r_E\le d\) gives \(d\ge2\). The first three terms of the
binomial expansion then give
\[
 \left(1+\frac1d\right)^d
 \ge
 1+1+\frac{\binom d2}{d^2}
 =2+\frac{d-1}{2d}
 \ge\frac94,
\]
and hence
\[
 \gamma_d\le\frac49.
\tag{50}
\]

Now take
\[
 \alpha=\frac1{32}
\tag{51}
\]
in (37). Under (48), this gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+D
 >
 \frac{31\gamma_d}{384}S-\frac{23\gamma_d}{12}G.
\tag{52}
\]
Put
\[
 \lambda_2:=\frac{31\gamma_d}{384}.
\tag{53}
\]
After discarding the other nonnegative capacity summands, (52) gives
\[
 \mathcal A(C)>
 R+\left(1-\frac{23\gamma_d}{12}\right)G+\lambda_2S.
\tag{54}
\]
Using (50), the coefficients left after subtracting \(\lambda_2\) times
the left side of (15) satisfy
\[
 1-\lambda_2\frac{67}{8}
 =1-\frac{2077\gamma_d}{3072}
 \ge1-\frac{2077}{6912}>0,
\tag{55}
\]
and
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 1-\frac{23\gamma_d}{12}
 -\lambda_2\frac{307}{256}
 &=1-\frac{197933\gamma_d}{98304}\\
 &\ge1-\frac{197933}{221184}
 =\frac{23251}{221184}>0.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{56}
\]
The strict lower bound \(\gamma_d>4/11\) also gives
\[
 \lambda_2>\frac{31}{1056}>\frac8{275},
\tag{57}
\]
where the last comparison is
\(31\cdot275=8525>8448=8\cdot1056\). Equations (15) and (54)-(57)
therefore imply
\[
 \mathcal A(C)>
 \lambda_2
 \left(S+\frac{67}{8}R+\frac{307}{256}G\right)
 >\frac Q{100},
\tag{58}
\]
again contradicting (1). Thus \(k\ne48\). Equations (38), (47), and this
exclusion prove \(k\ge56\).

For completeness, all empty, equality, collision, and accounting cases
have been used explicitly. Equation (16) rules out an empty \(J\), and
then (18)-(19) force \(G>0\), which covers an empty heavy-class family in
the strict estimate (25). Equality in
\(B_{\xi,\ell}=\alpha L_\xi\) belongs to the nonheavy case and gives the
non-strict bound (27); equality in the exceptional-edge estimate is
retained in (3). If several labels tie in (6), or their points
\(q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}\) coincide, choose any one maximizer. Equations
(30)-(35) use only that selected label's mass \(U_c\), point \(q_c\),
and representing densities; no masses or densities from different
labels are pooled. Each physical target appears once in (36), even if
several labels or source edges collide there. Finally,
\(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) is charged once and \(D\) once in (37); the
relation \(D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) does not identify them, and no
reservoir, edge-point, or additional \(D\)-certificate is added. This
completes the proof.
