## Statement

Let \(k\ge1\) and \(b\ge2\) be integers. For every \(i\in[k]=\lbrace1,\ldots,k\rbrace\), let \(B_i,C_{4,i}\) be integers with \(b\le B_i\le2b\) and \(C_{4,i}\ge2B_i-1\). Let the initial cap be any finite real number \(T\ge0\), replace it by its integer part, and continue to call that integer \(T\). Let \(\beta\ge0\) be any finite real number. Every spatial vector has multiplicity one. Define
$$
\begin{aligned}
g_i(0)&=0,&g_i(1)&=1,&g_i(2)&=B_i,&g_i(3)&=C_{4,i},\\
\Gamma(\mathbf x)&=\sum_{i=1}^k g_i(x_i),\\
A_T&=\lbrace(\mathbf x,t):\mathbf x\in\lbrace0,1,2,3\rbrace^k,
\ t\in\mathbb Z,\ \Gamma(\mathbf x)\le t\le T\rbrace,\\
Z_4&=\sum_{(\mathbf x,t)\in A_T}e^{-\beta t},\qquad
p_{(\mathbf x,t)}=e^{-\beta t}/Z_4,\\
\tau_p(\mathbf z)&=\inf_{H\ni\mathbf z}\sum_{\mathbf v\in A_T\cap H}p_{\mathbf v},\qquad
M_3(p)=\sum_{\mathbf z\in A_T}p_{\mathbf z}\tau_p(\mathbf z)^3.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
$$
Here \(H\) ranges over all closed affine halfspaces of the full ambient space \(\mathbb R^{k+1}\) with nonzero normal. There is an absolute constant \(\kappa_{\rm allwindow}>0\), specified in (7) below, such that every law (1) satisfies \(M_3(p)\ge\kappa_{\rm allwindow}8^{-(k+1)}\).

All original integer heights, both low costs zero and one, and every boundary atom remain literal. The assertion includes zero tilt, zero cap, tied or distinct costs, and lower affine ranks. The locked objects \(n,d,C,\Lambda,S,W,c_0\), and arithmetic \(N\) retain their locked meanings.
