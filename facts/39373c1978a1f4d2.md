## Statement

Let $\mu$ be the sum of $d$-volume on a nonempty finite compatible family of positive compact convex fibers $K_z$ with total mass $M>0$. Suppose finitely many closed caps with affine margins $L_i(z,x)=a_i\cdot z+u_i\cdot x-b_i$ cover all sampled fibers, $c_i=\mu\{L_i\ge0\}\le\tau M$, and $h_D(u_i)=1$ for a fixed centrally symmetric convex body $D$ about zero. Write $\Psi_i=\int(L_i)_+\,d\mu$. For each $z$ choose a feasible mass $t_z>0$ and let $M_{t_z}(z)$ be the continuous centroids of all submeasures dominated by $\mu$ of mass $t_z$ and base centroid $z$. Assume $p_z+\rho_z D\subset M_{t_z}(z)\subset K_z$ for some $\rho_z\ge0$.

Let $\lambda_z$ be any optimal probability weights for the exact minimax cover margin $C_z=\min_{x\in K_z}\max_iL_i(z,x)$, and let $U_z=\sum_i\lambda_{zi}u_i$. Then
$$
\sum_i\lambda_{zi}\Psi_i
\ge t_z\Big(C_z+h_{K_z}(-U_z)+\sum_i\lambda_{zi}h_{M_{t_z}(z)}(u_i)\Big)
\ge t_z(C_z+\rho_z).
$$
If $\eta_z,\kappa_i\ge0$ satisfy $c_i+\kappa_i=\sum_z\eta_z\lambda_{zi}$ and $\eta_z C_z=0$, then $\sum_i(c_i+\kappa_i)\Psi_i\ge\sum_z\eta_zt_z\rho_z$. With all $\kappa_i=0$ this gives $\tau\sum_i\Psi_i\ge\min_{\eta_z>0}t_z\rho_z$. Likewise if nonnegative $\alpha_0,r_i,S_i,\eta_z$ satisfy $\alpha_0c_i+r_iS_i=\sum_z\eta_z\lambda_{zi}$ and $\eta_z C_z=0$, then $\sum_i(\alpha_0c_i+r_iS_i)\Psi_i\ge\sum_z\eta_zt_z\rho_z$.

## Proof

For an admissible dominated density $0\le f\le1$ of mass $t_z$, write its continuous centroid as $m$. Then $\Psi_i\ge\int fL_i\,d\mu=t_z(u_i\cdot m+a_i\cdot z-b_i)$. Taking the supremum over such $m$ proves the one-cap estimate, including at the maximal feasible mass. Compactness of the moment body follows from weak-star compactness of $0\le f\le1$ with its closed mass and base-moment constraints. Its containment in $K_z$ also follows from compatibility by grouping the dominated mass over the sampled fibers. The minimax identity in 828211a20fa24681 gives $C_z=\sum_i\lambda_{zi}(a_i\cdot z-b_i)-h_{K_z}(-U_z)$. Substitute this in the sum of the one-cap estimates. Since $p_z\in K_z$, its first support term is at least $-U_z\cdot p_z$. The other support terms are at least $U_z\cdot p_z+\rho_z$ by the contained translate and $h_D(u_i)=1$. This proves the local lower bound.

For either coefficient equation, multiply the local bounds by $\eta_z$ and sum, using $\eta_zC_z=0$. In the zero-$\kappa_i$ case the weights sum to $\sum_i c_i\ge M$ by coverage, so some $\eta_z>0$. The left side is at most $\tau M\sum_i\Psi_i$, and the right side is at least $M\min_{\eta_z>0}t_z\rho_z$. Cancel $M$.
