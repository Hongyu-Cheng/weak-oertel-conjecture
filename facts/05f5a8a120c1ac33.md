## Statement

Use the simplex and direction conventions of the arbitrary-direction chord distribution lemma. For every convex subbody $B\subseteq T$ with $0<b=|B|\le V$, let $\eta\in(0,1]$ be the unique solution of
$$
b/V=d\eta^{d-1}-(d-1)\eta^d .
$$
Then $\nu(\pi_eB)\ge P\eta^{d-1}$. For every permitted direction and $b$ this bound is attained by $T\cap\pi_e^{-1}(\{y\in\pi_eT:\ell(y)\ge L(1-\eta)\})$.

## Proof

The arbitrary-direction chord distribution lemma applies in the same coordinate system and measure. The displayed polynomial is strictly increasing on $(0,1)$ because its derivative is $d(d-1)\eta^{d-2}(1-\eta)>0$, with limits zero and one. If $b<V$, set $v_0=L(1-\eta)>0$ and $E_0=\{y\in\pi_eT:\ell(y)\ge v_0\}$. Integrating the chord density of that lemma gives
$$
\nu(E_0)=P\eta^{d-1},\qquad
\int_{E_0}\ell\,d\nu=PL(d-1)\int_0^\eta (1-u)u^{d-2}\,du
=V[d\eta^{d-1}-(d-1)\eta^d]=b.
$$
For any $E\subseteq\pi_eT$, $\int(\mathbf1_{E_0}-\mathbf1_E)(\ell-v_0)\,d\nu\ge0$ by the threshold definition. Thus if $\nu(E)<\nu(E_0)$, then $\int_E\ell\,d\nu<\int_{E_0}\ell\,d\nu=b$. For $E=\pi_eB$, the section lengths of $B$ are at most $\ell$ and give $b\le\int_E\ell\,d\nu$, proving the bound. If $b=V$, equality of the total integrals implies that the section lengths of $B$ equal those of $T$ almost everywhere. Since $\ell>0$ almost everywhere in the projection of $T$, this gives $\nu(\pi_eB)=P$.

On $\pi_eT$ the chord length is concave: the interval section at an interpolated projected point contains the corresponding Minkowski interpolation of the two interval sections, and interval lengths add with the interpolation weights. Thus $E_0$ is convex, and taking all the chords over it gives a convex compact subset of $T$. Compactness follows also because the projected points admitting a chord of length at least $v_0$ form the projection of the compact overlap $T\cap(T-v_0e)$. This subbody has positive volume $b$ by the integral and projects onto $E_0$, attaining the bound. At $b=V$ the displayed optimizer is $T$.
