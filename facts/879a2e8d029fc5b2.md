## Statement

Let $K_z$ be a finite positive compatible family with canonical hull $C$ and labeled boundary surface measure $dS$ (endpoint counting when $d=1$). Take two finite nonnegative marginals absolutely continuous with respect to $dS$ and any physical martingale coupling between them. For almost every source $(p,X)$, let $u$ be its unique continuous outward unit normal. For every affine majorant $l$ of the sampled support values $h_{K_z}(u)$ with $l(p)=h_{K_p}(u)$, the conditional targets $(z,X')$ satisfy $u\cdot X'=h_{K_z}(u)=l(z)$ almost surely. Such a majorant exists at each source. In particular, the target has the same unique normal almost everywhere and the row's base probabilities $\lambda_z$ satisfy $\sum_z\lambda_z z=p$ and $\sum_z\lambda_z h_{K_z}(u)=h_{K_p}(u)$.

## Proof

At each unit $u$, compatibility says the support value at $p$ is the largest anchored average of the sampled support values with barycenter $p$. Finite linear programming duality gives an affine majorant attaining this value at $p$: the primal is feasible and bounded, and the dual has a feasible constant majorant. This is the same anchored-support construction as in 155691fc3768c06f. Regular points have full surface measure by the convex boundary argument in 311757ca7529ae19, including the two interval endpoints when $d=1$. On a single full source-measure set the physical row mean is the source. For every majorant touching there the affine slack $l(z)-u\cdot X'$ is nonnegative on all target fibers and has conditional mean zero. Its two nonnegative summands $l(z)-h_{K_z}(u)$ and $h_{K_z}(u)-u\cdot X'$ both vanish. This applies to every such majorant at any source with the full row mean; no plane selection is needed. In fact a finite basis of their affine coefficient vectors suffices to make all their equalities simultaneous in a row. At regular targets the exposed normal must be their unique one, and taking the base mean and averaging the contact values gives the two equations.
