## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let

\[
C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d
\]

be a convex body, and write

\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
\]

\[
E:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},\qquad
W:=\sum_{z\in E}a_z>0.
\tag{1}
\]

Let \(\mu_C\) be the mixed measure of \(C\).  For
\(p\in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), put

\[
h_C(p):=\inf\{\mu_C(H):H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}
\text{ is a closed halfspace containing }p\}.
\tag{2}
\]

We first define the packed score used below.  If
\(K\subseteq\mathbb R^q\times\mathbb R^d\) is a positive-mixed-mass convex
body with \(q\geq1\), let \(\mu_K\), \(h_K\), and \(W(K)\) be defined by
the analogues of (1)--(2), and put

\[
a(K):=2^{-q}W(K),\qquad \beta:=\frac1{2e},
\tag{3}
\]

and call the following data a legal packed tuple for \(K\):

1. a point \(m\in\mathbb Z^q\) such that every proper closed base
   halfspace \(J\ni m\) contains lattice-fiber mass at least \(a(K)\), and
   such that the complete core of the half-mass fiber metronoids
   \(\mathsf M_{\operatorname{vol}_d(K_z)/2}(K_z)\) at \(m\) is empty;
2. any support-minimal positive balanced circuit, legal affine minorants,
   balance weights, cap bodies, and common descendant \(B\subseteq K\)
   furnished by the construction of accepted fact `51ff186e1aaeaa85` for
   this empty-core datum;
3. positive measures \(\nu_B,\nu_R\) satisfying the packed-state conditions
   of accepted fact `ca3ef8792d78322a`, namely

\[
0\leq\nu_B\leq\mu_B,\qquad
0\leq\nu_R\leq\mu_K-\nu_B,
\tag{4}
\]

\[
|\nu_B|=\beta a(K),\qquad
|\nu_R|=(1-\beta)a(K),
\tag{5}
\]

\[
\int z\,d\nu_B=\beta a(K)m,\qquad
\int z\,d\nu_R=(1-\beta)a(K)m.
\tag{6}
\]

For such a tuple define

\[
y_B:=\overline\nu_B,\qquad
y_R:=\overline\nu_R,\qquad
y:=\overline{\nu_B+\nu_R}
=\beta y_B+(1-\beta)y_R.
\tag{7}
\]

These points lie in
\(K\cap(\mathbb Z^q\times\mathbb R^d)\).  Define

\[
P_{\mathrm{pack}}(K):=
\sup_{\text{legal packed tuples for }K}
\max\{h_K(y_B),h_K(y),h_K(y_R)\},
\tag{8}
\]

with value zero when there is no legal packed tuple.  In integer rank
\(q=0\), define \(P_{\mathrm{pack}}(K):=0\); indeed the sole
barycentric representation makes every nonempty half-mass fiber metronoid
core nonempty.

Let \(A\subsetneq\mathbb R^n\) be a primitive affine lattice subspace of
rank

\[
r:=\dim A<n
\]

which is the affine hull of a nonempty exposed face of
\(\operatorname{conv}E\).  Thus there is an affine functional
\(\psi:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R\) such that

\[
\psi=0\quad\text{on }A,\qquad
\psi(z)>0\quad(z\in E\setminus A).
\tag{9}
\]

Put

\[
W_A:=\sum_{z\in E\cap A}a_z,
\tag{10}
\]

and assume the target-heavy inequality

\[
W_A\geq2^{r-n}W.
\tag{11}
\]

Choose an affine lattice isomorphism

\[
T:\mathbb R^r\longrightarrow A,
\qquad T(\mathbb Z^r)=A\cap\mathbb Z^n,
\tag{12}
\]

and let

\[
\iota:\mathbb R^r\times\mathbb R^d
\longrightarrow A\times\mathbb R^d,
\qquad
\iota(u,x):=(T(u),x).
\tag{13}
\]

Define the relative body

\[
C_A:=\iota^{-1}\bigl(C\cap(A\times\mathbb R^d)\bigr).
\tag{14}
\]

It has mixed mass \(W_A\), and its relative target mass is

\[
a_A:=2^{-r}W_A.
\tag{15}
\]

Then every legal packed tuple for \(C_A\) has a legal ambient lift for
\(C\) with the following properties.  If
\(\nu_B^A,\nu_R^A\) are the relative measures and

\[
\lambda_*:=\frac{2^{-n}W}{a_A}
=2^{r-n}\frac{W}{W_A},
\tag{16}
\]

then \(0<\lambda_*\leq1\), and the lifted measures are

\[
\nu_B:=\lambda_*\,\iota_\#\nu_B^A,
\qquad
\nu_R:=\lambda_*\,\iota_\#\nu_R^A.
\tag{17}
\]

They satisfy all ambient versions of (4)--(6) with
\(a(C)=2^{-n}W\).  If \(B_A\) is the relative descendant and \(B\) is
the lifted ambient descendant, then

\[
B\cap(A\times\mathbb R^d)=\iota(B_A).
\tag{18}
\]

The three normalized barycenters are unchanged under the embedding:

\[
\overline\nu_B=\iota(\overline\nu_B^A),\qquad
\overline\nu_R=\iota(\overline\nu_R^A),\qquad
\overline{\nu_B+\nu_R}
=\iota\bigl(\overline{\nu_B^A+\nu_R^A}\bigr).
\tag{19}
\]

Moreover, every
\(p\in C_A\cap(\mathbb Z^r\times\mathbb R^d)\) satisfies the
relative-to-ambient depth domination

\[
\boxed{h_C(\iota(p))\geq h_{C_A}(p).}
\tag{20}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\boxed{P_{\mathrm{pack}}(C)\geq P_{\mathrm{pack}}(C_A).}
\tag{21}
\]

Equivalently, including the target normalizations,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{2^nP_{\mathrm{pack}}(C)}{W}
\geq
2^{n-r}\frac{W_A}{W}
\frac{2^rP_{\mathrm{pack}}(C_A)}{W_A}
\geq
\frac{2^rP_{\mathrm{pack}}(C_A)}{W_A}.}
\tag{22}
\]

## Proof

The set \(E\) is finite.  The relative set \(C_A\) in (14) is a convex
body in \(\mathbb R^r\times\mathbb R^d\).  Indeed, it is compact and
convex.  Because \(A\) is the affine hull of the exposed face, there are
\(r+1\) affinely independent points of \(E\cap A\); every corresponding
fiber has positive \(d\)-volume and therefore affine hull
\(\mathbb R^d\).  Points from these fibers span an affine space of dimension
\(r+d\), so \(C_A\) is full-dimensional.

If \(r=0\), then \(C_A\) has one positive
lattice fiber.  Its half-mass metronoid is nonempty, its only barycentric
representation is the unit mass at that fiber, and its complete core is that
metronoid.  Thus it has no legal packed tuple, so
\(P_{\mathrm{pack}}(C_A)=0\), and (21)--(22) are immediate.  Assume from
now on that \(1\leq r<n\), and fix an arbitrary legal packed tuple for
\(C_A\).

Write its relative centerpoint as \(m_A\in\mathbb Z^r\) and put

\[
m:=T(m_A)\in A\cap\mathbb Z^n.
\tag{23}
\]

We first prove that \(m\) is an allowed ambient centerpoint.  Let
\(J\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) be a proper closed base halfspace containing
\(m\).  The pullback of \(J\cap A\) under \(T\) is either all of
\(\mathbb R^r\), or a proper closed halfspace containing \(m_A\).  In the
first case, \(J\) contains all the face mass \(W_A\).  In the second case,
relative centerpoint balance gives face mass at least \(a_A\).  Since (11)
is equivalent to

\[
a_A\geq2^{-n}W,
\tag{24}
\]

both cases give

\[
\sum_{z\in E\cap J}a_z\geq2^{-n}W.
\tag{25}
\]

The exposed-face property also forces every ambient barycentric
representation of \(m\) to use only points of \(A\).  Indeed, if

\[
m=\sum_{z\in E}\alpha_zz,
\qquad
\alpha_z\geq0,
\qquad
\sum_{z\in E}\alpha_z=1,
\tag{26}
\]

then (9) gives

\[
0=\psi(m)=\sum_{z\in E}\alpha_z\psi(z).
\tag{27}
\]

Every summand is nonnegative and every off-face summand with positive
coefficient is strictly positive.  Hence

\[
\alpha_z=0\qquad(z\in E\setminus A).
\tag{28}
\]

After applying \(T^{-1}\), restriction and zero extension therefore give
an affine bijection between the ambient representation polytope of \(m\)
over \(E\) and the relative representation polytope of \(m_A\) over the
positive lattice support of \(C_A\).

For every \(z=T(u)\in E\cap A\), the relative and ambient fibers are the
same subset of \(\mathbb R^d\).  Their half-mass metronoids and support
functions are therefore identical.  Together with the representation
polytope identity, this shows that the ambient half-mass complete core at
\(m\) equals the embedded relative core at \(m_A\), and hence is empty.  It
also shows that the ambient support-envelope function \(\phi\) in accepted
fact `51ff186e1aaeaa85` equals the relative one for every continuous
direction.  Thus the relative support-minimal positive balanced circuit
remains a negative balanced circuit ambiently.  It remains support-minimal:
any smaller ambient negative balanced circuit would, by the same envelope
identity, be a smaller relative one.

It remains to extend the relative affine minorants without changing their
face values.  Let \(v_i,\theta_i,\ell_i^A\) be the directions, positive
balance weights, and relative legal affine minorants of the fixed tuple.
Define an affine function on \(A\) by

\[
\widehat\ell_i(T(u)):=\ell_i^A(u),
\tag{29}
\]

and choose any affine extension
\(\widetilde\ell_i:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R\).  For the ambient half-mass
metronoid support function \(h_z(v_i)\), set

\[
M_i:=\max\left(
\{0\}\cup
\left\{
\frac{\widetilde\ell_i(z)-h_z(v_i)}{\psi(z)}:
z\in E\setminus A
\right\}
\right)
\tag{30}
\]

and

\[
\ell_i(z):=\widetilde\ell_i(z)-M_i\psi(z).
\tag{31}
\]

The maximum in (30) is finite.  Equations (9), (29)--(31) give

\[
\ell_i(T(u))=\ell_i^A(u)\quad(u\in\mathbb R^r),
\qquad
\ell_i(z)\leq h_z(v_i)\quad(z\in E\setminus A).
\tag{32}
\]

At \(m\), the common envelope identity gives

\[
\ell_i(m)=\ell_i^A(m_A)=\phi(v_i).
\tag{33}
\]

Thus \(\ell_i\) are legal ambient affine minorants exact at \(m\), with the
same directions and balance weights.

Use these data in the common-descendant construction of accepted fact
`51ff186e1aaeaa85`, and call the ambient descendant \(B\).  Its cap section
at \(z=T(u)\in A\) is the embedded relative cap section because (32) is an
equality there.  The descendant is defined fiberwise by the same weighted
Minkowski formula.  Hence

\[
B_{T(u)}=(B_A)_u
\qquad(u\in\mathbb R^r),
\tag{34}
\]

which proves (18).  In particular, the pushforward of the relative mixed
measure \(\mu_{B_A}\) is the restriction of \(\mu_B\) to
\(A\times\mathbb R^d\).

Let

\[
\widehat\nu_B^A:=\iota_\#\nu_B^A,
\qquad
\widehat\nu_R^A:=\iota_\#\nu_R^A.
\tag{35}
\]

The target-heavy assumption gives \(0<\lambda_*\leq1\) in (16).  Relative
legality and (34) imply

\[
0\leq\lambda_*\widehat\nu_B^A
\leq\lambda_*\,\mu_B|_{A\times\mathbb R^d}
\leq\mu_B,
\tag{36}
\]

and, regarding the embedded relative measure as the restriction of
\(\mu_C\) to the same slice,

\[
\begin{aligned}
0\leq\lambda_*\widehat\nu_R^A
&\leq
\lambda_*\bigl(\mu_C|_{A\times\mathbb R^d}
-\widehat\nu_B^A\bigr)\\
&\leq\mu_C-\lambda_*\widehat\nu_B^A.
\end{aligned}
\tag{37}
\]

Thus the measures in (17) satisfy the ambient domination conditions (4).
Their masses are

\[
|\nu_B|=\lambda_*\beta a_A
=\beta2^{-n}W,
\qquad
|\nu_R|=\lambda_*(1-\beta)a_A
=(1-\beta)2^{-n}W.
\tag{38}
\]

Write the affine lattice isomorphism as \(T(u)=b+Uu\).  Relative legality
gives

\[
\int u\,d\nu_B^A=\beta a_A m_A,
\qquad
\int u\,d\nu_R^A=(1-\beta)a_A m_A.
\tag{39}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\int z\,d\nu_B
&=\lambda_*\left(b|\nu_B^A|+U\int u\,d\nu_B^A\right)
=\beta2^{-n}W\,T(m_A)
=\beta2^{-n}W\,m,\\
\int z\,d\nu_R
&=\lambda_*\left(b|\nu_R^A|+U\int u\,d\nu_R^A\right)
=(1-\beta)2^{-n}W\,T(m_A)
=(1-\beta)2^{-n}W\,m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{40}
\]

Equations (36)--(40) prove all ambient versions of (4)--(6).  They also
display the full scaling of the selected and residual masses and base first
moments.  The continuous first moments are simply

\[
\int x\,d\nu_B=\lambda_*\int x\,d\nu_B^A,
\qquad
\int x\,d\nu_R=\lambda_*\int x\,d\nu_R^A.
\tag{41}
\]

Dividing (40)--(41) by the corresponding masses proves the first two
identities in (19); adding the measures proves the third.  This also shows
directly that all three lifted barycenters have integral base coordinate
\(m\).  Their membership in \(B\) or \(C\) follows either from (18) and
relative legality, or from convexity and the support of the corresponding
measures, exactly as in accepted fact `ca3ef8792d78322a`.  Thus the
constructed data form a legal ambient packed tuple.

We prove the depth comparison independently of the packet data.  Fix
\(p\in C_A\cap(\mathbb Z^r\times\mathbb R^d)\), and let
\(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be any closed halfspace containing
\(\iota(p)\).  The pullback

\[
H_A:=\iota^{-1}\bigl(H\cap(A\times\mathbb R^d)\bigr)
\tag{42}
\]

is either all of \(\mathbb R^{r+d}\), or a proper relative closed
halfspace containing \(p\).  In the proper case,

\[
\mu_C(H)\geq\mu_{C_A}(H_A)\geq h_{C_A}(p).
\tag{43}
\]

In the whole-space case, \(H\) contains the entire relative slice, so

\[
\mu_C(H)\geq W_A\geq h_{C_A}(p).
\tag{44}
\]

Taking the infimum over \(H\) proves (20).

Apply (20) to each of the three barycenters in (19).  The ambient score of
the lifted tuple is at least the relative score of the original tuple.
Since the relative tuple was arbitrary, taking suprema proves (21).  Finally,

\[
\frac{2^nP_{\mathrm{pack}}(C)}{W}
\geq\frac{2^nP_{\mathrm{pack}}(C_A)}{W}
=2^{n-r}\frac{W_A}{W}
\frac{2^rP_{\mathrm{pack}}(C_A)}{W_A},
\tag{45}
\]

and (11) makes the middle prefactor at least one.  This proves (22).
