## Statement

Fix an integer \(k\ge8\). Use the exact tree construction of accepted fact 032423921efad6c8 and the exact \(\rho_t=2^{-t}\) height specialization of accepted fact 604889294a17d7df, with the following normalizations. Put
$$
b_\ell=0\quad(1\le\ell<8),\qquad
b_\ell=\left\lfloor\frac{2^\ell}{8\ell}\right\rfloor\quad(8\le\ell\le k),
\qquad Q_0=0,\qquad Q_\ell=\sum_{r=1}^{\ell}b_r,\qquad Q=Q_k,
$$
$$
m=2(Q+1)^4,\qquad n=\sum_{r=1}^{Q}m^r,\qquad N=n+1.
$$
Set \(a_0=0\) and \(a_s=\ell\) whenever \(Q_{\ell-1}<s\le Q_\ell\). Thus \(k\) is the height cutoff, \(Q\) is the tree depth, and \(n\) is the spatial dimension.

Write \([m]=\lbrace1,\ldots,m\rbrace\) and
$$
\mathcal T_{m,Q}=\bigcup_{r=1}^{Q}[m]^r.
$$
For a node \(v\) with \(|v|<Q\), including the empty root \(\varnothing\), its child-coordinate block is \(z[v]=(z_{v1},\ldots,z_{vm})\). Choose any symmetric saturated chain partition of \(\lbrace0,1\rbrace^m\). Let \(D_m\) be its nonterminal set and \(F_m(c)=c+e_{i_m(c)}\) its injective successor, where \(c_{i_m(c)}=0\).

For a Boolean source \(b\in\lbrace0,1\rbrace^{\mathcal T_{m,Q}}\), start at the root and select the child \(vi_m(b[v])\) whenever the current block \(b[v]\in D_m\), stopping at the first failure or at depth \(Q\). Let \(u_1,\ldots,u_{h(b)}\) be the resulting path. Retain the complete original spatial and height supports
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{m,Q}
&=\left\lbrace z_s(b)=b+2\sum_{r=1}^{s}e_{u_r}:
b\in\lbrace0,1\rbrace^{\mathcal T_{m,Q}},\quad 0\le s\le h(b)\right\rbrace,\\
\widehat A
&=\left\lbrace(z,t):z\in A_{m,Q},\quad
a_{\operatorname{level}(z)}\le t\le k,\quad t\in\mathbb Z\right\rbrace,
\end{aligned}
$$
where \(\operatorname{level}(z_s(b))=s\), equivalently the number of its coordinates equal to two.

For every real spatial field \(\gamma=(\gamma_u)_{u\in\mathcal T_{m,Q}}\in\mathbb R^n\), put
$$
w_{(z,t)}=\exp(\gamma\cdot z)2^{-t}\quad((z,t)\in\widehat A),
\qquad w_{\mathbf I}=0\quad(\mathbf I\notin\widehat A),
$$
$$
W=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}w_{\mathbf I},\qquad
p_{\mathbf I}=\frac{w_{\mathbf I}}W,\qquad
p(H)=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A\cap H}p_{\mathbf I}.
$$
Define the actual depth in the original ambient space by
$$
\tau_p(\mathbf I)=\inf\left\lbrace p(H):
H\subseteq\mathbb R^N\text{ is a closed halfspace containing }\mathbf I\right\rbrace,
\qquad
M_3(p)=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}p_{\mathbf I}\tau_p(\mathbf I)^3.
$$
Then, for every such \(k\), chain partition, and spatial field,
$$
M_3(p)\ge2^{-200}8^{-N}.
$$
Every original column and every original height in it is included in \(W\) and \(p\), and the depth infimum uses all containing closed halfspaces of \(\mathbb R^N\). There is no restriction on the depths, locations, signs, or magnitudes of the spatial coefficients. The height coefficient is exactly \(-\log2\), and \(M_3(p)\) has the depth-moment meaning above, distinct from the maximum-atom notation \(M(w)\) in 604889294a17d7df.

## Proof

The supports and parameters above agree exactly with 032423921efad6c8 and the \(\rho_t=2^{-t}\) specialization of 604889294a17d7df. In particular, \(b_8=4\), so \(Q\ge4\), and \(m,Q\) are positive. Also \(n=\sum_{r=1}^{Q}m^r\ge mQ\). Every spatial column is nonempty, and all its specified weights are positive and finite; hence \(0<W<\infty\).

Apply accepted fact dbbd63823b5f4870 to this exact field and construction. In its notation, put
$$
R(x)=\frac{8(1+e^{4x})}{(1+e^x)^4},\qquad
\beta_u=\log R(\gamma_u),\qquad \varepsilon=\frac1{mQ}.
$$
Choose \(T_0\subseteq\mathcal T_{m,Q}\) of exactly \(mQ\) coordinates with largest \(\beta_u\), with arbitrary tie breaking, and set
$$
E_{\mathrm{rem}}=\sum_{u\notin T_0}\beta_u.
$$
The cited fact uses exactly the original support, the weights \(e^{\gamma\cdot z}2^{-t}\), their full normalizer, and the actual ambient-halfspace definition of \(M_3\) in the statement.

If \(E_{\mathrm{rem}}\ge\log m\), dbbd63823b5f4870 gives
$$
M_3(p)\ge\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}p_{\mathbf I}^4\ge8^{-N}
\ge2^{-200}8^{-N}.
$$
This alternative includes the threshold equality \(E_{\mathrm{rem}}=\log m\).

Suppose instead that \(E_{\mathrm{rem}}<\log m\). The same accepted fact supplies the explicit set
$$
T=T_0\cup\left\lbrace u\notin T_0:|\gamma_u|>\varepsilon\right\rbrace,
\qquad
1\le mQ=|T_0|\le|T|\le64(Q+1)^{11}.
$$
It gives \(|\gamma_u|\le\varepsilon\) for every \(u\notin T\), and, with its child-block normalization,
$$
\Pi(\gamma\mathbf1_{\mathcal T_{m,Q}\setminus T})\le1,
\qquad
\Pi(\theta)=
\max_{v_0,\ldots,v_Q}\sum_{j=0}^{Q-1}\sum_{i=1}^{m}|\theta_{v_ji}|.
\tag{1}
$$
The maximum ranges over full rooted paths: \(v_0=\varnothing\), \(|v_j|=j\), and \(v_{j+1}\) is a child of \(v_j\). Thus the sum in (1) contains every coefficient of every visited child block, rather than only the coefficients at the selected nodes.

We embed every coordinate of \(T\) in a permissible full-spine family. For each \(u\in T\) of depth \(r=|u|\in\lbrace1,\ldots,Q\rbrace\), take its unique root-to-\(u\) path and extend it to depth \(Q\), for example by choosing child \(1\) at each later step. This gives one full rooted path through \(u\). Remove duplicate full paths and let \(B\) be the number retained. Because \(T\ne\varnothing\),
$$
1\le B\le|T|\le64(Q+1)^{11}.
\tag{2}
$$
Removing a duplicate loses no covered coordinate: the retained copy has the identical node at every depth. Therefore each \(u\in T\) still lies on at least one retained full path.

Let \(P_j\) be the set of depth-\(j\) nodes of the retained paths, for \(0\le j\le Q\), and define exactly the sets of 6edf2d6eb3514aa3:
$$
S=\bigcup_{j=0}^{Q-1}\ \bigcup_{v\in P_j}\lbrace vi:1\le i\le m\rbrace,
\qquad
U=S\cup\left\lbrace u\in\mathcal T_{m,Q}:|u|\le\min(7,Q)\right\rbrace.
\tag{3}
$$
For \(u\in T\) of depth \(r\), a retained path through \(u\) contains its parent \(v\in P_{r-1}\). Since \(r-1\le Q-1\), the entire child block of \(v\) appears in the union defining \(S\), and in particular \(u\in S\). This proves
$$
T\subseteq S\subseteq U.
\tag{4}
$$
This argument also covers coordinates of depth \(Q\): it uses their parents in \(P_{Q-1}\), so it does not require a child block below depth \(Q\).

Define a spatial field on the same original coordinates by
$$
\lambda_u=
\begin{cases}
0,&u\in U,\\
\gamma_u,&u\notin U.
\end{cases}
\tag{5}
$$
It vanishes on \(U\), as required by 6edf2d6eb3514aa3. By (4), for every coordinate \(u\),
$$
|\lambda_u|\le
|\gamma_u|\mathbf1_{\mathcal T_{m,Q}\setminus T}(u).
$$
Summing this inequality over each full path's child blocks and taking the maximum proves, in exactly the normalization (1),
$$
P_\lambda:=\Pi(\lambda)
\le\Pi(\gamma\mathbf1_{\mathcal T_{m,Q}\setminus T})\le1.
\tag{6}
$$
Equivalently, each of the \(Q\) visited child blocks has \(m\) distinct coordinates, all nonzero terms outside \(U\) have magnitude at most \(\varepsilon\), and their sum is at most \(mQ\varepsilon=1\). No bound on coefficients in \(U\), and no sign condition anywhere, is used.

For the actual restriction-label set
$$
R_U=\lbrace z|_U:z\in A_{m,Q}\rbrace,
$$
define
$$
G:R_U\longrightarrow(0,\infty),\qquad
G(\omega)=\exp\left(\sum_{u\in U}\gamma_u\omega_u\right).
\tag{7}
$$
All its values are positive and finite because the field is real and the coordinate set is finite. The accepted forest interface permits an arbitrary positive function on all actual labels \(R_U\), so (7) needs no comparison between different labels.

For every original index \((z,t)\in\widehat A\), (5)--(7) give the exact identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
G(z|_U)e^{\lambda\cdot z}2^{-t}
&=\exp\left(\sum_{u\in U}\gamma_uz_u
+\sum_{u\notin U}\gamma_uz_u\right)2^{-t}\\
&=e^{\gamma\cdot z}2^{-t}=w_{(z,t)}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
$$
Both tables have zero weight off the same original support. Consequently the full sums \(W\) are identical, the normalized probabilities \(p\) are identical at every original index, and \(p(H)\), \(\tau_p\), and \(M_3(p)\) are identical for every original ambient closed halfspace. In particular (8) does not restrict a column, omit a height, condition a law, or change the height coefficient.

The family constructed above satisfies the full-path threshold (2); its sets \(S,U\) are exactly (3); its field and multiplier satisfy (5)--(7). Therefore all hypotheses of accepted fact 6edf2d6eb3514aa3 hold with no rescaling of the spatial field, the child-block path variation, the weights, or their normalizer. That fact yields
$$
M_3(p)\ge2^{-200}8^{-N}
$$
for precisely the original law, using all containing closed halfspaces in the original space \(\mathbb R^N\). Its statement applies to every \(k\ge8\), including \(Q<7\); in that case (3) includes every coordinate in \(U\), so (5) gives \(\lambda=0\) and the same application remains valid. Thus no short-depth case is omitted. The two entropy alternatives are exhaustive, and the proof is complete.
