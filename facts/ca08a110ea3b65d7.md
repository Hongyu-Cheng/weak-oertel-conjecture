## Statement

Take positive full-dimensional compact convex fibers $C_z$ at finitely many bases, with a finite cap menu. Replace one cap by $H_a=\lbrace u\cdot x+a\cdot(z-p)\ge c\rbrace $ with fixed nonzero $u$, $p,c$, leaving the other caps fixed. Write $f_z(a)=|C_z\cap H_a^c|^{1/d}$ and $\bar e_z$ for the minimum residual root of the other caps, or $+\infty$ if there are none. The one-cap convex program minimizes $\sum_z h_z^d$ over $h_z\ge \min(\bar e_z,f_z(a))$ and sampled convex slope constraints $h_w\ge h_z+g_z\cdot(w-z)$. Fix an old optimum and its nonnegative KKT multipliers, writing $\lambda_z$ for the obstacle multipliers. It and the same multipliers remain optimal whenever $f_z(a)\ge h_z$ on $\lambda_z>0$ and $f_z(a)\le h_z$ on $\bar e_z>h_z$. These conditions are closed polyhedral in $a$ and hold at the old tilt. The source trace at $p$ is unchanged, and any row computed from the retained KKT data can be retained.

## Proof

If $\lambda_z>0$, old complementary slackness gives $\bar e_z\ge h_z$ and the old minimum obstacle equals $h_z$. When $\bar e_z=h_z$, the imposed lower bound forces the new minimum to equal $h_z$. When $\bar e_z>h_z$, both bounds apply and do the same. If $\lambda_z=0$, either $\bar e_z\le h_z$ or the imposed upper bound makes the new obstacle at most $h_z$. Thus the old primal data stay feasible and complementary slackness stays valid. Changing only the constant obstacle leaves all stationarity equations, all slope inequalities and their multipliers unchanged. KKT sufficiency follows from the convex objective on the nonnegative height domain and the linear constraints. This preserves the underlying optimizer and multipliers of any KKT row.

On a positive fiber the residual root is a continuous nonincreasing function of $a\cdot(z-p)$, with strictly decreasing values between zero and the full root: every nonempty open slab between two interior levels of $u\cdot x$ has positive fiber volume. Thus each imposed weak bound is a closed half-line condition in that scalar, or is all or empty. Endpoint levels use the same conclusion by continuity and exact full or zero residual saturation. The old tilt satisfies every bound by its feasibility and complementary slackness, so the intersection is a nonempty closed polyhedron. At the source base the variable affine term vanishes.
