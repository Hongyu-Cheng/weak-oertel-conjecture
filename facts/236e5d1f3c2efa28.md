## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), and retain the canonical hard-branch objects and normalizations of accepted fact `f6188983e8850f01`. Thus \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body and, for every \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\) with \(a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)>0\),
\[
\mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
\qquad
c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,
\qquad
D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z.
\]
The original load-minimal KKT stress is \(v=(v_e)\). For every original positive-load edge \(e=\{u,v\}\),
\[
m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2}\in\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad
D_e=\frac12(D_u+D_v),
\qquad
L_e=\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v),
\qquad
\omega_e=h_{D_e}(v_e)>0.
\]
Here and below an edge with zero original load may be retained in a finite ambient edge set by setting all of its displayed coefficients and vectors equal to zero.

Use the same-ray negative-circuit decomposition constructed in accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`. Namely, for \(\omega_e>0\), put
\[
u_e:=\frac{v_e}{\omega_e},
\qquad h_{D_e}(u_e)=1.
\]
There are finitely many nonnegative coefficient fields \((\omega_e^{(j)})_e\), \(1\leq j\leq N\), such that
\[
v_e^{(j)}:=\omega_e^{(j)}u_e,
\qquad
\sum_{j=1}^N\omega_e^{(j)}=\omega_e,
\qquad
v_e=\sum_{j=1}^Nv_e^{(j)}.
\tag{1}
\]
For
\[
C_j:=\{e:\omega_e^{(j)}>0\},
\]
each \(C_j\) is nonempty, and every component satisfies
\[
\sum_ev_e^{(j)}=0,
\qquad
\sum_ev_e^{(j)}m_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
\qquad
\sum_{e\in C_j}h_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)})<0.
\tag{2}
\]

For the canonical target multipliers of accepted fact `f6188983e8850f01`, define
\[
\lambda_e:=1-\chi_{m_e},
\qquad
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}:=\lambda_e\omega_e,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{cong}}:=\sum_e\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus \(0\leq\lambda_e\leq1\), and \(\lambda_e=0\) if and only if \(\chi_{m_e}=1\). Define the circuit bottleneck
\[
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}
:=
\sum_{j=1}^N
\min_{e\in C_j}
\lambda_e\omega_e^{(j)}.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), let \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}\) be the total gauge load of the fresh load-minimal dual optimization for \(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}\), under the alias map in accepted fact `f6188983e8850f01` from accepted fact `6a74481cad1d7e81`. If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), extend the notation by
\[
\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}:=0,
\qquad
R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}):=0.
\tag{5}
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
\geq B_{\mathrm{cong}}-R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}})
\geq\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}.}
\tag{6}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\boxed{
\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\forall j\ \exists e\in C_j:\ \chi_{m_e}=1.}
\tag{7}
\]
Equivalently, the set of lossless physical targets \(\{m:\chi_m=1\}\) meets the target set \(\{m_e:e\in C_j\}\) of every original negative circuit. Statement (7) is exact; no conclusion that lossless targets meet most circuits follows here from a small positive value of \(\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}\).

## Proof

We first prove the second inequality in (6), including the edge-capacity audit across overlapping circuits. By the definition of exact affine-retainable mass in accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`,
\[
B_{\mathrm{cong}}-R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}})
=
\min_s
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s(m_e)\notin L_e}}
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
\tag{8}
\]
where the minimum is over affine maps \(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\). Zero-charge edges may be included in the sum without changing it.

Fix one such affine map \(s\), and put
\[
M_s:=\{e:s(m_e)\notin L_e\}.
\]
Every circuit \(C_j\) meets \(M_s\). Indeed, if \(s(m_e)\in L_e\) for every \(e\in C_j\), then the support-function inequality and the two moment cancellations in (2) give
\[
\sum_{e\in C_j}h_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)})
\geq
\sum_{e\in C_j}\langle v_e^{(j)},s(m_e)\rangle
=0,
\tag{9}
\]
contrary to the strict negative objective in (2). To justify the equality, write \(s(m)=Am+b\); the two sums in (2) annihilate separately the \(Am_e\) and \(b\) terms.

For every \(j\), choose one edge \(e(j,s)\in C_j\cap M_s\). Even when many circuits choose the same edge, (1) gives the exact capacity bound
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}
&=\sum_j\min_{f\in C_j}\lambda_f\omega_f^{(j)}\\
&\leq\sum_j\lambda_{e(j,s)}\omega_{e(j,s)}^{(j)}\\
&=\sum_{e\in M_s}\lambda_e
  \sum_{\substack{j:e(j,s)=e}}\omega_e^{(j)}\\
&\leq\sum_{e\in M_s}\lambda_e
  \sum_j\omega_e^{(j)}\\
&=\sum_{e\in M_s}\lambda_e\omega_e
=\sum_{e\in M_s}\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
Thus a shared edge is never charged beyond its single available capacity \(\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\). Taking the minimum over \(s\) in (10) and using (8) proves
\[
B_{\mathrm{cong}}-R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}})
\geq\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}.
\tag{11}
\]

If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), accepted fact `6a74481cad1d7e81`, with the exact congestion alias map recorded in accepted fact `f6188983e8850f01`, gives
\[
\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
\geq B_{\mathrm{cong}}-R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}).
\tag{12}
\]
If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), both terms on the right of (6) vanish: (3) gives \(\lambda_e\omega_e=0\) for every edge, and, because \(0\leq\omega_e^{(j)}\leq\omega_e\), every minimum in (4) is zero. Thus (6) holds trivially in the zero-charge case. Together with (11) and the positive-charge inequality (12), this proves (6) in all cases.

It remains to prove the exact transversal assertion (7). Define
\[
t_j:=\min_{e\in C_j}\lambda_e\geq0
\tag{13}
\]
and the edge field
\[
w_e:=\sum_{j=1}^Nt_jv_e^{(j)}.
\tag{14}
\]
For every edge with \(\omega_e>0\), all vectors \(v_e^{(j)}\) lie on the same nonnegative ray generated by \(u_e\). Consequently, positive homogeneity of support functions gives the exact same-ray additivity identities
\[
\begin{aligned}
h_{D_e}(w_e)
&=\sum_jt_jh_{D_e}(v_e^{(j)})
=\sum_jt_j\omega_e^{(j)},\\
h_{L_e}(w_e)
&=\sum_jt_jh_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]
For an edge with \(\omega_e=0\), every vector and coefficient in (15) is zero, so the same identities hold. Since \(t_j\leq\lambda_e\) whenever \(e\in C_j\), (1) and (15) give
\[
h_{D_e}(w_e)
=\sum_jt_j\omega_e^{(j)}
\leq\lambda_e\sum_j\omega_e^{(j)}
=\lambda_e\omega_e
=\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{16}
\]
Linear combination of the moment equations in (2) also gives
\[
\sum_ew_e=0,
\qquad
\sum_ew_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0.
\tag{17}
\]
Hence \(w=(w_e)\) is feasible for the weighted dual associated with \(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}\). The second identity in (15), finite summation, and (2) yield
\[
\sum_eh_{L_e}(w_e)
=
\sum_jt_j
\sum_{e\in C_j}h_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)}).
\tag{18}
\]
Every inner sum in (18) is strictly negative. Therefore, if \(t_j>0\) for at least one \(j\), then
\[
\sum_eh_{L_e}(w_e)<0.
\tag{19}
\]

Suppose now that \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\). If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), then \(\lambda_e\omega_e=0\) for every \(e\). For \(e\in C_j\), one has \(0<\omega_e^{(j)}\leq\omega_e\), so \(\lambda_e=0\); in particular every \(C_j\) contains such an edge.

Assume instead that \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\). The zero-load characterization in accepted fact `6a74481cad1d7e81` gives \(\Delta_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}})=0\). If some \(t_j>0\), then (16)-(19) exhibit a feasible dual field of strictly positive value
\[
-\sum_eh_{L_e}(w_e)>0,
\]
contradicting the exact weighted dual equality of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`. Thus \(t_j=0\) for every \(j\). By (13), for every circuit \(C_j\) there is an edge \(e\in C_j\) with \(\lambda_e=0\), equivalently \(\chi_{m_e}=1\). This proves (7), including every zero-coefficient boundary case.
