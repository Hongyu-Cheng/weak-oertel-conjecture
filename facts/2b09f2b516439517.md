## Statement

Retain the common actual canonical hard \(n=3\), affine-target-rank-two instance and notation of accepted fact 2992eea9e109d58a. Thus
\[
 p:=|q^*|=\sum_{t\in T_*}x_t>0,
 \qquad
 A:=\operatorname{aff}(T_*),
 \qquad
 \dim A=2.
\tag{1}
\]
Every \(t\in T_*\) is integral, \(x_t>0\), and its canonical target center \(q_t\in\mathbb R^d\) and common full-dimensional compact origin-symmetric body \(D_0\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) satisfy
\[
 q_t+D_0\subseteq\mathsf M_{x_t}(C_t).
\tag{2}
\]
For every real number \(Y\) with
\[
 0<Y<\frac p2,
\tag{3}
\]
the following implication holds:
\[
 \max_{t\in T_*}x_t\geq\frac Y4
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \begin{array}{l}
 \text{there are a finite nonempty \(E\subseteq T_*\), masses \(0<y_t\leq x_t\),}\\
 \text{and an integral relative \(1/4\)-center \(m\in A\cap\mathbb Z^3\)}
 \end{array}
\tag{4}
\]
such that
\[
 \sum_{t\in E}y_t=Y,
 \qquad
 \rho_{D_0}(E,m)=0.
\tag{5}
\]
Here relative \(1/4\)-center and \(\rho_{D_0}\) have exactly the meanings in accepted fact 2992eea9e109d58a.

In particular, put
\[
 Y_0:=\frac{256}{553}p.
\tag{6}
\]
Since \(Y_0<p/2\), if
\[
 \max_{t\in T_*}x_t\geq\frac{64}{553}p,
\tag{7}
\]
then condition 2 of accepted fact 2992eea9e109d58a holds with total mass \(Y_0\), and therefore
\[
 G\geq\frac{512}{553}p>\frac Q{100}.
\tag{8}
\]
Consequently, on the strict canonical hard branch \(G<Q/100\),
\[
 \boxed{x_t<\frac{64}{553}p\quad(t\in T_*),}
 \qquad
 \boxed{|T_*|\geq9.}
\tag{9}
\]

## Proof

Fix \(Y\) satisfying (3), and choose \(t_0\in T_*\) with
\[
 x_{t_0}\geq\frac Y4.
\tag{10}
\]
If \(x_{t_0}\geq Y\), take
\[
 E:=\{t_0\},
 \qquad
 y_{t_0}:=Y,
 \qquad
 m:=t_0.
\tag{11}
\]
Every proper relative closed halfspace of \(A\) containing \(m\) contains the sole atom of mass \(Y\). Moreover,
\[
 \Lambda_m(E)=\{(1)\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal Q_m(E)=\{q_{t_0}\}.
\tag{12}
\]
Thus \(m\) is an integral relative \(1/4\)-center and \(\rho_{D_0}(E,m)=0\). This proves (5) in this case.

Assume henceforth that
\[
 \frac Y4\leq x_{t_0}<Y.
\tag{13}
\]
Because \(T_*\) is finite, there is a relative affine line \(L\subset A\) through \(t_0\) which contains no other point of \(T_*\). Its two open relative halfplanes partition \(T_*\setminus\{t_0\}\). Let \(S\) be the side with larger selected load. Then
\[
 \sum_{t\in S}x_t\geq\frac{p-x_{t_0}}2.
\tag{14}
\]
The strict inequality \(2Y<p\) gives
\[
 \frac{p-x_{t_0}}2-(Y-x_{t_0})
 =\frac{p+x_{t_0}-2Y}{2}>0.
\tag{15}
\]
Thus the actual capacities on \(S\) exceed \(Y-x_{t_0}>0\). Since \(S\) is finite, one may choose a nonempty \(E_1\subseteq S\) and positive numbers
\[
 0<y_t\leq x_t\quad(t\in E_1),
 \qquad
 \sum_{t\in E_1}y_t=Y-x_{t_0}.
\tag{16}
\]
For example, list the points of \(S\), take their full capacities successively, and truncate the last positive capacity when the required total is reached.

Put
\[
 E:=\{t_0\}\cup E_1,
 \qquad
 y_{t_0}:=x_{t_0},
 \qquad
 m:=t_0.
\tag{17}
\]
Equations (16)-(17) give \(0<y_t\leq x_t\) and total mass \(Y\). Every proper relative closed halfspace of \(A\) containing \(m=t_0\) contains the atom at \(t_0\), hence has \(y\)-mass at least
\[
 y_{t_0}=x_{t_0}\geq\frac Y4.
\tag{18}
\]
Therefore \(m\) is an integral relative \(1/4\)-center.

Choose the sign of a nonzero relative affine functional \(\ell:A\to\mathbb R\) so that
\[
 L=\{\ell=0\},
 \qquad
 \ell(t)>0\quad(t\in E_1),
 \qquad
 \ell(t_0)=0.
\tag{19}
\]
If \(\lambda\in\Lambda_{t_0}(E)\), applying \(\ell\) to the barycentric identity gives
\[
 0=\ell(t_0)=\sum_{t\in E}\lambda_t\ell(t)
 =\sum_{t\in E_1}\lambda_t\ell(t).
\tag{20}
\]
All summands in the last expression are nonnegative and every \(\ell(t)\) there is positive. Hence \(\lambda_t=0\) for every \(t\in E_1\), and the probability normalization forces \(\lambda_{t_0}=1\). Consequently,
\[
 \Lambda_{t_0}(E)=\{\delta_{t_0}\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal Q_{t_0}(E)=\{q_{t_0}\}.
\tag{21}
\]
Choosing \(z=q_{t_0}\) in the Chebyshev-radius definition proves
\[
 \rho_{D_0}(E,t_0)=0.
\tag{22}
\]
This proves (4)-(5) in the remaining case.

For the specialization, (6) and \(512<553\) give \(Y_0<p/2\), while (7) is exactly \(\max_t x_t\geq Y_0/4\). The first part therefore supplies condition 2 of accepted fact 2992eea9e109d58a, with radius zero rather than merely at most one. Its accepted conclusion is (8). Thus (7) is impossible when \(G<Q/100\), proving the strict targetwise screen in (9). Finally,
\[
 p=\sum_{t\in T_*}x_t
 <|T_*|\frac{64}{553}p.
\tag{23}
\]
Since \(p>0\), equation (23) gives
\[
 |T_*|>\frac{553}{64}>8.
\tag{24}
\]
Integrality gives \(|T_*|\geq9\). This proves every assertion.
