## Statement

Lemma (the minimal tetrahedral quadratic-roof family is complete-core dominated). Let

\[
 P:=\left\{z\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^3:z_1+z_2+z_3\leq32\right\},
 \qquad
 E:=P\cap\mathbb Z^3,
\]

and define the six integral linear forms

\[
 L_1(z)=z_1,\quad L_2(z)=z_2,\quad L_3(z)=z_3,
 \quad L_4(z)=z_1+z_2,\quad L_5(z)=z_1+z_3,
 \quad L_6(z)=z_2+z_3.
\tag{1}
\]

On \([0,32]\), let

\[
 \psi(t):=\max_{0\leq k\leq31}
 \bigl((2k+1)t-k(k+1)\bigr).
\tag{2}
\]

For an integer \(M\geq2048\), define the rational polytope

\[
 C_M:=\left\{(z,x)\in P\times\mathbb R^6:
 \psi(L_j(z))-M\leq x_j\leq0
 \text{ for }1\leq j\leq6\right\}.
\tag{3}
\]

Then \(C_M\subseteq\mathbb R^3\times\mathbb R^6\) is a full-dimensional convex body. Its positive-volume integer-fiber support, exact fibers, volumes, and centroids are

\[
 \left\{z\in\mathbb Z^3:\operatorname{vol}_6((C_M)_z)>0\right\}=E,
 \qquad |E|=\binom{35}{3}=6545,
\tag{4}
\]

\[
 (C_M)_z=\prod_{j=1}^6[L_j(z)^2-M,0],
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_6((C_M)_z)
 =\prod_{j=1}^6\bigl(M-L_j(z)^2\bigr),
\tag{5}
\]

\[
 c_z=\frac12
 \bigl(L_1(z)^2-M,\ldots,L_6(z)^2-M\bigr)
 \qquad(z\in E).
\tag{6}
\]

For \(\xi\in\{0,1\}^3\), put

\[
 E_\xi:=\{z\in E:z\equiv\xi\pmod2\},
 \quad
 A_\xi:=\sum_{z\in E_\xi}a_z,
 \quad
 B_\xi:=\sum_{z\in E_\xi}a_z^2.
\tag{7}
\]

Also put

\[
 W(C_M):=\sum_{z\in E}a_z.
\]

The parity counts are

\[
 |E_\xi|=
 \begin{cases}
 969,&|\xi|=0,\\
 816,&|\xi|=1,\\
 816,&|\xi|=2,\\
 680,&|\xi|=3,
 \end{cases}
\tag{8}
\]

where \(|\xi|\) is the Hamming weight. Every parity class is good in the exact normalization of accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66. Consequently the actual half-strength canonical product-reservoir mass is exactly

\[
 \boxed{
 Q(C_M)=\frac18\sum_{\xi\in\{0,1\}^3}
 \left(A_\xi-\frac{B_\xi}{A_\xi}\right).}
\tag{9}
\]

In particular, this is a positive, completely determined rational number obtained from the actual fiber volumes in (5), and

\[
 0<Q(C_M)\leq\frac{W(C_M)}8
 \leq\frac{6545}{8}M^6.
\tag{10}
\]

The intrinsic continuous residual rank of the full centroid field is exactly six:

\[
 \boxed{
 \min_{s:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^6\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_z-s(z):z\in E\}=6.}
\tag{11}
\]

Nevertheless, the complete metronoid-core capacity in accepted facts ae0161dd5d4ea672 and 3d1f63abf09b2abb satisfies

\[
 \boxed{
 G(C_M)\geq6545(M-1024)^6
 \geq\frac{6545}{64}M^6
 >\frac{Q(C_M)}{100}.}
\tag{12}
\]

Thus \(C_M\) never satisfies the canonical hard inequality of accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66. The obstruction occurs at \(G\), before affine retention, selector, off-diagonal, reflected-cloud, mixed-depth, optimizer, or atlas calculations.

Moreover, \(32\Delta_3\) is the first tetrahedral dilation with enough positive integer fibers even to evade the elementary hard-branch support-cardinality screen: the preceding dilation has

\[
 \left|31\Delta_3\cap\mathbb Z^3\right|=\binom{34}{3}=5984\leq6400,
\]

whereas (4) gives \(6545>6400\).

## Proof

The function \(\psi\) in (2) is the convex piecewise-affine interpolation of \(t\mapsto t^2\) at the integers \(0,1,\ldots,32\). Indeed, on \([k,k+1]\) the active affine function is

\[
 (2k+1)t-k(k+1),
\]

which takes the values \(k^2\) and \((k+1)^2\) at the two endpoints. In particular,

\[
 \psi(q)=q^2\qquad(0\leq q\leq32,\ q\in\mathbb Z).
\tag{13}
\]

Every lower inequality in (3) is the epigraph inequality for a maximum of finitely many rational affine functions, and every upper inequality is affine. Hence \(C_M\) is a rational polytope. It is compact because its base projection is \(P\) and every continuous coordinate is bounded. For \(z\in P\), all six forms in (1) lie in \([0,32]\), so

\[
 M-\psi(L_j(z))\geq M-1024>0.
\tag{14}
\]

Thus every real base fiber is a six-dimensional box with nonempty interior, and an interior base point together with an interior point of its box proves that \(C_M\) has dimension nine. Equation (13) gives (5) at every integer base. Outside \(P\) the fiber is empty. This proves (4)-(6), including positivity of every displayed integer fiber. Stars and bars gives \(|E|=\binom{35}{3}\).

If \(z=2u+\xi\), then \(z\in E\) is equivalent to

\[
 u\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}^3,
 \qquad
 u_1+u_2+u_3
 \leq\left\lfloor\frac{32-|\xi|}{2}\right\rfloor.
\]

Another stars-and-bars count gives (8). Equations (5) and (14), together with \(M\geq2048\), give the uniform bounds

\[
 \frac{M^6}{64}\leq a_z\leq M^6.
\tag{15}
\]

Since every parity class has at least \(680\) elements,

\[
 A_\xi\geq\frac{680}{64}M^6>4M^6
 \geq4\max_{z\in E_\xi}a_z.
\tag{16}
\]

Thus every class is good, with room to spare. For one good class, summing the exact reservoir weights from accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 over unordered distinct pairs gives

\[
 \sum_{\{u,v\}\subseteq E_\xi}
 \frac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi}
 =\frac{A_\xi^2-B_\xi}{8A_\xi}
 =\frac18\left(A_\xi-\frac{B_\xi}{A_\xi}\right).
\tag{17}
\]

Summing (17) proves the exact canonical identity (9). It is positive because each good class contains more than one positive atom. Dropping \(B_\xi/A_\xi>0\), then using (15) and (4), proves (10).

We next prove the rank calibration. The six homogeneous quadratic polynomials

\[
 L_1^2,L_2^2,L_3^2,L_4^2,L_5^2,L_6^2
\]

span all homogeneous quadratic polynomials in three variables: the first three are \(z_1^2,z_2^2,z_3^2\), and subtracting them from the last three recovers \(2z_1z_2,2z_1z_3,2z_2z_3\). The ten functions

\[
 1,z_1,z_2,z_3,L_1^2,\ldots,L_6^2
\tag{18}
\]

are linearly independent on \(E\). To check this without a genericity assertion, let a polynomial of degree at most two in their span vanish on \(E\). Evaluation at \(0\), at \(e_i\) and \(2e_i\) for \(1\leq i\leq3\), and at \(e_i+e_j\) for \(1\leq i<j\leq3\) successively makes its constant, linear, pure-quadratic, and mixed-quadratic coefficients zero. All these ten test points lie in \(E\).

It follows from (6) and (18) that the graph points \((z,c_z)\), \(z\in E\), have affine hull of dimension nine. If an affine map \(s:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^6\) made all residuals lie in a linear space of dimension \(r\), then all graph points would lie in an affine space of dimension at most \(3+r\). Hence \(9\leq3+r\), so \(r\geq6\). The reverse inequality is automatic because the residuals lie in \(\mathbb R^6\). This proves (11).

It remains to perform the fail-fast complete-core test. Put

\[
 s_0:=M-1024,
 \qquad
 K_0:=[-s_0,0]^6,
 \qquad
 t_0:=s_0^6,
 \qquad
 q_0:=(-s_0/2,\ldots,-s_0/2).
\tag{19}
\]

Equation (14) gives

\[
 K_0\subseteq(C_M)_z\qquad(z\in E).
\tag{20}
\]

The indicator of \(K_0\), viewed as a density in \((C_M)_z\), has mass \(t_0\) and barycenter \(q_0\). Therefore

\[
 q_0\in\mathsf M_{t_0}((C_M)_z)
 \qquad(z\in E).
\tag{21}
\]

Apply accepted fact 6d6fcf377e68c79c with continuous dimension zero to the counting measure on \(E\subseteq\mathbb Z^3\). It gives an \(m\in\mathbb Z^3\) such that every proper closed base halfspace containing \(m\) contains at least \(|E|/8\) points of \(E\). Strict separation shows \(m\in\operatorname{conv}E\). For every barycentric representation \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)\), equation (21) gives

\[
 q_0=\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_zq_0
 \in\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z
 \mathsf M_{t_0}((C_M)_z).
\tag{22}
\]

Thus \(q_0\) belongs to the complete intersection over all such representations. The profile \(E\), with the common selected mass \(t_0\) on every fiber, is admissible in the exact complete-core definition of accepted fact ae0161dd5d4ea672. Hence

\[
 G(C_M)\geq|E|t_0=6545(M-1024)^6.
\tag{23}
\]

Since \(M\geq2048\), one has \(M-1024\geq M/2\). Combining (23) with (10) gives

\[
 G(C_M)
 \geq\frac{6545}{64}M^6
 >\frac{6545}{800}M^6
 \geq\frac{Q(C_M)}{100},
\]

which proves (12). Since \(G\) is already one of the nonnegative summands in the hard ledger of accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66, this is the first terminal failure. No later capacity, optimizer, or atlas may be used to present this family as a hard-body counterexample.

Finally, we justify the minimal-support calibration. Under the canonical hard inequality, accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 gives

\[
 G<\frac{Q}{100}\leq\frac{W}{800}<\frac W2.
\tag{24}
\]

Apply the singleton case of the complete-core augmentation in accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb. For \(n=3\), equation (24) forces

\[
 a_z\leq\frac G8
 \qquad(z\in E).
\tag{25}
\]

If there are \(N\) positive-volume integer fibers, then (24)-(25) give

\[
 800G<W=\sum_za_z\leq\frac N8G,
\]

and therefore \(N>6400\). The lattice-point counts

\[
 \binom{34}{3}=5984,
 \qquad
 \binom{35}{3}=6545
\]

prove the last assertion. This completes the exclusion theorem.
