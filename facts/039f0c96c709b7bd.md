## Statement

Let $D$ be a nonempty compact convex planar set with horizontal projection $I=[a,b]$, and let $E=\{0\}\times[e_-,e_+]$ with $e_-\le e_+$. Let $A(t)$ be the area of $D+tE$ intersected with finitely many halfplanes $p_ix+q_iy\ge h_i+k_it$, each with nonzero normal. For $x\in I$, take the lower section bound $L_t(x)$ as the maximum of the lower endpoint of $D+tE$ and all lower bounds from $q_i>0$, and $U_t(x)$ as the corresponding minimum for the upper endpoint and $q_i<0$. At $t=0$ let $\dot L$ be the maximum velocity of the active lower bounds, and $\dot U$ the minimum velocity of the active upper bounds. Write $g_0=(U_0-L_0)_+$. The function $\dot g$ is $\dot U-\dot L$ where $U_0>L_0$, zero where $U_0<L_0$, and $(\dot U-\dot L)_+$ at equality.

The vertical cuts and the two bounds $a,b$ give a feasible horizontal interval with lower endpoint $a_t$ the maximum of the affine lower bounds and upper endpoint $b_t$ the minimum of the affine upper bounds. Compute $\dot a,\dot b$ by maximum and minimum of the corresponding tied endpoint velocities at zero. Then
$$
A'(0+)=
\begin{cases}
0,& a_0>b_0,\\
(\dot b-\dot a)_+g_0(a_0),&a_0=b_0,\\
\displaystyle\int_{a_0}^{b_0}\dot g(x)\,dx+\dot b\,g_0(b_0)-\dot a\,g_0(a_0),&a_0<b_0.
\end{cases}
$$
If $I$ is a singleton the area is identically zero.

## Proof

Write the old section as $[\ell(x),u(x)]$. It is a closed interval at every $x\in I$. The lower function is convex and lower semicontinuous and the upper one concave and upper semicontinuous. Both are continuous on $I$ when $a<b$: at each endpoint, interpolate its section extremum with a fixed interior section point to get the opposite inequality to semicontinuity. Extrusion changes the bounds to $\ell(x)+t e_-$ and $u(x)+t e_+$. Each nonvertical cut contributes the affine-in-$t$ bound $(h_i-p_ix)/q_i+t k_i/q_i$, lower if $q_i>0$ and upper otherwise. Each vertical cut contributes the horizontal bound $h_i/p_i+t k_i/p_i$, lower if $p_i>0$ and upper otherwise. Thus Fubini gives $\int_{a_t}^{b_t}(U_t-L_t)_+\,dx$ for a nonempty interval and zero otherwise.

For any finite maximum of affine functions of $t$, its right derivative is the largest slope among the active terms at zero. The analogous minimum derivative is the smallest slope. Applying these rules and the positive-part derivative gives the stated $\dot g$. These bounds are uniformly Lipschitz in $t$ independently of $x$, so dominated convergence gives the fixed-interval derivative. The endpoints $a_t,b_t$ are exactly affine for all sufficiently small $t\ge0$ because the lists are finite. A strictly negative gap stays negative. At a coincident endpoint, the interval born has length $t(\dot b-\dot a)_+$ and its integrand converges uniformly there to $g_0(a_0)$ by continuity. For an old positive length, replacing $g_t$ by $g_0$ in the moving endpoint strips costs $O(t^2)$, and continuity gives the two displayed endpoint terms with their indicated signs. A singleton $I$ stays a singleton under vertical extrusion.

For a single lower cut $y\ge\alpha(x)+t v$, the derivative of section length is $e_+-v$ at a proper crossing, $e_+-e_-$ for strict full capture, $e_+-\max(e_-,v)$ when the cut coincides with the lower endpoint of a positive-length section, and $(e_+-v)_+$ at the corresponding upper tie. At $\ell=u=\alpha$ the value is $(e_+-\max(e_-,v))_+$. For the unit square with $E=\{0\}\times[-1,1]$, the cuts $y\ge-2t$ and $y\ge1+2t$ have derivatives $2$ and $0$, respectively. With no cuts the total area derivative is $(b-a)(e_+-e_-)$. For finitely many such areas $A_i(t)$, the right derivative of $\sum_i A_i-c\min_i A_i$ is $\sum_i A_i'-c\min_{i:A_i(0)=\min_j A_j(0)}A_i'$, since the positive gaps exclude inactive terms and the finitely many first-order errors are uniformly $o(t)$.
