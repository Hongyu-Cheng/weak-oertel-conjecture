## Proof

Accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 gives the strict hard ledger in (9). In the zero-fresh branch it also gives \(C_\beta=B_{\mathrm{cong}}\), while its affine-retention bound gives \(R\geq C_\beta\). Hence \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}\leq R\). In particular,

\[
R+G<\frac Q{100},
\qquad
R<\frac Q{100},
\qquad
G<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{47}
\]

Because \(s_*\) is the weighted-distance primal optimizer paired with the load-minimal dual in accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, complementary saturation applies:

\[
e\in\mathscr M_*\Longrightarrow\omega_e=r_e.
\tag{48}
\]

The affine-retention definition (4) bounds the \(r\)-mass hit by this particular affine map \(s_*\), whether or not it maximizes (4):

\[
\sum_{e:s_*(m_e)\in L_e}r_e\leq R.
\]

Therefore its missed reservoir mass satisfies only the inequality that is needed below,

\[
\sum_{e\in\mathscr M_*}r_e
=Q-\sum_{e:s_*(m_e)\in L_e}r_e
\geq Q-R.
\tag{49}
\]

By (48),

\[
\sum_{e\in\mathscr M_*}(1-\chi_{m_e})r_e
=\sum_{e\in\mathscr M_*}(1-\chi_{m_e})\omega_e
\leq B_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{50}
\]

Equations (11), (49), and (50) give

\[
T\geq Q-R-B_{\mathrm{cong}}>\frac{49}{50}Q.
\tag{51}
\]

This proves (13) and shows that every positive \(\alpha_e\) has positive original load.

For a fixed target \(m\), (6), (11), and (48) give

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{\alpha_e}{\vartheta_e}
&=\chi_m\sum_{\substack{e\in\mathscr M_*\\m_e=m}}
\frac{r_e}{\vartheta_e}\\
&=\chi_m\sum_{\substack{e\in\mathscr M_*\\m_e=m}}
\frac{\omega_e}{\vartheta_e}
\leq\chi_m\kappa_m^\omega\leq1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{52}
\]

This proves (14). Independently choose arbitrary \(y_e\in L_e\) over \(m\). Accepted fact fc18c3c4832f0bfb gives \(y_e\in\mathsf M_{\vartheta_e}(C_m)\). If \(f_e\) is a representing density, then

\[
g_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{\alpha_e}{\vartheta_e}f_e
\tag{53}
\]

is pointwise at most one by (52), has mass \(t_m\), and has normalized first moment \(t_m^{-1}\sum_e\alpha_ey_e\). The choices \(y_e\) were independent, so every point of \(\mathcal Z_m\) lies in \(\mathsf M_{t_m}(C_m)\). This proves (17).

For a positive \(\alpha_e\), both endpoints have positive original load, so (10) is available. Averaging its endpoint inclusions gives

\[
F_e^\flat+\frac12D_e\subseteq L_e.
\tag{54}
\]

Also \(D_*\subseteq D_u\cap D_v\subseteq D_e\). Choosing the same \(h\in D_*/2\) on all edges over \(m\) and averaging with weights \(\alpha_e/t_m\) proves (19).

We prove (21) without a spurious \(\gamma_d\). Since there are finitely many targets, only finitely many hit subsets can occur, so the maximum in (20) is attained. Fix an affine map \(a\), and put \(J_a:=\{m:a(m)\in\mathcal Z_m\}\). By (17), \(a(m)\in\mathsf M_{t_m}(C_m)\) on \(J_a\). Apply accepted fact 10f1feebe06e22d0 with \(L=\mathbb R^n\), \(E=J_a\), masses \(t_m\), and selector \(a\). It gives

\[
G\geq\sum_{m\in J_a}t_m
\tag{55}
\]

when \(J_a\ne\varnothing\); the empty case is trivial. Maximizing proves \(R_{\mathcal Z}\leq G\).

We establish the target duality. Let \(\mathcal M:=\{m:t_m>0\}\),

\[
V:=\{(a(m))_{m\in\mathcal M}:a\text{ affine}\},
\qquad
K:=\prod_{m\in\mathcal M}\mathcal Z_m,
\]

and equip \(Y:=(\mathbb R^d)^{\mathcal M}\) with

\[
N((q_m)_m):=\sum_{m\in\mathcal M}t_m\|q_m\|_{E_m}.
\tag{56}
\]

Every \(E_m\) is an origin-symmetric full-dimensional convex body. Coordinatewise nearest-point minimization gives

\[
\Delta_{\mathcal Z}
:=\min_{a\ \mathrm{affine}}
\sum_m t_m\operatorname{dist}_{E_m}(a(m),\mathcal Z_m)
=\min_{v\in V,\ z\in K}N(v-z).
\tag{57}
\]

The dual norm is

\[
N^*((Y_m)_m)=\max_m\frac{h_{E_m}(Y_m)}{t_m}.
\tag{58}
\]

Finite-dimensional separation of \(V-K\) from the largest disjoint open \(N\)-ball gives

\[
\Delta_{\mathcal Z}
=\max\left\{
-\sum_mh_{\mathcal Z_m}(Y_m):
(Y_m)_m\in V^\perp,\
h_{E_m}(Y_m)\leq t_m\ \forall m
\right\}.
\tag{59}
\]

Weak duality follows by pairing with \(v-z\); a norm-one separator at the attained distance gives equality. The condition \(Y\in V^\perp\) is exactly (23), and the feasible dual set is compact.

By (13), (21), and (47), \(R_{\mathcal Z}<T\), so \(\Delta_{\mathcal Z}>0\). Choose primal and dual optimizers. For one coordinate,

\[
t_m\operatorname{dist}_{E_m}(y,\mathcal Z_m)
=\max_{h_{E_m}(Y)\leq t_m}
\bigl(\langle Y,y\rangle-h_{\mathcal Z_m}(Y)\bigr).
\tag{60}
\]

Primal-dual equality and nonnegativity of the coordinate Fenchel gaps show that \(Y_m\) attains (60) at the primal value \(a_*(m)\). If \(a_*(m)\notin\mathcal Z_m\), the value is positive, and positive homogeneity forces

\[
h_{E_m}(Y_m)=t_m.
\tag{61}
\]

Therefore

\[
\Lambda\geq\sum_{m:a_*(m)\notin\mathcal Z_m}t_m
\geq T-R_{\mathcal Z}.
\tag{62}
\]

Equations (51) and (21) prove the second inequality in (25). Moreover,

\[
R+B_{\mathrm{cong}}+G
\leq2R+G=(R+G)+R<\frac{2Q}{100},
\]

which proves its strict last inequality.

Choose each \(\ell_e\) to expose \(L_e\) in direction \(Y_m\). Minkowski additivity gives (26). Scaling a representing density of \(z_m\in\mathsf M_{t_m}(C_m)\) by \(\lambda_m/t_m\) gives \(z_m\in\mathsf M_{\lambda_m}(C_m)\).

Equations (27) and (22) give \(\sum_{e:m_e=m}b_e=\lambda_m\). They prove (28)-(31), including

\[
|r-b|=Q-\Lambda<\frac Q{50}.

\]

For (34), use \(d_z(b)\leq a_z/4\). Choose a half-metronoid density for every \(x_{e,z}^{\mathrm{anc}}\), multiply it by \(4b_e/a_z\), and sum over incidences at \(z\). Since \(b\leq r\), one has \(R_C(b)\leq R\). Applying accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d to \(b\) gives

which proves (37). The inequality \(\omega_e^b\leq b_e\), together with (30), proves (38).

c_z+\lambda_*D_z\subseteq\mathsf M_{a_z/4}(C_z).
\tag{65}
\]

The positive-root convexity of accepted fact dfd25e72c1f45a4e then gives

L_e^+
=\frac12\bigl((c_u+\lambda_*D_u)+(c_v+\lambda_*D_v)\bigr)
\subseteq\mathsf M_{\vartheta_e/2}(C_{m_e}).
\tag{66}
\]

Fix an affine \(s\), and let

At a physical target \(m\), use for each hit edge a density of mass \(\vartheta_e/2\) and barycenter \(s(m)\), multiplied by \(\omega_e^b/\vartheta_e\). Equation (38) makes the sum pointwise at most one, so

Taking the infimum over \(s\) proves

\Delta_C(\omega^b)\geq\delta_*(\Omega_b-2G).

Keep the unrounded bounds

\qquad
