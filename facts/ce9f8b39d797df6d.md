## Statement

Let $K_t=\operatorname{conv}\{(7-17t,-6+15t),(1,-5),(7,-6),(2,4)\}$. For every $t>29/70$, the listed polygon is strictly convex in the listed cyclic order with the origin in its interior. Set $v_1=(1,0),v_2=(0,1),v_3=(-1,-1)$, $k=|K_t|$, $Q_i=|K_t\cap\{v_i\cdot x\ge0\}|$, and $\sigma_i=\mathcal H^1(K_t\cap\{v_i\cdot x=0\})/\lVert v_i\rVert$. Define $f_i=2\sqrt{kQ_i}/(\sigma_i\sum_jQ_j/\sigma_j)$ and let $J$ be the largest eigenvalue of the zero-diagonal symmetric matrix with cyclic entries $f_i/2$. Then throughout $t>29/70$,
$$J<1,\qquad \sum_i f_i^2+f_1f_2f_3<4.$$
The stated parameter range is the whole strict convex, interior-origin domain in the listed order.

## Proof

The consecutive turn determinants are $73t,55,95t,168t-55$, and the edge-to-origin determinants are $70t-29,29,40,98t-40$. With four vertices, positivity of the turns puts both other vertices strictly to the left of each edge, giving the strict convex counterclockwise polygon. A clockwise cyclic order is impossible because one turn is $55$. Positivity of the edge-to-origin determinants is then exactly the interior condition. These inequalities reduce to $t>29/70$. The moving vertex has first margin negative and second margin positive, and its third margin is $2t-1$. All other margins are nonzero and constant, so the sole seam is $t=1/2$.

Using the edge intersections from the level test and the cyclic shoelace formula gives $k=84t$ and
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_1&=\frac{24463t^2-16282t+2695}{2(17t-6)(17t-5)},&
\sigma_1&=\frac{(17t-7)(168t-55)}{(17t-6)(17t-5)},\\
Q_2&=\frac{2520t^2-1313t+198}{2(15t-1)},&
\sigma_2&=\frac{130t-33}{15t-1}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For $29/70<t\le1/2$, $Q_3=584t/[5(5-2t)]$ and $\sigma_3=292t/[5(5-2t)]$. For $t\ge1/2$, $Q_3=(1680t^2+802t+55)/[10(2t+5)]$ and $\sigma_3=(548t-55)/[5(2t+5)]$. The two branches agree at the seam. To obtain the chords from the intersection pairs, take the absolute second-coordinate difference for $v_1$ and first-coordinate difference for $v_2,v_3$. For $v_3$ the Euclidean length contributes the same $\sqrt2$ factor as the normal norm.

Set $E=E_1$, $R=R_1$ from the spectral level test. The lower-branch reduced denominators, up to positive constants, are $(17t-7)(130t-33)^2(168t-55)$ and $(17t-7)^4(130t-33)^6(168t-55)^4$ for $E,R$ respectively. The upper-branch denominators multiply these by $(548t-55)^2$ and $(548t-55)^6$ respectively. Every factor is positive in its branch. The lower-branch numerator degrees are $5,18$. Substitute $t=(29/70+s/2)/(1+s)$ and multiply by $(1+s)$ to the respective degree. For the upper branch, remove the positive factors $t,t^3$ from the two numerators of remaining degrees $7,23$, then substitute $t=(1+s)/2$. In each case clear rational coefficient denominators with a positive multiplier and divide by the positive integer gcd. The resulting ascending coefficient vectors are as follows.
