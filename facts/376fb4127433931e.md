## Statement

Let \(d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), and let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^2\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body. For every integer base \(z\in\mathbb Z^2\), put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^2:a_z>0\},
\tag{1}
\]
and assume
\[
 W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z>0.
\tag{2}
\]
Let \(G=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)\) be the global complete metronoid-core capacity in the normalization of accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c.

For every parity class \(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^2\), define
\[
 A_\xi:=\sum_{\substack{z\in I_+\\z\equiv\xi\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 2)}}a_z,
 \qquad
 M_\xi:=
 \max_{\substack{z\in I_+\\z\equiv\xi\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 2)}}a_z,
\tag{3}
\]
where \(M_\xi:=0\) if \(A_\xi=0\). Call \(\xi\) good when
\[
 A_\xi>0,
 \qquad
 M_\xi\le\frac{A_\xi}{4}.
\tag{4}
\]
Define the actual half-strength canonical product reservoir of accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 by
\[
 r_{\{u,v\}}:=
 \begin{cases}
 \dfrac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi},
 &u\ne v,\quad u\equiv v\equiv\xi,\quad \xi\text{ good},\\[2mm]
 0,&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 Q:=\sum_{\{u,v\}}r_{\{u,v\}}.
\tag{5}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 G\ge\frac W{64}\ge\frac Q8.}
\tag{6}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{G\ge\frac Q{100},}
\tag{7}
\]
with strict inequality in (7) whenever \(Q>0\).

## Proof

Compactness of the base projection of \(C\) makes \(I_+\) finite, so every quantity in (1)-(5) is finite and is computed from the complete actual positive-fiber profile.

Apply accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c with ambient base dimension \(n=2\) and affine subspace
\[
 A:=\mathbb R^2.
\tag{8}
\]
In the notation of that fact,
\[
 D_A=I_+,
 \qquad
 B=\operatorname{aff}(I_+),
 \qquad
 q:=\dim B\in\{0,1,2\},
 \qquad
 W_A=W.
\tag{9}
\]
Its support-free global conclusion gives
\[
 G\ge\frac1{64}
 \min\{W,2^{2-q}W_A\}.
\tag{10}
\]
Since \(W_A=W\) and \(2^{2-q}\ge1\), equation (10) yields
\[
 G\ge\frac W{64}.
\tag{11}
\]
This argument includes support ranks zero and one as well as full planar support, and it uses no no-rank-drop hypothesis.

It remains to compare the reservoir in (5) with \(W\). Fix a good parity class \(\xi\). Its reservoir contribution is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_\xi
 &:=\sum_{\substack{\{u,v\}:u\ne v\\u\equiv v\equiv\xi}}
 \frac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi}\\
 &=\frac1{8A_\xi}
 \left(A_\xi^2-
 \sum_{\substack{z\in I_+\\z\equiv\xi\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 2)}}a_z^2\right)\\
 &\le\frac{A_\xi}{8}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
Bad and empty parity classes contribute zero. Since the parity classes are disjoint,
\[
 Q=\sum_{\xi\ \mathrm{good}}Q_\xi
 \le\frac18\sum_{\xi\ \mathrm{good}}A_\xi
 \le\frac W8.
\tag{13}
\]
Combining (11) and (13) proves
\[
 G\ge\frac W{64}\ge\frac Q8,
\tag{14}
\]
which is (6). If \(Q>0\), then \(Q/8>Q/100\), so (7) is strict. If \(Q=0\), equation (7) follows from \(G\ge0\).

The estimate uses the actual fiber volumes \(a_z\) throughout. Any positive integer fiber created by convex completion is automatically included in \(I_+\), \(W\), the parity masses \(A_\xi\), and the product reservoir \(Q\). A load-minimal optimizer, synchronized thinning, target aggregation, and greedy rank-raising order are downstream of this actual reservoir and cannot alter (14). Hence the complete-core terminal occurs before any such choices are required.
