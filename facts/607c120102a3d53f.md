## Statement

Fix an integer $d\ge2$ and $0<a<1$. Let $H=(1-a)/a$, $r(z)=(H-z)/(H+1)$ and consider the cone $-1\le z\le H$, $|x|\le r(z)$, with planar density $f(x,z)=r(z)^{d-2}/2$. For $s\ge0$, let $P(s)$ be the mass of $z+sx\ge0$, and $C(s),M(s)$ the section integrals of $f,zf$, respectively, with respect to $d\mathcal H^1/\sqrt{1+s^2}$. Put $V_0=(H+1)/d$, $C_0=(1-a)^{d-1}$ and $p=(1-a)^d$. For $s>0$ define
$$
e_s=\frac1{2s}\int_{-s}^{\min(s,1)}(1-ab)^{-d}\,db,\qquad
m_s=\partial_a e_s,\qquad k_s=\frac{(s-1)_+}{2s},\qquad v_s=1-k_s.
$$
At $s=0$ set $(e_0,m_0,k_0)=(1,0,0)$. Thus $e_s=C(s)/C_0$ and $M(s)/C_0=-(1-a)m_s/d$. A centered law is a probability law with average section moment zero.

Suppose $s_*>1$ is individually centered. Put $e_*=e_{s_*}$, $v_*=v_{s_*}=(s_*+1)/(2s_*)$ and $t_*=p/(1+as_*)^d$. Then
$$
2p\le1+t_*,\qquad 2v_*-1=2pe_*-t_*.
$$
For $0\le t\le t_*$ set $\kappa=(v_*+t)/(1+e_*)$, $B=1+t-\kappa$ and $\gamma=(\kappa-t)/(1-t)$. Then $4/9\le\gamma\le1/2$, $\gamma$ is nondecreasing in $t$, and $B\ge\max(1/2,\kappa)$. The proper frustum with cutoff $T\ge s_*$ has $t=p/(1+aT)^d$ in this range.

## Proof

At the cutoff $T=s_*$ the endpoint is $U_*=Hs_*/(H+1+s_*)$ and the section at slope $s_*$ spans $[-1,U_*]$. Indeed $U_*/r(U_*)=s_*>1$, and $-z/r(z)$ decreases from $1$ to $-s_*$. By f4be2679c83c2fd1, the frustum moment agrees with the cone moment at this slope, so $\int_{-1}^{U_*}zr(z)^{d-2}\,dz=0$. The section density in $z$ is proportional to $r^{d-2}$, which is positive, continuous and nonincreasing. Apply the decreasing-density median and tilt lemma, multiplying by $r$, to see that the $z\ge0$ share of the actual axial volume density $r^{d-1}$ is at most one half. The horizontal cone cap is $pV_0$ by 18e7bdf54588779d, and truncation subtracts $t_*V_0$ by f4be2679c83c2fd1. Thus $(p-t_*)/(1-t_*)\le1/2$, which gives $2p\le1+t_*$.

At the full section, f4be2679c83c2fd1 also gives $P_f=(1-t_*)V_0/2+M_f=(1-t_*)V_0/2$. The cone record and the same subtraction give $P_f/V_0=pe_*+1-v_*-t_*$. Comparing yields the second identity.

The centered Dirac law at $s_*$ satisfies $2v_*-1\le e_*\le(2v_*-1)/v_*\le v_*$ by 18e7bdf54588779d. Thus
$$
\gamma(t)-\gamma(0)=\frac{t(v_*-e_*)}{(1-t)(1+e_*)}\ge0,\qquad
\gamma(0)\ge \frac{v_*^2}{3v_*-1}\ge\frac49.
$$
The first difference is nondecreasing in $t$, and the last inequality follows from $(3v_*-2)^2\ge0$. At $t=t_*$ the numerator of $2\gamma-1$ after multiplication by the positive denominator is $2v_*-1-e_*+t_*(1-e_*)=e_*(2p-1-t_*)\le0$. This gives the upper bound for all $t$ by monotonicity, and $B-\kappa=(1-t)(1-2\gamma)\ge0$. Finally $B=1-v_*+\kappa e_*$ is increasing in $\kappa$. At $\kappa=v_*/(1+e_*)$ it is $1-v_*/(1+e_*)\ge1/2$ by the lower energy bound, hence $B\ge1/2$ throughout.
