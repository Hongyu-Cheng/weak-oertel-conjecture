## Statement

Let $B=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(\sqrt2,0),(0,\sqrt2)\}$, $A=(\sqrt2/10)B$, and $C$ the hull of $A,B,A$ at bases $-1,0,1$. Put $\ell(x)=1-(x_1+x_2)/\sqrt2$ and let $\mu$ be actual mixed area on $C$. Define $V(t)=\inf_{v\in\mathbb R}\mu\{\ell(x)+vz\ge t\}$. Writing $B(t)=|B\cap\{\ell\ge t\}|=\min(1,(1-t)_+^2)$, one has $V(t)=B(t)+\min(1/50,2(1-t)_+^2)$ for all real $t$, with an attaining finite tilt and $V(-\infty)=51/50$.

The probability survival $V(t)/(51/50)$ is the $\ell$-pushforward of $(\mathbf1_B+2\mathbf1_E)\,dx/(51/50)$ for $E=B\cap\{\ell\ge9/10\}$. At its mean $88/255$ the closed upper tail is $291895/663255=4/9-577/132651$. The original mixed area is $26/25$.

## Proof

The triangle $B$ has area one, while $A$ has area $a=1/50$ and is contained in $B$. The endpoint heights fix their fibers. Any hull combination at zero uses equal coefficients at the endpoints and their average is $A\subset B$, so the middle fiber is exactly $B$. The lifted hull is compact and full-dimensional with exactly these three integer sites. Its total mixed area is $1+2a=26/25$.

For $t\in[0,1]$, homothety about the origin gives $B(t)=(1-t)^2$. It equals one for $t\le0$ and zero for $t\ge1$. The smaller similarly oriented $A$ gives cap mass $\min(a,(1-t)_+^2)$ for every real threshold $t$. The two endpoint thresholds are $t+v,t-v$. If either endpoint cap is full, their sum is at least $a$. Otherwise convexity of $h\mapsto h_+^2$ gives a sum at least $2(1-t)_+^2$. If $2(1-t)_+^2\le a$, $v=0$ attains that value. If $2(1-t)_+^2>a$, some finite $v$ puts one cap in its full plateau and the other in its empty plateau, attaining $a$. These arguments include equality thresholds because a closed boundary line has zero area. Adding the fixed middle cap proves the optimization formula and the left plateau $1+a$.

The area of $E$ is $1/100=a/2$. Its doubled $\ell$-tail is $a$ below $9/10$ and $2(1-t)_+^2$ above, exactly the optimized endpoint contribution. The mean of $\ell$ on $B$ is the average of its three vertex values, $1/3$. The cap $E$ is a scaled copy of $B$ with scale $1/10$ about the vertex where $\ell=1$, so its mean is $1-(1/10)(1-1/3)=14/15$. The normalized combined mean is $[1/3+(1/50)(14/15)]/(51/50)=88/255$. This is between zero and $9/10$, so its upper tail is $[(167/255)^2+1/50]/(51/50)=291895/663255$. Subtracting from $4/9$ gives the positive rational $577/132651$.
