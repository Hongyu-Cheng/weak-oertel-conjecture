## Statement

Let \(T\subset\mathbb R^d\) be a compact convex body and let
\(\ell_i:T\to[0,\infty)\) be bounded upper-semicontinuous concave functions. Define
\[
C=\{(\mathbf x,\mathbf y):\mathbf y\in T,\ 0\le x_i\le1+\ell_i(\mathbf y)\},
\quad S=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d),
\]
\[
m_i=\lfloor\ell_i\rfloor,\quad A=\prod_i(m_i+2),\quad
B=\prod_i(m_i+1),\quad W=\int_TA.
\]
Here \(A=A_{\mathrm{asym}}\) and \(B=B_{\mathrm{asym}}\).
Use actual mixed depth \(h_S\) and continuous density depth \(\tau_B\), with closed halfspaces.

If \(0\le\ell_i\le2\), then for every \(\mathbf y_0\in T\),
\[
h_S((\mathbf1,\mathbf y_0))\ge\tfrac12\tau_B(\mathbf y_0).
\]
If \(0\le\ell_i\le3\), there is a point with integer coordinate \(\mathbf1\) satisfying
\[
h_S(\mathbf p_*)\ge
\frac{\kappa_{6/7}}2\,2^{-n}W,
\qquad
\kappa_\alpha=(3^{1-\alpha}-2^{1-\alpha})^{1/\alpha}.
\]

## Proof

The number of sites in \(\{0,\ldots,m+1\}\) surviving reflection about \(1\) is
\(r(0)=1\) and \(r(m)=3\) for \(m=1,2,3\).
Put \(R=\prod_ir(m_i)\).

For every ambient halfspace through \((\mathbf1,\mathbf y_0)\), restrict to its favorable base halfspace. At least half the reflected sites in each selected section belong to the ambient halfspace. Therefore
\[
h_S((\mathbf1,\mathbf y_0))\ge\tfrac12\tau_R(\mathbf y_0). \tag{1}
\]
For \(m_i\le2\), \(r(m_i)\ge m_i+1\), so \(R\ge B\), proving the first assertion.

Now suppose \(m_i\le3\). Put \(A_0=2^{-n}A\), let \(\mu\) be uniform probability on \(T\), and set \(d\nu/d\mu=A_0/\mathbb E_\mu A_0\). This is the same probability tilt as the full-count tilt in accepted fact d279c7ab11895287.

Its scale-three entropy inequality gives
\[
D(\nu\|\mu)\le\mathbb E_\nu\sum_ic(m_i),
\]
where
\[
c(0)=0,\quad c(1)=\log2,\quad
c(2)=\tfrac98\log3,\quad c(3)=\tfrac9{10}\log3.
\]
For all four values,
\[
c(m)\le6\log\frac{2r(m)}{m+2}.
\]
The last case follows from \((6/5)^{20}>32>27\); the other three are immediate scalar comparisons. Hence
\[
D(\nu\|\mu)\le6\mathbb E_\nu\log(R/A_0).
\]

Set \(\alpha=6/7\). Jensen under \(\nu\) gives
\[
\log\mathbb E_\mu R^\alpha
\ge
\alpha\log\mathbb E_\mu A_0
+\alpha\mathbb E_\nu\log(R/A_0)
-(1-\alpha)D(\nu\|\mu)
\ge\alpha\log\mathbb E_\mu A_0.
\]
Thus \((\mathbb E_\mu R^{6/7})^{7/6}\ge\mathbb E_\mu A_0\).
Apply accepted fact 9ee12600178ce4dd with density \(R\) and uniform reference on \(T\), then use (1). This gives
\[
h_S(\mathbf p_*)\ge
(\kappa_{6/7}/2)\int_TA_0
=(\kappa_{6/7}/2)2^{-n}W.
\]
