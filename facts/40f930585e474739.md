## Statement

Let $d\ge1$ and let $f,p$ be positive twice continuously differentiable functions on an open interval. Put $W=f^d+p^d$. Then
$$
(W^{1/d})''=W^{1/d-2}\left[
 W(f^{d-1}f''+p^{d-1}p'')
 +(d-1)f^{d-2}p^{d-2}(fp'-pf')^2
\right].
$$
In particular, suppose $d=2$ and on a proper-trace interval the actual middle and optimized endpoint cap volumes in one fixed continuous direction are $\alpha(s_0-s)^2$ and $\beta(s_1-s)^2$, with $\alpha,\beta>0$ and $s<\min(s_0,s_1)$. Put $A=\alpha+\beta$, $\bar s=(\alpha s_0+\beta s_1)/A$, and $E=(\alpha\beta/A)(s_0-s_1)^2$. The sum is $A(\bar s-s)^2+E$. Its inverse on this interval is $s_B=\bar s-\sqrt{(B-E)/A}$. If $s_0\ne s_1$, then $E>0$ and $R\mapsto s_{R^2}$ is strictly convex on the corresponding branch.

## Proof

Write $S=f^{d-1}f'+p^{d-1}p'$ and $T=f^{d-2}(f')^2+p^{d-2}(p')^2$. Then $W'=dS$ and $W''=d(f^{d-1}f''+p^{d-1}p'')+d(d-1)T$. The power rule reduces the second derivative to $W^{1/d-2}[W(f^{d-1}f''+p^{d-1}p'')+(d-1)(WT-S^2)]$. Expansion gives $WT-S^2=f^{d-2}p^{d-2}(fp'-pf')^2$.

For the planar branch expand the quadratic sum and complete the square. The assumption $s<\min(s_0,s_1)$ forces $s<\bar s$, which selects the negative square-root branch and ensures $B>E$ on its range. Differentiating $s_{R^2}=\bar s-\sqrt{R^2-E}/\sqrt A$ twice gives
$$
\frac{d^2}{dR^2}s_{R^2}=\frac{E}{\sqrt A\,(R^2-E)^{3/2}}.
$$
Thus the positive reserve and strict inverse curvature occur exactly when the two component roots vanish at different affine continuation points. When $E=0$ the inverse is affine in $R>0$.
