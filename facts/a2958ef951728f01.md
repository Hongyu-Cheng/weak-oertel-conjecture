## Statement

In the setting of the exact exponential base-moment correction, assume the old fibers are polytopes and take its solutions $s_t=\delta_t e^{h_t}$, $v_t$ with $(h_t,v_t)\to(h_0,v_0)$. Let $\nu_t$ be the added measure from $D$ to $E_t$, and $\rho_t$ the removed measure from $E_t$ to its eroded source. Along any subsequence with $\nu_t/\delta_t$ weakly convergent to $\nu$, the limit of $\rho_t/\delta_t$ is
$$
 \rho=\sum_{z\in P}e^{h_0+v_0\cdot(z-j)}\,\delta_z\otimes
          \mathcal H^{d-1}|_{\partial A_z}.
$$
Use the old centroid $(j,c)$ and a fixed nonvertical margin $L=a\cdot(z-j)+u\cdot(x-c)$ with $\|u\|=1$. Suppose its zero plane contains no fiber facet of positive $(d-1)$-area, the limiting measures $\nu,\rho$ give it zero mass, and the old cap $H_0=\{L\ge0\}$ has mass $\beta m$. Let $S_H=\sum_z\mathcal H^{d-1}(A_z\cap\{L=0\})$. For the cap of the same normal through the corrected true centroid the limit of its absolute deficit divided by $\delta_t$ is
$$
 \int W_H\,d(\nu-\rho),\qquad
 W_H=\mathbf1_{\{L\ge0\}}-\beta-\frac{S_H}{m}L .
$$
The old source has integral zero against $W_H$.

## Proof

For each fiber, apply distance-to-boundary coarea with a continuous position test to its removed inner layer. The integral is the integral in radius of the test against surface measure of the corresponding inner parallel bodies. These measures converge weakly to $\mathcal H^{d-1}|_{\partial A_z}$ uniformly over radii from zero to the chosen radius, since the bodies converge uniformly in Hausdorff distance with positive inradius. To justify the surface-measure assertion, for a converging sequence of convex bodies the vector boundary measures of their unit outer normals converge distributionally by the divergence theorem and convergence of the indicators in volume. Total surface areas converge by the Cauchy projection formula. For every continuous nonnegative test $\varphi$ on a common compact containing the bodies, the dual formula for total variation as the supremum against vector fields bounded by $\varphi$ gives a lower limit at least the limiting boundary integral. Apply the same lower bound to a constant minus $\varphi$ and use convergence of total areas to get the matching upper limit. This proves weak convergence, and applying it to every sequence of allowable radii proves the stated uniformity. For intervals the two endpoints also give this directly. The layer width divided by $\delta_t$ tends to $e^{h_0+v_0\cdot(z-j)}$, proving the formula for $\rho$.

On each old polytopal fiber the zero plane intersects its boundary in $(d-1)$-measure zero under the facet assumption. Its parallel section area is continuous at that level by dominated convergence of the translated fiber indicators integrated in the section plane. This includes a lower-dimensional tangency, whose section area is zero; when $d=1$ the assumption excludes an endpoint. Coarea therefore gives $\mu(H_s)=\mu(H_0)-s S_H+o(|s|)$. All measures lie in a fixed compact region, and the cap payment lemma gives $s_H/\delta_t\to\int L\,d(\nu-\rho)/m$. Weak convergence and the null limiting plane mass give the limits of the two normalized perturbed cap terms by squeezing moving levels between fixed nearby levels. Substituting into the signed deficit identity proves the formula, using $\int d(\nu-\rho)=1-\kappa$. The old active equality and $\int L\,d\mu=0$ give the last claim. If $S_H>0$, this weight is nonnegative for $0\le L\le(1-\beta)m/S_H$ and also for $L\le-\beta m/S_H$, by evaluating its two affine pieces.
