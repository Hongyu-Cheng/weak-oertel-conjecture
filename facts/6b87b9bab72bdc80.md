## Statement

Let $P$ be a finite set with positive compact convex $d$-dimensional fibers $K_z$, $d\ge1$. Let $F$ be a nonempty exposed face of $\operatorname{conv}P$, and let $\ell$ be affine, zero on $F$ and strictly negative at all off-face bases. Let $\eta$ be the sum over the fibers of their boundary surface measures marked with outward unit normal $n$, using endpoint counting in dimension one, and let $\eta_F$ be its restriction to bases in $F$. Suppose $\phi(n,z,x)$ is nonnegative and convex in $(z,x)$ for each fixed normal on $\operatorname{aff}F\times\mathbb R^d$, measurable on the boundary bundles involved, and uniformly bounded on the face bundles and on the images of all off-face boundary bundles under an affine projection $\pi$ of the base space onto $\operatorname{aff}F$. Then a finite $B\ge0$ makes
$$
\Phi(n,z,x)=[\phi(n,\pi(z),x)+B\ell(z)]_+
$$
equal to $\phi$ on the face and zero on the other sampled boundary bundles. It is nonnegative and convex in $(z,x)$ for each normal, and $\int\Phi\,d\eta=\int\phi\,d\eta_F$, $\int\Phi W\,d\eta=\int\phi W_F\,d\eta_F$ for any bounded measurable boundary profile $W$ and its face restriction $W_F$. In particular an efficiency ratio with positive denominator transfers with exactly the same value, including profiles using supporting-face allocations.

## Proof

At a fixed $n$, affine composition preserves convexity and taking the maximum with zero preserves it. On a face bundle $\pi(z)=z$ and $\ell(z)=0$, so nonnegativity of $\phi$ gives equality there. If any off-face base is present, the finitely many gaps $-\ell(z)>0$ have a positive minimum. Choose $B$ so its product with this minimum exceeds the assumed uniform upper bound on the projected off-face bundles. The positive part then vanishes on every such bundle, regardless of the selected normal and boundary allocation. If there are no off-face bases take $B=0$. The identities follow by the decomposition of the boundary measure over the sampled bases.
