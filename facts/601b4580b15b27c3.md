## Proof

The exact convexity, rationality, full dimension, support, and fiber-volume assertions for the two factors are supplied by accepted facts `604889294a17d7df` and `6748814480d5deaf`, at the parameters specified in (3)-(5). In particular no parameter from a log-affine tree profile has been substituted into the rational profile. Products and the indicated coordinate permutation preserve these geometric properties. Product Lebesgue volume gives exactly the table and total mixed volume of \(C_m\), including its zero-volume fibers.

We first establish a bound for every convex-majorant section in the enlarged continuous dimension. The bound holds for any finite positive first-factor table, not only the chosen tree table. Fix an integer \(D\ge m^2\), a nonempty finite set \(E\), positive numbers \((a_I)_{I\in E}\), and a positive convex function \(\varphi\) on \(\operatorname{conv}(E\times U_+)\) with \(\varphi(I,z)^D\ge a_Iu_z\) for every \((I,z)\in E\times U_+\).

At a fixed \(I\in E\), define \(\psi_I(z)=a_I^{-1/m^2}\varphi(I,z)^{D/m^2}\) on \(\operatorname{conv}(U_+)\). The section of \(\varphi\) is positive and convex. Since \(D/m^2\ge1\), the function \(s\mapsto s^{D/m^2}\) is increasing and convex on \((0,\infty)\); its composition with this section is convex. Thus \(\psi_I\) is a positive convex majorant for the original \(m^2\)-dimensional table of \(B_m\), and \(\psi_I(z)^{m^2}=a_I^{-1}\varphi(I,z)^D\ge u_z\).

For completeness, \(\gamma_s=(s/(s+1))^s\) decreases as the positive real variable \(s\) increases: the derivative of its logarithm is \(-\log(1+1/s)+1/(s+1)<0\), since \(\log(1+1/s)=\int_0^{1/s}(1+t)^{-1}dt>1/(s+1)\). Hence \(\theta_D\ge\theta_{m^2}\). It follows, with the required direction, that
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{z\in U_+}(a_Iu_z-\theta_D\varphi(I,z)^D)_+
&=a_I\sum_{z\in U_+}(u_z-\theta_D\psi_I(z)^{m^2})_+\\
&\le a_I\sum_{z\in U_+}(u_z-\theta_{m^2}\psi_I(z)^{m^2})_+.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
Apply the full convex-majorant consumer of accepted fact `0bcc51d8cd216cf3` to the actual body \(B_m\) and the just verified majorant \(\psi_I\). It selects a positive integer fiber whose actual centroid has ambient depth at least \(2^{-m}\sum_z(u_z-\theta_{m^2}\psi_I(z)^{m^2})_+\). Accepted fact `6748814480d5deaf` gives the simultaneous upper bound \(h_{B_m\cap(\mathbb Z^m\times\mathbb R^{m^2})}((z,c_z))<3\,4^{-m}W_B\) for every positive-fiber centroid, because \(m\ge1024\). Therefore
\[
\sum_{z\in U_+}(u_z-\theta_{m^2}\psi_I(z)^{m^2})_+
<3\,2^{-m}W_B.
\tag{10}
\]
Combining (9)-(10) and summing over \(I\in E\) gives \(\sum_{I,z}(a_Iu_z-\theta_D\varphi(I,z)^D)_+<3\,2^{-m}(\sum_Ia_I)W_B\). This conclusion applies to every positive convex majorant, not just affine sections. Taking \(E=\widehat A\), \(D=D_m\), and then the supremum over \(\varphi\) proves the excess bound in (6). The argument explicitly accounts for both the exponent and the coefficient change in the product.

Next write \(S_j(a)=\sum_v\min\{a_{j+v},a_{j-v}\}\), with the oriented, zero-inclusive meaning in accepted fact `604889294a17d7df`. That fact supplies \(M(a)=\max_jS_j(a)=1\) at the exact parameters above, and
\[
\frac{W_A}{2^{n_A}}\ge\frac3{64}(H_k-H_7),
\qquad H_k=\sum_{r=1}^k\frac1r.
\tag{11}
\]
The subscripted \(H_k\) here is the harmonic number, not a reflected Hellinger sum. Integrating \(1/x\) gives \(H_k-H_7\ge\log((k+1)/8)\ge\log k-\log8=(128\cdot16^m-3)\log2\). Also \(\log2\ge1/2\). Consequently
\[
\frac{W_A}{2^{n_A}}
\ge3\cdot16^m-\frac9{128}
\ge2\cdot16^m.
\tag{12}
\]
Since \(u_0=1\), we have \(W_B\ge1\) and \(M(a\otimes u)=1\). Equations (12) and \(2^{-N_m}W_m=2^{-m}(W_A/2^{n_A})W_B\) prove the claimed maximum-fiber ratio.

We bound every product center and every mixed displacement; no multiplicativity of \(K\) or of reflected minima is assumed. For nonnegative \(a,b,c,d\), one has \(\min\{ac,bd\}\le\min\{a,b\}\max\{c,d\}\): if \(a\le b\), use the bound by \(ac\), and otherwise use the bound by \(bd\). Fix any integer center \((j,\xi)\), and sum this elementary inequality over all \(v\in\mathbb Z^{n_A}\) and \(\eta\in\mathbb Z^m\). With all tables extended by zero,
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{(j,\xi)}(a\otimes u)
&=\sum_{v,\eta}\min\{a_{j+v}u_{\xi+\eta},a_{j-v}u_{\xi-\eta}\}\\
&\le S_j(a)\sum_\eta\max\{u_{\xi+\eta},u_{\xi-\eta}\}\\
&\le S_j(a)\sum_\eta(u_{\xi+\eta}+u_{\xi-\eta})
\le2W_B.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
Every sum here is finite after its zero summands are removed. Each of the last two translated sums is \(W_B\), for every center \(\xi\). In particular (13) includes all mixed displacements, all nonzero orientations, and zero once in the definition on its left.

Both factor tables have the global log-concave extensions, including all their zeros, given in accepted facts `604889294a17d7df` and `6748814480d5deaf`. Their product therefore has such an extension, so the curvatures in (1) are nonnegative on positive reflected pairs. At every positive center and every \(L\ge0\), the cutoff sum in (1) is at most the full sum in (13), and \(1+L\ge1\). Thus \(K(w^{(m)})\le2W_B\). Dividing by \(2^{-N_m}W_m\) and using (12) gives \(K(w^{(m)})/(2^{-N_m}W_m)\le2^{m+1}/(2\cdot16^m)=8^{-m}\). This proves the remaining small-certificate bounds in (6), and their stated linear combination proves (7).

We finally construct the unrestricted packing and check its complete source accounting. Apply accepted fact `f6828be957af3808` to exactly the rational tree profile already used above. It supplies \(2^{Q-1}\) triples \(E_a^A\subseteq\widehat A\), all at the common target \(J_A=(\mathbf1,k)\), each with coefficients \(1/3\), with all their source indices distinct across the entire collection. Every source and the target have volume \(\rho_k\). Its verified mass inequality is
\[
2^{Q-1}\rho_k\ge2^{-n_A}W_A.
\tag{14}
\]

Partition \(U_+\) into \(\{0\}\) and its unordered pairs \(\{z,-z\}\) for \(z\ne0\); call this finite partition \(\mathcal F\). For each member \(F\), let \(u_F\) be the common weight of its members. Give its points equal coefficients \(1/|F|\). Its barycenter is zero, and \(\sum_{F\in\mathcal F}u_F=(W_B+1)/2\).

For every pair consisting of a tree triple \(E_a^A\) and \(F\in\mathcal F\), use the source set \(E_{a,F}=E_a^A\times F\), with coefficients \(1/(3|F|)\) and mass \(x_{a,F}=\rho_k u_F\). These sets have size three or six, and every point in a set has the same actual product volume \(\rho_k u_F\). Their common barycenter is the single integer target \(J=(J_A,0)\), whose volume is \(w^{(m)}_J=\rho_k\).

The sets \(E_{a,F}\) are pairwise disjoint: distinct tree triples have disjoint sources, and distinct members of \(\mathcal F\) have disjoint second-factor sources. At every used physical source the total scalar usage is consequently exactly \(x_{a,F}=w^{(m)}_I\); at every unused source it is zero. This checks the globally shared inequalities (2). In particular the full mass, not \(x_{a,F}/3\) or \(x_{a,F}/6\), is charged at each used source. No central mass is added separately.

Because all weights within a component are equal, its root-volume mean in continuous dimension \(D_m\) is \((\rho_k u_F)^{1/D_m}\). Its cost is therefore exactly
\[
L_{a,F}=\log\frac{\rho_k}{\rho_k u_F}=\log\frac1{u_F}.
\tag{15}
\]
For every positive integer index \(z\in U_+\), the integer \(m^4-\|z\|_2^2\) is at least one. Thus \(1-\|z\|_2^2/m^4\ge m^{-4}\), so \(u_z\ge m^{-4m^2}\) and \(0\le\log(1/u_z)\le4m^2\log m\). The singleton has \(u_0=1\) and zero cost. The total mass of the feasible scheme is
\[
M_{\mathrm{pack}}=2^{Q-1}\rho_k\frac{W_B+1}{2}
\ge\frac{2^{-n_A}W_AW_B}{2}.
\tag{16}
\]
The arbitrary-arity reduction of accepted fact `793210f8a4be3647` applies to this finite scheme if its affinely independent program is desired: it preserves its mass, weakly decreases every source usage and cost, and uses the same target. Hence (15)-(16) give
\[
\mathcal B_{D_m}(w^{(m)})
\ge\frac{M_{\mathrm{pack}}}{1+4m^2\log m}
\ge\frac{2^{-n_A}W_AW_B}{2(1+4m^2\log m)}.
\tag{17}
\]
Dividing by \(2^{-N_m}W_m\) proves the last line of (6); its limit follows because an exponential dominates \(m^2\log m\).

Finally use the cutoff consumer in accepted fact `793210f8a4be3647` on this one feasible scheme. It chooses one continuous point in the fiber at \(J\), before any ambient halfspace, with actual mixed-integer depth at least \(M_{\mathrm{pack}}/[6(1+4m^2\log m)]\). Its transfer includes all containing closed halfspaces, vertical halfspaces, and boundaries containing entire integer fibers. Combining this bound with (16) gives precisely (8). Thus the constructed bodies do not disprove the locked conjecture, while (7) is a simultaneous obstruction to the three stated scalar certificates.
