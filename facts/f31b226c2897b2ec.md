## Statement

Let $m\ge1$ and let $A,B\subset\mathbb R^m$ be convex bodies with $\lambda A+t\subset B$, where $\lambda>0$, and $|B|\le C|A|$. Write $\kappa_s$ for unit $s$-ball volume, with $\kappa_0=1$, and let $R=m(m+2)$. Then for every direction $u$,
$$
\lambda\,\operatorname{width}_A(u)\ \le\ \operatorname{width}_B(u)\
\le\ \frac{m C\kappa_m R^m}{\kappa_{m-1}\lambda^{m-1}}\operatorname{width}_A(u).
$$

## Proof

The lower bound is containment and translation invariance. Use 77d36834ea2179e7 with integer block dimension $m$ and continuous block dimension zero, using just its affine normalization of the body $A$. Write $A'=L A+b$ for its normalized image, so $B_2^m\subset A'\subset R B_2^m$. Use $B'=LB+\lambda b-Lt$, so $\lambda A'\subset B'$ and $|B'|\le C|A'|\le C\kappa_mR^m$ by the common determinant. For $v\in B'\setminus\{0\}$ the cone with apex $v$ and base $\lambda B_2^m\cap v^\perp$ lies in $B'$. Its volume is $\kappa_{m-1}\lambda^{m-1}\lVert v\rVert/m$, also for $m=1$, when the cone is the segment from $0$ to $v$. Thus $B'$ lies in the origin-centered ball of the claimed upper multiplier, while $A'$ contains the unit ball. Comparing the two directional widths and taking covectors through $L^{\mathsf T}$ gives the bound in the original coordinates.
