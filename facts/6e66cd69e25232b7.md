## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and, for \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\), put
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
\qquad
W:=\sum_z a_z.
\]
Assume \(W>0\), write \(Z:=\{z:a_z>0\}\), and put
\[
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
\qquad
c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,
\qquad
\mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
\qquad
D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z
\quad(z\in Z).
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(q=(q_e)\) be a nonzero finite off-diagonal fractional same-parity matching: its positive support consists of unordered pairs \(e=\{z,z'\}\subseteq Z\) with \(z\ne z'\) and \(z\equiv z'\pmod 2\). Assume the strong endpoint bounds
\[
d_z(q):=\sum_{e\ni z}q_e\leq\gamma_da_z
\qquad(z\in Z).
\tag{2}
\]
In particular, this applies to every nonzero exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching supplied by accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`. Since \(\gamma_d\leq1/2\), (2) makes \(q\) a free matching in the normalization of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`.

For \(e=\{z,z'\}\), define
\[
m_e:=\frac{z+z'}2\in\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad
D_e:=\frac12(D_z+D_{z'}),
\qquad
L_e:=\frac12(\mathcal K_z+\mathcal K_{z'}).
\tag{3}
\]
Let \(\Delta_C(q)\) be the weighted affine defect of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`. Choose a primal optimizer and a gauge-load-minimal dual optimizer \(v=(v_e)\) from that fact, and put
\[
\omega_e:=h_{D_e}(v_e),
\qquad
\Omega:=\sum_e\omega_e.
\tag{4}
\]
Assume \(\Omega>0\). For every \(e=\{z,z'\}\) with \(\omega_e>0\), choose the contact decomposition furnished by accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`:
\[
\ell_e=\frac{x_{e,z}+x_{e,z'}}2,
\qquad
x_{e,z}\in\mathcal K_z,
\qquad
x_{e,z'}\in\mathcal K_{z'}.
\tag{5}
\]
Define
\[
r_z:=\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e,
\qquad
E_\omega:=\{z:r_z>0\},
\qquad
p_z:=\frac1{r_z}\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_ex_{e,z}
\quad(z\in E_\omega),
\tag{6}
\]
and, for every positive-load edge \(e=\{z,z'\}\), define
\[
b_e:=\frac{p_z+p_{z'}}2.
\tag{7}
\]
Then all of the following hold.

1. Exact load and simultaneous metronoid realization. For every \(z\in E_\omega\),
\[
0<r_z\leq d_z(q)\leq\gamma_da_z\leq\frac{a_z}{2},
\qquad
\sum_zr_z=2\Omega,
\tag{8}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{
p_z\in
\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\cap\mathsf M_{r_z}(C_z).
}
\tag{9}
\]

2. Fixed inner margin. For every \(z\in E_\omega\),
\[
\boxed{
p_z+\frac15D_z\subseteq\mathsf M_{a_z/4}(C_z).
}
\tag{10}
\]
More generally, for \(0<\alpha<1/2\), put
\[
\psi(\alpha):=
-\frac{1-\alpha}{\alpha}\log(1-\alpha),
\qquad
\lambda_\alpha:=
\frac{\psi(\alpha)}{\log2}-1>0.
\tag{11}
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
p_z+\lambda_\alpha D_z
\subseteq\mathsf M_{\alpha a_z}(C_z)
}
\qquad(z\in E_\omega).
\tag{12}
\]

3. Degree-saturated strong matching. The weights
\[
\widehat q_e:=\frac{\omega_e}{2}
\tag{13}
\]
form a nonzero off-diagonal strong same-parity matching. More precisely,
\[
|\widehat q|=\frac\Omega2,
\qquad
d_z(\widehat q)=\frac{r_z}{2}\leq\gamma_da_z,
\qquad
2d_z(\widehat q)=r_z,
\tag{14}
\]
and the degree-saturated masses are explicitly realized by
\[
p_z\in\mathsf M_{\,2d_z(\widehat q)}(C_z).
\tag{15}
\]

4. No loss of affine defect. Put
\[
U_z:=\frac12\sum_{e\ni z}v_e,
\qquad
\tau_z:=h_{D_z}(U_z).
\tag{16}
\]
Then
\[
0\leq\tau_z\leq r_z
\qquad(z\in Z),
\tag{17}
\]
where both sides vanish outside \(E_\omega\), and
\[
\boxed{
\inf_{\substack{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega)\to\mathbb R^d\\
s\ {\rm affine}}}
\sum_{z\in E_\omega}
r_z\|p_z-s(z)\|_{D_z}
\geq\Delta_C(q).
}
\tag{18}
\]
The edge-average field satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\inf_{\substack{s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\\
s\ {\rm affine}}}
\sum_{e:\omega_e>0}
\omega_e\|b_e-s(m_e)\|_{D_e}
\geq\Delta_C(q).
}
\tag{19}
\]
The infimum in (18) is unchanged if \(s\) is required to be defined on all of \(\mathbb R^n\). Consequently, if \(\Delta_C(q)\geq\delta\Omega\), then
\[
\inf_s
\frac{\sum_zr_z\|p_z-s(z)\|_{D_z}}{\sum_zr_z}
\geq\frac\delta2,
\qquad
\inf_s\frac1\Omega
\sum_e\omega_e\|b_e-s(m_e)\|_{D_e}
\geq\delta.
\tag{20}
\]

5. Dominated endpoint selector and rank bounds. Let \(\mu_C\) be the mixed-volume measure of \(C\), and set
\[
\eta_\omega:=
\frac12\sum_{z\in E_\omega}r_z\delta_{(z,p_z)}.
\tag{21}
\]
Then
\[
|\eta_\omega|=\Omega
\tag{22}
\]
and every closed ambient halfspace \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{\eta_\omega(H)\leq\mu_C(H).}
\tag{23}
\]
Define
\[
\rho_\omega:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega)
\tag{24}
\]
and
\[
r_\omega^{\rm sel}
:=
\min_{\substack{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega)\to\mathbb R^d\\
s\ {\rm affine}}}
\dim\operatorname{span}
\{p_z-s(z):z\in E_\omega\}.
\tag{25}
\]
With \(R_{\rm sel}(C)\) denoting the residual-selector capacity of accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`,
\[
\boxed{
h_{\max}(C)
\geq
\frac{\Omega}
{2^{\rho_\omega}(r_\omega^{\rm sel}+1)},
}
\tag{26}
\]
\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm sel}(C)
\geq
2^{n-\rho_\omega}
\frac{\Omega}{r_\omega^{\rm sel}+1}.
}
\tag{27}
\]
If
\[
\rho_q:=
\dim\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:\omega_e>0\},
\tag{28}
\]
then
\[
\rho_q\leq\rho_\omega.
\tag{29}
\]
In particular,
\[
\rho_q=n\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\rho_\omega=n.
\tag{30}
\]

6. Exact affine case. Suppose there is an affine map
\(s:\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega)\to\mathbb R^d\) such that
\[
p_z=s(z)
\qquad(z\in E_\omega).
\tag{31}
\]
Then the degree-saturated complete core associated with \(\widehat q\), whose selected masses are \(r_z=2d_z(\widehat q)\), is nonempty. Consequently, with
\[
G(C):=\mathfrak C_{\rm core}(C)
\]
denoting the complete metronoid-core capacity defined in accepted fact `fbeb0a501b268d12`,
\[
\boxed{G(C)\geq2\Omega.}
\tag{32}
\]

7. Affine-tube exclusion. Define
\[
H(C):=\frac{2^nh_{\max}(C)}{W}.
\tag{32a}
\]
Then every affine map
\(s:\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega)\to\mathbb R^d\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{\substack{z\in E_\omega:\\
\|p_z-s(z)\|_{D_z}\leq1/5}}
r_z
\leq4H(C)W.
}
\tag{32b}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` gives
\[
0\leq\omega_e\leq q_e.
\tag{33}
\]
Therefore
\[
r_z
=\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e
\leq\sum_{e\ni z}q_e
=d_z(q)
\leq\gamma_da_z.
\tag{34}
\]
The binomial theorem gives \((1+1/d)^d\geq2\), hence \(\gamma_d=(1+1/d)^{-d}\leq1/2\). Double-counting incidences gives
\[
\sum_zr_z
=\sum_z\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e
=2\sum_e\omega_e
=2\Omega.
\tag{35}
\]
This proves (8).

We prove both memberships in (9), including the density-capacity point. Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` says that each \(\mathcal K_z=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\) is convex. Equation (6) expresses \(p_z\) as a convex combination of \(x_{e,z}\in\mathcal K_z\), so
\[
p_z\in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z).
\tag{36}
\]
For every positive-load incidence \((e,z)\), choose a witness density
\[
0\leq f_{e,z}\leq1\quad{\rm a.e.},
\qquad
\int_{C_z}f_{e,z}=\frac{a_z}{2},
\qquad
\frac2{a_z}\int_{C_z}xf_{e,z}(x)\,dx=x_{e,z}.
\tag{37}
\]
Define the single pooled density
\[
g_z(x):=
\sum_{e\ni z}\frac{2\omega_e}{a_z}f_{e,z}(x).
\tag{38}
\]
Pointwise almost everywhere,
\[
0\leq g_z(x)
\leq\sum_{e\ni z}\frac{2\omega_e}{a_z}
=\frac{2r_z}{a_z}
\leq2\gamma_d
\leq1.
\tag{39}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\int_{C_z}g_z=r_z
\tag{40}
\]
and
\[
\int_{C_z}xg_z(x)\,dx
=\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_ex_{e,z}
=r_zp_z.
\tag{41}
\]
Thus \(g_z\) is one admissible witness for
\(p_z\in\mathsf M_{r_z}(C_z)\). This proves (9) without reusing fiber density capacity.

For the margin, write \(p_z=c_z+u_z\) with \(u_z\in D_z\), using (36). Since \(D_z\) is convex and contains the origin,
\[
p_z+\lambda D_z
\subseteq c_z+(1+\lambda)D_z
\qquad(\lambda\geq0).
\tag{42}
\]
Accepted fact `11e347c295e9430b`, applied with its parameters \(p=\alpha\) and \(q=1/2\), gives
\[
c_z+\frac{\psi(\alpha)}{\log2}D_z
\subseteq\mathsf M_{\alpha a_z}(C_z)
\qquad(0<\alpha<1/2).
\tag{43}
\]
That fact also proves that \(\psi\) is strictly decreasing and
\(\psi(1/2)=\log2\), so \(\lambda_\alpha>0\). Substituting
\(\lambda=\lambda_\alpha\) in (42) proves (12). At \(\alpha=1/4\),
\[
\frac{\psi(1/4)}{\log2}
=\frac{3\log(4/3)}{\log2}
>\frac65.
\tag{44}
\]
Thus \(\lambda_{1/4}>1/5\), and (10) follows from (12) and
\((1/5)D_z\subseteq\lambda_{1/4}D_z\).

For (13)--(15), the edges remain off-diagonal and same-parity, and
\[
d_z(\widehat q)
=\frac12\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e
=\frac{r_z}{2}
\leq\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2}
\leq\gamma_da_z.
\tag{45}
\]
Since \(\Omega>0\), the matching is nonzero, and
\[
|\widehat q|
=\sum_e\frac{\omega_e}{2}
=\frac\Omega2.
\tag{46}
\]
The remaining claims follow from (45) and the second membership in (9).

We prove endpoint defect retention. Accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` gives the affine stress cancellations
\[
\sum_zU_z=0,
\qquad
\sum_zU_zz^{\mathsf T}=0,
\tag{47}
\]
and
\[
\sum_zh_{\mathcal K_z}(U_z)
\leq-\Delta_C(q).
\tag{48}
\]
If \(z\notin E_\omega\), every incident \(\omega_e=0\). Since \(h_{D_e}\) is a norm, each corresponding \(v_e=0\), whence \(U_z=\tau_z=0\). For \(z\in E_\omega\), (36) and (48) give
\[
\sum_{z\in E_\omega}\langle U_z,p_z\rangle
\leq\sum_zh_{\mathcal K_z}(U_z)
\leq-\Delta_C(q).
\tag{49}
\]
Every affine map on \(\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega)\) extends to one on
\(\mathbb R^n\). Equation (47) therefore gives
\[
\sum_z\langle U_z,s(z)\rangle=0
\tag{50}
\]
for every affine \(s\) occurring in (18). Combining (49) and (50),
\[
\sum_{z\in E_\omega}
\langle-U_z,p_z-s(z)\rangle
\geq\Delta_C(q).
\tag{51}
\]
Polar Hölder gives
\[
\langle-U_z,p_z-s(z)\rangle
\leq\tau_z\|p_z-s(z)\|_{D_z}.
\tag{52}
\]
By support-function subadditivity,
\[
\tau_z
\leq\frac12\sum_{e\ni z}h_{D_z}(v_e).
\tag{53}
\]
For \(e=\{z,z'\}\), origin symmetry makes the support functions nonnegative, while (3)--(4) give
\[
h_{D_z}(v_e)
\leq h_{D_z}(v_e)+h_{D_{z'}}(v_e)
=2h_{D_e}(v_e)
=2\omega_e.
\tag{54}
\]
Thus
\[
\tau_z\leq\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e=r_z.
\tag{55}
\]
Equations (51), (52), and (55), followed by the infimum over \(s\), prove (17)--(18).

For edge-average defect, (6)--(7) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{e:\omega_e>0}\langle v_e,b_e\rangle
&=
\frac12\sum_{e=\{z,z'\}}
\bigl(
\langle v_e,p_z\rangle+
\langle v_e,p_{z'}\rangle
\bigr)\\
&=
\sum_z\langle U_z,p_z\rangle\\
&\leq-\Delta_C(q).
\end{aligned}
\tag{56}
\]
The two moment cancellations of the dual optimizer in accepted fact
`6496cb3b0fd44f9d` imply
\[
\sum_e\langle v_e,s(m_e)\rangle=0
\tag{57}
\]
for every affine \(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\). Since
\(h_{D_e}(v_e)=\omega_e\), polar Hölder, (56), and (57) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_e\omega_e\|b_e-s(m_e)\|_{D_e}
&\geq
\sum_e\langle v_e,s(m_e)-b_e\rangle\\
&=
-\sum_e\langle v_e,b_e\rangle\\
&\geq\Delta_C(q).
\end{aligned}
\tag{58}
\]
This proves (19). Dividing (18) by (35) and (19) by \(\Omega\) proves (20).

We prove domination and the rank bounds. Accepted fact
`6769574fc1bd9f8d`, applied to (36), gives
\[
\delta_C(z,p_z)
\geq\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2}
\geq\frac{r_z}{2}.
\tag{59}
\]
The atomic-source domination proved in accepted fact
`be359f8a6c628483`, with points \(p_z\) and weights \(r_z/2\), gives (23). Equation (35) gives (22). The dominated-measure residual theorem in the same fact, applied directly to \(\eta_\omega\), gives (26). The same points and weights are admissible in the definition of \(R_{\rm sel}(C)\) in that fact. Choosing an affine map attaining (25) gives
\[
R_{\rm sel}(C)
\geq
2^{n-\rho_\omega}
\frac{\sum_zr_z/2}{r_\omega^{\rm sel}+1}
=
2^{n-\rho_\omega}
\frac{\Omega}{r_\omega^{\rm sel}+1},
\]
which proves (27). Every positive-load midpoint lies in
\(\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega)\), so
\[
\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:\omega_e>0\}
\subseteq\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega).
\tag{60}
\]
This proves (29), and (30) follows because \(\rho_\omega\leq n\).

Finally suppose (31) holds. Apply accepted fact
`6d6fcf377e68c79c` with continuous dimension zero to the nonzero finite base measure
\[
\nu:=\sum_{z\in E_\omega}r_z\delta_z
\]
on \(\mathbb Z^n\). It gives \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\) such that every proper closed base halfspace \(J\ni m\) satisfies
\[
\sum_{z\in E_\omega\cap J}r_z
\geq2^{-n}\sum_zr_z.
\tag{61}
\]
Moreover \(m\in\operatorname{conv}(E_\omega)\): otherwise strict separation from this finite convex hull would give a proper closed halfspace containing \(m\) and no point of \(E_\omega\), contradicting (61).

For every
\[
\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E_\omega)
:=
\left\{
\lambda\in\mathbb R_+^{E_\omega}:
\sum_z\lambda_z=1,\quad
\sum_z\lambda_zz=m
\right\},
\]
affineness, (31), and (9) give
\[
s(m)
=\sum_z\lambda_zs(z)
=\sum_z\lambda_zp_z
\in
\sum_z\lambda_z\mathsf M_{r_z}(C_z).
\tag{62}
\]
Thus \(s(m)\) belongs to the intersection in (62) over every
\(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E_\omega)\). The masses satisfy
\(0<r_z\leq a_z\), their total is \(2\Omega\) by (35), and (61) is the required \(2^{-n}\) base balance. Hence these data form an admissible complete-core certificate in the definition from accepted fact
`fbeb0a501b268d12`. Therefore
\[
G(C)\geq\sum_zr_z=2\Omega,
\]
which proves (32).

It remains to prove the affine-tube exclusion. Fix an affine map
\(s:\operatorname{aff}(E_\omega)\to\mathbb R^d\), and put
\[
E_s:=
\left\{
z\in E_\omega:
\|p_z-s(z)\|_{D_z}\leq\frac15
\right\}.
\tag{63}
\]
If \(E_s=\varnothing\), (32b) is immediate. Assume \(E_s\ne\varnothing\).
For \(z\in E_s\), origin symmetry of \(D_z\) and the definition of its
Minkowski norm give
\[
s(z)\in p_z+\frac15D_z.
\]
The fixed margin (10) therefore gives
\[
s(z)\in\mathsf M_{a_z/4}(C_z).
\tag{64}
\]
Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d`, applied to (64), gives
\[
\delta_C(z,s(z))
\geq\frac{\gamma_da_z}{4}
\geq\frac{r_z}{4},
\tag{65}
\]
where the last inequality is (8). Thus the points \(s(z)\) and positive
weights \(r_z/4\), \(z\in E_s\), are admissible in the definition of
\(R_{\rm sel}(C)\) from accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`. Using the same
affine map \(s\) makes every residual zero, so the residual rank is zero.
Writing \(\rho_s:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_s)\leq n\), that definition gives
\[
R_{\rm sel}(C)
\geq
2^{n-\rho_s}\sum_{z\in E_s}\frac{r_z}{4}
\geq
\sum_{z\in E_s}\frac{r_z}{4}.
\tag{66}
\]
The accepted inequality
\[
h_{\max}(C)\geq2^{-n}R_{\rm sel}(C)
\]
from accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` and the definition (32a) imply
\[
R_{\rm sel}(C)
\leq2^nh_{\max}(C)
=H(C)W.
\tag{67}
\]
Combining (66) and (67) proves (32b).
