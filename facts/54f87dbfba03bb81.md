## Statement

Assume the canonical hard branch and retain the exact objects and normalizations of accepted facts 084a2e888b32fd2d, 2fe46f47d19112ea, 7b2a94c842113bf3, 844dca4a76115be3, be359f8a6c628483, dd3f2941757243c9, and e482e1c83a1dc247. Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body,
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
and, for the canonical mass \(Q>0\),
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D
 <\frac Q{100}.
\tag{1}
\]
All six terms in (1) are nonnegative, \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) are distinct summands, and
\[
 D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
\tag{2}
\]

Retain the finite rescaled internal whole-label atlas \(\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\). Every label \(\sigma\) has weight \(\eta_\sigma>0\), two distinct physical endpoints \(a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\in\mathbb Z^n\), and
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{3}
\]
For every physical endpoint \(y\) occurring in the atlas, its fiber has volume \(a_y>0\), centroid \(c_y\), and the accepted endpoint budget is
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma
 \le t_{e_2(y)}\le\frac{a_y}{4}.
\tag{4}
\]

Retain a nonempty maximum-weight chartable family \(\Gamma_0\subseteq\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\), a witnessing affine map
\[
 s_0:A_0:=\operatorname{aff}(S_{\Gamma_0})\longrightarrow\mathbb R^d,
\]
and a witnessing linear subspace \(W\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) with \(\dim W\le3\), as in accepted fact 084a2e888b32fd2d. Put
\[
 \overline V:=\mathbb R^d/W,
 \qquad
 \pi_W:\mathbb R^d\longrightarrow\overline V,
 \qquad
 \overline s_0:=\pi_W\circ s_0,
\]
\[
 \mathscr D:=\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\Gamma_0,
\]
and let
\[
 \mathcal E:=
 \{q:\mathbb R^n\to\overline V:
 q\text{ is affine and }q|_{A_0}=\overline s_0\}.
\tag{5}
\]
For \(\sigma\in\mathscr D\), define
\[
 \mathcal F_\sigma:=
 \{q\in\mathcal E:
 q(a(\sigma))=\pi_W(c_{a(\sigma)}),
 q(b(\sigma))=\pi_W(c_{b(\sigma)})\},
\tag{6}
\]
and, for \(q\in\mathcal E\), define its realized whole-label agreement pattern and centroid-bad mass by
\[
 \mathscr P(q):=\{\sigma\in\mathscr D:q\in\mathcal F_\sigma\},
 \qquad
 M_c(q):=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D\setminus\mathscr P(q)}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{7}
\]

Then \(M_c(q)\) attains its minimum on \(\mathcal E\). Fix a minimizer \(q_*\), and choose an affine lift \(s_*:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) satisfying
\[
 s_*|_{A_0}=s_0,
 \qquad
 \pi_W\circ s_*=q_*.
\tag{8}
\]
Put
\[
 \mathscr D_o:=\mathscr P(q_*),
 \qquad
 \mathscr D_c:=\mathscr D\setminus\mathscr P(q_*),
\tag{9}
\]
\[
 M_o:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_o}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 M_c:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c}\eta_\sigma=M_c(q_*).
\tag{10}
\]
The exact alias map is that (9) is the partition of accepted fact 084a2e888b32fd2d freshly instantiated with the affine extension \(s=s_*\): \(\mathscr D_c\) is exactly its centroid-bad family, and \(\mathscr D_o\) is exactly its centroid-good but original-occurrence-bad family. In the original-ancestry notation of accepted facts 7b2a94c842113bf3 and dd3f2941757243c9, the exact aliases are
\[
 s=s_*,
 \qquad
 W_{\mathrm{ch}}=W,
\tag{11}
\]
and their \(\mathscr D_c,\mathscr D_o,M_c,M_o\) are precisely (9)-(10). No identification with a partition obtained from a different affine extension is asserted.

For \(q\in\mathcal E\), define the whole-label repaired and lost sets
\[
 \mathscr R(q):=\{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c:q\in\mathcal F_\sigma\},
 \qquad
 \mathscr L(q):=\{\sigma\in\mathscr D_o:q\notin\mathcal F_\sigma\}.
\tag{12}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr R(q)}\eta_\sigma
 \le
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr L(q)}\eta_\sigma
 \qquad(q\in\mathcal E).}
\tag{13}
\]

Fix the accepted combined original-ancestry construction after the substitution (11). If \(M_o>0\), let \(\nu_o\) be its nonzero combined original-ancestry measure and put
\[
 P_o:=\operatorname{supp}\nu_o,
 \qquad
 A_o:=\operatorname{aff}\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbb R^n}(P_o),
 \qquad
 \rho_o:=\dim A_o,
\]
\[
 r_o:=
 \min_{a:A_o\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{x-a(z):(z,x)\in P_o\},
\]
\[
 L_o:=2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_o}\frac{M_o}{r_o+1}.
\tag{14}
\]
If \(M_o=0\), set \(L_o:=0\) and do not define \(P_o,A_o,\rho_o\), or \(r_o\).

Now let \(\varnothing\ne\mathscr B\subseteq\mathscr D_c\). For every \(\sigma\in\mathscr B\), choose exactly one endpoint
\[
 \chi(\sigma)\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}
 \quad\text{such that}\quad
 c_{\chi(\sigma)}-s_*(\chi(\sigma))\notin W.
\tag{15}
\]
Aggregate physical endpoint collisions only after making these one-per-label choices. Namely, put
\[
 Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}:=
 \{\chi(\sigma):\sigma\in\mathscr B\},
 \qquad
 \alpha_y:=
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr B\\\chi(\sigma)=y}}\eta_\sigma
 \quad(y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}),
\tag{16}
\]
\[
 M_{\mathscr B}:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr B}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathscr B,\chi}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}),
\tag{17}
\]
\[
 r_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c:=
 \min_{\varphi:\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathscr B,\chi})
 \to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{c_y-\varphi(y):y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}\},
\tag{18}
\]
\[
 X_{\mathscr B,\chi}:=
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathscr B,\chi}}
 \frac{M_{\mathscr B}}{r_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c+1},
\tag{19}
\]
\[
 S_{\mathscr B,\chi}:=
 \max\left\{
 2\gamma_d(H_{\mathrm{int}}-M_c),
 4\gamma_dX_{\mathscr B,\chi}
 \right\}.
\tag{20}
\]
Then the selection has exact whole-label ownership and the endpoint budgets
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}}\alpha_y=M_{\mathscr B},
 \qquad
 0<\alpha_y\le\frac{a_y}{4}
 \quad(y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}).}
\tag{21}
\]
The positive finite measure
\[
 \nu_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c:=
 4\gamma_d
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}}
 \alpha_y\delta_{(y,c_y)}
\tag{22}
\]
is carried by \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), has mass
\[
 |\nu_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c|=4\gamma_dM_{\mathscr B},
\tag{23}
\]
and satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 \nu_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c(H)\le\mu_C(H)
 \quad\text{for every closed ambient halfspace }H.}
\tag{24}
\]
Its support is exactly
\[
 \{(y,c_y):y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}\},
\]
its intrinsic base rank is \(\rho_{\mathscr B,\chi}\), and its exact intrinsic continuous residual rank is \(r_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c\). In particular, the minimum in (18) is attained and
\[
 r_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c
 =\dim\operatorname{aff}
 \{(y,c_y):y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}\}
 -\rho_{\mathscr B,\chi}.
\tag{25}
\]

For every such pair \((\mathscr B,\chi)\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge
 S_{\mathscr B,\chi}
 +\max\{S_{\mathscr B,\chi},L_o\}.}
\tag{26}
\]
Consequently every canonical hard instance satisfies both strict screens
\[
 \boxed{
 S_{\mathscr B,\chi}
 +\max\{S_{\mathscr B,\chi},L_o\}
 <\frac Q{100},}
\tag{27}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 X_{\mathscr B,\chi}
 <\frac{Q}{800\gamma_d}.}
\tag{28}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact 084a2e888b32fd2d supplies an affine extension \(s_{\mathrm{ref}}:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) of \(s_0\). Therefore \(q_{\mathrm{ref}}:=\pi_W\circ s_{\mathrm{ref}}\) belongs to \(\mathcal E\), so \(\mathcal E\ne\varnothing\). Since \(\mathscr D\) is finite, the nonempty collection
\[
 \{\mathscr P(q):q\in\mathcal E\}\subseteq2^{\mathscr D}
\]
is finite. Choose a realized pattern \(\mathscr P_*\) maximizing
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr P_*}\eta_\sigma,
\]
and choose \(q_*\in\mathcal E\) with \(\mathscr P(q_*)=\mathscr P_*\). For every \(q\in\mathcal E\),
\[
 M_c(q)
 =\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D}\eta_\sigma
 -\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr P(q)}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{29}
\]
Thus \(q_*\) minimizes \(M_c(q)\). This is finite attainment and uses no topology or compactness of \(\mathcal E\).

Choose a linear section \(\iota:\overline V\to\mathbb R^d\) of \(\pi_W\), so \(\pi_W\circ\iota=\operatorname{id}_{\overline V}\), and define
\[
 s_*:=s_{\mathrm{ref}}
 +\iota\circ(q_*-q_{\mathrm{ref}}).
\tag{30}
\]
This map is affine. Both \(q_*\) and \(q_{\mathrm{ref}}\) restrict to \(\overline s_0\) on \(A_0\), so (30) restricts to \(s_0\) there. Applying \(\pi_W\) to (30) gives \(q_*\). Hence (8) holds.

For every endpoint \(y\) of a discarded label,
\[
 c_y-s_*(y)\in W
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \pi_W(c_y)=q_*(y).
\tag{31}
\]
It follows directly from (6), (7), and (31) that \(\mathscr D\setminus\mathscr P(q_*)\) is exactly the centroid-bad family in the accepted partition instantiated with \(s=s_*\). If \(\sigma\in\mathscr P(q_*)\), both centroid residuals of \(\sigma\) belong to \(W\). Such a discarded label cannot also have both retained occurrence residuals in \(W\): the sourcewise equivalence and hereditary chartability proved in accepted fact 084a2e888b32fd2d would then chart \(\Gamma_0\cup\{\sigma\}\) with the same \(W\), contradicting the maximum weight of \(\Gamma_0\). Hence \(\mathscr P(q_*)\) is exactly the original-occurrence-bad family. This proves (9)-(11). Moreover, any two lifts of \(q_*\) differ pointwise by an element of \(W\), so membership of either a centroid residual or an occurrence residual in \(W\) is independent of the chosen lift.

The same alias audit permits the accepted facts downstream of 084a2e888b32fd2d to be instantiated with \(s=s_*\), not with an unspecified earlier extension. In particular, accepted fact e482e1c83a1dc247 supplies
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge2\gamma_d(H_{\mathrm{int}}-M_c),
\tag{32}
\]
and accepted fact 7b2a94c842113bf3 constructs, when \(M_o>0\), the combined original-ancestry measure \(\nu_o\) with the exact alias \(W_{\mathrm{ch}}=W\) and proves
\[
 D\ge
 2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_o}\frac{M_o}{r_o+1}
 =L_o.
\tag{33}
\]
If \(M_o=0\), that fact invokes no support rank and gives only \(D\ge0=L_o\). Accepted fact dd3f2941757243c9 verifies that these are the same original-ancestry objects and that the \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) charges belong to the two distinct ledger summands in (1).

For arbitrary \(q\in\mathcal E\), the partition \(\mathscr D=\mathscr D_c\mathbin{\dot\cup}\mathscr D_o\) and definitions (12) give
\[
 \mathscr D\setminus\mathscr P(q)
 =\bigl(\mathscr D_c\setminus\mathscr R(q)\bigr)
 \mathbin{\dot\cup}\mathscr L(q).
\]
Consequently
\[
 M_c(q)
 =M_c
 -\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr R(q)}\eta_\sigma
 +\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr L(q)}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{34}
\]
Minimality of \(q_*\) gives \(M_c\le M_c(q)\), and rearranging (34) proves (13). Both conditions defining \(\mathcal F_\sigma\) are used, so every repair and every loss in (13) is a whole-label event, not a one-endpoint event.

Fix \(\varnothing\ne\mathscr B\subseteq\mathscr D_c\). Condition (15) can be satisfied independently for every label by the definition of \(\mathscr D_c\). The selected incidence set
\[
 \{(\sigma,\chi(\sigma)):\sigma\in\mathscr B\}
\]
contains exactly one incidence for each label and no incidence twice. Summing after equal physical endpoints have been aggregated therefore gives
\[
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}}\alpha_y
 =\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr B}\eta_\sigma
 =M_{\mathscr B}.
\tag{35}
\]
For every \(y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}\), accepted facts 2fe46f47d19112ea and e482e1c83a1dc247 give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 0<\alpha_y
 &\le
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma\\
 &\le t_{e_2(y)}\le\frac{a_y}{4}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{36}
\]
This proves (21), including collision aggregation and endpoint ownership.

We next prove (24) directly. The centroid \(c_y\) belongs to \(C_y\), so every atom in (22) belongs to the mixed lattice section. Let \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be a closed halfspace. If \((y,c_y)\in H\), then the fiber section \(H_y\) contains \(c_y\). If \(H_y\) is proper, the centroid halfspace inequality in accepted facts 844dca4a76115be3 and be359f8a6c628483 and (36) give
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_y\cap H_y)
 \ge\gamma_da_y
 \ge4\gamma_d\alpha_y.
\tag{37}
\]
If \(H_y=\mathbb R^d\), the same conclusion follows from \(a_y\ge4\alpha_y\) and \(\gamma_d\le1\). A section containing \(c_y\) cannot be empty. Summing (37) over the distinct selected bases whose atoms lie in \(H\), while discarding every other nonnegative fiber contribution, proves
\[
 \mu_C(H)
 \ge4\gamma_d
 \sum_{\substack{y\in Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}\\(y,c_y)\in H}}\alpha_y
 =\nu_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c(H).
\]
This includes whole-space halfspaces, boundary atoms, and all physical collisions. Equation (35) proves the mass formula (23).

Every \(\alpha_y\) is positive, so the support in (25) is exact. The intrinsic-rank theorem in accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 now shows that its base rank is \(\rho_{\mathscr B,\chi}\), that the minimum in (18) is attained, and that its residual rank is exactly the value in (25). Repeating a centroid before aggregation would not alter either its affine hull or its residual span, but the measure and the endpoint budget use only the aggregated masses.

The legal centroid-selector theorem in accepted fact 844dca4a76115be3, applied once to the distinct support \(Y_{\mathscr B,\chi}\) with calibration weights \(\lambda_y=\alpha_y\), is legal by (36). Equations (17)-(19) give
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge
 4\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathscr B,\chi}}
 \frac{M_{\mathscr B}}{r_{\mathscr B,\chi}^c+1}
 =4\gamma_dX_{\mathscr B,\chi}.
\tag{38}
\]
Combining (32) and (38) gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\ge S_{\mathscr B,\chi}.
\tag{39}
\]
By (2), \(D\ge S_{\mathscr B,\chi}\), while (33), or its \(M_o=0\) version, gives \(D\ge L_o\). Therefore
\[
 D\ge\max\{S_{\mathscr B,\chi},L_o\}.
\tag{40}
\]
The two lower bounds (39)-(40) are charged once each to the distinct summands \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) of (1). Hence
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+D
 \ge S_{\mathscr B,\chi}
 +\max\{S_{\mathscr B,\chi},L_o\},
\]
which proves (26). Combining this with the strict hard inequality (1) proves (27).

Since the maximum in (27) is at least \(S_{\mathscr B,\chi}\), one has
\[
 2S_{\mathscr B,\chi}<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 S_{\mathscr B,\chi}<\frac Q{200}.
\tag{41}
\]
Definition (20) gives \(4\gamma_dX_{\mathscr B,\chi}\le S_{\mathscr B,\chi}\). Combining this with (41) and dividing by \(4\gamma_d>0\) proves (28).

The exchange inequality (13) concerns simultaneous satisfaction of both quotient centroid equations of a label. The hereditary construction (15)-(22) chooses only one bad endpoint per label, and the affine map attaining (18) need not extend \(\overline s_0\) or fit the other endpoint. Neither construction asserts metronoid membership of \(s_*(y)\), a common barycentric Minkowski point, a massive lattice simplex, a complete core, or a physical filling. Thus no physical filling conclusion has been used or claimed.
