## Statement

Let $P=\operatorname{conv}\{(-3,-1),(3,3),(1,3),(-1,2)\}\subset\mathbb R^2$. In the order used below, the complete support $S=P\cap\mathbb Z^2$ is
$$
(s_0,\ldots,s_9)=((-3,-1),(-2,0),(-1,1),(-1,2),(0,1),(0,2),(1,2),(1,3),(2,3),(3,3)).
$$
Use unit counting mass on $S$ and take depth at a site to be the minimum mass of a closed ambient halfspace containing it. The largest parity class modulo $2\mathbb Z^2$ has size four, and the maximum raw depth over $S$ is exactly three, or $3/10$ after division by the original total mass. Thus the largest parity cardinality need not lower bound the best lattice depth.

## Proof

The polygon is full-dimensional and has facet inequalities $3y-2x\ge3$, $y\le3$, $2y-x\le5$, and $2y-3x\le7$, obtained from its cyclically listed vertices. Its bounding box is $[-3,3]\times[-1,3]$. The integer $y$-lists in increasing order of $x=-3,\ldots,3$ are $(-1),(0),(1,2),(1,2),(2,3),(3),(3)$, giving the stated support. The classes $00,01,10,11$ have respective index sets $\{1,5\},\{4,8\},\{3,6\},\{0,2,7,9\}$.

For each row $i$ below, the closed cut $ax+by\ge c$ contains $s_i$ and contains exactly the displayed indices, by substitution.

| $i$ | $(a,b,c)$ | Captured indices |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | $(-1,0,3)$ | $0$ |
| 1 | $(-1,0,2)$ | $0,1$ |
| 2 | $(-1,-1,0)$ | $0,1,2$ |
| 3 | $(-1,1,3)$ | $3$ |
| 4 | $(3,-4,-4)$ | $4,9$ |
| 5 | $(-2,3,6)$ | $3,5,7$ |
| 6 | $(2,-1,0)$ | $6,8,9$ |
| 7 | $(-1,3,8)$ | $7$ |
| 8 | $(1,0,2)$ | $8,9$ |
| 9 | $(1,0,3)$ | $9$ |

For the lower bound, $s_2$ is the midpoint of both $\{s_0,s_7\}$ and $\{s_1,s_5\}$. A closed halfspace containing $s_2$ meets each of these disjoint pairs, since its open complement is convex. It also contains $s_2$, giving at least three sites. The rows give the matching upper bound over all sites.
