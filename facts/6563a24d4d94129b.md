## Statement

Let a finite compatible family of planar convex fibers $K_z$ have support functions $h_z(\theta)$ differentiable at a normal angle $\theta$. Put $n=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$, $t=n'$ and $X_z=h_z n+h_z't$. For every active anchored inclusion at that angle, with $q=\sum_w p_w w$, $p_w\ge0$, $\sum_w p_w=1$ and $h_q=\sum_w p_w h_w$, one has $X_q=\sum_w p_w X_w$. For any fixed ambient cap with margin $u_j\cdot x+v_j\cdot z-b_j$, its support-point margins $f_j(z)=u_j\cdot X_z+v_j\cdot z-b_j$ obey all these same relations. If $q_z=\sum_j\lambda_j\mathbf1_{\{f_j(z)>0\}}$ with any finite nonnegative weights $\lambda_j$, then each active anchored representation gives
$$
q_q\le\sum_{w:p_w>0}q_w.
$$

## Proof

The support gap $h_q-\sum_w p_w h_w$ is nonnegative in angle by the anchored Minkowski inclusion. At its zero at $\theta$, differentiability gives $h_q'=\sum_w p_w h_w'$. A differentiable support at $n$ has a unique supporting point, since any supporting point has normal coordinate $h_z$ and, by the two-sided supporting inequality in angle, tangent coordinate $h_z'$. Substitution into the support-point formula gives the stated relation. The base-coordinate relation and the weight sum give the same relation for every affine cap margin. If a margin at $q$ is positive, its weighted average of source margins is positive, so at least one positively weighted source margin is positive. Summing this indicator inequality with nonnegative cap weights proves the bound. The margins are vectors in the full active kernel. The argument provides no off-pattern zero coordinates when a curvature variation is restricted to fewer fibers.
