## Statement

Take an actual local minimum of the normalized body-tied buffered maximum $\beta=Q/M$ over positive compatible fibers on a fixed complete base $P$, at fixed scale. Alternatively take a local minimum of $\beta_\varepsilon-\tau\log\varepsilon$ with $\tau>0$ and interior scale. Suppose the canonical body is a polytope in the relative base-ambient space with projection $\operatorname{conv}(P)$. Assume the proper deepest-source and pure-base reserve conditions of the body-tied finite cover. Fix such a cover with finite groups, proper selected caps and finite old contact sets. Write $\rho_{ij}>0$ for its cap weights summing to one in group $i$.

Vary the nonvertical ambient facet offsets with signed derivatives $h$, retaining all ambient normals, pure-base offsets and selected cap normals. Write $s=\dot{\log\varepsilon}$ in the interior-scale case and set $s=0$ otherwise. On a sampled facet $F$, let $v_F$ be the maximum of the inward speeds $-h_f/|u_f|$ of active nonvertical ambient rows $f$ with continuous normal $u_f$, and let $D=\sum_F |F|v_F$. For each group let $\alpha_i=\min_a A_{ia}$, where $A_{ia}$ is the individual contact response of the body-tied continuation at fixed cap normals. For selected cap $ij$ write $b_{ij}=|u_{ij}|$, $S_{ij}>0$ for its proper-section coarea, $r_{ij}$ for its threshold derivative, and $B_{ij}$ for captured old-boundary loss excluding the supporting facets. Its actual loss is
$$
R_{ij}=B_{ij}+S_{ij}r_{ij}
+\sum_{\rm in}|F|\max(v_F,r_{ij}/b_{ij})
+\sum_{\rm out}|F|\min(0,v_F+r_{ij}/b_{ij}).
$$
Inside support means the captured halfspace contains the whole old fiber, and outside support means that it meets only its supporting face. The surface measure $|F|$ is counting measure in dimension one.

Choose one maximizing ambient row at each $F$, one minimizing contact in each group, and one branch for each inside maximum and outside minimum. Impose their weak branch inequalities together with $R_{ij}=L_i$ and $\sum_j\rho_{ij}r_{ij}=\alpha_i$. In each of the resulting finitely many closed polyhedral cones in $(h,s,r,L)$, the simultaneous linear inequalities $L_i-\beta D+M\tau s\ge1$ for all groups are infeasible. The scale term is omitted at fixed scale. Feasibility in any cone is equivalent to a strictly descending first-order test in the indicated directions.

## Proof

The signed polyhedral path theorem 3684b58f28a5c4e6 applies with $\dot A_f=0$ and $\dot b_f=h_f$. Its relative full dimension, fixed projection, complete base and positive fiber hypotheses are exactly those above. It preserves the same actual sampled support for small signed times, with uniform radial control and first inward speed $v_F$, and yields $M(t)=M-tD+o(t)$. Each old regular sampled facet has a fixed active row set almost everywhere. Holding the selected cap normals fixed in 5efc62439ac46cfb sets all normal velocities to zero. Its individual response is linear in the integrals of the speed against fixed coefficients and in $s$, so it is linear in $(h,s)$ on each choice of maximizing rows. The true budget derivative is their minimum over the old contacts.

The exact cap loss of 9ee0d9a1e9f65259 at zero cap-normal velocity gives the displayed formula. Its captured boundary contribution on an inside supporting facet is $|F|v_F$, which combined with its inside correction is $|F|\max(v_F,r_{ij}/b_{ij})$. At outside support the correction is $-|F|[-r_{ij}/b_{ij}-v_F]_+$. Lower-dimensional supporting traces have zero facet area. The assumptions give $S_{ij}>0$, so as a function of $r_{ij}$ the displayed loss is continuous, strictly increasing onto $\mathbb R$, with slopes at least $S_{ij}$. The increasing weighted sum of its inverses gives unique equalization for any prescribed $\alpha_i$. The same two accepted continuation facts give common group mass $Q-tL_i+o(t)$, assigned-fiber coverage and persistence of the strict gap off the old deepest bases.

On a branch the inequalities are $v_{\rm chosen}\ge v_{\rm other}$ for each active row, $A_{\rm chosen}\le A_{\rm other}$ for each contact, $v_F\ge r_{ij}/b_{ij}$ or its reverse on inside supports, and $v_F+r_{ij}/b_{ij}\ge0$ or its reverse on outside supports. The appropriate branch values are linear and agree on ties. Thus the equalization equations on this branch are linear and homogeneous. Every direction and its unique equalization belongs to at least one such cone, and every point in a cone has the actual response. Because there are finitely many groups, strict positivity of every $L_i-\beta D+M\tau s$ is equivalent by positive rescaling to all of them being at least one. Such a direction strictly improves the actual objective, since on each group the derivative of the upper ratio bound is $(\beta D-L_i)/M$, with the additional derivative $-\tau s$ in the scale-penalized case. The off-deepest gap persists. Local minimality rules out every feasible system.
