## Statement

For the standard triangle $T$ and $D=(1/10)T$ with dilation about the origin vertex, the density $f=\mathbf1_T+\mathbf1_D$ takes values in $[1,2]$ on $T$. Its optimal normalized closed-halfspace depth is at most $112061/252500<4/9$.

## Proof

The barycentric coordinates $\lambda_0=1-x-y,\lambda_1=x,\lambda_2=y$ sum to one. The closed caps $\{\lambda_i\ge a_i\}$ with $(a_0,a_1,a_2)=(169/500,331/1000,331/1000)$ cover all of $\mathbb R^2$ because their thresholds also sum to one. The $T$-area fractions are $(1-a_i)^2$ by similarity about each included vertex. Since $D$ lies entirely in the first cap and is disjoint from the other two, the respective raw $f$-masses in units of $|T|$ are
$$
(331/500)^2+1/100=112061/250000,\qquad
(669/1000)^2=447561/1000000
$$
for the first and for each of the other two. The first is largest. Divide by $1+|D|/|T|=101/100$ to get the stated uniform upper bound. The strict gap is certified by $4\cdot252500-9\cdot112061=1451>0$.
