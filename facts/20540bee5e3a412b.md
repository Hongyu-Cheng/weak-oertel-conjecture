## Statement

Retain the complete canonical setting, notation, and physical ledgers of accepted facts 09fe6e21ebd31ee6, 3024a86da358e4ea, 4f7a1ed043c380e1, and cf2520f72184b7ac. Thus \(\Lambda\ne\varnothing\) is the good-label family generated by one normalized affine family, \(o_+\) is a maximum-tag selector, and
\[
 y_\sigma:=y_{\sigma,o_+(\sigma)},
 \qquad
 r_\sigma:=r_\sigma^c,
 \qquad
 M_\Lambda=\frac14\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda}\theta_\sigma.
\tag{1}
\]
Write
\[
 \Lambda_t:=\{\sigma\in\Lambda:t(\sigma)=t\},
 \qquad
 \Lambda_0:=\{\sigma\in\Lambda:y_\sigma=t(\sigma)\},
\tag{2}
\]
and retain the selected loop mass \(M_0\), target-collision maximum mass \(H_{\mathrm{tar}}\), full-credit forward-ray capacity \(\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+\), rank-at-most-two selected-source slice score \(\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o_+}\), and complete-core capacity \(G\) defined there.

Assume the difficult residual branch
\[
 \mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+
 \leq\frac1{32}M_\Lambda,
 \qquad
 G+\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o_+}
 <\frac1{256}M_\Lambda.
\tag{3}
\]
For every physical target \(t\), define its nonloop whole-label load and maximum nonloop label mass by
\[
 L_t:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t\setminus\Lambda_0}\theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 h_t:=
 \begin{cases}
 \max_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t\setminus\Lambda_0}\theta_\sigma,
 &\Lambda_t\setminus\Lambda_0\ne\varnothing,\\
 0,&\Lambda_t\setminus\Lambda_0=\varnothing.
 \end{cases}
\tag{4}
\]
Put
\[
 T_*:=\{t:L_t>64h_t\},
 \qquad
 \Lambda_*:=\bigcup_{t\in T_*}
 (\Lambda_t\setminus\Lambda_0),
 \qquad
 M_*:=\frac14\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*}\theta_\sigma.
\tag{5}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{M_*\geq\frac{15}{32}M_\Lambda.}
\tag{6}
\]
Every retained label is a nonzero source-target segment, and at every \(t\in T_*\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \theta_\sigma\leq h_t<\frac{L_t}{64}
 \quad(\sigma\in\Lambda_*\cap\Lambda_t).}
\tag{7}
\]

Restrict the maximum-tag selector and every program to \(\Lambda_*\), and denote the resulting quantities by \(\mathsf R_*^+\) and \(\mathsf S_*\). Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf R_*^+\leq\frac1{15}M_*,
 \qquad
 G+\mathsf S_*<\frac1{120}M_*.}
\tag{8}
\]
Moreover, if \(g_\sigma\geq1\) is the lattice length of the selected nonzero segment from accepted fact 4f7a1ed043c380e1, then
\[
 \boxed{(g_\sigma+1)\theta_\sigma
 <\frac2{15}M_*\qquad(\sigma\in\Lambda_*).}
\tag{9}
\]
For every affine subspace \(L\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) for which
\(M_L^*:=\frac14\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*:\,y_\sigma\in L}
\theta_\sigma>0\) and
\(q_L:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(I_+\cap L)\leq2\),
\[
 \boxed{2^{n-q_L}M_L^*<\frac1{120}M_*.}
\tag{10}
\]
In particular the selected-source support of \(\Lambda_*\) is not contained in any affine subspace of dimension at most two.

There is also one simultaneous physical source measure with no reused density. For \(z\in I_+\), define
\[
 u_{\sigma,j,z}(x):=
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_z}
 \mathbf1_{\{y_{\sigma,j}=z\}}
 \mathbf1_{C_z}(x),
\tag{11}
\]
and let \(F_{\sigma,z}\) be the accepted labelwise blocker-to-parent replacement density. Put
\[
 \nu_{*,z}(x):=
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*}
 \left(
 \sum_{j=1}^4u_{\sigma,j,z}(x)+F_{\sigma,z}(x)
 \right).
\tag{12}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{0\leq\nu_{*,z}\leq\frac{17}{32}
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_z,}
\tag{13}
\]
and the single mixed-integer measure \(\nu_*\) with fiber densities
\((\nu_{*,z})_z\) has
\[
 \boxed{|\nu_*|=8M_*\geq\frac{15}{4}M_\Lambda.}
\tag{14}
\]
It decomposes without changing density into target components
\(\nu_*=\sum_{t\in T_*}\nu_{*,t}\), where, with
\[
 \bar r_t:=\frac1{L_t}
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*\cap\Lambda_t}
 \theta_\sigma r_\sigma,
\tag{15}
\]
one has
\[
 |\nu_{*,t}|=2L_t,
 \qquad
 \int(z,x)\,d\nu_{*,t}(z,x)
 =2L_t(t,\bar r_t).
\tag{16}
\]
Consequently \((t,\bar r_t)\in C\cap
(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\) for every \(t\in T_*\).

Finally, let \((\lambda_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*}\) be any feasible affine family in the restricted maximum-tag target cost, so
\[
 \lambda_\sigma(r_\sigma)=1,
 \qquad
 \lambda_\sigma(c_{y_\sigma})\geq1.
\tag{17}
\]
Define its target and full-occurrence source envelopes by
\[
 \mathcal I_{\mathrm{tar}}(\lambda)
 :=\sum_t\int_{C_t}
 \max\left\{0,
 \max_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*\cap\Lambda_t}
 \lambda_\sigma(x)\right\}\,dx,
\tag{18}
\]
\[
 \mathcal I_{\mathrm{src}}(\lambda)
 :=\sum_z\int_{C_z}
 \max\left\{0,
 \max_{\substack{\sigma\in\Lambda_*:\\
 \operatorname{mult}_z(\mathcal O_\sigma)>0}}
 \lambda_\sigma(x)\right\}\,dx.
\tag{19}
\]
where a maximum over an empty label set is understood to be zero.
Then every such family satisfies the collision-safe lower bound
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal I_{\mathrm{src}}(\lambda)\geq4M_*.}
\tag{20}
\]
On the other hand,
\[
 \boxed{
 \inf_\lambda\mathcal I_{\mathrm{tar}}(\lambda)
 =\mathsf R_*^+\leq\frac1{15}M_*.}
\tag{21}
\]
Thus, for every \(\varepsilon>0\), one feasible family has
\[
 \mathcal I_{\mathrm{tar}}(\lambda)
 \leq\frac1{15}M_*+\varepsilon,
 \qquad
 \mathcal I_{\mathrm{src}}(\lambda)
 \geq4M_*.
\tag{22}
\]
No joint feasibility of the individual target realizations and no sum of individual complete-core witnesses is asserted.

## Proof

Accepted fact cf2520f72184b7ac turns (3) into
\[
 M_0\leq\frac1{32}M_\Lambda,
 \qquad
 H_{\mathrm{tar}}\leq\frac1{32}M_\Lambda,
 \qquad
 E_{\mathrm{seg}}<\frac1{16}M_\Lambda.
\tag{23}
\]
Therefore the total nonloop whole-label mass is
\[
 \sum_tL_t
 =4(M_\Lambda-M_0)
 \geq\frac{31}{8}M_\Lambda.
\tag{24}
\]
Also
\[
 \sum_th_t\leq H_{\mathrm{tar}}
 \leq\frac1{32}M_\Lambda.
\tag{25}
\]
For every \(t\notin T_*\), definition (5) gives
\(L_t\leq64h_t\). Hence (24)-(25) yield
\[
 \sum_{t\in T_*}L_t
 \geq\frac{31}{8}M_\Lambda
 -64\sum_th_t
 \geq\frac{15}{8}M_\Lambda.
\tag{26}
\]
Dividing by four proves (6), and (7) is immediate from the definition of
\(T_*\).

Any feasible point of the restricted full-credit target program extends to a feasible point of the original program by giving zero density to every deleted label. Thus
\[
 \mathsf R_*^+\leq\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+
 \leq\frac1{32}M_\Lambda
 \leq\frac1{15}M_*,
\tag{27}
\]
where the last inequality uses (6). Likewise every restricted selected-source slice mass is at most the corresponding original slice mass, so
\(\mathsf S_*\leq\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o_+}\). Consequently
\[
 G+\mathsf S_*
 \leq G+\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o_+}
 <\frac1{256}M_\Lambda
 \leq\frac1{120}M_*.
\tag{28}
\]
This proves (8). The third inequality in (23), the definition of
\(E_{\mathrm{seg}}\), and (6) give
\[
 (g_\sigma+1)\theta_\sigma
 \leq E_{\mathrm{seg}}
 <\frac1{16}M_\Lambda
 \leq\frac2{15}M_*,
\tag{29}
\]
which is (9).

For every \(L\) occurring in (10), the restricted slice mass is bounded by the original one. The definition of the accepted score in 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 and (28) therefore give
\[
 2^{n-q_L}M_L^*
 \leq\mathsf S_*
 <\frac1{120}M_*.
\tag{30}
\]
If the whole selected-source support were contained in an affine subspace
\(L\) of dimension at most two, then
\(q_L\leq\dim L\leq2\) and \(M_L^*=M_*\), contradicting (30). This proves (10) and its stated consequence.

We verify the simultaneous measure. Accepted fact 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 retains the literal all-occurrence-plus-replacement source ledger
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\sum_{j=1}^4
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_z}
 \mathbf1_{\{y_{\sigma,j}=z\}}
 \mathbf1_{C_z}(x)
 +\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}F_{\sigma,z}(x)
 \leq\frac{17}{32}.
\tag{31}
\]
All terms are nonnegative. Restricting (31) to \(\Lambda_*\) proves (13). For one label, its four occurrence packets have total mass \(\theta_\sigma\) and lifted first moment
\[
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4}
 \sum_{j=1}^4(y_{\sigma,j},c_{y_{\sigma,j}})
 =\theta_\sigma(t(\sigma),r_\sigma).
\tag{32}
\]
The accepted replacement packet has the same mass and the same lifted first moment. Thus one label contributes mass \(2\theta_\sigma\) and moment
\(2\theta_\sigma(t(\sigma),r_\sigma)\) to (12). Summing first over \(\Lambda_*\cap\Lambda_t\), and then over \(t\in T_*\), proves (14)-(16). Each \(\nu_{*,t}\) is a nonzero finite measure supported in the convex body \(C\), so its barycenter belongs to \(C\). Its base coordinate is the integer point \(t\), proving the final assertion after (16).

It remains to prove the envelope gap. At fixed \(z,x\), the total all-occurrence density from labels in \(\Lambda_*\) is at most \(1/4\), by the occurrence load inequality retained in 09fe6e21ebd31ee6. For arbitrary real numbers \(a_i\) and nonnegative weights \(w_i\) with \(\sum_iw_i\leq1\),
\[
 \max\{0,\max_i a_i\}\geq\sum_iw_i a_i.
\tag{33}
\]
Apply (33) with one repeated weight \(u_{\sigma,j,z}(x)\) for every tagged occurrence at \(z\), and \(a_i=\lambda_\sigma(x)\). Integration and finite summation give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal I_{\mathrm{src}}(\lambda)
 &\geq
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*}\sum_{j=1}^4
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_{y_{\sigma,j}}}
 \int_{C_{y_{\sigma,j}}}\lambda_\sigma(x)\,dx\\
 &=\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*}
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4}
 \sum_{j=1}^4\lambda_\sigma(c_{y_{\sigma,j}})\\
 &=\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*}
 \theta_\sigma\lambda_\sigma(r_\sigma)
 =\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_*}\theta_\sigma
 =4M_*.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{34}
\]
The penultimate equality uses the four-tag barycenter identity and affineness, and the next one uses the exact normalization in (17). This proves (20). Accepted fact 3024a86da358e4ea identifies the infimum in (18) with the restricted collision-safe full-credit capacity. Combining that identity with (27) proves (21), and the defining property of an infimum proves (22). The restriction is still generated by one normalized affine family: retain the original pieces on \(\Lambda_*\) and set every deleted piece to the constant \(-1\), exactly as in the extension argument of accepted fact 09fe6e21ebd31ee6. Hence the reduction stays inside the canonical maximum-tag class and the proof is complete.
