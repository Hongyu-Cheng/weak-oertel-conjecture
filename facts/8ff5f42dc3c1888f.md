## Statement

All logarithms are natural. For every finite nonempty complete full-rank support \(A\subseteq\mathbb Z^r\) and every strictly positive log-affine original law \(p\) on \(A\), retain the exact capacity, entropy row, original capacity moment, capacity weights, and likelihood variance of bf1ff94487296374 and c5abe3c72b8b89eb:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf C_p(\mathbf j)
&=\inf_{\mathbf u\in\mathbb R^r}
       \sum_{\mathbf x\in A}p_{\mathbf x}
          e^{\mathbf u\cdot(\mathbf x-\mathbf j)},\\
\mathcal B_2(p)&=\sum_{\mathbf j\in A}
                      p_{\mathbf j}\mathsf C_p(\mathbf j)^2,\\
\omega_{p,\mathbf j}
&=\frac{p_{\mathbf j}\mathsf C_p(\mathbf j)^2}
         {\mathcal B_2(p)},\\
V_{p,\mathbf j}
&=\operatorname{Var}_{\nu_{p,\mathbf j}}
       \left[\log\frac{\nu_{p,\mathbf j}(\mathbf X)}
                         {p_{\mathbf X}}\right],\\
\gamma(p)&=\log\bigl(4^r\mathcal B_2(p)\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(\nu_{p,\mathbf j}\) is the unique maximum-entropy probability law on \(A\) with mean exactly \(\mathbf j\), equivalently the unique mean-\(\mathbf j\) relative-entropy minimizer against \(p\). It is exactly \(r_{A,\mathbf j}\) and \(r_{\mathbf j}\) of bf1ff94487296374, and exactly \(\nu_{i,\mathbf j}\) of c5abe3c72b8b89eb for a block with original law \(p_i=p\). All minimal faces are included, and the logarithm is evaluated only on the positive row support.

Let \(\mathbf e_1,\ldots,\mathbf e_r\) be the standard coordinate vectors. For every integer \(r\ge4\), define new points
\[
\mathbf b_{r,0}=\mathbf0,
\qquad
\mathbf b_{r,h}
=-\sum_{i=r-h+1}^{r}2^{\,i-r+h-1}\mathbf e_i
\quad(1\le h\le r),
\]
and set
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q_r
&=\operatorname{conv}\{\mathbf e_1,\ldots,\mathbf e_r,
                         \mathbf b_{r,r}\},\\
\mathcal A_r
&=\{\mathbf e_1,\ldots,\mathbf e_r\}
      \cup\{\mathbf b_{r,0},\ldots,\mathbf b_{r,r}\},\\
p^{(r)}_{\mathbf x}
&=\frac{e^{\mathbf1_r\cdot\mathbf x}}{\mathcal Z_r}
\quad(\mathbf x\in\mathcal A_r),\\
\mathcal Z_r
&=re+\sum_{h=0}^{r}e^{1-2^h}.
\end{aligned}
\]
These supports are complete, \(\mathcal A_r=\mathcal Q_r\cap\mathbb Z^r\), and have difference lattice \(\mathbb Z^r\) and full affine dimension \(r\). Their original laws have the fixed tilt \(\boldsymbol\ell=\mathbf1_r\). Every row retains its exact integral target mean. Write \(\gamma_r=\gamma(p^{(r)})\).

For every \(r\ge4\), the exact quantities above satisfy
\[
0\le\gamma_r\le r\log4,
\qquad
\sum_{\mathbf j\in\mathcal A_r}
       \omega_{p^{(r)},\mathbf j}V_{p^{(r)},\mathbf j}
\ge\frac{(4/\sqrt6)^r}{5120r^3}.
\]
Consequently, there is no finite universal constant \(K\) such that every complete full-rank support and every strictly positive log-affine original law satisfy
\(\mathbb E_{\omega_p}V_{p,\mathbf j}\le K(1+\gamma(p))^2\).

## Proof

The initial all-vertex test cannot produce a likelihood fluctuation: if every support point is a vertex, the only probability law with mean at any support point is its Dirac law. Thus every \(V_{p,\mathbf j}\) is zero, regardless of the original tilt. The supports below have sparse nonvertex sites whose entropy rows behave differently.

First prove completeness exactly. A point \(\mathbf x\in\mathcal Q_r\) has barycentric coordinates \(t,\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_r\), where
\[
t\ge0,\qquad \alpha_i\ge0,\qquad
t+\sum_i\alpha_i=1,\qquad
x_i=\alpha_i-2^{i-1}t.
\]
Summing its coordinates gives \(t=(1-\sum_i x_i)/2^r\). These barycentric coordinates are unique, so the polytope is a full-dimensional simplex. If \(\mathbf x\) is integral, then \(t=k/2^r\) for some integer \(0\le k\le2^r\). For \(k=0\), the nonnegative integers \(x_i=\alpha_i\) sum to one, giving exactly the coordinate vertices. For \(k=2^r\), all \(\alpha_i\) vanish, giving exactly \(\mathbf b_{r,r}\).

Suppose \(0<k<2^r\). Because each \(\alpha_i\in[0,1]\) and \(\alpha_i=x_i+k2^{i-1}/2^r\), it equals the fractional part of \(k2^{i-1}/2^r\), except that an integral argument could instead give \(\alpha_i=1\). Put
\[
J(k)=\frac{k}{2^r}
       +\sum_{i=1}^{r}\left\{\frac{k2^{i-1}}{2^r}\right\},
\]
where braces denote fractional part. If \(k=\sum_{h=0}^{r-1}\epsilon_h2^h\) with \(\epsilon_h\in\{0,1\}\), then
\[
\begin{aligned}
J(k)
&=\frac{k}{2^r}
       +\sum_{m=1}^{r}\left\{\frac{k}{2^m}\right\}\\
&=\sum_{h=0}^{r-1}\epsilon_h2^h
       \left(2^{-r}+\sum_{m=h+1}^{r}2^{-m}\right)
 =\sum_{h=0}^{r-1}\epsilon_h.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(J(k)\) is a positive integer. The barycentric sum equals one and is at least \(J(k)\), so \(J(k)=1\), and there is no extra unit at a zero fractional part. It follows that \(k=2^h\) for some \(0\le h\le r-1\), and necessarily
\(x_i=-\lfloor2^{h+i-1}/2^r\rfloor\). These are exactly \(\mathbf b_{r,h}\). Conversely, these choices have nonnegative barycentric coordinates summing to one by the same calculation, so they all belong to the simplex. This proves the asserted complete lattice-point list. Since \(\mathbf0\) and every \(\mathbf e_i\) are in the support, its difference lattice is \(\mathbb Z^r\). The points are distinct, so \(|\mathcal A_r|=2r+1\le2^r\) for \(r\ge4\). The barycentric coordinates of the origin are \(t=2^{-r}\) and \(\alpha_i=2^{i-1-r}>0\), so the origin is interior.

We next construct its exact mean-zero entropy row. For this fixed \(r\), define positive scalars recursively by
\[
\begin{aligned}
c_{r,r}&=1,
&c_{r,h}&=1+2\sqrt{c_{r,h+1}}
&& (h=r-1,\ldots,1),\\
q_{r,0}&=1,
&q_{r,h}&=\frac{q_{r,h-1}}{\sqrt{c_{r,h}}}
&& (1\le h\le r),\\
s_{r,h}&=c_{r,h}q_{r,h},
&Z_r^{\circ}&=1+\sum_{h=1}^{r}(q_{r,h}+s_{r,h}).
\end{aligned}
\]
The scalars \(q_{r,h}\) are unnormalized atom weights, and \(c_{r,h}\) and \(s_{r,h}\) are new auxiliary scalars. They do not change any capacity or target-weight normalization.

Define a positive probability law \(\nu^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}\) on the full support by
\[
\begin{aligned}
\nu^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}(\mathbf b_{r,0})&=\frac1{Z_r^{\circ}},\\
\nu^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}(\mathbf b_{r,h})&=\frac{q_{r,h}}{Z_r^{\circ}}
&& (1\le h\le r),\\
\nu^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}(\mathbf e_{r-h+1})
&=\frac{s_{r,h}}{Z_r^{\circ}}
&& (1\le h\le r).
\end{aligned}
\]
The recursions give \(s_{r,r}=q_{r,r}\) and, for \(h<r\),
\(s_{r,h}=q_{r,h}+2s_{r,h+1}\). Hence
\(s_{r,h}=\sum_{k=h}^{r}2^{k-h}q_{r,k}\). In coordinate \(r-h+1\), the positive unnormalized mean contribution is \(s_{r,h}\), while the negative contribution from the points \(\mathbf b_{r,k}\) is exactly \(-\sum_{k=h}^{r}2^{k-h}q_{r,k}\). Every coordinate mean therefore vanishes exactly.

Let \((\boldsymbol\lambda_r)_{r-h+1}=\log s_{r,h}\). Since \(\mathbf b_{r,h}=2\mathbf b_{r,h-1}-\mathbf e_{r-h+1}\) and \(q_{r,h}s_{r,h}=q_{r,h-1}^2\), induction gives
\(e^{\boldsymbol\lambda_r\cdot\mathbf b_{r,h}}=q_{r,h}\). Also \(e^{\boldsymbol\lambda_r\cdot\mathbf e_{r-h+1}}=s_{r,h}\). Thus the displayed law is exactly
\(\nu^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}(\mathbf x)=e^{\boldsymbol\lambda_r\cdot\mathbf x}/Z_r^{\circ}\). For any probability law \(\eta\) on \(\mathcal A_r\) with mean zero, nonnegative relative entropy gives
\(D(\eta\|\nu^{(r)}_{\mathbf0})=\log Z_r^{\circ}-H(\eta)\ge0\), with equality only at \(\eta=\nu^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}\). This proves that the constructed law is exactly the unique maximum-entropy row \(\nu_{p^{(r)},\mathbf0}\), and that its entropy equals \(\log Z_r^{\circ}\). Its mean is the full integral vector \(\mathbf0\).

The recursion gives \(1\le c_{r,h}<6\) for every \(h\): the bound follows backwards from \(c_{r,r}=1\), since \(1+2\sqrt6<6\). Also \(c_{r,h}\ge3\) for \(h\le r-1\). Consequently
\[
q_{r,r}\ge6^{-r/2},
\qquad
q_{r,h}\le3^{-h/2}\ (h\le r-1),
\qquad
q_{r,r}=q_{r,r-1}.
\]
Because \(s_{r,h}+q_{r,h}\le7q_{r,h}\), these bounds give
\[
Z_r^{\circ}
\le1+7\left(\sum_{h=1}^{r-1}3^{-h/2}
                         +3^{-(r-1)/2}\right)
\le1+7\left(\frac1{\sqrt3-1}+1\right)<20.
\]
The last strict inequality follows, for example, from \(\sqrt3>5/3\). Thus the apex has exact entropy-row probability at least \(6^{-r/2}/20\), while its coordinate sum is \(-(2^r-1)\).

We now calculate its likelihood variance against the original tilted law, without replacing that law by an entropy row. On the positive support,
\[
\log\frac{\nu_{p^{(r)},\mathbf0}(\mathbf x)}{p^{(r)}_{\mathbf x}}
=(\boldsymbol\lambda_r-\mathbf1_r)\cdot\mathbf x
     +\log\mathcal Z_r-\log Z_r^{\circ}.
\]
The linear term is centered because the row mean is zero. Its value at the apex is \(\log q_{r,r}+2^r-1\). Since \(\log q_{r,r}\ge-(r/2)\log6>-r\) and \(2^r-1-r\ge2^{r-1}\) for \(r\ge4\), that value is at least \(2^{r-1}\). The second moment of this centered linear term therefore yields
\[
V_{p^{(r)},\mathbf0}
\ge\nu_{p^{(r)},\mathbf0}(\mathbf b_{r,r})\,4^{r-1}
\ge\frac{(4/\sqrt6)^r}{80}.
\]

It remains to retain the true original capacity weight and all other rows. The entropy-capacity identity of bf1ff94487296374, with the exact row aliases above and the log-affine specialization proved in c5abe3c72b8b89eb, gives
\(\mathsf C_{p^{(r)}}(\mathbf0)=p^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}Z_r^{\circ}\ge p^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}\). For every target \(\mathbf j\in\mathcal A_r\), including every proper minimal face, its original capacity lies in \((0,1]\): its root term gives the lower bound \(p^{(r)}_{\mathbf j}>0\), and \(\mathbf u=\mathbf0\) in the infimum gives the upper bound one. Hence the full capacity moment, including all these targets, satisfies \(\mathcal B_2(p^{(r)})\le1\).

For \(h\ge1\), \(2^h-1\ge h\), so
\(\sum_{h=1}^{r}e^{1-2^h}\le\sum_{h=1}^{\infty}e^{-h}=1/(e-1)<1\). It follows that \(\mathcal Z_r\le re+2\le4r\), and thus \(p^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}\ge1/(4r)\). Using exactly the original target weight gives
\[
\omega_{p^{(r)},\mathbf0}
=\frac{p^{(r)}_{\mathbf0}
                  \mathsf C_{p^{(r)}}(\mathbf0)^2}
        {\mathcal B_2(p^{(r)})}
\ge(p^{(r)}_{\mathbf0})^3
\ge\frac1{64r^3}.
\]
All other row variances are nonnegative, so their omission from a lower bound requires neither a change of weights nor a calculation of their individual capacities. Combining the last two estimates proves
\(\mathbb E_{\omega_{p^{(r)}}}V_{p^{(r)},\mathbf j}\ge(4/\sqrt6)^r/(5120r^3)\). The existence, uniqueness, face support, and exact integral mean of every other row follow from bf1ff94487296374 on this complete support; no boundary row is discarded from \(\mathcal B_2\) or \(\omega\).

Finally, the complete-support log-affine baseline of bf1ff94487296374 gives \(\mathcal B_2(p^{(r)})\ge4^{-r}\). Together with the upper bound one, it proves \(0\le\gamma_r\le r\log4\). Therefore
\[
\frac{\mathbb E_{\omega_{p^{(r)}}}V_{p^{(r)},\mathbf j}}
     {(1+\gamma_r)^2}
\ge\frac{(4/\sqrt6)^r}
          {5120r^3(1+r\log4)^2}
\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
since \(4/\sqrt6>1\). This exact finite witness family contradicts every proposed finite universal \(K\) for the assigned gain-dependent inequality.
