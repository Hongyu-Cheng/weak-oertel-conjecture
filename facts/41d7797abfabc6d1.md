## Statement

For every integer $q\ge1$ use $C=[0,1]^2\times[0,1]^q$ with integer base coordinates $z$ and original closed caps $z_1-z_2\ge1/2$ and $z_2-z_1\ge1/2$. For $t=1$ or $t=2$, let $e_p$ be the minimum $q$th root of residual fiber volume after using at most $t$ original caps. The minimum of $\sum_p\ell(p)^q$ over finite nonnegative convex thresholds dominating $e$ at the sampled sites is $2$. Restricting $\ell$ to one affine hinge $L_+$ raises that minimum to $4$.

## Proof

The four integer fibers have volume one. At $(0,0),(1,1)$ both caps miss the fiber, and at each other vertex one cap takes the whole fiber. This yields residual roots one on the diagonal and zero elsewhere for both list sizes. The diagonal alone contributes at least two, and the convex function $|z_1+z_2-1|$ attains two. For an admissible affine hinge let the two diagonal affine values be $a,c$ and the two off-diagonal values $b,d$. Then $a,c\ge1$ and $b+d=a+c\ge2$. Convexity of the power on nonnegative numbers gives $b_+^q+d_+^q\ge2[(b_++d_+)/2]^q\ge2$, while $a^q+c^q\ge2$. The constant hinge one attains four.
