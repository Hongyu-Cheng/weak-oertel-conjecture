## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, let $C\subset\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^d$ be a convex body, and write $K_z=C_z$. Fix a unit continuous direction $e$ and integer $m$. Let $a$ be $C^1$ on $[b_0,b_1]$ and consider $H_b=\{e\cdot x+a(b)(z-m)\ge b\}$. At every level assume integer-tilt stationarity, a proper section of $K_m$, and that each boundary trace in a positive $d$-volume integer fiber meets the fiber boundary in zero $(d-1)$-measure. Use section masses $s_z$ equal to the ordinary $(d-1)$-volumes of those traces, and zero on zero-volume original fibers. Denote the mixed cap volume and its two closed integer-side volumes by $V,V_-,V_+$ and the corresponding section volumes by $S,S_-,S_+$. Set $T=\sum_{z<m}(m-z)s_z=\sum_{z>m}(z-m)s_z$. Put $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. Then
$$
V'=-S,\quad V_-'=-S_--a'T,\quad V_+'=-S_++a'T.
$$
For $W_-=V_-+\int_{b_0}^b a'(r)T(r)\,dr$ and $W_+=V_+-\int_{b_0}^b a'(r)T(r)\,dr$, each $W_\pm(b_0)-W_\pm(b_1)$ lies between $\gamma_d[V(b_0)-V(b_1)]$ and $V(b_0)-V(b_1)$. With fixed tilt the correction vanishes. The fixed-tilt assertion also holds if the stated section hypotheses hold almost everywhere, and yields the same inequalities for each remaining cap if the terminal cap has zero mixed mass.

## Proof

For fixed $z$ the cap tail as a function of the threshold is the integral of the bounded parallel-section volume profile above that threshold. The profile is continuous at interior levels by Brunn–Minkowski and at regular supporting levels because any limiting section lies in the original zero-area support section. At exterior levels it vanishes locally. The threshold $b-a(b)(z-m)$ therefore gives fiber derivative $-[1-a'(b)(z-m)]s_z(b)$. Its volume is absolutely continuous, since the tail is Lipschitz and the threshold is $C^1$ on the compact interval. Summing these derivatives, the zero integer moment cancels the tilt term in $V'$ and gives the displayed opposite signs for the side derivatives.

At each eligible level apply stationary-oblique-section-tails with central section anchor any $x_0\in\operatorname{int}K_m$ on the cut, which exists by properness. It gives $\gamma_dS\le S_\pm\le S$. The corrected derivatives are $-S_\pm$. Integrate the bounds and use $V'=-S$. For fixed tilt the same derivatives and inequalities hold almost everywhere whenever the stated hypotheses do, which suffices by absolute continuity. Integrating to a terminal cap of zero mixed mass gives the cap-composition assertion on any tail subinterval.
