## Statement

Fix any integer \(k\ge8\). Use the original specialization \(\rho_t=2^{-t}\) of accepted fact 604889294a17d7df, with the spatial tree construction of accepted fact 032423921efad6c8. Explicitly, define
$$
b_\ell=0\quad(1\le\ell<8),\qquad
b_\ell=\left\lfloor\frac{2^\ell}{8\ell}\right\rfloor\quad(8\le\ell\le k),
\qquad Q_0=0,\qquad Q_\ell=\sum_{r=1}^{\ell}b_r,\qquad Q=Q_k,
$$
$$
m=2(Q+1)^4,\qquad n=\sum_{r=1}^{Q}m^r,\qquad N=n+1.
$$
Put \(a_0=0\), and put \(a_s=\ell\) whenever \(Q_{\ell-1}<s\le Q_\ell\). The parameter \(k\) is the original height cutoff, \(Q\) is the original tree depth, and \(n\) is the spatial dimension.

Let \([m]=\lbrace1,\ldots,m\rbrace\) and \(\mathcal T_{m,Q}=\bigcup_{r=1}^{Q}[m]^r\). Coordinates of \(\mathbb R^n\) are indexed by these nonempty words. The empty word is the root. For a node \(v\) of depth less than \(Q\), write \(x[v]=(x_{v1},\ldots,x_{vm})\) for its complete child-coordinate block. Choose any partition of \(\lbrace0,1\rbrace^m\) into symmetric saturated chains. Let \(D_m\) be its nonterminal vectors, and let \(F_m:D_m\to\lbrace0,1\rbrace^m\) be its injective successor map, with
$$
F_m(c)=c+e_{i_m(c)},\qquad c_{i_m(c)}=0.
$$
These are the objects of 032423921efad6c8, with no change of normalization. For a Boolean source \(b\in\lbrace0,1\rbrace^{\mathcal T_{m,Q}}\), begin at the root and, whenever its current child block \(b[v]\) belongs to \(D_m\), select the child \(vi_m(b[v])\). Stop at the first failure or on reaching depth \(Q\). Write this selected path as \(u_1,\ldots,u_{h(b)}\), and define
$$
z_s(b)=b+2\sum_{r=1}^{s}e_{u_r}\quad(0\le s\le h(b)),
\qquad
A_{m,Q}=\lbrace z_s(b):b\in\lbrace0,1\rbrace^{\mathcal T_{m,Q}},\ 0\le s\le h(b)\rbrace.
$$
The level of a point of \(A_{m,Q}\) is its number of coordinates equal to two. Let \(P_{m,Q}=\operatorname{conv}(A_{m,Q})\), and retain the original polytope
$$
\phi(x)=\max\left(0,\max_{u\in\mathcal T_{m,Q}}a_{|u|}(x_u-1)\right),
\qquad
\widehat P=\lbrace(x,t):x\in P_{m,Q},\ \phi(x)\le t\le k\rbrace.
$$
As proved in 032423921efad6c8 and 604889294a17d7df, its exact lattice support is
$$
\widehat A=\widehat P\cap\mathbb Z^N
=\lbrace(z,t):z\in A_{m,Q},\quad a_{\operatorname{level}(z)}\le t\le k,\quad t\in\mathbb Z\rbrace.
$$
In particular every allowed integer height of each original column is present.

For every \(\gamma=(\gamma_u)_{u\in\mathcal T_{m,Q}}\in\mathbb R^n\) and every real \(\zeta\), define
$$
w_{(z,t)}=\exp(\gamma\cdot z+\zeta t)\quad((z,t)\in\widehat A),
\qquad w_{\mathbf I}=0\quad(\mathbf I\notin\widehat A),
\qquad W=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}w_{\mathbf I},
\qquad p_{\mathbf I}=w_{\mathbf I}/W.
$$
For \(\mathbf I\in\widehat A\), define its actual ambient closed-halfspace depth and its third moment by
$$
\tau_p(\mathbf I)=\inf\left\lbrace p(H):H\subseteq\mathbb R^N\text{ is a closed halfspace containing }\mathbf I\right\rbrace,
\qquad
M_3(p)=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}p_{\mathbf I}\tau_p(\mathbf I)^3.
$$
Then, simultaneously for all these choices,
$$
M_3(p)\ge2^{-400}8^{-N}.
$$
The constant is independent of \(k\), the chain partition, \(\gamma\), and \(\zeta\). None of \(b_\ell,Q,m,n,N,a_s,A_{m,Q},\widehat A\) is rebuilt when the field changes. The assertion uses the full original normalizer, actual sampling probabilities, and every containing closed halfspace of the original \(\mathbb R^N\).

## Proof

Set
$$
\eta=2^{-m}|D_m|=1-2^{-m}\binom m{m/2},\qquad \delta=1-\eta,
\qquad \mathcal L_s=\lbrace z\in A_{m,Q}:\operatorname{level}(z)=s\rbrace.
$$
Throughout this proof \(\delta\) denotes only the canonical tree failure probability. The exact support and census in 032423921efad6c8 and 604889294a17d7df give
$$
|\mathcal L_s|=2^n\eta^s\le2^n\quad(0\le s\le Q),
\qquad 0<\delta<(Q+1)^{-2},\qquad \eta^Q>\frac34.
$$
Indeed \(b_8=4\), so \(Q\ge4\); and \(\eta^Q\ge1-Q\delta>1-Q/(Q+1)^2>3/4\). Also \(b_9=7\), so \(k=8\) gives \(Q=4\), while \(k\ge9\) gives \(Q\ge11\). Thus all columns are nonempty, and there is no intermediate case \(5\le Q\le10\).

We first record the arbitrary-height column reduction proved in cdf4b723cb4dc8ec, including its normalization and restriction factor. For a nonempty spatial class \(E\subseteq A_{m,Q}\), let
$$
\widehat E=\lbrace(z,t)\in\widehat A:z\in E\rbrace,
\qquad W_{\widehat E}=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat E}w_{\mathbf I},
\qquad p^{\widehat E}_{\mathbf I}=w_{\mathbf I}/W_{\widehat E}\quad(\mathbf I\in\widehat E).
$$
These conditional laws retain every original column height and use closed halfspaces in the same ambient \(\mathbb R^N\). Put \(\alpha_{\widehat E}=W_{\widehat E}/W\). For each \(\mathbf I\in\widehat E\), every original containing halfspace \(H\) satisfies
$$
p(H)\ge\alpha_{\widehat E}p^{\widehat E}(H),\qquad
\tau_p(\mathbf I)\ge\alpha_{\widehat E}\tau_{p^{\widehat E}}(\mathbf I).
$$
Consequently, for any finite spatial partition into nonempty classes,
$$
M_3(p)\ge\sum_{\widehat E}\alpha_{\widehat E}^{4}M_3(p^{\widehat E}).
\tag{1}
$$
There are three restriction factors from the cubed depth and a fourth from the actual sampling probability \(p_{\mathbf I}=\alpha_{\widehat E}p^{\widehat E}_{\mathbf I}\). Thus (1) uses fourth powers of actual class masses, without replacing them by fair source fractions.

For any probability law \(q\) on finitely many ordered heights, put
$$
d_q(t)=\min\left(\sum_{r\le t}q_r,\ \sum_{r\ge t}q_r\right).
$$
The heights with first closed tail less than \(1/4\) have total mass at most \(1/4\), and the corresponding final heights for the other tail have total mass at most \(1/4\). A set of mass at least \(1/2\) therefore has \(d_q(t)\ge1/4\), giving
$$
\sum_tq_t d_q(t)^3\ge\frac1{128}.
\tag{2}
$$
This includes one-atom height laws.

Define the full height sums and spatial sums
$$
L_s^{\mathrm{ht}}(\zeta)=\sum_{t=a_s}^{k}e^{\zeta t},\qquad
Z_s(\gamma)=\sum_{z\in\mathcal L_s}e^{\gamma\cdot z},\qquad
W_s=L_s^{\mathrm{ht}}(\zeta)Z_s(\gamma).
$$
Every term is positive, and the full original normalizer satisfies \(W=\sum_{s=0}^{Q}W_s\). Conditional on \(\widehat{\mathcal L_s}\), the law factors into
$$
r_z=\frac{e^{\gamma\cdot z}}{Z_s(\gamma)},\qquad
q_{s,t}=\frac{e^{\zeta t}}{L_s^{\mathrm{ht}}(\zeta)}\quad(a_s\le t\le k).
$$
A containing ambient closed halfspace intersects the same vertical column in a closed tail containing the sample height, or in the whole column. Hence
$$
\tau_{p^{\widehat{\mathcal L_s}}}(z,t)\ge r_zd_{q_s}(t).
$$
By (2) this proves, exactly as in cdf4b723cb4dc8ec,
$$
M_3(p^{\widehat{\mathcal L_s}})
\ge\frac1{128}\sum_{z\in\mathcal L_s}r_z^4
=\frac1{128}\frac{Z_s(4\gamma)}{Z_s(\gamma)^4}
\ge\frac1{128|\mathcal L_s|^3}.
\tag{3}
$$
In particular, this reduction has no restriction on \(\zeta\), and has no dependence on a largest entrance or top height probability.

The smallest case can already be checked directly. If \(k=8\), then \(Q=4\), so the five spatial levels form a partition. By (3), each conditional moment is at least \(8^{-n}/128\). The finite fourth-power inequality gives \(\sum_{s=0}^{4}(W_s/W)^4\ge5^{-3}\). Therefore (1) yields
$$
M_3(p)\ge\frac{8^{-n}}{128\cdot5^3}>2^{-16}8^{-N}>2^{-400}8^{-N}.
\tag{4}
$$
This is valid for every \(\gamma,\zeta\), including columns consisting of one height. The remaining argument also has well-defined forest objects for \(Q=4\), but we may henceforth assume \(k\ge9\) and \(Q\ge11\).

Use the spatial entropy quantities from dbbd63823b5f4870 with their original normalization:
$$
R(x)=\frac{8(1+e^{4x})}{(1+e^x)^4},\qquad
\beta_u=\log R(\gamma_u),\qquad \varepsilon=\frac1{mQ}.
$$
Choose a set \(T_0\) of exactly \(mQ\) coordinates with largest values of \(\beta_u\), with arbitrary tie breaking, and set
$$
E_{\mathrm{rem}}=\sum_{u\notin T_0}\beta_u.
$$
The choice is possible because \(n=\sum_{r=1}^{Q}m^r\ge mQ\). The scalar properties proved in dbbd63823b5f4870 are \(\beta_u\ge0\), evenness of \(R\), monotonicity in \(|x|\), and
$$
\log R(x)\ge x^2/3\quad(0\le x\le\log2).
$$
Its accepted spatial transcript estimate is
$$
\frac{Z_s(4\gamma)}{Z_s(\gamma)^4}\ge8^{-n}e^{E_{\mathrm{rem}}}
\quad(0\le s\le Q).
\tag{5}
$$
This estimate is a statement solely about the spatial level sums and fixes no height coefficient. Its source transcripts fix complete child blocks along a path, including the block producing a depth-\(Q\) coordinate. Thus it applies unchanged to the present \(Z_s\) at every level, including \(s=0,Q\).

Suppose first that \(E_{\mathrm{rem}}\ge\log(8m)\). Some \(s_*\) has \(W_{s_*}/W\ge1/(Q+1)\). Apply (1) to the level partition, retain this one term, and then use (3) and (5). We obtain
$$
M_3(p)\ge\frac{8^{-n}e^{E_{\mathrm{rem}}}}{128(Q+1)^4}.
$$
Since \(m=2(Q+1)^4\) and \(N=n+1\), this is equivalently
$$
8^NM_3(p)\ge\frac{e^{E_{\mathrm{rem}}}}{8m}\ge1.
\tag{6}
$$
This is the arbitrary-height entropy threshold \(\log(8m)\) of cdf4b723cb4dc8ec, rather than the smaller threshold for its fixed-height specialization. In particular (6) is a statement about the original full \(W\).

It remains to handle \(E_{\mathrm{rem}}<\log(8m)\). Define
$$
T=T_0\cup\lbrace u\notin T_0:|\gamma_u|>\varepsilon\rbrace.
$$
The set is nonempty because it contains the \(mQ\) elements of \(T_0\). Since \(0<\varepsilon\le1/8<\log2\), scalar monotonicity and the lower bound above show that each added coordinate contributes at least \(\varepsilon^2/3\) to \(E_{\mathrm{rem}}\). Consequently, as in the changed-threshold calculation of cdf4b723cb4dc8ec,
$$
|T|\le mQ+3m^2Q^2E_{\mathrm{rem}}
<mQ+3m^2Q^2\log(8m).
\tag{7}
$$
For \(Q\ge4\), the elementary bounds \(\log2<1\) and \(\log(Q+1)\le Q\) give
$$
\log(8m)=4\log2+4\log(Q+1)\le4(Q+1).
$$
Substituting \(m=2(Q+1)^4\) in (7) therefore yields the explicit polynomial estimate
$$
|T|<2Q(Q+1)^4+48Q^2(Q+1)^9
\le50(Q+1)^{11}<64(Q+1)^{11}.
\tag{8}
$$
All coefficients outside \(T\) have magnitude at most \(\varepsilon\).

For any field \(\theta\) on the tree define
$$
\Pi(\theta)=\max_{v_0,\ldots,v_Q}
\sum_{j=0}^{Q-1}\sum_{i=1}^{m}|\theta_{v_ji}|,
\tag{9}
$$
where \(v_0=\varnothing\) and every \(v_{j+1}\) is a child of \(v_j\). This is exactly the child-block path variation of dbbd63823b5f4870, 97e317b7fd2413b6, and 3dabb6b9906a3e32. It sums every coordinate of all \(Q\) child blocks on a full path, not just the \(Q\) selected path coordinates. The child blocks have different depths and hence are disjoint; each has \(m\) coordinates. Thus
$$
\Pi\bigl(\gamma\mathbf1_{\mathcal T_{m,Q}\setminus T}\bigr)
\le mQ\varepsilon=1.
\tag{10}
$$
Here \(\gamma\mathbf1_{\mathcal T_{m,Q}\setminus T}\) denotes the field equal to \(\gamma_u\) outside \(T\) and zero on \(T\).

For each \(u\in T\), choose a full rooted path of length \(Q\) passing through that coordinate node, and discard duplicate paths. Such an extension exists for every node, including nodes at depth \(Q\). Let \(B\) be the resulting number of distinct paths, and let \(P_j\) be their depth-\(j\) node sets, with \(P_0=\lbrace\varnothing\rbrace\). Then
$$
1\le B\le|T|<64(Q+1)^{11}.
$$
Use the identical forest coordinate objects required by 97e317b7fd2413b6 and 3dabb6b9906a3e32:
$$
S=\bigcup_{j=0}^{Q-1}\ \bigcup_{v\in P_j}\lbrace vi:1\le i\le m\rbrace,
\qquad
U=S\cup\lbrace u\in\mathcal T_{m,Q}:|u|\le\min(7,Q)\rbrace,
\qquad n_U=|U|,
$$
$$
R_U=\lbrace z|_U:z\in A_{m,Q}\rbrace.
$$
In these formulas \(P_j\) consists of forest nodes, whereas \(S\) is the union of their complete child-coordinate blocks. For every \(u\in T\), its parent is in \(P_{|u|-1}\), with \(0\le|u|-1\le Q-1\). Therefore
$$
T\subseteq S\subseteq U,
\tag{11}
$$
including every selected element of \(T\) at depth \(Q\). In particular, neither exit children nor final-depth coordinates are dropped from a forest child block.

Define a residual spatial field and a positive label multiplier by
$$
\lambda_u=\begin{cases}0,&u\in U,\\ \gamma_u,&u\notin U,\end{cases}
\qquad
G:R_U\to(0,\infty),\qquad
G(\omega)=\exp\left(\sum_{u\in U}\gamma_u\omega_u\right).
\tag{12}
$$
The label domain is the full set of restrictions of original spatial support points to all of \(U\). Thus \(G\) is defined and strictly positive at every required label, with no sign or magnitude restriction on the absorbed coefficients. The field \(\lambda\) vanishes on \(U\), and (10)--(11) give
$$
P_\lambda:=\Pi(\lambda)\le1.
\tag{13}
$$
The comparison follows term by term in each complete child block because the support of \(\lambda\) is contained in \(\mathcal T_{m,Q}\setminus T\).

We now check the full interface match before using the two height ranges. Their support is exactly the original \(\widehat A\) above, their forest is a nonempty family of \(B\le64(Q+1)^{11}\) full rooted paths of original length \(Q\), their \(P_j,S,U,R_U\) are precisely those just defined, their label function is any positive \(G:R_U\to(0,\infty)\), and their residual field vanishes on \(U\) and has the normalization (9) with \(P_\lambda\le1\). All these conditions hold here. Moreover for every original point and every original height there is the exact equality
$$
\begin{aligned}
G(z|_U)e^{\lambda\cdot z+\zeta t}
&=\exp\left(\sum_{u\in U}\gamma_uz_u+\sum_{u\notin U}\gamma_uz_u+\zeta t\right)\\
&=e^{\gamma\cdot z+\zeta t}=w_{(z,t)}
\qquad((z,t)\in\widehat A).
\end{aligned}
\tag{14}
$$
Both tables are zero off \(\widehat A\). Hence their full normalizers, all sampling probabilities, and all masses of original ambient closed halfspaces agree exactly. No coefficient-dependent tree or smaller support has been introduced.

If \(\zeta\le-\log2\), accepted fact 97e317b7fd2413b6 applied to these objects and the exact weights (14) gives
$$
M_3(p)\ge2^{-200}8^{-N}\ge2^{-400}8^{-N}.
$$
Its range includes equality \(\zeta=-\log2\). If \(\zeta>-\log2\), accepted fact 3dabb6b9906a3e32 applies to the same objects and the same full table and gives
$$
M_3(p)\ge2^{-400}8^{-N}.
$$
These two alternatives exhaust every real \(\zeta\). Each interface already uses all original column heights and all original ambient containing closed halfspaces; (14) identifies their actual laws with the law in the statement. Together with the high-entropy bound (6) and the smallest-case check (4), this proves the theorem for every spatial field and every real height coefficient on the unchanged original support.
