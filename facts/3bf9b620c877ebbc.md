## Statement

Let finitely many compact convex bodies of positive $d$-volume, $d\ge1$, be measured by summed fiber volume over a finite set of distinct bases. Let $y$ be interior to its own fiber and let a nonvertical closed cap with boundary through $y$ attain the depth there. Normalize the continuous normal to length one. Write $\theta_P,\theta_I,\theta_O$ for the summed $\mathcal H^{d-1}$ on the proper, inside supporting, and outside supporting sections of the cap plane, respectively. An inside supporting section has its fiber in the cap; an outside supporting section has a zero-volume cap trace. Use counting measure for $d=1$. Put $m=\int(w-y)\,d\theta_P(w)$ and $Z(\theta)=\{\int\alpha(w)(w-y)\,d\theta(w):0\le\alpha\le1,\ \alpha\text{ Borel}\}$. Then
$$
-Z(\theta_I)\subseteq m+Z(\theta_O).
$$
Equivalently, each prescribed inside subdensity admits an outside subdensity so that the sum of their section moments and $m$ is zero. Also $\int|e\cdot(w-y)|\,d\theta_I\le\int|e\cdot(w-y)|\,d\theta_O$ for every ambient vector $e$. If $\theta_O=0$, then $\theta_I=0$ and the combined proper section centroid is $y$.

## Proof

Vary the full cap margin through $y$ by adding $t e\cdot(w-y)$. The signed fixed-body slice formula (4)–(5) in 6b80e85ff6937910 with density velocity zero gives the right derivative
$$
e\cdot m+\int\min(0,e\cdot(w-y))\,d\theta_I+\int\max(0,e\cdot(w-y))\,d\theta_O.
$$
That slice calculation is fiberwise and does not use local optimality of a projective orbit or a circuit. Every such cap still contains $y$ and remains nonvertical for small $t$, so the derivative is nonnegative. The compact convex sets $Z(\theta)$ are finite moment images of the weak-star compact set $0\le\alpha\le1$ in $L^\infty(\theta)$. Their support functions are $\int\max(0,e\cdot(w-y))\,d\theta$ by pointwise maximization. The derivative inequalities thus compare the support functions of $-Z(\theta_I)$ and $m+Z(\theta_O)$ in all directions, which is equivalent to inclusion. Writing membership for each inside subdensity gives the asserted allocation quantifier. Adding the derivatives for $e$ and $-e$ gives the absolute first-moment inequalities. If $\theta_O=0$, inclusion and $0\in Z(\theta_I)$ give $m=0$. The first-moment inequalities for a coordinate basis force $\theta_I$ to be supported at $y$. Its own fiber has a proper section and every other fiber has a different base coordinate, so $\theta_I=0$. The own proper section has positive area, so $m=0$ gives the stated centroid.
