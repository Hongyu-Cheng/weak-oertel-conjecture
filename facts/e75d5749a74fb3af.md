## Statement

For every integer \(n\geq2\), set
\[
d:=9n,\qquad m:=n+d=10n,\qquad R:=2^{1/d}.
\]
In coordinates
\[
(x,u,t)\in\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^{d-1}\times\mathbb R,
\]
let
\[
C_n:=
\operatorname{conv}\left(
\bigl([-R,R]^n\times[-1,1]^{d-1}\bigr)\times\{-1\},
\{(0,0,m)\}\right).
\tag{1}
\]
Accepted fact `30d37af5a6688c28` proves that \(C_n\) is a compact full-dimensional convex body. For its mixed measure, let
\[
w_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d((C_n)_z),\qquad
W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}w_z,\qquad
\pi_z:=\frac{w_z}{W}.
\]
Take \(z_0=0\). Then the positive-mass lattice support is
\[
D=\{-1,0,1\}^n,
\]
the marginal mean is \(z_0\), and the exact marginal closed-halfspace depth of \(z_0\) is
\[
\tau=\frac{3^n+3}{2(3^n+1)}\geq\frac12\geq2^{-n}.
\tag{2}
\]

For every \(\sigma\in[2^{-n},\tau]\), let \(q^\sigma\) be the normalized entropy-metronoid weights from accepted fact `87013cdfa11d1279`. Let \(c_z\) be the centroid of the fiber \((C_n)_z\), and define the probability measure on the fiber-centroid graph and its barycenter by
\[
\rho_\sigma:=\sum_{z\in D}q^\sigma_z\delta_{(z,c_z)},
\qquad
p_\sigma:=\sum_{z\in D}q^\sigma_z(z,c_z).
\]
Then
\[
q^\sigma=\pi
\qquad\text{for every }\sigma\in[2^{-n},\tau],
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{TukeyDepth}_{\rho_\sigma}(p_\sigma)
\leq\frac{2}{3^n+1}
\qquad\text{for every }\sigma\in[2^{-n},\tau].
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\frac{1}{2^{-n}}
\sup_{\sigma\in[2^{-n},\tau]}
\sigma\,\operatorname{TukeyDepth}_{\rho_\sigma}(p_\sigma)
\leq
\frac{2^n(3^n+3)}{(3^n+1)^2}
<2\left(\frac23\right)^n
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{5}
\]
In particular, no absolute \(c>0\) can make
\[
\sup_{\sigma\in[2^{-n},\tau]}
\sigma\,\operatorname{TukeyDepth}_{\rho_\sigma}(p_\sigma)
\geq c\,2^{-n}
\]
hold for all compact full-dimensional mixed-integer convex bodies.

## Proof

Use
\[
r:=\frac{m-t}{m+1}.
\]
At fixed \(r\in[0,1]\), the section of (1) is
\[
r[-R,R]^n\times r[-1,1]^{d-1}
\]
at height \(t=m-(m+1)r\). For a fixed base point \(x\), put
\[
s(x):=\frac{\lVert x\rVert_\infty}{R}.
\]
The fiber over \(x\) is parametrized by
\[
s(x)\leq r\leq1,\qquad
u\in[-r,r]^{d-1},\qquad
t=m-(m+1)r.
\tag{6}
\]
Accepted fact `30d37af5a6688c28` computes from (6) that \(1<R<2\), that the positive-mass lattice support is \(D=\{-1,0,1\}^n\), and that, for a positive constant \(\Gamma\),
\[
w_0=\Gamma,\qquad
w_z=\frac{\Gamma}{2}\quad(z\in D\setminus\{0\}),\qquad
W=\frac{\Gamma}{2}(3^n+1).
\tag{7}
\]
Therefore
\[
\pi_0=\frac{2}{3^n+1},
\qquad
\pi_z=\frac{1}{3^n+1}\quad(z\neq0).
\tag{8}
\]

The distribution \(\pi\) is invariant under \(z\mapsto-z\), so
\[
\sum_{z\in D}\pi_z z=0=z_0.
\tag{9}
\]
We next compute its exact depth. Every closed halfspace through \(0\) contains the atom at \(0\) and at least one member of every pair \(\{z,-z\}\), so its mass is at least
\[
\pi_0+\frac{1-\pi_0}{2}
=\frac{1+\pi_0}{2}
=\frac{3^n+3}{2(3^n+1)}.
\tag{10}
\]
Any closed halfspace containing \(0\) contains the parallel halfspace obtained by moving its boundary inward until it passes through \(0\), so the same lower bound holds for every closed halfspace containing \(0\).
This lower bound is attained. Indeed, take
\[
a=(1,3,3^2,\ldots,3^{n-1}).
\]
If \(z\in\{-1,0,1\}^n\) is nonzero, then \(a^\top z\neq0\): the term with largest nonzero coordinate has absolute value strictly larger than the sum of all preceding absolute values. Hence the halfspace
\[
\{z:a^\top z\geq0\}
\]
contains \(0\) and exactly one member of every nonzero pair. This proves (2).

The mean condition (9) is precisely the uniform-rescaling hypothesis in accepted fact `87013cdfa11d1279`. That fact therefore gives, simultaneously for every \(0<\sigma\leq\tau\),
\[
\lambda^\sigma_z=\sigma,\qquad
q^\sigma_z=\frac{\pi_z\lambda^\sigma_z}{\sigma}=\pi_z.
\tag{11}
\]
This proves (3) and controls the entire required scale interval, including both endpoints.

It remains to compute enough of the fiber centroids. The \(u\)-component of every \(c_z\) is \(0\) by the central symmetry of the cube in (6). Conditional on a fixed base point \(x\), the \(r\)-density is proportional to \(r^{d-1}\) on \([s(x),1]\). Hence the \(t\)-coordinate of the fiber centroid is
\[
T(s)
:=
m-(m+1)\frac{d}{d+1}
\frac{1-s^{d+1}}{1-s^d},
\qquad 0\leq s<1.
\tag{12}
\]
For \(z=0\), one has \(s(0)=0\), while every \(z\in D\setminus\{0\}\) has
\[
s(z)=R^{-1}\in(0,1).
\tag{13}
\]
Since
\[
\frac{1-s^{d+1}}{1-s^d}>1
\qquad(0<s<1),
\tag{14}
\]
equations (12)-(13) give two distinct centroid heights
\[
T_1:=T(R^{-1})<T_0:=T(0).
\tag{15}
\]
Thus
\[
c_0=(0,T_0),\qquad
c_z=(0,T_1)\quad(z\neq0),
\tag{16}
\]
where the first zero in each ordered pair denotes the \(u\)-coordinate in \(\mathbb R^{d-1}\).

By (3), the graph barycenter is independent of \(\sigma\). Its base and \(u\)-coordinates are zero, and its height is
\[
\overline T:=\pi_0T_0+(1-\pi_0)T_1.
\tag{17}
\]
Because \(0<\pi_0<1\), equation (15) implies
\[
T_1<\overline T<T_0.
\tag{18}
\]
The closed ambient halfspace
\[
G:=\{(x,u,t):t\geq\overline T\}
\tag{19}
\]
has boundary through \(p_\sigma=(0,0,\overline T)\). By (16) and (18), its intersection with the support of \(\rho_\sigma\) consists only of the graph point over the central fiber. Equations (3) and (8) therefore give
\[
\rho_\sigma(G)=\pi_0=\frac{2}{3^n+1}.
\tag{20}
\]
Taking the infimum over all closed halfspaces containing \(p_\sigma\) proves (4).

Since the right side of (4) is independent of \(\sigma\),
\[
\sup_{\sigma\in[2^{-n},\tau]}
\sigma\,\operatorname{TukeyDepth}_{\rho_\sigma}(p_\sigma)
\leq\tau\pi_0
=\frac{3^n+3}{(3^n+1)^2}.
\tag{21}
\]
After division by \(2^{-n}\), this is the first bound in (5). For \(n\geq2\),
\[
3^n+3<2(3^n+1),
\]
so
\[
\frac{2^n(3^n+3)}{(3^n+1)^2}
<
\frac{2^{n+1}}{3^n+1}
<
2\left(\frac23\right)^n.
\tag{22}
\]
The last expression tends to zero. This proves (5) and the claimed exact refutation.

For clarity about scope, if a universal lower bound of the refuted form had held, then fiberwise Grünbaum would indeed have transferred it to the selected fiber measure: whenever \((z,c_z)\) lies in a closed halfspace \(H\), the cap \(H\cap(\{z\}\times C_z)\) contains the centroid \(c_z\) and therefore has at least \((d/(d+1))^d\geq1/e\) of that fiber's volume. Thus
\[
\frac{\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(H)}{\sigma W}
\geq
\frac1e\,
\rho_\sigma\{(z,c_z):(z,c_z)\in H\}.
\]
The counterexample proves that the missing graph-depth premise, rather than this transfer step, is false.
