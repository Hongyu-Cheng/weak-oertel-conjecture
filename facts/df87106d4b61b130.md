## Statement

Let $K=\{(x,y):0\le x\le1,\ 0\le y\le h(x)\}$, with $h(x)=1+x$ on $[0,1/2]$ and $h(x)=3(1-x)$ on $[1/2,1]$. Its centroid has first coordinate $m=5/12$. If $X,Y$ are independent and uniform on $K$ and $H=\{(x,y):x\ge m\}$, then $\Pr(X\in H)=143/288$ and $\Pr((X+Y)/2\in H)=15343/31104$, a decrease of $101/31104$.

## Proof

The height is nonnegative and concave, making $K$ a convex quadrilateral. Its area is $5/8+3/8=1$, so the height extended by zero is the density of the first coordinate. Integrating $xh(x)$ gives $1/6+1/4=5/12$. The single cap mass is $1-m-m^2/2=143/288$.

Write the density for real $x$ as $\mathbf1_{\{x\ge0\}}+x_+-4(x-1/2)_++3(x-1)_+$. Integrate the product of two copies over $x,y\ge0$ and $x+y\le s$, with $1/2\le s\le1$. The unshifted product $(1+x)(1+y)$ integrates to $s^2/2+s^3/3+s^4/24$. Either of the two linear correction terms shifted by $1/2$ contributes $-4[(s-1/2)^3/6+(s-1/2)^4/24]$ after translation. Terms shifted by $1$ or shifted by $1/2$ in both coordinates have zero integral on this domain. The resulting sum CDF is
$$
G(s)=s^2/2+s^3/3+s^4/24-\frac43(s-1/2)^3-\frac13(s-1/2)^4 .
$$
There are no atoms, so the closed midpoint cap has mass $1-G(5/6)=15343/31104$. Subtracting from $143/288$ gives the claimed decrease. Both masses exceed $4/9$.
