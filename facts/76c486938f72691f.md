## Statement

Retain the complete canonical hard-branch transverse-atlas setting and notation of accepted fact `036a276c54ec5e66`. Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), the positive actual atlas labels have target atoms \((m_\sigma,X_\sigma)\in\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d\),
\[
A_\perp:=\operatorname{aff}\{m_\sigma:\theta_\sigma>0\},
\qquad
\rho_\perp:=\dim A_\perp,
\]
\[
r_\perp:=
\min_{a:A_\perp\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
\dim\operatorname{span}
\{X_\sigma-a(m_\sigma):\theta_\sigma>0\},
\qquad
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
\]
and the accepted strict rank screen is
\[
r_\perp+1>99\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_\perp}.
\]
Then
\[
d+1>99\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_\perp}.
\]
Consequently \(d\geq36\). If \(\rho_\perp<n\), then \(d\geq73\). In particular, no canonical hard-branch instance exists with \(n=3\) and \(d=5\).

## Proof

The constant map \(a_0:A_\perp\to\mathbb R^d\), \(a_0(m)=0\), is affine. Its residuals are the actual vectors \(X_\sigma\in\mathbb R^d\). Hence
\[
r_\perp
\leq\dim\operatorname{span}\{X_\sigma:\theta_\sigma>0\}
\leq d.
\]
Combining this with the strict rank screen of accepted fact `036a276c54ec5e66` preserves strictness and gives
\[
d+1\geq r_\perp+1
>99\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_\perp},
\]
which is the claimed ambient-dimension screen.

We now determine its two integer cutoffs exactly. For every integer \(k\geq1\), put
\[
\gamma_k:=\left(\frac{k}{k+1}\right)^k,
\qquad
q_k:=\left(1+\frac1k\right)^k,
\qquad
F_k:=\frac{k+1}{\gamma_k}
=(k+1)q_k
=\frac{(k+1)^{k+1}}{k^k}.
\]
The binomial theorem gives the exact expansion
\[
q_k
=\sum_{j=0}^k\frac1{j!}
\prod_{i=0}^{j-1}\left(1-\frac{i}{k}\right).
\]
For each \(0\leq j\leq k\), every factor in the corresponding \(j\)-term for \(q_{k+1}\) is at least the factor for \(q_k\), and the additional \(j=k+1\) term of \(q_{k+1}\) is positive. Thus \(q_{k+1}>q_k>0\), and therefore
\[
F_{k+1}=(k+2)q_{k+1}>(k+1)q_k=F_k.
\]
So \((F_k)_{k\geq1}\) is strictly increasing, with no appeal to decimal approximations.

At the first boundary, direct integer arithmetic gives
\[
99\cdot35^{35}-36^{36}
=2760883512344196091513381155480932728359461417259849489>0,
\]
\[
37^{37}-99\cdot36^{36}
=22786422329847458114695986502649965850778376014129620053>0.
\]
Equivalently, \(F_{35}<99<F_{36}\). Strict monotonicity therefore proves, for every positive integer \(d\),
\[
d+1>99\gamma_d
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
F_d>99
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
d\geq36.
\]

At the second boundary, direct integer arithmetic gives
\[
198\cdot72^{72}-73^{73}
=49500723570389187363349967229331846138859963622510974848370628732820443066323165773543680033117981287574488672117583955319989756002935>0,
\]
\[
74^{74}-198\cdot73^{73}
=18877688517589217984517711163420773537129495903130836547730464527261124801920291739490368967706517790116129699815691204863518035957573642>0.
\]
Equivalently, \(F_{72}<198<F_{73}\). Strict monotonicity therefore proves, for every positive integer \(d\),
\[
d+1>198\gamma_d
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
F_d>198
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
d\geq73.
\]

Since the nonempty atlas bases lie in \(\mathbb R^n\), one has \(0\leq\rho_\perp\leq n\). Thus \(2^{n-\rho_\perp}\geq1\), and the ambient screen implies
\[
F_d>99\,2^{n-\rho_\perp}\geq99.
\]
The first exact cutoff gives \(d\geq36\). If \(\rho_\perp<n\), then the integer \(n-\rho_\perp\) is at least one, so \(2^{n-\rho_\perp}\geq2\), whence \(F_d>198\); the second exact cutoff gives \(d\geq73\). This covers both boundary cases \(\rho_\perp=n\) and \(\rho_\perp<n\).

Finally, if \(n=3\) and \(d=5\), then \(r_\perp+1\leq6\), while \(\rho_\perp\leq3\) and the accepted strict screen give
\[
r_\perp+1
>99\left(\frac56\right)^5 2^{3-\rho_\perp}
\geq99\left(\frac56\right)^5
=\frac{34375}{864}
>\frac{5184}{864}=6,
\]
a contradiction. Therefore no canonical hard-branch instance exists with \(n=3\) and \(d=5\).
