## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $0<\varepsilon\le1/10$, and $T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_d)$ have volume $V$. Put $A=\varepsilon T$, $a=\varepsilon^d$ and $\Gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. The lifted hull with fibers $A,T,T$ at $-1,0,1$ has exactly these integer fibers and mixed volume $M=(2+a)V$, with each fiber of mass strictly below $M/2$.

For $y\in T$ let $f(y)$ be its ordinary raw halfspace depth in $T$, and $P(y)$ the raw depth of $(0,y)$ in the endpoint-only measure, using all closed ambient halfspaces. The raw mixed depth of $(0,y)$ is at least $f(y)+P(y)$, but
$$
\max_{y\in T}(f(y)+P(y))=\Gamma_d V<\frac{\Gamma_d}{2}M.
$$
The mixed raw depth maxima restricted to the bases $-1,1$ are $\Gamma_d aV$ and $\Gamma_d V$, respectively. For $d=2,\varepsilon=1/10$ the separated maximum is $2/9$, the target is $67/300$, and the difference is $1/900$.

## Proof

The two endpoint bases are exposed, all continuous generators lie in $T$, and the middle generator is $T$. The base interval is $[-1,1]$, proving the census. Summing the three volumes gives $M$, and $0<a<1$ proves the strict mass comparisons. Every ambient containing cap pays at least the two separate infima on the middle and endpoint measures, respectively.

The vertical cap $z\le0$ gives $0\le P(y)\le aV$. Set $r=(1+\varepsilon)/2$, so $(A+T)/2=rT$. For $y$ outside $rT$, take a strict separating functional $u\ne0$. Then $2u\cdot y>h_A(u)+h_T(u)$, where $h_K$ denotes the support function of $K$. Choose thresholds strictly above the two support values and with sum $2u\cdot y$. They are the two traces of one ambient halfspace $u\cdot x+\beta z\ge u\cdot y$ for some real $\beta$, and both traces are empty. At a boundary point of $rT$, take a supporting functional and the two support values as thresholds. The resulting two support faces have zero $d$-volume. Thus $P(y)=0$ outside the interior of $rT$.

For $y\in rT$ the ordinary cap $\sum_i x_i\le\sum_i y_i$ has volume $V(\sum_i y_i)^d$. Therefore $f(y)+P(y)\le(r^d+a)V\le[(11/20)^2+(1/10)^2]V=5V/16<\Gamma_d V$. The strict inequality follows from $(1+1/d)^d<3$, by bounding the binomial expansion by $\sum_{j\ge0}1/j!<3$. On all of $T$, some barycentric coordinate $\lambda_i(y)\ge1/(d+1)$, so the canonical cap at its value gives $f(y)\le\Gamma_d V$. Fact 53fe12b742a7608d gives equality at the centroid. That point lies outside $rT$ because its coordinates sum to $d/(d+1)\ge2/3>r$. This proves the exact separated maximum and its strict gap.

At an endpoint, restriction to its own fiber lower bounds any cap by the ordinary raw depth there. Conversely a sufficiently large finite base tilt extends any ordinary through-point cap at that endpoint to an ambient cap excluding the other two bounded fibers. Thus the endpoint depth equals its ordinary depth and the simplex centroid bound and canonical upper caps give the stated maxima. Substitution of $V=1/2$ and $a=1/100$ gives the planar values.
