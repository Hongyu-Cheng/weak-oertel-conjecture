## Statement

Let $P$, $K_p$, and $C=\operatorname{conv}\bigcup_{p\in P}(\{p\}\times K_p)$ satisfy the strict compatibility hypotheses of the open compatibility lemma. Let $\mu_C$ be summed $d$-dimensional Lebesgue measure on its integer fibers and $M=\mu_C(\mathbb R^{n+d})$. Choose finitely many distinct $w_i=(p_i,v_i)$, $p_i\in P$, each exposed in $C$ by an ambient linear functional with unique maximizer $w_i$. Assume $v_i$ has a unique unit supporting normal $u_i$ in $K_{p_i}$, whose support face is $\{v_i\}$, and fix $h_i$ with $u_i\cdot h_i>0$. For each fixed real vector $\xi=(\xi_i)$ and every sufficiently small $\varepsilon>0$ there is an actual convex body $C_\varepsilon$ with nonempty integer fibers of positive volume exactly at $P$, converging fiberwise in Hausdorff distance to $C$. The signed measure $\mu_{C_\varepsilon}-\mu_C$ is a sum of signed restrictions of mixed volume to pairwise disjoint regions shrinking to the respective $w_i$, each of exact signed mass $\varepsilon\xi_i$. In particular $M_\varepsilon=M+\varepsilon\sum_i\xi_i$. For every fixed finite family of closed halfspaces $H$ whose boundaries avoid all $w_i$, one has
$$
\mu_{C_\varepsilon}(H)-\mu_C(H)=\varepsilon\sum_i\xi_i\mathbf1_H(w_i)
$$
for all sufficiently small $\varepsilon$. The same identity holds for parallel threshold changes of order $O(\varepsilon)$, comparing the two measures at the same shifted threshold and keeping the old indicators on the right.

## Proof

For every $\xi_i>0$, use the exact inverse in the local hull-addition lemma to choose $t_i$ with added own-fiber volume $\varepsilon\xi_i$. In each fiber adjoin all its chosen apices by taking the convex hull with its original body. By the same lemma the added pieces within a fiber are disjoint and shrink to their marked points. The new fiber bodies are uniformly Hausdorff-close to the original ones. The open compatibility lemma makes them the actual integer fibers of their lifted hull. That hull is exactly $L_\varepsilon=\operatorname{conv}(C\cup\{(p_i,v_i+t_i h_i):\xi_i>0\})$, since the lifted generating bodies use only the original fibers and the listed apices.

For $\xi_i<0$, write $\ell_i$ for the exposing functional and $m_i=\ell_i(w_i)$. On all other original integer fibers $\ell_i$ has a strict gap from $m_i$ by compactness and finiteness. In its own original fiber, the piece $\{\ell_i>m_i-s\}$ shrinks to $w_i$ as $s\downarrow0$ by unique exposure and compactness. The restriction of $\ell_i$ to that plane is nonconstant, and the original fiber has nonempty interior. Its pushed-forward volume has no atoms, and every slab between distinct levels strictly between its extremal values contains a relatively open subset of the fiber. Consequently the removed volume is continuous and strictly increasing for small $s>0$, with limit zero at $s=0$. Choose $s_i$ to remove exactly $\varepsilon|\xi_i|$. Each such trim leaves the original other fibers untouched. The finitely many removed pieces are disjoint for small $\varepsilon$ by locality. The strict gap at the other selected marks and locality of their positive additions also ensure that no trim reaches any positive addition.

Intersect $L_\varepsilon$ with the closed trim halfspaces $\ell_i\le m_i-s_i$. Its integer sections have exactly the additions and deletions just computed. For each old fiber choose an interior point $c_p$. For any fixed $0<\delta<1$, the homothet $(1-\delta)K_p+\delta c_p$ satisfies every exposing inequality with strict slack since each $c_p$ does. All these homothets are retained for small $\varepsilon$. Together with the Hausdorff convergence of the expanded fibers, this proves fiberwise convergence and positivity. The projection is still $\operatorname{conv}(P)$, since all $P$ remain occupied and the expanded hull had that projection. One can take the lifted hull of the new integer sections for $C_\varepsilon$, which has these same sections since it is contained in the trimmed body. Its affine hull is $\mathbb R^{n+d}$ because the bases span $\mathbb R^n$ and the sections have interior. The new measures differ on precisely the added and removed regions, with the prescribed signs and exact masses.

Every selected cap has a fixed strict margin at each $w_i$. The complete corresponding changed region has the same membership for small $\varepsilon$, also after an $O(\varepsilon)$ shift of threshold. Summing its exact signed volume proves both cap formulas.
