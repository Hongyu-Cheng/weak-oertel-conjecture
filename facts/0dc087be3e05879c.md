## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be compact convex with positive mixed volume, $n,d\ge1$. For a fixed nonzero linear functional $f$, let $A_z(q)=\{x\in C_z:f(z,x)\ge q\}$, $a_z(q)=|A_z(q)|$, $G_r(q)=\sum_{z\equiv r\bmod2}a_z(q)$ and $G(q)=\sum_r G_r(q)$. Put $\Phi_d(A,B)=2^{-d}(A^{1/d}+B^{1/d})^d$. Fix $s\le t$, $h=(s+t)/2$, and apply the consecutive-interval overlap coupling in each of the $p$ classes with $G_r(t)>0$ to the positive arrays at $s$ and $t$, both in the same lexicographic order. Within class $r$ every positive overlap $\pi$ of $z,w$ has a distinct integer midpoint $k=(z+w)/2$. Set $c_{rk}=\Phi_d(a_z(s),a_w(t))$ and $v_{rk}=\pi\Phi_d(G_r(s),G_r(t))$ there and zero otherwise. Writing $\Phi_r=\Phi_d(G_r(s),G_r(t))$ on surviving classes and $\Omega=\sum_r\Phi_r$, one has
$$
G(h)\ge\sum_k\max\{a_k(t),\max_r c_{rk}\}\ge Q:=\sum_k\max_r v_{rk}
=\Omega-\sum_k\big(\sum_r v_{rk}-\max_r v_{rk}\big).
$$
The maxima over no contributions are zero. For $p>0$, $\max_r\Phi_r\le Q\le\Omega$ and $Q\ge\Omega/p\ge\Omega/2^n$. Disjoint target sets across the paths give $Q=\Omega$. If a class with $G_r(t)=0$ has a nonempty $t$-trace, pairing a point of it with every positive $s$-trace gives an additional collection of targetwise contributions of sum $2^{-d}G_r(s)$, included by taking maxima. Absent traces do not supply such contributions.

## Proof

For a surviving class write $z=r+2u,w=r+2v$. Convexity with the same linear functional gives $(A_z(s)+A_w(t))/2\subseteq A_{r+u+v}(h)$. The arrays of positive compact convex sets meet the hypotheses of bff19d5abf55e3e2 with output index $u+v$. Its overlap path gives distinct output sums. The normalized overlap is bounded by each of its two interval lengths, so $a_z(s)\ge\pi G_r(s)$ and $a_w(t)\ge\pi G_r(t)$. Brunn–Minkowski and homogeneity of $\Phi_d$ give $a_k(h)\ge c_{rk}\ge v_{rk}$. Cap monotonicity also gives $a_k(h)\ge a_k(t)$, so the targetwise maximum is a valid bound. Each class path has overlap sum one, hence $\sum_k v_{rk}=\Phi_r$. Subtracting the targetwise maxima from the full sum proves the loss identity. A maximum is at most the sum, at least its largest constituent, and at least the average over $p$ classes, which gives all comparisons including the disjoint case.

For a null but nonempty $t$-trace at $w$ choose a point $b$ in it. Each positive $s$-trace at a base $z$ in that class yields $(A_z(s)+b)/2$ in the actual midpoint trace, of volume $2^{-d}a_z(s)$. These targets are distinct because $w$ is fixed. Any collisions with other contributions are again handled by the same maximum. Closed traces throughout include whole boundary fibers for pure-base cuts.
