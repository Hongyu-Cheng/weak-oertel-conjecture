## Statement

Take the all-proper affine-only case of the weighted regular singleton joint continuation with arithmetic penalty coefficient $\varepsilon^2$, and retain its notation and common separator $\eta$. Put $\Delta=\sum_i\eta_i\delta_i$, $a=\Delta/(NM)>0$, $\kappa=a+\varepsilon^2P_{\rm ar}$ and
$B(z)=\varepsilon^2(\ell_z-P_{\rm ar})-\kappa\log\ell_z$. Let $\chi_{ij}(z,X)$ be the regular old fixed-cap inward loss selector and put $W_z(X)=\sum_{i,j}\eta_i\theta_{ij}\chi_{ij}(z,X)$. The body tests of this same separator are $\sum_z\int_{\partial K_z}\Xi_z(X)v_z(X)\,dS\le0$, where
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Xi_z(X)= W_z(X)-\beta
 &+\sum_i\eta_i\delta_i\mathbf1_{\{z=p_i\}}
 \frac{[1+a_i\cdot(X-y_i)]^{-N}}{N E_{K_{p_i}}(y_i)}
 +\frac1{2M}\sum_{i,j}\eta_i\delta_i\rho_{ij}[u_{ij}\cdot(X-g_z)]^2\\
 &-\frac{N+2}{2}a+B(z).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\sum_z w_z B(z)=0$ and $\nabla^2 B=\kappa(\nabla\ell)(\nabla\ell)^{\mathsf T}/\ell^2$. For every anchored convex combination $z_0=\sum_j\lambda_j z_j$ with positive $\ell$-values bounded above by $L_{\max}$,
$$
\sum_j\lambda_j B(z_j)-B(z_0)
=\kappa[\log\ell(z_0)-\sum_j\lambda_j\log\ell(z_j)]
\ge\frac{\kappa}{2L_{\max}^2}\sum_j\lambda_j(\ell(z_j)-\ell(z_0))^2 .
$$
The Jensen gap is zero exactly when all the positive-weight $\ell(z_j)$ agree.

## Proof

Set the free parameter direction to zero in the common separator and substitute its regular fixed-cap, energy and covariance integrals. The shape-induced term $-\Delta\sum_z D_z\log\ell_z/(NM)$ and the negative shape derivative of the penalty, $-\varepsilon^2\sum_zD_z[P_{\rm ar}(1+\log\ell_z)-\ell_z]$, collect into $\sum_zD_z B(z)$. The other contributions give the displayed $\Xi_z$ term by term. The volume average is zero by the definitions of $P_{\rm ar}$ and log normalization. Differentiate $-\log\ell$ with $\ell$ affine for the Hessian. Affineness cancels the $\varepsilon^2$ term in every anchored Jensen difference. The second derivative of $-\log t$ on the relevant positive interval is at least $1/L_{\max}^2$, yielding the bound by averaging its quadratic lower supporting estimate at $\ell(z_0)$; the linear part cancels by anchoring. Strict convexity gives the equality statement.
