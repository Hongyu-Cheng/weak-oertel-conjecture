## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and write
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z).
\tag{1}
\]
For every positive-volume fiber put
\[
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,
 \qquad
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
 \qquad
 D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z.
\tag{2}
\]

Retain one common canonical zero-fresh hard system of accepted facts 6bfe50fed9a5540b and cb96640c07ffd5fa.  Thus \(Q>0\) is the mass of the half-strength complete product reservoir, the six hard-ledger quantities are nonnegative and obey
\[
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D<\frac Q{100},
\tag{3}
\]
and \(q=q^{\mathrm{sat}}=(q_e)_{e\in I}\) is the actual self-saturated capacity on its finite positive support \(I\).  For \(e=\{u_e,v_e\}\in I\), put
\[
 m_e:=\frac{u_e+v_e}{2},
 \qquad
 \bar c_e:=\frac{c_{u_e}+c_{v_e}}2,
\tag{4}
\]
\[
 D_e:=\frac12(D_{u_e}+D_{v_e}),
 \qquad
 L_e:=\bar c_e+D_e
 =\frac12(\mathcal K_{u_e}+\mathcal K_{v_e}),
\tag{5}
\]
\[
 \vartheta_e:=2^{-(d+1)}
 \left(a_{u_e}^{1/d}+a_{v_e}^{1/d}\right)^d.
\tag{6}
\]
Every \(D_e\) is an origin-symmetric full-dimensional convex body, so \(h_{D_e}\) is a norm.  The capacity has total mass and physical budgets
\[
 \Omega_b:=\sum_{e\in I}q_e>\frac{97}{100}Q,
\tag{7}
\]
\[
 \sum_{\substack{e\in I\\e\ni z}}q_e\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 \sum_{\substack{e\in I\\m_e=m}}
 \frac{q_e}{\vartheta_e}\leq1.
\tag{8}
\]

In the exact normalization of accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, define
\[
 \mathcal F(q):=
 \left\{
 v=(v_e)_{e\in I}:
 \sum_{e\in I}v_e=0,
 \quad
 \sum_{e\in I}v_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \quad
 h_{D_e}(v_e)\leq q_e\ (e\in I)
 \right\},
\tag{9}
\]
\[
 \Phi(v):=-\sum_{e\in I}h_{L_e}(v_e),
 \qquad
 \Delta_C(q):=\max_{v\in\mathcal F(q)}\Phi(v),
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 \operatorname{Opt}(q):=
 \{v\in\mathcal F(q):\Phi(v)=\Delta_C(q)\}.
\tag{11}
\]
The same duality gives
\[
 \Delta_C(q)=
 \min_{s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \sum_{e\in I}q_e
 \operatorname{dist}_{D_e}(s(m_e),L_e),
\tag{12}
\]
where
\[
 \operatorname{dist}_{D_e}(y,L_e)
 :=\inf_{\ell\in L_e}\|y-\ell\|_{D_e}.
\tag{13}
\]
Accepted fact cb96640c07ffd5fa gives the full-face saturation
\[
 h_{D_e}(\widetilde v_e)=q_e
 \qquad
 (e\in I, \widetilde v\in\operatorname{Opt}(q)).
\tag{14}
\]

Let \(k\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\).  For \(r=1,\ldots,k\), let \(F_r\subseteq I\) be an actual positive-load child family furnished at one strictly descending dyadic level by accepted facts 320dcb7d85f22889 and ed01f226ec4aa450.  Assume the families \(F_1,\ldots,F_k\) are pairwise edge-disjoint.  Let \(w^{(r)}=(w_e^{(r)})_{e\in I}\) be the corresponding collision-pooled balanced witness, extended by zero outside \(F_r\).  Thus
\[
 \sum_{e\in I}w_e^{(r)}=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{e\in I}w_e^{(r)}m_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
\tag{15}
\]
\[
 0\leq h_{D_e}(w_e^{(r)})\leq q_e
 \qquad(e\in I),
\tag{16}
\]
and
\[
 \Phi(w^{(r)})>0.
\tag{17}
\]
Put
\[
 w:=\sum_{r=1}^kw^{(r)},
 \qquad
 \omega_e(w):=h_{D_e}(w_e),
 \qquad
 \Omega(w):=\sum_{e\in I}\omega_e(w).
\tag{18}
\]

Then \(w\in\mathcal F(q)\), and the edge-disjointness gives the exact identities and inherited bounds
\[
 \omega_e(w)=\sum_{r=1}^k h_{D_e}(w_e^{(r)})\leq q_e,
\tag{19}
\]
\[
 \Omega(w)=\sum_{r=1}^k\sum_{e\in F_r}
 h_{D_e}(w_e^{(r)}),
 \qquad
 \Phi(w)=\sum_{r=1}^k\Phi(w^{(r)})>0,
\tag{20}
\]
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e(w)\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 \sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{\omega_e(w)}{\vartheta_e}\leq1.
\tag{21}
\]
All sums in (21) use the one original capacity ledger \(q\); no levelwise capacity is added more than once.

Every stress in the original optimal face has total load \(\Omega_b\).  Consequently
\[
 \boxed{
 \Omega(w)<\Omega_b
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 w\notin\operatorname{Opt}(q).}
\tag{22}
\]
In particular, every eligible child stress with
\[
 \frac Q{100}\leq\Omega(w)<\Omega_b
\tag{23}
\]
is outside the original optimal face.

Define its unsaturated mass by
\[
 \boxed{
 U_q(w):=\sum_{e\in I}\bigl(q_e-\omega_e(w)\bigr)
 =\Omega_b-\Omega(w)\geq0.}
\tag{24}
\]
Then
\[
 U_q(w)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 h_{D_e}(w_e)=q_e\quad(e\in I).
\tag{25}
\]

Choose any primal optimizer \(s_q\) in (12), and put
\[
 d_e(s_q):=\operatorname{dist}_{D_e}(s_q(m_e),L_e).
\tag{26}
\]
For every \(e\in I\), define the coordinate KKT gap
\[
 g_e^{s_q}(w):=
 q_ed_e(s_q)-\langle w_e,s_q(m_e)\rangle+h_{L_e}(w_e).
\tag{27}
\]
Then
\[
 g_e^{s_q}(w)\geq0
 \qquad(e\in I),
\tag{28}
\]
and the total gap satisfies the exact identity
\[
 \boxed{
 \Gamma_q(w):=\sum_{e\in I}g_e^{s_q}(w)
 =\Delta_C(q)-\Phi(w)\geq0.}
\tag{29}
\]
Although the individual quantities in (27) can depend on the chosen primal optimizer \(s_q\), their sum in (29) does not.  Moreover,
\[
 \boxed{
 w\in\operatorname{Opt}(q)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \Gamma_q(w)=0.}
\tag{30}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \Gamma_q(w)=0\Longrightarrow U_q(w)=0.
\tag{31}
\]
No converse \(U_q(w)=0\Longrightarrow\Gamma_q(w)=0\) is asserted.

Because of the strict hard ledger (3), none of the six named terminal inequalities with threshold \(Q/100\) can hold in the retained system.  Therefore, for the class of eligible multilevel child stresses above, the proposed face-or-terminal assertion is equivalent to proving both of the following additional implications:
\[
 \Omega(w)\geq\frac Q{100}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 U_q(w)=0,
\tag{32}
\]
\[
 \Omega(w)\geq\frac Q{100},
 \quad U_q(w)=0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Gamma_q(w)=0.
\tag{33}
\]
Equation (32) is the unsaturated-load propagation step.  Equation (33) is the angular KKT propagation step.  This lemma does not prove (32) or (33), does not convert either positive defect into \(G\), \(R\), \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\), or \(D\), and does not prove the registered parity descent or the locked target.

## Proof

Accepted fact cb96640c07ffd5fa supplies (7), (8), and the full-face saturation (14) for the actual self-saturated capacity retained from accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b.  Summing (14) over \(I\) gives
\[
 \sum_{e\in I}h_{D_e}(\widetilde v_e)
 =\sum_{e\in I}q_e=\Omega_b
 \qquad(\widetilde v\in\operatorname{Opt}(q)).
\tag{34}
\]

We first verify the multilevel normalization.  Each witness in accepted fact ed01f226ec4aa450 is supported on the actual child family furnished by accepted fact 320dcb7d85f22889, satisfies the two affine moment cancellations in (15), is coordinatewise feasible as in (16), and has the positive value (17).  Summing (15) over \(r\) proves the two moment cancellations for \(w\).

Fix \(e\in I\).  Because the supports \(F_r\) are pairwise disjoint, at most one vector \(w_e^{(r)}\) is nonzero.  Hence either \(w_e=0\), or \(w_e=w_e^{(r)}\) for the unique level using \(e\).  Positive homogeneity of the support functions then gives both equalities in (19) and the objective identity in (20).  Equation (16) proves \(h_{D_e}(w_e)\leq q_e\), so \(w\in\mathcal F(q)\).  Summing (19) proves the load identity in (20).  Since every term \(\Phi(w^{(r)})\) is positive, so is \(\Phi(w)\).

The bounds (19) and the two original capacity budgets in (8) give
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e(w)
 \leq\sum_{\substack{e\in I\\e\ni z}}q_e
 \leq\frac{a_z}{4},
\tag{35}
\]
\[
 \sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{\omega_e(w)}{\vartheta_e}
 \leq\sum_{\substack{e\in I\\m_e=m}}
 \frac{q_e}{\vartheta_e}
 \leq1.
\tag{36}
\]
Thus the union uses one restriction of the original source and target ledger rather than a sum of separate levelwise ledgers.  This proves (19)-(21).

Equation (34) proves (22), and (23) is its stated special case.  Equation (19) shows that every summand in (24) is nonnegative.  Summation gives the equality in (24), and a finite sum of nonnegative numbers is zero exactly when every summand is zero.  This proves (25).

It remains to prove the KKT identity with the correct primal normalization.  Fix any optimizer \(s_q\) of (12).  The one-coordinate support formula in accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, applied with capacity \(q_e\), is
\[
 q_e\operatorname{dist}_{D_e}(y,L_e)
 =\max_{h_{D_e}(v)\leq q_e}
 \bigl(\langle v,y\rangle-h_{L_e}(v)\bigr).
\tag{37}
\]
Use \(y=s_q(m_e)\).  Because \(w\in\mathcal F(q)\), its coordinate \(w_e\) is admissible in the maximum in (37).  Therefore
\[
 q_ed_e(s_q)
 \geq\langle w_e,s_q(m_e)\rangle-h_{L_e}(w_e),
\tag{38}
\]
which is exactly (28).

Write \(s_q(x)=Ax+b\).  The two moment cancellations for \(w\) give
\[
 \sum_{e\in I}\langle w_e,s_q(m_e)\rangle
 =\operatorname{tr}\left(
 A^{\mathsf T}\sum_ew_em_e^{\mathsf T}\right)
 +\left\langle\sum_ew_e,b\right\rangle
 =0.
\tag{39}
\]
Because \(s_q\) attains the primal minimum in (12), exact primal-dual equality gives
\[
 \sum_{e\in I}q_ed_e(s_q)=\Delta_C(q).
\tag{40}
\]
Summing (27), and then using (39), (40), and the definition of \(\Phi\), yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{e\in I}g_e^{s_q}(w)
 &=\sum_eq_ed_e(s_q)
   -\sum_e\langle w_e,s_q(m_e)\rangle
   +\sum_eh_{L_e}(w_e)\\
 &=\Delta_C(q)-\Phi(w).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{41}
\]
This proves (29).  Its right side contains no choice of \(s_q\), proving the asserted independence of the total gap.  Since \(w\in\mathcal F(q)\), definition (11) gives
\[
 \Gamma_q(w)=0
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \Phi(w)=\Delta_C(q)
 \Longleftrightarrow
 w\in\operatorname{Opt}(q),
\tag{42}
\]
which proves (30).  Combining (30) with the full-face saturation (14), then using (25), proves (31).  No converse from \(U_q(w)=0\) follows from these identities: it asserts only coordinate saturation, whereas (30) additionally requires the zero KKT gap.

Finally, (3) and nonnegativity show that each of the six named hard-ledger quantities is strictly below \(Q/100\).  Hence, within the retained hard system, the terminal branch of the proposed face-or-terminal statement is unavailable.  For every eligible \(w\) with \(\Omega(w)\geq Q/100\), that statement therefore requires \(w\in\operatorname{Opt}(q)\).  Equations (24), (30), and (31) show that this forces first \(U_q(w)=0\) and then \(\Gamma_q(w)=0\), which are (32)-(33).  Conversely, if (32)-(33) were established for the eligible class, then (30) would put every such \(w\) in \(\operatorname{Opt}(q)\), proving the face branch.  This proves the claimed exact equivalence and the stated scope limitations, without asserting either missing propagation implication.
