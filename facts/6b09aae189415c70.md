## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
 Z:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
\]
\[
 W:=\sum_{z\in Z}a_z>0,\qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,\qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C).
\]
For \(z\in Z\), let
\[
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,\qquad
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),\qquad
 D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z.
\tag{1}
\]
Every \(D_z\) is an origin-symmetric convex body containing the origin in its interior. Assume
\[
 G<\frac W4.
\tag{2}
\]

Let \(r=(r_e)\) be the half-strength canonical product reservoir of accepted fact `c81f02314cb34f8b`. Thus, if \(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\),
\[
 A_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}a_z,
\]
the class \(\xi\) is called good when \(\max_{z\equiv\xi}a_z\leq A_\xi/4\), and, on unordered distinct same-parity pairs,
\[
 r_{\{u,v\}}=
 \begin{cases}
 \dfrac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi},&u,v\equiv\xi\text{ and }\xi\text{ is good},\\[2mm]
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\tag{3}
\]
Put
\[
 Q:=|r|=\sum_er_e.
\]
Then
\[
 Q\geq\frac3{32}(W-4G)>0.
\tag{4}
\]
For a reservoir edge \(e=\{u,v\}\), put
\[
 m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2},\qquad
 \bar c_e:=\frac{c_u+c_v}{2},\qquad
 D_e:=\frac12(D_u+D_v),\qquad
 L_e:=\bar c_e+D_e=\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v).
\tag{5}
\]
Let
\[
 R:=R_C(r)
\tag{6}
\]
be the maximum affinely half-metronoid-realizable submatching mass in the exact normalization of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`.

The symbols \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), and \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\) below use their accepted normalizations. Explicitly, \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) is the residual-selector capacity of accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`. For \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), take the supremum over every exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching for source degrees \(\gamma_da_z\), and then over every nonzero positive submeasure \(\sigma\) of its centroid midpoint measure, of
\[
 2^{n-\rho_\sigma}\frac{|\sigma|}{r_\sigma+1},
\tag{7}
\]
where \(\rho_\sigma\) is the affine rank of the base support and \(r_\sigma\) is its intrinsic continuous residual rank. For \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\), for every \(m\in Z\) take one representative from each unordered reflected pair \(\{m+v,m-v\}\), set
\[
 q_v:=\frac{c_{m+v}+c_{m-v}}2,\qquad
 \alpha_v:=\min\{a_{m+v},a_{m-v}\},
\]
adjoin \(q_0=c_m\), and take
\[
 2^n\sup_{m,J\ne\varnothing}
 \frac{\gamma_da_m\mathbf1_{\{0\in J\}}+
 \gamma_{2d}\sum_{v\in J\setminus\{0\}}\alpha_v}
 {\dim\operatorname{aff}\{q_i:i\in J\}+1}.
\tag{8}
\]
These are exactly the algebraic definitions denoted \(M_{\mathrm{off}}\) and \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}\) in accepted fact `c1e3b8400d5b5b10`; no no-rank-drop or small-capacity hypothesis from that fact is imposed here. Accepted facts `be359f8a6c628483` and `a0f7746b5927f3d9` give, with these normalizations,
\[
 h_{\max}(C)\geq2^{-n}R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C),\qquad
 h_{\max}(C)\geq2^{-n}M_{\mathrm{off}}(C),\qquad
 h_{\max}(C)\geq2^{-n}R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C).
\tag{9}
\]

The reservoir is visible to the two centroid capacities in the following exact sense.

(a) There exists an exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching \(\lambda\) for the source degrees \(\gamma_da_z\) such that \(\lambda_e\geq r_e\) for every reservoir edge. Hence, with
\[
 \bar\nu_r:=\sum_er_e\delta_{(m_e,\bar c_e)},
\tag{10}
\]
every nonzero positive \(\sigma\leq\bar\nu_r\) satisfies
\[
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\geq
 2^{n-\rho_\sigma}\frac{|\sigma|}{r_\sigma+1}.
\tag{11}
\]

(b) If \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\) and \(J\) is a nonempty family of reservoir edges all having midpoint \(m\), then \(m\in Z\) and
\[
 R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\geq
 4\gamma_{2d}\,2^n
 \frac{\sum_{e\in J}r_e}
 {\dim\operatorname{aff}\{\bar c_e:e\in J\}+1}.
\tag{12}
\]

Choose the load-minimal primal-dual optimizer for \(r\) from accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`, and write
\[
 \omega_e:=h_{D_e}(v_e),\qquad
 \Omega:=\sum_e\omega_e.
\tag{13}
\]
Then \(0\leq\omega_e\leq r_e\) and
\[
 Q-R\leq\Omega\leq Q.
\tag{14}
\]
If \(\Omega=0\), then \(R=Q\). Assume henceforth that \(\Omega>0\). Put
\[
 \beta_z:=\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e,
\qquad \sum_z\beta_z=2\Omega.
\tag{15}
\]
Accepted fact `6e66cd69e25232b7`, applied to \(r\), supplies points
\[
 p_z\in\mathcal K_z\cap\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z)
 \qquad(\beta_z>0).
\tag{16}
\]
Define
\[
 p_z^\flat:=\frac{c_z+p_z}{2},\qquad
 D_*:=\bigcap_{\beta_z>0}D_z.
\tag{17}
\]
Then \(D_*\) is an origin-symmetric full-dimensional convex body and
\[
 p_z^\flat+\frac12D_z\subseteq\mathcal K_z,
 \qquad
 p_z^\flat+\frac12D_*\subseteq\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z)
 \quad(\beta_z>0).
\tag{18}
\]

For every parity class having positive load, put
\[
 L_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\beta_z.
\tag{19}
\]
Retain precisely the classes satisfying
\[
 \max_{z\equiv\xi}\beta_z\leq\frac{L_\xi}{4},
 \qquad
 L_\xi\geq\frac{A_\xi}{64}.
\tag{20}
\]
The retained load obeys
\[
 \sum_{\xi\ \mathrm{retained}}L_\xi
 \geq2\Omega-G-\frac W{64}.
\tag{21}
\]
On each retained class define the unordered off-diagonal product matching
\[
 s_{\{u,v\}}:=\frac{\beta_u\beta_v}{L_\xi}
 \qquad(u<v,\ u,v\equiv\xi),
\tag{22}
\]
and set it to zero elsewhere. Then
\[
 |s|_\xi\geq\frac{3L_\xi}{8},\qquad
 d_z(s)\leq\beta_z,\qquad
 \frac1{16}s\leq r
\tag{23}
\]
coordinatewise. For every positive edge of \(s\), put
\[
 F_e^\flat:=\frac{p_u^\flat+p_v^\flat}{2}.
\tag{24}
\]
Then
\[
 F_e^\flat+\frac12D_*\subseteq L_e.
\tag{25}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \inf_{a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \sum_es_e\mathbf1_{\{\|F_e^\flat-a(m_e)\|_{D_*}>1/2\}}
 \geq
 \frac38\left(2\Omega-G-\frac W{64}\right)-16R.
\tag{26}
\]

For a retained label \(\xi\) and target \(c\in\mathbb Z^n\), put
\[
 S_{\xi,c}:=\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_e.
\tag{27}
\]
Whenever \(S_{\xi,c}>0\), define
\[
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ:=
 \frac1{S_{\xi,c}}
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_eF_e^\flat.
\tag{28}
\]
Then the exact ordered-pair normalization in accepted fact `66a179ea8a390b19` gives
\[
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{2S_{\xi,c}}(C_c).
\tag{29}
\]
Define the within-label and labelled-output defects
\[
 I:=\sum_{\xi,c}
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}
 s_e\mathbf1_{\{\|F_e^\flat-q_{\xi,c}^\circ\|_{D_*}>1/4\}},
\tag{30}
\]
\[
 O:=\inf_{a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \sum_{\xi,c}S_{\xi,c}
 \mathbf1_{\{\|q_{\xi,c}^\circ-a(c)\|_{D_*}>1/4\}},
\tag{31}
\]
where only labels with \(S_{\xi,c}>0\) are included. Then
\[
 \boxed{
 I+O\geq
 \frac{11}{16}Q-\frac{67}{4}R-\frac{51}{128}G.}
\tag{32}
\]

Put
\[
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\qquad
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\tag{33}
\]
If \(\Omega=0\), then \(\mathcal A(C)\geq Q\). If \(\Omega>0\), the quantities \(I,O\) above are defined and satisfy the hard alternative
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal A(C)\geq\frac Q{100}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \max\{I,O\}>\frac Q4.}
\tag{34}
\]
Thus in every case either \(\mathcal A(C)\geq Q/100\), or the optimizer has positive load and \(\max\{I,O\}>Q/4\).

Finally, suppose that the retained matching \(s\) is nonzero. Define
\[
 \sigma_s:=\gamma_d\sum_es_e\delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)},
\tag{35}
\]
\[
 \rho_s:=\dim\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:s_e>0\},
\qquad
 r_s:=\min_{a\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{F_e^\flat-a(m_e):s_e>0\},
\tag{36}
\]
where \(a\) is defined on the affine hull of the midpoint bases. The measure \(\sigma_s\) is carried by \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\) and is halfspace-dominated by the mixed-volume measure of \(C\). Therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 h_{\max}(C)\geq
 \frac{\gamma_d|s|}{2^{\rho_s}(r_s+1)},
 \qquad
 D\geq
 \gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_s}\frac{|s|}{r_s+1}.}
\tag{37}
\]
No implication bounding \(I\) or \(O\) below by \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}\), \(M_{\mathrm{off}}\), \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}\), \(D\), or a packet capacity is asserted.

## Proof

Accepted fact `c81f02314cb34f8b` gives (3)-(4), as well as
\[
 d_z(r)=\frac{a_z}{4}\left(1-\frac{a_z}{A_\xi}\right)
 \quad(z\equiv\xi\text{ good}),
\tag{38}
\]
and
\[
 \sum_\xi\max_{z\equiv\xi}a_z\leq G.
\tag{39}
\]
Since \(\gamma_d>e^{-1}>1/4\), (38) gives \(d_z(r)\leq\gamma_da_z\), so \(r\) satisfies the strong degree hypothesis of accepted fact `6e66cd69e25232b7`.

We first prove centroid visibility. Fix a good class \(\xi\), abbreviate \(A=A_\xi\), and put \(x_z=a_z/A\). Define the residual degrees
\[
 \eta_z:=\gamma_da_z-d_z(r)
 =A x_z\left(\gamma_d-\frac14+\frac{x_z}{4}\right)>0.
\tag{40}
\]
Their sum is
\[
 \sum_z\eta_z
 =A\left(\gamma_d-\frac14+\frac14\sum_zx_z^2\right).
\tag{41}
\]
Goodness gives \(x_z\leq1/4\). Hence, for every \(z\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \frac1A\left(\sum_u\eta_u-2\eta_z\right)
 &=(1-2x_z)\left(\gamma_d-\frac14\right)
   +\frac14\sum_ux_u^2-\frac12x_z^2\\
 &\geq\frac12\left(\gamma_d-\frac14\right)-\frac1{64}
 =\frac{\gamma_d}{2}-\frac9{64}>0.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{42}
\]
Thus \(2\max_z\eta_z\leq\sum_z\eta_z\). The exact fractional degree-sequence criterion used in `c81f02314cb34f8b` supplies an off-diagonal matching with degrees \(\eta_z\). Adding it to \(r\) produces degree exactly \(\gamma_da_z\) at every vertex in the good class. Since the largest such source degree is at most one quarter of the class total, the minimum unmatched mass in that class is zero. On every bad class choose any exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching supplied by accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`. The classwise union is an exact global minimum-unmatched centroid matching \(\lambda\), and \(\lambda_e\geq r_e\) everywhere. Therefore (10) is a submeasure of the corresponding \(\nu_{\mathrm{off}}^\lambda\). The definition (7) proves (11).

For (12), let \(e=\{m+v,m-v\}\in J\). Convexity gives
\[
 \frac12(C_{m+v}+C_{m-v})\subseteq C_m.
\]
The left side has positive \(d\)-volume, so \(m\in Z\). Distinct unordered edges in \(J\) correspond exactly to distinct reflected directions \(\{v,-v\}\), and their reflected-cloud centroids in (8) are \(\bar c_e\). Moreover,
\[
 r_e=\frac{a_{m+v}a_{m-v}}{4A_\xi}
 \leq\frac14\min\{a_{m+v},a_{m-v}\}.
\tag{43}
\]
Use in (8) the subcloud consisting of these reflected directions and no central index. Equation (43) gives (12). Accepted fact `a0f7746b5927f3d9` and the dominated-measure theorem in `be359f8a6c628483` also verify the three depth normalizations in (9). The reference to `c1e3b8400d5b5b10` is only the exact alias and normalization map (7)-(8); none of its later conditional hard-branch conclusions is used.

Now choose the load-minimal optimizer. Equations (13)-(14) are exactly the load conclusions of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`. If \(\Omega=0\), then (14) gives \(R\geq Q\), while the definition gives \(R\leq Q\), so \(R=Q\). This proves the asserted zero-load case. Suppose \(\Omega>0\). Equations (15)-(16) are the endpoint load and simultaneous metronoid realization furnished by accepted fact `6e66cd69e25232b7`.

The set \(D_*\) is a finite intersection of origin-symmetric convex bodies each containing a neighborhood of zero, so it is itself an origin-symmetric full-dimensional convex body. Write \(p_z=c_z+d_z'\) with \(d_z'\in D_z\). If \(h=d/2\) with \(d\in D_z\), then
\[
 p_z^\flat+h=c_z+\frac{d_z'+d}{2}\in c_z+D_z=\mathcal K_z,
\]
which proves the first inclusion in (18). Also
\[
 \beta_z\leq d_z(r)\leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{44}
\]
Every point of \(\mathcal K_z=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\) remains in \(\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z)\): multiply a representing half-mass density by \(2\beta_z/a_z\leq1\), which preserves its normalized first moment. Since \(D_*\subseteq D_z\), this proves the second inclusion in (18).

We next prove the retained-load estimates. If a positive-load class violates the first inequality in (20), choose \(z_\xi\) with \(\beta_{z_\xi}>L_\xi/4\). Then, by (38),
\[
 L_\xi<4\beta_{z_\xi}
 \leq4d_{z_\xi}(r)\leq a_{z_\xi}.
\]
Summing one chosen fiber per parity class and using (39) shows that the total load discarded for this reason is at most \(G\). Among the remaining classes, those violating the second inequality in (20) have total load less than
\[
 \frac1{64}\sum_\xi A_\xi=\frac W{64}.
\]
Since the total load is \(2\Omega\), this proves (21).

Fix a retained class. Since \(\sum_z\beta_z=L_\xi\) and \(\max_z\beta_z\leq L_\xi/4\),
\[
 |s|_\xi
 =\frac{L_\xi^2-\sum_z\beta_z^2}{2L_\xi}
 \geq\frac{3L_\xi}{8},
\tag{45}
\]
and
\[
 d_z(s)=\frac{\beta_z(L_\xi-\beta_z)}{L_\xi}\leq\beta_z.
\tag{46}
\]
For a positive edge \(\{u,v\}\) in this class, (20) and (44) give
\[
 \frac{s_{uv}}{r_{uv}}
 =\frac{4A_\xi}{L_\xi}
   \frac{\beta_u}{a_u}\frac{\beta_v}{a_v}
 \leq4\cdot64\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac14=16.
\tag{47}
\]
This proves (23). If \(h\in D_*/2\), then both \(p_u^\flat+h\in\mathcal K_u\) and \(p_v^\flat+h\in\mathcal K_v\). Averaging proves (25).

Fix an affine map \(a\). If
\[
 \|F_e^\flat-a(m_e)\|_{D_*}\leq\frac12,
\]
then (25) gives \(a(m_e)\in L_e\). The submatching having weight \(s_e/16\) on precisely those edges is bounded by \(r\) and is affinely half-metronoid realizable with the common affine map \(a\), by the equivalence in accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`. Its mass is therefore at most \(R\). Thus
\[
 \sum_{e:\|F_e^\flat-a(m_e)\|_{D_*}\leq1/2}s_e\leq16R.
\tag{48}
\]
Equations (21), (45), and (48), followed by the infimum over \(a\), prove (26).

We verify the conditioned output and its normalization. On a retained class apply accepted fact `66a179ea8a390b19` with input masses \(b_z=\beta_z\), total input mass \(L_\xi\), and input points \(p_z^\flat\). For a fixed target \(c\), its ordered off-diagonal sum is
\[
 \sum_{z\ne c}\beta_z\beta_{2c-z}
 =2L_\xi S_{\xi,c}.
\tag{49}
\]
Each unordered edge is counted in its two orientations. Therefore the output mass in that accepted theorem is exactly \(2S_{\xi,c}\), and its output point is exactly (28). This proves
\[
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ\in\mathsf M_{2S_{\xi,c}}(C_c).
\]
For any \(h\in D_*/2\), equation (18) permits replacing every input point \(p_z^\flat\) by \(p_z^\flat+h\). The same conditioned construction replaces every edge midpoint by \(F_e^\flat+h\), and hence replaces the output by \(q_{\xi,c}^\circ+h\), without changing the output mass. This proves the full inclusion (29), not merely membership of its center.

Fix an affine map \(a\). For an edge in label \((\xi,c)\), the triangle inequality in the common norm \(\|\cdot\|_{D_*}\) gives
\[
 \|F_e^\flat-a(c)\|_{D_*}>\frac12
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \|F_e^\flat-q_{\xi,c}^\circ\|_{D_*}>\frac14
 \ \text{or}\
 \|q_{\xi,c}^\circ-a(c)\|_{D_*}>\frac14.
\tag{50}
\]
In the second event all edge weights in that label total \(S_{\xi,c}\). Summing (50), then taking the infimum over \(a\), and using (26), yields
\[
 I+O\geq\frac38\left(2\Omega-G-\frac W{64}\right)-16R.
\tag{51}
\]
By (14), \(\Omega\geq Q-R\). By (4),
\[
 W\leq\frac{32}{3}Q+4G.
\tag{52}
\]
Substitution in (51) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 I+O
 &\geq\frac34Q-\frac{67}{4}R-\frac38G-\frac3{512}W\\
 &\geq\left(\frac34-\frac1{16}\right)Q
 -\frac{67}{4}R
 -\left(\frac38+\frac3{128}\right)G\\
 &=\frac{11}{16}Q-\frac{67}{4}R-\frac{51}{128}G,
 \end{aligned}
\]
which is (32).

If \(\mathcal A(C)\geq Q/100\), the first branch of (34) holds. Otherwise \(R+G<Q/100\). Since \(67/4>51/128\), equation (32) gives
\[
 I+O>
 \left(\frac{11}{16}-\frac{67}{400}\right)Q
 =\frac{13}{25}Q.
\tag{53}
\]
Therefore \(\max\{I,O\}>13Q/50>Q/4\). In the zero-load case, \(R=Q\) already implies \(\mathcal A(C)\geq Q\). This proves (34).

It remains to prove (37). Every atom \((m_e,F_e^\flat)\) lies in \(C\): both endpoint points lie in their fibers and the atom is their midpoint. Let \(H\) be a closed ambient halfspace. For every edge whose atom lies in \(H\), the defining affine functional shows that at least one of \((u,p_u^\flat)\) and \((v,p_v^\flat)\) lies in \(H\); orient that edge toward one such endpoint. The total oriented \(s\)-load at \(z\) is at most \(d_z(s)\leq\beta_z\). By (18), \(p_z^\flat\in\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z)\), and accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` gives fiber halfspace mass at least \(\gamma_d\beta_z\) in every fiber section containing that endpoint point. The same bound is immediate for a whole-fiber section. Summing over the pairwise distinct fibers gives
\[
 \sigma_s(H)
 =\gamma_d\sum_{e:(m_e,F_e^\flat)\in H}s_e
 \leq\mu_C(H).
\tag{54}
\]
Thus \(\sigma_s\) is halfspace-dominated. Its mass is \(\gamma_d|s|\), and its intrinsic ranks are exactly (36). The dominated-measure theorem in accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` gives the first inequality in (37); multiplication by \(2^n\) gives the second. This completes every asserted conclusion while leaving the labelled extraction implication unclaimed.
