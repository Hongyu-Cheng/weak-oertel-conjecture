## Statement

Let $L\ge5$. Put $q=(x+y)/2$, $r=(x-y)/2$, $A_L=\{|q|\le L,\ |r|\le3/4\}$, and $B=\{x\le1,\ y\le1,\ x+y\ge-1\}$. On the three fibers $A_L,K,B$ at bases $-1,0,1$, respectively, measure a cap by the sum of the captured planar areas. Let $K$ be a planar convex body with $0\in\operatorname{int}K$. For each of the two caps $x\ge z$ and $y\ge z$, suppose that the right derivative of its captured area under every ambient linear perturbation of its margin through $(z,x,y)=(0,0,0)$ is nonnegative.

The chords of $K$ on $x=0$ and $y=0$, with endpoint coordinates $-q_i,p_i$ respectively in the varying coordinate, satisfy $p_i^2-q_i^2=9$ and $p_i>3$ for $i=1,2$. If also $(A_L+B)/2\subset K$, then $|K|>|A_L|+|B|=6L+9/2$.

## Proof

For $x\ge z$, perturb its margin by $h(wy+\beta z)$ with $h\downarrow0$. Integrate on lines of fixed $y$ in each fiber. At an interior cut $x=z$ the signed derivative of captured length is $wy+\beta z$. At an empty-side supporting cut it is $(wy+\beta z)_+$. These formulas hold at almost all coordinates on the respective cut sections. A point in the relative interior of a proper section lies in the fiber interior, and the $B$ supporting edge has an interval extending toward $x<1$ on each line through its relative interior. Lines whose intersections with the fiber miss the cut have derivative zero by compactness. The difference quotient on every line is bounded in absolute value by $|wy+\beta z|$, so dominated convergence integrates the line derivatives.

In $A_L$ the section at $x=-1$ is $-5/2\le y\le1/2$: the condition on $r$ gives these bounds and the condition on $q$ is inactive there since $L\ge5$. The length is three and the centroid coordinate is $-1$, so its derivative contribution is $-3w-3\beta$. In $K$ the cut $x=0$ is proper, and its contribution is $w(p_1^2-q_1^2)/2$. In $B$ the supporting edge $x=1$ has $-2\le y\le1$, length three and centroid coordinate $-1/2$. For any given signed $w$, choose $\beta>2|w|$ to make the hinge $wy+\beta$ positive on the entire edge. Then its contribution is $-3w/2+3\beta$. The total derivative is $w((p_1^2-q_1^2)/2-9/2)$. Nonnegativity for both signs of $w$ gives $p_1^2-q_1^2=9$. Swapping the roles of the coordinate axes uses the same endpoint geometry to prove $p_2^2-q_2^2=9$, with its actual chord in $K$. Since each $q_i>0$, both $p_i>3$.

Now assume midpoint inclusion. Put
$$
U=(4L+11)/8,\quad V=(4L-7)/8,\quad W=(4L+7)/8,\quad J=(4L+1)/8.
$$
The four points $(-U,-V),(-V,-U),(W,J),(J,W)$ belong to $K$. The first is the midpoint of the $A_L$ vertex with $(q,r)=(-L,-3/4)$ and the $B$ vertex $(-2,1)$. The third is the midpoint of the $A_L$ vertex with $(q,r)=(L,3/4)$ and $(1,1)\in B$. The other two are their coordinate swaps. The segment from $(-V,-U)$ to $(p_2,0)$ crosses $x=0$ at height $-Up_2/(V+p_2)$; hence $q_1\ge Up_2/(V+p_2)$. The symmetric construction gives $q_2\ge Up_1/(V+p_1)$. For $p=\min(p_1,p_2)$, the chord equation for the smaller endpoint and monotonicity of $t\mapsto Ut/(V+t)$ imply
$$
\sqrt{1-9/p^2}\ge U/(V+p).
$$

Define $p_H=((9/2)L+57/16)/(L+3/2)$. Then $p_H-3=3(8L-5)/(8(2L+3))>0$. Substituting and multiplying out gives
$$
(p_H^2-9)(V+p_H)^2-U^2p_H^2
=-\frac{9}{4096(2L+3)^4}\left(
16384\tau^6+495616\tau^5+5835776\tau^4+34635712\tau^3
+113407856\tau^2+215388388\tau+217321309\right),
\qquad \tau=L-5.
$$
The right side is negative for all $L\ge5$. Since $\sqrt{1-9/p^2}$ increases on $p\ge3$ and $U/(V+p)$ decreases there, the preceding inequality is possible only for $p>p_H$.

Choose $p_H<p_0<p$. The six points in the cyclic order
$$
(-U,-V),\ (-V,-U),\ (p_0,0),\ (W,J),\ (J,W),\ (0,p_0)
$$
all belong to $K$, including the axis points because the corresponding chords contain zero. Consecutive rays about zero are in strict counterclockwise order with gaps less than $\pi$: the first two lie in the third quadrant with $U>V>0$, then come the positive $x$ axis, the two first-quadrant rays with $W>J>0$, and the positive $y$ axis. The six triangles formed by zero and consecutive points lie in $K$ and have disjoint interiors because they lie in those consecutive angular sectors. The sum of their areas is half the sum of the six ordered pair determinants, namely
$$
p_0(U+J)+\frac{U^2-V^2+W^2-J^2}{2}
=(L+3/2)p_0+(3/2)L+15/16.
$$
At $p_0=p_H$ the expression is $6L+9/2$, so the actual sum is strictly larger. Finally the $(q,r)$ change of variables has absolute Jacobian two, giving $|A_L|=6L$, and $B$ is a right triangle with legs three, giving $|B|=9/2$.
