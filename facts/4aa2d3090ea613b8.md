## Statement

Retain one common actual canonical hard instance and the common notation of accepted facts d5d5de35c3f25af9, b5739a77433de383, and f83d08a46c19ca2f. Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body,
\[
 C_z:=\{y\in\mathbb R^d:(z,y)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
\tag{1}
\]
the canonical scale satisfies \(Q>0\), and the global complete metronoid-core capacity
\[
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)
\tag{2}
\]
satisfies
\[
 G<\frac{Q}{100}.
\tag{3}
\]
Use the degree tag and the off-diagonal provenance color in those accepted facts. In particular, for every positive dominant physical target \(t\), retain its dominant mass \(b_t\), load \(0\leq\omega_t\leq b_t\), ordered probability cell
\[
 \mathcal P_t:=\{(r,s):r,s\in A_{\xi(t)},\ r+s=2t\},
 \qquad
 \sum_{(r,s)\in\mathcal P_t}p_{t;r,s}=1,
\tag{4}
\]
and the off-diagonal colored matching \(0\leq\bar q^{\ne}\leq q^{\ne}\). Its unordered off-diagonal edge \(e=\{r,s\}\), \(r\ne s\), at \(t=(r+s)/2\) has
\[
 q_e^{\ne}=p_{t;r,s}\omega_t,
\tag{5}
\]
where \(p_{t;r,s}=p_{t;s,r}\), and
\[
 0<b_t\leq\frac{a_t}{4}.
\tag{6}
\]

Let \(x=(x_e)\) be any nonzero family on these off-diagonal colored edges satisfying
\[
 0\leq x_e\leq\bar q_e^{\ne}.
\tag{7}
\]
No balance equation is needed for the conclusion. Put
\[
 m_e:=\frac{r+s}{2},
 \qquad
 x_t:=\sum_{e:m_e=t}x_e,
 \qquad
 |x|:=\sum_ex_e=\sum_tx_t,
\tag{8}
\]
and let
\[
 T_x:=\{t\in\mathbb Z^n:x_t>0\}.
\tag{9}
\]
If
\[
 \dim\operatorname{aff}(T_x)\leq1,
\tag{10}
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{G\geq2|x|.}
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently the hard inequality (3) implies
\[
 \boxed{|x|<\frac{Q}{200}.}
\tag{12}
\]
The conclusion applies in particular when \(T_x\) is a rank-one lattice-convex interval, namely \(\operatorname{conv}(T_x)\cap\mathbb Z^n=T_x\), regardless of the number of targets of either parity and regardless of how nonuniform their fiber volumes are.

In the retained-mass alternative of accepted fact f83d08a46c19ca2f, if the larger color is the off-diagonal color, its balanced optimizer \(q^*\) has
\[
 |q^*|>\frac{553}{51200}Q>\frac{Q}{200}.
\tag{13}
\]
Therefore its target support necessarily has affine dimension at least two.

## Proof

We first prove the exact targetwise bound without deleting collision multiplicity. Fix \(t\) with \(x_t>0\). By (7), every contributing edge belongs to the one dominant off-diagonal cell at \(t\). The ownership formulas in accepted fact f83d08a46c19ca2f, with the ordered probabilities of accepted fact b5739a77433de383, give (5). Since the probabilities are symmetric, every unordered off-diagonal pair corresponds to its two ordered orientations. Hence
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 x_t
 &\leq\sum_{e:m_e=t}q_e^{\ne}\\
 &=\frac{\omega_t}{2}
   \sum_{\substack{(r,s)\in\mathcal P_t\\r\ne s}}
   p_{t;r,s}\\
 &\leq\frac{\omega_t}{2}
 \leq\frac{b_t}{2}
 \leq\frac{a_t}{8}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]
The last inequality is exactly the dominant-cell target-fiber bound in accepted fact d5d5de35c3f25af9. Equation (14) counts all edges colliding at \(t\), but counts the physical target fiber \(C_t\) only once.

Let
\[
 I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
 \qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z,
 \qquad
 A:=\operatorname{aff}(T_x),
\tag{15}
\]
and use the slice notation of accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c:
\[
 D_A:=I_+\cap A,
 \qquad
 B:=\operatorname{aff}(D_A),
 \qquad
 q:=\dim B,
 \qquad
 W_A:=\sum_{z\in D_A}a_z.
\tag{16}
\]
Equation (14) implies \(T_x\subseteq I_+\), so \(D_A\ne\varnothing\). Moreover, (10) gives
\[
 q\leq\dim A\leq1.
\tag{17}
\]
Summing (14) over the distinct targets gives the single-use volume charge
\[
 W_A
 \geq\sum_{t\in T_x}a_t
 \geq8\sum_{t\in T_x}x_t
 =8|x|.
\tag{18}
\]

We now apply the accepted rank-at-most-two affine-slice complete-core theorem 8aac56c49321466c only in its cases \(q=0,1\). Its exact constants are
\[
 \alpha_0=1,
 \qquad
 \alpha_1=\frac14,
\tag{19}
\]
and it constructs an intrinsic core tuple of mass at least \(\alpha_qW_A\). If \(B=\mathbb R^n\), that accepted theorem gives directly
\[
 G\geq\alpha_qW_A\geq\frac14W_A.
\tag{20}
\]
If \(B\subsetneq\mathbb R^n\), its one-sided augmentation gives
\[
 G\geq
 \min\left\{
 \alpha_qW_A+\frac{W-W_A}{2},
 \ 2^{n-q}\alpha_qW_A
 \right\}.
\tag{21}
\]
Here \(W\geq W_A\), \(n\geq q\), and \(\alpha_q\geq1/4\), so both entries in the minimum in (21) are at least \(W_A/4\). Thus (20) holds in this case as well. Combining (18) and (20) proves
\[
 G\geq\frac14W_A\geq2|x|,
\]
which is (11).

Equations (3) and (11) immediately give
\[
 2|x|\leq G<\frac Q{100},
\]
and hence (12). The proof used only the affine-rank assumption (10), so the stated lattice-convex interval case follows without an additional hypothesis. Finally, (13) is the exact retained-mass lower bound in f83d08a46c19ca2f, and \(553/51200>1/200\) because \(553>256\). Therefore (12) rules out affine target rank zero or one for that off-diagonal balanced optimizer. This proves every assertion.
