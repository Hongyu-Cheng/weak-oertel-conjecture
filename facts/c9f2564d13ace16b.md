## Statement

Let $K=\operatorname{conv}\{v_0,v_1,v_2\}$ with $v_0=(-1/3,-1/3)$, $v_1=(2/3,-1/3)$ and $v_2=(-1/3,2/3)$. Put $a=1/8$, $c=257/256$ and $s=(-223/1536,-223/1536)$. The lifted convex hull of $s+aK,K,s+cK$ at bases $-1,0,1$ has exactly those three integer fibers, with areas $1/128,1/2,66049/131072$ and original mixed mass $M=132609/131072$. For $t=M/2$, a dominated mixed submeasure of mass $t$ with integer base mean $j$ exists only at $j=0$, also the unique weighted marginal median. The set of continuous means of all such submeasures at $j=0$ is a planar convex body whose ordinary area centroid is
$$
y=(-1784/397827,-1784/397827).
$$
The closed ambient cap $-x_1-x_2\ge 1/125+(91357/96000)z$ contains every $(0,h,h)$ with $h\le-1/250$, in particular $(0,y)$. It has original mixed mass $4045201/18000000$, smaller than $2M/9=44203/196608$ by $872713/9216000000$. The ordinary centroid of the feasible fixed-half-mass moment body therefore fails the normalized depth target $2/9$, even with free choice of feasible integer base.

## Proof

Write $s=\kappa v_0$ with $\kappa=1-(a+c)/2=223/512$. The endpoint midpoint body is $\kappa v_0+(1-\kappa)K\subseteq K$. Every point in the lifted hull at base zero is a convex combination of points of $K$ and these endpoint midpoints. The two extreme fibers are preserved and the base projection is $[-1,1]$. A full triangle and any point in a distinct fiber span the ambient space. Scaling areas gives the stated masses and $t=132609/262144$. Both endpoint masses are less than $t$, and an extreme base mean forces all selected mass into that endpoint. At base zero choose equal endpoint masses $m=1/128$, leaving feasible middle mass $m_0=t-2m\in(0,|K|)$. The weighted median is zero because both outside masses are less than half and the two inclusive middle sides have mass greater than half.

Let $Z_v(K)$ be the set of means of densities bounded by $\mathbf1_K$ and of mass $v$. The mixed balanced moment body is compact convex by weak compactness of dominated measures on the compact lifted source and continuity of the mass and moment constraints. Its points are in $K$ because each is the continuous coordinate of a barycenter in the lifted hull with base zero. To compute the support of the moment body, fix $u\ne0$. In a fiber $L$, write $R_L(v)$ for the maximum of the raw moment in direction $u$ over dominated measures of mass $v$. If $q$ is a top-cap threshold for mass $v_*$, the pointwise sign of $(u\cdot x-q)(f-\mathbf1_{\{u\cdot x\ge q\}})$ for $0\le f\le1$ gives
$$
R_L(v)\le R_L(v_*)+q(v-v_*).
$$
This includes $v_*=|L|$ with $q=\min_L u\cdot x$. For interior masses the thresholds exist by continuity and strict monotonicity of the projection distribution inside its support.

Write $b=\min_K u\cdot x$ and $L_u=\max_K u\cdot x-b>0$. In normalized direction coordinates in $[0,1]$, a top cap of relative area $p$ has threshold at least $1-\sqrt p$, because the homothet about a maximizing vertex with linear scale $\sqrt p$ is contained in the cap at that threshold. A bottom cap of relative area $\delta$ likewise has threshold at most $\sqrt\delta$, using a minimizing vertex.

An arbitrary feasible pair of endpoint selected masses must have a common value $\ell\le m$. At $\ell=m$ the three top-cap thresholds $q_-,q_0,q_+$ select masses $m,m_0,m$ at the respective fibers. The preceding exchange bounds show that changing $\ell$ from $m$ raises the three-fiber raw support by at most $(q_-+q_+-2q_0)(\ell-m)$. Here $p=a^2/c^2$ in the larger endpoint and $\delta=1-m_0/|K|=2559/131072$ in the middle. Thus
$$
q_-+q_+-2q_0
\ge (2-a-c)(u\cdot v_0-b)+L_u(c-a-2\sqrt\delta)>0,
$$
where the first term is nonnegative and $(c-a)^2-4\delta=45507/65536>0$ with $c>a$. Every nonzero support direction therefore maximizes at $\ell=m$. The fixed-$\ell$ moment body at that value is, by adding the three independent selected moments and dividing by $t$,
$$
\frac{2m}{t}s+\frac{mc}{t}Z_{m/c^2}(K)+\frac{m_0}{t}Z_{m_0}(K).
$$
Equality of support functions gives equality with the whole balanced body. The middle term of mass $m_0$ in $K$ has a full-dimensional mean body: homothetic copies $(1-\theta)x+\theta K\subseteq K$ of area $m_0$, where $x\in K$ and $\theta=(m_0/|K|)^{1/2}<1$, have centroids $(1-\theta)x$. Thus the balanced body has positive area. Every linear permutation of the three vertices of the centered triangle preserves area and both unshifted mean bodies. Their scaled Minkowski sum has its ordinary area centroid fixed by the same maps. The only common fixed vector is zero, since averaging the cyclic vertex permutations annihilates each vertex and hence the plane. The centroid is therefore $(2m/t)s=y$, with each coordinate below $-1/250$ because $1784\cdot250>397827$.

For the asserted halfspace write $u=(-1,-1)$. On $K$ the range of $u\cdot x$ is $[-1/3,2/3]$. At $z=1$ the threshold $1/125+91357/96000=737/768$ equals $\max_{s+cK}u\cdot x$ and captures just one vertex of zero area. At $z=-1$ the threshold $-90589/96000$ is below $\min_{s+aK}u\cdot x=191/768$, so the whole small fiber is retained. The middle cap is homothetic about the maximizing vertex with scale $2/3-1/125=247/375$, since the projection range has length one. Its area plus the small fiber's area is $1/128+(247/375)^2/2=4045201/18000000$. Subtraction from $2M/9$ gives the stated strict deficit. Since $-2h\ge1/125$ for every $h\le-1/250$, the very same cap proves the full conditional cap certificate as well.
