## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}\), let \(D\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{n+d}\) be a convex body, and let \(H\) and \(\bar H\) be complementary closed halfspaces with a common boundary hyperplane. Thus, for some \(\mathbf a\in\mathbb{R}^{n+d}\setminus\{\mathbf 0\}\) and \(c\in\mathbb{R}\),
\(H=\{\mathbf x:\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf x\geq c\}\) and \(\bar H=\{\mathbf x:\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf x\leq c\}\).
Then there exists \(\mathbf t\in\mathbb{R}^{n+d}\) such that either \(\mathbf t+\frac12D\subseteq D\cap H\) or \(\mathbf t+\frac12D\subseteq D\cap\bar H\). More explicitly, choose \(\mathbf y^+,\mathbf y^-\in D\) maximizing and minimizing \(\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y\) on \(D\), respectively, and put
\(\theta=\frac12(\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y^++\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y^-)\).
If \(c\leq\theta\), one may take \(\mathbf t=\frac12\mathbf y^+\); if \(c\geq\theta\), one may take \(\mathbf t=\frac12\mathbf y^-\). When \(c=\theta\), both stated choices and both corresponding inclusions are valid.

## Proof

Because \(D\) is compact, the continuous functional \(\mathbf y\mapsto\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y\) attains its maximum and minimum on \(D\), so the stated points \(\mathbf y^+\) and \(\mathbf y^-\) exist. Define
\(D^+=\frac12D+\frac12\mathbf y^+\) and \(D^-=\frac12D+\frac12\mathbf y^-\).
For every \(\mathbf y\in D\), convexity gives \(\frac12\mathbf y+\frac12\mathbf y^+\in D\) and \(\frac12\mathbf y+\frac12\mathbf y^-\in D\). Hence \(D^+\subseteq D\) and \(D^-\subseteq D\).

Suppose \(c\leq\theta\). Every \(\mathbf x\in D^+\) has the form \(\mathbf x=\frac12\mathbf y+\frac12\mathbf y^+\) for some \(\mathbf y\in D\). By the minimizing property of \(\mathbf y^-\),
\(\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf x=\frac12\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y+\frac12\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y^+\geq\frac12\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y^-+\frac12\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y^+=\theta\geq c\).
Thus \(D^+\subseteq H\), and therefore \(\frac12\mathbf y^++\frac12D=D^+\subseteq D\cap H\).

Suppose \(c\geq\theta\). Every \(\mathbf x\in D^-\) has the form \(\mathbf x=\frac12\mathbf y+\frac12\mathbf y^-\) for some \(\mathbf y\in D\). By the maximizing property of \(\mathbf y^+\),
\(\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf x=\frac12\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y+\frac12\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y^-\leq\frac12\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y^++\frac12\mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf y^-=\theta\leq c\).
Thus \(D^-\subseteq\bar H\), and therefore \(\frac12\mathbf y^-+\frac12D=D^-\subseteq D\cap\bar H\).

Every real \(c\) satisfies at least one of \(c\leq\theta\) and \(c\geq\theta\), which proves the claimed alternative. If \(c=\theta\), both inequalities hold, so both inclusions follow. All boundary cases are covered because \(H\) and \(\bar H\) are closed and are defined by weak inequalities.
