## Statement

The full-dimensional planar shadow system
$$
C_t=\operatorname{conv}\{(-1,-1),(-1,1),(1,-1+2t),(1,1-2t)\},\qquad 0\le t\le1,
$$
has normalized maximal ordinary depth $1/2$ at each endpoint and $4/9$ at $t=1/2$. Thus normalized maximal ordinary depth need not be quasiconcave along a shadow system of moving points with a common velocity direction.

## Proof

The four generating points move affinely in the vertical direction, and the hull always contains a nondegenerate segment at first coordinate $-1$ and a point at first coordinate $1$. Thus the collision of the two right generators at $t=1/2$ leaves a full-dimensional hull. The endpoint hulls are the same square. Its center bisects all through-center lines by central symmetry, and at every point the two closed halfplanes through it in opposite directions have total normalized area one because their common boundary is null. Hence the square has maximal depth $1/2$. At $t=1/2$ the hull is the nondegenerate triangle with vertices $(-1,-1),(-1,1),(1,0)$. By the simplex direction of ea91e021ff1f4416 in ordinary dimension two, its normalized maximum is $4/9$.
