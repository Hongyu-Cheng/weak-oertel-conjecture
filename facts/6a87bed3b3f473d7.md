## Statement

In $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$, let
$$
C=\{(z,x):0\le z\le2,\ |x|\le\sqrt z\},\qquad
D=\{(z,x):0\le z\le3/2,\ |x|\le\sqrt z-z/2\}.
$$
The identity is a determinant maximum among base-preserving affine ambient fits of $D$ into $C$. Their only ambient contact is $(0,0)$, where every supporting normal has continuous component zero. Consequently no stress supported on these ambient contacts has continuous tensor one. The only positive integer fiber of $D$ is $[-1/2,1/2]$ at $z=1$. Retaining $C_1=[-1,1]$ in an ambient convex body inside $C$ doubles the mixed mass with true integer-base centroid and local normalized closed-halfspace depth $1/2$.

## Proof

Both width functions are concave and nonnegative on the respective base intervals, positive for interior positive bases. This gives compact ambient convex bodies and $D\subset C$. If $(z,x)\mapsto(z,Tx+Bz+b)$ fits $D$ inside $C$, the point at $z=0$ gives $b=0$. For $0<z\le3/2$ the image interval width is at most the target width, so $|T|(\sqrt z-z/2)\le\sqrt z$. Sending $z$ down to zero gives $|T|\le1$. The identity attains the maximal determinant.

At each $z>0$ of $D$ the continuous bound is strictly smaller than the target's and $z<2$, so the point is interior to $C$. At $(0,0)$ let $(a,u)$ be a supporting normal oriented to maximize its linear functional there. The points $(t^2,t)$ and $(t^2,-t)$ are in $C$ for small $t>0$. Thus $at^2+|u|t\le0$ and letting $t\downarrow0$ after division by $t$ gives $u=0$. Every continuous contact gradient and hence every continuous tensor from these contacts is zero.

The integer base $0$ contributes zero length, and the only positive integer fiber of $D$ is the stated unit-length interval at $1$. The ambient convex body $C\cap\{1/2\le z\le3/2\}$ has precisely one integer fiber, $C_1$, of length two. Its mixed measure is supported on that interval, whose centered uniform measure has true centroid $(1,0)$ and normalized closed-halfspace depth $1/2$. This gives the actual doubled extraction.
