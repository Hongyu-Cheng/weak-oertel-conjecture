## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $m=d+1$, and let $v_0,\ldots,v_d$ span $\mathbb R^d$ with sole dependence generated by $\sum_i v_i=0$. Put $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$. Let $A,K,B$ be the positive-volume compact convex fibers at exactly $-1,0,1$, with $B\subseteq T$, $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$ and $0\in\operatorname{int}K$. Suppose each cap $H_i=\{(z,x):v_i\cdot x\ge z\}$ attains the genuine raw depth $Q$ at $(0,0)$, each cut on $A$ is proper, and each $B$ capture has zero $d$-volume. Put $M=|A|+|K|+|B|$ and $\Gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. If every closed corner $C_j=A\cap\bigcap_{i\ne j}\{v_i\cdot x\le-1\}$ is nonempty, then $Q/M>3\Gamma_d/5>\Gamma_d/2$. Consequently if $Q\le\Gamma_d M/2$, there is a closed corner $C_j$ which is empty and a probability vector $\lambda$ on the indices $i\ne j$ such that the nonzero averaged normal $u=\sum_{i\ne j}\lambda_i v_i$ satisfies $\min_Au\cdot x>-1$.

## Proof

Write $b=|B|$, $U_i=|A\cap\{v_i\cdot x\ge-1\}|$ and $q_i=|K\cap\{v_i\cdot x\ge0\}|$. Closed-corner containment gives $-T\subseteq A$, so every $U_i\ge |T|\ge b$. The zero $B$ captures and exact three-fiber mass give $Q=U_i+q_i$. Apply 03c59c70402b66a2 to the cap with $u=v_i$, $y=0$, and integer coefficient $-1$. Its hypotheses hold for these actual full-dimensional compatible fibers and the genuine raw minimizer. Since the $B$ capture is zero, its finite-tilt inequality gives $U_i\le2q_i$. Hence $U_i\le2Q/3$ and $q_i\le Q-b$ for every $i$.

Both the family $\{v_i\cdot x\ge-1\}$ and the family $\{v_i\cdot x\ge0\}$ cover $\mathbb R^d$ by the zero sum. In each of $A$ and $K$, choose a respective cap containing the body's centroid. The centroid halfspace inequality gives $\Gamma_d|A|\le2Q/3$ and $\Gamma_d|K|\le Q-b$. Adding with $b$ yields
$$
M\le\frac{5Q}{3\Gamma_d}-\frac{1-\Gamma_d}{\Gamma_d}\,b
<\frac{5Q}{3\Gamma_d}.
$$
Here $b>0$ and $0<\Gamma_d<1$. [Grünbaum's centroid bound, Theorem 2 and its proof](https://msp.org/pjm/1960/10-4/pjm-v10-n4-p18-s.pdf).

For the final consequence, if $Q\le\Gamma_d M/2$, some closed $C_j$ is empty by the proved bound. Map $A$ into $\mathbb R^d$ by the coordinates $v_i\cdot x+1$ for $i\ne j$. The image is a compact convex set disjoint from the closed negative orthant. Take a nearest pair $a,o$ in these two sets, and put $w=a-o\ne0$. The nearest-pair inequalities give $w\cdot a'\ge w\cdot a$ on the image and $w\cdot o'\le w\cdot o$ on the orthant. Testing the unbounded negative coordinate rays gives $w\ge0$, and testing $0$ and $2o$ gives $w\cdot o=0$. Thus every image point has $w\cdot a'\ge\|w\|^2>0$. Normalize $w$ by its positive coordinate sum to obtain the claimed probability vector. The $d$ retained normals are independent by the sole-dependence hypothesis, so $u\ne0$.
