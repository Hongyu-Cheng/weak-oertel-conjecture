## Statement

Use the hypotheses and notation of the saturated fixed-facet two-rate descent theorem. For each branch $\theta\in\Theta$ put
$$
\begin{aligned}
V_\theta&=\sum_i w_i^\theta\theta_i\sigma_i v_i,\\
C_\theta&=daQ\,W_\theta-d(a+k)\sum_i w_i^\theta U_i
-\sum_i w_i^\theta\theta_i\sigma_i[(a+k)\beta_i+a h_i].
\end{aligned}
$$
There are actual contraction centers $p\in A,q\in B$ and nonnegative rates giving the two strict normalized decreases if and only if
$$
\min_{\theta\in\Theta}\bigl[C_\theta-(a+k)h_A(-V_\theta)-a h_B(-V_\theta)\bigr]<0
\quad\text{or}\quad
\frac{\sum_i w_i^{\theta^\infty}\theta_i^\infty\sigma_i h_i+
 h_B(-V_{\theta^\infty})}{W_{\theta^\infty}}>drb.
$$
Both optimizations over the actual centers attain their extrema.

## Proof

The boundary trial loss is positive. The lower-envelope identity in the saturated menu loss-envelope lemma therefore applies with $\ell=daQ$, $u=a+k$, $v=a$. Substituting $c_i=\beta_i-v_i\cdot p$ and $e_i=h_i-v_i\cdot q$ gives
$$
\Phi(daQ;a+k,a)=\min_{\theta\in\Theta}
[C_\theta+(a+k)V_\theta\cdot p+aV_\theta\cdot q].
$$
The minimum over the finite branch set commutes with the minimum over compact $A\times B$. On each branch both linear forms minimize at support points in the same direction $-V_\theta$, and their two minima are the negative support terms in the statement. If a formal branch is not active at those points, the actual trial is smaller still, so any strict negative witness is valid. Conversely a negative actual trial has a minimizing branch there. For the other alternative, $\theta^\infty$ is fixed by the initial cut statuses, and maximizing its $\mathcal J_{\theta^\infty}(q)$ over $B$ gives the second displayed expression by the same support identity. The fixed-center descent test is a disjunction, so existence of actual centers requires either optimized alternative.
