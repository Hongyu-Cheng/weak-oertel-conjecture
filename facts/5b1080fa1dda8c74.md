## Statement

Use the shared original cap coordinates with finitely many actual positive compact convex fibers $K_z$. For one original cap $i$, assume its cuts are proper or strictly full or empty. For a reference group $g_0$ define its full mixed section force by
$$
R_i^{g_0}=\sum_z\int(x-q_{g_0}(z))\,d\tau_z,\qquad
d\tau_z=\|u_i\|^{-1}d\mathcal H^{d-1}\big|_{K_z\cap\{u_i\cdot x+c_i\cdot(z,1)=0\}},
$$
with zero terms on strict full/empty fibers and counting measure for $d=1$. For a representable differentiable path the cap mass derivative is
$$
\dot u_i\cdot R_i^{g_0}+
\sum_z\tau_z(K_z)\bigl(\dot F_{g_0}(z)-u_i\cdot\dot q_{g_0}(z)\bigr).
$$
Keeping all group $Q_g,f_g$ fixed, every $v\in\ker M_i$ gives an exact cover-preserving path moving this original alone with derivative $v\cdot R_i^{g_0}$. For a private cap take $\ker M_i=\mathbb R^d$. The projection of the force to $\ker M_i$ is reference-independent. It is zero exactly when the cap is stationary under all these paths. Otherwise one such path strictly decreases its mass with all other originals fixed.

## Proof

Apply the full ambient coarea derivative of 1269c554ca9a41b8 §2, since all positive sections are proper. Differentiating the represented margin on a section yields
$$
\dot u_i\cdot(x-q_{g_0}(z))+\dot F_{g_0}(z)-u_i\cdot\dot q_{g_0}(z).
$$
The shared equations ensure this is the same physical margin derivative from every occurrence. In particular $R_i^g-R_i^{g_0}=-(Q_g-Q_{g_0})\sum_z(z,1)\tau_z(K_z)$ lies in $\operatorname{range}M_i^{\mathsf T}$.

Take $u_i(t)=u_i+tv$ and $c_i(t)=f_{g_0}-Q_{g_0}^{\mathsf T}u_i(t)$. If $v\in\ker M_i$, the unchanged right sides of the shared systems give exact consistency for both signs of sufficiently small $t$. Every circuit persists and all projected cover inequalities are unchanged. The derivative formula now reads $v\cdot R_i^{g_0}$. The negative projected force gives strictly negative derivative when nonzero; vanishing on the entire kernel is precisely membership of $R_i^{g_0}$ in $\operatorname{range}M_i^{\mathsf T}$. If the raw normal must also remain unit, its first-order velocities are restricted to the tangent intersection with $u_i^\perp$, whose annihilator is $\operatorname{range}M_i^{\mathsf T}+\operatorname{span}(u_i)$. Positive normalization after a physical motion keeps the halfspaces but may change the raw equal-margin data.

In the five-cap construction the shared cap $x\ge0$ has sections of length $1/2$ at both bases and $\|u_i\|=1$. For reference $A$, the displacement in the $x$ coordinate is $z$, and the $y$ moments cancel individually by symmetry. Hence $R_i^A=(1/2,0)$ lies in the shared row space, with zero projection to the $y$ axis. Each exact kernel path $x+ty\ge0$ bisects both centered squares. Thus stationarity of the full cap does not force zero unprojected reference displacement.
