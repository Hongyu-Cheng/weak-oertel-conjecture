## Statement

There is an actual mixed source of six planar square fibers at bases $(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(2,1),(1,2)$ and a twelve-cap certificate, grouped into four positive rank-two circuits, whose reversed polytope is bounded, full-dimensional and has four indispensable projected facets for integer exclusion, each with strict cross-group contact. For centered square radii $r=1/1000$ on the first three bases and $R=1/100$ on the last three, set $v=4r^2$. In the order of the proof the four cap-mass rows in units of $v$ are $(3,0,0),(3,0,0),(3,0,1/2),(103,100,100)$ and the total mixed mass is $M=303v$. The cross-group correction is $4v$ and $\sum_s\max_j\mu(H_{s,j})-(4/9)M=-68v/3$. Keeping the caps and $R$ fixed, for $0<r<R/2$ the same incidences and the same half-square trace give
$$
\sum_s\max_j\mu(H_{s,j})-(4/9)M=(128r^2-4R^2)/3.
$$
The derivative is $256r/3$ and the sign crosses zero at $R/\sqrt{32}$. In the calibrated case the raw $104v$-depth region is empty.

## Proof

Take the lifted convex hull of the six squares. The projection is the polygon $z_1,z_2\ge0$, $|z_1-z_2|\le1$, $z_1+z_2\le3$, with the six listed integer points by direct enumeration. Five are its vertices and keep their prescribed fibers. At $(1,1)$ the prescribed square is maximal and contains all generators in the continuous coordinate, so it cannot enlarge. Thus the listed squares are the exact integer fibers. The projection and the fibers have interior in their respective spaces, and the hull is full-dimensional.

Let $u_1=(1,0),u_2=(0,1),u_3=(-1,-1)$, and take the four affine functions
$$
(\ell_0,\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)=
(1/10-z_1-z_2,\ z_1-2z_2-9/10,\ -2z_1+z_2-9/10,\ z_1+z_2-19/10).
$$
Define $q_s$ by the following rows applied to $(z_1,z_2,1)$ and divided by $100$:
$$
\begin{array}{c|rrr|rrr}
s&\multicolumn{3}{c|}{q_{s,1}}&\multicolumn{3}{c}{q_{s,2}}\\
0&236&236&-520&-267&31&260\\
1&225&197&-565&-192&139&203\\
2&197&225&-565&-336&-33&363\\
3&271&271&-656&-371&100&328
\end{array}
$$
The closed cap $H_{s,j}$ has margin $u_j\cdot(x-q_s(z))+\ell_s(z)\ge0$. Each group has the positive dependence with weights $1/3$ and rank two. The reversed polytope projects into the bounded set where all $\ell_s\le0$. Any integer point of this set would have $z_1+z_2=1$, then $z_1\le0$ and $z_1\ge1$ by the two middle inequalities. For each $s$, deleting its group leaves its corresponding binary base $(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$ feasible: at the base, the remaining groups require the two coordinate upper bounds $\min_{t\ne s}(q_{t,i}-\ell_t)$ and the sum lower bound $\max_{t\ne s}(q_{t,1}+q_{t,2}+\ell_t)$. Subtracting the latter from the sum of the former gives, in order, $11/10,133/100,133/100,207/50$. Each is positive.

At the four base points $(1/20,1/20),(19/20,1/40),(1/40,19/20),(19/20,19/20)$, choose $x=q_s(z)$ for its corresponding group. Substitution makes its own three margins zero and every other margin at most $-923/2000$. Consequently $x=q_s(z)$ remains feasible locally whenever $\ell_s(z)\le0$, proving that each constraint supports an actual projected facet. The average of the four contacts makes every margin strictly negative, proving full dimension. Boundedness follows from the bounded base set and the two upper coordinate bounds and one lower sum bound, which bound both coordinates.

For the mass calculation, at each base and cap evaluate the center margin $\ell_s(z)-u_j\cdot q_s(z)$. In the order of the six bases, the sign rows for the three caps in each group are as follows:
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
s&j=1&j=2&j=3\\
0&+++---&------&------\\
1&+++---&------&------\\
2&+++---&------&--0---\\
3&++++--&----+-&-----+
\end{array}
$$
Every nonzero center margin has magnitude greater than $(|u_{j,1}|+|u_{j,2}|)$ times the corresponding maximal radius $1/200$ or $1/100$, by the displayed coefficients. Thus it is fully present or absent on the whole square. The zero cuts the centered square into equal areas. This gives the mass rows at $R=1/100,r=1/1000$. The first three group unions are the three small fibers, and the fourth is the whole source. If $m$ counts these group unions, $\int_S(m-1)\,d\mu=9v$, giving cross-group correction $(4/9)9v=4v$. The full surplus is $112v-(4/9)303v=-68v/3$. For general $r$ in the specified range, the four maxima are $3v,3v,3v,3v+4R^2$ and $M=3v+12R^2$, giving the polynomial, its derivative and its zero.

At raw budget $104v$, every listed cap has smaller mass, so the depth region would be contained in the reversed polytope, hence in $\{z_1+z_2\le19/10\}$. This closed pure-base cap has only the three small fibers, of mass $3v<104v$, so no point of that region exists.
