## Statement

Use the rectangle, roof $\ell$, and source minor $J$ of the two-facet contact example. Let $R$ be the unique root in $(24/5,481/100)$ of $67R^3-735R^2+2685R-3365=0$. For the canonical mixed family with continuous dimension three and fibers $(R-|q(z)|)[-1/2,1/2]^3$, write $V_z=(R-|q(z)|)^3$ and $M=\sum V_z$. There is an $\ell$-tight martingale from $m(\delta_{p_0}+\delta_{p_1})$ to $\sum_z(V_z/\ell(z))\delta_z$, with $M=8m$. The contact cover still has minimum parity cost eight, while each crease singleton has volume exceeding $M/4$ by more than $13376977/6000000$. Multiplying $\ell$ by any positive scalar divides both marginals by that scalar and preserves contact and tightness.

## Proof

The cubic's derivative is $21$ at $4$ and has derivative $402R-1470>0$ on $[4,5]$. The cubic's values at $24/5$ and $481/100$ are $-217/125$ and $887447/1000000$. The root is unique in $[4,5]$. The radius $R-|q|$ is positive, concave and affine on the same two polygons with lattice vertices. This makes the hypograph family of homothetic centered cubes convex, and interpolation at those vertices gives every real-base fiber as an exact Minkowski sum of sampled fibers. Thus the family is canonical and its stated volumes are exact.

Put
$$
a=(R-4)^3,\quad b=(R-3)^3/2,\quad c=(R-2)^3/3,\quad e=(R-1)^3/4,\quad g=R^3/5.
$$
The cubic identity is equivalent to $g=3a+2b+2c$. On the seven left sites of the preceding construction put masses $(a,b,c,c,e,2a+b+c,a+b+c)$. They have total $m=4a+3b+4c+e$. Their moments are $-(a+b+c)+(a+b+c)=0$ and $-a+c-c+(2a+b+c)-(a+b+c)=0$. Use the reflected row at $p_1$. Each strict-side target receives exactly its own $V_z/\ell(z)$ by substitution. Each crease receives $3a+2b+2c=g$. The row $\ell$ means are both $4$ by affineness on the two facets, so $M=8m$. The cover minimum uses the identical $B,P,\ell,J$ in the preceding draft. At a crease the excess is $5g-2m=7a+4b+2c-2e$. Applying the lower root bound to the positive terms and the upper bound to the negative term gives
$$
7a+4b+2c-2e>
7(4/5)^3+2(9/5)^3+(2/3)(14/5)^3-(1/2)(381/100)^3
=13376977/6000000.
$$
A positive rescaling does not change the contact faces and rescales each row and its target by the inverse factor, preserving the martingale and the row equality.
