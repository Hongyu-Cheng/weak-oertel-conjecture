We now check the adaptive estimates and independence from the unrestricted field. For \(a\ge0\), let \(\mu_{a\lambda}\) be the Boolean product law on all spatial coordinates with parameter \(a\lambda\). At a child block \(v\), put \(A_v=\sum_i|\lambda_{vi}|\). Thus a prescribed forward or inverse label has conditional probability at most \(e^a\delta\); a one-step failure at most \(e^a\delta\), and a two-predecessor failure at most \(2e^a\delta\).

On a stopped forward path let \(S_{\mathrm{abs}}\) be the sum of the absolute values of its selected \(\lambda\)-coordinates. Conditional on querying \(v\), the expected next contribution is at most \(e^a\delta A_v\). The queried blocks form one root path and have total budget at most \(\Pi(\lambda)\le1\) on every outcome. Summing conditional expectations proves
$$

\mathbb E_{\mu_{a\lambda}}S_{\mathrm{abs}}\le e^a\delta.
\tag{7}
$$
This holds for stopping on ordinary failure, at a requested level, or before querying \(U\). For the corresponding signed sum \(S\),
$$
|e^{2S}-1|\le2e^2S_{\mathrm{abs}},\qquad
|e^{6S}-1|\le6e^6S_{\mathrm{abs}}.

The additional first-hit estimate is as follows. Fix a requested level \(s\ge1\) and extension length \(r\ge0\), with \(s+r\le Q\). Run the forward prefix and, when \(r\ge1\), its two inverse continuations, stopping before the first query of any child block contained in \(U\). Call this event \(\mathcal H_{s,r}\). If the algorithm would query a forbidden block, its first such hit occurs on arrival at a selected node \(v\in P\) at depth seven, before reading its child block. Arrival at the final requested endpoint without a further query is not a hit. In particular the hit is determined without reading any bit of \(U\).

For fixed \(v\in P\), if \(s\ge7\), a hit requires the first seven forward labels to be precisely those of \(v\). This is a cylinder on the seven fixed ancestor blocks of \(v\), each with a label set of size at most \(2^m\delta\). If \(s<7\), a hit must occur on one of the two inverse branches. For either fixed branch its route to \(v\) requires \(s\) prescribed forward labels, the prescribed first or second predecessor label in the split block at depth \(s\), and \(6-s\) prescribed inverse labels thereafter. These are seven conditions on the seven distinct fixed ancestor blocks of \(v\). The second-predecessor count above bounds that branch's prescribed split label by \(\delta\), just as for the first predecessor. Each branch cylinder has fair probability at most \(\delta^7\). An extension too short to query the block at \(v\) has no hit and satisfies the same upper bound.

All these seven blocks are outside \(U\). They form one fixed root path, so their total \(A_v\)-budget is at most one. Their joint density under \(\mu_{3\lambda}\), relative to fair product measure, is at most \(e^3\). A union over the two branch possibilities and over \(P\) therefore proves, in particular, the weaker convenient bound
$$

\le2e^3P_{\mathrm{count}}\delta^7
\le4e^6P_{\mathrm{count}}\delta^7.
\tag{9}

Without the avoidance requirement, the forward prefix and two inverse continuations use at most \(s+2r\le2Q\) units of one-step failure probability, counting a two-predecessor failure twice.
