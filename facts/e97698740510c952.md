## Statement

There is a convex body with three integer and two continuous coordinates, exactly 35 positive integer fibers with positive affine volume root, and an actual mixed centroid of integer base, such that a closed vertical halfspace through it retains strictly less than $3125/7776=(5/6)^5$ of the total mixed volume.

## Proof

Let $P_0=\{(x,y,z)\ge0:4x+2y+z\le8\}$ and use the fibers $hK$, where $K=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(2,0),(0,1)\}$ and $h=1+A(x-1)+B(y-1)$. Take $B$ to be the unique zero in $(1883682,1883684)/10^7$ of
$$
Q(B)=61281B^4+15298B^3-8940B^2-1944B+504
$$
and set $A=(1225620B^3+244679B^2-138654B+86268)/96110$. The polynomial is positive at the left endpoint and negative at the right endpoint by direct rational evaluation, and its derivative is at most $-170706/125$ on $[9/50,1/5]$ by bounding each monomial at the relevant endpoint. The derivative of the numerator defining $A$ is at least $8570088/125$ there. Substitution at the two root-bracketing endpoints bounds $A$ between $801411/10^6$ and $801412/10^6$, and $B$ between $188368/10^6$ and $188369/10^6$. Thus $1-A-B>0$, which bounds $h$ below throughout $P_0$. The lifted body is full-dimensional and convex because the fiber constraints are $s,t\ge0$ and $s/2+t\le h$, all affine.

The census is $x=0,1,2$, $0\le y\le4-2x$, $0\le z\le8-4x-2y$, with integer indices, giving $25+9+1=35$. The weighted moments of $x-1,y-1$ for weights $h^2$ obtained by summation over this census are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_x&=-24A^2+8AB+52A-34B^2-12B-24,\\
M_y&=4A^2-68AB-12A+42B^2+84B.
\end{aligned}
$$
After substituting the polynomial for $A$, the two expressions respectively equal $-2(73537200B^2+11003880B-7648837)Q(B)/2309283025$ and $(24512400B^2+3667960B-34586279)Q(B)/2309283025$. Both vanish. At fixed $(x,y)$ the mean of $z$ is $4-2x-y$, so the moment of $z-1$ is $-2M_x-M_y=0$. The full mixed centroid before the integer translation is $(1,1,1;2/3,1/3)$ because the centroid of $hK$ is $h(2/3,1/3)$ and $\sum h^3=\sum h^2+A M_x+B M_y=\sum h^2$.

Take the closed cap $5(x-1)+2(y-1)+4(z-1)\ge0$. Summing over the full census and over its members satisfying this weak inequality gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=26A^2-12AB-48A+42B^2+35,\\
T&=14A^2+2AB-28A+16B^2-8B+19 .
\end{aligned}
$$
In $(3125/7776)M-T$ bound each positive-coefficient monomial below by its value at the lower rectangle corner and each negative-coefficient monomial by its value at the upper corner. The resulting bound is $132828450791459/486000000000000>0$. This uses the actual closed cap and the original fiber areas.
