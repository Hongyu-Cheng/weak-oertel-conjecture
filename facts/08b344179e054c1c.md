## Statement

Let $P$ be a nonempty finite set in an affine Euclidean space, with masses $m_z>0$ for $z\in P$, and put $Q=\operatorname{conv} P$ and $M=\sum_{z\in P}m_z$. Fix $N>0$ and $p\in Q$. Let $F_p$ be the unique face of $Q$ with $p\in\operatorname{relint} F_p$, and write $P_p=P\cap F_p$ and $V_p=\operatorname{span}(F_p-p)$. Set
$$
L_z(\alpha)=1+\alpha\cdot(z-p),\qquad
E_p=\inf_{\substack{\alpha\in V_p\\L_z(\alpha)>0\ (z\in P_p)}}\sum_{z\in P_p}m_z L_z(\alpha)^{-N}.
$$

Let $\alpha_p$ be the unique relative minimizer and $M_p=\sum_{z\in P_p}m_z$. For $p\in Q$ define
$$
T_p=\max_{\substack{0\le q_z\le m_z\\\sum_{z\in P}q_z(z-p)=0}}\sum_{z\in P}q_z,\qquad
D_p=\min_{H\ni p}\sum_{z\in P\cap H}m_z,
$$
where $H$ ranges over closed affine halfspaces in $\operatorname{aff} Q$, with the whole-space trace allowed, including rank zero. The capacity and the halfspace depth are unchanged on restriction to $P_p$ in $\operatorname{aff}F_p$. With $a$ ranging over $\operatorname{span}(Q-p)$,
$$
T_p=\inf_a\sum_{z\in P}m_z(1+a\cdot(z-p))_+,\qquad
E_p\ge T_p\ge D_p .
$$
Writing $b_N(L)=L^{-N}-(1-N(L-1))_+$ for $L>0$, one has
$$
E_p-T_p\ge\sum_{z\in P_p}m_z b_N(L_z(\alpha_p)),\qquad
E_p=T_p\ \Longleftrightarrow\ p=M_p^{-1}\sum_{z\in P_p}m_z z .
$$
At equality $E_p=T_p=M_p$.

The escort masses $e_z=m_z L_z(\alpha_p)^{-N-1}$ on $P_p$ and zero off $P_p$ are dominated by the $m_z$ exactly when $\alpha_p=0$. The largest scalar $c\ge0$ with $c e_z\le m_z$ for all $z$ is $c=\min_{z\in P_p}L_z(\alpha_p)^{N+1}$. The resulting dominated row has total mass $cE_p$ and mean $p$. On the whole of $Q$, $E_p\le M$ with equality exactly at the original weighted base mean. If this mean is occupied and the compatible-fiber assumptions of the face-attainment result hold with $m_z=|K_z|$, its escort-selected continuous coordinate equals the original volume-weighted fiber centroid.

## Proof

The balanced feasible set is a nonempty compact slice of the box $\prod_{z\in P}[0,m_z]$, so the maximum is attained. For a proper face the nonnegative exposing functional of the face-attainment result has zero sum against every balanced row, forcing every off-face $q_z$ to vanish. Thus $T_p$ is unchanged on restriction. Similarly, restricting a containing closed halfspace gives a containing face halfspace trace. Conversely any containing face halfspace can be written $a\cdot(z-p)\ge h$ on the face with $h\le0$, allowing $a=0$ for the whole-space trace. Extend $a$ to $\operatorname{span}(Q-p)$, then subtract a sufficiently large multiple of the exposing functional. This excludes every off-face atom while preserving the trace and containment of $p$. Hence $D_p$ is unchanged as well. All depth minima exist because there are only finitely many traces on the atoms.

For the hinge formula, every feasible balanced row has
$$
\sum_z q_z=\sum_z q_z(1+a\cdot(z-p))\le\sum_z m_z(1+a\cdot(z-p))_+ .
$$
For the reverse inequality form the compact convex image of the box,
$$
\left\{\left(\sum_z q_z(z-p),\sum_z q_z\right):0\le q_z\le m_z\right\}.
$$
The point $(0,T_p+\varepsilon)$ is strictly separated from this image for each $\varepsilon>0$. Orient the separator so it is larger at that point. Its last coefficient is positive by comparison with $(0,T_p)$, which belongs to the image. Normalize this coefficient to one and call its vector coefficient $a$. Maximizing over the box coordinatewise gives $\sum_z m_z(1+a\cdot(z-p))_+<T_p+\varepsilon$. Letting $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ proves the exact formula. The identical argument applies to the restricted face box. For any full parameter $a$, integrate the weights of $\{z:1+a\cdot(z-p)\ge t\}$ over $t>0$ to obtain the hinge sum. On $0<t<1$ these are closed halfspace traces containing $p$, so that integral is at least $D_p$. This proves $T_p\ge D_p$ in the finite real model, including rank zero and the endpoint masses $D_p=0$ and $D_p=M$.

Strict convexity of $L^{-N}$ gives the tangent bound $L^{-N}\ge1-N(L-1)$ with equality only at $L=1$. Because $L^{-N}>0$, replacing the tangent by its positive part preserves the bound and its equality case. In the face hinge formula take $a=-N\alpha_p$. Its objective is at least $T_p$, so subtracting it from $E_p$ gives a lower bound for $E_p-T_p$ equal to the sum of $m_z b_N(L_z(\alpha_p))$. If $E_p=T_p$, every $L_z(\alpha_p)=1$ by positivity of the masses, and the spanning offsets force $\alpha_p=0$. The stationarity equation at zero is $\sum_{z\in P_p}m_z(z-p)=0$. Conversely, if this balance holds, then zero is the relative minimizer by convexity and the whole face row is feasible for $T_p$. Hence $E_p=T_p=M_p$.

The formula for $c$ follows from $m_z/e_z=L_z(\alpha_p)^{N+1}$ on the face. If $\alpha_p\ne0$, the relative ball about $p$ in $F_p$ contains a point where the linear offset is negative, so some $z\in P_p$ has $L_z(\alpha_p)<1$ and $e_z>m_z$. If $\alpha_p=0$, the escort is the face mass row. The escort identities give the mass and mean of its positive rescaling. Finally, $\alpha=0$ gives $E_p\le M_p\le M$. Equality with $M$ forces $F_p=Q$ and $\alpha=0$ to minimize, so stationarity gives the original mean. Conversely that mean lies in $\operatorname{relint}Q$ since all masses are positive, and at it zero is stationary and minimizes. The escort then uses the original masses and the claimed continuous coordinate follows.
