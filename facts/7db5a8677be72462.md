## Statement

Use the full-parity witness orbits on the lattice projection $P$ of a compact convex body in $\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$, $n,d\ge1$. Let $f(z)=|C_z|_d^{1/d}$ and $g(z)=2f(R(z))-f(z)-f(p_{[z]})\ge0$. For $z_0=z,\ldots,z_k=p_s$ one has
$$
f(z)=2^kf(p_s)-\sum_{j<k}2^jf(p_{[z_j]})-\sum_{j<k}2^jg(z_j).
$$
For equal root volume roots $f_0$ the last sum equals $f_0-f(z)\le f_0$. For any finite nonnegative weights $w_z$ on $P$, define $A_w(t)=\sum_{z,j<k(z):z_j=t}2^jw_z$ and let $C_w(r)$ sum the coefficients of $f(p_r)$ on the right side, weighted by $w_z$. Then
$$
\sum_z w_z f(z)+\sum_tA_w(t)g(t)=\sum_r C_w(r)f(p_r),\qquad
\sum_r C_w(r)=\sum_z w_z,\qquad
\sum_r C_w(r)p_r=\sum_z w_z z.
$$
The $A_w$ are nonnegative, while $C_w(0)=(L+1)(1-L/2)<0$ for $P=\{0,\ldots,L\}$, roots $0,1$, unit weights and integer $L>2$. Furthermore for each fixed $n,d\ge1$ there are such forests with all slack zero, all root volumes equal, and arbitrarily large ratios of total actual volume to the sum of root volumes.

## Proof

The midpoint case of the volume-root inequality gives $g(z)\ge0$. Repeated substitution of $f(z_j)=2f(z_{j+1})-f(p_{[z_j]})-g(z_j)$ gives the path formula, including an empty sum at a root. For equal root volume roots, $2^k-\sum_{j<k}2^j=1$. Multiplying by $w_z$ and summing gives the weighted identity. The coefficient sum identity follows from the same geometric sum, and the coefficient moment identity follows by substituting the base recurrence in place of the volume-root recurrence with zero slack. In dimension one with roots $0,1$, the coefficient sum and moment give $C_w(1)=L(L+1)/2$ and $C_w(0)=L+1-L(L+1)/2$.

For the stated volume example let $C=[0,L]^n\times K$, where $L\ge1$ is integral and $K\subset\mathbb R^d$ is any convex body of positive volume. Use $L_{\rm fac}=\{z:\sum_{i=1}^n|z_i-1/2|\le n/2\}$ as the expanded polyhedron. Its $2^n$ sign rows are nonzero, and each root in $\{0,1\}^n$ is tight in exactly the row whose signs agree with its coordinates relative to $1/2$. Every integer coordinate is at distance at least $1/2$ from $1/2$, with equality only at $0$ or $1$, so the only lattice points of $L_{\rm fac}$ are these witnesses and its interior contains none. This also supplies the dimension-one witnesses used above. All fibers on the projection have volume $|K|_d$, so every slack vanishes. The total-to-root volume ratio is $(L+1)^n/2^n$ and diverges with $L$.
