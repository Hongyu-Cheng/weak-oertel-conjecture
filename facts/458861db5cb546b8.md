## Statement

Let actual positive compatible $d$-fibers on a fixed finite real base $P$ satisfy $|K_z|\ge v>0$, $K_z\subset RB_d$, and contain possibly differently centered balls of common radius $r_b>0$. Let the within-fiber pooled covariance satisfy $\lambda_- I\le\Sigma\le\lambda_+ I$ with $\lambda_->0$. At sampled marks $Y=(p,y)$ let the actual buffer be a nonnegative scalar $\delta_Y$ times $\Gamma(u)=\sqrt{u^{\mathsf T}\Sigma u}$ in the margin of nonvertical through-mark caps, leaving pure-base caps unchanged. Suppose the raw buffered maximum $Q_\delta$ is attained, and put $\delta_{\max}=\sup_Y\delta_Y$. Use $b_{\rm base}(p)$ for the minimum mass of a closed base trace at $p$, including the whole-base trace. Put $A=|P|\omega_{d-1}R^{d-1}$, $\omega_0=1$, $S_{\max}=A\sqrt{\lambda_+}$, $q_*=\omega_dr_b^d/2$ and $r=q_*/(2A)$. At every mark the raw depths satisfy $0\le D_\delta-D_0\le S_{\max}\delta_{\max}$.

If $S_{\max}\delta_{\max}\le\min(v/4,q_*/2)$ and $\delta_{\max}\sqrt{\lambda_+}\le r/2$, then at every buffered maximizing mark $Y=(p,y)$ there is an own inball $B(y,r)\subseteq K_p$ and $b_{\rm base}(p)-Q_\delta\ge v/4$. For every $\Gamma$-unit nonvertical buffered through-mark cut at that mark, its own section is proper and has coarea at least $S_{\min}=\omega_{d-1}(\sqrt3r/2)^{d-1}\sqrt{\lambda_-}$. Any total coarea obtained by retaining all proper sections and allocating supporting sections with densities in $[0,1]$ is between $S_{\min}$ and $S_{\max}$. These bounds apply eventually in any such family sequence with $\delta_{\max}\to0$.

## Proof

For Euclidean unit continuous normal the threshold shift is at most $\delta_{\max}\sqrt{\lambda_+}$. The uniform slab comparison of 43ed87d91b76249c bounds the mass change of each nonvertical cap by $S_{\max}\delta_{\max}$, and pure-base cap masses are unchanged. Taking infima gives the comparison. The ordinary maximum is at least $q_*$ by testing the center of a displayed inball, so $Q_\delta\ge q_*$. At a buffered maximizing mark, the comparison gives $D_0(Y)\ge q_*/2$. Compatibility allows the supporting tilt of 155691fc3768c06f at each own-boundary mark, where ordinary depth is zero. Its within-fiber $A$-Lipschitz bound from 43ed87d91b76249c gives distance at least $r$ to that boundary. The ordinary atomic gap of 9583fbdf100e75ef gives
$$
b_{\rm base}(p)-Q_\delta\ge |K_p|/2-S_{\max}\delta_{\max}\ge v/4.
$$

For $\Gamma(u)=1$, $1/\sqrt{\lambda_+}\le\|u\|\le1/\sqrt{\lambda_-}$. The plane on the own fiber is $u\cdot(X-y)=-\delta_Y$, at Euclidean distance at most $\delta_{\max}\sqrt{\lambda_+}\le r/2$ from $y$. It cuts the inball in a $(d-1)$-ball of radius at least $\sqrt3r/2$, which has counting measure one in dimension $d=1$. Divide its section area by $\|u\|$ for $S_{\min}$. The total allocation includes this unambiguous proper contribution. The sum of all Euclidean section areas is at most $A$, and each coarea divides by $\|u\|$ with allocation density at most one. This proves the upper bound $S_{\max}$ and allows other-fiber supporting facets.

For the covariance buffer $\delta_Y=\varepsilon(L(Y)E_{K_p}(y)/M)^{1/(d+1)}$, uniform smallness in a compact minimal-bad-base application is a separate input. For example, 352454c77cb6558d supplies it for its globally $o(1)$-optimal pairs in the bounded frame, with its stated representative and limiting quota hypotheses.
