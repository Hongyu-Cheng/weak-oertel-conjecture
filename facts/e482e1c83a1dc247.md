## Statement

Assume the canonical hard branch and retain the exact objects and normalizations of accepted facts `036a276c54ec5e66`, `084a2e888b32fd2d`, `2fe46f47d19112ea`, `844dca4a76115be3`, and `be359f8a6c628483`. Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body,
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
and, for the canonical reservoir mass \(Q>0\),
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D
 <\frac Q{100}.
\tag{1}
\]
The terms \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) in (1) are distinct nonnegative summands, and
\[
 D\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
\tag{2}
\]

The rescaled internal atlas is a finite whole-label family \(\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\). Every label \(\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\) has weight \(\eta_\sigma>0\) and two distinct physical opposite endpoints \(a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\in\mathbb Z^n\). Put
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{3}
\]
For every physical endpoint \(y\) occurring in this family, let \(e_2(y)\) be its unique endpoint-disjoint dyadic edge, with weight \(t_{e_2(y)}>0\). Writing \(a_y:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_y)\) and \(c_y\) for the centroid of \(C_y\), the accepted endpoint budget and internal-mass bounds are
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma
 \leq t_{e_2(y)}\leq\frac{a_y}{4},
\tag{4}
\]
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 >\left(\frac9{200}-\frac1{100\gamma_d}\right)Q
 >\frac7{400}Q.
\tag{5}
\]

Retain the nonempty maximum-weight chartable family \(\Gamma_0\subseteq\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\), its witnessing space \(W\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) with \(\dim W\leq3\), and the fixed affine extension \(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) from accepted fact `084a2e888b32fd2d`. Its discarded labels are partitioned into \(\mathscr D_c\) and \(\mathscr D_o\), where every endpoint \(y\) of a label in \(\Gamma_0\cup\mathscr D_o\) satisfies
\[
 c_y-s(y)\in W,
\tag{6}
\]
whereas every \(\sigma\in\mathscr D_c\) has at least one endpoint \(y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}\) satisfying \(c_y-s(y)\notin W\). Put
\[
 M_c:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{7}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq
 2\gamma_d\bigl(H_{\mathrm{int}}-M_c\bigr),}
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 \boxed{M_c>
 \frac{18\gamma_d-5}{400\gamma_d}Q
 >\frac{141}{12800}Q.}
\tag{9}
\]

Define the physical support of the full internal centroid field by
\[
 Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c
 :=\bigcup_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}
 \{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{int}}^c:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c),
\tag{10}
\]
and define its intrinsic continuous centroid residual rank by
\[
 r_{\mathrm{int}}^c
 :=\min_{\varphi:\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c)\to\mathbb R^d
 \ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{c_y-\varphi(y):y\in Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c\}.
\tag{11}
\]
Let \(\pi_W:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R^d/W\) be the quotient map and put
\[
 k_c:=\dim\operatorname{span}
 \{\pi_W(c_y-s(y)):y\in Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c\}.
\tag{12}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{r_{\mathrm{int}}^c\geq10,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{k_c\geq10-\dim W\geq7.}
\tag{13}
\]

Finally, for every \(\sigma\in\mathscr D_c\), choose exactly one endpoint
\[
 y_{\mathrm{def}}(\sigma)\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}
 \quad\text{with}\quad
 c_{y_{\mathrm{def}}(\sigma)}-s(y_{\mathrm{def}}(\sigma))\notin W.
\tag{14}
\]
Aggregate labels that choose the same physical endpoint, and put
\[
 Y_{\mathrm{def}}
 :=\{y\in\mathbb Z^n:
 \text{there is a }\sigma\in\mathscr D_c
 \text{ with }y=y_{\mathrm{def}}(\sigma)\},
\tag{15}
\]
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{def}}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{def}}),
\tag{16}
\]
\[
 r_{\mathrm{def}}
 :=\min_{\varphi:\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{def}})\to\mathbb R^d
 \ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{c_y-\varphi(y):y\in Y_{\mathrm{def}}\}.
\tag{17}
\]
The aliases \(\rho_{\mathrm{def}},r_{\mathrm{def}}\) are new intrinsic ranks of this one bad-endpoint centroid selector; they do not denote the midpoint ranks \(\rho_{\mathrm{int}},r_{\mathrm{int}}\) or any previously defined congestion rank. The support in (15) is nonempty, every label of \(\mathscr D_c\) is used once, and
\[
 \boxed{
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq
 4\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}
 \frac{M_c}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}.}
\tag{18}
\]
Consequently the exact sufficient condition
\[
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}
 \frac{M_c}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 \geq\frac{Q}{800\gamma_d}
\tag{19}
\]
terminates the hard branch. In particular, the mass-independent condition
\[
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 \geq\frac1{36\gamma_d-10}
\tag{20}
\]
is sufficient. Conversely, every canonical hard instance satisfies the strict effective-rank screen
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 <\frac{Q}{800\gamma_dM_c}
 <\frac1{36\gamma_d-10}.}
\tag{21}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` gives \(h_{\max}(C)\geq2^{-n}R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\). Multiplication by \(2^n\) proves (2). Accepted fact `036a276c54ec5e66` gives (1) with \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) as distinct nonnegative summands. Therefore
\[
 2R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \leq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+D
 \leq\mathcal A(C)
 <\frac Q{100},
\]
so throughout the hard branch
\[
 \boxed{R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac Q{200}.}
\tag{22}
\]
This is the strengthened hard selector bound used below.

We first prove (8). For each physical endpoint \(y\), define the new aligned-centroid incidence calibration
\[
 \operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{aln}}(y)
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma_0\cup\mathscr D_o\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma,
\tag{23}
\]
and let \(Y_{\mathrm{aln}}:=\{y:\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{aln}}(y)>0\}\). This notation is a calibration weight only; it is not any canonical matching, transverse residual, complement mass, or stress load. The family \(\Gamma_0\) is nonempty and all label weights are positive, so \(Y_{\mathrm{aln}}\ne\varnothing\). Equation (4) gives, for every \(y\in Y_{\mathrm{aln}}\),
\[
 0<\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{aln}}(y)
 \leq\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma
 \leq\frac{a_y}{4}.
\tag{24}
\]
Every label has two distinct endpoints. The partition retained from accepted fact `084a2e888b32fd2d` therefore gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathrm{aln}}}\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{aln}}(y)
 &=2\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_0\cup\mathscr D_o}\eta_\sigma\\
 &=2\bigl(H_{\mathrm{int}}-M_c\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{25}
\]
By (6), the restriction of the fixed affine map \(s\) to \(\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{aln}})\) has all selected centroid residuals in \(W\), so their residual span has dimension at most three. Accepted fact `844dca4a76115be3`, applied once to the distinct physical bases in \(Y_{\mathrm{aln}}\) with centroid depth weights \(4\gamma_d\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{aln}}(y)\), is legal by (24) and yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 &\geq
 4\gamma_d\,2^{n-\dim\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{aln}})}
 \frac{\sum_y\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{aln}}(y)}{\dim W+1}\\
 &\geq
 \gamma_d\sum_y\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{aln}}(y)\\
 &=2\gamma_d\bigl(H_{\mathrm{int}}-M_c\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{26}
\]
Here \(\dim\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{aln}})\leq n\) and \(\dim W+1\leq4\). This proves (8) using one physical selector, not a sum of selector scores.

Accepted fact `2fe46f47d19112ea` proves
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 >\frac12\left(\frac9{100}-\frac1{50\gamma_d}\right)Q
 =\left(\frac9{200}-\frac1{100\gamma_d}\right)Q,
\tag{27}
\]
which is the first inequality in (5). Combining (8) with (22) gives
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}-M_c<\frac{Q}{400\gamma_d}.
\tag{28}
\]
Equations (27)-(28) imply
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 M_c
 &>H_{\mathrm{int}}-\frac{Q}{400\gamma_d}\\
 &>\left(\frac9{200}-\frac1{100\gamma_d}
 -\frac1{400\gamma_d}\right)Q\\
 &=\frac{18\gamma_d-5}{400\gamma_d}Q.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{29}
\]

For the numerical comparison in (9), the elementary estimate recorded in accepted fact `2fe46f47d19112ea` gives
\[
 \gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}>e^{-1}>\frac{32}{87}.
\tag{30}
\]
Indeed, \(\log(1+1/d)<1/d\), while the exponential series gives \(e<87/32\). Hence \(5/\gamma_d<435/32\), and therefore
\[
 \frac{18\gamma_d-5}{400\gamma_d}
 =\frac1{400}\left(18-\frac5{\gamma_d}\right)
 >\frac1{400}\left(18-\frac{435}{32}\right)
 =\frac{141}{12800}.
\tag{31}
\]
This proves (9), in particular \(M_c>0\) and \(\mathscr D_c\ne\varnothing\).

We next prove (13). For \(y\in Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c\), define the full internal centroid incidence calibration
\[
 \operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{int},c}(y)
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{32}
\]
The support \(Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c\) is nonempty because \(H_{\mathrm{int}}>0\). Equations (4) and the distinctness of the two endpoints of each label give
\[
 0<\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{int},c}(y)\leq\frac{a_y}{4},
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c}\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{int},c}(y)
 =2H_{\mathrm{int}}.
\tag{33}
\]
Apply accepted fact `844dca4a76115be3` once to these physical bases, their centroids, and the legal depth weights \(4\gamma_d\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{int},c}(y)\). By (10)-(11),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 &\geq
 4\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{int}}^c}
 \frac{2H_{\mathrm{int}}}{r_{\mathrm{int}}^c+1}\\
 &\geq
 \frac{8\gamma_dH_{\mathrm{int}}}{r_{\mathrm{int}}^c+1}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{34}
\]
Combining (22), (34), and the second lower bound in (5) gives
\[
 r_{\mathrm{int}}^c+1
 >1600\gamma_d\frac{H_{\mathrm{int}}}{Q}
 >28\gamma_d
 >\frac{896}{87}>10,
\tag{35}
\]
where (30) was used in the penultimate inequality. Since \(r_{\mathrm{int}}^c\) is an integer, (35) proves \(r_{\mathrm{int}}^c\geq10\).

Put
\[
 V_c:=\operatorname{span}
 \{c_y-s(y):y\in Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c\}.
\tag{36}
\]
The fixed map \(s\), restricted to \(\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{int}}^c)\), is an admissible map in (11), and hence
\[
 r_{\mathrm{int}}^c\leq\dim V_c.
\tag{37}
\]
The restriction of \(\pi_W\) to \(V_c\) has kernel \(V_c\cap W\) and image dimension \(k_c\). Consequently
\[
 \dim V_c
 =\dim(V_c\cap W)+k_c
 \leq\dim W+k_c.
\tag{38}
\]
Equations (35), (37), and (38) give
\[
 k_c\geq10-\dim W\geq7,
\]
which completes the proof of (13).

It remains to prove the defect-selector assertions. The choice in (14) exists by the definition of \(\mathscr D_c\) in accepted fact `084a2e888b32fd2d`; it can be fixed independently for every label because the family is finite. For each physical endpoint \(y\in Y_{\mathrm{def}}\), define the aggregated calibration
\[
 \operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{def}}(y)
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c\\
 y_{\mathrm{def}}(\sigma)=y}}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{39}
\]
Because \(M_c>0\), the support \(Y_{\mathrm{def}}\) is nonempty. Every label is assigned to exactly one endpoint, so
\[
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathrm{def}}}\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{def}}(y)=M_c.
\tag{40}
\]
Moreover, for each \(y\in Y_{\mathrm{def}}\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 0<\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{def}}(y)
 &\leq
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma\\
 &\leq t_{e_2(y)}\leq\frac{a_y}{4}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{41}
\]
Thus the centroids \(c_y\), with depth weights \(4\gamma_d\operatorname{inc}_{\mathrm{def}}(y)\), form one legal selector under accepted fact `844dca4a76115be3`. Applying that theorem with the intrinsic ranks (16)-(17) and using (40) gives exactly
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 4\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}
 \frac{M_c}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1},
\]
which proves (18). Physical collisions were aggregated before the selector was formed, while (40) counts every label once; hence neither an endpoint budget nor a selector score is duplicated.

If (19) holds, then (18), (2), and the fact that \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) are distinct terms in (1) give
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+D
 \geq2R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq8\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}
 \frac{M_c}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 \geq\frac Q{100},
\tag{42}
\]
contradicting the strict hard inequality (1). This proves the exact terminal condition (19).

The first inequality in (9) is strict and \(36\gamma_d-10=2(18\gamma_d-5)>0\) by (30). Hence (20) implies
\[
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}
 \frac{M_c}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 >\frac{18\gamma_d-5}{400\gamma_d}Q
 \frac1{36\gamma_d-10}
 =\frac Q{800\gamma_d},
\]
so (20) is sufficient as claimed. Conversely, (18) and the strict hard bound (22) force
\[
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}
 \frac{M_c}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 <\frac Q{800\gamma_d}.
\tag{43}
\]
Dividing by \(M_c>0\), then using (9), yields
\[
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 <\frac{Q}{800\gamma_dM_c}
 <\frac1{36\gamma_d-10},
\]
which is (21) and completes the proof.
