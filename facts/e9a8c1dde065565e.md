## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and assume the positive-load hypotheses and canonical notation of accepted facts 6b09aae189415c70, c9858b28a86fc01c, and 2ba562925dfac9ac. Thus
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
 c_z:=\frac1{\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)}\int_{C_z}x\,dx
 \quad(\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)>0),
\]
\[
 Q>0,\qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,\qquad
 \gamma_{2d}:=\left(\frac{2d}{2d+1}\right)^{2d},\qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\tag{1}
\]
The half-strength canonical reservoir is \(r=(r_e)\). The load-minimal optimizer supplies source loads \(\beta_z\), points \(p_z^\flat\), an origin-symmetric full-dimensional convex body \(D_*\), and the retained product matching \(s=(s_e)\), with
\[
 p_z^\flat+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z)
 \quad(\beta_z>0),\qquad
 d_z(s):=\sum_{e\ni z}s_e\le\beta_z.
\tag{2}
\]
For a positive retained edge \(e=\{u,v\}\), use
\[
 m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2},\qquad
 F_e^\flat:=\frac{p_u^\flat+p_v^\flat}{2},\qquad
 \bar c_e:=\frac{c_u+c_v}{2}.
\tag{3}
\]
For a positive retained label \((\xi,c)\), let \(q_{\xi,c}^\circ\) be its canonical conditioned-output point and put \(\ell(e):=(\xi,m_e)\) for \(e\subseteq\xi\). Define
\[
 \mathcal G:=
 \left\{e:s_e>0,
 \|F_e^\flat-q_{\ell(e)}^\circ\|_{D_*}\le\frac14\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Assume the exact hard \(O\)-side inequalities
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100},\qquad I\le\frac Q4.
\tag{5}
\]

Let \(w=(w_e)_{e\in\mathcal G}\) be arbitrary weights with
\[
 0\le w_e\le s_e,\qquad
 B_w:=\sum_{e\in\mathcal G}w_e>0.
\tag{6}
\]
Define
\[
 d_z(w):=\sum_{e\ni z}w_e,\qquad
 E_w:=\{z:d_z(w)>0\},\qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{src}}(w):=\dim\operatorname{aff}E_w,
\tag{7}
\]
\[
 r_{\mathrm{src}}(w):=
 \min_{b:\operatorname{aff}E_w\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{p_z^\flat-b(z):z\in E_w\}.
\tag{8}
\]
For an affine map \(b:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\), define
\[
 T_w(b):=
 \sum_{z\in E_w}d_z(w)
 \mathbf1_{\{\|p_z^\flat-b(z)\|_{D_*}\le1/2\}},\qquad
 T_w^*:=\sup_bT_w(b).
\tag{9}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{G\ge T_w^*,}
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\ge
 2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}(w)}
 \frac{B_w}{r_{\mathrm{src}}(w)+1}.}
\tag{11}
\]

For the full good weights \(w_e^g:=s_e\) on \(\mathcal G\), put
\[
 W^g_{\xi,c}:=
 \sum_{\substack{e\in\mathcal G,\ e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_e,\qquad
 B_g:=\sum_{\xi,c}W^g_{\xi,c},
\tag{12}
\]
and write \(d_z^g,T_g^*,\rho_{\mathrm{src}}^g,r_{\mathrm{src}}^g\) for the other resulting quantities. Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{T_g^*}{Q}
 +\frac{43}{50}
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}^g}}
 {r_{\mathrm{src}}^g+1}
 <\frac1{100},}
\tag{13}
\]
and in particular
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{src}}^g+1>
 86\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}^g}.}
\tag{14}
\]
If affine maps \(b_1,\ldots,b_K:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) satisfy
\[
 d_z^g>0\Longrightarrow
 \min_{1\le j\le K}\|p_z^\flat-b_j(z)\|_{D_*}\le\frac12,
\tag{15}
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{K\ge87.}
\tag{16}
\]
This is only a necessary hard-branch source-cover conclusion; it does not assert a cover with cardinality bounded independently of the data.

There is a simultaneous six-capacity refinement when \(w\) lies in one good-output affine tube. Namely, additionally fix an affine map \(a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) such that
\[
 w_e>0\Longrightarrow
 \|q_{\ell(e)}^\circ-a(m_e)\|_{D_*}\le\frac14.
\tag{17}
\]
Put
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{tar}}(w):=
 \dim\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:w_e>0\},
\tag{18}
\]
\[
 r_{\mathrm{cen}}(w):=
 \min_{b:\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:w_e>0\}\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{\bar c_e-b(m_e):w_e>0\}.
\tag{19}
\]
For each represented physical target \(c\), put
\[
 B_{w,c}:=\sum_{e:m_e=c}w_e,\qquad
 k_{w,c}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}
 \{\bar c_e:w_e>0,\ m_e=c\}.
\tag{20}
\]
Define
\[
 \kappa(w):=
 \frac1{16}
 +\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}(w)}
 +\frac{2\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}(w)}}
 {r_{\mathrm{src}}(w)+1}
 +\frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}(w)}}
 {16(r_{\mathrm{cen}}(w)+1)},
\tag{21}
\]
\[
 \chi(w):=
 \gamma_{2d}2^{n-2}
 \max_{c:B_{w,c}>0}\frac{B_{w,c}}{k_{w,c}+1}.
\tag{22}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal A(C)\ge T_w^*+\kappa(w)B_w+\chi(w),}
\tag{23}
\]
so the hard inequality forces
\[
 \boxed{T_w^*+\kappa(w)B_w+\chi(w)<\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{24}
\]
In particular, every represented \(c\) and every such tube submatching satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
 k_{w,c}+1>
 25\gamma_{2d}2^n\frac{B_{w,c}}Q,}
\tag{25}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{cen}}(w)+1>
 \frac{25}{4}\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}(w)}\frac{B_w}Q,}
\tag{26}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{src}}(w)+1>
 200\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}(w)}\frac{B_w}Q.}
\tag{27}
\]

Finally, suppose affine maps \(a_1,\ldots,a_K\) cover all positive good labels:
\[
 W^g_{\xi,c}>0\Longrightarrow
 \min_{1\le j\le K}
 \|q_{\xi,c}^\circ-a_j(c)\|_{D_*}\le\frac14.
\tag{28}
\]
Assign each positive good label to one covering map. Let \(w^{(j)}\) equal \(s\) on the good edges whose label is assigned to \(a_j\), and discard empty cells. For the remaining index set \(J\), put
\[
 \kappa_j:=\kappa(w^{(j)}),\qquad
 T_j^*:=T_{w^{(j)}}^*,\qquad
 \chi_j:=\chi(w^{(j)}).
\tag{29}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{j\in J}
 \frac{1-100(T_j^*+\chi_j)/Q}{\kappa_j}>43.}
\tag{30}
\]
Every numerator in (30) is positive. In particular,
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{j\in J}\frac1{\kappa_j}>43.}
\tag{31}
\]
Since \(\kappa_j>117/272\), equation (31) recovers \(K\ge19\), while (30) retains all additional source and centroid escape terms.

## Proof

Fix \(w\) satisfying (6). Since \(w_e\le s_e\), equations (2) and (7) give
\[
 0<d_z(w)\le d_z(s)\le\beta_z
 \qquad(z\in E_w).
\tag{32}
\]

We first prove (10). Fix an affine map \(b:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\), and put
\[
 E_b:=\{z\in E_w:\|p_z^\flat-b(z)\|_{D_*}\le1/2\}.
\tag{33}
\]
If \(E_b=\varnothing\), then \(T_w(b)=0\le G\). Suppose \(E_b\ne\varnothing\). Origin symmetry of \(D_*\), (2), and (33) give
\[
 b(z)\in p_z^\flat+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z)
 \qquad(z\in E_b).
\tag{34}
\]
Scale a density representing (34) by \(d_z(w)/\beta_z\le1\). Its normalized barycenter is unchanged, so
\[
 b(z)\in\mathsf M_{d_z(w)}(C_z)
 \qquad(z\in E_b).
\tag{35}
\]

Apply accepted fact 6d6fcf377e68c79c with continuous dimension zero to the positive atomic measure
\[
 \sum_{z\in E_b}d_z(w)\delta_z
\]
on \(\mathbb Z^n\). It supplies \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\) such that every closed base halfspace containing \(m\) has weight at least \(2^{-n}T_w(b)\). The point \(m\) lies in \(\operatorname{conv}E_b\): otherwise strict separation from that compact convex hull would give a closed halfspace containing \(m\) and carrying zero weight. For every \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E_b)\), affinity of \(b\) and (35) give
\[
 b(m)=\sum_{z\in E_b}\lambda_zb(z)
 \in\sum_{z\in E_b}\lambda_z
 \mathsf M_{d_z(w)}(C_z).
\tag{36}
\]
Thus \(b(m)\) belongs to the intersection over all such \(\lambda\). By the exact complete-core definition in accepted fact fbeb0a501b268d12, the masses \(d_z(w)\) on \(E_b\) are admissible and
\[
 G\ge\sum_{z\in E_b}d_z(w)=T_w(b).
\tag{37}
\]
Taking the supremum over \(b\) proves (10). This construction uses one metronoid density in each distinct physical source fiber and combines no densities at a target fiber.

For (11), equation (2) gives \(p_z^\flat\in\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z)\). The metronoid halfspace-depth theorem 6769574fc1bd9f8d and (32) imply
\[
 \delta_C(z,p_z^\flat)
 \ge\gamma_d\beta_z
 \ge\gamma_dd_z(w)
 \qquad(z\in E_w).
\tag{38}
\]
Use in the residual-selector capacity of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 the points \(p_z^\flat\), weights \(t_z:=\gamma_dd_z(w)\), and an affine map attaining (8). Their total weight is
\[
 \sum_{z\in E_w}t_z
 =\gamma_d\sum_zd_z(w)
 =2\gamma_dB_w.
\tag{39}
\]
Their base and residual ranks are exactly (7)-(8). Substitution in the defining selector score proves (11).

For the full good weights, accepted fact c9858b28a86fc01c gives
\[
 B_g>\frac{43}{100}Q.
\tag{40}
\]
The quantities \(G\) and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) are distinct nonnegative summands of (1). Equations (5), (10), (11), and (40) give
\[
 \frac Q{100}
 >G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge T_g^*
 +2\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}^g}
 \frac{B_g}{r_{\mathrm{src}}^g+1}
 >T_g^*
 +\frac{43}{50}\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}^g}
 \frac Q{r_{\mathrm{src}}^g+1}.
\tag{41}
\]
Division by \(Q\) proves (13), and dropping \(T_g^*/Q\) proves (14).

Suppose (15) holds. By (10) and (5),
\[
 T_g(b_j)\le G<\frac Q{100}
 \qquad(1\le j\le K).
\tag{42}
\]
Because the tubes cover every positive source degree, subadditivity and (40) give
\[
 \frac{86}{100}Q
 <2B_g
 =\sum_zd_z^g
 \le\sum_{j=1}^KT_g(b_j)
 <K\frac Q{100}.
\tag{43}
\]
Thus \(K>86\), proving (16). Overlaps only increase the middle upper bound.

Now impose (17). Accepted fact 2ba562925dfac9ac, applied literally to \(a\) and \(w\), gives the two distinct bounds
\[
 R\ge\frac{B_w}{16},\qquad
 D\ge\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}(w)}B_w.
\tag{44}
\]

Define the positive centroid-midpoint measure
\[
 \sigma_{\mathrm{cen}}
 :=\frac1{16}\sum_{e\in\mathcal G}w_e
 \delta_{(m_e,\bar c_e)}.
\tag{45}
\]
The canonical reservoir inequality in accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 is
\[
 \frac1{16}s\le r.
\tag{46}
\]
Hence \(w/16\le r\), and (45) is a nonzero positive submeasure of the reservoir centroid-midpoint measure visible to an exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching in that accepted fact. Its mass is \(B_w/16\), its base rank is \(\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}(w)\), and its residual rank is \(r_{\mathrm{cen}}(w)\). Therefore
\[
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 \ge
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}(w)}}
 {16(r_{\mathrm{cen}}(w)+1)}B_w.
\tag{47}
\]

Fix a represented target \(c\), and let \(J_c:=\{e:w_e>0,\ m_e=c\}\). Every edge in \(J_c\) is a positive reservoir edge by (46), and
\[
 \sum_{e\in J_c}r_e
 \ge\frac1{16}\sum_{e\in J_c}w_e
 =\frac{B_{w,c}}{16}.
\tag{48}
\]
The fixed-target reflected-cloud estimate in accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)
 &\ge4\gamma_{2d}2^n
 \frac{\sum_{e\in J_c}r_e}{k_{w,c}+1}\\
 &\ge\gamma_{2d}2^{n-2}
 \frac{B_{w,c}}{k_{w,c}+1}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{49}
\]
Taking the maximum over represented \(c\) proves
\[
 R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\ge\chi(w).
\tag{50}
\]

Add (10), (11), (44), (47), and (50). They lower-bound, respectively, the six pairwise distinct summands
\[
 G,\quad R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C),\quad R,\quad D,\quad
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C),\quad R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)
\]
of (1), each exactly once. Their sum is the right side of (23), proving (23). Equation (24) follows from (5). Keeping only the reflected-cloud, centroid-midpoint, or selector term in (24) and rearranging gives (25), (26), or (27), respectively. No witness-disjointness is needed because these are distinct numerical capacities; within each capacity no two lower bounds are added.

It remains to prove the cover tradeoff. Under (28), assign each positive good label to one index \(j\) for which it is covered. Each nonempty \(w^{(j)}\) satisfies (6) and (17), the cells are edge-disjoint, and
\[
 \sum_{j\in J}B_{w^{(j)}}=B_g.
\tag{51}
\]
Apply (24) separately to each cell:
\[
 \frac{B_{w^{(j)}}}{Q}
 <\frac{1/100-(T_j^*+\chi_j)/Q}{\kappa_j}.
\tag{52}
\]
The left side is positive, so every numerator is positive. Sum (52), use (40) and (51), and multiply by \(100\):
\[
 43
 <100\frac{B_g}{Q}
 <\sum_{j\in J}
 \frac{1-100(T_j^*+\chi_j)/Q}{\kappa_j}.
\tag{53}
\]
This proves (30). Dropping the nonnegative deductions in the numerators proves (31).

Accepted fact c9858b28a86fc01c includes \(\gamma_d>25/68\), and \(\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}(w^{(j)})\le n\). Thus
\[
 \kappa_j>
 \frac1{16}+\frac{25}{68}
 =\frac{117}{272}.
\tag{54}
\]
If \(K\le18\), equations (31) and (54) would give
\[
 43<\sum_{j\in J}\frac1{\kappa_j}
 <18\frac{272}{117}
 =\frac{4896}{117}<43,
\]
a contradiction. This recovers \(K\ge19\), while (30) retains the additional source and centroid escape data. Every target-tube cell was defined labelwise, but every density construction in this proof occurred in distinct source fibers or in the already accepted source-coupled measure of 2ba562925dfac9ac; no simultaneous target-fiber density was formed from different parity labels.
