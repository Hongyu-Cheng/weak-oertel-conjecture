## Statement

All logarithms are natural. Retain exactly the objects \(D_n,\mathcal P_n,q_n,Z_n^\star,\nu_{n,\mathbf0},I_L,\iota_L\), and \(A_{n,L}=D_n\times I_L\) of accepted fact `3e1cc0fa666b159b`. Explicitly, for every integer \(n\ge2\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
D_n&=\{0,1\}^n\cup\{-\mathbf1_n\},
&\mathcal P_n&=\operatorname{conv}(D_n),\\
q_n^{n+1}(1+q_n)^{n-1}&=1,
&\varphi^{-1}<q_n&<5/6,
\qquad \varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2,\\
Z_n^\star&=(1+q_n)^n+q_n^{-n},
&I_L&=\{0,\ldots,L\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The root \(q_n\) is the unique positive solution of its displayed equation. For every integer \(L\ge1\), \(\iota_L\) is uniform on \(I_L\).

For each \(n\), take the original base source \(p=\nu_{n,\mathbf0}\), with its dependence on \(n\) understood. This is the exact accepted origin entropy row, now used as the original probability law. It is not the predecessor's uniform original source \(p_n\). Its probabilities are
\[
p(\mathbf b)=\frac{q_n^{|\mathbf b|}}{Z_n^\star}
\quad(\mathbf b\in\{0,1\}^n),
\qquad
p(-\mathbf1_n)=\frac{q_n^{-n}}{Z_n^\star},
\qquad |\mathbf b|=\sum_i b_i.
\]
Set \(L_n=2^{4n}\), \(R=n+1\), and define the original product source \(P_n=p\otimes\iota_{L_n}\) on \(A_{n,L_n}\). Here \(P_n\) is a probability law, not the polytope \(\mathcal P_n\), and is not the predecessor's uniform product law \(P_{n,L_n}=p_n\otimes\iota_{L_n}\).

Use the apex probability, cube probability, and conditional Bernoulli parameter
\[
a_n=\frac{q_n}{1+2q_n},
\qquad l_n=\frac{1+q_n}{1+2q_n},
\qquad \beta_n=\frac{q_n}{1+q_n}.
\]
These are exactly \(1-\gamma_n,\gamma_n,\xi_n\), respectively, in the fourth-moment proof of `3e1cc0fa666b159b`; that proof's \(v_n\) is \(\beta_n(1-\beta_n)\). In particular,
\[
\frac14<a_n<\frac5{16},
\qquad \frac{11}{16}<l_n<\frac34,
\qquad \frac13<\beta_n<\frac5{11}.
\]
For \(\mathbf X\sim p\), its original mean is \(\mathbf0\), and its positive-definite covariance is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Sigma_n:=\operatorname{Cov}_p(\mathbf X)
&=\sigma_{\perp,n}^2 I_n+c_n\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^\top,\\
\sigma_{\perp,n}^2
&=l_n\beta_n(1-\beta_n)
=\frac{q_n}{(1+q_n)(1+2q_n)},\\
c_n&=a_n+l_n\beta_n^2=\beta_n.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(\sigma_{\perp,n}^2\) is the assignment's \(\sigma_\perp^2\) in dimension \(n\), and the coefficient \(c_n\) equals the predecessor's variance coefficient \(\xi_n\).

For an independent \(T\sim\iota_{L_n}\), put \(\mathbf Y=(\mathbf X,T)\). Its original mean \(\mathbf j_n=(\mathbf0,L_n/2)\) is an interior integer point of \(A_{n,L_n}\). The support is full-dimensional, complete, and saturated:
\[
\operatorname{conv}(A_{n,L_n})=\mathcal P_n\times[0,L_n],
\qquad
A_{n,L_n}=\operatorname{conv}(A_{n,L_n})\cap\mathbb Z^R,
\qquad
\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Z}(A_{n,L_n}-A_{n,L_n})=\mathbb Z^R.
\]
The original \(P_n\) is strictly positive and globally log-affine, with tilt \(((\log q_n)\mathbf1_n,0)\). It satisfies, for every \(\mathbf w\in\mathbb R^R\),
\[
\mathbb E_{P_n}\bigl[(\mathbf w\cdot(\mathbf Y-\mathbf j_n))^4\bigr]
\le80\left(\operatorname{Var}_{P_n}[\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf Y]\right)^2.
\]

Let \(\Xi_n\) be any affine covariance-whitening of this same original \(\mathbf Y\) about \(\mathbf j_n\). Thus \(\Xi_n=B_n(\mathbf Y-\mathbf j_n)\), where \(B_n\in\mathbb R^{R\times R}\) satisfies \(B_n\operatorname{Cov}_{P_n}(\mathbf Y)B_n^\top=I_R\). It has mean zero, covariance \(I_R\), and \(\mathbb E[(\mathbf w\cdot\Xi_n)^4]\le80\|\mathbf w\|^4\) for every \(\mathbf w\in\mathbb R^R\). With the fixed number \(t_0=1-\sqrt3/2\in(0,1)\), every \(n\ge9\) satisfies
\[
\Pr_{P_n}\!\left(\|\Xi_n\|\le(1-t_0)\sqrt R\right)
\ge a_n>\frac14.
\]

Define the original maximum atom \(P_{n,\max}=\max_{\mathbf y\in A_{n,L_n}}P_n(\mathbf y)\), the original entropy \(H(P_n)=-\sum_{\mathbf y}P_n(\mathbf y)\log P_n(\mathbf y)\), and \(\rho_n=2^R P_{n,\max}\). Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{n,\max}&=\frac{a_n}{L_n+1}\le\frac1{L_n+1},
&\rho_n&\le2^{1-3n}\longrightarrow0,\\
8^R\sum_{\mathbf y\in A_{n,L_n}}P_n(\mathbf y)^4
&\le\rho_n^3\longrightarrow0,\\
H(P_n)&=\log Z_n^\star+\log(L_n+1)\ge\log(L_n+1),\\
H(P_n)-R\log2&\ge(3n-1)\log2\longrightarrow+\infty,\\
\frac{|A_{n,L_n}|}{2^R}
&=\frac{(1+2^{-n})(L_n+1)}2\longrightarrow+\infty.
\end{aligned}
\]

Precisely, for every \(A,c,L_{\mathrm{threshold}},\varepsilon,K_0>0\), there is an integer \(n_0\ge9\) with \(n_0\ge K_0\) such that every integer \(n\ge n_0\) simultaneously has \(R\ge K_0\), \(|A_{n,L_n}|/2^R\ge K_0\),
\[
H(P_n)-R\log2\ge L_{\mathrm{threshold}},
\qquad \rho_n\le\varepsilon,
\qquad 8^R\sum_{\mathbf y}P_n(\mathbf y)^4\le\varepsilon,
\]
and
\[
\Pr_{P_n}\!\left(\|\Xi_n\|\le(1-t_0)\sqrt R\right)
>A\exp\!\left(-c\sqrt R\,t_0^3\right).
\]
Here \(K_0\) is an arbitrary prescribed lower rank and support-cardinality threshold. The directional fourth-moment ceiling is the fixed absolute number 80, independent of all these parameters. Consequently the violation also holds with any fixed allowed fourth-moment ceiling at least 80, even when \(A\) and \(c\) are chosen as functions of that ceiling. In particular, no finite positive coefficients \(A,c\) make
\[
\Pr_{P_n}\!\left(\|\Xi_n\|\le(1-t)\sqrt R\right)
\le A\exp\!\left(-c\sqrt R\,t^3\right)
\]
hold for all \(t\in(0,1)\) and all sufficiently large \(n\) in this family, even after any fixed entropy, atomic, rank, and cardinality restrictions just quantified.

The atomic fourth-power sum is not the actual third depth moment. No vanishing normalized actual depth moment or mixed-integer counterexample to the locked conjecture is asserted. In particular, the actual \(\mathcal M_2\) and \(\mathcal M_3\) bounds for \(p_n\otimes\iota_L\) in `3e1cc0fa666b159b` are not transferred to the different original source \(P_n\) here.

## Proof

The geometry, root bounds, exact origin row, mean zero, and directional fourth-moment bound for that row are accepted in `3e1cc0fa666b159b`. We use its exact row as the original \(p\), not its uniform source. Its root equation gives \(q_n^{-n}=q_n(1+q_n)^{n-1}\) and \(Z_n^\star=(1+q_n)^{n-1}(1+2q_n)\). Summing the weights on the apex and on the cube therefore gives exactly \(a_n\) and \(l_n\). Conditional on the cube, the coordinates are independent Bernoulli variables of parameter \(\beta_n\). This proves all three alias identifications. Since \(1/2<q_n<5/6\), monotonicity of \(q/(1+2q)\) and \(q/(1+q)\), together with \(l_n=1-a_n\), proves their displayed uniform bounds. Also \(l_n\beta_n=a_n\), independently confirming that each original coordinate has mean zero.

On the cube, the conditional second-moment matrix is \(\beta_n(1-\beta_n)I_n+\beta_n^2\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^\top\); at the apex it is \(\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^\top\). Since the original mean is zero, their mixture is exactly \(\Sigma_n\). Direct substitution gives the displayed formulas for \(\sigma_{\perp,n}^2\) and \(c_n\), including \(c_n=\beta_n\). In particular \(\sigma_{\perp,n}^2>0\), so \(\Sigma_n\) is positive definite. The vector \(\mathbf1_n\) is an eigenvector with eigenvalue \(\sigma_{\perp,n}^2+nc_n\). Hence the apex has original Mahalanobis squared norm
\[
(-\mathbf1_n)^\top\Sigma_n^{-1}(-\mathbf1_n)
=\frac{n}{\sigma_{\perp,n}^2+nc_n}
\le\frac1{c_n}\le\frac1{a_n}<4.
\]
The second inequality uses \(c_n=a_n+l_n\beta_n^2\ge a_n>0\), not an estimate in the opposite direction.

The predecessor proves \(D_n=\mathcal P_n\cap\mathbb Z^n\), full dimension, and interiority of \(\mathbf0\). The convex hull of a Cartesian product of finite sets equals the product of their convex hulls: for the nontrivial inclusion, multiply the convex-combination coefficients of the two factors. This gives \(\operatorname{conv}(A_{n,L_n})=\mathcal P_n\times[0,L_n]\). Taking integer points gives completeness. The support contains \((\mathbf0,0)\), \((\mathbf e_i,0)\) for every \(i\), and \((\mathbf0,1)\). Its differences thus generate \(\mathbb Z^R\), and its affine lattice is exactly \(\mathbb Z^R\). This verifies saturation, not just full real affine dimension. Because \(L_n\) is positive and even, \(\mathbf j_n\) is a supported integer point, and the interiority statements in both factors make it interior to the product convex hull.

The original probability table is, on the entire complete support,
\[
P_n(\mathbf x,k)
=\frac{\exp((\log q_n)\mathbf1_n\cdot\mathbf x)}{Z_n^\star(L_n+1)}
\quad(\mathbf x\in D_n,\ k\in I_{L_n}).
\]
This verifies strict positivity and the single global log-affine tilt. Independence gives its stated original mean. Moreover, this same \(P_n\) is its maximum-entropy row at \(\mathbf j_n\): any probability table \(\mu\) on the support with that mean has \(\mathbb E_\mu\log P_n=\mathbb E_{P_n}\log P_n\), by affinity of the logarithm. Therefore its relative entropy with respect to \(P_n\) equals \(H(P_n)-H(\mu)\ge0\), with equality only at \(\mu=P_n\). Here the elementary relative-entropy inequality follows by applying \(z\log z\ge z-1\) to \(z=\mu(\mathbf y)/P_n(\mathbf y)\) and summing with weights \(P_n(\mathbf y)\). Thus no additional entropy reweighting is concealed in the source.

For a uniform integer variable \(T\) on \(I_L\), finite sums give \(\mathbb ET=L/2\) and \(s_L^2:=\operatorname{Var}T=L(L+2)/12\). At every supported value,
\[
\frac{(T-L/2)^2}{s_L^2}
\le\frac{L^2/4}{L(L+2)/12}
=\frac{3L}{L+2}<3.
\]
Also \((T-L/2)^4\le(L^2/4)(T-L/2)^2\), and taking expectations yields \(\mathbb E[(T-L/2)^4]\le3s_L^4\).

To check the original product's fourth moments, fix \(\mathbf u\in\mathbb R^n\) and \(v\in\mathbb R\). The scalar projections \(\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf X\) and \(v(T-L_n/2)\) are independent and centered. The accepted fourth-moment bound for the exact original \(p=\nu_{n,\mathbf0}\), the interval bound, and expansion of the fourth power give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_{P_n}\bigl[(\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf X+v(T-L_n/2))^4\bigr]
&=\mathbb E_p[(\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf X)^4]
+6\operatorname{Var}_p[\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf X]v^2s_{L_n}^2
+v^4\mathbb E[(T-L_n/2)^4]\\
&\le80\left(\operatorname{Var}_p[\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf X]\right)^2
+6\operatorname{Var}_p[\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf X]v^2s_{L_n}^2
+3v^4s_{L_n}^4\\
&\le80\left(\operatorname{Var}_p[\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf X]+v^2s_{L_n}^2\right)^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Both odd mixed terms vanish by independence and centering. The expression inside the final square is the variance of the original product projection. This proves its directional bound, including directions with a zero coefficient or zero variance.

The product covariance is \(\operatorname{diag}(\Sigma_n,s_{L_n}^2)\), positive definite. Any whitening matrix \(B_n\) is invertible, and its covariance identity implies \(B_n^\top B_n=\operatorname{Cov}_{P_n}(\mathbf Y)^{-1}\). Thus all such whitenings give exactly the same squared norm,
\[
\|\Xi_n\|^2
=\mathbf X^\top\Sigma_n^{-1}\mathbf X
+\frac{(T-L_n/2)^2}{s_{L_n}^2}.
\]
The directional fourth-moment inequality is preserved because a direction \(\mathbf w\) after whitening is the original direction \(B_n^\top\mathbf w\), whose variance is \(\|\mathbf w\|^2\). Whitening only transports the original support and probabilities. In particular, since \(\mathbf j_n\in\mathbb Z^R\), its transported support is complete in the transported lattice \(B_n\mathbb Z^R\), and its difference lattice is exactly \(B_n\mathbb Z^R\). It remains a finite discrete law, not a Lebesgue density.

On the entire apex event \(\{\mathbf X=-\mathbf1_n\}\), regardless of \(T\), the two exact bounds above give \(\|\Xi_n\|^2\le1/a_n+3<7\). Its original \(P_n\)-probability is exactly \(a_n\). For every \(n\ge9\), \(R\ge10\) and \((1-t_0)^2R=3R/4\ge15/2>7\). Thus the whole apex event lies in the specified lower-tail event. This proves its lower bound \(a_n>1/4\), with the original mean and covariance used throughout.

Because \(0<q_n<1\), every cube weight \(q_n^{|\mathbf b|}\) is at most one, whereas the apex weight \(q_n^{-n}\) is greater than one. The largest base atom is therefore exactly \(a_n\), and independence proves \(P_{n,\max}=a_n/(L_n+1)\). Consequently \(\rho_n\le2^{n+1}/(2^{4n}+1)\le2^{1-3n}\). For any finite probability table,
\[
\sum_{\mathbf y}P_n(\mathbf y)^4
\le P_{n,\max}^3\sum_{\mathbf y}P_n(\mathbf y)
=P_{n,\max}^3.
\]
Multiplication by \(8^R=(2^R)^3\) gives the stated atomic fourth-power bound. The original base mean zero and its displayed logarithmic table give \(H(p)=\log Z_n^\star\). Independence then gives \(H(P_n)=\log Z_n^\star+\log(L_n+1)\). In particular \(H(p)\ge0\) and \(\log(L_n+1)\ge4n\log2\), proving the entropy-surplus estimate. The support-cardinality formula follows from \(|D_n|=2^n+1\).

For the full quantifier statement, fix any positive \(A,c,L_{\mathrm{threshold}},\varepsilon,K_0\). Choose an integer \(n_0\) sufficiently large that all \(n\ge n_0\) satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
n&\ge9,\qquad n\ge K_0,\qquad 2^{4n-1}\ge K_0,\\
(3n-1)\log2&\ge L_{\mathrm{threshold}},\\
2^{1-3n}&\le\min\{\varepsilon,\varepsilon^{1/3}\},\\
c\,t_0^3\sqrt{n+1}&>\log(4A).
\end{aligned}
\]
Such an \(n_0\) exists: the left sides of the increasing conditions tend to infinity, while \(2^{1-3n}\) tends to zero. The support ratio is at least \(2^{4n-1}\). All the entropy, rank, cardinality, and atomic requirements therefore follow, and the last condition gives \(A\exp(-c\sqrt R\,t_0^3)<1/4\). Combining it with the apex probability proves the required strict violation. This proves failure for every fixed pair of positive coefficients, including coefficients permitted to depend on a fixed fourth-moment ceiling of 80 or more.

Finally, `c34e2143f28d8c83` is used only for contrast. Within its triangle family, retain that fact's block count \(b\), outer-column probability \(\alpha\), and centered horizontal variable \(U\). The latter equals \(-1,0,1\) with probabilities \(\alpha,1-2\alpha,\alpha\). Hence its directional kurtosis is
\[
\frac{\mathbb E U^4}{(\operatorname{Var}U)^2}
=\frac{2\alpha}{(2\alpha)^2}
=\frac1{2\alpha}\ge\frac{b^2}{3}\longrightarrow+\infty,
\]
using exactly that fact's \(\alpha\le3/(2b^2)\). A product direction selecting that horizontal coordinate has the same kurtosis, and whitening preserves the set of directional kurtosis ratios. The present construction has uniformly bounded directional kurtosis instead, so this triangle obstruction does not explain away the new counterexample. Neither contrast nor the atomic estimates supply a vanishing actual third depth moment. The accepted uniform-source depth-moment estimates belong to the different original laws explicitly distinguished above, and no mixed-integer body for a disproof of the locked problem has been constructed here.
