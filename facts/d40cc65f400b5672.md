## Statement

Use the finite actual contact rows and coefficient-convergent touching planes of 9b0e9ed78338976f. Thus $\nu_{ij}=(\sigma_{ij}/L_j(Y_{ij}))\rho_{ij}$, $\rho_{ij}$ has barycenter $Y_{ij}$, $\nu_{ij}$ is carried by $A_{ij}=L_j$, and $\sum_i\nu_{ij}=dm_j/(M_jL_j)$. Let $c_{ij}\to c_i$ be the continuous gradients of the planes. Retain an actual cap law on uniformly bounded nonvertical cap records, with the same cap selections for every row and the geometric cells. Record the same bounded rate $r_j=\lambda_j(-\log L_j)_+$, $\lambda_j\to0$, in all inverse measures. Write $Z=\{L=0\}$ on the limiting sampled set. Take joint subsequential limits of the full and cap-selected measures, and let $\widehat\zeta_i$ and $\widehat\zeta_i^H$ be their labeled restrictions to $Z$ with rate retained.

For a unit continuous direction $e$, let $I_e$ contain the surviving labels with $c_i/|c_i|=e$. Let $N_e$ and $N_{e,H}$ be the corresponding geometric-cell inverse defects weighted by the continuous gradient length in original-volume normalization. Cells are taken on each fiber, with coinciding restrictions assigned to one representative. Then on physical-position and rate space,
$$
N_e=M\sum_{i\in I_e}|c_i|\widehat\zeta_i,\qquad
N_{e,H}=M\sum_{i\in I_e}|c_i|\widehat\zeta_i^H
$$
for almost every record of the actual limiting cap law $\theta$. The second identity holds jointly with the record and is valid before integration over any Borel subset of it. In particular
$$
\int (1,w)\,dN_e=M\sum_{i\in I_e}\frac{\sigma_i|c_i|}{L_i}(1,Y_i)
-\int_{\{\nabla_X L\ne0,\ \nabla_X L/|\nabla_X L|=e\}}\frac{|\nabla_XL|}{L}(1,w)\,dm.
$$
For an own cap through $Y_i=(p_i,y_i)$ with normal $(a_H,t c_i)$, $t>0$, and plane $A_i=L_i+a_i\cdot(z-p_i)+c_i\cdot(X-y_i)$, the margin on $A_i=0$ is $-tL_i+(a_H-t a_i)\cdot(z-p_i)$. Restriction to records whose normal is not a positive multiple of $e$ therefore gives the integral of the full row sum over that sublaw, with the original cap weights.

## Proof

The rows are positive summands of a measure with mixed-volume density. In each fiber the set of ties between unequal affine restrictions has $d$-volume zero. On each full-dimensional open cell all active planes have the same restriction and hence the same continuous gradient. Any row that charges that cell has an active plane there almost everywhere and contributes with that gradient. For large $j$, a label with limit gradient in direction $e$ cannot have the same current gradient as a label with limit gradient zero or in a different oriented direction. This follows from the finite list of coefficient limits. A label of limiting weight zero has inverse mass at most $\sigma_{ij}/\inf_{i,j}L_j(Y_{ij})\to0$. The gradients are bounded, so the geometric pieces that can be charged only by such labels also have vanishing weighted mass. Thus the sum of the $|c_{ij}|\nu_{ij}$ over $I_e$ agrees with the corresponding sum of weighted geometric-cell inverse measures up to an error tending to zero in total variation. Multiplying by $M_j$ puts the geometric weight in original-volume normalization. Assigning a coincident restriction to one representative does not alter this identity, since either all its surviving labels have limiting direction $e$ or none do.

Attaching the same rate and the same cap record with the same cap restriction preserves the equality up to the same error. In fact the selected measure is dominated by the product of the cap law and the full row measure with rate. Compactness and bounded total inverse mass give the joint subsequential limits and pass this domination to the limit. The positive selected limit therefore disintegrates over $\theta$, with a kernel dominated by the full limiting row with rate. Now restrict the joint equality of limiting measures to $Z$ and disintegrate. This proves the two formulas and allows any Borel record restriction without imposing continuity of the selected sublaw.

For the moment formula take $\chi(c)=|c|$ for nonzero limiting gradients of direction $e$ and $\chi(c)=0$ for the other gradients in the moment identity of 9b0e9ed78338976f. Forgetting rate projects each $\widehat\zeta_i$ to its row defect there. The equality on own caps is the substitution $c_i\cdot(X-y_i)=-L_i-a_i\cdot(z-p_i)$ on $A_i=0$. It gives strict exclusion in the own fiber and allows other fibers to contribute. Integrating the jointly disintegrated cap identity over $\{H:u_H\text{ is not a positive multiple of }e\}$ keeps every relevant cap–row pair, including caps associated with other sources.
