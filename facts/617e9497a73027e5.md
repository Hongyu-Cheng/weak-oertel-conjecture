## Statement

Fix $\lambda=(8/25,31/100,1/20,8/25)$ in the interior of a tetrahedron. In the family of affine margins with vertex values $(u/\lambda_0,(1-u)/\lambda_1,-v/\lambda_2,-(1-v)/\lambda_3)$ and $u,v\in(0,1)$, each plane passes through $\lambda$ and has strict split $\{0,1\}\mid\{2,3\}$. At $u=3/5$, the positive-side relative cap volume is not convex in $v$, even though every coordinate of $\lambda$ is strictly less than $1/3$.

## Proof

The affine value at $\lambda$ is $u+(1-u)-v-(1-v)=0$. The three samples $v_-=9/1000,v_0=1/100,v_+=11/1000$ are equally spaced. Insert the three raw heights in the positive-denominator volume formula of tetrahedral-quadrilateral-section-calculus. The respective fractions are
$$
\frac{268399175000}{502765515771},\qquad
\frac{2347280500}{4419318981},\qquad
\frac{35793986575000}{67735924512529}.
$$
Subtract twice the middle from the sum of the outer values. The result is $-216696601290923321682823000/135221182702189782497027701155183<0$, violating the midpoint convexity inequality.
