## Statement

Put
\[
p(x,y):=|x|+|y|+|x+y|,
\qquad
H:=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:p(x,y)<1\},
\]
and
\[
r(x,y):=\frac{1-2x-2y}{31}.
\]
Define the affine map \(s:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^{30}\) by
\[
s_1=x+r,\qquad s_2=y+r,\qquad s_3=x+y+r,\qquad
s_i=r\quad(4\leq i\leq30).
\tag{1}
\]
Define \(F_s\) by
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_s:=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:\;&
s_1\geq|x|,\ s_2\geq|y|,\ s_3\geq|x+y|,\\
&s_i\geq0\ (4\leq i\leq30),\ \sum_{i=1}^{30}s_i\leq1\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
On \(F_s\), define
\[
\lambda_0:=1-\sum_{i=1}^{30}s_i,\qquad
\lambda_1:=s_1-|x|,\quad
\lambda_2:=s_2-|y|,\quad
\lambda_3:=s_3-|x+y|,
\]
\[
\lambda_i:=s_i\quad(4\leq i\leq30),
\]
and put
\[
U(s):=\frac1{30!}\int_{F_s}
\left(1-p(x,y)-\max_{0\leq i\leq30}\lambda_i(x,y)\right)^{30}
\,dx\,dy,
\]
\[
W_\infty:=
\frac1{30!}\int_H(1-p(x,y))^{30}\,dx\,dy
=\frac3{1984\cdot30!}.
\]
Its feasible region is the quadrilateral
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_s
={}&\{(x,y):x+y\leq\tfrac12,\ 30x-y\geq-\tfrac12,\\
&\hspace{32mm}-x+30y\geq-\tfrac12,\ x+y\geq-\tfrac1{60}\}\\
={}&\operatorname{conv}\left\{
A:=\left(\tfrac12,0\right),\
B:=\left(0,\tfrac12\right),\
C:=\left(-\tfrac1{60},0\right),\
D:=\left(0,-\tfrac1{60}\right)
\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
Let
\[
O:=(0,0),\qquad
E:=\left(\tfrac1{62},-\tfrac1{62}\right),\qquad
E':=\left(-\tfrac1{62},\tfrac1{62}\right),
\tag{3}
\]
and, for \(q_0,q_1,q_2\in\mathbb Q\), let
\[
h_{30}(q_0,q_1,q_2)
:=\sum_{\substack{i,j,k\geq0\\i+j+k=30}}q_0^i q_1^j q_2^k.
\tag{4}
\]
With
\[
\alpha:=\frac{30}{31},\qquad
\varepsilon:=\frac{29}{31},\qquad
\theta:=\frac{14}{15},
\tag{5}
\]
the registered functional has the exact value
\[
\begin{aligned}
30!\,U(s)
={}&\frac{\alpha^{30}}{3968}
+\frac{h_{30}(\alpha,0,\varepsilon)}{61504}\\
&+\frac{h_{30}(\alpha,\varepsilon,\theta)}{1845120}
+\frac{h_{30}(\alpha,\theta,\theta)}{3571200}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
In particular,
\[
U(s)>\frac1{16}W_\infty.
\tag{7}
\]

There is also a true-depth statement for the same graph.  For
\((x,y)\in\overline H\), write
\[
K_{x,y}
:=
\bigl(|x|,|y|,|x+y|,0,\ldots,0\bigr)
+(1-p(x,y))\Delta_{30},
\tag{8}
\]
where
\[
\Delta_{30}:=\{u\in\mathbb R^{30}:u_i\geq0,\ \sum_i u_i\leq1\},
\]
and let \(\delta_{x,y}(v)\) be the absolute halfspace depth of
\(v\in K_{x,y}\) in \(K_{x,y}\).  Put
\[
T_0:=\operatorname{conv}\{O,A,B\},\qquad
T_1:=\operatorname{conv}\{O,A,E\}.
\tag{9}
\]
Then
\[
\delta_{x,y}(s(x,y))=\frac{(30r(x,y))^{30}}{30!}
\qquad((x,y)\in T_0),
\tag{10}
\]
and, on \(T_1\), if
\[
m(x,y):=r(x,y)+2y,
\tag{11}
\]
then
\[
\delta_{x,y}(s(x,y))\geq\frac{(30m(x,y))^{30}}{30!}.
\tag{12}
\]
Consequently
\[
\int_{T_0\cup T_1}\delta_{x,y}(s(x,y))\,dx\,dy
\geq
\frac1{30!}\frac{(30/31)^{30}}{3844}
>\frac1{16}W_\infty.
\tag{13}
\]

## Proof

For the map (1),
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{30}s_i=2x+2y+30r,
\]
so its 31 barycentric facet slacks are
\[
\lambda_0=\lambda_4=\cdots=\lambda_{30}=r,
\tag{14}
\]
\[
\lambda_1=r+x-|x|,\qquad
\lambda_2=r+y-|y|,\qquad
\lambda_3=r+x+y-|x+y|.
\tag{15}
\]
The inequalities defining feasibility are therefore
\[
r\geq0,\qquad r+2x\geq0,\qquad r+2y\geq0,\qquad
r+2x+2y\geq0.
\tag{16}
\]
Substitution of the definition of \(r\) turns (16) into the four
inequalities in (2).  Their successive intersections give exactly the
four displayed vertices, proving (2).  Since
\[
t-|t|\leq0\qquad(t\in\mathbb R),
\]
(14)-(15) also show throughout \(F_s\) that
\[
\max_{0\leq i\leq30}\lambda_i=r.
\tag{17}
\]
Thus the affine integrand base in the registered functional is
\[
L(x,y):=1-p(x,y)-r(x,y).
\tag{18}
\]

The three lines \(x=0\), \(y=0\), and \(x+y=0\) partition \(F_s\), up to
their area-zero boundaries, into the following six triangles.  The second
column is twice the triangle area, and the last column lists the three
vertex values of \(L\):
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{triangle}&2\,\operatorname{area}&\text{values of }L\\ \hline
\operatorname{conv}\{O,A,B\}&1/4&(\alpha,0,0)\\
\operatorname{conv}\{O,A,E\}&1/124&(\alpha,0,\varepsilon)\\
\operatorname{conv}\{O,E,D\}&1/3720&(\alpha,\varepsilon,\theta)\\
\operatorname{conv}\{O,D,C\}&1/3600&(\alpha,\theta,\theta)\\
\operatorname{conv}\{O,C,E'\}&1/3720&(\alpha,\theta,\varepsilon)\\
\operatorname{conv}\{O,E',B\}&1/124&(\alpha,\varepsilon,0).
\end{array}
\tag{19}
\]
All entries follow by direct substitution.  For example,
\[
L(O)=\frac{30}{31},\quad
L(E)=\frac{29}{31},\quad
L(C)=L(D)=\frac{14}{15},
\]
and the determinants of the two edge vectors give the areas in (19).

If \(T\) is a triangle of area \(A_T\) and an affine function has vertex
values \(q_0,q_1,q_2\), barycentric integration gives
\[
\int_T L^{30}
=\frac{2A_T}{31\cdot32}\,
h_{30}(q_0,q_1,q_2).
\tag{20}
\]
Indeed, expand \(L^{30}\) in barycentric coordinates and use
\[
\int_{\{u,v\geq0,\ u+v\leq1\}}
u^i v^j(1-u-v)^k\,du\,dv
=\frac{i!j!k!}{32!}
\quad(i+j+k=30).
\]
Applying (20) to all six rows of (19), and pairing the two congruent
mixed rows of each type, gives exactly (6).  This is a complete
piecewise integral over \(F_s\), including every absolute-value cone.
The boundary lines have area zero, so the use of closed inequalities in
\(F_s\) does not change the integral.

For completeness, the stated value of \(W_\infty\) follows by the same
calculation.  The hexagon \(H\) consists, up to boundaries, of six
linearity-cone triangles of area \(1/8\).  On each one,
\(1-p\) has vertex values \(1,0,0\).  Hence (20) gives
\[
\int_H(1-p)^{30}
=6\frac{1/4}{31\cdot32}
=\frac3{1984}.
\tag{21}
\]

It remains to prove the stronger true-depth comparison, which also gives
a short exact proof of (7).  On \(T_0\), all 31 slacks in (14)-(15) equal
\(r\), and \(1-p=31r\).  Thus \(s(x,y)\) is the centroid of the whole
simplex \(K_{x,y}\).  Accepted fact `4030392d64b42e37` records the exact
30-dimensional Grünbaum constant
\[
\gamma:=\left(\frac{30}{31}\right)^{30}
\]
and the matching facet-parallel cap at a simplex centroid.  It follows
that the true depth is exactly (10).

On \(T_1\) one has
\[
x\geq0,\qquad y\leq0,\qquad x+y\geq0.
\]
Consequently 30 of the slacks equal \(r\), while the remaining slack is
\[
\lambda_2=m=r+2y,\qquad 0\leq m\leq r.
\tag{22}
\]
Let \(g=(1/31,\ldots,1/31)\) be the centroid of
\(\Delta_{30}\).  The simplex
\[
Q_{x,y}:=s(x,y)+31m(x,y)(\Delta_{30}-g)
\tag{23}
\]
has centroid \(s(x,y)\), volume \((31m)^{30}/30!\), and lies in
\(K_{x,y}\).  To check the last assertion in barycentric coordinates,
each of its 31 coordinates has the form
\[
\lambda_i-m+31m\,\mu_i
\]
for a barycentric coordinate \(\mu_i\geq0\) of a point of
\(\Delta_{30}\); this is nonnegative by (22).  Every halfspace containing
\(s(x,y)\) therefore captures at least the Grünbaum fraction \(\gamma\)
of \(Q_{x,y}\).  Since \(Q_{x,y}\subseteq K_{x,y}\), this proves (12).
The statement remains valid on the boundary \(m=0\).

On \(T_0\), the affine function \(30r\) has vertex values
\(\alpha,0,0\).  On \(T_1\), the affine function \(30m\) has the same
three vertex values.  Their twice-areas are respectively \(1/4\) and
\(1/124\).  Equation (20) therefore gives
\[
\int_{T_0}(30r)^{30}\,dx\,dy
=\frac{\gamma}{3968},
\qquad
\int_{T_1}(30m)^{30}\,dx\,dy
=\frac{\gamma}{123008}.
\tag{24}
\]
Since \(123008=31\cdot3968=32\cdot3844\), (10), (12), and
(24) yield the first inequality in (13).

Finally, the remaining strict comparison is exact:
\[
7936\cdot30^{30}-2883\cdot31^{30}
=46518830145873519849210198297483727687839697917>0.
\tag{25}
\]
After division by positive factors, (25) is precisely
\[
\frac{(30/31)^{30}}{3844}
>\frac3{31744}
=\frac1{16}\frac3{1984}.
\tag{26}
\]
Equations (21) and (26) finish (13).  On \(T_0\) the registered integrand
equals the true depth density in (10).  On \(T_1\),
\[
1-p-r=29r+m\geq30m,
\]
so the registered density also dominates the lower bound in (12).
Thus (13) implies (7) independently of the full exact evaluation (6).
