## Statement

Retain the canonical rescaled internal whole-label atlas, source densities, and recomputed-complement notation of accepted fact de023bd405d38571. Thus \(\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\) is a finite family of labels with positive weights \(\eta_\sigma\), opposite endpoints \(a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\), occurrence contacts \(x_{\sigma,y}\in C_y\), and occurrence densities \(\widetilde g_{\sigma,y}\) of mass \(\eta_\sigma\) and barycenter \(x_{\sigma,y}\). The symbol \(Q>0\) is the canonical reservoir mass retained in that fact. Let \(\Gamma\subseteq\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\) be a whole-label subfamily with nonempty endpoint set, and put

\[
 R_y^\Gamma
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 S_\Gamma:=\{y\in S_{\mathrm{miss}}:R_y^\Gamma>0\},
\]

and, for \(y\in S_\Gamma\),

\[
 F_y^\Gamma
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \widetilde g_{\sigma,y},
 \qquad
 B_y^\Gamma:=\frac12-F_y^\Gamma,
\]

\[
 b_y^\Gamma:=\int_{C_y}B_y^\Gamma
 =\frac{a_y}{2}-R_y^\Gamma>0,
 \qquad
 \bar q_y^\Gamma
 :=\frac1{b_y^\Gamma}\int_{C_y}xB_y^\Gamma(x)\,dx.
\]

Define the corresponding full-atlas quantities by the exact alias
\(\mathrm{full}:=\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\):

\[
 F_y^{\mathrm{full}}:=F_y^{\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}},
 \quad
 B_y^{\mathrm{full}}:=B_y^{\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}},
 \quad
 b_y^{\mathrm{full}}:=b_y^{\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}},
 \quad
 \bar q_y^{\mathrm{full}}
 :=\bar q_y^{\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}.
\]

These full quantities are defined at every \(y\in S_\Gamma\), because
\(S_{\mathrm{full}}:=S_{\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\) contains \(S_\Gamma\), and accepted fact
de023bd405d38571 gives \(b_y^{\mathrm{full}}>0\).

Let

\[
 \Delta:=\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\Gamma,
 \qquad
 \Delta_y
 :=\{\sigma\in\Delta:y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}\},
\]

\[
 G_y^\Delta:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta_y}\widetilde g_{\sigma,y},
 \qquad
 \delta_y^\Delta:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta_y}\eta_\sigma.
\]

Then, pointwise on \(C_y\), and at the level of masses and first moments,

\[
 \boxed{B_y^\Gamma=B_y^{\mathrm{full}}+G_y^\Delta,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{b_y^\Gamma=b_y^{\mathrm{full}}+\delta_y^\Delta,}
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 b_y^\Gamma\bar q_y^\Gamma
 =b_y^{\mathrm{full}}\bar q_y^{\mathrm{full}}
 +\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta_y}\eta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}.}
\]

Equivalently, the recomputed complement barycenter is the genuine weighted
barycentric combination

\[
 \boxed{
 \bar q_y^\Gamma
 =\frac{b_y^{\mathrm{full}}}{b_y^\Gamma}\bar q_y^{\mathrm{full}}
 +\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta_y}
 \frac{\eta_\sigma}{b_y^\Gamma}x_{\sigma,y},}
\]

whose nonnegative coefficients sum to one.

Now fix one affine map
\(s:\operatorname{aff}(S_\Gamma)\to\mathbb R^d\), one linear subspace
\(W\subseteq\mathbb R^d\), and the quotient map
\(\pi_W:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R^d/W\). Define the sourcewise deleted
residual sum

\[
 d_y^\Delta(s)
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta_y}
 \eta_\sigma\bigl(x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\bigr).
\]

The exact residual update is

\[
 \boxed{
 b_y^\Gamma\bigl(\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\bigr)
 =b_y^{\mathrm{full}}
   \bigl(\bar q_y^{\mathrm{full}}-s(y)\bigr)
 +d_y^\Delta(s).}
\]

Consequently, without any prior containment assumption,

\[
 \boxed{
 \bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\in W
 \iff
 b_y^{\mathrm{full}}
 \pi_W\bigl(\bar q_y^{\mathrm{full}}-s(y)\bigr)
 +\pi_W\bigl(d_y^\Delta(s)\bigr)=0.}
\]

In particular, if the full complement already collapses modulo \(W\),

\[
 \bar q_y^{\mathrm{full}}-s(y)\in W
 \qquad(y\in S_\Gamma),
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{
 \pi_W\bigl(\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\bigr)
 =\frac1{b_y^\Gamma}\pi_W\bigl(d_y^\Delta(s)\bigr),}
\]

and hence

\[
 \boxed{
 \bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\in W
 \iff d_y^\Delta(s)\in W.}
\]

Thus containment of every deleted occurrence residual
\(x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\) in \(W\) is a sufficient condition. The exact
condition is only containment of the sourcewise \(\eta_\sigma\)-weighted
sum, so the formula also accounts for any cancellation among deleted
outliers at the same source.

More precisely, under the full-complement containment above, define

\[
 \mathcal D_\Delta(s,W)
 :=\operatorname{span}
 \left\{
 \pi_W\bigl(d_y^\Delta(s)\bigr):y\in S_\Gamma
 \right\}
 \subseteq\mathbb R^d/W.
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{span}
 \left\{
 \pi_W\bigl(\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\bigr):y\in S_\Gamma
 \right\}
 =\mathcal D_\Delta(s,W).}
\]

Equivalently, for the minimal extension of this fixed subspace \(W\) that
also contains all recomputed complement residuals,

\[
 W_\Gamma
 :=W+\operatorname{span}
 \{\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y):y\in S_\Gamma\},
\]

one has the exact identities

\[
 \boxed{
 W_\Gamma
 =W+\operatorname{span}
 \{d_y^\Delta(s):y\in S_\Gamma\},}
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 \dim W_\Gamma
 =\dim W+\dim\mathcal D_\Delta(s,W).}
\]

Moreover,

\[
 \dim\mathcal D_\Delta(s,W)
 \leq
 \left|\{y\in S_\Gamma:\delta_y^\Delta>0\}\right|
 \leq 2|\Delta|.
\]

Thus all deleted outliers incident at one physical source contribute at most
one new complement direction, namely their weighted residual sum.

If all retained occurrence residuals already lie in \(W\), then they remain
in \(W_\Gamma\), and the displayed quotient rank is exactly the number of
new independent complement directions forced relative to the fixed chart
\((s,W)\). Hence the same \(W\) is deletion-stable exactly when
\(\mathcal D_\Delta(s,W)=\{0\}\), while an extension of this fixed \(W\)
within a dimension cap \(k\) exists exactly when

\[
 \dim W+\dim\mathcal D_\Delta(s,W)\leq k.
\]

When \(\delta_y^\Delta>0\), define the deleted occurrence barycenter at the
source by

\[
 \bar x_y^\Delta
 :=\frac1{\delta_y^\Delta}
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Delta_y}\eta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}.
\]

Under full-complement containment, the quotient update becomes

\[
 \pi_W\bigl(\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\bigr)
 =\frac{\delta_y^\Delta}{b_y^\Gamma}
 \pi_W\bigl(\bar x_y^\Delta-s(y)\bigr).
\]

Thus, for fixed deleted source barycenter, a small deleted mass shrinks the
quotient residual by a positive scalar but does not remove its direction or
reduce its exact span. In particular, the displayed update identities alone do
not turn the bound
\(\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta}\eta_\sigma\leq Q/1000\) into a quotient-rank
bound. An additional directional or cancellation theorem is required. The
statement supplies no construction of \(\Gamma\), \(s\), or \(W\), and makes
no bulk-chart existence claim.

## Proof

Fix \(y\in S_\Gamma\). Since \(\Gamma\) and \(\Delta\) partition the
full whole-label family, their incident source densities partition the full
incident sum:

\[
 F_y^{\mathrm{full}}=F_y^\Gamma+G_y^\Delta.
\]

Subtracting this identity from \(1/2\) proves

\[
 B_y^\Gamma
 =\frac12-F_y^\Gamma
 =\frac12-F_y^{\mathrm{full}}+G_y^\Delta
 =B_y^{\mathrm{full}}+G_y^\Delta.
\]

Every \(\widetilde g_{\sigma,y}\) has mass \(\eta_\sigma\), so integration
gives

\[
 b_y^\Gamma
 =b_y^{\mathrm{full}}+\int_{C_y}G_y^\Delta
 =b_y^{\mathrm{full}}+\delta_y^\Delta.
\]

The same densities have barycenters \(x_{\sigma,y}\). Taking first moments
in the pointwise complement identity therefore yields

\[
 b_y^\Gamma\bar q_y^\Gamma
 =b_y^{\mathrm{full}}\bar q_y^{\mathrm{full}}
 +\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta_y}\eta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}.
\]

Division by the positive number \(b_y^\Gamma\) proves the barycentric
formula. Its coefficients sum to one because
\(b_y^\Gamma=b_y^{\mathrm{full}}+\delta_y^\Delta\).

Subtract \(b_y^\Gamma s(y)\) from the first-moment formula and use the mass
identity:

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 b_y^\Gamma\bigl(\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\bigr)
 &=b_y^{\mathrm{full}}
   \bigl(\bar q_y^{\mathrm{full}}-s(y)\bigr)\\
 &\quad+
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Delta_y}
 \eta_\sigma\bigl(x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
\]

This is the residual update. Applying \(\pi_W\) and using
\(b_y^\Gamma>0\) proves the general necessary and sufficient quotient
criterion. If the full complement residual belongs to \(W\), its quotient
term vanishes, giving the specialized formula and the equivalence
\(\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\in W\iff d_y^\Delta(s)\in W\).

For the global span claim, the specialized formula multiplies each
\(\pi_W(d_y^\Delta(s))\) by the nonzero scalar \(1/b_y^\Gamma\).
Multiplication of individual generators by nonzero scalars does not change
their span. This proves the exact quotient-span equality.

Still under full-complement containment, the residual update also gives both
inclusions in

\[
 W+\operatorname{span}
 \{\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y):y\in S_\Gamma\}
 =W+\operatorname{span}
 \{d_y^\Delta(s):y\in S_\Gamma\}.
\]

Taking the quotient by \(W\) proves

\[
 \dim W_\Gamma-\dim W
 =\dim\mathcal D_\Delta(s,W),
\]

which proves the minimal-extension and dimension assertions. The first rank
bound follows because \(\mathcal D_\Delta(s,W)\) has at most one displayed
generator for each source with positive deleted incident mass. Every deleted
whole label has two endpoints, which proves the second rank bound. The
criterion for preserving the same \(W\), and the criterion for extending this
fixed \(W\) within dimension \(k\), follow immediately.

Finally, if \(\delta_y^\Delta>0\), then

\[
 d_y^\Delta(s)
 =\delta_y^\Delta\bigl(\bar x_y^\Delta-s(y)\bigr).
\]

Substitution into the specialized quotient formula proves the last display.
Its coefficient is positive whenever a deleted occurrence is present. Scaling
a nonzero quotient vector by a positive scalar changes its size but not the
one-dimensional subspace that it spans. Therefore the displayed update algebra
controls coefficients but, without an additional geometric input, supplies no
quotient-collapse or rank conclusion from the deleted-mass bound. This
completes the proof.
