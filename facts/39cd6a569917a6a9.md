## Statement

Assume the actual hypotheses of a1081821f9c8a5b1 with one positively weighted group, positive fibers $K_z\subset y(z)+\ell(z)D$ for a fixed $d$-body $D$ with interior mark zero, and affine $y,\ell$ with $\ell>0$. At each positive-force contact assume $Y_a=(p_a,y(p_a))$, $K_{p_a}=y(p_a)+\ell_aD$ with $\ell_a=\ell(p_a)$, and $\delta_a=\varepsilon\ell_a f_D$ for a common $f_D>0$. Use the actual forces $\kappa_a\ge0$, $e_a=\kappa_a\delta_a$, $\kappa=\sum_a\kappa_a>0$, $\bar p=\sum_a\kappa_ap_a/\kappa$ and $K_{\rm base}=\sum_a\kappa_a(p_a-\bar p)(p_a-\bar p)^\top$. Write $N=d+1$, $P_D(\xi)=1+a_D\cdot\xi$ for the optimizing denominator at the mark, $E_D$ for its energy and $q_D=P_D^{-N}/(NE_D)+1/(dN|D|)$.

Pull the positive source term in the separator back at each contact by $X=y(p_a)+\ell_a\xi$. Its measure is the product $\varepsilon f_D(\sum_a\kappa_a\delta_{p_a})\otimes q_D\,dS_D$. Let $M$ be total mixed mass, $W$ the body selector average, $g_z$ the centroid of $K_z$, and use the same $\rho_{ij},u_{ij},e_i=\sum_a e_{ia}$ and $\Delta=\sum_i e_i$ as in the separator. Put
$$
R(z,X)=\beta+\frac{(d+2)\Delta}{2dM}-W(z,X)
-\frac1{2M}\sum_i e_i\sum_j\rho_{ij}\bigl[u_{ij}\cdot(X-g_z)\bigr]^2.
$$
For each facet $F$ of $D$ of normal $n$, put $c_F=\int_Fq_D\,dS_D$ and write $\sigma_n$ for the restriction of total physical surface area to this marked normal over all sampled fibers. The signed measure $R\sigma_n$ has mass $\varepsilon f_Dc_F\kappa$ and base first moment $\varepsilon f_Dc_F\kappa\bar p$. Its centered base second moment dominates $\varepsilon f_Dc_F K_{\rm base}$ in positive-semidefinite order. More generally for every affine vector $V(z,X)$,
$$
\int R\,VV^\top\,d\sigma_n \succeq
\varepsilon f_D\sum_a\kappa_a\int_F q_D(\xi)
V(p_a,y(p_a)+\ell_a\xi)V(p_a,y(p_a)+\ell_a\xi)^\top\,dS_D(\xi).
$$
The same forces work on every facet, and the same product law applies to every measurable subset of a source facet in pointed coordinates.

## Proof

At contact $a$ the correspondence of projective denominators is $v\cdot(X-y(p_a))=(\ell_a v)\cdot\xi$, so positivity and minimization are preserved, with optimizing denominator $P_D$ after pullback. Changing volume variables gives $E_a=\ell_a^d E_D$ and $|K_{p_a}|=\ell_a^d|D|$. A homothety preserves the normal and multiplies area by $\ell_a^{d-1}$. Multiply this Jacobian by the physical source density from a1081821f9c8a5b1, namely $e_a[P_a^{-N}/(NE_a)+1/(dN|K_{p_a}|)]$. The factor $\ell_a$ in $e_a$ cancels the remaining scale, yielding $\varepsilon f_D\kappa_a q_D\,dS_D$. Summing at the marked bases gives the asserted product.

The signed difference of the separator's source and target measures is precisely the positive source term minus $R\,d\sigma$. On each marked normal atom the affine tests vanish and convex tests are nonpositive against this difference. Indeed multiply any such physical test by continuous normal functions between zero and one that converge pointwise to the indicator of $\{n\}$, using both signs for affine tests, and pass to the limit by dominated convergence. All physical measures have finite mass and compact support. The source restriction at $n$ is the pullback product on $F$ by the source homotheties. Affine tests $1$ and $z$ give the two stated moments. For any fixed vector $v$ the function $(v\cdot V)^2$ is convex on the full physical space, so its test gives the scalar inequality for this $v$. The resulting matrix inequality yields the centered base bound on setting $V=z-\bar p$.
