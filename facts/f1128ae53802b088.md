## Statement

For $0<\varepsilon<1$, take the convex planar tent with integer fibers $[0,1],[0,1+\varepsilon],[0,1]$ at $-1,0,1$ and its normalized mixed-length probability. At median base $0$, pair the strict outer fibers independently and pair the proportional central parts diagonally. The resulting median target density is
$$
 f(x)=\frac{\mathbf1_{[0,1+\varepsilon]}(x)+2g(x)}{3+\varepsilon},
$$
where $g(x)=4x$ for $0\le x\le1/2$, $g(x)=4(1-x)$ for $1/2\le x\le1$, and $g(x)=0$ otherwise. The target has no log-concave density representative.

## Proof

Use the body $\{(z,x):-1\le z\le1,\ 0\le x\le 1+\varepsilon(1-|z|)\}$, whose defining upper function is concave. The exact integer fibers have lengths $1,1+\varepsilon,1$, so $M=3+\varepsilon$ and $0$ is a median. Each raw half-marginal in 2f355ace664c0068 has mass $M/2$, with one strict outer interval of length one and a central submeasure of density $1/2$. The strict-cross block of mass one has independent uniform continuous coordinates, with ordinary midpoint density $g$ by convolution. The central-central block has density $1/2$ on the central interval after the diagonal map. The raw target is their sum, and dividing by $M/2$ yields $f$.

The positive density is continuous in the interior of its support, including at $x=1$. The left derivative of $\log f$ there is $-8$ and its right derivative is $0$, contrary to the nonincreasing slopes of a concave function. Any log-concave representative agreeing almost everywhere with $f$ would be positive and continuous on this same open interval, hence would agree with $f$ throughout the interval. The same violation would follow.
