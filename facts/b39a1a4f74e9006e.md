## Statement

For $C=[-1,1]\times[-1,1]^2$, $0<b<1$ and $|a|<1-b$, the cap $H_{b,a}=\{x_2+az\ge b\}$ minimizes mixed mass through $(0,0,b)$ over all ambient closed caps. Its three derivative sections are proper and centered in aggregate at $(0,0,b)$, with both closed integer-side section fractions $2/3$. At $b=1/2$, $a=-9/20$, the actual cap fraction on $z\ge0$ is $11/30<4/9$. Along $b\in[2/5,1/2]$, $a(b)=4(b-9/20)$ is a regular minimizing sweep with $V'=-6$, $V_+'=4$ and $V_-'=-12$.

## Proof

The sections are intervals of length two at heights $b+a,b,b-a$ on integer bases $-1,0,1$. Hence the full section centroid and side fractions are as stated. The cap areas in those fibers are $2(1-b-a),2(1-b),2(1-b+a)$. Their sum is $6(1-b)$. The mixed subset $\{-1,0,1\}\times[-1,1]\times[2b-1,1]$ of $C$ is symmetric around $(0,0,b)$ and has mixed mass $12(1-b)$. Reflection forces every closed containing halfspace to retain at least half that mass, including vertical caps with boundary mass. Thus the displayed cap is globally minimizing.

At the specified $(b,a)$ the areas are $19/10,1,1/10$, so the upper closed side gives $(1+1/10)/3=11/30$. On the specified sweep, $|a(b)|\le1/5<1-b$. Differentiating the same three affine expressions along it gives $V'=-6$, $V_+'=4$, $V_-'=-12$.
