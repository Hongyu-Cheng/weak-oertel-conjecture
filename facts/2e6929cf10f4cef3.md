## Statement

There is a compact convex mixed body with four equal positive planar fibers, total mixed area $8$, and true maximal raw depth $3$ attained at two adjacent integer bases. It has a critical cap pair at the second base and a critical triple at the first. On their projectively normalized pair residual, moving toward the first contact is exactly a transverse extrusion. For the original three cap traces let $F=\sum_{i=1}^3 A_i-(3/4)\min_i A_i$, where $A_i$ are their actual residual areas, and let $V$ be residual area. For one choice of original triple at parameter $t=1/4$, the derivatives toward the contact are $F'=21/16$, $V'=2$, and $(F-V)'=-11/16$. For another genuine triple at parameter $t=1/40$, they are $F'=-1979/160$, $V'=2$, and $(F-V)'=-2299/160$. The first choice uses the smallest slopes for the three displayed normal directions.

## Proof

Let $C=[-1,2]\times K$ with $K=\{|x|+|y|\le1\}$, so its integer fibers are $K$ at $z=-1,0,1,2$ and each has area $2$. At $(0,0,0)$ any nonvertical through-cap has own area $1$ and total area $2$ from the two adjacent symmetric fibers, whose thresholds are opposite. At $(1,0,0)$ use the same argument with bases $0,2$. Pure-base closed caps through either point have area at least $4$, and reducing a containing cap to a through-cap can only decrease mass. This gives raw depth at least $3$ at the two marks.

At base $1$ take the pair $\pm x+(z-1)/2\ge0$. Each has area $3$, using the paired fibers at $0,2$, its half of the own fiber, and zero area at $-1$. At base $0$ take the triple $x-Lz\ge0$, $y-z/2\ge0$, $-x-y-z/2\ge0$ with $L\ge1/2$. Its caps each have area $3$: the thresholds over $-1,1$ are opposite, each own cap is central, and at $2$ every threshold is at or beyond the corresponding support value $1$. The three slopes have smallest possible values $1/2$ for these normals and base-zero through-caps of this sign pattern: a smaller slope contributes positive area at base $2$ while the paired contributions are unchanged. The triple covers every plane $z\le0$ because its affine margins sum to $-(L+1)z$. The pair covers every plane $z\ge1$. Thus the five mass-$3$ caps give the global upper bound $3$ and are all critical at their stated marks.

At the real section $z=-t/(1-t)$, rescale the continuous coordinates by $1-t$. The pair residual up to null boundaries is $D_t=(1-t)K\cap\{|x|\le1/2\}$. For $0\le t<1/2$, on $I=[-1/2,1/2]$ its sections are $[-r+|x|,r-|x|]$ with $r=1-t$. Hence $V(t)=3/2-2t$, and $D_t+\varepsilon(\{0\}\times[-1,1])=D_{t-\varepsilon}$ for $0\le\varepsilon\le t$. The three fixed original caps give the moving normalized cuts $x\ge-Lt$, $y\ge-t/2$, and $x+y\le t/2$.

For $0<t<\min(1/3,1/(2L))$, integrate $2(r-|x|)$ on $[-Lt,1/2]$ for $A_1$, and $r-|x|+t/2$ on $I$ for $A_2$. For $A_3$, the captured lengths on the consecutive intervals with endpoints $-1/2,-1/2+3t/4,0,1/2-t/4,1/2$ are $2(r+x)$, $r+t/2$, $r+t/2-2x$, and zero. This yields
$$
(A_1,A_2,A_3)=
\left(\frac34+(2L-1)t-(L^2+2L)t^2,\ \frac34-\frac t2,\ \frac34-\frac t2-\frac{t^2}{2}\right).
$$
Here $A_2-A_3=t^2/2>0$. For $L=1/2$ the difference $A_1-A_3=t/2-3t^2/4$ is positive on the stated interval, and for $L=10$ the difference $(39t-239t^2)/2$ is positive on $0<t<1/20$. Thus $F=A_1+A_2+A_3/4$. For $L=1/2$, $F=27/16-5t/8-11t^2/8$ and $F-V=3/16+(11/8)t(1-t)$. For $L=10$, $F=27/16+(147/8)t-(961/8)t^2$. The derivative in $\varepsilon$ of each function evaluated at $t-\varepsilon$ is the negative $t$-derivative. This gives the displayed values.

The triple core has vertices $(-Lt,-t/2),(t,-t/2),(-Lt,(L+1/2)t)$. Checking their strip coordinates and $\ell_1$ norms puts the triangle inside $D_t$ for $L=1/2$, $0<t<1/3$, and for $L=10$, $0<t<2/43$. Its area is $(L+1)^2t^2/2$, with respective derivatives $-9/16$ and $-121/40$ at the two marks of the calculation.
