## Statement

Under the hypotheses of the one-base continuous-count minorant, write $M$ for the original mixed volume. Let a finite menu of actual closed ambient halfspaces each have original mixed mass $Q$, and suppose each trace in the pointed chart on every sampled fiber is $\{u_j\cdot\xi+\delta\ge0\}$ there, where $\delta>0$. Assume there are weights $\rho_j>0$ summing to one and a vector $a$ with $\sum_j\rho_j u_j=\delta a$ and $1+a\cdot\xi>0$ on $D$. Then $Q\ge\frac12(d/(d+1))^d M$.

## Proof

The one-base continuous-count minorant supplies $f$ and $g$ on $D$, with $\int f=M$, $\int g\ge M/2$ and $g\le f$. The bound $m\ge3$ and $L\ge m-1$ make $g$ positive on $D$. The density $g$ is log-concave because it is nonnegative and concave on its convex support; extending by zero preserves log-concavity. Its extended inverse power $g^{-1/(d+1)}$ is convex, by composition of $-\log g$ with an increasing convex exponential. Boundedness, compact support, upper semicontinuity and positivity in $\operatorname{int}D$ verify all hypotheses of accepted fact d6a3cbae1da72277. Obtain $c\in\operatorname{int}D$ of raw $g$-depth at least $(d/(d+1))^d\int g$.

At every $\xi\in D$ the weighted sum of the pointed cap margins is $\delta(1+a\cdot\xi)>0$, so some selected halfspace contains $c$. It is proper as a halfspace whenever $u_j\ne0$; if $u_j=0$ its trace is all of $D$ and the same lower bound is immediate. Its original mass equals the integral of $f$ over its pointed halfspace because the trace agreement holds on all sampled fibers. Thus $Q\ge(d/(d+1))^d\int g\ge (d/(d+1))^d M/2$.
