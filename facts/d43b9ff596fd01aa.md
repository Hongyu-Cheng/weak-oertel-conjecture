## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and use the exact canonical hypotheses and notation of accepted facts `6b09aae189415c70` and `7ec63df101c30343`. Thus
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
 Z:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},\qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in Z}a_z,
\]
\[
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),\qquad
 \mathcal A(C):=R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
where \(Q>0\) is the half-strength canonical product-reservoir mass and \(R\) is its realizable-submatching capacity. Assume the canonical hard inequality
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(I,O\) be the canonical within-label and labelled-output defects, and assume
\[
 I\ge O.
\tag{2}
\]
Then \(I>Q/4\), so the actual \(I\)-triangle conclusions of accepted fact `7ec63df101c30343` apply. Let \(\mathcal E_I\) be its \(I\)-bad opposite-edge family, let \(s_e\) be the retained product-matching weights, let \(t_e\) be the actual opposite-edge triangle masses, and put
\[
 T_c:=\sum_\xi
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi,\ m_e=c\\e\in\mathcal E_I}}t_e,
 \qquad
 E:=\{c\in\mathbb Z^n:T_c>0\},
 \qquad
 \rho_E:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E).
\tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\rho_E\ge3.}
\tag{4}
\]
Equivalently, no configuration satisfying (1)--(3) can have \(\rho_E\le2\). This exclusion is unconditional within the canonical hard \(I\)-dominant branch: it makes no hypothesis on the heaviest-label continuous residual rank \(r_E\), on a residual-cell cover, or on no-rank-drop.

## Proof

All six summands of \(\mathcal A(C)\) are nonnegative. Accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` gives the hard alternative and the exact defect inequality
\[
 \max\{I,O\}>\frac Q4,
\qquad
 I+O\ge
 \frac{11}{16}Q-\frac{67}{4}R-\frac{51}{128}G.
\tag{5}
\]
Equation (2) and the first inequality in (5) give \(I>Q/4\). They also give
\[
 I\ge\frac{I+O}{2}
 \ge\frac{11}{32}Q-\frac{67}{8}R-\frac{51}{256}G.
\tag{6}
\]
Accepted fact `7ec63df101c30343` gives positive total actual \(I\)-triangle mass, so \(E\ne\varnothing\) and \(\rho_E\) is well-defined.

Suppose for contradiction that \(\rho_E\le2\), and put
\[
 P:=\operatorname{aff}(E),
 \qquad p:=\dim P=\rho_E\le2.
\tag{7}
\]
Accepted fact `7ec63df101c30343` supplies an exceptional edge family \(\mathcal B\) such that
\[
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal B}s_e\le G,
 \qquad
 t_e\ge\frac18s_e\quad(e\notin\mathcal B).
\tag{8}
\]
Define
\[
 \mathcal J:=\mathcal E_I\setminus\mathcal B,
 \qquad
 S:=\sum_{e\in\mathcal J}s_e.
\tag{9}
\]
Because \(I=\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_I}s_e\), equations (8)--(9) imply
\[
 S\ge I-G
 \ge\frac{11}{32}Q-\frac{67}{8}R-\frac{307}{256}G.
\tag{10}
\]

We next prove the source-side upper bound
\[
 \boxed{S\le32G.}
\tag{11}
\]
Fix one retained parity class \(\xi\), write
\[
 L_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\beta_z>0,
 \qquad
 s_{\{u,v\}}=\frac{\beta_u\beta_v}{L_\xi},
\tag{12}
\]
and let \(\mathcal J_\xi\) be the positive-weight edges of \(\mathcal J\) in this class. Put
\[
 S_\xi:=\sum_{e\in\mathcal J_\xi}s_e.
\tag{13}
\]
For a loaded endpoint \(u\equiv\xi\), define
\[
 N_u:=\{v:\{u,v\}\in\mathcal J_\xi\},
 \qquad
 B_u:=\sum_{v\in N_u}\beta_v.
\tag{14}
\]
If \(N_u=\varnothing\), then \(B_u=0\) and the \(\mathcal J_\xi\)-degree of \(u\) is zero. Assume henceforth that \(N_u\ne\varnothing\).

For every \(v\in N_u\), equations (8)--(9) and positivity of \(s_{\{u,v\}}\) give
\[
 t_{\{u,v\}}\ge\frac18s_{\{u,v\}}>0.
\]
Thus \(m_{\{u,v\}}=(u+v)/2\in E\subseteq P\), and consequently
\[
 v\in P_u:=2P-u.
\tag{15}
\]
The affine subspace \(P_u\) has dimension \(p\le2\); this includes ranks zero and one and the possible full-ambient case when \(n=p\). Put
\[
 Z_u:=Z\cap P_u,\qquad
 W_u:=\sum_{z\in Z_u}a_z,
 \qquad
 q_u:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(Z_u).
\tag{16}
\]
The set \(Z_u\) is nonempty because it contains every \(v\in N_u\), and \(q_u\le p\le2\). The support-free affine-slice bound in accepted fact `8aac56c49321466c` gives
\[
 G\ge\frac1{64}\min\{W,2^{n-q_u}W_u\}.
\tag{17}
\]
Accepted fact `7ec63df101c30343` gives \(Q\le W/8\). Hence (1) gives the strict estimate
\[
 64G<\frac{64Q}{100}\le\frac{2W}{25}<W.
\tag{18}
\]
Equations (17)--(18) show that the minimum in (17) cannot be \(W\), including when \(P_u\) is full ambient or when the two entries of the minimum would otherwise coincide. Therefore
\[
 2^{n-q_u}W_u\le64G,
 \qquad
 W_u\le64\,2^{q_u-n}G\le2^{8-n}G.
\tag{19}
\]

The load estimate in accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` is
\[
 0\le\beta_z\le\frac{a_z}{4}
 \qquad(z\in Z).
\tag{20}
\]
Since \(N_u\subseteq Z_u\), equations (14), (19), and (20) give
\[
 B_u\le\frac14W_u\le2^{6-n}G.
\tag{21}
\]
Using the exact product formula (12), the \(\mathcal J_\xi\)-degree of every loaded endpoint, including the empty-neighbor case, therefore satisfies
\[
 d_u(\mathcal J_\xi)
 :=\sum_{v\in N_u}s_{\{u,v\}}
 =\frac{\beta_u}{L_\xi}B_u
 \le\frac{\beta_u}{L_\xi}2^{6-n}G.
\tag{22}
\]
Every edge has two distinct endpoints, so summing (22) over the class gives
\[
 2S_\xi
 =\sum_{u\equiv\xi}d_u(\mathcal J_\xi)
 \le2^{6-n}G
 \sum_{u\equiv\xi}\frac{\beta_u}{L_\xi}
 =2^{6-n}G.
\tag{23}
\]
Thus \(S_\xi\le2^{5-n}G\). There are at most \(2^n\) retained parity classes. Empty classes and classes with \(\mathcal J_\xi=\varnothing\) contribute zero, so summing (23) proves (11).

Combining (10) and (11) yields
\[
 \frac{11}{32}Q
 \le\frac{67}{8}R+\frac{8499}{256}G.
\tag{24}
\]
Since \(67/8=2144/256<8499/256\), equation (24) gives
\[
 \mathcal A(C)\ge R+G
 \ge\frac{88}{8499}Q
 >\frac Q{100},
\tag{25}
\]
where the last strict comparison is the exact integer inequality
\[
 88\cdot100=8800>8499.
\]
Equation (25) contradicts (1). Therefore \(\rho_E>2\). Since \(\rho_E\) is an integer, \(\rho_E\ge3\), proving (4). No continuous residual-rank or residual-cover assertion was used.
