## Statement

In a nondegenerate planar triangle with barycentric coordinates $\xi$, take
$$
\begin{gathered}
\alpha=(52/125,203/1000,381/1000),\quad
(a,b,c,d)=(217/325,16/25,3823/5650,29/50),\\
(A,B)=(44051/93175,795184/835175),\qquad
(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)=(169/217,12769/15292,3/4).
\end{gathered}
$$
The three closed halfplanes $H_1=\{\xi_2/A+\xi_3/a\le1\}$, $H_2=\{\xi_1/B+\xi_3/c\le1\}$ and $H_3=\{\xi_1/b+\xi_2/d\le1\}$ concur at $\alpha$, isolate distinct vertices and cover the plane. Write $w=\sum_j\lambda_j\mathbf1_{H_j}$. The zeroth and first normalized edge moments of $w-1$ vanish on both edges incident to vertex $3$. On edge $12$, with $t=\xi_2$, they are $\sigma$ and $\sigma m$ with
$$
\sigma=20323200/124507889>0,\qquad m=4993093096/227226897425.
$$
Thus the affine shape stress equations
$$
\sum_e\int_e(w-1)u_e\,d\mathcal H_1=2\eta\,u_{12}(m)
$$
hold for the normal velocities $u_e$ of every affine variation, with one positive contact $\eta=\sigma\,\mathcal H_1(e_{12})/2$. The average of $w$ on the contact interval $[0,m]$ is $\lambda_1<1$, although its entire active-edge average is $1+\sigma$. Also $\sum_j\lambda_j>9/4$ and $\lambda_1Aa+\lambda_2Bc+\lambda_3bd=1+\sigma\alpha_3$.

## Proof

Each of the six intercepts is strictly between zero and one, so the halfplanes isolate the indicated vertices. Direct substitution places $\alpha$ on each of their boundary lines. For the covering property, let $D_{ij}$ be the margin of halfplane $i$ at vertex $j$, multiplied by $\alpha_j$. Its rows sum to zero, its diagonal is positive and off-diagonal entries are negative. Every two-by-two principal minor is strictly positive, since each diagonal exceeds in magnitude either of its two off-diagonal entries in that row. Thus $D$ has rank two and its right kernel is the span of the all-ones vector. Every column of its adjugate belongs to this kernel and is constant with positive value given by the corresponding principal minor. Every row of the adjugate is therefore a positive left nullvector. The positive dependence also holds for the unscaled vertex margins since all $\alpha_j$ are positive. As affine functions are determined by their vertex values, this positive combination of margins is identically zero, proving the covering assertion.

Parameterize $13$ and $23$ toward vertex $3$ on $[0,1]$. Their profiles are $\lambda_1\mathbf1_{[0,a]}+\lambda_3\mathbf1_{[1-b,1]}$ and $\lambda_2\mathbf1_{[0,c]}+\lambda_3\mathbf1_{[1-d,1]}$. Substitution gives $\lambda_1a+\lambda_3b=\lambda_2c+\lambda_3d=1$ and $\lambda_1a^2+\lambda_3(2b-b^2)=\lambda_2c^2+\lambda_3(2d-d^2)=1$. On edge $12$ the profile is $\lambda_1\mathbf1_{[0,A]}+\lambda_2\mathbf1_{[1-B,1]}$. Thus its excess moments are
$$
\lambda_1 A+\lambda_2 B-1=\sigma,\qquad
[\lambda_1 A^2+\lambda_2(2B-B^2)-1]/2=\sigma m.
$$
An affine normal velocity restricts to an affine function on each edge. The moment equations give zero on the inactive edges and $\sigma\,\mathcal H_1(e_{12})u_{12}(m)$ on the active edge. Conversely the two incident outward normals at every vertex are independent, so one can choose the three vertex velocities to prescribe any pair of incident normal velocities at each vertex. These velocities extend to an affine variation, establishing all six equations. The exact ordering $0<m<1-B<A<1$ gives the deficient interval. Finally the three cap area fractions are $Aa,Bc,bd$ from triangular side ratios, and direct substitution gives the stated weighted area identity and the strict sum of the weights.
