## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $m=d+1$, and let $\phi_d$ be the uniform-simplex row capacity $\inf_{t>0:F_x(t)>0}t/F_x(t)^{1/d}$ for $F_x(t)=\Pr(x\cdot U\le t)$ and uniform $U$ on $\Delta_d=\{u\in\mathbb R_+^m:\sum_j u_j=1\}$. Let $X$ be nonnegative and column-stochastic with $m$ columns, and let $V$ be its active-row span. Select any collection $S$ of strictly positive rows $X_i$ with proper minimizing thresholds $t_i$, and for each set $c_i=\mathbf 1-X_i/t_i$. Put $P(c)=\Pr(\sum_j c_jE_j\ge0)$ for independent unit exponentials, $g_i=\nabla P(c_i)$ and $H_i=\nabla^2P(c_i)$. Define
$$
T_i(v)=v+\frac{\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}H_i v}{dP(c_i)}\mathbf 1
\qquad (g_i\cdot v=0).
$$
Each $T_i$ is injective on $g_i^\perp$ and satisfies
$$
T_i(v)^{\mathsf T}\nabla^2\phi_d(X_i)T_i(v)
=-\frac{P(c_i)^{-1/d}}{t_i dP(c_i)}v^{\mathsf T}H_i v .
$$
For each tangent tuple $(v_i)_{i\in S}$ one has $\sum_i T_i(v_i)\in V$ if and only if $\sum_i v_i\in V$. In particular, if $X$ is a least-active maximizer of a positively weighted sum of $\phi_d$ and $N_i\subseteq g_i^\perp$ are negative-definite subspaces for $H_i$, then $k+\sum_{i\in S}\dim N_i\le m$, where $k$ is the number of active rows.

## Proof

The accepted optimized threshold Schur law applies to each normalized positive row $X_i/t_i$ with proper threshold one. It gives $g_i\cdot\mathbf 1=dP(c_i)>0$, local $C^2$ regularity and a strictly positive threshold denominator. The shift in $T_i(v)$ is precisely the value canceling the square in the accepted completed-square formula. This yields the asserted quadratic identity at $X_i/t_i$ without the factor $1/t_i$. By degree-one homogeneity, for $t_i>0$ one has $\nabla^2\phi_d(X_i)=t_i^{-1}\nabla^2\phi_d(X_i/t_i)$, giving the displayed identity. If $T_i(v)=0$, then $v$ is a multiple of $\mathbf 1$, and $g_i\cdot\mathbf 1>0$ and $g_i\cdot v=0$ imply $v=0$.

The rows sum to $\mathbf 1$, so $\mathbf 1\in V$. Each shift $T_i(v_i)-v_i$ is a multiple of $\mathbf 1$, proving the equivalence for sums. For the index consequence, each $T_i(N_i)$ has dimension $\dim N_i$ and the quadratic identity gives positive definiteness there. The joint positive-index bound applies with these subspaces and positive objective weights.
