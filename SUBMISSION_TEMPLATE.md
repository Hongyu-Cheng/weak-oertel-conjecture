# Mathematical submission

Copy this template into the submission or PR description. Use one section per independently citable result.

## Statement and scope

State the hypotheses, quantifiers, notation, and conclusion. Identify the part of the [locked problem](problem_statement.md) or auxiliary claim addressed. For a counterexample, name the exact assertion and scope it refutes.

## Source and prior results

Give source note IDs with section or equation locators, or stable external citations. State how the claim relates to each close fact by matching hypotheses and conclusions. If an accepted fact covers the claim, record the specialization or implication in the [crosswalk](assets/pk-nov6/CROSSWALK.md) and cite that fact.

## Proof and dependencies

Give the complete proof. List the accepted fact IDs actually invoked in `depends_on` and check their hypotheses at the point of use. Name external mathematical results separately. If the argument relies on a pending result, identify the exact missing statement and where it enters the proof.

## Reproduction and review

Give the commands and expected outputs for any supporting checks. A separate reviewer checks the exact statement, proof, dependency uses, and overlap reasoning. Record the verdict, remaining gaps, and review method. Bind the review to the version reviewed and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to add the fact, ledger entry, and index link after approval.
