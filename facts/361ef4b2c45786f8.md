## Statement

Let $A,B,C\subset\mathbb R^d$ be full-dimensional compact convex bodies at bases $-1,0,1$, with $(A+C)/2\subseteq B$ and $0<a=|A|\le b=|C|$, $d\ge1$. Use their actual unnormalized volume measure. For $y\in B$ and unit $u$, put $H_{u,s}=\{x:u\cdot x\ge s\}$ and $\delta_u(y)=\sup_s(|(2y-C)\cap H_{u,s}|-|A\cap H_{u,s}|)$. The raw closed ambient depth at $(0,y)$ is
$$
\min\left\{|B|+a,\ |B|+b,\
\inf_{\lVert u\rVert=1}\left[|B\cap H_{u,u\cdot y}|+b-\delta_u(y)\right]\right\}.
$$
In particular it is at least $f_B(y)+b-\Delta(A,2y-C)$, where $f_B$ is ordinary raw closed-halfspace depth in $B$ and $\Delta$ is absolute halfspace discrepancy over closed ordinary halfspaces.

## Proof

The compatibility inclusion ensures the lifted convex hull has exactly these fibers at its three integer bases: at base zero, a combination of the two endpoints gives a point of their Minkowski midpoint and any remaining coefficient belongs to $B$. It suffices to consider closed caps through $(0,y)$. For each fixed nonzero continuous normal, normalize it to unit $u$. The through-point cap $u\cdot x+t z\ge u\cdot y$ has fixed middle trace $H_{u,u\cdot y}$. As $t$ ranges over $\mathbb R$, the endpoint threshold $s=u\cdot y+t$ ranges over $\mathbb R$. The endpoint reflection calculation of pk-w35-reflected-discrepancy gives the endpoint sum $b+|A\cap H_{u,s}|-|(2y-C)\cap H_{u,s}|$, so the infimum over $t$ gives precisely $b-\delta_u(y)$. Infimizing over $u$ and adding the two pure base candidates proves the equality. Those latter caps count all of $B$, which lies in their boundary, and exactly one endpoint. For the lower bound, for each $u$ the middle mass is at least $f_B(y)$ and $\delta_u(y)\le\Delta$. The pure base candidates also satisfy the bound because $f_B(y)\le |B|$ and $b-\Delta\le a$.
