## Statement

Let $\sigma,\nu$ be finite positive measures of equal mass on a common compact subset of the closed affine halfspace $\{L\ge0\}$. If $\int f\,d\nu\le\int f\,d\sigma$ for every continuous convex ambient function $f$, then $\nu\{L=0\}\le\sigma\{L=0\}$. The conclusion also holds for a common compact subset of $\{L\le0\}$.

## Proof

For $\varepsilon>0$, the continuous convex function $(\varepsilon-L)_+/\varepsilon$ is one on $\{L=0\}$ and lies between zero and the indicator of $\{0\le L<\varepsilon\}$ on the common support. Comparing its two integrals gives $\nu\{L=0\}\le\sigma\{0\le L<\varepsilon\}$. Continuity from above along any decreasing positive sequence $\varepsilon\to0$ proves the claim. Replacing $L$ by $-L$ gives the other orientation.
