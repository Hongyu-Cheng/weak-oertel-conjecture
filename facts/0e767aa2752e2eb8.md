## Statement

Let $d\ge1$ be an integer, $B\subset\mathbb R^d$ a compact convex body of positive $d$-volume, $0\le\eta\le\mathbf 1_B\,dx$, and $\alpha=\eta(B)/|B|$. Let $g$ be the centroid of $\mathbf 1_B\,dx+k\eta$. For a closed halfspace $H$ containing $g$, write $v=(|B\cap H|+\eta(H))/|B|$, $q=1+1/d$, and $\gamma_d=q^{-d}$. Let $k\ge2$ be real. Write $\Xi_{d,k}=\inf v$ and $\Xi^{\rm norm}_{d,k}=\inf v/(1+\alpha)$ over all $B,\eta,H$, including all masses $\alpha$. Let $u_*\in(0,1)$ solve $u_*^d+du_*=d$ and set $E_*=u_*^d$, $A_d=(qE_*-E_*^q)/(1+E_*)^q$, $C_d=A_d^{-1/q}$. Let $R,R_+$ be the unique positive roots of
$$
qR^{1/d}+kA_dR^q=1,\qquad
q[R_+/(1+E_*)]^{1/d}+kA_dR_+^q=1.
$$
Then
$$
R\le\Xi_{d,k}\le R_+,\qquad
\Xi^{\rm norm}_{d,k}\ge
\frac{\Xi_{d,k}}{1-1/k+\Xi_{d,k}/\gamma_d}
\ge \frac{R}{1-1/k+R/\gamma_d}.
$$
For each fixed $d$ both infima are asymptotic to $C_d k^{-d/(d+1)}$ as $k\to\infty$, with $C_1=\sqrt3$.

## Proof

Use the all-mass formula over $u\in[0,1]$ and its one-band simplex realizations. For one of those costs $v=(1+u^d)t_k(u)^d$ put $E=u^d$. The root equation becomes
$$
1=q[v/(1+E)]^{1/d}+k A(E)v^q,\qquad A(E)=\frac{qE-E^q}{(1+E)^q}.
$$
On $[0,1]$, the derivative of $A(E)$ has the sign of $1-E^{q-1}-(q-1)E$, which decreases strictly from $1$ to a negative number and vanishes at $E=E_*$. Hence $A(E)\le A_d$, and $1\le qv^{1/d}+kA_dv^q$. This bounds the entire minimum below by $R$. Choosing $u=u_*$ makes the cost exactly $R_+$, bounding the minimum above. Both scalar equations have unique positive roots by strict increase, and $A_d>0$. Writing either root as $k^{-1/q}x$, the equation has form $1=c k^{-1/(dq)}x^{1/d}+A_dx^q$ for a fixed $c>0$. Thus $x\le C_d$, the first term tends to zero, and $x\to C_d$. For $d=1$ one has $E_*=1/2$ and $A_1=1/3$.

The linear budget bound gives $\alpha\le v/\gamma_d-1/k$ for every tested cap, so $v/(1+\alpha)\ge v/(1-1/k+v/\gamma_d)$. The latter function is increasing in $v$ for $k>1$, giving both displayed bounds on the normalized infimum. Their lower asymptotic constant is $C_d$ since $k\to\infty$ and $\Xi_{d,k}\to0$. The simplex choice $u=u_*$ attains cost $R_+$ with $\alpha=E_*[R_+/(1+E_*)]\to0$, so its normalized cost has the same leading constant. This gives the matching upper bound. In particular a positive multiplier of $\gamma_d(|B|+\eta(B))$ uniform in $k$ fails already at each fixed $d$.
