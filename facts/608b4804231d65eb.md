## Statement

Let $T_d=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:x_i\ge0,\ \sum_i x_i\le1\}$ and $r(z)=1-(1-a)\|z\|_\infty$ on $[-1,1]^n$ for integers $n,d\ge1$ and $0<a<1$. The fibers $r(z)T_d$ form a compact full-dimensional convex body with exact integer bases $\{-1,0,1\}^n$. Its globally concave even unconditional radius equals $1$ at zero and $a$ elsewhere on that integer support. Write $A(c)=\sum_{z\in\{-1,0,1\}^n}(r(z)-c)_+^d$. The lower bound $A(c)\ge (1-c)^{n+d}A(0)$ fails at $n=3,d=2,a=c=1/3$ by $148/2187$. The lower bound $A(c)\ge(1-c)^{d+1}A(0)$ fails at $n=d=2,a=c=1/3$ by $28/243$. The lower bound $A(c)\ge 2^{-n}(1-c)^d A(0)$ fails at $n=4,d=2,a=c=1/2$ by $5/64$, and for every fixed $d\ge1$ and $0<c\le1/2$ it fails at $a=c$ for all sufficiently large $n$.

## Proof

The body is given by the linear inequalities $-1\le z_j\le1$, $x_i\ge0$, and $\sum_i x_i+(1-a)\varepsilon z_j\le1$ for each $j$ and $\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}$. These inequalities are equivalent to $x\in r(z)T_d$. They bound the body, and they are all strict at $z=0$ and any strictly positive $x$ with $\sum_i x_i<a$. Since $r(z)\ge a>0$ on the cube, each prescribed fiber has positive volume and the base projection is exactly the cube. Concavity of $r$ follows from convexity of the sup norm, and invariance under sign changes and permutations follows from that norm. In particular every coordinate restriction is concave. At each nonzero integer base the sup norm equals one. Thus $A(0)=1+(3^n-1)a^d$, and at $a=c$ all noncentral terms vanish so $A(c)=(1-c)^d$.

For $(n,d,a,c)=(3,2,1/3,1/3)$ the two values in the $n+d$ comparison are $972/2187$ and $1120/2187$. For $(2,2,1/3,1/3)$ the $d+1$ comparison gives $108/243$ and $136/243$. Both thresholds belong to $[1/3,1/2]$. For $(4,2,1/2,1/2)$ the third comparison gives $16/64$ and $21/64$. In general at $a=c$ the third bound would require $1+(3^n-1)c^d\le2^n$, which fails eventually because $c^d(3/2)^n\to\infty$.
