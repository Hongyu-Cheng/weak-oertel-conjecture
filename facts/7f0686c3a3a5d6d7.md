## Statement

Let $T$ be a triangle of area $c>0$ and let $v$ be one of its vertices. For $x\in T$, let $f_T(x)$ be the infimum of $|T\cap H|$ over closed planar halfplanes $H$ containing $x$. If $0<k\le2/3$, then $\max_{x\in v+k(T-v)} f_T(x)=ck^2$. Also $\max_T f_T=4c/9$, attained at the centroid of $T$.

## Proof

The halfplane cutting off $v+k(T-v)$ gives $f_T(x)\le ck^2$ for every point of this cap. Let $g$ be the centroid of $T$. The triangle $L=v+(3k/2)(T-v)$ is contained in $T$. Its centroid $q=v+(3k/2)(g-v)$ is the midpoint of the cap's edge parallel to the opposite edge of $T$, since $g$ is two-thirds of the way from $v$ to the opposite edge along its median. The full-mass centroid bound of 6769574fc1bd9f8d applied to $L$ gives $|T\cap H|\ge |L\cap H|\ge (4/9)|L|=ck^2$ for every containing halfplane. This proves the first equality. At the centroid of $T$ the same bound gives depth at least $4c/9$. Every point has a barycentric coordinate at least $1/3$, so some vertex cap of ratio $2/3$ contains it and has area $4c/9$. This proves the second equality.
