## Statement

For the reflected seven-fiber body with $T=\operatorname{conv}\{(2,-1),(-1,2),(-1,-1)\}$, $p_A=e_1$, $p_B=e_2$, $p_C=-e_1-e_2$, and $D_j=(1-\sigma)v_j+\sigma T$ at $\pm p_j$ for $\sigma=1/100$, use pure uniform outward smoothing with actual covariance $(1+6\sigma^4)\Sigma_T/(1+6\sigma^2)$. For all sufficiently small $h>0$, the unique deepest mark is $(0,0)$, of raw depth $q_h+2v$, where $q_h$ is the ordinary-fiber smoothed minimum on $T$ at zero and $v=|D_j|$. The canonical normals $(1,0),(0,1),(-1,-1)$ with base tilts $(0,0),(10,-10),(10,0)$ give three actual active caps with respective whole outer captures $\{+p_A,-p_A\},\{+p_A,-p_B\},\{+p_A,-p_C\}$. They form a balanced response menu with weights $1/3$ each. The supremum of marked boundary efficiency over actual balanced active menus and nonnegative continuous normal-dependent fields convex in the physical point is exactly one.

## Proof

The hexagonal base hull is given by $|z_1|,|z_2|,|z_1-z_2|\le1$, so its only integer points are zero and the six exposed outer vertices. Their fibers are prescribed and every $D_j$ lies in $T$, keeping the central fiber exactly $T$. As each guard has area $v=\sigma^2V$ and covariance $\sigma^2\Sigma_T$ about its own centroid, summing the volume-weighted covariances gives the stated pooled covariance.

For one sign triple use the accepted cyclic-coupling quota from the all-normal lower-bound paragraph of ba7d50004292d433 on the three equal copies of $T$ at $p_j$. It gives at least $V$ captured by any ordinary closed cap through zero, on the triple alone. The linear map $(z,x)\mapsto(z,Bz+\sigma x)$ with $Bp_j=(1-\sigma)v_j$ is invertible and fixes zero; $B$ exists since both triples of vectors sum to zero. It transports the triple to the positive guards and multiplies measured areas by $\sigma^2$. Reflecting the base gives the negative guards. Thus each sign triple contributes at least $v$ to every closed cap through zero, and also to its outward-smoothed ramp because the ramp is one on the ordinary cap. The total outer quota is $2v$.

Write the three centered continuous linear margins as $m_A=x_1$, $m_B=x_2$, and $m_C=-x_1-x_2$. Their values on their corresponding vertices are $2$, and on the other vertices $-1$. On a matching guard the continuous margin is at least $2-3\sigma$, and on a nonmatching guard it is at most $-1+3\sigma$. All three covariance widths in these normal scales equal $3w$, where $w=h\sqrt{(1+6\sigma^4)/(18(1+6\sigma^2))}$. Take $0<w\le1/100$. By w31-triangle-outward-own-minimum the only minimizing own directions are these three positive rays, with $q_h=V(4/9+2w/3+w^2/3)$.

For margin $m_A$ the zero base tilt captures its matching pair and misses the other four guards. For $m_B$, tilt $(10,-10)$ gives base offsets $+10$ at $+p_A,-p_B$, $-10$ at $-p_A,+p_B$, and zero at the two C bases. The latter are nonmatching and strictly missed. For $m_C$, tilt $(10,0)$ gives $+10$ at $+p_A,-p_C$, $-10$ at $-p_A,+p_C$, and zero at the two B bases, again nonmatching. The continuous margins lie in $[-1,2]$, so all these classifications are strictly saturated beyond the width strip. Each has actual mass $q_h+2v$, attaining both lower bounds. Every pure-base cap through zero includes the whole central fiber and has mass at least $V>q_h+2v$. Thus the displayed caps are actual minima.

The three continuous normals positively span the plane and have equal positive coarea because only the own strips contribute and the barycentric permutations preserve area and the corresponding normalized widths. Their sum is zero. This gives a balanced response menu with equal weights. The same spanning argument proves unique deepestness: at another central point one selected normal has positive projection on its displacement, and increasing that cap's threshold strictly decreases its mass near the centroid's proper strip, after which mass remains nonincreasing. Every exposed outer base has depth at most $v$ by an isolating pure-base cap.

Every selected cap is one on $+p_A$'s whole fiber. The convex physical field $[2z_1-z_2-1]_+$ is one there and zero on the other six measured fibers. Its positive marked boundary integral is captured in full. Since every averaged ramp is between zero and one, the optimal efficiency is exactly one.
