## Statement

Let $T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,e_2)$ and $V=|T|=1/2$. For $a\in[1/2,1]$, $r\ge0$ and $q\in\mathbb R$ set $F_{a,r}(q)=|rT\cap\{ax_1+(1-a)x_2\ge q\}|/V$, using planar area. For all $q\ge0$,
$$
F_{a,r}(q)\ge 4q(r-2q)_+,\qquad s_+=\max\{s,0\}.
$$
For $1/2\le a\le2/3$, $F_{a,r}(r/3)\ge r^2/2=(4/9+1/18)r^2$. The coefficient $1/2$ is attained at $a=2/3$.

## Proof

The envelope is immediate if $r=0$, $q=0$, or $q\ge r/2$. Suppose $0<q<r/2$. If $1/2<a<1$, the values of the functional at the vertices of $rT$ are $0$, $ar$ and $(1-a)r$. For $q\le(1-a)r$, the excluded triangle at zero has intercepts $q/a$ and $q/(1-a)$, so $F_{a,r}(q)=r^2-q^2/[a(1-a)]$. For $(1-a)r\le q<ar$, the included triangle at $re_1$ has edge fractions $(ar-q)/(ar)$ and $(ar-q)/[(2a-1)r]$, so $F_{a,r}(q)=(ar-q)^2/[a(2a-1)]$. The two expressions agree at their common threshold.

On the latter branch, subtraction of $4q(r-2q)$ leaves $[ar-(4a-1)q]^2/[a(2a-1)]$. On the former branch the difference is $r^2-4qr+q^2[8-1/(a(1-a))]$, which increases in $r$ on $r\ge q/(1-a)\ge2q$. At $r=q/(1-a)$ it equals the nonnegative difference from the latter branch. For $a=1/2$ the cap area divided by $V$ is $r^2-4q^2$ in this threshold range, and for $a=1$ it is $(r-q)^2$. Their respective differences are $(r-2q)^2$ and $(r-3q)^2$.

For the centroid bonus it suffices to take $r>0$. If $a\le2/3$, then $r/3\le(1-a)r$, and the same excluded triangle formula applies, including the repeated positive vertex value at $a=1/2$. It gives $F_{a,r}(r/3)=r^2[1-1/(9a(1-a))]\ge r^2/2$, since $a(1-a)\ge2/9$ on $[1/2,2/3]$. Equality holds at $a=2/3$.
