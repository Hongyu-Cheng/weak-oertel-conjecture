## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and use the canonical notation of accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d:
\[
C_z:=\{x:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
W:=\sum_z a_z>0,
\]
\[
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,\qquad
\mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),\qquad
c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,\qquad
D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z
\]
on \(Z:=\{z:a_z>0\}\). Let \(q=(q_e)\) be a nonzero off-diagonal fractional same-parity matching, with positive edge support \(F\), mass \(Q:=\sum_eq_e\), and degrees
\[
d_z(q):=\sum_{e\ni z}q_e\leq\frac{a_z}{2}.
\]
For \(e=\{z,z'\}\in F\), put
\[
m_e:=\frac{z+z'}2,\qquad
\bar c_e:=\frac{c_z+c_{z'}}2,\qquad
D_e:=\frac12(D_z+D_{z'}),\qquad
L_e:=\bar c_e+D_e.
\]
Let \(v=(v_e)_{e\in F}\) be any optimizer in the exact dual problem of 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, so
\[
\sum_ev_e=0,\qquad
\sum_ev_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,\qquad
h_{D_e}(v_e)\leq q_e,
\]
and
\[
\Delta:=\Delta_C(q)=-\sum_eh_{L_e}(v_e).
\]
Assume \(\Delta>0\), and define
\[
\omega_e:=h_{D_e}(v_e),\qquad
\Omega:=\sum_e\omega_e,
\]
\[
U_z:=\frac12\sum_{e\ni z}v_e,\qquad
\tau_z:=h_{D_z}(U_z),\qquad
T:=\sum_z\tau_z.
\]
Then \(T>0\), and, with
\[
E_T:=\{z:\tau_z>0\}=\{z:U_z\ne0\},
\]
and
\[
A_T:=\frac1T
\inf_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_T)\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
\sum_{z\in E_T}\tau_z\|c_z-s(z)\|_{D_z},
\]
the following exact endpoint identities and sharpened affine-spread inequality hold:
\[
\boxed{\Delta=-\sum_z\langle U_z,c_z\rangle-\Omega,}
\tag{1}
\]
\[
\boxed{\sum_zh_{\mathcal K_z}(U_z)
=-\Delta-(\Omega-T),}
\tag{2}
\]
\[
\boxed{TA_T\geq\Delta+\Omega,\qquad
T(A_T-1)\geq\Delta+(\Omega-T).}
\tag{3}
\]
In particular,
\[
\boxed{\min\{\Omega-T,\ T(A_T-1)\}=\Omega-T.}
\tag{4}
\]

The local triangle-slack profile is
\[
\kappa_z:=
\frac12\sum_{e\ni z}h_{D_z}(v_e)-h_{D_z}(U_z).
\tag{5}
\]
It satisfies
\[
\boxed{\kappa_z\geq0,\qquad
\sum_z\kappa_z=\Omega-T,\qquad
0\leq2\kappa_z\leq a_z.}
\tag{6}
\]
More precisely, for every \(z\), \(\kappa_z=0\) if and only if the incident functionals have a common support maximizer in \(D_z\):
\[
\bigcap_{e\ni z}
\{x\in D_z:\langle v_e,x\rangle=h_{D_z}(v_e)\}
\ne\varnothing,
\tag{7}
\]
where the intersection over an empty incident family is understood as \(D_z\). Thus \(\Omega-T\) is exactly the aggregate endpoint triangle-inequality slack, stated in terms of intersecting exposed faces rather than an unspecified normal cone.

Put \(L:=\Omega-T\). If \(L>0\), let \(E_\kappa:=\{z:\kappa_z>0\}\). There is an integral weighted centerpoint \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}(E_\kappa)\) such that every proper closed base halfspace \(J\ni m\) satisfies
\[
\sum_{z\in E_\kappa\cap J}2\kappa_z
\geq2^{-n}\sum_{z\in E_\kappa}2\kappa_z
=2^{-n}(2L).
\tag{8}
\]
Define, without shadowing the already fixed half-metronoid residual body \(D_z\),
\[
\mathcal M_z^\kappa:=\mathsf M_{2\kappa_z}(C_z),\qquad
\mathcal K_m^\kappa:=
\bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E_\kappa)}
\sum_{z\in E_\kappa}\lambda_z\mathcal M_z^\kappa.
\tag{9}
\]
Exactly one of the following two alternatives occurs.

1. If \(\mathcal K_m^\kappa\ne\varnothing\), then, writing \(G(C):=\mathfrak C_{\rm core}(C)\),
   \[
   \boxed{G(C)\geq2L.}
   \tag{10}
   \]

2. If \(\mathcal K_m^\kappa=\varnothing\), the arbitrary-selected-mass alternative of accepted fact 07009c5047a9f7bb, with its local symbol \(D_z\) mapped to \(\mathcal M_z^\kappa\), supplies a support-minimal balanced negative family of nonzero directions \(w_1,\ldots,w_r\), where \(2\leq r\leq d+1\), positive weights, exact globally affine minorants \(\ell_i\), and full-dimensional individual cap bodies
   \[
   A_i:=C\cap\{(z,x):\langle w_i,x\rangle\geq\ell_i(z)\}.
   \]
   They satisfy
   \[
   \operatorname{vol}_d((A_i)_z)\geq2\gamma_d\kappa_z
   \qquad(z\in E_\kappa),
   \tag{11}
   \]
   and hence \(W(A_i)\geq2\gamma_dL\). For each \(i\), there is a nonempty affinely independent exact-contact set \(T_i\subseteq E_\kappa\), of cardinality at most \(\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_\kappa)+1\), such that
   \[
   \ell_i(z)=h_{\mathcal M_z^\kappa}(w_i),\qquad
   2\gamma_d\kappa_z
   \leq\operatorname{vol}_d((A_i)_z)
   \leq2\kappa_z
   \quad(z\in T_i).
   \tag{12}
   \]
   Every strictly positive same-base reweighting supplied by 07009c5047a9f7bb is also a full-dimensional convex body contained in \(C\), has every selected fiber of volume at least \(2\gamma_d\kappa_z\), and has total mass at least \(2\gamma_dL\). No legal balanced-cap-descendant status is asserted without the additional hypotheses required by that accepted fact.

Finally, the cancellation is always absorbed when its base support has affine rank at most two. If \(L=0\), the conclusion below is trivial. If \(L>0\) and
\[
\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_\kappa)\leq2,
\]
then
\[
\boxed{G(C)\geq\frac{\Omega-T}{32}=\frac L{32}.}
\tag{13}
\]

There is also a precise diffuse-sequence consequence. Let \(C_j\subseteq\mathbb R^{n_j}\times\mathbb R^{d_j}\) be any sequence with \(W_j>0\) and
\[
\frac{G(C_j)}{W_j}\longrightarrow0,
\]
and choose for each \(j\) any matching and dual optimizer satisfying the preceding hypotheses, with local slack profile \(\kappa_{j,z}\). Then, for every sequence of affine subspaces \(A_j\subseteq\mathbb R^{n_j}\) of dimensions \(q_j\leq2\),
\[
\boxed{
2^{n_j-q_j}
\frac{\sum_{z\in A_j\cap\mathbb Z^{n_j}}\kappa_{j,z}}{W_j}
\longrightarrow0.}
\tag{14}
\]
Consequently, if \(L_j/W_j\) stays bounded below by a positive constant, then eventually \(\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_{\kappa,j})\geq3\), and every weighted centerpoint core (9) for the profile \(2\kappa_{j,z}\) is empty.

## Proof

Accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d gives that every \(D_z\) and \(D_e\) is a full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex body and that the displayed dual problem has an optimizer. Since
\[
h_{L_e}(v_e)
=\langle v_e,\bar c_e\rangle+h_{D_e}(v_e)
=\langle v_e,\bar c_e\rangle+\omega_e,
\]
dual optimality gives
\[
\Delta=-\sum_e\langle v_e,\bar c_e\rangle-\Omega.
\tag{15}
\]
Double-counting the two endpoints of each edge gives
\[
\sum_z\langle U_z,c_z\rangle
=\frac12\sum_z\sum_{e\ni z}\langle v_e,c_z\rangle
=\sum_{e=\{z,z'\}}\left\langle v_e,\frac{c_z+c_{z'}}2\right\rangle
=\sum_e\langle v_e,\bar c_e\rangle.
\tag{16}
\]
Equations (15)-(16) prove (1). Since \(\mathcal K_z=c_z+D_z\),
\[
h_{\mathcal K_z}(U_z)=\langle U_z,c_z\rangle+\tau_z.
\]
Summing this identity and using (1) proves (2).

The edge moment equations imply the endpoint moment equations
\[
\sum_zU_z=\sum_ev_e=0,
\qquad
\sum_zU_zz^{\mathsf T}
=\frac12\sum_{e=\{z,z'\}}v_e(z+z')^{\mathsf T}
=\sum_ev_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0.
\tag{17}
\]
Because \(D_z\) is full-dimensional, origin-symmetric, and contains the origin in its interior, \(h_{D_z}(u)=0\) if and only if \(u=0\). Hence \(E_T=\{z:U_z\ne0\}\). Every affine map on \(\operatorname{aff}(E_T)\) extends to an affine map on \(\mathbb R^n\), and (17) gives
\[
\sum_{z\in E_T}\langle U_z,s(z)\rangle=0.
\tag{18}
\]
Using (1), (18), polar Hölder inequality, and the symmetry of \(D_z\), one obtains
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta+\Omega
&=-\sum_{z\in E_T}\langle U_z,c_z\rangle\\
&=\sum_{z\in E_T}\langle-U_z,c_z-s(z)\rangle\\
&\leq\sum_{z\in E_T}h_{D_z}(-U_z)\|c_z-s(z)\|_{D_z}\\
&=\sum_{z\in E_T}\tau_z\|c_z-s(z)\|_{D_z}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{19}
\]
If \(T=0\), the right-hand side would be zero, contradicting \(\Delta>0\); thus \(T>0\). Taking the infimum in (19) proves the first inequality in (3). Subtracting \(T\) proves the second. Since \(\Delta>0\), it follows that \(T(A_T-1)>\Omega-T\), which proves (4).

Positive homogeneity and subadditivity give
\[
h_{D_z}(U_z)
=\frac12h_{D_z}\left(\sum_{e\ni z}v_e\right)
\leq\frac12\sum_{e\ni z}h_{D_z}(v_e),
\]
so \(\kappa_z\geq0\). Moreover,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_z\kappa_z
&=\frac12\sum_z\sum_{e\ni z}h_{D_z}(v_e)-T\\
&=\sum_{e=\{z,z'\}}
\frac12\bigl(h_{D_z}(v_e)+h_{D_{z'}}(v_e)\bigr)-T\\
&=\sum_eh_{D_e}(v_e)-T
=\Omega-T.
\end{aligned}
\tag{20}
\]
For \(e=\{z,z'\}\), nonnegativity of support functions of bodies containing the origin and dual feasibility give
\[
h_{D_z}(v_e)
\leq h_{D_z}(v_e)+h_{D_{z'}}(v_e)
=2h_{D_e}(v_e)
\leq2q_e.
\tag{21}
\]
Therefore
\[
0\leq\kappa_z
\leq\frac12\sum_{e\ni z}h_{D_z}(v_e)
\leq\sum_{e\ni z}q_e
=d_z(q)
\leq\frac{a_z}{2},
\tag{22}
\]
which proves all of (6).

It remains to verify the equality case in the local triangle inequality. If one point \(x\in D_z\) maximizes every incident functional \(v_e\), then
\[
h_{D_z}\left(\sum_{e\ni z}v_e\right)
\geq\left\langle\sum_{e\ni z}v_e,x\right\rangle
=\sum_{e\ni z}h_{D_z}(v_e),
\]
and subadditivity gives the reverse inequality, so \(\kappa_z=0\). Conversely, if \(\kappa_z=0\), choose \(x\in D_z\) maximizing the sum of the incident functionals. Then
\[
0
=\sum_{e\ni z}h_{D_z}(v_e)
-h_{D_z}\left(\sum_{e\ni z}v_e\right)
=\sum_{e\ni z}
\bigl(h_{D_z}(v_e)-\langle v_e,x\rangle\bigr).
\]
Every summand is nonnegative, so all vanish. This proves (7), including the stated empty-family convention.

Assume \(L>0\). Apply accepted fact 6d6fcf377e68c79c with continuous dimension zero to the nonzero lattice measure
\[
\nu_\kappa:=\sum_{z\in E_\kappa}2\kappa_z\delta_z.
\]
It supplies \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\) satisfying (8). If \(m\notin\operatorname{conv}(E_\kappa)\), strict separation would give a closed base halfspace containing \(m\) and disjoint from \(E_\kappa\), contradicting (8); hence \(m\in\operatorname{conv}(E_\kappa)\). By (6), \(0<2\kappa_z\leq a_z\), so the arbitrary-selected-mass theorem 07009c5047a9f7bb applies with selected masses \(t_z=2\kappa_z\), total mass \(2L\), and the notation map stated after (9). Its nonempty-core branch and (8) give (10). Its empty-core branch gives the support-minimal family, individual caps, positive same-base reweightings, lower volume bounds, and exact-contact sets stated in (11)-(12). In particular, its bounds
\[
\gamma_dt_z\leq\operatorname{vol}_d((A_i)_z)\leq t_z
\]
at contact fibers become exactly (12). This proves the alternative without assigning the individual caps or general reweightings any stronger descendant status.

We prove (13). If \(L=0\), it is immediate. Suppose \(L>0\), put \(A:=\operatorname{aff}(E_\kappa)\), and let
\[
D_A:=\{z\in A\cap\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},\qquad
B:=\operatorname{aff}(D_A),\qquad
r:=\dim B,\qquad
W_A:=\sum_{z\in D_A}a_z.
\]
Then \(E_\kappa\subseteq D_A\subseteq A\), so \(r\leq\dim A\leq2\), and (6) gives
\[
W_A\geq\sum_{z\in E_\kappa}a_z
\geq\sum_{z\in E_\kappa}2\kappa_z
=2L.
\tag{23}
\]
Accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c, applied to the affine slice \(A\), gives
\[
G(C)\geq\frac1{64}\min\{W,2^{n-r}W_A\}.
\tag{24}
\]
Also \(L\leq\Omega\leq Q\), while double-counting degrees and matching feasibility give
\[
2Q=\sum_zd_z(q)\leq\frac W2.
\tag{25}
\]
Thus \(W\geq4L\). Since \(r\leq n\), equations (23)-(25) imply
\[
\min\{W,2^{n-r}W_A\}\geq2L.
\]
Substitution into (24) proves (13).

Finally, consider the sequence in (14). If \(A_j\) contains no positive-volume lattice fiber, the numerator vanishes. Otherwise set
\[
B_j:=\operatorname{aff}\{z\in A_j\cap\mathbb Z^{n_j}:a_{j,z}>0\},
\quad r_j:=\dim B_j,\quad
W_{A_j}:=\sum_{\substack{z\in A_j\cap\mathbb Z^{n_j}\\a_{j,z}>0}}a_{j,z}.
\]
Then \(r_j\leq q_j\leq2\), and 8aac56c49321466c gives
\[
\frac{G(C_j)}{W_j}
\geq\frac1{64}
\min\left\{1,2^{n_j-r_j}\frac{W_{A_j}}{W_j}\right\}.
\]
The left side tends to zero, hence
\[
2^{n_j-r_j}\frac{W_{A_j}}{W_j}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Since \(r_j\leq q_j\) and (22) gives \(\kappa_{j,z}\leq a_{j,z}/2\),
\[
0\leq
2^{n_j-q_j}
\frac{\sum_{z\in A_j\cap\mathbb Z^{n_j}}\kappa_{j,z}}{W_j}
\leq
\frac12\,2^{n_j-r_j}\frac{W_{A_j}}{W_j}
\longrightarrow0,
\]
which proves (14). If \(L_j/W_j\) is bounded below, (13) rules out affine rank at most two for all sufficiently large \(j\). For any weighted centerpoint \(m\) of the profile \(2\kappa_{j,z}\), a nonempty core would give \(G(C_j)\geq2L_j\) by the same application of 07009c5047a9f7bb, contradicting \(G(C_j)/W_j\to0\). This proves the final consequence.
