## Statement

Let $n\ge0$, $d\ge1$, and let $C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be a compact convex body whose integer fibers $K_p=\{x:(p,x)\in C\}$ have positive total $d$-volume. Include every nonempty integer fiber in the mixed set $S$ and let $\mu$ sum ordinary $d$-volume over these fibers. For $Y\in S$ define $\Delta(Y)=\inf_{H\ni Y}\mu(H)$ over all closed ambient halfspaces, and write $\mathcal Y$ for the points maximizing this depth. All points in $\mathcal Y$ are in the ordinary interiors of their own fibers. Use the physical retained mass $T_K(y)$ and its unique optimal cap $D_K(y)$, defined by maximizing $\int_K f$ over measurable $0\le f\le1$ with $\int_K(x-y)f(x)\,dx=0$.

There is an attained positive maximum $\tau$ of $T_{K_p}(y)$ over $(p,y)\in\mathcal Y$. Its attaining stratum $\mathcal Y_\tau$ has at most one mark at each base. For any positive convex combination of marks $(p_i,y_i)$ in this stratum whose target base $p=\sum_i\lambda_i p_i$ is integral, the target $(p,y)$ with $y=\sum_i\lambda_i y_i$ lies in $\mathcal Y_\tau$, and
$$
D_{K_{p_i}}(y_i)-y_i=D_{K_p}(y)-y\quad\text{for every }i,
\qquad D_{K_p}(y)=\sum_i\lambda_iD_{K_{p_i}}(y_i).
$$
The set of its bases equals the integer points of its convex hull, and at most $2^n$ translation classes of retained caps occur.

On $\mathcal Y_\tau$, let $\mathcal Z$ be the marks maximizing the ordinary projective energy
$$
E_K(y)=\inf_{a:\ 1+a\cdot(x-y)>0\ (x\in K)}\int_K[1+a\cdot(x-y)]^{-d-1}\,dx.
$$
Then $\mathcal Z$ is nonempty and finite. For each positive convex combination of its marks with integral target base, the target is in $\mathcal Z$ and
$$
K_{p_i}-y_i=K_p-y\quad\text{for every }i,
\qquad K_p=\sum_i\lambda_iK_{p_i}.
$$
There are at most $2^n$ translation classes of these centered whole fibers.
