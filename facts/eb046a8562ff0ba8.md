## Statement

Let $K$ be the unit disk and let $A$ be a proper closed arc with nonempty relative interior in its circle. The finite contact action $u\cdot x+\varepsilon\le1+\operatorname{dist}(u,A)^2$ for all unit $u$ has inward boundary speed $\mathbf1_A$. No continuous function on $K$ has this trace even up to arc-length null sets. There are globally finite nonnegative continuous convex potentials whose traces converge to $\mathbf1_A$ in $L^1$ of arc length.

## Proof

The baseline is continuous in $u$ and majorizes the unit-disk support $1$. The only old supporting direction at a circle point $n$ is $u=n$ and the baseline is tight there exactly on $A$. The radial speed formula in the contact approximation theorem therefore gives $\mathbf1_A$, and the same theorem gives the convex penalty approximation in arc-length $L^1$. If a continuous function had the indicated trace almost everywhere, continuity and the positive length of every open subarc would force it to be identically one on the interior of $A$ and identically zero on the open complement. The two limits at an endpoint contradict continuity.
