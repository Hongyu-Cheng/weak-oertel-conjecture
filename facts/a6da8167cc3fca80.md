## Statement

For $d\ge1$, let $A,C\subset\mathbb R^d$ be full-dimensional compact convex bodies of volumes $0<a=|A|\le b=|C|$. Let $p(y)$ be raw closed ambient halfspace depth at $(0,y)$ of the endpoint-only volume measure with $A,C$ at bases $-1,1$. Put $C'=2y-C$, let $h_K$ be the support function of $K$, and set $e_y(u)=\min\{1,(h_A(u)-h_{C'}(u))_+/(h_A(u)+h_A(-u))\}$ for $u\ne0$. Then $p(y)\le a(1-\sup_{u\ne0}e_y(u)^d)$. The supremum of the gap-to-width ratio is invariant under a common invertible affine change of the continuous coordinates of $A,C,y$.

## Proof

The width $w=h_A(u)+h_A(-u)$ is positive. If the support gap $h_A(u)-h_{C'}(u)$ is nonpositive, the bound for that $u$ is the pure base upper bound $a$. Otherwise let $v\in A$ attain $h_A(u)$ and put $e=e_y(u)$. The copy $v+e(A-v)$ is contained in $A$, has volume $ae^d$, and its minimum $u$-value is $h_A(u)-ew\ge h_{C'}(u)$. The closed halfspace $H=\{x:u\cdot x\ge h_{C'}(u)\}$ takes this copy and only a null supporting trace of $C'$. Its closed opposite halfspace therefore has signed discrepancy $|C'\cap H^{\mathrm{opp}}|-|A\cap H^{\mathrm{opp}}|\ge b-a+ae^d$. By pk-w35-reflected-discrepancy, $p(y)\le a(1-e^d)$. This holds also when $e=1$, when the copy is all of $A$. Taking the infimum of the upper bounds proves the formula. For a common affine map with linear part $L$ the translation cancels in the support difference and the width. Both quantities are evaluated at $L^\mathsf{T}u$ in the original coordinates, which ranges over all nonzero normals.
