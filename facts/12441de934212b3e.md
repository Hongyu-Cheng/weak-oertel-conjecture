## Statement

On the body of 2d1810145bad2afd, let the two planes be $\pm y-z/2=-\delta$, with $\delta=1/4000$. For the plus sign use $\xi=(z,x,y+\delta)=(z,x,z/2)$ on the plane and integrate in coarea $dx$. Write $m$ for the sum of these vector moments on the proper sections. For every measurable inside allocation $0\le q_I\le1$ on the length-one inside facet $I$ at $-2$, there is a measurable $0\le q_O\le1$ on the outside facet $O$ at $2$ such that $m+\int_O q_O\xi\,dx=-\int_I q_I\xi\,dx$. The reflected plane has the corresponding identity.

## Proof

The proper sections have widths $r_{-1}=51/50$, $r_0=51/50$, $r_1=203/200$ from the actual rectangle fact, so by symmetry in $x$ their combined moment is $m=(-1/200,0,-1/400)$. The coarea is $dx$ because the continuous normal is $(0,1)$. On the core $|x|\le1/2$ of the outside facet put $q_O(x)=q_I(-x)$; this reverses the full vector because the two bases are opposite and $\xi$ has last component $z/2$. On the two remaining strips, $1/2<|x|\le1/2+1/200$, put $q_O=1/4$. Their combined length is $1/100$ and their net $x$ moment zero, so their vector moment with this weight is $(1/200,0,1/400)=-m$. Adding proves the assertion for every measurable allocation. Reflection in $y$ transports the identity to the other cap.
