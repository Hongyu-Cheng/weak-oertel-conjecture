## Statement

For rational $R>2$ let $C_R=\operatorname{conv}\{(-1/(R-1),-1),(1+1/(R-1),-1),(1/2,R)\}$. These bodies have exactly the same mixed set, with interval fibers $[-1,1]$ at bases zero and one. The cap $x\ge z$ attains normalized mixed depth $1/4$ at the globally deepest mixed point $(0,0)$. Its ambient fraction equals $(R-1)^2(2R-1)/[(2R-3)(R+1)^2]$ and tends to one.

## Proof

For $R>2$ the projection endpoints lie strictly inside $(-1,0)$ and $(1,2)$, and the linear roofs at zero and one both have height one. Thus the two sampled intervals and the whole mixed depth law are fixed. Both bases are exposed, so 2acc8bc803acf1c0 gives raw maximum depth one at their interval midpoints, out of total mixed mass four. The cap $x\ge z$ captures length one at zero and length zero at one, proving optimality at $(0,0)$.

The cut meets the two roof edges at $(-1/(2R-3),-1/(2R-3))$ and $(1,1)$. Together with the apex these give cap area $(R-1)(2R-1)/[2(2R-3)]$. The whole triangle has area $(R+1)^2/[2(R-1)]$. Dividing gives the stated limit.
