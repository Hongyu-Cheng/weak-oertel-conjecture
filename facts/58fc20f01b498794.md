## Statement

Let $B$ be the standard $5$-simplex, $r=497/3000$, $\ell=11/12-(9/2)r$, and let $D$ have vertices $(0,11/12,0,0,0)$, $b_0=(r,\ell,0,0,0)$, and $b_0+(3979/6000)e_i$ for $i=2,3,4,5$. Put $q=(|D|/|B|)^{-1/5}$, $A=(2-q)D$ and $C=qD$, and use the actual fibers $A,B,C$ at bases $-1,0,1$ of their lifted convex hull. In units of $|B|$ these have masses $a,1,1$ with $0<a<10^{-5}$. Use the same units in the optimized directional survival $V_u(t)=\inf_v\mu\{u\cdot x+vz\ge t\}$, and let $h(u)$ be the mean of its scalar law. With $u=e_1$, $\Gamma_5=(5/6)^5$, original mass $M=2+a$ and capacity-law mass $D_0=1+a$,
$$
V_{e_1}(h(e_1))<\Gamma_5 M/2,\qquad
\frac{V_{e_1}(h(e_1))}{D_0}<\Gamma_5\left(1-\frac{D_0-M/2}{D_0}\right),
\qquad D_0-M/2=a/2>0.
$$

## Proof

Use accepted fact 0fcb7fa715e2af62 with the same $B,D,r,q$ and the same scaled volume $\mu(H)/|B|$ as $\mu(H)$ here. It gives the three actual positive, strictly nonheavy simplex fibers, their masses, $(A+C)/2=D\subseteq B$, and the cap
$$
H_1=\{x_1+(1-q)rz\ge1/6-(2/25)a\}
$$
of mixed mass strictly less than $\Gamma_5(1+a/2)$. Pair $(2-q)w$ with $qw$ for $w$ of law $a$ times uniform probability on $D$. The first marginal is the volume of $A$ in the common relative units because that body has mass $a$; the second is $a$ times uniform probability on $C$ and is dominated by its volume because $a<1=|C|/|B|$. The midpoint output is $a$ times uniform probability on $D$. Its centroid has first coordinate $5r/6$, since five vertices of $D$ have first coordinate $r$ and the apex has first coordinate zero.

For the point $y=(g_B+a g_D)/(1+a)$ in the directional-mean transfer lemma, one has
$$
y_1=\frac16-\frac{a}{1+a}\frac{103}{3600}>\frac16-\frac{2}{25}a,
$$
because $1/6-5r/6=103/3600<2/25$. Hence $(0,y)\in H_1$, and the lemma gives $V_{e_1}(h(e_1))\le\mu(H_1)<\Gamma_5 M/2$. Dividing by $D_0$ gives the second inequality, since $M/(2D_0)=1-(D_0-M/2)/D_0$.
