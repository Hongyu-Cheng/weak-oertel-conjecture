## Statement

Let \(h\ge6\) be an integer, put \(n=2^{h+1}-1\), and give the complete rooted binary tree on vertices \(1,\ldots,n\) the parent \(\lfloor v/2\rfloor\) at each nonroot vertex \(v\). Set \(m=n-1\), \(d=3n-2\), and \(\ell=2n-1\). Let \(k\in\mathbb Q\) satisfy \(1\le k\le n/100\), and put \(R=n/2+k\). Use the original ordered continuous coordinates \((\mathbf y,\mathbf t^+,\mathbf t^-)\in\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^m\times\mathbb R^m\). Define the body \(C\subset\mathbb R^{n+d}\), with real base coordinate \(\mathbf z\in\mathbb R^n\), by
\[
\begin{aligned}
C=\biggl\{(\mathbf z,\mathbf y,\mathbf t^+,\mathbf t^-):\;&
t_v^+,t_v^-\ge0,\quad g_v=t_v^+-t_v^-\quad(2\le v\le n),\\
&g_1=0,\qquad q_v=g_v-g_{2v}-g_{2v+1},\\
&y_v\ge\left|z_v-\tfrac12-q_v\right|\quad(1\le v\le n),\\
&\sum_{v=1}^n y_v+\sum_{v=2}^n(t_v^++t_v^-)\le R\biggr\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
A missing child's flow is zero. These parameters, coordinates, and flow conventions are exactly those of accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c. In particular, the body retains both nonnegative flow blocks and all \(n\) continuous coordinates \(y_v\) under the one common budget \(R\).

Put \(S=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\). For every \(\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n\), let \(C_{\mathbf z}=\{(\mathbf y,\mathbf t^+,\mathbf t^-):(\mathbf z,\mathbf y,\mathbf t^+,\mathbf t^-)\in C\}\), let \(w_{\mathbf z}=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_{\mathbf z})\) in precisely these original ordered continuous coordinates, and let \(W=\sum_{\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n}w_{\mathbf z}=\mathcal H_d(S)\). Empty fibers and fibers of zero \(d\)-dimensional volume have weight zero. Every integer base is included in the sum defining \(W\). For \(p\in S\), define \(h_S(p)=\inf_{H\ni p}\mathcal H_d(S\cap H)\), where \(H\) ranges over all closed halfspaces of \(\mathbb R^{n+d}\).

For a real base define \(E_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf g)=\sum_{v=2}^n|g_v|+\sum_{v=1}^n|z_v-1/2-q_v|\). Then \(C\) is a full-dimensional rational simplex, its nonempty integer support is finite, and \(0<W<\infty\). More precisely, for every \(\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n\), \(C_{\mathbf z}\ne\varnothing\) if and only if \(\min_{\mathbf g\in\mathbb R^m}E_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf g)\le R\), whereas \(w_{\mathbf z}>0\) if and only if this inequality is strict. At equality the nonempty fiber remains part of \(S\) and has weight zero.

Let \(\sigma_v=(-1)^{\operatorname{depth}(v)}\), let \(j_v=(1+\sigma_v)/2\), and set \(\mathbf j=(j_1,\ldots,j_n)\). Define the absolute constant \(\mathsf A=(6e^{29/4})^{63}\). Then \(w_{\mathbf j}>0\) and
\[
W\le\frac52\mathsf A\,2^n w_{\mathbf j}.
\tag{2}
\]

For each fixed \(\mathbf a\in\mathbb Z^n\) with \(w_{\mathbf a}>0\), use the same quantities as in accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c: for \(v\in\mathbb Z^n\), put \(\alpha(v)=\min\{w_{\mathbf a+v},w_{\mathbf a-v}\}\) and \(\mathcal U_{\mathbf a}=\{v\in\mathbb Z^n:\alpha(v)>0\}\). Define \(\Delta_{\mathbf a}(v)=\log(w_{\mathbf a}^2/(w_{\mathbf a+v}w_{\mathbf a-v}))\) only for \(v\in\mathcal U_{\mathbf a}\). For \(L\ge0\), put \(B_{\mathbf a}^{\Delta}(L)=\sum_{v\in\mathcal U_{\mathbf a},\ \Delta_{\mathbf a}(v)\le L}\alpha(v)\), \(K_{\mathbf a}(w)=\max_{L\ge0}B_{\mathbf a}^{\Delta}(L)/(1+L)\), and \(K(w)=\max_{\mathbf a\in\mathbb Z^n:w_{\mathbf a}>0}K_{\mathbf a}(w)\). The displacement sums are oriented: \(v\) and \(-v\) are separate indices when distinct, and zero is included. Thus no logarithm at a zero-weight endpoint is taken. With exactly this normalization and cutoff zero,
\[
K(w)\ge K_{\mathbf j}(w)\ge B_{\mathbf j}^{\Delta}(0)
\ge w_{\mathbf j}\ge\frac{2W}{5\mathsf A\,2^n}.
\tag{3}
\]

There exists one point \(p^\star\in C_{\mathbf j}\) such that every closed ambient halfspace \(H\) containing \((\mathbf j,p^\star)\) satisfies
\[
\mathcal H_d(S\cap H)\ge\frac{W}{30\mathsf A\,2^n},
\qquad
h_S((\mathbf j,p^\star))\ge\frac{W}{30\mathsf A\,2^n}.
\tag{4}
\]
The same point serves all such halfspaces, including vertical halfspaces and those whose boundaries contain entire fibers. In particular \(c_0=(30\mathsf A)^{-1}\in(0,1/e)\) is independent of \(h,k,n,d\) throughout this family. There is no additional finite partition or numerical hypothesis.

## Proof

We first fix the actual volume and support conventions. Accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c establishes for exactly (1) that the affine map from the nonnegative simplex with coordinates \((\mathbf u,\mathbf v,\mathbf t^+,\mathbf t^-)\) and total coordinate sum at most \(R\), given by \(y_i=u_i+v_i\) and \(z_i=1/2+u_i-v_i+q_i\), leaving the two flow blocks unchanged, is invertible. Its inverse has \(u_i=(y_i+z_i-1/2-q_i)/2\) and \(v_i=(y_i-z_i+1/2+q_i)/2\). This gives the full-dimensional rational simplex in the original ambient dimension \(n+d=4n-2\).

The original-coordinate fiber calculation in accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c gives
\[
w_{\mathbf z}=\frac{2^{-m}}{\ell!}
\int_{\mathbb R^m}(R-E_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf g))_+^\ell\,d\mathbf g,
\qquad (x)_+=\max\{x,0\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Here \(d\mathbf g\) is ordinary product Lebesgue measure on the \(m\) signed edge flows. The factor \(2^{-m}\) and exponent \(\ell=n+m\) incorporate the common nonnegative freedom in each pair \((t_v^+,t_v^-)\) and every residual slack in \(\mathbf y\); the left side is still the \(d\)-dimensional volume in \((\mathbf y,\mathbf t^+,\mathbf t^-)\). In particular (5) has not replaced the actual body by a lower-dimensional flow body.

The energy is continuous and coercive since \(E_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf g)\ge\sum_{v=2}^n|g_v|\), so its minimum is attained. A minimizing flow, realized by \(t_v^+=(g_v)_+\), \(t_v^-=(-g_v)_+\), and minimal \(y_v=|z_v-1/2-q_v|\), proves the nonempty-support assertion. Formula (5) proves the positive-support assertion: a strict budget gap gives a neighborhood with positive integrand, while a minimum equal to \(R\) makes the integrand identically zero. Moreover \(\sum_v|q_v|\le2\sum_{v=2}^n|g_v|\), so \(\sum_v|z_v-1/2|\le2E_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf g)\); only finitely many integer bases can therefore have nonempty fibers. For any Boolean base, choose \(t_v^+=t_v^-=\delta\) and \(y_v=1/2+\delta\), where \(0<d\delta<k\). Every fiber inequality is strict and the total budget is \(n/2+d\delta<R\). Hence all Boolean fibers, including \(C_{\mathbf j}\), have positive volume. Compactness and finite support now give \(0<W<\infty\), with all zero-volume boundary fibers retained in \(S\).

We next identify the partition functions without changing their measures or integer sums. Put \(\lambda=29/2\) and \(\xi_v=\sigma_vg_v\) for each nonroot edge, with \(\xi_1=0\) and every missing-child value zero. The gauge has absolute Jacobian one, preserves \(\sum_{v=2}^n|g_v|\), and satisfies \(\sigma_vq_v=\xi_v+\xi_{2v}+\xi_{2v+1}=\sum_{e\ni v}\xi_e\). As \(\sigma_v(j_v-1/2)=1/2\), the coloring energy is exactly the accepted \(\mathcal E_h(\boldsymbol\xi)=\sum_e|\xi_e|+\sum_v|1/2-\sum_{e\ni v}\xi_e|\).

For every integer \(r\ge0\), let \(N_r=2^{r+1}-1\) and use the complete tree of height \(r\). With one real variable for each of its \(N_r-1\) edges, define exactly the quantities of accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\vartheta_\lambda(t)&=\sum_{a\in\mathbb Z}e^{-\lambda|a-t|},\\
\mathsf Z_{\mathrm B}^{(r)}
&=\int_{\mathbb R^{N_r-1}}
e^{-\lambda\sum_e|\xi_e|}
\prod_v e^{-\lambda|1/2-\sum_{e\ni v}\xi_e|}\,d\boldsymbol\xi,\\
\mathsf Z_{\mathrm{all}}^{(r)}
&=\int_{\mathbb R^{N_r-1}}
e^{-\lambda\sum_e|\xi_e|}
\prod_v\vartheta_\lambda\left(\tfrac12-\sum_{e\ni v}\xi_e\right)\,d\boldsymbol\xi.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
The measure in both integrals is product Lebesgue measure on all the real edge variables. There is no incoming root variable to integrate; its value is fixed to zero, and missing children contribute zero. At \(r=0\) the empty edge integral is the sole vertex factor. These are continuous partition integrals, with no mesh factor or finite-group normalization.

For each vertex the map \(\mathbb Z\ni z_v\mapsto1/2-\sigma_v(z_v-1/2)\in\mathbb Z\) is a bijection. Also
\[
\left|z_v-\tfrac12-q_v\right|
=\left|\left(\tfrac12-\sigma_v(z_v-\tfrac12)\right)
       -\left(\tfrac12-\sum_{e\ni v}\xi_e\right)\right|.
\tag{7}
\]
Consequently the independent integer sums at the vertices give precisely the factors \(\vartheta_\lambda\) in (6). The same bijection sends the coloring base to zero at every vertex. The gauge and Tonelli's theorem for nonnegative integrands therefore give the exact identities
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf Z_{\mathrm B}^{(h)}
&=\int_{\mathbb R^m}e^{-\lambda E_{\mathbf j}(\mathbf g)}\,d\mathbf g,\\
\mathsf Z_{\mathrm{all}}^{(h)}
&=\sum_{\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\int_{\mathbb R^m}e^{-\lambda E_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf g)}\,d\mathbf g.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
Thus the full-lattice partition uses every original integer base, including non-Boolean bases, with exactly the half-shift and edge measure required by the reduction. These partition functions are distinct from the original-budget volumes in (5); accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c supplies their comparison with those volumes.

Now apply accepted fact 6400a1236f17e882. Its scalar \(s\) is exactly \(\lambda=29/2\), and its real edge variable called \(t_v\), for \(2\le v\le127\), is exactly \(\xi_v\) in (6). This is an alias for the gauged signed edge variable, not for either original nonnegative variable \(t_v^+\) or \(t_v^-\). Its conventions \(t_1=0\) and missing-child values zero agree with ours. At height six there are exactly 127 vertex factors and 126 edge factors, and its measure \(dt=\prod_{v=2}^{127}dt_v\) becomes \(d\boldsymbol\xi=\prod_{v=2}^{127}d\xi_v\), with Jacobian one. Finally its kernel \(\theta_s\) is exactly \(\vartheta_\lambda\). Hence its displayed continuous integrals satisfy the literal identifications \(Z_B=\mathsf Z_{\mathrm B}^{(6)}\) and \(Z_{\mathrm{all}}=\mathsf Z_{\mathrm{all}}^{(6)}\). No raw or normalized grid sum is being identified with a continuous integral.

Accepted fact 6400a1236f17e882 proves that these integrals are positive and finite and that their ratio is strictly smaller than \((8/5)^{127}\). Multiplying by the positive denominator gives
\[
\mathsf Z_{\mathrm{all}}^{(6)}
<\left(\frac85\right)^{127}\mathsf Z_{\mathrm B}^{(6)},
\tag{9}
\]
which implies the non-strict finite hypothesis (4) of accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c. We use this accepted standalone continuous-integral inequality directly. Its certificate need not be rerun, and the sufficient finite-array test additionally displayed in that reduction is not an extra premise.

All other hypotheses of accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c were fixed in (1): \(h\ge6\), \(n=2^{h+1}-1\), \(m=n-1\), \(d=3n-2\), rational \(1\le k\le n/100\), the common budget \(R=n/2+k\), the two original flow blocks, and the specified coloring. With (9), its conditional mass conclusion is therefore unconditional and gives (2), with exactly \(\mathsf A=(6e^{29/4})^{63}\).

At the fixed positive center \(\mathbf j\), zero is in \(\mathcal U_{\mathbf j}\), with \(\alpha(0)=w_{\mathbf j}\) and \(\Delta_{\mathbf j}(0)=0\). The original oriented sum therefore has \(B_{\mathbf j}^{\Delta}(0)\ge w_{\mathbf j}\). Taking \(L=0\) in the definition of \(K_{\mathbf j}(w)\), and then allowing \(\mathbf j\) among the centers defining \(K(w)\), proves (3) using (2). All logarithms used here are at positive endpoints.

The actual-body depth conclusion of accepted fact 657741d896d33f6c applies at this same center and cutoff zero. As proved there, it chooses one \(p^\star\in C_{\mathbf j}\) before any halfspace and gives depth at least \(B_{\mathbf j}^{\Delta}(0)/12\). Equation (3) yields \(B_{\mathbf j}^{\Delta}(0)/12\ge W/(30\mathsf A\,2^n)\), proving (4) with the original mixed measure and all closed ambient halfspaces. The center, cutoff, and point do not depend on the halfspace. Since \(\mathsf A=(6e^{29/4})^{63}>e\), the fixed coefficient \((30\mathsf A)^{-1}\) lies in \((0,1/e)\), as required for this entire family.
