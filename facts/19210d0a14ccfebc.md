## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, let $D$ be a convex body in $\mathbb R^d$, and let $F$ be a compact convex subset of a hyperplane normal to the unit vector $e$, with $f=\mathcal H^{d-1}(F)>0$. Translate $F$ to height zero in this direction, let $I$ be the open height interval of $D$, and write $h=|I|$, $m=d-1$, $U=|D|$ and $D_t$ for the height-$t$ section identified with $e^\perp$. Then $(D+F)_t=D_t+F$, and
$$
\begin{aligned}
|D+F|&\ge\int_I\bigl(|D_t|^{1/m}+f^{1/m}\bigr)^m\,dt\\
&=U+fh+\sum_{j=1}^{m-1}\binom mj f^{j/m}\int_I |D_t|^{(m-j)/m}\,dt .
\end{aligned}
$$
For $d=2$, $|D+F|=U+fh$.

In particular, suppose $A,K,B$ are convex bodies in $\mathbb R^d$ with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$, $u\ne0$, $D=A\cap\{u\cdot x\ge a_0\}$ is a proper positive-volume cap, and $B\subseteq\{u\cdot x\le b_0\}$ has a support facet $F=B\cap\{u\cdot x=b_0\}$ with $f=\mathcal H^{d-1}(F)>0$. For $q=|K\cap\{u\cdot x\ge(a_0+b_0)/2\}|$, the first bound above applies to $2^dq$ with $e=u/\lVert u\rVert$ and $h=(h_A(u)-a_0)/\lVert u\rVert$, where $h_A$ is the support function of $A$. In particular $2^dq\ge U+fh$. If these bodies are the positive-volume fibers at $-1,0,1$ of a mixed body and the closed ambient cap with these three thresholds has raw mixed mass $Q$, then $2^d Q\ge (2^d+1)U+fh$.

For $d=2$, $B=T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1,\ i=1,2,3\}$ and $(v_1,v_2,v_3)=((1,0),(0,1),(-1,-1))$, the cap $v_i\cdot x\ge z$ has $2^d Q_i=4Q_i\ge5U_i+3(h_A(v_i)+1)$ for every proper positive $A$ trace of area $U_i$.

## Proof

The section identity follows because the $e$-coordinate of each point of the translated $F$ is zero. Each section of $D$ at an interior height has positive $m$-volume, as can be seen by interpolating an interior ball with a point at height above or below the ball. Fubini ignores the two endpoint heights. Apply ordinary Brunn–Minkowski to the compact sections in $e^\perp$ and expand the integer power to obtain the formula. In dimension two both sections are intervals and their lengths add exactly.

For the cap application work with the original position of $F$. If $x\in D$ and $y\in F$, then $(x+y)/2\in K$ and $u\cdot(x+y)/2\ge(a_0+b_0)/2$. Properness gives the bottom height $a_0/\lVert u\rVert$ of $D$ and the top height is $h_A(u)/\lVert u\rVert$. Since halving scales $d$-volume by $2^{-d}$, the inclusion gives the full bound for $q$. The endpoint trace on $B$ is exactly $F$ of $d$-volume zero. The contributions from the two other named fibers give $Q\ge U+q$. For the triangle the vertices are $(1,1),(1,-2),(-2,1)$. Its supporting segment at $v_i\cdot x=1$ has length $3\lVert v_i\rVert$, and the threshold in $A$ is $-1$, giving the stated planar constant. For a smaller $B$, the same argument uses its actual facet length.
