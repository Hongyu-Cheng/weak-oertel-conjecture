## Statement

Let $A\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be a compact full-dimensional convex body with $n,d\ge1$ and total mixed volume $M>0$. For $s>0$, take the hull of $A$ in a new integer layer $r=0$ and its continuous dilation $(z,x)\mapsto(z,sx)$ in layer $r=1$, and call the resulting body $C$. Its mixed volume is $(1+s^d)M$. At each old base $j$, the feasible masses and their balanced continuous moment bodies are exactly preserved at $(j,0)$, as are the raw marginal depth and the raw ambient mixed depth of every old mark. In particular, if $t>0$ satisfies $D_j\ge t$ and $2^{n+1}t>M$, the choice $s^d=2^{n+1}t/M-1$ makes $t$ the new prescribed mass $M_C/2^{n+1}$. Any old balanced-body centroid of mass $t$ with depth below $\gamma_d t$ remains a bad prescribed-mass centroid at the eligible base $(j,0)$, where $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$.

## Proof

Only $r=0,1$ are integer levels in the hull, and at either level every convex combination is supported in that layer. Thus the actual sampled fibers are precisely those of the two specified copies and their volumes scale by $1,s^d$, respectively. Full dimension follows from the full-dimensional old layer and a point in the distinct layer $r=1$.

For an actual dominated measure of positive mass $t$ with mean $r=0$, its first $r$-moment is exactly its mass on $r=1$, so all its mass is in the old copy. The other moment and domination requirements are then precisely the old ones; conversely extend any old feasible measure by zero. This proves equality of the feasible mass sets and of the represented continuous means, and therefore preserves any well-defined centroid of the moment body.

For both the ambient probability and its base pushforward, the two normalized layer measures satisfy 1ca50f1bd2d34218 with old-layer weight $M/[(1+s^d)M]$. Its identity multiplies the old normalized depth at each old mark by that weight. On multiplying back by the new total mass, this gives equality of both raw depths, including pure-base and boundary caps. The displayed choice of dilation is positive by hypothesis and sets $M_C/2^{n+1}=t$. This preserves $D_j\ge t$ and the strict raw depth failure at the old centroid.
