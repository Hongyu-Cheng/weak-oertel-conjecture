## Statement

Let $C=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(3,0),(3,3),(2,10),(0,2)\}$ in coordinates $(z,x)$. Its four integer fibers at $0,1,2,3$ are $[0,2],[0,6],[0,10],[0,3]$. Their midpoints have containing closed caps with respective raw mixed lengths $1,5,5,3/2$, all below $21/4$, one quarter of the total mixed length.

## Proof

The lower edge is $x=0$ and the roof is the concave piecewise-affine graph $x=2+4z$ for $0\le z\le2$ and $x=24-7z$ for $2\le z\le3$. Together with the vertical endpoint edges, these bound the displayed compact full-dimensional pentagon. Its only integer bases are $0,1,2,3$, with the stated fibers and total length $21$.

For each midpoint, use a cap $x\ge a(z)$ with threshold function and clipped lengths as follows.

| Midpoint base | $a(z)$ | Lengths at bases $0,1,2,3$ |
|---|---|---|
| $0$ | $11z+1$ | $1,0,0,0$ |
| $1$ | $11(z-1)+3$ | $2,3,0,0$ |
| $2$ | $9-2z$ | $0,0,5,0$ |
| $3$ | $-11(z-3)+3/2$ | $0,0,0,3/2$ |

Each threshold is the corresponding half-length at the tested base. The entries are obtained by intersecting the given closed upper rays with $[0,h]$ at each base, whose captured length is $\max(0,h-\max(0,a(z)))$. Summing each row gives at most $5<21/4$.
