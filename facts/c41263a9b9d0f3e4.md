## Statement

Let $D\subset\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge2$, be a convex body with $0$ interior, $F$ a full facet of dimension $m=d-1$ with outer unit normal $n$, and $h=h_D(n)>0$. Let $P_D(\xi)=1+a_D\cdot\xi$ be positive on $D$, and $\nu_F=hP_D^{-d}\,dS_F$. For an actual subfamily of full homothetic source fibers $K_z=y(z)+\ell(z)D$ with $y,\ell$ affine and $\ell>0$, use the chart $R(z,X)=\ell(z)+a_D\cdot(X-y(z))$. On each full source facet the physical law $hR^{-d}\,dS_X$ pulls back to $\ell(z)^{-1}\nu_F$. One common $c_F\in\operatorname{relint}F$ gives compatible affine centers $X_z=y(z)+\ell(z)c_F$ at the original labels. Every closed relative face halfspace containing $X_z$ has at least fraction $\gamma_{d-1}=((d-1)/d)^{d-1}$ of that actual facet law.

If three such full source labels are $z_-,z_+,z_0=(z_-+z_+)/2$ with $\ell(z_-)\ne\ell(z_+)$, the three masses $W_z=\nu_F(F)/\ell(z)$ cannot be the volumes, even after a common positive rescaling, of compatible uniform convex fibers in any positive integer continuous dimension at the same labels.

## Proof

On $X=y(z)+\ell(z)\xi$ one has $R=\ell(z)P_D(\xi)$; the tangential area Jacobian is $\ell(z)^{d-1}$, giving exactly the claimed inverse scale. In the intrinsic $m$-dimensional affine space of $F$, the density $hP_D^{-d}$ extended by zero off the compact facet is bounded and upper semicontinuous. Its inverse $(m+1)$-st power, interpreted as $+\infty$ off the facet, is the positive affine $h^{-1/d}P_D$ on its convex domain, and is convex as an extended function. Apply d6a3cbae1da72277 at its exact threshold exponent $d=m+1$ to get an interior point $c_F$. Every source facet map is a positive homothety, preserving closed relative halfspaces and normalized mass. The same $c_F$ then gives the displayed affine assignment of physical centers and preserves all anchored affine relations between the labels.

For $r\ge1$, strict convexity of $s^{-1/r}$ for positive $s$, and affinity of $\ell$, give $W_{z_0}^{1/r}<\frac{1}{2}(W_{z_-}^{1/r}+W_{z_+}^{1/r})$. Compatible uniform convex $r$-fibers at these same labels would contain the midpoint Minkowski average in their middle fiber; Brunn–Minkowski requires the reverse inequality for their $r$-volume roots, unchanged by a common positive multiplier. This is conditional on the nonconstant-scale source relation; no such relation is claimed to occur among actual deepest sources in a given model.
