## Statement

Let m_0,m_1 be integers at least 100. Let X be partitioned into m_0 pairs of label zero and m_1 pairs of label one. Let c_0,c_1 be distinct atoms of X, with c_1 an endpoint of one label-zero pair and c_0 an endpoint of one label-one pair. Give the atoms positive original weights w_x, retain exactly Z=sum_{x in X}w_x and p_x=w_x/Z, and put a_a=p_{c_a}. Require the endpoint-probability product in every label-a pair to equal a_a^2. Thus the two forced pairs have respective totals L_0=a_1+a_0^2/a_1 and L_1=a_0+a_1^2/a_0. Let S_a be the total original probability of endpoints of label-a pairs, and define
$$
\begin{aligned}
K&=m_0+m_1,& S_0+S_1&=1,\\
\mu_a&=\frac{S_a/a_a+2}{2(m_a+1)},&
A&=K^2\sum_{x\in X}p_x^3,\\
\Delta_a&=4m_a(m_a+1)K^2a_a^3,&
H&=\sum_{a=0}^1\frac{\Delta_a}{\mu_a}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Then 1-A<=(3/4)H.

If also A<1 and A_full:=A+Delta_0+Delta_1<=9/8, define
$$
r_a=\frac12\left[\log\frac{A+\Delta_a}{A}
+\log\frac{A_{\mathrm{full}}}{A+\Delta_{1-a}}\right].
$$
Then the exact uniform weighted Shapley budget holds:
$$
-\log A\le\sum_{a=0}^1\frac{256}{256+2\mu_a}r_a.
$$
All these symbols have exactly the two-overlap meanings of d8676f01de073eb0: m_a counts total pairs, K is occupied parity count, and p is the unchanged original law. No additional-axis count or conditional normalizer is used.

## Proof

Write t_{a,j} for the total original probability of the j-th label-a pair. Relabel within each core so that t_{a,1}=L_a. Introduce only the algebraic rescalings
$$
\eta_a=Ka_a,\qquad v_{a,j}=Kt_{a,j},\qquad
\bar v_a=\frac{KS_a}{m_a},\qquad \lambda_a=KL_a.
$$
The endpoint sets partition X, so sum_{a,j}v_{a,j}=K. The product condition and arithmetic-geometric mean give v_{a,j}>=2eta_a, and therefore 2eta_a<=bar v_a. The two forced totals satisfy
$$
\lambda_0=\eta_1+\frac{\eta_0^2}{\eta_1},\qquad
\lambda_1=\eta_0+\frac{\eta_1^2}{\eta_0}.
\tag{1}
$$
These are rescalings of the original probabilities, not a new law.

Define \(\psi(v)=v^3-3v+2=(v-1)^2(v+2)\) for \(v\ge0\). Its sum over all scaled pair totals is nonnegative. The cubic identity for a pair of total \(t\) and endpoint product \(a_a^2\) is \(p_x^3+p_y^3=t^3-3a_a^2t\). Direct substitution into \(A\) and \(H\) consequently gives the exact identity
$$
K\left(A+\frac34H-1\right)
=\mathcal D-\mathcal C_0-\mathcal C_1,
\qquad \mathcal D:=\sum_{a,j}\psi(v_{a,j}),
\tag{2}
$$
where the core charges are
$$
\mathcal C_a
=3m_a\bar v_a\eta_a^2
-\frac{6m_a(m_a+1)^2\eta_a^4}
{m_a\bar v_a+2\eta_a}.
\tag{3}
$$
Indeed, \(KA=\sum_{a,j}v_{a,j}^3-3\sum_a\eta_a^2m_a\bar v_a\), while \(K(3/4)\Delta_a/\mu_a\) equals the second term of (3) with its sign reversed. Also \(\sum_{a,j}v_{a,j}^3-K=\mathcal D\), because \(\sum_{a,j}v_{a,j}=K\) and there are \(K\) pairs. This verifies (2) with the original occupied \(K\) throughout.

We shall prove that a negative left side in (2) is impossible. First, 2eta_a<=bar v_a implies m_a bar v_a+2eta_a<=(m_a+1)bar v_a. Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal C_a
&\le3m_a\bar v_a\eta_a^2
-\frac{6m_a(m_a+1)}{\bar v_a}\eta_a^4\\
&=\frac{3m_a\bar v_a^3}{8(m_a+1)}
-\frac{6m_a(m_a+1)}{\bar v_a}
 \left(\eta_a^2-\frac{\bar v_a^2}{4(m_a+1)}\right)^2\\
&\le\frac38\bar v_a^3.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
$$
The last bound is uniform in the unbounded counts and means.

Suppose for a contradiction that A+(3/4)H<1. Equation (2) gives
$$
\mathcal D<\mathcal C_0+\mathcal C_1
\le\frac38(\bar v_0^3+\bar v_1^3).
\tag{5}
$$
The function \(\psi\) is convex on the nonnegative half-line. Jensen within each core, followed by \(m_a\ge100\) and \(\psi\ge0\), gives
$$
\mathcal D\ge\sum_a m_a\psi(\bar v_a)
\ge100\sum_a\psi(\bar v_a).
\tag{6}
$$
For every \(s\ge0\) one has \(s^3\le2\psi(s)+2\). A rational sum-of-squares verification is
$$
2\psi(s)+2-s^3
=s\left(s-\frac32\right)^2
+3\left(s-\frac{11}{8}\right)^2+\frac{21}{64}>0.
$$
Applying this to (5), then using (6), yields \(\mathcal D<(3/4)\sum_a\psi(\bar v_a)+3/2\le3\mathcal D/400+3/2\).
Consequently
$$
\mathcal D<\frac{600}{397}.
\tag{7}
$$
Since \(\psi\) is increasing on \([1,\infty)\), (6)-(7) force \(\bar v_a<15/14\) for each \(a\). Indeed,
$$
100\psi(15/14)=\frac{1075}{686}>\frac{600}{397}.
$$
Returning to (5), we obtain the sharper absolute bound
$$
\mathcal D<\frac34\left(\frac{15}{14}\right)^3
=\frac{10125}{10976}.
\tag{8}
$$
Each forced pair contributes \(\psi(\lambda_a)\) as an actual term of \(\mathcal D\). The other terms are nonnegative. Since \(\psi\) decreases on \([0,1]\) and increases on \([1,\infty)\), and since
$$
\psi(3/8)=\frac{475}{512}>\frac{10125}{10976},
\qquad
\psi(8/5)=\frac{162}{125}>\frac{10125}{10976},
$$
we conclude that
$$
\frac38<\lambda_a<\frac85\qquad(a=0,1).
\tag{9}
$$
This is where both exact forced-spine cells enter the argument.

Solving (1), using positivity of every denominator, gives
$$
\eta_0=\frac{\lambda_0^2\lambda_1}
{\lambda_0^2+\lambda_1^2},\qquad
\eta_1=\frac{\lambda_0\lambda_1^2}
{\lambda_0^2+\lambda_1^2}.
\tag{10}
$$
For positive \(x,y\), the function \(x^2y/(x^2+y^2)\) is increasing in \(x\). After setting \(x\) to its lower bound \(l\), the function \(l^2y/(l^2+y^2)\) is decreasing for \(y\ge l\). It follows that its minimum over \([l,u]^2\) is \(l^2u/(l^2+u^2)\). Use \(l=3/8\) and \(u=8/5\) in (9)-(10) to obtain
$$
\eta_a>\frac{360}{4321},\qquad
\frac{\eta_a}{\bar v_a}>\frac{336}{4321}.
\tag{11}
$$
The second inequality also uses \(\bar v_a<15/14\).

The first line of (4) can now be written as
$$
\mathcal C_a\le3m_a\bar v_a\eta_a^2
\left[1-2(m_a+1)\left(\frac{\eta_a}{\bar v_a}\right)^2\right].
\tag{12}
$$
The bracket is strictly negative: m_a>=100 and (11) give
$$
2(m_a+1)\left(\frac{\eta_a}{\bar v_a}\right)^2
>202\left(\frac{336}{4321}\right)^2>1,
$$
where the last exact comparison is
\(202\cdot336^2-4321^2=4133951>0\).
Thus both \(\mathcal C_a\) are negative. This contradicts (5), because \(\mathcal D\ge0\). The contradiction proves \(1-A\le(3/4)H\) for every table in the statement, before imposing any energy or mean assumptions. The proof applies to arbitrary nonforced pair totals; no Jensen equalization was assumed.

For the stated Shapley consequence, X has exactly 2K atoms, so Holder on the unchanged original law gives A>=1/4. Also mu_a>=1. The scalar conversion proved in d8676f01de073eb0, equations (15)-(18), applies to any two positive increments with A>=1/4, A<1, A_full<=9/8, mu_a>=1, and the 3/4 atomic estimate. For completeness, its explicit bounds are
$$
r_a\ge\frac{2\Delta_a}{A+A_{\mathrm{full}}},\qquad
\frac{256}{256+2\mu_a}\ge\frac{128}{129\mu_a}.
$$
Writing delta=1-A and using the proved atomic estimate gives
$$
\sum_a\frac{256}{256+2\mu_a}r_a
\ge\frac{1024\,\delta}{387(A+9/8)}.
$$
The same accepted fact proves \((A+9/8)(-\log A)/(1-A)\le(11/6)\log4<1024/387\) on \(1/4\le A<1\). These bounds establish the exact logarithmic budget claimed above. Every term uses the original probabilities and the original occupied \(K\).

Finally, the exact identity (21) of d8676f01de073eb0 identifies A+(3/4)H-1, at the forced-spine Jensen minima, with its defect sum minus (K+1/4)/(K+1/2)^2. The atomic result therefore pays that full explicit amount in its (22) whenever both counts are at least 100. No conclusion for the remaining min(m_0,m_1)<=99 strips is implied by this certificate; the proof first loses uniform control there at (6), whose coefficient 100 is needed to turn (5) into the bounds (7)-(11).
