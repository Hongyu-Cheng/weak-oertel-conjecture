## Statement

Retain finitely many actual cap sequences in the allocated capwise flux theorem on the same full-body sequence and the same subsequence. Write $\theta_H\ge0$ for their limiting weights, $a=\sum_H\theta_Ha_H$ and $\zeta_i^{\mathrm{cap}}=\sum_H\theta_H\zeta_i^H$. Then
$$
-\pi_*(n\,a)=M\sum_i c_i\zeta_i^{\mathrm{cap}}-\sum_H\theta_Hu_Hs_H .
$$
If this very $a$ and the full-body defect $b$ satisfy the same-law reserve bound $\int\psi(n)f(w)\,d(a+q)\le\beta\int\psi(n)f(w)\,db$ with $q\ge0$, all continuous $\psi\ge0$ and all nonnegative globally finite convex $f$, then for each continuous-space vector $e$,
$$
\sum_H\theta_H|e\cdot u_H|\,s_H(\mathrm{all})
\le\beta\int|e\cdot n|\,db-\int|e\cdot n|\,dq
  +M\sum_i|e\cdot c_i|\,\zeta_i^{\mathrm{cap}}(\mathrm{all}) .
$$

## Proof

Sum the allocated capwise vector identities for the first conclusion. For each cap, test its physical identity by the constant function one and pair with $e$. Since $s_H$ is nonnegative, this gives $|e\cdot u_H|s_H(\mathrm{all})\le\int|e\cdot n|\,da_H+M\sum_i|e\cdot c_i|\zeta_i^H(\mathrm{all})$. Multiply by the nonnegative weights and sum. The reserve test with $\psi(n)=|e\cdot n|$ and $f=1$ bounds the first term by the difference of the two displayed boundary integrals.
