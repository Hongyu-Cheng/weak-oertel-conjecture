## Statement

Let $N\ge1$ be an integer and take interval fibers $[-1/2,1/2]$ at $-N,\ldots,N$ in one integer coordinate. Write $\mu$ for summed fiber length and $M=2N+1$. Both difference bodies of the canonical hull equal $[-1,1]$. The closed cap $z+x\ge0$ has mass $M/2$. Every closed affine cap with the same trace on the sampled set, normalized so that the absolute continuous coefficient is one, has margin $x+Az$ with $A\ge1/2$ and satisfies
$$
\int (x+Az)_+\,d\mu\ \ge\ \frac18+\frac{N(N+1)}4.
$$
In particular the minimum hinge lower bound divided by $M$ grows without bound. The horizontal pair $x\ge0$ and $-x\ge0$ covers the sampled set, with cap masses $M/2$ and total hinge $M/4$.

## Proof

The canonical hull is the rectangle $[-N,N]\times[-1/2,1/2]$, giving the difference bodies. The old cap contains the upper half of the central fiber, all positive-index fibers and no negative-index fiber, with total mass $N+1/2$. The proper upper-half trace at zero forces the sign of the continuous coefficient to be positive and the threshold there to be zero. Thus the normalized margin is $x+Az$. At $z=1$ containment of its lower endpoint forces $A\ge1/2$. Exact exclusion at $z=-1$ additionally forces strictness, which preserves the displayed bound.

The hinge integral on the center interval is $1/8$. At positive index $j$ the margin is nonnegative on the whole interval and its integral is $Aj$, while negative indices contribute zero. Hence the total is at least $1/8+\frac12\sum_{j=1}^Nj=1/8+N(N+1)/4$. For the horizontal pair each half-cap has length $1/2$ and hinge $1/8$ per interval. This gives both cap masses and total hinge as stated.
