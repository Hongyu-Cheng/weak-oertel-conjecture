## Statement

Retain the canonical hard-branch setting, the fixed \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\), and the notation of accepted facts d01de728d8b5e33c and d06d919de3083960. Thus

\[
Y:=\widehat Y(v),
\qquad
w_y:=\widehat\tau_y(v)>0\quad(y\in Y),
\]

and \(A_\star\subsetneq Y\) is the nonempty stopped set supplied by accepted fact d06d919de3083960. For \(y\in Y\), write

\[
C_y:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(y,x)\in C\},
\qquad
a_y:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_y),
\qquad
c_y:=\frac1{a_y}\int_{C_y}x\,dx.
\]

The synchronized endpoint bound in accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c gives

\[
0<w_y\le \frac{a_y}{4}
\qquad(y\in A_\star).
\tag{1}
\]

Let \(0\le q\le n\), and let

\[
\Delta:=\operatorname{conv}\{v_0,\ldots,v_q\}
\]

be a \(q\)-dimensional lattice simplex with \(v_0,\ldots,v_q\in A_\star\). Put \(L:=\operatorname{aff}\Delta\) and \(E:=\Delta\cap\mathbb Z^n\). This set \(E\) is finite, nonempty, and contains every vertex. Let \(\lambda_0,\ldots,\lambda_q:L\to\mathbb R\) be the affine barycentric-coordinate functions of the simplex. For \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E\), write

\[
\Lambda_m(E):=
\left\{
\eta\in\mathbb R_+^E:
\sum_{z\in E}\eta_z=1,\quad
\sum_{z\in E}\eta_z z=m
\right\}.
\]

For every \(z\in E\), define

\[
t_z
:=
\left(
\sum_{i=0}^q\lambda_i(z)(4w_{v_i})^{1/d}
\right)^d
=
4\left(
\sum_{i=0}^q\lambda_i(z)w_{v_i}^{1/d}
\right)^d
\tag{2}
\]

and define the affine map \(s_\Delta:L\to\mathbb R^d\) by

\[
s_\Delta(y):=\sum_{i=0}^q\lambda_i(y)c_{v_i}.
\tag{3}
\]

Then every lattice point of the simplex carries an actual positive feasible mass:

\[
0<t_z\le a_z,
\qquad
s_\Delta(z)\in\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z)
\qquad(z\in E).
\tag{4}
\]

There are \(m\in L\cap\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E\) and \(p\in\mathbb R^d\) such that every proper relative closed halfspace \(J_L\subseteq L\) containing \(m\) satisfies

\[
\sum_{z\in E\cap J_L}t_z
\ge
2^{-q}\sum_{z\in E}t_z,
\tag{5}
\]

and the same point \(p\) lies in every barycentric Minkowski sum:

\[
p\in
\sum_{z\in E}\eta_z\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z)
\qquad
\text{for every }\eta\in\Lambda_m(E).
\tag{6}
\]

Consequently, if \(q<n\), then

\[
\boxed{
G_L^{\mathrm{rel}}
\ge
4\sum_{z\in\Delta\cap\mathbb Z^n}
\left(
\sum_{i=0}^q\lambda_i(z)w_{v_i}^{1/d}
\right)^d.}
\tag{7}
\]

If \(q=n\), then \(L=\mathbb R^n\) and the analogous global bound is

\[
\boxed{
G
\ge
4\sum_{z\in\Delta\cap\mathbb Z^n}
\left(
\sum_{i=0}^n\lambda_i(z)w_{v_i}^{1/d}
\right)^d.}
\tag{8}
\]

## Proof

Fix \(i\in\{0,\ldots,q\}\). By (1), the function

\[
f_i:=\frac{4w_{v_i}}{a_{v_i}}\mathbf 1_{C_{v_i}}
\]

satisfies \(0\le f_i\le\mathbf 1_{C_{v_i}}\), and its mass is the strictly positive number \(4w_{v_i}\). Its normalized first moment is

\[
\frac1{4w_{v_i}}\int_{C_{v_i}}x f_i(x)\,dx
=
\frac1{a_{v_i}}\int_{C_{v_i}}x\,dx
=c_{v_i}.
\]

Therefore, with \(r_i:=(4w_{v_i})^{1/d}>0\),

\[
c_{v_i}\in\mathsf M_{r_i^d}(C_{v_i}).
\tag{9}
\]

Accepted fact dfd25e72c1f45a4e proves, before taking any closure, that the positive-mass set

\[
\left\{(y,x,r):r>0,\quad x\in\mathsf M_{r^d}(C_y)\right\}
\tag{10}
\]

is convex. Let \(z\in E\). Since \(z\in\Delta\), its barycentric coefficients satisfy \(\lambda_i(z)\ge0\), \(\sum_i\lambda_i(z)=1\), and \(z=\sum_i\lambda_i(z)v_i\). Applying the convexity of (10) to the finitely many positive-mass triples in (9) gives, exactly and without a limiting argument,

\[
\left(
z,
\sum_{i=0}^q\lambda_i(z)c_{v_i},
\sum_{i=0}^q\lambda_i(z)r_i
\right)
\in
\left\{(y,x,r):r>0,\quad x\in\mathsf M_{r^d}(C_y)\right\}.
\tag{11}
\]

The last coordinate in (11) is strictly positive because every \(r_i>0\) and the barycentric coefficients are nonnegative and sum to one. Equations (2), (3), and (11) therefore give

\[
s_\Delta(z)\in\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z)
\quad\text{with}\quad t_z>0.
\tag{12}
\]

Membership in the metronoid in (12) is represented by a density bounded above by \(\mathbf 1_{C_z}\). Integrating that pointwise bound shows \(t_z\le\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)=a_z\). This proves every assertion in (4), including feasibility at every lattice point \(z\in\Delta\cap\mathbb Z^n\).

The affine hull \(L\) is a primitive affine lattice subspace. Indeed,

\[
L=v_0+V,
\qquad
V:=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{v_i-v_0:1\le i\le q\},
\]

where \(v_0\in\mathbb Z^n\) and \(\dim V=q\). The lattice \(V\cap\mathbb Z^n\) has rank \(q\), because it contains the \(q\) linearly independent differences \(v_i-v_0\). It is saturated: if \(u\in\mathbb Z^n\) and \(ku\in V\cap\mathbb Z^n\) for some positive integer \(k\), then \(u=k^{-1}(ku)\in V\), so \(u\in V\cap\mathbb Z^n\).

We may thus apply accepted fact 10f1feebe06e22d0 to the primitive rank-\(q\) space \(L\), the finite nonempty set \(E\subseteq L\cap\mathbb Z^n\), the positive feasible masses in (4), and the affine selector \(s_\Delta\). That fact supplies an integer point

\[
m\in L\cap\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E
\]

with the balance (5), and it supplies the common point

\[
p:=s_\Delta(m).
\]

For completeness, if \(\eta\in\Lambda_m(E)\), affineness gives

\[
p=s_\Delta\!\left(\sum_{z\in E}\eta_z z\right)
=\sum_{z\in E}\eta_zs_\Delta(z)
\in\sum_{z\in E}\eta_z\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z),
\]

which proves (6) with one point independent of the barycentric representation.

If \(q<n\), the proper-rank conclusion of accepted fact 10f1feebe06e22d0 gives

\[
G_L^{\mathrm{rel}}\ge\sum_{z\in E}t_z.
\]

Substituting the second expression in (2) proves (7), including the exact coefficient \(4\). If \(q=n\), the affinely independent differences \(v_i-v_0\) span \(\mathbb R^n\), so \(L=\mathbb R^n\). The full-rank conclusion of accepted fact 10f1feebe06e22d0 gives \(G\ge\sum_{z\in E}t_z\), and the same substitution proves (8). The proof also covers \(q=0\): then \(E=\{v_0\}\), there are no proper relative halfspaces of \(L\), and the rank-zero case of accepted fact 10f1feebe06e22d0 applies directly.
