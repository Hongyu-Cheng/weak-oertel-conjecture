## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $m=d+1$, and let $v_0,\ldots,v_d$ span $\mathbb R^d$ with sole dependence generated by $\sum_jv_j=0$. Let $T=\{x:v_j\cdot x\le1\ \forall j\}$. Let $A,K,B$ be full-dimensional compact convex bodies with $B\subseteq T$, $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$, and $0\in\operatorname{int}K$. Let $\lambda$ be a probability vector on the indices other than $i$ and extend it by $\lambda_i=0$. Put $J=\{j:\lambda_j>0\}$, $k=|J|$, $u=\sum_j\lambda_j v_j$, and suppose $\min_Au\cdot x\ge-1$. Write $Q$ for the genuine raw mixed closed-halfspace depth at $(0,0)$ in the actual three-fiber set with fibers $A,K,B$ at $-1,0,1$. Put
$$
\Delta=|A|+|K\cap\{u\cdot x\ge0\}|-Q,\qquad
C=\frac{|T|\binom{d}{k}}{m^k\prod_{j\in J}\lambda_j}.
$$
For $\varepsilon\ge0$ let $L_A(\varepsilon)=|A\cap\{u\cdot x<-1+\varepsilon\}|$ and $B_\varepsilon=B\cap\{u\cdot x\ge1-\varepsilon\}$. Then $\Delta\ge0$ and
$$
\Delta\ge L_A(\varepsilon)-|B_\varepsilon|\ge L_A(\varepsilon)-C\varepsilon^k.
$$

If $\min_Au\cdot x=-1$ and the face $F=A\cap\{u\cdot x=-1\}$ has dimension $r>d-k$, form a $d$-simplex $D$ using $r+1$ affinely independent points of $F$ and $p=d-r$ interior vertices of $A$. Let $H_*>0$ be the maximum height $u\cdot x+1$ of those added vertices. For
$$
L=\frac{|D|\binom{d}{p}}{2^r H_*^p},
\qquad
\varepsilon_*=\min\{H_*/2,(L/(2C))^{1/(k-p)}\},
$$
one has $\Delta\ge L\varepsilon_*^p/2>0$. In the facet case $r=d-1$, one may also use $H_A=\max_{x\in A}(u\cdot x+1)$,
$$
L_f=\frac{\mathcal H^{d-1}(F)}{2^{d-1}\lVert u\rVert},\qquad
\varepsilon_f=\min\{H_A/2,(L_f/(2C))^{1/(k-1)}\},
\qquad
\Delta\ge L_f\varepsilon_f/2>0.
$$
In particular criticality $\Delta=0$ forces $L_A(\varepsilon)\le C\varepsilon^k$ for every $\varepsilon\ge0$, and any nonempty face at level $-1$ has dimension at most $d-k$.

## Proof

All $d$ normals other than $v_i$ are independent by the sole-dependence hypothesis, so $u\ne0$. The three lifted fibers are the exact integer fibers of their convex hull: the two endpoints are exposed by the base coordinate, and any convex combination over the middle has equal endpoint weights and hence has continuous coordinate in $K$ by $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$. The cap $u\cdot x\ge z$ contains the source $(0,0)$ and all of $A$. The average of the bounds $v_j\cdot x\le1$ gives $u\cdot x\le1$ on $B$, so its level-one capture has zero $d$-volume. The raw cap mass is therefore $Q+\Delta$, and $\Delta\ge0$. In the cap $u\cdot x\ge(1-\varepsilon)z$ the same middle trace is retained, exactly $L_A(\varepsilon)$ of $A$ is lost and exactly $B_\varepsilon$ of $B$ is captured. Its mass is at least $Q$ because it still contains the same source. The weighted cap-decay theorem pk-w36-abnormal-weighted-simplex-cap-decay has the same enclosing simplex and weights, so its upper bound $C\varepsilon^k$ for $|B_\varepsilon|$ gives the second inequality. These are exact closed-cap volumes with the lost $A$ layer using the strict complement.

For the face assertion, extend any $r+1$ independent face points successively by interior points of $A$. This gives $D\subseteq A$ with positive volume. The height $u\cdot x+1$ is zero at the first vertices and positive at each added vertex. In barycentric coordinates of $D$ it is at most $H_*$ times the sum of the $p$ added-vertex coordinates. For $0<\varepsilon\le H_*/2$, the simplex in these $p$ coordinates with sum at most $\varepsilon/H_*$ therefore describes a subset of the layer, up to its null height boundary. Uniform volume in $D$ gives these coordinates density
$$
\frac{d!}{r!}\left(1-\sum q_j\right)^r\ge\frac{d!}{r!\,2^r}
$$
on that simplex, by integrating the $r=d-p$ other independent barycentric coordinates. Its coordinate volume is $(\varepsilon/H_*)^p/p!$, proving $L_A(\varepsilon)\ge L\varepsilon^p$. The face assumption gives $1\le p<k$. At the stated $\varepsilon_*$ the competing $C\varepsilon_*^k$ is at most $L\varepsilon_*^p/2$, proving positive slack.

For a facet take the cone in $A$ with base $F$ and apex of height $H_A$. The cross section at height $t$ has $(d-1)$-area $(1-t/H_A)^{d-1}\mathcal H^{d-1}(F)$. Integrate with coarea factor $1/\lVert u\rVert$ over $0\le t\le\varepsilon\le H_A/2$ to obtain $L_A(\varepsilon)\ge L_f\varepsilon$. Here the face hypothesis gives $k\ge2$, so $\varepsilon_f$ is defined and its choice gives the stated positive bound. The criticality consequences now follow from the finite inequalities.
