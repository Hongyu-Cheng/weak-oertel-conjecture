## Statement

Let $C$ be the canonical hull of finitely many actual positive convex sampled fibers. Write $\eta$ for the sum of their own-fiber boundary areas, with endpoint counting in $d=1$. Suppose $g$ is a nonnegative affine slack on $C$ with zero set $F$ in $C$. For every bounded nonnegative convex function $\phi$ on $C$, the convex functions $\Phi_t=(\phi-tg)_+$ decrease to $\phi\mathbf1_F$ on $C$, with convergence in $L^1(\eta)$ for measurable $\phi$. If $\phi$ is a globally finite continuous nonnegative convex physical test, the sequence consists of actual inward speed fields on the original measured boundary. By contrast, if $w_0$ is in an affine section $S$ and is a strict convex combination of $w_-,w_+\in C\setminus S$, no sequence of nonnegative convex tests on $C$ can have a positive limit at $w_0$ and zero limit at both outside points.

## Proof

Subtracting an affine function preserves convexity and the maximum with zero preserves it as well. On $F$ the sequence is constant at $\phi$, and off $F$ it eventually vanishes. Its bound by $\phi$ and finiteness of the summed surface measure give convergence, including all sampled boundary-base faces. For globally admissible $\phi$, each $\Phi_t$ is a globally finite continuous convex function independent of the unit normal. Apply 8820984cf6a6942e to realize it as the inward speed for sufficiently small deformation, separately for each finite $t$. Finally if $w_0=\theta w_-+(1-\theta)w_+$ for $0<\theta<1$, convexity gives $\Phi_t(w_0)\le\theta\Phi_t(w_-)+(1-\theta)\Phi_t(w_+)$. The right side tends to zero under the asserted outside limits.
