## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), and retain the exact canonical hard pooled KKT system, notation, normalizations, and alias maps of accepted facts 236e5d1f3c2efa28, 637b5f7874d8c875, 0ba2ef9056bea8b9, 6a74481cad1d7e81, and e8b320da9989469f. Thus the finite canonical edge set has midpoint target bodies \(L_e\subseteq\mathbb R^d\), centered gauge bodies \(D_e\subseteq\mathbb R^d\), reservoir weights \(r_e\geq0\), original load-minimal vectors \(v_e\), and original loads
\[
 \omega_e:=h_{D_e}(v_e),
 \qquad 0\leq\omega_e\leq r_e,
 \qquad Q:=\sum_e r_e>0,

\tag{1}
\]

\[
 0<\chi_m\leq1,
 \qquad \lambda_e:=1-\chi_{m_e},

\tag{2}

For a nonnegative edge field \(q\), write
\[
 R_C(q):=
 \max_{s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}

\tag{3}
\]
In the present positive-fresh branch, the fresh load-minimal reoptimization of accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81 has
\[
 \widetilde\Omega:=\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0.
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently \(B>0\). Put
\[
 C_\beta:=R_C(\beta),
 \qquad
 \mathcal U_\beta:=
 \max_s\sum_{\substack{e:\,\beta_e>0\\s(m_e)\in L_e}}r_e.
\tag{5}
\]
Let
\[
 A:=\max\{P_{\mathrm{pool}},P_{\mathrm{anc}}\},
 \qquad
 F_{\mathrm{fr}}
 :=2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}

\]
be the exact ancestral and fresh quantities of accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875. Retain the positive-fresh ancestral rank \(r_{\mathrm{anc}}\), ancestral base rank \(\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}\), and drift rank \(k_{\mathrm{drift}}\) of accepted fact e8b320da9989469f.

Retain the same-ray negative-circuit decomposition of accepted fact 236e5d1f3c2efa28. Namely, for every original positive-load edge \(e\), set
\[
 u_e:=\frac{v_e}{\omega_e},
 \qquad h_{D_e}(u_e)=1,
\tag{7}
\]
and there are nonnegative coefficients \(\omega_e^{(j)}\), vectors
\[
 v_e^{(j)}:=\omega_e^{(j)}u_e,
 \qquad
 C_j:=\{e:\omega_e^{(j)}>0\},
 \qquad 1\leq j\leq N,
\tag{8}
\]
such that every \(C_j\) is nonempty,
\[
 \sum_j\omega_e^{(j)}=\omega_e,
 \qquad
 \sum_ev_e^{(j)}=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_ev_e^{(j)}m_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{e\in C_j}h_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)})<0.
\tag{9}
\]
Define
\[
 \delta_j:=\min_{e\in C_j}\lambda_e\omega_e^{(j)},
 \qquad
 \mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}:=\sum_{j=1}^N\delta_j,
 \qquad
 \tau_j:=\min_{e\in C_j}\lambda_e.
\tag{10}
\]

First, define the uniformly circuit-scaled ancestral field
\[
 w_e^{\circ}:=\sum_{j=1}^N\tau_jv_e^{(j)},
 \qquad
 \theta_e:=h_{D_e}(w_e^{\circ}).
\tag{11}

 \boxed{
 \theta_e=\sum_j\tau_j\omega_e^{(j)}

\tag{12}

\tag{13}

\tag{15}

If \(\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}>0\), the common value in (15) is strictly negative. In particular, uniform whole-circuit scaling supplies a legal positive dual witness without deleting any edge of a contributing circuit.

\tag{24}

More particularly, for every arbitrary choice of one omitted edge \(a_j\in I_j\) for each \(j\in J_+\),

 \boxed{
 \sum_{j:\,a_j=e}\delta_j\leq\beta_e

## Proof

We first prove the same-ray compensation. For every edge of positive original load, all vectors \(v_e^{(j)}\) lie on the nonnegative ray generated by \(u_e\). Positive homogeneity of support functions on one ray, together with (8)-(11), gives
\[
 h_{D_e}(w_e^{\circ})
 =\sum_j\tau_jh_{D_e}(v_e^{(j)})
 =\sum_j\tau_j\omega_e^{(j)}.
\tag{30}
\]
If \(\omega_e=0\), all terms in (30) are zero, so the identity remains valid. Whenever \(e\in C_j\), the definition of \(\tau_j\) gives \(\tau_j\leq\lambda_e\). Therefore (9) and (30) imply
\[
 \theta_e
 =\sum_j\tau_j\omega_e^{(j)}
 \leq\lambda_e\sum_j\omega_e^{(j)}
 =\lambda_e\omega_e
 =\beta_e.
\tag{31}
\]
This proves (12). Summing the two affine moment equations in (9) with coefficients \(\tau_j\) proves (13). Hence \(w^{\circ}\) satisfies every fresh weighted-dual constraint, with the componentwise allocation in (31); in particular, overlapping circuits never use an edge beyond \(\beta_e\).

Fix \(j\). Choose \(e_j\in C_j\) with \(\lambda_{e_j}=\tau_j\). By (10),
\[
 \delta_j
 \leq\lambda_{e_j}\omega_{e_j}^{(j)}
 =\tau_j\omega_{e_j}^{(j)}
 \leq\tau_j\sum_{e\in C_j}\omega_e^{(j)}.
\tag{32}
\]
Summing (30) over the edges and then using (32) proves (14).

The same-ray identity also holds for the uncentered target support functions:
\[
 h_{L_e}(w_e^{\circ})
 =\sum_j\tau_jh_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)}).
\tag{33}
\]
Summing (33) over \(e\) proves (15). If \(\delta_j>0\), every \(\lambda_e\) on \(C_j\) is positive, so \(\tau_j>0\). Conversely, if \(\tau_j>0\), then the finite set \(C_j\) has both \(\lambda_e>0\) and \(\omega_e^{(j)}>0\) at every edge, so \(\delta_j>0\). Thus \(\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}>0\) means that at least one strictly negative inner sum in (15) has a positive coefficient, while all other inner sums are also strictly negative whenever their coefficient is positive. This proves the strict assertion following (15). Notice that the construction scales every vector in one circuit by the same \(\tau_j\). It therefore preserves both cancellations exactly and never removes a small coefficient in isolation.

We next construct the minimal genuine non-hit families. Fix \(j\in J_+\). Then \(\delta_j>0\), so for every \(e\in C_j\),
\[
 0<\lambda_e\omega_e^{(j)}
 \leq\lambda_e\omega_e
 =\beta_e.
\tag{34}
\]
Suppose one affine map \(s\) hit every \(L_e\) on \(C_j\). The support-function inequality and (9) would give
\[
 \sum_{e\in C_j}h_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)})
 \geq
 \sum_{e\in C_j}\langle v_e^{(j)},s(m_e)\rangle
 =0.
\tag{35}
\]
Indeed, writing \(s(m)=Tm+b\), the two moment equations in (9) annihilate the \(Tm_e\) and \(b\) terms separately. Equation (35) contradicts the strict negative circuit objective in (9). Hence the finite family \((L_e)_{e\in C_j}\) is not simultaneously hit.

Choose an inclusion-minimal non-hit subfamily \(I_j\subseteq C_j\). A singleton is always hit by a constant affine map with value at any chosen point of its nonempty target body, so \(k_j\geq2\). Minimality gives (19) for every \(e\in I_j\), and (34) proves that all its edges lie in the positive congestion support.

For each \(e\in I_j\), the map \(s_{j,e}\) is admissible in (3) and (5). It therefore gives
\[
 C_\beta\geq\beta(I_j)-\beta_e,
 \qquad
 \mathcal U_\beta\geq r(I_j)-r_e.
\tag{36}
\]
Sum the first inequality in (36) over the \(k_j\) choices of \(e\). Every weight in \(\beta(I_j)\) then occurs exactly \(k_j-1\) times on the right, so
\[
 k_jC_\beta\geq(k_j-1)\beta(I_j).
\tag{37}
\]
The identical calculation with the second inequality in (36) gives
\[
 k_j\mathcal U_\beta\geq(k_j-1)r(I_j).
\tag{38}
\]
Equations (37)-(38) prove (21).

For every \(e\in I_j\), equations (10) and (34) give
\[
 \delta_j\leq\lambda_e\omega_e^{(j)}
 \leq\lambda_e\omega_e
 =\beta_e\leq r_e.
\tag{39}
\]
Thus \(k_j\delta_j\leq\beta(I_j)\). Combining this with the first inequality of (21) gives
\[
 (k_j-1)\delta_j\leq C_\beta.
\tag{40}
\]
Accepted fact 0ba2ef9056bea8b9 gives \(C_\beta\leq\mathcal U_\beta\), proving (22).

For a fixed edge \(e\), use (39), the inclusion \(I_j\subseteq C_j\), and the exact circuit decomposition in (9):
\[
 \sum_{\substack{j\in J_+\\e\in I_j}}\delta_j
 \leq
 \lambda_e
 \sum_{\substack{j\in J_+\\e\in I_j}}\omega_e^{(j)}
 \leq
 \lambda_e\sum_j\omega_e^{(j)}
 =\lambda_e\omega_e
 =\beta_e.
\tag{41}
\]
This proves (23). Summing (41) over \(e\) gives
\[
 \sum_{j\in J_+}k_j\delta_j
 \leq\sum_e\beta_e=B.
\tag{42}
\]
Circuits outside \(J_+\) have \(\delta_j=0\), so
\[
 \sum_{j\in J_+}\delta_j=\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}.
\tag{43}
\]
Subtracting (43) from (42) proves the second inequality in (24). If one edge \(a_j\in I_j\) is selected for each \(j\), the first inequality in (25) follows from (41) by restricting its left side further, and the second is (43). This proves every packing and no-reuse assertion.

It remains to audit how the new objects enter the accepted capacities. Accepted fact 0ba2ef9056bea8b9, together with the independent \(D\)- and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\)-bounds of accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875, gives exactly (26): \(D\) receives \(A\), \(R\) receives the one maximum of \(Q-\Omega\) and \(\mathcal U_\beta\), and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) receives the one maximum of \(A\) and \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}\). No lower bounds assigned to the same capacity are added.

The canonical hard ledger retained in accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 has nonnegative remaining summands and gives
\[
 D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{44}
\]
Combining (26) and (44) proves (27). Its left side is at least \(2A\), at least \(\mathcal U_\beta\), and at least \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}\). Together with \(C_\beta\leq\mathcal U_\beta\), this proves (28).

Finally, accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81 supplies the actual nonempty fresh support and fresh score in (4) and (6). Accepted fact e8b320da9989469f gives the support inclusion and residual-rank drift bound, and accepted facts 236e5d1f3c2efa28 and 637b5f7874d8c875 give
\[
 \widetilde\Omega
 \geq B-C_\beta
 \geq\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}},
 \qquad
 \widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}
 \leq r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}},
 \qquad
 \widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}\leq\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}.
\tag{45}
\]
Substitution of (45) into the definition of \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}\) proves (29).

The proof has produced one feasible same-ray field, one edge-capacity packing, and one legal three-capacity ledger. It has not produced a common affine map for the different local maps in (19). Summing their separate retained masses would reuse the single \(R\)-summand in (26), while adding \(A\) and \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}\) would reuse \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\). Therefore the broad split score inequality remains unproved, exactly as stated in the registered relation of this subgoal. This completes the proof.
