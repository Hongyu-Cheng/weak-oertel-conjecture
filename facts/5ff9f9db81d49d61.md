## Statement

Fix a strict $2+2$ section of a tetrahedron and choose barycentric labels so its normal is $n=(p,q,-r,-1)$ with $p,q,r>0$. Let $g(n')$ denote the actual area centroid of the section with normal $n'$ in this open sign region and put $b=g(n)$. On the affine chart $n'\cdot b=0,\ n'_4=-1$ choose affine coordinates $(t,u)$ with linearly independent directions $d,e$. For a centroid parameter $b'$ of coordinate sum one let $F_{b'}(n')=C_{b'}(p',r')$, using the explicit polynomial of fact 1aa346b10594ee87 with $p'=n'_1,\ r'=-n'_3$. At $(b,n)$ let $K$ represent its derivative in $b'$ modulo multiples of $\mathbf1$, and decompose $K-(b\cdot K)\mathbf1=\kappa_t d+\kappa_u e+\gamma n$.

With $d_1=d,\ d_2=e$, the paired Jacobian $J_{ij}=d_i\cdot Dg(n)[d_j]$ is symmetric and satisfies $J\kappa=-\nabla_{t,u}F_b(n)$. Write $a=J_{tt}$, $F_t=\partial_tF_b(n)$ and $E_0=\kappa^\top J\kappa=-\kappa\cdot\nabla_{t,u}F_b(n)$. For $\kappa_u\ne0$, $\det J=(aE_0-F_t^2)/\kappa_u^2$. If also $J$ is invertible, the inverse pairing with the covector $du$ is $C=(du)^\top J^{-1}(du)=\kappa_u^2a/(aE_0-F_t^2)$. For positive definite $J$, $C\ge\kappa_u^2/E_0$, with equality exactly when $F_t=0$. For indefinite $J$ with $a<0$ and $E_0>0$, $C\le\kappa_u^2/E_0$. If the line $u=0$ contains two distinct normals of this same split with actual centroid $b$, then $F_b(t,0)$ is a constant multiple of $(t-t_1)(t-t_2)$ and the two values of $\partial_tF_b$ are opposite, including the identically zero case.

## Proof

The formulas of 1462fa9ce6ced54e are smooth in the strict positive heights. For the normalized tetrahedral cap probability $P(n')$, fact 3e6e1eb15d927db8 gives $\nabla P(n')=3 f(n')g(n')$, where $f$ is the density at zero of $\sum_j n'_j E_j$ with independent unit exponentials. This factor is positive and differentiable here because $3f=\mathbf1\cdot\nabla P$ and $P$ is $C^2$. On pairing its derivative with $d_i$, the density derivative drops out at $g(n)=b$ since $d_i\cdot b=0$. Thus the chart Hessian equals $3f(n)J$ and $J$ is symmetric.

The polynomial in 1aa346b10594ee87 gives $F_{g(n')}(n')=0$ throughout the strict split, including on the chart. The vectors $d,e,n$ form a basis of $b^\perp$ because $d_4=e_4=0$ and $n_4=-1$, so the decomposition of $K$ exists and is independent of its representative. Differentiating $\mathbf1\cdot g(n')=1$ and $n'\cdot g(n')=0$ along $d_j$ gives $\mathbf1\cdot Dg(n)[d_j]=0$ and $n\cdot Dg(n)[d_j]=-d_j\cdot b=0$. The chain rule in the conic therefore yields $(J^\top\kappa)_j+\partial_jF_b=0$.

Write $J=\begin{pmatrix}a&c\\c&b_0\end{pmatrix}$. Since $-F_t=a\kappa_t+c\kappa_u$, subtraction gives $aE_0-F_t^2=(ab_0-c^2)\kappa_u^2$. The inverse formula follows from the cofactor $a$. In the definite case $a,E_0>0$ and $C=\kappa_u^2/(E_0-F_t^2/a)$ with positive denominator at most $E_0$. If $a<0<E_0$, the denominator in that expression is at least $E_0$. Finally the chart is affine and $C_b$ is quadratic, so the restriction to the line with the two distinct zeros has the asserted factorization and opposite endpoint derivatives.
