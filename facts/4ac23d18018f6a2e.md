## Statement

Let $b=9601/20000$ and $f=(1+x)\mathbf1_{[-1,b]\times[-1,1]}$. Its planar Ball radial body, with $\rho(u)^2=2\int_0^\infty r f(ru)\,dr$, is convex and preserves all origin-through halfplane mass fractions. The vertical density section at $x=0$ is uniform on the centered segment $[-1,1]$ in the $y$ coordinate, and the corresponding Ball chord is the same segment. In the normal direction $(1,0)$ the full centroids have projections $-133/10000$ and $16184667199/(312903500000\sqrt3)$ respectively. Both second coordinates are zero.

## Proof

Set $s(x)=\sqrt{1+2x/3}$. On each unit ray with endpoint $Ru$, direct integration gives radial endpoint $Rs(Ru_x)$. The map $(x,y)\mapsto s(x)(x,y)$ has Jacobian $1+x$ and radial derivative $(1+x)/s(x)>0$ in the interior. It is a bijection on interiors and fixes rays, giving the exact area pushforward and halfplane fractions. As a function of $X=xs(x)$, $S(X)=s(x(X))$ has decreasing derivative $1/[3(1+x(X))]$. The transformed graphs $\pm S(X)$ bound a convex body by continuity to the endpoints. On $x=0$ the map is the identity, so the centered section is its own chord.

Let $Z=(1+b)^2/2$ be the mass of the horizontal marginal. The original normalized $x$-mean is $(2b-1)/3=-133/10000$. In the transformed first moment, set $t=\sqrt{3+2x}$, whose endpoints are $1$ and $199/100$. Since $x=(t^2-3)/2$, $1+x=(t^2-1)/2$, and $dx=t\,dt$,
$$
\sqrt3\,g_{B,x}
=\frac1{4Z}\left[\frac{t^7}{7}-\frac{4t^5}{5}+t^3\right]_{1}^{199/100}
=\frac{16184667199}{312903500000}.
$$
Reflection in the second coordinate fixes both measures. The sign reversal is along the normal to their shared centered section.
