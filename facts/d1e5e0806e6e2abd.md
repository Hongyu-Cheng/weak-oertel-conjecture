## Statement

Let $P$ be finite, with compact positive-volume convex $d$-fibers $K_z$ satisfying $\sum_z\lambda_z K_z\subset K_p$ whenever $\lambda_z\ge0,\ \sum_z\lambda_z=1,\ \sum_z\lambda_z z=p\in P$. Write $w_z=|K_z|/M$, $M=\sum_z|K_z|$. Suppose $\ell_z>0$, $\sigma_p\ge0,\ \sum_p\sigma_p=1$, and a nonnegative matrix $\pi$ has marginals $\sigma_p/\ell_p,w_z/\ell_z$ with row moments
$$
\sum_z\pi_{pz}z=(\sigma_p/\ell_p)p,\qquad
\sum_z\pi_{pz}\ell_z=\sigma_p.
$$
Set $\widehat\pi_{pz}=\ell_z\pi_{pz}$, and $\alpha_{pz}=\widehat\pi_{pz}/\sigma_p$ on positive rows. With $Z_z=(z,1)/\ell_z$ and $D_z=K_z/\ell_z$, $\widehat\pi$ has probability marginals $\sigma$ and $w$, and each positive row satisfies
$$
\sum_z\alpha_{pz} Z_z=Z_p,\qquad \sum_z\alpha_{pz}D_z\subset D_p.
$$
Consequently $h_{D_p}(u)\ge\sum_z\alpha_{pz}h_{D_z}(u)$ for every $u$, and $R_p\ge\sum_z\alpha_{pz}R_z$ for $R_z=|K_z|^{1/d}/\ell_z$. In particular $\sum_p\sigma_p R_p\ge\sum_z w_z R_z$.

## Proof

The $\ell$-moment gives the row marginal of $\widehat\pi$, and multiplication by $\ell_z$ gives its column marginal $w_z$. In the lifted first moment, the $\ell_z$ factors cancel; the original mass and base moment give $(p,1)/\ell_p$. In a positive row use the probabilities $\lambda_{pz}=\ell_p\pi_{pz}/\sigma_p$. Their mean is $p$, so compatibility gives $\sum_z\lambda_{pz}K_z\subset K_p$. Divide by $\ell_p$ and use $\alpha_{pz}=\lambda_{pz}\ell_z/\ell_p$ to obtain the second assertion for exactly the same coupling. The support-function identity for Minkowski sums and Brunn–Minkowski give the two row inequalities. Average the volume-root inequality with $\sigma_p$ and use the column marginal $w_z$.
