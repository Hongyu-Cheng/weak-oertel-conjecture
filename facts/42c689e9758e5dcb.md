## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
 \qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z.
\tag{1}
\]
Retain the exact common canonical hard objects, normalizations, and alias maps of accepted facts 637b5f7874d8c875, f6188983e8850f01, and 6a74481cad1d7e81. Thus \(r=(r_e)\) is the half-strength canonical product reservoir, its original load-minimal optimizer has loads
\[
 0\leq\omega_e\leq r_e,
 \qquad
 Q:=\sum_er_e>0,
 \qquad
 \Omega:=\sum_e\omega_e,
 \qquad
 R:=R_C(r),
\tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\tag{3}
\]
Assume the canonical hard ledger
\[
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{4}
\]
In particular,
\[
 R<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 \Omega>\frac{99Q}{100}.
\tag{5}
\]

For every physical midpoint target \(m\), put
\[
 \Omega_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}\omega_e.
\tag{6}
\]
Retain the canonical multipliers \(0<\chi_m\leq1\) of accepted fact f6188983e8850f01 and define
\[
 \widehat\Omega:=\sum_m\chi_m\Omega_m,
 \qquad
 \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=(1-\chi_{m_e})\omega_e,
 \qquad
 B_{\mathrm{cong}}:=\sum_e\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
\tag{7}
\]
\[
 C_\beta:=R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}).
\tag{8}
\]
For every loaded target, retain its pooled point
\[
 x_m:=\frac1{\Omega_m}
 \sum_{e:m_e=m}\omega_e\ell_e,
\tag{9}
\]
and put
\[
 \widehat E:=\{m:\Omega_m>0\},
 \qquad
 \rho_J:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E),
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 r_J:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E)\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{x_m-s(m):m\in\widehat E\},
\tag{11}
\]
\[
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}
 :=\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}
 \frac{\widehat\Omega}{r_J+1}.
\tag{12}
\]

Apply accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81 afresh to
\(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}\), under the exact congestion alias map of accepted fact f6188983e8850f01. Assume the positive-fresh branch
\[
 \widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0.
\tag{13}
\]
Write
\[
 0\leq\widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \leq\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 \widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\sum_{e\ni z}\widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 E_{\mathrm{fr}}
 :=\{z:\widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}>0\},
\tag{14}
\]
and retain the fresh endpoint points
\[
 \widetilde p_z^{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\frac1{\widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \sum_{\substack{e\ni z\\
 \widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0}}
 \widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \widetilde x_{e,z}^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \qquad(z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}).
\tag{15}
\]
Define
\[
 \widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{fr}}),
\tag{16}
\]
\[
 \widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{fr}})
                    \to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{\widetilde p_z^{\mathrm{cong}}-s(z):
 z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}\},
\tag{17}
\]
\[
 F_{\mathrm{fr}}
 :=2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 {\widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}+1}.
\tag{18}
\]

Then the pooled and fresh masses satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
 \widehat\Omega+\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
 \geq\Omega-C_\beta
 \geq\Omega-R
 >\frac{49Q}{50}.}
\tag{19}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{
 \max\{\widehat\Omega,\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}\}
 >\frac{49Q}{100}.}
\tag{20}
\]

The pooled target support always obeys
\[
 \boxed{\rho_J\geq3.}
\tag{21}
\]
If
\[
 \widehat\Omega>\frac{49Q}{100},
\tag{22}
\]
then its residual rank satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 r_J+1
 >98\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_J}.}
\tag{23}
\]

If
\[
 \widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>\frac{49Q}{100},
\tag{24}
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}\geq3}
\tag{25}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 \widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}+1
 >49\,2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}.}
\tag{26}
\]
More precisely, before using the hard upper bound on \(G\), the actual low-base-rank implication is
\[
 \boxed{
 \widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>\frac{49Q}{100},
 \quad
 \widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}\leq2
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 G>\frac{49Q}{800}.}
\tag{27}
\]
Equations (19)-(27) do not assert that either macroscopic high-rank ledger is absorbed by a terminal capacity.

## Proof

Accepted fact f6188983e8850f01 gives the exact congestion identity
\[
 B_{\mathrm{cong}}=\Omega-\widehat\Omega.
\tag{28}
\]
Accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81, under the alias map fixed above, gives
\[
 \widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
 \geq B_{\mathrm{cong}}-R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}})
 =B_{\mathrm{cong}}-C_\beta.
\tag{29}
\]
Accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 gives
\[
 R\geq C_\beta.
\tag{30}
\]
Combining (28)-(30),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \widehat\Omega+\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
 &\geq
 \widehat\Omega+B_{\mathrm{cong}}-C_\beta\\
 &=\Omega-C_\beta\\
 &\geq\Omega-R.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{31}
\]
Equations (5) and (31) give
\[
 \widehat\Omega+\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
 >\frac{99Q}{100}-\frac Q{100}
 =\frac{49Q}{50},
\]
which proves (19). If both masses in (20) were at most \(49Q/100\), their sum would be at most \(49Q/50\), contradicting (19). This proves (20).

We next verify the pooled low-base-rank exclusion. Accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 gives
\[
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}<\frac Q{200}<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{32}
\]
Thus the present common canonical objects satisfy all three hard hypotheses of accepted fact 1f80aa23bb960de6:
\[
 G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 \Omega>\frac{99Q}{100},
 \qquad
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{33}
\]
Under the exact aliases (6)-(12), that accepted fact gives
\(\rho_J\geq3\), proving (21).

Suppose (22) holds. Using (12) and (32),
\[
 \gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}
 \frac{\widehat\Omega}{r_J+1}
 <\frac Q{200}.
\tag{34}
\]
All quantities are positive. Rearranging (34) and then using (22) gives
\[
 r_J+1
 >200\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}
 \frac{\widehat\Omega}{Q}
 >98\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}.
\tag{35}
\]
This proves (23).

It remains to prove the fresh low-base-rank implication and residual screen. Accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81 gives
\[
 \widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \leq\sum_{e\ni z}r_e
 \leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 \sum_{z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}}
 \widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}
 =2\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{36}
\]
Assume (24) and
\(\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}\leq2\). Put
\[
 \mathcal A:=\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{fr}}),
 \qquad
 D_{\mathcal A}:=I_+\cap\mathcal A,
 \qquad
 W_{\mathcal A}:=\sum_{z\in D_{\mathcal A}}a_z.
\tag{37}
\]
The inclusion \(E_{\mathrm{fr}}\subseteq D_{\mathcal A}\), equation (36), and (24) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W_{\mathcal A}
 &\geq\sum_{z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}}a_z\\
 &\geq4\sum_{z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}}
          \widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}\\
 &=8\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
 >\frac{98Q}{25}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{38}
\]
Because \(E_{\mathrm{fr}}\) spans \(\mathcal A\) and
\(E_{\mathrm{fr}}\subseteq D_{\mathcal A}\subseteq\mathcal A\),
\[
 \operatorname{aff}(D_{\mathcal A})=\mathcal A,
 \qquad
 \dim\operatorname{aff}(D_{\mathcal A})
 =\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}\leq2.
\tag{39}
\]
Accepted fact 1f80aa23bb960de6 gives the canonical product estimate
\[
 W\geq8Q.
\tag{40}
\]
Apply the actual rank-at-most-two affine-slice theorem of accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c to \(\mathcal A\). Equations (38)-(40), together with
\(2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}\geq1\), give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 G
 &\geq\frac1{64}
 \min\left\{
 W,\,
 2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 W_{\mathcal A}
 \right\}\\
 &>\frac1{64}\frac{98Q}{25}
 =\frac{49Q}{800}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{41}
\]
This proves (27). Since \(49/800>1/100\), equation (41) contradicts the hard inequality \(G<Q/100\) in (5). Therefore (24) forces (25).

Finally, accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 gives
\[
 F_{\mathrm{fr}}<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{42}
\]
Using (18), rearranging (42), and then applying (24),
\[
 \widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}+1
 >100\,2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}}{Q}
 >49\,2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}},
\tag{43}
\]
which proves (26). All conclusions are conditional screens and exclusions; no high-rank absorption has been used or asserted.
