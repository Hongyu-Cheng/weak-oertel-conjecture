## Statement

Let \(T\subset\mathbb R^d\) be a compact convex body, centrally symmetric about \(\mathbf y_0\). Let \(\ell_i^\pm\) be nonnegative bounded upper-semicontinuous concave roofs and define
\[
C=\{(\mathbf x,\mathbf y):
\mathbf y\in T,\ -\ell_i^-(\mathbf y)\le x_i\le1+\ell_i^+(\mathbf y)\},
\quad S=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d).
\]
Put \(a_i=\lfloor\ell_i^-\rfloor\), \(b_i=\lfloor\ell_i^+\rfloor\), and
\(B=\prod_i(a_i+b_i+1)\). Assume \(a_i,b_i\in\{0,1\}\) almost everywhere and
\(B(\mathbf y)=B(2\mathbf y_0-\mathbf y)\) almost everywhere.
Then some core vertex \(\mathbf J\in\{0,1\}^n\) satisfies
\[
h_S((\mathbf J,\mathbf y_0))\ge M/2=\Delta_B,
\qquad M=\int_TB,
\]
where both depths use original unnormalized mass and containing closed halfspaces.

## Proof

Write \(\mathbf y'=2\mathbf y_0-\mathbf y\), and let
\[
R_{\mathbf J}(\mathbf y)
=\#\{\mathbf k\in S_{\mathbf y}:2\mathbf J-\mathbf k\in S_{\mathbf y'}\}.
\]
For a single coordinate, average the overlap count over \(J_i=0,1\). The possible pairs with sum zero or two are
\[
(-1,1),(0,0),(1,-1),(0,2),(1,1),(2,0).
\]
Their availability gives the exact average
\[
\frac{2+a_i+b_i+a_i'+b_i'}2
=\frac{(a_i+b_i+1)+(a_i'+b_i'+1)}2.
\]
Average independently over all core coordinates and use arithmetic-geometric mean:
\[
\mathbb E_{\mathbf J}R_{\mathbf J}(\mathbf y)
\ge\sqrt{B(\mathbf y)B(\mathbf y')}=B(\mathbf y).
\]
Some \(\mathbf J\) therefore has total reflected mass at least \(M\).

The corresponding submeasure is invariant under
\((\mathbf k,\mathbf y)\mapsto(2\mathbf J-\mathbf k,2\mathbf y_0-\mathbf y)\).
It is a centrally symmetric submeasure of the original mixed measure, centered at a feasible core point. Every containing halfspace has at least half its mass. This proves the actual-depth bound.

The \(B\)-measure is centrally symmetric and has a Lebesgue density. Every halfspace through \(\mathbf y_0\) has mass \(M/2\), and opposite halfspaces show that no point has depth exceeding \(M/2\). Thus \(\Delta_B=M/2\).
