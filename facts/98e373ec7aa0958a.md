## Statement

Use the actual trapezoid and square $K_0,K_1$ at bases $0,1$ of a71ab5c885df1a85. Put $t=(4-\sqrt7)/3$, $\Delta=t-4/9$, $s=1/t-1$, $D=2t-t^2$, $V_1=\Delta^2/4$, and $M=3/2+V_1$. At the globally deepest mark $(0,0,t)$, use continuous normals $u_+=(1,-s),u_-=(-1,-s),u_0=(0,1)$ and real base tilts $v_+,v_-,v_0$. The three caps through this mark all have mass $D$ exactly when
$$
v_0\le\Delta/4,\qquad v_\pm\le-\Delta(3s+1)/4.
$$
At strict inequalities the coarea section factors are $2t,2t,2-t$, and center-gradient balancing weights are proportional to $(1,1,R)$, $R=4(1-t)/(2-t)$.

Write $J_j$ for the raw integer flux of 18ac4869642d018e in the displayed cap margin and normal scaling. For every allowed tilt triple, including equality in the inequalities,
$$
J_++J_-+RJ_0\ \ge\ \frac{D V_1\Delta}{4M}\,[2(5s+1)-3R]\ >0.
$$
At $v_0=0$, $v_\pm=-s\Delta$, the individual fluxes are $J_0=-C_0$ and $J_\pm=3s C_0$, where $C_0=D V_1\Delta/(2M)$. Their unnormalized gradient-weighted sum is $C_0(6s-R)$ and their sum with raw-normal weights $(1,1,2s)$ is $4sC_0$.

## Proof

The exact integer census and global deepest mark are given by a71ab5c885df1a85. By 3a5bf9df95c246b2 the three own traces at the displayed normal scalings have the same mass $D$. The second fiber is the square of center $(0,t-\Delta/2)$ and half-side $\Delta/4$. The largest upper-cap margin on it is $v_0-\Delta/4$. The largest margin for either oblique cap is $v_\pm+\Delta(3s+1)/4$. A nonconstant affine margin has positive captured area exactly when its maximum on this positive square is strictly positive. This gives the precise simultaneous tilt condition. In particular equality adds zero mass, and strict exclusion makes the second trace disjoint.

For either oblique normal the line on the trapezoid, in the reflected coordinate as needed, is $x=s(y-t)$. It runs from height zero to the right edge at height $(1+st)/(s+1/2)=2t$. Its length divided by $\sqrt{1+s^2}$ is $2t$. The horizontal section has length $2-t$. The negative center gradients are these coareas times the displayed normals. Their combination with weights $1,1,R$ vanishes exactly because $R(2-t)=4ts=4(1-t)$.

The centroids on the two fibers have heights $4/9$ and $t-\Delta/2$, and both first coordinates are zero. The differences of their scalar means, second minus first, are therefore $\delta m_0=v_0+\Delta/2$ and $\delta m_\pm=v_\pm-s\Delta/2$. Since the cap probabilities are $D/(3/2)$ on the first fiber and zero on the second, the accepted closed-tail flux formula gives $J_j=-(D V_1/M)\delta m_j$. Substitute the tilt bounds to obtain the displayed estimate. The positive factor in brackets has numerator $20t^2-38t+20$ over denominator $t(2-t)>0$. The numerator is strictly positive by its negative discriminant and positive leading coefficient.

For the special choice the upper cap strictly excludes the square, and the oblique maximum margins equal $\Delta(1-s)/4<0$ since $t<1/2$. Substitution gives the three fluxes and both combinations. The negative value of the individual upper flux is compatible with strictly positive full-circuit flux.
