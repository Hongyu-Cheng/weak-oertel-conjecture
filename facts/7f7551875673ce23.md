## Statement

Let $K\subset\mathbb R^d$ be a convex body with volume $V$, $d\ge1$, and let $m\ge3$ be an integer. Start with $[0,m-1]\times K$ and mixed measure the sum of fiber volumes at bases $0,\ldots,m-1$. Choose $u\ne0$, put $l=\min_Ku\cdot x$, $r=\max_Ku\cdot x$, $b=l-1$ and $L=r-l+3$. The closed caps $H_0=\{u\cdot x-Lz\ge b\}$ and $H_1=\{u\cdot x+L(z-(m-1))\ge b\}$ have locally stable full/empty traces on the first and last fibers, respectively.

For every sufficiently close array of actual convex fibers $J_0,\ldots,J_{m-1}$ and nearby caps with the same total $mV$ and respective exact cap masses $V,V$, every $J_j$ has volume $V$ and
$$
J_j=(1-j/(m-1))J_0+(j/(m-1))J_{m-1},\qquad 1\le j\le m-2 .
$$
In particular every directional support gap for these anchored inclusions vanishes and the array consists of translates of a common body along an affine path. No such array has an open neighborhood of independently perturbable compatible fibers. The endpoint cap fractions $1/m$ can be arbitrarily small.

## Proof

At the retained endpoint the cap margin is at least $1$. On every unwanted fiber it is at most $(r-l)-L+1=-2$. On the fixed finite set of bases, the margins vary uniformly under small Hausdorff changes of the fibers and small coefficient changes of the caps, because all nearby fibers remain in a common compact region. Thus the first cap captures exactly $J_0$ and the last exactly $J_{m-1}$, giving $|J_0|=|J_{m-1}|=V$.

For $s=j/(m-1)$, actual convex compatibility gives $(1-s)J_0+sJ_{m-1}\subseteq J_j$. Brunn–Minkowski gives volume at least $V$ on the left. Since the total is $mV$, all the volumes on both sides must equal $V$. Compact full-dimensional convex bodies with a proper containment have distinct volumes: a point of the larger body outside the smaller one can be joined to a ball in its interior, producing a positive-volume part separated from the smaller body. This proves the set equalities. The positive-volume equality case of Brunn–Minkowski at any interior $s$ makes $J_0,J_{m-1}$ homothetic, and their equal volumes make them translates. The interpolation formula gives the claimed common shape and affine translation path.

Every support gap for the endpoint interpolation is then zero. An arbitrarily small translation of $J_0$ alone increases its support in the translation direction, so it violates the inclusion at $j=1$ with all other fibers fixed. Each cap fraction is $1/m$, below $\Gamma_d/2$ for $m>2/\Gamma_d$ with $\Gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. These two endpoint caps only cover the endpoints. If their complements are added to the list, the same obstruction holds under nonincrease of all four budgets at fixed total, because the nonzero continuous normals give null mixed boundary and each pair partitions the mass. The complement budgets are $(m-1)V$.
