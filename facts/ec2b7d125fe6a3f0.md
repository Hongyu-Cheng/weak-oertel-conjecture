## Statement

Let $L\subset\mathbb R^r$, $r\ge1$, be a full-rank lattice of covolume $v$. Let $f\colon\mathbb R^r\to[0,\infty)$ be bounded and measurable with compact support and convex strict superlevels $\{f>t\}$ for all $t>0$. Put $T=\sum_{p\in a+L}f(p)>0$ and $M=\int_{\mathbb R^r}f$. There is an affine hyperplane $E$ spanned by $r$ affinely independent coset points such that
$$
\sum_{p\in(a+L)\setminus E}f(p)\le r!M/v,\qquad
\sum_{p\in(a+L)\cap E}f(p)\ge (T-r!M/v)_+,
$$
where $x_+=\max(x,0)$. If the positive sample set has full affine rank, take $E$ from the greedy rule which repeatedly chooses a highest-valued sample outside the previously selected affine span, starting with a highest-valued sample and stopping at $r$ points. For $b=\max_{p\in(a+L)\setminus E}f(p)>0$ the same $E$ satisfies
$$
M\ge \frac{v}{r!}\left[\sum_{p\in a+L}\min(f(p),b)-rb\right].
$$
If the positive sample set has smaller rank, one can choose $E$ containing all positive samples.

For a compact convex $Q\subset\mathbb R^r\times\mathbb R^s$, $s\ge1$, these conclusions apply to $f(u)=\operatorname{vol}_s(Q_u)$ with $M=\operatorname{vol}_{r+s}(Q)$, provided $T>0$. They apply separately to each closed halfspace intersection of $Q$ with positive sampled mass. In the counting case $s=0$, take $f=\mathbf 1_Q$ for compact convex $Q\subset\mathbb R^r$. The coefficient $r!$ in the outside-mass bound is sharp for every $r\ge1$ and $s\ge0$, including full-dimensional bodies whose nonempty sampled fibers all have positive mass.

## Proof

The set $P=\{p\in a+L:f(p)>0\}$ is nonempty and finite. If it has affine rank below $r$, extend an affine basis of $P$ to $r$ affinely independent coset points, using that the coset spans $\mathbb R^r$. Their affine hull contains $P$ and has zero outside mass.

Otherwise take the $r$ greedy points $p_1,\ldots,p_r$ and their affine hull $E$. An outside point attaining $b$ was eligible at every step, so $f(p_i)\ge b$ for all $i$. For $0<t<b$, the set $A_t=\{p\in P:f(p)>t\}$ contains all $p_i$ and a point outside $E$, and spans $\mathbb R^r$. Its convex hull lies in $\{f>t\}$. By the finite lattice hull volume bound and layer cake,
$$
M\ge\int_0^b\operatorname{vol}_r(\operatorname{conv} A_t)\,dt
\ge\frac{v}{r!}\int_0^b(|A_t|-r)\,dt
=\frac{v}{r!}\left[\sum_{p\in P}\min(f(p),b)-rb\right].
$$
The greedy points contribute $rb$ to the sum. Each point outside $E$ contributes its full value and all other terms are nonnegative. Subtracting outside mass from $T$ and using nonnegativity gives the inside bound. The integral of each strict-level indicator is $\min(f(p),b)$, including samples tied at $b$.

For $s\ge1$, sections of $Q$ lie in a common bounded box. If $u_j\to u$, closedness gives $\limsup_j \mathbf 1_Q(u_j,x)\le \mathbf 1_Q(u,x)$ for every $x$, so reverse Fatou on the box shows that the section volumes are upper semicontinuous. The base projection is compact, and Fubini gives the total integral. Convexity of $Q$ includes the Minkowski average of any two sections in the section at the averaged base. If both volumes exceed $t>0$, Brunn–Minkowski in dimension $s$ makes the averaged section volume exceed $t$. The same reasoning applies to each compact convex closed halfspace intersection and makes no common choice of $E$ across different cuts. For $s=0$, the indicator has the required superlevels directly.

To attain the coefficient, take the simplex $\Delta$ on $a$ and the $r$ points obtained by adding the vectors of a basis of $L$ to $a$. In lattice coordinates it is the standard simplex, whose only lattice points are its vertices. For $s\ge1$ take $Q=\Delta\times K$ with $K$ an $s$-dimensional convex body of volume $V>0$. The $r+1$ vertices carry mass $V$ each and $M=vV/r!$. Every hyperplane misses at least one vertex and a facet hyperplane misses exactly one. For $s=0$ use $\Delta$ and $V=1$.
