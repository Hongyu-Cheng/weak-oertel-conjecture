## Statement

Let $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be a full-dimensional compact polytope, $d\ge1$, whose nonempty integer fibers are all positive $d$-volume. Let $\eta$ be the sum of their boundary $\mathcal H^{d-1}$ measures, with the regular outward continuous unit normal marked, and let $\Pi$ be a physical martingale coupling from a finite $\nu\ll\eta$ to $\eta$ preserving that mark. Let $F=C\cap\{a\cdot z+u\cdot x=b\}$ be a nonvertical supporting facet with $|u|=1$ and $a\cdot z+u\cdot x\le b$ on $C$. Restrict the physical points to $F$ with their marks retained. The target of the rows starting in $F$ is $\eta|_F-\zeta_F$, where $\zeta_F$ is exactly the target law of the rows starting outside $F$ and ending in $F$. Its mass is
$$
L_F=\eta(F)-\nu(F)=\Pi\{w\notin F,\ w'\in F\}\ge0.
$$
The raw restriction of the coupling has target $\eta|_F$ exactly when $L_F=0$. In general it extends, by adding the diagonal coupling of $\zeta_F$, to a martingale from $\nu|_F+\zeta_F$ to $\eta|_F$. Without the correction, $\int_F\psi\,d\nu\le\int_F\psi\,d\eta$ for each finite nonnegative convex function on the affine plane of $F$.

Let $E:\mathbb R^{d-1}\to u^\perp$ be a linear isometry, $\Psi(z,t)=(z,Et+(b-a\cdot z)u)$, and $D=\Psi^{-1}(F)$. Then $D$ is full-dimensional in $\mathbb R^{n+d-1}$ with its entire integer trace, and
$$
(\Psi^{-1})_\#(\eta|_F)=\mu_F=\sum_{p\in\mathbb Z^n}\mathcal H^{d-1}|_{D_p}.
$$
The common mark $u$ is forgotten in this equality. If $\eta(F)>0$, $\mu_F$ is the intrinsic Hausdorff measure on the mixed trace of $D$. The ambient Jacobian of $\Psi$ is $\sqrt{1+|a|^2}$ and the corresponding base projection coarea Jacobian is its reciprocal. The displayed mixed fiber law has no slope factor. At $d=1$ the nonempty reduced fibers are singletons, with unit counting mass. The pullback laws satisfy $\widehat\nu_F+\widehat\zeta_F\preceq_{\mathrm{cx}}\mu_F$ in equal-mass convex order, with the correction of mass $L_F$.

If $d\nu=\sum_j\lambda_j\alpha_j\,d\eta$ for a finite cap menu with actual bounded Borel body allocations and $\lambda_j\ge0$, the reduced source density is $\sum_j\lambda_j(\alpha_j\circ\Psi)$ relative to $\mu_F$. An ambient cap $c_j\cdot z+v_j\cdot x\ge b_j$ pulls back to
$$
(c_j-(v_j\cdot u)a)\cdot z+(E^{\mathsf T}v_j)\cdot t
\ \ge\ b_j-(v_j\cdot u)b .
$$
A zero linear part gives the entire reduced space or the empty set. At strict traces the density selector is the literal indicator. At an equality locus of positive measure the original allocation is retained. If every affine cap-plane equality locus is $\mu_F$-null, the source is exactly the weighted sum of closed trace indicators almost everywhere.

If $\eta$-almost every measured point lies on exactly one nonvertical ambient facet, then $L_F=0$ for every such facet.

## Proof

For an input in $F$, the bounded output slack $b-a\cdot z'-u\cdot x'$ is nonnegative and has conditional mean zero. The output therefore lies in $F$ in almost every such row. Splitting the target on $F$ by whether its source lies in $F$ yields the two target laws and the mass formula. The added diagonal law preserves the row barycenter, so Jensen gives equal-mass convex order on the supporting hyperplane. Without the correction, restricted rows give convex comparison against their own target, which is at most $\eta|_F$. Nonnegativity of the test gives the last comparison in the first paragraph.

The map $\Psi$ is an affine bijection onto the supporting hyperplane and fixes $z$. Thus $D$ is a full-dimensional compact convex polytope and its integer trace corresponds exactly to that of $F$. For fixed $p$ the map in $t$ is an isometry. A nonempty face $F_p$ is exposed in $K_p$ by $u$. Its boundary restriction is precisely $\mathcal H^{d-1}|_{F_p}$, with zero contribution from lower-dimensional faces. On a positive-area face the marked normal is $u$ almost everywhere, including when another ambient facet induces the same own-fiber face. This proves the target formula. If any fiber has positive $\mathcal H^{d-1}$ mass, the finite union of these distinct reduced integer fibers has Hausdorff dimension exactly $d-1$ and its intrinsic measure is their sum. If the facet has mass zero, its transport and correction both vanish.

The Gram matrix of $\Psi$ has orthogonal blocks $I_n+aa^{\mathsf T}$ and $I_{d-1}$, with ambient area factor $\sqrt{1+|a|^2}$. Projection onto the base restricted to the tangent hyperplane has kernel given by the $t$-directions and is inverse to the base-direction block on its orthogonal complement. Its coarea factor is therefore the reciprocal. The equality of mixed fiber laws uses only the fiber isometry. Affine pullback preserves barycentric rows and the correction mass, proving the convex order.

Pulling the density back along the fiber isometry gives the stated source measure. Substituting $\Psi$ gives the trace inequality and the same strict-side indicator. A trace equality can contain all of a reduced fiber or the entire supporting hyperplane. The branch value on such an equality is part of the original density. When the equality loci are null the indicators agree almost everywhere.

Finally, under unique nonvertical ownership, almost every source and target point has a unique containing facet. Each row stays in its input facet, so an input from another facet cannot send positive mass to a uniquely owned target point in $F$. The incoming mass is zero. The target after reduction is a volume law, and the inherited source is a weighted cap law. The original bulk cap budget gives no recursive boundary-stress law by this argument.
