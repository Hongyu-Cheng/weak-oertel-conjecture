## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard instance and the degree tag \(j=d\) of accepted fact `d5d5de35c3f25af9`, with the square-label and tagged-occurrence definitions inherited from accepted fact `ce3ac24a3aedd198`. Suppress the tag \(d\) and write
\[
 w_y:=w_y^d=\frac{d_y}{2},
 \qquad
 W_d:=\sum_{y\in A_d}w_y=P_{\mathrm{tar}}>\frac{381}{800}Q,
 \qquad
 \Theta:=\Theta_\square^d=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal L_d^\square}\theta_\sigma.
\]
Extend \(w_y:=0\) outside \(A_d\). For every \(y\in\mathbb Z^n\), let
\[
 r_y^\square:=R_{d,y}
 =\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal L_d^\square}
 \theta_\sigma\operatorname{mult}_y(\mathcal O_{d,\sigma}),
\]
where \(R_{d,y}:=0\) outside \(A_d\). Let
\[
 I_+:=\{y\in\mathbb Z^n:a_y>0\},
 \qquad
 W:=\sum_{y\in I_+}a_y,
 \qquad
 U_y:=a_y-r_y^\square,
 \qquad
 U:=\sum_{y\in I_+}U_y.
\]
Then \(U_y\geq0\) for every \(y\in I_+\), and
\[
 \boxed{
 U=W-4\Theta
 =\sum_{y\in I_+}(a_y-4w_y)+4(W_d-\Theta),}
\]
with both summands on the right nonnegative.

For the degree-weighted midpoint coefficients, abbreviate
\[
 B_{\xi,t}:=B_{d,\xi,t},
 \qquad
 \alpha_t:=\sum_\xi B_{\xi,t}.
\]
The color-collision slack has the exact formula
\[
 \boxed{
 W_d-\Theta
 =\sum_{t:\alpha_t>0}
 \frac{\sum_\xi B_{\xi,t}^{2}}{\alpha_t}
 \geq 2^{-n}W_d.}
\]
Consequently, if
\[
 U<\frac Q{100},
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta>\frac{379}{800}Q,
 \qquad
 \sum_y r_y^\square=4\Theta>\frac{379}{200}Q,
 \qquad
 n\geq8.}
\]

Let \(G\) be the global complete metronoid-core capacity in the same hard instance, so \(G<Q/100\). For every positive whole square label \(\sigma\in\mathcal L_d^\square\), including labels with a zero-direction loop, one has
\[
 \boxed{2\theta_\sigma\leq G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad \theta_\sigma<\frac Q{200}.}
\]
Hence the branch \(U<Q/100\) contains at least ninety-five positive whole square labels. All identities and inequalities count the four tagged occurrences of each label with multiplicity; they remain valid when a loop repeats one source, when the two opposite pairs contain repeated occurrences, and when a physical source is reused by different labels.

## Proof

All sums below are finite because \(C\) is compact and the degree-weighted square atlas is finite. Accepted fact `d5d5de35c3f25af9`, using the occurrence ledger of accepted fact `ce3ac24a3aedd198`, gives
\[
 0\leq r_y^\square\leq4w_y\leq a_y
 \qquad(y\in A_d).
\]
Outside \(A_d\), both \(r_y^\square\) and \(w_y\) are zero. Therefore \(U_y\geq0\) on \(I_+\), and each term \(a_y-4w_y\) is nonnegative.

Every whole label \(\sigma\) has the four-entry tagged multiset
\[
 \mathcal O_{d,\sigma}=[t+h,t-h,t+k,t-k].
\]
Its cardinality with multiplicity is exactly four, even when \(h=0\) or \(k=0\), and even when a source occurring in this label also occurs in other labels. Since \(r_y^\square\) sums over all labels and retains \(\operatorname{mult}_y(\mathcal O_{d,\sigma})\), changing the order of the finite sums gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_y r_y^\square
 &=\sum_y\sum_\sigma
 \theta_\sigma\operatorname{mult}_y(\mathcal O_{d,\sigma})\\
 &=\sum_\sigma\theta_\sigma
 \sum_y\operatorname{mult}_y(\mathcal O_{d,\sigma})
 =4\sum_\sigma\theta_\sigma
 =4\Theta.
 \end{aligned}
\]
It follows that
\[
 U=\sum_{y\in I_+}(a_y-r_y^\square)=W-4\Theta.
\]
Also \(\sum_{y\in I_+}w_y=W_d\), and hence
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W-4\Theta
 &=\sum_{y\in I_+}(a_y-4w_y)
   +4\sum_{y\in I_+}w_y-4\Theta\\
 &=\sum_{y\in I_+}(a_y-4w_y)+4(W_d-\Theta).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The first right-hand summand is nonnegative by the source ledger. The same ledger gives \(4\Theta=\sum_y r_y^\square\leq4W_d\), so the second is nonnegative as well. This proves the exact two-slack identity.

We next compute the second slack more precisely. In the notation of `d5d5de35c3f25af9`, the direction-orbit masses over a fixed color and target satisfy
\[
 \sum_{[h]\in\mathcal H_{d,\xi,t}}
 \beta_{d,\xi,t,[h]}=B_{\xi,t}.
\]
Indeed, the zero orbit accounts once for the diagonal ordered pair, while every nonzero orbit accounts for the two ordered pairs obtained by reversing its endpoints. Thus, for a target with \(\alpha_t>0\), the total mass of whole labels over that target is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Theta_t
 &:=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathcal L_d^\square\\
                      \text{target}(\sigma)=t}}
       \theta_\sigma\\
 &=\frac{2}{\alpha_t}
   \sum_{\xi<\eta}
   \left(\sum_{[h]}\beta_{d,\xi,t,[h]}\right)
   \left(\sum_{[k]}\beta_{d,\eta,t,[k]}\right)\\
 &=\frac{2}{\alpha_t}\sum_{\xi<\eta}B_{\xi,t}B_{\eta,t}
 =\alpha_t-\frac{\sum_\xi B_{\xi,t}^{2}}{\alpha_t}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
When \(\alpha_t=0\), every relevant coefficient and \(\Theta_t\) is zero. Accepted fact `d5d5de35c3f25af9` gives \(\sum_t\alpha_t=W_d\). Summing the preceding identity over targets proves
\[
 W_d-\Theta
 =\sum_{t:\alpha_t>0}
   \frac{\sum_\xi B_{\xi,t}^{2}}{\alpha_t}.
\]
At most \(2^n\) parity classes occur over any target. For every \(t\) with \(\alpha_t>0\), Cauchy--Schwarz, applied to the nonzero \(B_{\xi,t}\), gives
\[
 \sum_\xi B_{\xi,t}^{2}
 \geq 2^{-n}\left(\sum_\xi B_{\xi,t}\right)^2
 =2^{-n}\alpha_t^2.
\]
Therefore
\[
 W_d-\Theta\geq2^{-n}\sum_t\alpha_t=2^{-n}W_d.
\]

Assume now that \(U<Q/100\). The two nonnegative slacks in the exact decomposition satisfy separately
\[
 \sum_{y\in I_+}(a_y-4w_y)<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 W_d-\Theta<\frac Q{400}.
\]
Using \(W_d>381Q/800\), the second inequality yields
\[
 \Theta>\left(\frac{381}{800}-\frac1{400}\right)Q
 =\frac{379}{800}Q,
\]
and the four-occurrence identity gives
\[
 \sum_y r_y^\square=4\Theta>\frac{379}{200}Q.
\]
The collision lower bound also gives
\[
 \frac Q{100}>U
 \geq4(W_d-\Theta)
 \geq2^{2-n}W_d
 >\frac{381}{200}\,2^{-n}Q.
\]
Thus \(2^n>381/2\). Since \(n\) is an integer and \(2^7<381/2<2^8\), one has \(n\geq8\).

It remains to bound one whole label. Fix a positive
\(\sigma=(t;\xi,[h];\eta,[k])\) and consider its first opposite pair
\[
 u:=t+h,
 \qquad
 v:=t-h.
\]
Both tagged occurrences carry coefficient \(\theta_\sigma\). If \(u\ne v\), then the aggregate occurrence ledger gives
\[
 \theta_\sigma\leq r_u^\square\leq a_u,
 \qquad
 \theta_\sigma\leq r_v^\square\leq a_v.
\]
Let \(c_u,c_v\) be the fiber centroids. The constant densities
\(\theta_\sigma/a_u\) on \(C_u\) and
\(\theta_\sigma/a_v\) on \(C_v\) show that
\[
 c_u\in\mathsf M_{\theta_\sigma}(C_u),
 \qquad
 c_v\in\mathsf M_{\theta_\sigma}(C_v).
\]
Because \(u\ne v\), an affine map
\(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) can be chosen with
\(s(u)=c_u\) and \(s(v)=c_v\). Applying accepted fact
`10f1feebe06e22d0` with \(L=\mathbb R^n\),
\(E=\{u,v\}\), and both masses equal to \(\theta_\sigma\) gives
\[
 G\geq2\theta_\sigma.
\]

If \(u=v\), then \(h=0\), so the same source occurs twice as two tagged occurrences of \(\sigma\). Multiplicity in the aggregate ledger gives
\[
 2\theta_\sigma\leq r_u^\square\leq a_u.
\]
The constant density \(2\theta_\sigma/a_u\) on \(C_u\) shows that
\(c_u\in\mathsf M_{2\theta_\sigma}(C_u)\). Apply
`10f1feebe06e22d0` with \(L=\mathbb R^n\), the singleton
\(E=\{u\}\), mass \(2\theta_\sigma\), and any affine map taking
\(u\) to \(c_u\). Again \(G\geq2\theta_\sigma\). This exhausts the nonloop and loop cases for an opposite pair. Reuse of the same source by other labels only adds terms to \(r_u^\square\), so it does not weaken either capacity inequality. The core theorem is applied separately to each fixed label; no invalid summation of its resulting certificates is used.

The hard-instance inequality in `d5d5de35c3f25af9` is
\(G<Q/100\). Hence every positive whole label satisfies
\[
 2\theta_\sigma\leq G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 \theta_\sigma<\frac Q{200}.
\]
Finally, let \(N:=|\mathcal L_d^\square|\). Under \(U<Q/100\), the already proved lower bound makes \(\Theta>0\), so \(N>0\), and
\[
 \frac{379}{800}Q<\Theta
 =\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal L_d^\square}\theta_\sigma
 <N\frac Q{200}.
\]
Thus \(N>379/4=94.75\), and integrality gives \(N\geq95\), as claimed.
