## Statement

Fix $k\ge0$ and $m\ge1$. Let $C_\ell\subset\mathbb R^k\times\mathbb R^m$ be compact and convex. Assume that all $C_\ell$ have the same finite nonempty integer fiber base set $P$, that $K_{z,\ell}=(C_\ell)_z$ for $z\in P$ lie in one bounded region, and that $M_\ell=\sum_{z\in P}|K_{z,\ell}|_m>0$ tends to zero. Suppose $K_{z,\ell}\to K_z$ in Hausdorff distance and $\mathbf1_{K_{z,\ell}}dx/M_\ell\rightharpoonup\mu_z$ for every $z\in P$. Put $p_z=|\mu_z|$ and $P_+=\{z:p_z>0\}$. Then $\sum_zp_z=1$ and $P_+=\operatorname{conv}(P_+)\cap\mathbb Z^k$. For $z\in P_+$, $\operatorname{supp}\mu_z=K_z$ and $q_z=\dim\operatorname{aff}K_z<m$. For $q_z>0$, the density $f_z$ of $\mu_z$ relative to $\mathcal H^{q_z}$ on $K_z$ has a bounded representative whose $1/(m-q_z)$ power is upper semicontinuous and concave on $K_z$ and positive and continuous on $\operatorname{relint}K_z$. For $q_z=0$, $\mu_z$ is a point mass of size $p_z$.

There exist compact convex $L_z\subset\mathbb R^m_x\times\mathbb R^m_y$ of intrinsic affine dimension $m$, using a single additional $\mathbb R^m_y$, with $\mathcal H^m(L_z)=p_z$ and $(\operatorname{pr}_x)_\#(\mathcal H^m|_{L_z})=\mu_z$. Every combination of bases $z_i\in P_+$ with $\lambda_i>0$, $\sum_i\lambda_i=1$ and $j=\sum_i\lambda_i z_i\in\mathbb Z^k$ satisfies $\sum_i\lambda_iL_{z_i}\subset L_j$. The hull $H$ of the placed $L_z$ has exactly those integer fibers and no other integer bases.

In saturated coordinates on the affine base span, $H$ is affinely equivalent to a full-dimensional convex body $H'\subset\mathbb R^{k'}\times\mathbb R^D$, where $k'=\dim\operatorname{aff}P_+\le k$ and $m\le D\le2m$. The equivalence preserves the sample census and is an isometry on each continuous fiber. An affine map $\Pi$ from the reduced ambient space pushes the intrinsic mixed $\mathcal H^m$ probability of $H'$ to $\sum_{z\in P_+}\delta_z\otimes\mu_z$. It maps every mixed point of $H'$ to the corresponding physical limiting fiber and its closed-halfspace depth cannot decrease under this pushforward.

## Proof

The common bounded region and simultaneous finite weak convergence give $\sum_zp_z=1$. Fix $z\in P_+$ and a point $x\in K_z$. Choose $x_\ell\in K_{z,\ell}$ tending to $x$ and a common radius $R>0$ for the original fibers. For each $\rho>0$ choose $0<t<\min(1,\rho/(4R))$. The homothetic copy $(1-t)x_\ell+tK_{z,\ell}$ lies in $K_{z,\ell}\cap\overline B(x,\rho)$ for all sufficiently large $\ell$. It carries fraction $t^m$ of the uniform measure of the fiber, whose volume is positive eventually because $p_z>0$. The conditional probabilities converge to $\mu_z/p_z$. Portmanteau on the closed ball gives it conditional limit mass at least $t^m$. Applying this inside every open neighborhood and using Hausdorff convergence to exclude all points outside $K_z$ proves exact support equality. By convex volume continuity and $|K_{z,\ell}|_m\le M_\ell\to0$, $q_z<m$.
