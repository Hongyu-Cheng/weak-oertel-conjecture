## Statement

For the body with fibers $K_z=[-1,1]\times[z-1,z]$ on $0\le z\le1$, there is a signed measure $W$ on the two integer traces of the upper shared facet $x_2=z$ with densities in $[-1,1]$, satisfying $\int dW=\int z\,dW=\int x\,dW=0$ but $\int z\otimes x\,dW=(-2/3,0)$.

## Proof

Use density $x_1$ at $z=0$ and $-x_1$ at $z=1$ with respect to length. Oddness makes each trace mass zero, so the base first moment and the second continuous coordinate moment vanish. The two $x_1$ moments are $2/3$ and $-2/3$, giving zero continuous first moment. Weighting these by $z$ gives $(-2/3,0)$. The intervals really are traces of the common affine facet of the displayed convex body.
