## Statement

Let $E\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be compact and convex with $d\ge1$. In one parity class let $M_r>0$ be the sum of the $d$-volumes of its integer fibers and $a>0$ their largest volume. Write $M$ for the total mixed $d$-volume. Then $M\ge2M_r-a$. If $b$ is the largest positive volume in the class below $a$, then
$$
M\ge 2M_r-a+\left(\frac{a^{1/d}+b^{1/d}}{2}\right)^d-b>2M_r-a.
$$
This applies to a compact closed cap $E=C\cap H$ for any ambient closed halfspace $H$.

## Proof

At every strict threshold $0<t<a$, let $A_t$ consist of the bases in the selected class with volume above $t$. The $m=2$ level inequality of d37d39ce91d322d5 supplies at least $2|A_t|-1$ actual integer bases of volume above $t$ inside $\operatorname{conv}A_t$. Its integral is $2M_r-a$.

Let $Q$ be the set of class bases with volume $a$, and take a class base $p$ of volume $b$. If $p\in\operatorname{conv}Q$, convexity and $d$-dimensional Brunn–Minkowski on the anchored combination give volume at least $a$ there. Thus a linear functional strictly separates $p$ above $\operatorname{conv}Q$. Choose $q\in Q$ maximizing that functional. The integer midpoint $(p+q)/2$ lies outside $\operatorname{conv}Q$ and its fiber has volume at least $c=[(a^{1/d}+b^{1/d})/2]^d$. For every $t\in(b,c)$ one has $A_t=Q$, so this base adds one to the preceding count inside $\operatorname{conv}A_t$. Integrate the improved count over that interval and the baseline elsewhere. Since $a>b>0$, $c>b$.
