## Statement

Fix any integer \(k\ge8\). Define
$$
\begin{aligned}
b_\ell&=0 &&(1\le\ell<8),&
b_\ell&=\left\lfloor\frac{2^\ell}{8\ell}\right\rfloor &&(8\le\ell\le k),\\
Q_0&=0,& Q_\ell&=\sum_{r=1}^{\ell}b_r,& Q&=Q_k,\\
m&=2(Q+1)^4,& n&=\sum_{r=1}^{Q}m^r,& N&=n+1.
\end{aligned}
$$
Let \(a_0=0\), and let \(a_s=\ell\) whenever \(Q_{\ell-1}<s\le Q_\ell\). Choose any allowed partition of \(\lbrace0,1\rbrace^m\) into symmetric saturated chains. Use exactly the source-block domain \(D_m\), successor \(F_m(c)=c+e_{i_m(c)}\), and rooted-tree construction for that same partition in accepted facts 3bb775511169ae7a and d63d54549cda4df4.

Explicitly, the spatial coordinate set \(\mathcal T_{m,Q}\) consists of the words over \(\lbrace1,\ldots,m\rbrace\) of lengths \(1,\ldots,Q\), so it has \(n\) coordinates. A Boolean source \(b\in\lbrace0,1\rbrace^{\mathcal T_{m,Q}}\) has the canonical forward path \(u_1,\ldots,u_{h(b)}\): starting at the empty word, at a visited node \(v\) inspect its child block \(c=(b_{vi})_{i=1}^m\); if \(c\in D_m\), select \(vi_m(c)\) as the next node, and otherwise stop. Stop also on reaching length \(Q\). The selected source coordinates are zero. Thus the exact base support is
$$
A_{m,Q}
=\left\lbrace
z_s(b):=b+2\sum_{r=1}^{s}e_{u_r}:
b\in\lbrace0,1\rbrace^{\mathcal T_{m,Q}},\quad 0\le s\le h(b)
\right\rbrace.
$$
The level of \(z_s(b)\) is \(s\), equivalently its number of coordinates equal to two. Retain every original height in every column:
$$
\widehat A
=\left\lbrace
(z,t):z\in A_{m,Q},\quad
a_{\operatorname{level}(z)}\le t\le k,\quad t\in\mathbb Z
\right\rbrace
\subset\mathbb Z^N.
$$
For every \(\gamma\in\mathbb R\), put
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma(z)&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal T_{m,Q}}z_u,\\
w_{(z,t)}&=e^{\gamma\sigma(z)}2^{-t}
\qquad ((z,t)\in\widehat A),\\
W&=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}w_{\mathbf I},
\qquad p_{\mathbf I}=\frac{w_{\mathbf I}}{W}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All other integer weights are zero. Define the actual depth using ambient closed halfspaces in \(\mathbb R^N\):
$$
\tau_p(\mathbf I)
=\inf\left\lbrace
\sum_{\mathbf J\in H\cap\widehat A}p_{\mathbf J}:
H\subseteq\mathbb R^N\text{ is a closed halfspace containing }\mathbf I
\right\rbrace.
$$
Then, for every such \(k\), partition, and common spatial tilt \(\gamma\),
$$
M_3(p):=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}
p_{\mathbf I}\tau_p(\mathbf I)^3
\ge 2^{-30}8^{-N}.
$$

## Proof

The definitions of \(Q,m,n,N,a_s,\mathcal T_{m,Q},A_{m,Q}\), and \(\widehat A\) are exactly the common definitions in accepted facts 3bb775511169ae7a and d63d54549cda4df4, with the same \(k\) and the same chosen chain partition. Their specialization \(\rho_t=2^{-t}\) is the height factor above. Their original weights \(w\), full normalizer \(W\), and normalized masses \(p\) consequently agree with ours atom by atom. The depth in 3bb775511169ae7a is precisely the displayed infimum over containing closed halfspaces in the same ambient space \(\mathbb R^N\). No parameter or measure is rescaled.

Since \(b_8=4\), we have \(Q\ge4\), so \(1/(mQ)>0\). If \(|\gamma|\le1/(mQ)\), accepted fact 3bb775511169ae7a directly gives the stated bound for this \(M_3(p)\). This case includes both boundary values \(\gamma=\pm1/(mQ)\).

If \(|\gamma|>1/(mQ)\), accepted fact d63d54549cda4df4 applies, since its hypothesis is the weak inequality \(|\gamma|\ge1/(mQ)\). It gives, for the same original masses,
$$
\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}p_{\mathbf I}^{\,4}\ge8^{-N}.
$$
Every closed halfspace containing an original atom \(\mathbf I\) includes that atom in its intersection with \(\widehat A\). All masses are nonnegative, so each sum in the infimum defining \(\tau_p(\mathbf I)\) is at least \(p_{\mathbf I}\). Hence \(\tau_p(\mathbf I)\ge p_{\mathbf I}\), and therefore
$$
M_3(p)
\ge\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}p_{\mathbf I}^{\,4}
\ge8^{-N}
\ge2^{-30}8^{-N}.
$$
The two cases exhaust every real \(\gamma\). Both predecessor conclusions hold for every allowed chain partition, so the result has all the stated quantifiers on this complete support family.
