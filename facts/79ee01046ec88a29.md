## Statement

Let $T$ be a triangle of area $V$ with barycentric coordinates $\xi_i$, $1\le i\le3$. Take the two sampled fibers $T$ at integer bases $0,1$ and the six cap thresholds $a_{Li}(z)=1/10+7z/10$ and $a_{Hi}(z)=4/5-7z/10$ in the directions $\xi_i$. The fixed-cap reference is the strict local maximum at the six global budgets $17V/20$ of fact 3ae39f34b6819016. Now allow the triangles and affine thresholds to move, keeping each group's threshold sum fixed at every base. All six multipliers $5/11$ still give joint stationarity, with normalized affine moments $\nu_L/V=(1,2/11)$ and $\nu_H/V=(1,9/11)$. There is a cap-neutral joint velocity with Lagrangian second derivative $3159V/11000>0$, and an actual nearby path preserving convexity and both group cover regions, strictly increasing total volume and strictly decreasing all six cap masses. At $t=1/1000$ the exact normalized volume gain is $503186538043641/3872000000000000000000$.

## Proof

Use fact 3ae39f34b6819016 for the initial budgets, physical stationarity, fixed-cap strict local maximality and actual maximal depth $4V/9$. Each group's affine moment equation from the moving-threshold lemma holds because the two endpoint threshold values are constant in $i$. The two multipliers times section factors divided by $V$ are $9/11$ and $2/11$ for group $L$ and the reverse for $H$, yielding the claimed moments.

Write $c=(1,-1,0)^{\mathsf T}$. Take $B_0=B$ and $B_1=-B$ with
$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-2/5&1/5\\2/5&1&-1/5\\3/5&-3/5&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
(h_{L0},h_{L1},h_{H0},h_{H1})=(-1/10,83/200,-83/200,1/10)c .
$$
The endpoint threshold motions extend affinely to the real base, and every group sum motion is zero. On a straight path $W_z=I+tB_z$ and $a_{giz}(t)=a_{giz}+th_{giz}$, evaluate the exact normalized trace area
$$
f_{giz}(t)=\det W_z(t)\frac{(W_{z,ii}(t)-a_{giz}(t))^2}{\prod_{j\ne i}(W_{z,ii}(t)-W_{z,ij}(t))}.
$$
For body velocity $B$ and one threshold value $a$, the body part of its derivative is $[(9/5)(1-a)^2-2(1-a)]c_i$ and the threshold part is $-2(1-a)h_i$. The body sign reverses at base 1. Thus each global cap derivative, the sum over both bases, is zero. Both $B_z$ have zero trace, so the total volume derivative is zero. Using the determinant and the rational trace formula, the normalized total-volume second derivative is $-108/25$, and each group's vector of global cap second derivatives is
$$
q_g=(-38439/20000,\ -38439/20000,\ -153/125).
$$
For example these coefficients follow by taking the second log derivative of the displayed trace area, $-\operatorname{tr}(B_z^2)-2(B_{z,ii}-h_i)^2/(1-a)^2+\sum_{j\ne i}(B_{z,ii}-B_{z,ij})^2$, then adding the square of the first log derivative and multiplying by $(1-a)^2$. Hence the normalized Lagrangian second derivative is $-108/25-(5/11)\sum_{gi}q_{gi}=3159/11000$.

The global cap derivative map has the following right inverse. For target normalized derivatives $f_{gi}$, let $S_g=\sum_i f_{gi}$ and set
$$
T_0=5(9S_L-2S_H)/77,\qquad T_1=5(-2S_L+9S_H)/77,\qquad
D_z=(T_z/2)(I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/3).
$$
The dilation velocities $D_z$ contribute equally across $i$ within each group. Their group sums are the product of the matrix with diagonal entries $9/5$ and off-diagonal entries $2/5$ with $(T_0,T_1)^{\mathsf T}$, which is $(S_L,S_H)^{\mathsf T}$. Use the endpoint threshold correction $k_{gi}(0)=-(f_{gi}-S_g/3)/(2(1-a_{gi}(0)))$ and $k_{gi}(1)=0$. The correction has zero group sums and restores each individual derivative. This checks all six constraints rather than imposing independent cap derivatives in each fiber.

Use the inverse for $f_{gi}=-1/100-q_{gi}$ and take $W_z(t)=I+tB_z+t^2D_z/2$, $a_{gi}(z,t)=a_{gi}(z)+t h_{gi}(z)+t^2k_{gi}(z)/2$. Then the global cap second derivatives are $-V/100$. Stationarity gives total-volume second derivative $V[3159/11000-(5/11)(6/100)]=2859V/11000$. The chamber is open by the strict signs of the vertex evaluations and positivity of the determinants. The exact analytic formulas now give strict feasibility and improvement for every sufficiently small positive $t$. To check the rational time directly, the inverse gives $T_0=T_1=50379/22000$, $k_L(0)=(-517/4000,-517/4000,517/2000)$ and $k_H(0)=(-4653/8000,-4653/8000,4653/4000)$. At $t=1/1000$ both determinants equal $7744000503186538043641/7744000000000000000000$. Substitution in the edge ratios gives one positive vertex and two negative vertices for each trace, and each pair of trace areas has normalized sum less than $17/20-1/250000000$. The determinant sum minus two gives the stated volume gain.

Each lifted convex hull has exactly the two displayed positive fibers, since the bases are exposed endpoints with no other integer base between them. The group sums remain the original affine functions throughout, so their whole-plane coverage sets are unchanged. This is a strict improvement for the original six individual budgets in the enlarged chart. The budgets remain different from the reference's actual critical depth.
