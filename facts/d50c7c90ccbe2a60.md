## Statement

Retain exactly the recursive symmetric saturated-chain partition, tree, successor, and complete lattice support of accepted facts 032423921efad6c8 and abbc24783c13e0f6. In particular, put
$$
n=\sum_{h=1}^4m^h,\qquad N=n+1,\qquad
\delta=2^{-m}\binom m{m/2},\qquad \eta=1-\delta,
$$
and let
$$
\widehat A=\lbrace(z,t):z\in A_{m,4},\quad
a_{\operatorname{lev}(z)}\le t\le8,\quad t\in\mathbb Z\rbrace.
$$
Here \(A_{m,4}\), its level, and its Boolean source are exactly those of 032423921efad6c8. The letter \(d\) remains reserved for continuous dimension and is not used for a tree parameter.

Reweight this fixed support by
$$
q=\frac45,\qquad w_{(z,t)}=q^t,\qquad
Z=\sum_{\mathbf I\in\widehat A}w_{\mathbf I},\qquad
p_{\mathbf I}=\frac{w_{\mathbf I}}Z.
$$
The height ratio \(q\) is unrelated to auxiliary center laws in the accepted mixed-row and reflection theorems. The support is built with \(\rho_t=2^{-t}\) before reweighting; the new weights are not asserted to satisfy the decay hypothesis of 604889294a17d7df. No support point or height-column point is removed.

At each supported target \(\mathbf J=(j,t)\), take its center and all its vertical reflection pairs \(\lbrace(j,t-u),(j,t+u)\rbrace\) in \(\widehat A\). Optionally add any subset of the disjoint triples from equation (5) of abbc24783c13e0f6 at one valid extension depth \(r\), using that fact's two canonical inverse paths and the target's actual height \(t\). Different targets may choose different depths or no triples. No other pairs or triples are permitted in this selector.

Use \(b^{(\mathrm m)}\), the mixed row geometric masses, and the center convention of 8ab782832d1c7da2 with one factor, its support equal to \(\widehat A\), and its integer dimension equal to \(N\). Put \(B=4^Nb^{(\mathrm m)}\), and use that predecessor's atom activity \(A_0=4^N\sum_{\mathbf I}p_{\mathbf I}^3\). Then every allowed selection satisfies

\tag{1}

The maximum \(B_{\max}\) is attained by choosing the deepest valid complete triple family separately at every height-eight target, and its exact formula is given below. For comparison, let \(b(p)\) be the canonical full reflected energy of 445a9e668ee1a705 on the same original law, with its dimension replaced by \(N\). Then

\tag{2}

These conclusions concern row energies. They assert no upper bound on actual halfspace depth and no impossibility for unrestricted mixed rows.

## Proof

The support and normalization are fixed first. At \(k=8\) the support-building count is \(b_8=\lfloor2^8/(8\cdot8)\rfloor=4\), so the claimed parameters follow from 604889294a17d7df. That accepted fact gives \(\widehat A=\widehat P\cap\mathbb Z^N\) for its full-dimensional compact convex polytope \(\widehat P\). The subsequent reweighting does not change this equality.

Use the new scalar notation
$$
\Sigma_h(\eta)=\sum_{\ell=0}^h\eta^\ell,\qquad
\mathcal D=\frac Z{2^n}.
$$
Thus \(\Sigma_h\) is the geometric level sum, not the weighted reflected sum \(S_{\mathbf J}\) in 604889294a17d7df. The scalar \(\mathcal D\) is the denominator called D in the proposed computation, not the Boolean successor domain \(D_m\) or the incidence parameter D of 445a9e668ee1a705.

By 032423921efad6c8, tree level \(s\) has exactly \(2^n\eta^s\) points. Consequently the exact lifted census is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\lbrace(z,t)\in\widehat A:\operatorname{lev}(z)=0\rbrace|
&=2^n &&(0\le t\le7),\\
|\lbrace(z,8)\in\widehat A:\operatorname{lev}(z)=s\rbrace|
&=2^n\eta^s &&(0\le s\le4),\\
|\widehat A|&=2^n(8+\Sigma_4(\eta)),\\
\mathcal D&=\sum_{t=0}^7q^t+q^8\Sigma_4(\eta),\qquad
p_{(z,t)}=\frac{q^t}{2^n\mathcal D}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
$$
Every following sum uses these original probabilities and this single Z.

We next count the valid triple depths exactly. Let \(\alpha_2\) be the fraction of Boolean \(m\)-blocks with at least two successive predecessors in the chosen symmetric saturated-chain partition. This is a new probability, not the minimum-endpoint-weight function \(\alpha(v)\) in 604889294a17d7df. Write \(\mathfrak c_m=\binom m{m/2}\) for the chain count. The number of singleton chains is
\(\mathfrak c_m-\binom m{m/2-1}\): at middle rank, exactly this many chains start, while every chain that starts at a lower rank contributes one element at the preceding rank. A nonsingleton chain has exactly two points lacking two predecessors, and a singleton chain has one. Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_2
&=1-2\delta+2^{-m}\left(\binom m{m/2}-\binom m{m/2-1}\right)\\
&=1-2\delta+\frac{2\delta}{m+2}
=1-\frac{1251}{626}\delta.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
$$

Fix a tree level \(s\) and choose \(j\) uniformly among its \(2^n\eta^s\) points. Conditional on its original prefix blocks and path, every descendant block below its last prefix node is an independent fair Boolean block. Indeed, reduction modulo two recovers the Boolean source; fixing its first \(s\) forward blocks fixes exactly those disjoint child blocks, leaving every other Boolean coordinate free. This is also the fresh-block argument in abbc24783c13e0f6. The original law conditional on any fixed level and allowed height is uniform, so the same assertion holds under (3).

For \(s<4\), let \(R(j)\in\lbrace0,\ldots,4-s\rbrace\) be the greatest valid extension depth for the canonical triple construction, with \(R=0\) when the initial block lacks two predecessors. This integer is distinct from the row sets in the accepted reflection theorem. The initial two predecessors select the two distinct child subtrees. Validity to depth \(r\) then requires exactly \(2(r-1)\) further inverse-successor tests in fresh, pairwise disjoint blocks. Each test succeeds with probability \(\eta\), by the image size of \(F_m\) in 032423921efad6c8. Adaptive child choices reveal no previously tested block. Therefore
$$
\mathbb P\lbrace R\ge r\mid\operatorname{lev}(j)=s\rbrace
=\alpha_2\eta^{2r-2}\qquad(1\le r\le4-s).
\tag{5}
$$
This is an equality, not a union bound. The valid depths are nested because truncating both inverse paths preserves all their tests. More explicitly, the probabilities for \(R\) are \(1-\alpha_2\) at zero, \(\alpha_2\eta^{2r-2}(1-\eta^2)\) at \(1\le r<4-s\), and \(\alpha_2\eta^{2(4-s)-2}\) at \(4-s\). At level four \(R=0\) surely. Multiplying these probabilities by \(2^n\eta^s\) gives the exact target census.

At heights zero through seven only Boolean tree bases occur, and no extension-depth triple can be lifted there, since two of its sources have positive tree level and entrance height eight. The complete vertical row sizes at these heights are
$$
(v_0,\ldots,v_7)=(1,3,5,7,9,7,5,3).
\tag{6}
$$
At height eight the vertical row is its center alone, at every tree level. The triple family at valid depth r has \(2^{r-1}\) disjoint triples, all at height eight, by equation (5) of abbc24783c13e0f6. None contains the center. These triples are disjoint from the vertical center. Thus the greatest allowed row size at a height-eight target is
$$
\mu_R=\begin{cases}1,&R=0,\\1+3\cdot2^{R-1},&R\ge1.\end{cases}
$$
The four successive squared-size increments are
\((\mu_1^2-\mu_0^2,\ldots,\mu_4^2-\mu_3^2)=(15,33,120,456)\).
Call these increments \(\Delta_1,\ldots,\Delta_4\). Since \(\mu_R\) strictly increases, no smaller valid depth or subset of a family increases row size. Rows at distinct centers are independently selectable under the convention of 8ab782832d1c7da2. This proves attainment of the pointwise and global maxima. Using (5), their exact conditional squared-size averages are
$$
\mathbb E[\mu_R^2\mid\operatorname{lev}(j)=s]
=1+\alpha_2\sum_{r=1}^{4-s}\Delta_r\eta^{2r-2}.
$$
After summing over tree levels, define the exact height-eight aggregate
$$
\mathcal F=\Sigma_4(\eta)
+\alpha_2\sum_{r=1}^4\Delta_r\eta^{2r-2}\Sigma_{4-r}(\eta).
\tag{7}
$$
This \(\mathcal F\) is the quantity called F8 in the proposed computation and has no relation to the Boolean successor \(F_m\).

For the affine weights \(q^t\), every mixed cycle has geometric probability equal to its center probability. Equation (9) of 8ab782832d1c7da2 therefore gives exactly
\(b^{(\mathrm m)}=\sum_{\mathbf J}|R^{(\mathrm m)}_{\mathbf J}|^2p_{\mathbf J}^3\), with its unique-center, disjoint-cycle convention and unchanged original normalization. Put

In particular, the factor 4 comes from \(4^{n+1}2^n/(2^n)^3\); no selected-row normalizer appears.

We finish with the exact full-reflection calibration. Retain the inverse reconstruction length \(h_-(j)\) of 032423921efad6c8, without changing its meaning. Conditional on level \(s\), its single inverse path tests fresh Boolean blocks, so
$$

For a Boolean target at height t<4 there are no spatial reflection pairs: a higher-level source has height eight, so its complementary height \(2t-8\) would be negative. Involutive reflection partitions all these points into disjoint pairs, so choosing this full reflected alternative is an admissible pair-only mixed row.
