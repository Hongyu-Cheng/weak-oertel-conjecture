## Statement

Let \(d\ge1\) and let convex bodies \(K_j\subset\mathbb R^d\) converge in Hausdorff distance to a convex body \(K\). If \(y_j\in K_j\) converges to \(y\in K\), then \(E_{K_j}(y_j)\to E_K(y)\), where
$$
E_A(v)=\inf_{a:\,1+a\cdot(x-v)>0\ \forall x\in A}
\int_A [1+a\cdot(x-v)]^{-d-1}\,dx.
$$
This same infimum is zero at each boundary mark. In a common Euclidean ball, if \(u\) is a unit normal and \(\delta=h_A(u)-u\cdot v>0\), then \(E_A(v)\le 2^{d+1}L\delta/d\), where \(L\) bounds the \((d-1)\)-volumes of sections perpendicular to \(u\). For \(d=1\) the section bound can be taken as one.

## Proof

If \(\delta>0\), the affine denominator
$$
1-\frac{u\cdot(x-v)}{2\delta}
=\frac12+\frac{h_A(u)-u\cdot x}{2\delta}
$$
is positive on \(A\) and has value one at \(v\). Integrating along \(t=h_A(u)-u\cdot x\ge0\) bounds its objective by
$$
L\int_0^\infty\left(\frac12+\frac{t}{2\delta}\right)^{-d-1}dt
=\frac{2^{d+1}}{d}L\delta.
$$
If \(v\in\partial A\), take an outward unit supporting direction at \(v\), with \(\delta=0\). The admissible denominator \(1+s(h_A(u)-u\cdot x)\) has its integrand bounded by one and tending to zero off the supporting plane as \(s\to\infty\). The plane has zero \(d\)-volume, so dominated convergence gives infimum zero.

When \(y\in\partial K\), fix a unit supporting direction there. Its support gaps at \(y_j\) tend to zero by Hausdorff convergence. The bodies lie in a common ball, so the section bound is uniform. The preceding estimates, including zero gaps, give \(E_{K_j}(y_j)\to0\).

When \(y\in\operatorname{int}K\), write \(A=K-y\), \(A_j=K_j-y_j\), and let \(\eta_j=d_H(A_j,A)\). Take \(r>0\) with \(rB_2^d\subseteq A\), where \(B_2^d\) is the unit Euclidean ball. Since \(h_A(u)\ge r\) on the unit sphere and \(\lvert h_{A_j}(u)-h_A(u)\rvert\le\eta_j\), for sufficiently large \(j\) the support-function criterion gives
$$
(1-\eta_j/r)A\subseteq A_j\subseteq (1+\eta_j/r)A.
$$
All three bodies now have \(0\) in their interiors. Affine scaling and inclusion monotonicity from [d90ba05577427854](d90ba05577427854.md) squeeze \(E_{A_j}(0)\) between \((1-\eta_j/r)^dE_A(0)\) and \((1+\eta_j/r)^dE_A(0)\). Translation covariance gives the claim.
