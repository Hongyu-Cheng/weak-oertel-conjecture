## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $\Gamma=(d/(d+1))^d$, and $\omega_{d-1}$ be the volume of the Euclidean unit ball in $\mathbb R^{d-1}$. Let $A,K,B$ be the only full-dimensional integer fibers at $-1,0,1$, with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$ and ordinary $d$-volumes $a,k,b$. Suppose $b\le a+k$ and $y\in\operatorname{int}K$. Suppose two actual ambient halfspaces through $(0,y)$ attain its raw mixed depth $Q$, have continuous unit normals $u,v$, and capture the supporting faces of $B$ at $h_B(u),h_B(v)$ with zero $d$-volume. If $r\mathbb B_d\subseteq B$ for $r>0$, set $L=d2^dk/(\omega_{d-1}r^{d-1})$ and $\delta=\lVert u+v\rVert$. The two conditions $\delta L\le2r$ and $\omega_{d-1}\delta L^d<(1-2\Gamma)b$ imply $Q>\Gamma(a+k+b)/2$. More precisely, the two actual traces cover $A$, and their union misses at most $\omega_{d-1}\delta L^d$ in $K$.

## Proof

For any $p\in A$, compatibility puts the radius-$r/2$ ball centered at $p/2$ inside $K$. For $x\in K$, the perpendicular $(d-1)$-ball of radius $r/2$ at $p/2$ and the apex $x$ form a cone inside $K$ with volume $\omega_{d-1}(r/2)^{d-1}\lVert x-p/2\rVert/d$ (zero if $x=p/2$). Thus $\lVert x-p/2\rVert\le L/2$. Applying this to $x$ and $y$ with the same $p$ gives $K-y\subseteq L\mathbb B_d$; using $y$ and varying $p$ gives $2y-A\subseteq L\mathbb B_d$.

The $A$ thresholds are $2u\cdot y-h_B(u)$ and $2v\cdot y-h_B(v)$ because the thresholds are affine in the integer base. If $x\in A$ misses both closed caps, then $(u+v)\cdot(2y-x)>h_B(u)+h_B(v)\ge2r$, contrary to $\delta L\le2r$. For a point $x\in K$ missing both, set $z=x-y$. The inequalities $u\cdot z<0$ and $v\cdot z<0$ imply $-\delta L\le (u+v)\cdot z<u\cdot z<0$. If $\delta=0$ the missed set is empty; otherwise it lies in a slab of width $\delta L$ in the radius-$L$ ball. Fubini bounds the missed volume by $E=\omega_{d-1}\delta L^d$.

The two actual masses therefore satisfy $2Q\ge a+k-E$. If $Q\le\Gamma(a+k+b)/2$, the nonheavy condition gives $Q\le\Gamma(a+k)$. Since $\Gamma<1/2$ for $d\ge2$ by $(1+1/d)^d>2$, we obtain $E\ge(1-2\Gamma)(a+k)\ge(1-2\Gamma)b$, a contradiction.
