## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $m=d+1$ and $\gamma=(d/m)^d$. Select $N$ positive-volume simplex fibers, of volumes $V_z$, in a finite actual convex fiber family. Assume an open neighborhood in the space of all their labeled vertex positions is admissible with the same integer support and the other fibers fixed. Fix a finite menu of ambient closed caps with feasible upper mass budgets; call a cap active at equality. Partition the active caps into $h$ groups of $m$. On each selected simplex each group restricts either to the full canonical centroid tuple $\{\xi_i\ge1/m\}_{i=1}^m$ in its physical barycentric chart, up to permutation, or to strictly full and strictly empty traces only. A cap is affected if it has a canonical or full trace on some selected fiber.

Write $M$ for total mixed volume and $C_a$ for cap masses. In each barycentric vertex chart use the $d$-dimensional velocity space given by $\sum_i c_i=0$, $B_{ii}=-c_i$ and $B_{ij}=(mc_j+c_i)/(dm-1)$ for $i\ne j$. On the sum $E$ of these spaces the map $L:B\mapsto(DC_a[B])_{a\ {\rm active}}$ has rank at most $dh$. Let $U$ be the set of blocks identically zero on $\ker L$. Then $|U|\le h$, and some $B\in\ker L$ is nonzero on every block outside $U$.

For any normal first stress $\lambda_a\ge0$ on affected active caps with $DM=\sum_a\lambda_a DC_a$ on the full physical tangent space, let $F_z$ be the sum of full-trace multipliers on fiber $z$ and $\alpha_{zi}$ the sum of the multipliers for canonical trace $i$ there. Then all $\alpha_{zi}$ equal $\alpha_z$, and $w_z=1-F_z=m\gamma\alpha_z\ge0$. On $E$ the Lagrangian Hessian is
$$
D^2(M-\textstyle\sum_a\lambda_a C_a)[B,B]=
\sum_z w_z V_z\frac{d^2-1}{d(dm-1)}\lVert c_z\rVert^2 .
$$
At a local fixed-budget volume maximum there exists such a stress with $\{z:w_z>0\}\subseteq U$, hence at most $h$ positive proper-trace blocks. If every normal first stress has more than $h$ such blocks, there is an actual degree-two vertex path increasing total volume and strictly lowering every affected active cap mass for all sufficiently small positive time. In particular this holds if $N>h$ and no selected fiber has a full active trace. Other budgets stay feasible.

## Proof

The open admissibility assumption makes every column-sum-zero matrix a physical direction at each reference vertex matrix $I$. Strict full and empty traces stay of the same type, and the fixed canonical cut formula in e42e4ad8eaa4f705 is smooth near $I$. This fact gives canonical cap derivative $\gamma V_z(\operatorname{tr}B+\operatorname{row}(B)_i)$ and Hessian of volume minus $(m\gamma)^{-1}$ times the sum of the canonical caps. For the displayed lift, the off-diagonal column sums are $c$, their row sums are $-c/(dm-1)$, and the full rows sum to $-dm c/(dm-1)$ componentwise. Its trace is zero and the normalized canonical Hessian is $(d^2-1)\lVert c\rVert^2/[d(dm-1)]$ by the same fact. Thus all first volume derivatives vanish on $E$ and each complete canonical block contributes zero to its group's derivative sum. Full or empty traces contribute zero individually. The group sums are $h$ independent row relations in the codomain of $L$, so $\operatorname{rank}L\le hm-h=dh$ and $\dim\ker L\ge d(N-h)$. The $d|U|$ coordinate covectors of blocks in $U$ are independent and annihilate $\ker L$, so $d|U|\le\operatorname{rank}L$. The kernel of each remaining block projection is a proper linear subspace of $\ker L$; a finite union of such subspaces cannot exhaust it. When no blocks remain, take the zero vector.

Translations of one simplex have arbitrary zero-sum row vectors in its physical tangent chart and have zero volume derivative, so first stationarity forces all $\alpha_{zi}$ equal. Centroid dilation gives $1=F_z+m\gamma\alpha_z$ because each canonical trace scales with the simplex and has fraction $\gamma$. Each full trace equals that simplex's volume locally and each empty trace contributes zero. Consequently the Lagrangian on that block, up to its constant contribution, is precisely $w_z$ times the canonical Lagrangian. Summing its Hessian yields the displayed formula.

Take any $B\in\ker L$ and let $q_0,q_a$ be the straight-path second derivatives of $M,C_a$ along $B$, with first covectors $g_0,g_a$. The affine image of the acceleration variable $D$ under $(q_0+g_0(D),(-q_a-g_a(D))_a)$ either meets the open positive orthant or admits a nonzero separating vector $(\theta,(\mu_a)_a)\ge0$. In the second case it annihilates the linear part and gives $\theta q_0-\sum_a\mu_a q_a\le0$. Simultaneously contract all selected simplices about their centroids; this admissible direction strictly decreases each affected cap because its full and proper positive-volume traces scale and its other traces are empty. Evaluating the annihilation identity in this direction excludes $\theta=0$. Therefore $\lambda=\mu/\theta$ is a normal stress with nonpositive Lagrangian Hessian on $B$. The same alternative with no affected caps holds since the first objective derivative is nonzero.

In the first case the path $I+tB+\frac12t^2D$ in each physical chart has vanishing first derivatives and the specified strict second signs. For sufficiently small $t>0$ it stays admissible; unaffected active caps are constant and inactive caps remain feasible by strict slack. Thus at a local maximum the separating case must hold for the kernel vector nonzero outside $U$. The Hessian formula then forces $w_z=0$ on every such block. If every stress has more than $h$ positive blocks the separating case is impossible, and if there are no full active traces, each $w_z=1$.
