## Statement

Let $A=[-L,L]\times[-e,e]$, $C=[-e,e]\times[-L,L]$, $0<e\le L$, and $V=4Le$. The convex hull of their lifts at $-1,1$ has actual middle fiber $B_0=[-(L+e)/2,(L+e)/2]^2$. Counting all three integer fibers with their original areas, its exact global raw closed-halfspace depth maximum is $(L+e)^2/2+(1+e/L)V/2$, attained at $(0,0,0)$. The ratio of middle area to either endpoint area is $(1+e/L)^2/(4e/L)$ and diverges as $e/L$ decreases to zero.

## Proof

The hull section at zero is exactly $(A+C)/2$ because the endpoint weights at zero must both be $1/2$. This is the stated square of area $(L+e)^2$. A nonvertical through-origin cap takes exactly half the square and at least $(V+4e^2)/2$ from the endpoints by the depth at the real midpoint in pk-w35-reflected-crossed-endpoint-exact. A pure base cap includes the entire square on its boundary and one full endpoint. Its mass $(L+e)^2+V$ is at least the asserted lower bound. Translating any containing cap to the origin proves the bound for all caps there.

The closed caps $x_2\ge-ez$ and $x_2\le ez$ cover the entire middle plane, and each takes half the square and exactly the endpoint budget $(1+e/L)V/2$ by the calculation in pk-w35-reflected-crossed-endpoint-exact under $z_{\rm new}=2z_{\rm old}-1$. The maximum on the middle plane thus equals the claimed value. Bases $-1,1$ are strictly exposed even with base zero present, so by 2acc8bc803acf1c0 their actual mixed maxima equal their ordinary symmetric maxima $V/2$. They are below the middle value, proving the global formula. Division of the area of the square by $V$ gives the stated ratio.
