## Statement

Retain the canonical hard-branch objects and notation of accepted facts `036a276c54ec5e66`, `2fe46f47d19112ea`, and `de023bd405d38571`. Thus \(Q>0\), and the rescaled internal atlas is a finite whole-label family \(\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\) satisfying
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma
 >\frac7{400}Q>0.
\tag{0}
\]
Each label has the canonical representation
\[
 \sigma=(z;e,f),
 \qquad
 e=\{z,a(\sigma)\},
 \qquad
 f=\{z,b(\sigma)\},
 \qquad
 a(\sigma)\ne b(\sigma),
\tag{1}
\]
Write \(e(\sigma):=e\) and \(f(\sigma):=f\). The label has
an original atlas weight \(\theta_\sigma>0\), and a rescaled weight
\[
 0<\eta_\sigma\leq\theta_\sigma.
\tag{2}
\]
The two rescaled occurrence contacts use the exact upstream aliases
\[
 x_{\sigma,a(\sigma)}:=x_{e,a(\sigma)},
 \qquad
 x_{\sigma,b(\sigma)}:=x_{f,b(\sigma)}.
\tag{3}
\]
For every upstream loaded edge \(j=\{u,v\}\), retain its canonical load \(\omega_j\), midpoint base, and midpoint support contact
\[
 m_j:=\frac{u+v}{2},
 \qquad
 \ell_j:=\frac{x_{j,u}+x_{j,v}}2.
\tag{4}
\]
The transverse-atlas capacity and the rescaling obey
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
                   j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma
 \leq
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_\perp\\
                   j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\}}}
 \theta_\sigma
 =\kappa_j
 \leq\omega_j.
\tag{5}
\]

For an integer base \(y\) with positive fiber volume, write
\[
 C_y:=\{x:(y,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_y:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_y),
 \qquad
 c_y:=\frac1{a_y}\int_{C_y}x\,dx.
\tag{6}
\]
For a nonempty whole-label subfamily \(\Gamma\subseteq\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\), put
\[
 S_\Gamma
 :=\bigcup_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}.
\tag{7}
\]
For \(y\in S_\Gamma\), retain the recomputed quantities of accepted fact `de023bd405d38571`:
\[
 F_y^\Gamma
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \widetilde g_{\sigma,y},
 \qquad
 B_y^\Gamma:=\frac12-F_y^\Gamma,
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 b_y^\Gamma:=\int_{C_y}B_y^\Gamma
 =\frac{a_y}{2}
 -\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma>0,
 \qquad
 \bar q_y^\Gamma
 :=\frac1{b_y^\Gamma}\int_{C_y}xB_y^\Gamma(x)\,dx.
\tag{9}
\]

Call a nonempty whole-label subfamily \(\Gamma\) chartable when there are an affine map
\[
 s_\Gamma:\operatorname{aff}(S_\Gamma)\longrightarrow\mathbb R^d
\tag{10}
\]
and a linear subspace \(W_\Gamma\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) with \(\dim W_\Gamma\leq3\) such that
\[
 x_{\sigma,y}-s_\Gamma(y)\in W_\Gamma
\tag{11}
\]
for every retained occurrence and
\[
 \bar q_y^\Gamma-s_\Gamma(y)\in W_\Gamma
 \qquad(y\in S_\Gamma).
\tag{12}
\]
Declare the empty family chartable as well.

Then the following assertions hold.

First, fix any nonempty \(\Gamma\), affine map \(s:\operatorname{aff}(S_\Gamma)\to\mathbb R^d\), and linear subspace \(W\). If all retained occurrence residuals belong to \(W\), then, source by source,
\[
 \boxed{
 \bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\in W
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 c_y-s(y)\in W
 }
 \qquad(y\in S_\Gamma).
\tag{13}
\]
Consequently chartability is hereditary: every whole-label subfamily of a chartable family is chartable, with complements recomputed after deletion.

Second, there exists a nonempty maximum-weight chartable family \(\Gamma_0\), where weight means
\[
 H_\Gamma:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{14}
\]
Fix a witnessing chart \((s_0,W)\) of \(\Gamma_0\), and fix any affine extension
\[
 s:\mathbb R^n\longrightarrow\mathbb R^d
 \quad\text{with}\quad
 s|_{\operatorname{aff}(S_{\Gamma_0})}=s_0.
\tag{15}
\]
Partition the discarded labels into the disjoint families
\[
 \mathscr D_c
 :=\left\{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\Gamma_0:
 c_y-s(y)\notin W
 \text{ for at least one }y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}
 \right\},
\tag{16}
\]
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathscr D_o
 :=\bigl\{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\Gamma_0:
 &\ c_{a(\sigma)}-s(a(\sigma))\in W,\\
 &\ c_{b(\sigma)}-s(b(\sigma))\in W,\\
 &\ x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\notin W
 \text{ for at least one }y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}
 \bigr\}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]
These families exhaust all discarded labels. Hence, with
\[
 M_{\mathrm{disc}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\Gamma_0}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 M_c:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 M_o:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_o}\eta_\sigma,
\tag{18}
\]
one has
\[
 M_{\mathrm{disc}}=M_c+M_o.
\tag{19}
\]
In particular,
\[
 M_{\mathrm{disc}}>\frac Q{1000}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \left(
 M_c>\frac Q{2000}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 M_o>\frac Q{2000}
 \right).
\tag{20}
\]

Third, for each \(\sigma=(z;e,f)\in\mathscr D_o\), choose exactly one bad occurrence. If the chosen endpoint is \(a(\sigma)\), set \(j_\sigma:=e\) and \(y_\sigma:=a(\sigma)\); if it is \(b(\sigma)\), set \(j_\sigma:=f\) and \(y_\sigma:=b(\sigma)\). Thus \(j_\sigma=\{z,y_\sigma\}\). The exact affine residual identity is
\[
 \boxed{
 x_{\sigma,y_\sigma}-s(y_\sigma)
 =2\bigl(\ell_{j_\sigma}-s(m_{j_\sigma})\bigr)
 -\bigl(x_{j_\sigma,z}-s(z)\bigr).
 }
\tag{21}
\]
Assign \(\sigma\) to \(\mathscr D_{\mathrm{mid}}\) when
\[
 \ell_{j_\sigma}-s(m_{j_\sigma})\notin W,
\tag{22}
\]
and otherwise assign it to \(\mathscr D_{\mathrm{ctr}}\). Then the two families partition \(\mathscr D_o\), every label in \(\mathscr D_{\mathrm{ctr}}\) satisfies
\[
 x_{j_\sigma,z}-s(z)\notin W,
\tag{23}
\]
and the edge-owned charges
\[
 q_j^{\mathrm{mid}}
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr D_{\mathrm{mid}}\\j_\sigma=j}}
 \eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 q_j^{\mathrm{ctr}}
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr D_{\mathrm{ctr}}\\j_\sigma=j}}
 \eta_\sigma
\tag{24}
\]
obey the nonduplication bound
\[
 \boxed{
 q_j^{\mathrm{mid}}+q_j^{\mathrm{ctr}}\leq\omega_j
 }
\tag{25}
\]
for every upstream edge \(j\). Moreover,
\[
 \sum_jq_j^{\mathrm{mid}}
 +\sum_jq_j^{\mathrm{ctr}}
 =M_o.
\tag{26}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 M_o>\frac Q{2000}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \left(
 \sum_jq_j^{\mathrm{mid}}>\frac Q{4000}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \sum_jq_j^{\mathrm{ctr}}>\frac Q{4000}
 \right).
\tag{27}
\]

Combining (20) and (27), discarded mass greater than \(Q/1000\) produces at least one of the three exact alternatives
\[
 \boxed{M_c>\frac Q{2000},}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\sum_jq_j^{\mathrm{mid}}>\frac Q{4000},}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\sum_jq_j^{\mathrm{ctr}}>\frac Q{4000}.}
\tag{28}
\]
No conclusion is asserted here that any quantity in (28) yields a selector, dominated-measure, complete-core, affine-retention, or packet capacity of size \(Q/100\).

## Proof

Fix a nonempty whole-label family \(\Gamma\), an affine map \(s\) on \(\operatorname{aff}(S_\Gamma)\), and a linear subspace \(W\) containing every retained occurrence residual. Every density \(\widetilde g_{\sigma,y}\) has mass \(\eta_\sigma\) and barycenter \(x_{\sigma,y}\). Equations (8)-(9), together with the definition of the fiber centroid in (6), therefore give the exact first-moment identity
\[
 \frac{a_y}{2}c_y
 =\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}
 +b_y^\Gamma\bar q_y^\Gamma.
\tag{29}
\]
The scalar coefficients on the right sum to \(a_y/2\) by (9). Subtracting \((a_y/2)s(y)\) from (29) yields
\[
 \frac{a_y}{2}\bigl(c_y-s(y)\bigr)
 =\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma\bigl(x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\bigr)
 +b_y^\Gamma\bigl(\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\bigr).
\tag{30}
\]
The sum over occurrences belongs to \(W\), while both \(a_y/2\) and \(b_y^\Gamma\) are positive. Since \(W\) is linear, (30) proves both implications in (13).

Now suppose \(\Gamma\) is chartable and \(\Gamma'\subseteq\Gamma\) is nonempty. Use the same witnessing affine map, restricted to \(\operatorname{aff}(S_{\Gamma'})\), and the same residual space. The retained occurrence residuals remain in that space. Equation (13) applied first to \(\Gamma\) puts every fixed centroid residual at a source of \(\Gamma\) in that space. In particular this holds on \(S_{\Gamma'}\). Applying the converse direction of (13) to the newly recomputed complements for \(\Gamma'\) proves that \(\Gamma'\) is chartable. The empty subfamily is chartable by convention, so chartability is hereditary.

We next verify that a nonempty maximum-weight chartable family exists. Fix any label \(\sigma\), whose opposite endpoints are distinct. There is an affine map \(s_\sigma\) on the line through those endpoints satisfying
\[
 s_\sigma(a(\sigma))=x_{\sigma,a(\sigma)},
 \qquad
 s_\sigma(b(\sigma))=x_{\sigma,b(\sigma)}.
\tag{31}
\]
Put
\[
 W_\sigma
 :=\operatorname{span}\left\{
 c_{a(\sigma)}-x_{\sigma,a(\sigma)},
 c_{b(\sigma)}-x_{\sigma,b(\sigma)}
 \right\}.
\tag{32}
\]
Then \(\dim W_\sigma\leq2\), both occurrence residuals vanish, and both centroid residuals belong to \(W_\sigma\). Equation (13) puts both recomputed singleton-complement residuals in \(W_\sigma\). Hence every singleton family is chartable. The internal atlas is finite, so the finite set of chartable subfamilies contains a nonempty member and has a maximizer of the positive weight (14). This proves the existence assertion for \(\Gamma_0\). A standard affine-basis extension gives (15).

Chartability of \(\Gamma_0\) and the forward implication in (13) give
\[
 c_y-s(y)\in W
 \qquad(y\in S_{\Gamma_0}).
\tag{33}
\]
Consider a discarded label \(\sigma\notin\Gamma_0\). If it does not belong to \(\mathscr D_c\), both of its centroid residuals belong to \(W\). If it belongs to neither \(\mathscr D_c\) nor \(\mathscr D_o\), both of its occurrence residuals also belong to \(W\). For the augmented family \(\Gamma_0\cup\{\sigma\}\), all old occurrence residuals remain in \(W\), the two new ones belong to \(W\), and (33) together with the failure of the centroid defect puts every centroid residual at every augmented source in \(W\). The converse implication in (13), now applied to the complements recomputed for \(\Gamma_0\cup\{\sigma\}\), places every augmented complement residual in \(W\). Thus the restriction of the affine extension \(s\) and the same \(W\) chart the augmented family. Its weight is \(H_{\Gamma_0}+\eta_\sigma>H_{\Gamma_0}\), contradicting maximality. Hence (16)-(17) exhaust the discarded labels. They are disjoint by definition, which proves (19). If both inequalities on the right of (20) failed, (19) would give \(M_{\mathrm{disc}}\leq Q/1000\). This proves (20).

It remains to establish the exact occurrence ownership. Fix \(\sigma=(z;e,f)\in\mathscr D_o\) and its one chosen bad occurrence. By (3), if \(y_\sigma=a(\sigma)\) then \(x_{\sigma,y_\sigma}=x_{e,a(\sigma)}\) and \(j_\sigma=e\); the analogous assertion holds with \(f\) at \(b(\sigma)\). In both cases (4) gives
\[
 2\ell_{j_\sigma}=x_{j_\sigma,z}+x_{j_\sigma,y_\sigma},
 \qquad
 2m_{j_\sigma}=z+y_\sigma.
\tag{34}
\]
Since the extension \(s\) is affine on all of \(\mathbb R^n\),
\[
 2s(m_{j_\sigma})=s(z)+s(y_\sigma).
\tag{35}
\]
Subtracting (35) from (34) and rearranging proves (21).

The left side of (21) is outside \(W\) by the choice of \(y_\sigma\). If its midpoint residual belongs to \(W\), then (21) and linearity of \(W\) force the common-center contact residual to lie outside \(W\). Thus the priority rule (22) partitions \(\mathscr D_o\) and proves (23). Each label is assigned to exactly one of these two types and to exactly one of its two upstream edges. This proves (26).

Fix an upstream edge \(j\). The labels charged to \(j\) form a subfamily of the internal transverse-atlas labels whose defining pair contains \(j\). Hence (2) and the transverse-pair capacity (5) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 q_j^{\mathrm{mid}}+q_j^{\mathrm{ctr}}
 &=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr D_o\\j_\sigma=j}}\eta_\sigma\\
 &\leq
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
                   j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma\\
 &\leq\kappa_j
 \leq\omega_j.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{36}
\]
This is (25), including the combined charge of both witness types; no label weight or edge load has been counted twice. Finally, if both alternatives on the right of (27) failed, (26) would give \(M_o\leq Q/2000\). Thus (27) holds. Combining it with (20) proves the trichotomy (28) and completes the proof.
