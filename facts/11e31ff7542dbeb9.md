## Statement

Let a proper affine cut of a $d$-simplex have vertex heights $p>q>0$, negative heights $-r_j$ for $j=1,\ldots,m$, where $m\ge1$ and $r_j>0$, and any remaining heights zero. Let $F$ be the closed nonnegative volume fraction and let $b_i$ be the barycentric coordinates of the uniform section centroid. Put $h=1-q/p$, $t=1-h$, and $a_j=r_j/(q+r_j)$. For the sum $N$ of independent Bernoulli variables of probabilities $a_j$, put $M(z)=\mathbb E z^N$, $p_0=M(0)$, and $\mu=\mathbb EN$. Let $\rho=M(t)$ and $L=h\rho/(1-\rho)$. The comparison $d^d\prod_{i\ne p} b_i\ge F(1-F)$ is equivalent to
$$
(1-L\mu)M(1+L)\ge (1-t\rho)
  \left[1-\frac{p_0(1-t\rho)}{h\rho}\right].
$$
For independent unit-rate exponentials $E_j$, set $T_E=\sum_j a_jE_j$. For real $z$ where the expectation is finite, $1/M(z)=\mathbb E e^{-(z-1)T_E}$. In particular, $\rho=1/\mathbb E e^{hT_E}$, $p_0=1/\mathbb E e^{T_E}$, $\mu=\mathbb ET_E$, $L=h/(\mathbb E e^{hT_E}-1)$, and $M(1+L)=1/\mathbb E e^{-LT_E}$.

## Proof

Independence of the Bernoulli variables gives $M(z)=\prod_j(1-a_j+a_jz)$, $p_0=\prod_j q/(q+r_j)$ and $\mu=\sum_j a_j$. Apply 7cae55c49007d765 to the given proper cut with $x_j=ha_j$. The exact section identities have $\prod_j(1-x_j)=M(t)=\rho$, $X=\prod_j p/(p+r_j)=p_0/\rho$, and $E=\rho^{-1}-1=h/L$. They give $F=(X/h)(1-t\rho)$ and
$$
d^d\prod_{i\ne p} b_i
  =\frac Xh(1-L\mu)\prod_j(1+La_j)
  =\frac Xh(1-L\mu)M(1+L).
$$
Every zero coefficient contributes the factor $db_i=1$, so these formulas use the original dimension $d$. Divide by $X/h>0$ and use $X=p_0/\rho$ in $1-F$ to obtain the equivalence.

Integrating each exponential density shows $\mathbb E e^{-(z-1)a_jE_j}=1/(1+a_j(z-1))$ when $1+a_j(z-1)>0$, the exact finiteness condition for that factor. Multiplication gives the transform, and $\mathbb ET_E=\sum_j a_j$. Here $0<a_j<1$, $0<h<1$, and $L>0$, so the transform is finite at $z=t,0,1+L$. Substitution proves the claimed identities.
