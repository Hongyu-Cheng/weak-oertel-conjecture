## Statement

Retain one common canonical small-defect intact instance of accepted facts 592d2827cca44396 and 624b3258dee4faea.  Thus \(P>Q/24\),
\[
 E_{\mathrm{gg}}>\frac{85}{194}P,
\tag{1}
\]
and the cross-contact atoms of 624b3258dee4faea have weights and points
\[
 b_{i,k}^{(r)}\geq0,
 \qquad
 (m_k,p_{i\mid k}^{(r)})
 \in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d).
\tag{2}
\]
Here all endpoint-slot multiplicities, including both slots of a loop, are already included in the accepted weights.  Their total weight and their packet-target normal balance are
\[
 \sum_{r,i,k}b_{i,k}^{(r)}=2E_{\mathrm{gg}},
 \qquad
 \sum_i b_{i,k}^{(r)}a_i^\circ=0
 \quad\text{for every }(r,k).
\tag{3}
\]

For every distinct physical target base \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\), define the nonconflicting aliases
\[
 t_m^\times
 :=\sum_{\substack{r,i,k\\m_k=m}}b_{i,k}^{(r)}.
\tag{4}
\]
When \(t_m^\times>0\), define
\[
 q_m^\times
 :=\frac1{t_m^\times}
 \sum_{\substack{r,i,k\\m_k=m}}
 b_{i,k}^{(r)}p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}.
\tag{5}
\]
No point \(q_m^\times\) is defined or used when \(t_m^\times=0\).  Then
\[
 \boxed{q_m^\times\in\mathsf M_{t_m^\times}(C_m)}
 \qquad(t_m^\times>0).
\tag{6}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \bar\nu_\times
 :=\gamma_d\sum_{m:t_m^\times>0}
 t_m^\times\delta_{(m,q_m^\times)}
\tag{7}
\]
is a nonzero positive measure carried by
\(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), and every closed ambient halfspace \(H\) satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\bar\nu_\times(H)\leq\mu_C(H).}
\tag{8}
\]
Its mass is exactly the mass of the uncompressed measure \(\nu_\times\) from 624b3258dee4faea:
\[
 \boxed{
 |\bar\nu_\times|
 =|\nu_\times|
 =2\gamma_dE_{\mathrm{gg}}
 >\frac{85\gamma_d}{97}P
 >\frac{340}{1067}P.}
\tag{9}
\]
Thus the compression incurs no further \(\gamma_d\)-loss.

The normal bundle remains balanced at every distinct physical base, including bases represented by several different physical edges:
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\substack{r,i,k\\m_k=m}}
 b_{i,k}^{(r)}a_i^\circ=0
 \quad\text{for every }m\in\mathbb Z^n.}
\tag{10}
\]
For \(t_m^\times=0\), nonnegativity in (4) makes every summand in (10) zero, so the identity is automatic.

Let \(\rho_\times\) and \(r_\times\) be respectively the intrinsic base rank and continuous residual rank of \(\bar\nu_\times\) in the notation of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483.  Equivalently,
\[
 \rho_\times
 =\dim\operatorname{aff}\{m:t_m^\times>0\},
\tag{11}
\]
\[
 r_\times
 =\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}\{m:t_m^\times>0\}\to\mathbb R^d
 \ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{q_m^\times-s(m):t_m^\times>0\}.
\tag{12}
\]
Writing the canonical depth term as
\[
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\tag{13}
\]
one has
\[
 \boxed{
 D\geq
 2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_\times}
 \frac{E_{\mathrm{gg}}}{r_\times+1}.}
\tag{14}
\]
Hence, whenever the canonical hard branch \(D<Q/100\) remains in force, in particular under the small-defect hypothesis \(R+G+D<Q/100\),
\[
 \boxed{
 r_\times+1>
 \frac{2125\gamma_d}{582}\,2^{n-\rho_\times}.}
\tag{15}
\]

No full-rank packet extraction, FT conclusion, terminal conclusion, or comparison between the compressed residual directions and the circuit decorations is asserted.

## Proof

Accepted fact 624b3258dee4faea proves the following simultaneous density realization of all atoms in (2).  For every index triple with positive weight there is a density \(F_{i,k}^{(r)}\) on \(C_{m_k}\) such that
\[
 0\leq F_{i,k}^{(r)}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int F_{i,k}^{(r)}=\vartheta_k,
 \qquad
 \frac1{\vartheta_k}\int xF_{i,k}^{(r)}(x)\,dx
 =p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}.
\tag{16}
\]
At each distinct physical base \(m\), that fact pools all repeated occurrences, all packet indices, and all physical edges with the same base into
\[
 \mathcal F_m
 :=\sum_{\substack{r,i,k\\m_k=m}}
 \frac{b_{i,k}^{(r)}}{\vartheta_k}F_{i,k}^{(r)},
 \qquad
 0\leq\mathcal F_m\leq1.
\tag{17}
\]
The accepted target ledger is used before (17), so repeated physical targets are not assigned separate unit capacities.

Equations (4), (5), (16), and (17) give, whenever \(t_m^\times>0\),
\[
 \int_{C_m}\mathcal F_m=t_m^\times,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_m}x\mathcal F_m(x)\,dx
 =t_m^\times q_m^\times.
\tag{18}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal F_m\) is an admissible metronoid density and proves (6).  In particular, \(t_m^\times\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(C_m)\), and positivity of \(t_m^\times\) makes the compact convex fiber \(C_m\) a full-dimensional convex body, as required by accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d.  That fact also gives
\(\mathsf M_{t_m^\times}(C_m)\subseteq C_m\).  Since every \(m=m_k\) is integral, (6) therefore proves that every atom in (7) lies in the mixed-integer support.

Fix a closed ambient halfspace \(H\).  For each \(m\) whose compressed atom lies in \(H\), its fiber section
\[
 H_m:=\{y:(m,y)\in H\}
\tag{19}
\]
is a closed fiber halfspace, possibly the whole space, containing \(q_m^\times\).  The metronoid-Grünbaum inclusion in accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d and (6) imply
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_m\cap H_m)
 \geq\gamma_dt_m^\times.
\tag{20}
\]
Summing (20) only over those distinct bases whose compressed atom is in \(H\) yields
\[
 \bar\nu_\times(H)
 =\gamma_d\sum_{\substack{m:t_m^\times>0\\(m,q_m^\times)\in H}}
 t_m^\times
 \leq\sum_m\operatorname{vol}_d(C_m\cap H_m)
 =\mu_C(H),
\tag{21}
\]
which proves (8).  The coefficient \(\gamma_d\) in (7) is exactly the single coefficient supplied by (20); no second application is made to an already \(\gamma_d\)-scaled mass.

Grouping the finite sum in (3) by its distinct physical bases gives
\[
 \sum_m t_m^\times
 =\sum_{r,i,k}b_{i,k}^{(r)}
 =2E_{\mathrm{gg}}.
\tag{22}
\]
Equations (7), (22), and (1) prove the first three quantities and the first strict inequality in (9).  Accepted fact 624b3258dee4faea records
\(\gamma_d>4/11\), so
\[
 \frac{85\gamma_d}{97}P
 >\frac{85}{97}\frac4{11}P
 =\frac{340}{1067}P,
\tag{23}
\]
which completes (9).  In particular, \(\bar\nu_\times\neq0\).

For each fixed pair \((r,k)\), the second identity in (3) is the pointwise packet-target balance proved in 624b3258dee4faea.  Summing those zero identities over every \(r\) and every \(k\) with the same physical base \(m_k=m\) proves (10).  This grouping remains valid when different edges have the same base and when a circuit loop contributes two endpoint slots, because those multiplicities are already encoded in \(b_{i,k}^{(r)}\).

Apply the dominated-measure residual theorem of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 to the nonzero measure \(\bar\nu_\times\), using (8).  Its intrinsic ranks are exactly (11) and (12), and it gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)
 \geq
 \frac{|\bar\nu_\times|}
 {2^{\rho_\times}(r_\times+1)}
 =\frac{2\gamma_dE_{\mathrm{gg}}}
 {2^{\rho_\times}(r_\times+1)}.
\tag{24}
\]
Multiplication by \(2^n\) proves (14).  Under \(D<Q/100\), equations (14), (1), and the accepted bound \(P>Q/24\) from 592d2827cca44396 give
\[
 \frac Q{100}
 >D
 >\frac{2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_\times}}
 {r_\times+1}
 \frac{85Q}{194\cdot24}.
\tag{25}
\]
Cancelling \(Q>0\) and rearranging yields
\[
 r_\times+1
 >\frac{100\cdot2\cdot85}{194\cdot24}
 \gamma_d2^{n-\rho_\times}
 =\frac{2125\gamma_d}{582}
 2^{n-\rho_\times},
\tag{26}
\]
which is (15).
