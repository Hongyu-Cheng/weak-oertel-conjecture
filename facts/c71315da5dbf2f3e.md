## Statement

Take actual centered rectangles $[-r_z/2,r_z/2]\times[-H_z,H_z]$ at integer bases $z=-2,-1,0,1,2$, all widths and halfheights positive. At the mark $(0,0,0)$ let $\delta\ge0$ be the physical offset for continuous normal $(0,1)$, fixed under changes of base tilt. Suppose $b>0$, $H_{-2}=2b+\delta$, $H_2=2b-\delta>0$, and $|bz-\delta|<H_z$ for $z=-1,0,1$. For the cap $y-bz\ge-\delta$ of raw mass $Q$ to be a local minimum under base tilt, $r_{-1}\ge r_1$ is necessary. For any $A>\max_z(H_z+\delta)$ the cap $y+Az\ge-\delta$ of mass $R$ satisfies
$$
R-Q=\sum_z z r_zH_z-b(r_{-1}-r_1)-\delta(r_{-1}+r_1).
$$
If the original-volume centroid is the mark and $\delta>0$, the specified contact cap cannot minimize globally. If the centroid is the mark and $\delta=0$, global minimality implies $r_{-1}=r_1$ and a nearby base tilt gives an inside-free equal-mass minimizer.

## Proof

In the margin $y+az+\delta$ at $a=-b$, decrease $a$ slightly. Then the negative endpoint becomes strictly full and the positive endpoint strictly empty, with the same saturated masses as before (the original outside face has zero area). The proper middle traces remain proper and the directional derivative for decreasing $a$ is $r_{-1}-r_1$. Local minimality requires it nonnegative. The opposite finite tilt $A$ is strict on all four non-own fibers, empty on the two negative bases and full on the two positive bases; the own trace is unchanged. Thus
$$
Q=2r_{-2}H_{-2}+r_{-1}(H_{-1}+b+\delta)+r_0(H_0+\delta)+r_1(H_1-b+\delta),\qquad
R=2r_1H_1+2r_2H_2+r_0(H_0+\delta).
$$
Subtract and collect to obtain the identity. Because the individual centroids are zero in the continuous coordinates, full centroid balance is exactly $\sum_z z r_zH_z=0$. For $\delta>0$ the identity and the necessary width ordering give $R<Q$. For $\delta=0$, global minimality and the identity force $r_{-1}=r_1$. The first, slightly decreased tilt then has exactly constant mass in an actual interval by linear integration of the proper traces. Its endpoint traces are strict and its middle traces proper, giving the stated inside-free minimizer.
