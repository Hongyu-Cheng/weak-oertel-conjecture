## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and write
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z).
\tag{1}
\]
For \(0<t\leq a_z\), use
\[
 \mathsf M_t(C_z):=
 \left\{
 \frac1t\int_{C_z}x\rho(x)\,dx:
 0\leq\rho\leq1\ \text{a.e.},\quad
 \int_{C_z}\rho=t
 \right\}.
\]
For a nonempty compact convex set \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^d\), write
\[
 h_K(u):=\max_{x\in K}\langle u,x\rangle.
\]
For every positive-volume fiber put
\[
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,
 \qquad
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
 \qquad
 D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z.
\tag{2}
\]

Retain the common canonical zero-fresh hard system of accepted fact 320dcb7d85f22889, including its total reservoir mass \(Q>0\), its hard-ledger quantities \(G\), \(R\), \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\), and \(D\), and the same global gauge-load-minimal optimizer \(v^b\) with self-saturated load \(q=(q_f)\).  Let \(E\) be a family of its positive-load edges \(e=\{u_e,v_e\}\) satisfying
\[
 v_e-u_e\in(8\mathbb Z)^n.
\tag{3}
\]
Define
\[
 x_e:=\frac{3u_e+v_e}{4},
 \qquad
 y_e:=\frac{u_e+3v_e}{4},
 \qquad
 T(e):=\{x_e,y_e\},
\tag{4}
\]
and retain the nonempty actual positive-load child family
\[
 F:=\{T(e):e\in E,\ q_{T(e)}>0\}.
\tag{5}
\]
For \(f=\{u_f,v_f\}\in F\), put
\[
 m_f:=\frac{u_f+v_f}{2},
 \qquad
 \bar c_f:=\frac{c_{u_f}+c_{v_f}}2,
\tag{6}
\]
\[
 D_f:=\frac12(D_{u_f}+D_{v_f}),
 \qquad
 L_f:=\frac12(\mathcal K_{u_f}+\mathcal K_{v_f})
 =\bar c_f+D_f,
\tag{7}
\]
\[
 \vartheta_f:=2^{-(d+1)}
 \left(a_{u_f}^{1/d}+a_{v_f}^{1/d}\right)^d.
\tag{8}
\]
The retained conclusions of accepted facts 320dcb7d85f22889 and 6bfe50fed9a5540b are
\[
 L_f\subseteq\mathsf M_{\vartheta_f}(C_{m_f}),
\tag{9}
\]
\[
 \sum_{\substack{f\in F\\f\ni z}}q_f\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 \sum_{\substack{f\in F\\m_f=m}}
 \frac{q_f}{\vartheta_f}\leq1.
\tag{10}
\]

Let
\[
 \mathcal M:=\{m_f:f\in F\},
 \qquad
 \eta_m:=\sum_{\substack{f\in F\\m_f=m}}q_f>0,
\tag{11}
\]
and define the collision-pooled actual child body
\[
 \mathcal P_m:=\frac1{\eta_m}
 \sum_{\substack{f\in F\\m_f=m}}q_fL_f
 \qquad(m\in\mathcal M).
\tag{12}
\]
Then every simultaneous choice \(p_m\in\mathcal P_m\), \(m\in\mathcal M\), has densities \(g_m:C_m\to[0,1]\) satisfying
\[
 \int_{C_m}g_m=\eta_m,
 \qquad
 \frac1{\eta_m}\int_{C_m}xg_m(x)\,dx=p_m.
\tag{13}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal P_m\subseteq\mathsf M_{\eta_m}(C_m),
 \qquad 0<\eta_m\leq a_m.}
\tag{14}
\]
The densities in (13) use one shared ledger for all child edges colliding at \(m\).

Fix a linear subspace \(V\subseteq\mathbb R^d\), and define
\[
 \mathscr A_V:=
 \left\{
 (a(m)+v_m)_{m\in\mathcal M}:
 a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\text{ affine},
 \ v_m\in V
 \right\}
 \subseteq(\mathbb R^d)^{\mathcal M}.
\tag{15}
\]
Exactly one of the following two alternatives holds.

1. There are an affine map \(a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) and points \(p_m\in\mathcal P_m\) such that
\[
 p_m-a(m)\in V
 \qquad(m\in\mathcal M).
\tag{16}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(\mathcal M)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{p_m-s(m):m\in\mathcal M\}
 \leq\dim V.
\tag{17}
\]

2. There are vectors \(Y_m\in V^\perp\), not all zero, such that
\[
 \sum_{m\in\mathcal M}Y_m=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{m\in\mathcal M}Y_mm^{\mathsf T}=0,
\tag{18}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{m\in\mathcal M}h_{\mathcal P_m}(Y_m)<0.}
\tag{19}
\]
In this case define, for \(f\in F\),
\[
 w_f:=\frac{q_f}{\eta_{m_f}}Y_{m_f},
\tag{20}
\]
\[
 \kappa:=\max_{f\in F}
 \frac{h_{D_f}(Y_{m_f})}{\eta_{m_f}}>0,
 \qquad
 \widehat w_f:=\frac{w_f}{\kappa},
 \qquad
 \widehat\omega_f:=h_{D_f}(\widehat w_f).
\tag{21}
\]
Then
\[
 \sum_{f\in F}\widehat w_f=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{f\in F}\widehat w_fm_f^{\mathsf T}=0,
\tag{22}
\]
\[
 0\leq\widehat\omega_f\leq q_f,
 \qquad
 -\sum_{f\in F}h_{L_f}(\widehat w_f)>0.
\tag{23}
\]
Thus \(\widehat w\) is a feasible positive-value dual witness for the affine defect of the actual child subcapacity \(q|_F\).  In the notation of accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d,
\[
 \Delta_C(q|_F):=
 \min_{s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \sum_{f\in F}q_f
 \operatorname{dist}_{D_f}(s(m_f),L_f),
\]
where \(\operatorname{dist}_{D_f}(y,L_f):=
\inf_{\ell\in L_f}\|y-\ell\|_{D_f}\) and \(\|\cdot\|_{D_f}\) is the Minkowski norm of \(D_f\).  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \Delta_C(q|_F)
 \geq-\sum_{f\in F}h_{L_f}(\widehat w_f)>0.}
\tag{24}
\]
Its gauge load inherits both physical budgets:
\[
 \sum_{\substack{f\in F\\f\ni z}}\widehat\omega_f
 \leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 \sum_{\substack{f\in F\\m_f=m}}
 \frac{\widehat\omega_f}{\vartheta_f}\leq1.
\tag{25}
\]

The injective child map and dyadic conclusion of accepted fact 320dcb7d85f22889 remain unchanged.  If every edge of \(E\) has one common minimum nonzero coordinatewise \(2\)-adic valuation \(s\geq3\), then every edge of \(F\) has valuation \(s-1\), and
\[
 F\cap E=\varnothing.
\tag{26}
\]

No lower bound of the form
\[
 \sum_{f\in F}\widehat\omega_f
 \geq c\sum_{f\in F}q_f
\tag{27}
\]
with an absolute \(c>0\) is claimed.  The theorem does not claim that \(\widehat w\) is the restriction of, is collinear with, or is a tangent perturbation of the original global optimizer \(v^b\).  It does not produce \(G\), \(R\), \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\), or \(D\) of value at least \(Q/100\), and it does not prove the registered finite parity descent.  The remaining interface is a multilevel optimizer-alignment or terminal-absorption theorem for the balanced witnesses in (18)-(25).

## Proof

Accepted fact 320dcb7d85f22889 gives the actual child family \(F\), the restriction of the same global load \(q\), the two inequalities in (10), injectivity of \(T\), and the dyadic conclusion (26).  Accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b gives (9) for every actual canonical reservoir edge.  We first pool these actual child bodies without choosing a new load or optimizer.

Fix \(m\in\mathcal M\) and \(p_m\in\mathcal P_m\).  By the definition of the weighted Minkowski sum in (12), choose \(p_f\in L_f\) for the child edges over \(m\) so that
\[
 p_m=\frac1{\eta_m}
 \sum_{\substack{f\in F\\m_f=m}}q_fp_f.
\tag{28}
\]
By (9), for each such \(f\) there is a density \(\rho_f:C_m\to[0,1]\) with
\[
 \int_{C_m}\rho_f=\vartheta_f,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_m}x\rho_f(x)\,dx=\vartheta_fp_f.
\tag{29}
\]
Put
\[
 g_m:=\sum_{\substack{f\in F\\m_f=m}}
 \frac{q_f}{\vartheta_f}\rho_f.
\tag{30}
\]
The target budget in (10) gives, almost everywhere on \(C_m\),
\[
 0\leq g_m
 \leq
 \left(
 \sum_{\substack{f\in F\\m_f=m}}
 \frac{q_f}{\vartheta_f}
 \right)\mathbf1_{C_m}
 \leq\mathbf1_{C_m}.
\tag{31}
\]
Equations (11), (28), and (29) give
\[
 \int_{C_m}g_m=\eta_m,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_m}xg_m(x)\,dx=\eta_mp_m.
\tag{32}
\]
This proves (13)-(14).  The construction is performed once after summing all colliding edges at each physical target, so it is one shared target ledger.  Since the target fibers are distinct for distinct \(m\), all choices are simultaneous.  Integrating (31) also gives \(\eta_m\leq a_m\).

We prove the exact transversal alternative.  Put
\[
 X:=(\mathbb R^d)^{\mathcal M},
 \qquad
 \mathcal P:=\prod_{m\in\mathcal M}\mathcal P_m.
\tag{33}
\]
Every \(\mathcal P_m\) is a nonempty compact convex set, so the same is true of \(\mathcal P\).  The set \(\mathscr A_V\) in (15) is a linear subspace of the finite-dimensional space \(X\), hence is closed.  Alternative 1 is exactly the assertion
\[
 \mathcal P\cap\mathscr A_V\ne\varnothing.
\tag{34}
\]
If (34) holds, (16) follows by reading the coordinates of a point in the intersection, and (17) follows by using the same affine map \(a\) in the displayed minimum.

Suppose (34) fails.  Separate the compact convex set \(\mathcal P\) strictly from the closed linear subspace \(\mathscr A_V\).  Because \(\mathscr A_V\) is a subspace, the separating functional must annihilate it.  After orienting the functional, there are vectors \((Y_m)_{m\in\mathcal M}\), not all zero, such that
\[
 \sum_m\langle Y_m,x_m\rangle<0
 \qquad
 \text{for every }(x_m)_m\in\mathcal P,
\tag{35}
\]
and
\[
 \sum_m\langle Y_m,a(m)+v_m\rangle=0
\tag{36}
\]
for every affine \(a\) and every independently chosen family \(v_m\in V\).

In (36), set \(a=0\), set all but one \(v_m\) to zero, and vary the remaining vector in \(V\).  This gives \(Y_m\in V^\perp\) for every \(m\).  Next set all \(v_m=0\).  Varying the constant part of \(a\) gives
\[
 \sum_mY_m=0.
\tag{37}
\]
Varying the linear part of \(a\) gives
\[
 \sum_mY_mm^{\mathsf T}=0.
\tag{38}
\]
Finally, the support function of a Cartesian product is the sum of the coordinate support functions.  Taking the supremum of the left side of (35) over \(\mathcal P\) gives (19).  This proves Alternative 2.

The alternatives are mutually exclusive.  Indeed, if \((p_m)_m\in\mathcal P\cap\mathscr A_V\), then (18) and \(Y_m\in V^\perp\) give
\[
 \sum_m\langle Y_m,p_m\rangle=0,
\tag{39}
\]
whereas (19) says that this quantity is at most a strictly negative number.  Hence exactly one alternative holds.

We now lift the balanced separator to the actual child edges.  Equations (11), (18), and (20) give
\[
 \sum_{f\in F}w_f
 =\sum_{m\in\mathcal M}Y_m=0,
\tag{40}
\]
\[
 \sum_{f\in F}w_fm_f^{\mathsf T}
 =\sum_{m\in\mathcal M}Y_mm^{\mathsf T}=0.
\tag{41}
\]
Minkowski additivity and positive homogeneity of support functions give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{f\in F}h_{L_f}(w_f)
 &=\sum_{m\in\mathcal M}
 \frac1{\eta_m}
 \sum_{\substack{f\in F\\m_f=m}}q_fh_{L_f}(Y_m)\\
 &=\sum_{m\in\mathcal M}h_{\mathcal P_m}(Y_m)<0.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{42}
\]

Accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d shows that each \(D_f\) is an origin-symmetric full-dimensional convex body, so \(h_{D_f}\) is a norm.  Some \(Y_m\) is nonzero, and at least one child edge lies over that target.  Therefore the maximum \(\kappa\) in (21) is strictly positive.  For every \(f\in F\), positive homogeneity and the definition of \(\kappa\) give
\[
 h_{D_f}(\widehat w_f)
 =\frac{q_f}{\kappa\eta_{m_f}}
 h_{D_f}(Y_{m_f})
 \leq q_f.
\tag{43}
\]
Dividing (40)-(42) by \(\kappa\) proves (22)-(23).  The matching capacity \(q|_F\) is nonzero, is supported on actual off-diagonal same-parity child edges, and has endpoint degrees at most \(a_z/4\), hence at most \(a_z/2\).  The exact weighted duality of accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d applies.  Equations (22)-(23) make \(\widehat w\) dual feasible with strictly positive value, proving (24).

Because \(\widehat\omega_f\leq q_f\), summing (43) and using (10) proves both inequalities in (25).  The pooling, separation, and normalization never change the edge set \(F\), its bases, or the capacity coordinates \(q_f\).  Thus injectivity and the dyadic no-reuse conclusion (26) remain exactly those of accepted fact 320dcb7d85f22889.

The proof supplies only the upper bounds \(\widehat\omega_f\leq q_f\).  It gives no lower bound on \(\sum_f\widehat\omega_f\), because \(\kappa\) is a maximum of child gauges while the total normalized load is a weighted average of those gauges.  It also constructs \(\widehat w\) from the separating functional, not from the directions of the retained global optimizer \(v^b\).  Therefore no original-optimizer alignment, terminal-capacity lower bound, or multilevel descent follows.  This is precisely the remaining gap stated in ADVANCES.
