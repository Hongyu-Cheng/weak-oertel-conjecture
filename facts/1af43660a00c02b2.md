## Statement

There is a convex body with three integer and three continuous coordinates, exactly 35 positive integer fibers with positive affine volume root, and an actual mixed centroid of integer base, such that a closed vertical halfspace through it has mixed mass fraction strictly below $367/1000<1/e$.

## Proof

Let $P_0=\{(x,y,z)\ge0:4x+2y+z\le8\}$, $K=\operatorname{conv}\{0,6e_1,e_2,e_3\}$ of volume one, and $h=1+A(x-1)+B(y-1)$. Use the body over $P_0$ with fibers $hK$, defined by the three nonnegative continuous coordinates and the affine bound $s_1/6+s_2+s_3\le h$. Its integer census is $0\le x\le2,\ 0\le y\le4-2x,\ 0\le z\le8-4x-2y$, giving 35 points. Let $(A,B)$ minimize $\Phi=\sum h^4$ on $R=[6013366,6013370]/10^7\times[15061245,15061267]/10^8$, with sums over that census. Since $h\ge1-A-B>0$ on $P_0$, this gives a full-dimensional convex body for every parameter in $R$. The Hessian is $12\sum h^2 vv^\top$ for $v=(x-1,y-1)$ and is positive definite because the vectors span $\mathbb R^2$.

Write $(N_x,N_y)=\nabla\Phi/4$. Expanding the finite sums gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_x&=26A^3-18A^2B-72A^2+108AB^2+24AB+78A-48B^3-102B^2-18B-24,\\
N_y&=-6A^3+108A^2B+12A^2-144AB^2-204AB-18A+150B^3+126B^2+126B .
\end{aligned}
$$
Their mixed partial $\partial_B N_x=\partial_A N_y=-18A^2+216AB+24A-144B^2-204B-18$ is at most $-1514823/62500$ on $[.6,.602]\times[.15,.151]$ by termwise endpoint bounds. At the lower-left corner of $R$, the two derivatives are $-337514033207339/125000000000000000000$ and $-4293268153408029/4000000000000000000000$. At the upper-right corner they are $13678807882229509/15625000000000000000000$ and $13222083910627821/4000000000000000000000$. The negative mixed partial makes $N_x<0$ on the left side, $N_x>0$ on the right, $N_y<0$ on the lower side, and $N_y>0$ on the upper side. At any boundary point an inward coordinate motion therefore decreases $\Phi$. The minimizer is interior, so $N_x=N_y=0$.

These are the moments of $x-1,y-1$ for weights $h^3$. Since $z$ has mean $4-2x-y$ at fixed $(x,y)$, the third centered base moment is $-2N_x-N_y=0$. Also $\sum h^4=\sum h^3+A N_x+B N_y=\sum h^3$, so the continuous part of the mixed centroid is the centroid of $K$. The same closed cap $5(x-1)+2(y-1)+4(z-1)\ge0$ has mass $T$ in the body of mass $M$, where direct summation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=-24A^3+12A^2B+78A^2-102AB^2-36AB-72A+42B^3+126B^2+35,\\
T&=-14A^3-3A^2B+42A^2-39AB^2+6AB-42A+2B^3+48B^2-12B+19 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Termwise lower bounds on $(367/1000)M-T$ over $R$, using the lower corner for positive coefficients and the upper one for negative coefficients, give $8681336352206248242879/4000000000000000000000000>0$. Finally $e\le163/60+7/4320<68/25$ by summing through $1/5!$ and bounding the remainder using $j!\ge720\cdot7^{j-6}$ for $j\ge6$. Since $(367/1000)(68/25)<1$, the stated strict bound follows.
