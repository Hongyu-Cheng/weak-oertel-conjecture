## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard synchronized-atlas instance of accepted facts 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a and 655a8b1d63af6ab2, and the complete-core normalization of accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb.  Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(n\geq2\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body, and
\[
 C_y:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(y,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_y:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_y),
 \qquad
 c_y:=\frac1{a_y}\int_{C_y}x\,dx
\]
whenever \(a_y>0\).  Put
\[
 W:=\sum_{y\in\mathbb Z^n}a_y,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C).
\]
The canonical hard and minimal-base hypotheses give
\[
 G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 W_{L'}<2^{\dim L'-n}W
 \quad
 \text{for every proper primitive affine lattice subspace }L',
\tag{1}
\]
and the canonical reservoir estimate gives \(Q\leq W/8\).

The internal whole-label family of accepted fact 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a is
\[
 \mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}
 =\{\sigma=(z_\sigma;e(\sigma),f(\sigma))\},
 \qquad
 e(\sigma)=\{z_\sigma,a(\sigma)\},
 \qquad
 f(\sigma)=\{z_\sigma,b(\sigma)\},
\tag{2}
\]
where the three source bases in
\[
 T_\sigma:=\{z_\sigma,a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}
\tag{3}
\]
are distinct, the two edge directions at \(z_\sigma\) are linearly independent, and the whole-label weights satisfy
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma
 >\frac7{400}Q.
\tag{4}
\]

Let \(\varnothing\ne\Gamma\subseteq\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\) be any whole-label subfamily.  Put
\[
 H_\Gamma:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 E_\Gamma:=\bigcup_{\sigma\in\Gamma}T_\sigma,
 \qquad
 L_\Gamma:=\operatorname{aff}E_\Gamma,
 \qquad
 q_\Gamma:=\dim L_\Gamma.
\tag{5}
\]
Assume that there is a physical base \(y_*\) such that
\[
 y_*\in T_\sigma\quad(\sigma\in\Gamma)
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 y_*\text{ is a vertex of }\operatorname{conv}E_\Gamma.
\tag{6}
\]
For \(y\in E_\Gamma\), define the collision-aggregated vertex load and selected mass
\[
 \kappa_y^\Gamma
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\y\in T_\sigma}}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 t_y^\Gamma:=4\kappa_y^\Gamma.
\tag{7}
\]
Then every selected mass is feasible,
\[
 0<t_y^\Gamma\leq a_y,
 \qquad
 c_y\in\mathsf M_{t_y^\Gamma}(C_y)
 \quad(y\in E_\Gamma),
\tag{8}
\]
and the tuple
\[
 \left(E_\Gamma,(t_y^\Gamma)_{y\in E_\Gamma},y_*\right)
\tag{9}
\]
is a legal rank-\(q_\Gamma\) relative complete metronoid-core profile when \(q_\Gamma<n\), and a legal global complete metronoid-core profile when \(q_\Gamma=n\).  Its exact mass is
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{y\in E_\Gamma}t_y^\Gamma=12H_\Gamma.}
\tag{10}
\]
Consequently, in both cases,
\[
 \boxed{
 G\geq12\,2^{n-q_\Gamma}H_\Gamma,}
\tag{11}
\]
and the hard inequality in (1) forces the strict exposed-block screen
\[
 \boxed{
 H_\Gamma
 <\frac{Q}{1200\,2^{n-q_\Gamma}}.}
\tag{12}
\]

Moreover, there is a partition
\[
 \mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}
 =\Gamma_1\mathbin{\dot\cup}\cdots\mathbin{\dot\cup}\Gamma_k
\tag{13}
\]
into nonempty whole-label subfamilies and pairwise distinct physical bases \(y_1,\ldots,y_k\), such that \(y_i\) belongs to every \(T_\sigma\) with \(\sigma\in\Gamma_i\) and is a vertex of \(\operatorname{conv}E_{\Gamma_i}\).  If \(q_i:=\dim\operatorname{aff}E_{\Gamma_i}\), then
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_i}\eta_\sigma
 <\frac{Q}{1200\,2^{n-q_i}}
 \leq\frac{Q}{1200}
 \quad(1\leq i\leq k),
\tag{14}
\]
and necessarily
\[
 \boxed{k\geq22.}
\tag{15}
\]
All constructions preserve whole labels.  Repeated physical source bases are aggregated in (7) before any metronoid is formed, so neither (8)-(12) nor the peeling partition splits a label or counts a fiber capacity twice.  No loop/cross distinction is used, and hence all conclusions include the allowed all-loop case.

## Proof

We first establish simultaneous source feasibility.  Accepted fact 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a retains, for every upstream edge \(j\), its transverse-pair capacity \(\omega_j\), and its source-incidence audit gives, at every physical source base \(y\),
\[
 \sum_{j\ni y}
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
                   j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma
 \leq
 \sum_{j\ni y}\omega_j
 \leq\frac{a_y}{4}.
\tag{16}
\]
For one label \(\sigma\), membership of \(y\) in \(T_\sigma\) contributes once to the left side of (7).  If \(y\) is an opposite endpoint, the same label contributes once to the double sum in (16); if \(y=z_\sigma\), it contributes once through each of the two distinct upstream edges and is counted twice there.  Therefore restricting (16) to \(\Gamma\) gives
\[
 0<\kappa_y^\Gamma
 \leq
 \sum_{j\ni y}
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
                   j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma
 \leq\frac{a_y}{4}
 \qquad(y\in E_\Gamma).
\tag{17}
\]
Thus \(0<t_y^\Gamma\leq a_y\).  The constant density
\[
 \frac{t_y^\Gamma}{a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y}
\tag{18}
\]
is bounded between zero and one, has mass \(t_y^\Gamma\), and has normalized barycenter \(c_y\).  This proves (8).  Notice that (18) is formed only after all coincident physical uses of \(y\) have been summed in \(\kappa_y^\Gamma\); it is one density in one physical fiber.

Because \(y_*\) is a vertex of the finite polytope \(\operatorname{conv}E_\Gamma\), there is an affine functional \(\varphi\) on \(L_\Gamma\) such that
\[
 \varphi(y_*)=0,
 \qquad
 \varphi(y)>0
 \quad(y\in E_\Gamma\setminus\{y_*\}).
\tag{19}
\]
It follows that the only barycentric representation of \(y_*\) by \(E_\Gamma\) is the unit mass at \(y_*\).  Hence the complete-core intersection is exactly
\[
 \bigcap_{\substack{\lambda\in\mathbb R_+^{E_\Gamma}\\
                    \sum_y\lambda_y=1\\
                    \sum_y\lambda_yy=y_*}}
 \sum_{y\in E_\Gamma}
 \lambda_y\mathsf M_{t_y^\Gamma}(C_y)
 =\mathsf M_{t_{y_*}^\Gamma}(C_{y_*})
 \ne\varnothing.
\tag{20}
\]
The three bases in every \(T_\sigma\) are distinct, so summing (7) first over physical bases and then over labels gives
\[
 \sum_{y\in E_\Gamma}\kappa_y^\Gamma
 =3H_\Gamma.
\tag{21}
\]
Every label contains \(y_*\) exactly once, and therefore
\[
 \kappa_{y_*}^\Gamma=H_\Gamma,
 \qquad
 t_{y_*}^\Gamma=4H_\Gamma,
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in E_\Gamma}t_y^\Gamma=12H_\Gamma.
\tag{22}
\]

The two independent upstream directions of any fixed label in \(\Gamma\) lie in \(L_\Gamma-y_*\), so
\[
 q_\Gamma\geq2.
\tag{23}
\]
Every proper relative closed halfspace of \(L_\Gamma\) containing \(y_*\) contains at least the selected mass at \(y_*\).  Equations (22)-(23) give
\[
 t_{y_*}^\Gamma
 =\frac13\sum_{y\in E_\Gamma}t_y^\Gamma
 \geq
 2^{-q_\Gamma}
 \sum_{y\in E_\Gamma}t_y^\Gamma.
\tag{24}
\]
Together, (8), (20), and (24) prove the claimed relative profile when \(q_\Gamma<n\); when \(q_\Gamma=n\), the same calculation is exactly the global \(2^{-n}\)-balance condition.  Equation (10) is (22).

For the lower-rank case, \(L_\Gamma\) is primitive.  Indeed, write
\[
 L_\Gamma=y_*+V,
 \qquad
 V:=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}(E_\Gamma-y_*).
\]
The space \(V\) is spanned by integer vectors and has dimension \(q_\Gamma\), so \(V\cap\mathbb Z^n\) has rank \(q_\Gamma\).  It is saturated because \(u\in\mathbb Z^n\) and \(mu\in V\cap\mathbb Z^n\) for \(m\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\) imply \(u=m^{-1}(mu)\in V\cap\mathbb Z^n\).

Accepted fact 655a8b1d63af6ab2 retains the strict minimal-base inequality in (1) and the reservoir estimate \(Q\leq W/8\).  The hard inequality gives
\[
 G<\frac Q{100}\leq\frac W{800}<\frac W4.
\tag{25}
\]
Thus the ambient-normalized relative-core domination of accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb applies to \(L_\Gamma\).  From (10) and the relative admissibility just proved,
\[
 G
 \geq
 2^{n-q_\Gamma}G_{L_\Gamma}^{\mathrm{rel}}
 \geq
 12\,2^{n-q_\Gamma}H_\Gamma
 \qquad(q_\Gamma<n).
\tag{26}
\]
When \(q_\Gamma=n\), the global profile itself gives
\[
 G\geq12H_\Gamma
 =12\,2^{n-q_\Gamma}H_\Gamma.
\tag{27}
\]
Equations (26)-(27) prove (11), and combining (11) with the strict hard bound \(G<Q/100\) proves (12).

It remains to construct and count the peeling partition.  Start with
\[
 \mathscr R_1:=\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}.
\]
Whenever \(\mathscr R_i\ne\varnothing\), choose a vertex \(y_i\) of
\[
 \operatorname{conv}
 \bigcup_{\sigma\in\mathscr R_i}T_\sigma,
\]
put
\[
 \Gamma_i:=\{\sigma\in\mathscr R_i:y_i\in T_\sigma\},
 \qquad
 \mathscr R_{i+1}:=\mathscr R_i\setminus\Gamma_i,
\tag{28}
\]
and continue.  The family is finite, every chosen vertex lies in at least one remaining source triangle, and hence every \(\Gamma_i\) is nonempty and the process terminates.  It plainly gives the disjoint partition (13).  An exposing functional for \(y_i\) on the larger remaining source set also exposes it on \(E_{\Gamma_i}\), so each block satisfies (6).  After \(\Gamma_i\) is removed, no remaining label contains \(y_i\); consequently the chosen bases are pairwise distinct.

Apply (12) to every block.  Since \(q_i\leq n\), it gives (14).  Finally, (4), (13), and (14) imply
\[
 \frac{21Q}{1200}
 =\frac{7Q}{400}
 <H_{\mathrm{int}}
 =\sum_{i=1}^k\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_i}\eta_\sigma
 <k\frac{Q}{1200}.
\tag{29}
\]
Because \(Q>0\), equation (29) gives \(k>21\), and integrality proves (15).  The proof used neither downstream loop/cross type nor a fractional label selection, completing the collision and all-loop audit.
