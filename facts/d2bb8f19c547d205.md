## Statement

Let $d\ge2$ and $n\ge0$. Let $P=\operatorname{conv}P\cap\mathbb Z^n$ be finite and nonempty, and let positive convex $d$-fibers $K_z$ be the exact sampled fibers of their canonical convex hull $C$. Write $\eta$ for the sum of their boundary surface measures marked by the outward unit normal $u$. For each normal let $H(u,\cdot)$ be finite concave and $L(u,\cdot)$ finite positive convex on $C$. Assume $W=H_+/L^d$ is Borel and bounded on the actual marked boundary ranges and the finitely many auxiliary pair-average boundary ranges. An auxiliary body $(K_z+K_w)/2$ is placed at the physical base $(z+w)/2$. Put $\omega=W\eta$.

Let equal-mass measures $\gamma_-,\gamma_+\le\omega$ have a same-normal coupling whose midpoints have integer base $p$ and lie on the actual support face $F_{K_p}(u)$. There exists a same-normal coupling of the same marginals with actual support contact and midpoint measure $\rho\le2^n\omega$. For almost every normal and every original endpoint parity class, it has the two original conditional marginals and its physical midpoint law dominates the old one in full physical convex order. Each class separately contributes at most one copy of the conditional $\omega$. An old standard Borel mark can retain its joint law with normal and parity and its conditional physical midpoint mean. The factor $2^n$ is sharp even when $W=1$.
