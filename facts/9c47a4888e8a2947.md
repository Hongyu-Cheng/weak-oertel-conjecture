## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body in the canonical hard-branch setting of accepted facts `036a276c54ec5e66`, `3100bf8082ca09d5`, and `3016926516cc39df`, and put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 Z:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\}.
\]
For \(z\in Z\), define
\[
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)
 =\left\{
 \frac2{a_z}\int_{C_z}x f(x)\,dx:
 0\leq f\leq1,\quad
 \int_{C_z}f=\frac{a_z}{2}
 \right\}.
\tag{1}
\]

Let \(E_\perp\) be the set of pooled physical endpoints from accepted fact `3100bf8082ca09d5`. For \(y\in E_\perp\), let \(\mathcal O(y)\) be the finite set of positive atlas occurrences in which \(y\) is an opposite endpoint. If \(\sigma\in\mathcal O(y)\), write \(\theta_\sigma>0\) for its occurrence weight and \(x_{\sigma,y}\) for the appropriate contact \(x_{e,y}\) from accepted fact `036a276c54ec5e66`. That accepted fact supplies a half-mass witness \(f_{\sigma,y}\) satisfying
\[
 0\leq f_{\sigma,y}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_y}f_{\sigma,y}=\frac{a_y}{2},
 \qquad
 \frac2{a_y}\int_{C_y}x f_{\sigma,y}(x)\,dx=x_{\sigma,y}.
\tag{2}
\]
The pooling of accepted fact `3100bf8082ca09d5` has
\[
 \tau_y=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal O(y)}\theta_\sigma>0,
 \qquad
 p_y=\frac1{\tau_y}
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal O(y)}\theta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}.
\tag{3}
\]
Then, for every \(y\in E_\perp\),
\[
 \boxed{
 p_y\in\mathcal K_y,
 \qquad
 p_y=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal O(y)}
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{\tau_y}x_{\sigma,y}.}
\tag{4}
\]
Thus the asserted half-mass membership is realized specifically as a convex combination of the occurrence contacts.

Let \(E_{1/20}\subseteq E_\perp\) and \(\mathcal M_2\) be the dyadic endpoint core and matching constructed in accepted fact `3016926516cc39df`. Put
\[
 Y_2:=\bigcup_{e\in\mathcal M_2}e.
\]
For \(e=\{u,w\}\in\mathcal M_2\), define
\[
 t_e:=\min\{\tau_u,\tau_w\},
 \qquad
 m_e:=\frac{u+w}{2},
 \qquad
 \bar p_e:=\frac{p_u+p_w}{2}.
\tag{5}
\]
There are two predecessor symbols called \(q_e\), with different types. The point called \(q_e\) in accepted fact `3016926516cc39df` is \(\bar p_e\) here, while the scalar matching weight called \(q_e\) in accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` is \(t_e\) here. No unqualified \(q_e\) is used below.

The family \(t=(t_e)\), extended by zero off \(\mathcal M_2\), is a nonzero free off-diagonal fractional same-parity half-metronoid matching in the sense of accepted fact `8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5`. Explicitly, every \(e=\{u,w\}\in\mathcal M_2\) has \(u\ne w\), \(u\equiv w\pmod2\), \(t_e>0\), and
\[
 d_z(t):=\sum_{\substack{e\in\mathcal M_2\\z\in e}}t_e
 \leq\frac{a_z}{2}
 \qquad(z\in Z).
\tag{6}
\]
For
\[
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,
 \qquad
 D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z,
\tag{7}
\]
and, for \(e=\{u,w\}\in\mathcal M_2\),
\[
 D_e:=\frac12(D_u+D_w),
 \qquad
 L_e:=\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_w)
      =\frac{c_u+c_w}{2}+D_e,
\tag{8}
\]
one has, edge by edge,
\[
 \boxed{
 \bar p_e\in L_e,
 \qquad
 \bar p_e\in\mathsf M_{t_e}(C_{m_e}).}
\tag{9}
\]
These are edge-labelled memberships, including when different edges have coincident midpoints or coincident midpoint atoms.

Finally, apply the weighted affine-frustration dual of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` with its scalar matching weights renamed \(t_e\). Thus let \(v=(v_e)_{e\in\mathcal M_2}\) be any dual-feasible stress, including the zero stress, meaning
\[
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}v_e=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}v_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 h_{D_e}(v_e)\leq t_e
 \quad(e\in\mathcal M_2).
\tag{10}
\]
Define the edge loads
\[
 \omega_e:=h_{D_e}(v_e),
 \qquad
 \Omega:=\sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}\omega_e.
\tag{11}
\]
Global endpoint-disjointness gives every \(y\in Y_2\) a unique incident edge \(e(y)\). Define the new endpoint stress field and its loads by
\[
 U_y:=\frac12v_{e(y)},
 \qquad
 \sigma_y:=h_{D_y}(U_y),
 \qquad
 T_*:=\sum_{y\in Y_2}\sigma_y.
\tag{12}
\]
These \(\sigma_y\) are stress loads and are unrelated to the original pooled endpoint weights \(\tau_y\). Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{y\in Y_2}U_y=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y_2}U_yy^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 T_*=\Omega.}
\tag{13}
\]
Equivalently, one may extend \(U_y=0\) and \(\sigma_y=0\) to unmatched \(y\in Z\), without changing (13).

All conclusions are edgewise or stresswise. They assert neither one simultaneous affine realization of the contacts, nor endpoint-frame aggregation, nor a terminal or final conclusion.

## Proof

Fix \(y\in E_\perp\). For \(\sigma\in\mathcal O(y)\), the occurrence density used in the pooling is
\[
 g_{\sigma,y}=\frac{2\theta_\sigma}{a_y}f_{\sigma,y}.
\tag{14}
\]
By (2), it has mass \(\theta_\sigma\) and first moment \(\theta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}\). Summing (14) over \(\mathcal O(y)\) and using the definitions of \(\tau_y\) and \(p_y\) in accepted fact `3100bf8082ca09d5` proves (3).

Put
\[
 \lambda_{\sigma,y}:=\frac{\theta_\sigma}{\tau_y}
 \qquad(\sigma\in\mathcal O(y)).
\]
Equation (3) gives \(\lambda_{\sigma,y}>0\) and \(\sum_\sigma\lambda_{\sigma,y}=1\). Hence
\[
 F_y:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal O(y)}
 \lambda_{\sigma,y}f_{\sigma,y}
\tag{15}
\]
satisfies \(0\leq F_y\leq1\) and \(\int_{C_y}F_y=a_y/2\). Equations (2)--(3) give
\[
 \frac2{a_y}\int_{C_y}xF_y(x)\,dx
 =\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal O(y)}
 \lambda_{\sigma,y}x_{\sigma,y}
 =p_y.
\tag{16}
\]
Thus \(F_y\) is a half-mass witness for \(p_y\), proving (4) directly from definition (1). In particular, repeated occurrences of one physical contact or one physical endpoint cause no issue: they simply appear with their occurrence weights in the convex combination.

We next verify legality of the dyadic matching. Accepted fact `3016926516cc39df` partitions elements inside each dyadic bin into unordered pairs. Consequently, the endpoints of every pair are distinct, lie in the same parity class, and no endpoint lies in two pairs. Also, for \(e=\{u,w\}\),
\[
 0<t_e\leq\tau_u\leq\frac{a_u}{4},
 \qquad
 0<t_e\leq\tau_w\leq\frac{a_w}{4}.
\tag{17}
\]
If \(z\in Y_2\), endpoint-disjointness makes its degree equal to \(t_{e(z)}\), so (17) gives
\[
 d_z(t)=t_{e(z)}\leq\frac{a_z}{4}\leq\frac{a_z}{2}.
\tag{18}
\]
If \(z\notin Y_2\), its degree is zero. This proves every constraint in (6), simultaneously over all dyadic bins and every parity class used by \(\mathcal M_2\). Accepted fact `3016926516cc39df` also proves \(\sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}t_e>0\), so the matching is nonzero. Thus the weighted dual of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` applies.

By (4), \(p_u\in\mathcal K_u\) and \(p_w\in\mathcal K_w\). Therefore
\[
 \bar p_e=\frac{p_u+p_w}{2}
 \in\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_w)=L_e,
\tag{19}
\]
which is the first membership in (9). Under the explicit alias above, the point \(\bar p_e\) is exactly the point denoted \(q_e\) in accepted fact `3016926516cc39df`; that accepted fact proves
\[
 \bar p_e\in\mathsf M_{t_e}(C_{m_e}),
\tag{20}
\]
which is the second membership in (9). Neither (19) nor (20) selects one affine map across different edges. Both remain separate labelled assertions when midpoint atoms coincide.

It remains to prove the exact stress collapse. Grouping the endpoint sums one edge at a time and using endpoint-disjointness gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{y\in Y_2}U_y
 &=\sum_{e=\{u,w\}\in\mathcal M_2}
 \left(\frac12v_e+\frac12v_e\right)
 =\sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}v_e=0,\\
 \sum_{y\in Y_2}U_yy^{\mathsf T}
 &=\sum_{e=\{u,w\}\in\mathcal M_2}
 \left(\frac12v_eu^{\mathsf T}
       +\frac12v_ew^{\mathsf T}\right)\\
 &=\sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}
 v_e\left(\frac{u+w}{2}\right)^{\mathsf T}
 =\sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}v_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{21}
\]
These are global cancellations over the entire matching. They do not require, or assert, a separate cancellation inside each parity class or any larger grouping of components.

Accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` gives that every \(D_z\) is an origin-symmetric convex body and defines \(D_e=(D_u+D_w)/2\). Support functions are positively homogeneous and additive under Minkowski sums. Hence, for every edge \(e=\{u,w\}\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sigma_u+\sigma_w
 &=h_{D_u}\!\left(\frac12v_e\right)
   +h_{D_w}\!\left(\frac12v_e\right)\\
 &=\frac12h_{D_u}(v_e)+\frac12h_{D_w}(v_e)\\
 &=h_{(D_u+D_w)/2}(v_e)
 =h_{D_e}(v_e)=\omega_e.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{22}
\]
If \(v_e=0\), every term in (22) is zero because a support function vanishes at the zero vector, so zero stress components and the all-zero feasible stress are included. Summing (22) over all endpoint-disjoint edges counts every matched endpoint exactly once and yields
\[
 T_*=\sum_{e=\{u,w\}\in\mathcal M_2}
 (\sigma_u+\sigma_w)
 =\sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}\omega_e
 =\Omega.
\tag{23}
\]
No primal optimizer, dual optimality, circuit decomposition, or load-minimal choice is used in (21)--(23). The equality is lossless precisely because each endpoint has one incident edge, so the subadditivity step in the general endpoint collapse is absent. This proves (13) and completes the proof.
