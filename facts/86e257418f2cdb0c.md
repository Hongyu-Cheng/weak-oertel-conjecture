## Statement

Let $P$ be a fixed finite nonempty lattice-complete base. Let $K_{j,z}\subset\mathbb R^d$ be nonempty compact convex fibers, $d\ge1$, with $\sum_w\lambda_wK_{j,w}\subseteq K_{j,z}$ for every anchored representation of $z$ and $\operatorname{diam}K_{j,z}\le D$. Choose arbitrary representatives $c_j(z)\in K_{j,z}$. For the anchored vertex matrix $T_P$ with $m$ nonzero rows and least positive singular value $\sigma_P$ when $T_P\ne0$, the Euclidean array distance satisfies
$$
\operatorname{dist}\bigl(c_j,L(P)\otimes\mathbb R^d\bigr)\le D\sqrt m/\sigma_P,
$$
with distance zero if $T_P=0$. Let $a_j$ be the orthogonal projection onto $A(P)\otimes\mathbb R^d$ and suppose $R_j=\|c_j-a_j\|_{\rm F}\to\infty$. The normalized residuals have cluster points, and each is a unit element of $(L(P)\otimes\mathbb R^d)\cap(A(P)\otimes\mathbb R^d)^\perp$. If $\operatorname{relint}(\operatorname{conv}P)\cap P\ne\varnothing$, all such ordinary-affine residuals are bounded.

## Proof

For an anchored row at $z$, both $c_j(z)$ and $\sum_w\lambda_wc_j(w)$ belong to $K_{j,z}$ by the inclusion. Thus the row norm is at most $D$. Summing squares gives $\|T_Pc_j\|_{\rm F}\le D\sqrt m$. Apply the smallest positive singular value to each coordinate of the orthogonal projection onto the complement of the kernel, and use $\ker T_P=L(P)$ from the anchored-face and matrix lemma. When $T_P=0$ the kernel is the full array space.

Affine functions preserve all anchored representations, so $T_Pa_j=0$. Thus $\|T_P(c_j-a_j)/R_j\|_{\rm F}\le D\sqrt m/R_j\to0$. Compactness of the finite-dimensional unit sphere and closedness of the orthogonal complement give the asserted unit limits and their membership in the kernel. If the hull has a relative-interior site, the anchored-face and matrix lemma gives $L(P)=A(P)$, so the same quantitative bound applies directly to $\|c_j-a_j\|_{\rm F}$.
