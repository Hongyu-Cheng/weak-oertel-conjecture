## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), and retain the exact canonical hard pooled KKT system, objects, normalizations, and alias maps of accepted facts 236e5d1f3c2efa28 and 637b5f7874d8c875. The canonical edge set is finite. For each edge \(e\), let \(m_e\in\mathbb R^n\) be its midpoint and let \(L_e\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) be its canonical midpoint target body. Let \(r=(r_e)\) be the half-strength canonical product reservoir, and let \(\omega=(\omega_e)\) be the original load-minimal KKT load, so that

\[
0\leq\omega_e\leq r_e,
\qquad
Q:=\sum_e r_e>0,
\qquad
\Omega:=\sum_e\omega_e.
\tag{1}
\]

For every nonnegative edge field \(q=(q_e)\), write

\[
R_C(q)
:=
\max_{s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s(m_e)\in L_e}}q_e,
\qquad
R:=R_C(r).
\tag{2}
\]

The maximum in (2) is the canonical exactly affine-retainable mass. Let \(D\) and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) be the other two capacities in the accepted split ledger. Retain the canonical target multipliers \(0<\chi_m\leq1\), and put

\[
\lambda_e:=1-\chi_{m_e},
\qquad
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}:=\lambda_e\omega_e,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{cong}}:=\sum_e\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{3}
\]

Thus

\[
0\leq\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\leq\omega_e\leq r_e.
\tag{4}
\]

Define

\[
C_\beta
:=
\begin{cases}
0,&B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0,\\
R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}),&B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0.
\end{cases}
\tag{5}
\]

Let \(A\) and \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}\) have exactly the branchwise meanings of accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875: \(A=\max\{P_{\mathrm{pool}},P_{\mathrm{anc}}\}\), with \(P_{\mathrm{anc}}=0\) and no ancestral support or rank when \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\); and \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}=0\), with no fresh support or rank, when the fresh load \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}\) is zero. If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), use the accepted convention \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\).

Retain also the original same-ray negative-circuit decomposition of accepted fact 236e5d1f3c2efa28. Thus there are nonnegative coefficients \(\omega_e^{(j)}\), vectors \(v_e^{(j)}\in\mathbb R^d\), and nonempty supports

\[
C_j:=\{e:\omega_e^{(j)}>0\},
\qquad 1\leq j\leq N,
\tag{6}
\]

such that

\[
\sum_{j=1}^N\omega_e^{(j)}=\omega_e,
\qquad
v_e^{(j)}=0\quad(e\notin C_j),
\qquad
\sum_e v_e^{(j)}=0,
\qquad
\sum_e v_e^{(j)}m_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
\qquad
\sum_{e\in C_j}h_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)})<0.
\tag{7}
\]

Define the original-reservoir support lift

\[
\mathcal U_\beta
:=
\max_{s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
\sum_{\substack{e:\ \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0\\
s(m_e)\in L_e}}r_e.
\tag{8}
\]

Then the maximum in (8) is attained, and

\[
\boxed{C_\beta\leq\mathcal U_\beta\leq R.}
\tag{9}
\]

Moreover, the accepted three-summand ledger strengthens to

\[
\boxed{
D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
\geq
A+\max\{Q-\Omega,\mathcal U_\beta\}
+\max\{A,F_{\mathrm{fr}}\}.}
\tag{10}
\]

Suppose now that \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), including the case \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\) under the conventions above. Then there is one affine map \(s_0:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) such that

\[
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
s_0(m_e)\in L_e
\tag{11}
\]

for every edge \(e\), and

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal U_\beta
=\sum_{\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0}r_e.}
\tag{12}
\]

For

\[
T_0:=\{e:s_0(m_e)\notin L_e\},
\tag{13}
\]

the following assertions hold simultaneously:

\[
T_0\cap C_j\neq\varnothing
\qquad(1\leq j\leq N),
\tag{14}
\]

\[
e\in T_0,\ \omega_e>0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\chi_{m_e}=1,
\tag{15}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{\substack{e\in T_0\\\chi_{m_e}=1}}\omega_e
\geq\Omega-R
>\frac{98Q}{100}.}
\tag{16}
\]

When \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), the support in (8) and (12) is empty, so \(\mathcal U_\beta=0\); no ancestral or fresh support rank is introduced. When \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\) but \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), no fresh support rank is introduced.

## Proof

We first audit attainment and the support lift. Because the canonical edge set is finite, an affine map \(s\) determines one of only finitely many subsets

\[
\{e:\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0,\ s(m_e)\in L_e\}.
\]

The corresponding objective in (8) is the sum of the fixed numbers \(r_e\) over that subset. At least one affine map exists, so the finite collection of attained objective values has a largest element. Thus the maximum in (8) is attained, including when its support is empty.

Accepted fact 236e5d1f3c2efa28, with the congestion aliases used in accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875, gives the attained exact affine-retention identity

\[
B_{\mathrm{cong}}-R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}})
=
\min_{s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s(m_e)\notin L_e}}
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{17}
\]

For every affine \(s\), the hit and missed edges partition the finite edge set, so

\[
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s(m_e)\in L_e}}
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
=
B_{\mathrm{cong}}
-
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s(m_e)\notin L_e}}
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{18}
\]

Combining (5), (17), and (18), and using the zero-charge convention when \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), yields

\[
C_\beta
=
\max_s
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s(m_e)\in L_e}}
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
=
\max_s
\sum_{\substack{e:\ \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0\\
s(m_e)\in L_e}}
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{19}
\]

For every affine \(s\), (4) gives

\[
\sum_{\substack{e:\ \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0\\
s(m_e)\in L_e}}
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
\leq
\sum_{\substack{e:\ \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0\\
s(m_e)\in L_e}}r_e.
\tag{20}
\]

Taking maxima in (20) and using (19) proves \(C_\beta\leq\mathcal U_\beta\). For every affine \(s\), nonnegativity of \(r_e\) also gives

\[
\sum_{\substack{e:\ \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0\\
s(m_e)\in L_e}}r_e
\leq
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s(m_e)\in L_e}}r_e
\leq R.
\tag{21}
\]

Taking the maximum in (21) proves \(\mathcal U_\beta\leq R\), completing (9).

Accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 proves the three independent capacity bounds

\[
D\geq A,
\qquad
R\geq Q-\Omega,
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq\max\{A,F_{\mathrm{fr}}\}.
\tag{22}
\]

The second inequality in (9) and the middle inequality in (22) give

\[
R\geq\max\{Q-\Omega,\mathcal U_\beta\}.
\tag{23}
\]

Adding the lower bounds for the three distinct capacities \(D\), \(R\), and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) in (22)-(23) proves (10). No two terms in (10) are charged to the same capacity.

Assume now \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\). Accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 gives

\[
C_\beta=B_{\mathrm{cong}},
\qquad
F_{\mathrm{fr}}=0.
\tag{24}
\]

If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), choose an affine map \(s_0\) attaining the maximum in (19). Equations (19) and (24) give

\[
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s_0(m_e)\in L_e}}
\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
=\sum_e\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{25}
\]

All summands are nonnegative, so (25) implies (11). If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), every \(\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\) is zero and any affine map may be chosen as \(s_0\); then (11) is vacuous. Thus (11) holds in both cases. The map \(s_0\) retains every edge in the support of \(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}\), so its objective in (8) is the full sum in (12). No objective in (8) can exceed that full sum. This proves (12), also when the support is empty.

Fix a circuit \(C_j\). Suppose for contradiction that \(s_0(m_e)\in L_e\) for every \(e\in C_j\). The support-function inequality then gives

\[
\sum_{e\in C_j}h_{L_e}(v_e^{(j)})
\geq
\sum_{e\in C_j}
\langle v_e^{(j)},s_0(m_e)\rangle.
\tag{26}
\]

Affine-linearity of \(s_0\) and the two moment cancellations in (7) imply

\[
\sum_{e\in C_j}
\langle v_e^{(j)},s_0(m_e)\rangle=0.
\tag{27}
\]

Here terms outside \(C_j\) vanish, so the sums over \(C_j\) may equivalently be taken over the whole edge set. Equations (26)-(27) contradict the strict negative circuit objective in (7). Hence \(T_0\cap C_j\neq\varnothing\), proving (14). Because \(\omega_e^{(j)}>0\) on \(C_j\) and \(\sum_j\omega_e^{(j)}=\omega_e\), every edge of \(C_j\), including any edge in \(T_0\cap C_j\), has \(\omega_e>0\).

Let \(e\in T_0\) have \(\omega_e>0\). By (11), a missed edge cannot have positive congestion charge, so

\[
0=\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
=(1-\chi_{m_e})\omega_e.
\tag{28}
\]

Since \(\omega_e>0\), equation (28) gives \(\chi_{m_e}=1\), proving (15). This argument also covers \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), when (28) holds for every edge.

All positive-load edges in \(T_0\) are therefore lossless, while zero-load edges do not affect the sum. Consequently,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{\substack{e\in T_0\\\chi_{m_e}=1}}\omega_e
&=\sum_{e\in T_0}\omega_e\\
&=\Omega-
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s_0(m_e)\in L_e}}\omega_e\\
&\geq\Omega-
\sum_{\substack{e:\ s_0(m_e)\in L_e}}r_e\\
&\geq\Omega-R.
\end{aligned}
\tag{29}
\]

The canonical hard ledger retained in accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 has nonnegative summands and gives

\[
R<\frac Q{100},
\qquad
\Omega>\frac{99Q}{100}.
\tag{30}
\]

Therefore

\[
\Omega-R>\frac{99Q}{100}-\frac Q{100}
=\frac{98Q}{100}.
\tag{31}
\]

Equations (29)-(31) prove (16).

Finally, if \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), nonnegativity of the charge gives empty charged support, so \(\mathcal U_\beta=0\); the branch conventions in accepted fact 637b5f7874d8c875 set \(P_{\mathrm{anc}}=F_{\mathrm{fr}}=0\) and leave all ancestral and fresh ranks undefined. Equation (10) then reduces, using \(Q-\Omega\geq0\), to

\[
D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
\geq2P_{\mathrm{pool}}+Q-\Omega,
\]

without an empty-support rank. If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\) but the fresh load is zero, the proof uses no fresh rank. This completes every zero-charge and zero-fresh boundary case.
