## Statement

Let \(n\geq2\) and \(d\geq1\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put
\[
 C_w:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(w,x)\in C\},
 \qquad A_w:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_w),
 \qquad \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\tag{1}
\]
For a convex body \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) and \(0<m\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(K)\), use the canonical metronoid
\[
 \mathsf M_m(K):=
 \left\{
 \frac1m\int_Kx f(x)\,dx:
 0\leq f\leq1\ \text{a.e.},\quad \int_Kf=m
 \right\}.
\]
For \((w,x)\in C\), define
\[
 \delta_C(w,x):=
 \inf_{\substack{J\subseteq\mathbb R^d\ \text{closed halfspace}\\x\in J}}
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_w\cap J).
\]
For an ambient closed halfspace \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\), put
\[
 \mu_C(H):=
 \sum_{w\in\mathbb Z^n}
 \operatorname{vol}_d\{x\in C_w:(w,x)\in H\},
\]
and define
\[
 h_{\max}(C):=
 \sup_{p\in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)}
 \inf_{\substack{H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\ \text{closed halfspace}\\p\in H}}
 \mu_C(H).
\]
Use the positive-load stress/contact notation supplied by accepted fact `3f9592d2fb367884`. Thus \(q=(q_e)\) is an off-diagonal same-parity fractional matching with
\[
 d_w(q):=\sum_{e\ni w}q_e\leq\gamma_dA_w,
\tag{2}
\]
\(F_\omega\) is the set of loaded edges, and, for every loaded edge \(e=\{w,w'\}\), there are positive incidence loads and endpoint contacts satisfying
\[
 \lambda_{e,w}+\lambda_{e,w'}=\omega_e\leq q_e,
 \qquad
 x_{e,w}\in\mathsf M_{A_w/2}(C_w),
 \qquad
 x_{e,w'}\in\mathsf M_{A_{w'}/2}(C_{w'}).
\tag{3}
\]
At every loaded endpoint \(w\), put
\[
 t_w:=\sum_{e\in F_\omega:e\ni w}\lambda_{e,w},
 \qquad
 \widetilde p_w:=\frac1{t_w}
 \sum_{e\in F_\omega:e\ni w}\lambda_{e,w}x_{e,w}.
\tag{4}
\]
Then `3f9592d2fb367884` gives
\[
 0<t_w\leq\gamma_dA_w\leq\frac{A_w}{2},
 \qquad
 \widetilde p_w\in\mathsf M_{t_w}(C_w).
\tag{5}
\]

Fix a loaded endpoint \(z\). Choose two disjoint families of distinct loaded edges
\[
 e_i=\{z,a_i\}\quad(1\leq i\leq r),
 \qquad
 f_j=\{z,b_j\}\quad(1\leq j\leq s),
\tag{6}
\]
where \(r,s\geq1\), \(r+s=n\), and the integer half-directions
\[
 h_i:=\frac{a_i-z}{2}\quad(1\leq i\leq r),
 \qquad
 k_j:=\frac{b_j-z}{2}\quad(1\leq j\leq s)
\tag{7}
\]
are jointly linearly independent. Set
\[
 t:=t_z,
 \qquad
 \lambda_i:=\lambda_{e_i,z},
 \qquad
 \mu_j:=\lambda_{f_j,z},
 \qquad
 \lambda_0:=\mu_0:=t.
\tag{8}
\]

Let
\[
 V_1:=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{h_1,\ldots,h_r\},
 \qquad
 V_2:=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{k_1,\ldots,k_s\}.
\tag{9}
\]
Choose matrices \(B_1\in\mathbb Z^{n\times r}\) and \(B_2\in\mathbb Z^{n\times s}\) whose columns are lattice bases of the saturated lattices \(V_1\cap\mathbb Z^n\) and \(V_2\cap\mathbb Z^n\), respectively. Write
\[
 h_i=B_1v_i\quad(v_i\in\mathbb Z^r),
 \qquad
 k_j=B_2w_j\quad(w_j\in\mathbb Z^s),
 \qquad
 v_0:=0,\quad w_0:=0.
\tag{10}
\]
Then
\[
 \Phi(u,v):=z+B_1u+B_2v
\tag{11}
\]
is an integral injective full-rank affine chart, and each factor block is primitive in the precise sense
\[
 B_1\mathbb Z^r=V_1\cap\mathbb Z^n,
 \qquad
 B_2\mathbb Z^s=V_2\cap\mathbb Z^n.
\tag{12}
\]
No saturation of the concatenated full-rank sublattice is asserted or needed.

Define the product-vertex points
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 X_{00}&:=\widetilde p_z,\\
 X_{i0}&:=\frac{x_{e_i,z}+x_{e_i,a_i}}2 &&(1\leq i\leq r),\\
 X_{0j}&:=\frac{x_{f_j,z}+x_{f_j,b_j}}2 &&(1\leq j\leq s),\\
 X_{ij}&:=\frac{x_{e_i,a_i}+x_{f_j,b_j}}2
 &&(1\leq i\leq r,\ 1\leq j\leq s).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
For every \(0\leq i\leq r\) and \(0\leq j\leq s\), put
\[
 m_{ij}:=\frac{\lambda_i\mu_j}{2t}.
\tag{14}
\]
Then all these masses are positive and the exact individual metronoid memberships are
\[
 \boxed{
 X_{ij}\in\mathsf M_{m_{ij}}
 \bigl(C_{\Phi(v_i,w_j)}\bigr)
 \qquad(0\leq i\leq r,\ 0\leq j\leq s).}
\tag{15}
\]
In particular, the four types of masses are
\[
 m_{00}=\frac t2,
 \qquad
 m_{i0}=\frac{\lambda_i}{2},
 \qquad
 m_{0j}=\frac{\mu_j}{2},
 \qquad
 m_{ij}=\frac{\lambda_i\mu_j}{2t}.
\tag{16}
\]
Moreover, the endpoint densities used to establish all memberships in (15) can be allocated simultaneously in each source fiber with pointwise total at most one. This is only a source-density statement; (15) asserts the resulting target metronoid memberships individually.

Put
\[
 \Delta_1:=\operatorname{conv}\{0,v_1,\ldots,v_r\},
 \qquad
 \Delta_2:=\operatorname{conv}\{0,w_1,\ldots,w_s\},
\tag{17}
\]
\[
 \mathcal D_1:=\Delta_1\cap\mathbb Z^r,
 \qquad
 \mathcal D_2:=\Delta_2\cap\mathbb Z^s,
\tag{18}
\]
and let \(\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_r\) and \(\beta_0,\ldots,\beta_s\) be the barycentric-coordinate functions on \(\Delta_1\) and \(\Delta_2\). Define the unscaled factor energies
\[
 \mathcal A_1:=
 \sum_{u\in\mathcal D_1}
 \left(\sum_{i=0}^r\alpha_i(u)\lambda_i^{1/d}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_2:=
 \sum_{v\in\mathcal D_2}
 \left(\sum_{j=0}^s\beta_j(v)\mu_j^{1/d}\right)^d.
\tag{19}
\]
There is an admissible full-rank two-factor lattice-polysimplex packet \(\Pi\), in the exact sense of accepted fact `5b6523682250e7a5`, with product-vertex points (13), chart (11), and exact energy
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal E(\Pi)=\frac{\gamma_d}{2t}\mathcal A_1\mathcal A_2.}
\tag{20}
\]
Its interaction dimension in the normalization of accepted fact `674b50aa42f420d8` satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\chi(\Pi)+1=rs+1.}
\tag{21}
\]
The exact packet-depth theorem `5b6523682250e7a5` also gives
\[
 \boxed{h_{\max}(C)\geq2^{-n}\mathcal E(\Pi).}
\tag{22}
\]

If the loaded half-directions at \(z\) span \(\mathbb R^n\), one may choose \(n\) independent directions and split them with \(r=1\) and \(s=n-1\). This gives one packet of the preceding form with
\[
 \chi(\Pi)+1=n.
\tag{23}
\]
This is a one-star statement only; it does not sum packet energies from different stars.

Finally, every mixed second difference is exactly
\[
 \boxed{
 X_{00}-X_{i0}-X_{0j}+X_{ij}
 =\widetilde p_z-\frac{x_{e_i,z}+x_{f_j,z}}2.}
\tag{24}
\]

## Proof

All properties in (1)--(5), including \(\omega_e\leq q_e\), the endpoint contacts, the incidence loads, and the pooled point \(\widetilde p_z\), come from accepted fact `3f9592d2fb367884` with exactly the same normalization. First note that
\[
 \gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}\leq\frac12,
\tag{26}
\]
because \((1+1/d)^d\geq2\).

We first prove the source-density assertion and the metronoid memberships. For every loaded incidence \((e,w)\), choose a half-mass witness for its contact:
\[
 0\leq f_{e,w}\leq\mathbf1_{C_w},
 \qquad
 \int f_{e,w}=\frac{A_w}{2},
 \qquad
 \frac2{A_w}\int x f_{e,w}(x)\,dx=x_{e,w}.
\tag{27}
\]
At the common source fiber \(z\), define
\[
 G_z:=\sum_{e\in F_\omega:e\ni z}
 \frac{2\lambda_{e,z}}{A_z}f_{e,z}.
\tag{28}
\]
Then
\[
 0\leq G_z\leq\frac{2t}{A_z}\leq2\gamma_d\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int G_z=t,
 \qquad
 \int xG_z(x)\,dx=t\widetilde p_z.
\tag{29}
\]
Allocate at \(z\) the source densities
\[
 g_{00,z}:=\frac12G_z,
 \qquad
 g_{i0,z}:=\frac{\lambda_i}{A_z}f_{e_i,z},
 \qquad
 g_{0j,z}:=\frac{\mu_j}{A_z}f_{f_j,z}.
\tag{30}
\]
Because the two selected edge families are disjoint subsets of all loaded edges at \(z\),
\[
 g_{00,z}+\sum_{i=1}^rg_{i0,z}+\sum_{j=1}^sg_{0j,z}
 \leq\frac12G_z+\frac12G_z=G_z\leq1.
\tag{31}
\]
Their respective masses and barycenters are \((t/2,\widetilde p_z)\), \((\lambda_i/2,x_{e_i,z})\), and \((\mu_j/2,x_{f_j,z})\).

For \(i,j>0\), put
\[
 \theta_{ij}:=\frac{\lambda_i\mu_j}{t}.
\tag{32}
\]
At the source fiber \(a_i\), allocate
\[
 g_{i0,a_i}:=\frac{\lambda_i}{A_{a_i}}f_{e_i,a_i},
 \qquad
 g_{ij,a_i}:=\frac{\theta_{ij}}{A_{a_i}}f_{e_i,a_i}
 \quad(1\leq j\leq s).
\tag{33}
\]
Since \(\sum_j\mu_j\leq t\), one has \(\sum_j\theta_{ij}\leq\lambda_i\). Also
\[
 \lambda_i\leq\omega_{e_i}\leq q_{e_i}
 \leq d_{a_i}(q)\leq\gamma_dA_{a_i}.
\tag{34}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 g_{i0,a_i}+\sum_{j=1}^sg_{ij,a_i}
 \leq\frac{\lambda_i+\sum_j\theta_{ij}}{A_{a_i}}
 \leq\frac{2\lambda_i}{A_{a_i}}
 \leq2\gamma_d\leq1.
\tag{35}
\]
These densities have respective masses \(\lambda_i/2\) and \(\theta_{ij}/2\), all with barycenter \(x_{e_i,a_i}\). The symmetric construction at \(b_j\), using \(\sum_i\lambda_i\leq t\), gives simultaneous densities of masses \(\mu_j/2\) and \(\theta_{ij}/2\), all with barycenter \(x_{f_j,b_j}\), and pointwise total at most one. This proves the claimed source-density legality without adding capacities of unrelated witnesses.

The density \(g_{00,z}\) directly proves
\[
 X_{00}=\widetilde p_z\in\mathsf M_{t/2}(C_z).
\tag{36}
\]
For an axis vertex \((i,0)\), the two endpoint densities in (30) and (33) have equal mass \(\lambda_i/2\) and barycenters \(x_{e_i,z}\) and \(x_{e_i,a_i}\). Accepted fact `dfd25e72c1f45a4e`, applied at the equal positive root \((\lambda_i/2)^{1/d}\), puts their midpoint barycenter in
\[
 \mathsf M_{\lambda_i/2}
 \left(C_{(z+a_i)/2}\right).
\tag{37}
\]
The proof of that accepted fact establishes convexity of the positive-root generating set itself, so (37) is exact metronoid membership and not merely membership in its closure.
The same argument gives the \((0,j)\) membership with mass \(\mu_j/2\). For a cross vertex \((i,j)\), the endpoint densities at \(a_i\) and \(b_j\) have equal mass
\[
 \frac{\theta_{ij}}2=\frac{\lambda_i\mu_j}{2t}
\tag{38}
\]
and barycenters \(x_{e_i,a_i}\) and \(x_{f_j,b_j}\). Another application of `dfd25e72c1f45a4e` gives the cross membership at \((a_i+b_j)/2\). These arguments prove (15)--(16). They use positive-root interpolation separately for each target vertex and do not assert an additional coupled target-density theorem.

We next verify the chart. Same parity in every loaded edge makes all vectors in (7) integral. Their joint independence and \(r+s=n\) give
\[
 V_1\oplus V_2=\mathbb R^n.
\tag{39}
\]
Each \(V_\ell\) is rational, and \(V_\ell\cap\mathbb Z^n\) is saturated. Thus the lattice bases in (10) exist and give (12). If
\[
 B_1(u-u')+B_2(v-v')=0,
\]
the direct sum (39) forces both summands to vanish; the lattice-basis matrices have full column rank, so \(u=u'\) and \(v=v'\). Hence \(\Phi\) is injective and its linear part has rank \(n\). Moreover,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Phi(v_0,w_0)&=z,\\
 \Phi(v_i,w_0)&=z+h_i=\frac{z+a_i}{2},\\
 \Phi(v_0,w_j)&=z+k_j=\frac{z+b_j}{2},\\
 \Phi(v_i,w_j)&=z+h_i+k_j=\frac{a_i+b_j}{2}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{40}
\]
Thus (13) and (15) use exactly the distinct product vertices of the integral injective chart. In particular, there are no product-vertex midpoint collisions.

Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` turns (15) into the vertex-depth estimates
\[
 \delta_C\bigl(\Phi(v_i,w_j),X_{ij}\bigr)
 \geq\gamma_dm_{ij}
 =\frac{\gamma_d\lambda_i\mu_j}{2t}.
\tag{41}
\]
Choose the two factor-root arrays
\[
 \rho_{1,i}:=\lambda_i^{1/d}\quad(0\leq i\leq r),
 \qquad
 \rho_{2,j}:=\left(\frac{\gamma_d\mu_j}{2t}\right)^{1/d}
 \quad(0\leq j\leq s).
\tag{42}
\]
Then (41) is exactly
\[
 \delta_C\bigl(\Phi(v_i,w_j),X_{ij}\bigr)^{1/d}
 \geq \rho_{1,i}\rho_{2,j},
\tag{43}
\]
the product-vertex hypothesis of accepted fact `5b6523682250e7a5`. Applying that theorem to the two full-dimensional lattice simplices (17) and the full-rank chart (11) produces the asserted admissible packet and gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal E(\Pi)
 &=\left[
 \sum_{u\in\mathcal D_1}
 \left(\sum_{i=0}^r\alpha_i(u)\lambda_i^{1/d}\right)^d
 \right]
 \left[
 \sum_{v\in\mathcal D_2}
 \left(\sum_{j=0}^s\beta_j(v)
 \left(\frac{\gamma_d\mu_j}{2t}\right)^{1/d}
 \right)^d
 \right]\\
 &=\frac{\gamma_d}{2t}\mathcal A_1\mathcal A_2.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{44}
\]
This proves (20), and the same accepted packet-depth theorem gives (22) because the total packet rank is \(r+s=n\).

In the interaction-dimension normalization of accepted fact `674b50aa42f420d8`, a two-factor packet of ranks \(r,s\) has
\[
 \chi(\Pi)
 =(r+1)(s+1)-1-(r+s)=rs,
\tag{45}
\]
which proves (21). If the loaded half-directions span \(\mathbb R^n\), choose \(n\) independent ones and split them into families of sizes \(1\) and \(n-1\). Equation (21) then gives (23). This extraction uses one star and one chart; it does not combine energies belonging to different stars.

Finally, direct expansion of (13) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 X_{00}-X_{i0}-X_{0j}+X_{ij}
 &=\widetilde p_z
 -\frac{x_{e_i,z}+x_{e_i,a_i}}2
 -\frac{x_{f_j,z}+x_{f_j,b_j}}2
 +\frac{x_{e_i,a_i}+x_{f_j,b_j}}2\\
 &=\widetilde p_z-\frac{x_{e_i,z}+x_{f_j,z}}2,
 \end{aligned}
\]
which proves (24).
