## Statement

There is a nondegenerate planar triangle with an interior mark and canonical balanced cuts strictly isolating its three distinct vertices for which the ordinary vector $f$ equals $(7/4,1/2,1/2)$ and $\sum_i f_i^2+\prod_i f_i=4$, while its period-three sector compression $N$ is positive definite. Write $r_{ij}=-N_{ij}/\sqrt{N_{ii}N_{jj}}$. No assignment of the three numbers $f_i/2$ to the edges of the three-node compression makes every assigned number at most the corresponding $r_{ij}$.

## Proof

Use the origin and the counterclockwise vertices
$$
(87/100,-49/100),\qquad (-1/200,1/100),\qquad (-1/200,-23/200).
$$
The determinants of consecutive vertices are $1/160,1/1600,41/400$, all positive, so the origin lies strictly inside. The signs of $x,y,-x-y$ give the three strictly isolated vertices in the displayed order. Solving the ray-edge intersections gives the following radii, and taking areas of the core triangles and the sectors with one vertex gives the corresponding areas:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=(1/80,1/140,1/200,1/200,41/350,41/200),\\
W&=(1/22400,17/560000,1/80000,13/22400,1681/140000,3361/80000).
\end{aligned}
$$
These computations can be obtained by putting $R_i=\det(A,B-A)/\det(e_i,B-A)$ on the edge $[A,B]$ hit by the ray $e_i$. A double sector is the core of area $R_iR_{i+1}/2$. In a unique sector with vertex $V$, the area is $(\det(R_i e_i,V)+\det(V,R_{i+1}e_{i+1}))/2$.

The full area is $k=7/128$. Summing the appropriate three sectors per cap gives $q=(7569/140000,7/80000,63/5000)$, and the cutting chord lengths divided by normal length are $\sigma=(87/700,7/400,21/100)$. For the third normal the length factor of the ray $(-1,1)$ cancels the normal length $\sqrt2$. Thus $M=\sum_i q_i/\sigma_i=1/2$ and $f_i=2\sqrt{kq_i}/(\sigma_iM)$ gives the asserted vector and cubic equality. For the signed-offset kernel test, the ratio $b_{12}b_{23}b_{31}/(b_{13}b_{32}b_{21})$ is $874/49$. The period-three compression theorem therefore gives $N$ positive definite. As a direct arithmetic check, its leading principal minors from the displayed radii and areas are $844802/1092325$, $164990791/18569525$ and $9338175/2971124$.

Let $B$ be $N$ normalized by positive diagonal congruence to have unit diagonal. It is positive definite with off-diagonal entries $-r_{ij}$. For any edge assignment let $C$ have diagonal $1$ and the negatives of the assigned $f_i/2$ off the diagonal. If the proposed entrywise domination held, then for every real $w\ne0$,
$$
w^\top Cw\ \ge\ |w|^\top C|w|\ \ge\ |w|^\top B|w|\ >\ 0,
$$
where $|w|$ is taken coordinatewise. The first inequality uses the nonpositive off-diagonal entries, and the second follows from entrywise comparison on nonnegative vectors. But $\det C=1-(\sum_i f_i^2+\prod_i f_i)/4=0$ for every assignment, a contradiction.
