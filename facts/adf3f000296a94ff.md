## Statement

Let $K_i=[l_i,r_i]$ be positive intervals on consecutive integer bases, with convex lower endpoints and concave upper endpoints. Assume their true centroid for the sum of interval lengths is $(0,0)$. Suppose a nonvertical through-centroid cap, in coordinates sheared along the base and reflected if needed so that the cap is $x\ge0$, properly cuts only $i=0$, has no endpoint coincidences and captures at least one exterior tail in full. Then its mixed length is at least $33M/98$, where $M=\sum_i(r_i-l_i)$.

## Proof

The shear $x\mapsto x+bi$ and reflection preserve interval lengths, concavity of the upper endpoints, convexity of the lower endpoints, and the centroid at the origin. Put $w_i=r_i-l_i$ and $Q=\sum_i w_i^2$. The centroid conditions are $\sum_i iw_i=0$ and $\sum_i w_i(l_i+r_i)=0$, so $\sum_i w_i r_i=Q/2$ and $\sum_i w_i l_i=-Q/2$. Jensen at the integer weighted mean gives $r_0\ge Q/(2M)>0$ and $l_0\le-Q/(2M)<0$. Thus the cap at $i=0$ contributes $r_0$.

The $w_i$ form a concave sequence. The open-tail theorem at mean zero gives at least $3(M-w_0)/7$ from the captured tail, in addition to $r_0$. With $p=w_0/M$ and $Q\ge w_0^2$, the cap fraction is at least
$$
\frac37(1-p)+\frac12p^2=\frac{33}{98}+\frac12(p-3/7)^2\ge\frac{33}{98}.
$$
