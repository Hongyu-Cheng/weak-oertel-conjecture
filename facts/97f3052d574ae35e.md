## Statement

Fix affinely independent bases $p_i$ with positive barycentric coefficients $\lambda_i$ of sum one, target base $j=\sum_i\lambda_i p_i$, and full-dimensional compact convex bodies $K_i\subset\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge1$. Put $D=\sum_i\lambda_i K_i$. Let $p_{\mathcal K}(y)$ be raw ambient closed-halfspace depth at $(j,y)$ for the sum of the unnormalized source $d$-volumes at the $p_i$, and let $f_D(y)$ be ordinary raw closed-halfspace depth in $D$. For every $y\in D$,
$$
f_D(y)\ge c_\lambda p_{\mathcal K}(y),\qquad
c_\lambda=\begin{cases}
(\sum_i\lambda_i^{d/(d-1)})^{d-1},&d>1,\\
\max_i\lambda_i,&d=1 .
\end{cases}
$$
In particular $c_\lambda=1/N$ for $N$ equally weighted sources in every dimension, so the two-source midpoint comparison is $p_{\mathcal K}(y)\le 2f_D(y)$.

## Proof

If the source depth is zero the inequality is immediate. Otherwise take $R=p_{\mathcal K}(y)^{1/d}$. By the accepted independent-array directional quantile criterion, $R\le\min_i|K_i|^{1/d}$ and, for every unit $u$ and every nonnegative allocation $\alpha$ with $\sum_i\alpha_i^d=1$,
$$
\sum_i\lambda_i q_{K_i,u}(R\alpha_i)\ge u\cdot y.
$$
The accepted criterion includes the full-source plateau. The quantile cap $E_i$ at this threshold is nonempty and has volume $(R\alpha_i)^d$. At zero allocation it is a supporting face, and at full allocation it is the entire body. Hence the Minkowski combination $\sum_i\lambda_i E_i$ is contained in $D\cap\{u\cdot x\ge u\cdot y\}$. Brunn–Minkowski for the nonempty compact convex summands gives its containing cap a volume root at least $R\sum_i\lambda_i\alpha_i$. Maximize over the allocations, which by Hölder duality gives $R\lVert\lambda\rVert_{d/(d-1)}$ when $d>1$ and $R\max_i\lambda_i$ when $d=1$. Raising to the $d$th power and taking the infimum over $u$ proves the comparison, since every containing cap contains its parallel through-mark cap.
