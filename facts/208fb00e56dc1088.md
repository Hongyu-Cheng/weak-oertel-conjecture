## Statement

Let $K$ be a compact convex $d$-body with $d\ge1$ and $L$ a finite nonnegative upper-semicontinuous concave function positive on its interior. Put $m=\max_KL$ and $H_d=\sum_{j=1}^d1/j$. Then for $0\le t\le1$,
$$
|\{L\ge tm\}|\ge (1-t)^d|K|,\qquad
\log m-H_d\le |K|^{-1}\int_K\log L\le\log m,\qquad
L(g_K)\ge m/(d+1).
$$
On a finite positive compatible fiber family, a jointly concave such weight has sampled maxima $m_z$ satisfying $m_p\ge\sum_z\lambda_z m_z$ whenever $p=\sum_z\lambda_z z$ is a sampled anchored combination. If the mixed mean of $\log L$ is zero, then $0\le\sum_z (V_z/M)\log m_z\le H_d$.

## Proof

Take $v\in K$ with $L(v)=m$. The superlevel at $tm$ contains $(1-t)K+tv$ by concavity and nonnegativity, proving the volume bound. For $s>0$ use $t=e^{-s}$ to bound the probability of $-\log(L/m)>s$ under normalized volume by $1-(1-e^{-s})^d$. Integrating this bound, using $t=e^{-s}$ and a finite geometric sum, gives
$$
|K|^{-1}\int_K-\log(L/m)\le
\int_0^1\frac{1-(1-t)^d}{t}\,dt=\sum_{j=1}^d1/j.
$$
The opposite log bound follows from $L\le m$. Integrating the superlevel bound over $t\in[0,1]$ gives mean $L$ at least $m/(d+1)$. A continuous-coordinate supporting supergradient at the interior centroid, integrated over $K$, proves Jensen's bound $L(g_K)\ge |K|^{-1}\int_K L$.

For an anchored combination, choose a maximizing point in each input fiber. Compatibility places their combination in the target fiber, and joint concavity gives the claimed inequality for the maxima. Multiply the log-integral bounds in each fiber by $V_z/M$ and sum. The mixed log normalization gives the asserted two-sided bound.
