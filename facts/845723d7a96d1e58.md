## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and retain all hypotheses and notation of the zero-fresh near-full lossless self-reproduction theorem `6bfe50fed9a5540b`. In particular, for the positive-volume fibers
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
\]
put
\[
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\]
There are a positive reservoir mass \(Q\), nonnegative target loads \(\lambda_m\), target contacts \(z_m\), lossless edge weights \(b_e\), endpoint degrees \(d_z(b)\), and endpoint-ancestor points \(p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\) such that
\[
\Lambda:=\sum_m\lambda_m=\sum_e b_e>\frac{49}{50}Q,
\tag{1}
\]
\[
z_m\in\mathsf M_{\lambda_m}(C_m)
\qquad(\lambda_m>0),
\tag{2}
\]
\[
d_z(b):=\sum_{e\ni z}b_e,
\qquad
p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\in\mathsf M_{2d_z(b)}(C_z)
\qquad(d_z(b)>0),
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
\sum_m\lambda_m(m,z_m)
=\frac12\sum_{z:d_z(b)>0}d_z(b)(z,p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}).
\tag{4}
\]
Here and below points in parentheses belong to \(\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\). Also
\[
\sum_zd_z(b)=2\sum_eb_e=2\Lambda.
\tag{5}
\]

The symbol \(\Lambda\) in this theorem is the target-load total in (1). To avoid a collision with the lattice symbol used in accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`, write
\[
\mathcal L:=\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d,
\qquad
S:=C\cap\mathcal L
\]
for the mixed lattice and its part in \(C\). Let \(\mu_C\) be the mixed-volume measure on \(S\). Retain the canonical capacities
\[
D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
\]
with the definitions of `be359f8a6c628483`. The hard ledger in `6bfe50fed9a5540b` implies
\[
D+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{6}
\]

Define the positive target and ancestor base sets
\[
E_{\mathrm{tar}}:=\{m:\lambda_m>0\},
\qquad
E_{\mathrm{anc}}:=\{z:d_z(b)>0\},
\tag{7}
\]
and the two atomic measures
\[
\eta_{\mathrm{tar}}
:=\gamma_d\sum_{m\in E_{\mathrm{tar}}}
\lambda_m\delta_{(m,z_m)},
\tag{8}
\]
\[
\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}
:=2\gamma_d\sum_{z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}}
d_z(b)\delta_{(z,p_z^{\mathrm{anc}})}.
\tag{9}
\]
Then both measures are nonzero, are carried by \(S\), and are halfspace-dominated by \(\mu_C\): for every closed halfspace \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\),
\[
\eta_{\mathrm{tar}}(H)\leq\mu_C(H),
\qquad
\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}(H)\leq\mu_C(H).
\tag{10}
\]
Their exact masses and first moments are
\[
|\eta_{\mathrm{tar}}|=\gamma_d\Lambda,
\qquad
|\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}|=4\gamma_d\Lambda,
\tag{11}
\]
\[
\int_Sp\,d\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}(p)
=4\int_Sp\,d\eta_{\mathrm{tar}}(p).
\tag{12}
\]
Equivalently, the two measures have the same normalized first moment:
\[
\frac1{|\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}|}
\int_Sp\,d\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}(p)
=
\frac1{|\eta_{\mathrm{tar}}|}
\int_Sp\,d\eta_{\mathrm{tar}}(p).
\tag{13}
\]

Because (1) makes both measures nonzero, define their intrinsic ranks by
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{tar}}),
\qquad
r_{\mathrm{tar}}
:=\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{tar}})\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
\dim\operatorname{span}\{z_m-s(m):m\in E_{\mathrm{tar}}\},
\tag{14}
\]
and
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{anc}}),
\qquad
r_{\mathrm{anc}}
:=\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{anc}})\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
\dim\operatorname{span}
\{p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}-s(z):z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}\}.
\tag{15}
\]
The minima are attained. Put
\[
P_{\mathrm{tar}}
:=\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}}
\frac{\Lambda}{r_{\mathrm{tar}}+1},
\qquad
P_{\mathrm{anc}}
:=4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}
\frac{\Lambda}{r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1}.
\tag{16}
\]
Then the strongest direct no-double-counting consequence for the two clouds is
\[
D\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
\geq\max\{P_{\mathrm{tar}},P_{\mathrm{anc}}\},
\tag{17}
\]
and hence
\[
2\max\{P_{\mathrm{tar}},P_{\mathrm{anc}}\}
\leq D+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{18}
\]
In particular,
\[
\boxed{
r_{\mathrm{tar}}+1
>200\gamma_d\frac{\Lambda}{Q}2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}}
>196\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}},}
\tag{19}
\]
\[
\boxed{
r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1
>800\gamma_d\frac{\Lambda}{Q}2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}
>784\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}.}
\tag{20}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
2^{\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}}(r_{\mathrm{tar}}+1)
>196\gamma_d2^n,
\qquad
2^{\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}(r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1)
>784\gamma_d2^n.
\tag{21}
\]
No sum \(P_{\mathrm{tar}}+P_{\mathrm{anc}}\) is asserted as a lower bound for a single capacity. No terminal absorption or convex filling is asserted.

## Proof

We first collect the exact consequences supplied by `6bfe50fed9a5540b`. Equation (1) is its target-load identity and near-full bound. Equations (2)-(4) are its target metronoid contact, ancestor endpoint metronoid membership, and exact target-to-source first-moment identity. Each unordered edge counted by \(b\) has two endpoints, so its degree identity gives (5). Its hard ledger is a strict sum of nonnegative capacities containing both \(D\) and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\); discarding the other terms gives (6).

For every \(m\in E_{\mathrm{tar}}\), apply the metronoid halfspace-depth theorem `6769574fc1bd9f8d` to (2). Every closed halfspace in \(\mathbb R^d\) containing \(z_m\) cuts from \(C_m\) volume at least
\[
\gamma_d\lambda_m.
\tag{22}
\]
Likewise, for every \(z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}\), apply that theorem to (3). Every closed halfspace in \(\mathbb R^d\) containing \(p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\) cuts from \(C_z\) volume at least
\[
2\gamma_dd_z(b).
\tag{23}
\]

Both clouds are supported in \(S\) because metronoids are subsets of their convex bodies. Let \(H\) be any closed ambient halfspace. If an atom \((m,z_m)\) of \(\eta_{\mathrm{tar}}\) lies in \(H\), then the fiber section \(H_m\) is either a proper closed halfspace containing \(z_m\) or the whole space. In the proper case, (22) bounds the atom weight by \(\operatorname{vol}_d(C_m\cap H_m)\). In the whole-space case, the same bound holds because \(0<\lambda_m\leq a_m\) and \(\gamma_d\leq1\). Summing over the distinct target bases whose atoms lie in \(H\) proves the first inequality in (10). The identical argument using (23) proves the second inequality. The whole ambient space is covered by the same calculation, and an empty halfspace contains no atom.

The first mass identity in (11) follows directly from (1) and (8). For the second, use (5):
\[
|\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}|
=2\gamma_d\sum_zd_z(b)
=4\gamma_d\Lambda.
\tag{24}
\]
Multiplying (4) by \(4\gamma_d\) gives
\[
4\gamma_d\sum_m\lambda_m(m,z_m)
=2\gamma_d\sum_zd_z(b)(z,p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}),
\]
which is exactly (12). Equations (11)-(12) and \(\Lambda>0\) give (13).

We now apply the generic rank machinery without adding the two cloud scores. For \(\eta_{\mathrm{tar}}\), its base and residual ranks in the dominated-measure theorem of `be359f8a6c628483` are exactly (14), and its mass is \(\gamma_d\Lambda\). Equations (10)-(11) therefore give
\[
D=2^nh_{\max}(C)
\geq
\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}}
\frac{\Lambda}{r_{\mathrm{tar}}+1}
=P_{\mathrm{tar}}.
\tag{25}
\]
Alternatively, choose an affine map attaining the minimum in (14). The target points \(z_m\), weights \(\gamma_d\lambda_m\), and this affine map are admissible in the definition of \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), by (22). Hence
\[
R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq P_{\mathrm{tar}}.
\tag{26}
\]

The same two applications to \(\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}\), using the mass \(4\gamma_d\Lambda\), the ranks in (15), and the admissible endpoint weights (23), give
\[
D\geq P_{\mathrm{anc}},
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq P_{\mathrm{anc}}.
\tag{27}
\]
The general selector-depth inequality in `be359f8a6c628483` also gives
\[
D=2^nh_{\max}(C)\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
\tag{28}
\]
Equations (26)-(28) prove (17). Notice that (17) uses the maximum of the two cloud scores. It does not sum two different measures inside \(D\) or inside \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\).

Combining (17) with (6) proves (18), so each score in (16) is strictly smaller than \(Q/200\). The target inequality gives
\[
r_{\mathrm{tar}}+1
>200\gamma_d\frac{\Lambda}{Q}2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{tar}}}.
\tag{29}
\]
The ancestor inequality gives
\[
r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1
>800\gamma_d\frac{\Lambda}{Q}2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}.
\tag{30}
\]
Finally, (1) turns the right sides of (29)-(30) into the strict constants \(196\gamma_d\) and \(784\gamma_d\), respectively. This proves (19)-(21). The proof uses only actual target-fiber contacts, their exact endpoint ancestors, and the two generic accepted mechanisms. It stops before any implication from high rank and ancestry to a terminal capacity or genuine convex filling.
