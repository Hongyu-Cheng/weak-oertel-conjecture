## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard synchronized-atlas instance of accepted facts `036a276c54ec5e66`, `2fe46f47d19112ea`, `d01de728d8b5e33c`, `5a1fe0ca819e2d1a`, `f571c88ebc6fa492`, and `f9a8ae959b19c4b1`. Thus the complete positive transverse atlas is a finite whole-label family
\[
 \Sigma=\{\sigma=(z(\sigma);e_\sigma,f_\sigma)\},
 \qquad
 P:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma}\theta_\sigma,
\tag{1}
\]
where every label has two distinct physical opposite endpoints
\(a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\), and the exact pooled opposite-endpoint masses are
\[
 \tau_y
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Sigma:\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\theta_\sigma.
\tag{2}
\]
Let \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{miss}}\) be the endpoint-disjoint missed dyadic block of accepted fact `2fe46f47d19112ea`, put
\[
 S:=S_{\mathrm{miss}}
 =\bigcup_{E\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{miss}}}E,
 \qquad
 L_{\mathrm{miss}}
 :=\sum_{E\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{miss}}}t_E,
\tag{3}
\]
and, for every \(y\in S\), let \(e_2(y)\) be its unique edge in this matching and put
\[
 \alpha_y:=\frac{t_{e_2(y)}}{\tau_y}.
\tag{4}
\]
The complete internal family and its synchronized weights are
\[
 \mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}
 :=\{\sigma\in\Sigma:
 a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\in S\},
\tag{5}
\]
\[
 \eta_\sigma
 :=\theta_\sigma
 \min\{\alpha_{a(\sigma)},\alpha_{b(\sigma)}\},
 \qquad
 \Theta_{\mathrm{int}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{6}
\]
Let
\[
 \Theta_{\bar S\bar S}
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Sigma:\\
 a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\notin S}}\theta_\sigma.
\tag{7}
\]
Then the exact whole-label deficit estimate is
\[
 \boxed{
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 \geq
 2L_{\mathrm{miss}}-P+\Theta_{\bar S\bar S}
 \geq2L_{\mathrm{miss}}-P.}
\tag{8}
\]
Consequently, the canonical reservoir and missed-block estimates imply
\[
 \boxed{
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 >\left(\frac9{100}-\frac1{50\gamma_d}\right)Q
 >\frac7{200}Q.}
\tag{9}

Now retain the deletion-stable full-base rank-five specialization in accepted fact `5a1fe0ca819e2d1a`. Thus, for the fixed load-minimal optimizer \(v\) used there,

 Y=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad

\tag{10}

and its synchronized atlas mass satisfies

\tag{12}
\]
In particular, for the registered subgoal, the alternative containing a legal \(Q/100\) selector always occurs. No assertion about the mass of bad labels is needed.

## Proof

Put
\[
 T_S:=\sum_{y\in S}\tau_y.
\tag{13}
\]
For each internal whole label \(\sigma\), choose once and for all one actual opposite endpoint
\[
 o(\sigma)\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}
\tag{14}
\]
at which the minimum in (6) is attained. Ties are resolved arbitrarily, but one endpoint is chosen and the label is not split. Then
\[
 \theta_\sigma-\eta_\sigma
 =\theta_\sigma(1-\alpha_{o(\sigma)}).
\tag{15}
\]

Fix one physical endpoint \(y\in S\). Because every label assigned to \(y\) is an actual full-atlas occurrence at \(y\), the exact pooled identity (2) gives
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}:\\
 o(\sigma)=y}}\theta_\sigma
 \leq
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Sigma:\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\theta_\sigma
 =\tau_y.
\tag{16}
\]
Multiplying (16) by the nonnegative number \(1-\alpha_y\), and using (4), yields the endpointwise no-reuse charge
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}:\\
 o(\sigma)=y}}
 (\theta_\sigma-\eta_\sigma)
 &=
 (1-\alpha_y)
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}:\\
 o(\sigma)=y}}\theta_\sigma\\
 &\leq(1-\alpha_y)\tau_y\\
 &=\tau_y-t_{e_2(y)}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]
Every deficit occurs on the left side of exactly one inequality (17). Summing over distinct physical endpoints therefore gives
\[
 \Theta_{\mathrm{int}}-H_{\mathrm{int}}
 \leq
 \sum_{y\in S}
 \bigl(\tau_y-t_{e_2(y)}\bigr).
\tag{18}
\]
The dyadic block is endpoint-disjoint, so each \(E\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{miss}}\) occurs exactly once at each of its two physical endpoints. Hence
\[
 \sum_{y\in S}t_{e_2(y)}=2L_{\mathrm{miss}},
\tag{19}
\]
and (18) becomes
\[
 \Theta_{\mathrm{int}}-H_{\mathrm{int}}
 \leq T_S-2L_{\mathrm{miss}}.
\tag{20}
\]

We next use the exact whole-label cut identity. Split the full atlas into labels with two, one, or zero opposite endpoints in \(S\), and denote the middle mass by \(\Theta_{S\bar S}\). Counting physical endpoint occurrences gives
\[
 T_S=2\Theta_{\mathrm{int}}+\Theta_{S\bar S},
 \qquad
 P=\Theta_{\mathrm{int}}
 +\Theta_{S\bar S}
 +\Theta_{\bar S\bar S}.
\tag{21}
\]
Subtracting the second equality from the first gives
\[
 \Theta_{\mathrm{int}}
 =T_S-P+\Theta_{\bar S\bar S}.
\tag{22}
\]
Combining (20) and (22) proves
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 &\geq
 \Theta_{\mathrm{int}}-T_S+2L_{\mathrm{miss}}\\
 &=2L_{\mathrm{miss}}-P
 +\Theta_{\bar S\bar S}\\
 &\geq2L_{\mathrm{miss}}-P,
 \end{aligned}
\tag{23}
\]
which is (8).

Accepted fact `2fe46f47d19112ea` gives the strict missed-block estimate
\[
 L_{\mathrm{miss}}
 >\left(\frac{59}{200}
 -\frac1{100\gamma_d}\right)Q.
\tag{24}
\]
Accepted fact `036a276c54ec5e66` gives
\[
 \Omega=2P+|\lambda^\perp|,
 \qquad
 \Omega\leq Q,
 \qquad
 |\lambda^\perp|\geq0,
\tag{25}
\]
and therefore \(P\leq Q/2\). Substitution into (23) yields
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 >
 \left(
 2\left(\frac{59}{200}
 -\frac1{100\gamma_d}\right)
 -\frac12
 \right)Q
 =\left(\frac9{100}
 -\frac1{50\gamma_d}\right)Q.
\tag{26}
\]
The elementary uniform estimate retained in accepted fact `2fe46f47d19112ea` is \(\gamma_d>4/11\). Thus
\[
 \frac1{50\gamma_d}<\frac{11}{200},
 \qquad
 \frac9{100}-\frac1{50\gamma_d}
 >\frac{18}{200}-\frac{11}{200}
 =\frac7{200},
\tag{27}
\]
which proves (9).

It remains to invoke one capacity once. Accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c`, as retained in `5a1fe0ca819e2d1a`, gives for the synchronized optimizer \(v\)
\[
 \widehat P(v)\geq H_{\mathrm{int}},
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y}w_y=2\widehat P(v).
\tag{28}
\]
Its collision-aggregated centroid-selector inequality is
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_Y}
 \frac{\sum_{y\in Y}w_y}{r_Y^c+1}.
\tag{29}
\]
Using (10) and (28) in (29) gives the single legal lower bound
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq\frac{4\gamma_d}{3}\widehat P(v)
 \geq\frac{4\gamma_d}{3}H_{\mathrm{int}}.
\tag{30}
\]
Equations (9), (27), and (30) now imply
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 >\frac43\frac4{11}\frac7{200}Q
 =\frac{14}{825}Q.
\tag{31}
\]
Finally,
\[
 \frac{14}{825}-\frac1{100}
 =\frac{23}{3300}>0,
\tag{32}
\]
so (31) proves (12).

PAIRING, COLLISION, AND CAPACITY AUDIT
The ownership map (14) uses the actual paired endpoints of each retained whole label. A pairing switch with identical original-parent, anchor, and completion marginals may change which two endpoint ratios occur in one minimum and may change the owner in (14). The proof recomputes (14) label by label after that switch. It never replaces the joint pairing by the marginal data \((\kappa_g,b_{g,z},L_z,q^{\mathscr F})\). The endpointwise bound (16) remains valid because it is checked against the physical pooled occurrence identity after the actual pairing is fixed.

Every label deficit is assigned once and is never divided between incompatible stars or bins. Repeated labels, repeated original parents, and repeated physical endpoints are aggregated in \(\tau_y\) before (16). The original parent pair \((e_\sigma,f_\sigma)\), its anchor, and its four-corner circuit remain attached to the label, but no parent-edge or anchor capacity is charged in (17). Hence those capacities are neither reused nor added to the selector. The final step invokes the one global quantity \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) once through (29), with the collision-aggregated physical weights \(w_y\).

Projected dyadic loops cause no exception. If
\(e_2(a(\sigma))=e_2(b(\sigma))\), the physical endpoints \(a(\sigma)\ne b(\sigma)\) still have two separate budgets in (17); the whole deficit is assigned to exactly one of them. Equation (19) intentionally counts the common dyadic edge once at each of its two endpoints. Thus the proof includes the all-loop case.

No quotient rank, barycentric lattice energy, completion rank, cap descendant, or transported KKT stress is used. This is legitimate because (12) is already one of the terminal capacities allowed by the registered success criterion. In particular, the positive circuits and the collision-safe parent, anchor, and complete-product-midpoint measures may all be retained without being combined or charged a second time.
