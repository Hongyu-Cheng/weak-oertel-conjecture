## Statement

Let \(n,d\geq1\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be a convex body, and set
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
w_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}w_z>0,
\]
\[
\pi_z:=\frac{w_z}{W},\qquad
D:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:w_z>0\}.
\]
Let \(z_0\in D\) have exact marginal closed-halfspace depth
\[
\tau:=
\inf_{u\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}}
\sum_{\substack{z\in D\\u^\top(z-z_0)\geq0}}\pi_z
\geq\delta:=2^{-n}.
\]
For \(0<\sigma\leq\tau\), define
\[
\Lambda_\sigma:=
\left\{
\lambda\in[0,1]^D:
\sum_z\pi_z\lambda_z=\sigma,\quad
\sum_z\pi_z\lambda_z(z-z_0)=0
\right\}.
\]
Accepted fact `725defc391f9d55d`, applied at level \(\sigma\), gives
\(\Lambda_\sigma\neq\varnothing\). Let \(\lambda^\sigma\) be the maximizer on
\(\Lambda_\sigma\) of
\[
\sum_{z\in D}\pi_z[-\lambda_z\log\lambda_z],
\qquad 0\log0:=0,
\]
and define
\[
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(A):=
\sum_{z\in D}\lambda^\sigma_z
\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z\cap C_z),
\qquad
A_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in A\}.
\]
Let \(p_\sigma\) be its barycenter, which belongs to
\(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\) by accepted fact
`725defc391f9d55d`. Put
\[
P:=\operatorname{conv}D,
\]
and let \(F\) be the minimal face of \(P\) containing \(z_0\).

For every \(0<\sigma\leq\tau\), the entropy maximizer \(\lambda^\sigma\) is unique, every feasible vector vanishes on \(D\setminus F\), and the map
\[
(0,\tau]\ni\sigma\longmapsto\lambda^\sigma
\]
is continuous. For \(0<\sigma<\tau\), all coordinates of \(\lambda^\sigma\) on \(D\cap F\) are positive, and there are \(a_\sigma\in\mathbb R\) and \(b_\sigma\in\mathbb R^n\), not necessarily unique when \(F\) is lower-dimensional, such that
\[
\lambda^\sigma_z
=
\min\left\{1,
\exp\!\left(a_\sigma+b_\sigma^\top(z-z_0)-1\right)
\right\}
\qquad(z\in D\cap F).
\tag{1}
\]
More precisely, if
\[
\Psi(s):=
\begin{cases}
e^{s-1},&s\leq1,\\
s,&s\geq1,
\end{cases}
\]
and \(V(\sigma)\) denotes the maximum entropy, then for \(0<\sigma<\tau\),
\[
V(\sigma)
=
\min_{a\in\mathbb R,\ b\in\mathbb R^n}
\left\{
\sum_{z\in D\cap F}\pi_z
\Psi\!\left(a+b^\top(z-z_0)\right)
-a\sigma
\right\},
\tag{2}
\]
and the minimum is attained. Formula (1) is the corresponding KKT formula, including all saturated coordinates.

Define
\[
\mathcal Q_\sigma:=
\left\{
q\in[0,1]^D:
\sum_zq_z=1,\quad
\sum_zq_z(z-z_0)=0,\quad
q_z=0\ (z\notin F),\quad
q_z\leq\frac{\pi_z}{\sigma}\ (z\in F)
\right\}.
\]
Then
\[
q^\sigma_z:=\frac{\pi_z\lambda^\sigma_z}{\sigma}
\tag{3}
\]
is the unique minimizer on \(\mathcal Q_\sigma\) of
\[
\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert\pi)
:=
\sum_{z\in D\cap F}q_z\log\frac{q_z}{\pi_z},
\qquad
0\log\frac0{\pi_z}:=0,
\tag{4}
\]
and
\[
V(\sigma)
=-\sigma\log\sigma
-\sigma\operatorname{KL}(q^\sigma\Vert\pi).
\tag{5}
\]
In particular, \(\operatorname{KL}(q^\sigma\Vert\pi)\) is nondecreasing in \(\sigma\).

Let \(q^\star\) be the unique minimizer of (4) over the same probability and moment constraints but without the upper bounds \(q_z\leq\pi_z/\sigma\). It is positive on \(D\cap F\). Put
\[
\sigma_0
:=
\min_{z\in D\cap F}\frac{\pi_z}{q^\star_z}
=
\frac{1}{\max_{z\in D\cap F}(q^\star_z/\pi_z)}
\in(0,1].
\tag{6}
\]
Then for every
\[
0<\sigma\leq\min\{\tau,\sigma_0\}
\]
one has the exact initial-ray formula
\[
q^\sigma=q^\star,\qquad
\lambda^\sigma_z=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle \sigma\,\frac{q^\star_z}{\pi_z},&z\in F,\\
0,&z\notin F.
\end{cases}
\tag{7}
\]
If
\[
\rho^\star(A):=
\sum_{z\in D\cap F}
q^\star_z\,
\frac{\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z\cap C_z)}{w_z},
\tag{8}
\]
then throughout this interval
\[
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}=\sigma W\rho^\star.
\tag{9}
\]
Thus its barycenter is one fixed point \(p^\star\), and
\[
\inf_{\substack{H\ {\rm closed\ halfspace}\\p^\star\in H}}
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(H)
=
\sigma W
\inf_{\substack{H\ {\rm closed\ halfspace}\\p^\star\in H}}
\rho^\star(H).
\tag{10}
\]
In particular, no scale below \(\min\{\tau,\sigma_0\}\) improves on that endpoint.

If, in addition,
\[
\sum_{z\in D}\pi_z z=z_0,
\tag{11}
\]
then \(F=P\), \(q^\star=\pi\), and \(\sigma_0=1\). Consequently, for every \(0<\sigma\leq\tau\),
\[
\lambda^\sigma_z=\sigma\quad(z\in D),\qquad
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}=\sigma\nu,
\qquad
p_\sigma=p,
\tag{12}
\]
where
\[
\nu(A):=\sum_{z\in D}\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z\cap C_z)
\]
and \(p\) is the barycenter of \(\nu\). Writing
\[
h_\nu(p):=
\inf_{\substack{H\ {\rm closed\ halfspace}\\p\in H}}\nu(H),
\]
one then has the exact identity
\[
\sup_{\sigma\in[\delta,\tau]}
\inf_{\substack{H\ {\rm closed\ halfspace}\\p_\sigma\in H}}
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(H)
=\tau h_\nu(p)
\leq\tau^2W.
\tag{13}
\]

The universal entropy-path assertion under consideration is the following fully quantified claim:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{there exists one absolute constant \(c>0\) such that, for every }
n,d\geq1,\text{ every convex body }C\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\\
\text{with \(W>0\), and every \(z_0\in D\) whose exact marginal depth }
\tau\text{ satisfies }\tau\geq2^{-n},\\
\sup_{\sigma\in[2^{-n},\tau]}
\inf_{\substack{H\ {\rm closed\ halfspace}\\p_\sigma\in H}}
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(H)
\geq c\,2^{-n}W.
\end{gathered}
\tag{EP}
\]
Here \(\Lambda_\sigma,\lambda^\sigma,\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}\), and \(p_\sigma\) are exactly those defined above, so the assertion includes the same fiberwise capacities at every scale.

Consider any sequence of instances
\[
\bigl(n_j,d_j,C^{(j)},z_0^{(j)}\bigr)_{j\geq1}
\]
satisfying all the preceding hypotheses, including positive mass at \(z_0^{(j)}\), and let \(W_j,\pi^{(j)},\tau_j\) denote the corresponding quantities. Suppose every instance satisfies the mean condition (11), and put
\[
\delta_j:=2^{-n_j}.
\]
If
\[
\tau_j\geq\delta_j
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\frac{\tau_j^2}{\delta_j}\longrightarrow0,
\tag{14}
\]
then this sequence refutes (EP): for every \(c>0\), some member of the sequence violates the lower bound \(c\,2^{-n_j}W_j\).

## Proof

For any \(\lambda\in\Lambda_\sigma\), the numbers
\[
\frac{\pi_z\lambda_z}{\sigma}\qquad(z\in D)
\]
are convex coefficients representing \(z_0\) as a convex combination of points of \(P\). Since \(z_0\) belongs to the face \(F\), the defining property of a face forces every point having a positive coefficient to lie in \(F\). Thus every feasible \(\lambda\) vanishes on \(D\setminus F\).

The feasible set is a nonempty compact convex polytope. The function
\[
h(t):=-t\log t,\qquad h(0):=0,
\]
is strictly concave on \([0,1]\). Since every \(\pi_z\) is positive on \(D\), the objective \(\sum_z\pi_zh(\lambda_z)\) is strictly concave on the feasible set. Hence its maximizer is unique for every \(0<\sigma\leq\tau\).

We next establish the relative strict-feasibility needed for KKT. Because \(z_0\) is in the relative interior of \(F\), there is a probability vector \(r\), positive at every point of \(D\cap F\), such that
\[
\sum_{z\in D\cap F}r_z(z-z_0)=0.
\tag{15}
\]
For completeness, for each \(y\in D\cap F\), extend the segment from \(y\) through the relative-interior point \(z_0\) slightly beyond \(z_0\) while remaining in \(F\). This writes \(z_0\) as a convex combination having positive coefficient at \(y\). Averaging these finitely many representations gives (15) with every coefficient positive.

Choose \(0<\varepsilon<\sigma\) so small that
\[
\lambda^{\varepsilon,r}_z:=\frac{\varepsilon r_z}{\pi_z}<1
\qquad(z\in D\cap F).
\]
This vector is feasible at mass \(\varepsilon\) and is strictly between \(0\) and \(1\) on \(D\cap F\). Accepted fact `725defc391f9d55d` supplies a vector \(\lambda^\tau_{\rm fea}\in\Lambda_\tau\). For \(0<\sigma<\tau\), set
\[
\theta:=\frac{\sigma-\varepsilon}{\tau-\varepsilon}\in(0,1).
\]
Then
\[
\bar\lambda
:=
\theta\lambda^\tau_{\rm fea}
+(1-\theta)\lambda^{\varepsilon,r}
\tag{16}
\]
belongs to \(\Lambda_\sigma\) and satisfies
\[
0<\bar\lambda_z<1
\qquad(z\in D\cap F).
\tag{17}
\]

The entropy maximizer itself has no zero coordinate on \(D\cap F\). Indeed, if \(\lambda^\sigma_y=0\), move from \(\lambda^\sigma\) toward \(\bar\lambda\). The \(y\)-coordinate contributes
\[
\pi_yh(t\bar\lambda_y)
=
\pi_y t\bar\lambda_y\log(1/t)+O(t),
\]
whereas the total change at coordinates that were positive is \(O(t)\), and every other formerly zero coordinate contributes nonnegatively. The resulting entropy is strictly larger for all sufficiently small \(t>0\), a contradiction.

On the reduced coordinate set \(D\cap F\), (17) is Slater's condition for the upper constraints. Finite-dimensional concave KKT therefore supplies equality multipliers \(a_\sigma,b_\sigma\) and upper-bound multipliers \(\mu_z\geq0\). Stationarity and complementary slackness are
\[
-\log\lambda^\sigma_z-1
+a_\sigma+b_\sigma^\top(z-z_0)
-\frac{\mu_z}{\pi_z}=0,
\qquad
\mu_z(\lambda^\sigma_z-1)=0.
\tag{18}
\]
If \(\lambda^\sigma_z<1\), then \(\mu_z=0\) and (18) gives the exponential in (1). If \(\lambda^\sigma_z=1\), then (18) gives
\[
a_\sigma+b_\sigma^\top(z-z_0)-1
=\frac{\mu_z}{\pi_z}\geq0.
\]
This proves the capped-exponential formula (1), including saturation and lower-dimensional \(F\).

The scalar conjugate calculation
\[
\sup_{0\leq t\leq1}\{h(t)+st\}
=\Psi(s)
\tag{19}
\]
is immediate: the unconstrained critical point is \(t=e^{s-1}\) when \(s\leq1\), while \(t=1\) is optimal when \(s\geq1\). Applying (19) coordinatewise to the Lagrangian
\[
\sum_z\pi_zh(\lambda_z)
+a\left(\sum_z\pi_z\lambda_z-\sigma\right)
+b^\top\sum_z\pi_z\lambda_z(z-z_0)
\]
gives the dual objective in (2). Slater's condition (17) gives equality of primal and dual values and attainment of the dual minimum. This proves (2).

To prove continuity, let \(\sigma_k\to\sigma\in(0,\tau]\). Compactness gives convergent subsequences of \(\lambda^{\sigma_k}\), and every such limit lies in \(\Lambda_\sigma\). Conversely, any \(\lambda\in\Lambda_\sigma\) can be approximated by feasible vectors at the levels \(\sigma_k\): if \(\sigma_k\leq\sigma\), use
\[
\frac{\sigma_k}{\sigma}\lambda;
\]
if \(\sigma<\sigma_k\leq\tau\) and \(\sigma<\tau\), take the convex combination of \(\lambda\) and \(\lambda^\tau_{\rm fea}\) having mass \(\sigma_k\). When \(\sigma=\tau\), only the first construction is needed. Continuity of the entropy objective implies that every subsequential limit is optimal at level \(\sigma\). Uniqueness forces that limit to be \(\lambda^\sigma\), proving continuity of the whole path.

For fixed \(\sigma\), the change of variables (3) is a bijection from \(\Lambda_\sigma\) to \(\mathcal Q_\sigma\). Moreover,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_z\pi_z[-\lambda_z\log\lambda_z]
&=
-\sigma\sum_zq_z\log\frac{\sigma q_z}{\pi_z}\\
&=
-\sigma\log\sigma
-\sigma\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert\pi).
\end{aligned}
\tag{20}
\]
Thus maximizing entropy is exactly minimizing (4), proving (3)--(5), including at \(\sigma=\tau\). If \(0<\sigma_1<\sigma_2\leq\tau\), then
\[
\mathcal Q_{\sigma_2}\subseteq\mathcal Q_{\sigma_1}.
\]
The minimum of (4) is therefore nondecreasing in \(\sigma\).

Remove the capacity inequalities from \(\mathcal Q_\sigma\). The resulting compact convex set contains the strictly positive vector \(r\) from (15). Strict convexity of relative entropy gives a unique minimizer \(q^\star\), and the same \(t\log(1/t)\) perturbation argument shows that it is positive on all of \(D\cap F\). Equality-constrained KKT gives
\[
q^\star_z
=
\pi_z\exp\!\left(\alpha+\beta^\top(z-z_0)\right)
\qquad(z\in D\cap F)
\tag{21}
\]
for suitable multipliers, although only \(q^\star\), not the multipliers, is needed below.

By (6), \(q^\star\in\mathcal Q_\sigma\) exactly when \(\sigma\leq\sigma_0\). Also \(\sigma_0\leq1\), since
\[
\sum_{z\in D\cap F}\pi_z\frac{q^\star_z}{\pi_z}=1
\]
forces \(\max_z(q^\star_z/\pi_z)\geq1\). Whenever \(0<\sigma\leq\min\{\tau,\sigma_0\}\), the unconstrained minimizer \(q^\star\) is feasible for the capacity-constrained problem, so uniqueness gives \(q^\sigma=q^\star\). This proves (7).

Using \(w_z=W\pi_z\), formula (7) gives, for every measurable \(A\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(A)
&=
\sum_{z\in D\cap F}
\sigma\frac{q^\star_z}{\pi_z}
\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z\cap C_z)\\
&=
\sigma W
\sum_{z\in D\cap F}
q^\star_z
\frac{\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z\cap C_z)}{w_z}\\
&=\sigma W\rho^\star(A).
\end{aligned}
\tag{22}
\]
Thus the normalized measure and its barycenter are independent of \(\sigma\), and taking infima over closed halfspaces through that fixed barycenter proves (9) and (10).

Finally assume (11). Since every \(\pi_z\) is positive, (11) expresses \(z_0\) as a convex combination using every point of \(D\) with positive coefficient. Hence \(z_0\) is in the relative interior of \(P\), so \(F=P\). The vector \(\pi\) is feasible for the unconstrained relative-entropy problem and has
\[
\operatorname{KL}(\pi\Vert\pi)=0.
\]
Nonnegativity and strict convexity of relative entropy give \(q^\star=\pi\), and (6) gives \(\sigma_0=1\). Equations (7)--(9) now prove (12) for every \(0<\sigma\leq\tau\).

Because both the measure and barycenter in (12) are fixed up to the scalar \(\sigma\),
\[
\inf_{\substack{H\ {\rm closed\ halfspace}\\p_\sigma\in H}}
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(H)
=\sigma h_\nu(p).
\]
The supremum on \([\delta,\tau]\) is therefore \(\tau h_\nu(p)\). Every marginal closed halfspace \(Q\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) containing \(z_0\) lifts to the vertical closed halfspace
\[
Q\times\mathbb R^d
\]
containing \(p\), with mixed mass \(W\pi(Q)\). Taking the infimum over such \(Q\) and using the exact marginal depth \(\tau\) gives
\[
h_\nu(p)\leq\tau W.
\]
This proves (13).

Now take a sequence satisfying (14), and for its \(j\)-th member write
\[
L_j:=
\sup_{\sigma\in[\delta_j,\tau_j]}
\inf_{\substack{H\ {\rm closed\ halfspace}\\p^{(j)}_\sigma\in H}}
\nu^{(j)}_{\lambda^{(j),\sigma}}(H).
\]
Applying (13) to that member gives
\[
\frac{L_j}{\delta_jW_j}
\leq
\frac{\tau_j^2}{\delta_j}
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{23}
\]
Let \(c>0\) be arbitrary. Equation (23) supplies an index \(j\) for which
\[
L_j<c\,\delta_jW_j=c\,2^{-n_j}W_j.
\]
That instance satisfies every hypothesis quantified in (EP), including
\(\tau_j\geq2^{-n_j}\), but violates the asserted lower bound for this \(c\).
Since this holds for every \(c>0\), no universal positive \(c\) in (EP) exists.
Thus the sequence refutes exactly the fully quantified entropy-path assertion.
