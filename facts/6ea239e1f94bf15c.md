## Statement

There are three identical actual square fibers at bases $-1,0,1$, a probability law of ambient caps with boundary incidence $W$, and a same-normal physical martingale from $\nu=(W/\beta)\eta$ to the marked surface law $\eta$. Its middle-to-endpoint crossflows $\gamma_-,\gamma_+$ admit a single conditional pairing with $\rho\le\eta_0$. Nevertheless a closed cap $H$ proper on all three fibers satisfies
$$
\nu_0(H_0)-\eta_0(H_0)-\gamma_-(H)-\gamma_+(H)=3/19.
$$
Here $\beta=19/36$ is the boundary normalization and the eight caps defining $W$ each have original mixed volume fraction $5/9$.

## Proof

Use $[-1,1]\times[-1,1]^2$ with the cap law in f79eb82cd3159a33, giving each $az+v\cdot X\ge-1/3$ weight $1/8$, where $a=\pm2$ and $v\in\{\pm e_1,\pm e_2\}$. That fact gives the endpoint source density $18/19$, middle edge source density $18/19+(9/19)\mathbf1_{|t|<1/3}$ for tangent $-1\le t\le1$, and the raw mixed fraction $5/9$.

Keep endpoint-source rows fixed. On each middle edge retain the common part with $(21/19)\,dt$ and send the central excess $6/19$ in equal parts to $t-2/3$ and $t+2/3$. Each exterior interval gets its missing $3/19$, giving $(21/19)\,dt$ on the whole edge while retaining every conditional mean. Choose base zero with probability $19/21$ and each endpoint with probability $1/21$, retaining the intermediate tangent and normal. The source physical means are unchanged and the target density on every edge is one. Each endpoint crossflow has density $1/19$ and their two conditional tangent laws agree. Diagonal pairing gives $\rho=\eta_0/19$.

For $H=\{X_2\le1/4\}$ the full bottom edge and two vertical pieces of length $5/4$ give $\eta_0(H_0)=9/2$. Each vertical piece contains $7/12$ of the central interval in length, so its middle-source capture is $(18/19)(5/4)+(9/19)(7/12)=111/76$. The bottom contributes $42/19$, giving $\nu_0(H_0)=195/38$. The proposed bound is $(21/19)(9/2)=189/38$. The difference is $3/19$. All traces are proper and have surface-null boundaries. Thus the inequality fails for the actual dominated midpoint law and exact original source.
