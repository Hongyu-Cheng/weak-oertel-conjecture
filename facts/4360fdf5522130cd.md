## Statement

Let $D\subseteq C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be ambient convex bodies with positive mixed $d$-mass for $D$, and let $P_D=\{z:|D_z|_d>0\}$. For $0\le t\le1$ the fibers $E_z(t)=(1-t)D_z+t C_z$, $z\in P_D$, are nested in $t$ and have an ambient convex realization in $C$ with exactly these integer fibers. If $w_z(t)=|E_z(t)|_d$, $m(t)=\sum_z w_z(t)$, and $j(t)$ is the base centroid, then
$
j(t)=\frac{\sum_z z\,w_z(t)}{m(t)},\qquad
j'(0)=\frac{\sum_z(z-j(0))w'_z(0)}{m(0)},\qquad
w'_z(0)=d[V(D_z[d-1],C_z)-|D_z|_d]\ge0 .
$
Even if $j(0)=j(1)\in\mathbb Z^n$, the intermediate base centroid can leave that integer. In $n=1,d=2$, take bases $-1,0,1$, unit square endpoint fibers for $D$, a common middle fiber $[0,3]\times[0,3/2]$, and endpoint fibers $[0,4]\times[0,1]$, $[0,2]\times[0,2]$ for $C$, respectively. Then $j(0)=j(1)=0$ and $j(t)=(t^2-t)/(13/2+5t+t^2)<0$ for $0<t<1$.

## Proof

The positive support $P_D$ is lattice-convex by the inclusion of a full-dimensional Minkowski combination in each integer barycenter fiber. For any integer barycenter $w=\sum_z a_z z$ of these sites, convexity of both original bodies gives $\sum_z a_z E_z(t)\subseteq(1-t)D_w+tC_w$. Hence the hull of the lifts is contained in $C$ and has exactly these fibers at every integer base. Full base rank makes this hull ambient-full because its fibers are full $d$-dimensional. At deficient rank apply the one-sided exact thickening construction in the intrinsic variable-shape attainment theorem. Each $E_z(t)$ is nested: for $s\le t$ with $t>0$, replace $b\in C_z$ in $(1-s)a+s b$ by $b'=(1-s/t)a+(s/t)b\in C_z$, using $a\in D_z\subset C_z$. This yields $(1-t)a+t b'$. The centroid identities now follow from the quotient and its derivative. The mixed-volume polynomial of $(1-t)D_z+tC_z$ yields the stated right derivative, nonnegative by nesting.

For the example the endpoint midpoint of $C$ equals its middle rectangle and that of $D$ is contained in it, so the canonical lifted hulls are actual and $D\subseteq C$. Their endpoint areas agree within each body, giving base centroid zero at $t=0,1$. Along the path the left, middle and right areas are $1+3t,9/2,(1+t)^2$. Taking the quotient gives the strict intermediate drift.
