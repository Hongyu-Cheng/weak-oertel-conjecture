## Statement

For every \(j\geq1\), let \(n_j,d_j\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C_j\subseteq\mathbb R^{n_j}\times\mathbb R^{d_j}\) be a convex body,
and put
\[
 (C_j)_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^{d_j}:(z,x)\in C_j\},\qquad
 a_{j,z}:=\operatorname{vol}_{d_j}((C_j)_z),
\]
\[
 Z_j:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^{n_j}:a_{j,z}>0\},\qquad
 W_j:=\sum_{z\in Z_j}a_{j,z}>0,
\]
\[
 \gamma_j:=\left(\frac{d_j}{d_j+1}\right)^{d_j},\qquad
 H_j:=\frac{2^{n_j}h_{\max}(C_j)}{W_j}.
\tag{1}
\]
For \(z\in Z_j\), let \(c_{j,z}\) be the centroid of \((C_j)_z\), and
write
\[
 \mathcal K_{j,z}:=\mathsf M_{a_{j,z}/2}((C_j)_z),\qquad
 D_{j,z}:=\mathcal K_{j,z}-c_{j,z}.
\tag{2}
\]
An affine subspace \(A\subseteq\mathbb R^{n_j}\) is primitive if
\(A=b+V\) for some \(b\in\mathbb Z^{n_j}\) and
\(V\cap\mathbb Z^{n_j}\) is saturated of rank \(\dim V\).  Put
\[
 W_{j,A}:=\sum_{z\in Z_j\cap A}a_{j,z}.
\]
Assume the strict no-rank-drop condition
\[
 W_{j,A}<2^{\dim A-n_j}W_j
 \quad\text{for every proper primitive affine }
 A\subsetneq\mathbb R^{n_j},
\tag{NR}
\]
and assume
\[
 H_j\longrightarrow0.
\tag{3}
\]

For every \(j\), apply accepted fact `cf896cdbe870d267` to choose an exact
minimum-unmatched centroid matching \(q_j=p_j+r_j\) whose main part
\(p_j\) is graph-theoretic.  Thus every endpoint is incident with at most
one positive \(p_j\)-edge.  Write
\[
 P_j:=|p_j|,\qquad
 d_{j,z}:=d_z(p_j).
\tag{4}
\]
Then, for every \(j\),
\[
 0\leq\frac{\gamma_j}{2}W_j-P_j
 =\frac12\sum_{z\in Z_j}(\gamma_ja_{j,z}-d_{j,z})
 \leq\frac32H_jW_j,
\tag{5}
\]
\[
 \gamma_j
 \sum_{e=\{z,z'\}\in\operatorname{supp}p_j}
 |a_{j,z}-a_{j,z'}|
 \leq3H_jW_j.
\tag{6}
\]
In particular, \(P_j>0\) for all sufficiently large \(j\).

For such \(j\), run accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` on \(p_j\) itself.
For \(e=\{z,z'\}\in\operatorname{supp}p_j\), put
\[
 m_{j,e}:=\frac{z+z'}2,\qquad
 D_{j,e}:=\frac12(D_{j,z}+D_{j,z'}),\qquad
 L_{j,e}:=\frac12(\mathcal K_{j,z}+\mathcal K_{j,z'}).
\tag{7}
\]
Let \(\Delta_j:=\Delta_{C_j}(p_j)\), choose a primal optimizer and a
gauge-load-minimal dual optimizer \((v_{j,e})_e\), and define
\[
 \omega_{j,e}:=h_{D_{j,e}}(v_{j,e}),\qquad
 \Omega_j:=\sum_e\omega_{j,e},
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 U_{j,z}:=\frac12\sum_{e\ni z}v_{j,e},\qquad
 \tau_{j,z}:=h_{D_{j,z}}(U_{j,z}),\qquad
 T_j:=\sum_z\tau_{j,z}.
\tag{9}
\]
The graph property gives the exact identities
\[
 \boxed{T_j=\Omega_j},
 \qquad
 \boxed{
 \sum_z h_{\mathcal K_{j,z}}(U_{j,z})=-\Delta_j.}
\tag{10}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 0\leq P_j-T_j
 \leq\frac{H_j}{\gamma_j}W_j<eH_jW_j,
\tag{11}
\]
and consequently
\[
 0\leq\frac{\gamma_j}{2}W_j-T_j
 \leq\left(\frac32+\frac1{\gamma_j}\right)H_jW_j
 <\left(e+\frac32\right)H_jW_j.
\tag{12}
\]
Thus
\[
 \frac{T_j}{W_j}=\frac{\gamma_j}{2}+o(1)
 \geq\frac1{2e}-o(1),
 \qquad
 \frac{T_j}{P_j}\longrightarrow1.
\tag{13}
\]

For every fixed \(0<s<1/e\), set
\[
 \psi(s):=-\frac{1-s}{s}\log(1-s),\qquad
 \delta_s:=\frac{\psi(s)}{\log2}-1>0.
\tag{14}
\]
Then the following finite-instance estimate holds for every sufficiently
large \(j\):
\[
 \boxed{
 \Delta_j\geq\delta_s
 \left(P_j-\frac{H_jW_j}{s}\right).}
\tag{15}
\]
Writing
\[
 \delta_*:=\frac1{\log2}-1,
\tag{16}
\]
one has
\[
 \boxed{
 \liminf_{j\to\infty}\frac{\Delta_j}{P_j}\geq\delta_*},
 \qquad
 \boxed{
 \liminf_{j\to\infty}\frac{\Delta_j}{T_j}\geq\delta_*}.
\tag{17}
\]

For all sufficiently large \(j\), \(\Delta_j,T_j,H_j>0\).  Put
\[
 E_{T,j}:=\{z\in Z_j:\tau_{j,z}>0\},\qquad
 \rho_{T,j}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_{T,j}),
\tag{18}
\]
\[
 r_{T,j}:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{T,j})\to\mathbb R^{d_j}
 \ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{c_{j,z}-s(z):z\in E_{T,j}\},
\tag{19}
\]
\[
 A_{T,j}:=
 \inf_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{T,j})\to\mathbb R^{d_j}
 \ \mathrm{affine}}
 \frac1{T_j}\sum_{z\in E_{T,j}}
 \tau_{j,z}\|c_{j,z}-s(z)\|_{D_{j,z}}.
\tag{20}
\]
Then
\[
 A_{T,j}-1\geq\frac{\Delta_j}{T_j},
 \qquad
 \liminf_{j\to\infty}A_{T,j}\geq\frac1{\log2}.
\tag{21}
\]
The stressed physical fibers and their affine hull satisfy
\[
 \sum_{z\in E_{T,j}}a_{j,z}
 \geq\frac{2T_j}{\gamma_j}
 \geq
 \left(1-\frac{(2e+3)H_j}{\gamma_j}\right)W_j,
\tag{22}
\]
\[
 W_{j,\operatorname{aff}(E_{T,j})}
 \geq
 \left(1-\frac{(2e+3)H_j}{\gamma_j}\right)W_j.
\tag{23}
\]
Consequently strict no-rank-drop forces
\[
 \boxed{\rho_{T,j}=n_j}
 \quad\text{for all sufficiently large }j.
\tag{24}
\]
On that range, the continuous endpoint residual rank obeys
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{T,j}+1
 \geq\frac{T_j}{H_jW_j}
 \geq\frac{\gamma_j}{2H_j}-e-\frac32
 \geq\frac1{2eH_j}-e-\frac32,}
\tag{25}
\]
so \(r_{T,j}\to\infty\).

The same load-minimal contacts have a fixed-margin endpoint selector.
For every \(e=\{z,z'\}\) with \(\omega_{j,e}>0\), choose the accepted
contact decomposition
\[
 \ell_{j,e}=\frac{x_{j,e,z}+x_{j,e,z'}}2,
 \qquad
 x_{j,e,z}\in\mathcal K_{j,z},\quad
 x_{j,e,z'}\in\mathcal K_{j,z'}.
\tag{26}
\]
Put
\[
 \varrho_{j,z}:=\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_{j,e},\qquad
 E_{\omega,j}:=\{z:\varrho_{j,z}>0\},
\tag{27}
\]
\[
 y_{j,z}:=\frac1{\varrho_{j,z}}
 \sum_{e\ni z}\omega_{j,e}x_{j,e,z}
 \qquad(z\in E_{\omega,j}).
\tag{28}
\]
Then
\[
 E_{\omega,j}=E_{T,j},\qquad
 \sum_z\varrho_{j,z}=2T_j,qquad
 0<\varrho_{j,z}\leq d_{j,z}\leq\gamma_ja_{j,z},
\tag{29}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 y_{j,z}\in
 \mathsf M_{a_{j,z}/2}((C_j)_z)
 \cap\mathsf M_{\varrho_{j,z}}((C_j)_z)},
\tag{30}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 y_{j,z}+\frac15D_{j,z}
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{a_{j,z}/4}((C_j)_z)}.
\tag{31}
\]
The measure
\[
 \eta_j:=\frac12\sum_{z\in E_{T,j}}
 \varrho_{j,z}\delta_{(z,y_{j,z})}
\tag{32}
\]
has mass \(|\eta_j|=T_j\) and is halfspace-dominated by the mixed-volume
measure of \(C_j\).  If
\[
 r^{\mathrm{ad}}_j:=
 \min_{s:\mathbb R^{n_j}\to\mathbb R^{d_j}\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{y_{j,z}-s(z):z\in E_{T,j}\},
\tag{33}
\]
then, for all sufficiently large \(j\),
\[
 r^{\mathrm{ad}}_j+1\geq\frac{T_j}{H_jW_j}\longrightarrow\infty.
\tag{34}
\]
Every affine \(s:\mathbb R^{n_j}\to\mathbb R^{d_j}\) satisfies the
fixed-tube exclusion
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\substack{z\in E_{T,j}:\\
 \|y_{j,z}-s(z)\|_{D_{j,z}}\leq1/5}}
 \varrho_{j,z}\leq4H_jW_j,}
\tag{35}
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\substack{z\in E_{T,j}:\\
 \|y_{j,z}-s(z)\|_{D_{j,z}}>1/5}}
 \varrho_{j,z}
 \geq\bigl(\gamma_j-(2e+7)H_j\bigr)W_j.}
\tag{36}
\]

There is also a full-base algebraic skeleton on distinct edges.  More
precisely, whenever
\[
 H_j<\frac{\gamma_j}{13},
\tag{37}
\]
there are pairwise distinct edges
\[
 e_{j,0},\ldots,e_{j,n_j}\in\operatorname{supp}p_j
\]
and choices \(z_{j,k}\in e_{j,k}\) such that
\[
 z_{j,0},\ldots,z_{j,n_j}
 \quad\text{are affinely independent in }\mathbb R^{n_j}.
\tag{38}
\]
Since \(p_j\) is graph-theoretic, the selected edges have pairwise

Finally, the matching supplies a near-full selected profile for one legal
cap-recycling step.  Put
\[
 \widetilde p_j:=\frac1{2\gamma_j}p_j,
 \qquad
 t_{j,z}:=2d_z(\widetilde p_j)
 =\frac{d_{j,z}}{\gamma_j}
 \quad(d_{j,z}>0).
\tag{39}
\]
Then \(\widetilde p_j\) is a nonzero free same-parity matching whenever
\(H_j<\gamma_j/4\), and its selected mass satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 T_j^{\mathrm{prof}}:=\sum_{d_{j,z}>0}t_{j,z}
 =\frac{2P_j}{\gamma_j},
 \qquad
 \left(1-\frac{3H_j}{\gamma_j}\right)W_j
 \leq T_j^{\mathrm{prof}}\leq W_j.}
\tag{40}
\]
Choose the integral weighted centerpoint supplied by accepted fact
`07009c5047a9f7bb` for this degree-saturated profile.  If

empty-core circuit therefore gives an integer \(r_j^{\mathrm{cap}}\) with

 2\leq r_j^{\mathrm{cap}}\leq d_j+1,
\tag{41}
\]
full-dimensional individual cap bodies
\(A_{j,1},\ldots,A_{j,r_j^{\mathrm{cap}}}\subseteq C_j\), and every

For every selected endpoint \(z\), every \(i\), and every strictly

 \operatorname{vol}_{d_j}((A_{j,i})_z)\geq d_{j,z},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{vol}_{d_j}((B_{j,\boldsymbol\vartheta})_z)
 \geq d_{j,z},
\tag{42}
\]
\[
 W(A_{j,i}),\ W(B_{j,\boldsymbol\vartheta})
 \geq2P_j\geq(\gamma_j-3H_j)W_j.
\tag{43}
\]
In every body in (42), the same edge support is retained with

 d_z(p_j/2)=\frac{d_{j,z}}2
 \leq\frac12\operatorname{vol}_{d_j}(\text{new fiber at }z),
\tag{44}

 d_z(\gamma_jp_j)=\gamma_jd_{j,z}
 \leq\gamma_j\operatorname{vol}_{d_j}(\text{new fiber at }z).
\tag{45}

Thus \(p_j/2\) is free and \(\gamma_jp_j\) is strong in every individual

 d_{j,z}\leq\operatorname{vol}_{d_j}((A_{j,i})_z)

 W_j-W(A_{j,i})

## Proof

Accepted fact `cf896cdbe870d267` gives \(q_j=p_j+r_j\), graph-theoreticity
of \(p_j\), the lower bound on \(P_j\), the endpoint-capacity deficit,
and (6).  Since \(d_{j,z}\leq\gamma_ja_{j,z}\), double-counting endpoint
degrees gives
\[
 2P_j=\sum_zd_{j,z}\leq\gamma_jW_j.
\]
Combining this with the accepted lower bound proves (5).  Since
\(\gamma_j>1/e\) and \(H_j\to0\), equation (5) also proves eventual
positivity of \(P_j\).

We next prove the exact collapse.  Fix a sufficiently large \(j\) and
suppress its index.  Because every endpoint is incident with at most one
positive \(p\)-edge, an endpoint \(z\) of \(e=\{z,z'\}\) has
\[
 U_z=\frac12v_e,qquad
 \tau_z=\frac12h_{D_z}(v_e).
\tag{48}
\]
Therefore
\[
 \tau_z+\tau_{z'}
 =\frac12\bigl(h_{D_z}(v_e)+h_{D_{z'}}(v_e)\bigr)
 =h_{D_e}(v_e)=\omega_e.
\tag{49}
\]
Distinct positive edges have disjoint endpoints, so summing (49) gives
\(T=\Omega\).  The same calculation with the uncentered metronoids gives
\[
 \sum_zh_{\mathcal K_z}(U_z)
 =\sum_e\frac12
 \bigl(h_{\mathcal K_z}(v_e)+h_{\mathcal K_{z'}}(v_e)\bigr)
 =\sum_eh_{L_e}(v_e)=-\Delta_C(p),
\]
where the last identity is dual optimality from accepted fact
`6496cb3b0fd44f9d`.  This proves (10).

The maximum affinely realizable submatching of \(p\) has mass
\(R_C(p)\) by `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`, so
\[
 R_C(p)\leq\mathfrak M_{\rm aff}(C).
\]
The affine matching certificate of accepted fact `8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5`
gives
\[
 \mathfrak M_{\rm aff}(C)
 \leq\frac{2^nh_{\max}(C)}{\gamma_d}
 =\frac{H}{\gamma_d}W.
\tag{50}
\]
The load estimate in `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` now gives
\[
 0\leq P-\Omega\leq R_C(p)
 \leq\frac{H}{\gamma_d}W.
\]
Using \(T=\Omega\), (5), and
\(e^{-1}<\gamma_d\leq1/2\) proves (11)-(13).

For fixed \(0<s<1/e\), one has \(s<\gamma_j\) for every \(j\).  The
static nested-metronoid inequality of accepted fact
`11e347c295e9430b`, applied to \(p_j\), gives
\[
 H_jW_j\geq s
 \left(P_j-\frac{\Delta_j}{\delta_s}\right)_+.
\tag{51}
\]
If the expression inside the positive part is nonpositive, then
\(\Delta_j\geq\delta_sP_j\).  If it is positive, rearranging (51) gives
(15).  Thus (15) holds in both cases.  Equation (5) gives
\[
 \frac{P_j}{W_j}\geq\frac1{2e}-o(1),
\]
so, for every fixed \(s\), division of (15) by \(P_j\) yields
\[
 \liminf_j\frac{\Delta_j}{P_j}\geq\delta_s.
\]
As \(s\downarrow0\), \(\psi(s)\to1\), and hence
\(\delta_s\to\delta_*\).  This proves the first inequality in (17).
Since \(0<T_j\leq P_j\) eventually, the second follows.  Taking one
fixed \(s\) in (15) also proves \(\Delta_j>0\) eventually.  The static
endpoint theorem `5889524b5832fb40` then gives positivity of \(T_j,H_j\)
and
\[
 A_{T,j}-1\geq\frac{\Delta_j}{T_j},
\]
which proves (21).

We prove the physical-mass and rank assertions.  Each \(D_{j,z}\) and
\(D_{j,e}\) is a full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex body, so its
support function is a norm.  Consequently
\[
 \omega_{j,e}>0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v_{j,e}\ne0.
\]
If this holds, (48) gives positive \(\tau\)-load at both endpoints of
\(e\).  Graph-theoreticity then gives
\[
 \sum_{z\in E_{T,j}}d_{j,z}
 =2\sum_{e:\omega_{j,e}>0}p_{j,e}
 \geq2\sum_e\omega_{j,e}=2T_j.
\tag{52}
\]
Since \(d_{j,z}\leq\gamma_ja_{j,z}\), this proves the first inequality
in (22).  Equation (12) proves the second.  The affine hull of a nonempty
set of lattice points is primitive: after translating by one of its
points, its direction lattice is
\(V\cap\mathbb Z^{n_j}\), which is saturated.  Hence, if
\(A_j:=\operatorname{aff}(E_{T,j})\) is proper, (NR) gives
\[
 W_{j,A_j}<2^{\rho_{T,j}-n_j}W_j\leq\frac12W_j.
\tag{53}
\]
On the other hand, (52) and (12) give (23), whose right side is greater
than \(W_j/2\) whenever
\[
 H_j<\frac{\gamma_j}{2(2e+3)}.
\]
Because \(\gamma_j>1/e\) and \(H_j\to0\), this inequality holds
eventually, contradicting (53).  Thus (24) holds.  The static residual
rank estimate of `5889524b5832fb40` is
\[
 r_{T,j}+1\geq
 2^{n_j-\rho_{T,j}}\frac{T_j}{H_jW_j}.
\]
Using (24) and (12) proves (25).

Apply accepted fact `6e66cd69e25232b7` to the same matching, optimizer,
and contact decompositions.  It gives (29)-(32), domination of \(\eta_j\),
its mass \(\Omega_j=T_j\), and (35).  In the graph matching, an endpoint
has positive \(\varrho\)-load exactly when its unique incident edge has
\(\omega>0\), which, by (48)-(49), is exactly when it has positive
\(\tau\)-load.  This proves \(E_{\omega,j}=E_{T,j}\).  The rank estimate
in that accepted fact, together with (24), gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C_j)
 \geq\frac{T_j}{2^{n_j}(r_j^{\mathrm{ad}}+1)},
\]
which is (34).  Finally, subtract (35) from
\(\sum_z\varrho_{j,z}=2T_j\) and use (12):
\[
 2T_j-4H_jW_j
 \geq\bigl(\gamma_j-(2e+7)H_j\bigr)W_j.
\]
This proves (36).

It remains to justify the distinct-edge skeleton.  Again fix \(j\) and
suppress its index.  On the positive-degree endpoints define
\[
 \pi(z):=\frac{d_z(p)}{2P}.
\tag{54}
\]
For every proper primitive affine subspace \(A\) of codimension \(r\),
(NR), \(d_z(p)\leq\gamma_da_z\), and (5) give, whenever
\(H<\gamma_d/3\),
\[
 \pi(A)
 <\frac{\gamma_d}{\gamma_d-3H}\,2^{-r}.
\tag{55}
\]
If \(H<\gamma_d/13\), the coefficient in (55) is less than \(4/3\).
Sample \(Z_0,\ldots,Z_n\) independently from \(\pi\).  Conditioned on
\(Z_0,\ldots,Z_{k-1}\) being affinely independent, their affine hull is
a primitive affine subspace of codimension \(n-k+1\).  The elementary
sequential calculation recorded in accepted fact `c1e3b8400d5b5b10`,
or directly (55), gives
\[
 \mathbb P(Z_0,\ldots,Z_n\text{ affinely independent})
 >\prod_{\ell=1}^{\infty}
 \left(1-\frac43\,2^{-\ell}\right)>\frac3{20}.
\tag{56}
\]
Indeed, the first four factors and the elementary tail-product bound give
\[
 \prod_{\ell=1}^{\infty}
 \left(1-\frac43\,2^{-\ell}\right)
 \geq
 \frac13\cdot\frac23\cdot\frac56\cdot\frac{11}{12}
 \left(1-\sum_{\ell=5}^{\infty}\frac43\,2^{-\ell}\right)
 =\frac{605}{3888}>\frac3{20}.
\]

Let \(p_{\max}:=\max_ep_e\).  The one-fiber centroid bound proved in
`8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5` gives
\(h_{\max}(C)\geq\gamma_d\max_za_z\), and hence
\(p_{\max}\leq h_{\max}(C)\).  The \(\pi\)-mass of the two endpoints of
edge \(e\) is \(p_e/P\).  Therefore a union bound gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb P(\text{two samples belong to the same }p\text{-edge})
 &\leq\binom{n+1}{2}\sum_e\left(\frac{p_e}{P}\right)^2\\
 &\leq\binom{n+1}{2}\frac{p_{\max}}P\\
 &\leq\binom{n+1}{2}
 \frac{2H2^{-n}}{\gamma_d-3H}\\
 &\leq\frac{3H}{2(\gamma_d-3H)}<\frac3{20}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{57}
\]
Here \(\binom{n+1}{2}2^{-n}\leq3/4\) for every \(n\geq1\), and the
last inequality is exactly implied by \(H<\gamma_d/13\).  Equations
(56)-(57) show that the event of affine independence and no edge
collision has positive probability.  One outcome supplies (38).

For cap recycling, the degree inequality
\(d_{j,z}\leq\gamma_ja_{j,z}\) shows that
\(\widetilde p_j=p_j/(2\gamma_j)\) is free.  Its degree-saturated selected
profile in `07009c5047a9f7bb` is exactly (39), and double-counting gives
(40).  Suppose its complete core were nonempty.  The nonempty-core branch
of that accepted fact would give
\[
 h_{\max}(C_j)
 \geq4\gamma_j2^{-n_j}|\widetilde p_j|
 =2^{-n_j}2P_j.
\]
Thus \(H_jW_j\geq2P_j\).  Equation (5) also gives
\(2P_j\geq(\gamma_j-3H_j)W_j\), so
\(H_j\geq\gamma_j-3H_j\), equivalently
\(H_j\geq\gamma_j/4\).  This contradiction proves empty-core whenever
\(H_j<\gamma_j/4\).

The empty-core branch of `07009c5047a9f7bb` now gives (41), the asserted
cap bodies and reweightings, and
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_{d_j}(\text{new fiber at }z)
 \geq2\gamma_jd_z(\widetilde p_j)=d_{j,z},
\]
\[
 W(\text{new body})
 \geq4\gamma_j|\widetilde p_j|=2P_j.
\]
These are (42)-(43).  Equations (44)-(45) follow immediately from the
fiber lower bound.  The accepted contact and pruning conclusions, after
substituting
\(2d_z(\widetilde p_j)=d_{j,z}/\gamma_j\), are exactly (46)-(47).
This completes every asserted conclusion.
