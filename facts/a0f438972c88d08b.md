## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put
\[
S:=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d),\qquad
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
a(z):=|C_z|_d
\]
for \(z\in\mathbb R^n\), with \(a(z)=0\) when \(C_z=\varnothing\). Assume
\[
T:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}a(z)>0,
\qquad
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
\qquad
\theta_d:=1-\gamma_d.
\]
For \(t\geq0\), define
\[
w_t(z):=(a(z)-t)_+,\qquad
W(t):=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}w_t(z),\qquad
D_t:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:w_t(z)>0\}.
\]
When \(W(t)>0\), let \(\mathcal C_t\) be the set of all \(z_t\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}(D_t)\) such that every proper closed halfspace \(J\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) containing \(z_t\) satisfies
\[
\sum_{z\in J\cap\mathbb Z^n}w_t(z)\geq2^{-n}W(t).
\tag{1}
\]
Then \(\mathcal C_t\neq\varnothing\), and every \(z_t\in\mathcal C_t\) satisfies \(a(z_t)>t\).

Fix \(t\geq0\) with \(W(t)>0\) and \(z_t\in\mathcal C_t\) such that
\[
t\geq\theta_da(z_t).
\tag{2}
\]
Let \(x_t\) be the centroid of \(C_{z_t}\). If
\[
\mu(A):=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}|C_z\cap A_z|_d,
\qquad
\operatorname{depth}_S(y):=\inf_{\substack{H\ni y\\H\text{ proper closed halfspace}}}\mu(H),
\]
where \(A_z:=\{x:(z,x)\in A\}\), then
\[
\operatorname{depth}_S(z_t,x_t)
\geq
\max\{\gamma_da(z_t),\,2^{-n}W(t)\}.
\tag{3}
\]

The feasible pairs in (2) are nonempty. Consequently, for
\[
\mathcal F(S):=\frac1T\sup_{y\in S}\operatorname{depth}_S(y),
\]
one has the normalized variational bound
\[
\mathcal F(S)\geq
\sup_{\substack{t\geq0,\ W(t)>0,\ z\in\mathcal C_t\\
t\geq\theta_da(z)}}
\frac{\max\{\gamma_da(z),\,2^{-n}W(t)\}}{T}.
\tag{4}
\]

Finally, set \(\theta_0:=1-1/e\). Consider the following assertion, denoted only here by \((\mathrm{FP})\): there is a universal \(c>0\) such that every profile \(a\) above admits \(t\geq0\) with \(W(t)>0\) and \(z_t\in\mathcal C_t\) satisfying
\[
t\geq\theta_0a(z_t)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\max\{W(t),\,2^na(z_t)\}\geq cT.
\tag{FP}
\]
If \((\mathrm{FP})\) is true, then
\[
\mathcal F(S)\geq\frac ce\,2^{-n}.
\tag{5}
\]
This is only a one-way sufficient conditional. Neither \((\mathrm{FP})\) nor any converse is asserted.

## Proof

The projection of \(C\) onto \(\mathbb R^n\) is compact, so only finitely many lattice fibers are nonempty. Thus all displayed lattice sums have finite support.

Let \(P:=\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbb R^n}(C)\). For \(z_0,z_1\in P\), \(0\leq\lambda\leq1\), and \(z_\lambda=(1-\lambda)z_0+\lambda z_1\), convexity gives
\[
(1-\lambda)C_{z_0}+\lambda C_{z_1}\subseteq C_{z_\lambda}.
\]
Brunn-Minkowski and monotonicity of Lebesgue measure therefore give
\[
a(z_\lambda)^{1/d}
\geq(1-\lambda)a(z_0)^{1/d}+\lambda a(z_1)^{1/d}.
\tag{6}
\]
Hence \(a^{1/d}\) is concave on \(P\).

Fix \(t\geq0\) with \(W(t)>0\). The weighted lattice centerpoint construction proved inside accepted fact \(bb2b5f9519ae23e8\) applies to the finite nonempty set \(D_t\) with the positive real weights \(w_t(z)\), whose total weight is \(W(t)\). That construction requires neither integral nor rational weights. It produces a lattice point \(z_t\in\operatorname{conv}(D_t)\) satisfying (1). Thus \(\mathcal C_t\neq\varnothing\), and the construction explicitly gives the required location in \(\operatorname{conv}(\operatorname{supp}w_t)\).

For any \(z_t\in\mathcal C_t\), write
\[
z_t=\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i z_i,
\qquad
z_i\in D_t,\quad
\lambda_i>0,\quad
\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i=1.
\]
Finite Jensen applied to (6) yields
\[
a(z_t)^{1/d}
\geq\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i a(z_i)^{1/d}
>\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i t^{1/d}
=t^{1/d}.
\]
The strict inequality is also valid for \(t=0\). Therefore \(a(z_t)>t\). In particular, \(C_{z_t}\) has positive \(d\)-volume, its centroid \(x_t\) lies in \(C_{z_t}\), and \((z_t,x_t)\in S\).

Assume (2). By accepted fact \(978e8555ec5bac42\), it suffices to bound halfspaces whose boundary contains \((z_t,x_t)\): every closed halfspace containing this point contains such a boundary-through-the-point halfspace.

First let \(H\) be nonvertical. Accepted fact \(c13409b4041cc35d\), applied with distinguished fiber \(z_t\) and its centroid \(x_t\), supplies a proper closed base halfspace \(J\ni z_t\) such that
\[
\mu(H)\geq
\sum_{z\in J\cap\mathbb Z^n}
\bigl(a(z)-\theta_da(z_t)\bigr)_+.
\tag{7}
\]
Because \(t\geq\theta_da(z_t)\), monotonicity of the positive part and (1) give
\[
\mu(H)
\geq\sum_{z\in J\cap\mathbb Z^n}(a(z)-t)_+
\geq2^{-n}W(t).
\tag{8}
\]
Since \(J\) contains \(z_t\), its \(z_t\)-summand in (7) is
\[
\bigl(a(z_t)-\theta_da(z_t)\bigr)_+
=\gamma_da(z_t),
\]
so (7) also gives \(\mu(H)\geq\gamma_da(z_t)\). Hence (3) holds for every nonvertical boundary-through-the-point halfspace.

Now let \(H\) be vertical. Then \(H=J\times\mathbb R^d\) for a proper closed base halfspace \(J\) whose boundary contains \(z_t\). Therefore
\[
\mu(H)=\sum_{z\in J\cap\mathbb Z^n}a(z)
\geq\sum_{z\in J\cap\mathbb Z^n}w_t(z)
\geq2^{-n}W(t)
\]
by (1). The entire distinguished fiber belongs to \(H\), so also
\[
\mu(H)\geq a(z_t)\geq\gamma_da(z_t).
\]
Thus (3) holds for vertical halfspaces as well. The boundary reduction from \(978e8555ec5bac42\), together with monotonicity of \(\mu\), now proves (3) for every proper closed halfspace containing \((z_t,x_t)\).

To prove feasibility, let
\[
M:=\max_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}a(z)>0.
\]
Choose
\[
\theta_dM<t<M.
\tag{9}
\]
Then \(W(t)>0\), so choose \(z_t\in\mathcal C_t\). Since \(a(z_t)\leq M\), (9) gives
\[
t>\theta_dM\geq\theta_da(z_t).
\]
Thus the feasible set in (4) is nonempty. Applying (3) to each feasible pair and then taking the supremum proves (4).

It remains only to verify the stated one-way consequence of \((\mathrm{FP})\). Since
\[
\log\left(1+\frac1d\right)\leq\frac1d,
\]
one has
\[
\gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}\geq e^{-1},
\qquad
\theta_d\leq1-e^{-1}=\theta_0.
\tag{10}
\]
Thus a pair supplied by \((\mathrm{FP})\) is feasible for (4), and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\max\{\gamma_da(z_t),\,2^{-n}W(t)\}
&=2^{-n}\max\{\gamma_d2^na(z_t),\,W(t)\}\\
&\geq e^{-1}2^{-n}\max\{2^na(z_t),\,W(t)\}\\
&\geq \frac ce\,2^{-n}T.
\end{aligned}
\]
Division by \(T\) and (4) prove (5), conditional on \((\mathrm{FP})\), without proving \((\mathrm{FP})\) or a converse.

\geq(1-\lambda)|A|_d^{1/d}+\lambda|B|_d^{1/d}.
\]
Applicability: the fibers \(C_{z_0}\) and \(C_{z_1}\) are nonempty compact sets, their Minkowski combination is compact and measurable, the theorem permits zero \(d\)-volume, and convexity supplies its inclusion in \(C_{z_\lambda}\), yielding (6) by measure monotonicity.
