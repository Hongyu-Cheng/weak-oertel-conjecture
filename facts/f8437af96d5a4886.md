## Statement

Let $G$ be a finite nonnegative density with convex extended inverse $\beta$-th root for some real $\beta>0$, where the value on $\{G=0\}$ is $+\infty$. For every $C\ge0$, the density $g=(G-C)_+$ has convex extended inverse $\beta$-th root.

## Proof

The case $C=0$ is immediate. For $C>0$ put $h=G^{-1/\beta}$ with the stated extension. The positive domain of $g$ is the convex strict sublevel $\{h<C^{-1/\beta}\}$. There,
$$
g^{-1/\beta}=\psi(h),\qquad \psi(u)=\frac{u}{(1-Cu^\beta)^{1/\beta}},\qquad 0<u<C^{-1/\beta}.
$$
The derivatives are
$$
\psi'(u)=(1-Cu^\beta)^{-1/\beta-1},\qquad
\psi''(u)=(\beta+1)Cu^{\beta-1}(1-Cu^\beta)^{-1/\beta-2}.
$$
Thus $\psi$ is increasing and convex. Composing with $h$ on this sublevel and assigning $+\infty$ off it gives the stated extended convexity, including the case of an empty sublevel.
