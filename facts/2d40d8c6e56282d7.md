## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, let $B=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1,\ 1\le i\le d+1\}$ be a full-dimensional simplex of volume $b$ with $\sum_i v_i=0$, and let $A,K$ be convex bodies of volumes $a,k$ with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$. Set $P_i=db(1+h_A(v_i))/(d+1)$. Write $X_j=V(A[j],B[d-j])$ in the normalization $|B+sA|=\sum_{j=0}^d\binom dj X_j s^j$. Then
$$
dX_1=\sum_iP_i-db,\qquad
2^dk\ge|A+B|\ge b+X_1\frac{(1+r)^d-1}{r},\qquad
r=(a/X_1)^{1/(d-1)} .
$$
For $d=3$ the bound is $8k\ge a+b+X+\sqrt{3aX}$ with $X=\sum_iP_i-3b$.

## Proof

Apply 41e91e1dea46f672 to the path $B+sA$, using it for both branches. The support increment is exactly $s h_A$ uniformly on the sphere. Thus the right volume derivative is $\int h_A\,dS_B=dX_1$. The surface area measure of the simplex has atoms at $v_i/\lVert v_i\rVert$ with masses equal to the facet areas. The centroid pyramids, each of height $1/\lVert v_i\rVert$ and volume $b/(d+1)$, give the corresponding support coefficient $db/(d+1)$. Hence $dX_1=\sum_i db\,h_A(v_i)/(d+1)=\sum_iP_i-db$.

By the Alexandrov–Fenchel inequality the mixed-volume sequence is log-concave. Specifically, in its two distinguished slots use $A,B$ and in the other slots use $j-1$ copies of $A$ and $d-j-1$ of $B$, giving $X_j^2\ge X_{j-1}X_{j+1}$ for $1\le j\le d-1$. The theorem applies to these full-dimensional convex bodies, whose mixed volumes are positive. [Shenfeld and van Handel, Mixed volumes and the Bochner method, Theorem 1.1](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~rvan/yairb190305.pdf)

Interpolate $\log X_j$ between indices $1$ and $d$ to obtain $X_j\ge X_1 r^{j-1}$ for every $1\le j\le d$, since $X_d=a$. The polynomial at $s=1$ then gives the bound on $|A+B|$, and midpoint compatibility supplies $2^dk\ge|A+B|$. For $d=3$, expand $X_1[(1+r)^3-1]/r=3X_1+3\sqrt{aX_1}+a$ and put $X=3X_1$.
