## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard synchronized-atlas instance, with the actual reservoir identity, strict no-rank-drop hypothesis, physical target aggregation, and partner involution, in the notation of accepted facts 2fe46f47d19112ea, 86944ca2dce34ab7, d01de728d8b5e33c, d06d919de3083960, and f50134018e214ece. Fix the same
\[
 v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t),
 \qquad
 Y:=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad
 w_y:=\widehat\tau_y(v),
\]
and fix once and for all the registered maximum-weight greedy order on the full physical whole-label representation, with its original tie-breaking and its physical target collisions already identified. For every nonempty \(A\subseteq Y\), put
\[
 \rho_A:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(A),
 \qquad
 r_A^c:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(A)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_y-s(y):y\in A\}.
\]
Suppose the registered first surviving endpoint-centroid continuous closure rank is five, namely
\[
 r_Y^c=5.
\]
Then at least one of the following holds:

1. There is a nonempty proper subset \(A\subsetneq Y\) such that
\[
 r_A^c\leq4,
 \qquad
 2^{n-\rho_A}\sum_{y\in A}w_y
 \geq\frac{Q}{80\gamma_d}.
\]

2. There is a lattice simplex \(\Delta\) on endpoints of the fixed greedy rank-raising labels such that its legal ambient-normalized root-affine score satisfies
\[
 \mathcal E_n(\Delta)\geq\frac Q{100}.
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact 2fe46f47d19112ea gives
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d>\frac4{11}.
\tag{1}
\]
For the fixed \(v\), accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c gives
\[
 n\geq2,
 \qquad
 |Y|\geq n+4,
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y}w_y
 =2\widehat P(v)>\frac{7Q}{200},
\tag{2}
\]
the canonical hard inequality
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac Q{100},
\tag{3}
\]
the whole-support rank screen
\[
 r_Y^c+1
 >14\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_Y},
\tag{4}
\]
and, for every nonempty \(A\subseteq Y\), the single collision-aggregated legal selector inequality
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_A}
 \frac{\sum_{z\in A}w_z}{r_A^c+1}.
\tag{5}
\]
Accepted fact d06d919de3083960 gives the strict synchronized endpoint cap
\[
 0<w_y<\frac{Q}{400\,2^n}
 \qquad(y\in Y).
\tag{6}
\]

We first recover the full base rank of the whole endpoint set. If \(\rho_Y\leq n-1\), then (1), (4), and \(r_Y^c=5\) imply
\[
 6
 >14\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_Y}
 \geq28\gamma_d
 >\frac{112}{11}>10,
\]
which is impossible. Hence
\[
 \rho_Y=n,
 \qquad
 r_Y^c=5.
\tag{7}
\]

Assume for contradiction that neither registered alternative holds. Fix \(y\in Y\) and put
\[
 A_y:=Y\setminus\{y\}.
\]
Equation (2) makes \(A_y\) nonempty and proper. Crucially, its weighted numerator, not merely its affine rank, crosses the registered threshold. Indeed, (2), (6), and \(n\geq2\) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 2^{n-\rho_{A_y}}
 \sum_{z\in A_y}w_z
 &\geq
 \sum_{z\in A_y}w_z\\
 &=\sum_{z\in Y}w_z-w_y\\
 &>\frac{7Q}{200}
   -\frac{Q}{400\,2^n}\\
 &\geq
 \frac{7Q}{200}-\frac{Q}{1600}
 =\frac{11Q}{320}
 >\frac{Q}{80\gamma_d},
 \end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
where the last strict inequality is exactly (1). Since the first registered alternative is assumed false, (8) forces
\[
 r_{A_y}^c\geq5.
\tag{9}
\]
The hereditary monotonicity in accepted fact 86944ca2dce34ab7 gives
\[
 r_{A_y}^c\leq r_Y^c=5.
\]
Thus
\[
 r_{Y\setminus\{y\}}^c=5
 \qquad(y\in Y).
\tag{10}
\]

Every one-point deletion also has full base rank. If \(\rho_{A_y}\leq n-1\), then (5), (8), and (10) give the sharper estimate
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 &\geq
 4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{A_y}}
 \frac{\sum_{z\in A_y}w_z}{6}\\
 &>
 \frac{4\gamma_d}{6}\cdot2\cdot\frac{11Q}{320}
 =\frac{11\gamma_d}{240}Q
 >\frac Q{60}
 >\frac Q{100},
 \end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
contradicting (3). Therefore
\[
 \rho_{Y\setminus\{y\}}=n,
 \qquad
 r_{Y\setminus\{y\}}^c=5
 \quad(y\in Y).
\tag{12}
\]

Failure of the second registered alternative says, for the same fixed maximum-weight order and the same physically identified rank-raising endpoints, that every eligible lattice simplex satisfies
\[
 \mathcal E_n(\Delta)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{13}
\]
Equations (7), (12), and (13) are exactly the deletion-stable full-base rank-five antecedent of accepted fact f50134018e214ece, with the identical body \(C\), reservoir mass \(Q\), optimizer \(v\), endpoint set \(Y\), endpoint weights \(w_y\), greedy order, and collision convention. That accepted fact proves that no canonical hard instance can satisfy this antecedent. This contradicts the assumed simultaneous failure of the two alternatives.

Hence at least one registered alternative holds. The reduction (1)-(12) uses only collision-aggregated physical endpoint weights and never splits a whole label or a target collision. It makes no loop/cross assumption. Accepted fact f50134018e214ece explicitly includes the all-loop case, so the conclusion remains valid when every internal label is a loop and no positive cross-label mass exists.
