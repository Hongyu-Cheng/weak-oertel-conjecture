## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard synchronized-atlas instance of accepted facts 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a, 655a8b1d63af6ab2, 86944ca2dce34ab7, c5b53e3fca777493, d01de728d8b5e33c, d85573bdf58eaa65, e0dcff96f9b90a96, f571c88ebc6fa492, and f9a8ae959b19c4b1. Thus \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is the actual common convex body, \(Q>0\) is the mass of its half-strength canonical product reservoir, and
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\]
Fix the same \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) throughout and use the exact aliases
\[
 Y:=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad
 w_y:=\widehat\tau_y(v),
 \qquad
 \widehat P:=\widehat P(v).
\]
Here \(\widehat P\) is the synchronized retained mass, called \(P\) in accepted fact 655a8b1d63af6ab2; it is not the original full-atlas mass \(P_{\rm orig}:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma}\theta_\sigma\), called \(P\) in the first part of accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493.

Assume
\[
 \rho_Y=n,
 \qquad
 r_Y^c=5,
 \qquad
 \rho_{Y\setminus\{y\}}=n,
 \qquad
 r_{Y\setminus\{y\}}^c=5
 \quad(y\in Y),
\]
and assume every lattice simplex on the maximum-weight greedy rank-raising endpoints has legal ambient-normalized root-affine score below \(Q/100\). Then no such canonical hard instance exists. Quantitatively, its single legal global selector would have to satisfy simultaneously
\[
 R_{\rm sel}(C)
 >\frac{14}{825}Q
 =\frac{Q}{100}+\frac{23}{3300}Q
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 R_{\rm sel}(C)<\frac{Q}{100}.
\]
Consequently the conditional assertion in the registered subgoal, including the required nonnegative-circuit or complete-core disjunction, holds because its full rank-five antecedent is inconsistent. This impossibility also covers the allowed all-loop case, with no hypothesis on positive cross-label mass.

## Proof

We first identify every recurring object and normalization. Accepted fact 655a8b1d63af6ab2 fixes \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) and defines exactly
\[
 Y=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad
 w_y=\widehat\tau_y(v),
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y}w_y=2\widehat P(v).
\tag{1}
\]
Accepted fact 86944ca2dce34ab7 uses these same aliases and defines
\[
 \rho_A:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(A),
 \qquad
 r_A^c:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(A)\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_y-s(y):y\in A\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Therefore the four registered rank conditions are literally the deletion-stable specialization in accepted fact 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a; there is no change of endpoint set, affine rank, residual rank, or optimizer. In particular, the first two registered equalities give the exact hypotheses \(\rho_Y=n\) and \(r_Y^c=5\) used by accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493. The deletion equalities for \(Y\setminus\{y\}\) are retained hypotheses, although the contradiction below already follows from the full set \(Y\).

There are two inherited uses of the symbol \(\eta_\sigma\), and their compatibility is exact. In accepted fact 655a8b1d63af6ab2, \(\eta_\sigma\) means \(\widehat\theta_\sigma(v)\) on the full synchronized label set. In accepted facts 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a, e0dcff96f9b90a96, and c5b53e3fca777493, it means the rescaled internal whole-label weight on \(\mathscr A_{\rm int}\). Accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c proves, for the same \(v\),
\[
 \widehat\theta_\sigma(v)=\eta_\sigma
 \quad(\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\rm int}),
 \qquad
 \widehat P\geq H_{\rm int},
 \qquad
 H_{\rm int}:=
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\rm int}}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{3}
\]
Accepted fact d85573bdf58eaa65 retains the same equality for every load-minimal optimizer and the same internal occurrence masses, so (3) is not an identification of two unrelated thinnings.

The exact whole-label deficit estimate of accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493, applied to this common internal family, gives
\[
 H_{\rm int}
 >\left(\frac9{100}-\frac1{50\gamma_d}\right)Q
 >\frac7{200}Q,
 \qquad
 \gamma_d>\frac4{11}.
\tag{4}
\]
Combining (1), (3), and (4) gives the synchronized internal-mass transfer required here:
\[
 \sum_{y\in Y}w_y
 =2\widehat P
 \geq2H_{\rm int}
 >\frac7{100}Q.
\tag{5}
\]
No original full-atlas mass has been substituted for \(\widehat P\) in (3)-(5).

Accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c supplies, for every nonempty \(A\subseteq Y\), the single collision-aggregated legal selector inequality
\[
 R_{\rm sel}(C)
 \geq
 4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_A}
 \frac{\sum_{y\in A}w_y}{r_A^c+1}.
\tag{6}
\]
Apply (6) once with \(A=Y\). Using the full-rank hypotheses, (1), and (3)-(4),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{\rm sel}(C)
 &\geq
 4\gamma_d
 \frac{2\widehat P}{6}
 =\frac{4\gamma_d}{3}\widehat P\\
 &\geq\frac{4\gamma_d}{3}H_{\rm int}
 >\frac43\frac4{11}\frac7{200}Q
 =\frac{14}{825}Q.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
This is the ambient-normalized global quantity \(R_{\rm sel}(C)\) itself. It is not a sum of simplex scores, relative scores, parent rectangles, or separately normalized fiber capacities.

The canonical hard ledger in accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c gives for the same body and the same reservoir normalization
\[
 R_{\rm sel}(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{8}
\]
The strict gap is
\[
 \frac{14}{825}-\frac1{100}
 =\frac{23}{3300}>0.
\tag{9}
\]
Since \(Q>0\), equations (7)-(9) would imply
\[
 \frac Q{100}
 >R_{\rm sel}(C)
 >\frac Q{100}+\frac{23Q}{3300},
\]
which is impossible.

We finally audit the hypotheses not used numerically in (7). The deletion-stability conditions match accepted fact 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a term for term, so they do not create a weaker or different rank-five case. The greedy-simplex assumption is an additional restriction on an already impossible antecedent and cannot remove the contradiction.

The all-loop convention is the one in accepted fact e0dcff96f9b90a96: a label is a loop when its two distinct physical opposite endpoints lie on the same missed dyadic edge. Accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493 proves (4) before any loop/cross split by charging each whole-label deficit to one physical endpoint and by counting each missed edge once at each of its two endpoints. That count remains
\[
 \sum_{y\in S_{\rm miss}}t_{e_2(y)}
 =2\sum_{E\in\mathcal E_{\rm miss}}t_E
\]
when every internal label is a loop. Accepted fact f571c88ebc6fa492 likewise preserves whole labels and aggregates repeated physical sources before using capacity, with no loop/cross hypothesis. Hence neither (3), (4), nor the selector estimate (7) assumes positive cross-label mass.

Finally, accepted facts 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a, e0dcff96f9b90a96, and f9a8ae959b19c4b1 retain the actual original load-minimal contacts, reservoir parents and anchors, and all positive-volume integer fibers created by convex completion inside the same body \(C\). The present proof neither replaces those fibers by formal equal-volume models nor discards them. Both sides of the contradiction concern the one global selector capacity of that actual \(C\). Thus additional completion fibers, contact collisions, and the all-loop specialization cannot change the alias map or either strict inequality. The registered antecedent is therefore empty, proving the statement.
