## Statement

In the global convex-potential contact approximation theorem, fix any finite list of old marks $y\in\operatorname{int}K_z$ and corresponding prescribed paths $y_\varepsilon=y+\varepsilon w+o(\varepsilon)$ with fixed velocities $w$. For each mark let
$T_K(y)=\max\{\int_K f:0\le f\le1,\ \int_K(x-y)f(x)\,dx=0\}$ over measurable $f$. As $L\to\infty$, the right derivative at zero of $T_{K_{z,\varepsilon}}(y_\varepsilon)/|K_{z,\varepsilon}|$ for the fixed-$L$ roof deformation converges to that for the original action. Any fixed finite list of strict inequalities between these first responses, or between them and fixed thresholds, persists for one sufficiently large finite $L$.

## Proof

Both deformations have the radial limit and uniform inner homothet from the finite global-cut radial lemma, using the touching-plane representation of the roof on the measured fiber. In particular the prescribed mark is interior for small $\varepsilon$. Apply dbb1331ec0f15edd to the same old fiber $K=K_z$, mark $y$ and velocity $w$ for each deformation. Let $U$ be its fixed compact old hinge optimizer set and $V=|K|$, $T=T_K(y)$. For speed $s$ the quotient derivative is
$$
\frac1V\min_{u\in U}\left\{
(T/V)\int_{\partial K}s\,dS-\int_{\partial K}[1+u\cdot(x-y)]_+s(x)\,dS-Tu\cdot w\right\}.
$$
The first term uses the constant-weight radial volume derivative, and the last term is the same for both deformations. The hinged weights are bounded uniformly on the compact set $U\times\partial K$. Boundary $L^1$ convergence therefore gives uniform convergence of the expression minimized on $U$, including when $U$ is not a singleton. The difference of the minima is bounded by the supremum of the differences. Apply this separately to the finite list and take one $L$ large enough for its strictly positive margins.
