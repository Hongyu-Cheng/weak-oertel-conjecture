## Statement

Put
\[
 c_0:=\frac{3}{204800}.
\]
Then \(c_0\in(0,1/e)\), and the following branch-level weak Oertel theorem holds with the locked quantifier order: \(c_0\) is fixed before \(n,d,C\); for every \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\) and every convex body \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\), put
\[
 \Lambda:=\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d,
 \qquad
 S:=C\cap\Lambda,
\]
and assume \(S\ne\varnothing\). For \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\), put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}a_z=\mathcal H_d(S).
\tag{1}
\]
If \(W=0\), the conclusion below holds without any further hypothesis. Suppose \(W>0\), and define
\[
 h_{\max}(C):=
 \sup_{p\in S}
 \inf_{\substack{H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\text{ closed halfspace}\\p\in H}}
 \sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z),
 \qquad
 H_z:=\{x:(z,x)\in H\},
\tag{2}
\]
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C).
\tag{3}
\]
Assume that at least one of the following two alternatives holds.

1. \(G\geq W/8\).

2. \(G<W/8\), and the exact common canonical ratio-cut instance of accepted facts 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, 6b09aae189415c70, 6bfe50fed9a5540b, 27024aed67e77532, 3e918c586cba7e00, and c877c9e47d37372d occurs. Explicitly, \(r=(r_e)\) is the half-strength canonical product reservoir of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70,
\[
 Q:=\sum_er_e>0,
 \qquad
 R:=R_C(r),
\tag{4}
\]
\(b\leq r\) and \(0\leq\omega^b\leq b\) are the completed-midpoint restart of accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b obtained from the load-minimal KKT system of accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, and
\[
 \beta_y^b:=\sum_{e\ni y}\omega_e^b.
\tag{5}
\]
The physical endpoint set \(Y\), weights \(w_y>0\), and whole labels \(\sigma\) are the same collision-aggregated objects in accepted facts 27024aed67e77532 and 3e918c586cba7e00; every label has distinct endpoints \(a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\) and weight \(\widehat\theta_\sigma>0\). With the exact common aliases
\[
 q_y:=\min\left\{1,\frac{\beta_y^b}{w_y}\right\},
 \qquad
 I_\cap:=\sum_\sigma\widehat\theta_\sigma
 \left|q_{a(\sigma)}-q_{b(\sigma)}\right|,
\tag{6}
\]
the ratio-cut alternative is
\[
 I_\cap>\frac{3Q}{400}.
\tag{7}
\]
All remaining hypotheses and normalizations are those of the one common instance in accepted fact c877c9e47d37372d; in particular, no object from a different optimizer or reservoir is substituted into (4)--(7).

Then there exists \(\mathbf y^\star\in S\) such that every closed halfspace \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) containing \(\mathbf y^\star\) satisfies
\[
 \mathcal H_d(S\cap H)
 \geq
 \frac{3}{204800}\,2^{-n}W.
\tag{8}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 h_S(\mathbf y^\star)\geq c_0\,2^{-n}\mathcal H_d(S).
\tag{9}
\]
No conclusion is asserted here for the \(G<W/8\) intact whole-label alternative when the ratio-cut hypothesis (7) does not occur.

## Proof

First, \(c_0>0\). Moreover,
\[
 e=2+\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac1{k!}<
 2+\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}2^{-j}=3,
\tag{10}
\]
because \(k!\geq2^{k-1}\) for \(k\geq2\), with strict inequality for \(k\geq3\). Hence
\[
 0<c_0=\frac3{204800}<\frac13<\frac1e.
\tag{11}
\]
This fixes the absolute constant before all remaining quantifiers.

Fix \(n,d,C\) as in the statement. If \(W=0\), choose any \(\mathbf y^\star\in S\), which exists by hypothesis. Halfspace depth is nonnegative, while the right side of (8) is zero, so (8)--(9) follow. Hence assume \(W>0\).

The numerical estimate used below is
\[
 \frac1{\gamma_d}
 =\left(1+\frac1d\right)^d
 =\sum_{k=0}^d\binom dk d^{-k}
 \leq\sum_{k=0}^d\frac1{k!}<3,
\tag{12}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \gamma_d>\frac13.
\tag{13}
\]
Accepted fact 41c6cbb26797981c, with the exact normalization (3), proves the two scalar depth implications
\[
 D\geq\gamma_dG,
\tag{14}
\]
and, whenever the canonical reservoir (4) is defined,
\[
 D\geq\gamma_dR.
\tag{15}
\]

Suppose first that \(G\geq W/8\). Equations (13)--(14) give
\[
 D\geq\gamma_dG>\frac G3\geq\frac W{24}
 >\frac{3W}{204800},
\tag{16}
\]
where the last comparison is strict because \(W>0\) and \(72<204800\).

Suppose instead that \(G<W/8\) and the exact ratio-cut instance (4)--(7) occurs. Accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives
\[
 Q\geq\frac3{32}(W-4G)
 >\frac3{32}\left(W-\frac W2\right)
 =\frac{3W}{64}.
\tag{17}
\]
The strict inequality uses exactly \(G<W/8\) and \(W>0\).

The objects in (4)--(7) are, by hypothesis, the exact common aliases in the ratio-cut predecessor chain 6496cb3b0fd44f9d to 6bfe50fed9a5540b to 27024aed67e77532 and 3e918c586cba7e00 to c877c9e47d37372d. Therefore accepted fact c877c9e47d37372d applies without changing the optimizer, physical collision aggregation, or reservoir, and gives
\[
 \max\{R,G\}>\frac{3Q}{3200}.
\tag{18}
\]
Equations (14)--(15) imply
\[
 D\geq\gamma_d\max\{R,G\}.
\tag{19}
\]
Using (13), (17), and (18) in (19), with every strict inequality preserved, yields
\[
 D>
 \frac13\frac{3Q}{3200}
 =\frac Q{3200}
 >\frac{3W}{204800}.
\tag{20}
\]
Thus (16) or (20), according to the asserted branch, gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)=2^{-n}D
 >\frac{3}{204800}\,2^{-n}W.
\tag{21}
\]

It remains to pass correctly from the supremum to one point. For \(p\in S\), let the inner infimum in (2) be \(\phi(p)\). Since the supremum of \(\phi(p)\) over the nonempty set \(S\) is strictly larger than the right side of (21), the definition of supremum gives a point \(\mathbf y^\star\in S\) with
\[
 \phi(\mathbf y^\star)
 >\frac{3}{204800}\,2^{-n}W.
\tag{22}
\]
For every closed ambient halfspace \(H\), the fiber identity
\[
 (S\cap H)\cap(\{z\}\times\mathbb R^d)
 =\{z\}\times(C_z\cap H_z)
\]
gives
\[
 \mathcal H_d(S\cap H)
 =\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z).
\tag{23}
\]
Consequently \(\phi(\mathbf y^\star)=h_S(\mathbf y^\star)\). Equations (1), (22), and (23) prove the strict version of (8), hence (8)--(9). The proof used the maximum of the two separately valid bounds (14)--(15), not a sum of incompatible depth witnesses, and it makes no assertion about the unhandled intact whole-label branch.
