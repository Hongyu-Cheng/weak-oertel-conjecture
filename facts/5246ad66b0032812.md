## Statement

Retain all hypotheses, objects, and notation of accepted facts `036a276c54ec5e66` and `3100bf8082ca09d5`. Thus the canonical hard branch has \(n\geq2\), pooled endpoint weights
\[
 \tau_y>0\qquad(y\in E_\perp),
\]
total endpoint mass
\[
 T_E:=\sum_{y\in E_\perp}\tau_y>
 \frac{99}{100}Q,
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C).
\tag{2}
\]
Put
\[
 B_{\mathrm{cap}}:=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C).
\tag{3}
\]
The hard inequality and the accepted endpoint-frame bound are
\[
 B_{\mathrm{cap}}+D<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 D\geq\gamma_d\mathfrak F_E.
\tag{4}
\]

Let
\[
 B=\{y_0,y_1,\ldots,y_n\}\subseteq E_\perp
\tag{5}
\]
be any same-parity affine basis, ordered so that
\[
 M:=\tau_{y_0}=\max_{0\leq i\leq n}\tau_{y_i}.
\tag{6}
\]
For \(1\leq i\leq n\), put \(w_i:=\tau_{y_i}\), and define
\[
 S:=\sum_{i=1}^nw_i,
 \qquad
 U_B:=M+S,
\tag{7}
\]
\[
 \Delta_B:=
 \min_{\varnothing\ne I\subsetneq\{1,\ldots,n\}}
 \left|
  \sum_{i\in I}w_i-
  \sum_{j\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\setminus I}w_j
 \right|.
\tag{8}
\]
The minimum in (8) is over a nonempty family because \(n\geq2\). If
\[
 \mathfrak f(B):=
 \max_{\substack{I\mathbin{\dot\cup}J=\{1,\ldots,n\}\\
                  I\ne\varnothing,\ J\ne\varnothing}}
 \frac{
  \left(M+\sum_{i\in I}w_i\right)
  \left(M+\sum_{j\in J}w_j\right)}{M},
\tag{9}
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak f(B)=
 M+S+\frac{S^2-\Delta_B^2}{4M}.}
\tag{10}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \boxed{\Delta_B\leq M,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\mathfrak f(B)\geq\frac{U_B^2}{4M}.}
\tag{11}
\]

Since \(T_E>0\), define
\[
 \tau_{\max}:=\max_{y\in E_\perp}\tau_y>0.
\tag{12}
\]
Let \(U_*\) be the maximum of \(U_B\) over all same-parity affine bases \(B\subseteq E_\perp\). If there is no such basis, set \(U_*:=0\), consistently with the accepted convention \(\mathfrak F_E:=0\). Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak F_E\geq
 \frac{U_*^2}{4\tau_{\max}}.}
\tag{13}
\]

For every real \(\kappa>0\) with \(\kappa\leq99/e\), the additional inequality
\[
 T_E\leq B_{\mathrm{cap}}+\kappa\mathfrak F_E
\tag{14}
\]
excludes the canonical hard branch. In particular, \(32<99/e\), so the concrete endpoint-frame target
\[
 \boxed{
 T_E\leq B_{\mathrm{cap}}+32\mathfrak F_E}
\tag{15}
\]
suffices. By (13), the stronger sufficient target
\[
 \boxed{
 B_{\mathrm{cap}}+
 \frac{8U_*^2}{\tau_{\max}}\geq T_E}
\tag{16}
\]
also suffices.

Retain from accepted fact `8d0a0563ad180fca` the reflected-tail quantity \(E_{74}\), which satisfies
\[
 E_{74}>\frac{37}{300}Q.
\tag{17}
\]
For any \(A_0,C_0\geq0\) with
\[
 \max\{A_0,eC_0\}\leq\frac{37}{3},
\tag{18}
\]
the additional reflected-tail inequality
\[
 E_{74}\leq A_0B_{\mathrm{cap}}+C_0\mathfrak F_E
\tag{19}
\]
excludes the canonical hard branch. In particular, the concrete target
\[
 \boxed{
 E_{74}\leq12B_{\mathrm{cap}}+4\mathfrak F_E}
\tag{20}
\]
suffices. Assertions (14), (15), (16), (19), and (20) are conditional targets only; none is proved here.

## Proof

Fix a basis (5). For a nontrivial partition \(I\mathbin{\dot\cup}J=\{1,\ldots,n\}\), put
\[
 A:=\sum_{i\in I}w_i.
\]
Then \(\sum_{j\in J}w_j=S-A\), and direct expansion of the accepted frame score gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \frac{(M+A)(M+S-A)}M
 &=M+S+\frac{A(S-A)}M\\
 &=M+S+
   \frac{S^2-(2A-S)^2}{4M}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{21}
\]
Maximizing (21) over all nontrivial partitions is exactly the same as minimizing \(|2A-S|\). Definition (8) therefore proves (10).

It remains to bound the minimum discrepancy. Start with the two nonempty parts \(I=\{1\}\) and \(J=\{2\}\). Their discrepancy is
\[
 |w_1-w_2|\leq M.
\tag{22}
\]
For each \(k=3,\ldots,n\), add \(k\) to a currently lighter part. If the discrepancy before this addition is \(\delta\leq M\), then the new discrepancy is
\[
 |\delta-w_k|\leq\max\{\delta,w_k\}\leq M,
\tag{23}
\]
because \(w_k\leq M\). Both parts remain nonempty throughout. The resulting nontrivial partition has discrepancy at most \(M\), which proves the first inequality in (11).

Substitute \(\Delta_B\leq M\) into (10). Since \(U_B=M+S\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathfrak f(B)
 &\geq M+S+\frac{S^2-M^2}{4M}\\
 &=\frac{U_B^2}{4M}+\frac{U_B}{2}
 \geq\frac{U_B^2}{4M}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{24}
\]
This proves the second inequality in (11).

If a same-parity affine basis exists, the finite endpoint set makes the maximum defining \(U_*\) attainable. Choose a maximizing basis and let \(M_*\) be its pivot weight. The definitions of \(\mathfrak F_E\) and \(\mathfrak f(B)\), followed by (11) and \(M_*\leq\tau_{\max}\), give
\[
 \mathfrak F_E
 \geq\mathfrak f(B)
 \geq\frac{U_*^2}{4M_*}
 \geq\frac{U_*^2}{4\tau_{\max}}.
\tag{25}
\]
If no such basis exists, both sides of (13) are zero by convention. Thus (13) holds in all cases.

We now prove only the terminal calibration. Accepted fact `3100bf8082ca09d5` gives
\[
 \gamma_d>\frac1e.
\tag{26}
\]
For completeness, this follows from
\[
 \left(1+\frac1d\right)^d<e,
 \qquad
 \gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}.
\]
Also,
\[
 e
 =\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac1{k!}
 <\frac83+\frac1{24}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac1{5^m}
 =\frac{87}{32}<\frac{11}{4}.
\tag{27}
\]
Hence the requested rational comparison is
\[
 \frac{49}{1600e}
 >\frac{49}{4400}
 >\frac1{100}.
\tag{28}
\]

Let \(0<\kappa\leq99/e\), and assume (14). Put
\[
 L:=\max\left\{1,\frac{\kappa}{\gamma_d}\right\}.
\tag{29}
\]
By (26),
\[
 \frac{\kappa}{\gamma_d}<e\kappa\leq99,
 \qquad
 L<99.
\tag{30}
\]
All terms in \(B_{\mathrm{cap}}\) are nonnegative. Therefore (4), (14), and (29) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T_E
 &\leq B_{\mathrm{cap}}+\kappa\mathfrak F_E\\
 &\leq L\left(B_{\mathrm{cap}}+
                  \gamma_d\mathfrak F_E\right)\\
 &\leq L\left(B_{\mathrm{cap}}+D\right)\\
 &<\frac{99}{100}Q,
 \end{aligned}
\tag{31}
\]
contrary to (1). This proves the sharp stated threshold \(\kappa\leq99/e\). Equation (27) gives
\[
 32e<88<99,
\tag{32}
\]
so \(32<99/e\), and (15) is a valid specialization. Also, (13) gives
\[
 32\mathfrak F_E\geq
 \frac{8U_*^2}{\tau_{\max}},
\tag{33}
\]
so (16) implies (15).

It remains to check the reflected-tail calibration. Assume (18)--(19), and put
\[
 L_{74}:=\max\left\{A_0,\frac{C_0}{\gamma_d}\right\}.
\tag{34}
\]
Equations (18) and (26) imply
\[
 L_{74}\leq\frac{37}{3}.
\tag{35}
\]
Using nonnegativity and (4) exactly as above,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_{74}
 &\leq A_0B_{\mathrm{cap}}+C_0\mathfrak F_E\\
 &\leq L_{74}
       \left(B_{\mathrm{cap}}+\gamma_d\mathfrak F_E\right)\\
 &\leq L_{74}\left(B_{\mathrm{cap}}+D\right)\\
 &<\frac{37}{300}Q,
 \end{aligned}
\tag{36}
\]
contradicting (17). Finally, (27) gives \(4e<11<12<37/3\). Hence \((A_0,C_0)=(12,4)\) satisfies (18), proving the calibration for (20). No step proves any of the conditional inequalities.
