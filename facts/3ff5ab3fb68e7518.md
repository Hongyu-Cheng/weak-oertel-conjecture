## Statement

Use the hypotheses and notation of the saturated fixed-facet response lemma. For any fixed actual centers and nonnegative rates, the common menu mass is at most $Q$ at all sufficiently small positive times, and every initially empty A trace remains of zero $d$-volume, including when its old threshold was at maximum support.

Let $\Theta$ be the finite set of branch vectors with $\theta_i=1$ for proper cuts, $\theta_i=0$ for empty cuts and strict exterior cuts, and $\theta_i\in\{0,1\}$ independently for full cuts at minimum support. Set $w_i^\theta=(k_i+2\theta_i\sigma_i)^{-1}$ and $W_\theta=\sum_i w_i^\theta$. Then the actual first loss is
$$
L(u,v)=\max_{\theta\in\Theta}
\frac{\sum_i w_i^\theta[duU_i+\theta_i\sigma_i(uc_i+ve_i)]}{W_\theta}.
$$
It is convex, piecewise affine and positively homogeneous in the nonnegative rates.

## Proof

At the old middle level $s_{i0}$, nesting of $A_t$ and the threshold increase $tv e_i\ge0$ give $f_i(s_{i0},t)\le Q$. Thus its local inverse at $Q$ is at most $s_{i0}$. Their sum is nonpositive there and strictly decreases with the common mass, so $Q(t)\le Q$. For an initially empty A cut equality holds at $s_{i0}$ because the A trace still has zero volume at that old middle level. Its inverse at $Q(t)\le Q$ therefore gives a middle level at least $s_{i0}$, so the actual threshold in $A_t$ is no lower than $\beta_i$. It still cuts zero $d$-volume.

In particular the actual loss satisfies $L\ge0$. For every trial loss $\ell\ge0$, initially empty cuts have $U_i=0$ and $c_i,e_i\ge0$, so their negative-part hinges vanish. For a full supporting cut, the inverse is the smaller of its inactive and active affine formulas: the active minus inactive value equals $-\sigma_i[2\ell-2duU_i+k_i(uc_i+ve_i)]/[k_i(k_i+2\sigma_i)]$. The proper and strict exterior terms use their fixed branches. Because each binary choice is independent when taking the sum of these minima, $\Phi(\ell;u,v)$ is the minimum over $\Theta$ of $W_\theta$ times $\ell$ minus the corresponding weighted average in the statement. At the actual zero every branch is nonnegative and some branch is zero, since all $W_\theta>0$. This is the maximum formula. Finiteness gives convexity, piecewise affinity and homogeneity.
