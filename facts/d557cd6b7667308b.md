## Statement

For $G=\{(p_i,w_i):0\le i\le12\}$ with the thirteen-site base and weights of the strict-quota centroid graph obstruction, place atom $w_i$ at each $(p_i,w_i)$. The maximum closed-halfspace raw depth over the graph points is exactly $443/2912>1/7$, attained at index $2$.

## Proof

Use the tight-support table of the strict-quota graph obstruction, and put $g_i=(p_i,w_i)$. If a closed halfspace contains $g_i$, the atoms it excludes have a convex hull missing $g_i$, because its strict complementary affine inequality is preserved by finite convex combinations. Conversely any subset of atoms whose hull misses $g_i$ can be strictly separated from $g_i$ by a closed halfspace containing $g_i$ and excluding the subset. The empty subset causes no exception. Thus the depth is one minus the maximum mass of a subset whose hull misses $g_i$. This is an exact equivalence with a finite maximum and retains all closed boundary atoms.

For target $i$, enumerate integer masks $0\le m<8192$. Reject a mask containing $i$ or containing any of its tight-support masks, and otherwise sum the corresponding integer weight numerators. The graph convex-hull test in the preceding fact proves that precisely these masks are admissible.
