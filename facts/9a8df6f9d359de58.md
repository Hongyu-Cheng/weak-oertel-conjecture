## Statement

\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\]
Fix the retained \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) and use the exact aliases
\[
 Y:=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad
 w_y:=\widehat\tau_y(v),
 \qquad
 \widehat P:=\widehat P(v).

Assume
\[
 \rho_Y=n,
 \qquad
 r_Y^c=5,
 \qquad
 \rho_{Y\setminus\{y\}}=n,
 \qquad

 \quad(y\in Y),

where \(r_A^c\) is the residual rank of the actual endpoint centroids \(c_y\) as defined in accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c.  Retain also the exact four-corner measure \(\nu_\square\), its mass

## Proof

We first verify that the target and accepted fact f50134018e214ece concern literally the same instance.  Accepted fact 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a fixes the displayed \(v\), \(Y=\widehat Y(v)\), \(w_y=\widehat\tau_y(v)\), and \(\widehat P=\widehat P(v)\), and states the four displayed rank equalities term for term.  Accepted fact 655a8b1d63af6ab2 uses the same fixed \(v\), the same endpoint set and weights, and writes \(P=\widehat P(v)\) for the synchronized retained mass.  This \(P\) is therefore exactly \(\widehat P\) above, not the original full-atlas mass denoted by \(P\) in the first part of accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493.

There is also no change of endpoint-centroid chart.  Accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c defines, for every nonempty \(A\subseteq\widehat Y(v)\),
\[
 \rho_A:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(A),
 \qquad
 r_A^c:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(A)\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_y-s(y):y\in A\}.
\]
Thus the target's ranks for \(Y\) and \(Y\setminus\{y\}\) are the exact ranks used in accepted fact f50134018e214ece, with the same centroids \(c_y\), the same optimizer \(v\), and the same deletion quantifier over every \(y\in Y\).  Accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493 uses this same synchronized optimizer and the same internal whole-label weights, while accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c identifies those weights with the retained weights on the common internal family.  Consequently the body \(C\), reservoir mass \(Q\), internal mass \(H_{\mathrm{int}}\), and global selector \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) in the target are the objects with those names in accepted fact f50134018e214ece.  The retained conclusions about \(\nu_\square\) are consequences of these hypotheses; they neither replace \(Y\) nor introduce a second normalization.

Suppose, for contradiction, that the registered disjunction fails.  Its first alternative then fails.  Hence every lattice simplex supported on the maximum-weight greedy rank-raising label endpoints has legal ambient-normalized root-affine score strictly below \(Q/100\).  This is exactly the remaining hypothesis in accepted fact f50134018e214ece.  If the greedy construction produced no such simplex, the same universal statement is vacuously true, so there is no uncovered empty-family case.  Failure of the complete-core alternative is not needed and may be discarded.

All hypotheses of accepted fact f50134018e214ece now hold under the exact aliases above.  That fact says that no such canonical hard instance exists.  Its contradiction uses the same single selector on the same body: accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493 gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)>\frac{14}{825}Q,
\]
whereas the canonical hard ledger of accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\]
These are incompatible because
\[
 \frac{14}{825}-\frac1{100}=\frac{23}{3300}>0
\]
and \(Q>0\).  Thus the assumed failure of the registered disjunction is impossible, proving the conditional conclusion.

Finally, the specialization does not assume positive cross-label mass.  Accepted fact 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a constructs \(\nu_\square\), proves its mass and quotient-rank conclusions, and audits its physical-source collisions without a loop/cross split.  Accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493 charges each whole-label deficit to one actual endpoint and counts a missed dyadic edge once at each of its two endpoints, explicitly including the case in which every internal label is a projected loop.  Accepted fact f50134018e214ece therefore states its impossibility for the allowed all-loop case as well.  The same contradiction applies there, so all-loop coverage is complete.
