## Statement

Retain the complete canonical hard-branch hypotheses, objects, and notation of accepted facts `2fe46f47d19112ea`, `d01de728d8b5e33c`, and `e0dcff96f9b90a96`. In particular,
\[
 D<\frac{Q}{100},
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac{Q}{100},
 \qquad
 n\geq 2,
 \qquad
 \gamma_d>\frac4{11},
 \tag{1}
\]
and the rescaled internal atlas is the finite family \(\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\) with positive whole-label weights \(\eta_\sigma\), two distinct opposite endpoints \(a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\in S_{\mathrm{miss}}\), and occurrence contacts \(x_{\sigma,y}\in C_y\). Put
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma
 >\frac7{400}Q.
 \tag{2}
\]
For \(y\in S_{\mathrm{miss}}\), retain the functions and masses from accepted fact `e0dcff96f9b90a96`,
\[
 F_y
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \widetilde g_{\sigma,y},
 \qquad
 R_y:=\int_{C_y}F_y
 =\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma,
 \tag{3}
\]
\[
 B_y:=\frac12-F_y,
 \qquad
 b_y:=\int_{C_y}B_y=\frac{a_y}{2}-R_y.
 \tag{4}
\]
Since that accepted fact gives \(b_y\geq a_y/4>0\), define the always-available complement barycenter
\[
 \bar q_y:=\frac1{b_y}\int_{C_y}xB_y(x)\,dx.
 \tag{5}
\]
Let
\[
 S_{\mathrm{int}}:=\{y\in S_{\mathrm{miss}}:R_y>0\}.
 \tag{6}
\]

Assume that there exist an affine map
\[
 s:\operatorname{aff}(S_{\mathrm{int}})\longrightarrow\mathbb R^d
\]
and a linear subspace \(W\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) with \(\dim W\leq3\) such that
\[
 x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\in W
 \quad
 \text{for every }\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}
 \text{ and }y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\},
 \tag{7}
\]
and
\[
 \bar q_y-s(y)\in W
 \quad(y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}).
 \tag{8}
\]

For \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) and every nonempty \(A\subseteq\widehat Y(v)\), use the quantities of accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c`,
\[
 \rho_A:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(A),
 \qquad
 r_A^c:=
 \min_{s_A:\operatorname{aff}(A)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_y-s_A(y):y\in A\},
 \tag{9}
\]
and define
\[
 \Lambda_v(A):=
 2^{n-\rho_A}
 \frac{\sum_{y\in A}\widehat\tau_y(v)}{r_A^c+1}.
 \tag{10}
\]
Then, for every \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\), there is a nonempty proper subset \(A\subsetneq\widehat Y(v)\) such that
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)>\frac{Q}{400\gamma_d}.
 \tag{11}
\]
More precisely, one may choose \(A=S_{\mathrm{int}}\) when \(S_{\mathrm{int}}\subsetneq\widehat Y(v)\), and then
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)>\frac7{800}Q.
 \tag{12}
\]
When \(S_{\mathrm{int}}=\widehat Y(v)\), one may delete a minimum-weight endpoint from \(S_{\mathrm{int}}\), and then
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)>\frac7{960}Q.
 \tag{13}
\]
Consequently the chart assumptions (7)-(8) contradict the canonical hard inequality in (1). No existence assertion for the common chart \((s,W)\) is made.

## Proof

First observe that \(S_{\mathrm{int}}\neq\varnothing\). Indeed, (2) is positive, every \(\eta_\sigma\) is positive, and both endpoints of every internal label receive that label's positive contribution in (3).

Fix \(y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}\). Every rescaled occurrence density \(\widetilde g_{\sigma,y}\) has mass \(\eta_\sigma\) and barycenter \(x_{\sigma,y}\). Hence (3) gives the exact first-moment identity
\[
 \int_{C_y}xF_y(x)\,dx
 =\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}.
 \tag{14}
\]
The pointwise identity \(F_y+B_y=1/2\), the definition
\[
 c_y=\frac1{a_y}\int_{C_y}x\,dx,
\]
and (5), (14) imply
\[
 \frac{a_y}{2}c_y
 =\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}
 +b_y\bar q_y.
 \tag{15}
\]
The scalar coefficients on the right have the exact sum
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma+b_y
 =R_y+\left(\frac{a_y}{2}-R_y\right)
 =\frac{a_y}{2}.
 \tag{16}
\]
Subtracting \((a_y/2)s(y)\) from (15) and using (16) therefore gives
\[
 \frac{a_y}{2}\bigl(c_y-s(y)\bigr)
 =\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma\bigl(x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\bigr)
 +b_y\bigl(\bar q_y-s(y)\bigr).
 \tag{17}
\]
Every term on the right belongs to \(W\) by (7)-(8). Since \(a_y>0\) and \(W\) is linear, (17) proves
\[
 c_y-s(y)\in W
 \quad(y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}).
 \tag{18}
\]
For every nonempty \(A\subseteq S_{\mathrm{int}}\), restrict the same affine map \(s\) to \(\operatorname{aff}(A)\). Equation (18) then yields
\[
 r_A^c
 \leq
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_y-s(y):y\in A\}
 \leq\dim W
 \leq3.
 \tag{19}
\]
This verifies the affine-rank claim without choosing separate charts for different subsets.

Now fix an arbitrary \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\). Accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c` preserves every internal whole label with its exact synchronized weight:
\[
 \widehat\theta_\sigma(v)=\eta_\sigma
 \quad(\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}).
 \tag{20}
\]
Consequently, for every \(y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}\),
\[
 \widehat\tau_y(v)\geq R_y>0,
 \tag{21}
\]
so \(S_{\mathrm{int}}\subseteq\widehat Y(v)\). Summing (21) before discarding the noninternal retained labels and then counting the two distinct endpoint occurrences of every internal whole label gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 &\geq
 \sum_{y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}}R_y\\
 &=2\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma
 =2H_{\mathrm{int}}
 >\frac7{200}Q.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{22}
\]

Suppose first that \(S_{\mathrm{int}}\subsetneq\widehat Y(v)\), and choose \(A=S_{\mathrm{int}}\). This set is nonempty and proper. Since \(A\subseteq\mathbb R^n\), one has \(\rho_A\leq n\) and hence \(2^{n-\rho_A}\geq1\). Equations (19) and (22) give
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)
 =2^{n-\rho_A}
 \frac{\sum_{y\in A}\widehat\tau_y(v)}{r_A^c+1}
 \geq\frac14\sum_{y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 >\frac7{800}Q.
 \tag{23}
\]

It remains to treat \(S_{\mathrm{int}}=\widehat Y(v)\). Accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c` proves
\[
 |\widehat Y(v)|\geq n+4\geq6.
 \tag{24}
\]
Let \(k:=|S_{\mathrm{int}}|\) and choose \(y_0\in S_{\mathrm{int}}\) minimizing \(\widehat\tau_y(v)\). Put
\[
 A:=S_{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\{y_0\}.
\]
Then \(A\) is nonempty and proper in \(\widehat Y(v)\), and minimality gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{y\in A}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 &\geq
 \left(1-\frac1k\right)
 \sum_{y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}}\widehat\tau_y(v)\\
 &\geq\frac56
 \sum_{y\in S_{\mathrm{int}}}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 >\frac7{240}Q.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{25}
\]
Again \(\rho_A\leq n\), and (19) applies to this \(A\). Thus
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)
 \geq\frac14\sum_{y\in A}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 >\frac7{960}Q.
 \tag{26}
\]

Both cases cross the required threshold strictly. Indeed, accepted fact `2fe46f47d19112ea` gives \(\gamma_d>4/11\), so
\[
 \frac{Q}{400\gamma_d}
 <\frac{11}{1600}Q
 <\frac7{960}Q,
 \tag{27}
\]
where the final comparison is exact because
\[
 \frac7{960}-\frac{11}{1600}
 =\frac1{2400}>0.
\]
Equation (23) is even stronger because \(7/800>7/960\). This proves (11)-(13) for the arbitrary load-minimal optimizer \(v\).

Finally, equation (38) of accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c` applies to the constructed nonempty \(A\subseteq\widehat Y(v)\) and gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq4\gamma_d\Lambda_v(A)
 >4\gamma_d\frac{Q}{400\gamma_d}
 =\frac{Q}{100},
 \tag{28}
\]
contradicting (1). Therefore the canonical hard branch cannot admit a common chart satisfying (7)-(8). The proof supplies no construction of that chart.
