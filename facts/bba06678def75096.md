## Statement

Consider the mixed trace of a compact convex set, with a finite family of closed ambient halfspaces whose open interiors cover all its positive $d$-volume integer fibers, $d\ge1$. For each halfspace suppose there is a witness in the interior of a positive fiber that lies strictly inside its halfspace and strictly outside all the others. Then at most $d+1$ witnesses share any one integer base. If two witnesses share a base, the two corresponding open caps intersect inside that fiber in positive $d$-volume.

## Proof

If $d+2$ witnesses share a base, apply Radon's theorem to their continuous coordinates. There is a point in the convex hull of each of two disjoint groups of these witnesses. In the common fiber it lies strictly outside every cap, using the representation by the group excluding that cap's witness. For a cap whose witness was not among the selected $d+2$, either group suffices. This contradicts the open cover.

For two witnesses in one fiber, the segment joining them lies in its interior and strictly outside all other closed caps. It is covered by the two open caps. The endpoints belong exclusively to their own caps, so connectedness forces a point of the segment in both open caps. Their overlap contains a neighborhood of this point in the continuous fiber and has positive $d$-volume.
