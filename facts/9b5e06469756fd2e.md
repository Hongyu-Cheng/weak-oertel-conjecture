## Statement

Let \(L\ge1\) and \(n_1,\ldots,n_L\ge1\) be integers. For every i let \(Q_i\subseteq\mathbb R^{n_i}\) be convex and let \(A_i=Q_i\cap\mathbb Z^{n_i}\) be finite and nonempty. Suppose that its set of nontrivial integral midpoint centers
$$
\mathcal C_i=\left\lbrace (x+y)/2:x,y\in A_i,\ x\ne y,\ (x+y)/2\in\mathbb Z^{n_i}\right\rbrace
$$
has cardinality at most two. For arbitrary \(\ell_i\in\mathbb R^{n_i}\), retain the original raw weights, normalizers, and laws
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_{i,x}&=e^{\ell_i\cdot x},&
Z_i&=\sum_{x\in A_i}w_{i,x},&
p_{i,x}&=w_{i,x}/Z_i,\\
P&=\bigotimes_{i=1}^Lp_i,&
N&=\sum_i n_i,&
K_i&=\left|\left\lbrace x+2\mathbb Z^{n_i}:x\in A_i\right\rbrace\right|,
\qquad \mathcal K=\prod_i K_i.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
$$
Extend all laws by zero off their supports.

When \(|\mathcal C_i|=2\), enumerate its members as \(c_{i,0},c_{i,1}\), and set
\(R_{i,a}=A_i\cap(2c_{i,a}-A_i)\), \(M_{i,a}=(|R_{i,a}|+1)/2\), \(m_{i,a}=M_{i,a}-1\), \(U_i=R_{i,0}\cup R_{i,1}\), and \(q_{\mathrm{ov},i}=|R_{i,0}\cap R_{i,1}|\). These are the full cores, capacities, total noncentral pair counts, union, and overlap of accepted fact 9e1805a461c7be2c; its overlap t is our \(q_{\mathrm{ov},i}\). In particular \(m_{i,a}\ge1\), \(q_{\mathrm{ov},i}\in\lbrace0,1,2\rbrace\), and the exact occupied parity count of \(U_i\) is \(k_{i,U_i}=M_{i,0}+M_{i,1}-q_{\mathrm{ov},i}\). Write \(s_i=|A_i\setminus U_i|\); then \(K_i=k_{i,U_i}+s_i\).

For \(q_{\mathrm{ov},i}\in\lbrace1,2\rbrace\), define the following quantities specifically for the conditional union law:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z_{i,U_i}&=\sum_{x\in U_i}w_{i,x},&
\beta_{i,U_i}&=Z_{i,U_i}/Z_i,&
\pi_{i,U_i}(x)&=\frac{p_{i,x}}{\beta_{i,U_i}}=\frac{w_{i,x}}{Z_{i,U_i}},\\
a_{i,a}&=\pi_{i,U_i}(c_{i,a}),&
S_{i,a}&=\sum_{x\in R_{i,a}\setminus\lbrace c_{i,a}\rbrace}\pi_{i,U_i}(x),&
\mu_{i,a}&=\frac{S_{i,a}/a_{i,a}+2}{2(m_{i,a}+1)},\\
A_{0,i,U_i}&=k_{i,U_i}^{\,2}\sum_{x\in U_i}\pi_{i,U_i}(x)^3,&
\Delta_{i,a}&=4m_{i,a}(m_{i,a}+1)k_{i,U_i}^{\,2}a_{i,a}^3,&
A_{\mathrm{full},i,U_i}&=A_{0,i,U_i}+\Delta_{i,0}+\Delta_{i,1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
$$
If \(q_{\mathrm{ov},i}=2\), require only the disjunction
$$
A_{0,i,U_i}\ge1
\quad\text{or}\quad
\max(\mu_{i,0},\mu_{i,1})\le2\min(\mu_{i,0},\mu_{i,1})
\quad\text{or}\quad
A_{\mathrm{full},i,U_i}>9/8.
\tag{3}
$$
There is no corresponding restriction when \(q_{\mathrm{ov},i}=1\), and no restriction on zero-center or one-center factors or on two-center factors with disjoint cores. The means in (2) are unchanged if computed from the original probabilities \(p_{i,x}\), because both masses in their ratios scale by \(\beta_{i,U_i}\). Activity is not invariant under conditioning: its exact original-law expression is
\(A_{0,i,U_i}=k_{i,U_i}^{\,2}\beta_{i,U_i}^{-3}\sum_{x\in U_i}p_{i,x}^3\).

Use the actual ambient-halfspace depth and moment conventions of accepted fact 445a9e668ee1a705:
