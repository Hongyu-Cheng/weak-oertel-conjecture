## Statement

Let $T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,e_2)\subset\mathbb R^2$, $\delta=1/100$, and write $\mu_K$ for uniform probability on a positive-area set $K$. The law $\nu=(1/5)\mu_{\delta T}+(4/5)\mu_T$ has compact convex support $T$ and is a mixture of two nested common-vertex homothetic simplex laws. Its maximal ordinary closed-halfspace depth is at most $(77-16\sqrt{13})/45<4/9$.

## Proof

Depth superlevel sets of any finite measure are convex: a halfspace containing a convex combination contains at least one point of positive weight in the combination. The law is invariant under swapping coordinates, so replacing a point by the midpoint of it and its swap cannot decrease depth. Points outside $T$ have depth zero by strict separation. It suffices to give an upper bound for $y=(t,t)$, $0\le t\le1/2$.

The closed cap $x_1+x_2\le 2t$ through $y$ has mass at most $1/5+(16/5)\delta^2$ for $t\le\delta$, since the large triangle contributes its weight times $(2t)^2$. For $t\ge\delta$ the small triangle lies wholly in this cap, whose mass is then $1/5+(16/5)t^2$. In the latter range the cap $x_1\ge t$ has mass $(4/5)(1-t)^2$: its small-triangle trace is null even at $t=\delta$, and its large-triangle trace is a vertex cap. The minimum of these two masses is maximized at the intersection of the increasing and decreasing functions, namely $t_*=(\sqrt{13}-2)/6\in(\delta,1/2)$. There the common value is $(77-16\sqrt{13})/45$. The small-$t$ bound is less than $13/45$, while the common value exceeds $13/45$ by $\sqrt{13}<4$. Finally $(77-16\sqrt{13})/45<4/9$ follows from $57^2<16^2\cdot13$.
