## Statement

For the lifted convex hull with intervals $[0,a],[0,b],[0,a]$ at $-1,0,1$, with $b\ge a>0$, put $r=b/a$. Its true mixed length centroid is $(0,a(r^2+2)/(2(r+2)))$. Its normalized closed mixed halfspace depth is
$$
\begin{cases}
(r^2+2r+6)/[2(r+2)^2],&1\le r\le2\ \text{or}\ r\ge4,\\
(-r^2+8r+2)/[2(r+2)^2],&2\le r\le1+\sqrt3,\\
(r^2+4r-2)/[2(r+2)^2],&1+\sqrt3\le r\le4.
\end{cases}
$$
Its unique minimum over $r\ge1$ is $(\sqrt3-1)/2$ at $r=1+\sqrt3$.

## Proof

The equal extreme widths give base mean zero, and integrating height on each original interval gives height $(b^2+2a^2)/(2(b+2a))$. Scale heights by $1/a$ so the extreme intervals are $[0,1]$, the middle is $[0,r]$, and the centroid height is $c=(r^2+2)/(2(r+2))$. The exact three-interval formula applies after subtracting $1/2$ from the heights. Its endpoint lengths are $1,1$, and the middle width and shift are $r$ and $(r-1)/2$. In the common-floor coordinates the lower and upper raw oblique minima at height $x$ are $L(x)=x+\min(2x,1)$ and $U(x)=r-x+\min(2(1-x)_+,1)$. The same formula shows the purely base caps do not lower the depth.

At the centroid, $c\ge1/2$, with $c\le1$ exactly for $r\le1+\sqrt3$. Thus $L(c)=c+1$ and $U(c)=r+2-3c$ up to that seam, then $U(c)=r-c$. In the first range $U(c)-L(c)=r+1-4c=-(r-1)(r-2)/(r+2)$, so $L$ is smaller for $1\le r\le2$. After the seam $U(c)-L(c)=r-1-2c=(r-4)/(r+2)$, so $L$ is smaller for $r\ge4$. Dividing the selected masses by $r+2$ gives the three formulas. In their displayed order the derivatives are $(r-4)/(r+2)^3$, $6(1-r)/(r+2)^3$, and $6/(r+2)^3$. They yield strict decrease before the claimed minimizer and strict increase after it, and direct substitution gives the stated value.
