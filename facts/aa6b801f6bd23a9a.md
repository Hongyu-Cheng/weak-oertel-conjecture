## Statement

Let $L\subset\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge2$, be a convex body, let $B(c,a)$ be an open Euclidean ball of positive radius, and suppose $L'=L\cap\overline{B(c,a)}$ is full dimensional. Write $\eta_L$ for $\mathcal H^{d-1}$ on its regular boundary marked by its unique outer unit normal, $S_L$ for the normal marginal, and $G(v,x)=v$ for projection to the normal. On the open normal set $V=\{v\in\mathbb S^{d-1}:h_L(v)<v\cdot c+a\}$,
$$
 S_{L'}|_V=\bigl[G_\#(\mathbf 1_{\{x\in B(c,a)\}}\eta_L)\bigr]|_V.
$$
If the exposed face of $L$ at $v$ meets the open ball, then $v\in V$ and the cut body is full dimensional.

## Proof

At a regular boundary point $x$ of $L'$ lying on the cutting sphere, the unit vector $(x-c)/a$ supports the ball and hence $L'$ at $x$. If $v$ is the unique outer unit normal there, then $x=c+av$, which would imply $h_L(v)\ge v\cdot c+a$. Thus the regular boundary of $L'$ with normal in $V$ lies in the open ball. Within that ball the bodies coincide locally, so their boundaries and unique normals coincide there. Conversely a regular boundary point of $L$ inside the ball belongs to the regular boundary of $L'$ with the same normal. The irregular boundary of each full-dimensional convex body is $\mathcal H^{d-1}$-null, yielding the stated equality.

If $x$ belongs to the exposed face and lies in the open ball, then $h_L(v)-v\cdot c=v\cdot(x-c)\le |x-c|<a$. Every open neighborhood of $x$ meets $\operatorname{int}L$, so the cut body contains an open set.
