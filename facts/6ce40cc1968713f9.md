## Statement

Let $\nu$ be a finite positive measure on labeled points $(i,z,x)$ with finite second moments, unit label vector $U=u_i$, and total mass $W$. Write $q=(1,z)$, and suppose $\int qU^T\,d\nu=0$ and $\int xU^T\,d\nu=Q$ with $Q=Q^T$. Put
$$
G=\int qq^T\,d\nu,\quad B=\int xq^T\,d\nu,\quad E=\int xx^T\,d\nu,\quad A=\int UU^T\,d\nu,\quad R_x=E-BG^+B^T
$$
with Moore–Penrose inverses. Then $R_x\succeq QA^+Q$ and $(\operatorname{tr}Q)^2\le W\operatorname{tr}R_x$. In the unframed stress setting the measure with label contributions $\mu|_{H_i}+r_i\,d\tau_i$ satisfies these hypotheses and $\operatorname{tr}Q=v(\tau)$.

## Proof

For a null vector $w$ of $G$, $w\cdot q=0$ almost everywhere and hence $Bw=0$. Therefore the residual $R=x-BG^+q$ is orthogonal in second moments to $q$, and its second moment is $R_x$ by expanding the product. This also gives the least-squares affine regression. Its cross moment with $U$ is $Q$ by the assumed zero affine-base moment. For a null vector $w$ of $A$, $w\cdot U=0$ almost everywhere and hence $Qw=0$. Now, for any $v$, minimizing the nonnegative integral $\int(v\cdot R-w\cdot U)^2\,d\nu$ over $w$ gives $v^T(R_x-QA^+Q)v\ge0$ by completing the square. Finally $\operatorname{tr}Q=\int R\cdot U\,d\nu$, so Cauchy–Schwarz and $|U|=1$ give the trace inequality. For the labeled cap-plus-section measure the zero force and base force are exactly the zero cross moment with $q$, and the source tensor identity gives $Q$ and its trace.
