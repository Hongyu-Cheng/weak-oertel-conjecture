## Statement

Let \(n\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^2\) be a convex body, and assume the canonical hypotheses of accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`.  Use its exact notation
\[
 W:=\sum_z\operatorname{area}(C_z)>0,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
its half-strength canonical product reservoir of mass \(Q>0\), its realizable-submatching capacity \(R\), its load-minimal optimizer of load \(\Omega\), and its canonical total capacity
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 :=R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
   +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\tag{1}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal A(C)\geq\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{2}
\]

Consequently, the common planar triangular-homothetic instance of accepted fact `4298be06631f6503` cannot satisfy its canonical hard assumption \(\mathcal A(C)<Q/100\).  Hence, under that canonical hard hypothesis, its registered descendant-retention implication holds in every base dimension \(n\), not merely for \(n\leq10\).  In particular, no actual instance realizes the simultaneous numerical survivor window
\[
 \frac GW<\frac{351}{517600},
 \qquad
 \frac6{2^n+1}\leq\eta<\frac{1053}{118400},
 \qquad n\geq11
\tag{3}
\]
derived in `4298be06631f6503`.

## Proof

Suppose for a contradiction that
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{4}
\]
All six summands in (1) are nonnegative.  Therefore
\[
 R<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 D<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{5}
\]

We now use the retained matching supplied by accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`, rather than adding another lower bound for either \(D\) or \(G\).  That accepted fact gives
\[
 \Omega\geq Q-R,
 \qquad
 Q\geq\frac3{32}(W-4G),
\tag{6}
\]
and, for its retained parity classes,
\[
 L_{\mathrm{ret}}
 :=\sum_{\xi\ \mathrm{retained}}L_\xi
 \geq2\Omega-G-\frac W{64}.
\tag{7}
\]
It also defines on every retained class the unordered off-diagonal product matching
\[
 s_{\{u,v\}}:=\frac{\beta_u\beta_v}{L_\xi}
 \quad(u<v,\ u,v\equiv\xi),
\tag{8}
\]
with
\[
 |s|_\xi\geq\frac38L_\xi.
\tag{9}
\]

The second inequality in (6) and the strict bound for \(G\) in (5) give an actual upper bound for \(W\) in terms of \(Q\):
\[
 W\leq\frac{32}{3}Q+4G
 <\left(\frac{32}{3}+\frac1{25}\right)Q
 =\frac{803}{75}Q.
\tag{10}
\]
Thus no lower bound on \(Q/W\) is being assumed silently.  The first inequality in (6), (5), (7), and (10) imply
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 L_{\mathrm{ret}}
 &>\frac{198}{100}Q-\frac1{100}Q-
    \frac1{64}\frac{803}{75}Q\\
 &=\frac{8653}{4800}Q.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
Summing (9) over the retained classes yields
\[
 |s|>\frac38\frac{8653}{4800}Q
 =\frac{8653}{12800}Q>0.
\tag{12}
\]
In particular, the retained matching is nonzero.

For this same matching, accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` defines
\[
 \rho_s:=\dim\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:s_e>0\},
\]
\[
 r_s:=\min_{a\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{F_e^\flat-a(m_e):s_e>0\},
\tag{13}
\]
and proves from the halfspace-dominated physical measure
\(
\frac49\sum_es_e\delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)}
\)
that
\[
 D\geq\frac49\,2^{n-\rho_s}\frac{|s|}{r_s+1}.
\tag{14}
\]
Here the factor \(4/9\) is exactly
\(
\gamma_2=(2/3)^2
\).

Every midpoint base \(m_e\) lies in \(\mathbb R^n\), so \(\rho_s\leq n\).  Every residual in (13) lies in \(\mathbb R^2\), and the zero affine map is admissible, so \(r_s\leq2\).  Therefore (12)-(14) give
\[
 D
 \geq\frac4{27}|s|
 >\frac4{27}\frac{8653}{12800}Q
 =\frac{8653}{86400}Q
 =\left(\frac1{10}+\frac{13}{86400}\right)Q
 >\frac Q{10}.
\tag{15}
\]
This contradicts \(D<Q/100\) in (5), proving (2).

Accepted fact `7ec63df101c30343` explicitly identifies its quantities \(W,G,Q,R,\Omega,L_\xi,s,D\), and \(\mathcal A(C)\) with the canonical quantities of `6b09aae189415c70`, without rescaling the matching \(s\).  Accepted fact `4298be06631f6503` in turn assumes that its planar triangular-homothetic body and the same \(W,G,D,Q\) lie in that exact canonical hard branch.  Hence (2) contradicts the additional hypothesis of `4298be06631f6503` before any use of its bounds on \(G/W\), \(\eta\), or \(n\).  The interval (3) is therefore a formally consistent scalar window but has no realization by an actual body satisfying the canonical hard hypotheses.  This proves the stated all-dimensional hard-branch descendant-retention conclusion.
