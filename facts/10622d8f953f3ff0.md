## Statement

In the exact fixed-cover continuation for the energy-only covariance buffer, assume the compatible path also satisfies the bounded signed radial hypotheses of the uniform marked-energy and covariance lemma. Use its $D,J,C_z(y)$, and for group $i$ write $E_i=E_{K_z}(y_z)$ and $f_i=(E_i/M)^{1/N}$ for the old common energy and profile at its contacts. Put
$$
J_i^{\rm cov}=\sum_j\rho_{ij}u_{ij}^{\mathsf T}J u_{ij}.
$$
Then $A_i(t)=1+t(D-J_i^{\rm cov})/(2M)+o(t)$, and
$$
\alpha_i(t)=t\,\dot\alpha_i+o(t),\qquad
\dot\alpha_i=\varepsilon f_i
\left[\min_{z\in J_i}\frac{C_z(y_z)}{NE_i}
+\frac{J_i^{\rm cov}}{2M}-\frac{(N+2)D}{2NM}\right].
$$
In the variant with the old norm held fixed and the unnormalized profile $E^{1/N}$, use the corresponding old cap groups (equivalently rescale the old buffer parameter by $M^{-1/N}$). For its fixed parameter $\varepsilon$ the formula becomes $\dot\alpha_i=\varepsilon E_i^{1/N}\min_{z\in J_i}C_z(y_z)/(NE_i)$.

## Proof

The uniform marked-energy and covariance lemma gives
$$
\dot A_i=\frac12\sum_j\rho_{ij}u_{ij}^{\mathsf T}\dot\Sigma u_{ij}
=\frac{D-J_i^{\rm cov}}{2M},
$$
because the old normals are $\Gamma$-unit. Its expansion for $f_t$ is uniform on compact own-interior neighborhoods of the finite old contacts, so the objective of $\alpha_i$ has a uniform expansion there. At each old contact its first-order coefficient is
$$
-\varepsilon\left[\dot A_i f_i+\frac{f_i}{N}
\left(-\frac{C_z(y_z)}{E_i}+\frac DM\right)\right].
$$
Testing every old contact gives an upper derivative bound by their smallest coefficient. For the lower bound, choose new minimizing marks. They approach the old contact set by exact continuation, and hence lie in those compact interior neighborhoods for small $t$. They also lie inside the corresponding old fibers there, so the old objective is nonnegative at each. The first-order coefficient on each neighborhood is continuous by the uniform energy lemma. Along a subsequence at a fixed base tending to an old contact this gives the lower derivative bound by the smallest old coefficient. This proves the minimum envelope without any first-order motion assumption on a minimizing mark. Substitution of $\dot A_i$ gives the formula. For the fixed-norm, unnormalized profile use $A_i(t)=1$ and apply the same argument to the uniform derivative of $E_{K_z(t)}^{1/N}$ from the energy expansion. Mark localization there follows by the same compactness and interior-test argument, with the norm fixed.
