## Statement

Let $\mu$ be the sum of $d$-dimensional volume on finitely many positive compact convex fibers, with total mass $M$. Let $\beta$ be its maximum normalized closed-halfspace depth over its mixed set. Take a group of nonvertical caps of mass $\beta M$ with margins $L_j(z,x)=v_j\cdot z+u_j\cdot x-b_j$ and positive weights $\rho_j$ such that $\sum_j\rho_j u_j=0$. At each assigned globally deepest base $p$ assume $\sum_j\rho_j L_j(p,x)\ge0$, and consider any globally deepest points there that lie in the interior of their own fibers. Every such point lies on every cap boundary of the group. Any convex combination of the points belongs to the convex hull of the mixed set, lies on those same cap boundaries, and has ambient normalized depth exactly $\beta$. Its base is the same convex combination of the assigned bases.

## Proof

If a critical cap contains one of these marks strictly, raise its offset through the mark. The mark stays in the resulting closed cap. A ball in its own fiber around the mark, together with $u_j\ne0$, gives a positive-volume strip removed on that fiber, so the cap's mass falls strictly below $\beta M$. Thus every group margin at the mark is nonpositive. Their positive weighted sum is nonnegative by the assignment, so each is zero. These affine equations persist under convex combination. Any closed ambient halfspace containing the combination contains at least one of the combined points of positive weight, so has mass at least $\beta M$. One of the group caps gives the reverse bound. No base-integrality statement is used.
