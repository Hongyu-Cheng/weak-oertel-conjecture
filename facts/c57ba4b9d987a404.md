## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and use the exact canonical hypotheses and notation of accepted facts 6b09aae189415c70 and 7ec63df101c30343. Thus, writing
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
 Z:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},\qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in Z}a_z,
\]
put
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,\qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),\qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C).
\]
Let \(Q>0\) be the half-strength canonical product-reservoir mass, let \(R\) be its realizable-submatching capacity, and put
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\]
Assume the hard inequality
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(I,O\) be the canonical within-label and labelled-output defects of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70, and assume
\[
 I\ge O.
\tag{2}
\]

Let \(\mathcal E_I\) be the actual \(I\)-bad opposite-edge family of accepted fact 7ec63df101c30343, let \(t_e\) be its actual opposite-edge triangle masses, and put
\[
 \Theta_{\xi,c}:=
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi,\ m_e=c\\e\in\mathcal E_I}}t_e,
 \qquad
 T_c:=\sum_\xi\Theta_{\xi,c},
\]
\[
 E:=\{c\in\mathbb Z^n:T_c>0\},
 \qquad
 \Theta_I:=\sum_{c\in E}T_c.
\tag{3}
\]
At every \(c\in E\), choose a retained parity label \(\xi(c)\) maximizing \(\Theta_{\xi,c}\), put
\[
 \widehat\theta_c:=\Theta_{\xi(c),c},
 \qquad q_c:=q_{\xi(c),c}^{\circ},
\]
and define
\[
 \rho_E:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E),
\qquad
 r_E:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{q_c-s(c):c\in E\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Then the following assertions hold.

1. For every rank pair \((\rho_E,r_E)\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal A(C)\ge
 \frac{11}{256}
 \min\left\{
 \frac{8\gamma_d}{2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1)},\frac{64}{67}
 \right\}Q.}
\tag{5}
\]
Consequently every configuration satisfying (1)--(4) obeys
\[
 \boxed{2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1)>12.}
\tag{6}
\]

2. If \(\rho_E=2\) and \(r_E\le3\), then the accepted rank-two target-slice bound gives the stronger estimate
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge\frac{88}{33075}(1+8\gamma_d)Q
 >\frac{5896}{562275}Q
 >\frac Q{100},}
\tag{7}
\]
contradicting (1). Hence every hard \(I\)-dominant configuration of target rank two has
\[
 \boxed{r_E\ge4.}
\tag{8}
\]
The integer \(r_E=4\) is the first rank-two value not excluded by (5) and (7).

3. The following additional conclusion is conditional. Assume, in addition to (1)--(4), the strict no-rank-drop hypothesis
\[
 W_L:=\sum_{z\in Z\cap L}a_z
 <2^{\dim L-n}W
\tag{NR}
\]
for every proper primitive affine lattice subspace
\(L=b+V\subsetneq\mathbb R^n\), where \(b\in\mathbb Z^n\) and
\(V\cap\mathbb Z^n\) is saturated of rank \(\dim V\). If \(r_E=0\), then
\[
 \boxed{G\ge2^{2-\rho_E}\Theta_I.}
\tag{9}
\]
In particular, the conditional pair \((\rho_E,r_E)=(4,0)\) satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge\frac{88}{8499}(1+2\gamma_d)Q
 >\frac{2596}{144483}Q
 >\frac Q{100},}
\tag{10}
\]
and is impossible. Thus, under (NR), target rank four also forces
\(r_E\ge1\). No conclusion excluding \((\rho_E,r_E)=(4,0)\) is asserted without (NR).

Together with the positive-mass-outside-every-line conclusion of accepted fact 07889c9b4686d8b4, these estimates leave the first arbitrary-branch rank possibilities
\[
 (\rho_E,r_E)=(2,4),\qquad(3,1),\qquad(4,0),
\]
while (NR) removes the third of these. No all-rank closure is asserted.

## Proof

Under (1), the hard alternative in accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives
\[
 \max\{I,O\}>\frac Q4.
\]
Equation (2) therefore gives \(I>Q/4\), so the actual \(I\)-triangle conclusions of accepted fact 7ec63df101c30343 apply. In particular, its exceptional edge family \(\mathcal B\) satisfies
\[
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal B}s_e\le G,
 \qquad
 t_e\ge\frac18s_e\quad(e\notin\mathcal B).
\]
Since \(I\) is exactly the \(s\)-mass of \(\mathcal E_I\), equation (3) gives
\[
 \Theta_I
 =\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_I}t_e
 \ge\frac18\left(I-\sum_{e\in\mathcal B}s_e\right)
 \ge\frac18(I-G).
\tag{11}
\]

The exact defect inequality in accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 is
\[
 I+O\ge
 \frac{11}{16}Q-\frac{67}{4}R-\frac{51}{128}G.
\tag{12}
\]
Because \(I\ge O\), equations (11)--(12) imply
\[
 I\ge\frac{11}{32}Q-\frac{67}{8}R-\frac{51}{256}G
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta_I+\frac{67}{64}R+\frac{307}{2048}G
 \ge\frac{11}{256}Q.}
\tag{13}
\]
Here \(307/2048=51/2048+1/8\), so all constants retain the non-strict directions in the accepted inequalities.

Apply the heaviest-label selector theorem e2263a787ace07a4 to the complete positive support \(E\). It gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge\frac{4\gamma_d}{2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1)}\Theta_I,
 \qquad
 D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
\tag{14}
\]
The terms \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) are distinct nonnegative summands of \(\mathcal A(C)\). Therefore, with
\[
 k:=\frac{8\gamma_d}{2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1)},
 \qquad B:=\frac{67}{64},
 \qquad C_0:=\frac{307}{2048},
 \qquad A_0:=\frac{11}{256}Q,
\tag{15}
\]
one has
\[
 \mathcal A(C)\ge R+G+k\Theta_I.
\tag{16}
\]
Put
\[
 m:=\min\left\{k,\frac{64}{67}\right\}.
\]
Then \(m\le k\), \(mB\le1\), and \(mC_0<1\). Nonnegativity and (13) give
\[
 R+G+k\Theta_I
 \ge m(\Theta_I+BR+C_0G)
 \ge mA_0,
\]
which proves (5).

We record once the rational lower bound on \(\gamma_d\) used below. The binomial theorem gives
\[
 \left(1+\frac1d\right)^d
 \le\sum_{j=0}^d\frac1{j!}<e.
\]
Moreover,
\[
 e
 <\sum_{j=0}^5\frac1{j!}
 +\frac1{720}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac1{7^j}
 =\frac{11743}{4320}<\frac{68}{25}.
\tag{17}
\]
The first strict inequality follows because, after \(1/6!\), every successive factorial ratio is at least \(7\); the last comparison is
\(11743\cdot25=293575<293760=4320\cdot68\). Thus
\[
 \boxed{\gamma_d>\frac{25}{68}.}
\tag{18}
\]

If \(2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1)\le12\), then (18) gives
\[
 k>\frac{8(25/68)}{12}=\frac{25}{102}.
\]
Also \(64/67>25/102\), so (5) yields
\[
 \mathcal A(C)>
 \frac{11}{256}\frac{25}{102}Q
 =\frac{275}{26112}Q
 >\frac Q{100},
\]
where \(27500-26112=1388>0\). This contradicts (1) and proves (6), including its strict endpoint.

We next sharpen the target-plane case. Suppose \(\rho_E=2\). Since \(E\subseteq Z\cap\operatorname{aff}(E)\) spans its target plane, the occupied-fiber rank of that plane is exactly two. Accepted fact 07889c9b4686d8b4 therefore gives
\[
 \Theta_I\le16G.
\tag{19}
\]
If also \(r_E\le3\), equation (14) and the two distinct summands give
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge R+G+\frac{\gamma_d}{2}\Theta_I.
\tag{20}
\]
Indeed, the coefficient supplied by (14) is
\(8\gamma_d/[4(r_E+1)]\ge\gamma_d/2\).

Define
\[
 \lambda:=\frac{1+8\gamma_d}{16+C_0},
 \qquad
 \mu:=\lambda-\frac{\gamma_d}{2}
 =\frac{1-(\gamma_d/2)C_0}{16+C_0}.
\tag{21}
\]
Since \(0<\gamma_d<1\) and \(0<C_0<1\), one has \(\mu>0\). Also
\[
 \lambda<\frac9{16},
 \qquad
 \lambda B<\frac9{16}\frac{67}{64}
 =\frac{603}{1024}<1.
\tag{22}
\]
Direct expansion gives the exact dual certificate
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R+G+\frac{\gamma_d}{2}\Theta_I
 &={}
 \lambda(\Theta_I+BR+C_0G)
 +\mu(16G-\Theta_I)
 +(1-\lambda B)R.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{23}
\]
Equations (13), (19), and (21)--(23) imply
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge\lambda A_0
 =\frac{88}{33075}(1+8\gamma_d)Q,
\tag{24}
\]
because \(16+C_0=33075/2048\). By (18),
\[
 \frac{88}{33075}(1+8\gamma_d)
 >\frac{88}{33075}\frac{67}{17}
 =\frac{5896}{562275}
 >\frac1{100},
\]
where \(589600-562275=27325>0\). This proves (7)--(8). Since \(r_E\) is a nonnegative integer, \(4\) is the first rank-two value left after this exclusion.

It remains to prove the conditional assertion. Assume (NR) and \(r_E=0\), and put
\[
 A:=\operatorname{aff}(E),\qquad \rho:=\dim A=\rho_E.
\]
The equality \(r_E=0\) supplies an affine map
\[
 s:A\to\mathbb R^d,
 \qquad q_c=s(c)\quad(c\in E).
\tag{25}
\]
For a retained label, use the canonical notation
\[
 S_{\xi,c}:=\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_e.
\]
Accepted fact 7ec63df101c30343 gives \(t_e\le s_e/2\), while accepted fact 07889c9b4686d8b4 gives the collision-safe single-label estimate \(\widehat\theta_c\le a_c/16\). Consequently
\[
 0<\widehat\theta_c\le\frac12S_{\xi(c),c},
 \qquad
 m_c:=4\widehat\theta_c\le
 \min\{2S_{\xi(c),c},a_c/4\}.
\tag{26}
\]
Accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives
\[
 q_c+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{2S_{\xi(c),c}}(C_c).
\]
Scaling a representing density by
\(m_c/[2S_{\xi(c),c}]\le1\) preserves its normalized barycenter and proves
\[
 q_c\in\mathsf M_{m_c}(C_c)
 \qquad(c\in E).
\tag{27}
\]

The affine space \(A\) is spanned by integer points. Choosing \(c_0\in E\), the lattice
\[
 \Gamma:=(A-c_0)\cap\mathbb Z^n
\]
has rank \(\rho\) and is saturated: if \(ku\in\Gamma\) for
\(u\in\mathbb Z^n\) and nonzero \(k\in\mathbb Z\), then
\(u\in A-c_0\), hence \(u\in\Gamma\). A lattice basis therefore identifies
\(A\cap\mathbb Z^n\) affinely with \(\mathbb Z^\rho\).

Apply the \(d=0\) weighted lattice centerpoint conclusion of accepted fact 6d6fcf377e68c79c to the atomic weights \(m_c\) on \(E\). It supplies
\[
 b\in A\cap\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E
\tag{28}
\]
such that every proper relative closed halfspace of \(A\) containing \(b\) carries at least
\[
 2^{-\rho}\tau,
 \qquad
 \tau:=\sum_{c\in E}m_c.
\tag{29}
\]
The convex-hull inclusion in (28) follows because otherwise strict separation in \(A\) would give a containing relative halfspace with zero selected mass.

For every barycentric representation
\(\eta\in\Lambda_b(E)\), equations (25) and (27) give
\[
 s(b)=\sum_{c\in E}\eta_cs(c)
 =\sum_{c\in E}\eta_cq_c
 \in\sum_{c\in E}\eta_c\mathsf M_{m_c}(C_c).
\tag{30}
\]
The left side is independent of \(\eta\), so the complete metronoid core is nonempty. Thus (26), (29), and (30) form an admissible relative complete-core tuple on \(A\), of mass \(\tau\) and relative balance \(2^{-\rho}\).

If \(A=\mathbb R^n\), then \(\rho=n\), this tuple is directly admissible for \(G\), and \(G\ge\tau\). Suppose \(A\subsetneq\mathbb R^n\). The space \(A\) is a proper primitive affine lattice subspace by the saturation check above. Also (1) and the accepted inequality \(Q\le W/8\) give
\[
 G<\frac Q{100}\le\frac W{800}<\frac W4.
\tag{31}
\]
Therefore the strict no-rank-drop domination in accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb, applied to the relative tuple (29)--(30), gives
\[
 G\ge2^{n-\rho}\tau.
\tag{32}
\]
Equation (32) also holds in the full-rank case because then its factor is one. Finally, heaviest-label selection at each physical target gives
\[
 \tau=4\sum_{c\in E}\widehat\theta_c
 \ge4\,2^{-n}\Theta_I.
\tag{33}
\]
Combining (32)--(33) proves (9).

Assume now \(\rho_E=4\) and \(r_E=0\). Equations (9) and (14) give
\[
 G\ge\frac14\Theta_I,
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\ge\frac{\gamma_d}{4}\Theta_I,
 \qquad
 D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C),
\]
and hence
\[
 \mathcal A(C)\ge R+G+\frac{\gamma_d}{2}\Theta_I.
\tag{34}
\]
Put
\[
 s_0:=\frac14,
 \qquad
 k_0:=\frac{\gamma_d}{2},
 \qquad
 \lambda_0:=\frac{s_0+k_0}{1+C_0s_0},
 \qquad
 \mu_0:=1-\lambda_0C_0.
\tag{35}
\]
Since \(\lambda_0<3/4\), one has
\(\mu_0>0\) and
\(1-\lambda_0B>0\). Direct expansion yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R+G+k_0\Theta_I
 &={}
 \lambda_0(\Theta_I+BR+C_0G)
 +\mu_0(G-s_0\Theta_I)
 +(1-\lambda_0B)R.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{36}
\]
Equations (13), (34)--(36) prove
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge\lambda_0A_0
 =\frac{88}{8499}(1+2\gamma_d)Q,
\tag{37}
\]
because \(1+C_0/4=8499/8192\). Using (18),
\[
 \frac{88}{8499}(1+2\gamma_d)
 >\frac{88}{8499}\frac{59}{34}
 =\frac{2596}{144483}
 >\frac1{100},
\]
where \(259600-144483=115117>0\). This proves (10) and the conditional exclusion. The proof of (9) used (NR) only through accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb; none of (5)--(8) depends on (NR).

Finally, accepted fact 07889c9b4686d8b4 shows that positive \(I\)-triangle mass lies outside every affine line, so \(\rho_E\ge2\). Equations (6) and (8) then give the stated first remaining arbitrary-branch pairs, and (10) removes only the conditional pair \((4,0)\). This completes all asserted rank screens without pooling labels in one physical target fiber or aggregating incompatible star packets.
