## Statement

Retain the complete setting, notation, and physical ledgers of accepted fact 09fe6e21ebd31ee6. In particular, fix a nonempty selected label family \(\Lambda\), a one-tag selector \(o\), and write
\[
 c_\sigma^o:=c_{y_{\sigma,o(\sigma)}},
 \qquad
 r_\sigma:=r_\sigma^c,
 \qquad
 \Lambda_t:=\{\sigma\in\Lambda:t(\sigma)=t\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus
\[
 M_\Lambda=\frac14\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda}\theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \mathsf S_{\Lambda,o}
 =\sup_{\substack{L:\ M_L^{\Lambda,o}>0\\q_L\leq2}}
 2^{n-q_L}M_L^{\Lambda,o},
 \qquad
 G=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C).
\tag{2}
\]

For every physical target \(t\) with \(\Lambda_t\ne\varnothing\), define the discounted selected-ray packing capacity
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathsf R_t:=\max\quad&
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}
 \left(q_\sigma-\frac{b_\sigma}{2}\right)\\
 \text{subject to}\quad&
 h_\sigma\in L^\infty(C_t),\qquad h_\sigma\geq0,\\
 &\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}h_\sigma\leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_t,\\
 &q_\sigma=\int_{C_t}h_\sigma(x)\,dx,\\
 &0\leq b_\sigma\leq2q_\sigma,\\
 &\int_{C_t}x h_\sigma(x)\,dx
 =q_\sigma r_\sigma
 +b_\sigma(c_\sigma^o-r_\sigma)
 \qquad(\sigma\in\Lambda_t).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
Put \(\mathsf R_t:=0\) when \(\Lambda_t=\varnothing\), and
\[
 \mathsf R_{\Lambda,o}:=\sum_t\mathsf R_t.
\tag{4}
\]
The maximum in (3) is attained. The exact primal identity is
\[
 \boxed{\mathsf J_{\Lambda,o}=\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o}.}
\tag{5}
\]
All labels at one physical target share the single pointwise capacity in (3). If \(q_\sigma>0\) and
\(\beta_\sigma:=b_\sigma/q_\sigma\), then
\[
 0\leq\beta_\sigma\leq2,
 \qquad
 \frac1{q_\sigma}\int_{C_t}x h_\sigma(x)\,dx
 =(1-\beta_\sigma)r_\sigma+\beta_\sigma c_\sigma^o,
\tag{6}
\]
and the contribution to the objective is
\[
 q_\sigma\left(1-\frac{\beta_\sigma}{2}\right).
\tag{7}
\]
Thus (3) packs barycenters on the closed ray segment from \(r_\sigma\) to its reflection \(2c_\sigma^o-r_\sigma\), with linearly vanishing credit at the reflected endpoint. Zero-mass packets are omitted and have \(b_\sigma=0\).

These rays use literal first moments in the accepted no-reuse ledger. For each label set
\[
 y_\sigma:=y_{\sigma,o(\sigma)},
 \qquad
 g_\sigma^{\mathrm{sel}}(x)
 :=\frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_{y_\sigma}}
 \mathbf1_{C_{y_\sigma}}(x).
\tag{8}
\]
The selected packet in (8) has mass \(\theta_\sigma/4\) and barycenter \(c_\sigma^o\). Together with the labelwise blocker-to-parent replacement densities \(F_{\sigma,z}\) of accepted fact 09fe6e21ebd31ee6, it satisfies the one physical source ledger
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Lambda\\y_\sigma=z}}
 g_\sigma^{\mathrm{sel}}(x)
 +\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda}F_{\sigma,z}(x)
 \leq\frac{17}{32}
 \quad\text{for every }z\text{ and a.e. }x\in C_z.
\tag{9}
\]
For every retained label, the replacement packet has mass and lifted first moment
\[
 \sum_z\int_{C_z}F_{\sigma,z}=\theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \sum_z\int_{C_z}(z,x)F_{\sigma,z}(x)\,dx
 =\theta_\sigma(t(\sigma),r_\sigma).
\tag{10}
\]

The selected source endpoint in (8), the replacement first moment in (10), and the accepted individual target realization of \(r_\sigma\) identify the same two barycentric endpoints. They generate an actual one-body positive-root segment. For \(0\leq\tau\leq1\), put
\[
 z_{\sigma,\tau}:=(1-\tau)t(\sigma)+\tau y_\sigma,
 \qquad
 p_{\sigma,\tau}:=(1-\tau)r_\sigma+\tau c_\sigma^o,
\tag{11}
\]
and
\[
 \mu_{\sigma,\tau}:=
 \left((1-\tau)\theta_\sigma^{1/d}
 +\tau(\theta_\sigma/4)^{1/d}\right)^d.
\tag{12}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 p_{\sigma,\tau}
 \in\mathsf M_{\mu_{\sigma,\tau}}
 (C_{z_{\sigma,\tau}}),
 \qquad
 \delta_C(z_{\sigma,\tau},p_{\sigma,\tau})
 \geq\gamma_d\mu_{\sigma,\tau},
 }
\tag{13}
\]
where \(\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d\). This is an individual segment statement; the interpolated packets belonging to different labels are not added.

There is an explicit complete-core charge for each individual lattice segment. If \(y_\sigma\ne t(\sigma)\), let
\[
 g_\sigma:=\gcd\{|(y_\sigma-t(\sigma))_i|:
 (y_\sigma-t(\sigma))_i\ne0\},
 \qquad
 u_\sigma:=\frac{y_\sigma-t(\sigma)}{g_\sigma}.
\tag{14}
\]
Then the one-factor lattice packet on
\(t(\sigma)+\{0,1,\ldots,g_\sigma\}u_\sigma\) has energy
\[
 \mathsf E_\sigma
 :=\gamma_d\theta_\sigma
 \sum_{k=0}^{g_\sigma}
 \left(1-\frac{k}{g_\sigma}
 +4^{-1/d}\frac{k}{g_\sigma}\right)^d,
\tag{15}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 G\geq\mathsf E_\sigma
 \geq\frac{\gamma_d}{4}(g_\sigma+1)\theta_\sigma.}
\tag{16}
\]
If \(y_\sigma=t(\sigma)\), then instead
\[
 \boxed{G\geq\theta_\sigma.}
\tag{17}
\]
Equations (16)-(17) are not summed over labels.

Finally, the existence of a finite absolute constant in (U) is equivalent to the following strictly target-poor geometric assertion. There are absolute constants \(\varepsilon,c>0\) such that every retained instance, selected family, and selector satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf R_{\Lambda,o}\leq\varepsilon M_\Lambda
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 G+\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o}\geq cM_\Lambda.}
\tag{TP}
\]
More precisely, (U) with constant \(C_{\mathrm U}\) implies (TP) with
\[
 \varepsilon=c=\frac1{2C_{\mathrm U}},
\tag{18}
\]
while (TP) with \((\varepsilon,c)\) implies (U) with
\[
 C_{\mathrm U}=\max\{\varepsilon^{-1},c^{-1}\}.
\tag{19}
\]
Thus the unresolved part of (U) has no affine minimization left: it is exactly the aggregation of the source-owned one-body segments (11)-(16), in the target-poor branch of the concrete collision-safe packing problem (3), into one complete-core witness or the already defined low-rank slice score. No such aggregation is asserted here.

## Proof

We first prove the exact target-local primal identity. Fix a physical target \(t\) and abbreviate \(K:=C_t\). Accepted fact 2c093df4eb8158a9 proves that
\[
 \mathcal F_t:=
 \left\{(h_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}:
 h_\sigma\geq0,
 \ \sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}h_\sigma\leq1
 \text{ a.e. on }K\right\}
\tag{20}
\]
is weak-star compact and convex, and that its finite-dimensional mass-and-moment image is compact and convex. It also proves the pointwise support identity
\[
 \int_K\max\left\{0,
 \max_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}\lambda_\sigma(x)\right\}dx
 =\max_{h\in\mathcal F_t}
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}
 \int_K\lambda_\sigma(x)h_\sigma(x)\,dx.
\tag{21}
\]

Every affine function satisfying \(\lambda_\sigma(r_\sigma)=1\) is uniquely of the form
\[
 \lambda_\sigma(x)
 =1+\langle v_\sigma,x-r_\sigma\rangle,
 \qquad v_\sigma\in\mathbb R^d.
\tag{22}
\]
Its selected-source constraint is
\[
 \langle v_\sigma,c_\sigma^o-r_\sigma\rangle
 \geq-\frac12.
\tag{23}
\]
Apply the finite-dimensional compact-convex bilinear minimax identity to the compact moment image of (20) and the product of the closed halfspaces (23). Equations (21)-(23) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathsf J_t
 &=\max_{h\in\mathcal F_t}
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}
 \inf_{v_\sigma:\,(23)}
 \left[q_\sigma+
 \left\langle v_\sigma,
 p_\sigma-q_\sigma r_\sigma\right\rangle\right],
 \end{aligned}
\tag{24}
\]
where
\[
 q_\sigma:=\int_Kh_\sigma,
 \qquad
 p_\sigma:=\int_Kx h_\sigma(x)\,dx.
\tag{25}
\]
This is the same compact moment-image separation used in accepted fact 2c093df4eb8158a9; in particular, no interiority of a prescribed barycenter is required.

We compute the inner infimum exactly. Put
\[
 d_\sigma^o:=c_\sigma^o-r_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \zeta_\sigma:=p_\sigma-q_\sigma r_\sigma.
\tag{26}
\]
If \(d_\sigma^o\ne0\), the linear functional in (24) is bounded below on the halfspace
\(\{v:\langle v,d_\sigma^o\rangle\geq-1/2\}\) if and only if
\[
 \zeta_\sigma=b_\sigma d_\sigma^o
 \quad\text{for some }b_\sigma\geq0.
\tag{27}
\]
Indeed, every direction orthogonal to \(d_\sigma^o\) is a two-sided recession direction, forcing \(\zeta_\sigma\) to be parallel to \(d_\sigma^o\), and the one-sided recession direction forces the coefficient to be nonnegative. Under (27), the minimum is attained on the boundary of (23) and equals
\[
 q_\sigma-\frac{b_\sigma}{2}.
\tag{28}
\]
If \(d_\sigma^o=0\), the feasible set is all of \(\mathbb R^d\); the infimum is finite exactly when \(\zeta_\sigma=0\), and its value is \(q_\sigma\). This is (27)-(28) with the maximizing choice \(b_\sigma=0\).

Substitution of (25)-(28) into (24) gives the program (3), initially with only \(b_\sigma\geq0\). A coordinate with \(b_\sigma>2q_\sigma\) has negative objective contribution. Replacing that one density by zero and setting \(q_\sigma=b_\sigma=0\) preserves every other density and the common capacity, while strictly increasing the objective. Hence a maximizer may be restricted to
\(0\leq b_\sigma\leq2q_\sigma\). Since
\(0\leq q_\sigma\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(K)\), these scalar variables lie in a compact box. Weak-star compactness of (20) and closedness of the moment equations now prove attainment in (3). This proves
\[
 \mathsf J_t=\mathsf R_t.
\tag{29}
\]
The affine variables and objectives for distinct physical targets are independent and the target family is finite, so summing (29) proves (5). Equations (6)-(7) are just the moment equation and objective of (3) divided by \(q_\sigma>0\).

We next verify that the ray endpoints are owned by the same accepted physical ledger. Equation (8) is exactly the label component of the selected occurrence density in accepted fact 09fe6e21ebd31ee6. Summing it over labels selected at one physical source gives that fact's density \(f_z^{\Lambda,o}\). Its equations (14)-(15), with the same blocker complements and every blocker-to-parent ancestry incidence retained, are exactly (9)-(10). Thus no endpoint packet in (8) or (10) is reconstructed on a new density budget.

The density in (8) directly gives the first membership below, while accepted fact 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 gives the second:
\[
 c_\sigma^o\in
 \mathsf M_{\theta_\sigma/4}(C_{y_\sigma}),
 \qquad
 r_\sigma\in
 \mathsf M_{\theta_\sigma}(C_{t(\sigma)}).
\tag{30}
\]
Apply the positive-root convexity of accepted fact dfd25e72c1f45a4e to the two positive triples in (30). Their convex combination with coefficient \(\tau\) is precisely the triple in (11)-(12), which proves the metronoid membership in (13). Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d then turns that membership into the depth bound in (13), including halfspaces whose boundary contains the barycenter.

Suppose \(y_\sigma\ne t(\sigma)\), and use the primitive parametrization in (14). The interval \([0,g_\sigma]\subseteq\mathbb R\) is a full-dimensional lattice simplex of rank one, its lattice points are \(0,1,\ldots,g_\sigma\), and
\[
 k\longmapsto t(\sigma)+ku_\sigma
\tag{31}
\]
is an integral affine injection. At its two vertices choose the points
\(r_\sigma\) and \(c_\sigma^o\), with factor values
\[
 \gamma_d^{1/d}\theta_\sigma^{1/d},
 \qquad
 \gamma_d^{1/d}(\theta_\sigma/4)^{1/d}.
\tag{32}
\]
Equation (13) verifies the corresponding interpolated depth inequalities at every lattice point. Hence this is an admissible one-factor lattice-polysimplex packet in the exact sense used by accepted fact fbeb0a501b268d12. Its one-factor energy is (15), and that accepted fact gives
\[
 \mathsf E_\sigma\leq G.
\tag{33}
\]
Every summand in (15) is at least \(1/4\), proving the second inequality in (16).

If \(y_\sigma=t(\sigma)\), equation (30) gives a nonempty metronoid
\(\mathsf M_{\theta_\sigma}(C_{t(\sigma)})\). In the definition of the complete-core capacity from accepted fact fbeb0a501b268d12, take the one-point base set \(E=\{t(\sigma)\}\), selected mass \(\theta_\sigma\), and center \(m=t(\sigma)\). The complete core is that nonempty metronoid, and every proper base halfspace containing \(m\) contains the only selected base. This admissible tuple has mass \(\theta_\sigma\), proving (17).

It remains to prove the exact target-poor reduction. Assume (U) with constant \(C_{\mathrm U}\), and use (5). If
\[
 \mathsf R_{\Lambda,o}
 \leq\frac{M_\Lambda}{2C_{\mathrm U}},
\tag{34}
\]
then
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 M_\Lambda
 &\leq C_{\mathrm U}
 \left(G+\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o}
 +\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o}\right)\\
 &\leq C_{\mathrm U}
 \left(G+\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o}\right)
 +\frac{M_\Lambda}{2}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{35}
\]
Rearrangement gives (TP) with the constants in (18).

Conversely, assume (TP). If
\(\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o}>\varepsilon M_\Lambda\), then
\[
 M_\Lambda<\varepsilon^{-1}\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o}.
\tag{36}
\]
If not, (TP) gives
\[
 M_\Lambda\leq c^{-1}
 \left(G+\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o}\right).
\tag{37}
\]
Equations (5), (36), and (37) prove (U) with (19). This establishes the claimed equivalence and identifies the sole remaining aggregation theorem without adding any of the labelwise core charges in (16)-(17).
