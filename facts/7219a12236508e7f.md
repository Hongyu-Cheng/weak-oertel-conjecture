## Statement

Let $P=\operatorname{conv}\{(2,-3),(4,-2),(3,3)\}$ and $C=P\times[-1/2,1/2]$. Normalize the mixed length of $C\cap(\mathbb Z^2\times\mathbb R)$ by its total mass. Its eight unit-length integer fibers each have mass $1<8/4$, and its maximum normalized closed-halfspace depth is at most $7/16$. For every $r\in\{0,1\}^2$, the body $C_r=\{((z-r)/2,t):(z,t)\in C\}$ has maximum normalized mixed depth exactly $1/2$. Thus every parity decimation strictly increases the maximum.

## Proof

The three edge inequalities of $P$ are $-x+2y\ge-8$, $-5x-y\ge-18$ and $6x-y\ge15$. The integer first coordinates lie in $\{2,3,4\}$. At $x=2,3,4$ the integer second coordinates are, respectively, $\{-3\}$, $\{-2,-1,0,1,2,3\}$ and $\{-2\}$. This follows by substituting each first coordinate into the three inequalities. The fibers have unit length, so total mixed length is eight.

For a candidate $(p,t_0)$, choose $e\in\{-1,1\}$ with $et_0\ge0$ and take the closed halfspace $et+2s(z_2-p_2)\ge0$, with $s=-1$ for $p_2\le-1$ and $s=1$ for $p_2\ge0$. It contains the candidate. It takes half the length from each fiber with $z_2=p_2$, all the length when $s(z_2-p_2)\ge1$, and zero when $s(z_2-p_2)\le-1$. The level counts at $-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3$ are $(1,2,1,1,1,1,1)$, giving cut masses $(1/2,2,7/2,7/2,5/2,3/2,1/2)$ with the specified signs. Each original candidate therefore has normalized depth at most $7/16$.

The even-even and even-odd classes are the singletons $(4,-2)$ and $(2,-3)$. The odd-even class is $\{(3,-2),(3,0),(3,2)\}$ and the odd-odd class is $\{(3,-1),(3,1),(3,3)\}$. The mixed length on each class times $[-1/2,1/2]$ is centrally symmetric about an actual point of the class, using base centers $(3,0)$ and $(3,1)$ for the latter two. At such a center a containing closed halfspace and its reflection cover the ambient space and have equal mass, so the normalized depth is at least $1/2$. At any point one of $t\ge t_0$ and $t\le t_0$ has normalized mass at most $1/2$ because the continuous marginal is nonatomic. The class maximum is thus exactly $1/2$. The affine map $(z,t)\mapsto((z-r)/2,t)$ sends precisely the points in the chosen parity class to the integer fibers of $C_r$. It preserves lengths and ambient halfspace incidence.
