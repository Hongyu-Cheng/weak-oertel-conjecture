## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z).
\tag{1}
\]
For every positive-volume fiber, write
\[
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,
 \qquad
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
 \qquad
 D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z.
\tag{2}
\]
For an off-diagonal same-parity edge \(g=\{u_g,v_g\}\), put
\[
 m_g:=\frac{u_g+v_g}{2},
 \qquad
 \bar c_g:=\frac{c_{u_g}+c_{v_g}}2,
\tag{3}
\]
\[
 D_g:=\frac12(D_{u_g}+D_{v_g}),
 \qquad
 L_g:=\bar c_g+D_g
 =\frac12(\mathcal K_{u_g}+\mathcal K_{v_g}),
\tag{4}
\]
\[
 \vartheta_g:=2^{-(d+1)}
 \left(a_{u_g}^{1/d}+a_{v_g}^{1/d}\right)^d.
\tag{5}
\]

Retain one common canonical zero-fresh hard instance of accepted facts 02a75ec03a8733a8, 320dcb7d85f22889, 6bfe50fed9a5540b, and ed01f226ec4aa450.  Thus \(r=(r_g)\) is the half-strength complete-product reservoir,
\[
 Q:=\sum_gr_g>0,
 \qquad
 R:=R_C(r)
 =\max_{s\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \sum_{g:s(m_g)\in L_g}r_g.
\]
Let \(s_0\) be its paired weighted-distance primal optimizer, retain its canonical target multipliers \(0<\chi_m\leq1\), and put
\[
 \mathscr M_*:=\{g:s_0(m_g)\notin L_g\},
 \qquad
 \alpha_g:=\chi_{m_g}r_g\mathbf1_{\mathscr M_*}(g),
\]
\[
 t_m:=\sum_{g:m_g=m}\alpha_g.
\]
Retain the canonical target loads \(0\leq\lambda_m\leq t_m\), and define
\[
 b_g:=
 \begin{cases}
 \dfrac{\lambda_{m_g}}{t_{m_g}}\alpha_g,&t_{m_g}>0,\\[2mm]
 0,&t_{m_g}=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Let \(s_b\) be the paired primal optimizer for \(b\), and let \(q=(q_g)\) be the self-saturated load of its gauge-load-minimal dual optimizer.  Then
\[
 0\leq q_g\leq b_g\leq r_g.
\tag{6}
\]
For a good parity class \(\xi\), with
\[
 A_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}a_z,
\tag{7}
\]
the positive reservoir edges in that class have
\[
 r_{\{u,v\}}=\frac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi}.
\tag{8}
\]
The self-saturated load obeys the global physical budgets
\[
 \sum_{g\ni z}q_g\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 \sum_{g:m_g=m}\frac{q_g}{\vartheta_g}\leq1.
\tag{9}
\]
Put
\[
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
\tag{10}
\]
and retain the strict hard ledger
\[
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{11}
\]

Let \(E\) be a family of positive-\(q\) parent edges \(p=\{u_p,v_p\}\) satisfying \(v_p-u_p\in(8\mathbb Z)^n\).  Define
\[
 x_p:=\frac{3u_p+v_p}{4},
 \qquad
 y_p:=\frac{u_p+3v_p}{4},
 \qquad
 T(p):=\{x_p,y_p\}.
\tag{12}
\]
Let \(F\) be a nonempty subfamily of the positive-\(q\) children \(T(E)\).  The map \(T\) is injective, so every \(f\in F\) has a unique parent
\[
 p(f):=T^{-1}(f)\in E.
\tag{13}
\]
Retain an eligible actual child stress \(w=(w_f)_{f\in F}\), in the normalization of accepted facts 02a75ec03a8733a8 and ed01f226ec4aa450, meaning
\[
 \sum_{f\in F}w_f=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{f\in F}w_fm_f^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 -\sum_{f\in F}h_{L_f}(w_f)>0,
\]
and
\[
 \omega_f:=h_{D_f}(w_f)>0,
 \qquad
 \omega_f\leq q_f,
 \qquad
 \Omega:=\sum_{f\in F}\omega_f\geq\frac Q{100}.
\tag{14}
\]
Define
\[
 j_f:=\min\{\omega_f,q_{p(f)}\},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak J:=\sum_{f\in F}j_f,
\tag{15}
\]
\[
 A_f:=(q_f-b_{p(f)})_+,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak A:=\sum_{f\in F}A_f,
\tag{16}
\]
\[
 B_f:=b_{p(f)}-q_{p(f)},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak B:=\sum_{f\in F}B_f.
\tag{17}
\]

Then the weights \(j_f\), placed on their parent edges \(p(f)\), form one subcapacity of \(q\), have total mass \(\mathfrak J\), and satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{p(f)\ni z}j_f\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 \sum_{m_{p(f)}=m}\frac{j_f}{\vartheta_{p(f)}}\leq1.}
\tag{18}
\]
If all parents have one common minimum nonzero coordinatewise \(2\)-adic valuation \(s\geq3\), then the parent support in (18) is disjoint from the child support \(F\).

For \(f=T(p)\), write \(m:=m_f=m_p\).  Both \(f\) and \(p\) are missed by \(s_0\), and
\[
 \boxed{
 b_f=\frac{\lambda_m\chi_m}{t_m}r_f,
 \qquad
 b_p=\frac{\lambda_m\chi_m}{t_m}r_p,
 \qquad
 b_f\geq b_p.}
\tag{19}
\]
If \(p=\{u,v\}\) lies in the good parity class \(\xi\) and \(f=\{x,y\}=T(p)\), then
\[
 \boxed{
 0\leq A_f
 \leq b_f-b_p
 =\frac{\lambda_m\chi_m}{t_m}(r_f-r_p)
 =\frac{\lambda_m\chi_m}{4A_\xi t_m}
 \bigl(a_xa_y-a_ua_v\bigr).}
\tag{20}
\]

The parent KKT slack has the two independent hard-capacity bounds
\[
 \boxed{\mathfrak B\leq R,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\gamma_d\mathfrak B\leq D.}
\tag{21}
\]
Consequently the flux satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak J\geq\Omega-\mathfrak A-\mathfrak B
 >\frac{\gamma_d}{1+\gamma_d}\Omega-\mathfrak A
 >\frac1{e+1}\Omega-\mathfrak A.}
\tag{22}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak J>\frac{\Omega}{2(e+1)}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \mathfrak A>\frac{\Omega}{2(e+1)}.}
\tag{23}
\]

If \(\mathfrak A>0\), define the amplified centroid submeasure
\[
 \sigma_{\mathrm{amp}}
 :=\sum_{f\in F}A_f\delta_{(m_f,\bar c_f)}.
\tag{24}
\]
Let \(\rho_{\mathrm{amp}}\) be the affine dimension of its aggregated base support, and let \(r_{\mathrm{amp}}\) be its intrinsic continuous residual rank, in the exact normalization of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70.  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\geq
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{amp}}}
 \frac{\mathfrak A}{r_{\mathrm{amp}}+1},
 \qquad
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{amp}}}
 \frac{\mathfrak A}{r_{\mathrm{amp}}+1}
 <\frac Q{200}.}
\tag{25}
\]
If the amplified alternative in (23) holds, then
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak A>\frac{Q}{200(e+1)},
 \qquad
 r_{\mathrm{amp}}+1>
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{amp}}}}{e+1}.}
\tag{26}
\]
Equations (20), (25), and (26) are only a product-increment upper bound and a residual-rank screen.  They do not assert a rank-free lower bound for any terminal capacity, product-curvature absorption, a \(Q/100\) terminal in the amplified branch, or a completed parity descent.

## Proof

Because \(T\) is injective, the parent edges \(p(f)\), \(f\in F\), are distinct.  Put \(c_{p(f)}:=j_f\) and set \(c_g:=0\) on every other edge.  By (15),
\[
 0\leq c_{p(f)}=j_f\leq q_{p(f)}.
\tag{27}
\]
Thus \(c\leq q\), and restricting the two global inequalities in (9) proves (18).  If all parent edges have valuation \(s\geq3\), accepted fact 320dcb7d85f22889 gives valuation \(s-1\) for every child edge, so the two supports are disjoint.

Fix \(f\in F\) and put \(p=p(f)\).  Positivity of \(q_f\) and \(q_p\), together with (6), implies \(b_f,b_p>0\).  In the canonical construction of accepted fact 320dcb7d85f22889, positivity of \(b_g\) implies that \(g\) belongs to the missed family of \(s_0\), and
\[
 b_g=\frac{\lambda_{m_g}}{t_{m_g}}\chi_{m_g}r_g.
\tag{28}
\]
The parent-child identities give \(m_f=m_p=m\).  Hence (28) gives the first two identities in (19).  Brunn-Minkowski, applied in accepted fact 320dcb7d85f22889 to the quarter points in (12), gives \(r_f\geq r_p\), proving the last assertion in (19).

Since \(q_f\leq b_f\) and \(b_f\geq b_p\),
\[
 A_f=(q_f-b_p)_+\leq b_f-b_p.
\tag{29}
\]
Substituting (19) proves the first equality in (20).  The parent and child lie in the same good parity class \(\xi\), so the product formula (8) gives
\[
 r_f-r_p=\frac{a_xa_y-a_ua_v}{4A_\xi}.
\tag{30}
\]
Substitution proves the last equality in (20), including its nonnegativity.

We prove the two bounds for \(\mathfrak B\).  Define a parent subcapacity \(d\) by
\[
 d_{p(f)}:=B_f=b_{p(f)}-q_{p(f)}
 \quad(f\in F),
 \qquad
 d_g:=0
 \quad(g\notin p(F)).
\tag{31}
\]
If \(B_f>0\), then \(q_p<b_p\).  The complementary-saturation implication in accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, applied to the fixed paired optimizer \(s_b,v^b\), says that every edge missed by \(s_b\) has self-saturated load \(q_p=b_p\).  Its contrapositive gives
\[
 s_b(m_p)\in L_p
 \qquad(B_f>0).
\tag{32}
\]
Thus \(0\leq d\leq b\leq r\) is an affinely half-metronoid-realizable matching, with the one common realizing map \(s_b\), and
\[
 |d|=\mathfrak B.
\tag{33}
\]
It is a realizable submatching of \(b\), so
\[
 \mathfrak B\leq R_C(b)\leq R_C(r)=R.
\tag{34}
\]
It is also admissible in the global affine matching capacity \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\) of accepted fact 8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5.  The affine-matching depth certificate in that fact gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)\geq
 \gamma_d2^{-n}\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)
 \geq\gamma_d2^{-n}\mathfrak B.
\tag{35}
\]
Multiplying by \(2^n\) proves the second inequality in (21).

The quantities \(R\) and \(D\) are distinct nonnegative summands of the hard ledger (11).  Therefore (21) gives
\[
 (1+\gamma_d)\mathfrak B
 \leq R+D
 <\frac Q{100}
 \leq\Omega.
\tag{36}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}>\frac1e,
\tag{37}
\]
because \(\log(1+1/d)<1/d\).

For each \(f\in F\), definitions (15)-(17) and \(\omega_f\leq q_f\) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \omega_f-j_f
 &=(\omega_f-q_p)_+\\
 &\leq(q_f-q_p)_+\\
 &\leq(q_f-b_p)_++(b_p-q_p)\\
 &=A_f+B_f.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{38}
\]
Summing (38) proves the first inequality in (22).  Equation (36) gives
\[
 \mathfrak B<\frac{\Omega}{1+\gamma_d}.
\tag{39}
\]
Substituting (39) proves the second inequality in (22), and (37) proves the third.

Put \(c_d:=\gamma_d/(1+\gamma_d)\).  If \(\mathfrak A\leq c_d\Omega/2\), equation (22) gives \(\mathfrak J>c_d\Omega/2>\Omega/(2(e+1))\).  Otherwise
\[
 \mathfrak A>\frac{c_d}{2}\Omega
 >\frac{\Omega}{2(e+1)}.
\tag{40}
\]
This proves (23).

Assume \(\mathfrak A>0\).  For every \(f\in F\), definition (16) and the inequalities \(0\leq q_f\leq r_f\) give
\[
 0\leq A_f\leq q_f\leq r_f.
\tag{41}
\]
Since \(\mathfrak A=\sum_{f\in F}A_f>0\), at least one coordinate satisfies \(A_f>0\).  Therefore \(\sigma_{\mathrm{amp}}\) in (24) is a nonzero positive submeasure of the canonical reservoir-centroid measure
\[
 \sum_gr_g\delta_{(m_g,\bar c_g)}.
\tag{42}
\]
Accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives the first inequality in (25).  The same accepted fact gives
\[
 D\geq M_{\mathrm{off}}(C).
\tag{43}
\]
The terms \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\) and \(D\) are distinct summands in (11).  If
\[
 S_{\mathrm{amp}}
 :=2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{amp}}}
 \frac{\mathfrak A}{r_{\mathrm{amp}}+1},
\tag{44}
\]
then (25) and (43) give \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)+D\geq2S_{\mathrm{amp}}\).  The strict hard ledger yields \(2S_{\mathrm{amp}}<Q/100\), which is the second inequality in (25).

Finally, the amplified alternative in (23), together with \(\Omega\geq Q/100\), gives the first inequality in (26).  Combining it with
\[
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{amp}}}
 \frac{\mathfrak A}{r_{\mathrm{amp}}+1}
 <\frac Q{200}
\tag{45}
\]
and rearranging positive quantities gives the second inequality in (26).  Nothing in this argument removes the residual-rank denominator in (25), so the stated scope limitations follow.
