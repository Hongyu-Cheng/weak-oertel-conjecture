## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard synchronized-atlas instance in the notation of accepted facts 2fe46f47d19112ea, 86944ca2dce34ab7, c5b53e3fca777493, d01de728d8b5e33c, and d06d919de3083960. Fix
\[
 v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t),
 \qquad
 Y:=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad
 w_y:=\widehat\tau_y(v),
 \qquad
 W(A):=\sum_{y\in A}w_y.
\tag{1}
\]
For every nonempty \(A\subseteq Y\), put
\[
 \rho_A:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(A),
 \qquad
 r_A^c:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(A)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_y-s(y):y\in A\},
\tag{2}
\]
and retain the exact score of accepted fact 86944ca2dce34ab7,
\[
 \Lambda_v(A):=
 2^{n-\rho_A}\frac{W(A)}{r_A^c+1}.
\tag{3}
\]
Assume the registered antecedent
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)<\frac{Q}{400\gamma_d}
 \qquad
 \text{for every nonempty proper }A\subsetneq Y.
\tag{4}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{Y\setminus\{y\}}^c\geq10
 \quad\text{for every }y\in Y,}
\tag{5}
\]
and consequently
\[
 \boxed{r_Y^c\geq10.}
\tag{6}
\]

Fix also the registered maximum-weight order on the full physical whole-label representation, with its original tie-breaking and physically identified target collisions, let \(\mathscr B\) be its rank-raising labels, and let \(Y_{\mathscr B}\) be their physical endpoint set. The first alternative of accepted fact 5945e0b917621583 is impossible under (4). Its second alternative is exactly a lattice simplex \(\Delta\) with
\[
 \operatorname{vert}\Delta\subseteq Y_{\mathscr B},
 \qquad
 \mathcal E_n(\Delta)\geq\frac Q{100},
\tag{7}
\]
using the same \(v\), endpoint weights, greedy order, tie-breaking, physical target aggregation, and legal ambient-normalized score as the registered conclusion. However, accepted fact 5945e0b917621583 additionally assumes \(r_Y^c=5\), whereas (6) holds under (4). Therefore (7) does not follow from the presently accepted rank-five result. No assertion that a simplex satisfying (7) exists in the rank-at-least-ten branch is made.

## Proof

We first derive the strengthened endpoint mass using only the universal part of accepted fact c5b53e3fca777493. That fact proves, before it subsequently specializes to the deletion-stable rank-five case,
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 >\left(\frac9{100}-\frac1{50\gamma_d}\right)Q
 >\frac{7Q}{200}.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus (8) requires no rank-five premise. Accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c identifies the same internal whole-label family for every fixed \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) and gives
\[
 \widehat P(v)\geq H_{\mathrm{int}},
 \qquad
 W(Y)=\sum_{y\in Y}w_y=2\widehat P(v).
\tag{9}
\]
Combining (8) and (9) yields the strict full endpoint-mass bound
\[
 W(Y)>\frac{7Q}{100}.
\tag{10}
\]

Accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c also gives
\[
 n\geq2,
 \qquad
 |Y|\geq n+4.
\tag{11}
\]
The strict synchronized endpoint cap retained in accepted fact d06d919de3083960 is
\[
 0<w_y<\frac{Q}{400\,2^n}
 \leq\frac{Q}{1600}
 \qquad(y\in Y),
\tag{12}
\]
where the final weak inequality uses \(n\geq2\). Fix \(y\in Y\) and put
\[
 A_y:=Y\setminus\{y\}.
\tag{13}
\]
Equation (11) makes \(A_y\) nonempty and proper. Since \(A_y\subseteq\mathbb R^n\), one has \(\rho_{A_y}\leq n\), and hence \(2^{n-\rho_{A_y}}\geq1\). Equations (10) and (12) therefore give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 2^{n-\rho_{A_y}}W(A_y)
 &\geq W(A_y)\\
 &=W(Y)-w_y\\
 &>\frac{7Q}{100}-\frac{Q}{1600}
 =\frac{111Q}{1600}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]
All quantities below are positive. Applying (4) to \(A_y\), substituting (3), and rearranging gives
\[
 r_{A_y}^c+1
 >400\gamma_d
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{A_y}}W(A_y)}Q.
\tag{15}
\]
By (14),
\[
 r_{A_y}^c+1
 >400\gamma_d\frac{111}{1600}
 =\frac{111}{4}\gamma_d.
\tag{16}
\]
Accepted fact 2fe46f47d19112ea gives the strict uniform estimate
\[
 \gamma_d>\frac4{11}.
\tag{17}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 r_{A_y}^c+1
 >\frac{111}{4}\frac4{11}
 =\frac{111}{11}>10.
\tag{18}
\]
The quantity \(r_{A_y}^c+1\) is an integer. Equation (18) therefore implies \(r_{A_y}^c+1\geq11\), equivalently
\[
 r_{A_y}^c\geq10.
\tag{19}
\]
Since \(y\in Y\) was arbitrary, (19) proves (5). Hereditary monotonicity from accepted fact 86944ca2dce34ab7 gives \(r_{A_y}^c\leq r_Y^c\), so (5) implies (6).

It remains to audit exactly what accepted fact 5945e0b917621583 supplies. Its first alternative would give a nonempty proper \(A\subsetneq Y\) satisfying
\[
 r_A^c\leq4,
 \qquad
 2^{n-\rho_A}W(A)\geq\frac{Q}{80\gamma_d}.
\tag{20}
\]
Using (3) and \(r_A^c+1\leq5\), equation (20) would imply
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)
 =2^{n-\rho_A}\frac{W(A)}{r_A^c+1}
 \geq\frac{Q}{80\gamma_d}\frac15
 =\frac{Q}{400\gamma_d},
\tag{21}
\]
which contradicts the strict inequality (4). Thus the proper-subset alternative is ruled out with no loss in the constant and with equality in (21) still contradictory to (4).

The interface to the other alternative has no hidden alias change. Accepted fact 5945e0b917621583 fixes the same \(v\), \(Y=\widehat Y(v)\), weights \(w_y=\widehat\tau_y(v)\), registered maximum-weight whole-label order, original tie-breaking, and physically identified target collisions. Its simplex is on endpoints of the rank-raising labels of that fixed order, so its vertex set is contained in the registered \(Y_{\mathscr B}\). It calls the resulting quantity the legal ambient-normalized root-affine score \(\mathcal E_n\), which is exactly the score in (7). Therefore its second alternative would prove the registered conclusion without any endpoint-set, ordering, weight, collision, or normalization mismatch if all of its premises held.

The missing premise is exact: accepted fact 5945e0b917621583 assumes \(r_Y^c=5\), while (6) proves \(r_Y^c\geq10\) from the registered antecedent and the retained canonical ledger. The accepted rank-five result contains no implication for \(r_Y^c\geq10\). The unresolved step is therefore a rank-uniform canonical localization theorem: in the same fixed physical greedy representation, one must show for the high-rank branch that either some nonempty proper \(A\subsetneq Y\) violates (4), or one simplex on \(Y_{\mathscr B}\) has score at least \(Q/100\). This proof establishes neither alternative in that high-rank branch and does not combine or sum any local simplex scores. Hence no simplex or terminal conclusion is claimed.
