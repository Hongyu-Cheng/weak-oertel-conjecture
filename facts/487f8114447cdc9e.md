## Statement

For the centered triangle triple with radii (19/20,1,21/20), original canonical fixed caps of mass 6, tilt −1/10, and β=800/1803, the source-only affine response is (2/601)tr A. Its cone has nonnegative speeds on the whole middle facets, and zero response implies V=0. Its positive multiplier at each actual paired endpoint vertex tuple on facet i is (Γ_2−β)|F_i(T)|/2. A coupled path with radii (19/20−t,1,21/20+t) has exact midpoint inclusion, volume M+(9/10)t+9t², and a retilted proper canonical cover of mass 6 with normalized-response numerator −240/601.

## Proof

Write $a=19/20$, $b=21/20$, $T=\operatorname{conv}\{(-1,-1),(2,-1),(-1,2)\}$, with barycentric coordinates $\lambda_i$. Use the three linear forms $s_i=3\lambda_i-1$ on the centered reference $T$. They sum to zero. The three caps $s_i(x)+(a-b)z\ge0$ cover the entire ambient space, and all of their fiber traces are proper. In the fiber $rT$, the canonical trace $s_i(x)+k\ge0$ has raw area $(2r+k)^2/2$ whenever $-2r<k<r$, by edge ratios. At the three sites the positive heights $2r+k$ are all $2$, so every global cap has mass $6$. The actual midpoint relation is equality since $(a+b)/2=1$. The total mass is $M=(9/2)(a^2+1+b^2)=5409/400$ and $6/M=800/1803$. By the affine vertex-permutation symmetries of $T$ the coareas of the three equally scaled margin forms agree, and their positive balancing weights can all be taken as one. The three coarea response weights are thus $1/3$.

On a middle facet the two caps corresponding to its endpoints retain intervals of relative length $2/3$ at those endpoints, and the third misses the facet. Their averaged indicator is reflection symmetric along the facet with mean $(2/3+2/3)/3=4/9$. Its integral against any affine function on the facet equals $4/9$ times the unweighted integral by reflection and the mean. The numerator of the middle-only response is therefore $(4/9-800/1803)\int_{\partial T} n\cdot V\,dS=(2/601)\operatorname{tr}A$. The midpoint sum of the endpoint facets is the entire middle facet, so the labeled inequalities are exactly nonnegative outward speed on each entire facet for middle-only fields, by affine interpolation. The boundary integral can vanish on that cone only if each facet speed vanishes, hence if $V$ vanishes at each vertex by independence of the incident normals and is identically zero. At each facet endpoint $v_j$ the actual tuple $(av_j,bv_j)$ has midpoint $v_j$. Assign the multiplier $(4/9-800/1803)|F_i|/2$ to each such label, and zero to the other labels. The affine trapezoid formula on each facet gives the exact response identity.

Under the coupled motion $(a-t,1,b+t)$ the midpoint remains exact. Retilt to coefficient $a-b-2t$, which keeps every one-vertex height equal to $2$ and every cap mass equal to $6$. Direct expansion gives $M(t)=M+(9/10)t+9t^2$. Thus the numerator of the normalized response is $0-(800/1803)(9/10)=-240/601$. At the original cap coefficients the first derivatives of the two endpoint contributions also cancel, so this is the regular full-family linear response on the displayed feasible velocity. The same labeled identity with nonnegative coefficients is impossible on the entire affine velocity space since the coupled velocity is feasible and has negative response. This proves the separation of the source-only and coupled cover responses using only the selected cover data.
