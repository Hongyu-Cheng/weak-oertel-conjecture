## Statement

Let $C=\{(z,x):-1\le z\le1,\ 0\le x\le3-2|z|\}$, with summed length $\mu$ on its integer trace of total mass $5$. Write $D$ for actual raw mixed depth, $F$ for ordinary raw interval depth and $P_\lambda=D+\lambda F$. At its middle fiber,
$$
D(0,x)=\min\{x+\min(2x,1),\ 3-x+\min(2(1-x)_+,1)\}.
$$
Its actual envelope is $E(\lambda)=\max\{2+\lambda,\,3/2+(3/2)\lambda\}$. For $0\le\lambda<1$ its unique source is $(0,1)$, for $\lambda>1$ its unique source is $(0,3/2)$, and at $\lambda=1$ exactly the continuum $\{(0,x):1\le x\le3/2\}$ is active. The canonical active slope jumps from $1$ to $3/2$ at one and the actual depth drops from $2$ to $3/2$. For $Y_0=(0,1)$ and $Y_1=(0,3/2)$, at $\lambda=1$, a depth-attaining cap through their midpoint has original slab mass $1/4$ upon shifting it to $Y_1$. The own-root midpoint gain is zero and $D(Y_0)-D(Y_1)=1/2$, giving equality in the one-half same-parameter slab bound. Every finite increasing subdivision of this active segment gives equality in the summed midpoint charges.

## Proof

The upper graph $3-2|z|$ is concave. Its integer fibers are $[0,1],[0,3],[0,1]$ at the three consecutive bases. Translate height to $X=x-1/2$. In c680a307d3f26cec take $a=b=1$, $U=3$ and $s=1$, which satisfy $U\ge1$ and $|s|\le U/2-1/2$. The upper-cap branch in that result is $3-x+\min(1,(2-2x)_+)$, and the lower-cap branch is $x+\min(1,(2x)_+)$. This proves the stated formula including pure-base halfspaces. On each endpoint fiber the same result gives depth $\min(x,1-x)$, equal to its own depth.

The middle formula reduces to $3x$ on $[0,1/2]$, $x+1$ on $[1/2,1]$, and $3-x$ on $[1,3]$. The middle own depth is $\min(x,3-x)$. Thus $P_\lambda$ strictly increases up to $x=1$ and strictly decreases after $x=3/2$ for every $\lambda\ge0$. On $[1,3/2]$ it equals $3+(\lambda-1)x$. The endpoint objectives are at most $(1+\lambda)/2$, strictly below $2+\lambda$. Maximizing the middle linear piece gives the claimed envelope and all its maximizers. In particular $Q=2$, $D_{\mathrm{top}}=3/2$, the canonical slope's sole Stieltjes atom is $1/2$ at one, and the global normalized actual mixed depth is $2/5$.

For any $1\le x_0<x_1\le3/2$, the through-midpoint cap $\{x\ge(x_0+x_1)/2\}$ is depth-attaining. The endpoints contribute zero length and the middle contributes $3-(x_0+x_1)/2$. Its shift to $x_1$ loses length $(x_1-x_0)/2$ on the original middle fiber and zero elsewhere. The root is affine on this interval and the actual depth drop is $x_1-x_0$. This proves equality for each such pair and each finite subdivision, including $x_0=1$, $x_1=3/2$.
