## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^d$ be a compact convex body, $d\ge1$, with mixed $d$-volume measure $\mu$ of positive mass $M$ and base mean $k+\theta$, where $k\in\mathbb Z$ and $0<\theta<1$. Let $\mathcal A$ be the set of continuous barycenters of measures $f\mu$ with $0\le f\le1$, mass $\theta M$ and base mean $k+1$. Define $\mathcal B$ with mass $(1-\theta)M$ and base mean $k$. Both are nonempty compact convex sets. If $g_x=M^{-1}\int x\,d\mu$, then
$$
\mathcal B=\frac{g_x-\theta\mathcal A}{1-\theta}.
$$
For every $u\ne0$, the maximizing source in direction $u$ for $\mathcal A$ is unique up to $\mu$-null sets. It is the restriction to an actual closed cap with continuous normal $u$. The opposite closed cap uniquely maximizes the opposite direction for $\mathcal B$. The same finite affine hyperplane partitions $C$ into the two corresponding convex bodies with null mixed overlap.

## Proof

Apply the top-mass lemma to the normalized marginal of $z-k$. It provides a dominated density with mass and first moment both $\theta$: at equality take the top selection, and at strict inequality take the mixture with the constant density. Regard the density as constant on each positive fiber. Thus the moment target $(\theta M,\theta M(k+1))$ is feasible on the original mixed source. Complementation $f\mapsto1-f$ bijects the two source classes by conservation of mass and base moment. It also gives the displayed barycenter identity. The classes of dominated measures with the given moments are convex and weakly compact: the support is compact, masses are fixed, and both domination (tested on nonnegative continuous functions) and the moment constraints are weakly closed. Continuous barycenters are continuous under this convergence, giving compactness and convexity of the images.

For each $u\ne0$ apply fact b1274a369dfa9af7 with $n=1$ and $q=d$, which holds because some fiber has positive $d$-volume. The fixed direction is nonconstant on every positive-volume fiber. The fact realizes the target by a finite affine margin $L=u\cdot x+az+b$ with $\mu(L=0)=0$. Write $H=\{L\ge0\}$. For any feasible $f$,
$$
\int u\cdot x\,(\mathbf1_H-f)\,d\mu
=\int L\,(\mathbf1_H-f)\,d\mu\ge0
$$
because the affine base terms cancel and the integrand on the right is nonnegative pointwise. Off the null boundary this integrand is zero only if $f=\mathbf1_H$. Thus the source and its supporting barycenter are unique. Complementation reverses the direction and identifies the other source with the opposite closed cap. Both cap pieces have positive mass and points of strict margin of their respective signs. Approaching these points from the interior of $C$ shows each piece has ambient interior. They cover $C$ and their common boundary is $\mu$-null.
