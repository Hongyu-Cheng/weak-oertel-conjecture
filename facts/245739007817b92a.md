## Statement

Put
\[
 c_0:=\frac{3}{64000}.
\]
Then \(c_0\in(0,1/e)\), and the following restricted weak Oertel theorem holds:
\[
 \exists c_0\in(0,1/e)\;
 \forall n\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\;
 \forall d\in\{1,\ldots,199\}\;
 \forall C\;
 \exists \mathbf y^\star\in S\;
 \forall H,
\]
where \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) is any convex body,
\[
 \Lambda:=\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d,
 \qquad S:=C\cap\Lambda\ne\varnothing,
\]
and \(H\) ranges over the closed halfspaces of \(\mathbb R^{n+d}\) containing \(\mathbf y^\star\). More explicitly, there is \(\mathbf y^\star\in S\) such that
\[
 h_S(\mathbf y^\star)
 :=\inf_{\substack{H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\text{ closed halfspace}\\
                    \mathbf y^\star\in H}}
   \mathcal H_d(S\cap H)
 \ge \frac{3}{64000}\,2^{-n}\mathcal H_d(S).
\tag{1}
\]

## Proof

First, \(c_0>0\). Also
\[
 e=2+\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac1{k!}
 <2+\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}2^{-j}=3,
\]
because \(k!\ge2^{k-1}\) for \(k\ge2\), with strict inequality for \(k\ge3\). Hence
\[
 \frac{3}{64000}<\frac13<\frac1e,
\]
so \(c_0\in(0,1/e)\).

Fix \(n\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(d\in\{1,\ldots,199\}\), and a convex body \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) with \(S\ne\varnothing\). For \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\), put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}a_z.
\tag{2}
\]
By the definition of mixed-integer volume,
\[
 W=\mathcal H_d(S).
\tag{3}
\]
If \(W=0\), any \(\mathbf y^\star\in S\) satisfies (1), so assume \(W>0\).

Define
\[
 h_{\max}(C):=
 \sup_{p\in S}
 \inf_{\substack{H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\text{ closed halfspace}\\p\in H}}
 \sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z),
 \qquad H_z:=\{x:(z,x)\in H\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Use \(G,D,Q\), and \(\mathcal A(C)\) with the exact common meanings and normalizations of accepted facts `41c6cbb26797981c` and `a3e5d5377e0dc367`: \(G=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)\), \(D=2^nh_{\max}(C)\), and, whenever \(G<W/4\), \(Q>0\) is the half-strength canonical product-reservoir mass and
\[
 \mathcal A(C)=R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\tag{5}
\]

There are two exhaustive cases. If \(G\ge W/8\), the first assertion of accepted fact `41c6cbb26797981c` gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)>\frac{3}{64000}\,2^{-n}W.
\tag{6}
\]

Suppose instead that \(G<W/8\). Then \(G<W/4\), so the canonical reservoir and all quantities in (5) satisfy the hypotheses and alias map of accepted fact `a3e5d5377e0dc367`. If \(\mathcal A(C)<Q/100\), that accepted fact gives \(d\ge200\), contradicting \(d\le199\). Therefore
\[
 \mathcal A(C)\ge\frac Q{100}.
\tag{7}
\]
The second assertion of accepted fact `41c6cbb26797981c`, applied with the same canonical quantities, now gives (6) in this case as well. Thus (6) holds for every \(C\) under consideration.

Put \(t=(3/64000)2^{-n}W\). Since the supremum in (4) is strictly larger than \(t\), there is \(\mathbf y^\star\in S\) for which the inner infimum in (4) is strictly larger than \(t\). For every closed ambient halfspace \(H\), the mixed-volume definition and the fiber identity
\[
 (S\cap H)\cap(\{z\}\times\mathbb R^d)
 =\{z\}\times(C_z\cap H_z)
\]
give
\[
 \mathcal H_d(S\cap H)
 =\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z).
\tag{8}
\]
Consequently the inner infimum at \(\mathbf y^\star\) in (4) is exactly \(h_S(\mathbf y^\star)\). Equations (3), (6), and (8) yield
\[
 h_S(\mathbf y^\star)
 >\frac{3}{64000}\,2^{-n}W
 =\frac{3}{64000}\,2^{-n}\mathcal H_d(S),
\]
which implies (1). The constant was fixed before \(n,d,C\), so the restricted quantifier order is exactly the locked order.
