## Statement

For every integer \(r\ge 1\), put
\[
k=2r,\qquad n=k+1=2r+1,\qquad M=3^r,
\]
and define
\[
P=\operatorname{conv}\!\left(\,[-1,1]^k\times\{-1\},\,(0,M)\,\right)\subseteq\mathbb R^k\times\mathbb R.
\]
Let
\[
\theta=\frac{3(M-1)}{2M^2+9M+1},\qquad g(u,t)=1+\theta t,
\]
and
\[
C=\{(u,t,x):(u,t)\in P,\ 0\le x\le g(u,t)\}\subseteq\mathbb R^{n}\times\mathbb R.
\]
Then \(C\) is a compact full-dimensional convex body, \(g>0\) on \(P\), and the fiber-length marginal
\[
\pi_{(u,t)}
=\frac{\operatorname{vol}_1(C_{(u,t)})}
{\sum_{z\in P\cap\mathbb Z^n}\operatorname{vol}_1(C_z)}
=\frac{g(u,t)}{\sum_{z\in P\cap\mathbb Z^n}g(z)}
\]
has mean \(z_0=(0,0)\in P\cap\mathbb Z^n\). Its closed-halfspace depth at its mean satisfies
\[
\tau_\pi(z_0)
\le
\pi\{(u,t):t\ge0\}
=\frac{7M+1}{4M^2+7M+1}
\le\frac2M.
\]
Consequently
\[
\frac{\tau_\pi(z_0)}{2^{-n/2}}
\le 2^{3/2}\left(\frac23\right)^r\longrightarrow0.
\]
In particular, there is no absolute \(c>0\) for which every finite lattice marginal whose fiber-volume root is concave and whose mean is an integer support point satisfies
\(\tau_\pi(\mathbb E_\pi Z)\ge c\,2^{-n/2}\), even when the continuous dimension is \(d=1\) and the fiber-length function is positive affine.

## Proof

The section of \(P\) at height \(t\in[-1,M]\) is
\[
\left[-\frac{M-t}{M+1},\frac{M-t}{M+1}\right]^k\times\{t\}.
\]
Hence its lattice points are exactly
\[
D:=P\cap\mathbb Z^n
=\bigl(\{-1,0,1\}^k\times\{-1\}\bigr)
\mathbin{\dot\cup}
\bigl(\{0\}^k\times\{0,1,\ldots,M\}\bigr).
\]
Indeed, at \(t=-1\) the section is \([-1,1]^k\), while for each integer \(0\le t<M\) its radius lies strictly between \(0\) and \(1\), and at \(t=M\) it is the singleton \(0\). Since \(k=2r\) and \(M=3^r\), the bottom layer has \(3^k=M^2\) lattice points.

Write \(Q=2M^2+9M+1\). Since
\[
Q-3(M-1)=2(M+1)(M+2)>0,
\]
we have \(0<\theta<1\). Thus \(g(u,t)\ge 1-\theta>0\) throughout \(P\). The set \(C\) is the intersection of the compact prism over \(P\) with the two affine inequalities \(x\ge0\) and \(x\le g(u,t)\); it is therefore compact and convex. Since \(P\) is full-dimensional and \(g>0\), \(C\) is full-dimensional in \(\mathbb R^{n+1}\). Its fiber over \((u,t)\in D\) is the interval \([0,g(u,t)]\), so its length is exactly \(g(u,t)\).

The \(u\)-coordinates of the \(\pi\)-mean vanish: the bottom layer is centrally symmetric in \(u\), its weights are constant there, and all remaining lattice points have \(u=0\). For the \(t\)-coordinate, the unnormalized first moment is
\[
\begin{aligned}
&-M^2(1-\theta)+\sum_{j=0}^M j(1+\theta j)\\
&\quad=-M^2(1-\theta)
+\frac{M(M+1)}2
+\theta\frac{M(M+1)(2M+1)}6\\
&\quad=\frac M6\left[-3(M-1)+\theta(2M^2+9M+1)\right]
=0,
\end{aligned}
\]
by the definition of \(\theta\). Hence \(\mathbb E_\pi Z=(0,0)=z_0\).

The closed halfspace \(t\ge0\) passes through \(z_0\) and contains precisely the \(M+1\) spine points at heights \(0,\ldots,M\). Its unnormalized mass is
\[
A=\sum_{j=0}^M(1+\theta j)
=(M+1)\left(1+\frac{\theta M}{2}\right)
=\frac{(M+1)(M+2)(7M+1)}{2Q}.
\]
The bottom layer has mass
\[
B=M^2(1-\theta)
=\frac{2M^2(M+1)(M+2)}{Q}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\pi\{t\ge0\}
=\frac{A}{A+B}
=\frac{7M+1}{4M^2+7M+1}
\le\frac2M,
\]
where the final inequality follows from
\[
M(7M+1)\le2(4M^2+7M+1).
\]
Since \(n=2r+1\) and \(M=3^r\),
\[
\frac{\tau_\pi(z_0)}{2^{-n/2}}
\le\frac{2}{3^r}\,2^{(2r+1)/2}
=2^{3/2}\left(\frac23\right)^r
\longrightarrow0.
\]
This proves the claimed failure of every dimension-free \(c\,2^{-n/2}\) lower bound. The construction only controls the depth of the integer marginal at its mean; it makes no claim about the best mixed-integer point depth in \(C\), so it is not a counterexample to the entropy-path metronoid statement or to Oertel's conjecture.
