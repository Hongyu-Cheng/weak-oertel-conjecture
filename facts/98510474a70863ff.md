## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a compact convex body, and use the notation of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483: \(\Lambda:=\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d\), \(S:=C\cap\Lambda\), \(C_z:=\{y:(z,y)\in C\}\), and \(a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)\). For Borel \(B\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\), let \(\mu_C(B):=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\operatorname{vol}_d\{y\in C_z:(z,y)\in B\}\). Assume \(W:=\mu_C(\mathbb R^{n+d})>0\), and put \(D_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\}\), \(c_z:=a_z^{-1}\int_{C_z}y\,dy\), and \(\gamma_d:=(d/(d+1))^d\).

For \(p\in S\), let \(h_S(p):=\inf_{H\ni p}\mu_C(H)\), where \(H\) ranges over closed ambient halfspaces, and let \(h_{\max}(C):=\sup_{p\in S}h_S(p)\). Set \(D:=2^nh_{\max}(C)\), exactly as in equation (33) of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70. Define the full-centroid ranks by
\[
\rho_c:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(D_+),\qquad
r_c:=\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(D_+)\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
\dim\operatorname{span}\{c_z-s(z):z\in D_+\}.
\]
Thus \(0\leq\rho_c\leq n\) and \(0\leq r_c\leq d\).

Retain exactly the residual-selector normalization of equations (20)-(21) of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483. Namely, for \(q\in C_z\), let \(\delta_C(z,q)\) be the infimum of \(\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap L)\) over proper closed halfspaces \(L\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) containing \(q\). Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
&:=\sup 2^{n-\rho_E}\frac{\sum_{z\in E}w_z}{r_s+1},\\
\rho_E&:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E),\qquad
r_s:=\dim\operatorname{span}\{q_z-s(z):z\in E\},
\end{aligned}
\]
where the supremum ranges over all nonempty \(E\subseteq D_+\), all \(q_z\in C_z\), all \(0<w_z\leq\delta_C(z,q_z)\), and all affine \(s:\operatorname{aff}(E)\to\mathbb R^d\).

Put \(U:=\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_c}W/(r_c+1)\) and \(H_c:=2^{n-\rho_c}W/(d+1)\). Then \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq U\) and \(D\geq\max\{R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C),H_c\}\). Consequently, every nonnegative number \(\mathcal A\) satisfying \(\mathcal A\geq D+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) obeys
\[
\mathcal A\geq U+\max\{U,H_c\}.
\tag{1}
\]
For every such \(\mathcal A\) and every \(Q\) with \(0<Q\leq W/8\), the hard-ledger inequality \(\mathcal A<Q/100\) therefore implies the joint rank tradeoff
\[
2^{n-\rho_c}\frac WQ
\left(
\frac{\gamma_d}{r_c+1}
+\max\left\{\frac{\gamma_d}{r_c+1},\frac1{d+1}\right\}
\right)<\frac1{100}.
\tag{2}
\]
Equivalently, \(2U<Q/100\) and \(U+H_c<Q/100\). In particular \(H_c<Q/100\), and the latter inequality can be solved without replacing the actual ratio \(W/Q\):
\[
r_c+1>
\frac{100\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_c}W/Q}
{1-\dfrac{100\,2^{n-\rho_c}W}{(d+1)Q}},
\tag{3}
\]
whose denominator is positive. The other inequality gives \(r_c+1>200\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_c}W/Q\).

For the canonical application, assume exactly the small-core setup \(G<W/4\) of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70, with its unchanged core capacity \(G=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)\). This packet's \(D_+\) is its \(Z\). For each source parity \(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\), let \(A_\xi:=\sum_{z\in D_+,\,z\equiv\xi}a_z\). A nonempty class is good precisely when \(\max_{z\in D_+,\,z\equiv\xi}a_z\leq A_\xi/4\). Retain the unordered distinct-pair reservoir of its equation (3):
\[
r_{\{u,v\}}=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi},
&u,v\in D_+,\ u\ne v,\ u,v\equiv\xi,\ \xi\text{ good},\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\qquad
Q:=\sum_{\{u,v\}}r_{\{u,v\}}.
\tag{4}
\]
Let \(R:=R_C(r)\) be exactly the maximum affinely half-metronoid-realizable submatching mass in equation (6) of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70. Let \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\) and \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\) be exactly its capacities in equations (7) and (8), respectively. These are aliases to the accepted quantities, with no change to their admissible objects, suprema, or scaling. Take the exact ledger from its equation (33):
\[
\mathcal A(C):=
R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
+R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\tag{5}
\]
Then \(0<Q\leq W/8\), and (1)-(3) apply with \(\mathcal A=\mathcal A(C)\). Moreover,
\[
\mathcal A(C)\geq
\frac{(1+\gamma_d)W}{d+1}
\geq\frac{8(1+\gamma_d)Q}{d+1}.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal A(C)<Q/100\) forces \(d+1>800(1+\gamma_d)\), and hence \(d\geq1094\). No assertion is made that this necessary dimension bound is sufficient for a hard body, or that \(1094\) is the first integer satisfying every displayed scalar or rank condition.

## Proof

Compactness makes \(D_+\) finite and nonempty. Each \(C_z\) with \(z\in D_+\) is a full-dimensional compact convex subset of \(\mathbb R^d\), so the support of its restricted Lebesgue measure is all of \(C_z\). Zero-volume fibers contribute no measure. Consequently
\[
P:=\operatorname{supp}\mu_C
=\bigcup_{z\in D_+}\bigl(\{z\}\times C_z\bigr).
\tag{7}
\]
The finite union on the right is closed. Its affine hull is contained in \(\operatorname{aff}(D_+)\times\mathbb R^d\). The direction space of this affine hull contains \(\{0\}\times\mathbb R^d\), because even one positive fiber has full vertical affine span. Its base projection has dimension \(\rho_c\), because that projection spans \(\operatorname{aff}(D_+)\). Rank-nullity therefore gives \(\dim\operatorname{aff}(P)=\rho_c+d\).

Apply the dominated-measure residual theorem, equations (5)-(9) of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483, with \(\sigma=\mu_C\). This is a nonzero finite positive Borel measure carried by \(S\), and its halfspace domination is equality. Its base rank is \(\rho_c\); the accepted rank identity gives its residual rank as \((\rho_c+d)-\rho_c=d\). The theorem produces a fixed point in \(S\) of depth at least \(W/(2^{\rho_c}(d+1))\). Multiplication by \(2^n\) proves \(D\geq H_c\).

Equation (60) of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 gives \(\delta_C(z,c_z)\geq\gamma_da_z\) for every \(z\in D_+\). In the exact selector definition, choose \(E=D_+\), \(q_z=c_z\), \(w_z=\gamma_da_z\), and an affine map attaining \(r_c\). Such a map exists because the nonempty collection of attained ranks is a subset of the finite set \(\{0,\ldots,d\}\). These choices are admissible and have total weight \(\gamma_dW\), proving \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq U\). Equation (22) of the same accepted fact gives \(D\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\).

It follows that
\[
\mathcal A\geq D+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+
\max\{R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C),H_c\}
\geq U+\max\{U,H_c\}.
\]
This proves (1). These are inequalities between numerical capacities. The point supplying the Helly bound and points approaching the selector capacity need not coincide; no addition of depth certificates at a common point, or disjoint geometric charging, is being asserted.

Under \(\mathcal A<Q/100\), equation (1) gives \(U+\max\{U,H_c\}<Q/100\). Dividing by \(Q\) and substituting \(U,H_c\) proves (2). The identity \(U+\max\{U,H_c\}=\max\{2U,U+H_c\}\) proves the stated equivalence. Since \(U>0\), it also gives \(H_c<Q/100\), so \(1-100\,2^{n-\rho_c}W/((d+1)Q)>0\). Rearranging \(U+H_c<Q/100\) now gives (3) with no reversal of the inequality. Rearranging \(2U<Q/100\) gives the other stated rank bound.

For the canonical reservoir, equation (4) of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives \(Q\geq3(W-4G)/32>0\). For a good source class, let \(Q_\xi\) be the mass of its unordered distinct-pair edges. The exact factor \(1/(4A_\xi)\) in (4) yields
\[
Q_\xi
=\frac{A_\xi^2-\sum_{z\in D_+,\,z\equiv\xi}a_z^2}{8A_\xi}
\leq\frac{A_\xi}{8}.
\]
Bad and empty classes contribute zero. Summing over good classes gives \(Q\leq W/8\). In particular, neither a diagonal contribution nor an ordered-pair factor has been inserted.

The remaining four terms of (5) are nonnegative in the accepted setup: \(R\) is a submatching mass; \(G\geq0\) follows also from equation (39) of accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70; and \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C),R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\) are suprema of nonnegative expressions in its equations (7)-(8). Their indexing families are nonempty here: its centroid-visibility assertion (a), together with \(Q>0\), supplies a nonzero admissible submeasure for \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), while a central index at any \(m\in D_+\) is available for \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\). Therefore \(\mathcal A(C)\geq D+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), proving that the general inequalities apply to the unchanged canonical ledger.

Finally, \(r_c\leq d\) and \(\rho_c\leq n\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A(C)
&\geq U+H_c\\
&\geq\frac{(1+\gamma_d)\,2^{n-\rho_c}W}{d+1}\\
&\geq\frac{(1+\gamma_d)W}{d+1}
\geq\frac{8(1+\gamma_d)Q}{d+1}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves (6); comparison with \(\mathcal A(C)<Q/100\) gives \(d+1>800(1+\gamma_d)\).

Here is an exact rational certificate for the integer exclusion. The inequality \(\log(1+t)<t\) for \(t>0\) gives \(\gamma_d=(1+1/d)^{-d}>e^{-1}\), as also proved in equation (62) of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483. For the exponential series, \((5+k)!\geq120\cdot6^k\) for every \(k\geq0\), with strict inequality for \(k\geq2\). Hence
\[
e=\frac{65}{24}+\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac1{(5+k)!}
<\frac{65}{24}+\frac1{120}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}6^{-k}
=\frac{1631}{600}<\frac{400}{147},
\]
where the final comparison is the integer inequality \(1631\cdot147=239757<240000=400\cdot600\). Thus \(\gamma_d>147/400\), and \(800(1+\gamma_d)>800(1+147/400)=1094\). It follows that \(d+1>1094\); integrality gives \(d\geq1094\), as claimed.
