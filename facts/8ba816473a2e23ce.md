## Statement

Let $C$ be a compact convex body in $\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$, $n,d\ge1$, with finitely many nonempty sampled fibers $K_z$, all of positive volume, and base set $P$. Use pure uniform outward smoothing of width $h\Gamma(u)$, $h>0$, where $\Gamma(u)=\sqrt{u^\top\Sigma u}$ for the pooled within-fiber covariance $\Sigma$. Assume that every deepest mark is attained with a nonvertical active cap of positive own strip coarea. Write the deepest marks as $Y_i=(p_i,y_i)$ and assume their bases $I=\{p_i\}$ occupy every parity represented in $P$. Assign a source $p(z)$ of the same parity to each $z$ once and iterate $z\mapsto (z+p(z))/2$ until reaching a root $p_i$. If $z\notin I$, this occurs after finitely many steps $t\ge1$ and yields
$$
p_i=\lambda z+\sum_{j\ne i}\lambda_jp_j,\qquad
0<\lambda\le\frac1{1+2^{t-1}},\qquad
\lambda_j\ge0,\qquad \lambda+\sum_{j\ne i}\lambda_j=1.
$$
For every finite menu of actual nonvertical active caps at the root, each attaining the common deepest raw mass and having positive own strip coarea and positive raw weights $\rho_\ell$ satisfying $\sum_\ell\rho_\ell u_\ell=0$, the sum of weighted margins at $(z,x)$ is strictly positive for every $x\in\mathbb R^d$. If all selected caps have zero strip coarea on $K_z$, one of the caps captures that whole fiber.

Suppose additionally that the selected caps have zero strip coarea on the other deepest fibers, that the other deepest marks are interior to their own fibers, and that $\Gamma(u_\ell)=1$, $\sum_\ell\rho_\ell=1$. Then the same weighted margin is at least $h+\min_{\lambda_j>0}\sum_\ell\rho_\ell h_{K_{p_j}-y_j}(u_\ell)>0$, where $h_K$ is the support function of $K$.

## Proof

Write $\Psi(s)=0$ for $s\le-1$, $\Psi(s)=1+s$ for $-1<s<0$, and $\Psi(s)=1$ for $s\ge0$. The mass for a fixed cap normal $N=(a,u)$ and threshold $c$ is the integral of $\Psi((N\cdot W-c)/(h\Gamma(u)))$ against the original summed volume. It is nonincreasing in $c$ and strictly decreasing immediately to the right of the active threshold when strip coarea is positive. If a distinct deepest mark $Y_j$ had $N\cdot Y_j>N\cdot Y_i$, its mass for this normal would be strictly below the common deepest value. If $N\cdot Y_j=N\cdot Y_i$, the same normalized cap is active at both. At buffer zero, accepted fact 88f06d5537aba0ae gives $SY=m-A(0,\nabla\Gamma(u))$ for this cap. All right-hand data and $S>0$ depend on the same cap, so equality would force the same mark. Thus $N\cdot(Y_j-Y_i)<0$. Averaging these inequalities and using the raw normal balance gives $\bar a_i\cdot(p_j-p_i)<0$, where $\bar a_i=\sum_\ell\rho_\ell a_\ell$.

On any positive fiber, zero strip coarea means the open transition strip has zero volume. By continuity of the integrand and density of the interior in the closed fiber, an intermediate value anywhere would imply intermediate values on an open set of positive volume. The integrand is consequently constant zero or one by connectedness. At another deepest fiber the strict exclusion of its mark rules out the constant one.

Fact 88f06d5537aba0ae also gives $\operatorname{conv}(I)\cap\mathbb Z^n=I$ and distinct parities. Each iterate stays in $P$ by actual midpoint inclusion of two positive fibers. Its distance to $\operatorname{conv}(I)$ at least halves because the root used in the step belongs to that hull. The finite set $P$ has a positive minimum among its nonzero distances to the hull, so an orbit enters the hull and stops. Expansion gives coefficient $2^{-t}$ on $z$ and nonnegative dyadic coefficients on the sources. The last selected source differs from the root at first arrival, since otherwise the midpoint equation would have required the preceding point to equal that root. Its coefficient is $1/2$. Removing the root coefficient and renormalizing therefore yields the stated bound on $\lambda$.

Apply $\bar a_i$ to the relation after subtracting $p_i$. At least one $\lambda_j>0$ since $z\ne p_i$. Hence $\bar a_i\cdot(z-p_i)>0$. At every $(z,x)$ the sum of the weighted margins equals this quantity, because the continuous normals cancel. At least one margin is positive. In the zero-coarea case, its integrand is identically one on $K_z$, which proves the whole-fiber claim.

For the quantitative conclusion the whole miss of each other source fiber gives
$$
a_\ell\cdot(p_j-p_i)+u_\ell\cdot(y_j-y_i)+h_{K_{p_j}-y_j}(u_\ell)\le-h.
$$
Average and use the balance. Substitution into the root relation gives a lower bound $\sum_{j\ne i}(\lambda_j/\lambda)(h+\sum_\ell\rho_\ell h_{K_{p_j}-y_j}(u_\ell))$. The coefficients sum to $(1-\lambda)/\lambda\ge1$, and each support height is positive by own-fiber interior and $u_\ell\ne0$. This gives the stated reserve.
