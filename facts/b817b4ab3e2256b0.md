## Statement

Let $C$ be a compact convex set, $m$ a finite nonzero Borel measure on $C$ of mass $M$, and $L>0$ continuous concave with $M^{-1}\int\log L\,dm=0$. Let $Y_i\in C$, $L_i=L(Y_i)$, $\sigma_i>0$ with $\sum_i\sigma_i=1$, and probability measures $\rho_i$ with $\int w\,d\rho_i=Y_i$, $\int L\,d\rho_i=L_i$, and $\sum_i(\sigma_i/L_i)\rho_i=dm/(ML)$. For each positive continuous concave $U$ put $R=U/L$, $R_i=U(Y_i)/L_i$, $d\bar\rho_i=(L/L_i)d\rho_i$, $b_i=\int R\,d\bar\rho_i$ and $d\tau_i=(R/b_i)d\bar\rho_i$. Then
$$
\sum_i\sigma_i\log R_i-\frac1M\int\log R\,dm
=\sum_i\sigma_i[\log(R_i/b_i)+D(\bar\rho_i\|\tau_i)]\ge0,
$$
where $D(\mu\|\nu)=\int\log(d\mu/d\nu)\,d\mu$ is relative entropy. Equality holds exactly when $\int U\,d\rho_i=U(Y_i)$ and $R=R_i$ $\rho_i$-almost everywhere for every $i$. The maximum of $M^{-1}\int\log U\,dm$ subject to $U(Y_i)\le L_i$ is zero, with $U=L$ $m$-almost everywhere for each maximizer. If $m$ is the summed $d$-volume on full-dimensional compact sampled convex fibers, all maximizers equal $L$ on every closed sampled fiber.

## Proof

The contact moment makes each $\bar\rho_i$ a probability, and multiplication of the target identity by $L$ gives $\sum_i\sigma_i\bar\rho_i=dm/M$. Concavity and the original barycenter give $b_i=\int U\,d\rho_i/L_i\le R_i$. All ratios are bounded above and below by positive constants on $C$, so the row changes and logarithms are finite. On each row the density relation gives $D(\bar\rho_i\|\tau_i)=\log b_i-\int\log R\,d\bar\rho_i$, which is nonnegative by concave Jensen for the logarithm. It vanishes exactly when $R$ is constant $\bar\rho_i$-almost everywhere. Adding the two nonnegative terms on each row and using the mixture proves the formula. Since $\sigma_i>0$, equality requires $b_i=R_i$ and the constant ratio $R=R_i$ on each row, equivalently on $\rho_i$ by positivity of the density. These conditions also suffice.

If $U(Y_i)\le L_i$, the source sum is at most zero. The target logarithmic mean is therefore at most zero, attained by $L$. For any maximizer both sums are zero, each $R_i=1$, and equality forces $R=1$ almost surely on the rows and hence $m$-almost everywhere. On full-dimensional sampled convex fibers, each nonempty relatively open subset has positive fiber volume. Continuity gives equality everywhere on each of those fibers.
