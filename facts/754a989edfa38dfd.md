## Statement

Retain the common actual canonical hard \(n=3\) off-diagonal common-normal instance and notation of accepted facts 2c7ef442c71a1564, b5739a77433de383, and f83d08a46c19ca2f.  Put
\[
 p:=|q^*|,
 \qquad
 A:=\operatorname{aff}(T_*),
 \qquad
 D_A:=A\cap\{z\in\mathbb Z^3:a_z>0\},
 \qquad
 W_A:=\sum_{t\in D_A}a_t.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus \(\dim A=2\),
\[
 p>\frac{553}{51200}Q,
 \qquad
 G<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{2}
\]
Use the degree-tag source set and weights of accepted fact b5739a77433de383, but denote that source set by \(\mathcal S\) to avoid collision with the target plane \(A\):
\[
 \mathcal S:=A_d,
 \qquad
 w_z:=w_z^d=\frac{d_z}{2},
 \qquad
 \mathcal S_\xi:=\{z\in\mathcal S:z\equiv\xi\pmod2\},
 \qquad
 M_\xi:=\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_\xi}w_z.
\tag{3}
\]
In particular,
\[
 0<w_z\leq\frac{a_z}{4}
 \qquad(z\in\mathcal S).
\tag{4}
\]

For a source parity class \(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^3\), define the complete set of its physical plane midpoints, with diagonal pairs retained, by
\[
 \mathcal T_\xi^A
 :=\left\{
 \frac{r+s}{2}:r,s\in\mathcal S_\xi,\ \frac{r+s}{2}\in A
 \right\}\subseteq D_A,
\tag{5}
\]
and put
\[
 W_\xi^A:=\sum_{t\in\mathcal T_\xi^A}a_t,
 \qquad
 A_\xi^{\max}:=
 \begin{cases}
 \max_{z\in\mathcal S_\xi}a_z,&\mathcal S_\xi\ne\varnothing,\\
 0,&\mathcal S_\xi=\varnothing.
 \end{cases}
\tag{6}
\]
Define the full off-diagonal half-product mass whose physical midpoint lies in \(A\) by
\[
 P_\xi^A:=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle
 \frac1{M_\xi}
 \sum_{\substack{\{r,s\}\subseteq\mathcal S_\xi\\
 r\ne s,\ (r+s)/2\in A}}
 w_rw_s,&M_\xi>0,\\[3mm]
 0,&M_\xi=0.
 \end{cases}
\tag{7}
\]
Then, for every source parity color,
\[
 \boxed{16P_\xi^A\leq W_\xi^A+A_\xi^{\max}.}
\tag{8}
\]

Let
\[
 p_\xi:=\sum_{\substack{e=\{r,s\}:q_e^*>0\\r,s\equiv\xi\pmod2}}q_e^*,
 \qquad
 \Xi_*:=\{\xi:p_\xi>0\},
 \qquad
 \kappa_A(t):=|\{\xi\in\Xi_*:t\in\mathcal T_\xi^A\}|,
\tag{9}
\]
and define the actual volume-weighted cross-color overlap excess
\[
 \mathcal E_A^{\mathrm{col}}
 :=\sum_{t\in D_A}(\kappa_A(t)-1)_+a_t.
\tag{10}
\]
Then
\[
 p=\sum_\xi p_\xi,
 \qquad
 p_\xi\leq P_\xi^A,
\tag{11}
\]
and the hard parity/core ledger gives
\[
 \boxed{16p\leq W_A+\mathcal E_A^{\mathrm{col}}+G.}
\tag{12}
\]
Consequently at least one of the following two alternatives holds:
\[
 \boxed{W_A\geq\frac{8192}{553}p,}
\tag{13}
\]
or
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal E_A^{\mathrm{col}}>\frac{144}{553}p.}
\tag{14}
\]
In particular, the requested planar-volume alternative (13) is forced whenever the complete plane-midpoint target sets \(\mathcal T_\xi^A\) of the active source parity colors are pairwise disjoint.  No conversion of the positive collision excess in (14) into the requested trimmed Chebyshev-radius core is asserted.

## Proof

We first prove a weighted max-convolution inequality that will be applied to the actual forced midpoint fibers.  Let \(\Gamma\) be a torsion-free abelian group, let \(U,V\subseteq\Gamma\) be finite and nonempty, and let \(f:U\to(0,\infty)\), \(g:V\to(0,\infty)\).  Put
\[
 H(s):=\max_{\substack{u\in U,\ v\in V\\u+v=s}}f(u)g(v),
\tag{15}
\]
with value zero when there is no representation, and abbreviate
\[
 f_\infty:=\max_Uf,
 \qquad
 g_\infty:=\max_Vg.
\tag{16}
\]
We claim
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{s\in\Gamma}H(s)
 \geq
 2\sqrt{\left(\sum_{u\in U}f(u)^2\right)
          \left(\sum_{v\in V}g(v)^2\right)}
 -f_\infty g_\infty.}
\tag{17}
\]

For completeness, every finite-subset sumset estimate needed for (17) has an elementary proof.  The subgroup generated by \(U\cup V\) is a finitely generated torsion-free abelian group and hence admits a translation-invariant total order.  If \(U'=\{u_1<\cdots<u_m\}\) and \(V'=\{v_1<\cdots<v_n\}\) are nonempty, then
\[
 u_1+v_1<\cdots<u_m+v_1<u_m+v_2<\cdots<u_m+v_n.
\tag{18}
\]
These are \(m+n-1\) distinct members of \(U'+V'\), so
\[
 |U'+V'|\geq|U'|+|V'|-1.
\tag{19}
\]

For \(0<\lambda\leq f_\infty g_\infty\), put
\[
 \alpha_\lambda:=
 \sqrt{\lambda\frac{f_\infty}{g_\infty}},
 \qquad
 \beta_\lambda:=
 \sqrt{\lambda\frac{g_\infty}{f_\infty}}.
\tag{20}
\]
The two superlevel sets \(U_\lambda:=\{u:f(u)\geq\alpha_\lambda\}\) and \(V_\lambda:=\{v:g(v)\geq\beta_\lambda\}\) are nonempty, and \(U_\lambda+V_\lambda\subseteq\{s:H(s)\geq\lambda\}\).  Thus (19) gives
\[
 |\{s:H(s)\geq\lambda\}|
 \geq|U_\lambda|+|V_\lambda|-1.
\tag{21}
\]
Integrating (21) and using finite layer cake yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_sH(s)
 &\geq
 \frac{g_\infty}{f_\infty}\sum_{u\in U}f(u)^2
 +\frac{f_\infty}{g_\infty}\sum_{v\in V}g(v)^2
 -f_\infty g_\infty\\
 &\geq
 2\sqrt{\left(\sum_uf(u)^2\right)
          \left(\sum_vg(v)^2\right)}
 -f_\infty g_\infty.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{22}
\]
Indeed, in the first superlevel integral use
\(\lambda=(g_\infty/f_\infty)\alpha^2\), and in the second use
\(\lambda=(f_\infty/g_\infty)\beta^2\); the last line is arithmetic-geometric mean.  This proves (17) without an external sumset theorem.

We now apply (17) to one physical source parity color.  Since the target plane \(A\) is affinely spanned by lattice points and has codimension one in \(\mathbb R^3\), there are a primitive integral linear functional \(\ell:\mathbb Z^3\to\mathbb Z\), a point \(b\in A\cap\mathbb Z^3\), and the rank-two lattice
\[
 \Gamma:=\ker\ell\cap\mathbb Z^3
\tag{23}
\]
such that
\[
 A=\{x\in\mathbb R^3:\ell(x-b)=0\}.
\tag{24}
\]
Write \(h(z):=\ell(z-b)\).  Fix \(\xi\) with \(M_\xi>0\), and for \(k\geq1\) put
\[
 U_k:=\sum_{\substack{z\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(z)=k}}w_z,
 \qquad
 V_k:=\sum_{\substack{z\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(z)=-k}}w_z,
 \qquad
 r_k:=\sqrt{U_kV_k},
\tag{25}
\]
while
\[
 U_0:=\sum_{\substack{z\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(z)=0}}w_z,
 \qquad
 r_0:=\frac{U_0}{2}.
\tag{26}
\]

Fix \(k\geq1\) with \(U_kV_k>0\).  Choose one source \(r_0^+\) at height \(k\) and one source \(r_0^-\) at height \(-k\).  Same parity implies that every source in the positive level has the form \(r_0^++2u\), \(u\in\Gamma\), and every source in the negative level has the form \(r_0^-+2v\), \(v\in\Gamma\).  Their physical midpoint is
\[
 \frac{r_0^++r_0^-}{2}+u+v\in A\cap\mathbb Z^3.
\tag{27}
\]
Convexity of the one actual body and the Brunn-Minkowski inequality in the exact fiber normalization recorded in accepted fact 11e347c295e9430b give, for every such pair with midpoint \(t\),
\[
 a_t^{1/d}
 \geq\frac{a_r^{1/d}+a_s^{1/d}}2,
 \qquad
 a_t\geq\sqrt{a_ra_s}.
\tag{28}
\]
Apply (17) on \(\Gamma\) with \(f(r)=\sqrt{a_r}\) and \(g(s)=\sqrt{a_s}\).  Every target in its max-convolution is in \(\mathcal T_\xi^A\), so (4), (6), (17), and (28) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W_\xi^A
 &\geq
 2\sqrt{
 \left(\sum_{\substack{r\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(r)=k}}a_r\right)
 \left(\sum_{\substack{s\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(s)=-k}}a_s\right)}
 -\sqrt{
 \max_{\substack{r\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(r)=k}}a_r
 \max_{\substack{s\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(s)=-k}}a_s}\\
 &\geq8\sqrt{U_kV_k}-A_\xi^{\max}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{29}
\]
Thus, with
\[
 L_\xi:=W_\xi^A+A_\xi^{\max},
\tag{30}
\]
one has
\[
 r_k\leq\frac{L_\xi}{8}
 \qquad(k\geq1).
\tag{31}
\]

If \(U_0=0\), then \(r_0=0\) and (33) below is immediate.  Suppose \(U_0>0\), and apply the same argument to two copies of the height-zero source set.  Diagonal pairs are retained in \(\mathcal T_\xi^A\), exactly as required by the max-convolution.  Equations (4), (17), and (28) give
\[
 W_\xi^A\geq
 2\sum_{\substack{z\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(z)=0}}a_z
 -\max_{\substack{z\in\mathcal S_\xi\\h(z)=0}}a_z
 \geq8U_0-A_\xi^{\max}.
\tag{32}
\]
Consequently
\[
 r_0\leq\frac{L_\xi}{16}.
\tag{33}
\]

The numerator in (7) decomposes exactly by opposite heights.  The height-zero off-diagonal term is bounded by \(U_0^2/2=2r_0^2\), while the two nonzero opposite levels contribute \(U_kV_k=r_k^2\).  Therefore
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\{r,s\}\subseteq\mathcal S_\xi\\
 r\ne s,\ (r+s)/2\in A}}w_rw_s
 \leq2r_0^2+\sum_{k\geq1}r_k^2
 \leq\frac{L_\xi}{8}
 \left(r_0+\sum_{k\geq1}r_k\right),
\tag{34}
\]
where (31) handles the positive heights and (33) gives
\(2r_0^2\leq(L_\xi/8)r_0\).  On the other hand, arithmetic-geometric mean gives
\[
 M_\xi
 =U_0+\sum_{k\geq1}(U_k+V_k)
 \geq2\left(r_0+\sum_{k\geq1}r_k\right).
\tag{35}
\]
If the final parenthesis vanishes, then \(P_\xi^A=0\).  Otherwise (34)-(35), after division by \(M_\xi\) as in (7), give in both cases
\[
 P_\xi^A\leq\frac{L_\xi}{16},
\tag{36}
\]
which is exactly (8).  This proof uses every opposite-height product block at once; blocks with identical physical midpoint sumsets are not added and cause no reuse of a target fiber.

We next compare the selected optimizer to the complete plane product.  Every positive selected edge \(e=\{r,s\}\) has physical target \(t=(r+s)/2\in T_*\subseteq A\), and its source parity is the retained dominant color \(\xi(t)\).  Accepted fact f83d08a46c19ca2f gives
\[
 q_e^*\leq\bar q_e^{\ne}\leq q_e^{\ne}.
\tag{37}
\]
Under the exact degree-tag product aliases of accepted fact b5739a77433de383,
\[
 q_e^{\ne}=p_{t;r,s}\omega_t,
 \qquad
 p_{t;r,s}=\frac{w_rw_s}{M_{\xi(t)}b_t},
 \qquad
 0\leq\omega_t\leq b_t.
\tag{38}
\]
Hence
\[
 q_e^*\leq\frac{w_rw_s}{M_{\xi(t)}}.
\tag{39}
\]
Summing (39) over the selected edges of source parity \(\xi\) proves \(p_\xi\leq P_\xi^A\), and summing the disjoint source-parity decomposition proves \(p=\sum_\xi p_\xi\).  This is (11).

Accepted fact 2c7ef442c71a1564 gives \(W\geq8Q\), so the canonical hard inequality implies \(G<W/2\).  Accepted fact c81f02314cb34f8b therefore applies and gives the hard parity-maximum ledger
\[
 \sum_{\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^3}
 \max_{z\equiv\xi\pmod2}a_z\leq G.
\tag{40}
\]
Since \(\mathcal S_\xi\) is a subset of the positive fibers of parity \(\xi\),
\[
 \sum_\xi A_\xi^{\max}\leq G.
\tag{41}
\]
Also, by (9)-(10),
\[
 \sum_{\xi\in\Xi_*}W_\xi^A
 =\sum_{t\in D_A}\kappa_A(t)a_t
 \leq W_A+\mathcal E_A^{\mathrm{col}}.
\tag{42}
\]
Sum (8), use (11), and then apply (41)-(42):
\[
 16p
 \leq\sum_{\xi\in\Xi_*}16P_\xi^A
 \leq\sum_{\xi\in\Xi_*}(W_\xi^A+A_\xi^{\max})
 \leq W_A+\mathcal E_A^{\mathrm{col}}+G.
\tag{43}
\]
This proves (12).

If (13) fails, then
\[
 W_A<\frac{8192}{553}p.
\tag{44}
\]
Equation (2) also gives
\[
 G<\frac Q{100}<\frac{512}{553}p.
\tag{45}
\]
Substituting (44)-(45) into (12) yields
\[
 \mathcal E_A^{\mathrm{col}}
 >\left(16-\frac{8192}{553}-\frac{512}{553}\right)p
 =\frac{144}{553}p,
\tag{46}
\]
which is (14).  If the sets \(\mathcal T_\xi^A\), \(\xi\in\Xi_*\), are pairwise disjoint, then \(\mathcal E_A^{\mathrm{col}}=0\), so (14) is impossible and (13) follows.  This proves every asserted conclusion and leaves only the explicitly disclaimed collision-to-trimmed-core conversion.
