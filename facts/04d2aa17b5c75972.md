## Statement

Take a finite complete sampled lattice base $P$ with actual compatible compact positive-volume convex $d$-fibers $K_z$, summed volume $\mu$, and a source $Y=(p,y)$ with $y\in\operatorname{int}K_p$. Fix $h>0$ and a norm $\Gamma$ on continuous covectors. Use the pure-width through-source outward ramp $\Psi(t)=\min(1,\max(0,1+t))$ with argument $[a\cdot(z-p)+u\cdot(x-y)]/h$ at $\Gamma(u)=1$, and let $Q$ be the raw through-source infimum, including ordinary closed pure-base caps. Write $V_z=|K_z|$ and let $B_p$ be the minimum of $\sum_{z:a\cdot(z-p)>0}V_z$ over generic base covectors $a$, meaning $a\cdot(z-p)\ne0$ for $z\ne p$. If $P=\{p\}$ put $B_p=0$.

There is a fixed minimizing generic covector $a_*$. For every $\Gamma$-unit $u$ a finite multiple of $a_*$ gives outside ramp mass exactly $B_p$ and leaves the own-fiber contribution $Q_p^{\rm own}(u)$ unchanged, so $Q\le B_p+Q_p^{\rm own}(u)$. For an actual active nonvertical cap $N=(a,u)$ of mass $Q$, write $Q_p^{\rm out}(N)$ for its outside ramp mass. Then
$$
0\le Q_p^{\rm out}(N)\le B_p,\qquad
\Delta_N=B_p-Q_p^{\rm out}(N)=B_p+Q_p^{\rm own}(u)-Q\ge0.
$$

If a finite actual active menu has $\Delta_N=0$ on every term and quantile probabilities $\rho_j$ with $\sum_j\rho_j u_j=0$, the caps can all be replaced by such finite saturating tilts with the same $u_j$ and remain active and quantile balanced. The new response weights are proportional to $\rho_j/S_j$, where $S_j$ is the new positive threshold slope in absolute value. If $B_p>0$ the retargeted menu has unit capture efficiency for one nonnegative admissible field supported on an exposed target fiber of the complete base hull. If $B_p=0$, the own fiber is exposed and the projective field there pays capture efficiency at least $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$ for every actual active menu at $Y$. Here efficiency means the boundary integral of field times ramp profile divided by its positive boundary integral.

## Proof

The finite base evaluations induce only finitely many generic sign patterns and the generic domain is nonempty, so the finite set of sign-pattern costs has a minimum. For the singleton base, any $a_*$ works. Otherwise the minimum absolute nonzero gap $|a_*\cdot(z-p)|$ is positive. The continuous margins on all the compact fibers are bounded for each fixed $u$. At a sufficiently large finite tilt $t a_*$ the raw margins on positive-sign fibers are nonnegative everywhere, and those on negative-sign fibers are at most $-h$ everywhere. Hence the outside mass is exactly $B_p$, and the own mass is unaffected by the tilt. The infimum bound and the formula for $\Delta_N$ follow by subtracting the unchanged own mass.

For each zero-deficit cap the new outside mass equals its old one, so its total mass stays $Q$ and the new cap is actually minimizing. A finite menu permits one common sufficiently large $t$. The $\rho_j$ continue to balance the same $u_j$. At fixed cap normal the derivative of mass with respect to its threshold is $-S_j$, with $S_j=\mu\{-h<m_j<0\}/h$ for the new through-source margin $m_j$. This follows by dominated differentiation, since each kink hyperplane is null in every fiber. The own interior mark puts positive own volume in this strip, so $S_j>0$. The continuous mark gradient of mass is $-S_j u_j$; therefore setting $\nu_j=(\rho_j/S_j)/\sum_i\rho_i/S_i$ produces the stated response balance.

If $B_p>0$, some base has strictly positive evaluation. The maximizing face of $a_*$ on the complete hull therefore lies on the positive side, and any vertex $q$ of that face is an exposed vertex of the finite hull. Choose an affine base gap $g$ zero at $q$ and positive at every other sampled base. The field $[1-Tg(z)]_+$, with $T$ sufficiently large, equals one on $q$ and zero on every other sampled fiber. It is continuous and convex in the physical variables, has positive boundary integral, and is paid in full by every retargeted cap. If $B_p=0$, every positive-volume base other than $p$ has strictly negative evaluation for $a_*$; thus $p$ is exposed, including the singleton convention. Apply ec9604cbb33cab37 to the same full positive compatible family at the own-interior mark $Y$, with zero buffer: its projective field gives the stated capture for every outward through-source probability menu.
