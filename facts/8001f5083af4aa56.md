## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, let $B=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1,\ 1\le i\le d+1\}$ be a full-dimensional simplex of volume $b$ with $\sum_i v_i=0$, and let $A$ be a convex body of volume $a$. Assume every cut $g_i(x)=v_i\cdot x+1=0$ is proper on $A$. Define
$$
H_i=\max_A g_i,\quad U_i=|A\cap\{g_i\ge0\}|,\quad P_i=\frac{db}{d+1}H_i,\quad
R_i=\int_{A\cap\{g_i<0\}}(-g_i)\,dx,\quad t_i=U_i/a .
$$
Every membership pattern $I=\{i:g_i(x)\ge0\}$ occurring on positive volume satisfies $\sum_{i\in I}P_i>db$. The common volume and first-moment bounds are
$$
\sum_iP_iU_i>db\,a,\qquad
\sum_iP_iU_i\ge(d+1)ba+b\sum_i R_i>(d+1)ba.
$$
For $\rho_i$ the ordinary volume density in the coordinate $g_i$, for almost every $s\ge0$ one has
$$
\rho_i(-s)\le \frac{dU_i}{H_i}(1+s/H_i)^{d-1},\qquad
R_i\ge H_i a\left[\frac{d}{d+1}t_i^{-1/d}-1+\frac{t_i}{d+1}\right].
$$
Consequently
$$
\sum_i H_i\left[1+\frac{d-1}{d+1}t_i-\frac{d}{d+1}t_i^{-1/d}\right]\ge d+1.
$$

## Proof

Put $m=d+1$. At every interior point of $A$, $\sum_i g_i(x)=m$ and the omitted coordinates are negative. Thus $\sum_{i\in I}g_i(x)\ge m$. Because each $v_i\ne0$, $H_i>g_i(x)$ at interior points. This proves the strict pattern bound and its integrated version $\sum_iP_iU_i>db\,a$. Also,
$$
\sum_i\int_{A\cap\{g_i\ge0\}}g_i(x)\,dx=m a+\sum_iR_i .
$$
For a single captured cap with range $[0,H_i]$, homothety about any point at $g_i=0$ by factor $s/H_i$ puts a copy of the cap of volume $(s/H_i)^d U_i$ in $\{g_i\le s\}$. Integrating the upper-tail bound gives its first moment at most $dH_iU_i/m$. Combine this with the identity and $P_i=dbH_i/m$. Properness gives $R_i>0$.

The density $\rho_i$ is the Euclidean section measure divided by $\lVert v_i\rVert$. Suppress the index and write $\rho_0=\rho(0)$. The cone from the cut section at zero to a highest point gives $U\ge \rho_0 H/d$. On the interior of the height support, $\rho^{1/(d-1)}$ is concave by Brunn–Minkowski for parallel sections. Compare its value at zero with its chord from $-s$ to a level tending to $H$, where the limiting value is nonnegative. This gives $\rho(-s)\le\rho_0(1+s/H)^{d-1}\le (dU/H)(1+s/H)^{d-1}$. Off the support the density is zero. The same estimate holds at support endpoints for the representative given by the one-sided section limit, and the almost-everywhere assertion suffices for the integrals.

Write $E(s)=(dU/H)(1+s/H)^{d-1}$ and choose $L=H(t^{-1/d}-1)$ with $\int_0^L E=a-U$ for $t=U/a$. The function $\rho(-s)-E(s)\mathbf 1_{[0,L]}(s)$ integrates to zero, is nonpositive below $L$ and nonnegative above $L$. Multiplication by $s-L$ and integration give $R\ge\int_0^L sE(s)\,ds$. Substituting $w=1+s/H$ gives $dUH[w^{d+1}/(d+1)-w^d/d]_1^{t^{-1/d}}$, the stated lower bound. Substitute it for each $R_i$ in $d\sum_i H_iU_i/m\ge m a+\sum_iR_i$ and divide by $a$ to obtain the last inequality.
