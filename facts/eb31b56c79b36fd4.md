## Statement

Let $\nu(ds,du)P_{s,u}(dt)$ be a finite physical martingale with actual marked surface source and target marginals $\nu,\eta$ on a compact convex physical domain $C$. Let $q$ be positive affine on $C$, let $L$ be continuous, positive and concave there, and set $F_q=q\log(q/L)$. At a source row put $q_s=q(s)$, $V_s=\int L(t)\,dP_{s,u}(t)$, $Q_s=qP_{s,u}/q_s$, and $T_s=LP_{s,u}/V_s$. Then
$$
\int F_q(t)\,dP_{s,u}(t)-F_q(s)
=q_s\left[\log\frac{L(s)}{V_s}+D(Q_s\|T_s)\right]\ge0.
$$
For any prescribed marked pair of endpoint-average marginal $\eta$ with midpoint law $\rho$ and source-to-midpoint martingale from $\nu$, one has $0\le\int F_q\,d(\eta-\rho)\le\int F_q\,d(\eta-\nu)$, and $\int q\,d\nu=\int q\,d\rho=\int q\,d\eta$.

## Proof

The affine martingale identity and positivity make both tilted laws probabilities. Their density ratio is $q(t)V_s/(q_s L(t))$. Expanding its logarithm, multiplying by $q_s$, and subtracting $q_s\log(q_s/L(s))$ from the original row integral gives the displayed identity. Both terms in brackets are nonnegative by concavity of $L$ and positivity of relative entropy. For the pair claim, $F_q$ is convex by the representation $F_q(w)=\sup_{\alpha\in\mathbb R}\{\alpha q(w)-e^{\alpha-1}L(w)\}$, since the displayed supremum is attained at $1+\log(q(w)/L(w))$. Apply Jensen along the source-to-midpoint martingale and within the two-endpoint pair, then use the two corresponding affine equalities for $q$. All integrands are bounded on the compact domain, and the total assertions also hold when the flow is zero.
