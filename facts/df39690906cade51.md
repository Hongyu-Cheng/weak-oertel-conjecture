## Statement

For every integer \(r\geq2\), put
\[
k=2r,\qquad n=2r+1,\qquad M=3^r,
\]
and define
\[
P=\operatorname{conv}\!\left([-1,1]^k\times\{-1\},(0,M)\right)
\subseteq\mathbb R^k\times\mathbb R,
\]
\[
\theta=\frac{3(M-1)}{2M^2+9M+1},\qquad
g(u,t)=1+\theta t,
\]
\[
C=\{(u,t,x):(u,t)\in P,\ 0\leq x\leq g(u,t)\}
\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R.
\]
Let
\[
D=P\cap\mathbb Z^n,\qquad
w_z=\operatorname{vol}_1(C_z)=g(z),\qquad
W=\sum_{z\in D}w_z,\qquad
\pi_z=\frac{w_z}{W},
\]
and let \(z_0=(0,0)\). Write
\[
\tau=
\inf_{(a,b)\neq0}
\sum_{\substack{(u,t)\in D\\a^\top u+bt\geq0}}\pi_{(u,t)}
\]
for the exact marginal closed-halfspace depth at \(z_0\), and put
\(\delta=2^{-n}\).

For \(0<\sigma\leq\tau\), let \(\lambda^\sigma\) maximize
\[
\sum_{z\in D}\pi_z[-\lambda_z\log\lambda_z]
\]
subject to
\[
0\leq\lambda_z\leq1,\qquad
\sum_z\pi_z\lambda_z=\sigma,\qquad
\sum_z\pi_z\lambda_z(z-z_0)=0.
\]
Define
\[
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(E)
=\sum_{z\in D}\lambda^\sigma_z
\operatorname{vol}_1(E_z\cap C_z),
\]
let \(p_\sigma\) be its barycenter, and set
\[
L_r=
\sup_{\sigma\in[\delta,\tau]}
\inf_{\substack{H\text{ closed halfspace}\\p_\sigma\in H}}
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}(H).
\]
Then
\[
\tau=\frac{7M+1}{4M^2+7M+1}=:q_M,
\qquad
\tau\geq\delta,
\]
and
\[
\frac{L_r}{\delta W}
\leq 8\left(\frac49\right)^r
\longrightarrow0.
\]
Consequently there is no absolute \(c>0\) for which (EP) holds for every mixed-integer convex body.

## Proof

Accepted fact `6bbf7eead06542f0` proves that \(C\) is a compact full-dimensional convex body, that \(g>0\) on \(P\), and that
\[
D=
\bigl(\{-1,0,1\}^k\times\{-1\}\bigr)
\mathbin{\dot\cup}
\bigl(\{0\}^k\times\{0,1,\ldots,M\}\bigr).
\tag{1}
\]
It also proves that the \(\pi\)-mean is \(z_0=(0,0)\). In particular, \(z_0\in D\) has positive mass.

Put \(Q=2M^2+9M+1\). The total unnormalized mass of the vertical spine and of the bottom ternary cube are, respectively,
\[
A=\sum_{j=0}^M(1+\theta j)
=\frac{(M+1)(M+2)(7M+1)}{2Q},
\tag{2}
\]
\[
B=M^2(1-\theta)
=\frac{2M^2(M+1)(M+2)}{Q}.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus \(W=A+B\).

We now compute the exact marginal depth. The closed halfspace
\[
\{(u,t):t\geq0\}
\tag{4}
\]
passes through \(z_0\), contains exactly the spine in (1), and has mass \(A\). Hence
\[
\tau\leq\frac{A}{A+B}.
\tag{5}
\]
Conversely, consider any closed halfspace through \(z_0\),
\[
H_{a,b}=\{(u,t):a^\top u+bt\geq0\},
\qquad (a,b)\neq0.
\tag{6}
\]
If \(b\geq0\), then for every spine point \((0,j)\), \(0\leq j\leq M\), one has \(a^\top0+bj\geq0\). Therefore \(H_{a,b}\) contains the whole spine and has mass at least \(A\).

Suppose \(b<0\), and write \(s=-b>0\). On the bottom layer \(t=-1\), condition (6) is
\[
a^\top u+s\geq0,
\quad\text{equivalently}\quad
a^\top u\geq-s.
\tag{7}
\]
The ternary cube \(\{-1,0,1\}^k\) is centrally symmetric and all its points have the same weight \(1-\theta\). For every antipodal pair \(u,-u\), at least one member satisfies (7), since
\[
\max\{a^\top u,-a^\top u\}=|a^\top u|\geq0>-s.
\]
The fixed point \(u=0\) also satisfies (7). Thus (7) contains at least half of the bottom mass, including all boundary mass, and \(H_{a,b}\) has mass at least \(B/2\).

Since \(r\geq2\), one has \(M\geq9\), and (2)--(3) give
\[
\frac B2-A
=\frac{(M+1)(M+2)}{2Q}
\bigl(2M^2-7M-1\bigr)
\geq0.
\tag{8}
\]
Hence every halfspace (6) has mass at least \(A\). Together with (5), this proves
\[
\tau=\frac{A}{A+B}
=\frac{7M+1}{4M^2+7M+1}
=q_M.
\tag{9}
\]

The comparison with the entropy-path lower endpoint is also exact enough for the sequel. For \(M\geq3\),
\[
q_M\geq\frac1M,
\tag{10}
\]
because after cross multiplication (10) is equivalent to
\[
3M^2-6M-1\geq0.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\frac1M=3^{-r}>2^{-(2r+1)}=\delta.
\tag{11}
\]
Thus \(\tau\geq\delta\), so every interval \([\delta,\tau]\) occurring above is nonempty. Also, as proved in `6bbf7eead06542f0`,
\[
\tau=q_M\leq\frac2M.
\tag{12}
\]

Because the \(\pi\)-mean is \(z_0\), the mean-centered conclusion of accepted fact `87013cdfa11d1279` applies. It gives, simultaneously for every \(0<\sigma\leq\tau\),
\[
\lambda^\sigma_z=\sigma\quad(z\in D),\qquad
\nu_{\lambda^\sigma}=\sigma\nu,\qquad
p_\sigma=p,
\tag{13}
\]
where \(\nu\) is the original mixed measure and \(p\) is its barycenter. It also gives
\[
L_r=\tau h_\nu(p),
\qquad
h_\nu(p)=
\inf_{\substack{H\text{ closed halfspace}\\p\in H}}\nu(H).
\tag{14}
\]
The base coordinate of \(p\) is the \(\pi\)-mean \(z_0=(0,0)\). Therefore the ambient vertical closed halfspace
\[
\widehat H=\{(u,t,x):t\geq0\}
\tag{15}
\]
contains \(p\). By (1)--(3), its mixed mass is exactly \(A=\tau W\). Consequently
\[
h_\nu(p)\leq\tau W,
\qquad
L_r\leq\tau^2W.
\tag{16}
\]
Using \(M=3^r\), \(n=2r+1\), and (12), we obtain
\[
\frac{L_r}{\delta W}
\leq
\frac{4M^{-2}}{2^{-(2r+1)}}
=8\left(\frac49\right)^r
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{17}
\]
For every proposed absolute \(c>0\), (17) supplies an \(r\) for which this instance satisfies all hypotheses of (EP) but has \(L_r<c\,2^{-n}W\). Thus (EP) is false.

The argument only controls the entropy-selected barycenters \(p_\sigma\). It does not bound the best depth over all points of \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R)\), so it is not a counterexample to the locked weak Oertel target.
