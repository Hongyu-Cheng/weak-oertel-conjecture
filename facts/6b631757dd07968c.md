## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and use the exact canonical objects and normalizations of accepted facts 23a7bea6c72cc440, 344f13d853731558, and 6b09aae189415c70.  Put
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\rm sel}(C)+M_{\rm off}(C)+R_{\rm cloud}(C)+D,
\tag{1}
\]
and assume the canonical hard \(I\)-dominant inequalities
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad I\ge O.
\tag{2}
\]

Let the load-minimal optimizer have load \(\Omega\), edge loads \(\omega_e\), source loads \(\beta_z\), and source points \(p_z^\flat\).  Define
\[
 P:=\{z:\beta_z>0\},
 \qquad A_P:=\sum_{z\in P}a_z,
 \qquad \rho_P:=\dim\operatorname{aff}P,
\tag{3}
\]
\[
 r_P:=
 \min_{b:\operatorname{aff}P\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{p_z^\flat-b(z):z\in P\},
\tag{4}
\]
and
\[
 x:=
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}}{r_P+1}.
\tag{5}
\]

Let \(s=(s_e)\) be the retained product matching, let
\[
 J:=\{e\in\mathcal E_I\setminus\mathcal B:s_e>0\},
\tag{6}
\]
and define
\[
 \chi_e:=
 \begin{cases}
  \min\{\omega_e,s_e/16\},&e\in J,\\
  0,&e\notin J,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 X:=\sum_e\chi_e>0.
\tag{7}
\]
For \(e=\{u,v\}\) with \(\chi_e>0\), put
\[
 m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2},
 \qquad
 \bar c_e:=\frac{c_u+c_v}{2},
 \qquad
 F_e^\flat:=\frac{p_u^\flat+p_v^\flat}{2},
\tag{8}
\]
where
\[
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}y\,dy,
\]
and let \(\ell_e\) be the actual support-contact midpoint of accepted fact 23a7bea6c72cc440.  Define
\[
 E_\chi:=\{m_e:\chi_e>0\},
 \qquad
 A_\chi:=\operatorname{aff}E_\chi,
 \qquad
 \rho_\chi:=\dim A_\chi.
\tag{9}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\rho_\chi\le\rho_P.}
\tag{10}
\]

Define the three residual ranks over \(A_\chi\) by
\[
 r_{\bar c}:=
 \min_{b:A_\chi\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{\bar c_e-b(m_e):\chi_e>0\},
\tag{11}
\]
\[
 r_\ell:=
 \min_{b:A_\chi\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{\ell_e-b(m_e):\chi_e>0\},
\tag{12}
\]
\[
 r_F:=
 \min_{b:A_\chi\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{F_e^\flat-b(m_e):\chi_e>0\}.
\tag{13}
\]
Let
\[
 \tau_*:=
 \gamma_d\sum_e\chi_e\delta_{(m_e,\ell_e)}
 \mathbin{+}16\gamma_d\sum_e\chi_e\delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)}
\tag{14}
\]
be the total-mass-optimal simultaneous measure of accepted fact 23a7bea6c72cc440, and define its residual rank explicitly by
\[
 r_*:=
 \min_{b:A_\chi\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\left(
 \{\ell_e-b(m_e):\chi_e>0\}
 \cup
 \{F_e^\flat-b(m_e):\chi_e>0\}
 \right).
\tag{15}
\]

For every affine map \(a:A_\chi\to\mathbb R^d\), let
\[
 t_*:=\frac{32\gamma_d}{17}
\]
and define the far-support measure
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \zeta_a^{\rm far}:=t_*\sum_e\chi_e\big(&
 \mathbf1_{\{\|\ell_e-a(m_e)\|_{D_*}\ge1/4\}}
       \delta_{(m_e,\ell_e)}\\
 &+
 \mathbf1_{\{\|F_e^\flat-a(m_e)\|_{D_*}\ge1/4\}}
       \delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)}\big).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]
Let \(\rho_a^{\rm far},r_a^{\rm far}\) be its intrinsic base and continuous residual ranks.  Define the normalized scores
\[
 y_{\bar c}:=
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_\chi}}{r_{\bar c}+1},
 \qquad
 y_\ell:=
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_\chi}}{r_\ell+1},
\tag{17}
\]
\[
 y_F:=
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_\chi}}{r_F+1},
 \qquad
 y_*:=
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_\chi}}{r_*+1},
\tag{18}
\]
\[
 y_a:=
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_a^{\rm far}}}{r_a^{\rm far}+1}.
\tag{19}
\]
In fact, \(\rho_a^{\rm far}=\rho_\chi\) for every \(a\).

Put
\[
 Z_a:=
 y_{\bar c}
 \mathbin{+}\max\left\{
  2\gamma_dy_\ell,\,
  16\gamma_dy_F,\,
  17\gamma_dy_*,\,
  \frac{32\gamma_d}{17}y_a
 \right\}.
\tag{20}
\]
Then, for every affine \(a:A_\chi\to\mathbb R^d\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{2048}{11}x+Z_a<\frac{128}{275}.}
\tag{21}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{2048}{11}\,
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}}{r_P+1}
 \mathbin{+}\frac{2^{n-\rho_\chi}}{r_{\bar c}+1}
 \mathbin{+}\max\left\{
  \frac{2\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_\chi}}{r_\ell+1},
  \frac{16\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_\chi}}{r_F+1},
  \frac{17\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_\chi}}{r_*+1},
  \frac{32\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_a^{\rm far}}}
       {17(r_a^{\rm far}+1)}
 \right\}
 <\frac{128}{275}.}
\tag{22}
\]
In particular, the total-mass-optimal, contact-only, edge-point-only, and far-support specializations are
\[
 \frac{2048}{11}x+y_{\bar c}+17\gamma_dy_*
 <\frac{128}{275},
\tag{23}
\]
\[
 \frac{2048}{11}x+y_{\bar c}+2\gamma_dy_\ell
 <\frac{128}{275},
\tag{24}
\]
\[
 \frac{2048}{11}x+y_{\bar c}+16\gamma_dy_F
 <\frac{128}{275},
\tag{25}
\]
\[
 \frac{2048}{11}x+y_{\bar c}
 +\frac{32\gamma_d}{17}y_a
 <\frac{128}{275}
 \qquad\text{for every affine }a.
\tag{26}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact 344f13d853731558 gives
\[
 R_{\rm sel}(C)\ge
 \Lambda_P:=
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}A_P}{2(r_P+1)}
 =\frac{xA_P}{2}.
\tag{27}
\]
The same accepted source data give
\[
 \sum_z\beta_z=2\Omega,
 \qquad
 \Omega\ge Q-R,
 \qquad
 \beta_z\le\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{28}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 A_P\ge4\sum_{z\in P}\beta_z
 =8\Omega\ge8(Q-R),
\tag{29}
\]
and therefore
\[
 R_{\rm sel}(C)\ge4x(Q-R).
\tag{30}
\]

If \(\chi_e>0\), then \(e\in J\) and \(s_e>0\).  The retained product-matching definition in accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 implies that both endpoints of \(e\) have positive source load, so they lie in \(P\).  Their midpoint \(m_e\) lies in \(\operatorname{aff}P\).  This proves (10), including collisions and lower-dimensional supports.

Accepted fact 23a7bea6c72cc440 gives
\[
 0<\chi_e\le\frac{s_e}{16}
 \qquad(\chi_e>0),
\tag{31}
\]
while accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives \(s_e/16\le r_e\) and makes every positive submeasure of
\[
 \bar\nu_r:=\sum_er_e\delta_{(m_e,\bar c_e)}
\]
visible to \(M_{\rm off}(C)\).  Hence the measure
\[
 \nu_{\bar c,\chi}:=
 \sum_e\chi_e\delta_{(m_e,\bar c_e)}
\]
has mass \(X\), intrinsic ranks \(\rho_\chi,r_{\bar c}\), and satisfies
\[
 M_{\rm off}(C)\ge Xy_{\bar c}.
\tag{32}
\]
This uses one centroid submeasure globally and does not sum fixed-target cloud scores.

The coefficient choices
\[
 (\alpha,\eta)=(2\gamma_d,0),
 \qquad
 (\alpha,\eta)=(0,16\gamma_d),
 \qquad
 (\alpha,\eta)=(\gamma_d,16\gamma_d)
\]
all satisfy the simultaneous-orientation region of accepted fact 23a7bea6c72cc440.  Their halfspace-dominated measures have masses \(2\gamma_dX\), \(16\gamma_dX\), and \(17\gamma_dX\), and intrinsic ranks \((\rho_\chi,r_\ell)\), \((\rho_\chi,r_F)\), and \((\rho_\chi,r_*)\), respectively.  The dominated-measure residual theorem of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 therefore gives
\[
 D\ge X\max\{
 2\gamma_dy_\ell,\,
 16\gamma_dy_F,\,
 17\gamma_dy_*
 \}.
\tag{33}
\]

For a fixed affine \(a\), accepted fact 23a7bea6c72cc440 proves that \(\zeta_a^{\rm far}\) is nonzero, halfspace-dominated, and has mass at least \(t_*X\).  Applying accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 to this one measure gives
\[
 D\ge\frac{32\gamma_d}{17}Xy_a.
\tag{34}
\]
For every \(\chi\)-positive edge, the accepted separation
\[
 \|\ell_e-F_e^\flat\|_{D_*}\ge\frac12
\]
forces at least one of its two atoms to occur in (16).  Thus every base \(m_e\in E_\chi\) occurs in the projection of \(\operatorname{supp}\zeta_a^{\rm far}\).  The reverse inclusion is immediate from (16), so \(\rho_a^{\rm far}=\rho_\chi\).

Combining (33) and (34) by a maximum, not a sum, gives
\[
 D\ge
 X\max\left\{
 2\gamma_dy_\ell,\,
 16\gamma_dy_F,\,
 17\gamma_dy_*,\,
 \frac{32\gamma_d}{17}y_a
 \right\}.
\tag{35}
\]
Equations (30), (32), and (35) charge the three distinct summands \(R_{\rm sel}(C)\), \(M_{\rm off}(C)\), and \(D\), respectively.  Retain also the distinct summands \(R\) and \(G\), and discard only the nonnegative \(R_{\rm cloud}(C)\).  Put
\[
 r_0:=\frac RQ,
 \qquad
 g_0:=\frac GQ,
 \qquad
 q:=\frac XQ.
\tag{36}
\]
Then (1)--(2) imply
\[
 r_0+g_0+4(1-r_0)x+qZ_a<\frac1{100}.
\tag{37}
\]
This is the promised single-use capacity accounting.

Accepted fact 23a7bea6c72cc440 proves the exact correlated overlap bound
\[
 q\ge
 \frac{11}{512}
 -\frac{195}{128}r_0
 -\frac{307}{4096}g_0.
\tag{38}
\]
Substitution into (37) gives
\[
 4x+\frac{11}{512}Z_a
 +r_0\left(1-4x-\frac{195}{128}Z_a\right)
 +g_0\left(1-\frac{307}{4096}Z_a\right)
 <\frac1{100}.
\tag{39}
\]
Set
\[
 A_R:=1-4x-\frac{195}{128}Z_a,
 \qquad
 A_G:=1-\frac{307}{4096}Z_a.
\tag{40}
\]
Their exact difference is
\[
 A_G-A_R
 =4x+
 \left(\frac{195}{128}-\frac{307}{4096}\right)Z_a>0.
\tag{41}
\]

Suppose \(A_R<0\).  Since every capacity in (1) is nonnegative, (2) gives
\[
 r_0+g_0<\frac1{100}.
\tag{42}
\]
Equations (41)--(42), with \(A_R<0\), imply
\[
 r_0A_R+g_0A_G
 \ge(r_0+g_0)A_R
 >\frac1{100}A_R.
\tag{43}
\]
The left side of (39) is therefore strictly larger than
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 4x+\frac{11}{512}Z_a+\frac1{100}A_R
 &=\frac1{100}
   +\frac{99}{25}x
   +\left(\frac{11}{512}
          -\frac{195}{12800}\right)Z_a\\
 &=\frac1{100}
   +\frac{99}{25}x
   +\frac1{160}Z_a
 >\frac1{100},
 \end{aligned}
\tag{44}
\]
contradicting (39).  Hence \(A_R\ge0\).  Equation (41) then gives \(A_G>0\).  The last two terms on the left side of (39) are nonnegative, so
\[
 4x+\frac{11}{512}Z_a<\frac1{100}.
\tag{45}
\]
Multiplying (45) by \(512/11\) yields
\[
 \frac{2048}{11}x+Z_a
 <\frac{512}{1100}
 =\frac{128}{275},
\]
which is (21).  Expanding \(Z_a\) proves (22), and taking each entry of the maximum separately proves (23)--(26).
