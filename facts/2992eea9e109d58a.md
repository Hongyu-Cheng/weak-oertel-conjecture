## Statement

Retain the common actual canonical hard instance and notation of accepted facts 2c7ef442c71a1564, 4aa2d3090ea613b8, 913e5fd1657385d5, and f83d08a46c19ca2f, and assume \(n=3\) and affine target rank two. Thus \(d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^3\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body,
\[
 C_z:=\{u\in\mathbb R^d:(z,u)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^3:a_z>0\},
\]
\[
 W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
 \qquad
 G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 W\geq8Q.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(q^*=(q_e^*)\) be the retained nonzero off-diagonal balanced optimizer. For its integral physical targets put
\[
 x_t:=\sum_{e:m_e=t}q_e^*,
 \qquad
 T_*:=\{t\in\mathbb Z^3:x_t>0\},
 \qquad
 p:=|q^*|=\sum_{t\in T_*}x_t,
\tag{2}
\]
\[
 A:=\operatorname{aff}(T_*),
 \qquad
 \dim A=2,
 \qquad
 D_A:=I_+\cap A,
 \qquad
 W_A:=\sum_{z\in D_A}a_z.
\tag{3}
\]
The accepted quantitative bounds are
\[
 p>\frac{553}{51200}Q,
 \qquad
 W_A\geq8p.
\tag{4}
\]
For clarity about the legacy notation collision, throughout this lemma \(x_t\) is the scalar target load in (2), exactly as in accepted facts 2c7ef442c71a1564 and 4aa2d3090ea613b8; the contact lift denoted \(x_t\) inside accepted fact 913e5fd1657385d5 is not used here.

For \(t\in T_*\), let \(b_t\) and \(q_t\in\mathbb R^d\) be its canonical dominant mass and target center, and put
\[
 D_0:=\frac65K,
\tag{5}
\]
where \(D_0\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) is the full-dimensional compact origin-symmetric convex body of accepted fact 913e5fd1657385d5. Then
\[
 q_t+D_0\subseteq\mathsf M_{x_t}(C_t)
 \qquad(t\in T_*),
\tag{6}
\]
and consequently
\[
 q_t+D_0\subseteq\mathsf M_{y_t}(C_t)
 \qquad(0<y_t\leq x_t).
\tag{7}
\]

For a finite nonempty \(E\subseteq T_*\), positive masses \(0<y_t\leq x_t\), and \(Y:=\sum_{t\in E}y_t\), call
\[
 m\in A\cap\mathbb Z^3\cap\operatorname{conv}E
\tag{8}
\]
an integral relative \(1/4\)-center if every proper relative closed halfspace \(J_A\subseteq A\) containing \(m\) satisfies
\[
 \sum_{t\in E\cap J_A}y_t\geq\frac Y4.
\tag{9}
\]
Such a center exists for every nonzero profile \((y_t)_{t\in E}\). For any such \(m\), put
\[
 \Lambda_m(E):=
 \left\{
 \lambda\in\mathbb R_+^E:
 \sum_{t\in E}\lambda_t=1,
 \quad
 \sum_{t\in E}\lambda_tt=m
 \right\},
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 \mathcal Q_m(E):=
 \left\{
 \sum_{t\in E}\lambda_tq_t:
 \lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)
 \right\}.
\tag{11}
\]
Writing
\[
 \|u\|_{D_0}:=\inf\{r\geq0:u\in rD_0\},
\tag{12}
\]
define the \(D_0\)-Chebyshev radius of all barycentric target-center sums at \(m\) by
\[
 \rho_{D_0}(E,m):=
 \min_{z\in\mathbb R^d}
 \max_{\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)}
 \left\|z-\sum_{t\in E}\lambda_tq_t\right\|_{D_0}.
\tag{13}
\]
Equivalently, \(\rho_{D_0}(E,m)\leq1\) means that there is one \(z\in\mathbb R^d\) such that
\[
 \left\|z-\sum_{t\in E}\lambda_tq_t\right\|_{D_0}\leq1
 \quad\text{for every }\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E).
\tag{14}
\]

Each of the following conditions separately implies
\[
 \boxed{G\geq\frac{512}{553}p>\frac Q{100}:}
\tag{15}
\]

1. \(W_A\geq8192p/553\).

2. There exist a finite nonempty \(E\subseteq T_*\), masses \(0<y_t\leq x_t\), and an integral relative \(1/4\)-center \(m\) satisfying
\[
 Y:=\sum_{t\in E}y_t\geq\frac{256}{553}p,
 \qquad
 \rho_{D_0}(E,m)\leq1.
\tag{16}
\]

Thus either condition contradicts the hard inequality in (1). No claim is made that conditions 1 and 2 are exhaustive or that one of them always holds.

## Proof

We first prove the owned-tube assertions (6)-(7), using only accepted predecessors. The off-diagonal provenance construction in accepted fact f83d08a46c19ca2f and the collision-preserving target accounting in accepted fact 4aa2d3090ea613b8 identify every \(t\in T_*\) with its retained dominant target cell and give
\[
 0<x_t\leq\frac{b_t}{2}\leq\frac{a_t}{8}.
\tag{17}
\]
Accepted fact 913e5fd1657385d5, on the same canonical common-normal instance, gives
\[
 q_t+D_0\subseteq\mathsf M_{b_t}(C_t).
\tag{18}
\]
The metronoid nesting proved in accepted fact 107b9182522633f9 says
\[
 \mathsf M_r(K)\subseteq\mathsf M_s(K)
 \qquad(0<s\leq r\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(K)).
\tag{19}
\]
Applying (19) first with \(r=b_t,s=x_t\), and then with \(r=x_t,s=y_t\), proves (6) and (7). Equation (17) also shows \(T_*\subseteq I_+\) and \(0<y_t\leq a_t\).

We next justify the planar-center assertion and fix its exact relative meaning. Choose \(b\in T_*\). As in the saturated intrinsic chart used in accepted fact bd7eeefd1636ff7d, put
\[
 \Gamma:=(A-b)\cap\mathbb Z^3
\]
and choose a linear isomorphism \(U:\mathbb R^2\to A-b\) with \(U\mathbb Z^2=\Gamma\). For a nonempty \(E\subseteq T_*\), push the measure \(\sum_{t\in E}y_t\delta_t\) back under \(u\mapsto b+Uu\). Accepted fact 6d6fcf377e68c79c, applied to this nonzero finite measure on \(\mathbb Z^2\), gives \(m_0\in\mathbb Z^2\) for which every containing planar closed halfspace has mass at least \(Y/4\). Strict separation from the finite support shows \(m_0\in\operatorname{conv}(U^{-1}(E-b))\). Therefore \(m:=b+Um_0\) satisfies (8)-(9), because the affine lattice chart bijects planar halfspaces with proper relative halfspaces of \(A\). This proves existence. Condition 2 requires, in addition, that one such center satisfy (16).

Accepted fact cfa5857cb649438e, in the present \(n=3\), rank-two setting, gives
\[
 G\geq\frac{W_A}{16}.
\tag{20}
\]
If condition 1 holds, (20) immediately yields
\[
 G\geq\frac1{16}\frac{8192}{553}p
 =\frac{512}{553}p
 >\frac{512}{553}\frac{553}{51200}Q
 =\frac Q{100},
\]
where the strict inequality is (4). This proves (15) under condition 1.

Assume condition 2. The set \(\Lambda_m(E)\) is a nonempty compact polytope, so \(\mathcal Q_m(E)\) is nonempty and compact. Since \(D_0\) is a full-dimensional compact origin-symmetric convex body, (12) is a norm. Hence the continuous function
\[
 z\longmapsto
 \max_{u\in\mathcal Q_m(E)}\|z-u\|_{D_0}
\]
is coercive and attains its minimum. Choose a minimizer \(z_0\). By (14), for every \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)\),
\[
 z_0\in\sum_{t\in E}\lambda_tq_t+D_0.
\tag{21}
\]
Because \(D_0\) is convex and \(\sum_t\lambda_t=1\),
\[
 \sum_{t\in E}\lambda_t(q_t+D_0)
 =\sum_{t\in E}\lambda_tq_t+D_0.
\tag{22}
\]
Combining (7), (21), and (22), for every \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)\) one has
\[
 z_0
 \in\sum_{t\in E}\lambda_t(q_t+D_0)
 \subseteq
 \sum_{t\in E}\lambda_t\mathsf M_{y_t}(C_t).
\tag{23}
\]
The same point \(z_0\) works for every barycentric representation. Thus
\[
 z_0\in
 \bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)}
 \sum_{t\in E}\lambda_t\mathsf M_{y_t}(C_t),
\tag{24}
\]
so this complete metronoid core is nonempty.

We audit every requirement of the relative tuple. Equations (2)-(3) and (17) give \(E\subseteq T_*\subseteq I_+\cap A\) and \(0<y_t\leq a_t\). Equation (8) makes \(m\) integral and puts it in \(\operatorname{conv}E\). Equation (9) is relative \(1/4\)-balance, and (24) is the complete-core condition over every element of \(\Lambda_m(E)\). Hence \((E,(y_t)_{t\in E},m)\) is a legal relative complete-core tuple on the proper affine plane \(A\subsetneq\mathbb R^3\), of total mass \(Y\).

Apply the support-free complete-capacity augmentation in accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb with \(n=3\) and \(\beta=1/4\). It gives
\[
 G\geq
 \min\left\{
 Y+\frac{W-W_A}{2},
 2^3\frac14Y
 \right\}
 =
 \min\left\{
 Y+\frac{W-W_A}{2},
 2Y
 \right\}.
\tag{25}
\]
It remains to identify which entry can lie below \(G\). Equation (20) and the hard inequality in (1) imply
\[
 W_A\leq16G<\frac4{25}Q.
\tag{26}
\]
Together with \(W\geq8Q\), this gives
\[
 Y+\frac{W-W_A}{2}
 >\frac12\left(8-\frac4{25}\right)Q
 =\frac{98}{25}Q
 >G.
\tag{27}
\]
Therefore the first entry in (25) cannot be the minimum bounded above by \(G\), and (25) forces
\[
 G\geq2Y.
\tag{28}
\]
Finally, (4), (16), and (28) give
\[
 G\geq2Y
 \geq\frac{512}{553}p
 >\frac{512}{553}\frac{553}{51200}Q
 =\frac Q{100}.
\]
This proves (15) under condition 2 and completes both conditional closures. Nothing in the proof establishes that condition 1 or condition 2 must occur.
