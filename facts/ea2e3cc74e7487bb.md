## Statement

Let $A,K,B$ be planar convex bodies with $K$ a triangle. Let $y\in\operatorname{int}K$ and let $v_1,v_2,v_3$ be nonzero spanning normals with $\sum_i v_i=0$. The three middle caps $v_i\cdot(x-y)\ge0$ strictly capture distinct vertices $P_i$ of $K$ and strictly exclude the other two vertices. Put $k=|K|$, $a=|A|$, $\beta=|B|/k$, $h_i=h_B(v_i)-v_i\cdot y$, $W=\sum_i h_i$, $D=|\det(v_1,v_2)|$, $H=\sum_i v_i\cdot(P_i-y)$, and $R=8Dk/H^2$. Let $q_i$ be the middle cap fractions, $f_i=2v_i\cdot(P_i-y)/(H\sqrt{q_i})$, and $U_i$ the area of the $A$ cap $v_i\cdot(x-y)\ge-h_i$. Denote the lengths of the cutting chords in $A$ and $K$, divided by $\lVert v_i\rVert$, by $\sigma_{A,i}$ and $\sigma_{K,i}$.

The support triangle $\{x:v_i\cdot(x-y)\le h_i \text{ for every }i\}$ contains $B$, has area $W^2/(2D)$, and gives $H/W\le2/\sqrt{\beta R}$. For an index $i$ with a proper $A$ cut and a nonempty residual corner $A\cap\bigcap_{j\ne i}\{x:v_j\cdot(x-y)<-h_j\}$, put $T_i=|A\cap\{x:v_i\cdot(x-y)\ge W-h_i\}|$ and $\delta_i=(a-U_j-U_k)_+$, with the other indices $j,k$. Then
$$
\frac{\sigma_{A,i}}{\sigma_{K,i}}
\le \frac{f_i}{\sqrt{q_i}\sqrt{\beta R}}\frac{U_i}{k}
       \left(1-\sqrt{T_i/U_i}\right)
\le \frac{f_i}{\sqrt{q_i}\sqrt{\beta R}}\frac{U_i}{k}
       \left(1-\sqrt{\delta_i/U_i}\right).
$$
If all budgets $U_i+kq_i=Q$ agree, these terms satisfy $U_i/k=Q/k-q_i$ and $\delta_i/k=(a/k-(Q/k-q_j)-(Q/k-q_k))_+$.

## Proof

In coordinates $(s,t)=(v_1\cdot(x-y),v_2\cdot(x-y))$ the support triangle becomes $s\le h_1$, $t\le h_2$, $s+t\ge-h_3$. The coordinates have area factor $D>0$. Since $W>0$ by the strict support gaps of an interior point of $B$, this is a right triangle with both legs of length $W$. Containment follows from the definitions of the supports, and $|B|\le W^2/(2D)$ gives $\beta R\le4W^2/H^2$.

The middle cap is a corner triangle whose altitude is $v_i\cdot(P_i-y)/\lVert v_i\rVert$. Its area is $kq_i=\sigma_{K,i}Hf_i\sqrt{q_i}/4$. Thus $\sigma_{K,i}=4k\sqrt{q_i}/(Hf_i)$. The shifted-tail chord lemma applies to this same $A,B,y$ and these same normals, with the stated proper cut and present corner. Divide its bounds by $\sigma_{K,i}$ and use the bound on $H/W$ to obtain both inequalities. The common-budget identities follow by substitution in $U_i$ and $\delta_i$.
