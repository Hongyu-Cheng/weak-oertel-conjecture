## Statement

Let \(d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\) and \(k\in\mathbb Z\) with \(k\geq3\). Let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and for every real \(x\) in its base projection write
\[
 C_x:=\{y\in\mathbb R^d:(x,y)\in C\},
 \qquad a_x:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_x).
\]
Assume that, for some \(a>0\),
\[
 a_z=a\qquad(z=0,1,\ldots,2k-1).
\tag{1}
\]
Then every fiber \(C_x\), \(0\leq x\leq2k-1\), has volume \(a\), all these fibers are translates of one another, and their centroids are the restriction of one affine map: there are a \(d\)-dimensional convex body \(K_0\subseteq\mathbb R^d\), \(u,b\in\mathbb R^d\), such that
\[
 C_x=K_0+ux+b
 \qquad(0\leq x\leq2k-1).
\tag{2}
\]

Let \(G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)\) be the exact complete metronoid-core capacity defined in accepted fact ae0161dd5d4ea672. Then
\[
 \boxed{G\geq2ka.}
\tag{3}
\]
Consequently, for any positive scalar \(Q\) satisfying
\[
 Q=\frac{k-1}{4}a,
\tag{4}
\]
one has
\[
 \boxed{G\geq\frac{8k}{k-1}Q>8Q,}
\tag{5}
\]
so the inequality \(G<Q/100\) is impossible. In particular, this applies to the exact \(k=1024\) two-parity uniform-reservoir calibration, whose formal reservoir mass is \(Q=1023a/4\).

## Proof

Apply accepted fact 4a0b86dc432ab3e1 with base dimension one and its parameter \(K=k\). The even fibers in its hypothesis are
\[
 C_{2j},\qquad j=0,\ldots,k-1,
\]
and all have volume \(a\) by (1). The accepted fact therefore gives
\[
 a_x=a\quad(0\leq x\leq2k-2),
\tag{6}
\]
all fibers in this interval are translates, and their centroid field is affine on this interval.

Now translate only the base coordinate by one and define the convex body
\[
 \widetilde C:=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^d:(x+1,y)\in C\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Its even fiber at \(2j\) is \(C_{2j+1}\), which again has volume \(a\) for every \(j=0,\ldots,k-1\). A second application of accepted fact 4a0b86dc432ab3e1 gives
\[
 a_x=a\quad(1\leq x\leq2k-1),
\tag{8}
\]
all fibers in this second interval are translates, and their centroid field is affine there.

The two centroid affine maps agree on the nondegenerate overlap \([1,2k-2]\), because both equal the actual centroid of \(C_x\) at every point of that overlap. Hence they are restrictions of one affine map
\[
 c_x=ux+b\qquad(0\leq x\leq2k-1).
\tag{9}
\]
Every fiber in the first interval is a translate of \(C_1\), and every fiber in the second interval is also a translate of \(C_1\). Thus all fibers in the union are translates of \(C_1\). Translation covariance of the centroid makes the translation from \(C_1\) to \(C_x\) equal to \(c_x-c_1\). With \(K_0:=C_1-c_1\), equation (9) gives (2), while (6) and (8) give the volume assertion including both endpoints.

It remains to audit one certificate in the exact definition of \(G\). Put
\[
 E:=\{0,1,\ldots,2k-1\},
 \qquad t_z:=a\quad(z\in E),
 \qquad m:=k-1.
\tag{10}
\]
Then \(E\) is a nonempty subset of the positive-volume lattice fibers, \(0<t_z\leq a_z\) for every \(z\in E\), and
\[
 T:=\sum_{z\in E}t_z=2ka.
\tag{11}
\]
The point \(m\) is an integer in \(E\subseteq\operatorname{conv}E\). Every proper closed halfspace \(J\subseteq\mathbb R\) containing \(m\) is a closed ray. If it points to the left, it contains \(0,1,\ldots,k-1\), while if it points to the right, it contains \(k-1,k,\ldots,2k-1\). Therefore, in both cases,
\[
 \sum_{z\in E\cap J}t_z\geq ka=\frac12T=2^{-1}T.
\tag{12}
\]
This verifies the exact base-balance quantifier for base dimension one, including both ray orientations and the chosen integer point.

For each \(z\in E\), the full-mass metronoid in the notation of accepted fact ae0161dd5d4ea672 is the centroid singleton
\[
 \mathsf M_a(C_z)=\{c_z\}.
\tag{13}
\]
Indeed, an admissible density \(0\leq f\leq1\) on \(C_z\) with integral \(a=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)\) satisfies \(f=1\) almost everywhere. Let
\[
 \Lambda_m(E):=
 \left\{\lambda\in\mathbb R_+^E:
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z=1,
 \ \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_zz=m\right\}.
\]
For every \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)\), equation (9) gives
\[
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_zc_z
 =u\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_zz
  +b\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z
 =um+b
 =c_m.
\tag{14}
\]
Using (13), every barycentric Minkowski sum in the complete core is therefore the same singleton:
\[
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z\mathsf M_a(C_z)=\{c_m\}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)}
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z\mathsf M_a(C_z)
 =\{c_m\}\ne\varnothing.
\tag{15}
\]
Equations (10), (12), and (15) verify every support, positivity, integer-center, all-halfspaces, and all-barycentric-representations condition in the complete-core capacity of accepted fact ae0161dd5d4ea672. The admissible selected mass is \(T=2ka\), so the defining supremum gives (3).

Finally, if (4) holds, then
\[
 2ka=\frac{8k}{k-1}Q>8Q,
\]
because \(k/(k-1)>1\). Combining this identity with (3) proves (5), and \(8Q>Q/100\) because \(Q>0\). For \(k=1024\), equation (4) is exactly \(Q=1023a/4\). This completes the claimed genuine-body exclusion.
