## Statement

Let $d\ge1$, $N=d+1$, and use original sampled mixed $d$-volume $dm$ of mass $M>0$ on a compact set of positive compatible fibers. Let $L$ be positive continuous and log-normalized by $\int\log L\,dm/M=0$. At finitely many own-interior marks $Y_a=(p_a,y_a)$ set $L_a=L(Y_a)$, $E_a=E_{K_{p_a}}(y_a)$, and for $\varepsilon>0$ take $\delta_a=\varepsilon(L_aE_a/M)^{1/N}$. Given positive coefficients $\kappa_a$, put $K=\sum_a\kappa_a$, $\Delta=\sum_a\kappa_a\delta_a$, $w_a=\kappa_a/K$ and $\sigma_a=\kappa_a\delta_a/\Delta$. Assume the same-coefficient probability rows $\rho_a$ satisfy $\int L\,d\rho_a=L_a$ and $\sum_a(\sigma_a/L_a)\rho_a=dm/(ML)$. Define $\bar\rho_a=(L/L_a)\rho_a$ and $K_V=\sum_a\sigma_a D(\bar\rho_a\|\rho_a)$. Then
$$
I:=\log(\varepsilon K/\Delta)=D(\sigma\|w)+\frac{K_V+\sum_a\sigma_a\log(M/E_a)}{N}\ge0.
$$
In particular $0<\Delta\le\varepsilon K$, $\Delta I\le\varepsilon K/e$, $\Delta D(\sigma\|w)\le\varepsilon K/e$, and $\Delta K_V\le N\varepsilon K/e$.

For a joint separator with effective coareas bounded above by $S_{\max}$, these upper bounds hold with $K$ replaced by $S_{\max}$ if its own coefficients supply the stated probability rows and $\Delta>0$. If the separator has total proper weight one and its coareas are also at least $S_{\min}>0$, then $K\ge S_{\min}$.

## Proof

The energy satisfies $0<E_a\le |K_{p_a}|\le M$ by d90ba05577427854. In particular $\Delta>0$, and both probability vectors are positive on the same contact occurrences. The hypotheses of e9fc678ed90ba6c3 hold for $L$ and the rows. The original-volume mixture is $\sum_a\sigma_a\bar\rho_a=dm/M$, and its entropy identity gives $K_V=-\sum_a\sigma_a\log L_a\ge0$. By the definitions of the scalar displacement and the two weights,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_a\sigma_a\log\delta_a
&=\log\varepsilon-\frac{K_V+\sum_a\sigma_a\log(M/E_a)}{N},\\
D(\sigma\|w)&=\sum_a\sigma_a\log\delta_a-\log(\Delta/K).
\end{aligned}
$$
Subtracting proves the identity. Both relative entropies and every $\log(M/E_a)$ are nonnegative. Since $\Delta=\varepsilon K e^{-I}$ and $I e^{-I}\le 1/e$ for $I\ge0$, each scaled estimate follows.

For the physical application, let $\omega_{ij}$ and $\kappa_{ia}$ be the common weights of 91ad59d2662839ca. Its relation $\omega_{ij}T_{ij}=(\sum_a\kappa_{ia})\rho_{ij}$ and $\sum_j\rho_{ij}=1$ give $K=\sum_{ij}\omega_{ij}T_{ij}$. The sum of the proper weights is at most one, and equals one if atomic weights vanish. This gives the two asserted bounds on $K$ under their respective assumptions. In particular the upper bound uses no positive coarea floor. For the rows, ff01846e056fd4d4 yields a martingale from masses $e_{ia}/L_a$ with $e_{ia}=\kappa_{ia}\delta_a$ to the target $(\Delta/M)dm/L$, whenever its stated same-separator parameter test and positivity hypotheses hold.
