## Statement

Let $\Pi(ds,dt)=\nu(ds)P_s(dt)$ be a finite physical martingale on a compact convex set $C$ with target $\eta$, and let $L=\min_{1\le i\le N}A_i$ be the minimum of finitely many affine functions on $C$. Choose the least active index $i(s)$ at each source. Then
$
\int L\,d\nu-\int L\,d\eta
=\int[A_{i(s)}(t)-L(t)]\,d\Pi(s,t)\ge0.
$
Equality holds exactly when for $\nu$-almost every source $s$, its row $P_s$ is concentrated in every closed contact cell $\{A_i=L\}$ active at $s$. If $\nu=(W/\beta)\eta$ with $\beta>0$, the dissipation equals $\beta^{-1}\int L(W-\beta)\,d\eta$. These assertions apply separately under any conditioning for which the conditional marginals and martingale row identity hold.

## Proof

The least-active selector is Borel, since the finite family and its minimum are continuous. Affine barycenter preservation gives $\int A_i(t)\,dP_s(t)=A_i(s)$ for every $i$ simultaneously outside one null set. For any active $i$ at $s$, subtracting $\int L(t)\,dP_s(t)$ yields the row gap
$
L(s)-\int L(t)\,dP_s(t)=\int[A_i(t)-L(t)]\,dP_s(t).
$
Its integrand is nonnegative and vanishes exactly in the closed cell $\{A_i=L\}$. Integrating with the least active selector gives the formula. A zero integral forces zero row gap almost everywhere, and the row identity for each active plane gives the simultaneous contact-cell criterion. The expression with $W$ is direct substitution of the source marginal. The same argument with conditional marginals gives its conditional form.
