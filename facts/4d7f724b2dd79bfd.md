## Statement

Let $A,C\subset\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge1$, be full-dimensional compact convex bodies centrally symmetric about zero. Give their layers at $0,1$ their ordinary unnormalized $d$-volumes. The raw closed ambient halfspace depth at the real middle point $(1/2,0)$ is at least $(\min\{|A|,|C|\}+|A\cap C|)/2$.

## Proof

Write $J=|A\cap C|$ and move a containing boundary parallel through the point. For a nonvertical cap the traces on the two layers are $u\cdot x\ge t$ and $u\cdot x\ge-t$ for one nonzero $u$. On $A\cap C$ the two masses sum to $J$, by reflection in zero and nullity of a continuous hyperplane. The two residual measures on $A\setminus C$ and $C\setminus A$ are nonnegative and symmetric. At least one trace threshold is nonpositive, so that trace takes at least half its residual mass, at least $(\min\{|A|,|C|\}-J)/2$. Adding the common contribution proves the bound for all nonvertical caps. A pure base cap through $1/2$ takes one full endpoint and hence has mass at least $\min\{|A|,|C|\}$. This also pays the stated bound.
