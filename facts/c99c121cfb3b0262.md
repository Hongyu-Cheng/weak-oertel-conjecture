## Statement

Let $C$ be a compact convex set in a finite-dimensional affine space and let $dm$ be a finite positive measure supported on a compact $S\subset C$, with mass $M>0$. Let $L>0$ be continuous and concave on $C$, with $\int_S\log L\,dm/M=0$. Let $Y_1,\ldots,Y_s\in C$ with $s\ge1$, and put $L_i=L(Y_i)$. Define
$$
\mathcal T_L=\{h\in C(C): h+cL\text{ is concave for some }c\ge0\},\qquad
B_i(h)=h(Y_i)/L_i-\int_S h/L\,dm/M.
$$
Assume no $h\in\mathcal T_L$ has $B_i(h)<0$ for all $i$. Then there are $\sigma_i\ge0$ summing to one, with $\mu=\sum_i(\sigma_i/L_i)\delta_{Y_i}$ and $d\nu=dm/(ML)$ of equal mass and full first moment. For every continuous convex $\phi$ on $C$, $\int\phi\,d\mu\le\int\phi\,d\nu$, and $\int L\,d\mu=\int L\,d\nu=1$. For positive indices there are measurable allocations $q_i\ge0$ on $S$, with $\sum_iq_i=1$ $\nu$-almost everywhere, satisfying
$$
\int q_i\,d\nu=\sigma_i/L_i,\qquad
\int wq_i\,d\nu=(\sigma_i/L_i)Y_i,\qquad \int Lq_i\,d\nu=\sigma_i.
$$
For every affine majorant $A\ge L$ on $C$ touching $L$ at $Y_i$, $A=L$ $q_i\nu$-almost everywhere.

## Proof

The cone $\mathcal T_L$ is exactly the set of continuous directions such that $L+th$ is positive concave for all sufficiently small $t\ge0$. For the forward direction write it as $(1-ct)L+t(h+cL)$ and use the positive minimum of $L$ and boundedness of $h$. A single feasible $t>0$ gives the converse with $c=1/t$. Nonnegative linear combinations of the individual $c$ give the cone property. It contains all continuous concave functions, both signs of every affine function, and both signs of $L$. Uniform Taylor expansion using $\min_C L>0$ gives the normalized path
$$
(L+th)\exp\!\left[-\int_S\log(L+th)\,\frac{dm}{M}\right]
=L+t\left[h-L\int_S\frac{h}{L}\,\frac{dm}{M}\right]+O(t^2).
$$

The linear image $B(\mathcal T_L)$ is a convex cone disjoint from the open negative orthant. Separate the two convex sets using the openness of the orthant. Scaling in the cones forces separation at level zero, and boundedness above on the orthant forces each component of the nonzero separating vector to be nonnegative. Normalize it to $\sigma$ with sum one. The resulting inequality $\sum_i\sigma_i B_i(h)\ge0$ gives equal mass and full first moment by testing both affine signs. Taking $h=-\phi$ gives the displayed convex order. The two $L$-integrals are one by definition.

Identify the affine ambient space with Euclidean space. The convex order here includes the restrictions of all globally finite continuous convex functions, so the compact coupling lemma ac53fe19fef9ec25 applies to $\mu,\nu$. Split at its finitely many source atoms, subdividing a repeated-source row in proportion to its occurrence masses. Each row measure is dominated by $\nu$, hence has a density $q_i$ with the required zeroth and first moments and with the densities summing to one. Rowwise concave Jensen for $L$ bounds $\int Lq_i\,d\nu\le(\sigma_i/L_i)L_i$. Since the sums on both sides are one, every bound is equality. For a touching affine majorant the barycenter equation gives $\int (A-L)q_i\,d\nu=0$, proving contact.
