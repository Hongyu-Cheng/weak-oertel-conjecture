## Statement

Let a family of finitely many positive $d$-dimensional fibers, $d\ge1$, vary continuously in the Hausdorff metric at one fixed base, with every other fiber and a closed ambient cap fixed. If the cap contains the initial selected fiber with a strict margin, its total mixed mass increment for all sufficiently small changes is exactly the selected fiber's $d$-volume increment, which is also the total mixed mass increment. Thus no such change can increase total mixed mass while keeping that cap's budget nonincreasing.

## Proof

Write the cap inequality as $\ell(z,x)\ge0$. On the compact selected fiber the minimum of $\ell$ is positive. The continuous affine functional is uniformly Lipschitz in $x$ (or constant on that base), so the nearby entire fiber still belongs to the cap. Every other cap contribution is fixed. Subtraction of the mixed mass sums gives the two exact equalities and the obstruction, even if the first derivative of the selected volume is zero.
