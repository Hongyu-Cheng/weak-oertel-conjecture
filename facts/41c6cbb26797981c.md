## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}a_z>0.
\]
Let
\[
 h_{\max}(C):=
 \sup_{p\in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)}
 \inf_{\substack{H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\text{ closed halfspace}\\p\in H}}
 \sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z),
 \qquad
 H_z:=\{x:(z,x)\in H\},
\]
and define
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
where \(G\) is the complete metronoid-core capacity of accepted fact `3d1f63abf09b2abb`.

Then the following two assertions hold.

1. If \(G\ge W/8\), then
\[
 \boxed{
 h_{\max}(C)>
 \frac{3}{64000}\,2^{-n}W.}
\tag{1}
\]

2. Suppose \(G<W/8\). Use the half-strength canonical product reservoir \(r\), its mass \(Q:=|r|\), its affinely realizable submatching capacity \(R:=R_C(r)\), and the nonnegative capacities \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), and \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\), all with exactly the definitions and normalizations of accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`. Put
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\tag{2}
\]
If
\[
 \mathcal A(C)\ge\frac Q{100},
\tag{3}
\]
then (1) holds.

Consequently, every positive-mass \(C\) satisfying
\[
 G\ge\frac W8
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \left(G<\frac W8\ \text{ and }\ \mathcal A(C)\ge\frac Q{100}\right)
\]
has a mixed-integer point of halfspace depth strictly greater than
\((3/64000)2^{-n}W\). No assertion is made here that (3) holds for every \(C\) in the second branch.

## Proof

We first record a numerical inequality used in both branches. By the binomial theorem,
\[
 \frac1{\gamma_d}
 =\left(1+\frac1d\right)^d
 =\sum_{k=0}^d\binom dk d^{-k}
 \le\sum_{k=0}^d\frac1{k!}<3.
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, \(\binom dk d^{-k}\le1/k!\), and
\(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}1/k!<3\), since
\(1/k!\le2^{-(k-1)}\) for \(k\ge2\), with strict inequality for some \(k\). Thus
\[
 \gamma_d>\frac13.
\tag{5}
\]

Accepted fact `3d1f63abf09b2abb` gives the complete-core depth implication
\[
 h_{\max}(C)\ge\gamma_d2^{-n}G,
\]
or equivalently
\[
 D\ge\gamma_dG.
\tag{6}
\]
For completeness, its capacity is defined as a supremum; the accepted depth implication holds for every admissible core mass, and taking the supremum gives (6).

If \(G\ge W/8\), equations (5)--(6) give
\[
 D\ge\gamma_dG>\frac G3\ge\frac W{24}>\frac{3W}{64000}.
\]
Dividing by \(2^n\) proves (1).

Assume now that \(G<W/8\). In particular \(G<W/4\), so all conclusions of accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` apply. Its canonical reservoir satisfies
\[
 Q\ge\frac3{32}(W-4G)
 >\frac3{32}\left(W-\frac W2\right)
 =\frac{3W}{64}>0.
\tag{7}
\]
The same accepted fact gives the three separate scalar depth normalizations
\[
 D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C),
 \qquad
 D\ge M_{\mathrm{off}}(C),
 \qquad
 D\ge R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C).
\tag{8}
\]

We next bound the remaining summand \(R\). By the exact definition in accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`, \(R=R_C(r)\) is the maximum mass of an affinely half-metronoid-realizable submatching of its reservoir \(r\). Every such submatching is an admissible affinely half-metronoid-realizable matching in the definition of \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\) in accepted fact `8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5`. Therefore
\[
 R\le\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C).
\tag{9}
\]
The affine matching certificate in that accepted fact gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)\ge\gamma_d2^{-n}\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C),
\]
so (9) implies
\[
 D\ge\gamma_dR.
\tag{10}
\]

Suppose (3) holds. If \(D=0\), equations (6), (8), and (10), together with nonnegativity of all six summands in (2), force
\[
 R=G=R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)=M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 =R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)=D=0.
\]
This would give \(\mathcal A(C)=0\), contradicting (3) and \(Q>0\). Hence
\[
 D>0.
\tag{11}
\]

Equations (4), (6), (10), and (11) yield the strict scalar bounds
\[
 R\le\frac D{\gamma_d}<3D,
 \qquad
 G\le\frac D{\gamma_d}<3D.
\tag{12}
\]
Applying (8) term by term in the literal sum (2), and retaining its final summand \(D\), gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal A(C)
 &=R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
   +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D\\
 &<3D+3D+D+D+D+D
 =10D.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
This is only an upper bound on six separate nonnegative numbers by the same scalar \(D\). It neither adds their depth witnesses nor assumes that any of those witnesses coincide, so there is no double counting of a depth certificate.

Combining (3), (7), and (13) gives
\[
 D>\frac{\mathcal A(C)}{10}
 \ge\frac Q{1000}
 >\frac{3W}{64000}.
\tag{14}
\]
Since \(D=2^nh_{\max}(C)\), division by \(2^n\) proves (1) in the second branch. This proves every asserted implication and nowhere assumes that (3) is universal.
