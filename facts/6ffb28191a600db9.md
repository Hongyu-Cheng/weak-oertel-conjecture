## Statement

Assume the all-proper affine-only balance of the weighted regular singleton joint continuation with penalty coefficient $\varepsilon^2>0$. Write $a=\Delta/(NM)>0$, $\kappa=a+\varepsilon^2 P_{\rm ar}$, $Z=\sum_z w_z/\ell_z$, $g_{\rm tilt}=\sum_z w_z z/(Z\ell_z)$, $g_{\rm vol}=\sum_z w_z z$, and $\nu_0=\sum_i(\eta_i\delta_i/(NM))\delta_{p_i}$. The measures $\nu_0/\ell+\varepsilon^2 w$ and $\kappa w/\ell$ have identical mass and first moment. The barycenter $h$ of $\nu_0/\ell$ belongs to the convex hull of the positively weighted active bases and is
$$
h=\frac{\kappa Zg_{\rm tilt}-\varepsilon^2 g_{\rm vol}}{\kappa Z-\varepsilon^2}.
$$
For nonconstant sampled $\ell$, $\ell(h)=a/(\kappa Z-\varepsilon^2)<1/Z<1<P_{\rm ar}$, so some positively weighted active base has $\ell<1$. For constant $\ell$ one has $\ell=1$ and $h=g_{\rm vol}$.

## Proof

Divide the affine equality in the common separator by $NM$ and test the constant and each coordinate to obtain the moments. The mass $\kappa Z-\varepsilon^2$ is positive because it equals that of $\nu_0/\ell$ and $\Delta>0$. Taking first moments gives the formula and the support gives the hull inclusion. Since $\ell$ is affine, its value at this barycenter equals its mean under the normalized $\nu_0/\ell$, which is $a/(\kappa Z-\varepsilon^2)$. Strict Jensen for the normalized log implies $P_{\rm ar}>1$ and $Z>1$ for nonconstant $\ell$; the first strict comparison is equivalent to $\varepsilon^2(P_{\rm ar}Z-1)>0$. As an affine weighted mean below one, it forces at least one positive source value below one. For constant normalized $\ell$, substitute $\ell=1$ directly.
