## Statement

In ordinary dimension $d\ge2$, let $K$ be a polytope and let proper caps $K_j=K\cap H_j$ of common volume fraction $\beta$ have no cap/body facet coincidences. Suppose $\lambda_j\ge0$, $\sum_j\lambda_j=1/\beta$, and the weighted sum of the retained old facet areas matches the original area on each old facet. For cut facet areas $S_j$ with outward unit normals $-u_j$ and any convex body $L$, one has
$$
\sum_j\lambda_j V(K_j[d-1],L)=V(K[d-1],L)+\frac1d\sum_j\lambda_jS_jh_L(-u_j).
$$
This implies $\beta^{-1/d}|K|\le |K|+\frac1d\sum_j\lambda_jS_jh_K(-u_j)$. The proposed upper bound $\sum_j\lambda_jS_jh_K(-u_j)\le |K|$ fails even with a fully realized centroidal circuit and all signed facet moments zero.

## Proof

The polytope formula for the first mixed volume sums support values at the outer unit facet normals weighted by their facet areas and divided by $d$. Split each cap's terms into its retained old faces and its additional cut face. The weighted old contributions reproduce the first mixed volume of $K$, giving the equality. For $L=K$, Minkowski's first inequality yields $V(K_j[d-1],K)\ge \beta^{(d-1)/d}|K|$. Summation with the stated multipliers gives the bound.

Take the planar diamond $K=\operatorname{conv}\{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)\}$, of area two, and the two opposite caps $x_1\ge0$ and $x_1\le0$. Each has area one, section length two and section centroid zero. Take $\lambda_+=\lambda_-=1$ and positive dependence weights $1/2,1/2$, with group stress four, so that $\lambda_jS_j=2=(1/2)\cdot4$ and the contact thresholds are zero. Apart from null points, the cap indicators partition each old facet, so $W_f=0$ there. Both support values in the cut normals are one, and the claimed upper bound has left side four and right side two.
