## Statement

Retain all hypotheses, objects, and normalizations of accepted facts `cdcefa1ca4f07196` and `23a7bea6c72cc440`. In particular, the canonical hard \(I\)-dominant inequalities hold, \(X>Q/160\), and the aggregate circuit-envelope matching \(\eta=(\eta_e)\) and saturated balanced stress \(\widehat v=(\widehat v_e)\) satisfy
\[
 \chi_e\leq\eta_e\leq\omega_e,
 \qquad
 |\eta|:=\sum_e\eta_e\geq X,
\tag{1}
\]
\[
 \sum_e\widehat v_e=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_e\widehat v_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 \widehat v_e=\eta_eu_e,
 \qquad
 h_{D_e}(u_e)=1
\tag{2}
\]
on every edge with \(\eta_e>0\). The canonical contact \(\ell_e\) supports \(L_e\) in direction \(u_e\). Define the strict aggregate stress margin
\[
 \delta_\eta:=-\sum_e h_{L_e}(\widehat v_e)>0.
\tag{3}
\]

For every edge \(e=\{u,v\}\) with \(\eta_e>0\), both endpoint loads are positive. Define the canonical edge point
\[
 F_e^\flat:=\frac{p_u^\flat+p_v^\flat}{2}.
\tag{4}
\]
Then, for every affine map \(a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_e\eta_e
 \left\|F_e^\flat-a(m_e)\right\|_{D_e}
 \geq \frac{|\eta|}{2}+\delta_\eta.}
\tag{5}
\]
Since \(D_*\subseteq D_e\) on every loaded edge, the same lower bound holds in the common gauge:
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_e\eta_e
 \left\|F_e^\flat-a(m_e)\right\|_{D_*}
 \geq \frac{|\eta|}{2}+\delta_\eta.}
\tag{6}
\]
The corresponding actual-contact inequalities are
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_e\eta_e\left\|\ell_e-a(m_e)\right\|_{D_e}
 \geq\delta_\eta,
 \qquad
 \sum_e\eta_e\left\|\ell_e-a(m_e)\right\|_{D_*}
 \geq\delta_\eta.}
\tag{7}
\]
Consequently all four bounds remain valid after taking the infimum over affine \(a\).

The positive edge-point envelope
\[
 \boxed{
 \sigma_\eta^F
 :=2\gamma_d\sum_e\eta_e
 \delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)}}
\tag{8}
\]
is carried by \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\) and is genuinely halfspace-dominated:
\[
 \boxed{\sigma_\eta^F(H)\leq\mu_C(H)}
\tag{9}
\]
for every closed ambient halfspace \(H\).

Define its intrinsic ranks by
\[
 \rho_F:=\dim\operatorname{aff}
 \{m_e:\eta_e>0\},
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 r_F:=
 \min_{a:\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:\eta_e>0\}\to\mathbb R^d
 \ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{F_e^\flat-a(m_e):\eta_e>0\}.
\tag{11}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 r_F+1>
 \frac{275\gamma_d}{64}\frac{|\eta|}{X}
 2^{n-\rho_F}
 \geq
 \frac{275\gamma_d}{64}2^{n-\rho_F}.}
\tag{12}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{
 \dim\operatorname{aff}
 \{(m_e,F_e^\flat):\eta_e>0\}
 =\rho_F+r_F\geq n+1.}
\tag{13}
\]

The scope is exact. Equation (7) does not replace \(\delta_\eta\) by any asserted absolute multiple of \(|\eta|\), and equations (5), (6), and (13) do not assert a capacity-respecting star, a complete product grid, a rank-one weight minorant, or a sum of energies from incompatible circuit packets.

## Proof

Fix an edge \(e=\{u,v\}\) with \(\eta_e>0\). Equation (1) gives \(\omega_e>0\), so both source loads \(\beta_u\) and \(\beta_v\) are positive and both points in (4) are defined. Accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` gives
\[
 p_z^\flat+\frac12D_z\subseteq\mathcal K_z
 \qquad(\beta_z>0).
\tag{14}
\]
Averaging (14) at \(u\) and \(v\), and using the canonical identities
\[
 D_e=\frac12(D_u+D_v),
 \qquad
 L_e=\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v),
\tag{15}
\]
gives
\[
 F_e^\flat+\frac12D_e\subseteq L_e.
\tag{16}
\]
The contact \(\ell_e\) supports \(L_e\) in direction \(u_e\), while \(h_{D_e}(u_e)=1\). Therefore
\[
 h_{L_e}(u_e)
 =\langle u_e,\ell_e\rangle
 \geq
 \langle u_e,F_e^\flat\rangle
 +\frac12h_{D_e}(u_e)
 =\langle u_e,F_e^\flat\rangle+\frac12.
\tag{17}
\]

Let \(a(m)=Am+b\) be affine. The two moment cancellations in (2) imply
\[
 \sum_e\eta_e\langle u_e,a(m_e)\rangle
 =\sum_e\langle\widehat v_e,a(m_e)\rangle=0.
\tag{18}
\]
Multiplying (17) by \(\eta_e\), summing, and using (3) and (18) yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_e\eta_e
 \langle u_e,F_e^\flat-a(m_e)\rangle
 &=\sum_e\eta_e\langle u_e,F_e^\flat\rangle\\
 &\leq
 \sum_eh_{L_e}(\widehat v_e)-\frac{|\eta|}{2}
 =-\delta_\eta-\frac{|\eta|}{2}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{19}
\]
Because \(D_e\) is origin-symmetric and \(h_{D_e}(u_e)=1\), polar Hölder gives
\[
 -\langle u_e,y\rangle\leq\|y\|_{D_e}
 \qquad(y\in\mathbb R^d).
\tag{20}
\]
Apply (20) to \(y=F_e^\flat-a(m_e)\), multiply by \(\eta_e\), and use (19). This proves (5).

For the actual contacts, support at \(\ell_e\), equations (2), (3), and (18) give the exact signed identity
\[
 \sum_e\eta_e
 \langle u_e,\ell_e-a(m_e)\rangle
 =\sum_eh_{L_e}(\widehat v_e)
 =-\delta_\eta.
\tag{21}
\]
Equation (20) now proves the first inequality in (7). Accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` gives \(D_*\subseteq D_z\) at every loaded source, hence \(D_*\subseteq D_e\). Therefore
\[
 \|y\|_{D_*}\geq\|y\|_{D_e}
\tag{22}
\]
for every \(y\), proving (6) and the second inequality in (7).

We next prove genuine domination of (8). Every point \(p_z^\flat\) in (14) belongs to
\[
 \mathcal K_z=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
\tag{23}
\]
and the loaded-source bounds in accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` give
\[
 0<\beta_z\leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{24}
\]
The midpoint in (4) lies in \(C_{m_e}\) by convexity, and \(m_e\) is integral because every loaded edge is same-parity. Thus (8) is carried by the mixed-integer body.

Fix a closed ambient halfspace \(H\). Whenever \((m_e,F_e^\flat)\in H\), at least one of \((u,p_u^\flat)\) and \((v,p_v^\flat)\) lies in \(H\); orient that occurrence toward one such endpoint. Let \(B_z\) be the total \(\eta\)-weight oriented toward \(z\). Equations (1) and the definition of \(\beta_z\) give
\[
 B_z\leq\sum_{e\ni z}\eta_e
 \leq\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e
 =\beta_z.
\tag{25}
\]
If \(B_z>0\), the fiber section \(H_z\) contains \(p_z^\flat\). The metronoid depth theorem in accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d`, followed by (24) and (25), gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z)
 \geq\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2}
 \geq2\gamma_d\beta_z
 \geq2\gamma_dB_z.
\tag{26}
\]
The same inequality is immediate if the fiber section is the whole fiber. Summing (26) over the pairwise distinct source fibers proves (9). This proof charges the single sum measure (8), not a sum of two separately dominated measures.

The measure (8) is nonzero and has mass
\[
 |\sigma_\eta^F|=2\gamma_d|\eta|.
\tag{27}
\]
Apply the general dominated-measure calibration (28) of accepted fact `23a7bea6c72cc440` to \(\sigma_\eta^F\). Its intrinsic ranks are exactly (10) and (11), so
\[
 \frac{2\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_F}|\eta|}
 {X(r_F+1)}<\frac{128}{275}.
\tag{28}
\]
Rearranging (28) and using \(|\eta|\geq X\) proves (12). Accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` gives the intrinsic identity
\[
 \dim\operatorname{aff}
 \{(m_e,F_e^\flat):\eta_e>0\}=\rho_F+r_F.
\tag{29}
\]
Also \(r+1\leq2^r\) for every integer \(r\geq0\), and \(\gamma_d>1/3\). Hence (12) gives
\[
 2^{\rho_F+r_F}
 \geq2^{\rho_F}(r_F+1)
 >\frac{275}{192}2^n
 >2^n.
\tag{30}
\]
The exponent in (30) is an integer, proving (13).

We finish by auditing the exact scope. The contact bound cannot be upgraded from the weighted-dual/contact interface alone. Accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` supplies genuine convex bodies and matchings with \(\Omega=2\) and \(\Delta_C(q)=2\varepsilon\), while its exact contact formula gives, at its primal optimizer \(s_*\),
\[
 \sum_e\omega_e
 \|s_*(m_e)-\ell_e\|_{D_e}=2\varepsilon.
\tag{31}
\]
Thus the infimum of the actual-contact \(L^1\) error divided by \(\Omega\) can tend to zero. Additional hard-branch input would be needed to replace \(\delta_\eta\) in (7) by an absolute multiple of \(|\eta|\).

The edge-point gap has a different but equally precise limitation. Inclusion (16) gives a depth-\(1/2\) interior point of each \(L_e\), while (5) gives only an average-distance lower bound at that same scale. It supplies no positive lower bound on the \(\eta\)-mass missed by an affine map, no collision-free completion cells, and no rank-one minorant across different bases. The circuit support bound in accepted fact `cdcefa1ca4f07196` is only the upper bound \(d(n+1)+1\) for each circuit; neither it nor the total marking identity gives a dimension-independent bound on the number of circuits or forces one circuit to carry an absolute fraction of \(X\). Finally, accepted fact `fbeb0a501b268d12` shows that an independently useful packet must have full base rank \(n\) and at least two factors. The graph-rank conclusion (13) does not produce such a Cartesian base chart, and no accepted fact cited here supplies a rule for summing energies from incompatible circuit packets. The direct capacity conversion proved here therefore stops at (12); a terminal use requires a new capacity-respecting completion theorem.
