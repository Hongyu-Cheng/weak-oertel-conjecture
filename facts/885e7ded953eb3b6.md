## Statement

Use the optimized projective facet transport on actual parallel faces at two bases and their midpoint. Write $R_A=R(L_A(X))$, $R_B=R(L_B(Y))$, $l=(R_A+R_B)/2$, $\Delta=(R_A-R_B)/2$ for the coupled pair, and let $I_A,I_B,I_K$ be the captured bits of one closed ambient halfspace at $X,Y,Z$. If $C_H$ is the sum of the endpoint capture integrals of $f(L_A)$ and $f(L_B)$, then
$$
C_H=\zeta(H)+J\int l(I_A+I_B-I_K)\,d\pi
             +J\int\Delta(I_A-I_B)\,d\pi
\ \ge\ \zeta(H)-\frac J2\int|R_A-R_B|I_K\,d\pi .
$$
The nonnegative uncovered source measure is $\mu_K-\zeta$, where $\mu_K=f(L_K)\mathcal H^k|_{F_K}$. The same coupling works for every closed ambient halfspace and, with selector integrals, every actual inward trace on the three facets. The latter uses one common inward direction for these parallel facets and retains the closed pure-base bit.

## Proof

For a closed affine margin, the value at $Z$ is the arithmetic mean of its endpoint values, including the base coordinate. Hence $I_A+I_B\ge I_K$. The endpoint marginals give $C_H=J\int(R_A I_A+R_B I_B)\,d\pi$ and the midpoint pushforward gives $\zeta(H)=J\int l I_K\,d\pi$. Expand the endpoint sum in $l,\Delta$ to obtain the identity. For the bound use $R_A I_A+R_B I_B\ge\min(R_A,R_B)I_K$ and $\min(R_A,R_B)=l-|R_A-R_B|/2$. Replacing $\zeta(H)$ by $\mu_K(H)-(\mu_K-\zeta)(H)$ displays both the uncovered source part and the denominator defect explicitly.

For an inward trace, translate every paired continuous point $X,Y,Z$ by $-s n$ with $n$ the common outward unit normal and $s\downarrow0$. Their physical midpoint identity persists. The affine margin bits have pointwise limits, so their midpoint inequality persists. On the relative interior of a facet, its normal is unique. A proper trace boundary within the facet is null unless the entire facet lies in the cap plane. In the latter case a nonzero continuous cap normal is parallel to $n$ and the translation gives precisely the wholly captured or interior-disjoint side. If the continuous cap normal is zero, the translation preserves its original closed bit, including equality. The triangular maps have absolutely continuous endpoint and midpoint marginals, so all partial-facet null sets may be ignored for any fixed cap. The unchanged old densities with these limiting bits give the actual selector integrals, and the identity and bound apply directly to them. The coupling was chosen before selecting any cap.
