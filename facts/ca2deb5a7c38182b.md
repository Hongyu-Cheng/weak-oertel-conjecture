## Statement

Take the three compatible intervals $K_{-1}=K_1=[-1/2,1/2]$ and $K_0=[-1/2,1]$ at bases $-1,0,1$. Their total mixed length is $7/2$ and their mixed centroid is $(0,3/28)$. Write $x$ for the continuous coordinate after subtracting $3/28$. The raw closed-halfspace depth at the centroid is $45/28$. Its minimizing caps are exactly $-x+az\ge0$ with $|a|\ge17/28$. Every such cap fully captures one endpoint fiber, and the upper endpoints in the margin coordinate $-x+az$ never achieve their maximum at base zero. At $|a|=17/28$ the maximum upper endpoint is $17/14<3/2=\max_i |K_i|$.

## Proof

Interpolating the sampled upper endpoints gives a concave roof, and the lower roof is constant. This realizes exactly the three stated integer fibers. The base moment vanishes by the equal endpoint lengths, and the continuous moment is $(3/2)(1/4)=3/8$, giving centroid height $(3/8)/(7/2)=3/28$ in the original coordinates. After centering the positive orientation has upper endpoints $11/28,25/28,11/28$. The negative orientation has upper endpoints all $17/28$.

For a through-origin cap of either orientation the endpoint contribution is $[v+a]_0^1+[v-a]_0^1$, where $[b]_0^1=\min(1,\max(0,b))$ and $v$ is the common endpoint upper coordinate for that orientation. This expression depends only on $s=|a|$. For $v=11/28$, it is $22/28$ on $0\le s\le11/28$, rises to $1$ on $11/28\le s\le17/28$, and stays $1$ thereafter. For $v=17/28$, it is $34/28$ on $0\le s\le11/28$, decreases as $1+17/28-s$ on $11/28\le s\le17/28$, and stays $1$ thereafter. The own fiber contributes $25/28$ and $17/28$ in the two orientations, so the minima are $47/28$ and $45/28$, respectively. The pure-base closed caps each have mass $5/2$. An outward translation of a through-origin oblique cap adds positive mass in the central interval, which contains the origin in its interior. Thus the negative minimizing slopes listed in the statement are exhaustive.

For $s\ge17/28$ one endpoint margin interval lies wholly in the nonnegative cap. The largest upper endpoint equals $17/28+s$, strictly above the central upper endpoint $17/28$. At $s=17/28$ this value is $17/14$.
