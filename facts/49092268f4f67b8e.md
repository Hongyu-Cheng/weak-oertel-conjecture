## Statement

Retain the complete canonical setting and notation of accepted fact 6e6a3b541bc64f05. In particular, let
\(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and write
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx
\tag{1}
\]
whenever \(a_z>0\). Let \(\Gamma\) be its finite positive whole-label family. Every \(\sigma\in\Gamma\) has mass \(\theta_\sigma>0\), physical target \(t(\sigma)\in\mathbb Z^n\), and a four-entry tagged occurrence multiset
\[
 \mathcal O_\sigma
 =[y_{\sigma,1},y_{\sigma,2},y_{\sigma,3},y_{\sigma,4}],
 \qquad
 \frac14\sum_{o=1}^4y_{\sigma,o}=t(\sigma),
\tag{2}
\]
where repeated physical sources remain separate tags. Put
\[
 r_\sigma^c:=\frac14\sum_{o=1}^4c_{y_{\sigma,o}},
 \qquad
 \widehat c_z:=(z,c_z),
 \qquad
 \widehat r_\sigma^c:=(t(\sigma),r_\sigma^c),
\tag{3}
\]
\[
 \Theta:=\Theta_{\mathcal P}
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma>0,
 \qquad
 \Gamma_t:=\{\sigma\in\Gamma:t(\sigma)=t\}.
\tag{4}
\]
The occurrence loads, with every tagged multiplicity retained, obey
\[
 R_z^\Gamma
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}
 \theta_\sigma\operatorname{mult}_z(\mathcal O_\sigma)
 \leq a_z.
\tag{5}
\]

For every \(\sigma\in\Gamma\), retain its fixed chosen blocker tag
\(o_{\mathcal B}(\sigma)\), and put
\[
 m_\sigma:=y_{\sigma,o_{\mathcal B}(\sigma)}.
\tag{6}
\]
For a chosen blocker \(m\), accepted fact 6e6a3b541bc64f05 supplies
\[
 \ell_m:=\sum_{\sigma:m_\sigma=m}\theta_\sigma>0,
 \qquad
 \alpha_m:=\frac{\ell_m}{4a_m},
\tag{7}
\]
an ancestry matching \(b=(b_e)\), indexed by unordered off-diagonal edges
\(e=\{u,v\}\), with
\[
 m_e=\frac{u+v}{2}\quad(e=\{u,v\}),
 \qquad
 \lambda_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}b_e,
\tag{8}
\]
and a physical density \(H_m\) on \(C_m\) such that
\[
 0\leq H_m\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_m}H_m=\lambda_m,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_m}xH_m(x)\,dx
 =\frac12\sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e(c_u+c_v).
\tag{9}
\]
Put
\[
 V_m:=a_m-\lambda_m>0,
 \qquad
 q_m:=\frac1{V_m}\int_{C_m}x(1-H_m(x))\,dx,
 \qquad
 \widehat q_m:=(m,q_m).
\tag{10}
\]
The exact lifted parent-child identity is
\[
 a_m\widehat c_m
 =V_m\widehat q_m
 +\frac12\sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}
 b_e(\widehat c_u+\widehat c_v).
\tag{11}
\]

For each physical target \(t\), define the capped prescribed-barycenter packing capacity
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathsf P_t:=\sup\quad&
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}\int_{C_t}h_\sigma(x)\,dx\\
 \text{subject to}\quad&
 h_\sigma\geq0,\qquad
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}h_\sigma\leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_t,\\
 &\int_{C_t}h_\sigma\leq\theta_\sigma,\qquad
 \int_{C_t}(x-r_\sigma^c)h_\sigma(x)\,dx=0
 \quad(\sigma\in\Gamma_t).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
Put
\[
 \mathsf P_\Gamma:=\sum_t\mathsf P_t.
\tag{13}
\]
The finite sum in (13) is over the actual distinct physical targets, so all target collisions remain inside the common pointwise constraint in (12).

Then the exact capped max-affine dual is
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf P_t=
 \inf_{\substack{\ell_\sigma:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R\ {\rm affine}\\
                   \ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\leq1}}
 \left[
 \int_{C_t}\Phi_t(x)\,dx
 +\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}
 \theta_\sigma\bigl(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\bigr)
 \right],}
\tag{14}
\]
where
\[
 \Phi_t(x):=
 \max\left\{0,\max_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}\ell_\sigma(x)\right\}.
\tag{15}
\]
For a global normalized affine family
\[
 \ell=(\ell_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Gamma},
 \qquad
 \ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\leq1,
\tag{16}
\]
define
\[
 A(\ell):=
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}
 \theta_\sigma\bigl(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\bigr),
 \qquad
 I(\ell):=
 \sum_t\int_{C_t}\Phi_t(x)\,dx.
\tag{17}
\]
Both quantities are nonnegative, and
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf P_\Gamma
 =\inf_\ell\{A(\ell)+I(\ell)\}.}
\tag{18}
\]

Let
\[
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)
\tag{19}
\]
be the canonical complete metronoid-core capacity. Uniformly over any class of canonical instances satisfying the preceding hypotheses, the following two assertions are equivalent up to a factor two in their constants:

1. There is an absolute \(\kappa>0\) such that every instance satisfies
\[
 \max\{\mathsf P_\Gamma,G\}\geq\kappa\Theta.
\tag{20}
\]

2. There is an absolute \(K_{\mathrm{fus}}<\infty\) such that every instance and every normalized affine family (16) satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta
 \leq
 K_{\mathrm{fus}}\bigl(G+A(\ell)+I(\ell)\bigr).}
\tag{21}
\]

More precisely, (20) with \(\kappa\) implies (21) with
\(K_{\mathrm{fus}}=1/\kappa\), while (21) with \(K_{\mathrm{fus}}\) implies (20) with
\(\kappa=1/(2K_{\mathrm{fus}})\). Neither (20) nor (21) is asserted unconditionally here.

There is an exact labelwise blocker-to-parent pullback. For
\(\sigma\in\Gamma\), with \(m=m_\sigma\), define
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Pi_\sigma:=&
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4}
 \sum_{\substack{1\leq o\leq4\\o\ne o_{\mathcal B}(\sigma)}}
 \delta_{\widehat c_{y_{\sigma,o}}}
 +\frac{\theta_\sigma V_m}{4a_m}\delta_{\widehat q_m}\\
 &+\frac{\theta_\sigma}{8a_m}
 \sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e
 \left(\delta_{\widehat c_u}+\delta_{\widehat c_v}\right).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{22}
\]
Every repeated nonchosen tag in (22) is a separate summand. These measures satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
 |\Pi_\sigma|=\theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \int p\,d\Pi_\sigma(p)
 =\theta_\sigma\widehat r_\sigma^c,
 \qquad
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\Pi_\sigma=\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}},}
\tag{23}
\]
where \(\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\) is exactly the replacement measure of accepted fact 6e6a3b541bc64f05.

More precisely, for \(z\) with \(a_z>0\), define the physical density
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 F_{\sigma,z}(x):={}&
 \sum_{\substack{o\ne o_{\mathcal B}(\sigma)\\
                   y_{\sigma,o}=z}}
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_z}\\
 &+\mathbf1_{\{z=m_\sigma\}}
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_{m_\sigma}}
 \bigl(1-H_{m_\sigma}(x)\bigr)\\
 &+\sum_{\substack{e\ni z\\m_e=m_\sigma}}
 \frac{\theta_\sigma b_e}
 {8a_{m_\sigma}a_z},
 \qquad x\in C_z.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{24}
\]
The first sum in (24) retains repeated tag multiplicities, and the final sum retains every ancestry incidence. The family \((F_{\sigma,z})\) physically represents (22), and the one common ledger satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}F_{\sigma,z}(x)
 \leq\frac{17}{32}
 \quad\text{for every physical source }z
 \text{ and a.e. }x\in C_z.}
\tag{25}
\]

For a normalized affine family (16), put
\[
 \Psi_z(x):=
 \max\left\{
 0,\max_{\sigma:F_{\sigma,z}\not\equiv0}\ell_\sigma(x)
 \right\},
\tag{26}
\]
with \(\Psi_z:=0\) if no label occurs in the maximum. Then the exact pulled-back score inequality is
\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta-A(\ell)
 =\sum_z\int_{C_z}
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}
 F_{\sigma,z}(x)\ell_\sigma(x)\,dx
 \leq
 \frac{17}{32}
 \sum_z\int_{C_z}\Psi_z(x)\,dx.}
\tag{27}
\]
Thus label-dependent active pieces are pulled through the physical replacement ledger without discarding any target collision, tag multiplicity, or ancestry incidence.

Finally, every normalized affine family has a constant-mass active original-occurrence profile. Call a label good when
\[
 \ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\geq\frac12.
\tag{28}
\]
Choose, for every good label, one tagged occurrence
\(o(\sigma)\in\{1,2,3,4\}\) satisfying
\[
 \ell_\sigma(c_{y_{\sigma,o(\sigma)}})\geq\frac12,
\tag{29}
\]
and put
\[
 w_z:=
 \frac14
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\ {\rm good}\\
                   y_{\sigma,o(\sigma)}=z}}
 \theta_\sigma.
\tag{30}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_z w_z
 \geq\frac{\Theta-2A(\ell)}4,
 \qquad
 0\leq w_z\leq\frac{R_z^\Gamma}{4}\leq\frac{a_z}{4}.}
\tag{31}
\]
For every \(z\) with \(w_z>0\), the constant density
\[
 f_z^{\mathrm{act}}
 :=\frac{w_z}{a_z}\mathbf1_{C_z}
\tag{32}
\]
has mass \(w_z\), barycenter \(c_z\), and is part of one simultaneous physical source ledger with
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\ {\rm good}\\
                   y_{\sigma,o(\sigma)}=z}}
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_z}\mathbf1_{C_z}
 =f_z^{\mathrm{act}}
 \leq\frac14\mathbf1_{C_z}.
\tag{33}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{c_z\in\mathsf M_{w_z}(C_z)\qquad(w_z>0).}
\tag{34}
\]
No affine selector through the profile (30), no nonempty complete core for it, and no lower bound in (20) or (21) is asserted.

## Proof

Fix a physical target \(t\). Let \(\mathcal F_t\) be the set of finite families
\((h_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}\) with
\[
 h_\sigma\in L^\infty(C_t),
 \qquad
 h_\sigma\geq0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}h_\sigma\leq1
 \quad\text{a.e.}
\tag{35}
\]
Every coordinate lies in the closed unit ball of \(L^\infty(C_t)\). As in the weak-star compactness argument of accepted fact 2c093df4eb8158a9, nonnegativity and the common capacity in (35) are weak-star closed because they can be tested against all nonnegative functions in \(L^1(C_t)\). Hence \(\mathcal F_t\) is nonempty, convex, and weak-star compact.

Define its finite-dimensional moment map by
\[
 \mathcal T_t(h):=
 \left(
 \int_{C_t}h_\sigma,\,
 \int_{C_t}(x-r_\sigma^c)h_\sigma(x)\,dx
 \right)_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}.
\tag{36}
\]
Compactness of \(C_t\) makes every coordinate of (36) weak-star continuous. Its image is therefore a compact convex subset of a finite-dimensional space. The program (12) is optimization of a linear functional over this moment image, subject to the affine equations
\[
 \int_{C_t}(x-r_\sigma^c)h_\sigma=0
\tag{37}
\]
and the inequalities
\[
 \int_{C_t}h_\sigma\leq\theta_\sigma.
\tag{38}
\]
We prove exact duality directly, including possible boundary barycenters. Write a point of the compact moment image as
\((m,u)=((m_\sigma,u_\sigma))_\sigma\), where
\[
 m_\sigma=\int_{C_t}h_\sigma,
 \qquad
 u_\sigma=\int_{C_t}(x-r_\sigma^c)h_\sigma(x)\,dx,
\tag{39}
\]
and write \(\theta=(\theta_\sigma)_\sigma\). Fix any
\(\beta>\mathsf P_t\). The compact convex set
\[
 \mathcal S_\beta:=
 \left\{
 \left(u,m-\theta,\sum_\sigma m_\sigma-\beta\right):
 (m,u)\in\mathcal T_t(\mathcal F_t)
 \right\}
\tag{40}
\]
is disjoint from the closed convex cone
\[
 \mathcal K:=
 \{(0,p,r):p_\sigma\leq0\ (\forall\sigma),\ r\geq0\}.
\tag{41}
\]
Indeed, a point in their intersection would have
\(u=0\), \(m\leq\theta\), and
\(\sum_\sigma m_\sigma\geq\beta\), contradicting the definition of
\(\mathsf P_t\).

Strong separation of the compact set \(\mathcal S_\beta\) from the closed cone \(\mathcal K\), followed by scaling the separator, gives vectors
\(v=(v_\sigma)_\sigma\), \(s=(s_\sigma)_\sigma\), and a scalar
\(\lambda\) such that
\[
 s_\sigma\geq0,\qquad \lambda\geq0,
\tag{42}
\]
and
\[
 \sum_\sigma\langle v_\sigma,u_\sigma\rangle
 +\sum_\sigma s_\sigma(m_\sigma-\theta_\sigma)
 -\lambda\left(\sum_\sigma m_\sigma-\beta\right)
 >0
\tag{43}
\]
for every \((m,u)\) in the moment image. The signs in (42) are forced by requiring the same linear functional to be nonpositive on the unbounded cone (41). The zero density family belongs to \(\mathcal F_t\); substituting its zero moment vector into (43) gives
\[
 -\sum_\sigma s_\sigma\theta_\sigma+\lambda\beta>0.
\tag{44}
\]
Because every \(\theta_\sigma>0\), equation (44) rules out
\(\lambda=0\). Hence \(\lambda>0\). Rearranging (43), dividing by
\(\lambda\), and replacing \(-v/\lambda\) by \(v\) and
\(s/\lambda\) by \(s\), gives
\[
 \sum_\sigma\theta_\sigma s_\sigma
 +
 \sup_{(m,u)\in\mathcal T_t(\mathcal F_t)}
 \sum_\sigma
 \left((1-s_\sigma)m_\sigma+
 \langle v_\sigma,u_\sigma\rangle\right)
 <\beta.
\tag{45}
\]
Weak duality gives the reverse lower bound by \(\mathsf P_t\). Since
\(\beta>\mathsf P_t\) was arbitrary, the infimum of the expressions in (45), over \(s_\sigma\geq0\) and unrestricted \(v_\sigma\), is exactly
\(\mathsf P_t\).

Equivalently, the expression on the left side of (45) is the Lagrangian supremum
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}\theta_\sigma s_\sigma
 +
 \sup_{h\in\mathcal F_t}
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}
 \int_{C_t}
 h_\sigma(x)
 \left(
 1-s_\sigma+\langle v_\sigma,x-r_\sigma^c\rangle
 \right)\,dx.
\tag{46}
\]
Put
\[
 \ell_\sigma(x):=
 1-s_\sigma+\langle v_\sigma,x-r_\sigma^c\rangle.
\tag{47}
\]
Then
\[
 \ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)=1-s_\sigma\leq1,
 \qquad
 \theta_\sigma s_\sigma
 =\theta_\sigma\bigl(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\bigr).
\tag{48}
\]
Conversely, every affine function satisfying
\(\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\leq1\) has a unique representation of the form (47), with
\(s_\sigma=1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\geq0\) and with its linear coefficient used as \(v_\sigma\).

For fixed affine pieces, pointwise maximization under
\(h_\sigma\geq0\) and \(\sum_\sigma h_\sigma\leq1\) gives
\[
 \sup_{h\in\mathcal F_t}
 \sum_\sigma\int_{C_t}h_\sigma\ell_\sigma
 =
 \int_{C_t}
 \max\left\{0,\max_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}\ell_\sigma(x)\right\}\,dx.
\tag{49}
\]
The equality includes all positive ties: order the finite labels and assign each point where the maximum is positive to its first maximizing label, exactly as in accepted fact 2c093df4eb8158a9. Equations (45)-(49) prove (14), without an attainment or interiority assumption.

For completeness, weak duality can also be checked directly without the separation theorem. Let \(h\) be feasible in (12), put
\(q_\sigma:=\int h_\sigma\), and let \(\ell\) be normalized as in (14). The moment equation and affineness give
\[
 \int_{C_t}h_\sigma(x)\ell_\sigma(x)\,dx
 =q_\sigma\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c).
\tag{50}
\]
Since \(q_\sigma\leq\theta_\sigma\) and
\(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\geq0\),
\[
 q_\sigma
 \leq
 \int_{C_t}h_\sigma\ell_\sigma
 +\theta_\sigma
 \bigl(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\bigr).
\tag{51}
\]
Pointwise,
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}h_\sigma(x)\ell_\sigma(x)
 \leq
 \Phi_t(x)\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}h_\sigma(x)
 \leq\Phi_t(x).
\tag{52}
\]
Summing (51) and applying (52) gives the objective in (14).

The target programs use disjoint density variables and disjoint physical fibers, and there are only finitely many nonempty \(\Gamma_t\). Therefore
\[
 \mathsf P_\Gamma
 =\sum_t\mathsf P_t
 =\sum_t\inf_{\ell|_{\Gamma_t}}
 \left[
 \int_{C_t}\Phi_t
 +\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}
 \theta_\sigma(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c))
 \right]
 =\inf_\ell\{A(\ell)+I(\ell)\}.
\tag{53}
\]
This proves (18), with every colliding label retained inside its target maximum.

We prove the equivalence. If (20) holds, then every normalized family satisfies
\[
 G+A(\ell)+I(\ell)
 \geq G+\mathsf P_\Gamma
 \geq\max\{G,\mathsf P_\Gamma\}
 \geq\kappa\Theta,
\tag{54}
\]
where (18) gives \(A(\ell)+I(\ell)\geq\mathsf P_\Gamma\). This is (21) with \(K_{\mathrm{fus}}=1/\kappa\). Conversely, if (21) holds for every normalized family, take a sequence of families whose objectives decrease to the infimum in (18). Passing to the limit gives
\[
 \Theta\leq K_{\mathrm{fus}}(G+\mathsf P_\Gamma)
 \leq2K_{\mathrm{fus}}\max\{G,\mathsf P_\Gamma\}.
\tag{55}
\]
This proves (20) with \(\kappa=1/(2K_{\mathrm{fus}})\). No dual attainment is needed.

We next prove the labelwise replacement assertions. Fix
\(\sigma\in\Gamma\) and abbreviate \(m=m_\sigma\). The nonchosen part of (22) has mass \(3\theta_\sigma/4\). The complement term and all parent terms together have mass
\[
 \frac{\theta_\sigma V_m}{4a_m}
 +\frac{\theta_\sigma}{8a_m}
 \sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}2b_e
 =
 \frac{\theta_\sigma(V_m+\lambda_m)}{4a_m}
 =\frac{\theta_\sigma}{4}.
\tag{56}
\]
Thus \(|\Pi_\sigma|=\theta_\sigma\). Multiplying (11) by
\(\theta_\sigma/(4a_m)\) shows that the last two terms of (22) have the same lifted first moment as
\[
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4}\delta_{\widehat c_m}.
\tag{57}
\]
Adding the three nonchosen tagged occurrences and using (2)-(3) gives
\[
 \int p\,d\Pi_\sigma(p)
 =
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{4}
 \sum_{o=1}^4\widehat c_{y_{\sigma,o}}
 =\theta_\sigma\widehat r_\sigma^c.
\tag{58}
\]

Summing (22) over labels, the nonchosen terms give exactly the nonchosen-occurrence line of \(\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\). At a fixed chosen blocker \(m\), equations (7) give
\[
 \sum_{\sigma:m_\sigma=m}
 \frac{\theta_\sigma V_m}{4a_m}
 =\alpha_mV_m,
\tag{59}
\]
and
\[
 \sum_{\sigma:m_\sigma=m}
 \frac{\theta_\sigma}{8a_m}
 =\frac{\alpha_m}{2}.
\tag{60}
\]
Therefore the remaining sums give exactly the complement and parent lines of \(\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\). This proves every identity in (23), including physical target collisions at the chosen blockers.

We verify the density representation. Every nonchosen tag in (24) has mass
\(\theta_\sigma/4\) and barycenter \(c_z\). The complement term has mass
\(\theta_\sigma V_m/(4a_m)\) and barycenter \(q_m\), by (10). For every ancestry incidence \(z\in e\), its last-line density in (24) has mass
\[
 a_z\frac{\theta_\sigma b_e}{8a_ma_z}
 =\frac{\theta_\sigma b_e}{8a_m}
\tag{61}
\]
and barycenter \(c_z\). Thus (24) represents (22) exactly, including repeated nonchosen tags and every parent incidence.

When (24) is summed over \(\sigma\), equations (59)-(60) show that it is exactly the physical density family representing \(\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\) in accepted fact 6e6a3b541bc64f05. That accepted fact represents
\(\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}+\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\) on one common ledger of pointwise density at most \(17/32\). The replacement density is a nonnegative subfamily of that ledger, so (25) follows.

Let \(\ell\) be normalized. Since \(F_{\sigma,z}\) represents a measure of mass \(\theta_\sigma\) and barycenter
\(\widehat r_\sigma^c\), affineness in the continuous coordinate gives
\[
 \sum_z\int_{C_z}
 F_{\sigma,z}(x)\ell_\sigma(x)\,dx
 =\theta_\sigma\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c).
\tag{62}
\]
After summing over labels, (17) gives the equality in (27). Pointwise on a fixed physical source,
\[
 \sum_\sigma F_{\sigma,z}(x)\ell_\sigma(x)
 \leq
 \Psi_z(x)\sum_\sigma F_{\sigma,z}(x)
 \leq\frac{17}{32}\Psi_z(x),
\tag{63}
\]
because every affine piece appearing with positive density is at most the nonnegative maximum in (26). Integrating (63) and summing over distinct physical sources proves the inequality in (27).

It remains to prove the active original-occurrence profile. A bad label, meaning one which fails (28), satisfies
\[
 \theta_\sigma
 <2\theta_\sigma
 \bigl(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\bigr).
\tag{64}
\]
Summing (64) over bad labels and using nonnegativity of every summand in \(A(\ell)\) gives
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\ {\rm bad}}\theta_\sigma
 \leq2A(\ell),
 \qquad
 \sum_{\sigma\ {\rm good}}\theta_\sigma
 \geq\Theta-2A(\ell).
\tag{65}
\]
For a good label, (3) and affineness give the exact four-tag identity
\[
 \ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)
 =\frac14\sum_{o=1}^4
 \ell_\sigma(c_{y_{\sigma,o}}).
\tag{66}
\]
Hence at least one tagged occurrence satisfies (29), even when two or more tags have the same physical source. Choose one tag, not merely one physical source, for every good label.

By (30),
\[
 \sum_z w_z
 =\frac14\sum_{\sigma\ {\rm good}}\theta_\sigma
 \geq\frac{\Theta-2A(\ell)}4.
\tag{67}
\]
At a fixed physical source \(z\), every chosen tag is one of the tagged occurrences counted, with its multiplicity, by \(R_z^\Gamma\). Therefore
\[
 4w_z
 \leq R_z^\Gamma
 \leq a_z,
\tag{68}
\]
which proves (31). The selected occurrence packet for a label of mass
\(\theta_\sigma\) is the constant density
\(\theta_\sigma/(4a_z)\) on its source fiber. Summing these selected packets at \(z\) gives exactly (32), and (68) gives the pointwise bound in (33). Its mass is \(w_z\), and its first moment is \(w_zc_z\). This proves (34) directly from the definition of the metronoid and completes every asserted dual, pullback, score, multiplicity, collision, and physical-capacity conclusion.
