## Statement

In the two preceding stationary diamond bodies, write $r_z=|K_z|^{1/2}$ for the full-fiber root, $c_z$ for the cap root of $y\ge0$, and $a_z$ for its section coarea. The two Jensen heights
$$
B_{\rm vol}=\frac23\frac{\sum_z r_z^3}{\sum_z r_z^2},
\qquad B_{\rm sec}=\frac{\sum_z a_zc_z}{\sum_z a_z}
$$
are both at most the central root $c_0$. For $C_A$ the values satisfy $B_{\rm vol}=10\sqrt2/9>3/2=B_{\rm sec}$, and for $C_B$ they satisfy $B_{\rm vol}=2\sqrt2/3<(5+\sqrt7)/8=B_{\rm sec}$.

## Proof

With base probabilities $r_z^2/\sum r_z^2$, use the conditional density $2t/r_z^2$ on $[0,r_z]$. The actual mixed centroid and the inverse-tail moment identity in b170894d1a8b4d11 make the joint mean of the inverse lift $U(z,t)$ zero, and the joint means of $z,t$ are $0,B_{\rm vol}$. In the section law use weights $a_z/\sum a_z$ at $(z,c_z)$. Its mean base is zero and $U(z,c_z)=0$ at every site. Joint concavity gives $U(0,B_{\rm vol})\ge0$ and $U(0,B_{\rm sec})\ge0$. Since the central positive fiber has a proper cut and its inverse is strictly decreasing, both heights are at most $c_0$.

Substitute the volume, coarea and root arrays from the stationary-diamond calculation. The first strict inequality reduces on squaring to $800>729$. For the second, after multiplying by $24$, the comparison is $16\sqrt2<15+3\sqrt7$. Squaring the positive sides reduces this to $224<90\sqrt7$, and a second squaring gives $50176<56700$.
