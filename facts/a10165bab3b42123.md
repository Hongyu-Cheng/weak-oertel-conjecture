## Statement

For planar convex bodies with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$ and a whole nondegenerate exposed segment $F$ of $A$ of outward unit normal $n$, put $E=\{x:x+B\subseteq2K\}$. Every relative-interior point of $F$ lies in $\operatorname{int}E$ exactly when $h_A(n)+h_B(n)<2h_K(n)$, and otherwise none does. At equality the actual exposed faces satisfy $(F_A(n)+F_B(n))/2\subseteq F_K(n)$, where $F_K(n)$ has positive length. Suppose the old cap menu meets the hypotheses of the face-bulge theorem with original global raw maximum $Q$ and ordinary $A$ maximum strictly below $Q$. At an attained local minimum against compatible changes with $K,B$ fixed, any face having negative tent cost at a relative-interior peak must satisfy support equality.

## Proof

By support inequalities $E$ is the intersection of $u\cdot x\le2h_K(u)-h_B(u)$ over unit $u$ and contains $A$. At a point $p$ in the relative interior of $F$, if no inequality is active, continuity and compactness of the unit circle give a positive uniform slack, hence an interior ball about $p$ in $E$. If a direction $u$ is active, it supports $A$ at $p$. Since $F$ is a nondegenerate segment with $p$ in its relative interior, $u$ annihilates its tangent. Since $A$ has interior, the outward sign is forced, so $u=n$. Conversely equality for $n$ makes the constraint active everywhere on $F$, excluding all such interior balls. This proves the equivalence. For points in $F_A(n)$ and $F_B(n)$ their midpoints lie in $K$ by compatibility and reach $h_K(n)$ by equality, proving face inclusion. A translate of half of $F_A(n)$ is contained there. If the tent cost is negative at an interior peak and the support gap is strict, that peak lies in $\operatorname{int}E$. The actual face-bulge theorem gives nearby compatible bodies of strictly smaller normalized maximum depth, contradicting the local minimum.
