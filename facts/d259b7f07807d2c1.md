## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and retain all
objects, assumptions, and normalizations of accepted fact
`23a7bea6c72cc440`.  In particular, assume the canonical hard
\(I\)-dominant inequalities
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad I\ge O,
\tag{1}
\]
and write
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 \chi_e:=\min\{\omega_e,s_e/16\}\quad(e\in J),
 \qquad
 X:=\sum_e\chi_e>\frac Q{160}.
\tag{2}
\]
For every edge \(e=\{u,v\}\) with \(\chi_e>0\), retain the source
points, support contacts, target base, actual contact point, and canonical
edge point
\[
 p_u^\flat,\ p_v^\flat,
 \qquad x_{e,u},\ x_{e,v},
 \qquad m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2},
\tag{3}
\]
\[
 \ell_e:=\frac{x_{e,u}+x_{e,v}}2,
 \qquad
 F_e^\flat:=\frac{p_u^\flat+p_v^\flat}{2}.
\tag{4}
\]
Define the paired center
\[
 \boxed{
 y_e:=\frac{F_e^\flat+\ell_e}{2}
 =\frac12\left(
   \frac{p_u^\flat+x_{e,u}}2+
   \frac{p_v^\flat+x_{e,v}}2
  \right).}
\tag{5}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{y_e\in\mathsf M_{64\chi_e}(C_{m_e}),}
\tag{6}
\]
and consequently its absolute fiber depth satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\delta_C(m_e,y_e)\ge64\gamma_d\chi_e.}
\tag{7}
\]

Put
\[
 t_*:=\frac{32\gamma_d}{17}
\tag{8}
\]
as in accepted fact `23a7bea6c72cc440`, and define the positive center
measure
\[
 \boxed{
 \nu_*:=t_*\sum_e\chi_e\delta_{(m_e,y_e)}.}
\tag{9}
\]
It is carried by
\(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), is halfspace-dominated by the
mixed-volume measure \(\mu_C\), and has mass
\[
 \boxed{|\nu_*|=t_*X.}
\tag{10}
\]
Let \(\rho_y,r_y\) be its intrinsic base and continuous residual ranks
in the sense of accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`.  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 r_y+1>
 \frac{275\gamma_d}{68}\,2^{n-\rho_y},}
\tag{11}
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{
 \dim\operatorname{aff}(\operatorname{supp}\nu_*)
 =\rho_y+r_y\ge n+1.}
\tag{12}
\]

Equations (6)-(12) do not authorize summing the individual masses
\(64\chi_e\) when several edges have the same physical target.  They do
not assert a collision-free target selection, a common-anchor star, a
full-rank product grid, a rank-one weight minorant, or an accepted final.

## Proof

Fix \(e=\{u,v\}\) with \(\chi_e>0\).  Then \(e\in J\), \(s_e>0\), and
the edge lies in one retained parity class \(\xi\).  Write
\[
 L_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\beta_z.
\tag{13}
\]
The retained-product definition in accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`
gives
\[
 s_e=\frac{\beta_u\beta_v}{L_\xi},
 \qquad
 0<\beta_u,\beta_v\le\frac{L_\xi}{4}.
\tag{14}
\]
In particular, all denominators below are positive.  From (2) and (14),
\[
 64\chi_e\le4s_e
 =\frac{4\beta_u\beta_v}{L_\xi}
 \le\beta_u,
 \qquad
 64\chi_e\le\beta_v.
\tag{15}
\]
Put
\[
 t:=64\chi_e>0.
\tag{16}
\]
Accepted fact `23a7bea6c72cc440` gives, at either endpoint
\(z\in\{u,v\}\),
\[
 0<\beta_z\le\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 p_z^\flat\in\mathsf M_{\beta_z}(C_z),
 \qquad
 x_{e,z}\in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
\tag{17}
\]
where \(a_z=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)>0\).  Choose literal witness
densities \(f_z^p,f_{e,z}^x\) for the two metronoid memberships in
(17), so
\[
 0\le f_z^p,f_{e,z}^x\le\mathbf1_{C_z},
 \qquad
 \int f_z^p=\beta_z,
 \qquad
 \int f_{e,z}^x=\frac{a_z}{2},
\tag{18}
\]
\[
 \int x f_z^p(x)\,dx=\beta_zp_z^\flat,
 \qquad
 \int x f_{e,z}^x(x)\,dx=\frac{a_z}{2}x_{e,z}.
\tag{19}
\]
Define
\[
 g_{e,z}:=
 \frac{t}{2\beta_z}f_z^p+
 \frac{t}{a_z}f_{e,z}^x.
\tag{20}
\]
Equations (15) and (17) give the pointwise audit
\[
 0\le g_{e,z}
 \le
 \left(\frac{t}{2\beta_z}+\frac{t}{a_z}\right)
 \mathbf1_{C_z}
 \le\left(\frac12+\frac14\right)\mathbf1_{C_z}
 <\mathbf1_{C_z}.
\tag{21}
\]
Moreover, (18)-(20) give
\[
 \int g_{e,z}=\frac t2+\frac t2=t,
\tag{22}
\]
and
\[
 \frac1t\int xg_{e,z}(x)\,dx
 =\frac{p_z^\flat+x_{e,z}}2.
\tag{23}
\]
Thus, with
\[
 q_{e,z}:=\frac{p_z^\flat+x_{e,z}}2,
\tag{24}
\]
one has
\[
 q_{e,z}\in\mathsf M_t(C_z)
 \qquad(z=u,v).
\tag{25}
\]
The endpoints \(u,v\) have the same parity, so
\(m_e=(u+v)/2\in\mathbb Z^n\).  Apply the positive-root metronoid
interpolation of accepted fact `dfd25e72c1f45a4e` to the two equal
masses in (25).  Equal roots remain equal under midpoint interpolation,
and therefore
\[
 \frac{q_{e,u}+q_{e,v}}2
 \in\mathsf M_t(C_{m_e}).
\tag{26}
\]
The left side of (26) is exactly \(y_e\) by (5), while \(t=64\chi_e\).
This proves (6).  Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` says that a point of
\(\mathsf M_t(C_{m_e})\) has absolute fiber depth at least
\(\gamma_dt\).  Equation (7) follows.

We next prove domination of the center measure.  Accepted fact
`23a7bea6c72cc440` proves that the balanced paired measure
\[
 \zeta_*:=t_*\sum_e\chi_e
 \left(
  \delta_{(m_e,\ell_e)}+
  \delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)}
 \right)
\tag{27}
\]
is carried by the mixed-integer body and is halfspace-dominated by
\(\mu_C\).  Let \(H\) be any closed ambient halfspace.  If
\((m_e,y_e)\in H\), then (5) and linearity of the defining halfspace
functional show that at least one of
\((m_e,\ell_e)\) and \((m_e,F_e^\flat)\) belongs to \(H\).  Hence,
edge by edge and including boundary equality,
\[
 \mathbf1_{\{(m_e,y_e)\in H\}}
 \le
 \mathbf1_{\{(m_e,\ell_e)\in H\}}+
 \mathbf1_{\{(m_e,F_e^\flat)\in H\}}.
\tag{28}
\]
Multiplying (28) by \(t_*\chi_e\), summing, and using (27) gives
\[
 \nu_*(H)\le\zeta_*(H)\le\mu_C(H).
\tag{29}
\]
Equation (6) also gives \((m_e,y_e)\in C\), so (9) is carried by the
mixed-integer body.  Since \(t_*>0\) and \(X>0\), it is nonzero, and
direct summation proves (10).

Finally apply the general calibrated dominated-measure screen in
accepted fact `23a7bea6c72cc440` to \(\nu_*\).  Equations (9)-(10) give
\[
 t_*\frac{2^{n-\rho_y}}{r_y+1}<\frac{128}{275}.
\tag{30}
\]
Substituting \(t_*=32\gamma_d/17\) and rearranging proves (11).
Accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` gives the intrinsic identity
\[
 \dim\operatorname{aff}(\operatorname{supp}\nu_*)=\rho_y+r_y.
\tag{31}
\]
Also \(\gamma_d>1/3\), as in the elementary binomial estimate recorded
in accepted fact `23a7bea6c72cc440`, so
\(275\gamma_d/68>275/204>1\).  Since \(r_y+1\le2^{r_y}\), equation
(11) yields
\[
 2^{\rho_y+r_y}
 \ge2^{\rho_y}(r_y+1)>2^n.
\tag{32}
\]
The exponent in (32) is an integer.  Equations (31)-(32) prove (12).
The final scope paragraph follows because every construction above was
performed edge by edge before only the separately dominated atomic center
measure was formed; no target-fiber witness densities were summed across
distinct edges.
