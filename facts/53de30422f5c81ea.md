## Statement

Let $P\subset\mathbb R^n$ be finite and nonempty. For each $p\in P$ let $R_p$ be the vertices of the polytope of probability vectors $\lambda$ on $P$ with mean $p$. Suppose $\ell$ is a positive array with $\ell_p\ge\sum_z\lambda_z\ell_z$ for all $p\in P$ and $\lambda\in R_p$. The one-sided tangent cone at $\ell$ of the positive sampled-concave arrays is the set $T_\ell$ of all $h$ satisfying $h_p\ge\sum_z\lambda_z h_z$ for every such row that is tight at $\ell$. Each $h\in T_\ell$ is realized by $\ell+th$ for all sufficiently small $t\ge0$. In particular it contains every concave sampled direction, both signs of every affine direction, and $\pm\ell$. If $w_z>0$, $\sum_z w_z=1$, and $\sum_z w_z\log\ell_z=0$, geometric normalization of $\ell+th$ gives $\dot\ell_z/\ell_z=h_z/\ell_z-\sum_q w_qh_q/\ell_q$.

## Proof

Necessity follows by differentiating each tight linear inequality along a feasible differentiable path, and also holds for limits of feasible secants. Conversely the finitely many inactive linear inequalities and the finitely many coordinates $\ell_z$ have strictly positive slack, so each fixed allowed $h$ yields a positive feasible ray segment. The roof characterization of the sampled-concave cone is given by the upper-envelope construction in 7227bbfcd12ad3d2: take the upper endpoints of the hull of the intervals $[\ell_z-1,\ell_z]$, with the same construction for the new array. Affine directions and $\pm\ell$ give equality at every tight row. Divide the raw ray by $\exp(\sum_z w_z\log(\ell_z+th_z))$ and differentiate its logarithm to obtain the formula.
