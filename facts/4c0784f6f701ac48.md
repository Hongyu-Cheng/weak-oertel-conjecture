## Statement

Let $\mu_t$ be the mixed measure of an actual compatible outward shift of just one facet $G$ of a sampled polytope by distance $t\ge0$, at base $p$, in a fixed finite positive sampled family. Put $M=\mu_0(\mathbb R^{n+d})$, $A=\mathcal H^{d-1}(G)$, and let $Q=\beta M$ be the old global raw maximum. At each old deepest base $p_i$, select finitely many old mass-$Q$ caps $H_{ij}=\{a_{ij}\cdot z+v_{ij}\cdot x\ge b_{ij}\}$ with unit continuous normals and weights $\rho_{ij}>0$ satisfying
$$
\sum_j\rho_{ij}v_{ij}=0,\qquad
\sum_j\rho_{ij}(b_{ij}-a_{ij}\cdot p_i)=0.
$$
At every other base assume a fixed finite whole-plane cover with each cap of mass strictly below $Q$. For each critical cap assume the old cap mass as a function of threshold has two-sided derivative $-S_{ij}$ at $b_{ij}$, with $S_{ij}>0$, and its cap plane meets the lifted $G$ in $\mathcal H^{d-1}$-measure zero. Set $\kappa_{ij}=(\rho_{ij}/S_{ij})/\sum_l(\rho_{il}/S_{il})$ and $\sigma_i=A^{-1}\sum_j\kappa_{ij}\int_G\mathbf1_{H_{ij}}(p,x)\,d\mathcal H^{d-1}(x)$. For the actual normalized maximum $D(t)$,
$$
\limsup_{t\downarrow0}\frac{D(t)-\beta}{t}
\le\frac{A}{M}\max_i(\sigma_i-\beta).
$$
Thus $\sigma_i<\beta$ for all chosen groups gives strict descent. At a local minimum every eligible facet and selected system of circuits has some group with $\sigma_i\ge\beta$.

## Proof

Write $A_{ij}=\int_G\mathbf1_{H_{ij}}(p,x)\,d\mathcal H^{d-1}(x)$, equal to the outward trace coefficient by the null-intersection assumption. Set $T_i=A\sigma_i$ and shift the $ij$-th threshold by $t\dot b_{ij}$ with $\dot b_{ij}=(A_{ij}-T_i)/S_{ij}$. The definition of $\kappa_{ij}$ gives $\sum_j\rho_{ij}\dot b_{ij}=0$. The shifted signed margins therefore still sum with the positive weights to zero at every point in the plane over $p_i$, so at least one cap contains each point there.

At the moved threshold the old measure contributes $Q-tS_{ij}\dot b_{ij}+o(t)$. In the one-moving-facet specialization of the strip proof of 7a5ea4f8a4c5094c on the changed fiber, adding $t\dot b_{ij}$ to the threshold does not affect the limiting indicator off the cap plane on $G$. The exceptional set there is null, so the added measure is $tA_{ij}+o(t)$ by the same domination. Thus each shifted cap in group $i$ has mass $Q+tT_i+o(t)$. The unchanged covers on all other planes keep their strict gaps since the full added volume is $O(t)$. A point in a containing closed cap has raw depth at most that cap's mass. Taking the finite maximum over critical groups and dividing by $M+tA+o(t)$ yields the estimate and descent assertion.
