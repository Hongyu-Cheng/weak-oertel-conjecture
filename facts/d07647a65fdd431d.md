## Statement

Let $C=[0,1]^n\times K$ with $n,d\ge1$ and $K$ a $d$-simplex of volume $v$. Use $D=K-K$, which is both same-base difference bodies, and let $M=2^n v$, $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. At cap budget $\gamma_d v$ and cap count $2^n(d+1)$, the exact width-normalized hinge value of every full cover is $M\gamma_d d/(d+1)$, and it is attained.

For any such cover, in the normalization $h_D(u_i)=1$ there is a width contact stress with objective multiplier $\alpha_0=1$ and all cap-budget multipliers zero. On each fiber $p$ use the simplex centroid $g$, probability weights $1/(d+1)$ on that fiber's canonical caps, and $\eta_p=(d+1)\gamma_d v$. For the cap of vertex $v_j$ take $R_j=v_j-g_j$ with $g_j$ the centroid of its opposite facet. If $c_i,P_i,m_i$ are raw cap mass and moments, the stress identities are
$$
c_i=\sum_p\eta_p\lambda_{pi},\qquad
P_i=\sum_p\eta_p\lambda_{pi}p,\qquad
m_i=\sum_p\eta_p\lambda_{pi}g+\Psi_iR_j,
$$
where $\Psi_i=\int_S(L_i)_+\,d\mu$ in the width representatives, $R_j$ maximizes $u_i\cdot v$ over $D$, and the fiber weights give zero minimax margin with genuine contacts at $g$.

## Proof

Rescale each margin to Euclidean unit continuous normal, which leaves every cap and its mass unchanged. The exact-count product theorem gives one cap of each canonical type in each fiber, zero other-fiber masses and an explicit realization. If $\beta_j$ is the barycentric coordinate of $v_j$, then $h_{K-K}(\nabla\beta_j)=1$ since the range of $\beta_j$ on $K$ is $[0,1]$. Thus the width representative on its own fiber has margin $\beta_j-1/(d+1)$. Integrating its barycentric tail yields $\Psi_i=v d\gamma_d/(d+1)^2$. Summing gives the stated value.

Each own-fiber cap has mass $c_i=\gamma_d v$ and base moment $c_i p$. Its continuous centroid is $g+(v_j-g)/(d+1)$ by homothety about $v_j$ of ratio $d/(d+1)$. Since $g=(v_j+d g_j)/(d+1)$, its continuous moment is $c_i g+\Psi_i(v_j-g_j)$. The vector $R_j=v_j-g_j$ attains width one, because $v_j$ and $g_j$ realize the maximum and minimum of $\beta_j$. The weights produce the cap masses and base moments and have resultant zero by $\sum_j\nabla\beta_j=0$. Their averaged margin on the fiber is identically zero, while all local margins vanish at $g$ and all off-fiber cap margins there are strictly negative by zero volume and the interiority of $g$. Thus the weights attain the minimax value zero and the asserted identities hold with actual contacts and zero budget multipliers.
