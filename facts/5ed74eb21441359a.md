## Proof

Reflection about $(z,a)\in S$ sends the other base to $-1$ or $2$, absent from $S$. The only overlap contribution is therefore the area of $K\cap(2a-K)$ from the own fiber, whose unique maximizing $a$ and value are given by pk-w35-reflected-overlap-polygon. In each case the other base contributes zero, since the maximum of $3x-y$ on $K$ is $8$. The own-fiber trace is exactly the planar cap from the predecessor. Its area divided by $18$ gives the displayed fraction and gap. By the binary-product depth identity of 2acc8bc803acf1c0 with $n=1,d=2$, the normalized mixed maximum equals half the ordinary normalized maximum on $K$. The planar predecessor proves the latter is at least $4/9$.
