## Statement

Fix a compact canonical hull $C$ with positive mixed fiber mass, and write $\langle\cdot\rangle$ for its mixed probability integral over the sampled fibers. Let $d\ge1$, $N=d+1$, $r\ge d$ and let $L$ be affine and positive on $C$ with $\langle L^{-r}\rangle=1$. For every affine $\phi$, both signs of sufficiently small $t$ are allowed in the normalized perturbation
$$
L_t=(L+t\phi)\,\langle(L+t\phi)^{-r}\rangle^{1/r}.
$$
Then $\dot L=\phi-L\langle L^{-r-1}\phi\rangle$ at $t=0$. At every fixed mark with positive projective energy, for $f_L=(LE/M)^{1/N}$ with the energy $E$ and mass $M$ fixed, the derivative is
$$
\frac{\dot f_L}{f_L}=\frac1N\left(\frac{\phi}{L}-\langle L^{-r-1}\phi\rangle\right).
$$

## Proof

The positive minimum of $L$ and boundedness of $\phi$ on $C$ give a uniform positive lower bound for $L+t\phi$ at small $|t|$. The scaling in $L_t$ makes its inverse-power mean one exactly. Differentiating under the fixed probability integral is justified by that lower bound. Since $\langle L^{-r}\rangle=1$, the derivative of the scaling factor at zero is $-\langle L^{-r-1}\phi\rangle$. The product rule gives $\dot L$, and differentiating the positive fixed-mark factor $(L_t E/M)^{1/N}$ gives the second formula.
