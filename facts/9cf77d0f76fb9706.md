## Statement

Let $D\subset\mathbb R^d$ be a full-dimensional compact convex body, $u\ne0$, $m=\min_D u\cdot x$, $h=\max_D u\cdot x$, and $m\le a<b<h$. Write $D_t=D\cap\{u\cdot x\ge t\}$. If $D_b=\lambda D_a+v$ with $\lambda>0$, the exposed maximum of $u$ on $D$ is a unique point $p$, $\lambda=(h-b)/(h-a)$, $v=(1-\lambda)p$, and $D_a=\operatorname{conv}(p,D\cap\{u\cdot x=a\})$. Conversely that cone identity gives the displayed positive homothety for every $a<b<h$.

## Proof

The band $a<u\cdot x<b$ meets the interior of $D$, since the projection of the interior is $(m,h)$. Thus the volumes strictly decrease and $\lambda<1$. Both truncations have the same top face $F$, so $F=\lambda F+v$. The diameters give $F=\{p\}$ and $v=(1-\lambda)p$. Translate $p$ to zero. In the height coordinate $t=h-u\cdot x$ before translation, let $L=D_a-p$ and set $A=h-a$, $B=h-b$. Then $L\cap\{t\le B\}=\lambda L$, and the maximal heights give $B=\lambda A$.

In a fixed linear transverse splitting, let $S(t)$ be the transverse coordinates of the section at height $t$. For $0<t\le A$, the truncation identity gives $S(\lambda t)=\lambda S(t)$. Convexity with the apex at zero gives $S(s)/s\supseteq S(t)/t$ for $0<s<t\le A$. The normalized sections agree at $\lambda t$ and $t$, hence throughout each interval between them. Taking $t=A,\lambda A,\lambda^2A,\ldots$ proves $S(t)=(t/A)S(A)$ for every $t>0$ in this range. The zero-height section is the singleton apex. These are precisely the sections of the stated cone. Conversely, scaling that cone about its apex by $(h-b)/(h-a)$ produces the higher truncation.
