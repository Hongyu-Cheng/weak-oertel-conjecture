## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and assume the hypotheses and canonical normalizations of accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`. Let \(r=(r_e)\) be its half-strength canonical product reservoir and put
\[
 Q:=|r|=\sum_e r_e>0,
 \qquad
 \bar\nu_r:=\sum_e r_e\,\delta_{(m_e,\bar c_e)}.
\]
Let
\[
 \rho_{\bar\nu_r}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:r_e>0\}
\]
and let its intrinsic continuous residual rank be
\[
 r_{\bar\nu_r}:=
 \min_{a:\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:r_e>0\}\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{\bar c_e-a(m_e):r_e>0\}.
\]
Use the nonnegative capacities of that accepted fact and put
\[
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\]
If
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100},
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\bar\nu_r}+1
 >200\,2^{n-\rho_{\bar\nu_r}}},
\tag{1}
\]
and consequently
\[
 \boxed{
 d\ge r_{\bar\nu_r}
 \ge200\,2^{n-\rho_{\bar\nu_r}}
 \ge200}.
\tag{2}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` proves that every nonzero positive submeasure \(\sigma\le\bar\nu_r\) satisfies
\[
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 \ge2^{n-\rho_\sigma}\frac{|\sigma|}{r_\sigma+1}.
\tag{3}
\]
Take \(\sigma=\bar\nu_r\). It is nonzero because \(|\bar\nu_r|=Q>0\). Thus
\[
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 \ge2^{n-\rho_{\bar\nu_r}}
       \frac{Q}{r_{\bar\nu_r}+1}.
\tag{4}
\]
The same accepted fact proves
\[
 h_{\max}(C)\ge2^{-n}M_{\mathrm{off}}(C),
\]
and hence, in the canonical normalization of \(D\),
\[
 D=2^nh_{\max}(C)\ge M_{\mathrm{off}}(C).
\tag{5}
\]
The quantities \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\) and \(D\) are two distinct nonnegative numerical summands of \(\mathcal A(C)\). Therefore (4)--(5) give
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)+D
 \ge2M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 \ge2^{n-\rho_{\bar\nu_r}+1}
       \frac{Q}{r_{\bar\nu_r}+1}.
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (6) with \(\mathcal A(C)<Q/100\), and cancelling \(Q>0\), yields
\[
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\bar\nu_r}+1}}
 {r_{\bar\nu_r}+1}<\frac1{100}.
\]
This is exactly (1).

For the integer consequence, the base support lies in \(\mathbb R^n\), so \(\rho_{\bar\nu_r}\le n\). Moreover, the zero affine map is admissible in the definition of \(r_{\bar\nu_r}\), and all residual vectors lie in \(\mathbb R^d\), so \(r_{\bar\nu_r}\le d\). Since \(r_{\bar\nu_r}+1\) and \(200\,2^{n-\rho_{\bar\nu_r}}\) are integers, the strict inequality (1) implies
\[
 r_{\bar\nu_r}
 \ge200\,2^{n-\rho_{\bar\nu_r}}.
\]
Together with \(r_{\bar\nu_r}\le d\) and \(2^{n-\rho_{\bar\nu_r}}\ge1\), this proves (2).

Equation (5) is the only lower bound on the single \(D\)-summand used in this proof. No lower bound on \(D\) from another measure, selector, or packet is added to it.
