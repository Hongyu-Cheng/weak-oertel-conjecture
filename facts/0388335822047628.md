## Statement

Let $C$ be a compact convex subset of a finite-dimensional Euclidean affine space, and let $L:C\to(0,\infty)$ be continuous. Let $\nu_i$ be finitely many positive measures on $C$ of masses $m_i>0$ and barycenters $Y_i$. In one group of these rows, suppose $B$ is affine, $B(Y_i)=L(Y_i)$ and $B=L$ almost everywhere for each grouped $\nu_i$. Let $W$ be positive affine on $C$ with $W(Y_i)=L(Y_i)$ at all grouped marks. Define $\eta=L\sum_i\nu_i$, $\widehat\eta=W\sum_i\nu_i$, $g=W/L$ and $s=\sum_i m_iL(Y_i)$, where all sums run over the group. With $\operatorname{Cov}_i$ the covariance of the probability $\nu_i/m_i$ about $Y_i$ in the full affine space and $b=\nabla(W-B)$, one has
$$
\eta(C)=\widehat\eta(C)=s,\qquad
\int w\,d(\widehat\eta-\eta)(w)=\sum_i m_i\operatorname{Cov}_i b.
$$
If $s>0$, the finite equal-mass entropies are
$$
D(\eta\|\widehat\eta)=-\int\log g\,d\eta\ge0,\qquad
D(\widehat\eta\|\eta)=\int g\log g\,d\eta\ge0.
$$
Each equality holds exactly when $W=L$ almost everywhere for $\eta$. For an empty group all measures and integrals are zero.

## Proof

For each row, affinity and its barycenter give $\int W\,d\nu_i=m_iW(Y_i)$. The same holds for $B$, and $L=B$ on that row almost everywhere. This gives the two equal masses. On the same row, $W-L=(W-B)(w)=b\cdot(w-Y_i)$ almost everywhere. In $\int w(W-L)\,d\nu_i$, the part with $w$ replaced by $Y_i$ vanishes by the barycenter identity. The remainder is $m_i\operatorname{Cov}_i b$. Summing proves the full moment formula, including base coordinates. Its value is independent of the affine origin because the two masses agree.

Since $\widehat\eta=g\eta$, the displayed formulas follow from the definition $D(\xi\|\zeta)=\int\log(d\xi/d\zeta)\,d\xi$ for equal masses. The ratio $g$ is bounded above and away from zero by positivity and compactness. The average of $g$ under $\eta/s$ is one, so Jensen for $-\log$ and for $t\mapsto t\log t$ gives both inequalities and the common equality condition by strict convexity.
