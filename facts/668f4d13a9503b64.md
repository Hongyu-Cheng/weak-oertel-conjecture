## Statement

On the mixed source of $C=[-1,1]\times[-4,4]$, use four closed caps $x\ge f_i(z)$ with $f=(0,2z-1,2z+1,2z)$ and four closed caps $x\le g_i(z)$ with $g=(z,z-1,z+1,3z)$. With coefficient $1/4$ on every cap, the weighted incidence is one almost everywhere on each integer fiber. Every restricted cut is proper and no opposite-oriented pair has the same ambient hyperplane. The cap masses in the two groups are $(12,15,9,12)$ and $(12,9,15,12)$, with total source mass $24$. The original pair $x\ge2z-1$ and $x\le z+1$ covers the whole source.

## Proof

At the bases $-1,0,1$ the $f$ and $g$ threshold multisets both equal, respectively, $\{-3,-2,-1,0\}$, $\{-1,0,0,1\}$ and $\{0,1,2,3\}$, with multiplicities. Each threshold is strictly between $-4$ and $4$. Away from the finite threshold set, the number of satisfied lower cuts plus the number of satisfied upper cuts equals four, and the weighted incidence is one. The slopes of the $f$ functions belong to $\{0,2\}$, while the $g$ slopes belong to $\{1,3\}$, so no two ambient boundary lines of opposite orientation coincide.

Each lower cap has mass $\sum_{z=-1}^1(4-f_i(z))$ and each upper cap mass $\sum_{z=-1}^1(4+g_i(z))$, giving the two displayed vectors. The source has three intervals of length eight. Finally $2z-1\le z+1$ throughout $[-1,1]$, so the specified original pair covers the whole rectangle and in particular its mixed source.
