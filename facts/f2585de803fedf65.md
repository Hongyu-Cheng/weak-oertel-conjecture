## Statement

Let $Q\subset\mathbb R^d$ be nonempty compact convex, let $p\in\mathbb R^d$ and put $Z=\operatorname{conv}(\{p\}\cup Q)$. For $y=(1-t)p+tq$ with $q\in Q$ and $0<t<1$, write $N_L^{\min}(x)=\{u:u\cdot(x'-x)\ge0\ \forall x'\in L\}$. Then $N_Z^{\min}(y)=N_Q^{\min}(q)\cap(q-p)^\perp$.

## Proof

If $u$ minimizes at $y$ over $Z$, the values at $p$ and $q$ are each at least $u\cdot y$, and their strictly weighted average equals $u\cdot y$. Both are therefore equal to it. Minimization over the subset $Q$ is then attained at $q$, and $u\cdot(q-p)=0$. Conversely, if $q$ minimizes over $Q$ and the endpoint values agree, the common value is a lower bound for $u$ on $\{p\}\cup Q$ and hence on its convex hull, with equality at $y$.
