## Statement

There is a sequence of six-cap framed whole-space covers in dimension two of a single source $K=[-1,1]^2$, with all cap masses strictly below $2|K|/3$ and total hinges tending to $41/16$, whose unique whole-space optimal contact diverges. The six caps include two variable-normal caps and the four fixed frame normals. This is a family of feasible covers.

## Proof

For $0<\varepsilon\le1/8$, set $s=\sqrt{1+\varepsilon^2}$ and $B=s/(4\varepsilon)$. Use two margins $x$ and $(-x+\varepsilon y)/s+1/4$ and the four frame margins $x-B,-x-B,y-B,-y-B$. Every normal has length one. The three margins $x,(-x+\varepsilon y)/s+1/4,-y-B$ have positive weights proportional to $1/s,1,\varepsilon/s$, with identically zero weighted sum. Thus at least one margin is nonnegative everywhere. At $(0,-B)$ those three margins are zero and the others are negative. A point where all margins are nonpositive must satisfy $x\le0$, $x\ge\varepsilon y+s/4$ and $y\ge-B$, which force exactly $(0,-B)$. Hence the whole-space minimax value is zero and that is its unique contact.

Since $B>1$, the frame caps have zero source mass and hinge. The first cap has mass $2$ and hinge $1$. The second boundary $x=\varepsilon y+s/4$ lies strictly between $-1$ and $1$ for every $y\in[-1,1]$. At each such $y$ its cap interval has length $1+\varepsilon y+s/4$ and hinge $(1+\varepsilon y+s/4)^2/(2s)$. Integration gives
$$
c_2=2(1+s/4),\qquad \Psi_2=((1+s/4)^2+\varepsilon^2/3)/s.
$$
The mass ratio $(1+s/4)/2$ is strictly below $2/3$ because $s<4/3$. As $\varepsilon\downarrow0$, the total hinge tends to $1+25/16=41/16$, while $B\to\infty$.
