## Source

- [9cc7f272d6dd1c14](../candidates/9cc7f272d6dd1c14.json), §§4–5, (8)–(9), two exact horizontal-anchor failures.

## Statement

For $K=\{(x,z):a\le z\le b,\ |x|\le r(z)\}$ with positive concave $r$ and $a<0<b$, write $p_0,C_0$ for the area fraction of $z\ge0$ and the coarea of $z=0$ divided by $|K|$. Write $p,C$ for the analogous records of $z+cx\ge0$, using the raw coarea $\mathcal H^1(K\cap\{z+cx=0\})/\|(c,1)\|_2$. The correction $p-[(p_0-4/9)/C_0]C$ can be below $4/9$ with an actual centroid-zero oblique section:

- For the piecewise affine graph through $(-5,5),(-4,11),(2,12)$, take $c=3/17$. The records are $(p_0,C_0,p,C)=(71/231,35/231,10/33,34/231)$, and the deficit below $4/9$ is $40/4851$.
- For the piecewise affine graph through $(-5,5),(-3,9),(-1,12),(3,10)$, take $c=1/3$. The records are $(129/316,23/158,65/158,18/158)$, and the deficit is $77/16353$. The positive $z$ direction points to the virtual apex of the affine piece at the source in this example.

## Proof

The first profile has decreasing slopes $6,1/6$, total area $2(8+69)=154$, and $r(z)=35/3+z/6$ on $[-4,2]$. Thus $p_0=2\int_0^2 r(z)dz/154=71/231$ and $C_0=2r(0)/154=35/231$. The section has endpoints $(34/3,-2)$ and $(-34/3,2)$. The lower point is on a side, below it the cap is empty, and the upper point is on the top facet. In fact $r(z)+(17/3)z$ is strictly increasing below $-2$ on both pieces and vanishes at $-2$; on $[-2,2]$ the threshold $-(17/3)z$ lies in the interval $[-r(z),r(z)]$. Hence the two opposite endpoints give a centered chord, and the captured interval length on $[-2,2]$ is $35/3+(35/6)z$. This integrates to $140/3$, giving $p=10/33$. Coarea is the length of the projection to the $x$ axis, so $C=(68/3)/154=34/231$. The horizontal scalar is $-19/21$, and $p-[(p_0-4/9)/C_0]C=2116/4851$. The virtual apex of the affine piece at $0$ points toward negative $z$ in this case.

The second profile has decreasing slopes $2,3/2,-1/2$, total area $2(14+21+44)=158$, and $r(z)=23/2-z/2$ on $[-1,3]$. Its affine extension vanishes at $z=23$. Direct horizontal integration gives $p_0=129/316$ and $C_0=23/158$. The oblique chord has opposite endpoints $(9,-3),(-9,3)$, the former at a side vertex and the latter on the top facet. The expression $r(z)+3z$ is negative below $-3$, and $-3z$ lies in $[-r(z),r(z)]$ on $[-3,3]$. The captured length there is $r(z)+3z$, with integral $21+44=65$. Projecting the chord onto the $x$ axis gives length $18$. Thus $p=65/158$, $C=18/158$ and the horizontal scalar is $-103/414$. The corrected fraction is $799/1817$. Subtraction from $4/9$ gives the two stated deficits.
