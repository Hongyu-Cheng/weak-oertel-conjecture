## Statement

Let $P$ be a finite nonempty base set with full-dimensional compact convex fibers $K_z\subset\mathbb R^d$, and use the measure $\mu=\sum_{z\in P}\delta_z\otimes(\mathbf1_{K_z}\,dx)$. Suppose finitely many affine maps $T_r(z,x)=(z,A_rx+B_rz+c_r)$ satisfy $\sum_r w_r(T_r)_\#\mu=\mu$ for positive weights summing to one. Suppose they all fix $(p,y)$ with $p\in P$ and $y\in\operatorname{int}K_p$. Then each $T_r$ maps every measured fiber bijectively onto itself. If additionally $\sum_r w_rA_r=0$, the true centroids satisfy $g_z=(\sum_r w_rB_r)z+\sum_r w_rc_r$ on $P$.

## Proof

The support of $\mu$ is the closed union of the full fibers. Positivity of the mixture implies each pushforward is supported in that union. By continuity, the image of every support point belongs to the support of its pushforward, so $T_r$ maps each fiber into itself. Since each map fixes the base, restricting stationarity to $p$ gives stationarity of uniform volume on $K_p$. Apply the same-source interior-fixed affine rigidity lemma to obtain $|\det A_r|=1$ for each $r$.

At every other base the same determinant gives equality of the volumes of the included image and the original body. A proper closed convex subset of a full-dimensional convex body has strictly smaller volume: if it misses a point, a neighborhood disjoint from the subset intersects the interior of the body. Hence all the fiber inclusions are equalities. Each resulting fiber automorphism preserves its uniform measure and fixes its centroid, so $g_z=A_rg_z+B_rz+c_r$. Averaging the identities gives the claimed affine graph when the averaged linear part vanishes.
