## Statement

For $d\ge1$, let $D_j$ be convex bodies containing zero in their interiors, and let finitely many indexed fibers have the form $K_{p,j}=y_{p,j}+l_{p,j}D_j$ with $l_{p,j}>0$. Suppose positive affine denominators on those fibers have the common form
$$
A_j(p,y_{p,j}+l_{p,j}\xi)=l_{p,j}(1+c_j\cdot\xi)>0 \qquad (\xi\in D_j).
$$
Fix $p_0$ and let $a_{p,j}=l_{p,j}/l_{p_0,j}$ and $T_{p,j}(X)=y_{p,j}+a_{p,j}(X-y_{p_0,j})$. For any common $M_j>0$, define $\nu^A_{p,j}=dX/(M_jA_j(p,X))$ restricted to $K_{p,j}$. Then
$$
\nu^A_{p,j}=a_{p,j}^{d-1}(T_{p,j})_\#\nu^A_{p_0,j}.
$$
Assume $y_{p,j}\to y_p$, $l_{p,j}\to l_p>0$ and $\lambda_j\downarrow0$ (or simply $\lambda_j>0$ and $\lambda_j\to0$). Mark each measure by $r^A_{p,j}(X)=(-\lambda_j\log A_j(p,X))_+$. Along any subsequence with simultaneous finite weak limits of the marked measures on $\mathbb R^d\times[0,\infty)$, writing $a_p=l_p/l_{p_0}$ and $T_p(X)=y_p+a_p(X-y_{p_0})$, those limits satisfy
$$
\widehat\nu^A_p=a_p^{d-1}(T_p,\operatorname{id})_\#\widehat\nu^A_{p_0}.
$$
In particular the equality holds after restriction to every Borel positive-rate tail.

## Proof

The map $T_{p,j}$ takes the whole reference fiber onto $K_{p,j}$ with volume Jacobian $a_{p,j}^d$. The common pointed denominator gives $A_j(p,T_{p,j}X)=a_{p,j}A_j(p_0,X)$, so the reciprocal contributes $a_{p,j}^{-1}$. This proves the first identity. On the marked product space the exact map is $(X,r)\mapsto(T_{p,j}X,(r-\lambda_j\log a_{p,j})_+)$ if $r$ is the untruncated log rate; expressed using the old truncated rate, its output differs from that rate by at most $\lambda_j|\log a_{p,j}|$ by the Lipschitz property of the positive part. This bound tends to zero uniformly. The physical maps converge uniformly on compact sets. Finite weak convergence of the reference marked measures gives uniform tightness and bounded total mass, so testing bounded continuous functions, using uniform continuity on compact sets and then letting the tightness error tend to zero, proves the limit identity. Both sides have the identical rate coordinate in this limit, which gives the tail identity including at atoms.
