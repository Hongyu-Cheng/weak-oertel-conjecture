## Statement

Let $d\ge2$ and let $v_0,\ldots,v_d$ span $\mathbb R^d$ and sum to zero. Suppose actual points $P_j,P_l$ in the same convex body $A$ lie in distinct strict residual regions $R_k=\{x\in A:v_i\cdot x<-1\text{ for all }i\ne k\}$. Put $m=d+1$, $\alpha=-1-v_l\cdot P_j$, $\beta=-1-v_j\cdot P_l$, and for $i\notin\{j,l\}$ put $a_i=-1-v_i\cdot P_j$, $b_i=-1-v_i\cdot P_l$. All are positive. With $H_k=1+v_k\cdot P_k$, $\Delta=P_j-P_l$, $D_j=v_j\cdot\Delta$, $D_l=-v_l\cdot\Delta$, the actual longitudinal scores satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_j&=m+\alpha+\sum_i a_i,& H_l&=m+\beta+\sum_i b_i,\
D_j&=H_j+\beta,&D_l&=H_l+\alpha,\
M_0&=m+\alpha+\beta+\sum_i\max(a_i,b_i).
\end{aligned}
$$
The direction parameters $\sigma_k=\sum_i(v_i\cdot e_k)_+$ for $e_j=\Delta/D_j$ and $e_l=-\Delta/D_l$ are $\sigma_j=M_0/D_j$ and $\sigma_l=M_0/D_l$. Both are at least one. The equality $\sigma_j=1$ holds exactly when $b_i\le a_i$ for all shared indices, and $\sigma_l=1$ exactly when $a_i\le b_i$ for all of them. In particular both equal one exactly when all shared deficits coincide.

## Proof

The zero-sum relation evaluated at each endpoint gives the two $H_k$ identities. The definitions of the other endpoint deficits give $D_j=H_j+\beta$ and $D_l=H_l+\alpha$, both greater than $m$. The increments $v_i\cdot\Delta$ are $D_j,-D_l$ in the longitudinal indices and $b_i-a_i$ in the shared indices. Their total is zero, so the sum of positive increments equals the sum of absolute negative increments. The first is $D_j+\sum_i(b_i-a_i)_+=m+\alpha+\beta+\sum_i\max(a_i,b_i)=M_0$. Dividing this common sum by the two positive longitudinal increments gives the two stated parameters, and the two excesses over their denominators are the respective sums of positive shared differences. All computations use the same actual segment between the two endpoints.
