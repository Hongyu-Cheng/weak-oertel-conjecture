## Statement

Let $T$ be a nondegenerate planar triangle with an interior mark and three balanced spanning cap normals. Suppose the through-mark caps strictly isolate its three distinct vertices. Let $C_T$ be the hull of the six cutting-ray endpoints. For any convex body $K$ with $C_T\subseteq K\subseteq T$, the full cutting chords are the same as those of $T$. With $k=|K|$, cap areas $Q_i$ and corresponding chord lengths divided by the respective normal norms $\sigma_i$, let $f_i=2\sqrt{kQ_i}/(\sigma_i\sum_jQ_j/\sigma_j)$. Then $\sum_i f_i^2+f_1f_2f_3\le4$ and each $\Phi_i=f_i+(f_j+f_l)^2/4\le2$, with all inequalities strict when $K\ne T$.

## Proof

On each edge joining two captured vertices the third cap margin is negative, so the incident cap margins sum to a positive number there. Their zeros are distinct interior edge points, and their positive portions overlap between them. The six-ray hull therefore removes precisely three vertex corners, up to null boundaries. Each corner lies in its own unique sector, since the other two margins are nonpositive at its vertices. The hull contains the full cutting chords, which pass through the interior mark, and has the mark interior because its six rays surround the mark. Hence $K$ preserves every chord, and all its cap areas are positive. If $E_i$ is the actual missing area in the corresponding unique corner, the raw data of $K$ are those of $T$ with total area reduced by $\sum_iE_i$ and cap $i$ reduced by $E_i$. The fixed-section premise is exact.

The strict triangle hypothesis gives $\Psi(T)\le4$ by 33488d4938a6ee4a. Apply the global planar cubic unique-loss lemma to the actual positive endpoint data. If $K\ne T$, compactness and strict separation of an omitted point give a positive-area region omitted from $T$, so $\Psi(K)<4$. Finally $0<f_i<2$ follows from positivity and $\Psi\le4$. With $g=f_j+f_l$, the bound $f_jf_l\le g^2/4$ gives $\Psi=f_i^2+g^2-(2-f_i)f_jf_l\ge f_i^2+(2+f_i)g^2/4$. Dividing $4-f_i^2$ by $2+f_i$ gives every claimed row bound, and strict cubic implies strict rows.
