## Statement

Let $P=\operatorname{conv}(P)\cap\mathbb Z^n$ be nonempty and finite, let $w_z>0$ for $z\in P$, and let $\phi$ be strictly convex on $\operatorname{conv}(P)$. In the fractional allocation polytope
$$
c_{jz}\ge0,\qquad
\sum_{j\in P}c_{jz}=w_z,\qquad
\sum_{z\in P}(z-j)c_{jz}=0\quad(j\in P)
$$
write $m_j=\sum_{z\in P}c_{jz}$. For every minimizer of $\sum_{j\in P}m_j\phi(j)$, the set $V=\{j:m_j>0\}$ is both the vertex set and the set of all lattice points of $\operatorname{conv}(V)$.

In the rectangle of the minimum-variance partition obstruction, every exhaustive positive two-piece integer-centroid convex partition with such a target set, allowing null mixed overlap, has target bases $0,1$ and is one of the two shallow partitions established there.

## Proof

The polytope is nonempty by the diagonal allocation and compact by its column bounds. If a lattice point $j$ is a convex combination $\sum_{a}\lambda_a j_a$ of at least two distinct positive targets with all $\lambda_a>0$, completeness puts $j$ in $P$. Choose $0<\varepsilon\le\min_a m_{j_a}/\lambda_a$. Move the subrow $\varepsilon\lambda_a c_{j_a z}/m_{j_a}$ out of each row $j_a$ into row $j$, for every source $z$. Each removed part has its row's base mean, and the combined row added at $j$ has mean $j$. Hence column sums, nonnegativity and all row moments are preserved, also if $j$ was one of the supplying rows. The objective decreases by
$$
\varepsilon\left(\sum_a\lambda_a\phi(j_a)-\phi(j)\right)>0
$$
by strict Jensen. This excludes any nonvertex positive target and any additional lattice point in the hull.

For the rectangle, one positive target cannot have base mean $1/2$. Two distinct integer vertices of an empty lattice segment are adjacent, and their interval must contain $1/2$, so the only possible pair is $0,1$. Conservation gives mass two at each. The objective for the quadratic is then two, so the minimum-variance partition obstruction classifies these pieces and gives the shallow centroids and contained caps.
