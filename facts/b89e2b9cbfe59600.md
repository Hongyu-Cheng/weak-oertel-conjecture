## Statement

Let $Q=\operatorname{conv}\{(1,1),(3,-2),(4,3)\}$, let $K=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:x\ge0,\ \sum_i x_i\le1\}$ for $d\ge1$, and for $R\ge0$ let $C$ be the compact convex body with fibers $K_z=(R+t(z))K$ over $z\in Q$, where $t(z)=\min\{14,17-z_1-2z_2\}$. In index order from zero its nine positive integer bases are
$$
(1,1),(2,0),(2,1),(3,-2),(3,-1),(3,0),(3,1),(3,2),(4,3),
$$
with values $(14,14,13,14,14,14,12,10,7)$ of $t$. The volume-maximal parity roots, with ties broken by minimum base Euclidean norm, are $0,1,2,5$. Every edge of the corresponding root-midpoint forest is an exact Minkowski midpoint of the original child fiber and its designated parity root.

Give each actual centroid graph point its original fiber volume as atomic weight and divide by the total. The maximum of closed-halfspace depth over graph points is exactly
$$
\frac{2(R+14)^d}{5(R+14)^d+(R+13)^d+(R+12)^d+(R+10)^d+(R+7)^d}.
$$
For $d=1$ this equals $2(R+14)/(9R+112)$, equals $1/4$ at $R=0$ and is strictly below $1/4$ for $R>0$. At $R=21$ it is $70/301=10/43$, with gap $3/172$ from $1/4$. For each $d$ it tends to $2/9$ as $R\to\infty$, and the choice $R=63d-7$ already gives strict failure of $1/4$.

## Proof

The three edge inequalities of $Q$ are $3z_1+2z_2\ge5$, $5z_1-z_2\le17$, and $-2z_1+3z_2\le1$. Their integer solutions at $z_1=1,2,3,4$ have second coordinates respectively $\{1\},\{0,1\},\{-2,-1,0,1,2\},\{3\}$. The roof is the minimum of two affine functions and is positive on $Q$, since it is at least seven at each vertex and concave. Equivalently, $C$ is given by $z\in Q$, $x\ge0$, $\sum_i x_i\le R+14$, and $\sum_i x_i\le R+17-z_1-2z_2$, so it is an actual full-dimensional compact convex polytope. Substitution gives the stated nine roof values. The true centroids are $g_i=(R+t_i)\mathbf1_d/(d+1)$, and the volumes are $(R+t_i)^d/d!$. Taking the convex hull of the nine original lifted fibers, if desired, leaves these fibers exact by containment in $C$ and inclusion of all of them.

In parities $(1,1),(0,0),(0,1),(1,0)$ the maximizing roots are $0,1,2,5$. The only ties for the maximum are $0$ against $4$ and $5$ against $3$, with squared norms respectively $2<10$ and $9<13$. The arrows $3\to4,4\to1,6\to2,7\to6,8\to7$ have parity partners $5,0,0,5,2$. The base identity is the midpoint in each case, and the paired roof sums $28,28,26,24,20$ are twice the target roofs. Since $aK+bK=(a+b)K$ for nonnegative $a,b$, these give exact set equalities for every edge. The listed arrows terminate at the roots.

The affine coordinate change on the graph sends $(p_i,g_i)$ to $G_i=(p_i,t_i)$ by $t_i=(d+1)(g_i)_1-R$ and is injective. For each index the table gives a closed halfspace $u\cdot G\ge b$ whose boundary contains it. Its last column follows by the displayed base and roof lists.

| Index | $u$ | $b$ | Included indices |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 0 | $(-1,0,0)$ | $-1$ | 0 |
| 1 | $(-1,0,1)$ | $12$ | 0, 1 |
| 2 | $(-2,1,0)$ | $-3$ | 0, 2 |
| 3 | $(0,-1,0)$ | $2$ | 3 |
| 4 | $(0,-1,0)$ | $1$ | 3, 4 |
| 5 | $(1,1,1)$ | $17$ | 5 |
| 6 | $(3,3,2)$ | $36$ | 5, 6 |
| 7 | $(0,0,-1)$ | $-10$ | 7, 8 |
| 8 | $(0,0,-1)$ | $-7$ | 8 |

Every table halfspace pulls back to a closed ambient halfspace via the same affine formula for the first continuous coordinate. In units of $1/d!$ each atom weighs at most $a=(R+14)^d$. Hence all graph depths are at most $2a$ in these units. On the other hand $G_1=(G_0+G_4)/2$, and the three atoms have weight $a$. Any closed halfspace containing $G_1$ also contains one of the two distinct endpoint atoms, so depth at graph point 1 equals $2a$. Dividing by the sum of all weights gives the formula. For $d=1$ the sum is $9R+112$ and substituting $R=21$ gives the displayed rational value and gap. For fixed $d$, divide numerator and denominator by $R^d$ to get the limit. At $R=63d-7$ one has
$$
\left(\frac{R+14}{R+7}\right)^d=\left(1+\frac1{9d}\right)^d
\le\sum_{j=0}^d9^{-j}<\frac98,
$$
by $\binom dj\le d^j$. Thus $2(R+14)^d<9(R+7)^d/4$, which is at most one quarter of the total weight in these units.
