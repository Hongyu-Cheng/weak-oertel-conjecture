## Statement

Let $T$ be a triangle containing $0$ in its interior, with distinct vertices $P_i$ lying strictly in the unique sectors of caps $v_i\cdot x\ge0$ for $(v_1,v_2,v_3)=((1,0),(0,1),(-1,-1))$. Add a point $Q$ in the open double sector captured by caps $2,3$ beyond edge $P_2P_3$, with convex quadrilateral in cyclic order $P_1,P_2,Q,P_3$ and ear area $E>0$. For cap $2$, put $p=v_2\cdot P_2>0$, $w=-v_2\cdot P_3>0$, $v=v_2\cdot Q>0$. If $Q_2$ is raw cap area and $\sigma_2$ its chord length divided by $\lVert v_2\rVert$, then
$$
\Delta Q_2=E\left(1-\frac{w^2}{(w+p)(w+v)}\right),\qquad
\Delta\sigma_2=\frac{2Ew}{(w+p)(w+v)},\qquad
\frac{2Q_2^{\rm new}}{\sigma_2^{\rm new}}
=p+\frac{\Delta\sigma_2}{\sigma_2^{\rm old}+\Delta\sigma_2}\,v(1+p/w).
$$
The analogous formulas hold for cap $3$ with the roles of $P_2,P_3$ exchanged. Cap $1$ and its chord are unchanged.

## Proof

At threshold $s$ near zero, the part of the ear $\operatorname{conv}(P_2,Q,P_3)$ with $v_2\cdot x<s$ is the triangle at $P_3$. Its intercept fractions on the incident edges are $(w+s)/(w+p)$ and $(w+s)/(w+v)$, so its area is $E(w+s)^2/[(w+p)(w+v)]$. At $s=0$ the complement gives the cap increment, and differentiation at zero gives the ear's coarea chord. The ear and old triangle meet on $P_2P_3$, whose intersection with $v_2^\perp$ is one point, so the two coareas add. The old cap is a triangle with scalar height $p$, so $2Q_2^{\rm old}=p\sigma_2^{\rm old}$. Substituting the first two identities gives $2\Delta Q_2-p\Delta\sigma_2=v(1+p/w)\Delta\sigma_2$ and the claimed ratio. The argument with cap $3$ uses its own positive vertex $P_3$ and negative vertex $P_2$. Every ear vertex is strictly negative for $v_1$, so that cap and chord miss the whole ear.
