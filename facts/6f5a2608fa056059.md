## Statement

Let $K\subset\mathbb R^d$ be a convex body, $d\ge2$, with centroid $g$, and let $X$ be uniform on $K$. Fix a linear functional $\ell$ of width one on $K$. Write $p$ for the density of $\ell(X)$ given by section volumes, $m=\ell(g)$, and $A=m-\min_K\ell$. Then $p\le d$ and $p(m)\ge1$. Lowering a cap threshold by $a\ge0$ increases its normalized mass by at most $da$. For $0\le a\le A$,
$$
\mathbb P\{m-a\le\ell(X)\le m\}\ge a/d.
$$
In particular, if $y\in K$ and $\ell(g-y)\ge0$, then
$$
\frac{|K\cap\{\ell(x)\ge\ell(y)\}|}{|K|}
\ge \left(\frac{d}{d+1}\right)^d+\frac{\ell(g-y)}{d}.
$$

## Proof

Write $\ell(x)=u\cdot x$ with $u\ne0$. The section at level $t$ and each of the two support points of $K$ in directions $\pm u$ span cones contained in $K$. Their interiors are disjoint, and the heights sum to $1/\|u\|$, including a possible zero height at an endpoint. If the section has area $S(t)$, their total volume is $S(t)/(d\|u\|)$. Since $p(t)=S(t)/(|K|\|u\|)$, this gives $p(t)\le d$ and bounds the mass added by lowering a threshold.

By the Brunn–Minkowski inequality on sections, $p^{1/(d-1)}$ is positive and concave on the open projection interval. Thus $\log p$ is concave there. The interval has length one, so Jensen's inequality for $s\log s$ gives $\int p\log p\ge0$. This integral is finite since $p\le d$ and $s\log s$ is bounded below on $[0,d]$. The mean $m$ lies in the open interval. Integrate a supporting affine upper bound to $\log p$ at $m$ against the probability density $p$. Its integral is its value at $m$, giving $\log p(m)\ge\int p\log p\ge0$.

Concavity of the section root and the nonnegative endpoint limit imply $p(m-s)\ge p(m)(1-s/A)^{d-1}$ for $0\le s<A$. Integrating yields
$$
\mathbb P\{m-a\le\ell(X)\le m\}
\ge \frac{A}{d}\bigl[1-(1-a/A)^d\bigr]\ge a/d.
$$
The inequality at $a=A$ follows by monotone convergence. Finally apply the full-mass centroid case of 6769574fc1bd9f8d to the through-centroid cap. The added slab has $a=\ell(g-y)\in[0,A]$. Its overlap with the through-centroid cap is a hyperplane of zero volume.
