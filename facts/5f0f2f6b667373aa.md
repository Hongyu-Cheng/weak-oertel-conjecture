## Statement

Let $h_0\in(19/100,1/5)$ and $h_*\in(23/100,7/30)$ be the respective unique roots in those intervals of $2h^4+5h^2+4h-1=0$ and $2h^3-6h^2-3h+1=0$. For $h\in[h_0,h_*]$ put
$$
a=\frac{2+4h^3}{3-6h^2},\quad b=a+h,\quad k=\frac1{b^2-a^2},\quad c=1-a,\quad \ell=1-b.
$$
Use the triangle $K=\{(x,y):0\le x\le1,\ |y|\le k(1-x)\}$ at base zero and $K\cap\{\ell\le x\le c\}$ at both bases $-1,1$ of their lifted convex hull. Then $0<a<b\le1$, $1<k\le3$, the endpoint volumes equal one, and the true mixed centroid is $(0,c,0)$. The cap $\{x\ge c\}$ through it has normalized mixed mass
$$
\rho(h)=\frac{4(2h^3+1)^2}{3(1-2h^2)(4h^4+8h+3)}.
$$
It strictly decreases up to $h_*$, where its derivative is zero, with minimum on the displayed branch
$$
\rho(h_*)=\frac{(1-h_*)^2}{1+4h_*-2h_*^2}.
$$

## Proof

For $0<a<b\le1$, the triangle has area $k$ and centroid $(1/3,0)$, and the strip in it has area $k(b^2-a^2)=1$. Integrating in the coordinate $v=1-x$ gives endpoint centroid coordinate $\mu_A=1-(2k/3)(b^3-a^3)$. The integer census is exactly $-1,0,1$ by extremality and containment of the identical endpoint strips in the middle triangle. Reflection gives zero second continuous centroid coordinate, and equal endpoint masses give zero base centroid. The first continuous coordinate equals $c$ exactly when
$$
(k+2)a-2k/3=(4k/3)(b^3-a^3).
$$
Substitute $k^{-1}=b^2-a^2$ and $b=a+h$ to reduce this to $3a-2-6ah^2-4h^3=0$, the defining equation for $a$. The endpoint cap traces have area zero. The middle cap is similar to the triangle with linear ratio $a$, so its area is $ka^2$. Dividing by $k+2$ gives the displayed $\rho(h)$. Also $k=3(1-2h^2)/[h(2h^3+3h+4)]$.

The quartic is strictly increasing on $[19/100,1/5]$ and the cubic strictly decreasing on $[23/100,7/30]$ by their derivatives; their endpoint signs are respectively $-,+$ and $+,-$ by substitution. The equation $k=3$ is the quartic equation and $b=1$ is the cubic equation. For $0<h<1/4$, $a>0$ and $a'>0$ by the signs of its numerator and denominator and their derivatives, so $b$ strictly increases. Since $k^{-1}=h(2a+h)$ increases strictly, $k$ decreases from $3$ to $1/(2h_*-h_*^2)>1$. Thus all three exact fibers remain admissible across the stated branch. For $h=1/5$ this recovers $k=1725/577$, $c=91/345$, $\ell=22/345$.

Differentiation gives
$$
\rho'(h)=
\frac{16(2h^3+1)(2h^3-3h-2)(2h^3-6h^2-3h+1)}
{3(2h^2-1)^2(4h^4+8h+3)^2}.
$$
The second factor in the numerator is strictly negative for $h<1/4$. The cubic is strictly decreasing throughout $[h_0,h_*]$ and is positive before its zero $h_*$. This proves the stated monotonicity and stationarity. At $h_*$ use $a=1-h_*$ and $k^{-1}=2h_*-h_*^2$ in $\rho=a^2/(1+2/k)$ to get the claimed minimum.
