## Statement

In the convex interval family $C=\{(z,x):-1\le z\le1,\ 0\le x\le 2-|z|\}$, for every $0<\tau<1/2$ the graph $\{(z,x)\in C:f_{C_z}(x)\ge\tau |C_z|\}$ of fixed relative ordinary-depth levels is nonconvex. Equivalently this occurs for every retained fraction in $(0,1)$ of the own maximum.

## Proof

The upper graph $2-|z|$ is concave, so $C$ is a compact convex body. In an interval of length $l$ with left endpoint zero, the raw ordinary depth is $\min(x,l-x)$. At level $\tau l$ its retained interval is $[\tau l,(1-\tau)l]$. At bases $-1$ and $1$ both points of height $\tau$ qualify, while their midpoint $(0,\tau)$ lies below the retained lower endpoint $2\tau$. The peak is $l/2$, so replacing the normalization by the peak just doubles the retained fraction.
