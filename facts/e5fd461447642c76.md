## Statement

Fix a unit continuous normal $n$ and write $h(p,x)=n\cdot x$. Let $w_q=(q,y_q)$ be finitely many physical points, $\alpha_q>0$ with $\sum_q\alpha_q=1$, and $w_0=\sum_q\alpha_qw_q$. Write $h_q=h(w_q)$, $h_0=h(w_0)$ and $J=\delta_{w_0}-\sum_q\alpha_q\delta_{w_q}$. Then
$$
hJ=h_0J+R_h,\qquad
R_h=\sum_q\alpha_q(h_0-h_q)\delta_{w_q},\qquad
\int w\,dR_h=-\operatorname{Cov}_{\alpha}(w,h).
$$
The residue has mass zero and its continuous first moment in direction $n$ is $-\operatorname{Var}_{\alpha}(h)$. For a pair with weights $1/2$, put $D=w_+-w_-$ and let $\zeta$ be the pushforward of uniform probability on $[0,1]$ by $t\mapsto w_-+tD$. The residue is $(h_+-h_-)(\delta_{w_-}-\delta_{w_+})/4$ and equals $\operatorname{div}((h_+-h_-)D\zeta/4)$ as an ambient distribution.

If $A$ is a finite horizontal positive semidefinite matrix-valued measure on lifted continuous faces with tangent planes perpendicular to $n$, in the sense that $An=0$ as a vector measure, then $h(-\operatorname{div}_x\operatorname{div}_x A)=-\operatorname{div}_x\operatorname{div}_x(hA)$ on compactly supported smooth tests.

For an active binary support relation at distinct endpoint bases $p_-,p_+$, replacing $h$ by the height about an affine reference point $Bp+c_0$ kills that pair's residue at $n$ exactly when $h_{K_+}(n)-h_{K_-}(n)=n\cdot B(p_+-p_-)$. If the relation is active at every normal, a single affine reference point field kills these residues at every normal exactly when the endpoint bodies are translates.

## Proof

Affineness gives $h_0=\sum_q\alpha_qh_q$. Expanding $hJ$ yields the signed correction, and summing its coefficients gives zero. Its first moment is $-\sum_q\alpha_q(w_q-w_0)(h_q-h_0)$, which is the stated covariance with a minus sign. Applying $w\mapsto n\cdot x$ gives the negative variance. For the binary case substitute $h_0=(h_-+h_+)/2$. Against a smooth test $f$, $\operatorname{div}(D\zeta)$ gives $-\int_0^1 \frac{d}{dt}f(w_-+tD)\,dt=f(w_-)-f(w_+)$. The segment representation concerns distributions and may traverse unsampled bases.

For the horizontal term, test against $f$ and apply the product rule to $\nabla_x^2(hf)=h\nabla_x^2f+n\otimes\nabla_x f+\nabla_x f\otimes n$. The last two terms vanish after contraction with the symmetric measure $A$ because $An=0$. This gives the asserted distributional identity.

On the endpoint support faces the adjusted heights are $h_{K_\pm}(n)-n\cdot(Bp_\pm+c_0)$. The distinct lifted endpoints make the signed binary residue zero exactly when the adjusted heights agree. Equality for every unit normal identifies $h_{K_+}$ with the support function of $K_-+B(p_+-p_-)$, hence identifies the bodies by their supporting halfspaces. Conversely, if their translation is $v$, choose $B$ with $B(p_+-p_-)=v$. In particular, for full-dimensional $K$ and $0<a<1$, the homothets $(1-a)K,K,(1+a)K$ at $-1,0,1$ have active midpoint support equality in every direction, yet their endpoint support difference $2a h_K$ has positive width in every direction and cannot be linear.
