## Statement

Let $dm$ be the sum of Lebesgue volume on the finitely many sampled positive fibers of a compact canonical hull $C$, with $M=\int dm>0$. Let $L$ be continuous and strictly positive on the compact hull. For finitely many positive affine charts $W_Q$ and constants $b_Q>0$, put $G_Q=b_QW_Q$ and $U=\min_Q G_Q$. Let nonnegative source weights $\sigma_i$ at marks $Y_i$ be assigned once each to groups $Q$. Assume finite nonnegative whole-row measures $\eta_Q=L\nu_Q$ satisfy $\sum_Q\eta_Q=dm/M$ and
$$
s_Q=\eta_Q(C)=\sum_{i\ {\rm assigned\ to}\ Q}\sigma_i,\qquad
\int (W_Q/L)\,d\eta_Q=s_Q.
$$ Assume further that $U(Y_i)=b_QL(Y_i)=\Psi(Y_i)$ for each positive source assigned to $Q$, where $\Psi(Y_i)>0$ is the prescribed profile value. Then for every $p>0$,
$$
\int (L/U)^p\,\frac{dm}{M}\ge\sum_{i:\sigma_i>0}\sigma_i[L(Y_i)/\Psi(Y_i)]^p.
$$
If $L$ is also concave, the path $V_t=(L^{-p}+tU^{-p})^{-1/p}$ is positive and concave for every $t\ge0$. If the fixed-row logarithmic comparison $\sum_i\sigma_i\log(V_t(Y_i)/L(Y_i))\ge\int\log(V_t/L)\,dm/M$ holds for all small $t\ge0$, its right derivative at zero yields the same inverse-moment inequality.

## Proof

On a row of positive mass $s_Q$, write $g_Q=W_Q/L$. Since $U\le b_QW_Q$, convexity of $x\mapsto x^{-p}$ and the unit mean of $g_Q$ under $\eta_Q/s_Q$ give
$$
\int (L/U)^p\,d\eta_Q\ge b_Q^{-p}\int g_Q^{-p}\,d\eta_Q
\ge b_Q^{-p}s_Q.
$$
Zero rows contribute zero. Sum the inequality using $\sum_Q\eta_Q=dm/M$ and $b_Q=\Psi(Y_i)/L(Y_i)$ at every positive source assigned to $Q$.

For the path assertion let $A(x,y)=(x^{-p}+t y^{-p})^{-1/p}$ on positive arguments and write $S=x^{-p}+t y^{-p}$. The first partial derivatives are $S^{-1/p-1}x^{-p-1}$ and $tS^{-1/p-1}y^{-p-1}$, both nonnegative. Its Hessian is
$$
-(p+1)t\,S^{-1/p-2}x^{-p-2}y^{-p-2}
\begin{pmatrix}y^2&-xy\\-xy&x^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The matrix in parentheses is the outer product of $(y,-x)$ with itself. Thus $A$ is concave and nondecreasing in each argument, so composing with the positive concave $L,U$ gives a positive concave $V_t$.

The source-minus-target logarithmic comparison has value zero at $t=0$ and is assumed nonnegative for small $t\ge0$. On the compact hull $L/U$ is bounded, and
$$
\log(V_t/L)=-p^{-1}\log(1+t(L/U)^p).
$$
Its right derivative is $-(L/U)^p/p$, with a bounded difference quotient for small $t$. The derivative of the comparison is therefore nonnegative, giving the same inequality upon evaluating $U(Y_i)=\Psi(Y_i)$.
