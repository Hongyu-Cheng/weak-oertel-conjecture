## Statement

Let $K_z$ be the nonempty integer fibers of a compact convex set on a finite base $P$ and let $C_*=\operatorname{conv}\bigcup_{z\in P}(\{z\}\times K_z)$. Set $D_{\mathrm{int}}=\operatorname{conv}\bigcup_z(K_z-K_z)$ and $D_{\mathrm{all}}=\{v:(0,v)\in C_*-C_*\}$. Suppose $z_0\in P\cap\operatorname{relint}\operatorname{conv}P$ and
$$
\operatorname{conv}P-z_0\subseteq R(z_0-\operatorname{conv}P)
$$
for some $R>0$. Then
$$
D_{\mathrm{all}}\subseteq(1+R)(K_{z_0}-K_{z_0})\subseteq(1+R)D_{\mathrm{int}}.
$$

## Proof

For a real base $p\in\operatorname{conv}P$, let $q=z_0-(p-z_0)/R\in\operatorname{conv}P$ and fix $x_q\in(C_*)_q$. The identity $z_0=(p+Rq)/(1+R)$ and convexity show that $(x+Rx_q)/(1+R)\in K_{z_0}$ for every $x\in(C_*)_p$, since the canonical hull preserves the sampled fibers. Subtract the images of any two points in $(C_*)_p$. Their difference divided by $1+R$ belongs to $K_{z_0}-K_{z_0}$. The union of the original differences over real bases is exactly $D_{\mathrm{all}}$, giving the first inclusion. The second follows by definition.
