## Statement

For $0<u<1$, set $p=(1+u)/2$, $e=u/p$, $h=(1-u)^2/[4(1+e)]$, $\kappa=p^2+h$, $K=p/[u(1-u)]$ and $E=1/[p(2-p)]$. The centered full-section upper scalar in dimension two has the exact positive residual
$$
2eh-(\kappa-2Kh)(E-1)=
\frac{(1-u)^3(8u^2-u+1)}{4u(3-u)(1+3u)}>0.
$$

## Proof

Substituting $p$ gives $2eh=u(1-u)^2/(1+3u)$, $E-1=(1-u)^2/[(1+u)(3-u)]$ and
$$
\kappa-2Kh=\frac{(1+u)(4u^3+3u^2+2u-1)}{4u(1+3u)}.
$$
After factoring out $(1-u)^2/[4u(3-u)(1+3u)]$ from the residual, the remaining polynomial is $4u^2(3-u)-(4u^3+3u^2+2u-1)=(1-u)(8u^2-u+1)$. The quadratic equals $8(u-1/16)^2+31/32$, and all other factors are positive on the interval.
