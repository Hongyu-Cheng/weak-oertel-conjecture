## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be a convex body with nonempty mixed set $S=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)$, and write $C_z=\{x:(z,x)\in C\}$. Put $q=\dim_H S\ge1$, $V_z=\mathcal H_q(C_z)$, $P=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:V_z>0\}$, $M=\mathcal H_q(S)=\sum_{z\in P}V_z$, and let $k$ count the occupied coordinate parity classes of $P$. Fix finitely many original closed ambient halfspaces $H_i$ and an integer $t\ge1$. The mass of a cap is $\mathcal H_q(S\cap H_i)$.

Put $U_J=C\cap\bigcap_{j\in J}H_j^c$ for every set of at most $t$ indices, including the empty set, and define the actual local residual root $e_z=\min_{|J|\le t}\mathcal H_q((U_J)_z)^{1/q}$. Write $a_+=\max(a,0)$. The convex program
$$
R_t=\min_{\substack{L\text{ affine on }\operatorname{aff}(P)\\L(z)\ge e_z\text{ for }z\in P\text{ with }e_z>0}}
\sum_{z\in P}L(z)_+^q
$$
attains its minimum. At most $kt$ original caps leave total residual at most $R_t$, so $ktB\ge M-R_t$ for a nonnegative common cap mass bound $B$. In particular $q=t=2$ and $R_2\le M/9$ give $B\ge4M/(9k)$. If all $e_z=0$ then $R_t=0$.

## Proof

The local minimum defining $e_z$ is attained because there are finitely many lists and $\mathcal H_q(C_z)<\infty$ on $P$. A nonnegative hinge $L_+$ bounds $e_z$ on $P$ precisely under the displayed constraints, with no constraint on $L(z)$ when $e_z=0$.

To show attainment, introduce $T_z\ge0$, $T_z\ge L(z)$ for all $z\in P$ along with the constraints on $L$. The feasible $T$-vectors form a closed polyhedron: the original joint constraints are nonstrict affine inequalities, and eliminating each affine coefficient pairs all lower bounds for it with all upper bounds for it. A one-sided list leaves no additional condition. A sufficiently large constant $L$ gives feasibility. The function $\sum_z T_z^q$ has bounded sublevels on the nonnegative orthant, so it attains its minimum on the closed projection. Choose a feasible affine preimage $L$ of a minimizing $T$. Replacing each $T_z$ by $L(z)_+$ preserves feasibility and can only lower the objective, so this $L$ attains the asserted minimum.

Use $\ell=L_+$ in the intrinsic convex-threshold subcover theorem. It is finite, convex and nonnegative, and the actual local minimum provides each required residual bound. The total allowance is $R_t$. This proves the claims, including $R_t=0$ at $L=0$ if all the local residual roots vanish.
