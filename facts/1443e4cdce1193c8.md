## Statement

Let $C$ be the canonical convex hull of a finite compatible positive-volume family of fibers $K_p\subset\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge1$. Put $F(z,x)=E_{C_z}(x)^{1/d}$, $v(z)=|C_z|^{1/d}$ and $\Psi=F^{d/(d+1)}v^{1/(d+1)}$, where $E_K$ is ordinary projective energy extended by zero at the own-fiber boundary. Let $L>0$ on $C$. The own-interior marks $Y_i=(p_i,y_i)$ are covered by finitely many source groups $Q$. For each $Q$, suppose a reference mark $Y_j$ in $Q$ has affine upper supports $S_Q\ge F$ on $C$ and $T_Q\ge v$ on the real base hull, with equality at $Y_j$ and $p_j$, respectively. Write $L_i=L(Y_i)$ and assume
$$
W_Q=L_j\left[\frac{dS_Q}{(d+1)F(Y_j)}+\frac{T_Q}{(d+1)v(p_j)}\right],
\qquad
W_Q(Y_i)=L_i,\quad
F(Y_i)/L_i=\kappa_Q,\quad v(p_i)/L_i=\upsilon_Q\quad(i\in Q),
$$
where $\kappa_Q,\upsilon_Q>0$ are constant in each group. Set $b_Q=\kappa_Q^{d/(d+1)}\upsilon_Q^{1/(d+1)}$, $G_Q=b_QW_Q$, and $U=\min_Q G_Q$. Then each $G_Q$ is a positive affine majorant of $\Psi$. The positive concave piecewise-affine $U$ majorizes $\Psi$ and satisfies $U(Y_i)=\Psi(Y_i)=b_RL_i$ at every $i\in R$, including when a source lies in several groups. At a touching source in $Q$, $G_Q/G_Q(Y_i)$ restricts on the own fiber to its minimizing projective denominator. Moreover for each $i\in R$,
$$
W_Q(Y_i)/L_i\ge \frac{d\kappa_R/\kappa_Q+\upsilon_R/\upsilon_Q}{d+1}\ge b_R/b_Q.
$$
Every cyclic product of these cross-group lower bounds is at least one.

## Proof

Since $b_Q=\Psi(Y_j)/L_j$, the scaled affine function is
$$
G_Q=\Psi(Y_j)\left[\frac{dS_Q}{(d+1)F(Y_j)}+\frac{T_Q}{(d+1)v(p_j)}\right].
$$
The upper supports satisfy $S_Q\ge F\ge0$ and $T_Q\ge v>0$. Apply the weighted arithmetic–geometric mean inequality to the two support ratios, allowing zero in the first ratio by continuity, and then use the upper-support bounds. This gives $G_Q\ge\Psi$, with $G_Q>0$ from the strictly positive volume term. The finite minimum is positive, piecewise affine and concave, and also majorizes $\Psi$.

At each mark of group $R$ one has $\Psi(Y_i)=b_RL_i$ by the ratio assumptions, and $G_R(Y_i)=b_RL_i$ by its contact. Therefore the minimum equals $\Psi$ there. If the mark belongs to two groups, the two group constants equal the same positive ratio $\Psi(Y_i)/L_i$. Every touching scaled chart equals $\Psi$ at an own-interior mark and dominates it on its own fiber. Fact a56f8711406a3870 gives $\nabla_x F(Y_i)=\frac{d+1}{d}F(Y_i)a_{Y_i}$, with $a_{Y_i}$ the minimizing parameter. Differentiating the product defining $\Psi$ in that fiber gives $\nabla_x\Psi(Y_i)=\Psi(Y_i)a_{Y_i}$. The affine touching majorant must have this same own-fiber gradient, so its normalization is $1+a_{Y_i}\cdot(x-y_i)$ on $K_{p_i}$.

Finally evaluate $S_Q\ge F$ and $T_Q\ge v$ at $Y_i$ in group $R$, use $F(Y_j)=\kappa_Q L_j$, $v(p_j)=\upsilon_Q L_j$, and divide by $L_i$. This gives the first ratio bound. The same weighted AM–GM gives the second. Around a cycle the products of the ratios $b_R/b_Q$ telescope to one.
