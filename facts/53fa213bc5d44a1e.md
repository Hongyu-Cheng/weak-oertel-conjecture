## Statement

Let positive full-dimensional polytope fibers on a fixed finite base have an actual cone $V$ of addable compatible signed velocities, in a finite-dimensional real vector space. Each velocity $\xi$ has a realizing path with uniform $O(t)$ radial displacements and almost-everywhere radial first speeds inducing inward speeds $v_l(\xi)$ constant on each old facet $l$ and linear in $\xi$. The total signed inward mass loss $D(\xi)$ is linear. Use the sum of the fiber volumes, of old total mass $M$. Fix one actual group of old mass-$Q$ nonvertical caps with fixed normals, weights $\rho_j>0$ summing to one, and positive total proper coareas $S_j$, computed by dividing each proper section area by $b_j=\|u_j\|$ for its cap's continuous normal $u_j$. Write $I_j,O_j$ for the inside and outside supporting facets and $A_l$ for their areas, using counting measure in dimension one. Let $B_j(\xi)$ be the whole-boundary loss on strict-full and inside-supporting fibers plus the captured old-boundary loss on proper fibers.

Suppose the exact current weighted-threshold budget increment is $t\alpha(\xi)+o(t)$, where $\alpha$ is linear or the minimum of finitely many linear forms. Continue the actual common cap mass by that exact weighted equation. Its first loss $\ell$ is determined by $\sum_j\rho_j R_j(\xi,\cdot)^{-1}(\ell)=\alpha(\xi)$, with
$$
R_j(\xi,r)=B_j(\xi)+S_jr+\sum_{l\in I_j} A_l(r/b_j-v_l(\xi))_+
-\sum_{l\in O_j} A_l(-r/b_j-v_l(\xi))_+ .
$$
Both $\ell$ and the normalized improvement numerator $G=\ell-(Q/M)D$ are continuous, positively homogeneous and piecewise linear on $V$. More precisely, they are restrictions to $V$ of linear forms on finitely many polyhedral sign cones. Choose bits $\zeta_{jl},\tau_{jl}\in\{0,1\}$ for the inside and outside hinges. On a chosen linear budget branch put
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_j&=S_j+\sum_{I_j} A_l\zeta_{jl}/b_j+\sum_{O_j} A_l\tau_{jl}/b_j,\qquad B=\sum_j\rho_j/T_j,\\
L_j&=B_j-\sum_{I_j}A_l\zeta_{jl}v_l+\sum_{O_j} A_l\tau_{jl}v_l,\\
\ell&=(\alpha+\sum_j\rho_j L_j/T_j)/B,\qquad r_j=(\ell-L_j)/T_j .
\end{aligned}
$$
The cone for these formulas is specified by the selected minimizing budget branch and by the chosen bits selecting the positive or zero branch of the respective hinge at $r_j$. Ties may select either branch.

## Proof

Apply the signed moving-cap expansion in 9ee0d9a1e9f65259 with normal speed zero. The hypotheses concern the realizing path and old geometry, not the contact profile: the proper plane gives $S_jr$, the inside term is added to the full inside fiber body loss, and the outside term is a gain with a minus sign. The result is uniform for bounded threshold speeds. A proper cut in at least one fiber gives a common neighborhood of the old threshold with strictly decreasing current cap tails: the cut crosses an old interior ball, and the new bodies retain a smaller ball there. Continuity and strict monotonicity give actual local upper quantiles. For fixed $\xi$, each $R_j(\xi,\cdot)$ is continuous, strictly increasing onto $\mathbb R$, with increments bounded below by $S_j$ times the threshold increment. Inverting the uniform tail expansion at the candidate masses $Q-tL$ with $L$ in a bounded interval gives actual quantile displacements $tR_j(\xi,\cdot)^{-1}(L)+o(t)$: bracket the shift at its predicted value plus or minus any fixed multiple of $t$, and use the strictly positive lower slope. Choose constants $L_-<L_+$ with the weighted inverse sum straddling $\alpha(\xi)$. The exact weighted equation brackets the actual common mass between $Q-tL_+$ and $Q-tL_-$ for small $t$. Taking any subsequential limit of its scaled loss and using uniform expansion and the lower slopes identifies that limit with the unique displayed equalized loss. Thus the formula computes the derivative of the actual continued mass.

With fixed bits, the corresponding affine-in-threshold loss has coefficient $T_j>0$ and constant term $L_j$. Substitution into the weighted threshold equation gives the displayed forms. The inequalities imposing the bits are linear: for an inside bit use the sign of $r_j/b_j-v_l$, and for an outside bit use the sign of $-r_j/b_j-v_l$. A finite minimum of linear budgets is split by linear comparisons to all its other branches. At any velocity take the actual inverse shifts and a minimizing budget branch. Their signs produce one of the listed cones. Conversely each list that satisfies its inequalities solves the original equations, so all formulas agree on overlaps. These are finitely many closed polyhedral cones intersected with $V$; their agreeing linear formulas give continuity and positive homogeneity. The normalized current mass is $Q/M-tG/M+o(t)$ since $M_t=M-tD+o(t)$. The calculation uses one actual velocity across all caps; any global descent conclusion additionally needs the complete continued cover and its strict off-assignment gaps.
