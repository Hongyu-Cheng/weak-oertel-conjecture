## Proof

For $a\in(-1/2,1/2)$ all terms in the denominators of $F(a)=\sum_z zV_z/(1+az)$ are positive. Its derivative is $-\sum_z z^2V_z/(1+az)^2<0$, and its limits at the left and right endpoints are respectively $+\infty$ and $-\infty$. Combining the four nonzero terms over the common denominator $800000(a-1)(a+1)(2a-1)(2a+1)$ gives the displayed cubic numerator. It is positive at $1/2000$ and negative at $1/1000$ by direct substitution (the denominator is positive there), giving the root bounds. The unweighted first base moment is $9715/800000$ by summing the five numerators against $z$.

The identity $\sum_z V_z/(1+\alpha z)=M-\alpha F(\alpha)=M$ gives the row's total probability. Its base first moment vanishes, and its two continuous means vanish because the conditional laws on the actual centered rectangles are uniform. The identities for $L$ and the tilted marginal follow by substitution. In particular this supplies the exact one-row hypotheses of 86609687edc6bef4 with positive weight one at the own-interior $Y$. Concretely $U(Y)/s\ge\mathbb E_\rho U/s=\int(U/L)\,d\mu/M$ by concavity, and strict log Jensen yields $\log(U(Y)/s)\ge\int\log(U/L)\,d\mu/M=0$. Equality forces $U/L=1$ almost everywhere for original volume, then everywhere on the sampled rectangles by continuity.

On base zero, $L=s$ identically, so the retuned $\varepsilon$ gives exactly the entire base-zero buffer of 2d1810145bad2afd at every mark. On the four other bases the strict pure-base upper bounds in that fact are unchanged, independently of the multiplier. This proves the complete deepestness and cap claims for $L$. For any $U$ the enlarged or equal buffer at $Y$ gives depth at least $Q$. If $U(Y)>s$ and depth still equals $Q$, the infimum is attained at a nonvertical cap: pure-base masses at $Y$ are strictly greater than $Q$, and for Euclidean-unit continuous normals the base tilt may be clipped to a common finite interval without changing any mass, since off-base fibers are compact and $|z|\ge1$ there. Mixed area is continuous in these parameters, so one can minimize on this compact domain. At a new minimizer the old cap with the same normal has mass at most $Q$ by monotonicity and at least $Q$ by the old certificate, so it is one of the two old minimizing caps. Its proper own trace gains a positive strip under every strictly positive increase of its offset, a contradiction.
