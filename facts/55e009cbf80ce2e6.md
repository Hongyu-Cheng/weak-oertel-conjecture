## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge1$, be a compact convex body of positive mixed $d$-volume $\mu$. Fix an actual point $y=(p,x)$ with $p\in\mathbb Z^n$. For $k\ge0$, put $T_k(w)=y+2^{-k}(w-y)$, $C_k=T_k(C)$, and write $\mu^{[k]}$ for the restriction to bases congruent to $p$ modulo $2^k$. The $C_k$ are nested, and their fibers and mixed measures satisfy
$$
(C_k)_q=x+2^{-k}\big(C_{p+2^k(q-p)}-x\big),\qquad
\mu_{C_k}=2^{-kd}(T_k)_\#\mu^{[k]}.
$$
For every affine closed cap $H_s=\{w:\ell(w-y)\ge s\}$ with $\ell$ a nonzero linear functional, $\mu_{C_k}(H_s)=2^{-kd}\mu^{[k]}(H_{2^ks})$, including pure-base normals. If $s\le0$ this is at least $2^{-kd}\mu^{[k]}(H_s)$, with equality at $s=0$. The moment about $y$ is $2^{-k(d+1)}\int(w-y)\,d\mu^{[k]}(w)$. When the filtered mass is positive the centroid is $T_k(g^{[k]})$, where $g^{[k]}$ is the filtered centroid. The masses $2^{kd}\mu_{C_k}(C_k)$ decrease to $|C_p|$.

For nonnegative summable $a_k$, let $R=\sum_{k\ge0}a_k\mu_{C_k}$. Write $\nu_p(z)=\max\{k:z-p\in2^k\mathbb Z^n\}$ with $\nu_p(p)=\infty$. Then $R\le(\sum_k a_k)\mu$, and for every through-$y$ closed halfspace $H$,
$$
R(H)=\sum_z |C_z\cap H_z|\sum_{k\le\nu_p(z)}a_k2^{-kd},\qquad
\int(w-y)\,dR(w)=\sum_z\left[\int_{C_z}((z,v)-y)\,dv\right]\sum_{k\le\nu_p(z)}a_k2^{-k(d+1)} .
$$
For $a_k=\tau^k$, $0<\tau<1$, these weights are the truncated geometric sums at $\tau2^{-d}$ and $\tau2^{-d-1}$, respectively, and $R\le\mu/(1-\tau)$.

## Proof

Convexity and $y\in C$ give $C_{k+1}\subseteq C_k\subseteq C$. Solving $q=p+2^{-k}(z-p)$ at an integer $q$ reindexes exactly the congruent integer bases. Changing continuous variables at each such base has Jacobian $2^{-kd}$, proving the fiber and measure equalities. The preimage of $H_s$ is exactly $H_{2^ks}$, with both inequalities closed. For $s\le0$ the latter contains $H_s$. The displacement vector itself scales by $2^{-k}$, proving the moment formula and, on dividing by the mass, the centroid formula. There are finitely many nonempty original integer fibers by compactness. Every base other than $p$ is excluded eventually, so the rescaled total masses decrease to $|C_p|$.

The measure domination follows from nesting and nonnegative coefficients. Sum the exact through-$y$ masses and the exact moments, grouping terms at each original base by its allowed $k$. Tonelli applies to the masses. The moment series is absolutely convergent in each coordinate because $C$ is bounded and $\sum_k a_k\mu(C)<\infty$. The resulting grouped weights give the displayed expressions. The geometric specialization sums to $1/(1-\tau)$ at the anchor in the domination bound.
