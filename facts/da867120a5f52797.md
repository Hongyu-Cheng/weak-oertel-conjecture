 g_m^{\mathrm{pool}}

Direct integration, followed by (10)-(11), gives every assertion in (18).

For every positive fresh incidence, choose a half-mass witness for (14):

Put

Equations (51) and (55) give the one pointwise budget (20), including all physical target-endpoint collisions. Equations (18)-(22) show directly that \(\Phi_z\) has mass \(t_z\) and normalized first moment \(y_z\). Hence \(\Phi_z\) itself is one admissible density proving (23). Double-counting fresh incidences and using (17),
