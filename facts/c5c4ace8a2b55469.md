## Statement

Put intervals $[0,4],[2,16],[7,11]$ at integer bases $0,1,2$ of their lifted convex hull in $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$. They are its exact integer fibers, and their original mixed length is $22$. At $j=1$ the raw marginal depth is $D_1=18$. For the mass $t=D_1$, the interval of continuous means of dominated sources with base mean $1$ is $[19/3,1873/216]$. Its centroid $y=3241/432$ has a containing closed ambient cap of raw length $3671/432< t/2$, with deficit $217/432$.

## Proof

The integer projection has exactly bases $0,1,2$, with exact endpoint intervals by extremality. At base $1$ the endpoint weights of any convex combination agree, so the endpoint contribution is in their Minkowski midpoint $[7/2,15/2]\subset[2,16]$. The lifted hull has nonempty planar interior since it contains a nondegenerate interval and a point off its supporting vertical line. The marginal depth at $1$ is $18$ since the two closed rays through $1$ each retain the middle and one endpoint, and every containing halfspace contains one such ray.

For the upper mean, take the portions above $x=5/6+(7/2)z$. They have masses $19/6,35/3,19/6$ and first moments $551/72,2135/18,2147/72$. Equal endpoint masses give base mean $1$, and the total mass and first moment are $18$ and $1873/12$. For any other feasible density $f$, the product $(x-5/6-(7/2)z)(\mathbf1_H-f)$ is pointwise nonnegative; integration cancels the mass and base moments and proves that this first moment is largest. For the lower mean, use the portions below $x=12+(7/2)(z-1)$, namely both whole endpoint intervals and $[2,12]$ in the middle. Their masses are $4,10,4$ and their first moments $8,70,36$, totaling $18$ and $114$. Apply the reversed affine-score exchange to prove minimality. Convex combinations of the two feasible densities realize all intermediate moments, giving precisely the claimed interval and its midpoint.

The closed cap $x\ge y+(1513/432)(z-1)$ passes through $(1,y)$ and has thresholds $4,y,2377/216$ at the three bases. Its first trace is a length-zero endpoint, and the last is empty because $2377/216>11$. Its mixed length is $16-y=3671/432<9$.
