## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and use the
exact canonical objects and normalizations of accepted facts
4b2c4fa5918f50d8 and 6b09aae189415c70. In particular, put
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad \gamma_{2d}:=\left(\frac{2d}{2d+1}\right)^{2d},
 \qquad D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D,
\tag{1}
\]
where \(Q>0\) is the half-strength canonical product-reservoir mass.
Assume the hard \(I\)-dominant inequalities
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad I\ge O.
\tag{2}
\]

Let \(s=(s_e)\) be the retained product matching, let
\(\mathcal E_I\) be the canonical family of \(I\)-bad opposite edges,
let \(t_e\) be the actual opposite-edge triangle masses, and let
\(\mathcal B\) be the canonical exceptional family. Put
\[
 J:=\{e\in\mathcal E_I\setminus\mathcal B:s_e>0\},
 \qquad S:=\sum_{e\in J}s_e.
\tag{3}
\]
For every physical target \(c\in\mathbb Z^n\), put
\[
 T_c:=\sum_\xi
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi,\ m_e=c\\e\in\mathcal E_I}}t_e,
 \qquad E:=\{c:T_c>0\}.
\tag{4}
\]
At each \(c\in E\), choose one retained parity label \(\xi(c)\)
maximizing its contribution to \(T_c\), and put
\[
 U_c:=\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi(c)\\m_e=c}}s_e,
 \qquad
 q_c:=\frac1{U_c}
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi(c)\\m_e=c}}s_eF_e^\flat.
\tag{5}
\]
Define
\[
 \rho_E:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E),
 \qquad
 r_E:=\min_a
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{q_c-a(c):c\in E\},
 \qquad
 k:=2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1),
\tag{6}
\]
where \(a:\operatorname{aff}(E)\to\mathbb R^d\) is affine. Then
\(S>0\) and \(k\ge56\).

For every nonzero family (w=(w_e)_{e\in J}) satisfying
\(0\le w_e\le s_e\), write \(\lvert w\rvert:=\sum_{e\in J}w_e\),
and let \((\rho_M(w),r_M(w))\) and
\((\rho_F(w),r_F(w))\) be the intrinsic base and continuous residual
ranks, in the sense of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483, of
\[
 \sigma_M(w):=
 \sum_{e\in J}\frac{w_e}{16}\delta_{(m_e,\bar c_e)},
 \qquad
 \sigma_F(w):=
 \gamma_d\sum_{e\in J}w_e\delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)}.
\tag{7}
\]
Define the optimized scores
\[
 \mathfrak m_J:=\frac1S
 \sup_{0\ne w\le s|_J}
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_M(w)-4}\lvert w\rvert}{r_M(w)+1},
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 \mathfrak f_J:=\frac1S
 \sup_{0\ne w\le s|_J}
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_F(w)}\lvert w\rvert}{r_F(w)+1}.
\tag{9}
\]
Put
\[
 \lambda:=\frac8{275},
 \qquad c_*:=\frac{307}{8800},
 \qquad \eta:=1-c_*=\frac{8493}{8800},
\tag{10}
\]
and
\[
 \Phi(k,\gamma_d):=
 \frac{4\gamma_d}{k}
 \left(1-\frac{3\gamma_d}{4\gamma_d+\eta k}\right),
\tag{11}
\]
\[
 \Psi(k,\gamma_d):=
 \frac{2\gamma_d}{k}
 \left(1-\frac{3\gamma_d}{4\gamma_d+2\eta k}\right).
\tag{12}
\]
Then the two strict optimized screens are
\[
 \boxed{\mathfrak m_J+\Phi(k,\gamma_d)<\frac8{275},}
\tag{13}
\]
\[
 \boxed{\mathfrak m_J+\mathfrak f_J+
 \Psi(k,\gamma_d)<\frac8{275}.}
\tag{14}
\]

For the full choice (w_e=s_e) on (J), abbreviate its ranks by
\(\rho_M,r_M,\rho_F,r_F\). The denominators below are positive, and
\[
 r_M+1>
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_M-4}}
 {\lambda-\Phi(k,\gamma_d)},
 \qquad
 r_F+1>
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_F}}
 {\lambda-\Psi(k,\gamma_d)}.
\tag{15}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \boxed{
 d+1>
 \max\left\{
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_E-4}}
 {\lambda-\Phi(k,\gamma_d)},
 \frac{(\gamma_d+1/16)2^{n-\rho_E}}
 {\lambda-\Psi(k,\gamma_d)}
 \right\}.}
\tag{16}
\]

Let
\[
 P:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:\beta_z>0\},
 \qquad A_P:=\sum_{z\in P}a_z,
\tag{17}
\]
and define the actual source-point ranks
\[
 \rho_P:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(P),
 \qquad
 r_P:=\min_b
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{p_z^\flat-b(z):z\in P\},
\tag{18}
\]
where \(b:\operatorname{aff}(P)\to\mathbb R^d\) is affine. The measure
\[
 \boxed{
 \sigma_P^\sharp:=
 \frac{\gamma_d}{2}\sum_{z\in P}a_z
 \delta_{(z,p_z^\flat)}}
\tag{19}
\]
is carried by \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\) and is
halfspace-dominated by the mixed-volume measure of \(C\). Consequently,
\[
 D\ge
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}A_P}{2(r_P+1)}
 \ge
 \frac{4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}\Omega}{r_P+1},
\tag{20}
\]
and every hard configuration satisfies the strict source escape
\[
 \boxed{2^{\rho_P}(r_P+1)>400\gamma_d2^n.}
\tag{21}
\]
In particular,
\[
 d+1>400\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}.
\tag{22}
\]

The source graph also contains all selected and edge-point graph atoms
in its affine hull. Precisely,
\[
 \boxed{\rho_E+r_E\le\rho_P+r_P,}
\tag{23}
\]
and, for every nonzero \(w\le s|_J\),
\[
 \boxed{\rho_F(w)+r_F(w)\le\rho_P+r_P.}
\tag{24}
\]

The following hereditary bounds also hold.

First, for every nonempty \(A\subseteq E\), define
\[
 T_A:=\sum_{\substack{e\in J\\m_e\in A}}t_e,
 \qquad
 \rho_A:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(A),
\]
\[
 r_A:=\min_a
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{q_c-a(c):c\in A\},
 \qquad k_A:=2^{\rho_A}(r_A+1).
\tag{25}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{T_A<\frac{k_A}{800\gamma_d}Q.}
\tag{26}
\]
Second, every nonzero \(w\le s|_J\) satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_M(w)-4}\lvert w\rvert}{r_M(w)+1}
 <\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_F(w)}\lvert w\rvert}{r_F(w)+1}
 <\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{27}
\]
Third, for a physical target (c) with
\(J_c:=\{e\in J:m_e=c\}\ne\varnothing\), put
\[
 S_c:=\sum_{e\in J_c}s_e,
 \qquad
 h_c:=\dim\operatorname{aff}\{\bar c_e:e\in J_c\}.
\tag{28}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 S_c<\frac{2^{2-n}(h_c+1)}{100\gamma_{2d}}Q.}
\tag{29}
\]
Fourth, for every nonempty \(P_0\subseteq P\), let
\(\rho_{P_0},r_{P_0}\) be the intrinsic ranks of
\(\{(z,p_z^\flat):z\in P_0\}\). Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{P_0}}}{2(r_{P_0}+1)}
 \sum_{z\in P_0}a_z<\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{30}
\]
Finally, if a canonical compatible star in the exact sense of accepted
fact 6c37fd58639bf070 has base rank \(q_\star\) and carries
\(I\)-triangle mass \(\Theta_\star\), then
\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta_\star<
 \frac{2^{q_\star-n}}{100\gamma_d}Q.}
\tag{31}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact 4b2c4fa5918f50d8 gives, with exactly the notation above,
\[
 S+\frac{67}{8}R+\frac{307}{256}G
 \ge\frac{11}{32}Q,
 \qquad S>0,
 \qquad k\ge56.
\tag{32}
\]
It also proves that, for every \(0<\alpha<3/4\),
\[
 T_J:=\sum_{e\in J}t_e
 >\frac{1-\alpha}{2}S
 -\frac{3-4\alpha}{8\alpha}G,
\tag{33}
\]
and that the one-label-per-physical-target choice in (5) satisfies
\[
 U_c\ge2^{1-n}
 \sum_{\substack{e\in J\\m_e=c}}t_e,
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge\frac{4\gamma_d}{k}T_J,
 \qquad
 D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
\tag{34}
\]
These statements include all collision and label-pooling bookkeeping;
in particular, no two parity labels are combined at one target fiber.

We first verify that (8)-(9) are legal capacity witnesses. Accepted fact
6b09aae189415c70 gives \(s_e/16\le r_e\) for every retained edge and
makes every positive submeasure of
\[
 \bar\nu_r:=\sum_er_e\delta_{(m_e,\bar c_e)}
\]
visible to \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\). Hence, for every admissible \(w\),
\(\sigma_M(w)\le\bar\nu_r\) and
\[
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\ge
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_M(w)-4}\lvert w\rvert}{r_M(w)+1}.
\tag{35}
\]
The same accepted fact proves that
\[
 \gamma_d\sum_es_e\delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)}
\]
is halfspace-dominated by the mixed-volume measure of \(C\). Positive
restriction preserves domination, so the dominated-measure residual
theorem of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 gives
\[
 D\ge
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_F(w)}\lvert w\rvert}{r_F(w)+1}.
\tag{36}
\]
Taking the two suprema proves
\[
 M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\ge\mathfrak m_JS,
 \qquad D\ge\mathfrak f_JS.
\tag{37}
\]

For \(0<\alpha<3/4\), put
\[
 a_\alpha:=\frac{2\gamma_d(1-\alpha)}k,
 \qquad
 b_\alpha:=\frac{\gamma_d(3-4\alpha)}{2\alpha k}.
\tag{38}
\]
Equations (33)-(34) give the strict estimate
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)>a_\alpha S-b_\alpha G.
\tag{39}
\]
There are two distinct legitimate capacity accountings. Using
\(D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) in the first and using the edge-point
bound in (37) for \(D\) in the second gives
\[
 \mathcal A(C)>
 R+(1-2b_\alpha)G+
 (\mathfrak m_J+2a_\alpha)S,
\tag{40}
\]
\[
 \mathcal A(C)>
 R+(1-b_\alpha)G+
 (\mathfrak m_J+\mathfrak f_J+a_\alpha)S.
\tag{41}
\]
In (40), the two copies of (39) are charged to the distinct summands
\(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\). In (41), (39) is charged only to
\(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), while (36) is charged only to \(D\). Thus no
two lower bounds are added inside one numerical \(D\)-capacity.

The exact calibration identity is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R+c_*G+\lambda S
 &={8\over275}
 \left(S+{67\over8}R+{307\over256}G\right)
 +{208\over275}R.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{42}
\]
Equation (32) therefore implies
\[
 R+c_*G+\lambda S\ge\frac Q{100}.
\tag{43}
\]

Choose
\[
 \alpha_2:=\frac{3\gamma_d}{4\gamma_d+\eta k}.
\tag{44}
\]
Then \(0<\alpha_2<3/4\), \(1-2b_{\alpha_2}=c_*\), and
\(2a_{\alpha_2}=\Phi(k,\gamma_d)\). If the left side of (13) were at
least \(\lambda\), then (40) and (43) would contradict (2). This proves
(13). Likewise, for
\[
 \alpha_1:=\frac{3\gamma_d}{4\gamma_d+2\eta k},
\tag{45}
\]
one has \(0<\alpha_1<3/4\), \(1-b_{\alpha_1}=c_*\), and
\(a_{\alpha_1}=\Psi(k,\gamma_d)\). Equations (41) and (43) prove (14).

Use now the full admissible choice \(w_e=s_e\) on \(J\). Equations
(8)-(9) give
\[
 \mathfrak m_J\ge
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_M-4}}{r_M+1},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak f_J\ge
 \frac{\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_F}}{r_F+1}.
\tag{46}
\]
Equations (13)-(14) show that their corresponding denominators in
(15) are positive, and direct rearrangement proves (15). Every
\(e\in J\) has \(t_e\ge s_e/8>0\), so \(m_e\in E\). Therefore
\[
 \rho_M,\rho_F\le\rho_E,
 \qquad r_M,r_F\le d.
\tag{47}
\]
The first term of the maximum in (16) follows from (13), (46)-(47).
For the second, (14), (46), and (47) give
\[
 \mathfrak m_J+\mathfrak f_J
 \ge
 \frac{(\gamma_d+1/16)2^{n-\rho_E}}{d+1}.
\tag{48}
\]
Rearranging proves (16).

We next construct the stronger source-point measure. Accepted fact
6b09aae189415c70 gives, for every \(z\in P\),
\[
 p_z^\flat+\frac12D_z
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
\tag{49}
\]
and hence \(p_z^\flat\in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\). It also gives
\(0\le\omega_e\le r_e\) and
\[
 \beta_z=\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e
 \le d_z(r)
 =\frac{a_z}{4}\left(1-\frac{a_z}{A_\xi}\right)
 \le\frac{a_z}{4}
\tag{50}
\]
in the good reservoir class containing \(z\). Thus
\[
 A_P\ge4\sum_{z\in P}\beta_z=8\Omega.
\tag{51}
\]

Let \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be a closed ambient halfspace. If
\((z,p_z^\flat)\in H\), then its fiber section \(H_z\) is either a
proper closed halfspace containing \(p_z^\flat\), or the whole fiber
space. From (49) and the metronoid-depth theorem of accepted fact
6769574fc1bd9f8d, in either case
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z)\ge\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2}.
\tag{52}
\]
The contributing bases \(z\) are distinct. Summing (52) proves
\(\sigma_P^\sharp(H)\le\mu_C(H)\), so (19) is genuinely
halfspace-dominated. Accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 now gives the first
inequality in (20), and (51) gives the second.

Accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 also gives
\[
 Q-R\le\Omega\le Q,
 \qquad 0\le R\le Q.
\tag{53}
\]
Put
\[
 \chi_P:=\frac{4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_P}}{r_P+1}.
\tag{54}
\]
Equations (20) and (53) imply
\[
 R+D\ge R+\chi_P\Omega
 \ge R+\chi_P(Q-R)
 \ge\min\{1,\chi_P\}Q.
\tag{55}
\]
But (1)-(2) give \(R+D<Q/100\). Hence
\(\chi_P<1/100\), which is exactly (21). Since
\(r_P\le d\), equation (22) follows.

For every retained edge \(e=\{u,v\}\), the graph atom has the exact
midpoint identity
\[
 (m_e,F_e^\flat)
 =\frac12\bigl((u,p_u^\flat)+(v,p_v^\flat)\bigr).
\tag{56}
\]
Moreover, (5) gives
\[
 (c,q_c)=\frac1{U_c}
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi(c)\\m_e=c}}
 s_e(m_e,F_e^\flat).
\tag{57}
\]
Thus every selected graph atom and every edge-point graph atom lies in
\(\operatorname{aff}\{(z,p_z^\flat):z\in P\}\). The coordinate-free
rank identity in accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 says that the affine
dimension of each such graph is its base rank plus its continuous
residual rank. Equations (56)-(57) therefore prove (23)-(24).

It remains to prove the hereditary conclusions. Restrict (34) to a
nonempty \(A\subseteq E\). The selected weights over \(A\) give
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\ge\frac{4\gamma_d}{k_A}T_A,
 \qquad D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
\tag{58}
\]
Since these are distinct summands of (1), (2) and (58) prove (26).
Equations (35)-(36), together with
\(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C),D<Q/100\), prove (27).

For (29), accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives, for the reservoir edges
in \(J_c\),
\[
 R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)
 \ge4\gamma_{2d}2^n
 \frac{\sum_{e\in J_c}r_e}{h_c+1}
 \ge\frac{\gamma_{2d}2^{n-2}S_c}{h_c+1},
\tag{59}
\]
where the last inequality uses \(s_e/16\le r_e\). Now use
\(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)<Q/100\). For (30), restrict the dominated
measure (19) to \(P_0\), apply accepted fact be359f8a6c628483, and use
\(D<Q/100\).

Finally, accepted fact 6c37fd58639bf070 gives, for every canonical
compatible star of base rank \(q_\star\),
\[
 D\ge\gamma_d2^{n-q_\star}\Theta_\star.
\tag{60}
\]
Together with \(D<Q/100\), this proves (31). Every conclusion above is
an actual-convex-body necessary condition. No assertion concerning the
separate formal finite model or the unproved contact-rank compensation
lemma is used.
