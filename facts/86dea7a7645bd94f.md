## Statement

Let $1/2\le a<2/3$, $0<k\le1$, $0\le\beta<k/3$ and $t=1/3$. Let $T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,e_2)$ and $F_r(s)=|rT\cap\{aX_1+(1-a)X_2\ge s\}|/|T|$. For real $h\ge0$ with $1-kh>0$ put
$$
f_-(h)=F_{1-kh}(t-\beta h),\qquad f_+(h)=F_{1-kh}(t+\beta(h+1)),
$$
and extend by zero for $h\ge1/k$. Both functions are nonnegative, compactly supported and convex on $[0,\infty)$. Write $I=\int_0^\infty(f_-+f_+)(h)\,dh$, $O=f_-(0)+f_+(0)$, and $J=\sum_{h\ge0}(f_-+f_+)(h)$ with integer indices in the sum. Then
$$
J\ \ge\ (1-k)I+\frac{1+k}{2}O\ \ge\ \frac49\left(\frac1{3k}+\frac12+\frac{k}{5}\right).
$$

## Proof

Put $c=1-a$. Along the positive-radius leg both thresholds are positive. Dividing each by the radius gives increasing functions of $h$, with derivative numerators $k/3-\beta$ and $\beta+k(1/3+\beta)$ over $(1-kh)^2$. Both ratios tend to infinity at the end of the leg, so the tails vanish before $1/k$.

For $a>1/2$, the two proper-cap formulas in the proof of b026600b8d8e369b are $r^2-s^2/(ac)$ and $(ar-s)^2/[a(a-c)]$, joined at $s=cr$. The first has second derivative $2(k^2-\beta^2/(ac))>0$ along either affine threshold, using $ac\ge2/9$ and $\beta<k/3$. The second is a nonnegative multiple of an affine square. At $s=cr$ the two formulas and their first derivatives along the leg agree, as is seen by substituting $s=cr$ into the value and both partial derivatives. At $s=ar$ the upper square and its derivative vanish. The derivatives along the leg are thus nondecreasing. When $a=1/2$, the positive part of $r^2-4s^2$ gives the tail for $s>0$. The nonzero piece has positive second derivative, and at its final knot its left derivative is $-4s(k-2\beta)$ for $f_-$ and $-4s(k+2\beta)$ for $f_+$, both negative. The right derivative is zero, preserving convexity. The trapezoid bound on each unit interval gives $J\ge I+O/2$, and nonnegativity gives $J\ge O$. Their convex combination with weights $1-k,k$ gives the first inequality.

For the second inequality first take $a>1/2$. For positive $s$, the cap formulas can be combined as
$$
F_r(s)=\frac{a(r-s/a)_+^2-c(r-s/c)_+^2}{a-c}.
$$
Let $L=c-t$, $U=a-t$, and write $I_\pm$ and $O_\pm$ for the separate contributions to $I$ and $O$. Define $B(p,\ell)=p^3/(3\ell)$ for $p\ge0,\ell>0$, the integral of $(p-\ell h)_+^2$ on $h\ge0$. Because $ak-\beta,ck-\beta>0$, the left integral and value are
$$
I_-=\frac{aB(1-t/a,k-\beta/a)-cB(1-t/c,k-\beta/c)}{a-c},
\qquad
O_-=1-\frac{t^2}{ac}.
$$
In region $0$ given by $0\le\beta\le L$, the right integral and value are
$$
I_+=\frac{aB(1-(t+\beta)/a,k+\beta/a)-cB(1-(t+\beta)/c,k+\beta/c)}{a-c},
\qquad O_+=1-\frac{(t+\beta)^2}{ac}.
$$
In region $1$ given by $L\le\beta\le U$ they are
$$
I_+=\frac{aB(1-(t+\beta)/a,k+\beta/a)}{a-c},
\qquad O_+=\frac{(a-t-\beta)^2}{a(a-c)}.
$$
In region $2$ given by $U\le\beta$ they both vanish. These formulas include their shared support boundaries. Each active affine hinge decreases in $h$ and vanishes before $1/k$, by the threshold-to-radius comparison above.

Let $H$ be $(1-k)(I_-+I_+)+(1+k)(O_-+O_+)/2$ minus the target right side. In the three regions respectively substitute $\beta=Lv,\ L+(a-c)v,\ U+Lv$ with $0\le v\le1$, then $k=3\beta+(1-3\beta)w$. For all original parameters we have $\beta<1/3$ and $w=(k-3\beta)/(1-3\beta)\in(0,1]$. In region 2 the upper bound on the original $\beta$ is at most $U+L=1/3$, so these three maps cover the required domain.

The following three expressions are positive on the required strict parameter range:
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_0&=27\cdot810ac k(ak-\beta)(ak+\beta)(ck-\beta)(ck+\beta),\\
D_1&=9\cdot810ac k(ak-\beta)(ak+\beta)(ck-\beta),\\
D_2&=810ac k(ak-\beta)(ck-\beta)/L.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each region the numerator $N_j$ is obtained by multiplying $H$ by $D_j$, applying the two substitutions and canceling. The rational expressions for $I_\pm,O_\pm$ above specify the products exactly. In region 0 the two divided differences cancel $a-c$. In region 1 the right terms cancel it because $a-t-\beta=(a-c)(1-v)$. The $L$ in region 2 cancels from $ck-\beta$ under the substitution, since $ck-\beta=c(1-3\beta)w+(3c-1)\beta$ and $1-3\beta=3L(1-v)$. The resulting $N_j$ and mapped $D_j$ are polynomials in $a,v,w$.
