## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard instance of accepted facts `b5739a77433de383` and `5ae5325c589884a7`.  Thus \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\), \(Q>0\),
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
\]
the actual occurrence multiset is \(\mathcal O\), and every occurrence \(o\in\mathcal O\) has source \(z_o\), load \(\ell_o\), and density \(g_o\) on \(C_{z_o}\), with
\[
 \int_{C_{z_o}}g_o=\ell_o,
 \qquad
 \sum_{o:z_o=z}g_o\leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_z.
\tag{1}
\]
Retain also the pruned centroid-host matching \(q^{\mathrm{out}}\) of `5ae5325c589884a7`, and put
\[
 u_r:=d_r(q^{\mathrm{out}}),
 \qquad
 E:=\{r:u_r>0\}.
\tag{2}
\]
For \(r\in E\), \(c_r\) denotes the centroid of \(C_r\); in the notation of `5ae5325c589884a7`, its legal selector weight is \(4\gamma_du_r\).  This use of \(u_r\) and \(c_r\) is distinct from the degree weight denoted \(w_r\) in `b5739a77433de383`.

Let \(\alpha=(\alpha_o)_{o\in\mathcal O}\) be arbitrary with \(0\leq\alpha_o\leq1\).  For a source \(z\), define
\[
 G_z^\alpha:=\sum_{o:z_o=z}\alpha_og_o,
 \qquad
 \kappa_z:=\int_{C_z}G_z^\alpha
 =\sum_{o:z_o=z}\alpha_o\ell_o.
\tag{3}
\]
Whenever \(\kappa_z>0\), put
\[
 y_z^\alpha:=\frac1{\kappa_z}
 \int_{C_z}yG_z^\alpha(y)\,dy.
\tag{4}
\]
Fix \(r\in E\) and a distinct source \(z\) with \(\kappa_z>0\).  Define the lattice content
\[
 g_{r,z}:=\gcd\bigl(
 |z_1-r_1|,\ldots,|z_n-r_n|
 \bigr)\in\mathbb Z_{>0},
 \qquad
 v_{r,z}:=\frac{z-r}{g_{r,z}}\in\mathbb Z^n.
\tag{5}
\]
Then the \(g_{r,z}+1\) points
\[
 z_j:=r+jv_{r,z}
 \qquad(0\leq j\leq g_{r,z})
\tag{6}
\]
support one legal residual-selector witness with base rank one and residual rank zero.  Quantitatively,
\[
 \boxed{
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 2^{n-1}\sum_{j=0}^{g_{r,z}}
 \left[
 \left(1-\frac j{g_{r,z}}\right)
 (4\gamma_du_r)^{1/d}
 +\frac j{g_{r,z}}(\gamma_d\kappa_z)^{1/d}
 \right]^d.}
\tag{7}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 \gamma_d2^{n-1}(g_{r,z}+1)
 \min\{4u_r,\kappa_z\}.}
\tag{8}
\]
Consequently, if
\[
 \gamma_d2^{n-1}(g_{r,z}+1)
 \min\{4u_r,\kappa_z\}
 \geq\frac Q{100},
\tag{9}
\]
then \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq Q/100\), which is one legal terminal capacity.  Conversely, on the strict canonical hard branch \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<Q/100\), every such pair obeys the exact screen
\[
 \boxed{
 (g_{r,z}+1)\min\{4u_r,\kappa_z\}
 <\frac{Q}{100\gamma_d2^{n-1}}.}
\tag{10}
\]
The coefficient family \(\alpha\) may in particular select any scaled, nonreused complementary occurrence-token family.  Equations (7)-(10) make no assertion that an arbitrary Euclidean first-moment defect controls \(g_{r,z}\), and they do not claim that primitive or low-content complementary leverage has been absorbed.

## Proof

By the simultaneous occurrence ledger (1),
\[
 0\leq G_z^\alpha
 \leq\sum_{o:z_o=z}g_o
 \leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_z.
\tag{11}
\]
Thus (3)-(4) give an admissible density of mass \(\kappa_z\) and normalized first moment \(y_z^\alpha\).  In the canonical metronoid notation,
\[
 y_z^\alpha\in\mathsf M_{\kappa_z}(C_z).
\tag{12}
\]
The metronoid-depth inclusion of accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` therefore gives
\[
 \delta_C(z,y_z^\alpha)\geq\gamma_d\kappa_z.
\tag{13}
\]
This step uses the aggregate density (11), so repeated occurrences at \(z\) are charged only through their total scaled density and no occurrence token is reused.

For the other endpoint, accepted fact `5ae5325c589884a7` gives directly
\[
 4\gamma_du_r\leq\delta_C(r,c_r).
\tag{14}
\]
Define the affine map on the line through \(r,z\) by
\[
 s((1-t)r+tz):=(1-t)c_r+ty_z^\alpha.
\tag{15}
\]
Convexity of \(C\) puts
\[
 (z_j,s(z_j))
 =\left(1-\frac j{g_{r,z}}\right)(r,c_r)
 +\frac j{g_{r,z}}(z,y_z^\alpha)
 \in C
\tag{16}
\]
for every \(j\).  Joint concavity of the \(d\)-th root of absolute fiber depth in accepted fact `4e868f774de77608`, followed by (13)-(14), yields
\[
 \delta_C(z_j,s(z_j))^{1/d}
 \geq
 \left(1-\frac j{g_{r,z}}\right)
 (4\gamma_du_r)^{1/d}
 +\frac j{g_{r,z}}(\gamma_d\kappa_z)^{1/d}.
\tag{17}
\]
Let the \(d\)-th power of the right side of (17) be \(b_j\).  Since \(u_r>0\) and \(\kappa_z>0\), every \(b_j>0\), and (17) says
\[
 0<b_j\leq\delta_C(z_j,s(z_j)).
\tag{18}
\]

The lattice vector \(v_{r,z}\) in (5) is primitive.  Hence the points in (6) are distinct lattice points, their affine hull has dimension one, and \(s\) is an affine map on that hull.  Use in the definition of \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) from accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` the source set \(\{z_0,\ldots,z_{g_{r,z}}\}\), the points \(s(z_j)\), the weights \(b_j\), and the same affine map \(s\).  Its residual vectors all vanish, so its residual rank is zero.  Its score is exactly
\[
 2^{n-1}\sum_{j=0}^{g_{r,z}}b_j,
\tag{19}
\]
which proves (7).

Every convex combination of two positive numbers is at least their minimum.  Therefore each summand in (7) is at least
\[
 \min\{4\gamma_du_r,\gamma_d\kappa_z\}
 =\gamma_d\min\{4u_r,\kappa_z\}.
\tag{20}
\]
There are \(g_{r,z}+1\) summands.  Substitution into (7) proves (8).  Equation (9) and (8) give the stated legal terminal.  If instead \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<Q/100\), combining this strict inequality with (8) and dividing by the positive factor \(\gamma_d2^{n-1}\) proves (10).  This completes the exact arithmetic-leverage extraction while leaving the explicitly disclaimed primitive/low-content case untouched.
