## Statement

Let $r$ be the piecewise affine profile through $(-8,15),(-2,20),(-1,15),(1,3)$, and let $f=1/2$ on $\{(x,z):-8\le z\le1,\ |x|\le r(z)\}$ and zero off this set. The profile is positive and concave and the mass is $V=281/2$. Let $P(s)$ be the mass of $z+sx\ge0$, and let $C(s)$ and $M(s)$ be the integrals of $f$ and $zf$ on $z+sx=0$ against $d\mathcal H^1/\sqrt{1+s^2}$. No real $c,\lambda$ satisfy
$$
\frac49 V\le P(s)-cC(s)-\lambda M(s)\le\frac59 V
\qquad\text{for all }s\ge0.
$$
An explicit witness uses slopes $0,1/10,3/5$, with the middle cap reversed, and total weights $18245,16128,5658$ divided by $40031$. Splitting each oblique type equally under $x\mapsto-x$ gives raw coarea vector balance and signed vertical section first-moment balance, with weighted mass fraction $4942884/11248711<4/9$.

## Proof

The profile slopes are $5/6,-5,-6$, and its integral is $105+35/2+18=281/2$. The records at the three slopes are
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
s&P(s)&C(s)&M(s)\\ \hline
0&6&9&0\\
1/10&607/64&205/16&-4715/512\\
3/5&44&15/2&-105/4
\end{array}
$$
At zero slope, $C(0)=r(0)=9$, $M(0)=0$, and $P(0)=\int_0^1r(z)\,dz=6$. For positive $s$ the cut is $x=-z/s$ on the interval $|z|\le sr(z)$. Its coarea and moment integrands in $z$ are $1/(2s)$ and $z/(2s)$. At $s=1/10$ the interval is $[-2,9/16]$, as follows by solving the two affine inequalities on the profile pieces. The cap is empty below this interval, has horizontal mass $(r(z)+10z)/2$ within it, and has mass $r(z)$ above it. The trapezoid integrals over $[-2,-1]$, $[-1,9/16]$ and $[9/16,1]$ give $P=5/4+1625/256+483/256=607/64$. At $s=3/5$, the inequalities $sr(z)\pm z>0$ hold at both axial endpoints and therefore throughout by concavity. The cap has horizontal mass $(r(z)+(5/3)z)/2$ throughout, which integrates to $44$. The coarea and moment integrals give the other entries.

The lower bound for a positive cap is $P-cC-\lambda M\ge(4/9)V$. For its reversed cap the upper bound yields the equivalent lower bound $V-P+cC+\lambda M\ge(4/9)V$. Multiply these lower bounds for the three specified types by $18245,16128,5658$. The corrections cancel because
$$
18245\cdot9-16128\cdot\frac{205}{16}+5658\cdot\frac{15}{2}=0,\qquad
-16128\left(-\frac{4715}{512}\right)+5658\left(-\frac{105}{4}\right)=0.
$$
Reflection cancels the horizontal components of the raw coarea vectors. The vertical component of each such vector is the raw coarea with its orientation sign. The resulting mean must be at least $4/9$, whereas the actual mean is
$$
\frac{18245\cdot6+16128(281/2-607/64)+5658\cdot44}{40031(281/2)}
=\frac{4942884}{11248711}
=\frac49-\frac{508888}{101238399}.
$$
Both oblique types have nonzero individual vertical moments.
