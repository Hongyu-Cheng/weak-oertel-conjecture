## Statement

For $L\ge2$ put $\epsilon=L^{-2}$ and $K_L=[-L,L]\times[-\epsilon,1]$. The margins $h_1=t+s$, $h_2=-t+s$, $h_0=-2s$ define a concurrent rank-two cover by closed caps. Write $U_i$ for the region of positive margin $i$ and negative other margins. The body has area $W=2L(1+\epsilon)$, private areas $L-1/2,L-1/2,\epsilon^2$, and double-or-more covered area $D_L=1+2L\epsilon-\epsilon^2$. Two private rectangles of area $L/4$ have identical positive height laws, with endpoint ratio two, but every equal-height midpoint is contained in a rectangle of area $1/4$. Thus every area-dominated measure on the equal-height midpoints has mass at most $1/L$ times either source mass.

Let $S=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-1/2&-3/2\\1/2&-1/2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and form the lifted hull of $K_L,SK_L,S^2K_L$ at the three bases $(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)$. These are exactly its integer fibers, with a common continuous interior section at zero. The three global cap budgets are all $W+D_L$, strictly less than half the total mass $3W$. The double-covered mixed mass is $3D_L$ and stays bounded as both private areas of labels $1,2$ at the first base diverge. In particular the ratio of this mixed overlap to the Brunn–Minkowski mean of the two private masses at that parity tends to zero. The normalized maximal depth of this actual source is at least $4/27$.

## Proof

The margins sum to zero, have rank two, and only their common zero point is in all three caps. Ignoring lines of area zero, the private regions in the rectangle are $0<s<1,\ s<t<L$ for label $1$, its reflection for label $2$, and $-\epsilon<s<0,\ s<t<-s$ for label $0$. Integrating gives $L-1/2,L-1/2,\epsilon^2$ respectively. Subtraction from $W$ gives $D_L$.

Take $P_+=[L/2,L]\times[1/2,1]$ and its reflection $P_-$ in the $s$-axis. The pieces belong to the corresponding private regions outside null sets, each has area $L/4$, and the reflection matches $h_1$ on $P_+$ with $h_2$ on $P_-$. The common range is $[L/2+1/2,L+1]$. For $(t,s)\in P_+$ and $(t',s')\in P_-$ with these heights equal, $t+t'=s'-s$. Thus their midpoint lies in $[-1/4,1/4]\times[1/2,1]$, which has area $1/4$. Any measure dominated by area on that support has at most that mass.

Direct multiplication gives $\det S=1$, $S^3=I$ and $(h_1,h_2,h_0)(Sx)=(h_0,h_1,h_2)(x)$. The base triangle contains only its three vertices as integer points. At each vertex, a convex combination lifting that base uses only its assigned body. This gives the exact integer fibers and full ambient dimension. The three bodies each contain a common sufficiently small ball about zero, which remains in every real fiber by convexity. Hence all caps cut all fibers properly. Cyclic symmetry gives equal global cap budgets, and counting once per covered point plus once more on the double-covered region gives budget $W+D_L$ in each label and total double-covered mass $3D_L$. The strict inequality $D_L<W/2$ follows from $W/2-D_L=L-1-1/L+1/L^4>0$. The first base has its own parity and $\Phi_2(L-1/2,L-1/2)=L-1/2$, whereas $3D_L$ is bounded. Finally each rectangular fiber is centrally symmetric about its own centroid. Every ambient halfspace through its lifted centroid contains at least $W/2$ of that fiber, giving normalized depth at least $1/6\ge4/27$.
