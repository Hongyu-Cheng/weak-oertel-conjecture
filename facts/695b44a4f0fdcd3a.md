## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and, for \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\), put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
 W:=\sum_z a_z.
\]
Assume \(W>0\), put \(Z:=\{z:a_z>0\}\), and define
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,\qquad
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),\qquad
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx,\qquad
 D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(q=(q_e)\) be a nonzero off-diagonal fractional same-parity matching as in accepted facts 6496cb3b0fd44f9d and 8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5. Thus its positive support \(F\) consists of unordered pairs \(e=\{z,z'\}\subseteq Z\) with \(z\ne z'\), \(z\equiv z'\pmod 2\), and
\[
 q_e>0,\qquad
 d_z(q):=\sum_{e\ni z}q_e\leq\frac{a_z}{2}.
\tag{2}
\]
For \(e=\{z,z'\}\in F\), put
\[
 m_e:=\frac{z+z'}2,\qquad
 \bar c_e:=\frac{c_z+c_{z'}}2,\qquad
 D_e:=\frac{D_z+D_{z'}}2,\qquad
 L_e:=\bar c_e+D_e=\frac{\mathcal K_z+\mathcal K_{z'}}2.
\tag{3}
\]
Write \(Q:=|q|:=\sum_eq_e\). Choose a primal optimizer \(s_*:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) for the weighted affine defect \(\Delta_C(q)\), and, among the dual optimizers paired with it in accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, choose a gauge-load minimizer \(v=(v_e)_{e\in F}\). Put
\[
 \omega_e:=h_{D_e}(v_e),\qquad
 \Omega:=\sum_e\omega_e.
\tag{4}
\]

For every family \(\alpha=(\alpha_e)_{e\in F}\) satisfying \(0\leq\alpha_e\leq q_e\), define
\[
 t_e(\alpha):=(\alpha_e-\omega_e)_+,
 \qquad
 O_\omega(\alpha):=\sum_{e\in F}\min\{\alpha_e,\omega_e\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Then \(t(\alpha)\) is an off-diagonal fractional same-parity matching affinely half-metronoid realizable by the single affine map \(s_*\), and the exact mass identity is
\[
 \boxed{|\alpha|=O_\omega(\alpha)+|t(\alpha)|.}
\tag{6}
\]
In particular, \(q-\omega=t(q)\) is realized by \(s_*\), and
\[
 \boxed{
 |q-\omega|=Q-\Omega,\qquad
 Q-\Omega\leq R_C(q)\leq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C).}
\tag{7}
\]
Here \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\) is the canonical affine half-metronoid matching capacity of accepted fact 8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5.

The following is the cap-synchronized consequence. Let
\[
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
\]
let \(R_{\mathrm{cap}}(C)\) and \(\mathscr H_{\mathrm{sat}}(C)\) be the individual-cap retention capacity and the universally quantified saturated-circuit family of accepted fact 179add32aa016da1, and assume, for some \(0<\eta\leq1/4\), that
\[
 G<\frac W4,
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{cap}}(C)\leq(1-\eta)W.
\tag{8}
\]
Then either
\[
 G\geq\frac{\eta^2}{100}W,
\tag{9}
\]
or all of the following conclusions hold.

For each parity class \(\xi\), put
\[
 A_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}a_z,
 \qquad
 M_\xi:=\max_{z\equiv\xi}a_z,
 \qquad
 \mathcal G:=\{\xi:A_\xi>0,\ M_\xi\leq A_\xi/4\},
\tag{10}
\]
and define the canonical product matching
\[
 p^\times_{uv}:=\frac{a_ua_v}{2A_\xi}
 \quad(u\ne v,\ u\equiv v\equiv\xi),
 \qquad
 q^\times:=2\gamma_dp^\times.
\tag{11}
\]
Put \(F^\times:=\{e:q_e^\times>0\}\).
Choose once a primal optimizer \(s_*^\times\) and a load-minimal dual optimizer \(v^\times\) for \(q^\times\) as above, and write
\[
 \omega_e^\times:=h_{D_e}(v_e^\times)
 \qquad(e\in F^\times).
\tag{12}
\]
This choice depends only on \(C\), not on a saturated matching, circuit, exact minorant, or cap label.

Fix arbitrarily \((p,m,\mathcal Q,i)\in\mathscr H_{\mathrm{sat}}(C)\), including any valid choice of the exact affine minorants, and let \(\phi_{i,z}\in[0,1]\) be its soft contact factors from equation (16) of accepted fact 179add32aa016da1. Define
\[
 s_{i,\{u,v\}}:=
 \begin{cases}
  \frac12q^\times_{\{u,v\}}\phi_{i,u}\phi_{i,v},
  &u,v\text{ lie in a class }\xi\in\mathcal G,\\
  0,&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 S_i:=\sum_{e\in F^\times}s_{i,e},
\tag{13}
\]
and put
\[
 O_i(v^\times):=\sum_{e\in F^\times}\min\{s_{i,e},\omega_e^\times\},
 \qquad
 t_{i,e}:=(s_{i,e}-\omega_e^\times)_+.
\tag{14}
\]
Then \(t_i\) is realized by the same map \(s_*^\times\), and
\[
 \boxed{
 S_i=O_i(v^\times)+|t_i|,
 \qquad
 O_i(v^\times)+\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)
 \geq S_i>\frac{\gamma_d\eta^2}{8}W.}
\tag{15}
\]
Consequently, for every member and every cap label quantified above, at least one of the following holds:
\[
 \boxed{O_i(v^\times)>\frac{\gamma_d\eta^2}{16}W,}
\tag{16}
\]
or
\[
 \boxed{
 |t_i|>\frac{\gamma_d\eta^2}{16}W,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)>\frac{\gamma_d\eta^2}{16}W,
 \qquad
 h_{\max}(C)>
 \frac{\gamma_d^2\eta^2}{16}\,2^{-n}W
 \geq\frac{\eta^2}{16e^2}\,2^{-n}W.}
\tag{17}
\]
Thus failure of macroscopic cap-synchronized load overlap is already terminal for mixed depth through affine matching retention. No bound by \(G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}+M_{\mathrm{off}}+R_{\mathrm{cloud}}\), and no lower bound on same-body interaction, is claimed.

## Proof

Accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d gives
\[
 0\leq\omega_e\leq q_e
 \quad(e\in F),
 \qquad
 s_*(m_e)\notin L_e\ \Longrightarrow\ \omega_e=q_e.
\tag{18}
\]
Fix \(\alpha\) with \(0\leq\alpha\leq q\). If \(t_e(\alpha)>0\), then
\[
 \omega_e<\alpha_e\leq q_e,
\]
so \(\omega_e<q_e\). The contrapositive of the second implication in (18) gives
\[
 s_*(m_e)\in L_e
 =\frac12(\mathcal K_z+\mathcal K_{z'})
 \qquad(e=\{z,z'\}).
\tag{19}
\]
Consequently there are points \(x_{e,z}\in\mathcal K_z\) and \(x_{e,z'}\in\mathcal K_{z'}\) such that
\[
 \frac{x_{e,z}+x_{e,z'}}2=s_*(m_e).
\tag{20}
\]
These are precisely the witnesses required for affine half-metronoid realizability. Moreover,
\[
 0\leq t_e(\alpha)\leq\alpha_e\leq q_e,
\]
so every endpoint degree of \(t(\alpha)\) is bounded by the corresponding endpoint degree of \(q\), hence by \(a_z/2\). Thus \(t(\alpha)\) is a legal off-diagonal same-parity matching, and (20) shows that the single map \(s_*\) realizes all of its positive edges.

For each edge, the elementary identity
\[
 \alpha_e=\min\{\alpha_e,\omega_e\}+(\alpha_e-\omega_e)_+
\tag{21}
\]
holds because both numbers are nonnegative. Summing (21) proves (6). Taking \(\alpha=q\) and using \(0\leq\omega\leq q\) gives
\[
 t_e(q)=q_e-\omega_e,
 \qquad
 |t(q)|=Q-\Omega.
\tag{22}
\]
Accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d identifies \(R_C(q)\) with the maximum mass of a realizable submatching of \(q\), so the explicit realization above gives \(Q-\Omega\leq R_C(q)\). Since every such submatching is among those defining the canonical capacity \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\) in accepted fact 8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5, one has \(R_C(q)\leq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\). This proves (7).

We now prove the halo consequence. Accepted fact 179add32aa016da1, under (8), gives exactly the alternative (9), or else gives all of its synchronized conclusions simultaneously and universally over \(\mathscr H_{\mathrm{sat}}(C)\). Work in the latter case.

First, \(q^\times\) is a legal matching of the kind used above. Indeed, for \(u\) in a nonempty parity class \(\xi\),
\[
 d_u(p^\times)
 =\sum_{\substack{v\equiv u\\v\ne u}}
   \frac{a_ua_v}{2A_\xi}
 =\frac{a_u(A_\xi-a_u)}{2A_\xi}
 \leq\frac{a_u}{2}.
\tag{23}
\]
Since \(\gamma_d\leq1/2\),
\[
 d_u(q^\times)=2\gamma_dd_u(p^\times)
 \leq\gamma_da_u\leq\frac{a_u}{2}.
\tag{24}
\]
For every admissible cap label, (13) gives
\[
 0\leq s_{i,e}\leq\frac12q_e^\times\leq q_e^\times.
\tag{25}
\]
The strict positive mass bound below also implies that \(q^\times\ne0\), so accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d applies to it.

Equation (25) lets us apply the already proved clipped-residual assertion with \(q=q^\times\) and \(\alpha=s_i\). It follows that \(t_i\) is realized by \(s_*^\times\) and that
\[
 S_i=O_i(v^\times)+|t_i|.
\tag{26}
\]
By the definition of \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\) in accepted fact 8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5,
\[
 \mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\geq|t_i|.
\tag{27}
\]
The synchronized mass estimate (25) of accepted fact 179add32aa016da1 is
\[
 S_i>\frac{\gamma_d\eta^2}{8}W.
\tag{28}
\]
Equations (26)-(28) prove (15). If both \(O_i(v^\times)\) and \(|t_i|\) were at most \(\gamma_d\eta^2W/16\), their sum would be at most \(\gamma_d\eta^2W/8\), contradicting (26) and (28). Hence (16) or the first inequality in (17) holds. In the latter case (27) gives the second inequality in (17).

Finally, the affine matching certificate in accepted fact 8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5 gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)
 \geq\gamma_d2^{-n}\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)
 >\frac{\gamma_d^2\eta^2}{16}\,2^{-n}W.
\tag{29}
\]
That same accepted fact records \(\gamma_d\geq e^{-1}\), so (29) implies the last inequality in (17). Every choice \(s_*^\times,v^\times\) was made before the arbitrary member of \(\mathscr H_{\mathrm{sat}}(C)\) and its label were fixed. Therefore the same primal-dual pair gives (15)-(17) for all of the universal cap data in accepted fact 179add32aa016da1. This completes the proof.
