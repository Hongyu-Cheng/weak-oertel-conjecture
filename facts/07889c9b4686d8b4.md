## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and use the
canonical notation of accepted facts 6b09aae189415c70 and
7ec63df101c30343:
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
Z:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},\qquad
W:=\sum_{z\in Z}a_z,
\]
and \(G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)\). Let \(Q\) be the mass of the
half-strength canonical product reservoir. Use the load-minimal optimizer,
with endpoint loads \(\beta_z\), and, for every retained parity class
\(\xi\), put
\[
L_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\beta_z,
\qquad
s_{\{u,v\}}:=\frac{\beta_u\beta_v}{L_\xi}
\quad(u<v,\ u,v\equiv\xi).
\]
For an edge \(e=\{u,v\}\), put \(m_e:=(u+v)/2\), and for every retained
label \((\xi,c)\), put
\[
S_{\xi,c}:=\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_e.
\]

Assume the canonical hard \(I\)-branch hypotheses of accepted fact
7ec63df101c30343: in particular,
\[
\mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100},\qquad n\ge2,
\qquad G<\frac Q{100},\qquad Q\le\frac W8,
\]
and its actual transverse triangle construction has opposite-edge masses
\(t_e\) satisfying
\[
0\le t_e\le\frac12s_e.
\]
Let \(\mathcal E_I\) be its canonical set of \(I\)-bad opposite edges, and
put
\[
\Theta_{\xi,c}:=
\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi,\ m_e=c\\e\in\mathcal E_I}}t_e,
\qquad
\Theta_I(E):=\sum_{c\in E}\sum_\xi\Theta_{\xi,c}
\quad(E\subseteq\mathbb Z^n).
\]
Thus accepted fact 7ec63df101c30343 gives
\[
\Theta_I(\mathbb Z^n)>\frac{3Q}{100}.
\]

Then every retained label satisfies
\[
\Theta_{\xi,c}\le\frac{a_c}{16}.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(A\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) be an affine subspace and set
\[
D_A:=Z\cap A,
\qquad
W_A:=\sum_{z\in D_A}a_z.
\]
If \(D_A\ne\varnothing\) and
\(q:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(D_A)\le2\), then
\[
\Theta_I(A\cap\mathbb Z^n)\le
\begin{cases}
G/16,&q=0,\\
G/2,&q=1,\\
16G,&q=2.
\end{cases}
\tag{2}
\]
If \(D_A=\varnothing\), the left side is zero. In particular, for every
physical target \(c\in\mathbb Z^n\) and every affine line
\(\ell\subseteq\mathbb R^n\),
\[
\Theta_I(\{c\})\le\frac G{16},
\qquad
\Theta_I(\ell\cap\mathbb Z^n)\le\frac G2.
\tag{3}
\]
The hard \(I\)-family therefore uses at least \(49\) distinct physical target
fibers and satisfies
\[
\Theta_I(\mathbb Z^n\setminus\ell)>\frac Q{40}
\tag{4}
\]
for every affine line \(\ell\). More generally, every union of \(k\) affine
subspaces whose intrinsic occupied-fiber ranks are at most two carries at most
\(16kG\) of \(\Theta_I\).

## Proof

Fix a retained label \((\xi,c)\). From accepted fact
7ec63df101c30343, \(t_e\le s_e/2\), and hence
\[
\Theta_{\xi,c}
\le\frac12
\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_e
=\frac12S_{\xi,c}.
\tag{5}
\]

Let \(e=\{u,v\}\) be one of the edges in the last sum. Since \(m_e=c\),
convexity of the actual body \(C\) gives
\[
\frac12(C_u+C_v)\subseteq C_c.
\]
Brunn--Minkowski in \(\mathbb R^d\) therefore gives
\[
a_c^{1/d}\ge
\frac{a_u^{1/d}+a_v^{1/d}}2
\ge(a_ua_v)^{1/(2d)},
\]
so
\[
\sqrt{a_ua_v}\le a_c.
\tag{6}
\]
In particular \(a_c>0\), so every target counted above is an actual occupied
fiber. Accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives
\[
\beta_z\le d_z(r)\le\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{7}
\]
Combining (6), (7), and the arithmetic--geometric mean inequality yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
\beta_u\beta_v
&=\sqrt{\beta_u\beta_v}\sqrt{\beta_u\beta_v}\\
&\le\frac{a_c}{4}\sqrt{\beta_u\beta_v}\\
&\le\frac{a_c}{8}(\beta_u+\beta_v).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]

For fixed \(c\), the distinct reflected edges
\(\{u,2c-u\}\) form a graph-theoretic matching: every endpoint has at most
one reflected partner. Consequently, within the fixed parity class \(\xi\),
\[
\sum_{\substack{e=\{u,v\}\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}
(\beta_u+\beta_v)\le L_\xi.
\tag{9}
\]
Using the exact normalization
\(s_{\{u,v\}}=\beta_u\beta_v/L_\xi\), equations (8)--(9) give
\[
S_{\xi,c}
=\frac1{L_\xi}
\sum_{\substack{e=\{u,v\}\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}
\beta_u\beta_v
\le\frac{a_c}{8}.
\tag{10}
\]
Equation (5) now proves (1). This estimate was obtained separately for each
label; no representing densities from different parity labels were pooled.

There are at most \(2^n\) parity labels at one physical target. Summing (1)
first over the labels and then over \(c\in A\cap\mathbb Z^n\) gives
\[
\Theta_I(A\cap\mathbb Z^n)
\le2^{n-4}W_A.
\tag{11}
\]

Assume \(D_A\ne\varnothing\) and
\(q=\dim\operatorname{aff}(D_A)\le2\). Write
\[
\alpha_0=1,\qquad \alpha_1=\frac14,\qquad
\alpha_2=\frac1{64}.
\]
We first check that the occupied affine hull
\(B:=\operatorname{aff}(D_A)\) is proper. If \(B=\mathbb R^n\), then
\(q=n\). The hard branch has \(n\ge2\), so \(q=n=2\). The full-rank clause
of accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c would give
\[
G\ge\alpha_2W=\frac W{64}.
\]
On the other hand, the hard assumptions give
\[
G<\frac Q{100}\le\frac W{800},
\]
a contradiction. Thus \(B\subsetneq\mathbb R^n\).

The proper-slice clause of accepted fact 8aac56c49321466c gives
\[
G\ge
\min\left\{
\alpha_qW_A+\frac{W-W_A}{2},
\ 2^{n-q}\alpha_qW_A
\right\}.
\tag{12}
\]
The first entry in this minimum is at least \(W/2\), \(W/4\), or \(W/64\)
when \(q=0,1,2\), respectively. Every one of these lower bounds is strictly
larger than \(G\), because \(G<W/800\). Hence the second entry in (12) must
be at most \(G\), and therefore
\[
W_A\le\frac{2^{q-n}}{\alpha_q}G.
\tag{13}
\]
Combining (11) and (13),
\[
\Theta_I(A\cap\mathbb Z^n)
\le\frac{2^{q-4}}{\alpha_q}G.
\tag{14}
\]
For \(q=0,1,2\), the coefficient in (14) is, respectively,
\(1/16,1/2,16\). This proves (2) and (3).

For every target \(c\), equations (3) and \(G<Q/100\) give
\[
\Theta_I(\{c\})<\frac Q{1600}.
\]
Since the total mass is strictly larger than
\(3Q/100=48Q/1600\), at least \(49\) distinct target fibers are required.
For every affine line \(\ell\),
\[
\Theta_I(\mathbb Z^n\setminus\ell)
>\frac{3Q}{100}-\frac{G}{2}
>\frac{3Q}{100}-\frac Q{200}
=\frac Q{40},
\]
which proves (4). Finally, subadditivity over a union of \(k\) slices and the
largest coefficient in (2) give the bound \(16kG\).
