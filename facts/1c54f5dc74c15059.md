## Statement

Let \(n\geq2\) and \(d\geq1\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
 Z:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
\]
\[
 W:=\sum_{z\in Z}a_z>0,
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\tag{1}
\]
For a positive-volume bounded measurable set \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) and \(0<m\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(K)\), use the canonical metronoid
\[
 \mathsf M_m(K):=
 \left\{
 \frac1m\int_Kx f(x)\,dx:
 0\leq f\leq1\ \text{a.e.},\quad \int_Kf=m
 \right\}.
\tag{2}
\]
For \((z,x)\in C\), define the absolute fiber depth
\[
 \delta_C(z,x):=
 \inf_{\substack{J\subseteq\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{closed\ halfspace}\\x\in J}}
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap J).
\tag{3}
\]
For a Borel set \(B\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\), put
\[
 \mu_C(B):=
 \sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}
 \operatorname{vol}_d\{x\in C_z:(z,x)\in B\},
\]
and define
\[
 h_{\max}(C):=
 \sup_{p\in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)}
 \inf_{\substack{H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\ \mathrm{closed\ halfspace}\\p\in H}}
 \mu_C(H).
\tag{3a}
\]
For every parity class \(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\), put
\[
 A_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi\ ({\rm mod}\ 2)}a_z,
\tag{4}
\]
and define the strong canonical product matching from accepted fact `cacf8017a747fd4c` by
\[
 q^\times_{\{u,v\}}
 :=\frac{\gamma_da_ua_v}{A_\xi}
 \quad
 (u\ne v,\ u\equiv v\equiv\xi\ ({\rm mod}\ 2),\ A_\xi>0),
\tag{5}
\]
with all other entries zero. In particular,
\[
 d_z(q^\times):=\sum_{e\ni z}q^\times_e\leq\gamma_da_z.
\tag{6}
\]

If \(q^\times=0\), the atlas below is empty and all conditional assertions are vacuous. Otherwise choose any load-minimal optimal stress and its support-contact data in the normalization of accepted facts `3f9592d2fb367884` and `cacf8017a747fd4c`. Write its canonical edge loads as \(\omega_e\), and put
\[
 F_+:=\{e:\omega_e>0\}.
\tag{7}
\]
one has
\[
 0<\omega_e\leq q^\times_e\quad(e\in F_+),
\tag{8}
\]
and, for every \(e=\{z,z'\}\in F_+\), there are endpoint contacts
\[
 x_{e,z}\in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
 \qquad
 x_{e,z'}\in\mathsf M_{a_{z'}/2}(C_{z'}).
\tag{9}
\]

If two distinct edges \(e=\{z,a\}\) and \(f=\{z,b\}\) in \(F_+\) share their unique common endpoint \(z\), put
\[
 h_{e,z}:=\frac{a-z}{2},\qquad h_{f,z}:=\frac{b-z}{2}.
\tag{10}
\]
Call \(\{e,f\}\) transverse when \(h_{e,z},h_{f,z}\) are linearly independent. Let \(\vartheta=(\vartheta_{\{e,f\}})\) be any feasible transverse line-graph pairing, not necessarily an optimizer: its variables are nonnegative, vanish on nontransverse pairs, and satisfy
\[
 \sum_{f:\{e,f\}\ \mathrm{transverse}}\vartheta_{\{e,f\}}
 \leq\omega_e
 \qquad(e\in F_+).
\tag{11}
\]

For every positive contribution \(\vartheta_{\{e,f\}}>0\), retain the full label
\[
 \sigma=(z;e,f),
 \qquad
 \theta_\sigma:=\vartheta_{\{e,f\}},
\tag{12}
\]
where \(e=\{z,a\}\) and \(f=\{z,b\}\), and define its opposite-endpoint midpoint base and point by
\[
 m_\sigma:=\frac{a+b}{2}\in\mathbb Z^n,
 \qquad
 X_\sigma:=\frac{x_{e,a}+x_{f,b}}2.
\tag{13}
\]
The resulting labeled transverse atlas is
\[
 \mathscr A(\vartheta):=
 \{(\sigma,m_\sigma,X_\sigma,\theta_\sigma):
 \theta_\sigma>0\}.
\tag{14}
\]
Every atlas element has the exact full-mass realization and depth
\[
 \boxed{
 X_\sigma\in\mathsf M_{\theta_\sigma}(C_{m_\sigma}),
 \qquad
 \delta_C(m_\sigma,X_\sigma)\geq\gamma_d\theta_\sigma.}
\tag{15}
\]

These realizations have the following simultaneous source-fiber form. For each loaded incidence \((e,y)\), choose once and for all a half-mass witness \(f_{e,y}\) for (9):
\[
 0\leq f_{e,y}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_y}f_{e,y}=\frac{a_y}{2},
 \qquad
 \frac2{a_y}\int_{C_y}x f_{e,y}(x)\,dx=x_{e,y}.
\tag{16}
\]
For \(\sigma=(z;e,f)\), with \(e=\{z,a\}\) and \(f=\{z,b\}\), define its two source densities by
\[
 g_{\sigma,a}:=\frac{2\theta_\sigma}{a_a}f_{e,a},
 \qquad
 g_{\sigma,b}:=\frac{2\theta_\sigma}{a_b}f_{f,b}.
\tag{17}
\]
Each density has mass \(\theta_\sigma\) and its indicated endpoint contact as barycenter. For every subfamily \(\mathscr Q\subseteq\mathscr A(\vartheta)\) and every physical source fiber \(C_y\), all source occurrences in \(\mathscr Q\) obey the pointwise bound
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr Q\\y\ \mathrm{is\ an\ opposite\ source\ of}\ \sigma}}
 g_{\sigma,y}
 \leq2\gamma_d\leq1.}
\tag{18}
\]
This is a simultaneous source-density assertion. It does not assert that the independently produced densities in a common target fiber can be added.

We now state the exact conditional gluing conclusion. Let \(r,s\geq1\) satisfy \(r+s=n\). Let
\[
 \Delta_1=\operatorname{conv}\{u_0,\ldots,u_r\}\subseteq\mathbb R^r,
 \qquad
 \Delta_2=\operatorname{conv}\{v_0,\ldots,v_s\}\subseteq\mathbb R^s
\tag{19}
\]
be full-dimensional lattice simplices, and put
\[
 \mathcal D_1:=\Delta_1\cap\mathbb Z^r,
 \qquad
 \mathcal D_2:=\Delta_2\cap\mathbb Z^s.
\tag{20}
\]
Let \(\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_r\) and \(\beta_0,\ldots,\beta_s\) be their barycentric-coordinate functions. Let
\[
 \Phi(u,v):=m+U_1u+U_2v,
 \qquad
 m\in\mathbb Z^n,
 \quad U_1\in\mathbb Z^{n\times r},
 \quad U_2\in\mathbb Z^{n\times s},
\tag{21}
\]
where the concatenated matrix \([U_1\ U_2]\) has rank \(n\). Thus \(\Phi\) is an integral affine injection.

Assume that there are pairwise distinct atlas labels
\[
 \sigma_{ij}\in\mathscr A(\vartheta)
 \qquad(0\leq i\leq r,\ 0\leq j\leq s)
\tag{22}
\]
whose actual midpoint bases form the complete product grid
\[
 m_{\sigma_{ij}}=\Phi(u_i,v_j)
 \qquad(0\leq i\leq r,\ 0\leq j\leq s).
\tag{23}
\]
Because \(\Phi\) is injective, these product-vertex bases are pairwise distinct; in particular there are no midpoint collisions. Write
\[
 \theta_{ij}:=\theta_{\sigma_{ij}},
 \qquad
 X_{ij}:=X_{\sigma_{ij}}.
\tag{24}
\]
Assume also that positive numbers \(\pi_0,\ldots,\pi_r\) and \(\rho_0,\ldots,\rho_s\) give a rank-one minorant
\[
 \pi_i\rho_j\leq\theta_{ij}
 \qquad(0\leq i\leq r,\ 0\leq j\leq s).
\tag{25}
\]
Define
\[
 B_1:=
 \sum_{u\in\mathcal D_1}
 \left(\sum_{i=0}^r\alpha_i(u)\pi_i^{1/d}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 B_2:=
 \sum_{v\in\mathcal D_2}
 \left(\sum_{j=0}^s\beta_j(v)\rho_j^{1/d}\right)^d.
\tag{26}
\]
Then the single chart \(\Phi\) and the actual labeled points \(X_{ij}\) determine one admissible full-rank two-factor lattice-polysimplex packet \(\Pi\), in the exact sense of accepted fact `5b6523682250e7a5`, with
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal E(\Pi)=\gamma_dB_1B_2.}
\tag{27}
\]
In the interaction-dimension normalization of accepted fact `674b50aa42f420d8`,
\[
 \boxed{\chi(\Pi)+1=rs+1.}
\tag{28}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{
 h_{\max}(C)\geq2^{-n}\gamma_dB_1B_2,
 \qquad
 \frac{\mathcal E(\Pi)}{\chi(\Pi)+1}
 =\frac{\gamma_dB_1B_2}{rs+1}.}
\tag{29}
\]
Equations (27)--(29) are conditional only on the explicit compatible-grid hypotheses (19)--(25). They do not assert that a compatible grid or a quantitatively large rank-one minorant always exists.

## Proof

Accepted fact `cacf8017a747fd4c` gives the canonical product normalization (5) and the degree estimate (6). Accepted fact `3f9592d2fb367884`, applied to this strong matching, supplies (7)--(9), including \(\omega_e\leq q^\times_e\). The same-parity support of \(q^\times\) shows that \(a\equiv z\equiv b\pmod2\), so \(m_\sigma=(a+b)/2\) in (13) is integral. Transversality also makes the two half-directions in (10) integral and linearly independent, although only the integrality of \(m_\sigma\) is needed for (15).

Fix \(\sigma=(z;e,f)\), where \(e=\{z,a\}\), \(f=\{z,b\}\), and put \(\theta:=\theta_\sigma\). Feasibility (11) gives
\[
 \theta\leq\omega_e\leq q^\times_e
 \leq d_a(q^\times)\leq\gamma_da_a\leq\frac{a_a}{2},
\tag{30}
\]
and symmetrically \(\theta\leq a_b/2\). The last inequality uses
\[
 \gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}\leq\frac12,
\tag{31}
\]
which follows from \((1+1/d)^d\geq2\).

Scale the two fixed witnesses in (16) by \(2\theta/a_a\) and \(2\theta/a_b\). Equation (30) makes the scaled functions admissible densities of mass \(\theta\), and their barycenters remain \(x_{e,a}\) and \(x_{f,b}\). Hence
\[
 (a,x_{e,a},\theta^{1/d}),
 \qquad
 (b,x_{f,b},\theta^{1/d})
\tag{32}
\]
belong to the positive-root metronoid generating set of accepted fact `dfd25e72c1f45a4e`. The proof of that accepted fact establishes convexity of the positive-root generating set itself, before taking its closure. Taking the midpoint of (32), with the common positive root unchanged, gives the exact membership
\[
 X_\sigma=\frac{x_{e,a}+x_{f,b}}2
 \in\mathsf M_\theta(C_{(a+b)/2}).
\tag{33}
\]
This is the full mass \(\theta\), not \(\theta/2\). Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` turns (33) into
\[
 \delta_C(m_\sigma,X_\sigma)\geq\gamma_d\theta,
\tag{34}
\]
proving (15). This is also the opposite-corner construction underlying the local transverse square in accepted fact `3f9592d2fb367884`, now applied to an arbitrary feasible transverse pairing rather than only to a maximizing full line-graph pairing.

We prove simultaneous source feasibility. Fix a subfamily \(\mathscr Q\subseteq\mathscr A(\vartheta)\) and a physical source base \(y\). For a loaded edge \(e=\{z,y\}\), only pairings of \(e\) at its other endpoint \(z\) use the opposite contact \(x_{e,y}\) as a source. Therefore (11) gives
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma=(z;e,f)\in\mathscr Q\\e=\{z,y\}}}
 \theta_\sigma
 \leq
 \sum_{f:\{e,f\}\ \mathrm{transverse}}
 \vartheta_{\{e,f\}}
 \leq\omega_e.
\tag{35}
\]
Using \(0\leq f_{e,y}\leq1\), summing (17), grouping first by the edge whose opposite contact lies in \(C_y\), and then applying (8), (6), and (31), gives pointwise on \(C_y\)
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr Q\\y\ \mathrm{is\ an\ opposite\ source\ of}\ \sigma}}
 g_{\sigma,y}
 &\leq
 \frac2{a_y}\sum_{e\ni y}\omega_e f_{e,y}\\
 &\leq\frac2{a_y}\sum_{e\ni y}\omega_e
 \leq\frac2{a_y}\sum_{e\ni y}q^\times_e
 =\frac{2d_y(q^\times)}{a_y}\\
 &\leq2\gamma_d\leq1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{36}
\]
The mass and barycenter claims following (17) are immediate from (16). This proves (18) simultaneously over an arbitrary subfamily. Notice that no endpoint-disjointness and no posterior choice of one label per target base is used.

It remains to prove the compatible-grid gluing conclusion. For every product vertex, (15), (23), and (24) give the actual point and depth estimate
\[
 X_{ij}\in C_{\Phi(u_i,v_j)},
 \qquad
 \delta_C(\Phi(u_i,v_j),X_{ij})
 \geq\gamma_d\theta_{ij}
 \geq\gamma_d\pi_i\rho_j.
\tag{37}
\]
Define the two arrays of factor roots by
\[
 a_{1,i}:=\gamma_d^{1/d}\pi_i^{1/d}
 \quad(0\leq i\leq r),
 \qquad
 a_{2,j}:=\rho_j^{1/d}
 \quad(0\leq j\leq s).
\tag{38}
\]
Then (37) says exactly
\[
 \delta_C(\Phi(u_i,v_j),X_{ij})^{1/d}
 \geq a_{1,i}a_{2,j},
\tag{39}
\]
which is the product-vertex hypothesis of accepted fact `5b6523682250e7a5`. The simplices in (19) are full dimensional, all their vertices are integral, the map (21) is integral, and its concatenated matrix has rank \(r+s=n\). Thus every base-map hypothesis of that accepted fact holds. Applying it produces one admissible full-rank two-factor packet \(\Pi\) on the single chart \(\Phi\), using the actual labeled points \(X_{ij}\).

The first factor function and energy are
\[
 g_1(u)=\gamma_d^{1/d}
 \sum_{i=0}^r\alpha_i(u)\pi_i^{1/d},
 \qquad
 \sum_{u\in\mathcal D_1}g_1(u)^d=\gamma_dB_1,
\tag{40}
\]
while the second factor function and energy are
\[
 g_2(v)=\sum_{j=0}^s\beta_j(v)\rho_j^{1/d},
 \qquad
 \sum_{v\in\mathcal D_2}g_2(v)^d=B_2.
\tag{41}
\]
The exact packet energy in `5b6523682250e7a5` is the product of the factor energies. Equations (40)--(41) therefore give
\[
 \mathcal E(\Pi)=\gamma_dB_1B_2,
\tag{42}
\]
with no additional factor \(1/2\). Since the total packet rank is \(n\), the same accepted fact gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)\geq2^{-n}\mathcal E(\Pi)
 =2^{-n}\gamma_dB_1B_2.
\tag{43}
\]

Finally, accepted fact `674b50aa42f420d8` defines the interaction dimension of a factorized packet of ranks \(r_1,\ldots,r_k\) by
\[
 \chi(\Pi)=\prod_{\ell=1}^k(r_\ell+1)-1-\sum_{\ell=1}^kr_\ell.
\tag{44}
\]
For the present two factors of ranks \(r,s\), with \(r+s=n\), this becomes
\[
 \chi(\Pi)=(r+1)(s+1)-1-r-s=rs.
\tag{45}
\]
Equations (42), (43), and (45) prove (27)--(29). The proof never sums packet energies from different charts and never assumes that the compatible labels (22)--(23) or the rank-one minorant (25) can always be extracted.
