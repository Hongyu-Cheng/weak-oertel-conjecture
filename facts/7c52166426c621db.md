## Statement

Let $d\ge2$ and let $A,B\subset\mathbb R^d$ be convex bodies of equal $d$-volume $a>0$. Set $M=(A+B)/2$, $r=|M|/a$, and for any nonzero $u$ put $w_A=h_A(u)+h_A(-u)$, $w_B=h_B(u)+h_B(-u)$ and $R=w_A/w_B$. Then
$$
 |A+B|\ge(w_A+w_B)(a/w_A+a/w_B),
 \qquad R+R^{-1}\le 2^d r-2 .
$$
In all $d\ge1$ one consequently has both $1+R\le 2^d r$ and $1+R^{-1}\le 2^d r$.

## Proof

Normalize $u$ to a unit vector, which leaves $R$ and the product in the first inequality invariant. Write $\alpha,\beta$ for the minimum $u$-coordinates of $A,B$. For $s\in(0,1)$ let $f(s),g(s)$ be their $(d-1)$-dimensional section volumes at heights $\alpha+w_As$ and $\beta+w_Bs$. These sections have nonempty relative interiors because the bodies are full-dimensional and the heights are interior. Their section sum lies in the section of $A+B$ at height $\alpha+\beta+(w_A+w_B)s$. Brunn–Minkowski in the parallel $(d-1)$-space bounds its volume below by
$$
 \bigl(f(s)^{1/(d-1)}+g(s)^{1/(d-1)}\bigr)^{d-1}\ge f(s)+g(s).
$$
For $d=2$ the last inequality is equality. Fubini in the $u$-coordinate gives $\int_0^1f(s)\,ds=a/w_A$, $\int_0^1g(s)\,ds=a/w_B$, and the section measure on $A+B$ integrates with differential $(w_A+w_B)\,ds$. The resulting first bound gives $2^d r\ge2+R+R^{-1}$. This implies the two separate comparisons for $d\ge2$. In $d=1$, $A,B$ are intervals of equal length, so $r=R=1$ and both comparisons hold with equality.
