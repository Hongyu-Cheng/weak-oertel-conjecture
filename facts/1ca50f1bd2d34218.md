## Statement

Let $\rho_0,\rho_1$ be compactly supported probabilities on $\mathbb R^m$, $m\ge1$, and let $0<s<1$. For the two-layer probability $\rho'=s\,\delta_0\otimes\rho_0+(1-s)\,\delta_1\otimes\rho_1$ one has
$$
\operatorname{depth}_{\rho'}(0,y)=s\operatorname{depth}_{\rho_0}(y)
\quad\text{for every }y\in\mathbb R^m,
$$
using proper closed ambient halfspaces.

For a full-dimensional compact convex mixed body $D\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ of total mixed mass $M_0>0$, with $n,d\ge1$, there are two applications. Binary product padding $[0,1]\times D$ multiplies both the normalized mixed and the normalized base-marginal depth at every old mark in layer zero by $1/2$. Thus a selected-fiber failure of the bound $\gamma_d\beta$, where $\beta$ is the old normalized marginal depth, becomes a failure of the bound $\gamma_d\min(1/2,\beta/2)$ in layer zero.

Given $p\in\mathbb Z^n$ and a compact convex $d$-body $K$ of volume $W>0$, the hull $\operatorname{conv}((\{0\}\times D)\cup(\{1\}\times\{p\}\times K))$ multiplies both depths on layer zero by $M_0/(M_0+W)$. For an old marginal depth $\beta$ with $2^{n+1}\beta>1$, the choice $W=M_0(2^{n+1}\beta-1)$ makes the corresponding marginal depth exactly $2^{-(n+1)}$. A strict old selected-fiber upper bound below $\gamma_d\beta$ becomes a strict upper bound below $\gamma_d 2^{-(n+1)}$. Here $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$.

## Proof

Every closed containing halfspace at $(0,y)$ restricts on layer zero to a closed halfspace containing $y$ or the whole layer. Its contribution from that layer is at least $s\operatorname{depth}_{\rho_0}(y)$ and the other contribution is nonnegative. Conversely take any proper old containing halfspace $a\cdot w\ge b$, $a\ne0$. Choose a finite $L>\max_{\operatorname{supp}\rho_1}(a\cdot w-b)$ and extend the test to $a\cdot w-Lu\ge b$ in the new coordinate $u$. The extension contains $(0,y)$, has precisely the old trace in layer zero and misses the supported layer one strictly. Infimizing over the old tests gives equality without assuming an attaining test.

The product has exactly two integer layers, each a copy of $D$, with identical mixed mass $M_0$. For the second hull, the integer values of $u$ are again just $0,1$, and extremality of each endpoint shows that the actual layers are precisely the two generating sets. Full dimension follows from the full-dimensional old layer and any point in the other layer. The added layer has one sampled base $p$ and mass $W$. Therefore the mixed probabilities have layer-zero weight $1/2$ and $M_0/(M_0+W)$, respectively, and so do their base pushforwards. Applying the pointwise identity in both ambient dimensions proves all of the scaling formulas and hence the selected-fiber comparisons, since the layer-zero fibers are unchanged as sets. For the product $\beta/2\le1/2$. For the hull with the displayed $W$, the new marginal depth is $M_0\beta/(M_0+W)=2^{-(n+1)}$. Every positive volume $W$ is possible by scaling one coordinate of a fixed positive simplex, since $d\ge1$.
