## Statement

Let $P=\operatorname{conv}(P)\cap\mathbb Z^n$ be finite and nonempty, and let the lifted convex hull $C$ of full-dimensional compact convex fibers $K_z\subset\mathbb R^d$, $z\in P$, realize exactly $K_z$ at each $z$. Let $d\ge1$, $B=\operatorname{conv}P$, and $q\in\mathbb R^n\setminus B$. For $p\in P$ define the representation polytope
$$
R_p(q)=\left\{\alpha\ge0:\alpha_q+\sum_{z\in P}\alpha_z=1,\quad
\alpha_q q+\sum_{z\in P}\alpha_z z=p\right\}.
$$
The hull of $C$ and $(q,x)$ preserves every old fiber exactly if and only if at every vertex $\alpha\in R_p(q)$ with $\alpha_q>0$ one has $\alpha_qx+\sum_z\alpha_zK_z\subseteq K_p$, for every $p\in P$. The locus of such $x$ is closed and convex, possibly empty. If the old fibers are polytopal, the constraints are the finite inequalities $\alpha_q u\cdot x\le h_{K_p}(u)-\sum_z\alpha_z h_{K_z}(u)$ for the facet normals $u$ of each $K_p$.

If $q\in\mathbb Z^n$ and $x$ is preserving, the extension adds no positive mixed mass outside $P$ if and only if $\operatorname{conv}(P\cup\{q\})\cap\mathbb Z^n=P\cup\{q\}$. The new $q$-fiber is $\{x\}$.

## Proof

At any old base $p$, the extended fiber is the union of $\alpha_qx+\sum_z\alpha_zK_z$ over $\alpha\in R_p(q)$. This follows by grouping the convex combination of lifted points according to their base and using convexity of each $K_z$. Conversely each represented sum belongs to the hull. For every direction, the requirement that the support of that sum be at most $h_{K_p}$ is linear in $\alpha$. It therefore suffices to test the vertices, and the $\alpha_q=0$ tests hold by original compatibility. The tests are intersections of closed halfspaces in $x$. For a polytopal target it is enough to use its facet inequalities.

Since $q\notin B$, it is an exposed vertex of $\operatorname{conv}(B\cup\{q\})$. Its fiber is the one point used in the extension, which has zero $d$-volume. If $r$ is another new integer site, write $r=\lambda q+(1-\lambda)b$ with $b\in B$ and $0<\lambda<1$. Every $b\in B$ is a convex combination of old bases, and the corresponding Minkowski combination of positive old fibers lies in $C_b$ and has positive $d$-volume, by varying a positive-coefficient fiber while fixing the other summands. The new $r$-fiber contains $(1-\lambda)C_b+\lambda x$, of positive volume. If no other integer site exists, there is no other new fiber.
