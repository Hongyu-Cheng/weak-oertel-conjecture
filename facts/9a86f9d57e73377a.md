## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and retain one common canonical hard \(I\)-dominant instantiation of accepted facts 14923f1f477896cc, 7ec63df101c30343, and a0c2429d87c7929b. In particular, put

\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]

\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D,
 \qquad
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad I\ge O.
\tag{1}
\]

Let \(a_*\) be a minimizer of the labelled-output defect \(O\), and define

\[
 \mathcal F_{\mathrm{cl}}:=
 \left\{e\in\mathcal E_I\setminus\mathcal B:
 s_e>0,\
 \left\lVert q_{\ell(e)}^\circ-a_*(m_e)\right\rVert_{D_*}
 \le\frac14
 \right\}.
\tag{2}
\]

Thus

\[
 B_{\mathrm{cl}}^\circ
 :=\sum_{e\in\mathcal F_{\mathrm{cl}}}s_e,
 \qquad
 X_{\mathrm{cl}}
 :=\sum_{e\in\mathcal F_{\mathrm{cl}}}
 \min\{\omega_e,s_e/16\}.
\tag{3}
\]

Let \(s_*^{\mathrm{KKT}}:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) be the canonical primal weighted-affine-defect minimizer paired with the load-minimal dual optimizer defining \(\omega_e\). Put

\[
 u_e:=
 \begin{cases}
  (s_e/16-\omega_e)_+,&e\in\mathcal F_{\mathrm{cl}},\\
  0,&e\notin\mathcal F_{\mathrm{cl}},
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 U_{\mathrm{hit}}:=\sum_eu_e.
\tag{4}
\]

Then \(u=(u_e)\) is an affinely half-metronoid-realizable submatching of the canonical reservoir \(r\), realized by the single affine map \(s_*^{\mathrm{KKT}}\). In particular,

\[
 \boxed{
 U_{\mathrm{hit}}\le R,
 \qquad
 D\ge\gamma_dU_{\mathrm{hit}}.}
\tag{5}
\]

Moreover,

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac1{16}B_{\mathrm{cl}}^\circ
 =X_{\mathrm{cl}}+U_{\mathrm{hit}}.}
\tag{6}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \boxed{
 B_{\mathrm{cl}}^\circ
 \le16X_{\mathrm{cl}}+\frac{16}{\gamma_d}D
 \le16\mathrm e\bigl(
 X_{\mathrm{cl}}+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D\bigr).}
\tag{7}
\]

Thus the registered overlap inequality holds with

\[
 \boxed{C_0=16\mathrm e.}
\tag{8}
\]

The accepted close-versus-far decomposition also gives

\[
 \boxed{
 I-O
 \le G+16X_{\mathrm{cl}}+16U_{\mathrm{hit}}
 \le G+16X_{\mathrm{cl}}
       +16\min\{R,D/\gamma_d\}.}
\tag{9}
\]

In particular, the hard inequality in (1) forces

\[
 \boxed{
 X_{\mathrm{cl}}
 >\frac{B_{\mathrm{cl}}^\circ}{16}-\frac Q{100}
 \ge\frac{(I-O-G)_+}{16}-\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{10}
\]

More strongly, the complete accepted defect ledger and (9) give the fused close/far inequality

\[
 \boxed{
 O+8X_{\mathrm{cl}}>\frac9{50}Q.}
\tag{10a}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \boxed{
 O>\frac Q8
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 X_{\mathrm{cl}}>\frac{11}{1600}Q>\frac Q{160}.}
\tag{10b}
\]

This is the hard-ledger alternative: if the primal-hit portion reaches \(Q/100\), then \(R\ge Q/100\) is already terminal; otherwise every remaining close mass, after the exact factor \(16\), is actual clipped-contact mass. Equation (10a) then forces either the far labelled-output branch or a close clipped-contact mass at least as large as the calibration used by the accepted rank screens.

For completeness, the combination with a representative accepted clipped rank screen can be made without adding two bounds on \(D\). If \(X_{\mathrm{cl}}>0\), define

\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{cl}}:=
 \dim\operatorname{aff}
 \left\{m_e:e\in\mathcal F_{\mathrm{cl}},\
 \min\{\omega_e,s_e/16\}>0\right\},
\]

\[
 r_{F,\mathrm{cl}}:=
 \min_{b\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \left\{F_e^\flat-b(m_e):
 e\in\mathcal F_{\mathrm{cl}},\
 \min\{\omega_e,s_e/16\}>0\right\},
\tag{11}
\]

where \(b\) is defined on the affine hull in the preceding line, and put

\[
 \kappa_{\mathrm{cl}}:=
 32\gamma_d
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{cl}}}}{r_{F,\mathrm{cl}}+1}.
\tag{12}
\]

The clipped simultaneous-domination theorem, restricted to this close subfamily, gives \(D\ge\kappa_{\mathrm{cl}}X_{\mathrm{cl}}\). Together with (5), using the single \(D\)-summand only once,

\[
 \boxed{
 D\ge
 \max\{\gamma_dU_{\mathrm{hit}},
        \kappa_{\mathrm{cl}}X_{\mathrm{cl}}\}
 \ge
 \frac{\gamma_d\kappa_{\mathrm{cl}}}
      {16(\gamma_d+\kappa_{\mathrm{cl}})}
 B_{\mathrm{cl}}^\circ.}
\tag{13}
\]

Hence the hard ledger implies

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cl}}^\circ}{Q}
 <\frac{16}{100}
 \left(\frac1{\gamma_d}+\frac1{\kappa_{\mathrm{cl}}}\right).}
\tag{14}
\]

Equations (6), (10), and (13) are the exact interface with the clipped-contact rank screens of accepted fact a0c2429d87c7929b; no close label is assigned to \(O_\triangle\).

## Proof

Accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives the coordinatewise reservoir comparison

\[
 \frac1{16}s_e\le r_e
\tag{15}
\]

for every retained product edge. Accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d gives

\[
 0\le\omega_e\le r_e,
 \qquad
 s_*^{\mathrm{KKT}}(m_e)\notin L_e
 \Longrightarrow\omega_e=r_e.
\tag{16}
\]

Fix an edge with \(u_e>0\). Equations (4) and (15) give

\[
 \omega_e<\frac{s_e}{16}\le r_e.
\tag{17}
\]

The contrapositive of the second implication in (16) therefore yields

\[
 s_*^{\mathrm{KKT}}(m_e)\in L_e
 =\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v)
 \qquad(e=\{u,v\}).
\tag{18}
\]

Thus there are actual points \(y_{e,u}\in\mathcal K_u\) and
\(y_{e,v}\in\mathcal K_v\) satisfying

\[
 \frac{y_{e,u}+y_{e,v}}2
 =s_*^{\mathrm{KKT}}(m_e).
\tag{19}
\]

Also \(0\le u_e\le s_e/16\le r_e\) coordinatewise. Since \(r\) is an off-diagonal fractional same-parity matching, the same is true of \(u\), including all endpoint-capacity inequalities. Equations (18)-(19) show that one affine map realizes every positive edge of \(u\). Hence \(u\) is an actual affinely half-metronoid-realizable submatching of \(r\). By the definition of \(R=R_C(r)\),

\[
 U_{\mathrm{hit}}=|u|\le R.
\tag{20}
\]

It is also admissible in the global affine-matching capacity
\(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\) of accepted fact 8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5. The affine matching certificate in that fact gives

\[
 D=2^nh_{\max}(C)
 \ge\gamma_d\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)
 \ge\gamma_dU_{\mathrm{hit}}.
\tag{21}
\]

This proves (5). Equation (18), not a degree-ledger assertion, is the KKT/contact-geometric input: an edge with deficient dual load must be hit by the primal affine selector, and (19) supplies its genuine half-metronoid witnesses.

For every nonnegative pair \((\omega,t)\),

\[
 t=\min\{\omega,t\}+(t-\omega)_+.
\tag{22}
\]

Apply (22) with \(t=s_e/16\) and sum over
\(e\in\mathcal F_{\mathrm{cl}}\). Definitions (3)-(4) give exactly (6). Combining (6) with the second inequality in (5) proves the first inequality in (7).

The elementary bound

\[
 \gamma_d^{-1}
 =\left(1+\frac1d\right)^d<\mathrm e
\tag{23}
\]

and \(\mathrm e>1\) give

\[
 16X_{\mathrm{cl}}+\frac{16}{\gamma_d}D
 <16\mathrm e(X_{\mathrm{cl}}+D).
\]

All the additional quantities on the right side of (7) are nonnegative. This proves (7)-(8).

Accepted fact 14923f1f477896cc gives that the total \(a_*\)-close \(I\)-bad \(s\)-mass is at least \(I-O\). Accepted fact 7ec63df101c30343 gives

\[
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal B}s_e\le G.
\]

Deleting the exceptional edges therefore gives

\[
 B_{\mathrm{cl}}^\circ\ge(I-O-G)_+.
\tag{24}
\]

Equations (5)-(6) and (24) prove (9). Under (1), equation (20) gives \(U_{\mathrm{hit}}\le R<Q/100\). Substitution in (6), followed by (24), proves (10). This uses the primal-hit mass once through \(R\); it does not infer that close mass contributes to the far-output defect.

Accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70 gives

\[
 I+O\ge
 \frac{11}{16}Q-\frac{67}{4}R-\frac{51}{128}G.
\]

Since \(R+G<Q/100\) and \(67/4>51/128\),

\[
 I+O>\left(\frac{11}{16}-\frac{67}{400}\right)Q
 =\frac{13}{25}Q.
\tag{25a}
\]

On the other hand, the first inequality in (9) gives

\[
 I+O\le2O+G+16X_{\mathrm{cl}}+16R.
\]

Therefore

\[
 2O+16X_{\mathrm{cl}}
 >\frac{13}{25}Q-G-16R
 >\left(\frac{13}{25}-\frac4{25}\right)Q
 =\frac9{25}Q,
\]

where \(G+16R\le16(G+R)<4Q/25\). Division by two proves (10a). If \(O\le Q/8\), then (10a) gives

\[
 8X_{\mathrm{cl}}>
 \left(\frac9{50}-\frac18\right)Q
 =\frac{11}{200}Q,
\]

which is the second branch in (10b); otherwise its first branch holds.

Finally, accepted fact d7b1da2c485d6f04 proves that

\[
 32\gamma_d\sum_e
 \min\{\omega_e,s_e/16\}
 \delta_{(m_e,F_e^\flat)}
\]

on the nonexceptional \(I\)-bad family is halfspace-dominated by the mixed-volume measure of \(C\). Restricting a positive halfspace-dominated measure to the subfamily (2) preserves domination and gives total mass \(32\gamma_dX_{\mathrm{cl}}\). The dominated-measure residual theorem of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483, with the ranks (11), gives

\[
 D\ge32\gamma_d
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{cl}}}}
      {r_{F,\mathrm{cl}}+1}X_{\mathrm{cl}}
 =\kappa_{\mathrm{cl}}X_{\mathrm{cl}}.
\tag{25}
\]

For positive \(a,b,x,y\),

\[
 \max\{ax,by\}\ge\frac{ab}{a+b}(x+y),
\tag{26}
\]

because \(x\le\max\{ax,by\}/a\) and
\(y\le\max\{ax,by\}/b\). Apply (26) with
\((a,b,x,y)=(\gamma_d,\kappa_{\mathrm{cl}},
U_{\mathrm{hit}},X_{\mathrm{cl}})\), and use (6), (21), and (25). This proves (13). Rearranging (13) with \(D<Q/100\) proves (14). Only the maximum of the two valid lower bounds on \(D\) is used. This completes the proof.
