## Statement

Let finitely many positive $d$-dimensional fibers, $d\ge1$, carry the marked boundary measure $\eta$ obtained by summing their surface measures with outward unit normals. Let $W=\sum_j\theta_j\mathbf1_{H_j}$ be a probability mixture of nonvertical caps $H_j=\{a_j\cdot z+v_j\cdot x\ge b_j\}$ whose traces are proper or strictly saturated, allowing supporting traces of zero surface measure. Write $\sigma_{jp}=\mathcal H^{d-1}(K_p\cap\partial H_j)/\lVert v_j\rVert$, with the section on fiber $p$. At an exposed base $p$ put
$$
C_p=\sum_j\theta_j|K_p\cap H_j|,\quad
T_p=\sum_j\theta_j\sigma_{jp}v_j,\quad
B_p=\sum_j\theta_j\sigma_{jp}(b_j-a_j\cdot p).
$$
The best efficiency among radial hinges supported by an affine penalty on the actual $p$ fiber and centered at a point of $K_p$ is attained and equals
$$
E_p=\frac{d C_p+B_p+h_{K_p}(-T_p)}{d|K_p|}.
$$
For the chain of bases $-1,0,1$ with endpoint fibers $A,B$ and middle fiber $K$, suppose all caps have common raw mass $Q$, pass through $(0,y)$, and have full mixed coarea centroids there. Then $\sigma_{j,-}=\sigma_{j,+}=s_j$ for every row. Put $T=\sum_j\theta_js_jv_j$ and $C_0=\sum_j\theta_j|K\cap H_j|$. The two endpoint optima obey
$$
d|A|E_-+d|B|E_+=d(Q-C_0)+2\bigl[T\cdot y+h_{(A+B)/2}(-T)\bigr].
$$
In particular one endpoint test pays at least this numerator divided by $d(|A|+|B|)$.

## Proof

Let $\ell$ be affine with $\ell(p)=0$ and $\ell(z)<0$ at every other measured base. For any center $c\in K_p$ choose a finite penalty $D_0$ making $[n\cdot(x-c)+D_0\ell(z)]_+$ vanish on other measured boundaries, uniformly in unit $n$. It suffices that every positive base gap times $D_0$ dominate the finite diameter bound for $\lVert x-c\rVert$ over the measured fibers. The test is continuous and convex at fixed $n$. At $p$ the support inequality $n\cdot(x-c)\ge0$ holds. Its total integral is $d|K_p|$ by divergence. On each proper trace the new planar part of its boundary has flux $-(b_j-a_j\cdot p-v_j\cdot c)\sigma_{jp}$, so subtracting it leaves the boundary integral $d|K_p\cap H_j|+(b_j-a_j\cdot p-v_j\cdot c)\sigma_{jp}$. Strictly saturated and surface-null supporting traces obey this identity with zero section, and dimension one follows by endpoint subtraction. The weighted numerator is thus $dC_p+B_p-T_p\cdot c$. Its maximum on the compact fiber is attained by a support point in direction $-T_p$. A finite penalty then realizes it.

At the two endpoints the centroid base equation gives the equal section weights. Each cap passes through $(0,y)$, so $b_j=v_j\cdot y$. The opposite base tilt terms cancel in the endpoint sum, leaving $B_-+B_+=2T\cdot y$. Common total cap mass gives $C_-+C_+=Q-C_0$. Finally support functions add under Minkowski sum, so the two support values sum to $2h_{(A+B)/2}(-T)$. The formula follows. The ratio bound is the elementary maximum bound by the volume-weighted mean. Its numerator is nonnegative because both terms on the left are actual integrals of nonnegative fields. The support correction is separately nonnegative if $y\in(A+B)/2$, a condition not needed for the identity. Equal endpoint spacing is used in the unweighted coarea equality.
