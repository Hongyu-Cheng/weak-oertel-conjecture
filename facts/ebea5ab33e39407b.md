## Statement

Let $K\subset\mathbb R^2$ be a convex body with $0$ in its interior. Suppose the endpoints of the $K$ chords on the negative $y$-axis and on the positive-$x$ ray of $x+y=0$ are $(0,-\beta)$ and $(\rho,-\rho)$, with $\beta,\rho>0$. In the coordinates $u=-y$, $v=x+y$, the sector $K\cap\{y<0<x+y\}$ lies in
$$
u>0,\qquad v>0,\qquad \beta u+(\beta-\rho)v\le\beta\rho.
$$
When $\beta>\rho$, its area is at most $\beta\rho^2/[2(\beta-\rho)]$. Equality holds exactly when the closure of the sector in $(u,v)$ is the corresponding closed triangle. This bound is attained for each $\beta>\rho$. When $\beta\le\rho$, the two endpoints alone give no finite uniform area bound for the sector.

## Proof

For a point $(u+v,-u)$ of the sector, its segment to $(0,-\beta)$ lies in $K$. At the crossing of $x+y=0$, its $x$-coordinate is $\beta(u+v)/(\beta+v)>0$. This is at most $\rho$ by the definition of the endpoint. Multiplying by the positive denominator gives the claimed inequality. The coordinate map has absolute determinant one. For $\beta>\rho$ the two positive intercepts of the resulting triangle are $\rho$ and $\beta\rho/(\beta-\rho)$, which gives the area bound.

The sector closure is compact and contained in the closed triangle. If it misses any point of the triangle, its complement contains a relatively open set of positive area. Conversely, when the closure is the triangle, all points of that triangle with $u,v>0$ belong to the sector by closedness of $K$, so the area bound is attained.

For attainment, in the $(u,v)$ plane intersect the closed halfplane $\beta u+(\beta-\rho)v\le\beta\rho$ with a sufficiently large centered disk. This body contains $0$ in its interior. The intersections on the rays $u=-v,\ u>0$ and $v=0,\ u>0$ have endpoints $(\beta,-\beta)$ and $(\rho,0)$, respectively, provided the disk contains both points. For $\beta>\rho$ choose the disk to contain the whole triangle. For $\beta\le\rho$ the sector of the halfplane contains $0<u<\rho,\ v>0$, and the area of its intersection with disks of increasing radii is unbounded while both specified chord endpoints stay fixed.
