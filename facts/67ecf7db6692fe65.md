## Statement

Let $d\ge1$ be an integer and let $A,C\subset\mathbb R^d$ be compact full-dimensional convex bodies with $0<a=|A|\le|C|$, and let $r=(a/|C|)^{1/d}$. For every nonconstant affine functional $f$, write $h_C(f)=\max_C f$ and $w_C(f)=\max_C f-\min_C f$. For every real $s,\beta$,
$$|A\cap\{f\ge s+\beta\}|+|C\cap\{f\ge s-\beta\}|\ge|A\cap\{f\ge2s-h_C(f)+w_C(f)r\}|.$$
These are actual closed cap volumes, not cap fractions of a midpoint body. No compatibility assumption between $A$ and $C$ is required.

## Proof

Take an $f$-maximizer $\mathbf c\in C$. Since $0<r\le1$, the homothetic copy $\mathbf c+r(C-\mathbf c)$ lies in $C$, has volume $r^d|C|=a$, and satisfies $f\ge h_C(f)-w_C(f)r$. Thus the cap of $C$ at that level has volume at least $a$.

Fix $s,\beta$. If the actual $C$ cap has volume at least $a$, it alone bounds the right-hand side, which is at most $|A|=a$. Otherwise its threshold must satisfy $s-\beta>h_C(f)-w_C(f)r$. Hence $s+\beta<2s-h_C(f)+w_C(f)r$, and the actual $A$ cap contains the comparison cap on the right. This proves the assertion for every tilt, without assuming an optimizing tilt exists. The strict inequality in the second branch is a consequence of a strict mass comparison, not the removal of boundary mass.
