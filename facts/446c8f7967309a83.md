## Statement

Retain one common canonical zero-fresh hard instance of accepted facts 6bfe50fed9a5540b and d5d5de35c3f25af9. Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body of positive mixed volume, and

\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \quad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \quad
 I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
\]

\[
 W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z,
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\tag{1}
\]

Let \(r=(r_e)\) be the half-strength canonical product reservoir in accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b. Its mass and degrees satisfy

\[
 Q:=\sum_er_e>0,
 \qquad
 d_z(r)\leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{2}
\]

Let \(G\) and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) have their canonical meanings. The hard ledger implies

\[
 G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{3}
\]

Retain the degree-weighted square atlas of accepted facts d5d5de35c3f25af9 and ce3ac24a3aedd198. Write its finite whole-label set as \(\mathcal L^\square\). Every \(\sigma\in\mathcal L^\square\) has mass \(\theta_\sigma>0\), a target \(t(\sigma)\in\mathbb Z^n\), and a four-entry tagged source multiset

\[
 \mathcal O_\sigma=
 [y_{\sigma,1},y_{\sigma,2},y_{\sigma,3},y_{\sigma,4}]
\]

with

\[
 \frac14\sum_{o=1}^4y_{\sigma,o}=t(\sigma).
\tag{4}
\]

Choose one simultaneous physical source realization. For each tagged occurrence \(e=(\sigma,o)\), put \(y(e):=y_{\sigma,o}\), \(t(e):=t(\sigma)\), and choose a density \(f_e\geq0\) on \(C_{y(e)}\) such that

\[
 \int_{C_{y(e)}}f_e=\theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_{y(e)}}x f_e(x)\,dx=\theta_\sigma p_e,
\tag{5}
\]

\[
 F_y^\square:=
 \sum_{e:y(e)=y}f_e
 \leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_y.
\tag{6}
\]

All four tags are separate summands, including repeated tags. The occurrence ledger in accepted facts ce3ac24a3aedd198 and d5d5de35c3f25af9 guarantees such realizations; the conclusions hold for every realization satisfying (5)-(6).

Let \(\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal L^\square\) be any whole-label subfamily. All conclusions therefore retain loops and repeated tagged sources; in particular, they apply to a loop-free subfamily, when the four sources within each label are distinct. Put

\[
 F_y^{\mathcal F}:=
 \sum_{\substack{e=(\sigma,o)\\
 \sigma\in\mathcal F,\ y(e)=y}}f_e,
 \qquad
 m_y^{\mathcal F}:=\int_{C_y}F_y^{\mathcal F},
\]

\[
 M_{\mathcal F}:=\sum_ym_y^{\mathcal F}.
\tag{7}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 M_{\mathcal F}
 =4\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal F}\theta_\sigma.}
\tag{8}
\]

For \(m_y^{\mathcal F}>0\), let \(p_y^{\mathcal F}\) be the barycenter of \(F_y^{\mathcal F}\). For every nonempty
\(E\subseteq Y_{\mathcal F}:=\{y:m_y^{\mathcal F}>0\}\), put

\[
 \rho_E:=\dim\operatorname{aff}E,
\]

\[
 r_E:=
 \min_{\substack{s:\operatorname{aff}E\to\mathbb R^d\\
 s\ \mathrm{affine}}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{p_y^{\mathcal F}-s(y):y\in E\}.
\tag{9}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 r_E+1>
 100\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_E}
 \frac{\sum_{y\in E}m_y^{\mathcal F}}{Q}.}
\tag{10}
\]

In particular, if

\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{src}}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}Y_{\mathcal F},
 \qquad
 r_{\mathrm{src}}:=r_{Y_{\mathcal F}},
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{
 M_{\mathcal F}>\frac{2Q}{9}
 \ \Longrightarrow\
 r_{\mathrm{src}}+1>
 \frac{200}{9}\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}}.}
\tag{11}
\]

For the full atlas, put

\[
 m_y^\square:=\int_{C_y}F_y^\square,
 \quad
 M_\square:=\sum_ym_y^\square,
 \quad
 \Theta:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal L^\square}\theta_\sigma.
\tag{12}
\]

Accepted fact b2d655373d3703a8 gives

\[
 M_\square=4\Theta,
 \qquad
 U:=W-M_\square=W-4\Theta.
\tag{13}
\]

If \(U<Q/100\), put

\[
 Y_\square:=\{y:m_y^\square>0\},
 \qquad
 \rho_\square:=\dim\operatorname{aff}Y_\square,
\]

\[
 r_\square:=
 \min_{\substack{
 s:\operatorname{aff}Y_\square\to\mathbb R^d\\
 s\ \mathrm{affine}}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{p_y^\square-s(y):y\in Y_\square\},
\]

where \(p_y^\square\) is the barycenter of \(F_y^\square\). Then

\[
 \boxed{
 r_\square+1>
 799\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_\square}.}
\tag{14}
\]

For \(\sigma\in\mathcal F\), define

\[
 r_\sigma:=\frac14\sum_{o=1}^4p_{(\sigma,o)}.
\tag{15}
\]

Assume that, at every target, the packets

\[
 \{(\theta_\sigma,r_\sigma):
 \sigma\in\mathcal F,\ t(\sigma)=t\}
\tag{16}
\]

are jointly packable in \(C_t\), in any of the equivalent senses of accepted fact 2c093df4eb8158a9. Choose target densities

\[
 h_{t,\sigma}\geq0,
 \quad
 \int_{C_t}h_{t,\sigma}=\theta_\sigma,
 \quad
 \int_{C_t}x h_{t,\sigma}(x)\,dx
 =\theta_\sigma r_\sigma,
\]

\[
 H_t:=
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathcal F\\t(\sigma)=t}}
 h_{t,\sigma}
 \leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_t.
\tag{17}
\]

Extend \(H_t:=0\) to every other \(t\in I_+\), and put

\[
 \ell_t:=\int_{C_t}H_t
 =\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathcal F\\t(\sigma)=t}}
 \theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 V_t:=a_t-\ell_t,
 \qquad
 V:=\sum_{t\in I_+}V_t.
\tag{18}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{W\geq8Q,}
\tag{19}
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 V=W-\frac{M_{\mathcal F}}4
 \geq\frac{3W}{4}
 \geq6Q.}
\tag{20}
\]

For \(V_t>0\), let \(q_t\) be the barycenter of \(1-H_t\); when \(V_t=0\), leave \(q_t\) undefined and use the formal convention \(V_t\widehat q_t:=0\). Put

\[
 T_{\mathrm{comp}}:=\{t\in I_+:V_t>0\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{comp}}:=
 \dim\operatorname{aff}T_{\mathrm{comp}},
\]

\[
 r_{\mathrm{comp}}:=
 \min_{\substack{
 s:\operatorname{aff}T_{\mathrm{comp}}\to\mathbb R^d\\
 s\ \mathrm{affine}}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{q_t-s(t):t\in T_{\mathrm{comp}}\}.
\tag{21}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{comp}}+1>
 100\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{comp}}}
 \frac VQ
 \geq
 600\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{comp}}}.}
\tag{22}
\]

There are exact source-target marginal identities. Put
\(U_y^{\mathcal F}:=a_y-m_y^{\mathcal F}\). For
\(U_y^{\mathcal F}>0\), let \(s_y^{\mathcal F}\) be the barycenter of
\(1-F_y^{\mathcal F}\); when \(U_y^{\mathcal F}=0\), leave
\(s_y^{\mathcal F}\) undefined and use
\(U_y^{\mathcal F}\widehat s_y^{\mathcal F}:=0\). Define

\[
 c_y:=\frac1{a_y}\int_{C_y}x\,dx,
 \qquad
 \widehat c_y:=(y,c_y),
\]

\[
 \widehat p_e:=(y(e),p_e),
 \qquad
 \widehat q_t:=(t,q_t),
 \qquad
 \widehat s_y^{\mathcal F}:=(y,s_y^{\mathcal F}).
\tag{23}
\]

With these zero-product conventions, for every \(t\in I_+\),

\[
 \boxed{
 4V_t\widehat q_t
 =
 4a_t\widehat c_t
 -
 \sum_{\substack{e=(\sigma,o)\\
 \sigma\in\mathcal F,\ t(\sigma)=t}}
 \theta_\sigma\widehat p_e.}
\tag{24}
\]

For every \(y\in I_+\),

\[
 \boxed{
 U_y^{\mathcal F}\widehat s_y^{\mathcal F}
 =
 a_y\widehat c_y
 -
 \sum_{\substack{e=(\sigma,o)\\
 \sigma\in\mathcal F,\ y(e)=y}}
 \theta_\sigma\widehat p_e.}
\tag{25}
\]

Summing gives

\[
 \boxed{
 4\sum_{t\in T_{\mathrm{comp}}}
 V_t\widehat q_t
 =
 3\sum_{y\in I_+}a_y\widehat c_y
 +
 \sum_{\substack{y\in I_+\\U_y^{\mathcal F}>0}}
 U_y^{\mathcal F}\widehat s_y^{\mathcal F}.}
\tag{26}
\]

Finally, let \(\Gamma_{\mathcal F}\) be the bipartite tagged-incidence multigraph. Its source vertices are \(Y_{\mathcal F}\), its target vertices are
\(T_{\mathcal F}:=\{t(\sigma):\sigma\in\mathcal F\}\), and it has one edge \(e=(\sigma,o)\), joining \(y(e)\) to \(t(e)\), of weight \(\theta_e:=\theta_\sigma\) for each tag. Repeated tags are parallel edges and target collisions are common target vertices. Let \(c(\Gamma_{\mathcal F})\) be its number of connected components. Define the weighted marginal maps

\[
 (\mathcal S a)_y:=
 \sum_{e:y(e)=y}\theta_ea_e,
 \qquad
 (\mathcal T a)_t:=
 \sum_{e:t(e)=t}\theta_ea_e.
\tag{27}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \dim(\ker\mathcal S\cap\ker\mathcal T)
 =
 |E(\Gamma_{\mathcal F})|
 -|Y_{\mathcal F}|
 -|T_{\mathcal F}|
 +c(\Gamma_{\mathcal F}).}
\tag{28}
\]

For vector-valued edge tables, the joint-kernel dimension is \(d\) times the right side of (28). Equations (24)-(25) depend on occurrence first moments only through \(\mathcal S\) and \(\mathcal T\). Hence the occurrence-moment directions invisible to both marginal identities are exactly the weighted cycle-space directions in (28). This is an algebraic localization only; it does not assert that an arbitrary kernel perturbation preserves nonnegativity or targetwise physical packing.

No conclusion above bounds this cycle space using convexity of the common body, couples the two residual ranks, produces a complete-core or affine target-complement profile, or proves the registered used/unused dichotomy.

## Proof

Accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b gives (2)-(3). Every reservoir edge is off-diagonal and contributes its weight to two endpoint degrees. Therefore

\[
 2Q=\sum_zd_z(r)
 \leq\frac14\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z
 =\frac W4.
\tag{29}
\]

This proves (19).

Accepted facts d5d5de35c3f25af9 and ce3ac24a3aedd198 give the whole labels, the four tags with multiplicity, (4), and the scalar occurrence ledger

\[
 R_y:=\sum_{e:y(e)=y}\theta_e\leq a_y.
\]

For example, the constant densities
\(f_e=(\theta_e/a_{y(e)})\mathbf1_{C_{y(e)}}\)
give one simultaneous physical realization, since their sum at source \(y\) is \(R_y/a_y\leq1\). The theorem allows any realization satisfying (5)-(6). Restriction to \(\mathcal F\) preserves the pointwise capacity. Reversing the finite sums, every label contributes four masses \(\theta_\sigma\), so

\[
 M_{\mathcal F}
 =
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal F}4\theta_\sigma.
\]

This proves (8), including repeated-tag accounting.

Fix nonempty \(E\subseteq Y_{\mathcal F}\). Since
\(0\leq F_y^{\mathcal F}\leq1\), one has

\[
 p_y^{\mathcal F}
 \in\mathsf M_{m_y^{\mathcal F}}(C_y).
\]

Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives

\[
 \delta_C(y,p_y^{\mathcal F})
 \geq\gamma_dm_y^{\mathcal F}.
\tag{30}
\]

Use the points \(p_y^{\mathcal F}\) and weights
\(\gamma_dm_y^{\mathcal F}\) in the exact definition of
\(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) from accepted fact be359f8a6c628483. Then

\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 2^{n-\rho_E}
 \frac{\gamma_d\sum_{y\in E}m_y^{\mathcal F}}{r_E+1}.
\tag{31}
\]

All hard-ledger summands in accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b are nonnegative, so (3) gives
\(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<Q/100\). Comparing with (31) proves (10). Taking \(E=Y_{\mathcal F}\) and using \(M_{\mathcal F}>2Q/9\) proves (11), with coefficient \(200/9\).

For the full family, accepted fact b2d655373d3703a8 gives (12)-(13). If \(U<Q/100\), equations (13) and (19) give

\[
 M_\square=W-U
 >
 8Q-\frac Q{100}
 =\frac{799}{100}Q.
\tag{32}
\]

Apply (10) to the full source family and use (32). This proves (14), with coefficient \(799\).

The targetwise joint-packing hypothesis is condition (i) of accepted fact 2c093df4eb8158a9, equivalently either of its max-affine or nonnegative-convex-order conditions, so it supplies (17). Summing (18), then using (8), gives

\[
 V
 =W-\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal F}\theta_\sigma
 =W-\frac{M_{\mathcal F}}4.
\tag{33}
\]

The source ledger gives \(M_{\mathcal F}\leq W\), so
\(V\geq3W/4\). Equation (19) gives \(V\geq6Q\). This proves (20), including the normalizations \(M_{\mathcal F}=4\sum_\sigma\theta_\sigma\) and \(V=W-M_{\mathcal F}/4\).

For \(V_t>0\), the density \(1-H_t\) lies between zero and one, has mass \(V_t\), and has barycenter \(q_t\). Hence

\[
 q_t\in\mathsf M_{V_t}(C_t).
\]

Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives
\(\delta_C(t,q_t)\geq\gamma_dV_t\). Apply the
\(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) definition from accepted fact
be359f8a6c628483 to all \(t\in T_{\mathrm{comp}}\):

\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{comp}}}
 \frac{\gamma_dV}{r_{\mathrm{comp}}+1}.
\tag{34}
\]

Comparison with \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<Q/100\), followed by \(V\geq6Q\), proves (22), with coefficient \(600\).

We prove the marginal identities. Equations (4)-(5) and (15) give, for every whole label,

\[
 4\theta_\sigma(t(\sigma),r_\sigma)
 =
 \sum_{o=1}^4
 \theta_\sigma\widehat p_{(\sigma,o)}.
\tag{35}
\]

The lifted first moment of \(C_t\) is \(a_t\widehat c_t\). Subtract the first moment of \(H_t\), use (35), and multiply by four. This gives (24) when \(V_t>0\). If \(V_t=0\), then \(0\leq H_t\leq1\) and \(\int H_t=a_t\), so \(H_t=1\) almost everywhere on \(C_t\); the right side of (24) is zero, and the same equation holds under the zero-product convention. The identical argument at a source shows that \(F_y^{\mathcal F}=1\) almost everywhere when \(U_y^{\mathcal F}=0\). Subtracting the lifted first moment of \(F_y^{\mathcal F}\) from the full source-fiber moment therefore gives (25) in every case.

Sum (24) over \(t\). Every tagged occurrence has exactly one target. Sum (25) over \(y\) to express the resulting occurrence sum as

\[
 \sum_ya_y\widehat c_y
 -
 \sum_{\substack{y\\U_y^{\mathcal F}>0}}
 U_y^{\mathcal F}\widehat s_y^{\mathcal F}.
\]

Substitution proves (26). Every step retains the four tags separately, so repeated tags, reused sources, and target collisions are preserved.

It remains to prove (28). Let \(B\) be the unsigned vertex-edge incidence matrix of the bipartite multigraph
\(\Gamma_{\mathcal F}\), with one column per tag. Multiplying all target rows by \(-1\) turns \(B\) into an oriented incidence matrix. Therefore

\[
 \operatorname{rank}B
 =
 |Y_{\mathcal F}|+|T_{\mathcal F}|
 -c(\Gamma_{\mathcal F}).
\tag{36}
\]

Let \(D_\theta\) be the invertible diagonal matrix with edge entries \(\theta_e>0\). Stacking the maps in (27) gives

\[
 \begin{bmatrix}\mathcal S\\ \mathcal T\end{bmatrix}
 =BD_\theta.
\]

Its rank is (36). Rank-nullity proves (28). Applying the same scalar matrix independently in every continuous coordinate multiplies the joint-kernel dimension by \(d\). Equations (24)-(25) use occurrence vectors only through their weighted source and target sums, precisely \(\mathcal S\) and \(\mathcal T\). This proves the cycle-space localization.

No established inequality relates the cycle space or its weighted mass to (10) or (22), so none of the explicitly excluded terminal conclusions follows.
