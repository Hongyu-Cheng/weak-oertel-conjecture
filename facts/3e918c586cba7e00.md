Retain the fresh restarted endpoint loads and points of accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b:

Assume the ratio-cut branch of accepted fact 27024aed67e77532.  Thus

\[

and both displayed choices of \(A\) are nonempty and proper.  For such an \(A\), retain

 \qquad

 \min_{a:\operatorname{aff}(A)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_y-a(y):y\in A\}.

For \(\delta_\sigma>0\), orient the label from its unique higher-\(q\) endpoint \(h(\sigma)\) to its lower-\(q\) endpoint.  Write its original transverse parents as

 \sigma=(z;e,f),
 \qquad e=\{z,a\},

with original contact points \(x_{e,z},x_{e,a},x_{f,z},x_{f,b}\).  Replace only the contact at \(h(\sigma)\) by \(p_{h(\sigma)}^b\), and denote the resulting opposite contacts by

## Proof

The only new source-budget issue is the replacement at the higher-ratio endpoint.  We first construct its density without reusing any physical source.  For \(y\in Y\), put

\[
 o_y:=\sum_{\sigma:\,h(\sigma)=y}\delta_\sigma.
\tag{24}
\]

For every term in (24), if \(y'\) is the other endpoint, then

\[
 \delta_\sigma
 =\widehat\theta_\sigma(q_y-q_{y'})
 \le\widehat\theta_\sigma q_y.
\]

Equations (2)--(3) therefore give

\[
 o_y\le q_yw_y
 =\min\{w_y,\beta_y^b\}
 \le\beta_y^b.
\tag{25}
\]

If \(o_y>0\), then \(\beta_y^b>0\).  By (4), choose a half-mass density \(F_y^b\) with barycenter \(p_y^b\), and put

\[
 \phi_y^b:=\frac{2\beta_y^b}{a_y}F_y^b.
\tag{26}
\]

It has mass \(\beta_y^b\), barycenter \(p_y^b\), and, because \(\beta_y^b\le a_y/4\),

\[
 0\le\phi_y^b\le\frac12.
\tag{27}
\]

For a label oriented out of \(y\), assign

\[
 \phi_{\sigma,y}^b
 :=\frac{\delta_\sigma}{\beta_y^b}\phi_y^b.
\tag{28}
\]

This density has mass \(\delta_\sigma\) and barycenter \(p_y^b\).  Equations (25), (27), and (28) give the fresh one-use ledger

\[
 \sum_{\sigma:\,h(\sigma)=y}\phi_{\sigma,y}^b
 =\frac{o_y}{\beta_y^b}\phi_y^b
 \le\phi_y^b\le\frac12.
\tag{29}
\]

We next record the original-incidence ledger at the retained weights.  Accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 fixes, for every original loaded incidence \((j,y)\), a half-mass witness \(f_{j,y}\).  Define

\[
 g_{\sigma,j,y}
 :=\frac{2\widehat\theta_\sigma}{a_y}f_{j,y}
 \qquad
 (j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\},\ y\in j).
\tag{30}
\]

It has mass \(\widehat\theta_\sigma\) and the original contact as barycenter.  Since \(\widehat\theta_\sigma\) is a thinning of the original atlas weight, the original transverse-pair capacity and reservoir degree bound give, pointwise on each physical source fiber,

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma,
 j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\}\\y\in j}}
 g_{\sigma,j,y}
 &\le
 \frac2{a_y}\sum_{j\ni y}\omega_j f_{j,y}\\
 &\le\frac2{a_y}\sum_{j\ni y}\omega_j
 \le\frac12.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{31}
\]

For a positive-difference label, multiply each of the three original incidence densities which remains in (12) by \(\delta_\sigma/\widehat\theta_\sigma\le1\).  Combining the resulting subledger of (31) with the fresh ledger (29) gives one physical source ledger bounded pointwise by one:

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{all source densities used by the modified labels sum to at most }1
 \text{ on every }C_y.}
\tag{32}
\]

Every one of the four source densities attached to a positive-difference label has the common mass \(\delta_\sigma\) and the barycenter displayed in (12).  The positive-root convexity of accepted fact dfd25e72c1f45a4e, applied at the common positive root \(\delta_\sigma^{1/d}\), proves the four metronoid memberships in (13).  The parent edges are same-parity and transverse, so the four bases in (12) are the distinct integer vertices of the same nondegenerate parallelogram as in accepted fact 5a1fe0ca819e2d1a.  Direct expansion proves (14), and the base projection proves that every three corners are affinely independent.

At the extreme base vertex \(z\), the only barycentric representation by the four parallelogram bases is the unit mass at \(z\).  The complete metronoid-core intersection therefore contains the continuous coordinate of \(\widetilde p_{\sigma,00}\).  Every halfspace containing \(z\) contains its mass \(\delta_\sigma\), and \(\delta_\sigma\ge2^{-n}(4\delta_\sigma)\) because \(n\ge2\).  This proves (15).

Equation (1) gives \(G<Q/100\).  Combining this strict bound with (15) proves (15a).

We prove (18) without assuming distinct physical targets.  Fix a closed ambient halfspace \(H\).  For one label, the two corners used by \(\nu_0\) are the midpoints of the disjoint pairs of source-incidence slots

\[
 \{(e,z),(f,z)\},
 \qquad
 \{(e,a),(f,b)\},
\tag{33}
\]

where the higher opposite slot is the fresh slot.  Likewise, the two corners used by \(\nu_1\) are the midpoints of the disjoint slot pairs

\[
 \{(e,z),(e,a)\},
 \qquad
 \{(f,z),(f,b)\}.
\tag{34}
\]

If a midpoint lies in \(H\), at least one of its two source points lies in \(H\).  For a fixed \(i\), orient every \(\nu_i\)-atom in \(H\) to one such source point.  The disjointness in (33) or (34) means that no labelled source-incidence density is used twice.  After aggregating labels at a physical source \(y\), equation (32) gives a density \(\Psi_y\) with \(0\le\Psi_y\le1\), supported through source points whose barycenter lies in the fiber section \(H_y\).  If \(T_y:=\int_{C_y}\Psi_y>0\), then that barycenter belongs to \(\mathsf M_{T_y}(C_y)\cap H_y\).  Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives

\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_y\cap H_y)\ge\gamma_dT_y,
\tag{35}
\]

with the same inequality immediate when \(H_y=C_y\).  Summing (35) over distinct physical sources counts every atom of \(\nu_i(H)\) exactly once and proves (18).  This argument starts with labelled source slots and aggregates only after orientation, so coincident target atoms and repeated physical source fibers cause no overcount.

Equation (9) gives

\[
 \sum_\sigma\delta_\sigma=I_\cap.
\tag{36}
\]

Each measure has two atoms of weight \(\gamma_d\delta_\sigma\) per label, so (5) and (36) prove (19).  The circuit identity (14), multiplied by \(\gamma_d\delta_\sigma\) and summed, proves (20).

Apply the dominated-measure residual theorem of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 separately to \(\nu_0\) and \(\nu_1\).  With the ranks in (21), it gives

\[
 D\ge
 2\gamma_dI_\cap
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_i}}{r_i+1}
 \qquad(i=0,1).
\tag{37}
\]

Taking the maximum proves (22); the two right sides in (37) are not added.

Finally fix either \(A=S\) or \(A=Y\setminus S\).  The collision-aggregated centroid-selector inequality of accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c and (7) give

\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge
 4\gamma_d
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_A}}{r_A^c+1}
 \sum_{y\in A}w_y.
\tag{38}
\]

Equations (1), (5), (7), (22), and (38) therefore imply

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \frac Q{100}
 &>R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+D\\
 &\ge
 4\gamma_d
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_A}}{r_A^c+1}
 \sum_{y\in A}w_y
 +2\gamma_dI_\cap\Xi\\
 &>
 \frac{3\gamma_dQ}{100}
 \left(
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_A}}{r_A^c+1}
 +\frac12\Xi
 \right).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{39}
\]

Division by the positive factor in (39) proves (23) for both proper sides.  Every capacity in (39) is used once: one legal selector is charged to \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), and only the larger of the two dominated-measure scores is charged to \(D\).  This completes the physical ratio-cut absorption and the coupled rank reduction.
