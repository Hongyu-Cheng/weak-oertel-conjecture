## Statement

Let $K\subset\mathbb R^D$ be a convex body of volume $V$ covered by $m$ proper closed halfspaces $H_i$, where $3\le m\le D+1$. Put $r=m-1$, $A_i=|K\cap H_i|$, and $\gamma_r=(r/(r+1))^r$. For $\alpha>0$,
$$
\sum_i(A_i-\alpha)_++\frac{\alpha}{\gamma_r}=V
$$
holds exactly when the normals span a space of dimension $r$, the orthogonal pushforward of ordinary volume on $K$ to that span is a log-concave nonincreasing gauge profile of a centroid-centered $r$-simplex translated to a point $y$, and the projected halfspaces are its distinct canonical vertex halfspaces through $y$. In this case every $A_i=\alpha=\gamma_r V$. At $m=3$, taking $\alpha=\min_i A_i>0$ gives the same equality classification for the overlap bound with coefficient $3/4$.

## Proof

Choose an inclusion-minimal subcover of size $j$. If $j<m$, the effective profile theorem 23a563a735c07c25 gives its inequality with $\gamma_{j-1}>\gamma_r$, taking $\gamma_0=1$. Increasing the coefficient of $\alpha$ strictly and adding the remaining nonnegative terms excludes equality. Thus the original family is minimal.

Write $\rho$ for its normal rank. If $\rho<r$, the same theorem gives the profile with $\gamma_\rho>\gamma_r$ for this fixed minimal subcover, again excluding equality. If $\rho=m$, the independent-normal theorem f497d1290c37f335 gives strictness at $\alpha>0$. Hence equality requires $\rho=r$.

Project orthogonally onto the normal span. Each cap is a pullback of a proper closed halfspace in the span, so the cover and its cap masses are preserved. The projected measure has a full-dimensional log-concave density, by the section argument in 23a563a735c07c25, or the uniform indicator if $D=r$. The log-concave equality theorem for a cover by $r+1$ halfspaces applied in dimension $r$ gives exactly the claimed profile, arrangement and masses. Conversely those conditions give equality by the same theorem. For $m=3$, the positive-part sum at $\alpha=\min_i A_i>0$ is $\sum_i A_i-3\alpha$ and $1/\gamma_2=9/4$, proving the overlap equivalence.
