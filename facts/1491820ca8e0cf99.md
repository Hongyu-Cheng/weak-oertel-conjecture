## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and write
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx
\]
whenever \(a_z>0\).  For \(0<s\le a_z\), put
\[
 \mathsf M_s(C_z):=
 \left\{\frac1s\int_{C_z}x f(x)\,dx:
 0\le f\le1\ \text{a.e.},\ \int_{C_z}f=s\right\},
\]
and define the half-mass body and its centered translate by
\[
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),\qquad
 D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z.
\tag{1}
\]

Retain one common instantiation of the canonical zero-fresh self-saturated switch atlas of accepted fact `cb96640c07ffd5fa` and the full pooled bodies of accepted fact `2d4c7439acb19da3`.  Thus there is a finite nonempty label set \(I\), with positive loads \(q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\),
\[
 \Omega_b:=\sum_{j\in I}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}>0,
 \qquad
 \pi_i:=\frac{q_i^{\mathrm{sat}}}{\Omega_b},
 \qquad
 \sum_{i\in I}\pi_i=1,
 \qquad
 \sum_{i\in I}\pi_i u_i=0,
\tag{2}
\]
and nonzero canonical directions \(u_i\in\mathbb R^d\).  Write
\(
\{z_j^-,z_j^+\}
\)
for the two original source-fiber endpoints of label \(j\); this is only an unambiguous alias for the unordered reservoir edge underlying \(j\).  Its physical target is
\(
m_j=(z_j^-+z_j^+)/2\in\mathbb Z^n
\), and its pair body is
\[
 L_j=\frac12(\mathcal K_{z_j^-}+\mathcal K_{z_j^+}),
\tag{3}
\]
Contacts may be chosen simultaneously and arbitrarily as
\[
 p_{ij}\in F_{L_j}(u_i)
 :=\{x\in L_j:\langle u_i,x\rangle=h_{L_j}(u_i)\}
 \qquad(i,j\in I).
\tag{4}
\]
Define
\[
 \Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}
 :=\sum_{j\in I}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}
 \sum_{i\in I}\pi_i h_{L_j}(u_i)\ge0.
\tag{5}
\]
The atlas has a number \(\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}>0\) and parameter
\[
 t:=\frac{\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}}
 {\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}+\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}},
 \qquad
 \mathcal O=t\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}.
\tag{6}
\]
In particular, the canonical branch does not permit \(t=0\): one always has \(0<t\le1\).  If \(\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}=0\), then \(t=1\), and all conclusions below remain valid with zero energy.

For every used physical target \(m\), put
\[
 I_m:=\{j\in I:m_j=m\},\qquad
 \eta_m:=\sum_{j\in I_m}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}>0,
\tag{7}
\]
\[
 c_j^{\mathrm{sw}}:=\sum_{i\in I}\pi_i p_{ij},\qquad
 c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
 :=\frac1{\eta_m}\sum_{j\in I_m}
 q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}c_j^{\mathrm{sw}},
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 b_{ik}^{(j)}:=\frac{tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{2}\pi_i\pi_k,
 \qquad
 g_{ik}^{(j,m)}
 :=\frac{b_{ik}^{(j)}}{\eta_m}(p_{ij}-p_{kj}),
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal Z_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
 :=c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
 +\sum_{j\in I_m}\sum_{i,k\in I}
 [-g_{ik}^{(j,m)},g_{ik}^{(j,m)}],
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 \mathcal P_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
 :=\frac1{\eta_m}\sum_{j\in I_m}
 q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}L_j
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{\eta_m}(C_m).
\tag{11}
\]
Then, simultaneously for every permitted contact choice (4), every used physical target \(m\), and every real number \(\alpha\) with \(0\le\alpha\le1/t\),
\[
 \boxed{
 c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
 +\alpha(\mathcal Z_m^{\mathrm{sw}}-c_m^{\mathrm{sw}})
 \subseteq\mathcal P_m^{\mathrm{sw}}.}
\tag{12}
\]
In particular, with
\[
 \widetilde g_{ik}^{(j,m)}:=\frac1t g_{ik}^{(j,m)}
 =\frac{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{2\eta_m}\pi_i\pi_k
 (p_{ij}-p_{kj}),
\tag{13}
\]
the maximally dilated actual switch body satisfies
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal Z}_m
 :=c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
 +\frac1t(\mathcal Z_m^{\mathrm{sw}}-c_m^{\mathrm{sw}})
 =c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
 +\sum_{j\in I_m}\sum_{i,k\in I}
 [-\widetilde g_{ik}^{(j,m)},\widetilde g_{ik}^{(j,m)}]
 \subseteq\mathcal P_m^{\mathrm{sw}}.
\tag{14}
\]
Every generator-normal term below is nonnegative, and its exact total is
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{m:\eta_m>0}\eta_m
 \sum_{j\in I_m}\sum_{i,k\in I}
 \langle u_i-u_k,\widetilde g_{ik}^{(j,m)}\rangle
 =\frac{\mathcal O}{t}=\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}.}
\tag{15}
\]

The same energy is an exact actual-source-cap quantity.  Define the source endpoint load
\[
 \beta_z:=d_z(q^{\mathrm{sat}})
 :=\sum_{j\in I}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}
 \bigl(\mathbf1_{\{z=z_j^-\}}+\mathbf1_{\{z=z_j^+\}}\bigr).
\tag{16}
\]
The source endpoint loads satisfy
\[
 \sum_z\beta_z=2\Omega_b,
 \qquad
 0<\beta_z\le\frac{a_z}{4}\quad(\beta_z>0).
\tag{17}
\]
For a nonzero vector \(u\in\mathbb R^d\), choose the threshold \(s_z(u)\) for which the top cap
\[
 A_z(u):=\{x\in C_z:\langle u,x\rangle\ge s_z(u)\}
\]
has volume \(a_z/2\), and put
\[
 p_z(u):=\frac2{a_z}\int_{A_z(u)}x\,dx\in F_{\mathcal K_z}(u).
\tag{18}
\]
Changing a cap on its threshold hyperplane does not change any integral.  For all nonzero \(u,v\in\mathbb R^d\), one has the exact symmetric-difference identity
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \langle u-v,p_z(u)-p_z(v)\rangle
 &=\frac2{a_z}
 \int_{A_z(u)\triangle A_z(v)}
 \bigl(
 |\langle u,x\rangle-s_z(u)|\\
 &\hspace{42mm}
 +|\langle v,x\rangle-s_z(v)|
 \bigr)\,dx.
 \end{aligned}}
\tag{19}
\]
Consequently the exact source-cap representation, including every factor, is
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}=\frac{\mathcal O}{t}
 &=\frac12\sum_{z:\beta_z>0}\beta_z
 \sum_{i\in I}\pi_i h_{D_z}(u_i)\\
 &=\frac14\sum_{z:\beta_z>0}\beta_z
 \sum_{i,k\in I}\pi_i\pi_k
 \langle u_i-u_k,p_z(u_i)-p_z(u_k)\rangle\\
 &=\sum_{z:\beta_z>0}\frac{\beta_z}{2a_z}
 \sum_{i,k\in I}\pi_i\pi_k
 \int_{A_z(u_i)\triangle A_z(u_k)}
 \bigl(
 |\langle u_i,x\rangle-s_z(u_i)|\\
 &\hspace{43mm}
 +|\langle u_k,x\rangle-s_z(u_k)|
 \bigr)\,dx.
 \end{aligned}}
\tag{20}
\]
Thus (20) uses literal half-volume caps in the actual fiber \(C_z\), and its only endpoint-load coefficient at source \(z\) is the canonical \(\beta_z\le a_z/4\); in the last line its normalized coefficient obeys \(\beta_z/(2a_z)\le1/8\).  Repeated physical targets, repeated contacts, vanishing generators, equal directions, and zero collected coefficients do not change (12)-(20).

The conclusions assert actual pooled width and its exact source-cap energy only.  They do not imply that the centers \(c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}\) admit an affine alignment, that a macroscopic subfamily has low continuous residual rank, or that \(\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}\) is absorbed by the complete-core, direct-depth, residual-selector, or any other terminal capacity.

## Proof

Accepted fact `cb96640c07ffd5fa` supplies (2)-(10), the strict positivity of \(\Delta^{\mathrm{sat}}\), the nonnegativity and finiteness of \(\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}\), the identity \(\mathcal O=t\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}\), and
\[
 \sum_{m:\eta_m>0}\eta_m
 \sum_{j\in I_m}\sum_{i,k\in I}
 \langle u_i-u_k,g_{ik}^{(j,m)}\rangle
 =\mathcal O,
\tag{21}
\]
with every summand in (21) nonnegative.  Accepted fact `2d4c7439acb19da3` supplies the full pooled bodies (11), including the common-density inclusion into \(\mathsf M_{\eta_m}(C_m)\).  Equation (6) shows directly that \(0<t\le1\), so every division by \(t\) below is legitimate; there is no \(t=0\) case in this canonical system.

Fix a used target \(m\), \(0\le\alpha\le1/t\), and a point
\[
 x\in c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}
 +\alpha(\mathcal Z_m^{\mathrm{sw}}-c_m^{\mathrm{sw}}).
\]
There are numbers \(\varepsilon_{ik}^{(j)}\in[-1,1]\) such that
\[
 \eta_mx
 =\sum_{j\in I_m}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}c_j^{\mathrm{sw}}
 +\alpha\sum_{j\in I_m}\sum_{i,k\in I}
 \varepsilon_{ik}^{(j)}b_{ik}^{(j)}(p_{ij}-p_{kj}).
\tag{22}
\]
For \(j\in I_m\) and \(\ell\in I\), collect the coefficient of \(p_{\ell j}\) as
\[
 \zeta_{\ell j}^{(\alpha)}
 :=q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_\ell
 +\alpha\left(
 \sum_{k\in I}\varepsilon_{\ell k}^{(j)}b_{\ell k}^{(j)}
 -\sum_{i\in I}\varepsilon_{i\ell}^{(j)}b_{i\ell}^{(j)}
 \right).
\tag{23}
\]
The product coefficients (9) give exactly
\[
 \sum_kb_{\ell k}^{(j)}+\sum_ib_{i\ell}^{(j)}
 =\frac{tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{2}\pi_\ell\sum_k\pi_k
 +\frac{tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}{2}\pi_\ell\sum_i\pi_i
 =tq_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_\ell.
\tag{24}
\]
Therefore
\[
 \zeta_{\ell j}^{(\alpha)}
 \ge(1-\alpha t)q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\pi_\ell\ge0.
\tag{25}
\]
The outgoing and incoming sums in (23) cancel after summing over \(\ell\), so
\[
 \sum_{\ell\in I}\zeta_{\ell j}^{(\alpha)}
 =q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}.
\tag{26}
\]
Hence
\[
 \widehat p_j^{(\alpha)}
 :=\frac1{q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}}
 \sum_{\ell\in I}\zeta_{\ell j}^{(\alpha)}p_{\ell j}
 \in L_j.
\]
Collecting (22) now yields
\[
 x=\frac1{\eta_m}\sum_{j\in I_m}
 q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\widehat p_j^{(\alpha)}
 \in\mathcal P_m^{\mathrm{sw}},
\]
which proves (12).  At the endpoint \(\alpha=1/t\), (25) may be an equality, but zero coefficients are valid convex coefficients.  Taking \(\alpha=1/t\) also proves (13)-(14).  Dividing the nonnegative identity (21) by the strictly positive number \(t\), then using \(\mathcal O=t\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}\), proves (15) with all signs preserved.

We prove the cap statements directly.  Fix a source \(z\) with \(\beta_z>0\).  Its fiber has positive \(d\)-volume.  For nonzero \(u\), a threshold hyperplane has \(d\)-dimensional measure zero, and a threshold \(s_z(u)\) with half-volume upper cap exists.  If \(f:C_z\to[0,1]\) has integral \(a_z/2\), then the equality of the masses of \(f\) and \(\mathbf1_{A_z(u)}\) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \int_{C_z}\langle u,x\rangle
 (\mathbf1_{A_z(u)}-f(x))\,dx
 &=\int_{A_z(u)}
 (\langle u,x\rangle-s_z(u))(1-f(x))\,dx\\
 &\quad+\int_{C_z\setminus A_z(u)}
 (s_z(u)-\langle u,x\rangle)f(x)\,dx\ge0.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{27}
\]
Thus the cap indicator maximizes the \(u\)-moment among all half-mass densities, which proves the face membership in (18).

For nonzero \(u,v\), set
\[
 B:=A_z(u)\setminus A_z(v),\qquad
 E:=A_z(v)\setminus A_z(u).
\]
Both caps have volume \(a_z/2\), so \(\operatorname{vol}_d(B)=\operatorname{vol}_d(E)\).  Moreover,
\[
 p_z(u)-p_z(v)
 =\frac2{a_z}\left(\int_Bx\,dx-\int_Ex\,dx\right).
\tag{28}
\]
Away from the null threshold hyperplanes, the integrand on the right side of (19) equals
\[
 \langle u-v,x\rangle-s_z(u)+s_z(v)
 \quad\text{on }B,
\]
and
\[
 -\langle u-v,x\rangle+s_z(u)-s_z(v)
 \quad\text{on }E.
\]
After integration, the two threshold constants cancel because \(B\) and \(E\) have equal volume.  Equation (28) then proves (19), including the case \(u=v\), where both sides vanish.

It remains to derive all factors in (20).  From the pair decomposition (3),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}
 &=\frac12\sum_{j\in I}q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}
 \sum_{i\in I}\pi_i
 \bigl(h_{\mathcal K_{z_j^-}}(u_i)
 +h_{\mathcal K_{z_j^+}}(u_i)\bigr)\\
 &=\frac12\sum_{z:\beta_z>0}\beta_z
 \sum_{i\in I}\pi_i h_{\mathcal K_z}(u_i).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{29}
\]
Since \(\mathcal K_z=D_z+c_z\) and \(\sum_i\pi_i u_i=0\), the centroid term in (29) vanishes separately for every \(z\):
\[
 \sum_i\pi_i h_{\mathcal K_z}(u_i)
 =\sum_i\pi_i h_{D_z}(u_i).
\tag{30}
\]
By (18),
\[
 h_{D_z}(u_i)=\langle u_i,p_z(u_i)-c_z\rangle.
\]
Writing \(\bar p_z:=\sum_k\pi_kp_z(u_k)\) and expanding an ordered double sum gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\frac12\sum_{i,k}\pi_i\pi_k
 \langle u_i-u_k,p_z(u_i)-p_z(u_k)\rangle\\
 &\quad=\sum_i\pi_i\langle u_i,p_z(u_i)\rangle
 -\left\langle\sum_i\pi_i u_i,\bar p_z\right\rangle\\
 &\quad=\sum_i\pi_i h_{D_z}(u_i).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{31}
\]
Substituting (30)-(31) into (29) gives the first two lines of (20).  Substituting (19) term by term contributes the factor \(2/a_z\), changing \(\beta_z/4\) into \(\beta_z/(2a_z)\), and gives the last two lines of (20).  Every label in \(I\) has two distinct source endpoints, so summing (16) counts each \(q_j^{\mathrm{sat}}\) exactly twice and gives \(\sum_z\beta_z=2\Omega_b\).  The coordinatewise inequality in (17) is the canonical source endpoint ledger retained in accepted fact `cb96640c07ffd5fa`.  This completes the proof of every asserted identity and inclusion.  Nothing in the coefficient collection or cap symmetrization selects an affine map through the pooled bodies, reduces continuous residual rank, or charges \(\Psi^{\mathrm{sat}}\) to a terminal capacity, so the stated limitation is essential.
