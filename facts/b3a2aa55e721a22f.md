## Statement

Let $A,K,B\subset\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge2$, be compatible convex bodies at $-1,0,1$ of volumes $a,1,b$, $0<a\le b$. Take $m$ nonvertical ambient caps through one middle point, of common mass $Q$, with middle traces covering $K$ and traces at one endpoint covering that body of volume $e$. Let the middle masses be $q_1\le\cdots\le q_m$. Put $\gamma_j=(j/(j+1))^j$ for $j\ge1$ and $\kappa=1/\gamma_d-2$. If $m\ge3$, then
$
q_m+q_{m-1}+\kappa q_{m-2}\ge1,\qquad
q_1+q_2+\kappa q_3\le Q/\gamma_d-e.
$
These also hold with $\gamma_d$ replaced throughout by $\gamma_{\min(d,m-1)}$. If $m\le2$, then $mQ\ge1+e$.

In particular, for $m=3$,
$
1+\kappa e\le \kappa Q/\gamma_d+(1-\kappa)q_2+(1-\kappa^2)q_3.
$
If the caps attain the depth of the source, the fibers are nonheavy (every volume is at most $M/2$, $M=1+a+b$) and $0<Q\le\gamma_dM/2$, the last right-hand side is at most $\kappa Q/\gamma_d+(1-\kappa)(2+\kappa)h(Q)$, where $h(Q)$ is the residual root defined by $(a-Q+h)^{1/d}+b^{1/d}=2(1-h)^{1/d}$ and $\max(0,Q-a)<h<1$. Using the effective profile gives $4+a\le (9/4)Q+(27/4)h(Q)$ for three caps in every $d\ge2$.

## Proof

For a cover by $m\ge3$ closed halfspaces of a full-dimensional convex body, ac7ba1e1e0ebc955 gives the ordinary weighted top-three inequality. With the effective profile of 23a563a735c07c25, evaluate its positive-part inequality at the third-largest mass to obtain the same weighted sum with $\gamma_{\min(d,m-1)}$. In both versions the weights are $1,1,\kappa$ with $0<\kappa<1$, because $2<(1+1/j)^j<e<3$ for $j\ge2$. Apply the chosen profile to $K$ to get the first inequality. For each index the endpoint capture $W_i$ obeys $W_i\le Q-q_i$, since the three nonnegative captures on that very cap sum to $Q$. Order statistics are coordinatewise monotone, so the weighted top-three sum on the covered endpoint is at most the sum of the three largest $Q-q_i$ with the same weights. This gives $e\le (2+\kappa)Q-q_1-q_2-\kappa q_3$, the second inequality. The two covered fibers' union bound directly gives $1+e\le\sum_i(q_i+W_i)\le mQ$ for $m\le2$.

For three caps, multiply the endpoint inequality by $\kappa$ and subtract its left side from the left side of the middle inequality to cancel $q_1$; rearrangement gives the stated bound. The nonnegative coefficients allow $q_2,q_3\le h(Q)$ by the residual-root lemma (critical-middle-cap-residual-root-bound). Their coefficient sum is $(1-\kappa)(2+\kappa)$. In the effective three-cap version $\gamma_2=4/9$, $\kappa=1/4$; multiplying by four and using $e\ge a$ proves the final inequality. Neither the separate profiles nor this combination presumes that a scalar mass table is geometrically realizable.
