## Statement

Use the actual finite positive compatible family and fixed invariant energy-power buffer of 088f4667ef9ef0b0. Choose a positive buffer sufficiently small that each shifted own section at a maximizing mark is proper, or assume that properness directly. Let $P$ be the original complete base, $M$ the original total mixed volume, $Q_0$ the maximum original raw depth over the sampled fibers, and $I$ the set of buffered maximizing bases. Partition $I$ into its anchored components $J_c$, equivalently by the intersection graph of maximal minimal faces of $\operatorname{conv}I$. Write $M_c=\sum_{z\in J_c}|K_z|_d$, $M_I=\sum_cM_c$, and $k_c=k(J_c)$. The actual fibers in each component are translates of one common convex body and
$$
Q_0\ge\gamma_d\max_c \frac{M_c}{k_c}\ge\gamma_d\frac{M_I}{k(I)},
\qquad \gamma_d=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
$$
If $I=P$, this gives the original occupied-parity bound. If $\beta=Q_0/M<\gamma_d/k(P)$ then
$$
M_I/M\le\beta k(I)/\gamma_d< k(I)/k(P),\qquad
(M-M_I)/M\ge1-\beta k(I)/\gamma_d.
$$

## Proof

The fixed buffered face cover 088f4667ef9ef0b0 applies under the stipulated properness, which holds for sufficiently small positive buffers by the Euclidean uniform assertion of 0b2fbdaf8d3134a0 after the fixed whitening and scalar rescaling of 088f4667ef9ef0b0. It gives a complete $I$ and one unique maximizing mark $y_z$ at each site on every maximal minimal face, with the whole centered bodies $K_z-y_z$ constant on that face. A nonempty intersection of two such faces contains an integer vertex of $\operatorname{conv}I$. At a shared base the mark is the same, because each is the unique maximizing mark there. Therefore the equality of the whole centered bodies propagates throughout each face component. The anchored-component lemma identifies these components with $J_c$, makes them lattice-complete, and gives $k(I)=\sum_c k_c$.

Fix a component, with $K_z=y_z+L_c$ for a single convex body $L_c$. For any anchored convex representation inside it, actual compatibility gives the inclusion of a translate of $L_c$ in $L_c$ after removing the target translation. A nonzero translation cannot be included in a bounded body, as the support value in its direction would strictly increase. Thus $y_z$ and the actual centroids interpolate in every such representation. In particular the graph of actual centroids is midpoint-closed for the $k_c$ occupied base parity labels. Apply the weighted graph clause of f4547c4166b39683 with weights equal to original volumes: it selects a graph point whose every containing closed ambient halfspace has atomic weight at least $M_c/k_c$. These are the hypotheses of c2180d0c4d7ea592 on this actual subfamily. Its depth transfer gives at least $\gamma_d M_c/k_c$ in the same unweighted-by-density original fiber volumes. The point is an original sampled point, and every cap gains nonnegative mass from the other fibers, so the same bound applies to the original global maximum $Q_0$. Taking the largest component quotient and comparing it to its average with weights $k_c/k(I)$ gives the displayed chain. The remaining assertions follow by substituting $I=P$ or dividing the chain by $M$ and rearranging.
