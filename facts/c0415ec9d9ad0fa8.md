## Statement

Let finitely many positive convex fibers have total volume $M$, and let a finite collection of nonvertical caps through $Y=(p_0,c)$ have common raw mass $Q$, with proper or strictly saturated traces, allowing supporting traces of zero surface measure. Suppose each cap's full mixed coarea measure has positive mass and centroid $Y$. Write $W$ for any probability mixture of their indicators and $\eta$ for the summed fiber boundary measure marked by the outward unit normal $n$. For an affine center field $f_p=c+A(p-p_0)$ put $V_0=n\cdot(x-f_p)$, $V=(V_0)_+$ and $\Xi=(-V_0)_+$. Then
$$
\int V_0\,d\eta=dM,\qquad \int V_0W\,d\eta=dQ,\qquad
\frac{\int VW\,d\eta}{\int V\,d\eta}=
\frac{dQ+\int\Xi W\,d\eta}{dM+\int\Xi\,d\eta}.
$$
In particular the excess above $\beta=Q/M$ is $\int\Xi(W-\beta)\,d\eta/(dM+\int\Xi\,d\eta)$. For the canonical binary triangle circuit at a fixed base and $N=2^n\ge2$, the maximum efficiency of these matching-source radial hinges and their nonzero finite nonnegative sums is $4/(9N)$, attained by the common centroid field.

## Proof

The radial hinge is a continuous nonnegative function, convex in $(p,x)$ for each fixed normal. In a complete compatible sampled family it is an actual erosion speed by 8820984cf6a6942e. On a full fiber the divergence of $x-f_p$ is $d$. For a cap $H_j=\{a_j\cdot p+v_j\cdot x\ge b_j\}$, its proper cut plane has outward normal $-v_j/\lVert v_j\rVert$. Subtracting its planar flux from the divergence integral gives
$$
\int_{\partial K_p\cap H_j}V_0\,d\mathcal H^{d-1}
=d|K_p\cap H_j|+(b_j-a_j\cdot p-v_j\cdot f_p)\sigma_{jp},
$$
where $\sigma_{jp}$ is its section area divided by $\lVert v_j\rVert$. This identity holds for strictly saturated or surface-null supporting traces with $\sigma_{jp}=0$. The proper cut meets the original boundary in a surface-null set, and in dimension one the formula is endpoint subtraction. The centroid equation gives $\sum_p\sigma_{jp}p=S_jp_0$, with $S_j=\sum_p\sigma_{jp}$. By affinity the sum of the corrections is therefore $S_j(b_j-a_j\cdot p_0-v_j\cdot c)=0$. This proof uses the base component of the full centroid and the fact the plane passes through the continuous source. At a full-parameter differentiable attaining cap the full centroid condition follows by differentiating the mass through the fixed source. Summing and averaging gives the two identities. Since $V=V_0+\Xi$, the denominator is at least $dM$ and the displayed quotient and excess follow.

For the binary product, use the actual centered critical profile and masses from the binary-simplex payment theorem in dimension two. Its profile vanishes at every other base, while the matching-source field is centered at the own interior centroid, so $\Xi W=0$ everywhere on the measured boundary. The quotient is therefore at most $Q/M=4/(9N)$, and $A=0$ makes $\Xi=0$ at every base, attaining equality. Nonnegative sums satisfy the same bound by linearity of the two integrals. The binary theorem's base hinge attains $4/9$ for the same profile, demonstrating the distinction between the two test classes.
