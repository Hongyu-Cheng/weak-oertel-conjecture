## Statement

Let $d\ge1$ and let a finite family of compact positive $d$-volume fibers $K_z$ be placed over bases $z\in P$ with summed fiber-volume measure $m$. Write $b(p)$ for the closed-halfspace depth of a base $p$ with weights $|K_z|$; in base rank zero set $b(p)=m(S)$. Let $e$ be a unit continuous covector and let an affine function $A$ have continuous gradient $ce$, $c>0$, and be nonnegative on the entire sampled family. Let a closed cap $H$ have raw mass $Q=m(H)$ and margin $(t/c)A+\ell(z)$ with $t>0$ and affine $\ell$. Suppose $A_{\rm sec}>0$ bounds the sum of maximal areas of sections of the individual $K_z$ perpendicular to $e$, using counting measure on zero-dimensional sections for $d=1$. If $b(p)\ge Q+g_0$ for all $p\in P_+$ and $g_0>0$, then
$$
\ell(z)\le -t g_0/A_{\rm sec}\qquad (z\in\operatorname{conv}P_+).
$$
Consequently the same cap misses every point over that hull with $A/c<g_0/A_{\rm sec}$, including its $A=0$ face.

## Proof

Fix $p\in P_+$. The base trace $\ell(z)\ge\ell(p)$ contains $p$ and has weight at least $b(p)$. If $\ell(p)\ge0$, its entire mass belongs to $H$ since $A\ge0$, contradicting $b(p)>Q$. Otherwise a missed point on a fiber of this trace has
$$
0\le A(z,X)/c < -\ell(z)/t\le-\ell(p)/t .
$$
Since $A(z,X)/c$ has unit continuous gradient $e$, these missed points occupy a slab of width at most $-\ell(p)/t$ on each fiber. Fubini bounds the total missing mass there by $A_{\rm sec}[-\ell(p)/t]$. The missing mass is at least $b(p)-Q\ge g_0$, proving the bound. The trace may be the full base, in which case the same bound follows from its full mass. Affinity extends the result to the convex hull. For $A/c<g_0/A_{\rm sec}$ the entire margin is strictly negative, including for the original closed-cap convention.
