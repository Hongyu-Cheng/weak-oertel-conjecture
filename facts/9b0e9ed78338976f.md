## Statement

Use the finite-source setting of the bounded touching-plane compactness lemma, with the chosen sampled representatives and their coefficientwise limits $A_i$, $L=\min_i A_i$. After passing to a further subsequence, $\sigma_{ij}\to\sigma_i$ and $\rho_{ij}\rightharpoonup\rho_i$. Discard zero weights in weighted identities and write $L_i=L(Y_i)>0$. Each retained $\rho_i$ is a probability on $S$ with $\int w\,d\rho_i=Y_i$ and $\int L\,d\rho_i=L_i$. Put $Z=\{w\in S:L(w)=0\}$ and $\zeta_i=(\sigma_i/L_i)\rho_i|_Z$. Then
$$
\frac{dm_j}{M_jL_j}\rightharpoonup\nu=\sum_i(\sigma_i/L_i)\rho_i=\frac{dm}{ML}+\zeta,\qquad \zeta=\sum_i\zeta_i\ge0,
$$
where the reciprocal density is taken off the mixed-null set $Z$. This tilted coupling is a martingale from $\sum_i(\sigma_i/L_i)\delta_{Y_i}$, and the defect has moment vector
$$
\int_Z(1,w)\,d\zeta=\sum_i(\sigma_i/L_i)(1,Y_i)-M^{-1}\int_S(1,w)\,dm/L.
$$
Every retained row is carried by the minimal base face of its source in $\operatorname{conv}P$ and by equality with every affine majorant of $L$ on $S$ touching at $Y_i$. Its defect lies on the zero face of each such plane. The probabilities $\bar\rho_{ij}=(L_j/L_{ij})\rho_{ij}$ converge weakly to $\bar\rho_i=(L/L_i)\rho_i$, with $\sum_i\sigma_i\bar\rho_i=dm/M$. If $a_i$ is the full slope of any touching affine majorant, then
$$
\int w\,d\bar\rho_i=Y_i+\operatorname{Cov}(\rho_i)a_i/L_i.
$$
Each retained source has a unique continuous slope $c_i$ among its touching affine majorants, and $\nabla_X L=c_i$ at $\bar\rho_i$-almost every target. For every function $\chi$ on the finite set of retained labels,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_i(\sigma_i/L_i)\chi(c_i)(1,Y_i)
&=M^{-1}\int_S\chi(\nabla_X L)(1,w)\,dm/L+\sum_i\chi(c_i)\int_Z(1,w)\,d\zeta_i,\\
M^{-1}\int_S\chi(\nabla_X L)(1,w)\,dm
&=\sum_i\sigma_i\chi(c_i)(1,Y_i+\operatorname{Cov}(\rho_i)a_i/L_i).
\end{aligned}
$$
In particular the tilted mass identity for $\chi(c)=|c|$ retains the term $\sum_i|c_i|\zeta_i(Z)$.

## Proof

All probabilities are on a common compact ambient set. Finite diagonal weak compactness, convergence of the bodies, and uniform convergence of the representatives give the probability limits on $S$ and preserve both displayed row moments. The prelimit mixed reciprocal measure is exactly the weighted row mixture, of total mass at most $1/l_*$. Its weak limit is therefore $\nu$, and the exact prelimit identity $L_j[dm_j/(M_jL_j)]=dm_j/M_j$ passes to $L\nu=dm/M$ against continuous tests by uniform convergence and bounded masses. The ordinary mixed-volume convergence follows from symmetric-difference convergence of the sampled positive convex bodies. Off $Z$, dividing by $L$ on compact positive level sets and then exhausting identifies $\nu|_{S\setminus Z}=dm/(ML)$, with finite mass. The restriction $\nu|_Z$ is precisely $\zeta$. The row barycenters prove the martingale and the moment difference.

A face-exposing affine functional for the source's minimal base face is nonnegative on the base polytope and zero at the source, so its row mean forces support on that face. If an affine majorant $A$ touches at $Y_i$, then $\int(A-L)d\rho_i=A(Y_i)-L_i=0$. This gives contact on the whole row, including its part on $Z$. Uniform convergence of $L_j$ and $L_{ij}\to L_i>0$ gives the row limits after reweighting and proves their volume mixture. They are probabilities by the retained $L$-mean. On the full tilted row $L=L_i+a_i\cdot(w-Y_i)$, hence
$$
\int(w-Y_i)\,d\bar\rho_i=L_i^{-1}\int(w-Y_i)(w-Y_i)^{\mathsf T}\,d\rho_i\,a_i.
$$
In particular the covariance here includes all tilted mass, even the portion killed by multiplication by $L$.

Each positive $\bar\rho_i$ is absolutely continuous with respect to mixed volume by the mixture. Two touching majorants with distinct continuous slopes would have an equality hyperplane of volume zero in every sampled fiber, contrary to contact on the entire probability $\bar\rho_i$. At its volume-almost-everywhere interior points where $L$ is differentiable along the fiber, any affine majorant touching there must have the same continuous derivative. The absolutely continuous part of the positive $\rho_i$ off $Z$ has the same labels, since there $\rho_i=(L_i/L)\bar\rho_i$. The portions on $Z$ retain the source labels even if the target is on a crease. Splitting each tilted moment into its part off $Z$ and on $Z$ gives the first label formula. The reweighted mixture and the covariance identity give the second, without assigning the original-volume row the tilted barycenter. Taking the first coordinate of the first identity at $\chi(c)=|c|$ gives the claimed term.
