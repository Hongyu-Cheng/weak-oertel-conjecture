## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $m=d+1$, and $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1,\ 0\le i\le d\}$ be a full-dimensional simplex of volume $V$ with spanning normals satisfying $\sum_i v_i=0$. Fix $e$ with $v_0\cdot e=1$ and write $x=y+te$ with $t=v_0\cdot x$, $y\in\ker v_0$. Use $\pi_e x=y$ and $d\nu=d\mathcal H^{d-1}/\lVert v_0\rVert$. For $h>d$, set $H=h+1$, $\beta=H/m$, and $\sigma=\sum_i(v_i\cdot e)_+$. For a convex body $B\subseteq T$ of volume $b$, write $U_B(y)$ for its upper $t$ endpoint and $R_e(B,h)=\int_{\pi_e B}(h+U_B)\,d\nu$.

Set $g(u)=u^d/\sigma+[(\sigma-1)/\sigma]\beta u^d/(\beta-1+u)$ on $[0,1]$. For each $0<b\le V$ there is a unique $u\in(0,1]$ with
$$
b/V=g(u)+(1-u)g'(u).
$$
The minimum roof cost over convex $B\subseteq T$ of volume $b$ is $V\sigma\beta g'(u)$. For $b<V$, an optimizer consists of the whole $T$ columns over $\pi_e[T\cap F_\lambda(T)]$, where $\lambda=\sigma\beta/(1-u)$ and $F_\lambda$ is the affine map of the result “Exact affine overlap for simplex roof costs”. For $b=V$, $B=T$ gives the value $V[d\sigma\beta-(\sigma-1)]$.

In particular, for $\eta\in(0,1]$ defined by $b/V=d\eta^{d-1}-(d-1)\eta^d$, all directions satisfy $R_e(B,h)\ge Vd\beta\eta^{d-1}$. It is strict when $\sigma>1$ and attained for every $b$ when $\sigma=1$.

## Proof

The hypotheses of the result “Exact affine overlap for simplex roof costs” hold in this same $T,e,t,\nu$. With $u=1-\sigma\beta/\lambda$, $\lambda>\sigma\beta$, the exact formula there gives $W(\lambda)=Vg(u)$ and $\Phi(\lambda):=\int(\lambda-r_e)_+\ell\,d\nu=\lambda Vg(u)$. The function $g$ is strictly convex on $(0,1)$. To check the nonpolynomial summand, for $a=\beta-1>0$,
$$
\left(\frac{u^d}{a+u}\right)''
=\frac{u^{d-2}[d(d-1)a^2+2d(d-2)au+(d-1)(d-2)u^2]}{(a+u)^3}>0 .
$$
Since $g(0)=g'(0)=0$ and $g(1)=1$, the function $g(u)+(1-u)g'(u)$ increases continuously from zero to one, with derivative $(1-u)g''(u)>0$ in the interior.

The difference quotients of the defining integral for $\Phi$ are dominated by the integrable chord length $\ell$. Its left and right derivatives are respectively the chord-volume masses of $\{r_e<\lambda\}$ and $\{r_e\le\lambda\}$. The explicit formula is differentiable, so the equality level has zero chord-volume mass. It gives
$$
\Phi'(\lambda)=V[g(u)+(1-u)g'(u)],\qquad
\lambda\Phi'(\lambda)-\Phi(\lambda)=V\sigma\beta g'(u).
$$
For $b<V$ choose the prescribed $\lambda$, so $\Phi'=b$. The dual inequality gives the lower bound for every $B$. On each positive-length column the affine image moves both endpoints down, so the overlap is nonempty exactly when $r_e\le\lambda$. The overlap has positive volume by the exact formula. Its projection is compact and convex, and taking the whole $T$ columns over that projection produces a convex compact subbody of volume $\Phi'=b$. Its roof cost is $\int_{\{r_e\le\lambda\}}r_e\ell\,d\nu=\lambda\Phi'-\Phi$, proving attainment.

At $b=V$ any such convex subbody is $T$ since a proper closed subset omits positive volume. The integrals of $\min(\lambda\ell,h+U)$ increase to $R_e(T,h)$ by monotone convergence on the projection interior. They equal $\lambda[V-W(\lambda)]$, whose limit from the overlap formula is $V[d\sigma\beta-(\sigma-1)]=V\sigma\beta g'(1)$.

When $\sigma=1$, $g(u)=u^d$ so $u=\eta$ and the fixed-direction value is the asserted universal bound. When $\sigma>1$ and $b<V$, apply the strict overlap comparison at $\lambda=\beta/(1-\eta)>\beta$. The dual bound is strictly greater than $\lambda[b-V(1-\beta/\lambda)^d]=Vd\beta\eta^{d-1}$. At $b=V$, the exact value exceeds $Vd\beta$ by $V(\sigma-1)(d\beta-1)>0$.
