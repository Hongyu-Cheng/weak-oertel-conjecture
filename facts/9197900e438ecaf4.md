## Statement

Set $T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,e_2)$, $B=(1/3,1/6)+T/3$, and $C=\operatorname{conv}((\{-1\}\times B)\cup(\{0\}\times T)\cup(\{1\}\times B))$. The actual integer fibers are $B,T,B$, with total mixed area $11/18$. At $y_*=(0,1/3,1/3)$, the cap $x\le1/3$ is a strict local minimum of original raw cap mass over all nearby oriented through-point halfspaces, with mass $5/18$ and normalized fraction $5/11$. It strictly contains two vertices of $T$; the middle section has centroid with barycentric coordinates $(1/3,1/3,1/3)$ and the two endpoint sections are full supporting edges with centroid barycentric coordinates $(1/2,0,1/2)$ in their respective triangles. The cap mass in the full normal chart is locally Lipschitz and nondifferentiable at the cap, with zero in its Clarke subdifferential.

## Proof

All vertices of $B$ lie in $T$. Thus the lifted hull has exactly the designated fibers: the endpoint levels are extreme and every combination at zero uses equal endpoint weights, giving a point of $T$. Their areas are $1/18,1/2,1/18$. The cut $x=1/3$ meets $T$ in the segment from $(1/3,0)$ to $(1/3,2/3)$ and each $B$ in its full left edge, so their midpoints give the stated barycentric values. The baseline cap contains $(0,0)$ and $(0,1)$ strictly in the middle fiber and has raw mass $5/18$.

In a neighborhood of the given oriented halfspace, normalize the positive coefficient of $x$ on the left to one. This gives the full chart
$$
H(a,t)=\{x\le1/3+t(y-1/3)+az\}.
$$
For $|t|<1/16$, the excluded region in $T$ is the triangle adjacent to $(1,0)$. Along its two incident edges the intercepted proportions are $(2+t)/3$ and $(2+t)/(3(1+t))$, obtained by evaluating the cutting affine functional at the three vertices. Thus the middle contribution is
$$
\frac12-\frac{(2+t)^2}{18(1+t)}=\frac5{18}-\frac{t^2}{18(1+t)}.
$$
Both endpoint copies contain $R=[1/3,5/12]\times[1/4,5/12]$, as follows from $x\ge1/3$, $y\ge1/6$ and $x+y\le5/6$. With $b=1/12$ write $x=1/3+s$ and $y=1/3+r$ on $R$. If $|a|+b|t|\le b$, the total endpoint area captured within the rectangles is
$$
\int_{-b}^b ((a+tr)_++(-a+tr)_+)\,dr=\int_{-b}^b |a+tr|\,dr.
$$
The equality is by reflection in $r$ in the second summand. Convexity and evenness in $a$ bound the last integral below by its value at $a=0$, namely $b^2|t|$. Jensen's inequality bounds it below by $2b|a|$. The full endpoint area is at least either bound. If $0<|t|<1/16$, the net gain is at least $|t|/144-t^2/9>0$. If $t=0$ and $a\ne0$, the net gain is at least $|a|/6>0$. The rectangle condition holds throughout $|a|<1/24$, $|t|<1/16$, proving strictness in all normal directions. Dividing the baseline $5/18$ by $11/18$ gives $5/11$.

A parameter change alters each cut threshold in $x$ by a uniformly bounded multiple of the parameter change on each bounded fiber. Integrating in $y$ gives local Lipschitz continuity of cap mass. At $t=0$, for small $|a|$, exactly one endpoint contributes a strip of width $|a|$ with length $1/3-s$ at horizontal offset $s$. Its area is $|a|/3-a^2/2$, which gives opposite nonzero one-sided derivatives. For a locally Lipschitz function at a local minimum the Clarke upper directional derivative is nonnegative in every direction, by including the minimizing point as the fixed starting point in its limsup. This puts zero in its Clarke subdifferential.
