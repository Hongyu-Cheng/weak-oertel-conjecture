## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^d$ be compact convex and the hull of its exact nonempty integer fibers $C_j$ with sampled projection $\{0,\ldots,N\}$, $N\ge2$, and put $M=\sum_{j=0}^N |C_j|_d$. There is an affine shear $x'=x-a z-b$ such that, writing $C'_j$ for the sheared fibers and $k=\lfloor N/2\rfloor$,
$$
C'_j\subseteq \frac{N}{\min(k,N-k)}C'_k\quad(0\le j\le N),\qquad
|\operatorname{pr}_{x'} C'|_d\le
\left(\frac{N}{\lfloor N/2\rfloor}\right)^d |C_k|_d\le 3^d M.
$$
For even $N$, the uniform factor is $2^d$. The statement also applies in a primitive affine lattice coordinate on a rank-one base, without a continuous volume change.

## Proof

Choose a point in each endpoint fiber and subtract their affine interpolation in $z$. Then both endpoint fibers contain zero, and their segment gives $0\in C'_j$ at each intermediate site. For $j\le k$, mixing a point of $C'_j$ with the zero point over $N$ gives $(N-k)/(N-j)\,C'_j\subseteq C'_k$. For $j\ge k$, mix with the zero point over $0$ to obtain $(k/j)\,C'_j\subseteq C'_k$. All displayed denominators in their respective cases are positive since $0<k<N$. Each coefficient is at least $\min(k,N-k)/N$. Because $C'_k$ is convex and contains zero, the claimed common dilation contains all fibers. Its convexity and the exact hull hypothesis then contain the continuous shadow, which is the hull of those fiber projections. Take volumes; translations within fibers preserve them, and $N/\lfloor N/2\rfloor\le3$, with equality of this quotient to two for even $N$.
