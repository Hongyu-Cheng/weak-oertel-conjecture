## Statement

Let $K$ be a nondegenerate planar triangle of area $k$ with $y\in\operatorname{int}K$. Let $v_1,v_2,v_3$ be nonzero spanning normals with $\sum_i v_i=0$. Suppose the caps $v_i\cdot(x-y)\ge0$ strictly capture distinct vertices $P_i$ and strictly exclude the other two vertices. Let $q_i$ be their area fractions, and use cyclic indices. Put $H=\sum_i v_i\cdot(P_i-y)$, $D=|\det(v_1,v_2)|$, $a_i=v_i\cdot(P_i-y)/H$, $b_{ij}=-v_i\cdot(P_j-y)/H$ for $i\ne j$, $d_i=b_{i,i+1}-b_{i,i+2}$, and $f_i=2a_i/\sqrt{q_i}$. Then
$$
R:=1+\Bigl(\sum_i d_i\Bigr)^2-2\sum_i d_i^2=4-\sum_i f_i^2=\frac{8Dk}{H^2}>0.
$$
If all $q_i\le128/279$, then $F:=\sum_i f_i\ge2/\sqrt{128/279}>59/20$ and $R\ge F^2+4F-20>201/400$.

## Proof

The strict cap incidences and the balanced spanning normals satisfy the hypotheses of fact a19fb2ae234afb2a. In its notation $g_i=1-d_{i+1}+d_{i+2}$, $2(a_i+b_{i,i+1})=g_i+d_i$, $2(a_i+b_{i,i+2})=g_i-d_i$, and $f_i^2=g_i^2-d_i^2$. Expanding $\sum_i g_i^2$ yields $3+3\sum_i d_i^2-(\sum_i d_i)^2$, giving $4-\sum_i f_i^2=R$.

Map $x$ to $(v_1\cdot(x-y),v_2\cdot(x-y))/H$. The determinant magnitude of this map is $D/H^2$, positive because every pair of normals is independent by balance and spanning. In these coordinates the determinant of the first two vertex position vectors is $a_1a_2-b_{12}b_{21}>0$ by the column identities $a_1=b_{21}+b_{31}$ and $a_2=b_{12}+b_{32}$. The origin is interior, so the ordered vertices have positive orientation. The two edge vectors from the third vertex, in order toward the first and second vertices, have columns
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
(g_1-d_1)/2 & -d_1\\
d_2 & (g_2+d_2)/2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The determinant is $[1-(d_1+d_2-d_3)^2]/4+d_1d_2=R/4$. It is twice the transformed area, hence $R=8Dk/H^2$.

The signed-row moment gives $F\ge 2/\sqrt{128/279}$ under the cap ceiling. Its square exceeds $(59/20)^2$ by $13/800$. The three row inequalities $f_i+(f_j+f_k)^2/4\le2$ sum to $F+(F^2+\sum_i f_i^2)/4\le6$. Substitution into $R=4-\sum_i f_i^2$ yields $R\ge F^2+4F-20>(59/20)^2+4(59/20)-20=201/400$.
