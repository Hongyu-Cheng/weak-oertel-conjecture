## Statement

Let $d\ge2$ and let the positive fibers at finitely many bases be $K_z=t_z+s_zT$, $s_z>0$, for a reference $d$-simplex $T=\operatorname{conv}(v_0,\ldots,v_d)$ with $\sum_kv_k=0$. Assume $z_0$ is a positive base and $t_z=x_0+A(z-z_0)$ for one matrix $A$. Consider a cap through $(z_0,x_0)$ with ambient normal $(a,u)$, $u\ne0$. Let $i,j$ be indices of a largest and second-largest $u\cdot v_k$. Assume every fiber is strictly full, strictly empty, or properly cut. In every proper fiber at least two vertices are strictly included and its actual section centroid has barycentric coordinates at $i,j$ at most $1/d$. Then the cap is not a local minimum of mass among ambient caps through the anchor. A single coupled direction has strictly negative first derivative if $u\cdot v_i>u\cdot v_j$, and zero first and strictly negative second derivative if $u\cdot v_i=u\cdot v_j$. Other proper-fiber margins may be zero.

## Proof

Write $b_k=u\cdot v_k$, $r_z=a\cdot(z-z_0)+u\cdot(t_z-x_0)$ and $c_{zk}=r_z+s_zb_k$. The vertex-value map of linear functionals has rank $d$ and image the zero-sum vectors, by affine independence and the normalization of the vertices. Choose $\delta u$ with vertex values $+1,-1$ at $i,j$ and zero elsewhere. With $\delta a=-A^\top\delta u$, all $r_z$ are constant along this normal path and the only margin changes are $s_z$ and $-s_z$ at $i,j$. The ordering is common to all fibers, so both chosen margins are positive in every proper fiber. The center fiber is proper since $x_0=t_{z_0}$ is its interior centroid and $u\ne0$.

For a proper fiber write $f_z$ for the density of $\sum_k c_{zk}E_k$. Its two positive phases and one negative phase meet 511d89569241b0d6 and 3e6e1eb15d927db8. Write $\phi_z(c)=f_{\sum_k c_{zk}E_k+cE'}(0)/f_z(0)$ for an independent unit exponential $E'$. The first derivative and section-centroid identities in those two results give $\phi_z(c_{zk})=d\lambda_{zk}$ for the positive pair, where $\lambda_{zk}$ is its section centroid coordinate. The normalized reflected density $f_z(-x)/f_z(0)$ on $[0,\infty)$ is positive, continuous, integrable and log-concave by 511d89569241b0d6. Its reciprocal Laplace transform in 506e246f680da4b5 is $1/\phi_z(c)$, so it is convex and has strictly positive derivative wherever it is at least one. Both selected values are at least one.

If $b_i>b_j$, the selected margins are strictly ordered in all proper fibers. Convexity and the positive derivative at the smaller margin give $\phi_z(c_{zi})<\phi_z(c_{zj})$. Thus the joint derivative $\sum_{\mathrm{proper}\ z}w_zs_z f_z(0)[\phi_z(c_{zi})-\phi_z(c_{zj})]$ is strictly negative, with $w_z=|K_z|$. At equality of the two $b$ values the first derivative is zero. The repeated positive-value Hessian formulas from 511d89569241b0d6 give the second derivative $\sum_{\mathrm{proper}\ z}2w_zs_z^2 f_z(0)\phi_z'(c_{zi})<0$. Both expressions follow by summing the actual per-fiber responses to the same normal path. Nonproper fibers remain strictly saturated and all other margins are fixed, so even zero margins in proper fibers cause no change in the argument.
