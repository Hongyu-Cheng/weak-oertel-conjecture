## Statement

Let $T$ be an area-one triangle with centroid $g$, let $m\ge1$ be an integer and let $S$ be the actual integer-fiber source of $[0,2m-1]\times T$. For any direction $u$ whose vertex values $u\cdot(x-g)$ are $(-1,-1,2)$, the closed cap
$$
u\cdot(x-g)\ge \frac{6(z-(m-1))}{2m^2+1}
$$
at $(m-1,g)$ has mass $4m(4m^2-1)/(9(2m^2+1))$. The global maximum raw depth under summed fiber area is at most this mass. For $m\ge4$ this strictly defeats the step-cap formula $m-1+4/9$. At $m=4$ the cap has mass $112/33$ with gap $5/99$ below $31/9$.

## Proof

The product has exactly the $2m$ indicated integer fibers, each of area one. The upper projection tail in the chosen direction is $F(s)=(2-s)^2/9$ on $[-1,2]$, by triangle similarity. Set $L=6/(2m^2+1)$ and $k=z-(m-1)$. All thresholds $kL$ lie in that interval: $2m^2+1-6(m-1)=2(m-3/2)^2+5/2>0$ and $2(2m^2+1)-6m=2(2m-1)(m-1)\ge0$. Summing with $\sum k=m$ and $\sum k^2=m(2m^2+1)/3$ gives
$$
\sum_{k=-(m-1)}^m F(kL)=\frac{8m-4mL+m(2m^2+1)L^2/3}{9}
=\frac{4m(4m^2-1)}{9(2m^2+1)}.
$$
An ambient depth superlevel is convex: it is the intersection of the open complements of all closed caps of mass strictly below the level. The six affine symmetries permuting the vertices of $T$ preserve the source and halfspace mass. Averaging any source point over these permutations puts it at its own fiber centroid without decreasing depth. Base reflection about $m-1/2$ also preserves depth, and the segment between any integer fiber centroid and its reflection contains both middle centroids. Thus the depth at $(m-1,g)$ dominates that of every mixed point.

Subtracting $m-1+4/9$ gives numerator $-2m^3+10m^2-13m+5$ over $9(2m^2+1)$. The numerator is $-15$ at $m=4$ and its forward difference is $-6m^2+14m-5<0$ for every $m\ge4$. At $m=4$ substitution gives the stated rational masses.
