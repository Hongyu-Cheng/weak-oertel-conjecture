## Statement

Let $T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_m)$, $m\ge1$, and let $K\subset\mathbb R^m$ be a convex body with $d_{H,\infty}(K,T)\le\delta\le 1/(8m^2)$. Here $d_{H,\infty}$ denotes Hausdorff distance for the infinity norm. For every convex body $S$ containing $K$ with $|S|\le(1+2m\delta)^m|T|$,
$$
d_{H,\infty}(S,T)\le8m^3\delta.
$$
In particular the bound applies to every minimum-volume enclosing simplex $S$. If $\delta\le1/(64m^3)$, the vertices $a_0,\ldots,a_m$ of every such simplex can be labeled to satisfy $\max_i\lVert a_i-v_i\rVert_\infty\le8m^3\delta$, where $v_0=0$ and $v_i=e_i$ for $i\ge1$. The centroid of each facet of $S$ belongs to $K$ and lies within $8m^3\delta$ of the corresponding facet centroid of $T$.

## Proof

Write
$$
T_-=\{x_i\ge\delta,\ \textstyle\sum_i x_i\le1-m\delta\},\qquad
T_+=\{x_i\ge-\delta,\ \textstyle\sum_i x_i\le1+m\delta\}.
$$
We have $T_-\subset K\subset T_+$. In fact $T_-+[-\delta,\delta]^m\subset T$ and $h_K(u)\ge h_T(u)-\delta\lVert u\rVert_1$, giving the inner inclusion by support-function comparison. The outer inclusion follows by applying the defining inequalities of $T$ to a point within $\delta$ of each $x\in K$. These inner and outer simplices have volumes $(1-2m\delta)^m|T|$ and $(1+2m\delta)^m|T|$, respectively. Every minimum-volume enclosing simplex has volume at most $|T_+|$.

If $\delta=0$, then $K=T$. Any convex body containing $T$ and an additional point has a positive-volume cone beyond some facet of $T$, so the volume bound forces $S=T$. Take $\delta>0$ and put
$$
\Delta=\frac{(1+2m\delta)^m-(1-2m\delta)^m}{(1-2m\delta)^{m-1}},\qquad E=\Delta-\delta.
$$
For any $x\in S$ outside a facet inequality of $T_-$, take the cone from $x$ over that facet. It lies in $S$ and is disjoint from the interior of $T_-$. Its volume is $(1-2m\delta)^{m-1}|T|$ times the positive deficit $\delta-x_i$ or $\sum_i x_i-(1-m\delta)$ of the violated inequality. For the latter facet the $\sqrt m$ factor in its area cancels the inverse factor in the perpendicular height. The volume formula also holds in dimension one with the point facet. The volume budget $|S|-|T_-|$ bounds each positive deficit by $\Delta$. Thus
$$
x_i\ge -E,\qquad \sum_i x_i\le 1-m\delta+\Delta\le1+E.
$$
The linear term in the odd-binomial expansion of the numerator gives $\Delta\ge4m^2\delta$, so $E\ge m\delta$. Put $a=2m\delta\le1/(4m)$. The mean-value theorem bounds the numerator by $4m^2\delta(1+a)^{m-1}$. Bernoulli's inequality gives $(1-a)^{m-1}\ge3/4$. The binomial expansion is bounded by the geometric series with ratio $(m-1)a$, so $(1+a)^{m-1}\le4/3$. Hence $\Delta\le8m^2\delta$. The preceding inequalities give $S\subset -E\mathbf1+[1+(m+1)E]T$. Each point of that simplex differs from its corresponding point of $T$ by at most $mE$ in infinity norm. Each point of $T$ is within $\delta$ of $K\subset S$. This gives the Hausdorff bound.

For the vertex labeling suppose $S$ is a minimizing simplex and $\delta\le1/(64m^3)$. For $i\ge1$ choose a vertex $a_i$ maximizing $x_i$. Then $x_i(a_i)\ge1-\delta$ since $K\subset S$ approximates $e_i$. The inequalities on $S$ give $x_i(a_i)\le1+mE$ and $-E\le x_j(a_i)\le\delta+(m-1)E\le mE$ for $j\ne i$. Choose $a_0$ minimizing $\sum_i x_i$, which there is at most $m\delta$ because $K$ approximates zero. Then $-E\le x_i(a_0)\le m\delta+(m-1)E\le mE$ for all $i$. Each vertex lies within $mE\le8m^3\delta\le1/8$ of its assigned reference vertex. These neighborhoods are disjoint, so the chosen vertices exhaust the $m+1$ vertices of $S$. Averaging the $m$ vertices of each facet preserves the same error bound, and the minimum-simplex facet-contact result places those averages in $K$.
