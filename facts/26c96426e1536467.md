## Statement

In $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^2$ take
$
C=\{(z,x,w):-1\le z\le1,\ -1\le x\le1+(9/5)(1-|z|),\ |w|\le1/2\}.
$
Its integer fibers have areas $2,19/5,2$, each strictly less than half the total. The point $Y=(0,9/20,0)$ attains the global maximum raw mixed depth $69/20$ over all closed ambient halfspaces. The caps $H_0=\{x\ge9/20\}$ and $H_\pm=\{x\le9/20\pm(2z+w/2)\}$ are an actual positively balanced rank-two continuous circuit, all critical at $Y$, each containing exactly one lifted fiber centroid. For $H_0$, one minus the sum of the two endpoint captured square roots divided by twice the middle captured square root is $1-\sqrt{11/47}>1/3$.

## Proof

The upper endpoint in $x$ is concave in $z$, so this is an actual convex body with exactly the indicated three integer fibers. Put $c=9/20$, $R=1+2c$, and $M_0=6+4c$. We first prove the all-direction lower bound at $Y$ by the paired-tail argument of 907b6bf347e73ada. For $L>0$ let $T_L(t)=\min(2L,(L-t)_+)$ be the upper tail length of $[-L,L]$, and write $D_L(v,r)=T_L(r-v)-T_L(r+v)$ for $r\ge0$. It is odd in $v$, with derivative $\mathbf1_{\{|v-r|<L\}}+\mathbf1_{\{|v+r|<L\}}$ almost everywhere. Let $G_a(s)$ be the sum of the three interval tail lengths for $x-c+az\ge s$. The interval centers in that margin are $c,a-c,-a-c$, with radii $R,1,1$. Using $T_L(-t)=2L-T_L(t)$ and oddness gives
$
G_a(r)+G_a(-r)-M_0
=D_R(c,r)-[D_1(a+c,r)-D_1(a-c,r)].
$
Here $0\le D_R(c,r)\le2c$ and the bracket is between $0$ and $4c$, by the derivative formula. For $r\le1+c$, the middle term is $D_R(c,r)=2c$. For $r>1+c$, the two intervals supporting the derivative of $D_1$ have a gap greater than $2c$, so the bracket, integrated over an interval of length $2c$, is at most $2c$. Thus the paired tail sum is between $M_0-2c$ and $M_0+2c$ for all $a,r$.

Move a containing cap parallel to its boundary through $Y$. If its $x$ coefficient is positive, divide by that coefficient; the resulting $w$-dependent threshold is a linear function of $w$. Pair $w$ with $-w$ in the unit-width padding interval. The preceding lower paired bound gives at least $(M_0-2c)/2=69/20$. If the coefficient is negative, the complementary lower tails have no boundary length and the upper paired bound gives the same minimum. If the coefficient is zero, the projected measure on $(z,w)$ is centrally symmetric, with equal endpoint weights. A closed halfspace through its center retains at least half of the mass, including in a pure integer direction.

The three stated caps have margins with positive relation $2L_0+L_++L_-=0$ and their continuous normals span dimension two. Thus they cover the ambient space. Their fiber areas, in row order $A,K,B$ and column order $H_0,H_+,H_-$, are
$
\begin{pmatrix}11/20&0&2\\47/20&29/20&29/20\\11/20&2&0\end{pmatrix}.
$
Indeed, each tilted cap saturates the endpoints in opposite senses, and its middle threshold stays within the middle interval with mean $c$ over $w$. Each cap has mass $69/20$, proving the global upper bound and actual criticality at $Y$. The endpoint continuous centroids are $(0,0)$ and the middle centroid is $(9/10,0)$. Their lifted margin table, with the same row and column order, is
$
\begin{pmatrix}-9/20&-31/20&49/20\\9/20&-9/20&-9/20\\-9/20&49/20&-31/20\end{pmatrix}.
$
Each column has one positive entry. Strict nonheaviness follows from $19/5<39/10$, and the last deficit follows from the first column of areas and $11/47<4/9$. The normalized depth $23/52$ is above $\gamma_2/2$.
