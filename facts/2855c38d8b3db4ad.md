## Statement

Let $A,K,B\subset\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge1$, be convex bodies at bases $-1,0,1$ with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$, volumes $a,c,b$, and total mixed mass $M=a+c+b$. Set $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. Let finitely many ambient closed caps $u_i\cdot(x-y)+\beta_i z\ge0$, with $u_i\ne0$, attain raw mixed depth $Q$ at the same point $(0,y)$. Write $U_i,q_i,V_i$ for their respective fiber masses. Suppose $\sum_i\lambda_i u_i=0$ with $\lambda_i>0$, and relabel the endpoints, if necessary, so the $A$ traces cover $A$. For every $0\le\tau\le Q$,
$
\frac{Q}{\gamma_d}+\sum_i\operatorname{dist}\bigl(\tau,[q_i,q_i+V_i]\bigr)\ge c+a.
$
The distance summand equals $|q_i-\tau|-\min\{V_i,(\tau-q_i)_+\}$. For exactly three caps with $q_1\le q_2\le q_3$, this implies $q_3-q_1\ge c+a-Q/\gamma_d+\min\{V_1,q_2-q_1\}$. If $a,b,c<M/2$ and $Q<\gamma_d M/2$, then $q_3-q_1>(c+a-b)/2+\min\{V_1,q_2-q_1\}$.

## Proof

The weighted sum of margins over a base $z$ is $z\Delta$ with $\Delta=\sum_i\lambda_i\beta_i$. Thus the traces cover the whole continuous space at $z=0$ and at one endpoint (both when $\Delta=0$); choose the names so the covered endpoint is $A$. Every trace has nonzero continuous normal, so ordinary $d$-volume sees null cut boundaries even when a trace takes a full, supporting or empty part of a fiber.

Apply the ordinary specialization of ac7ba1e1e0ebc955 to $K$ at threshold $\tau$ and $A$ at threshold $Q-\tau$. Both thresholds are nonnegative. Adding the inequalities gives $c+a\le Q/\gamma_d+\sum_i[(q_i-\tau)_++(U_i-Q+\tau)_+]$. Since $U_i+q_i+V_i=Q$ with the same index in all three fibers, the bracket is $(q_i-\tau)_++(\tau-q_i-V_i)_+$, the stated distance. For $\tau<q_i$ the correction to $|q_i-\tau|$ is zero; for $\tau\ge q_i$ it is $\min\{V_i,\tau-q_i\}$. At $\tau=q_2$ the sum of the absolute values is $q_3-q_1$, and only the first correction can be nonzero, including in case of ties. The strict budget gives $c+a-Q/\gamma_d>c+a-M/2=(c+a-b)/2$.
