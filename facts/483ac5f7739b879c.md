## Statement

In the body $C=\{(z,x_1,x_2):|z|\le1,\ (x_1-z)^2+x_2^2\le1\}$ the mixed section of $z/2+x_2=0$ has centroid zero, and the two opposite caps are depth-minimizing at zero with mass fraction $1/2$. With $\nu_z$ length measure on the three section fibers, the centered cross moment is $(2\sqrt3,-\sqrt3)$ and $\sum_z(-z/2)\int x\,d\nu_z=(-\sqrt3,\sqrt3/2)$.

## Proof

This convex body has disks centered at $(z,0)$ on integer bases $-1,0,1$. The chord lengths on the section are $\sqrt3,2,\sqrt3$ and their centers are $(z,-z/2)$. The lifted outer centers are negatives of each other and have equal weights, so the full centroid is zero. Multiplying each continuous centroid by its chord length and by $z$ gives $(2\sqrt3,-\sqrt3)$. Multiplying by $-z/2$ gives the other identity. The mixed law is centrally symmetric about zero, so every closed containing cap has at least half mass. The two tested caps have null mixed boundary, hence each has exactly half.
