## Statement

Let
$$
C=\{(z,x):z\in[-2,2]^4,\ -1\le x\le\min(1,2-\lVert z\rVert_\infty)\}.
$$
The cap $x\ge0$ has mixed mass $81$ out of $706$, proper sections at $I=\{-1,0,1\}^4$ and outside supporting sections at the other sampled bases $J=\{-2,-1,0,1,2\}^4\setminus I$, all with section coarea one. All fibers lie in $[-1,1]$ and agree with that envelope over the entire real cube $[-1,1]^4$. The occupied full-rank core $B=\operatorname{conv}\{\pm e_i:1\le i\le4\}$ has the interior lattice point zero and lies in the centroid region of the laws with weight one on $I$ and any weights $\tau_z\in[0,1]$ on $J$. At every $p\in\{0,\pm e_1,\ldots,\pm e_4\}$, the cap $x\ge0$ is a local minimum over base tilts through $(p,0)$ with positive continuous normal.

## Proof

The roof is a minimum of finitely many affine functions, hence concave. The bounded source is closed, convex and contains an open set. At the $3^4=81$ inner sites the fiber is $[-1,1]$; at the $5^4-3^4=544$ outer sites it is $[-1,0]$. A one-dimensional section by $x=0$ has counting coarea one, giving the stated sections and both masses. The full-dimensional crosspolytope $B$ lies in the real cube where the envelope is exactly $[-1,1]$, has zero in its interior, and has sampled vertices.

For $J_+=\{z\in J:z_1>0\}$, its cardinality is $2\cdot5^3-3^3=223$ and its first-coordinate sum is $(1+2)5^3-3^3=348$. All other coordinate sums vanish by sign symmetry. The inner sites also sum to zero. Use selector $\tau_z=81/125$ on $J_+$ and zero elsewhere. Its weighted centroid has first coordinate $(81/125)348/[81+(81/125)223]=1$ and all other coordinates zero. Permuting and changing coordinate signs gives all $\pm e_i$; taking all selectors zero gives zero. The centroid region is convex: mix the probability laws of any two selected weight systems, and rescale by their common weight on $I$. The new outer-to-inner weight at each site is a weighted average of the two original selectors, so remains in $[0,1]$. Thus the entire hull $B$ lies in the region.

For $p$ among these marks the cap of positive normalized continuous normal with tilt $a\in\mathbb R^4$ is $x+a\cdot(z-p)\ge0$. For $a$ small enough that all absolute shifts are less than one, its actual mixed mass is
$$
F_p(a)=81-81a\cdot p+\sum_{z\in J}[a\cdot(z-p)]_+.
$$
Indeed each inner interval contributes $1+a\cdot(z-p)$ and each outer interval contributes $[a\cdot(z-p)]_+$. A selector law of centroid $p$ satisfies $\sum_{z\in J}\tau_z(z-p)=81p$. Therefore $[a\cdot(z-p)]_+\ge\tau_z a\cdot(z-p)$ gives $F_p(a)\ge81=F_p(0)$. The cap has one continuous coordinate, so positive continuous-normal normalization leaves only these base tilts. This proves exact local minimality at each stated mark.
