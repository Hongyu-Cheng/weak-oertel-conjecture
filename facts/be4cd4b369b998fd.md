## Statement

Use the smooth, positively curved compatible finite family of the support-path lemma, and assume it is a local minimum of the maximum normalized closed-halfspace depth $\beta$ among nearby actual positive compatible families on $P$. Let $M$ be its total mass. Fix any finite collection of complete positive critical cap groups covering the deepest base planes, with old cap masses $\beta M$. In each group $i$ the caps $H_{ij}$ have unit continuous normals $v_{ij}$, through an interior old anchor, and positive balance coefficients $\rho_{ij}$ with $\sum_j\rho_{ij}v_{ij}=0$. Each group's weighted old margin is nonnegative on the base planes it is assigned. Set $S_{ij}>0$ equal to the total section area of its cap and $\kappa_{ij}=(\rho_{ij}/S_{ij})/\sum_l(\rho_{il}/S_{il})$.

There is a probability vector $\pi_i$ such that, with $\lambda_{ij}=\pi_i\kappa_{ij}/\beta$, $\mu_p(u)=J_p(u)$ and $\nu_p(u)=J_p(u)\sum_{ij}\lambda_{ij}\mathbf1_{H_{ij}}(p,x_p(u))$, the following hold for every $u$ off the selected cap-boundary preimages. These exceptional sets are surface-null.

- The signed weights $\mu_p-\nu_p$ have zero moments against $(1,p,x_p(u))$. The weights $\nu_p$ are dominated by $\mu_p$ in convex order on $(p,h_p(u))$.
- There is a nonnegative Borel array $T_{pq}(u)$ with row and column masses $\nu_p,\mu_q$. Each positive row has barycenter $(p,x_p(u))$ in full physical coordinates and is an active anchored support representation at $u$. If all nontrivial anchored inequalities are strict at a normal, the array there is diagonal. Setting it to zero on the exceptional set gives an integrable array, and $T_{pq}(u)\,du$ couples the cap-weighted boundary measure $\nu_\partial=\sum_{ij}\lambda_{ij}(\mu_\partial|_{H_{ij}})$ to the summed boundary area $\mu_\partial$, retaining the normal and the full physical barycenter. In particular they have equal masses and first moments and $\int\Phi\,d\nu_\partial\le\int\Phi\,d\mu_\partial$ for every continuous convex ambient $\Phi$.
- Across any spherical arc on which all incidences are constant separately on its two sides and avoid selected cap boundaries there, let $\Delta_{ij,p}$ be the right incidence minus the left at $u_*$. Then
$
\sum_{p,i,j}\lambda_{ij}\Delta_{ij,p}J_p(u_*)\,(1,p,x_p(u_*))=0.
$
If the convex hulls of the positive-jump and negative-jump physical points are disjoint, or one sign set is empty, the multiplier of every cap with a nonzero jump is zero. The same follows from the weaker base-only disjointness test. A finite set of such crossings meeting a cap from every selected group contradicts local minimality.

If all $L_i-\beta D$ were strictly negative, these groups and the persistent slack covers would place every base plane under a strictly smaller cap budget than $\beta M(t)$. The responses $L_i-\beta D$ are linear in the first velocities.
