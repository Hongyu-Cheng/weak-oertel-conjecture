## Statement

Let \(n\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^2\) be a convex body, and take the arbitrary-selected-mass empty-core branch of accepted fact `07009c5047a9f7bb`.  Thus \(E\subseteq\mathbb Z^n\) is finite and nonempty,
\[
 0<t_z\leq a_z:=\operatorname{area}(C_z)\quad(z\in E),
 \qquad T:=\sum_{z\in E}t_z,
\]
\(m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E\),
\[
 D_z:=\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z),
 \qquad
 \Lambda_m(E):=
 \left\{\lambda\in\mathbb R_+^E:
 \sum_z\lambda_z=1,
 \ \sum_z\lambda_zz=m\right\},
\]
and a support-minimal balanced negative circuit is given by
\[
 2\leq r\leq3,
 \qquad
 w_i\in\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\},
 \qquad
 \theta_i>0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i=1,
 \qquad
 \sum_{i=1}^r\theta_iw_i=0,
\tag{1}
\]
together with globally affine functions \(\ell_i:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R\) satisfying
\[
 \ell_i(z)\leq h_{D_z}(w_i)\quad(z\in E),
 \qquad
 \ell_i(m)=\phi_t(w_i),
 \qquad
 \sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i\phi_t(w_i)<0.
\tag{2}
\]
Let
\[
 A_i:=C\cap\{(z,x):\langle w_i,x\rangle\geq\ell_i(z)\},
 \qquad
 a_{i,z}:=\operatorname{area}((A_i)_z),
\tag{3}
\]
where \(a_{i,z}\) is the quantity denoted \(f_i(z)\) in `07009c5047a9f7bb`.  Define
\[
 h_{i,z}:=h_{D_z}(w_i),
 \qquad
 q_{i,z}:=h_{i,z}-\ell_i(z)\geq0,
 \qquad
 \varepsilon:=-\sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i\phi_t(w_i)>0.
\tag{4}
\]
Then every \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)\) satisfies the exact common-support gap budget
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z
 \sum_{i=1}^r\theta_iq_{i,z}\geq\varepsilon.}
\tag{5}
\]
In particular, for every such \(\lambda\), some pair \((i,z)\) with \(\lambda_z>0\) satisfies
\[
 q_{i,z}\geq\varepsilon.
\tag{6}
\]

For \(s\geq0\), put
\[
 V_{i,z}(s):=
 \operatorname{area}\left(
 C_z\cap\{x:\langle w_i,x\rangle\geq h_{i,z}-s\}
 \right).
\tag{7}
\]
The finite, body-dependent modulus
\[
 \Omega_\varepsilon:=
 \min_{1\leq i\leq r,\ z\in E}
 \left(V_{i,z}(\varepsilon)-\frac49t_z\right)
\tag{8}
\]
is strictly positive, and
\[
 \boxed{
 \max_{1\leq i\leq r}
 \left(\sum_{z\in E}a_{i,z}-\frac49T\right)
 \geq\Omega_\varepsilon>0.}
\tag{9}
\]
Consequently it is impossible that
\[
 a_{i,z}=\frac49t_z
 \qquad(1\leq i\leq r,\ z\in E).
\tag{10}
\]

In the three-cap case \(r=3\), use the notation of accepted fact `b7646e88958a76a4`:
\[
 Z_\cap:=\{z:(A_i)_z\ne\varnothing\text{ for }i=1,2,3\},
 \qquad
 \mu_i:=\sum_{z\in Z_\cap}a_{i,z},
 \qquad
 L:=\frac49T.
\tag{11}
\]
Then \(E\subseteq Z_\cap\) and
\[
 \boxed{\max_{1\leq i\leq3}(\mu_i-L)\geq\Omega_\varepsilon>0.}
\tag{12}
\]
Thus the scalar equality \(\mu_1=\mu_2=\mu_3=L\), and therefore the stronger pointwise pattern in which all selected cap fibers have area \(4t_z/9\) and are pairwise homothetic with a common area profile, cannot occur in a genuine support-minimal balanced negative circuit.  This excludes the proposed canonical \(n=4\) homothetic equality body without any assumption on its parity grid, matching, exceptional-fiber count, or rationality.

## Proof

For each fixed \(z\in E\), the map \(u\mapsto h_{D_z}(u)\) is a support function.  Positive homogeneity and subadditivity, together with (1), give
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i h_{i,z}
 =\sum_{i=1}^r h_{D_z}(\theta_iw_i)
 \geq h_{D_z}\left(\sum_{i=1}^r\theta_iw_i\right)
 =h_{D_z}(0)=0.
\tag{13}
\]
Fix \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)\).  Affineness of every \(\ell_i\), the barycentric constraints defining \(\Lambda_m(E)\), and (2) imply
\[
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z\ell_i(z)
 =\ell_i(m)=\phi_t(w_i).
\tag{14}
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z\sum_{i=1}^r\theta_iq_{i,z}
 &=\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z\sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i h_{i,z}
   -\sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i\phi_t(w_i)\\
 &\geq\varepsilon,
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]
which proves (5).  The numbers \(\lambda_z\theta_i\) are nonnegative and sum to one, so (6) follows.

We next establish the strict cap-volume property used in (8).  Fix \((i,z)\), abbreviate \(K:=C_z\), \(t:=t_z\), \(w:=w_i\), and \(h:=h_{\mathsf M_t(K)}(w)\), and let
\[
 \alpha:=\min_{x\in K}\langle w,x\rangle,
 \qquad
 \beta:=\max_{x\in K}\langle w,x\rangle.
\]
The constant admissible density \(t/\operatorname{area}(K)\) shows that the centroid of \(K\) belongs to \(\mathsf M_t(K)\).  Since \(K\) has nonempty interior, integration of \(\langle w,x\rangle-\alpha\) over \(K\) is strictly positive, so \(h>\alpha\).  On the other hand, compactness of the metronoid from accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` gives a support point represented by an admissible density \(f\).  The supporting line \(\{x:\langle w,x\rangle=\beta\}\) has planar measure zero, while \(f\) has positive integral \(t\).  Hence
\[
 \int_K(\beta-\langle w,x\rangle)f(x)\,dx>0,
\]
and therefore \(h<\beta\).  Thus
\[
 \alpha<h<\beta.
\tag{16}
\]

The projection of \(\operatorname{int}K\) under \(x\mapsto\langle w,x\rangle\) is the open interval \((\alpha,\beta)\).  Consequently, for every \(s>0\), the slab
\[
 K\cap\{x:h-s\leq\langle w,x\rangle<h\}
\]
has positive area.  In the notation (7), this proves
\[
 V_{i,z}(s)>V_{i,z}(0)\qquad(s>0).
\tag{17}
\]
Choose a support point \(y\in D_z\) with \(\langle w_i,y\rangle=h_{i,z}\).  The closed halfspace in the definition of \(V_{i,z}(0)\) contains \(y\) on its boundary.  Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d`, specialized to dimension two, therefore gives
\[
 V_{i,z}(0)\geq\left(\frac23\right)^2t_z=\frac49t_z.
\tag{18}
\]
Equations (17)-(18), applied with \(s=\varepsilon>0\), show that every entry in the finite minimum (8) is positive.  Hence \(\Omega_\varepsilon>0\).

By (3)-(4) and (7), the actual cap area is exactly
\[
 a_{i,z}=V_{i,z}(q_{i,z}).
\tag{19}
\]
Equations (17)-(18) and \(q_{i,z}\geq0\) give
\[
 a_{i,z}\geq\frac49t_z
 \qquad(i,z).
\tag{20}
\]
For any \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)\), choose a pair supplied by (6).  Monotonicity of \(V_{i,z}\), (8), and (19) give for that pair
\[
 a_{i,z}-\frac49t_z
 =V_{i,z}(q_{i,z})-\frac49t_z
 \geq V_{i,z}(\varepsilon)-\frac49t_z
 \geq\Omega_\varepsilon.
\tag{21}
\]
All other summands in (20) are nonnegative.  Summing over the index \(i\) selected in (21) proves (9), and (10) follows immediately.

Finally suppose \(r=3\).  Equation (20) is positive because \(t_z>0\), so every \(z\in E\) belongs to the common support \(Z_\cap\).  Therefore
\[
 \mu_i-L
 \geq\sum_{z\in E}\left(a_{i,z}-\frac49t_z\right)
 \qquad(1\leq i\leq3).
\tag{22}
\]
Combining (22) with (9) proves (12).  The homothetic equality pattern considered after accepted fact `b7646e88958a76a4` includes \(\mu_i=L\) for all three indices, so (12) rules it out.  The contradiction occurs before any global matching or descendant-capacity audit and therefore applies, in particular, to every proposed rational \(n=4\) Cayley realization.
