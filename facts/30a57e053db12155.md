## Statement

For equal-volume convex bodies $A,B\subset\mathbb R^d$ of volume $a>0$ let $M=(A+B)/2$, $m=|M|$, $g=g_M$, $K=g+(a/m)^{1/d}(M-g)$ and $\Gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. The minimum $E=\min_{h\in\mathbb R^d}(|(A-h)\mathbin{\triangle}K|+|(B+h)\mathbin{\triangle}K|)/2$ exists. The infimum of the total closed cap volume of $A,B$ with common nonzero normal $u$ and thresholds $u\cdot g\pm t$ is at least $[2\Gamma_d a-E]_+$.

## Proof

Opposite translations $A-h,B+h$ preserve $M$ and the all-shift infimum because they replace the pair of shifts by $t+u\cdot h$ and its negative when the translated cuts are read back on $A,B$. For any halfspace $H$, the cap of $A-h$ has volume at least $|K\cap H|-|K\setminus(A-h)|$, with the analogous estimate for $B+h$. These losses are half the corresponding symmetric differences because $|A|=|B|=|K|=a$. The centroid of $K$ is $g$, so accepted fact 6a7d56e450b3126f applied to uniform volume on $K$ bounds the sum of its opposite-shift cap volumes below by $2\Gamma_d a$, with negative shifts handled by exchanging the terms. Apply this at every $u,t$ and choose $h$ minimizing the losses. The objective is continuous by continuity of translations in $L^1$, and it is the constant $2a$ outside a large ball because $A,B,K$ are bounded. It therefore attains a minimum, and nonnegative cap volume gives the positive part.
