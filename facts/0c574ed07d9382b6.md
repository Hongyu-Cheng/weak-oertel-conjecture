## Statement

Let $A,B$ be convex bodies in $\mathbb R^d$, $0<a=|A|\le b=|B|$, and fix $u\ne0$. Use the mass-$a$ coupling $\pi_u$ with marginal volumes $1_A\,dx,(a/b)1_B\,dx$ and comonotone $u$-projections, and let $\eta_u$ be its midpoint measure. For all $h,\tau\in\mathbb R$, write $U_A,U_B$ for actual cap volumes at thresholds $h+\tau,h-\tau$, and $p=U_A/a$, $q=U_B/b$, $m=\eta_u\{u\cdot x\ge h\}/a$. Then
$$
U_A+U_B=a m+(1-a/b)U_B+E,\qquad E=a(p+q-m)\ge0,
$$
where $E$ integrates a $\{0,1\}$-valued indicator difference. This identity includes saturated thresholds. Always $\min(p,q)\le m\le\max(p,q)$, and when all cuts are proper, $Q_A(p)+Q_B(q)=Q_A(m)+Q_B(m)=2h$.

## Proof

Use 486fe778f838d99d with coordinates whose first axis is $u/\|u\|$, weight $1/2$, and containing body $(A+B)/2$. Its transport proof sends uniform probability on $A$ to that on $B$, uses increasing first-coordinate quantile transport, and realizes the midpoint pushforward of that coupling. Multiply the coupling and midpoint law by $a$ to obtain the specified measures. After multiplying the first coordinate by $\|u\|$, their projected values have the form $Q_A(R),Q_B(R)$ for a shared uniform upper rank $R$, hence the midpoint projected upper quantile is $(Q_A+Q_B)/2$. The projected marginals are nonatomic by positive-dimensional Lebesgue volume, including the midpoint by the density bound.

On the product let $I_A,I_B,I_M$ indicate the two endpoint cuts and the midpoint cut, respectively. Both endpoint cuts imply the midpoint cut and two endpoint misses imply its miss, by the identity for the common thresholds. Hence $I_A+I_B-I_M\in\{0,1\}$ pointwise. Its integral is $U_A+(a/b)U_B-a m=a(p+q-m)$; adding the unused actual larger-endpoint term gives the formula. The endpoint events are initial intervals of the same rank up to null sets, including empty and full events. The midpoint event contains their intersection and lies inside their union. Their respective masses are $a\min(p,q)$ and $a\max(p,q)$, which proves the bounds on $m$. At proper thresholds the quantiles invert the three tail masses, giving the displayed sum identity. Each term of the exact accounting uses the same tilt $\tau$.
