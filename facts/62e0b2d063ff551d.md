## Statement

Let $B$ be a full-relative-rank hull of deepest source bases in a finite positive sampled-fiber family. Suppose their continuous marks and positive buffer profiles are $y(z)$ and $\ell(z)f_D$ at the source bases, where $y,\ell$ are affine, $\ell>0$, and $f_D$ is constant. Fix a mass-$Q$ closed cap $N\cdot(z,X)\ge b$ with $N=(a,u)$, $u\ne0$, and assume its summed upper tail is strictly decreasing at threshold $b$. Suppose each source has buffered depth at least $Q$ for the buffer $\varepsilon\ell(z)f_D\Gamma(u)$. The affine function
$$
\alpha(z)=N\cdot(z,y(z))-\varepsilon f_D\ell(z)\Gamma(u)-b
$$
is nonpositive on $B$. If it vanishes at an interior source or at every source in a positive combination with base in $\operatorname{relint}B$, it vanishes on the whole affine base span. For $X=y(z)+\ell(z)\xi$ the literal cap margin is then $\ell(z)[u\cdot\xi+\varepsilon f_D\Gamma(u)]$.

## Proof

At each source, the cap with the same normal through its buffered threshold has mass at least $Q$. If its threshold were greater than $b$, the monotonic upper tail and strict decrease near $b$ would instead give mass less than $Q$. Thus $\alpha\le0$ at every generating source and hence on $B$. In the combination case affine linearity gives an interior zero as well. A nonpositive affine function on $B$ with an interior zero has zero slope in every direction of $\operatorname{aff}B$ by the two-sided intervals about the zero. Full relative rank gives the assertion on the whole affine base span, and substituting the pointed coordinate gives the margin. At a boundary zero the same argument applies only along its minimal face, so that condition alone does not force propagation.
