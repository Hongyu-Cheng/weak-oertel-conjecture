## Statement

For $C=\operatorname{conv}\{(-1,0),(3,0),(0,5),(-1,2)\}$ in base and continuous coordinates $(z,x)$, the integer-fiber lengths at $-1,0,1,2,3$ are $2,5,10/3,5/3,0$. The unique weighted marginal median is zero. The midpoint of its actual fiber has normalized mixed depth at most $17/72<1/4$, witnessed by $x\ge z/2+5/2$.

## Proof

The polygon is bounded by $x=0$, the left edge $z=-1$, and the roof with $x=3z+5$ for $-1\le z\le0$ and $x=5-5z/3$ for $0\le z\le3$. Its interior is nonempty. Substituting the five bases gives the stated lengths and total $12$. The strict sides of zero have respective masses $2$ and $5$, each below $6$. For any real base to the left of zero its closed left mass is at most $2$, and for any real base to the right its closed right mass is at most $5$. Thus zero is the unique median, in particular among integer bases.

The cap boundary passes through $(0,5/2)$. At bases $-1,0,1,2,3$ its thresholds are $2,5/2,3,7/2,4$. The corresponding clipped lengths are $0,5/2,1/3,0,0$, including the null endpoint at $-1$. Their total is $17/6$ and normalization gives $17/72<18/72=1/4$.
