## Statement

Retain the common actual canonical hard instance and the notation of accepted fact 2c7ef442c71a1564, and assume \(n=3\). Thus \(d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^3\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body,
\[
 C_z:=\{y\in\mathbb R^d:(z,y)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^3:a_z>0\},
\]
\[
 W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
\]
and, for the common half-strength product-reservoir mass \(Q>0\),
\[
 G<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 W\geq8Q.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(q^*\ne0\) be the retained off-diagonal balanced optimizer from that fact, let \(T_*\) be its physical target set, and put
\[
 A:=\operatorname{aff}(T_*),
 \qquad
 D_A:=I_+\cap A,
 \qquad
 W_A:=\sum_{z\in D_A}a_z.
\tag{2}
\]
Assume, as in the affine-target-rank-two branch of accepted fact 2c7ef442c71a1564, that
\[
 \dim A=2,
 \qquad
 W_A\geq8|q^*|.
\tag{3}
\]

Then the tuple supplied by the planar extraction in accepted fact bd7eeefd1636ff7d consists of a finite nonempty \(E\subseteq D_A\), masses \(0<t_z\leq a_z\), and
\[
 m\in A\cap\mathbb Z^3\cap\operatorname{conv}E,
 \qquad
 \tau:=\sum_{z\in E}t_z\geq\frac{W_A}{32},
\tag{4}
\]
such that, with
\[
 \Lambda_m(E):=
 \left\{\lambda\in\mathbb R_+^E:
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z=1,
 \ \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_zz=m\right\},
\]
one has
\[
 \bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)}
 \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z)
 \ne\varnothing,
\tag{5}
\]
and every proper relative closed halfspace \(J_A\subseteq A\) containing \(m\) satisfies
\[
 \sum_{z\in E\cap J_A}t_z\geq\frac\tau4.
\tag{6}
\]
For this same tuple,
\[
 \boxed{
 G\geq2\tau\geq\frac{W_A}{16}\geq\frac{|q^*|}{2}.}
\tag{7}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact bd7eeefd1636ff7d, applied to the two-dimensional affine lattice section \(A\) in (2)-(3), gives exactly the tuple (4)-(6). Because \(n=3\) and \(\dim A=2\), the affine subspace \(A\subsetneq\mathbb R^3\) is proper. Apply the support-free complete-capacity conclusion of accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb to this tuple with relative balance \(\beta=1/4\). It gives
\[
 G\geq
 \min\left\{
 \tau+\frac{W-W_A}{2},
 \ 2^3\frac14\tau
 \right\}
 =
 \min\left\{
 \tau+\frac{W-W_A}{2},
 \ 2\tau
 \right\}.
\tag{8}
\]
By (4), \(0\leq W_A\leq W\), and (1), the first entry in (8) satisfies
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \tau+\frac{W-W_A}{2}
 &\geq\frac{W_A}{32}+\frac{W-W_A}{2}\\
 &=\frac W{32}+\frac{15}{32}(W-W_A)\\
 &\geq\frac W{32}
 \geq\frac Q4
 >G.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
Thus the first entry of the minimum in (8) cannot be the entry bounded above by \(G\). Consequently \(G\geq2\tau\). Combining this with (4) and then (3) gives
\[
 G\geq2\tau
 \geq\frac{W_A}{16}
 \geq\frac{8|q^*|}{16}
 =\frac{|q^*|}{2},
\]
which is (7).
