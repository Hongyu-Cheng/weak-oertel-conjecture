## Statement

In $\mathbb R^2$ put $v_1=(1,0)$, $v_2=(0,1)$, $v_3=(-1,-1)$ and $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$. Let $K$ be a triangle, and $A,B$ convex bodies with $B\subseteq T$ and $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$. Suppose for one $y$ all three lines $v_i\cdot x=2v_i\cdot y-1$ meet $\operatorname{int}A$. Write $a=|A|$, $k=|K|$, and $b=|B|$. Assume $h_K(-v_i)=1$ for all $i$, $a<b/32$ and $k\le a+b$. Let $\rho$ be the actual $B$-enclosure scale in the true facets of $K$, and put $h=2-\rho$ and $\lambda_i(x)=(v_i\cdot x+1)/3$. Either $K$ contains a vertex of $-T$, or its vertices can be labeled $P_i$ with $\lambda_i(P_i)=0$ and all off-diagonal $\lambda_i(P_j)>0$. In the second case, for $m=\min_{i\ne j}\lambda_i(P_j)$,
$$
m<\frac{(h-1)/h}{1-a/(h^2 k)}
 <\frac{32h(h-1)}{32h^2-1}<\frac{65}{4097}<\frac1{63}.
$$
In particular a vertex of $K$ on an outer edge has one outer barycentric coordinate below $1/63$ and another above $62/63$.

## Proof

The true-facet erosion and matched-support shape bound apply with the reference triangle $K$, unit scale and zero translation. In the original coordinates they give $A\subseteq p+hK$, $B\subseteq -p+\rho K$, $h=2-\rho$ and $0<\rho<1$. Since $b\le\rho^2 k<k$ and $a<b/32$, we have $a/k<1/32$. Also $k\le a+b<(33/32)b$, so $\rho>\sqrt{32/33}>63/64$. Hence $1<h<65/64$.

Each outer edge contacts $K$ because its support is matched. If no vertex of $-T$ lies in $K$, each vertex of $K$ can lie on at most one outer edge, and each of the three outer edges contains at least one vertex of $K$. Thus the contacts label the three vertices distinctly and all off-diagonal entries are positive.

For each $i$ select an actual point $a_i\in A$ strictly below its proper cut. Pull it back to $X_i=(a_i-p)/h\in K$ and let $\epsilon_i=(2v_i\cdot y-v_i\cdot p+h-1)/(3h)$. Then $0\le\lambda_i(X_i)<\epsilon_i$ and $\sum_i\epsilon_i=(h-1)/h$. Write $X_i=\sum_jQ_{ji}P_j$ with a nonnegative column-stochastic matrix $Q$. We have $m(1-Q_{ii})\le\lambda_i(X_i)<\epsilon_i$. Set $L=I-Q$. Its columns sum to zero, so $\det L=0$. Each principal two-by-two minor of $L$ is nonnegative because $1-Q_{ii}\ge Q_{ji}$ and $1-Q_{jj}\ge Q_{ij}$. In dimension three expansion of $\det(I-L)$ gives
$$
\det Q\ge 1-\operatorname{tr}L>1-\frac{\sum_i\epsilon_i}{m}.
$$
The triangle with vertices $X_i$ has area $|\det Q|k$ and lies in $(A-p)/h$. Consequently $a/(h^2k)\ge|\det Q|\ge\det Q$, proving the first strict bound. Substitution of $a/k<1/32$ gives the second. The function $32h(h-1)/(32h^2-1)$ increases for $h>1$ by differentiation, and at $65/64$ is $65/4097<1/63$. The two positive coordinates at a contact vertex sum to one.
