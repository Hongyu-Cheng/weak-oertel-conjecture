## Statement

Let a finite family of sampled positive fibers $K_z$ lie in $y(z)+\ell(z)D$ for one convex $d$-body $D$, $0\in\operatorname{int}D$, affine $y,\ell$ and $\ell>0$. Write $\xi=(x-y(z))/\ell(z)$. Suppose a finite actual cap group with weights $\omega_i$ has margins $\ell(z)(u_i\cdot\xi+b_i)$ whose cuts on $D$ are proper. On the physical outer normal-bundle contact $E=\{(n,z,x):n\cdot(x-y(z))=\ell(z)h_D(n)\}$ the combined actual boundary selector is surface-almost everywhere
$$
W(z,x)=w(\xi)=\sum_i\omega_i\mathbf1_{\{u_i\cdot\xi+b_i\ge0\}},
$$
for any allocations taking the ordinary indicator off the cutting planes.

## Proof

An affine hyperplane that crosses the interior of $D$ has a full-dimensional section in the hyperplane. Every relative interior point of that section is in $\operatorname{int}D$: a supporting linear functional at it would have to be constant on the entire section hyperplane, which would make that plane a supporting plane and contradict proper crossing. Thus the cutting plane meets $\partial D$ only in the relative boundary of its section, which is $\mathcal H^{d-1}$-null. For $d=1$ the cutting point is interior and the intersection is empty. Any physical contact in $E$ lies in the homothetic image of $\partial D$, so for each fiber and cap its contact on the cutting plane is surface-null. The finite union of these coincidences is null for the summed fiber boundary measures. Off this union the selector equals the literal indicator, giving the result.
