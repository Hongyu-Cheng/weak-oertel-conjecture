## Statement

Let $K=\{x\in\mathbb R^5:x_j\ge0,\ \sum_jx_j\le1\}$, $C=[-1,1]\times K$, and $H=\{(z,x):x_1\le3/100-z/100\}$. For each integer fiber $z=-1,0,1$ let $c_z$ be the cap volume and $S_z$ the Euclidean four-volume of the section by $\partial H$. With $t_z=(97+z)/100$ these are $c_z=(1-t_z^5)/120$ and $S_z=t_z^4/24$. The normalized mixed volume fraction in $H$ is $1406574933/10^{10}<\frac12(5/6)^5$. At the prescribed multiplier $1/2$, the weights $W_z=c_z+S_z/2$ violate log-concavity and every positive-power midpoint concavity inequality at $z=0$.

## Proof

The three integer fibers are copies of the standard simplex of five-volume $1/120$, hence the mixed five-volume is $1/40$. The complementary open cap $x_1>1-t_z$ differs by a null slice from a simplex of scale $t_z$ about $e_1$. The section at equality is a copy of the standard four-simplex scaled by $t_z$ in the Euclidean hyperplane with unit normal $e_1$. These give the claimed cap and section volumes, with all cuts proper. The mixed fraction is $1-[(96/100)^5+(97/100)^5+(98/100)^5]/3=1406574933/10^{10}$. Its gap below the stated budget is $3125/15552-1406574933/10^{10}=146483541281/2430000000000>0$.

Up to the common volume factor $1/120$, the three weights are samples of $h(t)=1-t^5+(5/2)t^4>0$ at $t=96/100,97/100,98/100$. Differentiating gives
$$
hh''-(h')^2=5t^2(1-t)(1+t)[(t-1)^4+5]>0 \qquad (0<t<1).
$$
Thus $\log h$ is strictly convex on the interval of all proper cuts, and the sampled product gap $h(96/100)h(98/100)-h(97/100)^2$ is the positive rational $13912864519608719/10^{20}$. For every $a>0$, the second derivative of $h^a$ is $a h^a[(\log h)''+a((\log h)')^2]>0$. The center sample of $h^a$ is therefore strictly less than the average of the two neighbors, and the same holds for the actual weights after multiplying by $(1/120)^a$. The calculation uses a prescribed coefficient with unit-normal Euclidean sections.
