## Statement

For every integer $N\ge3$ and $0<\varepsilon\le1/100$, let
$$
T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,e_2),\quad g=(1/3,1/3),\quad E=\operatorname{conv}(0,g+\varepsilon e_1,g+\varepsilon e_2),\quad e=\varepsilon(2/3+\varepsilon),
$$
and $C=\operatorname{conv}(([0,N-1]\times T)\cup(\{N\}\times E))$. Its integer fibers are $N$ copies of $T$ followed by $E$, each of positive area, and the point $p=g-\frac{e}{4N}(1,1)$ is interior to every real fiber over $[0,N]$. The continuous-coordinate projection of summed mixed area has density $\rho=N\mathbf1_T+\mathbf1_E$. Its maximum ordinary closed-halfspace depth $D(\rho)$, normalized by its total mass, satisfies
$$
\frac{(4/9)N}{N+e}\le D(\rho)\le \frac49-\frac{4447}{57600}\frac{e}{N+e}<\frac49.
$$

## Proof

Put $b=1/3$, $c=2/3$, $\theta=e/(4N)$, and $V=|T|=1/2$. The nonzero vertices of $E$ have nonnegative coordinates and coordinate sum $c+\varepsilon<1$, so $E\subset T$. Their determinant is $(b+\varepsilon)^2-b^2=e$, hence $|E|=eV$. Up to base $N-1$ the product generator gives the entire $T$ fiber, which contains all generators in continuous coordinates. At the exposed last base the fiber is $E$. Every real fiber in the range contains $E$, by interpolating two identical copies of each point of $E$ at the extreme bases. The two non-apex barycentric coordinates of $p$ in $E$ equal $(b-\theta)/(c+\varepsilon)$, which is positive, and their sum $(c-2\theta)/(c+\varepsilon)$ is less than one. Thus $p$ is interior to $E$ and every fiber. The projection counts the integer fibers, giving the stated density and total $(N+e)V$.

The caps $H_0=\{1-x_1-x_2\ge b+2\theta\}$ and $H_i=\{x_i\ge b-\theta\}$ for $i=1,2$ cover $\mathbb R^2$ because the three barycentric coordinates sum to one and so do the thresholds. Each boundary passes through $p$. Dividing their projected masses by $V$ gives
$$
B_0=N(c-2\theta)^2+e\left(\frac{c-2\theta}{c+\varepsilon}\right)^2,\qquad
B_i=N(c+\theta)^2+e\left(1-\frac{(b-\theta)^2}{b(b+\varepsilon)}\right)\quad(i=1,2).
$$
The first $E$ trace is the homothety about zero with ratio $(c-2\theta)/(c+\varepsilon)$. The complement of each other trace in $E$ is a triangle with edge ratios $(b-\theta)/b$ and $(b-\theta)/(b+\varepsilon)$ on the edges from zero. Endpoints and cut lines have zero area. On $T$ these are the usual barycentric vertex caps with area fractions equal to the squares of their homothety ratios.

Using $4N\theta=e$, the first mass is at most $Nc^2+e(1/3+\theta)$. For the others the $E$ fraction is at most $3\varepsilon+6\theta$, since its numerator is $b\varepsilon+2b\theta-\theta^2$ over denominator $b(b+\varepsilon)$. Thus $B_i\le Nc^2+e(1/3+3\varepsilon+25\theta/4)$. As $N\ge3$ and $c+\varepsilon<3/4$, we have $\theta\le\varepsilon/16$, so all three masses are at most
$$
\frac49 N+e\left(\frac13+\frac{217}{6400}\right)=\frac49(N+e)-\frac{4447}{57600}e.
$$
Every point is in a cap of this cover, proving the normalized upper bound. At $g$, each of the $N$ original triangle copies supplies at least $(4/9)V$ to every containing planar halfspace by 53fe12b742a7608d for the centroid of the full-dimensional simplex $T$. This gives the projected lower bound.
