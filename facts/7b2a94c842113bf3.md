## Statement

Retain the canonical objects and normalization of accepted facts 036a276c54ec5e66 and 084a2e888b32fd2d. In particular, \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body,
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
and \(\mu_C\) is the mixed-volume measure of \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\). For every positive-load reservoir edge \(e=\{u,v\}\), write
\[
 \omega_e>0,
 \qquad
 m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2}\in\mathbb Z^n,
 \qquad
 \ell_e:=\frac{x_{e,u}+x_{e,v}}2,
\]
where
\[
 x_{e,u}\in\mathsf M_{a_u/2}(C_u),
 \qquad
 x_{e,v}\in\mathsf M_{a_v/2}(C_v),
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)>0.
\]
Let
\[
 F_+:=\{e:\omega_e>0\},
 \qquad
 Z_+:=\{z:z\in e\text{ for some }e\in F_+\}.
\]
Every \(z\in Z_+\) has \(a_z>0\). The canonical quarter-degree reservoir gives
\[
 \sum_{\substack{e\in F_+\\z\in e}}\omega_e
 \leq \frac{a_z}{4}
 \qquad(z\in Z_+).
\tag{1}
\]

For every \(e=\{u,v\}\in F_+\), let
\[
 \beta_e\geq0,
 \qquad
 b_{e,u}\geq0,
 \qquad
 b_{e,v}\geq0
\]
satisfy the positive-load per-edge ownership condition
\[
 \boxed{\beta_e+b_{e,u}+b_{e,v}\leq\omega_e.}
\tag{2}
\]
Define
\[
 \nu_{\beta,b}:=
 2\gamma_d\left[
 \sum_{e\in F_+}\beta_e\delta_{(m_e,\ell_e)}
 +\sum_{e=\{u,v\}\in F_+}
 \left(
 b_{e,u}\delta_{(u,x_{e,u})}
 +b_{e,v}\delta_{(v,x_{e,v})}
 \right)
 \right].
\tag{3}
\]
Then \(\nu_{\beta,b}\) is carried by
\[
 C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d),
\]
has mass
\[
 |\nu_{\beta,b}|=
 2\gamma_d\sum_{e=\{u,v\}\in F_+}
 (\beta_e+b_{e,u}+b_{e,v}),
\tag{4}
\]
and satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\nu_{\beta,b}(H)\leq\mu_C(H)}
\tag{5}
\]
for every closed ambient halfspace \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\). This includes boundary atoms, repeated physical endpoint contacts, coincident midpoint atoms, and zero charges.

We now state the three canonical specializations. To avoid conflict with the total-mass notation in 036a276c54ec5e66, write \(W_{\mathrm{ch}}\) for the linear subspace denoted by \(W\) in 084a2e888b32fd2d. Retain its maximum-weight chartable family, affine extension \(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\), discarded family
\[
 \mathscr D_o=\mathscr D_{\mathrm{mid}}\mathbin{\dot\cup}\mathscr D_{\mathrm{ctr}},
\]
and, for every \(\sigma=(z_\sigma;e,f)\in\mathscr D_o\), its one selected edge
\[
 j_\sigma=\{z_\sigma,y_\sigma\}.
\]
The predecessor defines
\[
 q_e^{\mathrm{mid}}
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr D_{\mathrm{mid}}\\j_\sigma=e}}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 q_e^{\mathrm{ctr}}
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr D_{\mathrm{ctr}}\\j_\sigma=e}}\eta_\sigma,
\tag{6}
\]
and proves
\[
 q_e^{\mathrm{mid}}+q_e^{\mathrm{ctr}}\leq\omega_e,
 \qquad
 \sum_eq_e^{\mathrm{mid}}+\sum_eq_e^{\mathrm{ctr}}=M_o.
\tag{7}
\]
Put
\[
 B_{\mathrm{mid}}:=\sum_eq_e^{\mathrm{mid}},
 \qquad
 B_{\mathrm{ctr}}:=\sum_eq_e^{\mathrm{ctr}}.
\tag{8}
\]
For an incidence \((e,z)\), define the central-incidence charge
\[
 b_{e,z}^{\mathrm{ctr}}
 :=\sum_{\substack{
 \sigma\in\mathscr D_{\mathrm{ctr}}\\
 j_\sigma=e,\ z_\sigma=z}}
 \eta_\sigma.
\tag{9}
\]
Thus, for \(e=\{u,v\}\),
\[
 b_{e,u}^{\mathrm{ctr}}+b_{e,v}^{\mathrm{ctr}}=q_e^{\mathrm{ctr}}.
\tag{10}
\]

The separate midpoint measure is
\[
 \nu_{\mathrm{mid}}:=
 2\gamma_d\sum_eq_e^{\mathrm{mid}}\delta_{(m_e,\ell_e)}.
\tag{11}
\]
It is carried by the mixed lattice section, is halfspace-dominated by \(\mu_C\), has mass
\[
 |\nu_{\mathrm{mid}}|=2\gamma_dB_{\mathrm{mid}},
\tag{12}
\]
and preserves the exact original bad ancestry:
\[
 q_e^{\mathrm{mid}}>0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \ell_e-s(m_e)\notin W_{\mathrm{ch}}.
\tag{13}
\]

The separate central-incidence measure is
\[
 \nu_{\mathrm{ctr}}:=
 2\gamma_d
 \sum_{e=\{u,v\}}
 \left(
 b_{e,u}^{\mathrm{ctr}}\delta_{(u,x_{e,u})}
 +b_{e,v}^{\mathrm{ctr}}\delta_{(v,x_{e,v})}
 \right).
\tag{14}
\]
It is carried by the mixed lattice section, is halfspace-dominated by \(\mu_C\), has mass
\[
 |\nu_{\mathrm{ctr}}|=2\gamma_dB_{\mathrm{ctr}},
\tag{15}
\]
and preserves the exact original central bad ancestry:
\[
 b_{e,z}^{\mathrm{ctr}}>0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 x_{e,z}-s(z)\notin W_{\mathrm{ch}}.
\tag{16}
\]

The combined original-ancestry measure is
\[
 \nu_o:=\nu_{\mathrm{mid}}+\nu_{\mathrm{ctr}}.
\tag{17}
\]
It is carried by the mixed lattice section, is halfspace-dominated by \(\mu_C\), and has mass
\[
 \boxed{|\nu_o|=2\gamma_dM_o.}
\tag{18}
\]
Every \((z,x)\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_o\) satisfies
\[
 \boxed{x-s(z)\notin W_{\mathrm{ch}}.}
\tag{19}
\]

For \(*\in\{\mathrm{mid},\mathrm{ctr},o\}\), put \(B_o:=M_o\). Only when \(B_*>0\), define the nonempty intrinsic support and its ranks by
\[
 P_*:=\operatorname{supp}\nu_*,
 \qquad
 A_*:=\operatorname{aff}
 \operatorname{proj}_{\mathbb R^n}(P_*),
 \qquad
 \rho_*:=\dim A_*,
\tag{20}
\]
\[
 r_*:=
 \min_{a:A_*\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{x-a(z):(z,x)\in P_*\}.
\tag{21}
\]
Equivalently, by the coordinate-free intrinsic-rank identity of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483,
\[
 r_*=\dim\operatorname{aff}(P_*)-\rho_*.
\tag{21a}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 D\geq
 2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_*}
 \frac{B_*}{r_*+1}}
 \qquad
 (*\in\{\mathrm{mid},\mathrm{ctr},o\},\ B_*>0).
\tag{22}
\]
These are intrinsic support ranks, so coincident atoms are aggregated before the ranks are computed, while their masses remain summed. Formula (22) includes \(\rho_*=0\) and \(r_*=0\). If \(B_*=0\), then \(\nu_*=0\); no support rank is assigned, and the corresponding conclusion is only the tautological \(D\geq0\).

## Proof

Accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 supplies, for every \(e=\{u,v\}\in F_+\), fixed half-mass representing densities
\[
 0\leq f_{e,u}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_u}f_{e,u}=\frac{a_u}{2},
 \qquad
 \frac2{a_u}\int_{C_u}x f_{e,u}(x)\,dx=x_{e,u},
\tag{23}
\]
and analogously at \(v\). It also gives \(0<\omega_e\leq r_e\) and the canonical reservoir degree bound
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}\omega_e
 \leq\sum_{e\ni z}r_e
 \leq\frac{a_z}{4},
\tag{24}
\]
which is (1).

We first prove carriedness. Each endpoint contact \(x_{e,z}\) belongs to \(\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\subseteq C_z\), the inclusion following also from accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d. Hence every endpoint atom in (3) belongs to the mixed lattice section. Moreover,
\[
 (m_e,\ell_e)
 =\frac12(u,x_{e,u})+\frac12(v,x_{e,v})\in C
\tag{25}
\]
by convexity. Every reservoir edge is same-parity, so \(m_e\in\mathbb Z^n\). Thus every midpoint atom also belongs to the mixed lattice section. Summing its displayed atomic coefficients proves (4).

Fix a closed ambient halfspace \(H\). Whenever \((m_e,\ell_e)\in H\), at least one of \((u,x_{e,u})\) and \((v,x_{e,v})\) belongs to \(H\), because (25) is their midpoint and the complement of a closed halfspace is convex. Choose exactly one such endpoint and orient this occurrence of the midpoint charge toward it. For \(z\in e\), let \(\varepsilon_{e,z}(H)\in\{0,1\}\) record that orientation, so
\[
 \varepsilon_{e,u}(H)+\varepsilon_{e,v}(H)
 =\mathbf1_H(m_e,\ell_e).
\tag{26}
\]
Define the charge assigned from \(e\) to \(z\) by
\[
 w_{e,z}(H)
 :=\beta_e\varepsilon_{e,z}(H)
 +b_{e,z}\mathbf1_H(z,x_{e,z}),
\tag{27}
\]
and put
\[
 R_z(H):=\sum_{\substack{e\in F_+\\z\in e}}w_{e,z}(H)
 \qquad(z\in Z_+).
\tag{28}
\]
Every included midpoint charge is assigned exactly once, and every included endpoint-incidence charge is assigned to its own physical endpoint. Therefore
\[
 \nu_{\beta,b}(H)=2\gamma_d\sum_{z\in Z_+}R_z(H).
\tag{29}
\]

For \(e=\{u,v\}\) and \(z\in e\), equations (2) and (27) give
\[
 0\leq w_{e,z}(H)
 \leq\beta_e+b_{e,z}
 \leq\beta_e+b_{e,u}+b_{e,v}
 \leq\omega_e.
\tag{30}
\]
Consequently, (24) gives the quarter-degree budget
\[
 0\leq R_z(H)\leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{31}
\]

For \(z\in Z_+\), aggregate on \(C_z\) the already fixed original-contact witnesses by
\[
 F_z^H(x):=
 \sum_{\substack{e\in F_+\\z\in e}}
 \frac{2w_{e,z}(H)}{a_z}f_{e,z}(x).
\tag{32}
\]
Equations (23) and (31) give
\[
 \int_{C_z}F_z^H=R_z(H),
 \qquad
 0\leq F_z^H\leq
 \frac{2R_z(H)}{a_z}\leq\frac12.
\tag{33}
\]
This is the density-reuse audit. If the midpoint and endpoint channels both use the same incidence \((e,z)\), its one witness \(f_{e,z}\) appears only once in (32), scaled by their total assigned charge. Charges from different edges may reuse the same physical fiber or even the same contact, but (24) controls their pointwise sum. Condition (2) charges all three channels to the one positive load \(\omega_e\), so no edge or occurrence budget is duplicated.

If \(R_z(H)=0\), define no barycenter and make no metronoid assertion at that source. Suppose \(R_z(H)>0\). Then
\[
 \bar x_z^H
 :=\frac1{R_z(H)}\int_{C_z}xF_z^H(x)\,dx
 =\frac1{R_z(H)}
 \sum_{e\ni z}w_{e,z}(H)x_{e,z}.
\tag{34}
\]
Every contact with positive coefficient in (34) lies in the fiber section \(H_z\): it was either selected by the midpoint orientation or counted by the endpoint indicator in (27). Hence \(\bar x_z^H\in H_z\). The density \(2F_z^H\) is admissible, has mass \(2R_z(H)\leq a_z/2\), and has barycenter \(\bar x_z^H\). Thus
\[
 \bar x_z^H\in\mathsf M_{2R_z(H)}(C_z).
\tag{35}
\]
If \(H_z\) is a proper closed halfspace, accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d applied to (35) gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z)
 \geq2\gamma_dR_z(H).
\tag{36}
\]
If \(H_z=\mathbb R^d\), the same inequality follows from \(a_z\geq4R_z(H)\) and \(\gamma_d\leq1\). A section with \(R_z(H)>0\) cannot be empty. Thus (36) holds for every contributing base, including ambient halfspaces with zero continuous normal and atoms on the boundary. Summing (36), then using (29), proves
\[
 \mu_C(H)
 \geq2\gamma_d\sum_{z\in Z_+}R_z(H)
 =\nu_{\beta,b}(H),
\]
which is (5).

We apply the hybrid lemma to the canonical charges. Accepted fact 084a2e888b32fd2d proves (6), (7), and the one-edge, one-label nonduplication that underlies them. For the midpoint specialization, take
\[
 \beta_e=q_e^{\mathrm{mid}},
 \qquad
 b_{e,u}=b_{e,v}=0.
\]
For the central-incidence specialization, take
\[
 \beta_e=0,
 \qquad
 b_{e,z}=b_{e,z}^{\mathrm{ctr}}.
\]
For the combined specialization, take both choices simultaneously. Equation (10) and the first equation in (7) show in the combined case that
\[
 \beta_e+b_{e,u}+b_{e,v}
 =q_e^{\mathrm{mid}}+q_e^{\mathrm{ctr}}
 \leq\omega_e.
\tag{37}
\]
Thus (2) holds for all three choices. Equations (11), (14), and (17), their carriedness, their halfspace domination, and their masses now follow from (3)-(5), (7), and (8).

It remains to verify the support assertions. If \(q_e^{\mathrm{mid}}>0\), some \(\sigma\in\mathscr D_{\mathrm{mid}}\) has \(j_\sigma=e\). The defining assertion for \(\mathscr D_{\mathrm{mid}}\) in accepted fact 084a2e888b32fd2d is exactly
\[
 \ell_{j_\sigma}-s(m_{j_\sigma})\notin W_{\mathrm{ch}},
\]
which proves (13). If \(b_{e,z}^{\mathrm{ctr}}>0\), some \(\sigma\in\mathscr D_{\mathrm{ctr}}\) has \(j_\sigma=e\) and common endpoint \(z_\sigma=z\). The exact bad-ancestry conclusion of the same accepted fact is
\[
 x_{j_\sigma,z_\sigma}-s(z_\sigma)\notin W_{\mathrm{ch}},
\]
which proves (16). Every positive atom of \(\nu_o\) comes from one of these two cases. If several terms coincide as one physical atom, their base and continuous coordinates coincide as well, so the same residual is outside \(W_{\mathrm{ch}}\). This proves (19), including all collisions.

Finally fix \(*\in\{\mathrm{mid},\mathrm{ctr},o\}\) with \(B_*>0\). Then \(\nu_*\) is a nonzero positive finite measure carried by the mixed lattice section and halfspace-dominated by \(\mu_C\). Its mass is \(2\gamma_dB_*\), and (20)-(21) are exactly the intrinsic base and continuous residual ranks of the dominated-measure residual theorem in accepted fact be359f8a6c628483. That theorem, including its rank-zero and coincident-atom cases, gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)
 \geq\frac{2\gamma_dB_*}{2^{\rho_*}(r_*+1)}.
\]
Multiplying by \(2^n\) proves (22). When \(B_*=0\), the corresponding displayed measure is the zero measure, so the nonzero-measure theorem is not invoked and no rank of an empty support is introduced. This completes every claimed specialization and boundary case.
