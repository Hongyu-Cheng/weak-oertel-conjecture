## Statement

Place the triangles $Q_{0,\varepsilon}=\{(t,s):-1\le t\le1,\ |s|\le\varepsilon(t+1)/2\}$ and $Q_{1,\varepsilon}=\{(s,t):(t,s)\in Q_{0,\varepsilon}\}$ at integer bases $0,1$, respectively, and take their convex hull, with $\varepsilon>0$. Each original normalized mixed law has maximum closed-halfspace depth $2/9$. Its weak limit has density $(t+1)/4$ on each of the two perpendicular support segments, and maximum depth $1/4$. The explicit compatible intrinsic ball lift obtained by giving each segment a shared orthogonal hidden coordinate with radius $(t+1)/8$ has maximum depth $2/9$. Thus the projection can strictly increase maximum depth, and the original maxima need not converge to the weak law's maximum.

## Proof

The integer fiber census of the hull is precisely the two supplied triangles, since its base projection is $[0,1]$ and the endpoint sections of a hull over those two bases are exactly the supplied fibers. Both areas are $2\varepsilon$. At each coordinate $t$, the normalized section length is $\varepsilon(t+1)/(4\varepsilon)=(t+1)/4$. For a continuous test on the common bounded region for $0<\varepsilon\le1$, replacing the thin coordinate by zero changes its values uniformly by a quantity tending to zero. Integrating the normalized sections proves the claimed weak limit, with total mass $1/2$ on each segment.

In any of these three models each fiber can be isolated in a cap through a chosen mark. A continuous-coordinate affine functional through the mark in the affine hull of its own fiber extends to the continuous ambient space. Add a multiple of $z-j$ with sign and sufficiently large magnitude to make the margin strictly negative on the other bounded fiber. Conversely every ambient cap through the mark restricts to a containing closed halfspace or the entire own fiber. This proves that mixed depth at a mark equals the mass of that fiber times its conditional ordinary or intrinsic depth.

The prelimit conditional laws are uniform on full planar triangles, so 03bc771bddd39a8a in dimension two gives maximum $4/9$, hence mixed maximum $2/9$. For the weak law on either segment, its conditional CDF is $(t+1)^2/4$, so the median is $\sqrt2-1$. At that point each containing ray or full line captures at least half of the conditional law, whereas at every point at least one of the two rays through it captures at most half. The mixed maximum is therefore $1/4$. In the lift the hidden radius $(t+1)/8$ gives projected line density $(t+1)/4$ by doubling the radius. Each lifted fiber is an intrinsic two-dimensional triangle of area $1/2$ in its own plane, and it pushes forward exactly to its segment law. With only the two bases, compatibility and the exact census of the lift follow from the endpoint construction. Using the isometric planar chart for each triangle, 03bc771bddd39a8a again gives conditional maximum $4/9$ and mixed maximum $2/9$. The gain is $1/36$.
