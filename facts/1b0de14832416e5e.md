## Statement

There is a volume-one regular six-dimensional simplex $K$ and a measure $\eta$ supported in $K$ with mass $a=100^{-6}$ and centroid equal to the centroid $g$ of $K$. For $b=k=1$ and $v_*=((a^{1/6}+b^{1/6})/2)^6=(101/200)^6$, it satisfies $\eta\le(a/v_*)\mathbf1_K\,dx\le\mathbf1_K\,dx$. However, the supremum of ambient continuous halfspace depths for $\nu=\mathbf1_K\,dx+\eta$, in raw mass, is strictly less than $(\Gamma_6/2)(k+a+b)$, with $\Gamma_6=(6/7)^6$. Every measure $\mathbf1_K\,dx+c\eta$ for $c\ge0$ has centroid $g$.

## Proof

Write $P_i$ for the seven vertices and $\lambda_i$ for their barycentric coordinates. For $i=1,\ldots,7$ let $T_i=P_i+(3/8)(K-P_i)=K\cap\{\lambda_i\ge5/8\}$. The corners are disjoint because two barycentric coordinates cannot both be at least $5/8$. Their union $U$ has volume $w=7(3/8)^6$. Put $\eta=(a/w)\mathbf1_U\,dx$. Its mass is $a$, and vertex-permutation symmetry forces its centroid to be $g$, as for $\mathbf1_K\,dx$. Thus each stated mixture also has that centroid. The density ceiling follows from $w-v_*=92164729387/32000000000000>0$ and $a\le v_*$.

Let $H_i=\{\lambda_i\ge1/7\}$, extending the affine barycentric coordinates to the ambient space. Their union covers $K$ because the seven coordinates sum to one. Each $K\cap H_i$ is a copy scaled by $6/7$ about $P_i$, so its volume is $\Gamma_6$. The corner $T_i$ is wholly captured. On $T_j$ for $j\ne i$, the pullback of $\lambda_i$ to $K$ under the homothety about $P_j$ is $(3/8)\lambda_i$. Thus the captured fraction of $T_j$ is $(1-8/21)^6=(13/21)^6$. Every $H_i$ consequently has $\nu$-mass $\Gamma_6+a c_6$, where
$$
c_6=\frac{1+6(13/21)^6}{7}=\frac{38242325}{200120949},\qquad
\frac{\Gamma_6}{2}-c_6=\frac{1438603}{200120949}>0.
$$
For every point in $K$ some $H_i$ is a containing halfspace of that mass. For a point outside $K$ strict separation gives a containing closed halfspace of zero mass. Hence the supremum of the depths is at most $\Gamma_6+a c_6 < (\Gamma_6/2)(2+a)$, as required.
