## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $m=d+1$, and let $v_0,\ldots,v_d$ span $\mathbb R^d$ with sole dependence generated by $\sum_i v_i=0$. Put $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$. Let $A$ be a convex body. If each closed corner $C_j=A\cap\bigcap_{i\ne j}\{v_i\cdot x\le-1\}$ is nonempty, choose any $P_j\in C_j$. Then
$$
-T\subseteq\operatorname{conv}\{P_0,\ldots,P_d\}\subseteq A.
$$
In particular the same set containment holds when all the strict residual regions are nonempty.

## Proof

Use affine coordinates $\lambda_i(x)=(1+v_i\cdot x)/m$. They have sum one and identify $-T$ with the nonnegative coordinate simplex. Let $C$ have columns $\lambda(P_j)$. Its diagonal entries are at least one, off-diagonal entries are nonpositive, and every column sum is one. Writing $C=D-N$ with positive diagonal $D$ and entrywise nonnegative $N$, the column sums imply $\|ND^{-1}\|_1<1$. Thus $C^{-1}=D^{-1}\sum_{q\ge0}(ND^{-1})^q$ is entrywise nonnegative. Also $\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}C^{-1}=\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}$. For any coordinate vector $\lambda\ge0$ of sum one, $C^{-1}\lambda$ is a convex combination vector and maps back to $\lambda$. This gives the first inclusion, and convexity of $A$ gives the second.
