## Statement

Let $dm$ be the sum of Lebesgue volume on the finitely many sampled positive fibers of a compact canonical hull $C$, with $M=\int dm>0$. Let $L$ be continuous and strictly positive on the compact hull. For finitely many positive affine charts $W_Q$ and constants $b_Q>0$, put $G_Q=b_QW_Q$ and $U=\min_Q G_Q$. Let nonnegative source weights $\sigma_i$ at marks $Y_i$ be assigned once each to groups $Q$. Assume finite nonnegative whole-row measures $\eta_Q=L\nu_Q$ satisfy $\sum_Q\eta_Q=dm/M$ and
$$
s_Q=\eta_Q(C)=\sum_{i\ {\rm assigned\ to}\ Q}\sigma_i,\qquad
\int (W_Q/L)\,d\eta_Q=s_Q.
$$ Let $\Psi(z,x)=[E_{C_z}(x)|C_z|^{1/d}]^{1/(d+1)}$ with ordinary projective energy, and assume $U\ge\Psi$ and $\Psi(Y_i)/L(Y_i)=b_Q$ on the sources of $Q$. Set $\widehat\eta_Q=W_Q\nu_Q$ and, for $A=U,\Psi$, set
$$
\Delta_A=\sum_{i:\sigma_i>0}\sigma_i\log\!\frac{\Psi(Y_i)}{L(Y_i)}
-\int\log(A/L)\,\frac{dm}{M}.
$$
For equal-mass measures use $D(\eta\|\widehat\eta)=\int\log(d\eta/d\widehat\eta)\,d\eta$, with zero for a zero row. Then the following finite nonnegative slacks satisfy
$$
\Delta_U=\sum_Q D(\eta_Q\|\widehat\eta_Q)+\sum_Q\int\log(G_Q/U)\,d\eta_Q,\qquad
\Delta_\Psi=\Delta_U+\int\log(U/\Psi)\,\frac{dm}{M}.
$$
Equality in $\Delta_U\ge0$ holds exactly when $W_Q=L$ and $G_Q=U$ $\eta_Q$-almost everywhere for every $Q$. Equality in $\Delta_\Psi\ge0$ also requires $U=\Psi$ $dm$-almost everywhere.

## Proof

For the chart ratio $g_Q=W_Q/L$, the hypotheses give $\widehat\eta_Q=g_Q\eta_Q$ and $\int g_Q\,d\eta_Q=s_Q$. One sufficient way to check this last hypothesis for supplied old whole rows is as follows. If $\nu_i$ has mass $m_i=\sigma_i/L(Y_i)$, barycenter $Y_i$, and $\int L\,d\nu_i=\sigma_i$, then grouping $\nu_Q=\sum_{i\ {\rm assigned\ to}\ Q}\nu_i$ gives $\eta_Q(C)=s_Q$. Affinity and $W_Q(Y_i)=L(Y_i)$ give $\int W_Q\,d\nu_i=m_iW_Q(Y_i)=\sigma_i$. If the original $\nu_i$ sum to $dm/(ML)$, the grouped $\eta_Q$ sum to $dm/M$. This calculation groups each supplied row whole and allows overlapping row supports.

Because $\int\log g_Q\,d\eta_Q=-D(\eta_Q\|\widehat\eta_Q)$ and $\log(G_Q/L)=\log b_Q+\log g_Q$,
$$
\int\log(U/L)\,d\eta_Q
=s_Q\log b_Q-D(\eta_Q\|\widehat\eta_Q)-\int\log(G_Q/U)\,d\eta_Q.
$$
Summing, $s_Q\log b_Q$ sums to the asserted source term, and the left side uses exactly $dm/M$. Subtracting the two definitions of slack gives the displayed $\Delta_\Psi$ identity.

Strict positivity and continuity bound $\log g_Q$ and $\log(G_Q/U)$ on the compact hull. For a sampled positive fiber $K$ and own-interior $x$, the ball centered at $x$ of radius $\delta(x)=\operatorname{dist}(x,\partial K)$ lies in $K$. Fact d90ba05577427854 gives $E_K(x)\ge\omega_d\delta(x)^d$ by energy monotonicity and the centered ball energy, and $E_K(x)\le |K|$. Fix an inball of radius $r>0$ centered at $o$. For $0<t<r$, the set $o+(1-t/r)(K-o)$ lies in the closed inner set at distance at least $t$ from the boundary: each of its points is the center of a ball of radius $t$ in $K$ by convexity. The open collar of width $t$ thus has volume at most $|K|[1-(1-t/r)^d]=O(t)$. This implies integrability of $\log\delta$ by its negative-tail integral. Consequently $\log\Psi$ is integrable on each sampled fiber and on their finite union. Boundary values of $\Psi$ at null sets do not enter the integrals.

For $s_Q>0$, Jensen for $\log$ against $\eta_Q/s_Q$ gives $D(\eta_Q\|\widehat\eta_Q)\ge0$, with equality exactly when $g_Q=1$ $\eta_Q$-almost everywhere. Zero rows contribute zero. The other integrands are nonnegative by $G_Q\ge U\ge\Psi$, so equality in each slack has precisely the stated conditions.
