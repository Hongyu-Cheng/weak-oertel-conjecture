## Statement

Let $F$ be a compact convex $m$-body in its affine span and $x_0\in\operatorname{relint}F$. For $m>0$, take its actual projective-energy minimizer $a$ and denominator $L(x)=1+a\cdot(x-x_0)>0$, with $E=E_F(x_0)=\int_F L^{-m-1}\,dx$. Let $\sigma=\mathbf1_F L^{-m-2}\,dx/E$ be its escort probability. Define $T(x)=(x-x_0)/L(x)$, $D=T(F)$ and $\ell(y)=1-a\cdot y$. The set $D$ is a convex body of volume $E$ and centroid zero. The escort pushforward is the affine density $\mathbf1_D\ell(y)\,dy/E$, which is the projection of normalized volume on the compact convex $(m+1)$-body
$$
B=\{(y,s):y\in D,\ 0\le s\le\ell(y)\}.
$$
If $\bar y$ is the $D$-coordinate of the centroid of $B$, the point $x_*=x_0+\bar y/(1-a\cdot\bar y)$ lies in $F$ and every closed physical halfspace containing it has $\sigma$-mass at least $\Gamma_{m+1}=((m+1)/(m+2))^{m+1}$. For $m=0$ the escort is a point mass and the corresponding bound is one. If the face has codimension at least two in a $d$-body, the lift dimension for $m>0$ is at most $d-1$.

## Proof

Use intrinsic coordinates in $F-x_0$. Fact d90ba05577427854 gives the positive minimizing denominator and the identities
$$
\int_F(x-x_0)L(x)^{-m-2}\,dx=0,\qquad \int_F L(x)^{-m-2}\,dx=E.
$$
The inverse of the projective chart on $D$ is $y\mapsto x_0+y/(1-a\cdot y)$, with $\ell(y)=1/L(x)>0$. A segment in $F$ maps onto the segment of its endpoint images by reweighting the convex combination with the two positive endpoint denominators. The image is compact and convex with nonempty intrinsic interior. The Jacobian of $T$ is $L^{-m-1}$ by the rank-one determinant formula. Consequently $|D|=E$, and the displayed first moment gives $\int_D y\,dy=0$. The same Jacobian gives $T_\#\sigma=\ell(y)\mathbf1_D(y)\,dy/E$. Positivity and affineness of $\ell$ give convexity and nonempty interior of $B$. Fubini gives $|B|=\int_D\ell(y)\,dy=E$ and the exact projection formula.

Apply [Grünbaum's centroid inequality](https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.1960.10.1257) in dimension $m+1$ to the inverse image in $B$ of each halfspace containing $\bar y$. In intrinsic coordinates a physical margin $u\cdot(x-x_0)\ge b$ becomes $(u+b a)\cdot y\ge b$, since $\ell>0$. A constant transformed margin containing the selected point contains all of $D$. Thus the inequality applies to every original orientation. The projection $\bar y$ belongs to $D$, so the inverse point lies in $F$. The singleton case follows directly.

For the interval calibration, let $F=[0,1]$ and $x_0=t\in(0,1)$. The reciprocal-square objective at parameter $a$ is $1/[(1-at)(1+a(1-t))]$ by elementary integration, with the formula continuous at $a=0$. Maximizing the positive denominator gives $a=(1-2t)/(2t(1-t))$ and objective $4t(1-t)$. The minimizing endpoint denominators are $1/[2(1-t)]$ and $1/(2t)$. Integrating $L^{-3}/E$ on $[t,1]$ gives $1/4+t/2$, with complementary mass $3/4-t/2$ on $[0,t]$ and no mass at $t$. These formulas extend through $a=0$ by continuity. The escort's original barycenter is $t$ by the stationarity identity, but its depth there tends to $1/4$ as $t\downarrow0$. The lifted center uses the centroid of $B$.
