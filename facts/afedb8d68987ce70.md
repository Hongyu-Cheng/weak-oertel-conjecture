## Statement

Use a positive-width outward-smoothed problem on actual compatible positive $d$-fibers with a finite complete lattice base $P$. The outward CDF $\Psi$ is continuous, is one on $[0,\infty)$, and admits the attained active nonvertical caps with positive continuous threshold coarea considered below. Let $\varepsilon f\ge0$ be an affine-equivariant differentiable own buffer (including zero) and $\Gamma$ the pooled covariance norm. Assume the deepest sources are own-interior, one per deepest base, and use normalized active normals and response probabilities balancing $S_N(u-\varepsilon\nabla_x f)$. Write $W$ for the probability average of their boundary ramps, $\sigma$ for the sum of actual fiber surface measures marked by their unit normals, and $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$.

Fix a source and an exposed vertex $q$ of the complete base hull. Among active caps capturing the entire $q$-fiber with ramp one, a balanced finite menu exists exactly when $\varepsilon\nabla_x f$ lies in the convex hull of their continuous normals. For this menu there is a nonnegative continuous finite normal-dependent convex field $\Phi_q$ supported on the $q$-fiber on the measured normal bundle, with $\int W\Phi_qd\sigma=\int\Phi_qd\sigma>0$.

Alternatively suppose an affine center graph $c(z)$ and a single finite linear group $G\subset\operatorname{GL}(d)$ with common fixed-vector space zero make every $K_z-c(z)$ invariant under $G$. At each deepest source there is a finite actual menu of at most $d+1$ active caps, balanced in both quantile and response weights, and a nonnegative field supported on one exposed target fiber of the complete base hull, with $\int W\Phi_qd\sigma\ge\gamma_d\int\Phi_qd\sigma>0$. Common central symmetry gives $1/2$ in place of $\gamma_d$. Families of positive homothets of one centered simplex with affine center graph meet the symmetry hypothesis.

If each source meets one of these criteria, with $r$ distinct targets, the sum of one normalized isolating field per target has integral $r$ and capture integral at least $\gamma_d$ for every source, or at least one if every menu was obtained by the saturation criterion. For the uniform ramp and tied or zero buffer on the compact proper-source class, this yields strict descent on an actual compatible path when $\beta+C(\varepsilon+h)$ is strictly less than the corresponding capture lower bound divided by $r$, with $C$ uniform on the class. Here $r$ is at most the number of sources, hence at most $k(P)$ in the positive-width empty-lattice source regime.
