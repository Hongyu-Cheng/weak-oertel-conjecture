## Statement

Consider the finite budget model near $(s,x)=(0,0)$ given by
$$
M=2e^s,\qquad F_0=e^s[1+\min(x,2x)],\qquad F_1=e^s[1+\min(-x,-2x)].
$$
Its budget ratio $\max(F_0,F_1)/M=(1+|x|)/2$ has a local minimum at the origin with $\beta=1/2$. Every pair of selected linear first-order majorants $g_0=(1,a)$ and $g_1=(1,-b)$, $a,b\in[1,2]$, admits positive weights balancing $DM=(2,0)$ with total weight $1/\beta$. Both cap covectors have value $1$ on the signed common scale direction $(1,0)$ and value $-1$ on the common erosion direction $(-1,0)$. No single normalized pair of weights works for all majorant selections.

## Proof

For $x\ge0$ the first minimum is $x$ and the second is $-2x$. For $x\le0$ they are $2x$ and $-x$. This gives the stated ratio and minimum. The directional derivatives of the two budgets at the origin are the common first coordinate plus these respective minima, which are bounded above by $a x$ and $-b x$ for every $a,b\in[1,2]$. The stated scale and erosion directions have the same budget evaluations independently of $a,b$, and the objective evaluations there are respectively $2,-2$.

For each selection, $\lambda_0=2b/(a+b)$ and $\lambda_1=2a/(a+b)$ are positive and satisfy $\lambda_0+\lambda_1=2$, $a\lambda_0-b\lambda_1=0$. If a single pair worked for all $a$ with $b$ fixed, subtracting two such balances would force $\lambda_0=0$. Then the second coordinate with $b>0$ forces $\lambda_1=0$, contrary to normalization.
