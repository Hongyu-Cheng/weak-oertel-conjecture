## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$ be a compact convex body with integer interval fibers $I_z$, writing $w_z=|I_z|$ for their lengths and taking zero for empty or singleton fibers. Let $I_j$ have positive length, let $m_j$ be its midpoint, and let $R(j,m_j)$ be the original mixed length retained by central reflection about $(j,m_j)$. Then
$$
R(j,m_j)\ge B_j
=w_j+2\sum_{k\ge1}\min\{w_{j-k},w_{j+k},(w_{j-k}+w_{j+k}-w_j)_+\},
$$
where $(x)_+=\max\{x,0\}$.

## Proof

Fix a pair of positive endpoints $A=I_{j-k}=[a_0,a_0+a]$ and $D=I_{j+k}=[b_0,b_0+b]$, and write $I_j=[l,l+w_j]$. The inclusion $(A+D)/2\subseteq I_j$ gives $a+b-w_j\le s\le w_j$ at $s=2m_j-a_0-b_0$. The overlap length is
$$
|A\cap(2m_j-D)|=[\min(a,s)-\max(0,s-b)]_+=[\min(a,b,s,a+b-s)]_+.
$$
The first equality is interval intersection after subtracting $a_0$. The second follows from expanding the difference of the minimum and maximum as the minimum of the four differences. If $a+b>w_j$, both $s$ and $a+b-s$ are at least $a+b-w_j$, giving the required lower bound. If $a+b\le w_j$, the lower bound is zero. Reflection gives the same contribution from the reversed ordered pair. The own interval contributes $w_j$ at its midpoint, and empty or null fibers contribute zero. Summing proves the bound.
