## Statement

Use the shared polygon-face enlargement and notation of pk-w33-coupled-shared-polygon-face-enlargement. Put $\Delta=L_K-(L_A+L_B)/2\ge0$ and $C_A=\int_{F_A}T_p(W_A-r)\,ds$. If $p$ lies on none of the three old $A$ cutting lines, the actual coupled cost obeys
$$
C\le \tfrac54 C_A+\tfrac{L_B}{4}(W_A(p)-r)+\tfrac{1-r}{4}\Delta.
$$
If $\Delta=0$, $C_A<0$ and $W_A(p)<r$ suffice for a negative cost. If also $L_B=0$, $C_A<0$ alone suffices, including for apex points on old cuts.

## Proof

The inner middle trapezoid with base $I_0=(F_A+F_B)/2$ and top $J+\varepsilon n/2$ is the midpoint sum of the added $A$ triangle and $F_B$. At each normal height of the triangle its tangential interval contains the coordinate of $p$. Its Minkowski sum with $F_B$ splits into the left part of the triangle row translated by the left endpoint of $F_B$, the length-$L_B$ middle interval obtained by adding the full $F_B$ to the point directly above $p$, and the right part of the row translated by the right endpoint. The pieces are disjoint up to endpoints. For any paired $x_A,x_B$, capture of their midpoint by middle cap $i$ implies $v_i\cdot x_A\ge 2v_i\cdot y_0-v_i\cdot x_B\ge b_i$. Hence its load is at most $W_A(x_A)$ with the original weights. Subtract $r$ and integrate. Midpoint scaling multiplies area by $1/4$. The two translated pieces give at most $C_A/4$ after division by $\varepsilon$ and passage to the limit. The middle strip has area $\varepsilon L_B$ before scaling, and the vertical $A$ points above $p$ keep its old strict signature for small $\varepsilon$. Its contribution is at most $L_B(W_A(p)-r)/4$. When $L_B=0$ this strip is absent.

The actual trapezoidal profile on $F_K$ dominates the inner profile pointwise: extending the two ramp bases to the endpoints of $F_K$ increases the height and keeps the same plateau. Its extra area coefficient is exactly $\Delta/4$. Its weighted normalized cost is at most $(1-r)\Delta/4$ since $W_K\le1$. Adding the old $A$ cost and the inner bound gives the inequality and its strict consequences.
