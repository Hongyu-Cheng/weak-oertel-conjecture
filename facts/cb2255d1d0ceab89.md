## Statement

Use the normal-dependent endpoint projective erosion with a fixed finite nonempty list of original ambient closed halfspaces $H_j$ and fixed normalization $\beta$. For $L=A,B$, let $\kappa_{Lj}$ be one on the old boundary if the whole fiber is captured, zero if its interior is missed, and the strict cap indicator for a proper trace. Let $\sigma_{Lj}$ be the pushforward under the old a.e. unit normal of $(\kappa_{Lj}-\beta)U_L(n_L(x),x)\,d\mathcal H^{d-1}(x)$. Let $r_{Kj}=D_K(H_j^K)-\beta D_K$ be the middle surplus for the same actual cap. Write $\nu_j=\sigma_{Aj}-\sigma_{Bj}$ and $c_j=r_{Kj}+\sigma_{Bj}(\mathbb S^{d-1})$. The compact convex range for measurable $0\le t\le1$ is
$$
R=\{(c_j+\textstyle\int t\,d\nu_j)_j:0\le t\le1\}.
$$
Continuous allocations give a dense subset of $R$ and are actual erosions. There is a continuous allocation paying all fixed rows strictly if and only if
$$
\min_{\lambda_j\ge0,\,\sum_j\lambda_j=1}
\left[\sum_j\lambda_j c_j+\left(\sum_j\lambda_j\nu_j\right)^+(\mathbb S^{d-1})\right]>0,
$$
where $\nu^+$ is the positive part of a signed measure. For polytopal inputs the range is a zonotope indexed by the distinct facet normals and every point is attained by a continuous allocation. Let $Q$ be the old maximum raw depth and $M$ the old total mass. If $\beta=Q/M$, the listed critical rows each have old mass $Q$ and cover the old deepest base planes, and fixed covers of the remaining bases have a strict slack gap below $Q$, then paying all critical rows gives strict actual normalized descent.

## Proof

With the ceilings and fields of continuous-normal-wise-endpoint-projective-erosion, apply 84cd8a85417b0d7a separately on each singleton. It gives the total first loss by integrating the field against surface measure and the fixed-cap loss by inserting the old selector, including pure-base and supporting cuts. In dimension one use counting measure. Subtract $\beta$ times the total loss from the captured loss. The exact expression is
$$
r_{Kj}+\int t\,d\sigma_{Aj}+\int(1-t)\,d\sigma_{Bj}=c_j+\int t\,d\nu_j.
$$
Every restriction is from the same original ambient cap.

For compactness of $R$ take $\mu=\sum_j|\nu_j|$. If $\mu=0$, $R$ is a singleton. Otherwise the densities $g_j=d\nu_j/d\mu$ satisfy $|g_j|\le1$. Any sequence of allocations is bounded in $L^2(\mu)$ and has a weakly convergent subsequence. The inequalities $0\le t\le1$ pass to the limit by testing against indicators, and each $\int t g_j\,d\mu$ converges. This proves closedness. Boundedness and convexity are immediate. Continuous $[0,1]$ functions approximate each measurable allocation in $L^1(\mu)$. To see this, approximate first by a bounded simple function, approximate each indicator by a continuous function between zero and one using finite Radon inner and outer regularity and interpolation between compact and open sets, then clamp the resulting function to $[0,1]$. The same approximation holds if $\mu$ is enlarged by the positive endpoint cost measures, so total and cap costs can also be approximated together.

For any signed finite measure $\nu$, the supremum of $\int t\,d\nu$ over measurable $0\le t\le1$ is $\nu^+(\mathbb S^{d-1})$ by the indicator of a positive Hahn set. Thus the bracketed expression is the support function of $R$ at $\lambda$. If $R$ meets the open positive orthant, each such support value is bounded below by the least coordinate of any strictly positive point, and continuity approximation preserves strictness. Otherwise separate the compact convex $R$ from the open positive orthant. The separating functional has nonnegative coordinates because the orthant is unbounded above in every coordinate, and its infimum on the orthant is zero. Normalizing the nonzero functional gives a simplex point with bracketed value at most zero. The support function is continuous on the compact simplex, so its minimum exists. If the measures are supported on finitely many normals, $R$ is the affine image of their coordinate cube. Every finite $[0,1]$ assignment extends continuously to the sphere, by continuous bump functions of disjoint support about those normals.

For the final implication, each old critical cap has mass $Q=\beta M$ and has normalized response $-r_j/M$, by the singleton expansions. Its normalized mass is therefore below $\beta$ for sufficiently small positive parameter. Slack rows retain a strict gap by continuity of mass and total. The cover remains valid because it is fixed and the new fibers are nested in the old fibers. Every point is bounded by the mass of a containing cover cap, which proves the descent.
