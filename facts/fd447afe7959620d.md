## Statement

Let $C=[-1,1]^2$ with normalized length measure on the three integer fibers $z=-1,0,1$. Let $D_{1/12}$ be the ambient Tukey depth region formed from all containing closed halfspaces. Its sections at the three sampled bases are $[-1/2,1/2]$, $[-5/6,5/6]$, and $[-1/2,1/2]$. The fiber lengths removed by this region are $(1,1/3,1)$, so the deficit fails midpoint quasiconcavity and midpoint concavity of every positive power. The Minkowski erosions $C_z\ominus(D_{1/12})_z=\{u:u+(D_{1/12})_z\subseteq C_z\}$ are $[-1/2,1/2]$, $[-1/6,1/6]$, and $[-1/2,1/2]$. They fail the set-valued midpoint inclusion, and their lengths fail midpoint concavity of every positive power. The fiberwise sums $C_z+(-(D_{1/12})_z)$ instead have lengths $(3,11/3,3)$, which satisfy the midpoint length inequality.

## Proof

In c680a307d3f26cec take $a=b=U=2$ and shift $s=0$. All three integer fibers are $[-1,1]$ and the total raw mass is $6$. The pointwise raw endpoint depth is $1-|x|$. At the middle fiber the formula uses $g(x)=\min\{2,\max\{0,2-2x\}\}$. For $0\le x\le1$, the two masses in its minimum are $3-3x$ and $3+x$. Reflection gives raw middle depth $3(1-|x|)$ on $[-1,1]$. A mark outside $C$ has zero depth by separation. Thus dividing the raw depths by $6$ and taking the $1/12$ superlevel gives the claimed intervals at the sampled bases.

Their lengths are $1,5/3,1$, yielding deficits $1,1/3,1$ from the original length $2$. For a centered interval of radius $r\le1$, its erosion from $[-1,1]$ has radius $1-r$ and the Minkowski sum with its negative has radius $1+r$. Substituting $r=1/2,5/6,1/2$ gives the three erosion intervals and the stated sums. The midpoint of the two equal endpoint erosion intervals is $[-1/2,1/2]$, which strictly contains the central interval $[-1/6,1/6]$. The deficit and erosion-length triples have smaller middle than either endpoint, which persists under every positive power.
