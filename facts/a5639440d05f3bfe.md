## Statement

Let \(n\ge1\), \(k\ge0\), and \(q\ge1\) be integers, let \(j_1,\ldots,j_n\) be nonnegative integers, let \(u=(u_i)_{i=1}^n\in\mathbb Z^n\), and let \(A=(a_{ri})\in\mathbb R^{k\times n}\). Suppose some \(h\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\) satisfies \(\gcd(u_h,q)=1\) and \(j_h\ge q\). Put \(B:=\prod_{i=1}^n\{-j_i,\ldots,j_i\}\), let \(V\) be uniform on \(B\), and use \(a_rv:=\sum_{i=1}^n a_{ri}v_i\). For each row let \(\sigma_r\ge0\) satisfy \(\sigma_r^2=\sum_{i=1}^n a_{ri}^2j_i(j_i+1)/3\). These \(j_i,A,V,\sigma_r\) and \(a_rv\) have exactly the meanings and normalization in accepted fact 4d7d60d01a9ce8e5; write its constant as \(C_{\mathrm{slab}}:=2\log64/(1-\sqrt{7/8})\).

Then the set
\[
T:=\{v\in B:u\cdot v\equiv0\pmod q,\quad |a_rv|\le3\sigma_r\text{ for every }1\le r\le k\}
\]
satisfies
\[
|T|\ge\frac{\exp(-C_{\mathrm{slab}}k)}{2q-1}\prod_{i=1}^n(2j_i+1)
\ge\frac{\exp(-C_{\mathrm{slab}}k)}{2q}\prod_{i=1}^n(2j_i+1),
\qquad T=-T.
\]
Equivalently, \(\mathbb P(V\in T)\ge\exp(-C_{\mathrm{slab}}k)/(2q-1)\), so absolute constants \(c=1/2\) and width factor \(3\) suffice. The claim includes \(q=1\), \(k=0\), all zero-variance rows, and arbitrary zero anchor coefficients \(a_{rh}=0\).

## Proof

By accepted fact 4d7d60d01a9ce8e5, the set \(G:=\{v\in B:|a_rv|\le\sigma_r\text{ for every }1\le r\le k\}\) has cardinality at least \(\exp(-C_{\mathrm{slab}}k)|B|\). We construct a map \(F:B\to B\) that changes only coordinate \(h\), forces the congruence, moves that coordinate by at most \(q-1\), and has fibers of size at most \(2q-1\).

Fix the other coordinates \(w=(v_i)_{i\ne h}\). Since \(\gcd(u_h,q)=1\), there is a unique \(\rho(w)\in\{0,\ldots,q-1\}\) satisfying \(u_h\rho(w)+\sum_{i\ne h}u_iv_i\equiv0\pmod q\). For \(q=1\) this is the unique residue \(0\). Set
\[
m(w):=\rho(w)+q\left\lceil\frac{-j_h-\rho(w)}q\right\rceil,
\qquad
M(w):=m(w)+q\left\lfloor\frac{j_h-m(w)}q\right\rfloor.
\]
Here \(m(w)\) is the least integer at least \(-j_h\) in the required residue, and \(-j_h\le m(w)\le-j_h+q-1\le j_h\), using \(j_h\ge q\). Thus \(M(w)\) is well-defined, is the greatest integer at most \(j_h\) in that residue, and satisfies \(j_h-q+1\le M(w)\le j_h\). In particular the finite progression \(R(w):=\{m(w),m(w)+q,\ldots,M(w)\}\) is nonempty and lies in \([-j_h,j_h]\cap\mathbb Z\).

For \(t\in\{-j_h,\ldots,j_h\}\), let \(f_w(t)\) be a point of \(R(w)\) minimizing \(|s-t|\), choosing the smaller point in case of a tie. This uniquely specifies \(f_w\). If \(t<m(w)\), its distance to \(m(w)\) is at most \(q-1\); if \(t>M(w)\), its distance to \(M(w)\) is at most \(q-1\). If \(m(w)\le t\le M(w)\), the largest element of \(R(w)\) not exceeding \(t\) has distance at most \(q-1\), since consecutive elements differ by \(q\). Consequently \(|f_w(t)-t|\le q-1\) in every case. Define \(F(v)_i=v_i\) for \(i\ne h\) and \(F(v)_h=f_w(v_h)\). The construction proves \(F(v)\in B\) and \(u\cdot F(v)\equiv0\pmod q\).

For any fixed output \(y\in B\), a preimage \(v\) must have \(v_i=y_i\) for \(i\ne h\) and \(|v_h-y_h|\le q-1\). There are at most \(2q-1\) integer choices for \(v_h\), so \(|F^{-1}(\{y\})|\le2q-1\). This includes the endpoints of the anchor interval. When \(q=1\), \(R(w)\) is the whole anchor interval, hence \(F\) is the identity and its fibers have size \(1\).

For each row, the original variance formula and \(q\le j_h\) give
\[
|a_{rh}|(q-1)\le |a_{rh}|q\le |a_{rh}|\sqrt{j_h(j_h+1)}\le\sqrt3\,\sigma_r.
\]
No division by \(\sigma_r\) is used. Thus, for \(v\in G\),
\[
|a_rF(v)|\le |a_rv|+|a_{rh}|\,|F(v)_h-v_h|
\le(1+\sqrt3)\sigma_r\le3\sigma_r.
\]
Therefore \(F(G)\subseteq T\). In particular, if \(\sigma_r=0\), then \(j_h>0\) forces \(a_{rh}=0\), and the same inequality gives \(a_rF(v)=0\). More directly every such row vanishes throughout \(B\), since \(a_{ri}=0\) on every coordinate with \(j_i>0\), while \(v_i=0\) on the remaining coordinates. An anchor coefficient \(a_{rh}=0\) causes no row shift regardless of the other coefficients. The proof also works when \(k=0\), with no row inequalities.

The fiber bound implies \(|G|\le(2q-1)|F(G)|\le(2q-1)|T|\). Combining this with the accepted lower bound for \(|G|\) and \(|B|=\prod_i(2j_i+1)\) proves the two cardinality bounds, because \(2q-1\le2q\).

Finally \(B=-B\), the congruence \(u\cdot v\equiv0\pmod q\) is preserved by \(v\mapsto-v\), and \(|a_r(-v)|=|a_rv|\). Hence the full set \(T\) satisfies \(T=-T\), independently of whether the chosen map \(F\) preserves signs. This proves all claims.
