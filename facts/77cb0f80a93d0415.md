## Statement

Let $d\ge1$ be an integer, $B\subset\mathbb R^d$ a compact convex body of positive $d$-volume, $0\le\eta\le\mathbf 1_B\,dx$, and $\alpha=\eta(B)/|B|$. Let $g$ be the centroid of $\mathbf 1_B\,dx+k\eta$. For a closed halfspace $H$ containing $g$, write $v=(|B\cap H|+\eta(H))/|B|$, $q=1+1/d$, and $\gamma_d=q^{-d}$. Let $k\ge2$ be real and $\Xi_{d,k}=\inf v$ over all $B,\eta,H$, including all $\alpha$. For each $0\le u\le1$ let $t_k(u)>0$ be the unique solution of $qt+ku^d(q-u)t^{d+1}=1$. Then
$$
\Xi_{d,k}=\min_{0\le u\le1}(1+u^d)t_k(u)^d,
$$
and for each $u$ the value inside the minimum is realized by a simplex with a single upper vertex band of mass ratio $\alpha=u^d t_k(u)^d$. Let $u_*\in(0,1)$ be the root of $u_*^d+du_*=d$. Set $Z=d(1-u)-u^d$ and $D=(d+1)(1-u)-u^{d+1}$. If $k\le1/(d\gamma_d)$, the minimum is $\gamma_d$ at $u=0$. Otherwise the unique minimizing $u\in(0,u_*)$ solves $D^d/Z^{d+1}=k$, and $\Xi_{d,k}=(1+u^d)(Z/D)^d$, with $\alpha=u^d(Z/D)^d$. For $d=1$, $\Xi_{1,k}=(\sqrt{3(k+1)}-2)/k$; for integer $k\ge2$ the endpoint case occurs only at $d=1,k=2$.

## Proof

Use the fixed-mass one-copy program, with its attained minimum at every $\alpha$. For fixed $s,r$ the nonlinear slack has derivative in $\alpha$ equal to $kq[s^{q-1}-(s+\alpha-r)^{q-1}]\le0$. Thus one may lower $\alpha$ to $r$ without violating feasibility or changing the cost $s+r$. Conversely the reduced program is part of the original program. It is therefore exact to minimize over $0\le r\le s\le1$ under $q(1+kr)s^{1/d}\ge1+kr^q$. No feasible $s$ is zero. Write $t=s^{1/d}$ and $u=(r/s)^{1/d}$. At fixed $u$ the left side of $qt+ku^d(q-u)t^{d+1}\ge1$ increases strictly in $t$, so the equality root in $(0,1)$ minimizes the cost. At equality, the simplex construction of the fixed-mass theorem uses the band $[0,r]$ and an empty second band, realizing each value in the asserted minimum.

Implicit differentiation gives for $0<u<1$ the sign of the objective derivative as the sign of $1-kt^d Z$. In detail, set $F=qt+ku^d(q-u)t^{d+1}-1$. After removing the positive factor $d u^{d-1}t^{d-1}/F_t$, the numerator is $tF_t-(1+u^d)F_u/u^{d-1}=qt(1-kt^d Z)$. When $Z\le0$ it is positive. When $Z>0$, evaluate $F+1$ at $t_0=(kZ)^{-1/d}$: the value is $t_0D/Z=(D^d/(kZ^{d+1}))^{1/d}$, using $D=qZ+u^d(q-u)$. Thus $1-kt^d Z$ has the sign of $D^d/Z^{d+1}-k$.

The function $Z$ decreases strictly from $d$ to a negative value and vanishes at $u_*$. On $0<u<u_*$ the logarithmic derivative of $D^d/Z^{d+1}$ is
$$
d(d+1)\left[\frac{1+u^{d-1}}{Z}-\frac{1+u^d}{D}\right].
$$
Its bracket has numerator $(1-u)[1+u^d+u^{d-1}(d+1-du)]>0$ after multiplication by $ZD$. The ratio starts at $(d+1)^d/d^{d+1}=1/(d\gamma_d)$ and tends to infinity because $D(u_*)=u_*^d(q-u_*)>0$. This proves global minimality and uniqueness in $u$, including the endpoint. At an interior optimum $kt^dZ=1$, and $F=0$ then gives $t=Z/D$ and the asserted masses. The binomial bound $(1+1/d)^d<d+1$ for $d\ge2$ follows since its $d+1$ binomial terms are at most one and at least one is strictly smaller. It gives $1/(d\gamma_d)<2$ when $d\ge2$; in dimension one the threshold equals two.

In dimension one put $y=1+kr\ge1$. Equality in the reduced program gives $s=(1+kr^2)/(2(1+kr))$ and $s+r=(3y-4+(k+1)/y)/(2k)$. Its minimum over $y\ge1$ is at $m=\sqrt{(k+1)/3}\ge1$; there $r=(m-1)/k$ and $s=(2m-1)/k$, which satisfy $0\le r\le s\le1$ since $k=3m^2-1$. Substitution gives the stated value.
