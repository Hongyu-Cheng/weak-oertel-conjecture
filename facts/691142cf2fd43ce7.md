## Statement

Let $P=\operatorname{conv}\{(-4,-4),(4,0),(0,4)\}\cap\mathbb Z^2$ and let $K$ be any nondegenerate planar triangle of area $V$, centroid $g$, and barycentric coordinates $\lambda_j$ for $j=0,1,2$. Define $\ell=(-z_1-z_2,2z_1-z_2,2z_2-z_1)$ and
$$
H_{ij}=\{(z,x):\ell_i(z)+\lambda_j(x)\ge1/3\}\qquad(0\le i,j\le2).
$$
On $P\times K$, with the sum of areas on its 31 integer fibers, all nine caps attain raw depth $40V/3$ at $(0,g)$. They have only proper or strictly saturated traces. Their raw mixed coarea masses are $4V$. The nonnegative profiles with $\sum_{i,j}\theta_{ij}=1$ and balanced continuous mark response are exactly those with $\sum_i\theta_{ij}=1/3$ for every $j$.

For such a profile let $W_\theta=\sum_{i,j}\theta_{ij}\mathbf1_{H_{ij}}$ and $\beta=40/93$. On each continuous facet of $K$, use the sum $\eta$ of arclength over its copies on $P$, marked by the facet's outer normal. The source $\mu_\theta=(W_\theta/\beta)\eta$ has the full target mass on each facet. A physical martingale from this source to the capacity $\eta$ preserving the continuous normal exists if and only if $\sum_j\theta_{ij}=1/3$ for every $i$. Here a physical martingale fixes the source point as the conditional mean of the full target coordinates $(z,x)$, and a transport to the capacity means a target submeasure of $\eta$. Exactly six of the 27 extreme balanced profiles are feasible.

## Proof

The base is the set of integer points with all three $\ell_i\ge-4$. The map $z\mapsto\ell(z)$ identifies $\mathbb Z^2$ with the triples of integers summing to zero and pairwise congruent modulo three. Indeed $z_1=(\ell_1-\ell_0)/3$ and $z_2=(\ell_2-\ell_0)/3$. A permutation of the three $\ell_i$ therefore acts on the actual base by an invertible integer linear map. The continuous triangle's affine vertex permutations preserve area and fix its centroid. Apply the two permutations independently to the canonical caps of cb254ea93a82679e. This gives all nine caps at the same depth, with the same proper and strictly saturated trace counts. Raw coarea is the derivative with respect to the raw scalar threshold, so it remains $4V$ under these area-preserving symmetries. The derivative of mass for a moving continuous mark is $-4V\nabla\lambda_j$ for cap $H_{ij}$. The three gradients span the plane with the sole relation $\sum_j\nabla\lambda_j=0$. Thus balance and total profile weight one give the column sums $1/3$. The extreme profiles select one row independently in each of three columns.

On a facet $\lambda_k=0$ of length $L$, the trace of $H_{ij}$ has length $L$ at $\ell_i>0$ and zero at $\ell_i<0$. At $\ell_i=0$ its length is zero if $j=k$ and $2L/3$ otherwise. The following finite census uses the coordinate bounds $-4\le z_1,z_2\le4$. Loop over these integer pairs, keep those with $\min_i\ell_i(z)\ge-4$, and add the vector $z$ to each applicable sign class.
$$
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r|r}
i & \#\{\ell_i>0\} & \sum_{\ell_i>0}z & \#\{\ell_i=0\} & \sum_{\ell_i=0}z\\
0&12&(-21,-21)&3&(0,0)\\
1&12&(21,0)&3&(0,0)\\
2&12&(0,21)&3&(0,0)
\end{array}
$$
The full base sum is zero, also by permutation symmetry. Writing $r_i=\sum_j\theta_{ij}$, the column sums give source mass $L[12+2(1-1/3)]/\beta=31L$ and base first moment $(21L/\beta)(r_1-r_0,r_2-r_0)$. The target uses its entire capacity by equality of mass. Its first moment is zero, forcing $r_i=1/3$ for all $i$.

Conversely, $3\theta$ then has all row and column sums one. It is a convex combination of permutation matrices. To see this, its positive support satisfies the matching condition since any set of rows sends its total mass to its neighboring columns, each of capacity one. A maximal-cardinality matching that leaves a row unmatched would give an alternating reachable set of rows with fewer neighboring columns, a contradiction. Subtract the smallest positive matched entry times its permutation matrix. If a positive common row sum remains, rescale and repeat. Each subtraction eliminates at least one positive entry and the procedure terminates.

The canonical profile $\theta_{ij}=\mathbf1_{i=j}/3$ has a physical martingale to capacity. In the facet proof of 7559aec0ef3647a6, for each common third parameter $r$, the integer triples of profile level two have source density $62/40$ relative to unit target density. Those of level one have density $31/40$. Keep the common identity mass at every triple and send the excess $22/40$ from each level-two triple to the level-one triples using the rational entries $\pi_{hl}/40$ of that fact. Their column deficits are $9/40$, and their row moments preserve all physical coordinates under the affine realization at fixed $r$. Integrate with the common arclength factor. The same construction applies on each facet and preserves its normal. A base permutation transports this coupling to each permutation profile, leaving the continuous coordinate and label unchanged and preserving the target. The convex combination of the transported couplings has source $\mu_\theta$ and target $\eta$. Among the 27 extreme balanced profiles the row sums select exactly the six permutations.
