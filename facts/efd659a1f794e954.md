## Statement

Let $P$ be a finite complete affine-lattice base with $k$ occupied inherited parities and let $d\ge1$, $N=d+1$, $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. Range over all actual positive compatible convex $d$-fiber families $K=(K_z)_{z\in P}$ and use their canonical hull, summed sampled volume $dm$, total mass $M$ and ordinary maximum normalized depth $\beta_0$. Write $\beta_*=\inf_K\beta_0(K)$. Assume $\beta_*<b<\gamma_d/k$ and that the whole original sublevel $\{\beta_0\le b\}$ has representatives in a compact positive compatible family under maps $(z,X)\mapsto(z,AX+s(z))$ with $s$ affine. In the frame let $M=1$, $V_z=|K_z|\ge v>0$ and $K_z\subset RB_d$. Assume every limit in that frame satisfies $V(T)\le k(T)/k$ for every nonempty proper closed minor $T=P\cap\operatorname{conv}(T)$, with $V(T)=\sum_{z\in T}V_z$ and inherited parity count $k(T)$.

An admissible parameter $L$ is a finite, nonnegative upper-semicontinuous concave function on the canonical hull, positive on all sampled fiber interiors and normalized by $\int\log L\,dm=0$. Use unchanged pure-base caps, including the whole-base trace in rank zero. The physical buffer at $(p,y)$ in the margin of a nonvertical through-mark cap is $\varepsilon(L(p,y)E_{K_p}(y)/M)^{1/N}G_K(u)$, with marked projective energy extended by zero on the own boundary and with the pooled within-fiber covariance norm $G_K$. Write $\beta_\varepsilon(K,L)$ for the maximum normalized buffered depth and $b(\varepsilon)=\inf_{K,L}\beta_\varepsilon(K,L)$.

There exist $0<c\le C<\infty$ and $0<\bar\varepsilon<1$ such that $\beta_*+c\varepsilon\le b(\varepsilon)\le\beta_*+C\varepsilon$ for $0<\varepsilon\le\bar\varepsilon$. For all sufficiently small $\tau>0$, $\beta_\varepsilon(K,L)-\tau\log\varepsilon$ attains its infimum over the same actual families and parameters and $0<\varepsilon\le\bar\varepsilon$. Every minimizer satisfies
$$
\frac{\tau}{eC}\le\varepsilon_\tau\le \frac{2\tau}{c}\log(2C/c)<\bar\varepsilon.
$$
It minimizes the unpenalized problem at that scale, and it has an equivalent minimizing representative whose $L$ is the minimum of at most $|P|$ affine functions and is strictly positive on a neighborhood of the canonical hull. Equivalence here preserves the sampled buffered depths and logarithmic normalization. The physical shifts at these minimizing pairs tend uniformly to zero as $\tau\downarrow0$ in the bounded frame, and all their maximizing marks eventually have a strict gap below their closed pure-base depth and proper shifted own traces.
