## Statement

Let $X$ be a local maximizer of $L(X)=\sum_i f_i(X_i)$ on the nonnegative $N\times m$ matrices with each column sum one, where each $f_i$ is finite and positively homogeneous of degree one on $\mathbb R_+^m$. Write $I=\{i:X_i\ne0\}$. Choose a set $S\subseteq I$ of strictly positive rows and vectors $v_i\in\mathbb R^m$ for $i\in S$, with $v_i=0$ for $i\in I\setminus S$. Suppose $\sum_{i\in S}v_i\in\operatorname{span}\{X_i:i\in I\}$, and choose $a_i$ with $\sum_{i\in I}(v_i+a_iX_i)=0$. Assume for $i\in S$ that the two-sided directional derivative $\ell_i=\frac{d}{ds}f_i(X_i+s v_i)|_{s=0}$ exists.

Then the matrix with rows $X_i(t)=(1+t a_i)X_i+t v_i$ for $i\in I$ and zero elsewhere is feasible for sufficiently small signed $t$, and, with $s_i=t/(1+t a_i)$,
$$
L(X(t))-L(X)
=\sum_{i\in S}(1+t a_i)
 \bigl[f_i(X_i+s_i v_i)-f_i(X_i)-s_i\ell_i\bigr].
$$
In particular, for $S=\{r\}$ and $f_r=\omega_r\phi$ with $\omega_r>0$, every such direction in the active span has nonpositive capacity remainder for all small $s_r$. If a second directional coefficient exists along that direction, it is nonpositive.

## Proof

All factors $1+t a_i$ are positive for small signed $t$. Pure scaling retains support, and strict positivity at the selected rows permits their additional perturbations. The relation defining the coefficients $a_i$ preserves all column sums exactly. For a selected row, homogeneity gives
$$
 f_i(X_i(t))=(1+t a_i) f_i(X_i+s_i v_i).
$$
Subtract the baseline value, adding and subtracting $(1+t a_i)s_i\ell_i=t\ell_i$. A purely scaled row contributes exactly $t a_i f_i(X_i)$. Consequently the objective change is the displayed sum of remainders plus
$$
t\left[\sum_{i\in I}a_i f_i(X_i)+\sum_{i\in S}\ell_i\right].
$$
The remainders are $o(|t|)$ by the directional derivatives. Local maximality for both signs of $t$ forces the bracket to vanish, giving the exact identity. In the one-row case $t\mapsto s_r$ maps a neighborhood of zero onto a neighborhood of zero, and the prefactor $(1+t a_r)\omega_r$ is positive. Nonpositivity of the capacity remainder follows. If $\phi(X_r+s_rv_r)-\phi(X_r)-s_rD_{v_r}\phi(X_r)=\frac12 s_r^2 Q+o(s_r^2)$, dividing by $\frac12 s_r^2$ gives $Q\le0$.
