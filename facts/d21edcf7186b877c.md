## Statement

In the standard tetrahedron, at the common actual section centroid
$\lambda=(851/2610,11218/35061,4657/40455,1621/6786)$, a section-normal balanced menu that allows a quadrilateral cap has relative mean $12431494665571935/26212512742946401$. It is strictly less than the all-vertex-side balanced mean $36033317871223/75836660403033$ at $\lambda$ and strictly greater than $27/64$. All coordinates of $\lambda$ are in $(0,1/3)$.

## Proof

Choose the raw signed heights $(6,7,-24,-6)$. The formulas of tetrahedral-quadrilateral-section-calculus yield the displayed $\lambda$, normalized coarea $261/4030$ and cap fraction $506/2015$. Use the complementary cap and the three vertex-side caps at vertices $0,1,2$ supplied by tetrahedral-vertex-cap-affine-envelope. Give them weights with common denominator $N=52425025485892802$ and respective numerators
$$
23097356755453943,\quad13014678602879811,\quad15094034160118200,\quad1218955967440848.
$$
These are positive and sum to $N$. For the complement the section area vector divided by tetrahedron volume is $-S(7-6,-24-6,-6-6)$. For vertex cap $i$ it is $f_i$ times the gradient of $3-\sum_{j\ne i}\xi_j/\lambda_j$. In the same order as the four weights, this gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
&( -261/4030,\ 783/403,\ 1566/2015),\\
&( -7548997/10167690,\ -9092189/4405999,\ -52242226/52533065),\\
&( 7548997/10167690,\ -9221869/6778460,\ -79169/292175),\\
&( -10395473/264359940,\ 9092189/4405999,\ -572118/759655).
\end{aligned}
$$
Multiplying by the listed numerators and summing gives zero in all three coordinates. The fractions $1-F,f_0,f_1,f_2$ are $1509/2015$, $42342324173/178244689545$, $6424196447/26537670900$, and $7737452839/11499657390$. Their weighted sum gives the stated mean, whose difference from $27/64$ is $87877814537051013/1677600815548569664>0$.

In the notation of tetrahedral-vertex-cap-affine-envelope, substitution gives $R=36033317871223/75836660403033$ and the complement's affine slack after multiplying by $D$ is $-76232299376/232609319856225$. Each vertex cap has affine equality, and the four area vectors balance, so the menu's mean minus $R$ is the complementary slack times its positive weight divided by $D>0$. This proves the strict comparison.
