## Statement

On the consecutive bases $(-1,0,1,2)$ start with identical intervals $[-1,1]$. The maximum normalized mixed closed-halfspace depth is $3/8$, attained at both inner centers $(0,0)$ and $(1,0)$. Seed constant support speeds $1$ at bases $0,1$ and zero at the outer bases. In the increasing order charge each nonself extreme anchored relation to its higher input and in the decreasing order to its lower input. The resulting speed arrays are $(0,1,2,4)$ and $(4,2,1,0)$. Their pointwise maximum and sum give, for $0<e<1/4$, actual compatible centered interval families of radii $1-eg_z$, with mixed masses $8-24e$ and $8-28e$. The exact raw depth maxima of these two families are $3-10e$ and $3-11e$, attained at both inner centers. Their normalized values satisfy
$$
\frac{3-10e}{8-24e}<\frac{3-11e}{8-28e}<\frac38
$$
and their derivatives at zero are $-1/8$ and $-1/16$. The original critical caps $\pm x-z\ge0$ at base $0$ and $\pm x+(z-1)\ge0$ at base $1$ give first normalized payments $1$ and $1/2$ on the two paths, where payment is first cap loss minus $3/8$ times first total loss.

## Proof

For constant fields the transfer of d1b238a7600118e2 is division by the chosen input coefficient. Each nonself extreme anchored representation on a line has exactly two input sites, one on each side of the target. At $0$ the two representations use $(-1,1)$ and $(-1,2)$ with higher coefficients $1/2$ and $1/3$. At $1$ they use $(-1,2)$ and $(0,2)$ with higher coefficients $2/3$ and $1/2$. Thus at base $1$ the forward speed is $\max(1,2)=2$, and at base $2$ it is $\max(3,3,4)=4$. The lower bases retain speeds $0,1$. Reflection of the base about $1/2$ gives the reverse propagation. The pointwise maximum and sum are $(4,2,2,4)$ and $(4,3,3,4)$. The forward, reverse, maximum and sum sequences are convex on this ordered base, as their consecutive differences are nondecreasing. Linear interpolation of their positive radii $1-eg_z$ is therefore concave for the indicated range, and its centered hypograph in absolute height is a convex body with those exact four integer fibers. The max fibers are the intersections of the individual integer fibers. Summing the lengths gives the displayed masses.

In either of the two combined families write $R=1-4e$ for the outer radius and $r$ for the inner radius, equal to $1-2e$ or $1-3e$.
