## Statement

Let $\nu$ be a finite nonnegative measure on labeled points $(i,z,x)$ with $z\in\mathbb R^n$, $x\in\mathbb R^d$, and with label vector $U=u_i\in\mathbb R^d$. Assume the moments below exist and $\int U\,d\nu=0$, $\int zU^\top\,d\nu=0$, $\int xU^\top\,d\nu=Q$. If $x\in c+Bz+L$ almost everywhere for a fixed vector $c$, linear map $B$, and linear space $L$, then $\operatorname{range}Q\subseteq L$. In particular, if $Q=\sum_i\Psi_i u_i u_i^\top$ for finitely many $\Psi_i\ge0$, then $\dim L\ge\dim\operatorname{span}\{u_i:\Psi_i>0\}$.

## Proof

Write $r=x-c-Bz$. By the zero force and base first-moment assumptions, $Q=\int rU^\top\,d\nu$. If $P_{L^\perp}$ denotes orthogonal projection onto $L^\perp$, then $P_{L^\perp}r=0$ almost everywhere, so $P_{L^\perp}Q=0$. This puts every column of $Q$ in $L$.

For the stated positive semidefinite representation, $v^\top Qv=\sum_i\Psi_i(v\cdot u_i)^2$. Its vanishing is equivalent to $v$ being perpendicular to all $u_i$ with $\Psi_i>0$, and it is also equivalent to $Qv=0$. Hence the range of the symmetric $Q$ is precisely that span.
