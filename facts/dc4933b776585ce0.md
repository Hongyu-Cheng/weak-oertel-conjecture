## Statement

Use the exact fixed-cover continuation and its finite-contact derivative on one actual compatible signed radial path for the energy-only buffer. Put $\beta=Q/M$ and $\dot M=-D$. Suppose for each selected old cap its actual tail has the regular joint expansion
$$
\mu_t\{N_{ij}\cdot(Z,X)\ge c_{ij}+t s\}
=Q-t(L_{ij}+S_{ij}s)+o(t),\qquad S_{ij}>0,
$$
uniformly on bounded $s$ intervals. Here $L_{ij}$ is the first loss of mass at the fixed threshold. A sufficient trace condition is that each selected plane on each old fiber be a proper section, a strict full or empty cut, or a supporting section of zero $(d-1)$-dimensional measure. Under this condition $S_{ij}$ is the sum of proper section measures divided by $\lVert u_{ij}\rVert$, and $L_{ij}$ integrates the signed inward speed on the captured old boundary.

Let $B_i=\sum_j\rho_{ij}/S_{ij}$ and $\theta_{ij}=(\rho_{ij}/S_{ij})/B_i$. Then the right derivatives of the continued budgets satisfy
$$
\dot q_i=-\sum_j\theta_{ij}L_{ij}-\frac{\dot\alpha_i}{B_i},
\qquad
\left.\frac{d}{dt^+}\right|_{0}\frac{q_i(t)}{M_t}
=-\frac{\sum_j\theta_{ij}L_{ij}+\dot\alpha_i/B_i-\beta D}{M}.
$$
If $\sum_j\theta_{ij}L_{ij}+\dot\alpha_i/B_i>\beta D$ for every old group on this one path, then $\beta_\varepsilon(K(t))<\beta$ for all sufficiently small $t>0$.

## Proof

First verify the sufficient trace condition. For the old fixed body in each fiber, linear coarea gives the threshold variation at speed $s$ as $-ts$ times the old section area divided by $\lVert u_{ij}\rVert$, plus $o(t)$ uniformly on bounded $s$. At a proper section the area is continuous at the threshold. In fact the projections of nearby sections into the old plane lie in shrinking neighborhoods of the old section by compactness, and convex combinations of the old section with an interior point on either side give the matching lower limit. For a strict full or empty cut no sections occur nearby. At a supporting section of zero measure the same compactness upper bound gives area tending to zero from inside; outside the area vanishes. Each old source section is proper, so the summed coefficient $S_{ij}$ is positive.

For the body change at the shifted threshold, use the signed radial shells of the uniform energy and covariance lemma with the cap indicator as integrand. For almost every old boundary point the indicator tends to that of the old cap, uniformly over the $O(t)$ radial shell and bounded threshold speeds. Indeed it is eventually constant whenever the point is off the old cutting plane. A proper plane meets the old boundary only in the relative boundary of its section, which has zero section measure. To see this, a supporting hyperplane at a relative-interior point of that section would have to contain the section plane, contradicting properness. Supporting sections of zero area contribute a null set, and strict cuts have no boundary point on their planes. The uniform radial bound and dominated convergence therefore give the shell contribution $-tL_{ij}+o(t)$ uniformly for bounded $s$. This proof includes dimension one with endpoint counting measure. Positive-area supporting sections can instead contribute a coupled shape and threshold term; the sufficient trace condition excludes them.

For bounded $\eta$, use the old threshold plus $t s$ with $s=-(L_{ij}+\eta)/S_{ij}$. The cap expansion and strict local inversion from the exact continuation show
$$
c_{ij}(t,Q+t\eta)
=c_{ij}-t\,\frac{L_{ij}+\eta}{S_{ij}}+o(t)
$$
uniformly on bounded $\eta$ intervals. To see the uniform error, perturb this proposed $s$ by any fixed positive and negative increment. The two masses then bracket $Q+t\eta$ for all small $t$ uniformly in the interval, and the increment can be arbitrarily small.

In the scalar budget equation the left side at $q=Q+t\eta$ is
$$
h_i-t\left[\sum_j\rho_{ij}L_{ij}/S_{ij}+B_i\eta\right]+o(t).
$$
The right side is $h_i+t\dot\alpha_i+o(t)$. For sufficiently large opposite fixed $\eta$ the left sides bracket it. Thus $q_i(t)=Q+O(t)$, and the uniform inverse expansion applied at $\eta=(q_i(t)-Q)/t$ gives the formula for $\dot q_i$. Expanding $M_t=M-tD+o(t)$ gives the normalized derivative. If every normalized derivative is negative, then each $q_i(t)/M_t<\beta$ for small $t$ on this same path. The finite exact cover bounds the new normalized maximum by the largest of those budgets.
