## Statement

Let $p_0,\ldots,p_7$ in order be $(-1,-2),(-1,-1),(-1,0),(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,2),(2,3)$, and put $Q=\operatorname{conv}\lbrace p_0,p_2,p_5,p_7\rbrace$. For each real $R\ge0$ and integer $d\ge1$, let the actual body over $Q$ have sections $(R+t(z)/8)\Delta_d$, where $\Delta_d$ is the standard unit simplex and $t(z)=\min\lbrace16+2z_1+3z_2,16+2z_1-4z_2,16-40z_1+24z_2\rbrace$. Let $T$ be the total mixed mass and $W_F$ the mass on $F=\lbrace2,3,4,5,6,7\rbrace$. Then $W_F/3>T/4$. Some actual fiber centroid over $F$ has normalized mixed depth at least $\gamma_d W_F/(3T)>\gamma_d/4$, where $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. When $R=1,d=2$, in units $|\Delta_2|/64$, the masses on $E=\lbrace0,1,2\rbrace$, $F$, and $E\cap F$ are $1101,2296,484$, and the total is $2913$.

## Proof

The minimum of the three affine functions is concave, and its values at the four vertices are at least eight. Hence the body defined by $z\in Q$, $x_j\ge0$, and $\sum_jx_j\le R+t(z)/8$ is compact, convex, and full-dimensional. By the integer census and heights in the declared eight-base upper-face lemma, its eight positive fibers have volumes $|\Delta_d|(R+t(p_i)/8)^d$ and centroids $(R+t(p_i)/8)\mathbf1/(d+1)$, where $\mathbf1$ is the vector of ones.

Write $s=R+1$. The complement of $F$ consists of indices $0,1$, with radii $s,s+3/8$. Dividing by $|\Delta_d|$ and collecting terms gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{W_F-3(T-W_F)}{|\Delta_d|}
={}&[(s+1)^d-(s+3/8)^d]+[(s+3/4)^d-(s+3/8)^d]\\
&+[(s+1/2)^d-(s+3/8)^d]+[(s+1/4)^d-s^d]>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each bracket is positive because $s\ge1$ and $d\ge1$. This is equivalent to $W_F/3>T/4$. At $R=1,d=2$ the eight weights in the stated units are $(256,361,484,576,400,256,324,256)$, giving the claimed masses.

The actual centroid graph is affinely isomorphic to the finite height graph, since the centroid is an affine function of $t$ with nonzero slope. The subset over $F$ therefore has relative Helly number three by the declared upper-face lemma. Apply accepted fact 6d6fcf377e68c79c to the finite closed centroid subset over $F$ with its positive atomic measure of mass $W_F$. It gives one mark over $F$ with containing-halfspace atomic weight at least $W_F/3$. The full centroid graph gives that mark at least as much atomic weight. The fibers are compact full-dimensional convex bodies with true centroids, so accepted fact c2180d0c4d7ea592 applies to the full graph with $\alpha=W_F/3\in(0,T]$ and gives raw mixed depth at least $\gamma_d W_F/3$. Dividing by $T$ proves the claim.
