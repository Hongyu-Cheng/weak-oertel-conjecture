## Statement

Retain one common canonical zero-fresh hard instance of accepted facts `6bfe50fed9a5540b`, `9d6f430dbb8bce7c`, `446c8f7967309a83`, and `b2d655373d3703a8`.  Thus \(r=(r_e)\) is the half-strength reservoir,
\[
 Q:=\sum_e r_e>0,
 \qquad
 \delta_z:=d_z(r)\leq \frac{a_z}{4},
\tag{1}
\]
and \(b=(b_e)\leq r\) is the accepted ancestry matching.  For
\[
 m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2}
 \quad(e=\{u,v\}),
\]
its target loads and total mass are
\[
 \lambda_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}b_e,
 \qquad
 \Lambda:=\sum_m\lambda_m=|b|>\frac{49}{50}Q.
\tag{2}
\]
The canonical half-metronoid edge bodies and positive-root masses are
\[
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
 \qquad
 L_e:=\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v),
\]
\[
 \vartheta_e:=2^{-(d+1)}
 \left(a_u^{1/d}+a_v^{1/d}\right)^d,
\tag{3}
\]
and accepted fact `6bfe50fed9a5540b` gives
\[
 L_e\subseteq\mathsf M_{\vartheta_e}(C_{m_e}),
 \qquad
 \sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{b_e}{\vartheta_e}\leq1
 \quad\text{for every }m.
\tag{4}
\]

Let \(s^{\mathrm{tar}}\) be the accepted off-diagonal target matching, with degrees \(d_m\) and mass
\[
 P_{\mathrm{tar}}:=|s^{\mathrm{tar}}|,
 \qquad
 \sum_m d_m=2P_{\mathrm{tar}},
\tag{5}
\]
so that
\[
 0\leq d_m\leq\lambda_m\leq\frac{a_m}{2},
 \qquad
 P_{\mathrm{tar}}>\frac{381}{800}Q.
\tag{6}
\]
Let \(\mathcal L^\square\) be the full degree-weighted whole-label family, let \(\theta_\sigma\) be its label masses, and put
\[
 \Theta:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal L^\square}\theta_\sigma,
\qquad
 R_z:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal L^\square}
 \theta_\sigma\operatorname{mult}_z(\mathcal O_\sigma).
\tag{7}
\]
All four tagged occurrences are counted, including repeated tags.  The accepted square ledgers give
\[
 R_z\leq2d_z\leq a_z,
 \qquad
 \sum_zR_z=4\Theta,
 \qquad
 \Theta\leq P_{\mathrm{tar}},
 \qquad
 4\Theta\leq2Q.
\tag{8}
\]

There is a simultaneous physical occurrence realization for which the exact complements have centroid barycenters.  Namely, for every occurrence \((\sigma,o)\) at \(z=y(\sigma,o)\), set
\[
 f_{\sigma,o}:=\frac{\theta_\sigma}{a_z}\mathbf1_{C_z}.
\tag{9}
\]
Then
\[
 F_z:=\sum_{(\sigma,o):y(\sigma,o)=z}f_{\sigma,o}
 =\frac{R_z}{a_z}\mathbf1_{C_z}\leq1,
\]
\[
 G_z:=\mathbf1_{C_z}-F_z
 =\frac{U_z}{a_z}\mathbf1_{C_z},
 \qquad
 U_z:=a_z-R_z,
\tag{10}
\]
and, whenever \(U_z>0\), the barycenter of \(G_z\) is the fiber centroid
\[
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx.
\tag{11}
\]

For \(\delta_z>0\), define
\[
 \varepsilon_z:=\frac{(2\delta_z-U_z)_+}{2\delta_z},
\tag{12}
\]
and put \(\varepsilon_z:=0\) when \(\delta_z=0\).  For \(e=\{u,v\}\), put
\[
 \zeta_e:=r_e(1-\varepsilon_u-\varepsilon_v)_+,
 \qquad
 \eta_e:=\min\{\zeta_e,b_e\}.
\tag{13}
\]
Then
\[
 2d_z(\eta)\leq U_z
 \quad\text{for every }z,
\tag{14}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 |\eta|:=\sum_e\eta_e
 \geq\Lambda-\Theta
 >\frac{12}{25}Q.}
\tag{15}
\]

This overlap has simultaneous source and target realizations.  For every incidence \(z\in e\) with \(\eta_e>0\), define
\[
 g_{e,z}:=\frac{\eta_e}{U_z}G_z.
\tag{16}
\]
Then \(g_{e,z}\) has mass \(\eta_e\), barycenter \(c_z\), and
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}g_{e,z}\leq\frac12G_z.
\tag{17}
\]
Writing
\[
 \bar c_e:=\frac{c_u+c_v}{2},
\tag{18}
\]
accepted fact `dfd25e72c1f45a4e` gives
\[
 \bar c_e\in\mathsf M_{\eta_e}(C_{m_e}).
\tag{19}
\]
More strongly, there are target densities \(k_e\) with mass \(\eta_e\) and barycenter \(\bar c_e\) such that
\[
 \sum_{e:m_e=m}k_e\leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_m
 \quad\text{for every }m.
\tag{20}
\]
Here and below zero-mass edges are assigned the zero density and are omitted from barycenter and max-affine packet families.
Consequently, by accepted fact `2c093df4eb8158a9`, every max-affine packing inequality holds for the packet family
\[
 \{(\eta_e,\bar c_e):m_e=m\}
\tag{21}
\]
at every ancestry target \(m\).

Put
\[
 h_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}\eta_e,
 \qquad
 K_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}k_e.
\tag{22}
\]
Thus \(0\leq K_m\leq1\), \(\int K_m=h_m\), and \(h_m\leq\lambda_m\).  The exact overlap of these jointly packed loads with the degree-weighted square-source capacities obeys
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_m\min\{h_m,d_m\}
 \geq2P_{\mathrm{tar}}-\Theta
 >\frac{381}{800}Q,}
\tag{23}
\]
and, with
\[
 \tau_m:=\min\{h_m,2d_m\},
\tag{24}
\]
one has
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_m\tau_m
 \geq4P_{\mathrm{tar}}-\Lambda-\Theta
 >\frac{81}{200}Q.}
\tag{25}
\]

The latter overlap is an actual marked part of one simultaneous selectable-source representation for the degree-weighted square atlas.  If \(h_m>0\), put
\[
 \widetilde K_m:=\frac{\tau_m}{h_m}K_m,
\tag{26}
\]
and put \(\widetilde K_m:=0\) when \(h_m=0\).  For \(a_m>\tau_m\), define
\[
 \alpha_m:=\frac{2d_m-\tau_m}{a_m-\tau_m},
 \qquad
 J_m:=\alpha_m(1-\widetilde K_m),
\tag{27}
\]
while, in the only remaining case \(a_m=\tau_m=2d_m\), put \(J_m:=0\).  Then
\[
 0\leq\alpha_m\leq1,
 \qquad
 P_m:=\widetilde K_m+J_m\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_m}P_m=2d_m.
\tag{28}
\]
For \(d_m>0\), let \(x_m\) be the barycenter of \(P_m\).  Then
\[
 x_m\in\mathsf M_{2d_m}(C_m)
 =\mathsf M_{4w_m^d}(C_m),
\tag{29}
\]
so \((x_m)_{d_m>0}\) is one legal simultaneous selectable-source choice for the full degree-weighted square atlas.  Its representing densities contain the jointly ancestry-packed marked subdensities \(\widetilde K_m\) of total mass
\[
 \sum_m\int_{C_m}\widetilde K_m
 =\sum_m\tau_m>\frac{81}{200}Q.
\tag{30}
\]
For every positive-mass edge, scaling each \(k_e\) and each corresponding pair of source densities \(g_{e,u},g_{e,v}\) by the common factor \(\tau_{m_e}/h_{m_e}\) realizes (30) with the same edgewise masses and first moments, preserves (17), and preserves the targetwise capacity (20).

No assertion is made that the marked density in (30) is already synchronized across all four occurrences of a positive whole square label, or that the resulting whole-label packets are jointly packed at their next physical targets.

## Proof

Equations (1)-(8) are the common canonical conclusions of accepted facts `6bfe50fed9a5540b`, `9d6f430dbb8bce7c`, `446c8f7967309a83`, and `b2d655373d3703a8`.  In particular, \(b\leq r\) gives
\[
 \Lambda\leq Q,
\tag{31}
\]
and the off-diagonal target matching gives \(\sum_md_m=2P_{\mathrm{tar}}\).  The exact color-collision identity in `b2d655373d3703a8` gives \(\Theta\leq P_{\mathrm{tar}}\), while `9d6f430dbb8bce7c` gives \(\Theta\leq Q/2\).

For (9), every positive occurrence at \(z\) has \(a_z>0\).  Its density has mass \(\theta_\sigma\) and barycenter \(c_z\).  Summing at \(z\) gives (10), because every repeated tag is a separate summand in \(R_z\).  The bound \(R_z\leq a_z\) proves the pointwise capacity.  Equation (11) follows because \(G_z\) is constant on \(C_z\).

We prove the clipping estimates.  Since \(0\leq U_z\leq a_z\) and \(2\delta_z\leq a_z/2\), one has \(0\leq\varepsilon_z\leq1\).  For an endpoint \(z\in e\),
\[
 (1-\varepsilon_u-\varepsilon_v)_+
 \leq1-\varepsilon_z.
\]
Therefore
\[
 d_z(\zeta)
 \leq(1-\varepsilon_z)\delta_z
 =\min\left\{\delta_z,\frac{U_z}{2}\right\},
\tag{32}
\]
which implies (14) because \(\eta\leq\zeta\).

Also
\[
 1-(1-a-b)_+\leq a+b
 \qquad(0\leq a,b\leq1),
\]
so
\[
 Q-|\zeta|
 \leq\sum_z\varepsilon_z\delta_z
 =\frac12\sum_z(2\delta_z-U_z)_+.
\tag{33}
\]
If \(y_z:=2\delta_z-U_z=2\delta_z-a_z+R_z>0\), then
\[
 2y_z=4\delta_z-2a_z+2R_z
 \leq-a_z+2R_z
 \leq R_z,
\tag{34}
\]
where the two inequalities use \(4\delta_z\leq a_z\) and \(R_z\leq a_z\), respectively.  Thus (33), (34), and \(\sum_zR_z=4\Theta\) give
\[
 |\zeta|\geq Q-\Theta.
\tag{35}
\]
Since \((\zeta_e-b_e)_+\leq r_e-b_e\),
\[
 |\eta|
 =|\zeta|-\sum_e(\zeta_e-b_e)_+
 \geq |\zeta|-(Q-\Lambda)
 \geq\Lambda-\Theta.
\tag{36}
\]
Using \(\Lambda>49Q/50\) and \(\Theta\leq Q/2\) proves (15).

If \(\eta_e>0\) and \(z\in e\), then (14) forces \(U_z>0\), so (16) is well-defined.  Equations (10)-(11) show that it has mass \(\eta_e\) and barycenter \(c_z\).  Summing (16) and using (14) gives
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}g_{e,z}
 =\frac{d_z(\eta)}{U_z}G_z
 \leq\frac12G_z,
\]
which is (17).  Applying the positive-root convexity theorem `dfd25e72c1f45a4e` to the two equal endpoint masses gives (19).

For the simultaneous target realization, the constant half-density on each endpoint fiber shows \(c_z\in\mathcal K_z\), so \(\bar c_e\in L_e\).  Choose a density \(\phi_e\) on \(C_{m_e}\) of mass \(\vartheta_e\) and barycenter \(\bar c_e\), as permitted by (4), and put
\[
 k_e:=\frac{\eta_e}{\vartheta_e}\phi_e.
\tag{37}
\]
Then \(k_e\) has the required mass and first moment.  Since \(0\leq\phi_e\leq1\), \(\eta_e\leq b_e\), and (4) holds,
\[
 \sum_{e:m_e=m}k_e
 \leq\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{\eta_e}{\vartheta_e}
 \leq\sum_{e:m_e=m}\frac{b_e}{\vartheta_e}
 \leq1.
\tag{38}
\]
This proves (20).  The max-affine conclusion follows from the simultaneous-packing implication of accepted fact `2c093df4eb8158a9`.

It remains to prove the two dimension-free overlap bounds and the extension.  From \(h_m\leq\lambda_m\) and \(d_m\leq\lambda_m\),
\[
 \min\{h_m,d_m\}
 =h_m+d_m-\max\{h_m,d_m\}
 \geq h_m+d_m-\lambda_m.
\]
After summing and using (15),
\[
 \sum_m\min\{h_m,d_m\}
 \geq |\eta|+2P_{\mathrm{tar}}-\Lambda
 \geq2P_{\mathrm{tar}}-\Theta.
\tag{39}
\]
Because \(\Theta\leq P_{\mathrm{tar}}\), equation (6) proves (23).

Similarly, \(h_m\leq\lambda_m\) and \(2d_m\leq2\lambda_m\) give
\[
 \tau_m=\min\{h_m,2d_m\}
 \geq h_m+2d_m-2\lambda_m.
\]
Hence
\[
 \sum_m\tau_m
 \geq |\eta|+4P_{\mathrm{tar}}-2\Lambda
 \geq4P_{\mathrm{tar}}-\Lambda-\Theta.
\tag{40}
\]
Using \(P_{\mathrm{tar}}>381Q/800\), \(\Lambda\leq Q\), and \(\Theta\leq Q/2\) gives
\[
 4P_{\mathrm{tar}}-\Lambda-\Theta
 >\left(\frac{381}{200}-1-\frac12\right)Q
 =\frac{81}{200}Q,
\]
which proves (25).

Finally, \(0\leq\widetilde K_m\leq1\) and \(\int\widetilde K_m=\tau_m\).  Since \(2d_m\leq a_m\), the quotient in (27) belongs to \([0,1]\) whenever its denominator is positive.  Direct integration gives \(\int J_m=2d_m-\tau_m\), and
\[
 P_m
 =\widetilde K_m+\alpha_m(1-\widetilde K_m)
 =\alpha_m+(1-\alpha_m)\widetilde K_m
 \leq1.
\]
This proves (28)-(29).  Scaling the edgewise decompositions of \(K_m\) gives the last assertion and preserves all inequalities because every scaling factor lies in \([0,1]\).  Summing their masses gives (30).
