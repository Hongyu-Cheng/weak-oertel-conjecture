## Statement

For the uniform menu of the $d+1$ canonical centroid caps of a $d$-simplex, use the preceding section projection notation, and put $a=d/(d+1)$ and $\beta=\Gamma_d=a^d$. Then $R(e)/(2B(e))=\Gamma_d/d$ in every unit direction. The bound $\beta\ge\sup_e R(e)/(2B(e))$ therefore has a factor $d$ loss on this menu for $d\ge2$, also when obtained by averaging directions. The universal coefficient $2$ in $R(e)\le2\min(\beta,1-\beta)B(e)$ is attained by a centered box with the two opposite coordinate caps of equal weight and the direction of their common axis.

## Proof

Write $\lambda_i$ for the barycentric coordinates. The canonical cap $\lambda_i\ge 1/(d+1)$ is a homothetic copy of the simplex of ratio $a$ and is critical by 700febf4b7a92759 at the equal-coordinate centroid. Thus it projects with ratio $a^{d-1}$ in every direction. On the old facet $\lambda_k=0$ only the other $d$ caps can contribute. Each cuts out a homothetic facet piece of $(d-1)$-area fraction $a^{d-1}$. The mean body selector at this fixed normal is therefore $d\,a^{d-1}/(d+1)=\beta$. This includes the endpoint counting case $d=1$. The normal test in the section projection bound holds, so its equality gives $R(e)/(2B(e))=a^{d-1}-a^d=a^d/d$. Since the ratio is constant in $e$, integrating both numerator and denominator gives the same value.

For a centered box, the two opposite coordinate caps through its center have volume fraction $1/2$ and are critical by reflection symmetry. Their body selectors average to $1/2$ almost everywhere on the old boundary. With unit continuous normals along the axis of the two caps, both new sections project onto the entire box shadow in that direction. Thus $R(e)=B(e)$ and $\beta=1/2$, so equality holds.
