## Statement

Consider finitely many actual positive $d$-fibers cut by one affine margin with unit continuous normal. Let $c_z,h_z,S_z$ be its cap volume, hinge integral, and effective coarea section mass at base $z$, with full density on proper sections and a density between zero and one on supporting sections. Put $I=\{z:S_z>0\}$, $S=\sum_I S_z>0$, $C=\sum_I c_z$, $h_*=\sum_{z\notin I}h_z$, and $\delta_z=c_z/S_z$ on $I$. Then
$$
\sum_z h_z\le h_*+\frac{d}{d+1}
 \left[\frac{C^2}{S}+\sum_I S_z(\delta_z-C/S)^2\right],
$$
where the bracket equals $\sum_I c_z^2/S_z$. Equality holds if every local positive-section hinge bound is attained. Suppose also $C>0$ and every positive cap volume has positive $S_z$. Give $I$ probabilities $\pi_z=S_z/S$, let $\bar z_s$ and $\bar z_c$ be the section and cap base means, let $\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}_\pi(Z)$ and $v=\operatorname{Cov}_\pi(Z,\delta)$. Then $v=(C/S)(\bar z_c-\bar z_s)\in\operatorname{range}\Sigma$ and
$$
\operatorname{Var}_\pi(\delta)=v^{\mathsf T}\Sigma^+v+
 \mathbb E_\pi[(\delta-C/S-(\Sigma^+v)\cdot(Z-\bar z_s))^2].
$$
If a contact measure has the same mass and base first moment as the cap measure plus $r\ge0$ times the section measure, its base mean is $(C\bar z_c+rS\bar z_s)/(C+rS)$. Equality of all three base means forces $v=0$, and if $\delta$ is affine in the base on $I$ it then forces zero variance.

## Proof

On each proper trace apply the positive-half survival bound of 17087f3e780e00fb with $U=c_z$, $\sigma=S_z$. Its integral gives $h_z\le dc_z^2/[(d+1)S_z]$. A full lower-support trace follows by taking proper thresholds to support, using the accepted parallel section limit and dominated hinge convergence, then reducing the section mass to its effective value. An upper-support empty trace has zero hinge and volume. The indices with $S_z=0$ contribute exactly $h_*$. Expanding the weighted square proves
$$
\sum_I S_z\delta_z^2=\frac{C^2}{S}+\sum_I S_z(\delta_z-C/S)^2
$$
because $\sum_I S_z\delta_z=C$, so summing the local inequalities gives the aggregate bound.

Under the extra hypotheses, $\mathbb E_\pi\delta=C/S$ and $\mathbb E_\pi[Z\delta]=(C/S)\bar z_c$, so the covariance identity follows. A vector in $\ker\Sigma$ pairs with $Z-\bar z_s$ to zero on the support of $\pi$, hence it pairs with $v$ to zero. Thus $\Sigma\Sigma^+v=v$. Expanding the square of the centered residual with coefficient $\Sigma^+v$ gives the stated decomposition. The contact mean formula follows by dividing its prescribed base moment by its mass. Equal cap and section means give $v=0$. If $\delta$ is affine on $I$, its centered form lies in the span of the coordinates of $Z-\bar z_s$, so this form is identically zero when its covariance with all these coordinates is zero.
