## Statement

Take the three actual integer fibers $K_z=[-1,1]^2$ at $z=-1,0,1$, with summed boundary area $\eta$ marked by the outer normal. Let $W$ be the mean boundary incidence of the eight equally weighted closed ambient caps $az+v\cdot X\ge-1/3$, where $a=\pm2$ and $v\in\{\pm e_1,\pm e_2\}$. Set $\beta=19/36$. There is a physical same-normal martingale from $\nu=(W/\beta)\eta$ to $\eta$. For $L(z,X)=1-\varepsilon|X_1|$, $0<\varepsilon<1$, the dissipation $\int L\,d(\nu-\eta)$ is $2\varepsilon/19$ on each of the two horizontal faces with normals $\pm e_2$, and zero on the vertical faces with normals $\pm e_1$. The corresponding $\int L(W-\beta)\,d\eta$ is $\varepsilon/18$ on each horizontal face and $\varepsilon/9$ in total.

## Proof

The body is the product $[-1,1]\times[-1,1]^2$, so the stated fibers are actual. At either endpoint base exactly half the caps contain the entire square. On a middle edge write $X=n+te$ with outward coordinate normal $n$ and unit tangent $e$, $-1\le t\le1$. The normal cap includes the edge, its opposite misses it, and among the two tangent caps both include the central interval $|t|<1/3$ and only one includes either outside interval. The two base-slope choices coincide at this base, so $W=3/4$ in the central interval and $W=1/2$ elsewhere, up to finitely many area-null points.

The source density is therefore $18/19$ on all edges, plus $9/19$ in the central third of each middle edge. Send the common $18/19$ portion identically. Split the remaining middle source at tangent $t$ into equal thirds and send them to $(0,n+3te),(-1,n+3te),(1,n-3te)$. The full physical barycenter is $(0,n+te)$ and all endpoints retain the same normal. Each branch contributes density $1/19$ on its full target edge by the linear change of variables. This gives the prescribed target $\eta$.

After summing over the three bases at any fixed normal, the tangent density difference is $[-3/19+(9/19)\mathbf1_{\{|t|<1/3\}}]\,dt$ and has total mass zero. At normals $\pm e_2$, $L=1-\varepsilon|t|$. The integrals of $|t|$ on $[-1,1]$ and $[-1/3,1/3]$ are $1$ and $1/9$. Substituting gives $2\varepsilon/19$. At normals $\pm e_1$, $L$ is constant, giving zero. Multiplication by $\beta$ yields the two scaled claims. Each of the eight caps has volume $4+2(4/3)=20/3$ out of $12$, so the surface normalization $19/36$ differs from their raw mixed volume fraction $5/9$.
