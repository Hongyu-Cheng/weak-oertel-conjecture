## Statement

At bases $-1,0,1$ put $K_-=K_+=[-1,1]\times[-3,0]$ and
$K_0=\operatorname{conv}\{(-2,0),(2,0),(2,-1),(1,-3),(-1,-3),(-2,-1)\}$.
These are exactly the integer fibers of the convex hull of their lifts. They have areas $(6,10,6)$ and centroids $(0,-3/2),(0,-4/3),(0,-3/2)$. For every nonempty proper closed base minor $T=P\cap\operatorname{conv}(T)$, with $P=\{-1,0,1\}$ and $k(T)$ the number of its parities, $\sum_{z\in T}|K_z|<22k(T)/2$.

Let $D_z$ be the fiber at $z$ of the convex hull of the three placed centroids. For every $s\ge0$ each $(1+s)h_{K_z}-s h_{D_z}$ is a support function. For every $s>0$ the reverse family fails the upward anchored support inequality at base zero by exactly $-s/6$.

## Proof

The middle hexagon contains the common endpoint rectangle. In a convex combination at zero the two endpoint weights are equal, so the middle section stays in $K_0$. Extremality gives the two endpoint sections, and there are no other integer bases. The horizontal width of $K_0$ at height $t$ is $4$ on $[-1,0]$ and $t+5$ on $[-3,-1]$. Its area and vertical first moment are $10$ and $-40/3$, respectively, by integration, and symmetry gives zero horizontal mean. The rectangle's area and centroid follow directly. The proper closed minors are the three singletons and the two adjacent pairs. A singleton has area at most $10<11$ and an adjacent pair has area $16<22$.

At the endpoints $D_z$ is the corresponding centroid point, and $D_0=\{0\}\times[-3/2,-4/3]$ by taking equal endpoint coefficients in the centroid hull. The endpoint reverse supports are those of the homothetic expansions of the rectangle about its centroid by $1+s$. At zero consider the hexagon with vertices given by the two signs in each of
$$
(\pm2(1+s),4s/3),\qquad
(\pm2(1+s),-1+s/2),\qquad
(\pm(1+s),-3-3s/2).
$$
It has the same six outer normal rays as $K_0$ for all $s\ge0$: the vertical side length is $1+5s/6>0$, and the other side vectors are scaled by $1+s$. On the upper, horizontal, downward oblique and lower normals $(0,1),(\pm1,0),(\pm2,-1),(0,-1)$, its support values are respectively $4s/3,2(1+s),5+7s/2,3+3s/2$. These are exactly $(1+s)h_{K_0}-s h_{D_0}$ at those normals, and all the functions are linear on the common fan cones. Thus every proposed fiber individually exists. The upward support at zero is $4s/3$, whereas both endpoint supports are $3s/2$. The necessary midpoint inclusion fails by $4s/3-3s/2=-s/6$.
