## Statement

Let $A,B,K$ be positive convex bodies in $\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge2$, with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$ and $y\in\operatorname{int}K$. Use the centered projective parameter $a$, its inverse-power tangent continuation $f$, and $H(n)=h_{K-y}(n)$. Choose continuous centers $c_A(n),c_B(n)$ of midpoint $y$. Erode each input $L=A,B$ by support cuts with field $H(n)f(1+a\cdot(x-c_L(n)))$, and $K$ by its projective gauge cut about $y$. For small $\varepsilon\ge0$ the bodies are nested, positive and exactly midpoint-compatible. At a common active facet normal with positive endpoint facet areas, use the weighted total and inward-captured masses $a_0,b_0,m_0$ and $U_A,U_B,U_K$ of the harmonic facet-credit lemma with these centers. The combined first total loss contributed by these facets is $H(n)(a_0+b_0+m_0)$, and their first loss in the same original ambient cap is at least $H(n)[U_K+\operatorname{Credit}(m_0-U_K)]$.
