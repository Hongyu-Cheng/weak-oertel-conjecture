## Statement

Let $(w_i)$ be a finite nonnegative concave sequence on consecutive integers, with $M=\sum_i w_i>0$ and integer weighted mean $j=\sum_i i w_i/M$. Then
$$
\min\left\{\sum_{i<j} w_i,\sum_{i>j}w_i\right\}\ge \frac37(M-w_j).
$$
The factor $3/7$ is optimal for each open tail.

## Proof

The positive support is consecutive by concavity. Discard zero endpoints and translate the positive support to $0,\ldots,L$ with integer mean $a$. A singleton gives equality. Otherwise the mean is interior, so $L\ge2$ and $1\le a\le L-1$.

Write $A_i=(L-i)/L$, $B_i=i/L$ and $T^j_i=\min\{i/j,(L-i)/(L-j)\}$ for $1\le j<L$. These generate the nonnegative concave cone: after subtracting the endpoint chord, a sequence is the sum of its nonnegative negative second differences times $G_j(i)=\min(i,j)-ij/L=j(L-j)T_i^j/L$. This follows because $G_j$ vanishes at the endpoints and has negative second difference one at $j$ and zero elsewhere. The means of $A,B,T^j$ are $(L-1)/3,(2L+1)/3,(L+j)/3$ respectively, with masses $(L+1)/2,(L+1)/2,L/2$, by summing the affine pieces. Thus $L-1\le3a\le2L+1$.

For any generator $v$, put $D(v)=\sum_i(i-a)v_i$ and $F(v)=7\sum_{i>a}v_i-3(\sum_i v_i-v_a)$. If $3a=2L+1$, only $B$ can appear with positive coefficient, and summation gives $F(B)=(4L+5)(L-1)/(9L)\ge0$. Otherwise put $b=2L+1-3a$, $R=L-a$, $A_0=4L(L+1)-7a^2-a$ and $\lambda=3A_0/[L(L+1)b]$. The value of $A_0$ is positive since it decreases with $a\ge0$ and its value at $(2L+1)/3$ is $2(4L+5)(L-1)/9>0$. Direct summation gives $F(B)=\lambda D(B)$. The residuals for $A$ and the two tent pieces have the following numerators with the indicated positive denominators.
$$
\begin{array}{c|c|l}
v & \text{denominator} & \text{numerator}\\ \hline
A &2Lb &P_0=4L^3-28L^2a-4L^2+63La^2+13La-42a^3-6a^2\\
T^j,\ j\le a &2(L-j)(L+1)b&
P=(R(7R-1)-3L(L-j))(L+1)b-(L-j)A_0(L+j-3a)\\
T^j,\ j\ge a &2j(L+1)b&
P_+=(4Lj-a(7a+1))(L+1)b-jA_0(L+j-3a).
\end{array}
$$
For example, the two tent expressions for $F$ are $R(7R-1)/(2(L-j))-3L/2$ and $2L-a(7a+1)/(2j)$, and $D(T^j)=L(L+j-3a)/6$. The polynomial $P_+$ is concave in $j$ since its quadratic coefficient is $-A_0$. At $j=a$ it equals $a(R+1)$ times $14(a-L/2-5/28)^2+(28L^2-28L-81)/56$, positive for $L\ge3$. At $j=L-1$ it equals $2[4L^3-4L-3a(L-a)(7a+1)]$. Since $a^2(L-a)\le4L^3/27$ and $a(L-a)\le L^2/4$, the bracket is at least $8L^3/9-3L^2/4-4L>0$ for $L\ge3$. If $L=2$, the sole case $a=j=1$ gives zero.

For $P_0$ and $P$ use the following finite exact certificate. When $L\ge11$, substitute $L=11+x$, $a=(L-1+(L+2)t)/3$ and $j=1+(a-1)u$ with $x\ge0$ and $(t,u)\in[0,1]^2$. These substitutions cover the required ranges. For each rectangle listed below map its $t,u$ coordinates affinely to $[0,1]^2$. If $c_{kij}$ are the resulting coefficients of $x^k t^i u^j$, then the Bernstein coefficients at degree $k$ in $x$ are
$$
B_{kIJ}=\sum_{i\le I,\ j\le J} c_{kij}
\frac{\binom I i}{\binom{d_t}i}\frac{\binom J j}{\binom{d_u}j}.
$$
Here $d_t,d_u$ are the degrees in $t,u$. This identity follows by expanding each monomial in the Bernstein basis in each variable. Thus a nonnegative minimum for every $k$ suffices. For $P_0$ the minima for $k=0,\ldots,3$ on the whole square are $(65/9,161/9,25/9,1/9)$. For $P$, the degrees are $(4,2)$ and the minima for $k=0,\ldots,4$ are as follows.

| $t$ interval | $u$ interval | $k=0$ | $k=1$ | $k=2$ | $k=3$ | $k=4$ |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $[0,1/4]$ | $[0,1/2]$ | $4904269/9216$ | $1345403/6912$ | $24071/768$ | $7715/3456$ | $221/3456$ |
| $[1/4,1/2]$ | $[0,1/2]$ | $6713755/13824$ | $1617115/6912$ | $196775/4608$ | $8057/2304$ | $8051/82944$ |
| $[0,1/2]$ | $[1/2,1]$ | $496429/864$ | $196495/864$ | $10501/288$ | $2317/864$ | $1/16$ |
| $[1/2,1]$ | $[0,1/2]$ | $99693/64$ | $20861/32$ | $807/8$ | $219/32$ | $11/64$ |
| $[1/2,1]$ | $[1/2,1]$ | $61281/64$ | $5867/16$ | $1677/32$ | $53/16$ | $5/64$ |

For $L=2,\ldots,10$, evaluate $P_0$ at each integer $1\le a<L$ with $L-1\le3a<2L+1$, and $P$ at the same $a$ and each integer $1\le j\le a$. The (number of entries, minimum) pairs in order are $(2,0),(5,0),(5,0),(7,0),(12,12),(12,18),(15,76),(22,54),(22,72)$. Both certificates can be checked by the displayed substitutions and exact coefficients. [Exact verifier](../assets/pk-nov6/computations/verify_sharp_open_tail_concave_sequence.py).

Thus $F(v)\ge\lambda D(v)$ for every generator in the case $b>0$. Sum with the cone coefficients and use $D(w)=0$. This proves the right-tail inequality, and reflection proves the left one. For $w=(4,3,2,1)$ at $(-1,0,1,2)$, the first moment is $-4+2+2=0$, the right tail is $3$ and the noncentral mass is $7$. Reflection yields the other sharpness example.
