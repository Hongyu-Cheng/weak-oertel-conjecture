## Statement

For a positive-mass convex body \(B\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\), write
\[
B_z:=\{x:(z,x)\in B\},\qquad b_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(B_z),
\qquad W(B):=\sum_zb_z,
\]
\[
G(B):=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(B),\qquad
H(B):=\frac{2^nh_{\max}(B)}{W(B)},\qquad
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\]
An off-diagonal fractional same-parity matching \(r=(r_e)\) in \(B\) is called free if
\[
d_z(r):=\sum_{e\ni z}r_e\leq \frac{b_z}{2}
\quad\text{for every }z,
\]
and strong if
\[
d_z(r)\leq\gamma_db_z
\quad\text{for every }z.
\]
Its mass is \(|r|:=\sum_er_e\). Since \(e^{-1}<\gamma_d\leq1/2\), every strong matching is free.

Let \(C_0=C\) be a positive-mass mixed convex body, let \(p_0=p\) be a nonzero free same-parity matching in \(C\), and put \(P:=|p|\). For each current pair \((C_j,p_j)\), put
\[
E_j:=\{z:d_z(p_j)>0\},\qquad t_{j,z}:=2d_z(p_j)\quad(z\in E_j),
\]
choose an integral centerpoint \(m_j\in\operatorname{conv}E_j\) for the finite measure
\(\sum_{z\in E_j}t_{j,z}\delta_z\), and call
\[
\bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda_{m_j}(E_j)}
\sum_{z\in E_j}\lambda_z
\mathsf M_{t_{j,z}}((C_j)_z)
\]
the degree-saturated core at stage \(j\). For every \(L\geq1\), exactly the following finite-horizon alternative is available.

Either, for some \(0\leq j<L\), the degree-saturated core at stage \(j\) is nonempty, where \(p_j=\gamma_d^jp\), and then
\[
G(C)\geq4\gamma_d^jP,
\qquad
h_{\max}(C)\geq4\gamma_d^{j+1}2^{-n}P,
\]
or there is a nested chain
\[
C=C_0\supseteq C_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq C_L
\]
of full-dimensional individual arbitrary-selected-mass cap bodies or positive same-base reweightings such that, for \(0\leq j\leq L\),
\[
p_j=\gamma_d^jp,\qquad
\operatorname{supp}p_j=\operatorname{supp}p,
\qquad p_j\text{ is free in }C_j,
\]
\[
W(C_j)\geq4\gamma_d^jP.
\]
In particular, the sharp off-by-one implication is
\[
G(C)<4\gamma_d^{L-1}P
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{all stages }j=0,\ldots,L-1\text{ recycle, producing }C_L.
\]

Now let \(C_{k,0}=C_k\subseteq\mathbb R^{n_k}\times\mathbb R^{d_k}\) be a sequence with
\[
W_k:=W(C_k)>0,
\qquad
H_{k,0}:=\frac{2^{n_k}h_{\max}(C_k)}{W_k}\longrightarrow0.
\]
At level \(0\), choose an exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching \(p_{k,0}=q_k\) in the normalization of accepted fact `11e347c295e9430b`. Explicitly, if \(a_{k,z}:=\operatorname{vol}_{d_k}((C_k)_z)\), then there are \(u_{k,z}\geq0\) with
\[
\gamma_{d_k}a_{k,z}=u_{k,z}+d_z(q_k),
\]
and, in every parity class \(\xi\), the total unmatched mass is
\[
\sum_{z\equiv\xi}u_{k,z}
=\max\left\{2\max_{z\equiv\xi}\gamma_{d_k}a_{k,z}
-\sum_{z\equiv\xi}\gamma_{d_k}a_{k,z},0\right\}.
\]
Here both the maximum and the sum are zero for an empty parity class.

At a current fixed level \(\ell\), write
\[
M_{k,\ell}:=W(C_{k,\ell}),\qquad
P_{k,\ell}:=|p_{k,\ell}|,
\qquad
H_{k,\ell}:=
\frac{2^{n_k}h_{\max}(C_{k,\ell})}{M_{k,\ell}}.
\]
All fiber objects in the following definitions belong to the current body \(C_{k,\ell}\). For its positive fiber of volume \(a_z\), let
\[
\mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}((C_{k,\ell})_z),
\qquad c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{(C_{k,\ell})_z}x\,dx,
\qquad D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z.
\]
For \(e=\{z,z'\}\in\operatorname{supp}p_{k,\ell}\), put
\[
m_e:=\frac{z+z'}2,
\quad \bar c_e:=\frac{c_z+c_{z'}}2,
\quad D_e:=\frac{D_z+D_{z'}}2,
\quad L_e:=\bar c_e+D_e.
\]
Define the current weighted affine defect
\[
\Delta_{k,\ell}:=
\min_{s\ {\rm affine}}
\sum_e p_{k,\ell,e}
\bigl(\|s(m_e)-\bar c_e\|_{D_e}-1\bigr)_+.
\]
Choose a primal optimizer in this minimum. Among the maximizers of
\[
-\sum_eh_{L_e}(v_e)
\]
subject to
\[
\sum_ev_e=0,
\qquad
\sum_ev_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
\qquad
h_{D_e}(v_e)\leq p_{k,\ell,e},
\]
choose one minimizing its gauge load, and set
\[
\omega_{k,\ell,e}:=h_{D_e}(v_e),
\qquad
\Omega_{k,\ell}:=\sum_e\omega_{k,\ell,e}.
\]
Let \(\ell_e\in L_e\) be the associated support contact point from accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`, and define
\[
\sigma_{k,\ell}:=\frac12\sum_e
\omega_{k,\ell,e}\delta_{(m_e,\ell_e)},
\]
\[
\rho_{k,\ell}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}
\{m_e:\omega_{k,\ell,e}>0\},
\]
\[
r_{k,\ell}:=
\min_{\substack{s:\operatorname{aff}\{m_e:\omega_{k,\ell,e}>0\}
\to\mathbb R^{d_k}\\ s\ {\rm affine}}}
\dim\operatorname{span}
\{\ell_e-s(m_e):\omega_{k,\ell,e}>0\}.
\]

For every fixed \(L\), for all sufficiently large \(k\) one can construct
\[
C_{k,0}\supseteq C_{k,1}\supseteq\cdots\supseteq C_{k,L}
\]
and nonzero strong matchings \(p_{k,\ell}\) as follows. At every level \(\ell<L\), the current degree-saturated core is empty, one chooses any individual arbitrary-selected-mass cap body or positive same-base reweighting furnished by accepted fact `07009c5047a9f7bb`, and one sets
\[
\boxed{p_{k,\ell+1,e}:=2\gamma_{d_k}^2\omega_{k,\ell,e}.}
\]
If \(b_{k,\ell+1,z}:=\operatorname{vol}_{d_k}((C_{k,\ell+1})_z)\), then the exact endpoint bounds are
\[
d_z(p_{k,\ell+1})
\leq2\gamma_{d_k}^2d_z(p_{k,\ell})
\leq\gamma_{d_k}b_{k,\ell+1,z}
\leq\frac{b_{k,\ell+1,z}}2.
\]
Thus the current nested-metronoid affine-defect and strong half-contact theorems may be reapplied at the next level after recomputing its fibers, centroids, gauges, primal and dual optimizers, and contacts.

For every fixed \(0\leq\ell\leq L\),
\[
\liminf_{k\to\infty}
\frac{P_{k,\ell}}{W_k}
\geq
\frac1{2e}\left(\frac2{e^2}\right)^\ell,
\qquad
\liminf_{k\to\infty}
\frac{M_{k,\ell}}{W_k}
\geq
\left(\frac2{e^2}\right)^\ell,
\]
and
\[
H_{k,\ell}\longrightarrow0.
\]
For the current load-minimal dual,
\[
\frac{\Omega_{k,\ell}}{P_{k,\ell}}\longrightarrow1,
\qquad
\liminf_{k\to\infty}
\frac{\Delta_{k,\ell}}{P_{k,\ell}}
\geq\frac1{\log2}-1.
\]
The measure \(\sigma_{k,\ell}\) is halfspace-dominated by the mixed-volume measure of \(C_{k,\ell}\), and
\[
\liminf_{k\to\infty}
\frac{|\sigma_{k,\ell}|}{M_{k,\ell}}
\geq
\frac1{4e}\left(\frac2{e^2}\right)^\ell>0.
\]
Moreover \(\rho_{k,\ell}\leq n_k\),
\[
H_{k,\ell}(r_{k,\ell}+1)
\geq\frac{\Omega_{k,\ell}}{2M_{k,\ell}},
\]
and therefore
\[
r_{k,\ell}+1\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

There are two separate companion clauses.

First, let \(B_k\subseteq\mathbb R^{n_k}\times\mathbb R^{d_k}\) be any positive-mass sequence with \(H(B_k)\to0\). One may discard every previously transported matching and choose a fresh exact minimum-unmatched centroid matching \(q_k^B\) in \(B_k\). If \(Q_k^B:=|q_k^B|\) and \(\Omega_k^B\) is its current load-minimal dual mass, then
\[
\frac{Q_k^B}{W(B_k)}\geq\frac1{2e}-o(1),
\qquad
\frac{\Omega_k^B}{Q_k^B}\to1,
\qquad
\frac{\Omega_k^B}{W(B_k)}\geq\frac1{2e}-o(1).
\]
Its degree-saturated core is empty for all sufficiently large \(k\). Every cap body or positive same-base reweighting \(B_k^+\) selected from the empty-core branch satisfies
\[
\frac{W(B_k^+)}{W(B_k)}\geq\frac2{e^2}-o(1),
\qquad
H(B_k^+)\to0.
\]
The sharp defect, half-contact, and residual-rank conclusions above apply to \(q_k^B\), and they restart in \(B_k^+\) after choosing another fresh exact matching. This fresh matching is not the inherited matching \(2\gamma_{d_k}^2\omega_k^B\).

Second, let \(B\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a positive-mass convex body with fiber volumes \(b_z\), and define its entire positive-fiber index set by
\[
E:=D_+(B):=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:b_z>0\}.
\]
Fix \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E\), and put
\[
h_z(v):=h_{\mathsf M_{b_z/2}(B_z)}(v)
\quad(z\in E),
\qquad
\phi_m(v):=\min_{\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)}
\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_zh_z(v).
\]
Take \(v\ne0\) and an affine \(\ell:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R\) satisfying the exact-minorant conditions
\[
\ell(z)\leq h_z(v)\quad(z\in E),
\qquad \ell(m)=\phi_m(v),
\]
and put
\[
A:=B\cap\{(z,x):\langle v,x\rangle\geq\ell(z)\}.
\]
Then
\[
\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z)\geq\frac{\gamma_d}{2}b_z
\quad(z\in\mathbb Z^n),
\qquad
W(A)\geq\frac{\gamma_d}{2}W(B).
\]
If \(r\) is strong in \(B\), then \((\gamma_d/2)r\) is strong in \(A\). If \(r\) is merely free in \(B\), then \(\gamma_d^2r\) is strong in \(A\). For a sequence of such caps \(A_k\subseteq B_k\),
\[
H(A_k)\leq\frac2{\gamma_{d_k}}H(B_k)\leq2eH(B_k).
\]
Hence every such cap of a hard sequence is again hard and permits the fresh exact-matching restart in the preceding clause. Any construction that supplies data satisfying the displayed exact-minorant conditions is an application of this local clause. No centroid, gauge, dual vector, affine defect, contact point, contact domination, or optimality assertion is inherited into \(A_k\).

In the main hereditary tower, only the edge weights \(p_{k,\ell+1}=2\gamma_{d_k}^2\omega_{k,\ell}\) are transported. All other objects are current-body objects and are recomputed. The constructed bodies are arbitrary-selected-mass cap bodies or positive same-base reweightings. They need not be legal half-mass descendants. No bounded stopping, primitive localization, or global stress-absorption conclusion is asserted.

## Proof

The elementary inequalities
\[
\left(1+\frac1d\right)^d\geq2,
\qquad
\left(1+\frac1d\right)^d<e
\]
give
\[
e^{-1}<\gamma_d\leq\frac12.
\tag{1}
\]
If \(A\subseteq B\), then every mixed lattice point of \(A\) is one of \(B\), and for every closed halfspace the mixed mass cut from \(A\) is at most the mixed mass cut from \(B\). Consequently
\[
h_{\max}(A)\leq h_{\max}(B).
\tag{2}
\]
Accepted fact `4e1586f0ec77dc6b` gives the complete-core inclusion direction
\[
G(A)\leq G(B).
\tag{3}
\]
These are the two inclusion directions used below.

We first prove the finite-horizon statement. Since \(p\) is free,
\[
2P=\sum_zd_z(p)\leq\frac12W(C),
\]
so \(W(C)\geq4P\), which is the level-zero mass estimate. Suppose inductively that \(p_j=\gamma_d^jp\) is free in \(C_j\). The selected masses \(t_{j,z}=2d_z(p_j)\) have total mass
\[
\sum_zt_{j,z}=2\sum_zd_z(p_j)=4|p_j|=4\gamma_d^jP.
\tag{4}
\]
Apply accepted fact `07009c5047a9f7bb` to the current body and matching. If the degree-saturated core is nonempty, that fact gives
\[
G(C_j)\geq4|p_j|=4\gamma_d^jP.
\]
Since \(C_j\subseteq C\), (3) gives
\[
G(C)\geq4\gamma_d^jP.
\tag{5}
\]
The complete-core master bound in accepted fact `ae0161dd5d4ea672` then gives
\[
h_{\max}(C)
\geq\gamma_d2^{-n}G(C)
\geq4\gamma_d^{j+1}2^{-n}P.
\tag{6}
\]

If the degree-saturated core is empty, accepted fact `07009c5047a9f7bb` supplies any asserted descendant \(C_{j+1}\subseteq C_j\), with fiber volumes satisfying
\[
\operatorname{vol}_d((C_{j+1})_z)
\geq2\gamma_dd_z(p_j)
\tag{7}
\]
at every endpoint, and
\[
W(C_{j+1})\geq4\gamma_d|p_j|
=4\gamma_d^{j+1}P.
\tag{8}
\]
Set \(p_{j+1}=\gamma_dp_j\). Then
\[
d_z(p_{j+1})=\gamma_dd_z(p_j)
\leq\frac12\operatorname{vol}_d((C_{j+1})_z),
\]
so \(p_{j+1}\) is free. Since \(\gamma_d>0\), its support equals the support of \(p\). This proves the induction and the finite alternative.

If \(j<L\), then \(j\leq L-1\), and (1) gives
\[
\gamma_d^j\geq\gamma_d^{L-1}.
\]
Thus (5) is incompatible with
\(G(C)<4\gamma_d^{L-1}P\). Every one of the \(L\) stages
\(j=0,\ldots,L-1\) must therefore take the empty-core branch. This proves the stated off-by-one threshold.

We turn to the hereditary tower. For brevity, all limits in the next argument are as \(k\to\infty\) with the level \(\ell\) fixed. No convergence of \(n_k\), \(d_k\), or \(\gamma_{d_k}\) is assumed.

At level zero, accepted fact `11e347c295e9430b` gives the exact minimum-unmatched matching and the estimates
\[
P_{k,0}\geq
\frac{\gamma_{d_k}W_k-R_{\rm sel}(C_k)}2,
\qquad
h_{\max}(C_k)\geq2^{-n_k}R_{\rm sel}(C_k).
\tag{9}
\]
The second estimate says
\[
\frac{R_{\rm sel}(C_k)}{W_k}\leq H_{k,0}=o(1).
\]
Using (1) in the first estimate gives
\[
\frac{P_{k,0}}{W_k}\geq\frac1{2e}-o(1).
\tag{10}
\]
The exact endpoint identity in the statement gives
\[
d_z(p_{k,0})\leq\gamma_{d_k}a_{k,z},
\tag{11}
\]
so \(p_{k,0}\) is strong and, by (1), free. It is nonzero for all sufficiently large \(k\). Thus the induction starts with
\[
\liminf\frac{M_{k,0}}{W_k}=1,
\qquad
\liminf\frac{P_{k,0}}{W_k}\geq\frac1{2e}.
\tag{12}
\]

Assume the construction has reached a fixed level \(\ell\), and put
\[
\alpha_\ell:=\frac1{2e}\left(\frac2{e^2}\right)^\ell,
\qquad
\beta_\ell:=\left(\frac2{e^2}\right)^\ell.
\]
The induction hypotheses are
\[
\liminf\frac{P_{k,\ell}}{W_k}\geq\alpha_\ell,
\qquad
\liminf\frac{M_{k,\ell}}{W_k}\geq\beta_\ell,
\tag{13}
\]
and \(p_{k,\ell}\) is strong. Nesting gives
\(M_{k,\ell}\leq W_k\). By (2),
\[
H_{k,\ell}
=\frac{2^{n_k}h_{\max}(C_{k,\ell})}{M_{k,\ell}}
\leq
H_{k,0}\frac{W_k}{M_{k,\ell}}
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{14}
\]
Also
\[
\liminf\frac{P_{k,\ell}}{M_{k,\ell}}
\geq\alpha_\ell>0.
\tag{15}
\]

We next prove current-load saturation without treating the inherited matching as a fresh exact one. Let
\(R_{C_{k,\ell}}(p_{k,\ell})\) be the exactly affine-retainable mass from accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`. That fact gives
\[
0\leq P_{k,\ell}-\Omega_{k,\ell}
\leq R_{C_{k,\ell}}(p_{k,\ell}).
\tag{16}
\]
Its realizability characterization gives
\[
R_{C_{k,\ell}}(p_{k,\ell})
\leq\mathfrak M_{\rm aff}(C_{k,\ell}).
\]
The affine matching certificate in accepted fact `8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5` gives
\[
\frac{R_{C_{k,\ell}}(p_{k,\ell})}{M_{k,\ell}}
\leq\frac{H_{k,\ell}}{\gamma_{d_k}}
\leq eH_{k,\ell}=o(1).
\tag{17}
\]
Divide (16) by \(P_{k,\ell}\) and use (15). We obtain
\[
\frac{\Omega_{k,\ell}}{P_{k,\ell}}\longrightarrow1.
\tag{18}
\]

For the affine-defect margin, fix any \(a\in(0,1/e)\) and define
\[
\psi(a):=-\frac{1-a}{a}\log(1-a),
\qquad
\delta_a:=\frac{\psi(a)}{\log2}-1.
\]
By (1), \(a<\gamma_{d_k}\) for every \(k\). Since the current matching is strong, the general nested-metronoid estimate in accepted fact `11e347c295e9430b` applies and gives
\[
H_{k,\ell}\geq
a\left(
\frac{P_{k,\ell}}{M_{k,\ell}}
-\frac{\Delta_{k,\ell}}{\delta_aM_{k,\ell}}
\right)_+.
\tag{19}
\]
If the expression inside the positive part is nonpositive, then
\(\Delta_{k,\ell}/P_{k,\ell}\geq\delta_a\). If it is positive, rearranging (19) gives
\[
\frac{\Delta_{k,\ell}}{P_{k,\ell}}
\geq\delta_a\left(
1-\frac{H_{k,\ell}}
{a(P_{k,\ell}/M_{k,\ell})}
\right).
\]
Equations (14)-(15) therefore imply
\[
\liminf\frac{\Delta_{k,\ell}}{P_{k,\ell}}\geq\delta_a.
\]
Letting \(a\downarrow0\), one has \(\psi(a)\to1\), so
\[
\liminf\frac{\Delta_{k,\ell}}{P_{k,\ell}}
\geq\frac1{\log2}-1.
\tag{20}
\]

The strong endpoint inequality lets us use the stronger contact conclusion of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d`. It says exactly that
\[
\sigma_{k,\ell}
=\frac12\sum_e\omega_{k,\ell,e}
\delta_{(m_e,\ell_e)}
\]
is halfspace-dominated by the current mixed-volume measure, and that
\[
h_{\max}(C_{k,\ell})
\geq
\frac{\Omega_{k,\ell}}
{2^{\rho_{k,\ell}+1}(r_{k,\ell}+1)}.
\tag{21}
\]
Because \(\rho_{k,\ell}\leq n_k\), (21) yields
\[
H_{k,\ell}(r_{k,\ell}+1)
\geq\frac{\Omega_{k,\ell}}{2M_{k,\ell}}.
\tag{22}
\]
Also \(M_{k,\ell}\leq W_k\), so (13) and (18) give
\[
\liminf
\frac{|\sigma_{k,\ell}|}{M_{k,\ell}}
=\liminf\frac{\Omega_{k,\ell}}{2M_{k,\ell}}
\geq\frac{\alpha_\ell}{2}
=\frac1{4e}\left(\frac2{e^2}\right)^\ell.
\tag{23}
\]
Combining (14), (22), and (23) proves
\(r_{k,\ell}+1\to\infty\). Notice that it is the continuous residual rank \(r_{k,\ell}\), with the normalization \(2^{\rho_{k,\ell}+1}(r_{k,\ell}+1)\), that diverges. No divergence of the base contact rank \(\rho_{k,\ell}\) is asserted.

We rule out the current core branch. If it were nonempty along an infinite subsequence, accepted fact `07009c5047a9f7bb` would give
\[
G(C_{k,\ell})\geq4P_{k,\ell}.
\]
By nesting and (3), \(G(C_k)\geq4P_{k,\ell}\). The master bound in accepted fact `ae0161dd5d4ea672` would then imply
\[
H_{k,0}\geq
4\gamma_{d_k}\frac{P_{k,\ell}}{W_k}.
\tag{24}
\]
Equations (1) and (13) make the right side have positive liminf, contradicting \(H_{k,0}\to0\). Thus, for all sufficiently large \(k\), the degree-saturated core is empty.

Choose a current individual cap body or positive same-base reweighting \(C_{k,\ell+1}\) from accepted fact `07009c5047a9f7bb`. Its newly computed fiber volumes satisfy
\[
b_{k,\ell+1,z}
\geq2\gamma_{d_k}d_z(p_{k,\ell}),
\tag{25}
\]
and
\[
M_{k,\ell+1}\geq4\gamma_{d_k}P_{k,\ell}.
\tag{26}
\]
Define
\[
p_{k,\ell+1,e}
:=2\gamma_{d_k}^2\omega_{k,\ell,e}.
\]
Because \(0\leq\omega_{k,\ell,e}\leq p_{k,\ell,e}\), equations (25) and the exact endpoint degree sum give
\[
\begin{aligned}
d_z(p_{k,\ell+1})
&=2\gamma_{d_k}^2
\sum_{e\ni z}\omega_{k,\ell,e}\\
&\leq2\gamma_{d_k}^2d_z(p_{k,\ell})\\
&\leq\gamma_{d_k}b_{k,\ell+1,z}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{27}
\]
This is the advertised factor \(2\gamma_{d_k}^2\). By (1), the last quantity is at most \(b_{k,\ell+1,z}/2\), so the new matching is both strong and free. Equation (18) makes it nonzero for all sufficiently large \(k\).

Its mass is exactly
\[
P_{k,\ell+1}
=2\gamma_{d_k}^2\Omega_{k,\ell}.
\tag{28}
\]
Using (1), (13), (18), and (26),
\[
\liminf\frac{P_{k,\ell+1}}{W_k}
\geq\frac2{e^2}\alpha_\ell
=\alpha_{\ell+1},
\tag{29}
\]
\[
\liminf\frac{M_{k,\ell+1}}{W_k}
\geq\frac4e\alpha_\ell
=\left(\frac2{e^2}\right)^{\ell+1}
=\beta_{\ell+1}.
\tag{30}
\]
Equations (27), (29), and (30) close the induction. For a fixed finite \(L\), only finitely many eventual statements are used, so taking the maximum of their thresholds constructs every level \(0,\ldots,L\) for all sufficiently large \(k\). The constants (29)-(30) use only the uniform inequalities in (1), so varying dimensions cause no hidden limit exchange. Every \(o(1)\) above is taken after the level is fixed.

At the next level, (27) is the only inherited analytic input. The half-mass metronoids, centroids, bodies \(D_z,D_e,L_e\), primal optimizer, load-minimal dual, contact points, contact domination, affine defect, and ranks are all defined again from \(C_{k,\ell+1}\) and \(p_{k,\ell+1}\). In particular, optimality of the old dual is not transported. This proves the registered hereditary statement.

We prove the fresh-restart companion. Let \(B_k\) be any sequence with \(H(B_k)\to0\). Apply the level-zero argument (9)-(12), with \(B_k\) in place of \(C_k\), to a fresh exact minimum-unmatched matching \(q_k^B\). This gives
\[
\frac{Q_k^B}{W(B_k)}\geq\frac1{2e}-o(1)
\tag{31}
\]
and the strong endpoint bound. Equations (16)-(18), now in the current body \(B_k\), give
\[
\frac{\Omega_k^B}{Q_k^B}\to1,
\qquad
\frac{\Omega_k^B}{W(B_k)}\geq\frac1{2e}-o(1).
\tag{32}
\]
Equations (19)-(23) give the sharp defect, half-contact, and residual-rank assertions.

If the degree-saturated core of \(q_k^B\) were nonempty infinitely often, accepted fact `07009c5047a9f7bb` would give directly in \(B_k\)
\[
H(B_k)\geq4\gamma_{d_k}
\frac{Q_k^B}{W(B_k)},
\]
contradicting (1), (31), and \(H(B_k)\to0\). Thus the core is eventually empty. Every selected descendant \(B_k^+\) satisfies
\[
W(B_k^+)\geq4\gamma_{d_k}Q_k^B,
\]
so (1) and (31) give
\[
\frac{W(B_k^+)}{W(B_k)}\geq\frac2{e^2}-o(1).
\tag{33}
\]
Since \(B_k^+\subseteq B_k\), equation (2) and (33) give
\[
H(B_k^+)
\leq H(B_k)\frac{W(B_k)}{W(B_k^+)}
\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus one may apply the same argument in \(B_k^+\) after choosing a new exact matching there. This operation discards \(q_k^B\); it is distinct from retaining the current load through the matching \(2\gamma_{d_k}^2\omega_k^B\).

Finally, consider a generic exact half-metronoid-minorant cap \(A\subseteq B\) from the statement. Fix \(z\in E=D_+(B)\). Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` says that
\[
\mathsf M_{b_z/2}(B_z)
\]
is nonempty and compact, and every point of this metronoid has absolute depth at least
\[
\gamma_d\frac{b_z}{2}
\tag{34}
\]
inside \(B_z\). Choose a support point
\[
q_z\in\mathsf M_{b_z/2}(B_z),
\qquad
\langle v,q_z\rangle=h_z(v).
\]
The affine-minorant inequality on the entire set \(E\) gives
\[
\langle v,q_z\rangle=h_z(v)\geq\ell(z),
\]
so \(q_z\) belongs to the closed fiber halfspace
\[
J_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:\langle v,x\rangle\geq\ell(z)\}.
\]
Because \(v\ne0\), this is a proper closed halfspace. Applying the universal depth conclusion of accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` to \(q_z\in J_z\) yields
\[
\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z)
=\operatorname{vol}_d(B_z\cap J_z)
\geq\frac{\gamma_d}{2}b_z.
\tag{35}
\]
If \(z\notin E\), then \(b_z=0\) and \(A_z\subseteq B_z\) has zero \(d\)-volume, so (35) also holds there. Summing over all lattice fibers proves
\[
W(A)\geq\frac{\gamma_d}{2}W(B).
\tag{36}
\]

The cap is full-dimensional. Indeed, \(E\ne\varnothing\), and (35) gives a positive-volume cap section at every \(z\in E\). Its boundary hyperplane has zero \(d\)-volume, so one such section contains a point with strict cap inequality. Moving this point a sufficiently small distance toward an interior point of \(B\) produces an ambient interior point that still satisfies the strict cap inequality.

If \(r\) is strong in \(B\), then (35) gives
\[
d_z\!\left(\frac{\gamma_d}{2}r\right)
\leq\frac{\gamma_d^2}{2}b_z
\leq\gamma_d\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z).
\tag{37}
\]
If \(r\) is only free in \(B\), then
\[
d_z(\gamma_d^2r)
\leq\frac{\gamma_d^2}{2}b_z
\leq\gamma_d\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z).
\tag{38}
\]
Thus both asserted transports have the strong endpoint normalization in the new cap. For a sequence, (2) and (36) imply
\[
H(A_k)
\leq H(B_k)\frac{W(B_k)}{W(A_k)}
\leq\frac2{\gamma_{d_k}}H(B_k)
\leq2eH(B_k).
\tag{39}
\]
Hence hard normalized-depth failure passes to these caps, where the fresh-restart clause applies. Equations (37)-(38) transport only matching weights. Every fiber-dependent geometric and dual object must be recomputed. This proves both companion clauses and completes the theorem.
