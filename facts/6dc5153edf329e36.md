## Statement

Let $K=\operatorname{conv}(P_0,\ldots,P_d)$ be a $d$-simplex of volume $k>0$, $d\ge1$. Let $y$ be interior, with barycentric coordinates $\lambda_j>0$, and let $v_0,\ldots,v_d$ be nonzero spanning normals with $\sum_i v_i=0$. For each cap $H_i=\{v_i\cdot(x-y)\ge0\}$ put
$$
t_{ij}=v_i\cdot(P_j-y),\quad
Q_i=\frac{|K\cap H_i|}{k},\quad
S_i=\frac{\mathcal H^{d-1}(K\cap\partial H_i)}{k\|v_i\|}.
$$
Let $F_{ij}$ be the fraction of the facet opposite $P_j$ in $H_i$, using its own $\mathcal H^{d-1}$ measure. Then $F_{ij}=Q_i-t_{ij}S_i/d$, including cuts through vertices and, in $d=1$, using endpoint counting measure. Put $M=\sum_iQ_i/S_i$ and $X_{ij}=F_{ij}/(S_iM)$. The matrix $X$ is nonnegative and column-stochastic. With $a_i=\sum_j\lambda_jX_{ij}$, one has $a_i=Q_i/(S_iM)$, $\sum_i a_i=1$, $t_{ij}=dM(a_i-X_{ij})$, and the normalized row quantity $d Q_i^{1-1/d}/(S_iM)$ equals $d a_i/Q_i^{1/d}$.

## Proof

Each cut is proper because its plane contains the interior point $y$. Apply the divergence theorem on $K\cap H_i$ to $x-P_j$, whose divergence is $d$. The original facets through $P_j$ contribute zero. The flux on the opposite full facet is $dk$ by the pyramid volume formula, so the captured part contributes $dkF_{ij}$. On the cut face the outward normal is $-v_i/\|v_i\|$ and the flux density is $t_{ij}/\|v_i\|$. Thus $dkQ_i=dkF_{ij}+t_{ij}kS_i$. Lower-dimensional intersections are null for surface measure. In dimension one the divergence identity is the endpoint fundamental theorem of calculus with the same signs, and all proper cuts lie strictly between the endpoints.

Divide by $S_iM>0$ and sum in $i$. Normal balance gives $\sum_i t_{ij}=0$, so the columns sum to one. Taking the barycentric mean across $j$ gives $\sum_j\lambda_j t_{ij}=0$ and then $a_i=Q_i/(S_iM)$. The other identities are direct rearrangements.
