## Statement

On the actual body $[-1,1]\times[-1,1]\subset\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$, use the length marginals of mass two at bases $-1$ and $1$. There is an old midpoint law and compatible source both equal to $2\delta_{(0,0)}$. Every midpoint law of a repaired coupling dominated by length on the middle fiber has closed cap mass $3/2$ in $H=\{(z,x):x\ge-1/2\}$, while the old law has mass $2$. Both laws give zero mass to the cut plane, and a repair with convex order above the old law exists.

## Proof

Antithetically pair $x$ at base $-1$ with $-x$ at base $1$, producing the stated midpoint atom. Use its identity martingale for the old source. All feasible pairings have their midpoint in $\{0\}\times[-1,1]$ and preserve mass two. A midpoint law dominated by length on that interval must equal length because the dominating measure has the same mass. Pairing $x$ with $x$ across the bases realizes it. Its mean is zero, so Jensen gives convex order above the old law. The midpoint atom is strictly inside $H$, and $H$ captures length $3/2$ of the repaired law. Neither law charges $x=-1/2$. At threshold $-1/2$ the old hinge objective is $1$, and the paired equal-coordinate law has objective $\int_{-1/2}^1(x+1/2)\,dx=9/8$.
