## Statement

Retain the complete canonical hard synchronized-atlas setting and notation of accepted facts 036a276c54ec5e66, 2fe46f47d19112ea, and d01de728d8b5e33c.  Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body, \(Q>0\) is the half-strength canonical product-reservoir mass,
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
and the canonical hard ledger is
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D
 <\frac Q{100}.
\tag{1}
\]
In particular \(n\geq2\).  The rescaled internal whole-label atlas is
\[
 \mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}
 =\{\sigma=(z;e,f)\},
 \qquad
 e=\{z,a(\sigma)\},
 \qquad
 f=\{z,b(\sigma)\},
\tag{2}
\]
with transverse half-directions
\[
 h_e:=\frac{a(\sigma)-z}{2},
 \qquad
 h_f:=\frac{b(\sigma)-z}{2},
\tag{3}
\]
and positive rescaled weights \(\eta_\sigma\leq\theta_\sigma\).  Put
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma
 >\frac7{400}Q.
\tag{4}
\]
For the two original loaded edges of a label, retain the original load-minimal endpoint contacts and midpoint contacts
\[
 x_{e,z}\in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z),
 \quad
 x_{e,a}\in\mathsf M_{a_a/2}(C_a),
 \quad
 \ell_e:=\frac{x_{e,z}+x_{e,a}}2,
\tag{5}
\]
and analogously \(x_{f,z},x_{f,b},\ell_f\).  Here \(a_y:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_y)\), and \(a=a(\sigma)\), \(b=b(\sigma)\) when a label is fixed.  Also retain
\[
 m_e:=\frac{z+a}{2},
 \qquad
 m_f:=\frac{z+b}{2},
 \qquad
 m_\sigma:=\frac{a+b}{2},
 \qquad
 X_\sigma:=\frac{x_{e,a}+x_{f,b}}2.
\tag{6}
\]

For every \(\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\), define the four actual mixed-integer points
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 p_{\sigma,00}
 &:=\left(z,\frac{x_{e,z}+x_{f,z}}2\right),\\
 p_{\sigma,10}&:=(m_e,\ell_e),\\
 p_{\sigma,01}&:=(m_f,\ell_f),\\
 p_{\sigma,11}&:=(m_\sigma,X_\sigma).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Then all four bases are distinct integer points, each continuous coordinate in (7) belongs to the selected-mass metronoid of mass \(\eta_\sigma\) in its displayed fiber, and
\[
 \boxed{p_{\sigma,00}+p_{\sigma,11}
 =p_{\sigma,10}+p_{\sigma,01}.}
\tag{8}
\]
Moreover, the four points in (7) form an affine circuit: every three are affinely independent.  The four equal calibration masses \(\eta_\sigma\), anchored at the base \(z\), form one legal global complete-core profile of total mass \(4\eta_\sigma\).  Consequently
\[
 G\geq4\eta_\sigma
 \qquad(\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}).
\tag{9}
\]

Aggregate all the circuits, with coincident atoms aggregated as measures, into
\[
 \boxed{
 \nu_\square
 :=\gamma_d
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}
 \eta_\sigma
 \sum_{i,j\in\{0,1\}}\delta_{p_{\sigma,ij}}.}
\tag{10}
\]
Then \(\nu_\square\) is carried by
\(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), and for every closed ambient halfspace \(K\),
\[
 \boxed{\nu_\square(K)\leq\mu_C(K).}
\tag{11}
\]
Its exact mass is
\[
 \boxed{
 |\nu_\square|
 =4\gamma_dH_{\mathrm{int}}
 >\frac{7\gamma_d}{100}Q.}
\tag{12}
\]
This construction uses all internal labels without a loop/cross split, so (8)-(12) remain valid in the allowed all-loop case.

Let
\[
 A_\square
 :=\operatorname{aff}
 \operatorname{proj}_{\mathbb R^n}
 (\operatorname{supp}\nu_\square),
 \qquad
 \rho_\square:=\dim A_\square,
\tag{13}
\]
and let
\[
 r_\square
 :=\min_{s:A_\square\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{x-s(y):(y,x)\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_\square\}.
\tag{14}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 D\geq
 4\gamma_dH_{\mathrm{int}}
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_\square}}{r_\square+1}.}
\tag{15}
\]

Finally fix \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\), put
\[
 Y:=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad
 w_y:=\widehat\tau_y(v),
 \qquad
 \widehat P:=\widehat P(v),
\tag{16}
\]
and assume the deletion-stable full-base rank-five case
\[
 \rho_Y=n,
 \qquad
 r_Y^c=5,
 \qquad
 \rho_{Y\setminus\{y\}}=n,
 \qquad
 r_{Y\setminus\{y\}}^c=5
 \quad(y\in Y).
\tag{17}
\]
Then the canonical hard ledger forces
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_\square}}{r_\square+1}<\frac1{18},
 \qquad
 r_\square+1>18\,2^{n-\rho_\square}.}
\tag{18}
\]
In particular \(r_\square\geq18\).  If \(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) attains \(r_Y^c=5\) and
\[
 W:=\operatorname{span}\{c_y-s(y):y\in Y\},
 \qquad \dim W=5,
\tag{19}
\]
then, with
\[
 V_\square(s)
 :=\operatorname{span}
 \{x-s(y):(y,x)\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_\square\},
\tag{20}
\]
one has
\[
 \boxed{
 \dim\pi_W(V_\square(s))\geq13,}
\tag{21}
\]
where \(\pi_W:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R^d/W\) is the quotient map.  Thus the exact positive circuits (8) necessarily carry at least thirteen independent quotient directions in a canonical hard rank-five instance.

## Proof

Fix an internal label \(\sigma=(z;e,f)\), and abbreviate its opposite endpoints by \(a,b\).  The original transverse-atlas capacity in accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 and the rescaling in accepted fact 2fe46f47d19112ea give
\[
 0<\eta_\sigma\leq\theta_\sigma\leq\omega_e,\omega_f.
\tag{22}
\]
The original load bounds and canonical reservoir degree bounds give, at every endpoint (y\) of (e) or (f),
\[
 \omega_e\leq r_e\leq d_y(r)\leq\frac{a_y}{4}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \omega_f\leq r_f\leq d_y(r)\leq\frac{a_y}{4},
\tag{23}
\]
as appropriate.  Thus every half-mass witness of a contact in (5) may be multiplied by \(2\eta_\sigma/a_y\leq1/2\).  The resulting density has mass \(\eta_\sigma\) and the same barycenter.  Hence
\[
 x_{e,z}\in\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_z),
 \quad
 x_{e,a}\in\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_a),
 \quad
 x_{f,z}\in\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_z),
 \quad
 x_{f,b}\in\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_b).
\tag{24}
\]
The set \(\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_z)\) is convex, directly by convexly combining its representing densities.  Therefore the continuous coordinate of \(p_{\sigma,00}\) lies in that metronoid.  Applying the positive-root convexity of accepted fact dfd25e72c1f45a4e at equal positive root \(\eta_\sigma^{1/d}\) to the two contacts belonging to \(e\), to the two contacts belonging to \(f\), and to the two opposite contacts, respectively, gives
\[
 \ell_e\in\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_{m_e}),
 \quad
 \ell_f\in\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_{m_f}),
 \quad
 X_\sigma\in\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_{m_\sigma}).
\tag{25}
\]
This proves all four carriedness and selected-mass assertions.

Because (e,f) are transverse at (z), the vectors (h_e,h_f) in (3) are linearly independent.  The four bases in (7) are exactly
\[
 z,
 \qquad z+h_e,
 \qquad z+h_f,
 \qquad z+h_e+h_f.
\tag{26}
\]
They are therefore four distinct integer points forming a nondegenerate parallelogram, and every three are affinely independent.  Direct expansion of (5)-(7) gives
\[
 \frac{x_{e,z}+x_{f,z}}2+X_\sigma
 =\ell_e+\ell_f,
\tag{27}
\]
while (26) gives the same relation in the base coordinates.  This proves (8).  Since the projection of every three full points is affinely independent, every three full points are affinely independent, so (8) is an affine circuit rather than a nonminimal dependence.

The base (z) is an extreme vertex of the parallelogram (26).  Hence its only barycentric representation by the four displayed bases is the unit mass at (z).  The complete-core intersection at anchor (z) is therefore just
\(\mathsf M_{\eta_\sigma}(C_z)\), and it contains the continuous coordinate of \(p_{\sigma,00}\).  Every proper closed halfspace of \(\mathbb R^n\) containing \(z\) contains at least the calibration mass \(\eta_\sigma\) placed at \(z\).  Since \(n\geq2\),
\[
 \eta_\sigma
 \geq2^{-n}(4\eta_\sigma).
\tag{28}
\]
Thus the four equal masses form an admissible global complete-core tuple of total mass \(4\eta_\sigma\), proving (9).

We next prove the simultaneous domination (11).  For every loaded incidence ((j,y)), accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 fixes a half-mass witness (f_{j,y}) with
\[
 0\leq f_{j,y}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_y}f_{j,y}=\frac{a_y}{2},
 \qquad
 \frac2{a_y}\int_{C_y}x f_{j,y}(x)\,dx=x_{j,y}.
\tag{29}
\]
For every internal label using (j\), define
\[
 g_{\sigma,j,y}:=\frac{2\eta_\sigma}{a_y}f_{j,y}.
\tag{30}
\]
It has mass \(\eta_\sigma\) and barycenter \(x_{j,y}\).  At a fixed physical source \(y\), sum over both endpoints of both original edges of every internal label.  The transverse-pair capacity, the rescaling \(\eta_\sigma\leq\theta_\sigma\), and the reservoir degree bound give pointwise
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 G_y
 &:=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}},\ j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\}\\y\in j}}
 g_{\sigma,j,y}\\
 &\leq
 \frac2{a_y}
 \sum_{j\ni y}
 \sum_{\sigma:\,j\in\{e(\sigma),f(\sigma)\}}
 \eta_\sigma\\
 &\leq\frac2{a_y}\sum_{j\ni y}\omega_j
 \leq\frac{2d_y(r)}{a_y}
 \leq\frac12.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{31}
\]
This ledger includes both the common-center and opposite incidences.  It does not use the downstream partner construction.

Let \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be a closed halfspace.  Each corner in (7) is the midpoint of the following two actual source points:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c}
 p_{\sigma,00}&(z,x_{e,z}),\ (z,x_{f,z})\\
 p_{\sigma,10}&(z,x_{e,z}),\ (a,x_{e,a})\\
 p_{\sigma,01}&(z,x_{f,z}),\ (b,x_{f,b})\\
 p_{\sigma,11}&(a,x_{e,a}),\ (b,x_{f,b}).
 \end{array}
\tag{32}
\]
If a corner belongs to \(K\), at least one of its two source points belongs to \(K\).  Orient the corner toward one such point.  For every physical source \(y\), let \(\Psi_y\) be the sum of the corresponding densities (30), with the resulting multiplicities.  Every labelled incidence in (32) occurs in exactly two corners.  Therefore (31) gives
\[
 0\leq\Psi_y\leq2G_y\leq1.
\tag{33}
\]
Put \(T_y:=\int_{C_y}\Psi_y\).  If \(T_y>0\), the barycenter of \(\Psi_y\) is a convex combination of source points in the fiber section \(K_y\), and hence belongs to \(K_y\).  Thus it belongs to \(\mathsf M_{T_y}(C_y)\).  Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_y\cap K_y)\geq\gamma_dT_y
\tag{34}
\]
when \(K_y\) is a proper closed halfspace.  If \(K_y=\mathbb R^d\), the same inequality follows from \(T_y\leq a_y\) and \(\gamma_d\leq1\); an empty section receives no orientation.  Summing (34) over distinct physical sources counts exactly one mass \(\eta_\sigma\) for each corner of \(\nu_\square(K)\).  Hence
\[
 \nu_\square(K)
 =\gamma_d\sum_yT_y
 \leq\sum_y\operatorname{vol}_d(C_y\cap K_y)
 \leq\mu_C(K),
\tag{35}
\]
which proves (11).  The proof starts with labelled incidences and aggregates only afterward, so physical collisions cause no duplication.  Equation (12) follows from (4) and (10).

Apply the dominated-measure residual theorem of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 to the nonzero measure \(\nu_\square\).  With the intrinsic ranks (13)-(14), it gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)
 \geq\frac{|\nu_\square|}
 {2^{\rho_\square}(r_\square+1)}.
\tag{36}
\]
Multiplying by (2^n) and using (12) proves (15).

It remains to combine the two distinct hard-ledger summands in the rank-five case.  Accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c gives
\[
 \widehat P\geq H_{\mathrm{int}}>\frac7{400}Q,
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y}w_y=2\widehat P,
\tag{37}
\]
and its legal centroid-selector inequality, applied to (A=Y), gives under (17)
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq4\gamma_d\frac{2\widehat P}{5+1}
 =\frac{4\gamma_d}{3}\widehat P.
\tag{38}
\]
The quantities \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) are separate nonnegative summands in (1).  Combining (15) and (38) therefore gives the single-use ledger
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \geq
 \frac{4\gamma_d}{3}\widehat P
 +4\gamma_dH_{\mathrm{int}}
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_\square}}{r_\square+1}.
\tag{39}
\]

For every positive integer \(d\),
\[
 \gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}>e^{-1}.
\tag{40}
\]
Indeed, \(\log(1+1/d)<1/d\), so \(d\log(1+1/d)<1\).
Also
\[
 e
 =\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac1{k!}
 <1+1+\frac12+\frac16
 +\frac1{24}\sum_{j=0}^\infty\frac1{4^j}
 =\frac{49}{18},
\tag{41}
\]
where strictness follows because the factorial tail is strictly smaller than the displayed geometric tail.  Hence
\[
 \gamma_d>\frac{18}{49}.
\tag{42}
\]
If the first ratio in (18) were at least \(1/18\), equations (37), (39), and (42) would give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal A(C)
 &>
 \frac{4\gamma_d}{3}\frac{7Q}{400}
 +4\gamma_d\frac{7Q}{400}\frac1{18}\\
 &=\frac{49\gamma_d}{1800}Q
 >\frac{18}{1800}Q
 =\frac Q{100},
 \end{aligned}
\tag{43}
\]
contradicting (1).  This proves both inequalities in (18).  Since
\(\rho_\square\leq n\), equation (18) gives
\(r_\square+1>18\), and integrality gives \(r_\square\geq18\).

Finally choose \(s,W\) as in (19).  The map \(s|_{A_\square}\) is an admissible affine map in the minimization (14), so
\[
 \dim V_\square(s)\geq r_\square\geq18.
\tag{44}
\]
Since \(\dim W=5\),
\[
 \dim\pi_W(V_\square(s))
 =\dim V_\square(s)-\dim(V_\square(s)\cap W)
 \geq18-5=13,
\tag{45}
\]
which proves (21).  Subtracting the affine values of (s) from (8) preserves the same circuit relation, so these quotient directions are carried by the exact positive square circuits themselves.  The argument never assumes positive cross-label mass and therefore includes the all-loop case.
