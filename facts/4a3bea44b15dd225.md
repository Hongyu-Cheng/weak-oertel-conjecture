## Statement

Let $r(z)=1-z$ on $[-3,1/2]$, and let $f(x,z)=r(z)/2$ on $\{(x,z):-3\le z\le1/2,\ |x|\le r(z)\}$ and zero elsewhere. This is the positive affine profile with the interval-layer density exponent $d-2=1$ in dimension $d=3$. Its mass is $V=511/24$. Let $P(s)$ be the mass of $z+sx\ge0$, and let $C(s)$ and $M(s)$ be the integrals of $f$ and $zf$ on $z+sx=0$ against $d\mathcal H^1/\sqrt{1+s^2}$. No real $c,\lambda$ satisfy
$$
\frac{27}{64}V\le P(s)-cC(s)-\lambda M(s)\le\frac{37}{64}V
\qquad\text{for all }s\ge0.
$$
An explicit witness uses slopes $0,1/2,3/4$, with the middle cap reversed, and total weights $5120,3888,343$ divided by $9351$. Splitting each oblique type equally under $x\mapsto-x$ gives raw coarea vector balance and signed vertical section first-moment balance, with weighted mass fraction $31511/75847<27/64$.

## Proof

Integrating $(1-z)^2$ gives $V=511/24$. For $0<s\le3/4$ the section interval is $[-s/(1-s),s/(1+s)]$, lying in $[-3,1/2]$. Parametrizing by $x=-z/s$ gives coarea and moment integrands $(1-z)/(2s)$ and $z(1-z)/(2s)$ in $z$. Integrating them gives $C(s)=(1-s^2)^{-2}$ and $M(s)=-4s^2/[3(1-s^2)^3]$. At $s=0$ the section directly gives $C(0)=1$ and $M(0)=0$.

Temporarily extend the profile to its apex at $z=1$. In this extension each section $z+sx=q$ for $0\le q<1$ is the image of the section at zero by homothety of scale $1-q$ about $(0,1)$. Indeed the lower endpoint of the zero cut has $z\ge-3$, and contraction toward the apex puts the corresponding endpoint of the parallel cut at $z\ge-3$. On these cuts the density also scales by $1-q$. The extended cap mass is therefore $\int_0^1(1-q)^2 C(s)\,dq=C(s)/3$. For $z\in[1/2,1]$ the smallest margin on the horizontal slice is $z-s(1-z)\ge1/8$, so truncation removes the mass $\int_{1/2}^1(1-z)^2\,dz=1/24$ from every cap here. Thus $P(s)=C(s)/3-1/24$. The records are
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
s&P(s)&C(s)&M(s)\\ \hline
0&7/24&1&0\\
1/2&119/216&16/9&-64/81\\
3/4&1999/1176&256/49&-3072/343
\end{array}
$$
Use the lower inequality at $0$ and $3/4$ and the reversed lower inequality $V-P+cC+\lambda M\ge(27/64)V$ at $1/2$. Multiply by $5120,343,3888$, respectively. The coarea correction is proportional to $5120+1792-6912=0$, and the moment correction to $-3072+3072=0$. Reflection cancels the horizontal components of the raw coarea vectors. The resulting mean must be at least $27/64$, whereas the actual mean is
$$
\frac{5120(7/24)+3888(511/24-119/216)+343(1999/1176)}{9351(511/24)}
=\frac{31511}{75847}
=\frac{27}{64}-\frac{31165}{4854208}.
$$
Both oblique types have nonzero individual vertical moments.
