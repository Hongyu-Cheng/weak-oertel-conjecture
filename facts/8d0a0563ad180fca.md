## Statement

Retain all hypotheses, objects, and notation of accepted fact d41b002f3091f5c4. In particular, \(Q>0\), \(E_\perp\) is the finite physical endpoint support, and, for every \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\),
\[
 \mathcal P_m:=
 \left\{
  \{u,v\}\subseteq E_\perp:
  u\ne v,\ u+v=2m
 \right\}
\tag{1}
\]
is the family of reflected endpoint pairs with midpoint \(m\). For each \(m\) with
\[
 k_m:=|\mathcal P_m|>0,
\tag{2}
\]
order the pairs as
\[
 e_{m,1},e_{m,2},\ldots,e_{m,k_m}
\tag{3}
\]
so that, writing \(e_{m,j}=\{u_{m,j},v_{m,j}\}\),
\[
 u_{m,j}^{\rm gm}:=
 \sqrt{\tau_{u_{m,j}}\tau_{v_{m,j}}},
 \qquad
 u_{m,1}^{\rm gm}\geq u_{m,2}^{\rm gm}
 \geq\cdots\geq u_{m,k_m}^{\rm gm}>0.
\tag{4}
\]
Ties may be ordered arbitrarily. Define the \(74\)-th-and-later tail at \(m\) by
\[
 E_{74}(m):=
 \begin{cases}
  \displaystyle\sum_{j=74}^{k_m}u_{m,j}^{\rm gm},&k_m\geq74,\\
  0,&0\leq k_m\leq73,
 \end{cases}
\tag{5}
\]
where the second case includes \(\mathcal P_m=\varnothing\). Put
\[
 E_{74}:=\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z^n}E_{74}(m).
\tag{6}
\]
Only finitely many summands are nonzero. Then
\[
 \boxed{E_{74}>\frac{37}{300}Q.}
\tag{7}
\]

Moreover, if \(e_{m,j}\) contributes to \(E_{74}\), so \(j\geq74\), then the \(73\) distinct pairs
\[
 e_{m,1},e_{m,2},\ldots,e_{m,73}
\tag{8}
\]
have the same midpoint \(m\) and satisfy
\[
 u_{m,i}^{\rm gm}\geq u_{m,j}^{\rm gm}
 \qquad(1\leq i\leq73).
\tag{9}
\]

The quantity \(E_{74}\) is only a sum of geometric-mean weights over pair occurrences. No assertion is made that these retained pairs form a matching, that pairs belonging to different midpoints are endpoint-disjoint, that \(E_{74}\leq Q\), or that \(E_{74}\) may be summed into \(R\), \(G\), \(R_{\rm sel}(C)\), \(M_{\rm off}(C)\), \(R_{\rm cloud}(C)\), or \(D\). In particular, (7) does not prove an endpoint-frame aggregation inequality or the locked final theorem.

## Proof

For a nonempty family \(\mathcal P_m\), retain the notation of accepted fact d41b002f3091f5c4:
\[
 S_m:=\sum_{e=\{u,v\}\in\mathcal P_m}
 \sqrt{\tau_u\tau_v},
 \qquad
 M_m:=\max_{e=\{u,v\}\in\mathcal P_m}
 \sqrt{\tau_u\tau_v}.
\tag{10}
\]
The ordering (4) gives
\[
 S_m=\sum_{j=1}^{k_m}u_{m,j}^{\rm gm},
 \qquad
 M_m=u_{m,1}^{\rm gm}.
\tag{11}
\]

Suppose first that \(k_m\geq74\). By (5) and (11),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_{74}(m)
 &=S_m-\sum_{j=1}^{73}u_{m,j}^{\rm gm}\\
 &\geq S_m-73M_m.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
If \(1\leq k_m\leq73\), then \(E_{74}(m)=0\), while
\[
 S_m=\sum_{j=1}^{k_m}u_{m,j}^{\rm gm}
 \leq k_mM_m\leq73M_m.
\tag{13}
\]
Thus (12) remains valid in this case. Empty families contribute zero to all sums and require no value of \(M_m\). Summing (12) over the nonempty families gives
\[
 E_{74}
 \geq
 \sum_m S_m-73\sum_m M_m.
\tag{14}
\]

The proof of accepted fact d41b002f3091f5c4 establishes the safe global estimates
\[
 \sum_mS_m>\frac{39104}{200}Q,
\tag{15}
\]
and
\[
 \sum_mM_m\leq\frac W4,
 \qquad
 W<\frac{803}{75}Q.
\tag{16}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \sum_mM_m<\frac{803}{300}Q.
\tag{17}
\]
Substituting (15) and (17) into (14) yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_{74}
 &>
 \left(
  \frac{39104}{200}
  -73\frac{803}{300}
 \right)Q\\
 &=
 \left(
  \frac{117312}{600}
  -\frac{117238}{600}
 \right)Q\\
 &=\frac{74}{600}Q
 =\frac{37}{300}Q.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{18}
\]
This proves (7).

Finally, a pair contributes to (5) only when its index \(j\) is at least \(74\). The first \(73\) pairs in the ordering are distinct pair occurrences at the same midpoint, and (4) gives (9). This proves the no-lighter-companion assertion and completes the theorem.
