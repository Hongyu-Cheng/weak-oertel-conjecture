## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and retain the common canonical hard-branch objects and normalizations of accepted facts fc18c3c4832f0bfb and 6a74481cad1d7e81. Thus, for every \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\),
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)
 \quad(a_z>0),
\]
and \(r=(r_e)\) is the half-strength canonical product reservoir on unordered distinct same-parity edges. Put
\[
 Q:=\sum_e r_e>0,
 \qquad
 R:=R_C(r),
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C).
\]
For \(e=\{u,v\}\), write
\[
 m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2},
 \qquad
 L_e:=\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v).
\]
Retain the original load-minimal optimizer, its loads, and its exposed contacts in the notation
\[
 0\leq\omega_e\leq r_e,
 \qquad
 \ell_e\in L_e\quad(\omega_e>0),
 \qquad
 \Omega:=\sum_e\omega_e.
\]
The exact alias map between the two accepted predecessors identifies their \(C,r,Q,R,D,\omega_e,m_e,L_e\) and original exposed contacts with the objects above. Their common hard hypothesis is the canonical ledger
\[
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D
 <\frac Q{100}.
\tag{1}
\]
In particular, all six summands are nonnegative, and accepted fact fc18c3c4832f0bfb gives
\[
 \Omega>\frac{99Q}{100}.
\tag{2}
\]

For every physical target \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\), put
\[
 \Omega_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}\omega_e.
\tag{3}
\]
When \(\Omega_m>0\), use the exact color-target congestion quantities of accepted fact fc18c3c4832f0bfb with its alias \(\alpha_e=\omega_e\): for \(e=\{u,v\}\),
\[
 \vartheta_e:=2^{-(d+1)}
 \bigl(a_u^{1/d}+a_v^{1/d}\bigr)^d,
\]
\[
 \kappa_{\xi,m}
 :=\sum_{\substack{e=\{u,v\}:\,\omega_e>0,\ m_e=m\\
 u\equiv v\equiv\xi\pmod 2}}
 \frac{\omega_e}{\vartheta_e},
 \qquad
 \kappa_m:=\sum_\xi\kappa_{\xi,m},
 \qquad
 \chi_m:=\frac1{\max\{1,\kappa_m\}}.
\tag{4}
\]
Extend this definition to empty targets by
\[
 \chi_m:=1\qquad(\Omega_m=0).
\tag{5}
\]
Then \(0<\chi_m\leq1\) for every \(m\). Put
\[
 \widehat E:=\{m:\Omega_m>0\},
 \qquad
 \widehat\Omega:=\sum_m\chi_m\Omega_m,
\tag{6}
\]
and, for \(m\in\widehat E\),
\[
 x_m:=\frac1{\Omega_m}
 \sum_{\substack{e:m_e=m\\\omega_e>0}}\omega_e\ell_e.
\tag{7}
\]
Define
\[
 \rho_J:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E),
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 r_J:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{x_m-s(m):m\in\widehat E\},
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}
 :=\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}
 \frac{\widehat\Omega}{r_J+1}.
\tag{10}
\]

Define the edge-owned congestion charge and its total mass by
\[
 \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=(1-\chi_{m_e})\omega_e,
 \qquad
 B_{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\sum_e\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{11}
\]
Then
\[
 B_{\mathrm{cong}}=\Omega-\widehat\Omega.
\tag{12}
\]
If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), apply accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81 afresh to \(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}\). To keep this fresh optimization distinct from the pooled load \(\widehat\Omega\) in (6), use the exact alias map
\[
 \beta_e\longleftrightarrow\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 B\longleftrightarrow B_{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 \widehat v_e\longleftrightarrow\widetilde v_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 \widehat\omega_e\longleftrightarrow
 \widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 \widehat\Omega\longleftrightarrow
 \widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{13}
\]
Here the symbols on the left are the charge and fresh-reoptimization symbols local to accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81, while the symbols on the right are the ones used in this statement. Let \(\widetilde L_{\mathrm{cong}}\) denote that fact's fresh endpoint factor \(\widehat L_\beta\) under (13), whenever \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), and put
\[
 \Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}:=
 \begin{cases}
 1,&B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0,\\[1mm]
 1,&B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0
       \text{ and }\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0,\\[1mm]
 \min\{1,\widetilde L_{\mathrm{cong}}\},
   &B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0
       \text{ and }\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0.
 \end{cases}
\tag{14}
\]
No affine rank is assigned to an empty fresh support.

Then
\[
 D\geq P_{\mathrm{pool}},
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq P_{\mathrm{pool}},
\tag{15}
\]
\[
 R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}},
\tag{16}
\]
and consequently
\[
 \boxed{
 D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)

 +\max\{P_{\mathrm{pool}},
          \Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}\}.}
\tag{17}
\]
Under the hard ledger (1),
\[
 \boxed{
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}
 +\max\{P_{\mathrm{pool}},
          \Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}\}
 <\frac Q{100},}
\tag{18}
\]
and in particular
\[
 \boxed{P_{\mathrm{pool}}<\frac Q{200}.}
\tag{19}
\]

Therefore the following is a precise sufficient condition for eliminating this hard branch: for some constant \(C_0\) with \(0<C_0\leq99\),
\[
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}
 +\max\{P_{\mathrm{pool}},
          \Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}\}
 \geq\frac{\Omega}{C_0}.
\tag{20}
\]
No inequality of the form (20) is asserted here.

## Proof

The reservoir support is finite. Equations (4)-(5) give \(0<\chi_m\leq1\) at every target. By (2), \(\Omega>0\), so \(\widehat E\ne\varnothing\); moreover, the positivity of every \(\chi_m\) gives \(\widehat\Omega>0\). Hence (7)-(10) are all defined.

Under the alias \(\alpha_e=\omega_e\), the pooled target mass \(\alpha_m\) of accepted fact fc18c3c4832f0bfb is exactly \(\Omega_m\), its pooled total mass \(\widehat\alpha\) is exactly the \(\widehat\Omega\) in (6), and its pooled points, support, base rank, and residual rank are exactly \(x_m,\widehat E,\rho_J,r_J\) in (7)-(9). The two capacity conclusions of that accepted fact therefore give (15).

Since \(0<\chi_{m_e}\leq1\), equation (11) gives
\[
 0\leq\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\leq\omega_e
 \qquad(e).
\tag{21}
\]
Grouping the finite edge sum by physical target and using (3) and (6),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 B_{\mathrm{cong}}
 &=\sum_e(1-\chi_{m_e})\omega_e\\
 &=\sum_m(1-\chi_m)\Omega_m\\
 &=\Omega-\widehat\Omega,
 \end{aligned}
\]
which proves (12). The extension (5) makes every empty-target contribution identically zero and leaves the original pooling construction unchanged.

Suppose first that \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\). Then (21) makes \(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}\) a nonzero legal edge-owned charge for accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81. Under the exact fresh-optimization alias map (13), its unconditional retention-or-selector inequality is precisely (16), with \(\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}\) as defined in the last two cases of (14). If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), the right side of (16) is zero by the first case of (14), while \(R\) and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) are nonnegative. Thus (16) also holds in the zero-charge case without applying the nonzero-charge theorem.

Put
\[
 S:=D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
\]
The two inequalities in (15), together with \(R\geq0\), give
\[
 S\geq D+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq2P_{\mathrm{pool}}.
\tag{22}
\]
The first inequality in (15) and (16) give independently
\[
 S
 =D+\bigl(R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\bigr)
 \geq P_{\mathrm{pool}}
      +\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{23}
\]
Taking the larger of (22) and (23), rather than adding two bounds that both use \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 S
 &\geq\max\{2P_{\mathrm{pool}},
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}+
 \Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}\}\\
 &=P_{\mathrm{pool}}
 +\max\{P_{\mathrm{pool}},
 \Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}\},
 \end{aligned}
\]
which proves (17).

All other summands in (1) are nonnegative, so (1) gives \(S<Q/100\). Combining this strict upper bound with (17) proves (18). The left side of (18) is at least \(2P_{\mathrm{pool}}\), proving (19).

Finally, if the additional inequality (20) were proved for some \(0<C_0\leq99\), then (2) would give
\[
 \frac{\Omega}{C_0}
 \geq\frac{\Omega}{99}
 >\frac Q{100},
\]
contradicting (18). This proves only the stated sufficiency of (20), not (20) itself, and completes the proof.
