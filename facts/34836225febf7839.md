## Statement

Let $F\subset\mathbb R^m$ be a compact convex body with $x\in\operatorname{int} F$, and $Q\subset\mathbb R^k$, $v$, $n_v$, and $J_*(v)$ as in the normalized cone Laplace minimum. Put $d=m+k$ and
$$
C=\frac{m!}{d!}\frac{(d+1)^{d+1}J_*(v)}{k^k(m+1)^{m+1}}.
$$
Use singleton counting measure, $E_F=1$, and no tangent parameter if $m=0$. Let $E_F(x)$ be compact-body projective energy and $L_F(z)=1+a_F\cdot(z-x)$ its optimal denominator. On $F\times Q$, define energy at $(x,v)$ by minimizing the integral of $L^{-d-1}$ over affine denominators $L=1+c\cdot(z-x)+b\cdot(q-v)>0$ everywhere. Its value is $CE_F(x)$ with unique parameters $c_*=(m+1)a_F/(d+1)$, $b_*=k n_v/(d+1)$. At $(x,\delta v)$ its value is $\delta^kCE_F(x)$ for every $\delta>0$. Writing $L_*=(m+1)L_F/(d+1)+b_*\cdot q$, the density $L_*^{-d-2}/[CE_F(x)]$ is a probability. Its tangential marginal is $L_F^{-m-2}/E_F(x)$ and its conditional normal mean is $L_F(z)v$, so its full mean is $(x,v)$.

## Proof

A positive denominator of finite integral must have $b\in\operatorname{int}Q^*$. Negative values on a cone ray violate positivity, and a zero value on a nonzero ray gives disjoint translates of an interior ball in $Q$ on which the integral is unchanged, for each $z$. Put $\beta=b\cdot v\in(0,1)$, $n=b/\beta$, and $H(z)=1-\beta+c\cdot(z-x)>0$ on $F$. Tonelli with the gamma integral and $J_Q(tb)=t^{-k}J_Q(b)$ gives, for $r>k$,
$$
\int_Q (H+b\cdot q)^{-r}\,dq
=\frac{\Gamma(r-k)}{\Gamma(r)}J_Q(b)H^{k-r}.
$$
With $r=d+1$, the tangential minimization in $c$ reduces by scaling to the compact energy minimum, giving $c=(1-\beta)a_F$. The value to minimize in $(n,\beta)$ is
$$
\frac{m!}{d!}J_Q(n)\beta^{-k}(1-\beta)^{-m-1}E_F(x).
$$
The normalized cone lemma gives $n=n_v$ uniquely, and the scalar factor has its unique minimum at $\beta=k/(d+1)$. The resulting denominator is positive on the product. Normal dilation gives the exact power $\delta^k$ by change of variables.

For $r=d+2$, the gamma-factor ratio relative to $r=d+1$ is $(m+1)/(d+1)$, canceled by the extra inverse power of the tangential coefficient in $H=(m+1)L_F/(d+1)$. This gives the stated marginal and its unit mass by compact escort normalization. The same gamma calculation with a factor $q$ uses $\int_Q q e^{-t b\cdot q}\,dq=t^{-k-1}\int_Q q e^{-b\cdot q}\,dq$; dividing by the integral without $q$ gives conditional mean $-\nabla\log J_Q(b)H/(m+1)$. At $b_*$, homogeneity and the normalized cone mean give $-\nabla\log J_Q(b_*)=(d+1)v$. Thus the normal conditional mean is $L_F(z)v$. The compact escort has mean $x$ and expectation of $L_F$ equal to one, which gives the full mean.
