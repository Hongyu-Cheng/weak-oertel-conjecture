## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard instance of accepted facts `b5739a77433de383`, `c0efb33112a166d5`, `5ae5325c589884a7`, and their degree-weighted complete-product ancestor `d5d5de35c3f25af9`.  Thus \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\), \(Q>0\),
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d>\frac4{11},
 \qquad
 D_0:=\frac65K
\tag{1}
\]
with \(D_0\) a full-dimensional compact origin-symmetric convex body.  For every positive-volume integer fiber write
\[
 C_z:=\{y:(z,y)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}y\,dy.
\tag{2}
\]

Let \(s^{\mathrm{tar}}\) be the off-diagonal same-parity target matching from `d5d5de35c3f25af9`, and, in the degree tag used by `b5739a77433de383`, put
\[
 w_z:=\frac12d_z(s^{\mathrm{tar}}),
 \qquad
 A:=\{z:w_z>0\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
 0<w_z\leq\frac{a_z}{4}
 \qquad(z\in A).
\tag{4}
\]

Retain the occurrence multiset \(\mathcal O\) of `b5739a77433de383`.  For \(o\in\mathcal O\), let \(t(o)\) be its target, \(z_o\in A\) its source, \(h_o:=h_{t(o)}\in D_0\) its support shift, \(\ell_o\geq0\) its scalar load, \(\theta_o\in(\mathbb R^d)^*\) its covector, and \(g_o\) its density on \(C_{z_o}\).  Thus
\[
 y_o:=c_{z_o}+h_o,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_{z_o}}g_o=\ell_o,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_{z_o}}y g_o(y)\,dy=\ell_oy_o,
\tag{5}
\]
\[
 \sum_{o:z_o=z}g_o\leq1\quad\text{a.e. on }C_z,
 \qquad
 L_z:=\sum_{o:z_o=z}\ell_o\leq w_z,
\tag{6}
\]
where a zero-load occurrence has zero density.  The exact support-contact and balance identities are
\[
 h_{D_0}(\theta_o)=\theta_o(h_o)=\ell_o,
\tag{7}
\]
\[
 \sum_o\theta_o=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_oz_o\otimes\theta_o=0,
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 \sum_o\theta_o(y_o)=-\Delta_H,
 \qquad
 \Delta_H>0,
 \qquad
 \Omega_H:=\sum_o\ell_o>\frac{1077}{6400}Q.
\tag{9}
\]

Retain the pruned matching \(q^{\mathrm{out}}\) and exact minimum-unmatched centroid host \(\lambda^{\mathrm{cen}}\) from `5ae5325c589884a7`.  They are off-diagonal same-parity matchings,
\[
 0\leq q_e^{\mathrm{out}}\leq\lambda_e^{\mathrm{cen}},
 \qquad
 u_z:=d_z(q^{\mathrm{out}}),
\tag{10}
\]
and \(\lambda^{\mathrm{cen}}\) is exact minimum-unmatched for the degree capacities
\[
 b_z:=\gamma_da_z
\tag{11}
\]
in every parity class of positive-volume fibers.

There are coefficients \(\rho_{e,o}\geq0\), indexed by incidences \(z_o\in e\), with
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z_o}\rho_{e,o}\leq1
 \qquad(o\in\mathcal O),
\tag{12}
\]
\[
 \sum_{o:z_o=z}\rho_{e,o}\ell_o=q_e^{\mathrm{out}}
 \qquad(z\in e).
\tag{13}
\]
Thus \(\rho\) is an actual nonreused occurrence-token lift of the retained host incidences, not only a scalar edge inclusion.  Put
\[
 \alpha_o:=1-\sum_{e\ni z_o}\rho_{e,o},
 \qquad
 m_z:=\sum_{o:z_o=z}\alpha_o\ell_o=L_z-u_z,
\tag{14}
\]
and define the unused host-edge capacity and its total at a source by
\[
 \varepsilon_e:=\lambda_e^{\mathrm{cen}}-q_e^{\mathrm{out}},
 \qquad
 s_z:=\sum_{e\ni z}\varepsilon_e
 =d_z(\lambda^{\mathrm{cen}})-u_z.
\tag{15}
\]

The complete-product ancestry forces the strict pointwise host slack
\[
 \boxed{
 d_z(\lambda^{\mathrm{cen}})
 =\min\left\{b_z,\sum_{\substack{y\ne z,\ a_y>0\\y\equiv z\ ({\rm mod}\ 2)}}b_y\right\}
 >L_z
 \qquad(z\in A).}
\tag{16}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \boxed{s_z-m_z=d_z(\lambda^{\mathrm{cen}})-L_z>0}
 \qquad(z\in A).
\tag{17}
\]

Let \(\mathcal I\) be the finite list of unused incidences
\[
 i=(e,z),
 \qquad z\in e,
 \qquad \varepsilon_e>0,
\tag{18}
\]
with base \(r_i:=z\) and capacity \(s_i:=\varepsilon_e\), and let
\[
 Z:=\{z\in A:m_z>0\}.
\tag{19}
\]
Define the affine-invariant transport congestion by
\[
 \mathsf T:=\inf\ \kappa
\tag{20}
\]
over \(\kappa\geq0\) and real \(x_{i,z}\) satisfying
\[
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal I}x_{i,z}=m_z,
 \qquad
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal I}r_ix_{i,z}=m_zz
 \qquad(z\in Z),
\tag{21}
\]
\[
 \sum_{z\in Z}|x_{i,z}|\leq\kappa s_i
 \qquad(i\in\mathcal I).
\tag{22}
\]
If \(Z=\varnothing\), put \(\kappa_0:=0\).  Otherwise put
\[
 \kappa_0:=\max_{z\in Z}\frac{m_z}{s_z}.
\tag{23}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\mathsf T\leq\kappa_0<1.}
\tag{24}
\]
An explicit feasible flow is
\[
 x_{(e,z),z}:=\frac{m_z}{s_z}\varepsilon_e,
 \qquad
 x_{(e,z),y}:=0\quad(y\ne z).
\tag{25}
\]
Thus the transport is nonnegative and never moves a token away from its original base.

The flow has the following simultaneous physical realization.  For every incidence \(z\in e\) and occurrence \(o\) with \(z_o=z\), put
\[
 \eta_{e,o}:=\alpha_o\frac{\varepsilon_e}{s_z},
 \qquad
 a_{e,o}:=\rho_{e,o}+\eta_{e,o},
\tag{26}
\]
where a missing edge coefficient is zero, and define
\[
 F_{e,z}:=\sum_{o:z_o=z}a_{e,o}g_o,
 \qquad
 p_{e,z}:=\int_{C_z}F_{e,z}.
\tag{27}
\]
Then
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}a_{e,o}=1
 \qquad(z_o=z),
\tag{28}
\]
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}F_{e,z}=\sum_{o:z_o=z}g_o\leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_z,
\tag{29}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 p_{e,z}
 =q_e^{\mathrm{out}}+\frac{m_z}{s_z}\varepsilon_e
 \leq\lambda_e^{\mathrm{cen}},
 \qquad
 \sum_{e\ni z}p_{e,z}=L_z.}
\tag{30}
\]
Every nonzero token density \(\eta_{e,o}g_o\) has normalized barycenter exactly
\[
 c_z+h_o,
\tag{31}
\]
so every complementary support shift is physically realized on an unused incidence.  The retained and complementary densities together use every original occurrence density exactly once.  In particular, if \(p_{e,z}>0\), the normalized barycenter of \(F_{e,z}\) belongs to
\[
 (c_z+D_0)\cap\mathsf M_{p_{e,z}}(C_z).
\tag{32}
\]

Finally define the aggregate incidence covectors
\[
 \Xi_{e,z}:=\sum_{o:z_o=z}a_{e,o}\theta_o.
\tag{33}
\]
They satisfy the exact capacity, force, and base-moment relations
\[
 h_{D_0}(\Xi_{e,z})\leq p_{e,z}\leq\lambda_e^{\mathrm{cen}},
\tag{34}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{e}\sum_{z\in e}\Xi_{e,z}=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_e\sum_{z\in e}z\otimes\Xi_{e,z}=0.}
\tag{35}
\]
No support-shift objective is lost:
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_e\sum_{z\in e}\sum_{o:z_o=z}
 a_{e,o}\theta_o(h_o)
 =\Omega_H
 >\frac{1077}{6400}Q.}
\tag{36}
\]
Moreover the aggregated incidence family retains the exact positive source-tube dual score
\[
 \boxed{
 -\sum_e\sum_{z\in e}h_{c_z+D_0}(\Xi_{e,z})
 \geq\Delta_H>0.}
\tag{37}
\]
Equations (24), (29)-(32), and (35)-(37) are the claimed bounded-congestion and physical-incidence realization.  They do not assert that the balanced incidence dual in (37) is already one of the canonical terminal capacities.

## Proof

We first construct the retained-token coefficients.  The matching \(q\) in accepted fact `c0efb33112a166d5` is the sum of its off-diagonal outside-pair part \(q^{\ne}\) and its rematched diagonal part \(q^\Delta\).  For an off-diagonal edge \(e=\{r,s\}\) with midpoint \(t\), the incidence at \(r\) uses exactly the two occurrences
\[
 (t;r,s;1),
 \qquad
 (t;s,r;2),
\tag{38}
\]
each with its full coefficient one.  Their two loads sum to \(q_e^{\ne}\).  The analogous two occurrences at \(s\) also have total load \(q_e^{\ne}\).  No off-diagonal occurrence occurs in another edge component.

For a diagonal outside pair at \(r\), let \(\delta_r\) be its full two-tag load.  If \(e\) is an edge of \(q^\Delta\) incident to \(r\), give each of the two diagonal occurrences at \(r\) coefficient
\[
 \frac{q_e^\Delta}{\delta_r}.
\tag{39}
\]
The edge is positive only when \(\delta_r>0\).  Since
\[
 d_r(q^\Delta)\leq\delta_r,
\tag{40}
\]
the sum of the coefficients (39), over all rematching edges incident to either diagonal occurrence, is at most one.  At the incidence \((e,r)\), their loads sum to \(q_e^\Delta\).  The off-diagonal and diagonal occurrence lists are disjoint.  Hence these rules define preliminary coefficients \(\rho^q_{e,o}\) with
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z_o}\rho^q_{e,o}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \sum_{o:z_o=z}\rho^q_{e,o}\ell_o=q_e
 \quad(z\in e),
\tag{41}
\]
including an edge receiving both an off-diagonal and a diagonal-rematching component.

Accepted fact `5ae5325c589884a7` gives
\[
 0\leq q_e^{\mathrm{out}}\leq q_e.
\tag{42}
\]
For \(q_e>0\), set
\[
 \rho_{e,o}:=\frac{q_e^{\mathrm{out}}}{q_e}\rho^q_{e,o},
\tag{43}
\]
and set it to zero on a zero edge.  Equations (41)-(43) prove (12)-(13).  Scaling the original density and covector by the same coefficient gives the promised literal occurrence-token lift; it is not merely a reconstruction from the endpoint barycenters of \(q\).

We next prove the strict host slack.  We use an elementary fact about a finite loopless fractional matching with vertex capacities \((b_y)\) in one parity class.  Put \(B:=\sum_yb_y\), let \(b_*:=\max_yb_y\), and let \(\lambda\) minimize total unmatched capacity.  Then
\[
 d_z(\lambda)=\min\{b_z,B-b_z\}
 \qquad(z).
\tag{44}
\]
Indeed, every matching has mass at most \(B/2\).  It also has mass at most \(B-b_*\), because every loopless edge has an endpoint other than a fixed maximizing vertex and the sum of all degrees away from that vertex is at most \(B-b_*\).  If \(b_*>B/2\), joining the maximizing vertex to every other vertex with the full other capacity attains mass \(B-b_*\).  Any optimal matching then has no edge between two nonmaximizing vertices and saturates every nonmaximizing vertex, so (44) follows.  If \(b_*\leq B/2\), arrange consecutive half-open intervals \(I_y\) of lengths \(b_y\) on a circle of circumference \(B\), let \(\tau\) be antipodal translation, and put
\[
 \lambda_{y,y'}:=\operatorname{len}(I_y\cap\tau(I_{y'})).
\]
Antipodal translation is an involution, so these weights are symmetric.  No interval overlaps its antipodal translate in positive measure, so they are loopless, and the translated intervals partition the circle, so \(\sum_{y'}\lambda_{y,y'}=b_y\).  Thus every vertex is saturated.  The minimum unmatched capacity is zero, every exact minimizer saturates every vertex, and (44) follows again.  This also covers equality and singleton classes.

Apply (44) to the exact centroid host.  Fix \(z\in A\), and let \(\xi\) be its parity class.  Because \(s^{\mathrm{tar}}\) is loopless, every unit of degree at \(z\) is also counted in the degree of a distinct vertex of the same parity.  Therefore
\[
 \sum_{\substack{r\in A\\r\ne z,\ r\equiv z\ ({\rm mod}\ 2)}}
 d_r(s^{\mathrm{tar}})
 \geq d_z(s^{\mathrm{tar}}),
\tag{45}
\]
and (3) gives
\[
 \sum_{\substack{r\in A\\r\ne z,\ r\equiv z\ ({\rm mod}\ 2)}}w_r
 \geq w_z.
\tag{46}
\]
This is the product-ancestry inequality absent from a general scalar host calibration.

Equations (1), (4), and (11) yield
\[
 b_z=\gamma_da_z\geq4\gamma_dw_z>w_z.
\tag{47}
\]
The sum of all other centroid capacities in the parity class includes those indexed by the other members of \(A\), so (4) and (46) give
\[
 \sum_{\substack{y\ne z,\ a_y>0\\y\equiv z\ ({\rm mod}\ 2)}}b_y
 \geq
 4\gamma_d
 \sum_{\substack{r\in A\\r\ne z,\ r\equiv z\ ({\rm mod}\ 2)}}w_r
 \geq4\gamma_dw_z>w_z.
\tag{48}
\]
By (6), \(L_z\leq w_z\).  Substituting (47)-(48) into (44) proves (16).  Subtracting \(u_z\) from both sides proves (17).  Notice that this is a covector-free capacity comparison derived from the exact product degrees; no scalar load has been used to bound a covector.

We now prove the transport assertion directly.  Equation (17) gives \(s_z>m_z\geq0\) for every possible complementary source, so every denominator in (23), (25), and (26) is positive.  For fixed \(z\in Z\), (15) and (25) give
\[
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal I}x_{i,z}
 =\frac{m_z}{s_z}\sum_{e\ni z}\varepsilon_e
 =m_z,
\tag{49}
\]
and every incidence carrying this flow has base \(r_i=z\).  Hence
\[
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal I}r_ix_{i,z}=m_zz.
\tag{50}
\]
At an incidence \(i=(e,z)\), no other source sends flow, and therefore
\[
 \sum_{y\in Z}|x_{i,y}|
 =\frac{m_z}{s_z}\varepsilon_e
 \leq\kappa_0s_i.
\tag{51}
\]
Equations (49)-(51) prove feasibility of (25) with congestion \(\kappa_0\).  Equation (17) gives \(m_z/s_z<1\) on the finite nonempty set \(Z\), while the empty convention is explicit.  This proves (24), including every statement made about the transport program.

For completeness, the value in (20) is invariant under every base change
\[
 r\longmapsto Ur+b,
 \qquad
 U\in\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb Z),
 \quad b\in\mathbb Z^n.
\tag{51a}
\]
Indeed, the scalar equation in (21) transforms its moment equation into
\[
 \sum_i(Ur_i+b)x_{i,z}
 =U(m_zz)+bm_z
 =m_z(Uz+b),
\]
while (22) is unchanged.  Applying the inverse base change proves equality of the two infima.  Thus the adjective affine-invariant in (20) invokes no norm, diameter, or conditioning estimate.

It remains to verify simultaneous physical realization and the dual identities.  From (15) and (26),
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}\eta_{e,o}
 =\alpha_o\frac{\sum_{e\ni z}\varepsilon_e}{s_z}
 =\alpha_o.
\tag{52}
\]
Equations (12), (14), and (52) prove (28).  Multiplying (28) by \(g_o\), summing over occurrences, and using (6) proves (29).  Thus no pointwise density is reused.

The mass identities (5), (13), (14), and (26) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 p_{e,z}
 &=\sum_{o:z_o=z}(\rho_{e,o}+\eta_{e,o})\ell_o\\
 &=q_e^{\mathrm{out}}
   +\frac{\varepsilon_e}{s_z}
    \sum_{o:z_o=z}\alpha_o\ell_o\\
 &=q_e^{\mathrm{out}}+\frac{m_z}{s_z}\varepsilon_e.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{53}
\]
Since \(m_z/s_z<1\) whenever \(m_z>0\), and the complementary term is zero when \(m_z=0\), equation (53) is at most
\(q_e^{\mathrm{out}}+\varepsilon_e=\lambda_e^{\mathrm{cen}}\).  Summing (53) over \(e\ni z\), then using (14)-(15), gives
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}p_{e,z}=u_z+m_z=L_z.
\tag{54}
\]
This proves (30).

Every nonzero scalar multiple of \(g_o\) has the same normalized barycenter \(y_o=c_z+h_o\), proving (31).  For \(p_{e,z}>0\), the normalized barycenter of \(F_{e,z}\) is a convex combination of these points.  Since every \(h_o\in D_0\) and \(D_0\) is convex, that barycenter lies in \(c_z+D_0\).  The density \(F_{e,z}\) is pointwise at most one by (29), has mass \(p_{e,z}\), and has this barycenter, so it also lies in \(\mathsf M_{p_{e,z}}(C_z)\).  This proves (32) directly from the original occurrence densities.  In particular, it does not infer physical realization from the scalar inequality (30).

By subadditivity and positive homogeneity of the support function, (7), (27), and (33) give
\[
 h_{D_0}(\Xi_{e,z})
 \leq\sum_{o:z_o=z}a_{e,o}h_{D_0}(\theta_o)
 =\sum_{o:z_o=z}a_{e,o}\ell_o
 =p_{e,z}.
\tag{55}
\]
This proves (34).  Equations (8) and (28) give
\[
 \sum_{e,z}\Xi_{e,z}
 =\sum_o\left(\sum_{e\ni z_o}a_{e,o}\right)\theta_o
 =\sum_o\theta_o=0,
\tag{56}
\]
and the same regrouping, with every piece retaining the base \(z_o\), gives
\[
 \sum_{e,z}z\otimes\Xi_{e,z}
 =\sum_oz_o\otimes\theta_o=0.
\tag{57}
\]
This proves (35).  Likewise, (7), (9), and (28) give
\[
 \sum_{e,z,o:z_o=z}a_{e,o}\theta_o(h_o)
 =\sum_o\theta_o(h_o)
 =\sum_o\ell_o
 =\Omega_H,
\tag{58}
\]
which proves (36) with no scaling loss.

Finally, for every nonnegative coefficient \(a_{e,o}\), equation (7) gives
\[
 h_{c_z+D_0}(a_{e,o}\theta_o)
 =a_{e,o}\theta_o(c_z)+a_{e,o}h_{D_0}(\theta_o)
 =a_{e,o}\theta_o(c_z+h_o)
 =a_{e,o}\theta_o(y_o).
\tag{59}
\]
Subadditivity of the support function, followed by (28), (59), and (9), yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{e,z}h_{c_z+D_0}(\Xi_{e,z})
 &\leq
 \sum_{e,z,o:z_o=z}
 h_{c_z+D_0}(a_{e,o}\theta_o)\\
 &=\sum_o\theta_o(y_o)
 =-\Delta_H.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{60}
\]
Negating (60) proves (37).  All transported coefficients were nonnegative, all bases and support shifts were preserved, and (29) is the simultaneous physical ledger.  This completes the bounded-congestion and physical-incidence theorem.
