## Statement

Use the concave homothetic profile, $R$, $w$ and interval $I$ of the projective axial coordinate lemma. If the through-origin section of raw normal $(c,1)$ has centroid $0$, put $s=-c\cdot q$ and take any affine lower support $L$ of $R$ at $0$. The raw coarea of the section is
$$
R(0)\int_D w(-c\cdot q)\,dq+J_c,\qquad
J_c=\int_{D\cap\{s\in I\}}w(s)[R(s)-L(s)]\,dq\ge0.
$$
For affine $r$ one has $J_c=0$.

## Proof

The raw coarea from the axial lemma is the integral of $w(s)R(s)$ on the indicated domain. Subtract and add $w(s)L(s)$. Since $L(s)=R(0)+\lambda s$, the first moment identity from that lemma gives $\int_D w(s)s\,dq=-c\cdot\int_D q\,w(s)\,dq=0$. Convexity of $R$ makes the remaining integrand nonnegative. If $r$ is affine then $R$ is affine and its support at the interior point $0$ is $R$ itself.
