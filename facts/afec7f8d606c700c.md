## Statement

For positive raw data $k,Q_i,\sigma_i$, $i=1,2,3$, let $q_i=Q_i/k$, $M=\sum_iQ_i/\sigma_i$, $f_i=2\sqrt{kQ_i}/(\sigma_iM)$ and $\Phi_i=f_i+(f_j+f_l)^2/4$ for $\{i,j,l\}=\{1,2,3\}$. There are data with every $q_i\ge20/49$, $\sum_i q_i-(3/4)\max_iq_i<1$ and every $\Phi_i\le2$ for which the derivative of the tight $\Phi_1$ is strictly positive under the loss $k(t)=k-t,\ Q_2(t)=Q_2-t$, with all other raw data fixed. The exact derivative is $66193/210250$.

## Proof

Take $k=1$ and prescribe
$$
f=(1/25,41/25,29/25),\qquad r=(16/25,29/40,3927/5800),\qquad
Q_i=r_i^2,\qquad \sigma_i=2r_i/f_i.
$$
Then $\sum_i f_i r_i=2$ gives $M=1$, so the definitions recover the prescribed $f_i$. The cap fractions are $256/625,841/1600,15421329/33640000$. The smallest is $256/625=20/49+44/30625$, the largest is $841/1600$, and the sorted profile is $134483117/134560000<1$. Direct substitution gives $(\Phi_1,\Phi_2,\Phi_3)=(2,2,1166/625)$.

At $t=0$, logarithmic differentiation gives $f_i'/f_i=-1/(2k)-\mathbf1_{\{i=2\}}/(2Q_2)+1/(\sigma_2M)$. For the first row put $z=(f_2+f_3)/2=7/5$ and $C=f_1+z(f_2+f_3)=99/25$. Using $1/(\sigma_2M)=f_2/(2kr_2)$ and $Q_2=kr_2^2$, the chain rule gives
$$
-2k\Phi_1'(0)=C+zf_2/r_2^2-Cf_2/r_2=-66193/105125.
$$
Since $k=1$, this proves the strictly outward derivative under the stated scalar conditions.
