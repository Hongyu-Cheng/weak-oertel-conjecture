## Statement

Let $P(A,B;g)$ denote the infimum of $|A\cap\{u\cdot x\ge u\cdot g+t\}|+|B\cap\{u\cdot x\ge u\cdot g-t\}|$ over $u\ne0$, $t\in\mathbb R$. For every $\alpha>3/4$ and every finite $C$, there are full-dimensional rational planar trapezoids of equal area $a=1/2$ with $P(A,B;g_M)<(8/9)a-C(m-a)^\alpha$, where $M=(A+B)/2$, $m=|M|$ and $g_M$ is its centroid. In particular, for $A=K_{5051/5000}$ and $B=K_{101/100}$, where $K_w=\{(x,z):(w-w^{-1})/2\le x\le(w+w^{-1})/2,\ |z|\le x/2\}$, one has $m=102030201/204060400$ and a paired-cap certificate with raw area below $(8/9)(2a-m)_+$ by $13214404325059960523/60958950114838265933040900$.

## Proof

Write $h(w)=(w+w^{-1})/2$ and $\ell(w)=(w-w^{-1})/2$. The section at $x$ is centered of length $x$. Integration gives area $a=1/2$ and first centroid coordinate $\mu(w)=w/2+1/(6w^3)$. For $A=K_u,B=K_v$ the midpoint has the same centered section of length $x$ between $L=(\ell(u)+\ell(v))/2$ and $H=(h(u)+h(v))/2$, because any feasible pair of sections adds to the entire required interval. Integrating $x$ and $x^2$ gives
$$
m=\frac{(u+v)^2}{8uv},\qquad
g=\frac{(u+v)(3u^2v^2+1)}{12u^2v^2},\qquad
\frac{\mu(u)+\mu(v)}2-g=\frac{(u-v)^2(u+v)}{12u^3v^3}.
$$
The transverse centroid coordinate is zero. Use normal $(1,0)$ and shift $h(u)-g$ to obtain a null supporting cap on $A$ and threshold $\tau=2g-h(u)$ on $B$.

For rational $0<s\le1/100$, take $v=1+s$ and $u=1+s+2s^2$. Put $\tau_0=\mu(u)+\mu(v)-h(u)$. The function $h(w)-\mu(w)$ is positive and decreasing for $w>1$, so $1/3\le 2\mu(v)-h(v)\le\tau_0<\mu(v)<1\le h(v)$. The first inequality follows from the value at $w=1$ and derivative $(w^2-1)(w^2+2)/(2w^4)$ of $2\mu(w)-h(w)$. The centroid displacement is $s^4(u+v)/(3u^3v^3)<s^4$, using $(u+v)/(u^3v^3)\le2$. Thus $\tau_0-2s^4<\tau\le\tau_0$, and $\tau>1/3-2s^4>s\ge\ell(v)$. The selected cap sum is $(h(v)^2-\tau^2)/2$.

Substitute the displayed $g$, $h(v)$, $u$ and $v$ into that sum and clear denominators. This gives
$$
m-a=\frac{s^4}{2uv},\qquad
\frac89 a-\frac{h(v)^2-\tau^2}{2}=\frac{s^3}{18u^4v^4}
(4+30s+132s^2+374s^3+735s^4+1035s^5+1033s^6+710s^7+300s^8+56s^9).
$$
The polynomial is at least four. Since $1<u\le51/50$ and $1<v\le101/100$, the quotient of the raw shortfall by $(m-a)^\alpha$ is bounded below by a positive constant times $s^{3-4\alpha}$ and diverges as $s\downarrow0$. For $s=1/100$, direct substitution gives $\tau=17358032822901/52050808560200$, $m=102030201/204060400$ and
$$
\frac89(1-m)-\frac{h(v)^2-\tau^2}{2}
=\frac{13214404325059960523}{60958950114838265933040900}>0.
$$
The same calculation applies after opposite translations aligning the two source centroids, because they preserve $M$ and translate the two thresholds by opposite offsets. The reference point for the paired caps is still the true centroid of $M$.
