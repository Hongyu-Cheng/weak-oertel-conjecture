## Proof

We first derive the uniform physical-fiber atom cap. In a good parity class \(\xi\), the exact half-strength product formula gives reservoir mass at most \(A_\xi/8\), where \(A_\xi:=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}a_z\). Hence \(Q\leq W/8\), where \(W:=\sum_za_z\). Equation (3) gives \(G<Q/100\leq W/800<W/2\). Accepted fact c81f02314cb34f8b therefore applies and gives
\[
 a_z\leq2^{-n}G<\frac{Q}{100\,2^n}
 \qquad(a_z>0).
\tag{46}
\]

Fix \((\mathscr F,\lambda)\) as in (9). Every label uses two distinct parent edges. The transverse-pairing edge-capacity constraints in accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66, together with \(\lambda_\sigma\leq\theta_\sigma\), give
\[
 \kappa_g\leq
 \sum_{\sigma:g\in\{e_\sigma,f_\sigma\}}
 \theta_\sigma
 \leq\omega_g.
\tag{47}
\]
Every label is counted once at each of its two parents, proving
\[
 \sum_g\kappa_g=2L.
\tag{48}
\]
At a fixed anchor \(z\), every anchored label again contributes once to each of two distinct incident parents, so
\[
 \sum_{g\ni z}b_{g,z}=2L_z.
\tag{49}
\]
Moreover \(b_{g,z}\leq\kappa_g\leq\omega_g\). The canonical reservoir degree bound gives
\[
 2L_z
 \leq\sum_{g\ni z}\omega_g
 \leq\sum_{g\ni z}r_g
 \leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{50}
\]
Equations (46) and (50) prove (12)--(13).

We next complete all charged original parent pairs. For \(g=\{u,v\}\) with \(\kappa_g>0\), define
\[
 h_{g,u}:=\frac{2\kappa_g}{a_u}f_{g,u},
 \qquad
 h_{g,v}:=\frac{2\kappa_g}{a_v}f_{g,v}.
\tag{51}
\]
Each density has mass \(\kappa_g\), has barycenter \(x_{g,u}\) or \(x_{g,v}\), and is pointwise at most one. Thus both contacts belong to the corresponding \(\kappa_g\)-metronoids. The edges are same-parity, so \(m_g\in\mathbb Z^n\). Applying accepted positive-root metronoid convexity dfd25e72c1f45a4e at equal mass \(\kappa_g\) proves (14).

We verify simultaneous domination rather than adding separate depth inequalities. For every physical source \(u\), equations (47) and the reservoir degree bound give
\[
 \sum_{g\ni u}h_{g,u}
 \leq\frac{2}{a_u}\sum_{g\ni u}\kappa_g
 \leq\frac12.
\tag{52}
\]
Fix a closed ambient halfspace \(H\). If \((m_g,\ell_g)\in H\), at least one of \((u,x_{g,u})\), \((v,x_{g,v})\) belongs to \(H\); orient that occurrence to one such endpoint. At one source \(u\), aggregate the oriented densities in (51). The result has mass \(R_u\), is pointwise at most \(1/2\), and its barycenter is a convex combination of endpoint contacts lying in the convex section \(H_u\). Twice this aggregate is a legal density of mass \(2R_u\) with the same barycenter. Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d therefore gives at least \(2\gamma_dR_u\) of \(C_u\)-volume in \(H_u\). Summing over the distinct physical sources proves
\[
 \nu_{\rm par}^{\mathscr F}(H)
 \leq\mu_C(H).
\tag{53}
\]
This proof is unchanged when midpoint or contact atoms collide, because orientation is edgewise and the source densities are aggregated before the volume bound. Equations (48) and (15) give (16).

We now pool every anchor-side occurrence. On \(C_z\), put
\[
 F_z:=\sum_{g\ni z}\frac{2b_{g,z}}{a_z}f_{g,z}.
\tag{54}
\]
Equations (49)--(50) give
\[
 \int_{C_z}F_z=2L_z,
 \qquad
 0\leq F_z\leq\frac{4L_z}{a_z}\leq\frac12.
\tag{55}
\]
Let \(p_z\) be the barycenter of \(F_z\). The density \(2F_z\) is legal, has mass \(4L_z\), and has the same barycenter. Hence \(p_z\in\mathsf M_{4L_z}(C_z)\), and accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives (17). If a closed halfspace contains \((z,p_z)\), its section in \(C_z\) has volume at least \(4\gamma_dL_z\). Summing over distinct anchor fibers proves domination of (18), and \(\sum_zL_z=L\) proves (19).

Suppose \(r_{\rm anc}^{\mathscr F}=0\). By the definition of intrinsic residual rank, there is an affine map \(s\) on \(\operatorname{aff}Z_{\mathscr F}\) with \(s(z)=p_z\) for every anchor. Apply accepted fact 6d6fcf377e68c79c with continuous dimension zero to the finite lattice measure
\[
 \sum_{z\in Z_{\mathscr F}}4L_z\delta_z.
\tag{56}
\]
It supplies \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\) such that every proper closed base halfspace containing \(m\) carries at least \(2^{-n}\) of the total mass. Strict separation would give a proper closed halfspace containing \(m\) and disjoint from \(Z_{\mathscr F}\) if \(m\notin\operatorname{conv}Z_{\mathscr F}\), so in fact \(m\in\operatorname{conv}Z_{\mathscr F}\). For every barycentric representation \(\alpha\in\Lambda_m(Z_{\mathscr F})\), affineness and (17) give
\[
 s(m)=\sum_z\alpha_zp_z
 \in\sum_z\alpha_z\mathsf M_{4L_z}(C_z).
\tag{57}
\]
Thus the complete metronoid core is nonempty for the selected masses \(4L_z\). The definition retained in accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb proves \(G\geq4L\), which is (32).

We construct the complete anchor-product matching. Direct summation of (21) gives (22). Summing its degrees and dividing by two gives (23). In one nonempty parity class,
\[
 \frac{\sum_{z\equiv\xi}L_z^2}{L_\xi}
 \leq\max_{z\equiv\xi}L_z.
\tag{58}
\]
There are at most \(2^n\) nonempty classes, and (13) is strict. Hence
\[
 \sum_{\xi:L_\xi>0}
 \frac{\sum_{z\equiv\xi}L_z^2}{L_\xi}
 <2^n\frac{Q}{800\,2^n}=\frac Q{800},
\tag{59}
\]
which proves (24).

For an edge \(\{u,v\}\) of \(q^{\mathscr F}\), define
\[
 F_{uv,u}:=\frac{q_{uv}^{\mathscr F}}{2L_u}F_u,
 \qquad
 F_{uv,v}:=\frac{q_{uv}^{\mathscr F}}{2L_v}F_v.
\tag{60}
\]
They both have mass \(q_{uv}^{\mathscr F}\) and barycenters \(p_u,p_v\). Equation (22) gives the simultaneous source ledger
\[
 \sum_{v:q_{uv}^{\mathscr F}>0}F_{uv,u}
 =\frac{d_u(q^{\mathscr F})}{2L_u}F_u
 \leq F_u\leq\frac12.
\tag{61}
\]
The same-parity condition makes \(m_{uv}\) integral. Convexity gives the first inclusion in (26), and accepted fact dfd25e72c1f45a4e, applied to the equal endpoint masses in (60), gives the metronoid membership in (26).

For domination of (27), fix a closed ambient halfspace and orient every included midpoint atom to one of its endpoint atoms \((u,p_u)\), \((v,p_v)\) lying in the halfspace. At one source \(u\), the sum of the oriented densities from (60) is a scalar multiple of \(F_u\), has mass \(R_u\), is pointwise at most \(1/2\), and has barycenter \(p_u\) in the halfspace section. Twice it is a legal density of mass \(2R_u\). Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives at least \(2\gamma_dR_u\) of source volume in that section. Summing over sources proves
\[
 \nu_{\rm mid}^{\mathscr F}(H)\leq\mu_C(H).
\tag{62}
\]
The argument starts with every edge occurrence and aggregates only after orientation, so repeated midpoint bases and repeated full atoms do not duplicate source capacity. Equation (28) follows by summing the atomic coefficients.

Apply the dominated-measure residual theorem in accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 to (15), (18), and (27). After multiplying its depth bound by \(2^n\), their masses (16), (19), and (28) give the three \(D\)-bounds in (29)--(31). For the anchor points, use the legal selector weights \(4\gamma_dL_z\leq\delta_C(z,p_z)\) and an affine map attaining the residual rank. The definition of \(R_{\rm sel}(C)\) in the same accepted fact gives the second bound in (30).

Equation (3) and nonnegativity imply that the right side of (29) is strictly smaller than \(Q/100\), and likewise for (31). Since both \(D\) and \(R_{\rm sel}(C)\) are at least \(S_{\rm anc}^{\mathscr F}\), equation (3) gives
\[
 2S_{\rm anc}^{\mathscr F}<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{63}
\]
Rearranging proves (33)--(35).

For the full specialization, equations (5), (13), and \(\sum_zL_z=P\) give
\[
 |Z_\Sigma|>
 \frac{(99/200)Q}{Q/(800\,2^n)}
 =396\,2^n.
\tag{64}
\]
Equations (5) and (24) give
\[
 \Lambda_\Sigma>
 \left(\frac{99}{200}-\frac1{800}\right)Q
 =\frac{79Q}{160}.
\tag{65}
\]
Substitution into (33)--(35) proves (38)--(39).

For the complete internal specialization, accepted fact 2fe46f47d19112ea gives (8), including every internally rescaled label rather than only \(\mathscr B\). Thus the same atom cap gives
\[
 |Z_{\mathscr A_{\rm int}}|>
 \frac{(7/400)Q}{Q/(800\,2^n)}
 =14\,2^n,
\tag{66}
\]
while (8) and (24) give
\[
 \Lambda_{\mathscr A_{\rm int}}
 >\left(\frac7{400}-\frac1{800}\right)Q
 =\frac{13Q}{800}.
\tag{67}
\]
This proves (37)--(40).

Finally, both complete product families satisfy the free-matching hypothesis of accepted fact c612596313a0ff6c by (22). Its finite-horizon alternative says that
\[

\tag{68}
\]
forces recycling at stages \(0,\ldots,J-1\), preserves the edge support under \(q_j=\gamma_d^jq\), and produces \(C_J\) with all displayed mass bounds. Accepted fact 2fe46f47d19112ea gives \(\gamma_d>4/11\). The exact computations (41)--(42), together with \(G<Q/100\), verify (68) with \(J=6\) for the full family and \(J=2\) for the internal family. Applying the accepted theorem proves (43)--(45) and completes the proof.
