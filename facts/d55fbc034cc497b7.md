## Statement

Let $A,K,B$ be planar convex bodies of areas $a,k,b$ with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$ and $1\le b/a\le21/20$. For their actual lifted hull at integer bases $-1,0,1$, some point of the middle fiber has normalized mixed closed-halfspace depth strictly greater than $2/9$. The point can be chosen as $g_K$ if $k>a+b$ and as the centroid of $(A+B)/2$ if $k\le a+b$.

## Proof

The lifted hull has exactly these fibers by the midpoint inclusion and the equality of the two endpoint weights at base zero. At $(0,g_K)$ the full-mass planar centroid bound of 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives raw depth at least $4k/9$, which is strictly larger than $2(k+a+b)/9$ when $k>a+b$.

For $k\le a+b$, put $\beta=b/a$, $M=(A+B)/2$ and $r=|M|/a$. Brunn–Minkowski gives $r\ge r_{\min}=(1+\sqrt\beta)^2/4\ge1$, and $r\le k/a\le1+\beta$. Tighten each containing halfspace through $(0,g_M)$. If its continuous normal is nonzero, it pays at least $4|M|/9$ from the actual subset $M$ by the same centroid bound. Its endpoints are paired caps with opposite shifts. Apply the complementary-cap estimate of f0399a8f0bae48c6 §1 with midpoint container $M$ and opposite middle area at most $5|M|/9$. Dividing by $a$ and expanding gives paired payment at least
$$
F_\beta(r)=1-\beta-20r/9+(4/3)\sqrt{5\beta r},
$$
because $2\sqrt{5r/9}>\sqrt\beta$ and the outer positive part can only increase the bound. By pk-w34-payments-planar-section-width-credit, the same paired payment divided by $a$ is also at least $W(r)=2r/[9(2r-1)]$.

It suffices to show $4r/9+\max\{F_\beta(r),W(r)\}>4(1+\beta)/9$. Use $r_s=(19\beta+14)/20$, which satisfies $r_{\min}\le\beta<r_s<1+\beta$. Below $r_s$ the residual difference multiplied by nine is the concave function
$$
12\sqrt{5\beta r}-16r+5-13\beta .
$$
At $r_{\min}$ it exceeds $132/5+5-29(21/20)=19/20$, using $r_{\min}\le\beta\le21/20$ and $\sqrt{5\beta r_{\min}}\ge\sqrt5>11/5$. At $r_s$ positivity is equivalent to $6\sqrt{\beta(19\beta+14)}>(141\beta+31)/5$. Both sides are positive. The difference of their squares multiplied by $25$ is $-2781\beta^2+3858\beta-961$, a concave polynomial with positive endpoint values $116$ and $9539/400$. This proves strict positivity over the lower interval.

For $r\ge r_s$, the derivative of $4r/9+W(r)$ is $4/9-2/[9(2r-1)^2]>0$. At $r_s$ its excess over $4(1+\beta)/9$ is
$$
\frac{46-23\beta-19\beta^2}{45(19\beta+4)}>0,
$$
because the numerator decreases and equals $361/400$ at $\beta=21/20$. Thus the nonvertical cap bound exceeds $4(a+b)/9$ by a fixed positive amount for the given bodies, uniformly in the cap. Purely base through-caps contain all of $K$ and an endpoint, giving at least $k+a\ge2a>4(a+b)/9$. Since $4(a+b)/9\ge2(k+a+b)/9$ when $k\le a+b$, the infimum of all containing cap masses retains the strict inequality.
