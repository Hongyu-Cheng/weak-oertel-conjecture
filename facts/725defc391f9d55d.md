## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be a convex body, and for \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\) set
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
w_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z).
\]
Assume
\[
W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}w_z>0,
\qquad
\pi_z:=\frac{w_z}{W}.
\]
Let \(z_0\in\mathbb Z^n\), and suppose that for some \(\tau\in(0,1]\),
\[
\sum_{\substack{z\in\mathbb Z^n\\
u^\top(z-z_0)\geq0}}\pi_z\geq\tau
\qquad\text{for every }u\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}.
\]
Then there are coefficients \(\lambda_z\in[0,1]\), zero outside the finite set \(\{z:w_z>0\}\), such that
\[
\sum_z\lambda_z\pi_z=\tau,
\qquad
\sum_z\lambda_z\pi_z z=\tau z_0.
\]
Define the mixed measure
\[
\nu(A):=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z\cap C_z),
\qquad
A_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in A\},
\]
and its fiberwise selected submeasure
\[
\nu_\lambda(A):=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\lambda_z\operatorname{vol}_d(A_z\cap C_z).
\]
Then \(0\leq\nu_\lambda\leq\nu\), its total mass is exactly \(\tau W\), and its barycenter is a point
\[
p_\lambda=(z_0,x_\lambda)\in
C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d).
\]

Consequently, accepted fact `bb2b5f9519ae23e8` supplies a point \(z_0\in\mathbb Z^n\) to which the theorem applies with \(\tau=2^{-n}\). Thus every positive-mass mixed-integer convex body admits a fiberwise dominated submeasure of mass \(2^{-n}W\) whose barycenter belongs to \(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\).

## Proof

Because \(C\) is compact, its projection onto \(\mathbb R^n\) is bounded. Hence
\[
D_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:w_z>0\}
\]
is finite. The weights \((\pi_z)_{z\in D_+}\) form a probability distribution.

Define the level-\(\tau\) metronoid
\[
\mathcal M_\tau(\pi):=
\left\{
\frac1\tau\sum_{z\in D_+}\eta_z z:
0\leq\eta_z\leq\pi_z,\
\sum_{z\in D_+}\eta_z=\tau
\right\}.
\]
The coefficient set in this definition is a nonempty compact convex polytope: it is nonempty because the total capacity is \(\sum_z\pi_z=1\geq\tau\). Therefore \(\mathcal M_\tau(\pi)\) is a nonempty compact convex set.

Fix \(u\in\mathbb R^n\). If \(u\neq0\), the depth hypothesis gives
\[
\sum_{z\in D_u}\pi_z\geq\tau,
\qquad
D_u:=\{z\in D_+:u^\top(z-z_0)\geq0\}.
\]
If \(u=0\), the same inequality holds with \(D_u=D_+\). Since \(D_u\) is finite and its capacities have total at least \(\tau\), one can select exactly mass \(\tau\) from it: enumerate its points, take each capacity in order until the accumulated mass first reaches \(\tau\), take only the required fraction of the last capacity, and set all remaining coefficients to zero. Thus there are coefficients \(\eta_z^{(u)}\) satisfying
\[
0\leq\eta_z^{(u)}\leq\pi_z,\qquad
\eta_z^{(u)}=0\ \text{for }z\notin D_u,\qquad
\sum_z\eta_z^{(u)}=\tau.
\]
Every selected \(z\) satisfies \(u^\top z\geq u^\top z_0\), and hence
\[
\frac1\tau\sum_z\eta_z^{(u)}u^\top z
\geq u^\top z_0.
\]
It follows that the support function of the metronoid satisfies
\[
h_{\mathcal M_\tau(\pi)}(u)
=
\frac1\tau
\max_{\substack{0\leq\eta_z\leq\pi_z\\
\sum_z\eta_z=\tau}}
\sum_z\eta_z u^\top z
\geq u^\top z_0
\qquad\text{for every }u\in\mathbb R^n.
\]
If \(z_0\notin\mathcal M_\tau(\pi)\), strong separation of a point from a nonempty compact convex set would give some \(u\in\mathbb R^n\) with
\[
u^\top z_0>
\sup_{m\in\mathcal M_\tau(\pi)}u^\top m
=h_{\mathcal M_\tau(\pi)}(u),
\]
contradicting the preceding support-function inequality. Therefore
\[
z_0\in\mathcal M_\tau(\pi).
\]
By the definition of the metronoid, there are coefficients \(\eta_z\) such that
\[
0\leq\eta_z\leq\pi_z,\qquad
\sum_z\eta_z=\tau,\qquad
\sum_z\eta_z z=\tau z_0.
\]
Set
\[
\lambda_z:=
\begin{cases}
\eta_z/\pi_z,&z\in D_+,\\
0,&z\notin D_+.
\end{cases}
\]
Then \(0\leq\lambda_z\leq1\), and
\[
\sum_z\lambda_z\pi_z=\tau,
\qquad
\sum_z\lambda_z\pi_z z=\tau z_0.
\]

For every measurable \(A\), each summand defining \(\nu_\lambda(A)\) is between zero and the corresponding summand defining \(\nu(A)\). Thus \(0\leq\nu_\lambda\leq\nu\). Its total mass is
\[
\nu_\lambda(\mathbb R^{n+d})
=\sum_z\lambda_z w_z
=W\sum_z\lambda_z\pi_z
=\tau W.
\]

For each \(z\in D_+\), let
\[
c_z:=\frac1{w_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx
\]
be the centroid of the positive-volume compact convex fiber \(C_z\). Its centroid belongs to \(C_z\), so \((z,c_z)\in C\). The barycenter of \(\nu_\lambda/(\tau W)\) is
\[
\begin{aligned}
p_\lambda
&=\frac1{\tau W}
\sum_z\lambda_z\int_{C_z}(z,x)\,dx\\
&=\sum_{z\in D_+}
\frac{\lambda_z w_z}{\tau W}(z,c_z).
\end{aligned}
\]
The coefficients in the last expression are nonnegative and sum to one. Hence \(p_\lambda\in C\) by convexity. Its first coordinate is
\[
\frac1{\tau W}\sum_z\lambda_z w_z z
=\frac1\tau\sum_z\lambda_z\pi_z z
=z_0.
\]
Thus \(p_\lambda=(z_0,x_\lambda)\) has integer first coordinate and belongs to \(C\), proving
\[
p_\lambda\in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d).
\]

For the final corollary, accepted fact `bb2b5f9519ae23e8` proves that the fiber-mass distribution has a lattice point \(z_0\) for which every closed marginal halfspace containing \(z_0\) has mass at least \(2^{-n}W\). After division by \(W\), the hypothesis above holds with \(\tau=2^{-n}\). Applying the lifting theorem gives the asserted dominated submeasure and admissible barycenter.
