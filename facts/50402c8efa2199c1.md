## Statement

Let $v_\ell=(z_\ell,x_\ell)$ be finitely many labeled physical atoms with positive capacities $e_\ell$ and fixed normal labels $n_\ell$. For finitely many caps $H_i$ with given response vectors $b_i$, put
$$
T=\{\theta\ge0:\ \sum_i\theta_i=1,\ \sum_i\theta_i b_i=0\},
\qquad
\mu_\ell(\theta)=\frac{e_\ell}{\beta}\sum_i\theta_i\mathbf1_{H_i}(v_\ell),\quad \beta>0.
$$
A label-preserving physical martingale from this source to a submeasure of the full capacity exists exactly when a nonnegative array $\pi_{\ell k}$ satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
\pi_{\ell k}&=0 &&\text{if }n_\ell\ne n_k,\\
\sum_k\pi_{\ell k}&=\mu_\ell(\theta),&
\sum_\ell\pi_{\ell k}&\le e_k,\\
\sum_k\pi_{\ell k}(v_k-v_\ell)&=0 &&\text{for every }\ell.
\end{aligned}
$$
The feasible set $T_{\rm cap}\subseteq T$ is a compact convex polytope, possibly empty. There exists a response profile in $T\setminus T_{\rm cap}$ exactly when an extreme point of $T$ lies outside $T_{\rm cap}$.

## Proof

A joint atomic law yields the array of masses transported between ordered labeled atoms. Its first marginal and target capacity give the row and column conditions. On a row of positive mass the martingale condition is the last equation divided by that row's mass; on a zero row it is automatic. Conversely an array with these properties is exactly such a joint law. Identical atoms with the same label can be regarded as indexed copies; aggregating their rows preserves the equations, and a coupling on aggregated atoms can be split using their source weights and target capacities.

The joint feasible set in $(\theta,\pi)$ is a finite linear system. The response probabilities are bounded by one and the nonnegative transport entries by the total capacity, so the joint set is a compact convex polytope. Projection onto $\theta$ gives the stated feasible polytope. If every extreme point of $T$ is feasible, its convex hull is contained in the convex feasible set, and this hull is $T$ since $T$ is a bounded polyhedron. The converse is immediate, and the empty-$T$ case is vacuous.
