## Statement

For $r\in\{3,4\}$ let $D_r=\operatorname{conv}\{(-r,-r),(r,0),(0,r)\}$, with actual base $D_r\cap\mathbb Z^2$, and let $K$ be any nondegenerate planar triangle with barycentric coordinates $\lambda_j$. Put $\ell(z)=(-z_1-z_2,2z_1-z_2,2z_2-z_1)$ and $W(z,x)=\frac13\sum_{j=0}^2\mathbf1_{\{\lambda_j(x)-1/3+\ell_j(z)\ge0\}}$. In the $D_4\times K$ product, restrict to any one of the three exposed base edges and sum arclength on $\partial K$ over its five integer sites, with the outer continuous edge normal $n$ attached. The supremum of $\int\Phi W/\int\Phi$ for finite nonnegative $\Phi(n,z,x)$ convex in $(z,x)$ for each fixed $n$, with finite positive denominator and both integrals using this edge measure, is $2/5$. A constant potential attains it. For $D_3\times K$ on the base edge $z_1+z_2=3$, restricting the continuous integration to the facet $\lambda_0=0$, the constant test gives exactly $4/9$.

## Proof

On the exposed edge $z_1+z_2=4$ the integer sites are $(j,4-j)$ for $j=0,\ldots,4$. The offsets there are $(-4,3j-4,8-3j)$. A positive integer offset makes its whole trace full since $\lambda_i\ge0$. A negative integer offset makes it empty since $\lambda_i\le1$. None of these offsets vanishes, so the five profiles are $(1,1,2,1,1)/3$, constant on $K$.

For the five sites write $f_j$ for the integral of $\Phi$ over the whole marked continuous boundary. At each fixed boundary point off the finitely many vertices, its outer normal is the same for all five sites, and the physical base points are equally spaced along a line. Convexity gives $f_0+f_4\ge2f_2$ and $f_1+f_3\ge2f_2$ by integration. Hence $\sum_j f_j\ge5f_2$ and the quotient is
$$
\frac13+\frac{f_2}{3\sum_j f_j}\le\frac25.
$$
The constant test has equal positive $f_j$. For either other base edge, permute the forms $\ell_i$ and the corresponding barycentric labels. The base map permuting the primitive vertices $e_1,e_2,-e_1-e_2$ and its inverse are integral since they send the integer basis vertices to integer vectors. The five sites remain equally spaced and their profile constants are the same up to reversal. The identical argument works with the actual arclength on the original $K$.

On the $D_3$ edge the offsets at $(j,3-j)$ are $(-3,3j-3,6-3j)$ for $j=0,\ldots,3$. At the two endpoints exactly one cap is full and the other two are empty. At each of the two inner sites exactly one cap is full and the cap with zero offset has its canonical partial trace. On $\lambda_0=0$, the corresponding $\lambda_1$ or $\lambda_2$ varies linearly and uniformly from zero to one under normalized arclength, so the partial trace occupies $2/3$ of that facet. Its mean profile there is $(1+2/3)/3=5/9$. The uniform average over the four equally weighted sites is $(1/3+5/9+5/9+1/3)/4=4/9$.
