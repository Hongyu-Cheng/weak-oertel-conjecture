## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and retain the exact canonical hard pooled KKT objects, normalizations, and alias maps of accepted facts `f6188983e8850f01`, `e8b320da9989469f`, `6a74481cad1d7e81`, and `695b44a4f0fdcd3a`. Thus \(r=(r_e)\) is the half-strength canonical product reservoir, its original load-minimal optimizer has loads \(0\leq\omega_e\leq r_e\), and
\[
 Q:=\sum_e r_e>0,
 \qquad
 \Omega:=\sum_e\omega_e,
 \qquad
 R:=R_C(r),
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C).
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(G\), \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), and \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\) have their canonical meanings, and assume the canonical hard ledger
\[
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D<\frac Q{100},
\tag{2}
\]
whose six summands are nonnegative. Retain the pooled target score
\[
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}
 :=\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}
 \frac{\widehat\Omega}{r_J+1},
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
\tag{3}
\]
where \(\widehat\Omega\), \(\rho_J\), and \(r_J\) are exactly the pooled total mass, base rank, and residual rank of accepted fact `f6188983e8850f01`. In particular,
\[
 \Omega>\frac{99Q}{100}.
\tag{4}
\]

For the canonical target multipliers \(0<\chi_m\leq1\), put
\[
 \lambda_e:=1-\chi_{m_e},
 \qquad
 \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}:=\lambda_e\omega_e,
 \qquad
 B_{\mathrm{cong}}:=\sum_e\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 =\Omega-\widehat\Omega.
\tag{5}
\]
Define the affine-retainable congestion mass by
\[
 C_\beta:=
 \begin{cases}
  0,&B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0,\\[1mm]
  R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}),&B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0.
 \end{cases}
\tag{6}
\]
If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), set \(P_{\mathrm{anc}}:=0\) and introduce no ancestral support, base rank, residual rank, occurrence rank, or drift rank. If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), retain the nonempty ancestral endpoint support and ranks of accepted fact `e8b320da9989469f`, and put
\[
 P_{\mathrm{anc}}
 :=4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cong}}}{r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1}.
\tag{7}
\]
In both cases define
\[
 A:=\max\{P_{\mathrm{pool}},P_{\mathrm{anc}}\}.
\tag{8}
\]

When \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), apply the fresh load-minimal reoptimization of accepted fact `6a74481cad1d7e81` to \(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}\), with the congestion alias map fixed in accepted fact `f6188983e8850f01`, and denote its total load by \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}\). When \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), extend the notation by \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}:=0\). Define
\[
 F_{\mathrm{fr}}:=0
 \qquad\text{if }\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0,
\tag{9}
\]
and in this case introduce no fresh support, fresh base rank, fresh residual rank, or fresh drift rank. Only when \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), retain the nonempty fresh endpoint support and ranks of accepted fact `e8b320da9989469f` and define
\[
 F_{\mathrm{fr}}
 :=2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 {\widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}+1}.
\tag{10}
\]

Then the following three lower bounds hold independently:
\[
 \boxed{D\geq A,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{R\geq\max\{Q-\Omega,C_\beta\},}
 \qquad
 \boxed{R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq\max\{A,F_{\mathrm{fr}}\}.}
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{
 D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 A+\max\{Q-\Omega,C_\beta\}
 +\max\{A,F_{\mathrm{fr}}\}.}
\tag{12}
\]
The exact strict hard-branch screen is
\[
 \boxed{
 A+\max\{Q-\Omega,C_\beta\}
 +\max\{A,F_{\mathrm{fr}}\}
 <\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{13}
\]
In particular,
\[
 A<\frac Q{200},
 \qquad
 \max\{Q-\Omega,C_\beta\}<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 F_{\mathrm{fr}}<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{14}
\]

Retain the same-ray negative-circuit decomposition
\((\omega_e^{(j)})_{e,1\leq j\leq N}\) and supports
\[
 C_j:=\{e:\omega_e^{(j)}>0\}
\]
of accepted fact `236e5d1f3c2efa28`, and define its congestion circuit bottleneck
\[
 \mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}
 :=\sum_{j=1}^N
 \min_{e\in C_j}\lambda_e\omega_e^{(j)}.
\tag{15}
\]

For the additional positive-fresh conclusion, assume \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\). Only in this positive-fresh case define the drift rank
\[
 k_{\mathrm{drift}}
 :=\dim\operatorname{span}
 \{\widetilde p_z^{\mathrm{cong}}-p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}:
 z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}\}.
\tag{16}
\]
Then \(E_{\mathrm{fr}}\subseteq E_{\mathrm{anc}}\), and the exact circuit/drift chain is
\[
 \boxed{
 F_{\mathrm{fr}}
 \geq
 2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cong}}-C_\beta}
 {r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}}+1}
 \geq
 2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}}
 {r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}}+1}
 \geq
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}
 \frac{\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}}
 {r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}}+1}.}
\tag{17}
\]
Moreover, \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}-C_\beta>0\), and (13) forces the strict circuit/drift rank screen
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}}+1
 >100\,2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cong}}-C_\beta}{Q}
 \geq100\,2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}}{Q}.}
\tag{18}
\]

Finally, suppose \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), including the zero-charge case under the conventions above. Then
\[
 \boxed{C_\beta=B_{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad F_{\mathrm{fr}}=0,}
\tag{19}
\]
and (12)-(13) reduce exactly to
\[
 \boxed{
 D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq2A+\max\{Q-\Omega,B_{\mathrm{cong}}\},}
\tag{20}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 2A+\max\{Q-\Omega,B_{\mathrm{cong}}\}
 <\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{21}
\]
Equivalently, the second term in (20)-(21) is
\(\max\{Q-\Omega,\Omega-\widehat\Omega\}\). Every original negative circuit also contains an edge \(e\) with \(\chi_{m_e}=1\). If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), then \(A=P_{\mathrm{pool}}\), all ancestral and fresh ranks appearing in (7), (10), and (16) remain undefined rather than being assigned empty-set values, and (20) reads
\[
 D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq2P_{\mathrm{pool}}+Q-\Omega.
\tag{22}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact `f6188983e8850f01` gives
\[
 D\geq P_{\mathrm{pool}},
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq P_{\mathrm{pool}}.
\tag{23}
\]
If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), accepted fact `e8b320da9989469f` gives
\[
 D\geq P_{\mathrm{anc}},
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq P_{\mathrm{anc}}.
\tag{24}
\]
If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), equation (24) holds under the convention \(P_{\mathrm{anc}}=0\) by nonnegativity of \(D\) and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), without forming an ancestral support or rank. Taking the larger of the two lower bounds separately for each capacity in (23)-(24) proves
\[
 D\geq A,
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq A.
\tag{25}
\]

Apply accepted fact `695b44a4f0fdcd3a` with its matching \(q\) identified with the present canonical reservoir \(r\), its total mass with \(Q\), and its load-minimal dual loads with the canonical \(\omega_e\) of (1). The reservoir is a legal nonzero off-diagonal same-parity fractional matching, and its endpoint degree bound is even the stronger half-strength bound used by the canonical construction. That fact therefore gives
\[
 R=R_C(r)\geq Q-\Omega.
\tag{26}
\]
If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), the charge in (5) satisfies \(0\leq\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\leq\omega_e\), so accepted fact `6a74481cad1d7e81`, under the exact congestion alias map, gives
\[
 R\geq R_C(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}})=C_\beta.
\tag{27}
\]
If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), (27) follows from \(C_\beta=0\) and \(R\geq0\), without applying a nonzero-charge theorem. Taking the larger of the two independent lower bounds (26)-(27) proves the middle assertion of (11).

Suppose next that \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\). Then necessarily \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), all ranks in (10) are defined on the actual nonempty fresh support, and the fresh endpoint-selector conclusion of accepted fact `6a74481cad1d7e81` gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 {\widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}+1}
 =F_{\mathrm{fr}}.
\tag{28}
\]
If \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), equation (28) holds under the convention \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}=0\) by nonnegativity of \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), without defining a fresh support or rank. Taking the larger of (25) and (28), both of which bound the same single capacity but are not added, proves
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq\max\{A,F_{\mathrm{fr}}\}.
\tag{29}
\]
Equations (25), (26)-(27), and (29) prove all three independent assertions in (11). They concern the three distinct summands \(D\), \(R\), and \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), respectively. Adding those three assertions once, while taking only maxima among competing lower bounds for any one summand, proves (12). No term has been charged twice to the same capacity.

By nonnegativity of the other three summands in (2), the hard ledger gives
\[
 D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{30}
\]
Combining (12) with (30) proves (13). The left side of (13) is at least \(2A\), proving the first inequality in (14). Each of the other two displayed quantities in (14) is at most the nonnegative left side of (13), proving the remaining inequalities.

Assume now \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\). Accepted fact `e8b320da9989469f` gives
\[
 E_{\mathrm{fr}}\subseteq E_{\mathrm{anc}},
 \qquad
 \widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}
 \leq r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}}.
\tag{31}
\]
The support inclusion also gives
\[
 \widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}
 \leq\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}.
\tag{32}
\]
Accepted fact `236e5d1f3c2efa28` gives, with the same congestion aliases,
\[
 \widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
 \geq B_{\mathrm{cong}}-C_\beta
 \geq\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}.
\tag{33}
\]
Using (31) and then (33) in the definition (10),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 F_{\mathrm{fr}}
 &=2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 {\widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}+1}\\
 &\geq
 2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cong}}-C_\beta}
 {r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}}+1}\\
 &\geq
 2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \frac{\mathcal B_{\mathrm{circ}}}
 {r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}}+1}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{34}
\]
Equation (32) proves the last inequality in (17). The zero-fresh characterization of accepted fact `6a74481cad1d7e81` says, for a nonzero charge,
\[
 \widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 C_\beta=B_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{35}
\]
Because the present fresh load is positive and always \(C_\beta\leq B_{\mathrm{cong}}\), equation (35) gives
\(B_{\mathrm{cong}}-C_\beta>0\). Combining the first lower bound in (17) with \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}<Q/100\) from (14), and rearranging positive quantities, proves the first strict inequality in (18). The second follows from (33).

It remains to treat \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\). If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), equation (35) gives \(C_\beta=B_{\mathrm{cong}}\). If \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), the same equality follows from the convention \(C_\beta=0\). Equation (9) gives \(F_{\mathrm{fr}}=0\) in both cases, and \(A\geq P_{\mathrm{pool}}>0\). Hence
\[
 \max\{A,F_{\mathrm{fr}}\}=A,
 \qquad
 \max\{Q-\Omega,C_\beta\}
 =\max\{Q-\Omega,B_{\mathrm{cong}}\}.
\tag{36}
\]
Substitution of (36) into (12)-(13) proves (20)-(21). Equation (5) gives the equivalent form involving \(\Omega-\widehat\Omega\).

The zero-fresh conclusion of accepted fact `236e5d1f3c2efa28` also gives, in both the positive-charge and zero-charge cases, that every original negative circuit \(C_j\) contains an edge \(e\) with \(\chi_{m_e}=1\). Finally, if \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), then \(P_{\mathrm{anc}}=0\), \(A=P_{\mathrm{pool}}\), and \(Q-\Omega\geq0\) because \(0\leq\omega_e\leq r_e\) edgewise. Thus (20) becomes (22), with no rank assigned to an empty support. This completes the proof.
