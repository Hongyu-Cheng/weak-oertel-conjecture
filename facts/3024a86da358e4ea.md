## Statement

Retain the complete canonical setting, notation, and physical ledgers of accepted facts 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 and 4f7a1ed043c380e1. Thus every positive whole label \(\sigma\in\Gamma\) has mass \(\theta_\sigma>0\), physical target \(t(\sigma)\), four tagged sources

\[
 \mathcal O_\sigma=[y_{\sigma,1},y_{\sigma,2},y_{\sigma,3},y_{\sigma,4}],
 \qquad
 r_\sigma^c=\frac14\sum_{j=1}^4c_{y_{\sigma,j}},
\tag{1}
\]

and \(G=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C)\). Fix a normalized affine family

\[
 \ell=(\ell_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Gamma},
 \qquad
 \ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\leq1,
\tag{2}
\]

and put

\[
 A(\ell):=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma
 \bigl(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\bigr),
 \qquad
 I(\ell):=\sum_t\int_{C_t}
 \max\left\{0,\max_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}\ell_\sigma(x)\right\}\,dx.
\tag{3}
\]

Let

\[
 \Lambda:=\left\{\sigma\in\Gamma:
 \ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\geq\frac12\right\},
 \qquad
 a_\sigma^\ell:=\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\quad(\sigma\in\Lambda).
\tag{4}
\]

For every \(\sigma\in\Lambda\), choose a maximum tag

\[
 o_+(\sigma)\in
 \operatorname*{arg\,max}_{1\leq j\leq4}
 \ell_\sigma(c_{y_{\sigma,j}}),
\tag{5}
\]

and abbreviate

\[
 c_\sigma^+:=c_{y_{\sigma,o_+(\sigma)}},
 \qquad
 d_\sigma^+:=c_\sigma^+-r_\sigma^c.
\tag{6}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{\ell_\sigma(c_\sigma^+)\geq a_\sigma^\ell,}
 \qquad
 \lambda_\sigma^\ell:=\frac{\ell_\sigma}{a_\sigma^\ell}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \lambda_\sigma^\ell(r_\sigma^c)=1,
 \quad
 \lambda_\sigma^\ell(c_\sigma^+)\geq1.
\tag{7}
\]

For a nonempty \(\Lambda\) and a maximum-tag selector \(o_+\), define the maximum-tag target cost

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathsf J_{\Lambda,o_+}^+
 :=\inf_{(\lambda_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Lambda}}\quad&
 \sum_t\int_{C_t}
 \max\left\{0,\max_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}\lambda_\sigma(x)\right\}\,dx,\\
 \text{subject to}\quad&\lambda_\sigma:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R
 \text{ affine},\\
 &\lambda_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)=1,\\
 &\lambda_\sigma(c_\sigma^+)\geq1
 \qquad(\sigma\in\Lambda).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]

At a physical target \(t\), define the full-credit forward-ray packing capacity

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathsf R_t^+:=\max\quad&
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}q_\sigma,\\
 \text{subject to}\quad&
 h_\sigma\in L^\infty(C_t),\qquad h_\sigma\geq0,\\
 &\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda_t}h_\sigma\leq1
 \quad\text{a.e. on }C_t,\\
 &q_\sigma=\int_{C_t}h_\sigma(x)\,dx,\\
 &b_\sigma\geq0,\\
 &\int_{C_t}x h_\sigma(x)\,dx
 =q_\sigma r_\sigma^c+b_\sigma d_\sigma^+
 \qquad(\sigma\in\Lambda_t).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]

When \(d_\sigma^+=0\), take \(b_\sigma=0\); when \(q_\sigma=0\), the same convention is imposed. Put

\[
 \mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+:=\sum_t\mathsf R_t^+.
\tag{10}
\]

The maxima in (9) are attained, and the exact collision-safe primal identity is

\[
 \boxed{\mathsf J_{\Lambda,o_+}^+
 =\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+.}
\tag{11}
\]

For \(q_\sigma>0\), the barycenter in (9) is

\[
 r_\sigma^c+\frac{b_\sigma}{q_\sigma}
 (c_\sigma^+-r_\sigma^c),
\tag{12}
\]

so it lies on the entire closed forward ray from \(r_\sigma^c\) through \(c_\sigma^+\), and its full mass \(q_\sigma\) is credited. There is no upper bound on \(b_\sigma/q_\sigma\), no reflected endpoint, and no discount.

For the same \(\Lambda,o_+\), let \(M_\Lambda\) and \(\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o_+}\) be the selected mass and rank-at-most-two slice score of accepted facts 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 and 4f7a1ed043c380e1. The maximum-tag choice is a legal selector for every source ledger and every source-owned one-body segment in 4f7a1ed043c380e1. Moreover,

\[
 \boxed{\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+\leq2I(\ell).}
\tag{13}
\]

For comparison, if \(\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}\) is the discounted finite-ray capacity of 4f7a1ed043c380e1 for the same family and selector, then

\[
 \boxed{\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+\geq
 \mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}.}
\tag{14}
\]

Consequently the following assertion is strictly narrower than (TP) and is sufficient for the hard square branch.

\(\mathrm{MTP}^+\): There are absolute constants
\(\varepsilon_+,c_+>0\) such that, for every retained canonical hard zero-fresh square instance and every normalized family (2) with \(\Lambda\ne\varnothing\), at least one maximum-tag selector (5) satisfies

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+\leq\varepsilon_+M_\Lambda
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 G+\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o_+}\geq c_+M_\Lambda.}
\tag{15}
\]

If \((\mathrm{MTP}^+)\) holds, then every normalized family (2) in the canonical hard square branch satisfies

\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta\leq K_+\bigl(G+A(\ell)+I(\ell)\bigr),
 \qquad
 K_+:=\max\left\{4,\frac{16}{\varepsilon_+},\frac{136}{c_+}\right\}.}
\tag{16}
\]

For the capped prescribed-barycenter packing capacity
\(\mathsf P_\Gamma\) of accepted fact 49092268f4f67b8e, its exact dual identity then gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta\leq K_+(G+\mathsf P_\Gamma),
 \qquad
 \max\{G,\mathsf P_\Gamma\}
 >\frac{1143}{25600K_+}Q.}
\tag{17}
\]

Thus any absolute constants in (15) close the square branch. The only remaining assertion is now (15): it concerns dual-generated good labels, a maximum tag, and a full-credit forward-ray packing score. It does not quantify over arbitrary selected subfamilies or arbitrary selectors. No aggregation of individual complete-core witnesses is asserted here.

## Proof

Affineness and the four-tag identity (1) give

\[
 a_\sigma^\ell
 =\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)
 =\frac14\sum_{j=1}^4
 \ell_\sigma(c_{y_{\sigma,j}}).
\tag{18}
\]

The maximum of four real numbers is at least their average, so (5) and (18) give the first inequality in (7). Since \(a_\sigma^\ell\geq1/2>0\), division proves the remaining assertions in (7). In particular, every maximum tag also satisfies the weaker legal endpoint inequality used in accepted facts 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 and 4f7a1ed043c380e1. Hence all their selected source densities, blocker-to-parent replacements, no-reuse bounds, positive-root one-body segments, and individual segment core energies remain available without changing a coefficient.

We prove the exact primal identity. Fix a physical target \(t\), abbreviate \(K:=C_t\), and use the compact moment image of the common-capacity density family from accepted fact 2c093df4eb8158a9. This is exactly the compact convex image used in the proof of the discounted identity in accepted fact 4f7a1ed043c380e1. Every affine piece satisfying its normalization in (8) is uniquely

\[
 \lambda_\sigma(x)
 =1+\langle v_\sigma,x-r_\sigma^c\rangle.
\tag{19}
\]

Its maximum-tag endpoint constraint is

\[
 \langle v_\sigma,d_\sigma^+\rangle\geq0.
\tag{20}
\]

For a common-capacity density family, write

\[
 q_\sigma:=\int_Kh_\sigma,
 \qquad
 p_\sigma:=\int_Kxh_\sigma(x)\,dx,
 \qquad
 \zeta_\sigma:=p_\sigma-q_\sigma r_\sigma^c.
\tag{21}
\]

The compact-moment-image bilinear minimax identity used in 4f7a1ed043c380e1 gives the target cost as the maximum, over this compact moment image, of the sum of the coordinatewise inner infima

\[
 \inf_{v_\sigma:\,\langle v_\sigma,d_\sigma^+\rangle\geq0}
 \left[q_\sigma+\langle v_\sigma,\zeta_\sigma\rangle\right].
\tag{22}
\]

If \(d_\sigma^+\ne0\), every direction orthogonal to \(d_\sigma^+\) is a two-sided recession direction in (20), so (22) is finite only if
\(\zeta_\sigma=b_\sigma d_\sigma^+\) for some scalar \(b_\sigma\). If \(b_\sigma<0\), taking \(v_\sigma=T d_\sigma^+\) and letting \(T\to+\infty\) sends (22) to \(-\infty\). If \(b_\sigma\geq0\), then

\[
 \langle v_\sigma,\zeta_\sigma\rangle
 =b_\sigma\langle v_\sigma,d_\sigma^+\rangle\geq0,
\tag{23}
\]

and the infimum is zero, attained at \(v_\sigma=0\). Thus (22) equals \(q_\sigma\) exactly when

\[
 \zeta_\sigma=b_\sigma d_\sigma^+,\qquad b_\sigma\geq0.
\tag{24}
\]

When \(d_\sigma^+=0\), the feasible set in (20) is all of \(\mathbb R^d\); the infimum is finite exactly when \(\zeta_\sigma=0\), and then again equals \(q_\sigma\). This is (24) with the convention \(b_\sigma=0\).

Substituting (24) into the compact moment-image maximization gives exactly (9) at the fixed target. The feasible moment vectors form a closed subset of a compact set, because the ray
\(\mathbb R_+d_\sigma^+\) is closed. Therefore the maximum is attained. When \(d_\sigma^+\ne0\), the coefficient \(b_\sigma\) in a feasible moment vector is unique; when it is zero, we use the stated convention. Distinct physical targets have independent density variables and objectives, so summing the target identities proves (11). Dividing the moment equation by \(q_\sigma>0\) proves (12).

We prove (13). The family \((\lambda_\sigma^\ell)_{\sigma\in\Lambda}\) from (7) is feasible in (8). Fix \(t\) and \(x\in C_t\). If the maximum of the normalized pieces is positive, choose a label attaining a positive term. Since \(a_\sigma^\ell\geq1/2\),

\[
 \lambda_\sigma^\ell(x)
 =\frac{\ell_\sigma(x)}{a_\sigma^\ell}
 \leq2\ell_\sigma(x)
 \leq2\max\left\{0,\max_{\tau\in\Gamma_t}\ell_\tau(x)\right\}.
\tag{25}
\]

If the normalized maximum is nonpositive, the same pointwise inequality after taking a maximum with zero is trivial. Integrating, summing over targets, using (3), and then (11) gives

\[
 \mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+
 =\mathsf J_{\Lambda,o_+}^+\leq2I(\ell),
\]

which is (13).

Every feasible point of the discounted program in accepted fact 4f7a1ed043c380e1 satisfies the moment equation in (9), because it has \(b_\sigma\geq0\). Its discounted contribution
\(q_\sigma-b_\sigma/2\) is at most the full-credit contribution \(q_\sigma\). Maximizing proves (14). Thus the antecedent in (15) is stronger than the antecedent of (TP), even before restricting the label family and selector.

It remains to prove that (15) suffices for the hard square terminal. Fix a normalized family (2). If
\(A(\ell)\geq\Theta/4\), then

\[
 \Theta\leq4A(\ell).
\tag{26}
\]

Assume \(A(\ell)<\Theta/4\). The good-label mass estimate in accepted facts 49092268f4f67b8e and 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 gives

\[
 M_\Lambda
 =\frac14\sum_{\sigma\in\Lambda}\theta_\sigma
 \geq\frac{\Theta-2A(\ell)}4
 >\frac\Theta8.
\tag{27}
\]

In particular \(\Lambda\ne\varnothing\). Choose a maximum-tag selector promised by \((\mathrm{MTP}^+)\). If
\(\mathsf R_{\Lambda,o_+}^+>\varepsilon_+M_\Lambda\), then (13) and (27) give

\[
 2I(\ell)>\frac{\varepsilon_+\Theta}{8},
 \qquad
 \Theta<\frac{16}{\varepsilon_+}I(\ell).
\tag{28}
\]

Otherwise (15) and (27) give

\[
 G+\mathsf S_{\Lambda,o_+}
 >\frac{c_+\Theta}{8}.
\tag{29}
\]

The hard slice screen of accepted fact 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 applies to every legal active-tag choice, hence to \(o_+\), and says

\[
 \mathsf S_{\Lambda,o_+}\leq16G.
\tag{30}
\]

Equations (29)-(30) imply

\[
 \Theta<\frac{136}{c_+}G.
\tag{31}
\]

The alternatives (26), (28), and (31) prove (16).

Accepted fact 49092268f4f67b8e gives the exact capped target dual

\[
 \mathsf P_\Gamma=\inf_\ell\{A(\ell)+I(\ell)\}.
\tag{32}
\]

Take a sequence of normalized families decreasing to the infimum in (32), apply (16), and pass to the limit. This gives

\[
 \Theta\leq K_+(G+\mathsf P_\Gamma)
 \leq2K_+\max\{G,\mathsf P_\Gamma\}.
\tag{33}
\]

The canonical square mass bound retained in 09fe6e21ebd31ee6 is
\(\Theta>1143Q/12800\). Substitution into (33) proves (17). Hence the smaller assertion (15), with any positive absolute constants, supplies the required absolute square terminal.
