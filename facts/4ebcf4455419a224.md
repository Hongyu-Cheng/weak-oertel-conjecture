## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put \(S=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), \(C_z=\{x:(z,x)\in C\}\), \(a_z=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)\), \(Z=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\}\), and \(W=\sum_z a_z>0\). Thus the atlas volume \(a_z\) is the original fiber volume, and \(W=\mathcal H_d(S)\). The set \(Z\) includes every positive-volume integer fiber created by convexity. Set \(\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d\), \(c_z=a_z^{-1}\int_{C_z}x\,dx\), \(\mathcal K_z=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\), and \(D_z=\mathcal K_z-c_z\).

Use one common canonical zero-fresh hard system of accepted fact `dcca4b6d66d80dd4` and the original complete reservoir and hard ledger of accepted facts `c81f02314cb34f8b` and `637b5f7874d8c875`. Specifically, for a positive parity class \(\xi\), put \(A_\xi=\sum_{z\equiv\xi}a_z\); it is good exactly when \(\max_{z\equiv\xi}a_z\le A_\xi/4\). The original reservoir is
\[
r_{\{u,v\}}=\frac{a_ua_v}{4A_\xi}
\quad(u\ne v,\ u\equiv v\equiv\xi,\ \xi\text{ good}),
\qquad r_e=0\text{ otherwise},
\qquad Q=|r|>0.
\]
Let \(R=R_C(r)\), let \(G\) be the original global complete metronoid-core capacity, and retain the original scaled depth \(D=2^nh_{\max}(C)\). Assume the global hard ledger in `637b5f7874d8c875`, in particular \(R+G<Q/100\). No continuous body \(D_z\) is identified with the scalar \(D\).

Fix any synchronous parameters from `dcca4b6d66d80dd4`. Its positive first-target atom labels form a finite set \(\mathscr T\). For \(\tau=(\alpha,j,i,k)\in\mathscr T\), make the exact aliases
\[
\zeta_\tau=m_j,\quad
w_\tau^{(1)}=w_{\alpha,j,i,k}
=\frac{q_j^\alpha\pi_i^\alpha\pi_k^\alpha}{2},\quad
y_\tau=y_{\alpha,j,i,k},\quad
x_\tau^\pm=x_{\alpha,j,i,k}^\pm,\quad
z_\tau^\pm=z_j^\pm.
\]
In particular \(y_\tau=(x_\tau^-+x_\tau^+)/2\), with \(x_\tau^\pm\in\mathcal K_{z_\tau^\pm}\). The quantities \(\beta_z\) and \(\Omega_b\) below mean exactly the source budget and total saturated load of `dcca4b6d66d80dd4`. Put
\[
t_u=\sum_{\tau:\zeta_\tau=u}w_\tau^{(1)}=\frac{\eta_u}{2},
\qquad T_1=\sum_u t_u=\frac{\Omega_b}{2}>\frac{97Q}{200},
\qquad T_\xi=\sum_{u\equiv\xi}t_u,
\]
where unused targets have \(t_u=0\). Then \(t_u\le a_u/2\). Define the second-generation matching by
\[
q^{(2)}_{\{u,v\}}=\frac{t_ut_v}{A_\xi}
\quad(u<v,\ u\equiv v\equiv\xi,\ \xi\text{ originally good}),
\qquad q^{(2)}_e=0\text{ otherwise},
\qquad B_2=|q^{(2)}|.
\tag{1}
\]
Goodness and \(A_\xi\) always refer to the original volumes, rather than being recomputed from \(t\). The order \(u<v\) is any fixed total order on the finite physical support.

Let
\[
P_2=\frac12\sum_{\xi\text{ good}}\frac{T_\xi^2}{A_\xi},
\qquad
F_{\mathrm{diag}}^{(2)}
=\frac12\sum_{\xi\text{ good}}
\frac{\sum_{u\equiv\xi}t_u^2}{A_\xi}.
\tag{2}
\]
These are the full ordered product mass, with its factor \(1/2\), and its entire physical equal-base diagonal. They obey
\[
B_2=P_2-F_{\mathrm{diag}}^{(2)},
\qquad F_{\mathrm{diag}}^{(2)}\le\frac G8,
\qquad 0\le q^{(2)}\le r,
\qquad d_u(q^{(2)})\le\frac{t_u}{2}\le\frac{a_u}{4}.
\tag{3}
\]
The new diagonal includes all pairs of first labels at one physical target, even when those labels differ. It is not the old circuit-coordinate diagonal \(F_{\mathrm{diag}}\) of `dcca4b6d66d80dd4`.

The following strict retention margin holds:
\[
B_2-R>
\frac{2627}{2560000}Q
+\frac{2509}{3200}G
+\frac{3G^2}{16Q}
\ge\frac{2627}{2560000}Q.
\tag{4}
\]
In particular \(B_2>0\). Apply the weighted dual of accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` afresh to \(q^{(2)}\). Its exact alias map is \(q\mapsto q^{(2)}\), \(Q\mapsto B_2\), \(v_e\mapsto v_e^{(2)}\), \(\omega_e\mapsto\omega_e^{(2)}\), and \(\Omega\mapsto\Omega_2\). Every positive new physical pair is an edge of the original complete reservoir, so its \(m_e=(u+v)/2\), \(D_e=(D_u+D_v)/2\), and \(L_e=(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v)/2\) keep their original meanings. The fresh load-minimal optimizer satisfies
\[
\Omega_2=\sum_e\omega_e^{(2)}
\ge B_2-R_C(q^{(2)})
\ge B_2-R
>\frac{2627}{2560000}Q.
\tag{5}
\]
For its fresh contacts \(\ell_e^{(2)}\in L_e\), the measure
\(\sigma_2^{\mathrm{fresh}}=\frac12\sum_e\omega_e^{(2)}\delta_{(m_e,\ell_e^{(2)})}\)
is carried by \(S\), has mass \(\Omega_2/2>2627Q/5120000\), and is halfspace-dominated by the original mixed-volume measure \(\mu_C\).

There is also a second generation retaining the complete geometric ancestry. For first labels \(\tau,\sigma\) at distinct physical targets \(u<v\) in one originally good parity class, put
\[
\alpha_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)}
=\frac{w_\tau^{(1)}w_\sigma^{(1)}}{A_\xi},
\qquad
Y_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)}
=\frac{y_\tau+y_\sigma}{2}
=\frac{x_\tau^-+x_\tau^++x_\sigma^-+x_\sigma^+}{4}.
\tag{6}
\]
Extend \(\alpha_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)}\) symmetrically when the physical order is reversed. All double sums defining atom measures or total source charge below count only \(\zeta_\tau<\zeta_\sigma\) in a single originally good parity class, once per pair. Its base is \((u+v)/2\), and its atoms have total mass \(B_2\). Count every original source occurrence at its full atom weight, including repeated original leaves. Its full source charge is
\[
L_z^{(2)}
=\sum_{\tau,\sigma}\alpha_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)}
\bigl(
\mathbf1_{\{z_\tau^-=z\}}+
\mathbf1_{\{z_\tau^+=z\}}+
\mathbf1_{\{z_\sigma^-=z\}}+
\mathbf1_{\{z_\sigma^+=z\}}\bigr)
\le\frac{\beta_z}{4}\le\frac{a_z}{16}.
\tag{7}
\]
Thus this charge is not the charge obtained by multiplying by the barycentric coefficient \(1/4\). Moreover
\[
\sigma_2^{\mathrm{anc}}
:=8\gamma_d\sum_{\tau,\sigma}\alpha_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)}
\delta_{((\zeta_\tau+\zeta_\sigma)/2,Y_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)})}
\tag{8}
\]
is carried by \(S\), has mass \(8\gamma_dB_2\), and is halfspace-dominated by \(\mu_C\). Both domination conclusions hold for every closed ambient halfspace, with all boundary atoms included. These are target halfspace-congestion bounds. Neither one identifies the fresh contact points with the four-leaf descendants or asserts one jointly packed family of densities at the second physical targets.

## Proof

The first-target packing in accepted fact `dcca4b6d66d80dd4` has density at most \(\frac12\mathbf1_{C_u}\) and mass \(\eta_u/2=t_u\) at each target \(u\). Hence \(0\le t_u\le a_u/2\), and positive first targets lie in the actual set \(Z\). Its exact total-mass identity gives \(T_1=\Omega_b/2>97Q/200\). The old source occurrence identity in that same fact, with \(w=2s\), gives
\[
L_z^{(1)}
:=\sum_\tau w_\tau^{(1)}
\bigl(\mathbf1_{\{z_\tau^-=z\}}+
\mathbf1_{\{z_\tau^+=z\}}\bigr)
=\frac{\beta_z}{2},
\qquad \beta_z\le\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{9}
\]
The two source points of every first label are convex combinations of half-metronoid contacts, so they belong to the corresponding \(\mathcal K_z\), including synchronous parameters equal to zero or one.

The reservoir formula gives \(Q\le W/8\). By the strict hard ledger of accepted fact `637b5f7874d8c875`, \(G<Q/100\), so \(G<W/2\). Therefore accepted fact `c81f02314cb34f8b` applies in its original normalization. Write \(M_\xi=\max_{z\equiv\xi}a_z\), with zero for an empty class. That fact gives
\[
\sum_\xi M_\xi\le G,
\qquad
\sum_{\xi\text{ bad}}A_\xi\le4G.
\tag{10}
\]
Its parity index is here denoted by \(\xi\); its \(a_z,W,G,r\) are exactly the present objects, and its \(|r|\) is the present \(Q\).

Set \(W_g=\sum_{\xi\text{ good}}A_\xi\) and \(T_g=\sum_{\xi\text{ good}}T_\xi\). The bad first-target mass is at most half the original bad-class volume, so
\[
T_g\ge T_1-2G>\frac{97Q}{200}-2G>0.
\tag{11}
\]
For one good class, its reservoir mass is
\(Q_\xi=(A_\xi^2-\sum_{u\equiv\xi}a_u^2)/(8A_\xi)\ge3A_\xi/32\).
Summing gives \(0<W_g\le32Q/3\). Empty parity classes have both zero original volume and zero first-target mass; they never occur in a denominator. Good classes with \(T_\xi=0\) contribute zero to every displayed product sum.

For a positive edge in (1), the bound \(t_u\le a_u/2\) gives
\(q^{(2)}_{\{u,v\}}\le a_ua_v/(4A_\xi)=r_{\{u,v\}}\).
Also \(T_\xi\le A_\xi/2\), and direct summation gives
\[
d_u(q^{(2)})
=\frac{t_u(T_\xi-t_u)}{A_\xi}
\le\frac{t_u}{2}\le\frac{a_u}{4}.
\tag{12}
\]
Thus the matching uses distinct same-parity physical sources, its targets are integral, and it obeys the required source capacities.

Expanding the square of \(T_\xi\) proves \(B_2=P_2-F_{\mathrm{diag}}^{(2)}\). This expansion starts with every ordered pair of first labels, including identical labels and distinct labels sharing one physical base; the equal-base sum is exactly \(t_u^2\). Hence it discards no additional unaccounted ancestry mass. Since \(\max_{u\equiv\xi}t_u\le M_\xi/2\) and \(T_\xi/A_\xi\le1/2\),
\[
F_{\mathrm{diag}}^{(2)}
\le\frac12\sum_{\xi\text{ good}}
\frac{(M_\xi/2)T_\xi}{A_\xi}
\le\frac18\sum_{\xi\text{ good}}M_\xi
\le\frac G8.
\tag{13}
\]
This proves (3), including its diagonal estimate.

Cauchy-Schwarz applied to the finite good-class sums gives
\(\sum_{\xi\text{ good}}T_\xi^2/A_\xi\ge T_g^2/W_g\).
Using (11), \(W_g\le32Q/3\), and (13),
\[
B_2>\frac{3}{64Q}
\left(\frac{97Q}{200}-2G\right)^2-\frac G8.
\tag{14}
\]
The strict bound \(R+G<Q/100\) then yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
B_2-R
&>\frac{3}{64Q}
\left(\frac{97Q}{200}-2G\right)^2
-\frac G8-\frac Q{100}+G\\
&=\frac{2627}{2560000}Q
+\frac{2509}{3200}G
+\frac{3G^2}{16Q}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]
All quantities used in the squaring step are positive by (11). This proves (4) and \(B_2>0\).

Every affine-realizable submatching of \(q^{(2)}\) is also an affine-realizable submatching of \(r\), because \(q^{(2)}\le r\) and the actual pair bodies \(L_e\) are identical. The affine-retention characterization in accepted fact `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` therefore gives \(R_C(q^{(2)})\le R\). Applying its exact weighted dual with the alias map in the statement gives
\(\Omega_2\ge B_2-R_C(q^{(2)})\ge B_2-R\), proving (5). This is a fresh optimization; no restriction or transport of the original dual vectors is used. The binomial estimate \((1+1/d)^d<3\) gives \(\gamma_d>1/3>1/4\), so (12) implies \(d_u(q^{(2)})\le\gamma_da_u\). The stronger contact-domination conclusion of `6496cb3b0fd44f9d` consequently applies and proves the claimed domination and mass of \(\sigma_2^{\mathrm{fresh}}\).

We verify the complete four-leaf ancestry separately. Summing \(\alpha_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)}\) over labels at a fixed pair of distinct physical targets gives \(t_ut_v/A_\xi=q^{(2)}_{\{u,v\}}\), so the total mass is \(B_2\). Each full ambient position is the average of \((z_\tau^-,x_\tau^-)\), \((z_\tau^+,x_\tau^+)\), \((z_\sigma^-,x_\sigma^-)\), and \((z_\sigma^+,x_\sigma^+)\). Convexity of \(C\) puts this average in \(C\); its base is \((u+v)/2\), which is integral by same parity. Fix a first label \(\tau\) at a retained target \(u\). Its total second-generation incidence weight is exactly
\[
\sum_{\sigma:\,\zeta_\sigma\equiv u,\ \zeta_\sigma\ne u}
\alpha_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)}
=w_\tau^{(1)}\frac{T_\xi-t_u}{A_\xi}
\le\frac{w_\tau^{(1)}}2.
\tag{16}
\]
Here an unordered pair is included in this incidence sum whichever of its two physical bases precedes the other. First labels in excluded bad classes have incidence zero. Counting every leaf with multiplicity and using (9), equation (16) gives
\(L_z^{(2)}\le L_z^{(1)}/2=\beta_z/4\le a_z/16\), which proves (7). No factor \(1/4\) from the barycenter was inserted into this full occurrence charge.

Finally let \(H\) be any closed ambient halfspace. For each second ancestry atom in \(H\), at least one of its four original leaves is in \(H\), since its position is their average. Choose one such occurrence and assign the atom's full weight \(\alpha_{\tau\sigma}^{(2)}\) to it. Let \(O_z\) be the resulting total charge at source \(z\). Then \(O_z\le L_z^{(2)}\le a_z/16\). Whenever \(O_z>0\), the fiber trace \(H_z\) contains a point of \(\mathcal K_z=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\). The closed-halfspace metronoid bound of accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` gives
\[
\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z)
\ge\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2}
\ge8\gamma_d O_z.
\tag{17}
\]
A whole-space trace satisfies the same estimate. Summing (17) over the distinct physical original sources proves \(\sigma_2^{\mathrm{anc}}(H)\le\mu_C(H)\). Repeated leaves were already included in \(L_z^{(2)}\), and each atom was assigned only once. Non-strict halfspace membership retains every atom on the boundary. Thus the source and target halfspace-congestion certificates refer to the original mixed volume on all actual fibers, and all stated conclusions follow.
