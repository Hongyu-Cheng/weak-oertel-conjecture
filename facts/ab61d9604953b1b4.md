## Statement

Let $D$ be a convex body in $\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge2$, and let $F$ be a convex body of positive $(d-1)$-volume in a hyperplane perpendicular to unit $e$. Let $U=|D|$, $P=\mathcal H^{d-1}(F)(\max_D e\cdot x-\min_D e\cdot x)$, $r=(dU/P)^{1/(d-1)}$, and $J_d(r)=\int_0^1(1+rt)^{d-1}\,dt=((1+r)^d-1)/(dr)$. Then $|D+F|\ge P J_d(r)$, with equality for a cone whose sections are scaled copies of $F$. For $d=2$ the value is exactly $U+P$ for every such pair. For $d=3$ the bound is $U+P+\sqrt{3UP}$, and for $d=4$ it is $U+P+(3/2)4^{1/3}U^{1/3}P^{2/3}+4^{2/3}U^{2/3}P^{1/3}$.

Let $d\ge2$ and $B=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1,\ 1\le i\le d+1\}$ be a full-dimensional simplex of volume $b$ with $\sum_i v_i=0$. Let $A,K$ be convex bodies with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$ and $a=|A|$, $k=|K|$. Assume each cut $v_i\cdot x=-1$ is proper on $A$. Put $U_i=|A\cap\{v_i\cdot x\ge-1\}|$, $q_i=|K\cap\{v_i\cdot x\ge0\}|$, $H_i=1+\max_A v_i\cdot x$, and $P_i=dbH_i/(d+1)$.
Then
$$
2^dq_i\ge P_i J_d\big((dU_i/P_i)^{1/(d-1)}\big).
$$
If $A,K,B$ are the only positive-volume fibers at $-1,0,1$ and the ambient cuts $v_i\cdot x\ge z$ all have raw mixed mass $Q$, then $q_i=Q-U_i$ and $2^d Q\ge 2^d U_i+P_i J_d((dU_i/P_i)^{1/(d-1)})$. In $d=3$ this is $8Q\ge9U_i+P_i+\sqrt{3U_iP_i}$.

## Proof

Write $m=d-1$, $L=\max_D e\cdot x-\min_D e\cdot x$, and $f=\mathcal H^{d-1}(F)$. Apply 19210d0a14ccfebc to $D,F$ after translating $F$ to height zero. This gives the integral of $(f^{1/m}+h(t))^m$ as a lower bound, where $h(t)=|D_t|^{1/m}$. Brunn–Minkowski for the interior sections makes $h$ positive and concave on the height interval, and $\int h^m=U$ by Fubini. Apply the result “Sharp normalized moments of concave functions on an interval” to every power $1\le p\le m$ in the binomial expansion. The term for $p=0$ is exact. Using $P=fL$ gives
$$
\int (f^{1/m}+h)^m\,dt
\ge P\sum_{p=0}^m\binom mp\frac{r^p}{p+1}
=P J_d(r).
$$
The coefficient formulas in dimensions $2,3,4$ follow by expansion. In dimension two the lengths add exactly by 19210d0a14ccfebc. To attain equality, first translate $F$ to height zero and form the cone with height interval of length $L$ and slices $trF$ at fractional height $t\in[0,1]$. It has $U=Pr^m/d$. Its slice-root function is affine and the summands $trF,F$ are homothetic in the hyperplane, giving equality at each step.

For the cap statement, $(1-v_i\cdot x)/(d+1)$ are barycentric coordinates on $B$, so $0$ is its centroid. The pyramid from $0$ to facet $i$ has volume $b/(d+1)$ and height $1/\lVert v_i\rVert$. Hence the facet's Euclidean measure divided by $\lVert v_i\rVert$ equals $db/(d+1)$. Pair the actual cap $D=A\cap\{v_i\cdot x\ge-1\}$ with the actual full facet $F=B\cap\{v_i\cdot x=1\}$. Properness gives a $D$ width of $H_i/\lVert v_i\rVert$, so $P=P_i$. Their Minkowski sum is contained in $2K\cap\{v_i\cdot x\ge0\}$, and dilation by $1/2$ accounts for $2^d$. At the $B$ endpoint the trace is the facet, of zero $d$-volume. Hence for the stated three-fiber measure the common mass is exactly $U_i+q_i$ and substitution proves the final bounds.
