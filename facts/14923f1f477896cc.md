## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and assume the positive-load hypotheses and canonical notation of accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`.  In particular, \(Q>0\),
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D,
\tag{1}
\]
and, on every positive retained parity label \((\xi,c)\),
\[
 S_{\xi,c}:=
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_e>0,
 \qquad
 q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}:=
 \frac1{S_{\xi,c}}
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_eF_e^\flat,
\tag{2}
\]
with an origin-symmetric full-dimensional convex body \(D_*\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) satisfying
\[
 q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{2S_{\xi,c}}(C_c).
\tag{3}
\]
For such a label put
\[
 B_{\xi,c}:=
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi,\ m_e=c\\
 \|F_e^\flat-q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}\|_{D_*}>1/4}}s_e,
\tag{4}
\]
so that
\[
 0\le B_{\xi,c}\le S_{\xi,c},
 \qquad I=\sum_{\xi,c}B_{\xi,c}.
\tag{5}
\]

Fix an affine map \(a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\).  Call a positive retained label \(a\)-close when
\[
 \|q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}-a(c)\|_{D_*}\le\frac14.
\tag{6}
\]
For every physical target \(c\), define
\[
 B_c(a):=
 \sum_{\substack{\xi:\ (\xi,c)\ a\text{-close}}}B_{\xi,c},
 \qquad
 \kappa_c(a):=
 \sum_{\substack{\xi:\ (\xi,c)\ a\text{-close}\\B_{\xi,c}>0}}
 \frac{B_{\xi,c}}{S_{\xi,c}},
\tag{7}
\]
and, when \(B_c(a)>0\), put
\[
 \chi_c(a):=
 \frac1{\max\{1,\kappa_c(a)\}}.
\tag{8}
\]
Let
\[
 E_a:=\{c\in\mathbb Z^n:B_c(a)>0\},
 \qquad
 B_\chi(a):=
 \sum_{c\in E_a}\chi_c(a)B_c(a).
\tag{9}
\]
If \(E_a\ne\varnothing\), define
\[
 \rho_a:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_a).
\tag{10}
\]
Then, for every \(c\in E_a\),
\[
 \boxed{
 a(c)\in
 \mathsf M_{\,2\chi_c(a)B_c(a)}(C_c).}
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{G\ge2B_\chi(a),}
\tag{12}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_a}B_\chi(a),
 \qquad
 D\ge2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_a}B_\chi(a),}
\tag{13}
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge
 \left(2+4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_a}\right)B_\chi(a).}
\tag{14}
\]

There are at most \(2^n\) retained parity labels over one physical target, and therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 0<\kappa_c(a)\le2^n,
 \qquad
 \chi_c(a)\ge2^{-n},
 \qquad
 B_\chi(a)\ge2^{-n}\sum_{c\in E_a}B_c(a).}
\tag{15}
\]

Let
\[
 F(a):=
 \sum_{\xi,c}S_{\xi,c}
 \mathbf1_{\{\|q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}-a(c)\|_{D_*}>1/4\}},
\tag{16}
\]
so that the canonical labelled-output defect is \(O=\inf_aF(a)\).  The infimum is attained.  Let \(a_*\) be any minimizer.  If \(I>O\), then \(E_{a_*}\ne\varnothing\); writing \(\rho_*:=\rho_{a_*}\), one has
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal A(C)
 \ge
 \left(2^{1-n}+4\gamma_d2^{-\rho_*}\right)(I-O)
 >\frac43\,2^{-\rho_*}(I-O).}
\tag{17}
\]
In particular, the canonical hard inequality
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100}
\tag{18}
\]
forces
\[
 \boxed{
 I-O<\frac{3}{400}\,2^{\rho_*}Q.}
\tag{19}
\]
Thus \(\rho_*\le3\) implies \(I-O<3Q/50\).  Equations (14) and (19) are sufficient closures only for the stated congestion-weighted or bounded-rank subbranches; no universal lower bound on \(B_\chi(a_*)\) beyond (15) is asserted.

## Proof

All positive fibers and all positive retained labels form finite sets because the base projection of \(C\) is compact.  Fix an affine map \(a\) and a target \(c\in E_a\).  For every \(a\)-close label \((\xi,c)\) with \(B_{\xi,c}>0\), equation (6) and origin symmetry of \(D_*\) give
\[
 a(c)\in q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}+\frac14D_*
 \subseteq q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}+\frac12D_*.
\tag{20}
\]
By the accepted inclusion (3), choose a representing density \(f_{\xi,c}\) on \(C_c\) such that
\[
 0\le f_{\xi,c}\le1\quad\text{a.e.},
 \qquad
 \int_{C_c}f_{\xi,c}=2S_{\xi,c},
 \qquad
 \int_{C_c}x f_{\xi,c}(x)\,dx
 =2S_{\xi,c}a(c).
\tag{21}
\]
Define one density in the physical target fiber by
\[
 g_c:=
 \chi_c(a)
 \sum_{\substack{\xi:\ (\xi,c)\ a\text{-close}\\B_{\xi,c}>0}}
 \frac{B_{\xi,c}}{S_{\xi,c}}f_{\xi,c}.
\tag{22}
\]
The definition of \(\kappa_c(a)\) and \(\chi_c(a)\) gives the pointwise audit
\[
 0\le g_c
 \le\chi_c(a)\kappa_c(a)\le1.
\tag{23}
\]
Equations (21)--(22) give
\[
 \int_{C_c}g_c
 =2\chi_c(a)B_c(a),
\tag{24}
\]
\[
 \int_{C_c}xg_c(x)\,dx
 =2\chi_c(a)B_c(a)a(c).
\tag{25}
\]
Thus \(g_c\) is one simultaneous target-fiber witness for (11).  It combines different parity labels only after multiplying all their density witnesses by the single exact congestion factor \(\chi_c(a)\); equation (23) is the full no-reuse audit.

We next prove the complete-core charge.  Put
\[
 t_c:=2\chi_c(a)B_c(a)>0
 \qquad(c\in E_a).
\tag{26}
\]
Equation (11) gives \(a(c)\in\mathsf M_{t_c}(C_c)\), and the density in (22) gives \(t_c\le\operatorname{vol}_d(C_c)\).  Let
\[
 A:=\operatorname{aff}(E_a),
 \qquad \rho:=\dim A=\rho_a.
\]
Because \(A\) is the affine hull of integer points, choose \(c_0\in E_a\), let
\[
 \Gamma:=\operatorname{lin}(A-c_0)\cap\mathbb Z^n,
\]
choose a lattice basis of the saturated rank-\(\rho\) lattice \(\Gamma\), and use it to identify \(A\cap\mathbb Z^n\) affinely with \(\mathbb Z^\rho\).  Apply accepted fact `6d6fcf377e68c79c`, with continuous dimension zero, to the transported finite measure \(\sum_{c\in E_a}t_c\delta_c\).  It supplies
\[
 m\in A\cap\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}(E_a)
\tag{27}
\]
such that every proper relative closed halfspace of \(A\) containing \(m\) carries at least
\[
 2^{-\rho}\sum_{c\in E_a}t_c
 \ge2^{-n}\sum_{c\in E_a}t_c.
\tag{28}
\]
The inclusion in the convex hull follows as usual: otherwise strict separation from the finite convex hull gives a relative halfspace containing \(m\) and carrying zero mass, contrary to positive centerpoint depth.

For every
\[
 \lambda\in\Lambda_m(E_a)
 :=\left\{\lambda\in\mathbb R_+^{E_a}:
 \sum_c\lambda_c=1,\ \sum_c\lambda_cc=m\right\},
\]
affinity of \(a\) and (11) give
\[
 a(m)=\sum_c\lambda_ca(c)
 \in\sum_c\lambda_c\mathsf M_{t_c}(C_c).
\tag{29}
\]
Thus \(a(m)\) belongs to the intersection of the right side of (29) over every \(\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E_a)\).  An ambient base halfspace containing \(m\) intersects \(A\) either in all of \(A\) or in a relative closed halfspace containing \(m\), so (28) gives the exact ambient \(2^{-n}\) balance.  The definition of the complete metronoid-core capacity in accepted fact `fbeb0a501b268d12` now makes the masses \((t_c)_{c\in E_a}\) admissible.  Therefore
\[
 G\ge\sum_{c\in E_a}t_c=2B_\chi(a),
\tag{30}
\]
which is (12).

For the selector charge, accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` applied to (11) gives
\[
 \delta_C(c,a(c))
 \ge\gamma_dt_c
 =2\gamma_d\chi_c(a)B_c(a)
 \qquad(c\in E_a).
\tag{31}
\]
Use in the residual-selector capacity of accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` the physical target points \(a(c)\), the weights on the right side of (31), and the same affine map \(a\).  Their base rank is \(\rho_a\), their continuous residual rank is zero, and their total weight is \(2\gamma_dB_\chi(a)\).  Hence
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \ge2\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_a}B_\chi(a).
\tag{32}
\]
The same accepted fact gives \(D\ge R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), proving (13).  The quantities \(G\), \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), and \(D\) are three distinct nonnegative summands of (1).  Adding (30), (32), and the identical lower bound for \(D\) proves (14), without adding two witnesses inside any one capacity.

For (15), every ratio in (7) lies in \((0,1]\), and there are at most \(2^n\) parity classes \(\xi\) over a fixed target.  Thus
\[
 0<\kappa_c(a)\le2^n.
\]
If \(\kappa_c(a)\le1\), then \(\chi_c(a)=1\); otherwise \(\chi_c(a)=1/\kappa_c(a)\ge2^{-n}\).  Hence \(\chi_c(a)\ge2^{-n}\) in every case, and summation proves the final inequality in (15).

It remains to relate the construction to the canonical defects.  The finite label set makes \(F(a)\) take values in a finite set of subset sums of the positive numbers \(S_{\xi,c}\).  Therefore its infimum is attained; fix a minimizer \(a_*\) with \(F(a_*)=O\).  The close and far threshold conditions are complementary, with equality assigned to the close side.  Since \(B_{\xi,c}\le S_{\xi,c}\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{c\in E_{a_*}}B_c(a_*)
 &=I-
 \sum_{\substack{\xi,c:\
 \|q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}-a_*(c)\|_{D_*}>1/4}}
 B_{\xi,c}\\
 &\ge I-F(a_*)=I-O.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{33}
\]
If \(I>O\), the left side is positive, so \(E_{a_*}\ne\varnothing\) and \(\rho_*\) is defined.  Equations (14), (15), and (33) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal A(C)
 &\ge
 \left(2+4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_*}\right)
 B_\chi(a_*)\\
 &\ge
 \left(2+4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_*}\right)
 2^{-n}(I-O)\\
 &=\left(2^{1-n}+4\gamma_d2^{-\rho_*}\right)(I-O).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{34}
\]
The binomial theorem gives
\[
 \frac1{\gamma_d}
 =\left(1+\frac1d\right)^d
 \le\sum_{j=0}^d\frac1{j!}<3,
\tag{35}
\]
so \(\gamma_d>1/3\).  Dropping the positive term \(2^{1-n}(I-O)\) in (34) and using (35) proves the strict last inequality in (17).  If both (18) and
\[
 I-O\ge\frac{3}{400}2^{\rho_*}Q
\]
held, (17) would give \(\mathcal A(C)>Q/100\), a contradiction.  This proves (19).  For \(\rho_*\le3\), equation (19) gives
\[
 I-O<\frac{3}{400}2^3Q=\frac{3Q}{50}.
\]
Every conclusion has used actual target-fiber densities, one physical target at a time, with the exact cross-label congestion audit (23); no abstract finite stress system or unproved simultaneous density pooling is used.
