## Statement

Let $n,d\ge1$ and let $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be a convex body. For $z\in\mathbb Z^n$ write $C_z=\{x:(z,x)\in C\}$. Let $K\subseteq\mathbb R^d$ be full-dimensional, compact and convex with volume centroid zero and volume $V>0$. Suppose that for $p\in\mathbb Z^n$, $v\in\mathbb Z^n\setminus\{0\}$, $a_-,a_+\in\mathbb R^d$ and $0<r\le s$, the actual fibers $C_{p-v}$ and $C_{p+v}$ contain $a_-+rK$ and $a_++sK$, respectively. Put $g=(a_-+a_+)/2$ and $q=(r+s)/2$. Then $g+qK\subseteq C_p$.

Write $\Gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$ and $c_d=(2\Gamma_d)^{1/d}$. Define
$$
\Phi_d(r,s)=
\begin{cases}
r, & d=1,\\
\left[r c_d+\dfrac{s-r}{d-1}(1-c_d)\right]^d, & d\ge2,\ r\le s\le dr,\\
r^d, & d\ge2,\ s\ge dr.
\end{cases}
$$
The two $d\ge2$ branches agree at $s=dr$.

Every closed ambient halfspace $H$ containing $(p,g)$ captures total $d$-volume at least
$$
V\bigl[\Phi_d(r,s)+\Gamma_d q^d\bigr]
$$
from the three retained copies on the distinct bases $p-v,p,p+v$. In particular this lower bounds the raw depth of $(p,g)$ for $C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)$.

## Proof

Convexity puts $(a_-+rK+a_++sK)/2=g+qK$ in $C_p$. As $0\in K$, the mark $(p,g)$ belongs to this actual fiber. By containment it suffices to consider halfspaces whose boundaries pass through the mark. Write their margin as $A\cdot(z-p)+u\cdot(x-g)$, with base normal $A\in\mathbb R^n$ and continuous normal $u\in\mathbb R^d$.

For $u\ne0$ the thresholds in coordinates centered at $a_-$ and $a_+$ are $t=A\cdot v-u\cdot(a_--g)$ and $-t$, since $(a_--g)+(a_+-g)=0$. The unequal-scale paired-source inequality therefore bounds the sum of the two retained endpoint traces below by $V\Phi_d(r,s)$. In the middle fiber the halfspace passes through the volume centroid $g$ of $g+qK$. Apply 6769574fc1bd9f8d with body $g+qK$, $m=|g+qK|=q^dV$ and density $f=1$ in the metronoid definition. It gives at least $\Gamma_d q^dV$ on this retained copy at the same mark. The three traces have distinct integer bases, so their volumes add.

For $u=0$, the halfspace contains the whole retained middle copy and at least one entire retained endpoint copy, including both when $A\cdot v=0$. This gives at least $(q^d+r^d)V$, which is at least the claimed bound because $\Gamma_d\le1$ and $\Phi_d(r,s)\le r^d$. The latter inequality follows from the piecewise formula, since the interpolating bracket is a convex combination of $r c_d$ and $r$ and $c_d\le1$. The raw mixed depth bound follows from monotonicity under inclusion of these retained copies in the actual body. The centroid used for the middle contribution is the centroid of the retained copy. Excess volume of the actual middle fiber and all other fibers contribute nonnegative mass.
