## Statement

Let \(D=\operatorname{conv}(D)\cap\mathbb Z^N\) be finite and nonempty, with positive log-affine probability law \(p\). Use exactly the objects of accepted fact c16327a288b708bd:
\[
t_{\mathbf z}=\tau_p(\mathbf z),\quad
E_{\mathbf z}
=\{\mathbf x\in D\cap H_{\mathbf z}:2\mathbf z-\mathbf x\in D\},
\]
\[
K_{\mathbf z,\mathbf x}
=p_{\mathbf z}\mathbf1_{\{\mathbf x\in E_{\mathbf z}\}},
\qquad
\Delta=\operatorname{diag}(t_{\mathbf z}),
\]
where \(H_{\mathbf z}\) is a generic depth-minimizing closed halfspace through \(\mathbf z\).
For each parity class \(\mathbf c\), let
\(\alpha_{\mathbf c}=p(D\cap(\mathbf c+2\mathbb Z^N))\).
Then
\[
K^{\mathsf T}\Delta^{-1}K
\preceq\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_{[\mathbf x]}).
\]

## Proof

For a real vector \(\mathbf v\), weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives
\[
(K\mathbf v)_{\mathbf z}^2
\le p_{\mathbf z}^2
\left(\sum_{\mathbf x\in E_{\mathbf z}}p_{\mathbf x}\right)
\left(\sum_{\mathbf x\in E_{\mathbf z}}
\frac{v_{\mathbf x}^2}{p_{\mathbf x}}\right).
\]
The first sum is at most \(t_{\mathbf z}\), by the actual-cap inclusion in accepted fact c16327a288b708bd. Log-affinity gives
\(p_{\mathbf z}^2/p_{\mathbf x}=p_{2\mathbf z-\mathbf x}\), so
\[
\frac{(K\mathbf v)_{\mathbf z}^2}{t_{\mathbf z}}
\le\sum_{\mathbf x\in E_{\mathbf z}}
p_{2\mathbf z-\mathbf x}v_{\mathbf x}^2.
\]
For fixed \(\mathbf x\), the map
\(\mathbf z\mapsto2\mathbf z-\mathbf x\) is injective and takes values in the parity class \([\mathbf x]\). Summing over \(\mathbf z\) proves
\[
\mathbf v^{\mathsf T}K^{\mathsf T}\Delta^{-1}K\mathbf v
\le\sum_{\mathbf x}\alpha_{[\mathbf x]}v_{\mathbf x}^2.
\]
