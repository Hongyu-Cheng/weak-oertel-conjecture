## Statement

For a strict quadrilateral section of a tetrahedron with oriented raw margins $(p,q,-r,-1)$, $p,q,r>0$, let $b$ be its actual area centroid in barycentric coordinates. Set $A=3b_1-1$, $B=3b_2-1$, $G=1-3b_3$, $D=1-3b_4=1+A+B-G$. Then $q=[G-A-B+(1-G)r-(1+A)p]/(1+B)$ and $C_b(p,r)=0$, where $C_b=c_0+c_1p+c_2r+c_3p^2+c_4pr+c_5r^2$ and
$$
\begin{aligned}
c_0&=(G-A)^2(G-A-B),\\
c_1&=-2A^3-A^2B+5A^2G-2A^2+AB^2-3AG^2+3AG-B^2G+BG^2+BG-2G^2,\\
c_2&=-(G-D)(AB+2A-GD),\\
c_3&=(A-B)(1+A)(G-D),\qquad c_4=-c_1(A,B,D),\\
c_5&=(G-1)(G-1-B)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
The homogeneous symmetric matrix in $(p,r,1)$ has determinant $(A-B)(1+B)(G-D)(AB-GD)^2/4$.

## Proof

Put $(X,Y,Z,W)=(1/p,1/q,1/r,1)$ and $L=X+Y+Z+W$. The four centroid formulas from 1462fa9ce6ced54e are
$$
\begin{aligned}
3b_1&=X/(X+Z)+X/(X+W)-X/L,&
3b_2&=Y/(Y+Z)+Y/(Y+W)-Y/L,\\
3b_3&=Z/(X+Z)+Z/(Y+Z)-Z/L,&
3b_4&=W/(X+W)+W/(Y+W)-W/L.
\end{aligned}
$$
Indeed the four edge intersections are the four normalized adjacent coordinate pairs in those sums, and the diagonal intersection is $(X,Y,Z,W)/L$. Incidence $pb_1+qb_2-rb_3-b_4=0$ gives the displayed expression for $q$.

Here are explicit polynomial verification details. Set $J=pqr+pq+pr+qr$, and substitute
$$
A=\frac{r}{p+r}+\frac1{p+1}-\frac{qr}{J}-1,\quad
B=\frac{r}{q+r}+\frac1{q+1}-\frac{pr}{J}-1,\quad
G=1-\frac{p}{p+r}-\frac{q}{q+r}+\frac{pq}{J}
$$
in $C_b$. Multiply by $[(p+r)(q+r)(p+1)(q+1)J]^3$, expand, and collect in $p,q,r$. Every coefficient is zero. This verifies the identity with positive denominators and without any division by differences of heights or centroid coordinates. For the determinant, expanding four times its value gives
$$
4c_3c_5c_0-c_3c_2^2-c_5c_1^2-c_0c_4^2+c_4c_1c_2.
$$
Substituting the coefficients and $D=1+A+B-G$ and collecting gives $(A-B)(1+B)(G-D)(AB-GD)^2$.
