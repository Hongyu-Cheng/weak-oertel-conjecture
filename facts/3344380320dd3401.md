## Statement

Take $K=\operatorname{conv}\{(4,-3),(-3,4),(-1,-1)\}$ and $v_1=(1,0),v_2=(0,1),v_3=(-1,-1)$. The origin is its centroid; the three spanning normals sum to zero. The through-origin caps $\{v_i\cdot x\ge0\}$ have area fractions $16/35,16/35,4/9$, all strictly below $128/279$. The section densities $\sigma_i=\mathcal H^1(K\cap\{v_i\cdot x=0\})/\|v_i\|$ are $12/5,12/5,14/3$, so for $\lambda_i=1/\sigma_i$ the third normalized weight is $9/44<2/9$.

## Proof

The vertices sum to zero and the determinant of the edge differences at $(-1,-1)$ is $21$, giving area $k=21/2$. The ordered functional vertex values are $(4,-3,-1)$, $(-3,4,-1)$, and $(-1,-1,2)$. A cut at zero with positive apex height $h$ and other heights $-r,-s$ cuts an apex triangle with area fraction $h^2/[(h+r)(h+s)]$, by the two edge ratios. This gives the asserted fractions and areas $(24/5,24/5,14/3)$; $16\cdot279<128\cdot35$ and $4/9<16/35$. Near zero, moving the threshold to $t$ multiplies the cap area by $(1-t/h)^2$. Its negative derivative at zero is therefore $2q_i/h$; it equals the normalized chord length by Fubini and continuity of the interior section length (or by the same similar triangles). Substitution gives the three densities. The resulting normalized third inverse is $(3/14)/(5/12+5/12+3/14)=9/44$, below $2/9$ since $81<88$.
