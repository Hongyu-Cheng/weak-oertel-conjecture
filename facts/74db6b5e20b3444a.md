## Statement

Let a finite family of positive-volume compact convex $d$-fibers, $d\ge1$, be sampled at integer bases $z$ in one integer coordinate, with summed $d$-volume. Let $a<b$ be integer bases of two marks at which the same closed nonvertical buffered cap minimizes the cap mass, at least locally under base-tilt changes keeping the respective buffer offsets and the continuous normal fixed. Let $S_z,A_z,B_z$ denote the section $\mathcal H^{d-1}$ divided by the continuous normal length at proper, outside-supporting and inside-supporting traces respectively, with zero for each other status and with counting measure for $d=1$. Then
$
(b-a)\sum_z S_z+\sum_z B_z[(z-a)_++(b-z)_+]
\le \sum_z A_z[(a-z)_++(z-b)_+].
$

## Proof

At a source base $p$ change the base coefficient by $tv$, leaving the continuous coefficient and buffer offset fixed and keeping the source reference at $p$. The physical margin increment is $tv(z-p)$. The slicing paragraphs of b319e7dcda1486e9 apply to this affine increment and the original nonvertical plane, independently of whether the old plane passes through the mark. The raw right derivative is
$
\sum_z S_z v(z-p)+\sum_z A_z[v(z-p)]_+ + \sum_z B_z\min(v(z-p),0).
$
Use the two nonnegative derivatives with $(p,v)=(a,-1),(b,1)$ for the same original physical traces. Adding gives $-(b-a)\sum_z S_z$ from the proper terms, the stated outside contributions, and the negative inside penalty. Rearranging proves the quota.
