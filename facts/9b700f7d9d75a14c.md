## Statement

Fix an integer $d\ge2$ and $r_0,L,U>0$. On $-L\le z\le U$, $|x|\le r_0$, use planar density $r_0^{d-2}/2$ and the cap and section records $P,C,M$ of $z+sx\ge0$, with $C,M$ using $d\mathcal H^1/\sqrt{1+s^2}$ and $z$ in the first moment. Then for $s>0$,
$$
M(s)=\frac{r_0^{d-2}}{4s}\bigl[\min(U,sr_0)^2-\min(L,sr_0)^2\bigr].
$$
With total mass $V$ and the infinity record $(V/2,0,0)$, every centered slope law has corrected cap $P-cC=V/2$ on average for $c=(U-L)/2$.

For an affine radius $r(z)$ on $[-L,U]$ with the corresponding even transverse density $r(z)^{d-2}/2$, axial reflection replaces $r(z)$ by $r(-z)$ on $[-U,L]$ and sends each record to $(V-P,C,-M)$. In particular it transfers a common interval $[\beta V,(1-\beta)V]$ on the corrected caps of centered laws by replacing $c$ by $-c$.

## Proof

For $s>0$, parametrize the section by $(x,z)=(-z/s,z)$, so the coarea weight is $dz/s$. The interval is $[-\min(L,sr_0),\min(U,sr_0)]$. Multiplying by $zr_0^{d-2}/2$ and integrating gives the stated moment. At $s=0$ the moment is zero and $C=r_0^{d-1}$.

If $U\ne L$, all nonzero finite moments have the sign of $U-L$. Their finite zeros are exactly the slopes with $sr_0\le\min(L,U)$, including $0$. At each such slope, the section has $C=r_0^{d-1}$. The rectangular band $-sr_0\le z\le sr_0$ contributes half of its mass to the cap by central symmetry. Above this band all points are captured, and below it none are captured, so $P=r_0^{d-1}U$, also at $s=0$. Thus $P-cC=V/2$ there, and also at infinity, the limit obtained by integrating the full section once $sr_0\ge\max(L,U)$. Centering excludes every other slope because all nonzero moments have the same sign. If $U=L$, central symmetry gives $P=V/2$ at every finite slope and $c=0$.

In the reflection claim the map $(x,z)\mapsto(-x,-z)$ preserves the original density after transport to the reflected profile, using evenness in $x$. It reverses the cap margin and preserves the section and its coarea weight under the isometry. Cap boundaries are area-null, and multiplication by $z$ changes sign. These facts give the record map, including infinity by its definition. Averaging preserves centering and sends $P-cC$ to $V-(P-cC)$ when the new correction is $-c$.
