## Statement

Let \(n,d,k\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z)
 \quad(z\in\mathbb Z^n).
\]
For \((z,x)\in C\), define
\[
 \delta_C(z,x):=
 \inf\{\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H):
 H\subseteq\mathbb R^d\text{ is a closed halfspace and }x\in H\}.
\tag{1}
\]
For a \(d\)-dimensional convex body \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) and \(0<t\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(K)\), define
\[
 \mathsf M_t(K):=
 \left\{\frac1t\int_Kx f(x)\,dx:
 f\in L^\infty(K),\ 0\leq f\leq1\text{ a.e.},\ \int_Kf\,dx=t\right\}.
\tag{2}
\]
For a finite nonempty \(E\subseteq\mathbb Z^n\), \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E\), and positive masses \(0<t_z\leq a_z\), put
\[
 \Lambda_m(E):=
 \left\{\alpha\in\mathbb R_+^E:
 \sum_{z\in E}\alpha_z=1,\ \sum_{z\in E}\alpha_zz=m\right\},
\tag{3}
\]
\[
 \mathcal K_m(\mathsf M_t):=
 \bigcap_{\alpha\in\Lambda_m(E)}
 \sum_{z\in E}\alpha_z\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z).
\tag{4}
\]
The complete metronoid-core capacity from accepted fact `ae0161dd5d4ea672` is
\[
 \mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C):=
 \sup\left\{\sum_{z\in E}t_z:
 \begin{array}{l}
 \varnothing\ne E\subseteq\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},\quad0<t_z\leq a_z,\\
 \text{there is }m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E
 \text{ with }\mathcal K_m(\mathsf M_t)\ne\varnothing,\\
 \displaystyle\sum_{z\in E\cap J}t_z\geq
 2^{-n}\sum_{z\in E}t_z
 \text{ for every proper closed halfspace }J\subseteq\mathbb R^n
 \text{ containing }m
 \end{array}\right\},
\tag{5}
\]
with the supremum of the empty family equal to zero.

Consider an admissible factorized lattice-polysimplex packet in the notation of accepted fact `5b6523682250e7a5`. Thus, for \(1\leq i\leq k\), let \(r_i\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\[
 \Delta_i=\operatorname{conv}\{v_{i,0},\ldots,v_{i,r_i}\}
 \subseteq\mathbb R^{r_i}
\]
be a full-dimensional lattice simplex, let \(D_i:=\Delta_i\cap\mathbb Z^{r_i}\), and put \(q:=\sum_{i=1}^kr_i\leq n\). Let
\[
 \Phi(t_1,\ldots,t_k):=b+\sum_{i=1}^kU_it_i,
 \qquad
 b\in\mathbb Z^n,\quad U_i\in\mathbb Z^{n\times r_i},
\tag{6}
\]
where the concatenated matrix \(U=[U_1\ \cdots\ U_k]\) has rank \(q\). At every product vertex \(j=(j_1,\ldots,j_k)\), suppose the packet gives
\[
 x_j\in C_{\Phi(v_{1,j_1},\ldots,v_{k,j_k})}
\]
and numbers \(a_{i,j_i}\geq0\) such that
\[
 \delta_C\!\left(\Phi(v_{1,j_1},\ldots,v_{k,j_k}),x_j\right)^{1/d}
 \geq\prod_{i=1}^ka_{i,j_i}.
\tag{7}
\]
Writing \(\lambda_{i,j}\) for the barycentric coordinate of \(v_{i,j}\) on \(\Delta_i\), define
\[
 x(t_1,\ldots,t_k):=
 \sum_{j_1=0}^{r_1}\cdots\sum_{j_k=0}^{r_k}
 \left(\prod_{i=1}^k\lambda_{i,j_i}(t_i)\right)x_{j_1,\ldots,j_k},
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 g_i(t_i):=\sum_{j=0}^{r_i}\lambda_{i,j}(t_i)a_{i,j},
 \qquad
 A_i:=\sum_{u_i\in D_i}g_i(u_i)^d,
 \qquad
 \mathcal E(\Pi):=\prod_{i=1}^kA_i.
\tag{9}
\]
Suppose there is an affine map
\[
 s:b+\operatorname{im}_{\mathbb R}U\longrightarrow\mathbb R^d
\tag{10}
\]
such that
\[
 x(u_1,\ldots,u_k)=s\!\left(\Phi(u_1,\ldots,u_k)\right)
\tag{11}
\]
for every \((u_1,\ldots,u_k)\in\prod_iD_i\) satisfying
\[
 \prod_{i=1}^kg_i(u_i)^d>0.
\tag{12}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal E(\Pi)\leq\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C).}
\tag{13}
\]

For the explicit bodies \(C_j\), mixed volumes \(W_j\), and accepted \(1+1+1\) packets \(\Pi_j\) of accepted fact `c168604b1c021192`, one consequently has
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C_j)
 \geq\mathcal E(\Pi_j)>\frac1eW_j
 }
 \qquad(j\geq2).
\tag{14}
\]

## Proof

For \(u=(u_1,\ldots,u_k)\in\prod_iD_i\), put
\[
 \omega_i(u_i):=g_i(u_i)^d,
 \qquad
 \omega(u):=\prod_{i=1}^k\omega_i(u_i).
\tag{15}
\]
Finite distributivity gives
\[
 \sum_{u\in\prod_iD_i}\omega(u)
 =\prod_{i=1}^k\sum_{u_i\in D_i}\omega_i(u_i)
 =\mathcal E(\Pi).
\tag{16}
\]
Accepted fact `5b6523682250e7a5` proves for the canonical interpolation (8) that
\[
 \delta_C(\Phi(u),x(u))\geq\omega(u)
 \qquad\left(u\in\prod_iD_i\right).
\tag{17}
\]

If \(\mathcal E(\Pi)=0\), then (13) follows from the nonnegativity in definition (5). Assume henceforth that \(\mathcal E(\Pi)>0\), and set
\[
 \mathcal D_+:=\{u\in\textstyle\prod_iD_i:\omega(u)>0\}.
\tag{18}
\]
This is a finite nonempty subset of \(\mathbb Z^q\). Apply the discrete specialization \(n=q,d=0\) of accepted fact `6d6fcf377e68c79c` to the finite measure
\[
 \nu:=\sum_{u\in\mathcal D_+}\omega(u)\delta_u.
\tag{19}
\]
It supplies \(c\in\mathbb Z^q\) such that every proper closed halfspace \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^q\) containing \(c\) has
\[
 \nu(K)\geq2^{-q}\mathcal E(\Pi).
\tag{20}
\]
Moreover \(c\in\operatorname{conv}\mathcal D_+\). Indeed, otherwise strict separation from the finite convex hull would give a proper closed halfspace containing \(c\) and disjoint from \(\mathcal D_+\), contradicting (20).

Put
\[
 E:=\Phi(\mathcal D_+),
 \qquad m:=\Phi(c),
 \qquad t_{\Phi(u)}:=\omega(u)\quad(u\in\mathcal D_+).
\tag{21}
\]
The rank assumption in (6) makes \(\Phi\) injective, so the masses in (21) are well defined and
\[
 \sum_{z\in E}t_z=\mathcal E(\Pi).
\tag{22}
\]
Also \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E\). Equation (17) gives \(0<t_{\Phi(u)}\leq a_{\Phi(u)}\). The left metronoid inclusion \(D_t(K)\subseteq\mathsf M_t(K)\) from accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d`, applied with \(K=C_{\Phi(u)}\) and (17), therefore gives
\[
 x(u)\in\mathsf M_{t_{\Phi(u)}}(C_{\Phi(u)})
 \qquad(u\in\mathcal D_+).
\tag{23}
\]

Let \(J\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) be a proper closed halfspace containing \(m\). Its pullback
\[
 \Phi^{-1}(J)=\{u\in\mathbb R^q:\Phi(u)\in J\}
\]
contains \(c\) and is either all of \(\mathbb R^q\) or a proper closed halfspace. In the first case its \(\nu\)-mass is \(\mathcal E(\Pi)\); in the second case (20) applies. Since \(q\leq n\), in either case
\[
 \sum_{z\in E\cap J}t_z
 =\nu(\Phi^{-1}(J))
 \geq2^{-q}\mathcal E(\Pi)
 \geq2^{-n}\mathcal E(\Pi).
\tag{24}
\]

It remains to check every barycentric representation in the complete core. Fix arbitrary \(\alpha\in\Lambda_m(E)\). By (10)-(11), affineness of \(s\), and (23),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 s(m)
 &=s\!\left(\sum_{z\in E}\alpha_zz\right)
 =\sum_{z\in E}\alpha_zs(z)\\
 &=\sum_{u\in\mathcal D_+}\alpha_{\Phi(u)}x(u)
 \in\sum_{z\in E}\alpha_z\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{25}
\]
Because (25) holds for every \(\alpha\in\Lambda_m(E)\),
\[
 s(m)\in\mathcal K_m(\mathsf M_t).
\tag{26}
\]
Thus (21)-(26) are an admissible configuration in the complete-core capacity (5), with total mass \(\mathcal E(\Pi)\). Definition (5) proves (13). This use of the complete core is precisely the all-representations requirement in accepted fact `ae0161dd5d4ea672`, not a choice of one preferred barycentric representation.

Finally consider the packet \(\Pi_j\) in accepted fact `c168604b1c021192`. Its three factors are the intervals
\[
 [1,j],\qquad[-j,j],\qquad[-j,j],
\]
embedded by \(\Phi(t,u,v)=(t,u,v)\). At each product vertex, the chosen point depends only on the first coordinate: it is \(c_1\) when \(t=1\) and \(c_j\) when \(t=j\). Hence its canonical interpolation (8) is
\[
 x(t,u,v)=
 \frac{j-t}{j-1}c_1+
 \frac{t-1}{j-1}c_j,
\tag{27}
\]
which is affine in \((t,u,v)\) and independent of \(u,v\). Therefore (11) holds at every positive-energy lattice point. Applying (13), followed by the accepted estimate
\[
 \frac{\mathcal E(\Pi_j)}{W_j}>\frac1e
\]
from `c168604b1c021192`, proves (14).
