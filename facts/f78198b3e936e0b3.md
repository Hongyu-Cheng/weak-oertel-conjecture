## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), and let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be in the canonical dyadic setting of accepted fact 9c47a4888e8a2947. Retain from that fact the finite, nonempty, globally endpoint-disjoint same-parity matching \(\mathcal M_2\), its positive scalar weights \(t_e\), its midpoints \(m_e\), and, for each endpoint \(y\), the full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex body
\[
 D_y:=\mathsf M_{a_y/2}(C_y)-c_y.
\]
For \(e=\{u,w\}\in\mathcal M_2\), put
\[
 D_e:=\frac12(D_u+D_w),
 \qquad
 \bar c_e:=\frac{c_u+c_w}{2},
 \qquad
 L_e:=\bar c_e+D_e.
\tag{1}
\]
These are the objects in the weighted affine-frustration dual of accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, with that fact's scalar weights renamed \(t_e\).

Let \(\mathcal A(t)\) be the dual-feasible set
\[
 \mathcal A(t):=
 \left\{
 v=(v_e)_{e\in\mathcal M_2}:
 \sum_ev_e=0,
 \quad
 \sum_ev_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \quad
 h_{D_e}(v_e)\leq t_e\ (e\in\mathcal M_2)
 \right\},
\tag{2}
\]
and define
\[
 J(v):=-\sum_eh_{L_e}(v_e),
 \qquad
 \Delta:=\max_{v\in\mathcal A(t)}J(v),
 \qquad
 \mathcal O(t):=\{v\in\mathcal A(t):J(v)=\Delta\}.
\tag{3}
\]
For \(v\in\mathcal A(t)\), define
\[
 \omega_e(v):=h_{D_e}(v_e),
 \qquad
 \Omega(v):=\sum_e\omega_e(v).
\tag{4}
\]
The set of gauge-load-minimal dual optimizers is
\[
 \mathcal V_{\min}(t):=
 \operatorname*{argmin}_{v\in\mathcal O(t)}\Omega(v),
 \qquad
 \Omega_{\min}:=\min_{v\in\mathcal O(t)}\Omega(v).
\tag{5}
\]

Global endpoint-disjointness gives every matched endpoint \(y\) a unique incident edge \(e(y)\). For \(v\in\mathcal A(t)\), define
\[
 U_y(v):=\frac12v_{e(y)},
 \qquad
 \sigma_y(v):=h_{D_y}(U_y(v)),
 \qquad
 T_*(v):=\sum_y\sigma_y(v).
\tag{6}
\]

Then the following statements hold.

1. The feasible set \(\mathcal A(t)\) is compact and convex, and \(\mathcal O(t)\) is nonempty, compact, and convex.

2. On \(\mathcal O(t)\), the gauge load agrees with an affine functional:
\[
 \Omega(v)=-\Delta-\sum_e\langle\bar c_e,v_e\rangle.
\tag{7}
\]
Consequently, \(\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) is a nonempty compact, possibly improper, exposed face of \(\mathcal O(t)\). This only asserts a face of the convex optimizer set \(\mathcal O(t)\), not a face of the entire feasible set \(\mathcal A(t)\).

3. Every edge load and every endpoint load is affine on \(\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) in the exact restricted sense that, for all \(v,w\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\), all \(0\leq\theta\leq1\), every edge \(e\), and every matched endpoint \(y\),
\[
 \omega_e((1-\theta)v+\theta w)
 =(1-\theta)\omega_e(v)+\theta\omega_e(w),
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 \sigma_y((1-\theta)v+\theta w)
 =(1-\theta)\sigma_y(v)+\theta\sigma_y(w).
\tag{9}
\]
The functions in (8)-(9) are support functions composed with coordinate projections and need not be affine outside \(\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\). For every \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\),
\[
 T_*(v)=\Omega(v)=\Omega_{\min}.
\tag{10}
\]

4. Fix any primal optimizer \(s_*:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) for the weighted affine defect
\[
 \Delta=
 \min_{s\ {\rm affine}}
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal M_2}
 t_e\operatorname{dist}_{D_e}(s(m_e),L_e)
\tag{11}
\]
from accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d. If \(s_*(m_e)\notin L_e\), then
\[
 \boxed{\omega_e(v)=t_e\quad\text{for every }v\in\mathcal O(t).}
\tag{12}
\]
In particular, if \(e=\{u,w\}\) is missed by this fixed primal optimizer, then every \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) satisfies the fixed pair-sum constraint
\[
 \sigma_u(v)+\sigma_w(v)=t_e.
\tag{13}
\]
Thus the endpoint-load image of \(\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) is a compact convex set with all pair sums indexed by the fixed missed-edge set prescribed by (13).

No statement above proves \(T_*\leq4R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+8\mathfrak F_*\), extracts one high-mass low-rank selector, aggregates distinct selectors or frames, or proves the weak Oertel conjecture.

## Proof

Accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d proves that each \(D_e\) in (1) is a full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex body. Hence \(h_{D_e}\) is a norm on the finite-dimensional dual fiber. The capacity constraint \(h_{D_e}(v_e)\leq t_e\) therefore bounds \(v_e\). The product of these coordinate balls is compact, and intersecting it with the two closed linear moment constraints in (2) shows that \(\mathcal A(t)\) is compact. It is nonempty because it contains the zero field, and it is convex because norms are convex and the moment constraints are linear.

Every \(h_{L_e}\) is a support function and hence convex, so \(J\) is continuous and concave. Compactness gives a maximizer, consistently with the attained dual maximum in accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d; thus \(\mathcal O(t)\) is nonempty and compact. If \(v,w\in\mathcal O(t)\) and \(0\leq\theta\leq1\), feasibility and concavity give
\[
 J((1-\theta)v+\theta w)
 \geq(1-\theta)J(v)+\theta J(w)=\Delta.
\tag{14}
\]
The left side is at most the maximum \(\Delta\), so equality holds. Therefore \(\mathcal O(t)\) is convex.

Because \(L_e=\bar c_e+D_e\), translation of a convex body gives
\[
 h_{L_e}(v_e)=\langle\bar c_e,v_e\rangle+h_{D_e}(v_e).
\tag{15}
\]
For \(v\in\mathcal O(t)\), equations (3)-(4) and (15) yield
\[
 \Delta=J(v)
 =-\sum_e\langle\bar c_e,v_e\rangle-\Omega(v),
\]
which is (7). Thus minimizing \(\Omega\) on \(\mathcal O(t)\) is the same as maximizing the ambient linear functional
\[
 v\longmapsto\sum_e\langle\bar c_e,v_e\rangle
\]
on the nonempty compact convex set \(\mathcal O(t)\). Its maximizer set is a nonempty compact, possibly improper, exposed face of \(\mathcal O(t)\), proving statement 2 and, in particular, the convexity of \(\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\).

Fix \(v,w\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\), \(0\leq\theta\leq1\), and put \(z=(1-\theta)v+\theta w\). Since \(\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) is convex, \(z\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\). For each edge define its convexity defect
\[
 \delta_e:=(1-\theta)\omega_e(v)+\theta\omega_e(w)-\omega_e(z)\geq0.
\tag{16}
\]
All three fields have gauge load \(\Omega_{\min}\), so
\[
 \sum_e\delta_e
 =(1-\theta)\Omega_{\min}+\theta\Omega_{\min}-\Omega_{\min}=0.
\tag{17}
\]
Every term in (17) is nonnegative, hence every \(\delta_e=0\). This proves (8).

For \(e=\{u,w\}\), accepted fact 9c47a4888e8a2947 proves, for every dual-feasible field,
\[
 \sigma_u(v)+\sigma_w(v)=\omega_e(v).
\tag{18}
\]
Indeed, this is the support-function identity
\[
 h_{D_u}(v_e/2)+h_{D_w}(v_e/2)
 =h_{(D_u+D_w)/2}(v_e).
\]
The two endpoint functions in (18) are convex. Their two nonnegative convexity defects between \(v,w,z\) sum to the edge defect \(\delta_e=0\). Both endpoint defects therefore vanish, proving (9). Summing (18) once over the endpoint-disjoint matching gives \(T_*(v)=\Omega(v)\) for every dual-feasible \(v\). Equation (10) follows on \(\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\).

It remains to prove the simultaneous saturation claim. Fix the primal optimizer \(s_*\) in (11), and set
\[
 x_e:=s_*(m_e),
 \qquad
 d_e:=\operatorname{dist}_{D_e}(x_e,L_e).
\tag{19}
\]
Take an arbitrary \(v\in\mathcal O(t)\). Choose a nearest point \(\ell_e\in L_e\) to \(x_e\). The dual norm inequality, the definition of a support function, and dual feasibility give, edge by edge,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \langle v_e,x_e\rangle-h_{L_e}(v_e)
 &\leq\langle v_e,x_e-\ell_e\rangle\\
 &\leq h_{D_e}(v_e)\lVert x_e-\ell_e\rVert_{D_e}\\
 &=\omega_e(v)d_e\\
 &\leq t_ed_e.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{20}
\]
Because \(s_*\) is affine and \(v\) satisfies both affine moment cancellations,
\[
 \sum_e\langle v_e,x_e\rangle
 =\sum_e\langle v_e,s_*(m_e)\rangle=0.
\tag{21}
\]
The sum of the leftmost expressions in (20) is therefore
\[
 -\sum_eh_{L_e}(v_e)=J(v)=\Delta,
\tag{22}
\]
while the sum of the rightmost expressions is the value of the primal optimizer in (11), also \(\Delta\). Hence the sum of all nonnegative gaps in the chain (20) is zero. In particular, for every edge,
\[
 (t_e-\omega_e(v))d_e=0.
\tag{23}
\]
If \(s_*(m_e)\notin L_e\), then \(d_e>0\), so (23) gives \(\omega_e(v)=t_e\). The choice of \(v\in\mathcal O(t)\) was arbitrary, proving (12). Equation (13) follows from (18).

Finally, the coordinate map \(v\mapsto(\sigma_y(v))_y\) is continuous and is affine on \(\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) by (9). Its image is therefore compact and convex, and (13) supplies the asserted fixed pair sums. The proof does not combine any of these pair constraints into one selector or stress frame, so none of the explicitly excluded terminal conclusions follows.
