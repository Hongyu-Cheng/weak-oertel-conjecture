## Statement

Let $P\subset\mathbb Z^k$ be finite and let $Q_{\ell,z}\subseteq\mathbb R^m$, $z\in P$, $m\ge1$, be nonempty compact convex fibers. Assume that every integer convex combination $j=\sum_i t_i z_i$ of bases in $P$ belongs to $P$ and $\sum_i t_iQ_{\ell,z_i}\subseteq Q_{\ell,j}$. Suppose the fibers converge in Hausdorff distance, $M_\ell=\sum_z|Q_{\ell,z}|>0$, and $|Q_{\ell,z}|/M_\ell\to p_z$. Set $P_+=\{z:p_z>0\}$. For every such representation with $z_i\in P_+$, $p_j\ge(\sum_i t_i p_{z_i}^{1/m})^m$, and $P_+=\operatorname{conv}(P_+)\cap\mathbb Z^k$.

In any fixed representation using actual bases and a source $z\in P_+$ of coefficient $\lambda>0$, one has $p_j\ge\lambda^m p_z$. The source and target have the same limiting affine direction space. Whenever $|Q_{\ell,z}|\ge\eta M_\ell$ for $\eta>0$, their widths are comparable by the two-body Minkowski-volume bound with $a=\lambda^m$ and $D=(1+\lambda^{-1})^m/\eta$. In particular each positive parity class has one limiting direction space, so there are at most $2^k$ such strata. Directly comparing two same-parity sources whose masses are at least $\eta M_\ell$ gives the same two-body bound with $a=\eta$ and $D=2^m/\eta$.

## Proof

All volumes are full $m$-dimensional Lebesgue volumes. By the stated Minkowski inclusion and classical Brunn–Minkowski,
$$
|Q_{\ell,j}|^{1/m}\ge\sum_i t_i |Q_{\ell,z_i}|^{1/m}
$$
when the used sources have positive volume, which holds eventually for the representations within $P_+$. Divide by $M_\ell^{1/m}$ and take limits. After zero coefficients are dropped, the right side is positive, so this also proves that the positive support is lattice-complete.

For the general fixed representation, choose a point of each of the other nonempty source fibers. Compatibility gives $\lambda Q_{\ell,z}+b_\ell\subseteq Q_{\ell,j}$ for their weighted sum $b_\ell$, including if some of them have zero volume. In particular $|Q_{\ell,j}|\ge\lambda^m |Q_{\ell,z}|$ and $p_j\ge\lambda^m p_z$. Put $K=Q_{\ell,z}$ and $L=Q_{\ell,j}$. For all sufficiently large $\ell$ both are convex bodies. Convexity of $L$ gives
$$
K+L\subseteq \lambda^{-1}(L-b_\ell)+L=(1+\lambda^{-1})L-\lambda^{-1}b_\ell.
$$
Since $|L|\le M_\ell$ and $|K|\ge\eta M_\ell$ eventually for any fixed $0<\eta<p_z$, the two-body comparison applies with precisely the stated constants and its Hausdorff consequence gives equality of the limiting direction spaces. The argument applies to each positive source of the representation. For a zero-weight source the translated scaled inclusion survives in the limit, by choosing convergent points in the other fibers, but equality of directions is not implied.

For distinct positive same-parity bases $z,w$, the midpoint is integral and belongs to $P_+$. The preceding argument gives equality of the two directions through their midpoint. Also compatibility directly gives $|Q_{\ell,z}+Q_{\ell,w}|\le 2^m|Q_{\ell,(z+w)/2}|\le2^m M_\ell$. If both masses are at least $\eta M_\ell$, then $|Q_{\ell,w}|\ge\eta|Q_{\ell,z}|$ and $2^m M_\ell\le(2^m/\eta)|Q_{\ell,z}|$, which proves the specified direct bound. This uses only relative masses; $M_\ell$ may tend to zero.
