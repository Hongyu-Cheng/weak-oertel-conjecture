## Statement

Let the finite actual full-dimensional sampled fibers $K_z$ be contained in $y(z)+\ell(z)D$, $\ell(z)>0$, with $0\in\operatorname{int}D$. Write $D_z=(K_z-y(z))/\ell(z)$, $P_D(\xi)=1+a_D\cdot\xi$ for the ordinary positive projective minimizing denominator of $(D,0)$, and $R(z,X)=\ell(z)+a_D\cdot(X-y(z))$. Let $n$ be the outer unit normal at a regular physical fiber boundary point. The summed surface integral of the field $h_D(n)R(z,X)^{-d}$ over contacts $n\cdot(X-y(z))=\ell(z)h_D(n)$ is
$$
\int_{\partial D} h_D(n)P_D(\xi)^{-d}
\sum_z \ell(z)^{-1}\mathbf1_{D_z}(\xi)\,d\mathcal H^{d-1}(\xi).
$$
For a cap-weighted integral, each summand includes the actual inward selector evaluated at $(z,y(z)+\ell(z)\xi)$, with its supporting-face allocation retained.

## Proof

The projective minimizer theorem d90ba05577427854 gives $P_D>0$ on $D$, and $R=\ell(z)P_D$ under the homothety. A contact in $K_z$ necessarily belongs to the homothetic image of $D_z\cap\partial D$. Conversely at each regular common point, a supporting normal of the outer body also supports the inner body, so their unit normals agree whenever both are regular. The singular subsets have zero surface area for each convex boundary, and remain null after rescaling. Thus up to null sets the contact is precisely the homothetic image of $D_z\cap\partial D$, including the endpoint interpretation with counting measure when $d=1$. Surface measure has scaling $\ell(z)^{d-1}$, and the field has scaling $\ell(z)^{-d}$. Multiplication and summation give the formula. Inserting a measurable selector under the same change of variables keeps the actual original contact value. In particular if $L=L_*[\ell(z)+b\cdot(X-y(z))]$ is a positive volume denominator, its reciprocal mixed-volume density pulls back on each fiber to $\ell(z)^{d-1}\mathbf1_{D_z}(\xi)/[M L_*(1+b\cdot\xi)]$ times $d\xi$, a different measure and scaling.
