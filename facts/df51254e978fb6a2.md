## Statement

In the setting of the body-tied buffer, fix an own-interior sampled mark $Y=(p,y)$. Write $F(z,x)=E_{C_z}(x)^{1/d}$, $v(z)=V(z)^{1/d}$, and let $a_Y$ minimize the energy at $Y$. There are affine upper supports $S\ge F$ and $T\ge v$ on $C$ and $\operatorname{conv}(P)$ respectively, touching at $Y$ and $p$. The affine function
$$
R_Y=\frac dN\frac{S}{F(Y)}+\frac1N\frac{T}{v(p)}
$$
satisfies $H\le H(Y)R_Y$ on $C$ with equality at $Y$. It restricts on $K_p$ to $P_Y(X)=1+a_Y\cdot(X-y)$, and $\nabla_y\log f(Y)=a_Y$. On $C$, $R_Y\ge v_{\min}/(Nv_{\max})$, where $v_{\min}=\min v$ and $v_{\max}=\max v$ on the real base hull. With a common fiber inradius $r$ and outer radius $R$ this floor is at least $r/(NR)$. On $K_p$ one has $P_Y\ge1/N$.

If a positive own-interior sampled mark $Y$ is a convex combination $\sum_i\lambda_i Y_i$ of own-interior sampled marks with $\lambda_i>0$ and $H(Y)=\sum_i\lambda_i H(Y_i)$, then the centered fibers $K_{p_i}-y_i$ are positively homothetic and $F(Y_i)/v(p_i)$ is constant. The same majorant $H(Y)R_Y$ touches all these marks, and $R_Y/R_Y(Y_i)$ is a normalized positive affine majorant at $Y_i$, with own restriction $P_{Y_i}$.

## Proof

The energy support at an own-interior sampled mark, including at a boundary base, is d7ff9f9aad2b6512, and extends to own boundaries by continuity. For the volume-root support, 7227bbfcd12ad3d2 gives a Hausdorff-Lipschitz family with common inradius $r>0$. Two fibers at Hausdorff distance $h<r$ fit within the factors $1+h/r$ about their respective inball centers, by their support inequalities. Thus $v$ is Lipschitz on the relative base hull. The negative convex function $-v$ on the base hull has a convex extension to its affine span by taking the infimum of $-v(q)+L|z-q|$ over $q$ in the hull, for a Lipschitz constant $L$. The extension is finite and agrees on the hull, so it has a subgradient at $p$, giving $T$.

Weighted AM–GM gives the majorant, with $S\ge0$ and $T\ge v_{\min}$ giving the floor. The gradient in the own fiber is $(1/N)\nabla_y\log E=a_Y$ by a56f8711406a3870, so the differentiable own-interior restriction of a touching affine chart has exactly this slope after normalization. The own-fiber denominator floor is the same accepted energy floor. If $Y$ is as in the last assertion, the strict increase of the weighted geometric mean at positive arguments forces equality in both concavity inequalities for $F$ and $v$. Equality in the concavity of the weighted geometric mean forces the positive pairs $(F(Y_i),v(p_i))$ to have constant ratio, by strict concavity of the one-variable power after normalizing by the second component. The marked homothety equality in 5aeb5916857f97a9 applies to the convex representation and the actual containing fiber at $p$. Finally the nonnegative affine-majorant gaps have zero weighted sum, so each is zero. The normalized charts at the other marks have the same required own derivatives.
