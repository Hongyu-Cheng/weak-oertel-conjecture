## Statement

Let $n,d\ge1$, $D$ be a $d$-simplex of volume $V$ and centroid $y$, and let $C=[0,1]^n\times D$ with $N=2^n$ sampled fibers. Use the uniform outward ramp with positive width $\delta$, nonnegative tied buffer $\varepsilon f$, and the pooled covariance norm $\Gamma$. For sufficiently small $\varepsilon,\delta>0$, every base has an actual deepest mark at $y$; an attaining nonvertical cap can be chosen at any one source so that all other fiber profiles are identically zero by a finite tilt. Its smooth own mass is $Q_{\varepsilon,\delta}$. The canonical common chart at that source may be chosen $R=1$, with associated field $h_y(n)=h_D(n)-n\cdot y$ on every fiber. Its normalized payment for the isolated cap (or any probability menu of such caps) is at most $Q_{\varepsilon,\delta}/(NV)$, with $Q_{\varepsilon,\delta}/(NV)\to\gamma_d/N$ as $\varepsilon,\delta\downarrow0$. Thus this field does not give the individual fraction $\gamma_d$ for small positive parameters.
