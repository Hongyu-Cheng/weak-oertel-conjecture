## Statement

Let $K$ be a nondegenerate planar triangle with interior source $y$ and nonzero spanning normals $v_1,v_2,v_3$ that sum to zero. Suppose the through-source caps strictly capture three distinct vertices and strictly exclude the other vertices in each cap. Write $k=|K|$, $Q_i$ for the cap areas, $\sigma_i$ for the chord lengths divided by the respective $\lVert v_i\rVert$, and $f_i=2\sqrt{kQ_i}/(\sigma_i\sum_\ell Q_\ell/\sigma_\ell)$. In the exact parameterization $f_i=2\sqrt{u_i(1-u_{i+1})}$ with $0<u_i<1$ and $1<\sum_i u_i<2$, the cubic deficit satisfies
$$
4-\sum_i f_i^2-f_1f_2f_3
=4\left(\sqrt{\prod_i u_i}-\sqrt{\prod_i(1-u_i)}\right)^2\ge0.
$$
Consequently each row satisfies $f_i+(f_j+f_k)^2/4\le2$. Equality in this row holds exactly when the cubic deficit is zero and $f_j=f_k$, for $\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\}$.

## Proof

The strict triangle row image theorem supplies the asserted $u$-coordinates. Set $P=\prod_i u_i$ and $Q=\prod_i(1-u_i)$. The cyclic formulas give $\sum_i f_i^2/4=\sum_i u_i-\sum_{i<j}u_i u_j$, $f_1f_2f_3=8\sqrt{PQ}$, and $P+Q=1-\sum_i u_i+\sum_{i<j}u_i u_j$. Substitution proves the cubic deficit formula.

For fixed $i$ put $g=f_j+f_k$. We have $0<f_i<2$ directly from $f_i=2\sqrt{u_i(1-u_{i+1})}$. Since $f_jf_k\le g^2/4$,
$$
4\ge\sum_\ell f_\ell^2+f_1f_2f_3
=f_i^2+g^2-(2-f_i)f_jf_k
\ge f_i^2+(2+f_i)g^2/4 .
$$
Rearrange and divide by $2+f_i>0$. Equality requires equality in both inequalities, which is exactly the claimed criterion.
