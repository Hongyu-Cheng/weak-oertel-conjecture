## Statement

Let \(n,d,L\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), put \(m=n+1\), and let \(\delta\ge0\). Here \(L\) denotes the lattice dilation, not an affine majorant. Define the full-dimensional simplex \(P_L=\{\mathbf x\in\mathbb R^n:x_i\ge0,\ \sum_{i=1}^n x_i\le L\}\) and its complete integer support \(D_+=P_L\cap\mathbb Z^n\). Its barycentric coordinates are \(p_0(\mathbf x)=1-L^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n x_i\) and \(p_i(\mathbf x)=x_i/L\) for \(1\le i\le n\). For a probability vector \(\mathbf p=(p_0,\ldots,p_n)\), set \(H(\mathbf p)=-\sum_{i=0}^n p_i\log p_i\), using natural logarithms and \(0\log0=0\). Put \(r(\mathbf x)=1+\delta H(\mathbf p(\mathbf x))\), \(w_{\mathbf z}=r(\mathbf z)^d\), and \(W=\sum_{\mathbf z\in D_+}w_{\mathbf z}\).

For every \(\varepsilon\ge0\), the positive concave function \(r\) obeys the uniform real midpoint-volume inequality

\[
r\left(\frac{\mathbf x+\mathbf y}{2}\right)^d
\le(1+\varepsilon)
\left(\frac{r(\mathbf x)+r(\mathbf y)}2\right)^d
\qquad(\mathbf x,\mathbf y\in P_L)
\tag{1}
\]

if and only if \((1+\delta\log2)^d\le1+\varepsilon\). Suppose (1) holds and \(0\le\varepsilon<\log2/\log3\). The positive constant function \(\varphi(\mathbf x)=1+\delta\log\min\{m,L\}\), defined on \(P_L=\operatorname{conv}(D_+)\), is convex, dominates \(r\) at every integer site, and satisfies

\[
\begin{aligned}
0\le\sum_{\mathbf z\in D_+}
\bigl(\varphi(\mathbf z)^d-w_{\mathbf z}\bigr)
&\le
\frac{(\log3/\log2)\varepsilon}
{1-(\log3/\log2)\varepsilon}\,W,\\
\sum_{\mathbf z\in D_+}
\bigl(\varphi(\mathbf z)^d-w_{\mathbf z}\bigr)
&\le2\varepsilon W
\qquad(0\le\varepsilon\le1/10).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

Every \(r\) above is an actual root-volume profile of a convex body: with \(B=[-1/2,1/2]^d\), take \(C=\{(\mathbf x,\mathbf y):\mathbf x\in P_L,\ \mathbf y\in r(\mathbf x)B\}\). Its positive integer fiber support is exactly \(D_+\), and its integer fiber volumes are the displayed \(w_{\mathbf z}\). These are the actual-volume notation \(D_+,w_{\mathbf z},W,\varphi\) of accepted fact \(\texttt{0bcc51d8cd216cf3}\); \(r(\mathbf z)=w_{\mathbf z}^{1/d}\) has the actual root-volume normalization. More generally, any convex body \(C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) with this positive integer support and these integer volumes has, for \(\varepsilon\le1/10\), one point \(\mathbf y^\star\in S=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), chosen before every containing halfspace, with

\[
h_S(\mathbf y^\star)
\ge[\gamma_d-2\theta_d\varepsilon]_+\,2^{-n}W,
\qquad
\gamma_d=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
\quad \theta_d=1-\gamma_d,
\tag{3}
\]

where \(h_S\) is the locked actual mixed-integer halfspace depth and \([t]_+=\max\{t,0\}\).

## Proof

The function \(-t\log t\), extended continuously at zero, is concave on \([0,1]\): its second derivative is \(-1/t<0\) on \((0,1]\), and continuity extends the concavity inequalities to zero. Therefore \(H\), and hence \(r\), are finite continuous concave functions; \(r\ge1\).

For any two probability vectors \(\mathbf p,\mathbf q\), write \(s_i=(p_i+q_i)/2\). For indices with \(s_i>0\), put \(t_i=p_i/(p_i+q_i)\). Direct expansion, with the zero terms omitted, gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
H\left(\frac{\mathbf p+\mathbf q}2\right)
-\frac{H(\mathbf p)+H(\mathbf q)}2
&=\sum_{i:s_i>0}s_i
\bigl(\log2+t_i\log t_i+(1-t_i)\log(1-t_i)\bigr)\\
&\le\log2\sum_i s_i
=\log2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]

The inequality uses \(t\log t\le0\) on \([0,1]\). Since the barycentric coordinate map is affine and \((r(\mathbf x)+r(\mathbf y))/2\ge1\), (4) implies \(r((\mathbf x+\mathbf y)/2)\le(1+\delta\log2)(r(\mathbf x)+r(\mathbf y))/2\). This proves sufficiency in the assertion about (1). For necessity, choose two distinct vertices of \(P_L\). Their entropies are zero, whereas their midpoint has entropy \(\log2\), so their root values give exactly \((1+\delta\log2)^d\le1+\varepsilon\). In particular, the binomial expansion with nonnegative terms gives

\[
1+d\delta\log2
\le(1+\delta\log2)^d
\le1+\varepsilon,
\qquad d\delta\log2\le\varepsilon.
\tag{5}
\]

We now average over the original integer sites, each counted once. Every \(\mathbf z\in D_+\) corresponds bijectively to the weak composition \(\mathbf k=(k_0,\ldots,k_{m-1})\) of \(L\), where \(k_0=L-\sum_{i=1}^n z_i\) and \(k_i=z_i\). Let \(N=|D_+|=\binom{L+m-1}{m-1}\), and let \(\sum_{\mathbf k}\) mean summation over all these compositions. An integer-site probability vector \(\mathbf k/L\) has at most \(\min\{m,L\}\) positive entries. If it has \(s\) positive entries, concavity of logarithm gives \(H(\mathbf k/L)=\sum_{k_i>0}(k_i/L)\log(L/k_i)\le\log s\). Consequently every integer-site entropy is at most \(\log\min\{m,L\}\), proving the sitewise domination by \(\varphi\). The vertices belong to \(D_+\), so \(\operatorname{conv}(D_+)=P_L\).

The second moment of the uniform weak-composition table is exact. For any fixed coordinate, its factorial second-moment sum is \(2\binom{L+m-1}{m+1}\). Indeed, the formal generating series of that sum is \(2t^2(1-t)^{-(m+2)}\): the selected coordinate contributes \(\sum_{j\ge0}j(j-1)t^j=2t^2(1-t)^{-3}\), and the other \(m-1\) coordinates contribute \((1-t)^{-(m-1)}\). Extracting the coefficient of \(t^L\) proves the identity, with the coefficient zero when \(L=1\). Summing over the \(m\) symmetric coordinates and using \(\sum_i k_i=L\) gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac1{NL}\sum_{\mathbf k}\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}k_i^2
&=1+
\frac{2m}{NL}\binom{L+m-1}{m+1}\\
&=1+\frac{2(L-1)}{m+1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]

For the last equality when \(L\ge2\), the binomial ratio is \(\binom{L+m-1}{m+1}/\binom{L+m-1}{m-1}=L(L-1)/(m(m+1))\); when \(L=1\), both added terms are zero.

Write \(\overline H=N^{-1}\sum_{\mathbf k}H(\mathbf k/L)\). The numbers \(k_i/(NL)\), indexed by all pairs \((\mathbf k,i)\) with \(k_i>0\), sum to one. Jensen's inequality for logarithm with precisely these weights and (6) yield

\[
\begin{aligned}
\overline H
&=\log L-\frac1{NL}\sum_{\mathbf k}\sum_{i:k_i>0}k_i\log k_i\\
&\ge\log L-
\log\left(\frac1{NL}\sum_{\mathbf k}\sum_i k_i^2\right)\\
&=\log L-\log\left(1+\frac{2(L-1)}{m+1}\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]

Thus the integer entropy deficit satisfies

\[
0\le\log\min\{m,L\}-\overline H
\le\log\left(
\frac{\min\{m,L\}(m+2L-1)}{L(m+1)}
\right)
\le\log3.
\tag{8}
\]

To check the final bound without asymptotics, if \(L\le m\), the fraction is \((m+2L-1)/(m+1)\le(3m-1)/(m+1)<3\). If \(L\ge m\), it is \((m/(m+1))(2+(m-1)/L)\le(3m-1)/(m+1)<3\). The initial nonnegativity in (8) follows from the sitewise entropy bound already proved. These estimates include \(L=1\), where every site entropy and the deficit are zero.

Set \(\varphi_0=1+\delta\log\min\{m,L\}\), the constant value of \(\varphi\), and \(a_0=\log3/\log2\). By (5), \(d\delta\log3\le a_0\varepsilon<1\). Since \(\varphi_0\ge1\), (8) gives \(N^{-1}\sum_{\mathbf z\in D_+}r(\mathbf z)\ge\varphi_0-\delta\log3\ge\varphi_0(1-\delta\log3)>0\). Convexity of \(u\mapsto u^d\) for \(u>0\), followed by Bernoulli's inequality \((1-u)^d\ge1-du\) for \(0\le u\le1\), therefore proves

\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac WN
&\ge\left(\frac1N\sum_{\mathbf z\in D_+}r(\mathbf z)\right)^d\\
&\ge\varphi_0^d(1-\delta\log3)^d\\
&\ge\varphi_0^d(1-d\delta\log3)\\
&\ge\varphi_0^d(1-a_0\varepsilon).
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]

All sums retain their original unit integer-site multiplicity. Since \(\sum_{\mathbf z}\varphi(\mathbf z)^d=N\varphi_0^d\), rearranging (9) and subtracting \(W\) proves the first bound in (2). For \(\varepsilon\le1/10\), the exact inequality \(3^3=27<32=2^5\) gives \(a_0<5/3\), and hence \(a_0/(1-a_0\varepsilon)\le a_0/(1-a_0/10)<(5/3)/(1-1/6)=2\). This proves the second bound in (2), also when \(\varepsilon=0\) or \(\delta=0\).

The constant majorant is admissible even when \(L<m\). In that case its height is \(1+\delta\log L\); for \(\delta>0\), the real barycenter has height \(1+\delta\log m\), which is larger. No step required real-profile domination.

For the stated convex-body realization, \(r\) is continuous, positive, and concave on the compact simplex. Thus \(C\) is compact. For \(\mathbf y_i\in r(\mathbf x_i)B\) and \(0\le t\le1\), convexity of \(B\) gives \((1-t)\mathbf y_0+t\mathbf y_1\in((1-t)r(\mathbf x_0)+t r(\mathbf x_1))B\). Concavity of \(r\) and \(\mathbf0\in B\) place this set inside \(r((1-t)\mathbf x_0+t\mathbf x_1)B\), proving convexity of \(C\). It contains \(\operatorname{int}(P_L)\times\operatorname{int}(B)\), because \(r\ge1\), so it has nonempty ambient interior. Its fibers have volume \(r(\mathbf x)^d\) on \(P_L\) and are empty outside \(P_L\); thus its positive integer support and its original mixed-integer volume are exactly \(D_+\) and \(W\).

Finally, for any convex body with the asserted integer support and volumes, \(\varphi\) is positive and convex on \(\operatorname{conv}(D_+)\) and satisfies \(\varphi(\mathbf z)^d\ge w_{\mathbf z}\) at every site. Accepted fact \(\texttt{0bcc51d8cd216cf3}\), its aggregate-majorant conclusion (3), applies with aggregate error parameter \(2\varepsilon\) from (2). Its selected point is an actual positive-fiber centroid, fixed before the containing halfspace. It therefore gives exactly (3) of this statement, with the same original volumes, unit multiplicities, and locked halfspace depth.
