## Statement

Let $P=\operatorname{conv}(P)\cap\mathbb Z^n$ be nonempty and finite. Let $K_z=B_z\times[-H_z,H_z]$, $H_z>0$, be the actual sampled fibers of a canonical convex hull, where each $B_z$ is a positive $(d-1)$-dimensional compact convex body. For $d=1$, use a singleton of measure one. Write $S=\bigcup_{z\in P}(\{z\}\times K_z)$ for the placed sampled fibers, $\mu$ for their summed original mixed $d$-volume, $M=\mu(S)$ for its mass, and $\eta$ for summed roof area at the points $s=(\xi,H)=(z,X,H_z)$ with $X\in B_z$.

Let $\Pi(ds,ds')=\nu(ds)P_s(ds')$ be any finite martingale coupling from a measure $\nu$ on this same roof to $\eta$. Let $t$ have the uniform probability law $dt/2$ on $[-1,1]$. Under the joint measure $d\Omega=2H'\,d\Pi\,dt/2$ define $w=(\xi,tH)$ and $w'=(\xi',tH')$. Its target is exactly $\mu$ and its source, denoted $\mu_\nu$, is the column lift of $2H\nu$ by the uniform law of $t$. Both masses equal $M$. If $d\nu=f\,d\eta$, then $d\mu_\nu(w)=f(z,X,H_z)\,d\mu(w)$.

The physical conditional drift of $w'$ from $w$ is
$$
\left(\frac{\operatorname{Cov}_{P_s}(\xi',H')}{H},
      \frac{t\,\operatorname{Var}_{P_s}(H')}{H}\right).
$$
The lift is a physical martingale exactly when $H'=H$ $\Pi$-almost everywhere, equivalently when $\int H^2\,d(\eta-\nu)=0$. The target-minus-source first moment of $\xi$ is $2\int\operatorname{Cov}_{P_s}(\xi',H')\,d\nu(s)$ and both vertical first moments vanish. In the projective coordinates $(\xi/H,y/H,1/H)$ the lift is always a martingale. The resulting convex order in those coordinates also follows directly by a perspective test on the roof.

## Proof

The conditional mean under $P_s$ is $E_s(\xi',H')=(\xi,H)$. On a target column $2H'\,dt/2$ is Lebesgue measure on $[-H',H']$. Integrating out the target before pushing forward the source gives $2E_sH'=2H$. This yields the asserted original marginals and masses by Fubini. It also yields the density formula by comparison with the column lift of $2H\eta$.

The input point $w$ determines $s$ and $t$ since the height is positive and fixed at its base. Its conditional target roof row is $P_s^H(ds')=(H'/H)P_s(ds')$. Thus $E_s^H\xi'=\xi+\operatorname{Cov}_{P_s}(\xi',H')/H$ and $E_s^H(tH')=tH+t\operatorname{Var}_{P_s}(H')/H$. If this drift vanishes, the second component for almost every $t\ne0$ forces zero row height variance. Conversely row height constancy makes $P_s^H=P_s$ and the full drift is zero. Expanding the conditional variance and using $E_sH'=H$ gives $\int H^2\,d(\eta-\nu)=\int\operatorname{Var}_{P_s}(H')\,d\nu(s)$. Integrating the physical horizontal drift against $2H\nu$ gives its first moment formula. The mean of $t$ is zero on both marginals.

Under $P_s^H$ the factor $H'$ cancels against the denominators in $\xi'/H'$ and $1/H'$, giving conditional means $\xi/H$ and $1/H$. The normalized vertical coordinate is the common $t$. The projective map is injective since it includes $1/H$, so the conditional mean conclusion is a martingale in these coordinates. For a convex test $\varphi$ on the projective coordinates, the roof function $(\xi,H)\mapsto 2H\varphi(\xi/H,t,1/H)$ is convex for fixed $t$ by the perspective construction applied to the affine numerator $(\xi,tH,1)$. Applying the roof order and then integrating in $t$ yields the same projective convex order.
