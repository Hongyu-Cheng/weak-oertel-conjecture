## Statement

Let $T$ be a regular $4$-simplex with facets $F_i=\lbrace \lambda_i=0\rbrace $, and let $A_i=F_i\cap\lbrace \lambda_2+\lambda_3+\lambda_4\le\delta\rbrace $ for $0<\delta<1/10$. Integrate with normalized facet area. For every affine $\ell$ of mean $s>0$ on $F_i$,
$$
\int_{F_i}\ell_-+\int_{A_i}\ell_+\ge 2^{-23}\delta^4s.
$$
If $\phi\ge0$ is continuous convex on $F_i$ and $\ell$ supports $\phi$ at its centroid, then
$$
\int_{F_i}(\phi-\ell)+\int_{A_i}\phi\ge2^{-24}\delta^4\int_{F_i}\phi.
$$

## Proof

Choose a high vertex $v_j$ in $F_i$, and denote the facet centroid by $g_i$. The subsimplex
$$
E_i=(1-\delta/2)v_j+(\delta/2)(g_i/2+F_i/2)
$$
has area fraction $(\delta/4)^3=\delta^3/64$, lies in $A_i$ since its low barycentric sum is at most $\delta/2$, and has each of its four facet coordinates at least $\delta/16$.

Normalize the mean of $\ell$ to one. Its four vertex values $c_k$ sum to four. Set $\eta=\max(0,-\min_k c_k)$ and $M=\max_k c_k\le4+3\eta$. If $\eta\le\delta/8$, then on $E_i$,
$$
\ell=\sum_{k\ne i}(c_k+\eta)\lambda_k-\eta\ge(\delta/16)(4+4\eta)-\eta\ge\delta/8.
$$
Thus the integral over $A_i$ is at least $\delta^4/512$. If $\eta>\delta/8$, the homothetic corner about a vertex of value $-\eta$, of linear scale $\eta/[2(M+\eta)]$, has $\ell\le-\eta/2$ and yields
$$
\int_{F_i}\ell_-\ge\frac{\eta^4}{16(M+\eta)^3}\ge\frac{\eta^4}{1024(1+\eta)^3}\ge 2^{-23}\delta^4.
$$
Indeed the last function of $\eta$ is increasing, and at $\eta=\delta/8$ its value is $\delta^4/[2^{22}(1+\delta/8)^3]$, with $(1+\delta/8)^3\le(9/8)^3<2$. Restore the affine mean. For the second assertion put $I=\int_{F_i}\phi$ and $\varepsilon=\int_{F_i}(\phi-\ell)$. One has $\phi\ge\ell_+$ and $\phi-\ell\ge\ell_-$, and the affine mean is $I-\varepsilon=\phi(g_i)\ge0$. If that mean vanishes the claim is immediate. Otherwise $\varepsilon+\int_{A_i}\phi\ge2^{-23}\delta^4(I-\varepsilon)$. Since $\varepsilon\le\varepsilon+\int_{A_i}\phi$ and $2^{-23}\delta^4\le1$, this yields the stated second inequality.
