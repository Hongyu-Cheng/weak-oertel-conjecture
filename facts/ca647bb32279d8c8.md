## Statement

For $0<\epsilon\le1$, let $P=\{0,1,2\}\subset\mathbb Z$, $T=\operatorname{conv}\{(-1,-1),(2,-1),(-1,2)\}$ and use the genuine product body $[0,2]\times T$. Keep fixed the three cylindrical caps $H_i=\{u_i\cdot x\ge2-\epsilon\}$ with $(u_1,u_2,u_3)=(e_1,e_2,-e_1-e_2)$. The total mixed area is $M=27/2$, each cap has mixed area $Q=3\epsilon^2/2$ and $Q/M=\epsilon^2/9<2/9$. Among actual compatible positive triangle fibers on $P$ with this fixed outward normal fan and these three common upper budgets, the product has the unique maximizing sampled family.

The two-sided actual radius path $(1-s,1,1+s)$ with zero centers has zero first mixed area and cap area derivatives within each parity class. Near $s=0$ it has $M(s)=27/2+9s^2$ and $C_i(s)=Q+4s^2$ for each cap. Its volume Hessian is $18$, and the Lagrangian Hessian with objective coefficient one and every cap multiplier $\lambda=3/(2\epsilon)$ is $18-36/\epsilon<0$. These multipliers give first-order stationarity on the full fixed-fan chart. All the cap margins miss the fiber centroids, so this cap menu fails to cover the product.

## Proof

Every triangle of the fixed normal fan is $c+rT$ with $r>0$. Indeed, in inequalities $v_i\cdot x\le b_i$ for the outward normals $v_i=-u_i$, solve $b_i=v_i\cdot c+r$. The sum fixes $r$ because $\sum_i v_i=0$, the rank-two normals determine $c$, and positivity of the area forces $r>0$. The reference product has exactly the three displayed integer fibers. Each has area $9/2<M/2$ and cap area $\epsilon^2/2$ by the proper cut formula in pk-w29-sharp-aligned-triangle-fixed-budget-bound. For any competitor apply the sharp inequality from that lemma to each of its three fibers and sum. If the total cap areas are $C_i'$, then $M'\le\lambda\sum_i C_i'+3(9/4)(2-\epsilon)\le3\lambda Q+3(9/4)(2-\epsilon)=27/2$. Equality requires equality on each fiber in the sharp inequality, hence exactly $T$ at each site.

The convex hull along the radius path has projection $[0,2]$. The endpoints are exposed and the average of the endpoint bodies is exactly $T$, since Minkowski averages of positive homothets of a common convex set add their weighted radii. This ensures that the middle integer fiber is exactly $T$ as well. For small signed $s$ every cut remains proper. The cap areas on the two changed fibers are $(\epsilon-2s)^2/2$ and $(\epsilon+2s)^2/2$, with the middle cap area fixed. Their linear terms cancel within the even parity class and the odd fiber is constant. The total area uses the same parity cancellation in $\frac92[(1-s)^2+1+(1+s)^2]$. This gives the two asserted quadratic laws and therefore $M''-\lambda\sum_i C_i''=18-24\lambda=18-36/\epsilon$.

At the reference point of each single-fiber chart $(c,r)$, the full area derivative is $9\,dr$, whereas the sum of cap derivatives is $\epsilon\sum_i(2\,dr+u_i\cdot dc)=6\epsilon\,dr$. Thus these multipliers are stationary even before restricting to compatible directions. The global upper bound excludes any feasible acceleration of the displayed velocity that increases volume. Finally $2-\epsilon>0$, whereas every centroid is $x=0$, so the caps neither cover the original mixed set nor form a through-source critical circuit. The numerical comparison uses the old sharp target $\Gamma_2/2=2/9$ and can be arbitrarily strict as $\epsilon$ tends to zero.
