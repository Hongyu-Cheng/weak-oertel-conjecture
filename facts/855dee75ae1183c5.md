## Statement

Let $n\ge0$ and $d\ge1$ be integers, $P\subset\mathbb R^n$ nonempty and finite, $j\in P$, and $r_p>0$ for $p\in P$. Put $T_d=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_d)$, $\Delta_p=p-j$ and $\mathcal W_d=\{w\in\mathbb R^d:w\ge0,\ \sum_\ell w_\ell=1\}$. Assume a touching majorant $r_p\le r_j+b\cdot\Delta_p$ for some $b\in\mathbb R^n$. Let $G_j(y)$ be the raw depth of $(j,y)$ for the sum of ordinary $d$-volume on the fibers $\{p\}\times r_pT_d$. For every $y\in\mathbb R^d$ define
$$
P_j(y)=\inf_{a\in\mathbb R^n,\ w\in\mathcal W_d}\sum_{p\in P}
\left|r_pT_d\cap\{X:a\cdot\Delta_p+w\cdot X\ge w\cdot y\}\right|.
$$
If $y\in r_jT_d$ and $r_j-\sum_\ell y_\ell\le\min_i y_i$, then $G_j(y)=P_j(y)$. In particular, for $q=r_j/(d+1)$ and $q\le t\le r_j/d$, the equality holds at $y=t\mathbf1$, where $\mathbf1=(1,\ldots,1)$, and $G_j(t\mathbf1)$ is nonincreasing in $t$. If $0\le t\le q$ and $t'=r_j-dt$, then
$$
P_j(t'\mathbf1)\le G_j(t\mathbf1)\le P_j(t\mathbf1).
$$
In particular $G_j(t\mathbf1)\ge G_j(t'\mathbf1)$ for $r_j(d-1)/d^2\le t\le q$, when $t'\mathbf1$ lies in the own fiber.

## Proof

Tighten any containing halfspace to pass through $(j,y)$. Write its margin as $L(p,X)=a\cdot\Delta_p+u\cdot(X-y)$. A nonzero nonnegative $u$ is already in the restricted class after normalization. If $u=0$, use the cap with margin $R a\cdot\Delta_p+X_1-y_1$ for sufficiently large finite $R>0$. Finiteness of $P$ and boundedness of the fibers ensure that each strict base trace stays full or empty according to its sign. At a zero base trace the original closed cap contained the entire fiber, and the replacement takes a subset.

If $u_i=\min_\ell u_\ell<0$, use on fiber $p$ the affine involution $\sigma_{p,i}$ that exchanges coordinate $i$ with $r_p-\sum_\ell X_\ell$, preserving the other coordinates. It permutes simplex vertices and preserves volume. Write $s_i=\sigma_{j,i}y$ and $v=u-u_i\mathbf1$, with its $i$th coordinate replaced by $-u_i$. Thus $v\ge0$ and $v_i>0$. Direct substitution gives
$$
L(p,\sigma_{p,i}X)
=a\cdot\Delta_p+v\cdot(X-s_i)+u_i(r_p-r_j)
\ge (a+u_i b)\cdot\Delta_p+v\cdot(X-s_i).
$$
The right side is one ambient affine margin, with a shared tilt. Dividing by $\sum_\ell v_\ell$ puts it in the restricted class through $(j,s_i)$. Its mass on each fiber is at most that of the original cap. The three cases give
$$
\min\{P_j(y),P_j(s_1),\ldots,P_j(s_d)\}\le G_j(y)\le P_j(y).
$$
The upper bound follows from inclusion of the restricted class in the original class. The function $P_j$ is coordinatewise nonincreasing because all $w_\ell\ge0$. If $r_j-\sum_\ell y_\ell\le\min_i y_i$, then each $s_i\le y$ coordinatewise, so the lower bound equals $P_j(y)$.

At $t\mathbf1$ the swapped coordinate is $r_j-dt$. For $t\ge q$ it is at most $t$, which gives same-point equality and then monotonicity from $P_j$. For $t\le q$ we have $t\mathbf1\le s_i\le t'\mathbf1$, so every restricted quantity in the minimum is at least $P_j(t'\mathbf1)$. If $t\ge r_j(d-1)/d^2$, then $q\le t'\le r_j/d$, and same-point equality on the upper segment identifies this bound as $G_j(t'\mathbf1)$.
