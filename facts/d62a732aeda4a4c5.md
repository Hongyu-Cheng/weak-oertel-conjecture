## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $T=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_d)$, $0<e\le1/(64d^4)$, $A_e=T\cap\{\sum_jx_j\ge e\}$, and $B=tT$ for $t>0$. The unique minimum-volume enclosing simplices of $A_e$ and $A_e+B$ are $T$ and $(1+t)T$, respectively. Their missing volumes are both $e^d|T|$. For all $a,b>0$,
$$
|aA_e+bB|=[(a+bt)^d-(ae)^d]|T|.
$$
In particular every cross mixed-volume defect between the two enclosures and the two summands vanishes, and the factor one in the parallel simplex transfer inequality is attained by two positive weights.

## Proof

The infinity-norm Hausdorff distance from $A_e$ to $T$ is $e/d$. Indeed a point of coordinate sum $s<e$ reaches $A_e$ by adding $(e-s)/d$ to every coordinate, and the origin is at distance at least $e/d$. For any minimizing enclosing simplex, e823c82617a0bc70 applies with $\delta=e/d\le1/(64d^3)$. After labeling, each corresponding vertex and facet centroid is within $8d^3\delta=8d^2e\le1/(8d^2)$ of its counterpart in $T$. Each of the minimizing simplex's facet centroids lies in $A_e$.

At the centroid of old facet $F_i$, all barycentric coordinates other than $\lambda_i$ equal $1/d$. The change in any barycentric coordinate at distance at most $1/(8d^2)$ is at most $1/(8d)$, since $\lambda_0=1-\sum_jx_j$ and the other coordinates are $x_j$. Thus at the matched centroid $p$ all the other coordinates are at least $7/(8d)$. The coordinate sum at $p$ is at least $(d-1)/d-1/(8d)>e$. The matched centroid lies on $\partial A_e$, since it lies in $A_e$ on a supporting plane of an enclosure, and every defining inequality of $A_e$ except the old $i$th facet inequality is strict there. Therefore $p$ lies in the relative interior of $F_i$ and its unique supporting hyperplane is the plane of $F_i$. Uniqueness follows by testing both directions tangent to a small relative disk in that facet and one point strictly inward. The matching holds for all facets, so the minimizing simplex is $T$.

For any positive $a,b$, the set $aA_e+bB$ is exactly $\{x:x_j\ge0,\ ae\le\sum_jx_j\le a+bt\}$. One inclusion follows by adding the inequalities. For the other, let $s=\sum_jx_j$ and choose $r\in[ae,a]\cap[s-bt,s]$, which is nonempty for the stated range. Then $(r/s)x\in aA_e$ and $(1-r/s)x\in bB$. The volume follows by subtracting the smaller simplex, and extends by continuity to zero weights. For $a=b=1$, the sum divided by $1+t$ is $A_{e/(1+t)}$, so its unique minimizing enclosure is $(1+t)T$. The parallel enclosures of the summands are $T,tT$, as follows by their coordinate minima and maximum coordinate sum. Both the defect of the sum and the only nonzero pure summand defect are $e^d|T|$. The volume polynomial differs from that of the parallel simplices only by $(ae)^d|T|$, giving the vanishing of cross coefficients. Equality for the unit weights gives equality for $1/2,1/2$ by common scaling.
