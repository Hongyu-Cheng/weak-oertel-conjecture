## Statement

There is a nonnegative concave symmetric sequence with peak $R=1$ for which the linear-power bound $Q_1(c)\ge(1-c)^2Q_1(0)$ fails both at a knot and away from the knots, where $Q_1(c)=\sum_i(r_i-c)_+$.

## Proof

Take $(r_{-1},r_0,r_1)=(1/3,1,1/3)$. It is concave, since its two successive differences are $2/3$ and $-2/3$, and $Q_1(0)=5/3$. At $c=1/3$ the two sides are $18/27$ and $20/27$, so the deficit is $2/27$. At $c=1/4$ the two sides are $44/48$ and $45/48$, so the deficit is $1/48$.
