## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and write
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 c_z:=\frac1{a_z}\int_{C_z}x\,dx
\tag{1}
\]
whenever \(a_z>0\).  Let \(\mu_C\) be the mixed-volume measure, put
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\tag{2}
\]
and retain one common canonical zero-fresh hard instance of accepted facts
`6bfe50fed9a5540b` and `ffbb2163fbf06107`.  In particular,
\[
 D<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{3}
\]

Retain the half-strength reservoir \(r=(r_e)\), the ancestry matching
\(b=(b_e)\leq r\), and their degrees
\[
 Q:=\sum_er_e>0,
 \qquad
 \delta_z:=d_z(r)\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 d_z(b)\leq\delta_z.
\tag{4}
\]
For \(e=\{u,v\}\), put \(m_e:=(u+v)/2\), and retain
\[
 \lambda_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}b_e
 \leq\frac{a_m}{2}.
\tag{5}
\]

Let \(\mathcal L^\square\) be the canonical degree-weighted complete-product square-label family.  Every
\(\sigma\in\mathcal L^\square\) has mass \(\theta_\sigma>0\), physical target \(t(\sigma)\), and a four-entry tagged occurrence multiset
\[
 \mathcal O_\sigma=[y_{\sigma,1},y_{\sigma,2},y_{\sigma,3},y_{\sigma,4}],
 \qquad
 \frac14\sum_{o=1}^4y_{\sigma,o}=t(\sigma),
\tag{6}
\]
where loops and repeated occurrences remain separate tags.  Its occurrence loads satisfy
\[
 R_z:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal L^\square}
 \theta_\sigma\operatorname{mult}_z(\mathcal O_\sigma)
 \leq2d_z\leq a_z.
\tag{7}
\]

Retain the zero-synchronization objects of `ffbb2163fbf06107`: the blocker set \(\mathcal B\), the ancestry-parent set \(\mathcal P\), and the whole-label subfamily
\[
 \Gamma:=\mathcal F_{\mathcal P}
 =\{\sigma\in\mathcal L^\square:
 \mathcal O_\sigma\text{ has an occurrence in }\mathcal P\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Every label in \(\Gamma\) has at least one blocker occurrence in \(\mathcal B\), and its mass obeys
\[
 \Theta_{\mathcal P}:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma
 >\frac{1143}{12800}Q.
\tag{9}
\]

At every \(m\in\mathcal B\), retain the simultaneously packed ancestry-target density and its physical complement from `ffbb2163fbf06107`:
\[
 0\leq H_m\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_m}H_m=\lambda_m,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_m}xH_m(x)\,dx
 =\frac12\sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e(c_u+c_v),
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 V_m:=a_m-\lambda_m\geq\lambda_m>0,
 \qquad
 q_m:=\frac1{V_m}\int_{C_m}x(1-H_m(x))\,dx.
\tag{11}
\]

Choose, once and for all, one tagged blocker occurrence
\(o_{\mathcal B}(\sigma)\in\{1,2,3,4\}\) for every \(\sigma\in\Gamma\).  Put
\[
 \ell_m:=
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma:\
 y_{\sigma,o_{\mathcal B}(\sigma)}=m}}
 \theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \mathcal B^+:=\{m\in\mathcal B:\ell_m>0\},
 \qquad
 \alpha_m:=\frac{\ell_m}{4a_m}
 \quad(m\in\mathcal B^+).
\tag{12}
\]
Then
\[
 \sum_{m\in\mathcal B^+}\ell_m=\Theta_{\mathcal P},
 \qquad
 0<\alpha_m\leq\frac14.
\tag{13}
\]

Use lifted points
\[
 \widehat c_z:=(z,c_z),
 \qquad
 \widehat q_m:=(m,q_m),
\tag{14}
\]
and define the centroid square output
\[
 r_\sigma^c:=\frac14\sum_{o=1}^4c_{y_{\sigma,o}},
 \qquad
 \widehat r_\sigma^c:=(t(\sigma),r_\sigma^c).
\tag{15}
\]
Define three positive finite atomic measures by
\[
 \Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}
 :=\frac14\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma
 \sum_{o=1}^4\delta_{\widehat c_{y_{\sigma,o}}},
\tag{16}
\]
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}
 :=&\frac14\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma
 \sum_{\substack{1\leq o\leq4\\o\ne o_{\mathcal B}(\sigma)}}
 \delta_{\widehat c_{y_{\sigma,o}}}\\
 &+\sum_{m\in\mathcal B^+}\alpha_mV_m\delta_{\widehat q_m}\\
 &+\frac12\sum_{m\in\mathcal B^+}\alpha_m
 \sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e
 \left(\delta_{\widehat c_u}+\delta_{\widehat c_v}\right),
 \end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]
and
\[
 \Xi_\square
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma
 \delta_{\widehat r_\sigma^c}.
\tag{18}
\]
They have exactly the same mass and full first moment:
\[
 \boxed{
 |\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}|
 =|\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}|
 =|\Xi_\square|
 =\Theta_{\mathcal P},}
\tag{19}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 \int p\,d\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}(p)
 =\int p\,d\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}(p)
 =\int p\,d\Xi_\square(p).}
\tag{20}
\]

More precisely, let
\[
 \Xi_{\mathrm{blk}}
 :=\frac14\sum_{m\in\mathcal B^+}
 \ell_m\delta_{\widehat c_m},
\tag{21}
\]
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Xi_{\mathrm{cp}}
 :=&\sum_{m\in\mathcal B^+}\alpha_mV_m\delta_{\widehat q_m}\\
 &+\frac12\sum_{m\in\mathcal B^+}\alpha_m
 \sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e
 \left(\delta_{\widehat c_u}+\delta_{\widehat c_v}\right).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{22}
\]
Then \(\Xi_{\mathrm{blk}}\leq\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}\),
\(\Xi_{\mathrm{cp}}\leq\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\), and
\[
 \boxed{
 |\Xi_{\mathrm{blk}}|
 =|\Xi_{\mathrm{cp}}|
 =\frac{\Theta_{\mathcal P}}4,
 \qquad
 \int p\,d\Xi_{\mathrm{blk}}(p)
 =\int p\,d\Xi_{\mathrm{cp}}(p).}
\tag{23}
\]

There is one physical source-fiber density ledger representing every packet in
\(\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}+\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\), with no tagged occurrence, complement density, or ancestry incidence used more than its displayed coefficient, and with total pointwise density at every physical source bounded by
\[
 \boxed{\frac{17}{32}<1.}
\tag{24}
\]
Consequently accepted fact `b3fce494e3b37130`, applied to the complete packet family in each physical source fiber, gives
\[
 \boxed{
 \nu_{\mathrm{src}}
 :=\frac1{\mathrm e}
 \left(\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}+\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\right)
 \preceq_{\mathrm{hs}}\mu_C.}
\tag{25}
\]
Both summands
\[
 \nu_{\mathrm{occ}}:=\frac1{\mathrm e}\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}},
 \qquad
 \nu_{\mathrm{rep}}:=\frac1{\mathrm e}\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}
\tag{26}
\]
are therefore halfspace-dominated as well.  The blocker-complement-parent restriction
\[
 \boxed{
 \nu_{\mathrm{cp}}
 :=\frac1{\mathrm e}
 \left(\Xi_{\mathrm{blk}}+\Xi_{\mathrm{cp}}\right)
 \preceq_{\mathrm{hs}}\mu_C}
\tag{27}
\]
uses one jointly feasible subledger of (24).

The centroid choice in accepted fact `d5d5de35c3f25af9` also gives the collision-safe physical square-output measure
\[
 \boxed{
 \nu_\square:=\frac1{\mathrm e}\Xi_\square
 \preceq_{\mathrm{hs}}\mu_C.}
\tag{28}
\]
Thus \(\nu_{\mathrm{occ}}\), \(\nu_{\mathrm{rep}}\), and
\(\nu_\square\) have equal mass and equal full first moment, while the first two coexist in the one source ledger (24).  The only ancestry-target density used is the already simultaneous family \(H_m\) and its literal complement \(1-H_m\); all target collisions are summed before the complement is taken.

The masses have the explicit dimension-free lower bounds
\[
 \boxed{
 |\nu_{\mathrm{occ}}|
 =|\nu_{\mathrm{rep}}|
 =|\nu_\square|
 =\frac{\Theta_{\mathcal P}}{\mathrm e}
 >\frac{1143}{35200}Q
 >\frac Q{100},}
\tag{29}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 |\nu_{\mathrm{src}}|
 =\frac{2\Theta_{\mathcal P}}{\mathrm e}
 >\frac{1143}{17600}Q,}
\tag{30}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 |\nu_{\mathrm{cp}}|
 =\frac{\Theta_{\mathcal P}}{2\mathrm e}
 >\frac{1143}{70400}Q
 >\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{31}
\]

Define the actual coupled blocker-parent base set
\[
 \mathcal A^+
 :=\mathcal B^+
 \cup
 \{u,v:e=\{u,v\},\ b_e>0,\ m_e\in\mathcal B^+\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{cp}}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(\mathcal A^+),
\tag{32}
\]
and its coupled residual rank
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 r_{\mathrm{cp}}:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(\mathcal A^+)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\Bigl(
 &\{c_z-s(z):z\in\mathcal A^+\}\\
 &\cup\{q_m-s(m):m\in\mathcal B^+\}
 \Bigr).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{33}
\]
This is exactly the intrinsic residual rank of \(\nu_{\mathrm{cp}}\).  The parent-child identity gives the sharper exact coupling
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{cp}}
 =
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(\mathcal A^+)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{c_z-s(z):z\in\mathcal A^+\}.}
\tag{34}
\]
In particular, the complement-only residual rank on \(\mathcal B^+\) is at most \(r_{\mathrm{cp}}\), while (34) shows that the same one-dimensional denominator is controlled by the joint blocker-and-parent centroid rank, not by an abstract label hypergraph rank.

Accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` applied to (27) gives the terminal capacity bound
\[
 \boxed{
 D
 \geq
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{cp}}}
 \frac{\Theta_{\mathcal P}}
 {2\mathrm e(r_{\mathrm{cp}}+1)}.}
\tag{35}
\]
Consequently, either the right side of (35) is greater than \(Q/100\), or the canonical hard branch (3) forces the coupled normalized-rank inequality
\[
 \boxed{
 2^{\rho_{\mathrm{cp}}}(r_{\mathrm{cp}}+1)
 >\frac{50\Theta_{\mathcal P}}{\mathrm e Q}\,2^n
 >\frac{1143}{704}\,2^n.}
\tag{36}
\]

For completeness, let \(\rho_{\mathrm{src}},r_{\mathrm{src}}\) be the intrinsic ranks of the full jointly feasible source cloud \(\nu_{\mathrm{src}}\).  Its stronger mass gives
\[
 \boxed{
 D
 \geq
 2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}}
 \frac{2\Theta_{\mathcal P}}
 {\mathrm e(r_{\mathrm{src}}+1)},}
\tag{37}
\]
and hence, in the hard branch,
\[
 \boxed{
 2^{\rho_{\mathrm{src}}}(r_{\mathrm{src}}+1)
 >\frac{200\Theta_{\mathcal P}}{\mathrm e Q}\,2^n
 >\frac{1143}{176}\,2^n.}
\tag{38}
\]
No assertion is made that (36) or (38) is already absorbed in every dimension, or that the square-output packets are jointly packable at their physical targets.

## Proof

For every label \(\sigma\in\Gamma\), choose one blocker tag as in (12).  Since it is one of the tagged occurrences counted by \(R_m\),
\[
 0\leq\ell_m
 \leq
 R_m^\Gamma
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}
 \theta_\sigma\operatorname{mult}_m(\mathcal O_\sigma)
 \leq R_m\leq a_m.
\tag{39}
\]
Every label is chosen once, so summing \(\ell_m\) proves the first identity in (13); (39) proves \(\alpha_m\leq1/4\).  All involved fibers have positive volume because every blocker occurrence has \(d_m>0\).

The full lifted parent-child identity follows from (10)-(11).  Its continuous coordinate is
\[
 V_mq_m
 =a_mc_m-\frac12
 \sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e(c_u+c_v).
\tag{40}
\]
Its base coordinate holds because \(u+v=2m\) and
\(\sum_{e:m_e=m}b_e=\lambda_m\):
\[
 V_mm
 =a_mm-\frac12
 \sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e(u+v).
\tag{41}
\]
Together,
\[
 \boxed{
 a_m\widehat c_m
 =V_m\widehat q_m
 +\frac12\sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e
 (\widehat c_u+\widehat c_v).}
\tag{42}
\]
Multiplying (42) by \(\alpha_m\), and using
\(\alpha_ma_m=\ell_m/4\), proves exactly that (22) replaces the selected blocker part (21) without changing mass or full first moment.  This proves (23).

Every label has four tagged occurrences, even when two or more tags have the same physical source.  Therefore (16) has mass
\(\Theta_{\mathcal P}\), while its first moment is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \int p\,d\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}(p)
 &=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma
 \frac14\sum_{o=1}^4\widehat c_{y_{\sigma,o}}\\
 &=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma
 \widehat r_\sigma^c
 =\int p\,d\Xi_\square(p).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{43}
\]
The first equality counts repeated tags with their multiplicity, and the second uses both identities in (6) and (15).  Removing exactly one tagged term from every label leaves mass
\(3\Theta_{\mathcal P}/4\) in the first line of (17).  Equation (23) puts back mass \(\Theta_{\mathcal P}/4\) with the same full first moment as all removed terms.  This proves (19)-(20).

We now give the one physical source ledger.  For every tagged occurrence of \(\sigma\in\Gamma\) at \(y\), represent its packet in \(\Xi_{\mathrm{occ}}\) by
\[
 f^{(0)}_{\sigma,o}
 :=\frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y}.
\tag{44}
\]
For every nonchosen tag in \(\Xi_{\mathrm{rep}}\), use a second packet
\[
 f^{(1)}_{\sigma,o}
 :=\frac{\theta_\sigma}{4a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y}.
\tag{45}
\]
These packets have mass \(\theta_\sigma/4\) and barycenter \(c_y\).  Repeated occurrences remain separate functions and are included separately in the following sum.

At a chosen blocker \(m\in\mathcal B^+\), represent its complement packet by
\[
 g_m^{\mathrm{comp}}
 :=\alpha_m(1-H_m).
\tag{46}
\]
It has mass \(\alpha_mV_m\) and barycenter \(q_m\).  For every positive ancestry incidence \(z\in e=\{u,v\}\) with
\(m_e=m\in\mathcal B^+\), represent its parent-centroid packet by
\[
 g_{m,e,z}^{\mathrm{par}}
 :=\frac{\alpha_mb_e}{2a_z}\mathbf1_{C_z}.
\tag{47}
\]
It has mass \(\alpha_mb_e/2\) and barycenter \(c_z\).

Fix a physical source \(y\).  Extend \(\ell_y:=0\) outside
\(\mathcal B^+\).  Equations (44)-(45) contribute the constant density
\[
 \frac{2R_y^\Gamma-\ell_y}{4a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y}.
\tag{48}
\]
If \(y\in\mathcal B^+\), equation (46) is pointwise at most
\[
 \alpha_y\mathbf1_{C_y}
 =\frac{\ell_y}{4a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y};
\tag{49}
\]
otherwise it contributes zero.  Hence (48) plus every complement packet is pointwise at most
\[
 \frac{R_y^\Gamma}{2a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y}
 \leq\frac12\mathbf1_{C_y},
\tag{50}
\]
where (39) and (7) were used.

For the parent packets, (13), (4), and (47) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{\substack{m\in\mathcal B^+,\ e\ni y\\m_e=m}}
 g_{m,e,y}^{\mathrm{par}}
 &\leq
 \frac1{a_y}\frac12\frac14
 \sum_{e\ni y}b_e\,\mathbf1_{C_y}\\
 &=\frac{d_y(b)}{8a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y}
 \leq\frac1{32}\mathbf1_{C_y}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{51}
\]
Equations (50)-(51) prove the pointwise bound \(17/32\) in (24).  Thus all displayed packets coexist in one actual physical source ledger.  This is stronger than checking their scalar masses, and it remains valid when one source plays several roles, when several ancestry edges collide, and when a square label has repeated tags.

Apply accepted fact `b3fce494e3b37130` to the entire jointly feasible finite packet family in each positive-volume fiber \(C_y\).  It gives
\(1/\mathrm e\) times every packet atom as one halfspace-dominated fiber measure.  For an ambient closed halfspace, apply that conclusion to its fiber section and sum over the distinct integer bases.  Whole-fiber sections are also valid because the packet masses have sum at most \(a_y\).  This proves (25).  Equations (26)-(27) are positive submeasures of (25), proving their domination without a second ledger.

For every square source with \(d_y>0\), the constant density
\[
 \frac{2d_y}{a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y}
\tag{52}
\]
is admissible and has barycenter \(c_y\), by (7).  Hence the simultaneous centroid choice is legal in accepted fact `d5d5de35c3f25af9`.  That fact gives
\[
 \gamma_d\Xi_\square\preceq_{\mathrm{hs}}\mu_C.
\tag{53}
\]
The elementary inequality
\[
 \left(1+\frac1d\right)^d<\mathrm e
\]
gives \(\gamma_d>1/\mathrm e\).  Scaling (53) proves (28).  Equations (19)-(20) then give the asserted equal masses and full first moments for (26) and (28).  Notice that no targetwise joint packing of the square outputs has been inferred.  The target density in (10) is already summed over all ancestry edges with target \(m\), and (46) uses only its single nonnegative complement.

For the numerical bounds, the exponential series gives
\[
 \mathrm e
 <1+1+\frac12+\frac16
 +\frac1{24}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}4^{-j}
 =\frac{49}{18}<\frac{11}{4},
\tag{54}
\]
so \(1/\mathrm e>4/11\).  Combining this with (9), (19), and (23) gives
\[
 \frac{\Theta_{\mathcal P}}{\mathrm e}
 >\frac{1143}{12800}\frac4{11}Q
 =\frac{1143}{35200}Q,
\tag{55}
\]
and the remaining bounds in (29)-(31) follow by multiplication by two or one half.  The final comparisons with \(Q/100\) are strict because
\(1143>352\) and \(1143>704\).

We verify the coupled rank identity.  Fix any affine map
\(s:\operatorname{aff}(\mathcal A^+)\to\mathbb R^d\).  Subtract
\(a_ms(m)\) from the continuous coordinate of (42).  Affineness and
\(m=(u+v)/2\) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 V_m(q_m-s(m))
 &=a_m(c_m-s(m))\\
 &\quad-\frac12\sum_{e=\{u,v\}:m_e=m}b_e
 \bigl((c_u-s(u))+(c_v-s(v))\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{56}
\]
Thus every complement residual in (33) belongs to the span of the blocker and ancestry-parent centroid residuals.  Conversely, every centroid residual displayed on the right side of (34) is already in the support residual family in (33), with a positive coefficient in either (21) or (22).  The two spans are therefore equal for every affine \(s\); minimizing proves (34).  Restricting an attaining map to \(\mathcal B^+\) also proves the complement-only rank comparison stated after (34).

The measure \(\nu_{\mathrm{cp}}\) is nonzero, has base rank
\(\rho_{\mathrm{cp}}\), residual rank \(r_{\mathrm{cp}}\), mass
\(\Theta_{\mathcal P}/(2\mathrm e)\), and is halfspace-dominated by (27).  Accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483` therefore gives
\[
 h_{\max}(C)
 \geq
 \frac{\Theta_{\mathcal P}}
 {2\mathrm e\,2^{\rho_{\mathrm{cp}}}(r_{\mathrm{cp}}+1)}.
\tag{57}
\]
Multiplying by \(2^n\) proves (35).  If its right side exceeds \(Q/100\), this is the displayed terminal alternative.  Otherwise (3), (9), and (54) imply
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 2^{\rho_{\mathrm{cp}}}(r_{\mathrm{cp}}+1)
 &>\frac{50\Theta_{\mathcal P}}{\mathrm e Q}2^n\\
 &>50\frac{1143}{35200}2^n
 =\frac{1143}{704}2^n,
 \end{aligned}
\]
which proves (36).

Finally, \(\nu_{\mathrm{src}}\) has mass
\(2\Theta_{\mathcal P}/\mathrm e\) by (19) and is halfspace-dominated by (25).  Applying the same dominated-measure theorem with its intrinsic ranks proves (37).  Comparing with (3), and again using (55), gives
\[
 2^{\rho_{\mathrm{src}}}(r_{\mathrm{src}}+1)
 >\frac{200\Theta_{\mathcal P}}{\mathrm e Q}2^n
 >\frac{1143}{176}2^n,
\]
which is (38).  This completes every asserted physical-ledger, equal-moment, domination, and coupled-rank conclusion.
