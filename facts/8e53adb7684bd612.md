## Statement

On a fixed finite lattice base let positive compatible fibers $K_z(t)\subseteq K_z$ satisfy the inward radial hypotheses of the finite-contact energy minimum. Use the fixed Euclidean buffered depth with parameter $\varepsilon>0$ and profile $f_t(z,X)=E_{K_z(t)}(X)^{1/(d+1)}$. A direction with continuous normal of unit length uses a through-mark threshold lowered by $\varepsilon f_t(z,X)$, and pure-base directions use the closed through-mark cap. Write $\mu_t$ for actual summed fiber volume. Fix a nonempty old assigned face set $J$ and a finite menu of caps with normals $N_j$ of unit continuous part and old thresholds $c_j$, each of mass $q>0$ at $t=0$ and proper on at least one old fiber. Let $\rho_j>0$ sum to one, $h_0=\sum_j\rho_j c_j$ and $\ell_z(X)=\sum_j\rho_j N_j\cdot(z,X)-h_0$. Assume $\ell_z-\varepsilon f_0$ is nonnegative on each $K_z$, $z\in J$, with unique interior zero $y_z$. Write $\alpha(t)$ for its current contact minimum with $f_t$ in place of $f_0$. Assume the actual joint tail expansions
$$
\mu_t\{N_j\cdot(Z,X)\ge c_j+s\}=q-S_j s-tL_j+o(t+|s|),\qquad S_j>0 .
$$
Then the current proper local quantiles $c_j(t,\tau)$ give a unique budget $\tau(t)$ near $q$ by $\sum_j\rho_j c_j(t,\tau(t))=h_0+\alpha(t)$. Every point of every current assigned fiber has buffered depth at most $\tau(t)$, and
$$
\tau'(0+)=-\sum_j\theta_jL_j-\alpha'(0+)/H,
\qquad H=\sum_j\rho_j/S_j,\qquad \theta_j=(\rho_j/S_j)/H .
$$
Here $\alpha'(0+)=(\varepsilon/(d+1))\min_{z\in J}E_{K_z}(y_z)^{-d/(d+1)}C_z(y_z)$ for the signed shell coefficient $C_z$ in the finite-contact energy minimum.

## Proof

The finite-contact energy minimum with $A=1$ gives $\alpha(t)=t\alpha'(0+)+o(t)$ and the stated coefficient. At every selected old proper section the plane crosses an interior ball, a smaller copy of which is retained by the moving fiber. This yields a common threshold neighborhood where the actual tails decrease strictly: every nontrivial slab there contains positive volume in that ball. They are continuous because the nonzero continuous normal makes all section planes fiber-volume null. Uniform convergence of the tails over thresholds follows from the vanishing summed symmetric differences of the fibers. In particular all local inverses exist in a common mass interval about $q$ and are strictly decreasing and continuous.

For any bounded $\eta$, substituting $s=-t(L_j+\eta)/S_j$ in the joint expansion gives mass $q+t\eta+o(t)$. Perturbing the proposed threshold by $\pm bt$ for any $b>0$ brackets the exact inverse, uniformly on bounded $\eta$ intervals, since $S_j>0$. Thus
$$
c_j(t,q+t\eta)=c_j-t(L_j+\eta)/S_j+o(t).
$$
The weighted sum equals $h_0-t(\sum_j\rho_j L_j/S_j+H\eta)+o(t)$. Opposite large fixed values of $\eta$ bracket $h_0+\alpha(t)$ for small $t>0$. The intermediate value theorem and strict monotonicity give the unique local root with $\tau(t)-q=O(t)$. Substituting $\eta=(\tau(t)-q)/t$ in the uniform expansion yields the derivative.

For every current $(z,X)$ with $z\in J$, the contact minimum and the exact scalar equation give
$$
\sum_j\rho_j[N_j\cdot(z,X)-\varepsilon f_t(z,X)-c_j(t,\tau(t))]\ge0 .
$$
A nonnegative summand gives a buffered through-mark cap contained in the actual quantile cap of mass $\tau(t)$, proving the asserted bound throughout the assigned current fibers.

One sufficient geometry for the assumed joint expansion is that each old cutting plane has a proper trace on at least one fiber and meets each old fiber boundary in surface measure zero, allowing strictly full or empty traces. Linear coarea and continuity of nearby section areas at such traces give $-S_js+o(|s|)$ for the threshold change on the old fibers. For the shape change at threshold $c_j+s$, the uniform radial bound and the shell formula from da49875a9737b172 apply with the cap indicator. Off the old cutting plane the bit is eventually constant throughout the shrinking shell as $(t,s)\to(0,0)$, so dominated convergence gives $-tL_j+o(t)$, with $L_j$ the captured inward surface integral. Positive-surface-measure coincidences are covered only if the actual joint expansion is separately supplied.
