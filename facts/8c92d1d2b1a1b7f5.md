## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be a convex body, and for \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\) put
\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
a_z:=\mathcal H_d(C_z),\qquad
W:=\sum_z a_z.
\]
Assume \(W>0\), let \(Z:=\{z:a_z>0\}\), let \(A_*:=\max_{z\in Z}a_z\), and put
\[
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\]
For every \(z\in Z\), define the half-mass metronoid
\[
\mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)
:=
\left\{
\frac{2}{a_z}\int_{C_z}x f(x)\,dx:
0\leq f\leq1\ \text{a.e.},\ \int_{C_z}f(x)\,dx=\frac{a_z}{2}
\right\}.
\]

An off-diagonal fractional same-parity matching is a family \(q=(q_e)\) indexed by unordered pairs \(e=\{z,z'\}\subseteq Z\) with \(z\ne z'\) and \(z\equiv z'\pmod 2\), such that \(q_e\geq0\) and
\[
d_z(q):=\sum_{e\ni z}q_e\leq\frac{a_z}{2}\qquad(z\in Z).
\]
Its mass is \(|q|:=\sum_e q_e\). It is affinely half-metronoid realizable if there are an affine map \(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) and, for every edge \(e=\{z,z'\}\) with \(q_e>0\), points \(x_{e,z}\in\mathcal K_z\) and \(x_{e,z'}\in\mathcal K_{z'}\) such that
\[
\frac{x_{e,z}+x_{e,z'}}2
=s\!\left(\frac{z+z'}2\right).
\]
Define
\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)
:=\sup\{|q|:q\text{ is affinely half-metronoid realizable}\},
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{free}}(C)
:=\sup\{|q|:q\text{ is an off-diagonal fractional same-parity matching}\}.
\]
Let \(S=C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), let \(h_S\) be its mixed-integer halfspace depth, and let
\[
h_{\max}(C):=\max_{p\in S}h_S(p),
\]
whose attainment is supplied by accepted fact `978e8555ec5bac42`.

Then the following assertions hold.

1. Affine matching certificate:
\[
h_{\max}(C)\geq \gamma_d\cdot 2^{-n}\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C).
\]

2. Exact free capacity: for \(\omega\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\), put
\[
A_\omega:=\sum_{z\equiv\omega}a_z,
\qquad
M_\omega:=\max_{z\equiv\omega}a_z,
\]
with \(M_\omega=0\) for an empty parity class. Then
\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{free}}(C)
=\sum_\omega
\min\left\{\frac{A_\omega}{4},\frac{A_\omega-M_\omega}{2}\right\},
\]
and hence
\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{free}}(C)
\geq\frac{W-\sum_\omega M_\omega}{4}
\geq\frac{W-2^nA_*}{4}.
\]

3. Exact affine-stress alternative: fix any finite set \(F\) of eligible same-parity edges and write
\[
m_e:=\frac{z+z'}2\in\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad
\mathcal L_e:=\frac12(\mathcal K_z+\mathcal K_{z'})
\quad(e=\{z,z'\}\in F).
\]
For a nonempty compact convex set \(K\), write \(h_K(u):=\max_{x\in K}\langle u,x\rangle\). Exactly one of the following holds:

(a) there is an affine map \(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\) such that \(s(m_e)\in\mathcal L_e\) for every \(e\in F\);

(b) there are vectors \(v_e\in\mathbb R^d\), not all zero, such that
\[
\sum_{e\in F}v_e=0,
\qquad
\sum_{e\in F}v_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
\qquad
\sum_{e\in F}h_{\mathcal L_e}(v_e)<0.
\]
In case (b), define
\[
U_z:=\frac12\sum_{\substack{e\in F\\e\ni z}}v_e
\]
at every endpoint of \(F\), and put \(U_z=0\) for the remaining \(z\in Z\). Then \((U_z)_{z\in Z}\) is not identically zero and
\[
\sum_{z\in Z}U_z=0,
\qquad
\sum_{z\in Z}U_zz^{\mathsf T}=0,
\qquad
\sum_{z\in Z} h_{\mathcal K_z}(U_z)<0.
\]
If \(c_z\) is the centroid of \(C_z\) and \(D_z:=\mathcal K_z-c_z\), then \(D_z=-D_z\), and for every affine map \(s_0:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\), the last strict inequality is equivalently
\[
\sum_{z\in Z}\langle U_z,c_z-s_0(z)\rangle
+\sum_{z\in Z} h_{D_z}(U_z)<0.
\]

4. If there is one affine map \(s\) satisfying \(s(z)\in\mathcal K_z\) for every \(z\in Z\), then every free matching is affinely realizable and
\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)=\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{free}}(C).
\]

5. Conditional implication only: if some absolute \(\lambda\in(0,1]\) satisfies
\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}(C)\geq\lambda\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{free}}(C)
\]
for every positive-mass mixed convex body \(C\), then the full weak Oertel conjecture holds with
\[
c_0=\frac{\lambda}{e(4+\lambda)}.
\]
No such universal comparison, and no stress-absorption theorem, is asserted here.

## Proof

Because \(C\) is compact, only finitely many integer fibers are nonempty, so \(Z\) is finite and every sum above is finite. For \(z\in Z\), \(C_z\) is a \(d\)-dimensional convex body. Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d`, applied with mass \(a_z/2\), says that \(\mathcal K_z\) is a nonempty compact convex subset of \(C_z\) and that every \(x\in\mathcal K_z\) satisfies
\[
\mathcal H_d(C_z\cap G)\geq\frac{\gamma_d a_z}{2}
\tag{1}
\]
for every proper closed halfspace \(G\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) containing \(x\). The same lower bound is immediate when \(G=\mathbb R^d\).

We first prove the affine matching certificate. Fix an affinely realizable \(q\). The assertion is trivial when \(|q|=0\), so suppose \(|q|>0\). For a positive edge \(e=\{z,z'\}\), write
\[
m_e:=\frac{z+z'}2\in\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad
p_e:=(m_e,s(m_e)).
\]
The witness identity and convexity of \(C\) give
\[
p_e=\frac12(z,x_{e,z})+\frac12(z',x_{e,z'})\in C.
\tag{2}
\]
Consider the base measure
\[
\bar\sigma_q:=\sum_{e:q_e>0}q_e\delta_{m_e}.
\]
It is a nonzero finite measure supported on \(\mathbb Z^n\), of total mass \(|q|\). Accepted fact `6d6fcf377e68c79c`, specialized to \(\mathbb Z^n\), supplies \(m_0\in\mathbb Z^n\) such that every proper closed base halfspace \(J\ni m_0\) has
\[
\bar\sigma_q(J)\geq2^{-n}|q|.
\tag{3}
\]
This remains true for the whole base space. Moreover, \(m_0\) lies in \(\operatorname{conv}\{m_e:q_e>0\}\): otherwise strict separation would give a proper closed halfspace containing \(m_0\) and disjoint from the finite support of \(\bar\sigma_q\), contradicting (3). Therefore
\[
p_0:=(m_0,s(m_0))\in\operatorname{conv}\{p_e:q_e>0\}\subseteq C
\tag{4}
\]
by affinity of \(s\) and (2); its base coordinate is integral, so \(p_0\in S\).

Let \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\) be any proper closed halfspace containing \(p_0\). Write
\[
H=\{(y,x):\alpha\cdot y+\beta\cdot x\geq t\}.
\]
Its pullback to the graph of \(s(y)=Ay+b\) is
\[
J_H:=\{y:(\alpha+A^{\mathsf T}\beta)\cdot y+\beta\cdot b\geq t\}.
\tag{5}
\]
It contains \(m_0\). If \(\alpha+A^{\mathsf T}\beta\ne0\), it is a proper closed base halfspace and (3) applies. If that normal is zero, (5) is either empty or the whole base space; because it contains \(m_0\), it is the whole base space. Thus in all cases
\[
\sum_{e:m_e\in J_H}q_e\geq2^{-n}|q|.
\tag{6}
\]

For every edge counted in (6), \(p_e\in H\). Since the defining affine functional of \(H\) at \(p_e\) is the average of its values at \((z,x_{e,z})\) and \((z',x_{e,z'})\), at least one of these endpoint witnesses belongs to \(H\). Orient the edge toward one such endpoint, and put
\[
R_z:=\sum_{\substack{e:m_e\in J_H\\e\text{ oriented toward }z}}q_e.
\]
Then \(R_z\leq d_z(q)\leq a_z/2\), and \(R_z>0\) implies that the fiber section
\[
H_z:=\{x:(z,x)\in H\}
\]
contains a point of \(\mathcal K_z\). If \(\beta\ne0\), then \(H_z\) is a proper closed halfspace and (1) gives
\[
\mathcal H_d(C_z\cap H_z)\geq\frac{\gamma_d a_z}{2}\geq\gamma_d R_z.
\tag{7}
\]
If \(\beta=0\), then \(H_z\) is either empty or all of \(\mathbb R^d\); because it contains the endpoint witness it is all of \(\mathbb R^d\), and (7) again holds. Summing over fibers, without counting any fiber more than once, yields
\[
\mathcal H_d(S\cap H)
=\sum_z\mathcal H_d(C_z\cap H_z)
\geq\gamma_d\sum_zR_z
=\gamma_d\sum_{e:m_e\in J_H}q_e
\geq\gamma_d2^{-n}|q|.
\tag{8}
\]
The whole ambient space has mass \(W\geq |q|\geq\gamma_d2^{-n}|q|\), so it causes no exception if improper halfspaces are admitted. Hence \(h_S(p_0)\geq\gamma_d2^{-n}|q|\). Taking the supremum over realizable \(q\) proves assertion 1.

We next compute the free capacity. Fix one parity class and put \(s_z:=a_z/2\), \(S_\omega:=\sum_{z\equiv\omega}s_z=A_\omega/2\), and \(s_{\max}:=M_\omega/2\). If a matching in this class has mass \(Q\), its endpoint degrees have total \(2Q\), so
\[
Q\leq\frac{S_\omega}{2}=\frac{A_\omega}{4}.
\tag{9}
\]
The largest vertex can only be paired with the supply at all other vertices, while edges not incident with it use two units of that remaining supply per unit of edge mass. Equivalently, summing the degree constraints away from a largest vertex gives
\[
Q\leq S_\omega-s_{\max}=\frac{A_\omega-M_\omega}{2}.
\tag{10}
\]
Both bounds are attainable. If \(s_{\max}>S_\omega/2\), pair every other vertex completely with a largest vertex; its used degree is \(S_\omega-s_{\max}<s_{\max}\), and the matching mass is \(S_\omega-s_{\max}\). If \(s_{\max}\leq S_\omega/2\) and \(S_\omega>0\), partition a circle of circumference \(S_\omega\) into half-open arcs \(I_z\) of lengths \(s_z\), and let \(T\) be rotation by \(S_\omega/2\). Each \(I_z\) is disjoint from \(T(I_z)\) up to a null set because \(|I_z|\leq S_\omega/2\). For distinct vertices set
\[
q_{zz'}:=\operatorname{length}\{t\in I_z:T(t)\in I_{z'}\}.
\]
The measure-preserving involution \(T\) makes these weights symmetric, and the degree at \(z\) is exactly \(|I_z|=s_z\). Thus every supply is used and \(Q=S_\omega/2\). Empty and singleton parity classes give zero on both sides. Combining (9), (10), and these constructions proves the exact formula in assertion 2 after summing independently over parity classes.

For every \(\omega\),
\[
\min\left\{\frac{A_\omega}{4},\frac{A_\omega-M_\omega}{2}\right\}
\geq\frac{A_\omega-M_\omega}{4}.
\]
Summing and using \(\sum_\omega M_\omega\leq2^nA_*\) proves both lower bounds in assertion 2.

We prove the affine-stress alternative. Let \(\mathscr A\) be the vector space of affine maps \(\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\), and define the linear subspace
\[
V:=\{(s(m_e))_{e\in F}:s\in\mathscr A\}\subseteq(\mathbb R^d)^F
\]
and the nonempty compact convex set
\[
L:=\prod_{e\in F}\mathcal L_e.
\]
The feasible case is exactly \(V\cap L\ne\varnothing\). Suppose first that \(V\cap L=\varnothing\). Let \(P\) be orthogonal projection onto \(V^\perp\). Then \(0\notin P(L)\). Choose \(w_0\in P(L)\) of minimum Euclidean norm. Convexity gives
\[
\langle w_0,w-w_0\rangle\geq0\qquad(w\in P(L)).
\]
With \(v:=-w_0=(v_e)_{e\in F}\), it follows that \(v\ne0\), \(v\perp V\), and
\[
h_L(v)=\sup_{\ell\in L}\langle v,\ell\rangle
\leq-\|w_0\|^2<0.
\tag{11}
\]
Because support functions add over Cartesian products,
\[
h_L(v)=\sum_{e\in F}h_{\mathcal L_e}(v_e).
\]
Writing an arbitrary affine map as \(s(y)=Ay+b\), the condition \(v\perp V\) is equivalent to
\[
\sum_ev_e=0,
\qquad
\sum_ev_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0,
\tag{12}
\]
because \(A\) and \(b\) are arbitrary. Thus (b) holds. Conversely, if (b) holds and \((s(m_e))_e\in L\), then (12) gives
\[
0=\sum_e\langle v_e,s(m_e)\rangle
\leq\sum_eh_{\mathcal L_e}(v_e)<0,
\]
a contradiction. Hence exactly one alternative holds.

For the collapse, positive homogeneity and subadditivity of support functions give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{z\in Z} h_{\mathcal K_z}(U_z)
&=\frac12\sum_{z\in Z} h_{\mathcal K_z}\!\left(\sum_{e\ni z}v_e\right)\\
&\leq\frac12\sum_{z\in Z}\sum_{e\ni z}h_{\mathcal K_z}(v_e)\\
&=\sum_{e=\{z,z'\}\in F}\frac12\left(h_{\mathcal K_z}(v_e)+h_{\mathcal K_{z'}}(v_e)\right)\\
&=\sum_eh_{\mathcal L_e}(v_e)<0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
The two balance identities follow directly from (12):
\[
\sum_{z\in Z}U_z=\sum_ev_e=0,
\qquad
\sum_{z\in Z}U_zz^{\mathsf T}
=\frac12\sum_{e=\{z,z'\}\in F}v_e(z+z')^{\mathsf T}
=\sum_ev_em_e^{\mathsf T}=0.
\tag{14}
\]
If all \(U_z\) vanished, the left side of (13) would be zero, contradicting its strict negativity, so the collapsed field is nonzero.

For the centered form, if a density \(f\) of mass \(a_z/2\) represents \(x\in\mathcal K_z\), then \(1-f\) has the same mass and represents \(2c_z-x\). Hence \(D_z=\mathcal K_z-c_z\) is centrally symmetric. Therefore
\[
h_{\mathcal K_z}(U_z)=\langle U_z,c_z\rangle+h_{D_z}(U_z).
\]
For every affine \(s_0(z)=A_0z+b_0\), (14) implies \(\sum_{z\in Z}\langle U_z,s_0(z)\rangle=0\). Subtracting this zero term from (13) proves the equivalent displayed inequality in assertion 3.

If an affine \(s\) satisfies \(s(z)\in\mathcal K_z\) on \(Z\), then for every free edge \(e=\{z,z'\}\) choose \(x_{e,z}=s(z)\) and \(x_{e,z'}=s(z')\). Affinity gives
\[
\frac{x_{e,z}+x_{e,z'}}2=s\!\left(\frac{z+z'}2\right),
\]
so every free matching is affinely realizable. The reverse inequality \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{aff}}\leq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{free}}\) is tautological, proving assertion 4.

Finally assume the comparison in assertion 5 for a fixed \(\lambda\in(0,1]\). Let \(z_*\) satisfy \(a_{z_*}=A_*\), and let \(c_{z_*}\) be the centroid of \(C_{z_*}\). Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d`, now applied with mass \(A_*\), gives fiber halfspace depth at least \(\gamma_dA_*\) at \(c_{z_*}\). For an ambient closed halfspace containing \((z_*,c_{z_*})\), its section in the \(z_*\)-fiber is either a proper closed halfspace containing the centroid or, when its continuous normal vanishes, the whole fiber. Thus
\[
h_{\max}(C)\geq\gamma_dA_*.
\tag{15}
\]
Assertions 1 and 2 and the assumed comparison also give
\[
h_{\max}(C)
\geq\gamma_d2^{-n}\frac{\lambda}{4}(W-2^nA_*).
\tag{16}
\]
Put \(x:=2^nA_*/W\). If \(0\leq x\leq1\), (15)-(16) imply
\[
\frac{2^nh_{\max}(C)}{W}
\geq\gamma_d\max\left\{x,\frac{\lambda(1-x)}4\right\}
\geq\gamma_d\frac{\lambda}{4+\lambda}.
\]
If \(x\geq1\), (15) gives the same final lower bound because \(\lambda/(4+\lambda)<1\). Finally,
\[
\gamma_d\geq e^{-1},
\]
since \(d\log(1+1/d)\leq1\). Hence
\[
h_{\max}(C)\geq\frac{\lambda}{e(4+\lambda)}\,2^{-n}W.
\]
By the attainment conclusion of accepted fact `978e8555ec5bac42`, a point of \(S\) attains this depth. The constant lies in \((0,1/e)\), so this is exactly the claimed conditional implication. Neither the comparison hypothesis nor any conversion of a stress into realizable matching mass is proved in this packet.
