## Statement

Retain the full canonical hard synchronized-atlas setting and the minimal-base no-rank-drop setting of the accepted predecessors. Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body, and
\[
 C_y:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(y,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_y:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_y),
 \qquad
 c_y:=\frac1{a_y}\int_{C_y}x\,dx,
\]
\[
 W:=\sum_{y\in\mathbb Z^n}a_y,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
\]
while \(Q>0\) is the mass of the half-strength canonical product reservoir. Fix
\[
 v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t).
\]
For a positive-volume bounded measurable set \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) and \(0<s\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(K)\), write
\[
 \mathsf M_s(K):=
 \left\{
 \frac1s\int_Kx f(x)\,dx:
 0\leq f\leq1\ {\rm a.e.},\quad
 \int_Kf=s
 \right\}.
\]
For every proper primitive affine lattice subspace \(L'\subsetneq\mathbb R^n\), put
\[
 W_{L'}:=\sum_{y\in L'\cap\mathbb Z^n}a_y.
\]
The retained minimal-base hypothesis is
\[
 W_{L'}<2^{\dim L'-n}W
 \quad
 \text{for every such }L'.
\]
Let \(\Sigma\) be the finite original label set of the synchronized transverse atlas. For every \(\sigma\in\Sigma\), put
\[
 \eta_\sigma:=\widehat\theta_\sigma(v)\geq0,
 \qquad
 P:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma}\eta_\sigma=\widehat P(v),
\tag{1}
\]
and put
\[
 Y:=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad
 w_y:=\widehat\tau_y(v)>0
 \quad(y\in Y).
\tag{2}
\]
Then:

1. Every retained whole label satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\eta_\sigma<\frac{Q}{600\,2^n}.}
\tag{3}
\]

2. The number of positive retained whole labels satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 |\{\sigma:\eta_\sigma>0\}|
 >\frac{21}{2}\,2^n.}
\tag{4}
\]

3. Every parity class in \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\) contains at most one endpoint \(y\in Y\) with
\[
 w_y\geq\frac{Q}{600\,2^n}.
\tag{5}
\]

4. The complementary light endpoints satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\substack{y\in Y\\
 w_y<Q/(600\,2^n)}}w_y
 >\frac{13Q}{400},}
\tag{6}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 \left|
 \left\{y\in Y:
 w_y<\frac{Q}{600\,2^n}
 \right\}
 \right|
 >\frac{39}{2}\,2^n.}
\tag{7}
\]

## Proof

We first collect the exact accepted input. Accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c gives
\[
 n\geq2,
 \qquad
 P>\frac{7Q}{400},
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y}w_y=2P,
\tag{8}
\]
and, for every \(y\in Y\),
\[
 0<w_y\leq\frac{a_y}{4}.
\tag{9}
\]
The strict synchronized atom cap established in accepted fact d06d919de3083960 gives
\[
 w_y<\frac{Q}{400\,2^n}
 \qquad(y\in Y).
\tag{10}
\]
For a label \(\sigma\), the exact endpoint incidence identity in accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c is
\[
 w_y
 =\sum_{\substack{\tau\\
 y\in\{a(\tau),b(\tau)\}}}\eta_\tau.
\tag{11}
\]
Hence, whenever \(\eta_\sigma>0\),
\[
 \eta_\sigma\leq w_{a(\sigma)},
 \qquad
 \eta_\sigma\leq w_{b(\sigma)}.
\tag{12}
\]
The transverse atlas of accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 has distinct opposite endpoints and target base
\[
 a(\sigma)\ne b(\sigma),
 \qquad
 m_\sigma=\frac{a(\sigma)+b(\sigma)}2\in\mathbb Z^n.
\tag{13}
\]
Accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c retains each whole label and both occurrences under the common factor \(\eta_\sigma/\theta_\sigma\). Thus (13) is unchanged after thinning. In particular,
\[
 a(\sigma)\equiv b(\sigma)\pmod2.
\tag{14}
\]

We next prove the same-parity segment screen used for both (3) and (5). Let \(u,w\in Y\) be any two distinct endpoints with
\[
 u\equiv w\pmod2.
\tag{15}
\]
Put
\[
 \Delta:=[u,w],
 \qquad
 L:=\operatorname{aff}\{u,w\},
 \qquad
 E:=\Delta\cap\mathbb Z^n.
\tag{16}
\]
For \(z\in L\), let \(\lambda_u(z),\lambda_w(z)\) be the affine barycentric coordinates relative to \(u,w\). Define
\[
 t_z:=
 4\left(
 \lambda_u(z)w_u^{1/d}
 +\lambda_w(z)w_w^{1/d}
 \right)^d
 \quad(z\in E),
\tag{17}
\]
and define the affine map
\[
 s(z):=
 \lambda_u(z)c_u+\lambda_w(z)c_w
 \quad(z\in L).
\tag{18}
\]

We justify the root-affine construction at these arbitrary synchronized endpoints, rather than invoking the stopped-set restriction in the statement of accepted fact f340b1dc790fbf38. By (9), for \(y\in\{u,w\}\) the density
\[
 f_y:=\frac{4w_y}{a_y}\mathbf1_{C_y}
\tag{19}
\]
satisfies \(0<f_y\leq1\), has mass \(4w_y\), and has normalized first moment \(c_y\). Therefore
\[
 c_y\in\mathsf M_{4w_y}(C_y).
\tag{20}
\]
The proof of accepted fact dfd25e72c1f45a4e establishes convexity before closure of the positive-root set
\[
 \left\{
 (y,x,r):
 r>0,\quad
 x\in\mathsf M_{r^d}(C_y)
 \right\}.
\tag{21}
\]
Every \(z\in E\) lies in \([u,w]\), so its two barycentric coefficients are nonnegative and sum to one. Applying (21) to the two positive-root triples in (20) gives
\[
 s(z)\in\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z),
 \qquad
 t_z>0.
\tag{22}
\]
Any density representing the metronoid membership in (22) is bounded above by \(\mathbf1_{C_z}\), hence
\[
 0<t_z\leq a_z.
\tag{23}
\]
This reproduces the rank-one root-affine construction of accepted fact f340b1dc790fbf38 using only the synchronized endpoint inequality (9). In particular, it applies whether or not \(u,w\) belong to the stopped set \(A_\star\).

The affine line \(L\) is primitive. Indeed, let
\[
 g:=\gcd\{|w_i-u_i|:1\leq i\leq n\}>0,
 \qquad
 h:=\frac{w-u}{g}\in\mathbb Z^n.
\tag{24}
\]
The vector \(h\) is primitive, \(L=u+\mathbb Rh\), and
\[
 (\mathbb Rh)\cap\mathbb Z^n=\mathbb Zh.
\tag{25}
\]
Thus the direction lattice is saturated. Apply accepted fact 10f1feebe06e22d0 to the primitive rank-one affine lattice space \(L\), the finite nonempty set \(E\), the positive feasible masses (17), and the affine selector (18). It gives
\[
 G_L^{\mathrm{rel}}
 \geq
 T(u,w),
\tag{26}
\]
where
\[
 T(u,w):=
 4\sum_{z\in[u,w]\cap\mathbb Z^n}
 \left(
 \lambda_u(z)w_u^{1/d}
 +\lambda_w(z)w_w^{1/d}
 \right)^d.
\tag{27}
\]

We now audit the relative-to-global normalization. In the retained minimal-base setting, accepted fact 1e3269a0e9b5695a gives
\[
 W_{L'}<2^{\dim L'-n}W
\tag{28}
\]
for every proper primitive affine lattice subspace \(L'\subsetneq\mathbb R^n\). Also, the canonical reservoir formula in accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66 gives
\[
 A_\xi:=\sum_{y\equiv\xi\ ({\rm mod}\ 2)}a_y
\]
for each parity class and
\[
 Q
 =\sum_{\xi\ {\rm good}}
 \frac{A_\xi^2-\sum_{y\equiv\xi}a_y^2}{8A_\xi}
 \leq\frac18\sum_{\xi\ {\rm good}}A_\xi
 \leq\frac W8.
\tag{29}
\]
The canonical hard inequality has \(G\) as a nonnegative summand, so
\[
 G<\frac Q{100}
 \leq\frac{W}{800}
 <\frac W4.
\tag{30}
\]
Because \(n\geq2\), the rank-one line \(L\) is proper. Equations (28) and (30) are exactly the hypotheses of the relative-to-global conclusion in accepted fact 3d1f63abf09b2abb. Combining that conclusion with (26) yields the strict segment screen
\[
 \boxed{
 2^{n-1}T(u,w)
 \leq
 2^{n-1}G_L^{\mathrm{rel}}
 \leq G
 <\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{31}
\]
The factor \(2^{n-1}\) is essential; no relative profile is treated as a global profile without it.

We apply (31) first to a positive retained label \(\sigma\). By (13)-(14), its endpoints are distinct and have the same parity, and their midpoint \(m_\sigma\) is a third, distinct lattice point of their segment. The contributions to (27) at the two endpoints and the midpoint give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T(a(\sigma),b(\sigma))
 &\geq
 4w_{a(\sigma)}
 +4w_{b(\sigma)}\\
 &\quad+
 4\left(
 \frac{
 w_{a(\sigma)}^{1/d}
 +w_{b(\sigma)}^{1/d}}2
 \right)^d.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{32}
\]
By (12), both endpoint weights are at least \(\eta_\sigma\), so every one of the three terms in (32) is at least \(4\eta_\sigma\). Hence
\[
 T(a(\sigma),b(\sigma))
 \geq12\eta_\sigma.
\tag{33}
\]
Equations (31) and (33) give
\[
 6\,2^n\eta_\sigma
 =12\,2^{n-1}\eta_\sigma
 \leq2^{n-1}T(a(\sigma),b(\sigma))
 <\frac Q{100}.
\tag{34}
\]
Since \(Q>0\), division by \(6\,2^n\) proves (3) for every positive retained label. When \(\eta_\sigma=0\), (3) is immediate, so (3) holds for every retained whole label.

Let
\[
 N:=|\{\sigma:\eta_\sigma>0\}|,
 \qquad
 \delta:=\frac{Q}{600\,2^n}>0.
\tag{35}
\]
The label family is finite. Equation (3) is strict at every positive label, so
\[
 P=\sum_{\eta_\sigma>0}\eta_\sigma<N\delta.
\tag{36}
\]
Combining (8), (35), and (36) gives
\[
 N>
 \frac{7Q/400}{Q/(600\,2^n)}
 =\frac{21}{2}\,2^n,
\tag{37}
\]
which proves (4).

Next suppose two distinct endpoints \(u,w\in Y\) have the same parity and both satisfy
\[
 w_u,w_w\geq\delta.
\tag{38}
\]
The endpoint and midpoint calculation in (32), now with \(\delta\) in place of \(\eta_\sigma\), gives
\[
 T(u,w)\geq12\delta.
\tag{39}
\]
Using (35),
\[
 2^{n-1}T(u,w)
 \geq12\,2^{n-1}\delta
 =6\,2^n\frac{Q}{600\,2^n}
 =\frac Q{100},
\tag{40}
\]
contradicting the strict upper bound (31). Thus each parity class has at most one endpoint with weight at least \(\delta\), proving (5).

Let
\[
 Y_{\mathrm{heavy}}:=\{y\in Y:w_y\geq\delta\},
 \qquad
 Y_{\mathrm{light}}:=Y\setminus Y_{\mathrm{heavy}}.
\tag{41}
\]
There are \(2^n\) parity classes, so (5) gives
\[
 |Y_{\mathrm{heavy}}|\leq2^n.
\tag{42}
\]
The strict atom cap (10) then yields
\[
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathrm{heavy}}}w_y
 <
 2^n\frac{Q}{400\,2^n}
 =\frac Q{400}.
\tag{43}
\]
The synchronized endpoint identity in (8) gives
\[
 \sum_{y\in Y}w_y
 =2P
 >\frac{7Q}{200}
 =\frac{14Q}{400}.
\tag{44}
\]
Subtracting (43) from (44) proves
\[
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathrm{light}}}w_y
 >\frac{13Q}{400},
\tag{45}
\]
which is (6).

Finally, put \(M:=|Y_{\mathrm{light}}|\). Every endpoint in \(Y\) has positive weight, and every light endpoint has weight strictly smaller than \(\delta\). Therefore
\[
 \sum_{y\in Y_{\mathrm{light}}}w_y<M\delta.
\tag{46}
\]
Equations (35), (45), and (46) give
\[
 M>
 \frac{13Q/400}{Q/(600\,2^n)}
 =\frac{39}{2}\,2^n,
\tag{47}
\]
which proves (7). Every use of (31) concerned one segment at a time. The proof sums only the exact label and endpoint incidence masses in (8), (11), (36), and (41)-(46); it never adds relative-core or simplex capacities from different segments.
