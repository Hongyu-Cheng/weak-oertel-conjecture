## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^d$ be a full-dimensional $(d+1)$-simplex, $d\ge1$, with actual closed section volumes $w(s)=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_s)$. Let $h_i$ be the height of vertex $v_i$, with barycentric coordinate $\lambda_i$. For $0\le c<1$, the raw mixed mass of the closed ambient cap $\lambda_i\ge c$ in $C$ is
$$
(1-c)^d\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z} w\left(\frac{j-ch_i}{1-c}\right).
$$
For $c<0$ its mass is the full mixed mass, and for $c\ge1$ it has zero mixed mass. The formula includes vertical caps and closed boundary fibers.

## Proof

For $0\le c<1$, substituting $\lambda_i=c+(1-c)\lambda_i'$ and $\lambda_k=(1-c)\lambda_k'$ for $k\ne i$ shows the cap is $cv_i+(1-c)C$. Its actual fiber at integer height $j$ is the image of the fiber at real height $(j-ch_i)/(1-c)$ under a continuous-coordinate homothety of ratio $1-c$. Sum the resulting $d$-volumes over the original integer heights. At $c=1$ the cap is one vertex, of zero $d$-volume since $d\ge1$. The ranges $c<0$ and $c>1$ follow from the range of $\lambda_i$. The homothety is an identity of closed sets, so no endpoint fiber is discarded. For the cap through a mixed centroid of integral base, evaluate at its barycentric coordinate $c$. For the cap through the own centroid of a positive integer fiber, evaluate at the barycentric coordinate of that section centroid.
