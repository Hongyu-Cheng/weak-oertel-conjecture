## Statement

Let $K$ be the unit disk in $\mathbb R^2$ and fix $0<a<1$. The contact action $u\cdot x+\varepsilon(1+au_1)\le1$ for all unit $u$ has normalized physical retained mass $T_{K_\varepsilon}(y_\varepsilon)/|K_\varepsilon|=1$ at $y_\varepsilon=-\varepsilon a e_1$ for small $\varepsilon\ge0$. Its max-gap penalty potential is $\Phi_L(x)=[1-L+|Lx+a e_1|]_+$. For the corresponding old-roof deformation at every finite $L\ge0$, at the same prescribed mark the normalized right derivative is $-|B_L-\pi a e_1|/\pi<0$, where $B_L=\int_{\partial K}n\,\Phi_L(n)\,dS$. This derivative tends to zero as $L\to\infty$.

## Proof

The original action is the disk of radius $1-\varepsilon$ centered at $-\varepsilon a e_1$. It is nested since its radii decrease faster than its centers move. The mark is its centroid, so the full indicator satisfies the retained first moment. Maximizing $1-L+u\cdot(Lx+a e_1)$ over unit $u$ gives the stated penalty. On the unit circle it is positive since $1-L+|L-a|\ge1-a$. Write $c=n_1$. The speed error on that circle is
$\delta_L(c)=\sqrt{L^2+2Lac+a^2}-L-ac$. For $0<c<1$,
$$
\delta_L(c)-\delta_L(-c)=2ac\left(\frac{2L}{\sqrt{L^2+2Lac+a^2}+\sqrt{L^2-2Lac+a^2}}-1\right)<0 .
$$
The denominator is the sum of the lengths of $Le_1+an$ and $Le_1-an$. It is strictly larger than $2L$ by the strict triangle inequality when $L>0$ and $|c|<1$, and is $2a$ when $L=0$. Reflection of the first coordinate and integration give $\int n_1\delta_L(n_1)\,dS<0$. Reflection of the second coordinate gives $\int n_2\delta_L(n_1)\,dS=0$. As $\int n(1+an_1)\,dS=\pi a e_1$, it follows that $B_L\ne\pi a e_1$ for every finite $L$.

The roof's fixed-$L$ radial limit and inner homothet are provided by the contact approximation theorem. Apply the centroid formula of dbb1331ec0f15edd to the unit disk with $V=\pi$, Euclidean gauge and $w=-ae_1$. Dividing the retained mass by its own deformed volume cancels the term $-\int\Phi_L\,dS$ in the numerator derivative, since $T_K(0)=V$. This gives exactly $-|B_L-\pi ae_1|/\pi$. The $L^1$ speed convergence implies $B_L\to\pi ae_1$ by boundedness of the coordinate tests. The convex potential $[1+ax_1]_+$ realizes the original trace exactly, since $1+an_1>0$ on the circle.
