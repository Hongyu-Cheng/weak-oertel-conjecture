## Statement

Retain the complete canonical setting, objects, and normalization of accepted fact `49092268f4f67b8e`. Thus \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body of positive mixed volume,
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 I_+:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},
\tag{1}
\]
\[
 W:=\sum_{z\in I_+}a_z,
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
 \qquad
 \Theta:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\theta_\sigma>0.
\tag{2}
\]
Let
\[
 \ell=(\ell_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Gamma},
 \qquad
 \ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\leq1,
\tag{3}
\]
be any normalized target-affine family, and retain
\[
 A(\ell):=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}
 \theta_\sigma\bigl(1-\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\bigr),
 \qquad
 I(\ell):=\sum_t\int_{C_t}
 \max\left\{0,\max_{\sigma\in\Gamma_t}\ell_\sigma(x)\right\}\,dx.
\tag{4}
\]
Call \(\sigma\) good when
\(\ell_\sigma(r_\sigma^c)\geq1/2\). For every good label choose one tagged occurrence \(o(\sigma)\) such that
\[
 \ell_\sigma(c_{y_{\sigma,o(\sigma)}})\geq\frac12,
\tag{5}
\]
and define the canonical active original-occurrence profile
\[
 w_z:=\frac14
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\ {\mathrm{good}}\\
 y_{\sigma,o(\sigma)}=z}}\theta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 M:=\sum_z w_z.
\tag{6}
\]
Accepted fact `49092268f4f67b8e` gives
\[
 M\geq\frac{\Theta-2A(\ell)}4,
 \qquad
 0\leq w_z\leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\tag{7}
\]

Let \(L\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) be an affine subspace for which
\[
 M_L:=\sum_{z\in L}w_z>0,
 \qquad
 D_L:=I_+\cap L,
 \qquad
 B_L:=\operatorname{aff}(D_L),
 \qquad
 q:=\dim B_L\leq2.
\tag{8}
\]
Put
\[
 W_L:=\sum_{z\in D_L}a_z,
 \qquad
 \alpha_0:=1,
 \quad
 \alpha_1:=\frac14,
 \quad
 \alpha_2:=\frac1{64}.
\tag{9}
\]
Then the following collision-free physical slice bound holds:
\[
 \boxed{G\geq\alpha_qW_L\geq4\alpha_qM_L.}
\tag{10}
\]

If \(M>0\), put \(\beta_L:=M_L/M\in(0,1]\). Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta
 \leq
 \frac{1}{\alpha_q\beta_L}G+2A(\ell)
 \leq
 K(q,\beta_L)\bigl(G+A(\ell)+I(\ell)\bigr),}
\tag{11}
\]
where
\[
 K(q,\beta):=
 \max\left\{2,\frac{1}{\alpha_q\beta}\right\}.
\tag{12}
\]
If \(M=0\), the same conclusion holds with the first right side in (11) replaced by \(2A(\ell)\).

In particular, suppose \(M>0\) and the full active support
\[
 E_{\mathrm{act}}:=\{z:w_z>0\}
\tag{13}
\]
has affine rank
\(q:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{act}})\leq2\). Taking
\(L:=\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{act}})\) gives \(\beta_L=1\) and
\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta\leq
 \begin{cases}
 2\bigl(G+A(\ell)+I(\ell)\bigr),&q=0,\\
 4\bigl(G+A(\ell)+I(\ell)\bigr),&q=1,\\
 64\bigl(G+A(\ell)+I(\ell)\bigr),&q=2.
 \end{cases}}
\tag{14}
\]
The bound with constant \(64\) also covers \(M=0\).

Finally, assume that for every normalized family (3), either \(M=0\), or the good labels admit a choice of tags satisfying (5) whose active support (13) has affine rank at most two. Let \(\mathsf P_\Gamma\) be the capped prescribed-barycenter target-packing capacity of accepted fact `49092268f4f67b8e`. Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta\leq64\bigl(G+\mathsf P_\Gamma\bigr),
 \qquad
 \max\{G,\mathsf P_\Gamma\}\geq\frac{\Theta}{128}.}
\tag{15}
\]
No assertion is made that the active-support hypothesis in (14) or its uniform version preceding (15) holds in the remaining high-rank canonical square branch.

## Proof

Fix the normalized family and the legal active-tag choice. Equations (6)-(7) are exactly the active original-occurrence conclusions of accepted fact `49092268f4f67b8e`, with the same label masses, tagged multiplicities, fiber centroids, and factor \(1/4\). In particular, \(w_z>0\) implies \(a_z>0\), so every active base belongs to \(I_+\).

Fix \(L\) satisfying (8). Apply accepted fact `8aac56c49321466c` to the affine slice \(A=L\). Its positive-fiber set is exactly \(D_L\), its occupied affine hull is \(B_L\), its intrinsic rank is \(q\leq2\), and its slice mass is \(W_L\). If \(B_L=\mathbb R^n\), equation (11) of that accepted fact gives
\[
 G\geq\alpha_qW=\alpha_qW_L.
\tag{16}
\]
If \(B_L\subsetneq\mathbb R^n\), equation (15) of the same accepted fact gives
\[
 G\geq
 \min\left\{
 \alpha_qW_L+\frac{W-W_L}{2},
 \ 2^{n-q}\alpha_qW_L
 \right\}.
\tag{17}
\]
Both entries in the minimum are at least \(\alpha_qW_L\), because
\(W\geq W_L\) and \(q\leq n\). Thus (16)-(17) prove the first inequality in (10) in every case.

Every active base in \(L\) lies in \(D_L\), and the pointwise physical capacity in (7) gives
\[
 W_L
 =\sum_{z\in D_L}a_z
 \geq\sum_{\substack{z\in D_L\\w_z>0}}a_z
 \geq4\sum_{z\in L}w_z
 =4M_L.
\tag{18}
\]
Combining (16)-(18) proves all of (10). This uses one actual affine slice of the common convex body. It does not add complete cores belonging to different slices or labels.

Suppose first that \(M>0\). By definition,
\(M_L=\beta_LM\). Equations (7) and (10) give
\[
 G
 \geq4\alpha_q\beta_LM
 \geq\alpha_q\beta_L
 \bigl(\Theta-2A(\ell)\bigr).
\tag{19}
\]
If \(\Theta>2A(\ell)\), rearranging (19) proves the first inequality in (11). If \(\Theta\leq2A(\ell)\), that inequality is immediate from nonnegativity of \(G\). Since \(G,A(\ell),I(\ell)\geq0\), equations (11)-(12) follow. If \(M=0\), equation (7) directly gives \(\Theta\leq2A(\ell)\), proving the stated zero-profile case.

If the full active support has affine rank \(q\leq2\), take
\(L=\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{act}})\). Then every positive active weight lies in \(L\), so \(M_L=M\) and \(\beta_L=1\). Substituting the three values in (9) into (11)-(12) gives exactly (14). The rank of \(B_L\) is also \(q\): the set \(D_L\) contains \(E_{\mathrm{act}}\), which spans \(L\), while \(D_L\subseteq L\). Thus no hidden rank increase occurs when the other positive fibers on the same affine slice are included.

Under the uniform low-rank active-choice hypothesis preceding (15), equation (14) gives
\[
 \Theta\leq64\bigl(G+A(\ell)+I(\ell)\bigr)
\tag{20}
\]
for every normalized family. Accepted fact `49092268f4f67b8e` gives the exact dual identity
\[
 \mathsf P_\Gamma
 =\inf_\ell\bigl(A(\ell)+I(\ell)\bigr).
\tag{21}
\]
Take a sequence of normalized families whose objectives decrease to this infimum and pass to the limit in (20). This gives the first inequality in (15). The elementary bound
\[
 G+\mathsf P_\Gamma
 \leq2\max\{G,\mathsf P_\Gamma\}
\tag{22}
\]
gives the second. This proves every claimed slice screen, conditional fusion estimate, and packing-or-core consequence.
