## Statement

In the open actual compatibility and $C^2$ cap chart of the first-stress lemma, assume $\ell\cdot DG(0)=0$. Let a critical velocity $(B,\eta)$ mean $f(B)+DG(0)[\eta]=0$. Define at the reference configuration
$$
Q(B,\eta)=V D^2\det(I)[B,B]-\sum_i\ell_i\left(D^2_{WW}F_i[B,B]+2D^2_{W\theta}F_i[B,\eta]+D^2_{\theta\theta}G_i[\eta,\eta]\right).
$$
There is a $C^2$ path with a critical first velocity, positive total mixed mass second derivative and strictly negative second derivative of every selected global cap mass if and only if $Q(B,\eta)>0$ for some critical velocity. If $q$ is the vector of cap second derivatives on the straight path with such a velocity, adding the physical acceleration $\mathcal R(-q-\gamma Q\mathbf1/2)$ makes the mass second derivative $Q/2$ and every cap second derivative $-\gamma Q/2$.

## Proof

The first-stress lemma applies to the given actual cover chart and supplies the physical right inverse and $\ell=(m\gamma)^{-1}\mathbf1$. By $\ell\cdot DG(0)=0$ the Lagrangian equal to total mixed mass minus $\sum_i\ell_i$ times the actual cap masses is stationary in both variables. The exact cap identity shows that its Hessian on $(B,\eta)$ is the displayed $Q$: the pure $\theta$ derivatives of $F_i(W,\theta)-F_i(I,\theta)$ cancel at $W=I$, leaving the full original-source derivative $D^2G_i$.

A critical velocity also gives zero mass first derivative. For $Q>0$ use $W(t)=I+tB+\tfrac12t^2\mathcal R(-q-\gamma Q\mathbf1/2)$ and $\theta(t)=t\eta$. Surjectivity changes each cap second derivative by the corresponding component of $-q-\gamma Q\mathbf1/2$. By stationarity the Lagrangian second derivative remains $Q$, so the mass second derivative is $Q-(\gamma Q/2)\sum_i\ell_i=Q/2$. The chart hypotheses keep the path actual and its selected caps a cover. Taylor expansion gives the strict finite changes for sufficiently small positive $t$. Conversely on any $C^2$ path with critical first velocity, stationarity cancels all path accelerations from the Lagrangian second derivative. It equals $Q$ and is positive if the mass second derivative is positive and every cap second derivative is negative, since all $\ell_i$ are positive.
