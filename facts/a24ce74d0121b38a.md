## Statement

Let $L>1$. In $\mathbb R^3\times\mathbb R$, let $C$ be the convex hull of the points $(z,Lz_1z_2z_3+\varepsilon)$ for $z\in\{-1,1\}^3$ and $\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}$. With $D=C$, this is a global maximum-mass extraction among ambient convex bodies inside $C$ with true integer-base centroid and normalized closed-halfspace depth at least $1/2$. Its centroid is $(0,0)$. There are no real $T,B,b$ with $TD_z+Bz+b\subset\operatorname{int}(C_z)$ at every positive integer fiber. There is nevertheless a normalized integer-fiber contact stress with continuous tensor one and zero base and translation balances.

## Proof

The base projection is the full cube and one corner supplies a nondegenerate interval, so $C$ has full ambient dimension. Its mixed measure is invariant under $(z,x)\mapsto(-z,-x)$ because that map permutes the generating points and preserves fiber length. Thus the true centroid is the origin. Reflection of any closed halfspace through the origin gives a complementary closed halfspace of the same mass. Any containing closed halfspace contains its through-origin translate, giving local depth at least $1/2$. A through-origin nonvertical cut has null boundary on each fiber and attains $1/2$. No subset of $C$ has more mixed mass, so $D=C$ is a global maximum.

At a corner $z$, write $s_z=z_1z_2z_3$. Extremality of the corner in the base projection gives the exact fiber $[Ls_z-1,Ls_z+1]$. The strict fitting inequalities there are
$$
|(T-1)Ls_z+Bz+b|+|T|<1 .
$$
Multiplying the center error by $s_z$ and averaging over all corners annihilates $Bz+b$ because the averages of $s_z$ and of each $s_z z_ell$ vanish. The triangle inequality yields $L|T-1|<1-|T|$. For $|T|\ge1$ the strict inequality is impossible. For $|T|<1$ use $|T-1|\ge1-|T|>0$ and $L>1$ for a contradiction.

At any one corner take its upper and lower endpoints with normals $+1$ and $-1$ and weight $1/2$ each. Their weighted normal and weighted base-normal sums vanish. The weighted sum of normal times endpoint is half the interval length, which is one. This is the stated normalized stress.
