## Statement

In the positive outside-strip branch of the simplex-base saturation alternative, with $K_p=\sum_j\alpha_jK_{v_j}$ and $S_{v_j}=t\alpha_j$ for $t>0$, one has
$$
S_p\ge t\left(\sum_j\alpha_j^{1+1/d}\right)^d .
$$
For equal weights on $m=n+1$ vertices this is $S_p\ge t/m$ and $S_p/(S_p+t)\ge1/(m+1)$.

For $d\ge2$, along a sequence of such caps with $h\downarrow0$, convergent nonzero continuous normals and convergent thresholds, suppose the limiting sections on all participating fibers are proper. Then the limiting section coareas obey the corresponding bound with exponent $d-1$ in place of $d$. In particular the equal-weight fraction is again at least $1/(m+1)$.

## Proof

The barycentric threshold identity $\sum_j\alpha_j c_{v_j}=c_p$ follows from $\sum_j\alpha_j(v_j-p)=0$. All strips have width $h$ in the same linear coordinate, so $\sum_j\alpha_jE_{v_j}\subset E_p$ by the exact fiber equality and the strict slab inequalities. With positive strip volumes, the closures have the same volumes. Brunn–Minkowski gives $(hS_p)^{1/d}\ge\sum_j\alpha_j(hS_{v_j})^{1/d}$ and substitution proves the first bound. Equal weights give $\sum_j\alpha_j^{1+1/d}=m^{-1/d}$.

For proper limiting planes, integrate the section area divided by the normal length over each width interval. The accepted proper-section continuity gives convergence of these shrinking averages to the limiting area divided by the limiting normal length: every intermediate level tends to the same proper limiting level and has the same limit by the sequential criterion. Thus the rank relation persists with $t$ replaced by the sum of the positive limiting outside coareas. The Minkowski combination of the closed limiting outside sections is contained in the limiting own section by the same threshold identity. After translating the parallel planes to a common linear hyperplane, apply $(d-1)$-dimensional Brunn–Minkowski. The normal length is common to all fibers, so after dividing by that common length the argument gives the stated bound with $d-1$.
