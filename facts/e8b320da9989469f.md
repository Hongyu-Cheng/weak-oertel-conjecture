## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and retain the exact canonical objects, normalizations, and alias maps of accepted facts f6188983e8850f01 and 6a74481cad1d7e81. Thus, for every \(z\in\mathbb Z^n\),
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 \mathcal K_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)
 \quad(a_z>0),
\]
and \(r=(r_e)\) is the half-strength canonical product reservoir on unordered distinct same-parity edges. Put
\[
 Q:=\sum_e r_e>0,
 \qquad
 R:=R_C(r),
 \qquad
 D:=2^n h_{\max}(C),
 \qquad
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
\]
Let \(G\) be the complete metronoid-core capacity and let \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) be the residual-selector capacity in the normalizations of accepted facts fbeb0a501b268d12 and be359f8a6c628483. Assume the canonical hard ledger
\[
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{1}
\]

For \(e=\{u,v\}\), write
\[
 m_e:=\frac{u+v}{2},
 \qquad
 L_e:=\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v).
\]
Retain the original load-minimal loads and exposed contacts
\[
 0\leq\omega_e\leq r_e,
 \qquad
 \ell_e\in L_e\quad(\omega_e>0).
\]
For every edge with \(\omega_e>0\), choose one endpoint decomposition
\[
 \ell_e=\frac{x_{e,u}+x_{e,v}}2,
 \qquad
 x_{e,u}\in\mathcal K_u,
 \qquad
 x_{e,v}\in\mathcal K_v.
\tag{2}
\]

Use the pooled target quantities of accepted fact f6188983e8850f01:
\[
 \Omega_m:=\sum_{e:m_e=m}\omega_e,
 \qquad
 \widehat E:=\{m:\Omega_m>0\},
 \qquad
 \widehat\Omega:=\sum_m\chi_m\Omega_m,
\]
where \(0<\chi_m\leq1\), and, for \(m\in\widehat E\),
\[
 x_m:=\frac1{\Omega_m}\sum_{e:m_e=m}\omega_e\ell_e.
\]
Put
\[
 \rho_J:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E),
\]
\[
 r_J:=
 \min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(\widehat E)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{x_m-s(m):m\in\widehat E\},
\]
\[
 P_{\mathrm{pool}}
 :=\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_J}\frac{\widehat\Omega}{r_J+1}.
\tag{3}
\]
Define
\[
 \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=(1-\chi_{m_e})\omega_e,
 \qquad
 B_{\mathrm{cong}}:=\sum_e\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
\tag{4}
\]
and assume \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\). Apply the fresh reoptimization of accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81 to the nonzero charge \(\beta^{\mathrm{cong}}\), using the alias map of accepted fact f6188983e8850f01. Denote its fresh loads and total load by
\[
 0\leq\widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \leq\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 \widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\sum_e\widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{5}
\]
If \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\), put
\(\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}:=1\), and assign no fresh support, intrinsic fresh rank, or drift rank. If \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), choose the fresh endpoint contacts supplied by 6a74481cad1d7e81,
\[
 \widetilde\ell_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 =\frac{\widetilde x_{e,u}^{\mathrm{cong}}
       +\widetilde x_{e,v}^{\mathrm{cong}}}{2}
 \quad(\widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0),
\]
put
\[
 \widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\sum_{e\ni z}\widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 E_{\mathrm{fr}}
 :=\{z:\widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}>0\},
\]
\[
 \widetilde p_z^{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\frac1{\widetilde d_z^{\mathrm{cong}}}
 \sum_{\substack{e\ni z\\
                   \widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0}}
 \widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \widetilde x_{e,z}^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \quad(z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}),
\tag{6}
\]
\[
 \widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{fr}}),
\]
\[
 \widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{fr}})
                  \to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{\widetilde p_z^{\mathrm{cong}}-s(z):z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}\},
\]
and define
\[
 \Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}
 :=\min\left\{1,
 \frac{2^{n-\widetilde\rho_{\mathrm{cong}}}}
      {\widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}+1}\right\}.
\tag{7}
\]
This is exactly the factor \(\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}\) of f6188983e8850f01, with no rank assigned when the fresh load is zero.

For the original congestion charge, define
\[
 d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 :=\sum_{e\ni z}\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}},
 \qquad
 E_{\mathrm{anc}}
 :=\{z:d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}>0\},
\tag{8}
\]
and, for \(z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}\),
\[
 p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 :=\frac1{d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}}
 \sum_{e\ni z}\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}x_{e,z}.
\tag{9}
\]
Put
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{anc}}
 :=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{anc}}),
\]
\[
 r_{\mathrm{anc}}
 :=\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{anc}})
                  \to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}-s(z):z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}\},
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 P_{\mathrm{anc}}
 :=4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cong}}}{r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1},
 \qquad
 A:=\max\{P_{\mathrm{pool}},P_{\mathrm{anc}}\}.
\tag{11}
\]

Then, for every \(z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}\),
\[
 d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\leq\frac{a_z}{4},
 \qquad
 p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 \in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)
 \cap\mathsf M_{2d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}}(C_z).
\tag{12}
\]
The measure
\[
 \eta_{\mathrm{anc}}
 :=2\gamma_d\sum_{z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}}
 d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 \delta_{(z,p_z^{\mathrm{anc}})}
\tag{13}
\]
is halfspace-dominated by the mixed-volume measure \(\mu_C\), and
\[
 |\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}|=4\gamma_dB_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{14}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{
 D\geq P_{\mathrm{anc}},
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq P_{\mathrm{anc}}.}
\tag{15}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{anc}}=0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 G\geq4B_{\mathrm{cong}}.}
\tag{16}
\]

The strongest direct no-double-counting ledger obtained from the pooled and ancestral selectors is
\[
 \boxed{
 D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq
 A+\max\{A,\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}\}.}
\tag{17}
\]
In the hard branch (1), this gives
\[
 \boxed{
 A+\max\{A,\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}\}
 <\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 A<\frac Q{200},}
\tag{18}
\]
and therefore the strict ancestral rank escape
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1
 >800\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cong}}}{Q}.}
\tag{19}
\]

Define the original charged endpoint-occurrence measure
\[
 \nu_{\mathrm{occ}}
 :=2\gamma_d
 \sum_{e=\{u,v\}:\,\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0}
 \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \left(
 \delta_{(u,x_{e,u})}+\delta_{(v,x_{e,v})}
 \right),
\tag{20}
\]
and its residual rank
\[
 r_{\mathrm{occ}}
 :=\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{anc}})
                  \to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{x_{e,z}-s(z):
   \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0,\ z\in e\}.
\tag{21}
\]
This measure is halfspace-dominated by \(\mu_C\), has mass
\[
 |\nu_{\mathrm{occ}}|=4\gamma_dB_{\mathrm{cong}},
\tag{22}
\]
and hence also gives
\[
 D\geq4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cong}}}{r_{\mathrm{occ}}+1}.
\tag{23}
\]

Let
\[
 J_B:=\{m\in\widehat E:(1-\chi_m)\Omega_m>0\}.
\tag{24}
\]
This set is nonempty because \(B_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\). Define
\[
 r_{J_B}
 :=\min_{s:\operatorname{aff}(J_B)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{x_m-s(m):m\in J_B\},
\tag{25}
\]
and
\[
 f_{\mathrm{occ}}
 :=\dim\operatorname{span}
 \{x_{e,z}-p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}:
   \beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0,\ z\in e\}.
\tag{26}
\]
Then the ancestral and lossy pooled ranks have the common parent
\[
 \boxed{
 \max\{r_{\mathrm{anc}},r_{J_B}\}
 \leq r_{\mathrm{occ}}
 \leq r_{\mathrm{anc}}+f_{\mathrm{occ}}.}
\tag{27}
\]

Finally, only in the case \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\), use the actual fresh support \(E_{\mathrm{fr}}\) from (6) and define
\[
 k_{\mathrm{drift}}
 :=\dim\operatorname{span}
 \{\widetilde p_z^{\mathrm{cong}}-p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}:
   z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}\}.
\tag{28}
\]
Then \(E_{\mathrm{fr}}\subseteq E_{\mathrm{anc}}\), and
\[
 \boxed{
 \widetilde r_{\mathrm{cong}}
 \leq r_{\mathrm{anc}}+k_{\mathrm{drift}}.}
\tag{29}
\]
No assertion in (28)-(29), and no fresh rank or drift rank, is made when \(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}=0\).

## Proof

Accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81 gives
\[
 \sum_{e\ni z}r_e\leq\frac{a_z}{4}.
\]
Equations (4) and \(0<\chi_m\leq1\) imply
\[
 0\leq\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\leq\omega_e\leq r_e.
\]
Therefore
\[
 d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 \leq\sum_{e\ni z}r_e
 \leq\frac{a_z}{4},
\tag{30}
\]
which proves the first part of (12). Also, every endpoint in (9) is defined whenever its coefficient is positive, because \(\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}>0\) implies \(\omega_e>0\).

Fix \(z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}\). For every incident edge with positive congestion charge, choose a density \(f_{e,z}\) representing the endpoint contact in (2):
\[
 0\leq f_{e,z}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_z}f_{e,z}=\frac{a_z}{2},
 \qquad
 \int_{C_z}x f_{e,z}(x)\,dx
 =\frac{a_z}{2}x_{e,z}.
\tag{31}
\]
Define
\[
 g_z(x)
 :=\sum_{e\ni z}
 \frac{2\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}}{a_z}f_{e,z}(x),
\tag{32}
\]
where zero-charge edges contribute zero. Then
\[
 0\leq g_z\leq\frac{2d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}}{a_z}\leq\frac12,
\tag{33}
\]
and direct integration gives
\[
 \int_{C_z}g_z=d_z^{\mathrm{anc}},
 \qquad
 \int_{C_z}xg_z(x)\,dx
 =d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}.
\tag{34}
\]
Thus \(2g_z\) is an admissible density of mass \(2d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\) with normalized first moment \(p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\), proving
\[
 p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\in
 \mathsf M_{2d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}}(C_z).
\tag{35}
\]
Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d proves that \(\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\) is convex. Equation (9) is a convex combination of the points \(x_{e,z}\in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)\), so
\[
 p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\in\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z).
\tag{36}
\]
Equations (35)-(36) prove the second part of (12). Notice also the exact degree sum
\[
 \sum_{z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}}d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 =2\sum_e\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 =2B_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{37}
\]

By accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d and (35), every closed halfspace in \(\mathbb R^d\) containing \(p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\) cuts from \(C_z\) volume at least
\[
 2\gamma_d d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}.
\tag{38}
\]
Let \(H\) be a closed ambient halfspace. For every atom of \(\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}\) lying in \(H\), its fiber section \(H_z\) is either a proper closed halfspace containing \(p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\) or the whole continuous space. In both cases (38) bounds that atom by the corresponding fiber contribution to \(\mu_C(H)\). Summing over the distinct base fibers proves
\[
 \eta_{\mathrm{anc}}(H)\leq\mu_C(H).
\]
Equation (37) gives
\[
 |\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}|
 =2\gamma_d\sum_zd_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 =4\gamma_dB_{\mathrm{cong}},
\]
which proves (13)-(14).

Apply the dominated-measure residual theorem of accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 to \(\eta_{\mathrm{anc}}\). Its base rank and residual rank are exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}\) and \(r_{\mathrm{anc}}\). Hence
\[
 h_{\max}(C)
 \geq\frac{4\gamma_dB_{\mathrm{cong}}}
 {2^{\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}(r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1)}.
\]
Multiplying by \(2^n\) gives the first inequality in (15). Alternatively, use the points \(p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\), weights \(2\gamma_dd_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\), and an affine map attaining (10) in the definition of \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) in accepted fact be359f8a6c628483. The admissibility of the weights is (38), their sum is \(4\gamma_dB_{\mathrm{cong}}\), and their base and residual ranks are \(\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}\) and \(r_{\mathrm{anc}}\). This proves the second inequality in (15).

Suppose now that \(r_{\mathrm{anc}}=0\). Then an affine map
\(s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{anc}})\to\mathbb R^d\) satisfies
\[
 p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}=s(z)
 \qquad(z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}).
\tag{39}
\]
Put \(t_z:=2d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\). Equations (30), (35), and (37) give
\[
 0<t_z\leq a_z,
 \qquad
 p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\in\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z),
 \qquad
 \sum_zt_z=4B_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{40}
\]
Apply accepted fact 6d6fcf377e68c79c to the nonzero base measure
\[
 \sum_{z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}}t_z\delta_z
\]
on \(\mathbb Z^n\). It gives \(m_*\in\mathbb Z^n\) such that every proper closed halfspace containing \(m_*\) has mass at least
\(2^{-n}\sum_zt_z\). Moreover,
\[
 m_*\in\operatorname{conv}(E_{\mathrm{anc}}).
\tag{41}
\]
Indeed, otherwise strict separation from the finite convex hull would give a proper closed halfspace containing \(m_*\) and no support point, contradicting its positive guaranteed depth.

For every \(\alpha\in\Lambda_{m_*}(E_{\mathrm{anc}})\), affineness of \(s\), (39), and (40) give
\[
 s(m_*)
 =\sum_z\alpha_zp_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 \in\sum_z\alpha_z\mathsf M_{t_z}(C_z).
\tag{42}
\]
Thus \(s(m_*)\) belongs to the intersection over every barycentric representation of \(m_*\). Equations (40)-(42), together with the halfspace balance supplied by the base centerpoint, make \((E_{\mathrm{anc}},(t_z),m_*)\) admissible in the complete metronoid-core definition of accepted fact fbeb0a501b268d12. Its total mass is \(4B_{\mathrm{cong}}\), proving (16).

Accepted fact f6188983e8850f01 gives
\[
 D\geq P_{\mathrm{pool}},
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq P_{\mathrm{pool}},
 \qquad
 R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{43}
\]
Combining the first two inequalities in (43) with (15) gives
\[
 D\geq A,
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq A.
\tag{44}
\]
Therefore, with \(S:=D+R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\),
\[
 S\geq2A
\tag{45}
\]
and, independently,
\[
 S=D+\bigl(R+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\bigr)
 \geq A+\Theta_{\mathrm{cong}}B_{\mathrm{cong}}.
\tag{46}
\]
Taking the larger of (45) and (46), rather than adding two inequalities that both use \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), proves (17). All the other terms in (1) are nonnegative, so (1) gives \(S<Q/100\). This proves the first inequality in (18). Its left side is at least \(2A\), so \(A<Q/200\), proving the second.

Since \(P_{\mathrm{anc}}\leq A<Q/200\), equation (11) gives
\[
 4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{anc}}}
 \frac{B_{\mathrm{cong}}}{r_{\mathrm{anc}}+1}
 <\frac Q{200}.
\]
All quantities being positive, rearrangement proves (19).

We next prove that (20) is a legal dominated occurrence measure. Let \(H\) be a closed ambient halfspace and fix \(z\in E_{\mathrm{anc}}\). The contribution of all occurrence atoms over \(z\) that lie in \(H\) is at most
\[
 2\gamma_d\sum_{e\ni z}\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 =2\gamma_dd_z^{\mathrm{anc}}
 \leq\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2},
\tag{47}
\]
where (30) was used. If no such atom lies in \(H\), the contribution is zero. Otherwise choose one such occurrence \((z,x_{e,z})\). Its fiber section \(H_z\) contains
\[
 x_{e,z}\in\mathcal K_z=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z).
\]
Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d therefore gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z)
 \geq\frac{\gamma_da_z}{2},
\tag{48}
\]
also when \(H_z\) is the whole space. Equations (47)-(48) dominate the entire occurrence contribution over \(z\) by the one physical fiber contribution. Summing over \(z\) proves
\[
 \nu_{\mathrm{occ}}(H)\leq\mu_C(H).
\]
Every charged edge contributes two atoms of weight \(2\gamma_d\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\), which proves (22). The base support of \(\nu_{\mathrm{occ}}\) is exactly \(E_{\mathrm{anc}}\), and duplicate occurrence points do not alter the residual span. Applying accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 to \(\nu_{\mathrm{occ}}\) and multiplying its depth bound by \(2^n\) proves (23).

For the common-parent ranks, first fix any affine map
\(s:\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{anc}})\to\mathbb R^d\). Equation (9) gives
\[
 p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}-s(z)
 =\frac1{d_z^{\mathrm{anc}}}
 \sum_{e\ni z}\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 \bigl(x_{e,z}-s(z)\bigr).
\tag{49}
\]
Thus every ancestral residual lies in the span of the occurrence residuals for the same \(s\). Minimizing over \(s\) gives
\[
 r_{\mathrm{anc}}\leq r_{\mathrm{occ}}.
\tag{50}
\]

For every \(m\in J_B\), the congestion multiplier is constant over all edges with target \(m\), so (4) gives
\[
 \sum_{e:m_e=m}\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}
 =(1-\chi_m)\Omega_m>0,
\]
and
\[
 x_m
 =\frac1{(1-\chi_m)\Omega_m}
 \sum_{e:m_e=m}\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\ell_e.
\tag{51}
\]
If \(s\) is affine, then (2) gives
\[
 \ell_e-s(m_e)
 =\frac12\bigl(x_{e,u}-s(u)\bigr)
  +\frac12\bigl(x_{e,v}-s(v)\bigr).
\tag{52}
\]
Every target in \(J_B\) lies in \(\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{anc}})\), because it is the midpoint of endpoints of a positively charged edge. Choose \(s\) attaining \(r_{\mathrm{occ}}\) and restrict it to \(\operatorname{aff}(J_B)\). Equations (51)-(52) show that every pooled residual \(x_m-s(m)\) lies in the occurrence residual span. Hence
\[
 r_{J_B}\leq r_{\mathrm{occ}}.
\tag{53}
\]

Finally choose an affine map \(s\) attaining \(r_{\mathrm{anc}}\). For every charged occurrence,
\[
 x_{e,z}-s(z)
 =\bigl(x_{e,z}-p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\bigr)
  +\bigl(p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}-s(z)\bigr).
\tag{54}
\]
The two families on the right span spaces of dimensions at most
\(f_{\mathrm{occ}}\) and \(r_{\mathrm{anc}}\), respectively. Therefore
\[
 r_{\mathrm{occ}}
 \leq r_{\mathrm{anc}}+f_{\mathrm{occ}}.
\tag{55}
\]
Equations (50), (53), and (55) prove (27).

It remains to verify the carefully scoped fresh drift statement. Assume
\(\widetilde\Omega_{\mathrm{cong}}>0\). Accepted fact 6a74481cad1d7e81 gives (5)-(6). Since
\(\widetilde\omega_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\leq\beta_e^{\mathrm{cong}}\), every endpoint in the actual fresh support \(E_{\mathrm{fr}}\) belongs to \(E_{\mathrm{anc}}\). Thus
\[
 E_{\mathrm{fr}}\subseteq E_{\mathrm{anc}}.
\tag{56}
\]
Choose an affine map \(s\) attaining \(r_{\mathrm{anc}}\) and restrict it to \(\operatorname{aff}(E_{\mathrm{fr}})\). For every \(z\in E_{\mathrm{fr}}\),
\[
 \widetilde p_z^{\mathrm{cong}}-s(z)
 =\bigl(\widetilde p_z^{\mathrm{cong}}-p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}\bigr)
  +\bigl(p_z^{\mathrm{anc}}-s(z)\bigr).
\tag{57}
\]
The first family on the right spans a space of dimension
\(k_{\mathrm{drift}}\), and the second spans a subspace of dimension at most \(r_{\mathrm{anc}}\). Hence the fresh residual rank obtained from this restricted map is at most their sum. Minimizing over affine maps on the actual fresh support proves (29). The proof used neither a fresh support nor a fresh rank in the zero-fresh-load case, completing all assertions.
