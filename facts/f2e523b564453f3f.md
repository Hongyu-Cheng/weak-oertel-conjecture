## Statement

On a facet of a centroid-marked triangle, parameterize its uniform law by $t\in[0,1]$. The trace of the three equally weighted canonical centroid caps is $W(t)=(\mathbf1_{t\ge1/3}+\mathbf1_{t\le2/3})/3$ almost everywhere. Put $C=[0,1/6]\cup[5/6,1]$, $\rho=1+4\mathbf1_C$, and $B=2/5<\gamma_2=4/9$. For every nonnegative convex $\phi$,
$
\int_0^1\phi(W-B)\rho\,dt\le-\frac1{30}\int_0^1\phi\,dt.
$
For any measurable $0\le r(t)\le1/150$, $\int_0^1\phi(W-B+r)\rho\,dt\le0$. The law includes the full uniform source facet and four added copies of each convex end-sixth.

## Proof

Use the two free barycentric coordinates $t,1-t$; the opposite cap misses the facet and each other cap tests that its own coordinate is at least $1/3$. Thus $W=2/3$ on $J=[1/3,2/3]$ and $W=1/3$ elsewhere up to endpoints. Hermite–Hadamard on $J$, the expression of the pair $1/3,2/3$ as opposite convex combinations of $1/12,11/12$, and Jensen on the two end-sixths give
$
\int_J\phi\le\frac{\phi(1/3)+\phi(2/3)}6
\le\frac{\phi(1/12)+\phi(11/12)}6\le\int_C\phi .
$
Writing $E=[0,1]\setminus(C\cup J)$, the original signed integral is $(4/15)\int_J\phi-(5/15)\int_C\phi-(1/15)\int_E\phi$. It is at most $-(\int_C\phi+\int_E\phi)/15$, which is at most $-\int_0^1\phi/30$ by the same comparison and nonnegativity. Since $\rho\le5$, the correction adds at most $(1/30)\int_0^1\phi$. This establishes the exact reserve. The profile is the limiting unbuffered face trace; the added end footprints are an abstract face law, not a realized collection with the whole mixed family and its actual critical menu.
