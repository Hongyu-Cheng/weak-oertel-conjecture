## Statement

Assume the canonical hard branch and retain the exact objects and normalizations of accepted facts e482e1c83a1dc247 and 7b2a94c842113bf3. Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body,
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\]
and, for the canonical mass \(Q>0\),
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D
 <\frac Q{100}.
\tag{1}
\]
All six terms in (1) are nonnegative, \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) are distinct summands, and
\[
 D\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
\tag{2}
\]

Retain the canonical whole-label family \(\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\), its masses
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 M_c:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_c}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 M_o:=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr D_o}\eta_\sigma,
\tag{3}
\]
and the fixed affine map \(s:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\). The chart subspace denoted by \(W\) in accepted fact e482e1c83a1dc247 and the chart subspace denoted by \(W_{\mathrm{ch}}\) in accepted fact 7b2a94c842113bf3 are exactly the same linear subspace, with the same affine map \(s\); throughout this statement the exact alias map is
\[
 W_{\mathrm{ch}}=W.
\tag{4}
\]
The families \(\mathscr D_o\), their label weights \(\eta_\sigma\), and the mass \(M_o\) in the two accepted facts are likewise the same canonical objects.

For every \(\sigma\in\mathscr D_c\), retain the one selected bad-centroid endpoint \(y_{\mathrm{def}}(\sigma)\). Put
\[
 Y_{\mathrm{def}}:=
 \{y_{\mathrm{def}}(\sigma):\sigma\in\mathscr D_c\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{def}}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{def}}),
\tag{5}
\]
and
\[
 r_{\mathrm{def}}:=
 \min_{\varphi:\operatorname{aff}(Y_{\mathrm{def}})\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{c_y-\varphi(y):y\in Y_{\mathrm{def}}\}.
\tag{6}
\]
Accepted fact e482e1c83a1dc247 gives \(M_c>0\), so this support and both ranks are defined. Set
\[
 S_c:=
 \max\left\{
 2\gamma_d(H_{\mathrm{int}}-M_c),
 \,
 4\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}
 \frac{M_c}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 \right\}.
\tag{7}
\]

If \(M_o>0\), retain the nonzero combined original-ancestry measure \(\nu_o\) of accepted fact 7b2a94c842113bf3 and define
\[
 P_o:=\operatorname{supp}\nu_o,
 \qquad
 A_o:=\operatorname{aff}\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbb R^n}(P_o),
 \qquad
 \rho_o:=\dim A_o,
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 r_o:=
 \min_{a:A_o\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{x-a(z):(z,x)\in P_o\},
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
 L_o:=
 2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_o}\frac{M_o}{r_o+1}.
\tag{10}
\]
If \(M_o=0\), set \(L_o:=0\) and do not define \(P_o,A_o,\rho_o\), or \(r_o\).

Then the direct max-ledger is
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal A(C)\geq S_c+\max\{S_c,L_o\}.}
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently every canonical hard instance satisfies the strict screen
\[
 \boxed{S_c+\max\{S_c,L_o\}<\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{12}
\]
In particular,
\[
 S_c<\frac Q{200},
 \qquad
 \max\{S_c,L_o\}<\frac Q{100}-S_c.
\tag{13}
\]
Since \(M_c>0\), the centroid-selector rank screen can be written as
\[
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}
 <\frac{Q}{800\gamma_dM_c}.
\tag{14}
\]
If \(M_o>0\), the simultaneous original-ancestry rank screen is
\[
 \frac{2^{n-\rho_o}}{r_o+1}
 <\frac{Q/100-S_c}{2\gamma_dM_o}.
\tag{15}
\]
No relation between \((\rho_{\mathrm{def}},r_{\mathrm{def}})\) and \((\rho_o,r_o)\) is asserted.

## Proof

Accepted fact e482e1c83a1dc247 proves both
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq
 2\gamma_d(H_{\mathrm{int}}-M_c)
\]
and
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq
 4\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{def}}}
 \frac{M_c}{r_{\mathrm{def}}+1}.
\]
These are two lower bounds on one selector capacity, not two additive selector charges. Taking their maximum and using (7) gives
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\geq S_c.
\tag{16}
\]
Equation (2) and (16) then give
\[
 D\geq S_c.
\tag{17}
\]

Suppose first that \(M_o>0\). Accepted fact 7b2a94c842113bf3, applied to its combined original-ancestry measure with the intrinsic ranks (8)-(9), gives
\[
 D\geq
 2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_o}\frac{M_o}{r_o+1}
 =L_o.
\tag{18}
\]
Combining (17) and (18) yields \(D\geq\max\{S_c,L_o\}\). If \(M_o=0\), accepted fact 7b2a94c842113bf3 assigns no ranks to the zero original-ancestry measure and gives only the tautology \(D\geq0\). Since \(L_o=0\) by definition, (17) again gives
\[
 D\geq\max\{S_c,L_o\}.
\tag{19}
\]
Thus (19) holds in both cases without ever introducing a rank of empty support.

Because \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) are distinct nonnegative summands of (1), equations (16) and (19) may be charged once each to those separate summands:
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+D
 \geq S_c+\max\{S_c,L_o\}.
\]
This proves (11). In particular, the contribution \(S_c\) in the first term uses only \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), while the maximum in the second term uses only \(D\); neither capacity is reused.

Combining (11) with the strict hard inequality (1) proves (12). Subtracting \(S_c\) from (12) gives the second inequality in (13). Since \(\max\{S_c,L_o\}\geq S_c\), equation (12) also gives \(2S_c<Q/100\), which is the first inequality in (13). The second term in the maximum defining \(S_c\) is at most \(S_c<Q/200\); division by \(4\gamma_dM_c>0\) proves (14). When \(M_o>0\), equation (10) and the second inequality in (13) give
\[
 2\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_o}\frac{M_o}{r_o+1}
 =L_o<\frac Q{100}-S_c;
\]
division by \(2\gamma_dM_o>0\) proves (15). These are separate screens for the two already-defined intrinsic ranks and make no rank-transfer claim.
