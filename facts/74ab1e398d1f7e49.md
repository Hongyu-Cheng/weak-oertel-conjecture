## Statement

Let $P$ be a finite complete affine-lattice base and $K_z$ positive compatible compact convex $d$-fibers with $d\ge1$, canonical hull $C$, sampled volume $dm$ of mass $M$, and fiber boundary measure $dS$ summed over $z$ (endpoint counting if $d=1$). Let $L$ be a finite minimum of affine functions, positive on a neighborhood of $C$ and normalized by $\int\log L\,dm=0$. In an actual regular singleton mixed buffered comparison, assume one joint separator has the following properties. Its proper rows have positive coefficients $e_i$ at old sources $Y_i$, with $\Delta=\sum_i e_i>0$, $N=d+1$, $L_i=L(Y_i)$, and $\sigma_i=e_i/\Delta$. Its same-coefficient volume martingale has probability rows $\rho_i$ of mean $Y_i$ and $L$-mean $L_i$, and $\sum_i(\sigma_i/L_i)\rho_i=dm/(ML)$; each row is carried by equality of $L$ with a touching affine plane at its source. Its covering response on simultaneously realized signed body speeds $v$ and raw parameter first jets $h$ of the type $h+cL$ concave on a common positive neighborhood is
$$
\int\Xi v\,dS+\frac1N\left[\frac{\Delta}{M}\int\frac hL\,dm-\sum_i e_i\frac{h(Y_i)}{L_i}\right]\le0
$$
with inward $v$ positive and with the moving normalization included in the body term. Assume this response applies to the actual complete cuts by globally finite convex fields and the simultaneous normalized parameter paths, including all finite nonnegative combinations of those directions. In particular the physical inequality holds for all such convex fields with $h=0$.

Set $\kappa=\Delta/(NM)$, $B=-\kappa\log L$, $R=-\int\Xi B\,dS$ and $K_V=-\sum_i\sigma_i\log L_i$. The compatible signed body speed $B$ and the parameter path $L^{1-t}$ renormalized on the moving bodies give the weighted covering response
$$
-R-\Delta K_V/N\le0,\qquad R\ge0,\quad K_V\ge0 .
$$
Finite independent nonnegative multiples of these directions give the corresponding nonnegative linear combination of the two costs. If the unscaled joint response is zero, both costs vanish separately. Then every positive volume row preserves $L$ almost surely and $L$ is constant on each sampled fiber. The separate surface martingale from $\Xi_+dS$ to $\Xi_-dS$ also preserves $L$ almost surely when $R=0$.

## Proof

On a compact neighborhood with positive floor, the raw power $L^{1-t}$ is positive and concave for $0\le t<1$ by increasing concave composition. At fixed bodies its log mean is identically zero and its raw derivative $h=-L\log L$ has $\int h/L\,dm=0$. The function $s\mapsto cs-s\log s$ is increasing and concave on the positive range of $L$ on that neighborhood for $c\ge1+\log\max L$, so $h+cL$ is concave. The exact power path and $L+th$ have the same first jet uniformly there. Its actual moving normalization is multiplication by $\exp[-M_t^{-1}\int_{S_t}\log(L^{1-t})\,dm_t]$. The signed shape identity in 5fa497a06b11cbcb applies to the continuous fixed $\log L$ and gives derivative $s(B)=M^{-1}\int B\log L\,dS$ for this scalar's logarithm. Thus the body term retains the full change of volume normalization.

Write $L=\min_j A_j$. On the chosen compact neighborhood all $A_j$ have a common positive lower bound. Extend $s\mapsto-\kappa\log s$ below this bound by its tangent line and compose with the $A_j$. The maximum of the resulting finite convex functions is a globally finite convex field equal to $B$ there. Use it as a normal-independent field in the complete-cut theorem 8820984cf6a6942e to obtain an actual signed compatible inward speed, simultaneously with the raw power path. The same construction covers nonnegative multiples; for the parameter multiple $b\ge0$ use $L^{1-bt}$. Since the physical response with $h=0$ is nonpositive, $R\ge0$.

The accepted volume entropy identity e9fc678ed90ba6c3 yields $K_V=\sum_i\sigma_i D((L/L_i)\rho_i\|\rho_i)\ge0$ with zero exactly for $L=L_i$ on every row. Substitution of $h$ in the stated response gives parameter term $\sum_i e_i\log L_i/N=-\Delta K_V/N$, so the costs add. At zero response both vanish. A positive volume row is absolutely continuous with respect to sampled volume by the row mixture, and equality with its touching affine plane on a fixed level $L_i$ would confine it to fiberwise null slices if the continuous slope of that plane were nonzero. Therefore every positive row has zero continuous slope. The normalized rows exhaust sampled volume. For a finite minimum of affine planes, at almost every point of each sampled fiber every active plane has the continuous gradient of that fiber restriction, since ties with different slopes occur on finitely many null hyperplanes. Thus the continuous gradient vanishes almost everywhere, and each restriction is constant by continuity and piecewise affinity. Constancy here is per fiber.

For the surface measure apply 442c4c99600062dc with $k=\kappa$ and the supplied physical inequality. It gives a separate martingale $\pi_S$, the identity $R=\kappa\int[\log L(w)-\log L(w')]\,d\pi_S$, and the bound $R\ge\kappa/(2\max_C L^2)\int[L(w')-L(w)]^2\,d\pi_S$. Since $\kappa>0$, $R=0$ forces level preservation for that coupling. The signed response concerns the supplied weighted covering functional.
