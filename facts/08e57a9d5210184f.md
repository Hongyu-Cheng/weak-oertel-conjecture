## Statement

Let $d\ge2$ and let $c\cdot U=0$ be a proper section of the uniform barycentric simplex, with centroid $b$. Suppose the signed exponential sum $V=\sum_jc_jE_j$ for independent unit exponentials has a positive $C^1$ log-concave density. Then all indices with $b_j>1/d$ have the same strict sign of $c_j$. If $c_j=0$ then $b_j=1/d$. The hypotheses hold in particular for every proper minimizing section of a strictly positive uniform-simplex capacity row.

## Proof

Under the stated density hypothesis the coefficients have at least three nonzero entries: with just one of each sign the density is proportional to an exponential on each half-line, with different left and right derivatives at zero. Indeed, for coefficients $a,-r$ with $a,r>0$ that density is $e^{-z/a}/(a+r)$ for $z\ge0$ and $e^{z/r}/(a+r)$ for $z\le0$. Thus we may choose the orientation with two positive phases and one negative phase. In that orientation the joint cap derivative lemma gives $g_j=d f_V(0)b_j$ and accepted fact 511d89569241b0d6 gives $g_j=f_{V+c_jE'}(0)$ for an independent unit exponential $E'$. It follows that
$$
d b_j=\int_0^\infty e^{-s}\frac{f_V(-c_js)}{f_V(0)}\,ds.
$$
Let $\ell=f_V'(0)/f_V(0)$. The log-density tangent bound gives $f_V(-c_js)/f_V(0)\le\exp(-\ell c_js)$. If $\ell c_j\ge0$, the integral is at most one. Thus $b_j>1/d$ requires $\ell c_j<0$ for every such index, forcing their margins to have the same strict sign. If $c_j=0$, the integral equals one. Reversing orientation does not change the centroid or the assertion about the signs. For a proper minimizing strictly positive row the threshold lemma supplies at least two strictly positive margins and one strictly negative margin, and accepted fact 511d89569241b0d6 supplies the stated density properties.
