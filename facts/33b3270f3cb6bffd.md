## Statement

Let $S$ be the union of finitely many placed compact positive-volume $d$-fibers, let $C=\operatorname{conv}S$, and let $dm$ be the summed fiber Lebesgue measure of total $M>0$. Let $L$ be continuous and positive on $C$, and put $\Phi(w)=(w,1)/L(w)$. Suppose nonnegative weights $\sigma_i$ and probability rows $\rho_i$ on $S$ for the positive weights have marks $Y_i\in C$, $L_i=L(Y_i)$, and satisfy
$$
\int w\,d\rho_i=Y_i,\qquad \int L\,d\rho_i=L_i,\qquad
\sum_{i:\sigma_i>0}\frac{\sigma_i}{L_i}\rho_i=\frac{dm}{ML}.
$$
Then the labeled row measures $\Phi_*[(L/L_i)\rho_i]$ are probabilities with means $\Phi(Y_i)$. Their weighted sum with the unchanged $\sigma_i$ is $\Phi_*(dm/M)$. If $L=\min_j A_j$ for finitely many affine $A_j$, then for each $j$ with $A_j(Y_i)=L_i$, the corresponding lifted row is concentrated on $A_j^{\rm hom}=1$, where $A_j^{\rm hom}(U,s)=s A_j(U/s)$ for $s>0$.

## Proof

The $L$-moment gives each reweighted row unit mass. Within the lifted first moment the $L$ factors cancel, so it is $L_i^{-1}\int(w,1)\,d\rho_i=(Y_i,1)/L_i$. Multiplying the measure identity by $L$ and pushing forward proves the asserted mixture, including $\sum_i\sigma_i=1$. These calculations use each actual row separately even if different rows have overlapping supports.

For an active $A_j$ the gap $A_j-L$ is nonnegative on $C$. Its expectation is $A_j(Y_i)-L_i=0$, by affinity and the two row moments, so the original and reweighted row both have $A_j=L$ almost surely. At their lifted points $s=1/L$, which gives $A_j^{\rm hom}=1$. This applies to every active affine piece at the mark.
