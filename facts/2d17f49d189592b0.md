## Statement

Let $T$ be standard Borel with a finite Borel reference measure $\zeta$. Let $\gamma_{i,t}$, $\rho_{\mathrm{old},t}$ and $\sigma_t$ be Borel kernels of finite nonnegative measures, with uniformly bounded masses and supports in fixed compact Euclidean sets. Assume the $\gamma_{i,t}$ have common mass for each $t$. For fixed positive weights summing to one let $b$ be the physical barycentric map. Suppose that for $\zeta$-almost every $t$ there exists a coupling $\pi$ of the $\gamma_{i,t}$ such that
$$
 \rho_{\mathrm{old},t}\preceq_{\mathrm{cx}} b_\#\pi,\qquad b_\#\pi\le\sigma_t.
$$
Then there is a Borel kernel $t\mapsto\pi_t$ of finite nonnegative measures realizing all these conditions on one Borel full-$\zeta$-measure set. Here convex order requires equal mass and compares every finite convex function on the physical space. The integrated joint law $\zeta(dt)\pi_t$ keeps the parameter. Its output $\zeta(dt)b_\#\pi_t$ is dominated by $\zeta(dt)\sigma_t$ as a joint measure and satisfies the convex comparisons with the integrated old law for every jointly Borel test whose physical sections are finite convex functions and which is uniformly bounded on the common compact output set. Any Borel joint output support relation which holds for the feasible couplings almost everywhere holds also for the assembled joint law.

## Proof

Take $X$ to be the weakly compact metrizable space of nonnegative measures of mass at most the uniform marginal bound on the product of the fixed compact endpoint supports. Enlarge the fixed compact output set, if needed, to contain the image of that product under $b$. Impose marginal equality by absolute differences of integrals of a countable uniformly dense family of continuous functions on each endpoint support, including the constant one. Impose the measure ceiling by the inequalities for a countable uniformly dense family of nonnegative continuous functions on the compact output set. Uniform approximation gives the constraints for all such tests, which determine equality and domination of finite measures on compact spaces.

For convex order impose equality of the masses and the integral inequalities for all finite maxima of rational affine functions on the physical Euclidean space. This is a countable family. To see it determines the order, for any finite convex $f$ choose a supporting affine function at each point of the compact output set. The places where such a function lies within any given positive tolerance of $f$ form a relative open cover. Finitely many suffice, and the maximum of that finite subfamily approximates $f$ uniformly there. Approximate the finitely many coefficients by rationals, preserving the uniform approximation. Affine tests in both signs also give equality of first moments. All constraints are Borel in $t$ by the kernel hypotheses and continuous in $\pi$ by weak convergence.

The compact countable-constraint selector now applies. Its feasible set of parameters is Borel and has full reference measure by assumption. Weak Borel measurability of the selected finite measures gives a Borel kernel, since evaluation on Borel sets follows from evaluation of continuous functions by a monotone class argument. Marginals enforce their prescribed parameter-dependent supports whenever those are given. For any joint Borel event, integrate the conditional domination on its sections to obtain the joint measure bound. For each bounded Borel test convex in the physical variable, the conditional convex comparisons hold on the same full-measure set, so integrating proves the joint comparison. Integrating the indicator of the complement proves the Borel support assertion.
