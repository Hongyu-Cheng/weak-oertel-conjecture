## Statement

Retain one canonical hard instance of accepted facts `6b09aae189415c70`, `7ec63df101c30343`, and `c6844ff8c7c73470`. Thus \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body, \(Q>0\),
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D,
 \qquad D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal A(C)<\frac Q{100}.
\tag{2}
\]
The load-minimal canonical optimizer has endpoint loads \(\beta_y\), points \(p_y^\flat\), and an origin-symmetric full-dimensional body \(D_*\) satisfying
\[
 p_y^\flat+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{\beta_y}(C_y)
 \qquad(\beta_y>0).
\tag{3}
\]
For every retained parity class \(\xi\), write
\[
 L_\xi:=\sum_{y\equiv\xi}\beta_y,
 \qquad
 s_{\{u,v\}}:=\frac{\beta_u\beta_v}{L_\xi}
 \quad(u<v,\ u,v\equiv\xi),
\tag{4}
\]
so \(\max_{y\equiv\xi}\beta_y\leq L_\xi/4\). For a positive retained edge \(e=\{a,b\}\), put
\[
 m_e:=\frac{a+b}{2},
 \qquad
 F_e^\flat:=\frac{p_a^\flat+p_b^\flat}{2}.
\tag{5}
\]
For a positive label \((\xi,c)\), use
\[
 S_{\xi,c}:=\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_e,
 \qquad
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ:=
 \frac1{S_{\xi,c}}
 \sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_eF_e^\flat,
\tag{6}
\]
and the accepted tube
\[
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{2S_{\xi,c}}(C_c).
\tag{7}
\]

Let \(K:=D_*^\circ\), let \(F\) be an exposed face of \(K\), and put
\[
 E(F):=\{y:\beta_y>0,\ D_y^\circ\cap F\ne\varnothing\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Let \(\mathcal U\) be any nonempty family of positive canonical \(I\)-triangles
\[
 \tau=(z;\{a,b\})
\tag{9}
\]
where \(\xi\) denotes the retained parity class containing \(z,a,b\). Assume that all three sources \(z,a,b\) lie outside \(E(F)\), and that, with \(e_\tau:=\{a,b\}\), \(c_\tau:=m_{e_\tau}\),
\[
 x_\tau:=q_{\xi,c_\tau}^\circ-F_{e_\tau}^\flat,
\tag{10}
\]
there is a chosen norming covector
\[
 \varphi_\tau\in\operatorname{relint}F,
 \qquad
 h_{D_*}(\varphi_\tau)=1,
 \qquad
 \langle\varphi_\tau,x_\tau\rangle
 =\|x_\tau\|_{D_*}>\frac14.
\tag{11}
\]
The triangle weights are the canonical weights
\[
 \theta_\tau:=\frac{\beta_z\beta_a\beta_b}{2L_\xi^2},
 \qquad
 M:=\sum_{\tau\in\mathcal U}\theta_\tau>0.
\tag{12}
\]

For a loaded source \(y\), let \(P_y(\mathcal U)\) and \(E_y(\mathcal U)\) be the pivot and opposite-endpoint occurrence masses of accepted fact `c6844ff8c7c73470`, and put
\[
 O_y:=P_y(\mathcal U)+E_y(\mathcal U),
 \qquad
 Y:=\{y:O_y>0\}.
\tag{13}
\]
Then \(Y\cap E(F)=\varnothing\),
\[
 O_y\leq\frac34\beta_y,
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y}O_y=3M,
\tag{14}
\]
and the points \(p_y^\flat\), with weights
\[
 w_y:=\frac{4\gamma_d}{3}O_y,
\tag{15}
\]
form one legal residual-selector certificate supported entirely on remote sources. More precisely, if
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{src}}:=\dim\operatorname{aff}Y,
 \qquad
 r_{\mathrm{src}}:=
 \min_{a:\operatorname{aff}Y\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{p_y^\flat-a(y):y\in Y\},
\tag{16}
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}}
 \frac{M}{r_{\mathrm{src}}+1},
 \qquad
 D\geq4\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}}
 \frac{M}{r_{\mathrm{src}}+1}.}
\tag{17}
\]
In particular, (2) forces
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{\mathrm{src}}+1
 >800\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}}\frac MQ.}
\tag{18}
\]

There is also a lossless within-label target package. Let \(\mathcal J\) be the set of label-target cells \(j=(\xi,c)\) used by at least one triangle of \(\mathcal U\). For \(j=(\xi,c)\in\mathcal J\), define
\[
 t_{j,e}:=
 \sum_{\substack{\tau\in\mathcal U:\ e_\tau=e,\ \\ \tau\text{ has label }(\xi,c)}}
 \theta_\tau,
 \qquad
 T_j:=\sum_et_{j,e},
\tag{19}
\]
and
\[
 \overline F_j:=\frac1{T_j}\sum_et_{j,e}F_e^\flat.
\tag{20}
\]
Write \(q_j^\circ:=q_{\xi,c}^\circ\) and \(c_j:=c\) for \(j=(\xi,c)\).
Then
\[
 \sum_{j\in\mathcal J}T_j=M.
\tag{21}
\]
For
\[
 N_K(F):=
 \{x\in\mathbb R^d:
 \langle x,\psi-\varphi\rangle\leq0
 \text{ for all }\psi\in K,\ \varphi\in F\},
 \qquad
 V_F:=\operatorname{span}N_K(F),
\tag{22}
\]
choose any one \(\varphi_F\in\operatorname{relint}F\). Every triangle and every nonempty cell satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
 x_\tau\in N_K(F),
 \qquad
 \langle\varphi_F,x_\tau\rangle
 =\|x_\tau\|_{D_*}>\frac14,}
\tag{23}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ-\overline F_j\in N_K(F),
 \qquad
 \left\langle\varphi_F,
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ-\overline F_j\right\rangle>\frac14,}
\tag{24}
\]
and the two exact physical target-fiber tubes
\[
 \boxed{
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{2T_j}(C_c),
 \qquad
 \overline F_j+\frac12D_*
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{2T_j}(C_c).}
\tag{25}
\]
In particular, the two centers in (25) have the same image in \(\mathbb R^d/V_F\).

Finally, let \(\mathcal J_0\subseteq\mathcal J\) be nonempty and have pairwise distinct physical targets. Put
\[
 T_0:=\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_0}T_j,
 \qquad
 \rho_0:=\dim\operatorname{aff}\{c_j:j\in\mathcal J_0\}.
\tag{26}
\]
For any affine map \(a:\operatorname{aff}\{c_j:j\in\mathcal J_0\}\to\mathbb R^d\), define
\[
 r_{F,a}:=\dim\operatorname{span}
 \left(V_F\cup
 \{q_j^\circ-a(c_j):j\in\mathcal J_0\}\right).
\tag{27}
\]
Equation (24) gives the exact identity
\[
 \operatorname{span}
 \left(V_F\cup\{q_j^\circ-a(c_j):j\in\mathcal J_0\}\right)
 =
 \operatorname{span}
 \left(V_F\cup\{\overline F_j-a(c_j):j\in\mathcal J_0\}\right).
\tag{28}
\]
Using either center from each cell gives
\[
 \boxed{
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq2\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_0}\frac{T_0}{r_{F,a}+1},
 \qquad
 D\geq2\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_0}\frac{T_0}{r_{F,a}+1}.}
\tag{29}
\]
Hence (2) forces, for every such \(a\),
\[
 \boxed{
 r_{F,a}+1
 >400\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_0}\frac{T_0}{Q}.}
\tag{30}
\]
Choosing, at each physical target, one cell of maximum \(T_j\) produces an admissible \(\mathcal J_0\) with
\[
 T_0\geq2^{-n}M,
\tag{31}
\]
because there are at most \(2^n\) parity labels over one target. Thus (29)-(31) audit the full cross-label target-collision loss. No assertion is made that either residual rank in (16) or (27) is absolutely bounded.

## Proof

Accepted fact `c6844ff8c7c73470` gives the occurrence bound in (14). Every triangle contributes once as a pivot and twice as an opposite-endpoint occurrence, so summing the occurrence masses over all sources gives
\[
 \sum_yO_y=3\sum_{\tau\in\mathcal U}\theta_\tau=3M.
\tag{32}
\]
All three sources of every triangle are outside \(E(F)\), hence \(Y\cap E(F)=\varnothing\).

By (3), \(p_y^\flat\in\mathsf M_{\beta_y}(C_y)\). Accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d` therefore gives
\[
 \delta_C(y,p_y^\flat)\geq\gamma_d\beta_y
 \geq\frac{4\gamma_d}{3}O_y=w_y.
\tag{33}
\]
The weights in (15) have total
\[
 \sum_{y\in Y}w_y
 =\frac{4\gamma_d}{3}\sum_yO_y
 =4\gamma_dM.
\tag{34}
\]
Use these distinct physical source fibers, their points, their weights, and an affine map attaining (16) in the definition of the residual-selector capacity in accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`. Equations (33)-(34) give the first inequality in (17), and the accepted normalization \(D\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) gives the second. Since \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) and \(D\) are two distinct nonnegative summands of (1),
\[
 \mathcal A(C)
 \geq8\gamma_d2^{n-\rho_{\mathrm{src}}}
 \frac{M}{r_{\mathrm{src}}+1}.
\tag{35}
\]
Comparing (35) with (2) proves (18).

We next prove the common-face assertion. Fix \(\tau\), and let
\[
 G_\tau:=\{\psi\in K:
 \langle\psi,x_\tau\rangle=h_K(x_\tau)\}.
\tag{36}
\]
The norm identity in (11) says that \(\varphi_\tau\in G_\tau\). The set \(G_\tau\) is an exposed face of \(K\). Since \(\varphi_\tau\in\operatorname{relint}F\cap G_\tau\), the face property gives \(F\subseteq G_\tau\). Indeed, for any \(\psi\in F\), relative interior permits a point \(\psi'\in F\) and \(0<\lambda<1\) with \(\varphi_\tau=\lambda\psi+(1-\lambda)\psi'\); because \(G_\tau\) is a face and contains this strict convex combination, it contains \(\psi\). Thus every point of \(F\), and in particular \(\varphi_F\), maximizes the functional defined by \(x_\tau\) on \(K\). This is exactly
\[
 x_\tau\in N_K(F),
 \qquad
 \langle\varphi_F,x_\tau\rangle
 =h_K(x_\tau)=\|x_\tau\|_{D_*}.
\tag{37}
\]
Together with (11), this proves (23).

Fix a cell \(j=(\xi,c)\). Accepted fact `7ec63df101c30343` gives, for the full canonical triangle family,
\[
 \sum_{\tau:e_\tau=e}\theta_\tau\leq\frac12s_e.
\tag{38}
\]
The selected subfamily is smaller, so
\[
 0\leq t_{j,e}\leq\frac12s_e,
 \qquad
 T_j\leq\frac12S_{\xi,c}.
\tag{39}
\]
Scale a representing density for each point in the accepted tube (7) by \(T_j/S_{\xi,c}\leq1/2\). This proves the first inclusion in (25).

For the second inclusion, fix \(h\in D_*/2\). For every edge \(e=\{a,b\}\) with \(t_{j,e}>0\), put
\[
 \mu_e:=\min\{\beta_a,\beta_b\}>0.
\tag{40}
\]
Equation (3), followed by scaling the representing densities, gives
\[
 p_a^\flat+h\in\mathsf M_{\mu_e}(C_a),
 \qquad
 p_b^\flat+h\in\mathsf M_{\mu_e}(C_b).
\tag{41}
\]
The proof of accepted fact `dfd25e72c1f45a4e` establishes convexity of the positive-root metronoid generating set before closure. Taking the midpoint of the two equal positive roots in (41) therefore gives the exact membership
\[
 F_e^\flat+h\in\mathsf M_{\mu_e}(C_c).
\tag{42}
\]
Choose a representing density \(f_{e,h}\) for (42). Distinct unordered edges in one fixed cell \((\xi,c)\) are distinct reflected pairs \(\{a,2c-a\}\), so their endpoint sets are pairwise disjoint. Equations (4), (39), and (40) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{e:t_{j,e}>0}\frac{2t_{j,e}}{\mu_e}
 &\leq
 \frac1{L_\xi}
 \sum_{e=\{a,b\}:t_{j,e}>0}\max\{\beta_a,\beta_b\}\\
 &\leq
 \frac1{L_\xi}
 \sum_{e=\{a,b\}:t_{j,e}>0}(\beta_a+\beta_b)
 \leq1.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{43}
\]
Consequently the single target-fiber density
\[
 g_{j,h}:=
 \sum_{e:t_{j,e}>0}\frac{2t_{j,e}}{\mu_e}f_{e,h}
\tag{44}
\]
satisfies \(0\leq g_{j,h}\leq1\) almost everywhere. Its mass and first moment are
\[
 \int_{C_c}g_{j,h}=2T_j,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_c}xg_{j,h}(x)\,dx
 =2T_j(\overline F_j+h).
\tag{45}
\]
This proves the second inclusion in (25), with every selected occurrence retained and no target density reused within the label.

Equations (10), (19), and (20) give
\[
 q_{\xi,c}^\circ-\overline F_j
 =\frac1{T_j}
 \sum_{\substack{\tau\in\mathcal U:\ \\ \tau\text{ has cell }j}}
 \theta_\tau x_\tau.
\tag{46}
\]
The normal cone in (22) is a convex cone. Equations (23) and (46) therefore prove membership in (24), and evaluation by \(\varphi_F\) gives the strict inequality there. Summing (19) over the disjoint cells proves (21). Membership in \(V_F\) gives the quotient assertion and the span identity (28).

Fix \(\mathcal J_0\) and \(a\). By (25) and accepted fact `6769574fc1bd9f8d`, either choice
\[
 y_j\in\{q_j^\circ,\overline F_j\}
\tag{47}
\]
satisfies
\[
 \delta_C(c_j,y_j)\geq2\gamma_dT_j.
\tag{48}
\]
The targets are pairwise distinct. Use the points \(y_j\), weights \(2\gamma_dT_j\), and the affine map \(a\) in the residual-selector capacity of accepted fact `be359f8a6c628483`. Equations (27)-(28) bound the continuous residual rank by \(r_{F,a}\), so (48) proves the first inequality in (29); again \(D\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) proves the second. Adding these two separate summands of (1) and comparing with (2) proves (30).

At a fixed physical target there are at most \(2^n\) parity labels. Therefore the maximum cell mass at that target is at least \(2^{-n}\) times the sum of all cell masses there. Summing this bound over the distinct targets and using (21) proves (31). This completes the source no-reuse certificate, the common-face target pooling, and both exact hard-ledger rank screens.
