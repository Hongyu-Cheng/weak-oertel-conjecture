## Statement

In the freely variable physical $d$-simplex with the fixed proper caps above, let $d\ge2$ and suppose the thresholds are unequal. Pick $i\ne j$ with $J_{ij}=a_j+(a_i-\sum_l a_l)/d\ne0$. Let $E$ have entries $E_{ij}=\operatorname{sgn}J_{ij}$, $E_{jj}=-\operatorname{sgn}J_{ij}$ and zero elsewhere. With $H=\sum_l1/h_l$, $\varepsilon=|J_{ij}|/(2H)$ and the above right inverse, set
$$
B=E-S(f(E))+S(-\varepsilon\mathbf 1).
$$
Then $\operatorname{tr} B=|J_{ij}|/2>0$ and $F_l'(0)=-V\varepsilon<0$ for every cap. For every sufficiently small $t>0$ the simplex with vertex matrix $I+tB$ has larger volume and strictly smaller actual mass in every fixed cap. In dimension two, at $a=(1/2,1/3,1/6)$ and $(i,j)=(0,1)$, the normalized total gain rate is $1/24$, each normalized cap loss rate is $5/282$, and the strict inequalities already hold at $t=1/100$.

## Proof

Apply the fixed-cap stationarity and right-inverse formula to these proper fixed cuts on the free vertex space. If all $J_{ij}$ vanished, comparing rows within a fixed column and then varying the column would make all thresholds equal. The coefficient of the independent off-diagonal entry $B_{ij}$ in $\operatorname{tr} B-\sum_l f_l(B)/h_l$ is $J_{ij}$: substitute $\eta_l=\Gamma_l/h_l=(1-a_l)/d$ in the derivative coefficient formula. Thus $E-S(f(E))$ has zero cap derivatives and trace $|J_{ij}|$. The added term $S(-\varepsilon\mathbf 1)$ subtracts $\varepsilon$ from every normalized cap derivative and has trace $-\varepsilon H=-|J_{ij}|/2$. This proves the strict first derivatives. The exact cap formula is analytic near $I$, so the positive determinant and the strict cap-incidence chamber persist along the path for small $t$ and the claimed strict gains follow.

For the example the construction gives $h=(1,4/3,5/3)$, $J_{01}=1/12$, $H=47/20$ and $\varepsilon=5/282$. At $t=1/100$ its vertex matrix is
$$
W=\frac1{225600}\begin{pmatrix}
225560 & 1041 & -1215\\
299 & 224818 & 299\\
-259 & -259 & 226516
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Each row is above its threshold only at the diagonal. Substituting into the determinant and cap formulas gives $\det W-1=361/921600$ and the three normalized cap decreases $(5639/31809600,154150789/810423782400,2951787343/15348132000000)$, all positive.
