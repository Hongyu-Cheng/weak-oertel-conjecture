## Statement

Start with nonzero coefficients $p_1,-p_1,\ldots,p_k,-p_k$ with $k\ge1$, $p_i>0$, and allow any number of appended zero coefficients. Use the notation $c_i(t)=c_i/(1-tc_i)$, $Z(t)=\prod_i(1-tc_i)$, $F_t=\Pr(\sum_i c_i(t)Y_i\ge0)$, and actual section means $\mu_i(t)$ for unit-rate independent exponentials. For $|t|<1/\max_i p_i$,
$$
 \prod_i\mu_i(t)\ge Z(t)/4\ge F_t(1-F_t).
$$
The full-product variance comparison is strict if $k\ge2$ and an equality if $k=1$. Removing an index with $\mu_j(t)\le1$ preserves the variance lower bound. A family of paired positive coefficients $P_i$ and negative magnitudes $R_i$ is in this class exactly when all $1/P_i-1/R_i$ equal one common value $-2t$. The pretilt magnitudes are $p_i=2/(1/P_i+1/R_i)$.

## Proof

For the symmetric starting law, a5e572c99d30c71a gives $\prod_i\mu_i\ge1/4$ with strictness for $k\ge2$ and equality for $k=1$, and $F_0=1/2$. Its density is even, hence $A(-t)=B(t)$ in the orbit lemma. Thus $H$ is convex and even on a symmetric interval, and $H(t)\ge H(0)=4$. This gives $G(t)\le1/4$. Multiplication of the tilted means and the identity $F_t(1-F_t)=Z(t)G(t)$ give both inequalities and strictness. When $k=1$, the starting density is $(2p_1)^{-1}e^{-|v|/p_1}$, so $A(t)=1/[2(1-p_1t)]$, $B(t)=1/[2(1+p_1t)]$, and $H(t)=4$ throughout. Zero coefficients are unaffected. The omission rule divides the full product by a positive factor at most one.

The forward reciprocal identities are $1/P_i=1/p_i-t$ and $1/R_i=1/p_i+t$. Conversely the stipulated common difference and harmonic-mean formula give both identities, with rates $1-tp_i=p_i/P_i>0$ and $1+tp_i=p_i/R_i>0$. Hence the recovered common tilt is in the open admissible interval and gives exactly the stipulated coefficients.
