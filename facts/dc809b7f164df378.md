## Statement

Let $m\ge1$, $d=m+1$, $a<0<b$, and let $r:[a,b]\to(0,\infty)$ be finite and concave. In the planar layer with density $r(z)^{m-1}/2$ on $a\le z\le b,\ |x|\le r(z)$, let $V=\int_a^b r(z)^m\,dz$. For $s\ge0$, let $P(s)$ be the mass of $z+sx\ge0$. Let $\sigma_s$ be the pushforward to the $z$-coordinate of the section measure obtained by integrating the layer density against $d\mathcal H^1/\sqrt{1+s^2}$ on $z+sx=0$. Set $C(s)=\int d\sigma_s$ and $M(s)=\int z\,d\sigma_s$. Let $D\subset\mathbb R^m$ be a convex body centrally symmetric about $0$ and $K=\{(X,z):a\le z\le b,\ X\in r(z)D\}$. Consider any finite list of unit normals $u_i$, with caps $H_i=\{Y:u_i\cdot Y\ge0\}$, sections $K\cap u_i^\perp$ with centroid $0$ and area $S_i=\mathcal H^m(K\cap u_i^\perp)$, and weights $\theta_i\ge0$ with $\sum_i\theta_i=1$ and $\sum_i\theta_iS_i u_i=0$. If $M(s)>0$ for every $s>0$, or if $M(s)<0$ for every $s>0$, then $\sum_i\theta_i |K\cap H_i|/|K|=1/2$.

## Proof

Any section with nonzero horizontal and vertical normal components can be written as $z+t e\cdot X=0$ with a horizontal unit $e$ and $t\ne0$. By 8f1256d28f434b09 its raw vertical first moment divided by $|D|$ is the average of $M(|t|R)$ over a probability law on $R>0$. This integrand has the same strict sign at every radius, contradicting the assumed centroid $0$. All sections in the menu therefore have either vertical normal or zero vertical normal. The latter caps have half volume fiber by fiber. The former all use the same plane $z=0$ with positive common area, and the vertical component of the balance forces equal total weights on its two orientations. Their cap fractions complement each other, giving the asserted mean even if either type has zero total weight.
