## Statement

For every $0<\eta\le1/4096$, let the five actual integer fibers at $z=-2,-1,0,1,2$ be centered rectangles of halfheight one and widths
$$
(r_z)=(1,1+4\eta,1+4\eta,1+3\eta,1+2\eta).
$$
Their linear interpolation gives the actual convex body $-2\le z\le2$, $|x|\le r(z)/2$, $|y|\le1$. The total mixed area is $M=10+26\eta$; all fibers are strictly nonheavy, and every nonempty proper closed minor has mass less than its occupied parity count times $M/2$.

At the fixed interior mark $Y_\eta=(0,0,\delta)$ with $\delta=2048\eta$, write $f_\eta=(E_{K_0}(0,\delta)/M)^{1/3}$, where $E_K(w)=\inf_c\int_K[1+c\cdot(w'-w)]^{-3}\,dw'$ over denominators positive on $K$. Use the covariance buffer of strength $\varepsilon_\eta=\delta\sqrt3/f_\eta$, so the cap of normal $(a,u)$ has minimal margin $az+u\cdot((x,y)-(0,\delta))+\varepsilon_\eta f_\eta\sqrt{u^{\mathsf T}\Sigma u}$, with $\Sigma$ the pooled within-fiber covariance. Then $0<\varepsilon_\eta\le 2048\eta\sqrt3\,11^{1/3}\to0$. The unique minimizing closed buffered halfspace, including outward threshold relaxations, is $y-z/2\ge0$, of raw mass $Q_\eta=5+(23/2)\eta$. Its base $-2$ inside supporting facet has length one; its base $2$ outside supporting facet has length $1+2\eta$; all other traces are proper. The positive and negative base-tilt directional derivatives in upward unit-vertical coordinates are $3\eta$ and $\eta$. In particular inside-free minimizing selection at a fixed proper mark can fail even along actual invariant buffers tending to zero.

## Proof

The width increments are $4\eta,0,-\eta,-\eta$, hence the interpolation is positive concave and defines exactly the claimed compatible integer fibers. Their areas $2r_z$ sum to $M$; the largest is $2+8\eta<M/2$. Every closed minor with more than one site is a consecutive chain of both parities, so a proper such chain misses positive area and has mass less than $M$. The covariance is diagonal with $\Sigma_{yy}=1/3$ and $c_\eta:=\Sigma_{xx}/\Sigma_{yy}=\sum_zr_z^3/(4\sum_zr_z)$. For $\eta\le1/16$ all widths lie in $[1,5/4]$, so $1/4\le c_\eta\le25/64$. In the source rectangle, the subrectangle centered at $(0,\delta)$ of halfheight $1-\delta$ and the full horizontal width has area at least one. Jensen's inequality on it gives energy at least one, since every admissible denominator has mean one there. The constant denominator gives finite energy. Since $M<11$, the stated positive upper bound on $\varepsilon_\eta$ follows.

With upward vertical coefficient one, reflect $x$ to take $t\ge0$. The minimally buffered margin is $y+tx+az+g(t)$, with $g(t)=\delta(\sqrt{1+c_\eta t^2}-1)$. First let $F_0(t,a)$ be the mass with zero added shift. A centrally symmetric core consisting of the entire own rectangle, width $1+3\eta$ at both bases $-1,1$ and width one at both bases $-2,2$ contributes exactly half its mass, $5+10\eta$, to every nonvertical through-origin cap. The disjoint residuals are the height-two strips $(1+3\eta)/2\le|x|\le(1+4\eta)/2$ at $-1$ and $1/2\le|x|\le1/2+\eta$ at $2$. Their areas are $2\eta$ and $4\eta$. If $a\le0$ the first gives at least half its area by symmetry and nonnegative margin $-a$; if $a\ge0$ the second gives at least half. Consequently $F_0\ge Q_\eta-\eta/2$ always, and $F_0\ge Q_\eta+\eta/2$ for $a\ge0$. These statements include unshifted horizontal cuts.

For $t=0$ and $a=-b\le0$, direct vertical clipping gives the piecewise masses
$$
5+13\eta-3\eta b\quad(0\le b\le1/2),\qquad
5+11\eta+\eta b\quad(1/2\le b\le1),\qquad
5+12\eta\quad(b\ge1).
$$
This proves the unique axial minimum at $b=1/2$, the stated two directional derivatives, and a gap at least $\eta/4$ for $b$ outside $[1/4,3/4]$. Set $T=1/16$. As the symmetric core is independent of angle, the change from $t=0$ is bounded by the sum of the residual horizontal integrals of $t|x|$, since the clipped vertical length is $1$-Lipschitz in its margin. The resulting bound is
$$
t(3\eta/2+11\eta^2/4)\le(107/64)\eta t .
$$
For $0\le t\le T$ this is less than $\eta/4$. It leaves only $1/4\le b\le3/4$ in this angular range, since $a\ge0$ was already strict.

For these remaining $b,t$ the three middle cuts are proper, since $3/4+(5/8)T<1$. The left endpoint cannot be empty at any horizontal column and the right endpoint cannot be full at any horizontal column. Put $s=2b-1$, $I_r(s)=\int_{-r/2}^{r/2}(s+tx)_+\,dx$, and $J(s)=I_{1+2\eta}(s)-I_1(s)$. The middle cuts contribute $3+11\eta+\eta b$. The left and right endpoint contributions are $1+2b-I_1(s)$ and $I_{1+2\eta}(-s)$, by the clipped column lengths and reflection in $x$. Substitution of $I_r(-s)=I_r(s)-rs$ gives
$$
F_0(t,-b)-Q_\eta=J(s)-(3\eta/2)s .
$$
The integration domain for $J$ is symmetric of length $2\eta$, so $J(s)\ge2\eta(s)_+$ by Jensen. This makes the gap positive for $s\ne0$. At $s=0$ and $t>0$ the gap is $t(\eta/2+\eta^2/2)>0$. Thus every $0<t\le T$ is already strict before the nonnegative buffer gain.

For $t\ge T$ use the actual own-rectangle gain
$$
\Delta_0(t)=r_0\,\Pr\{|X+Y'/t|\le g(t)/t\},
$$
where $X,Y'$ are independent uniform variables on $[-r_0/2,r_0/2]$ and $[-1,1]$. By interval overlap the probability for a fixed threshold is even and nonincreasing in the absolute value of the shift of $X$. Conditioning on $Y'$ makes it nondecreasing in $t$ for a fixed threshold; $g(t)/t$ is also increasing. At $T$, the gained strip lies inside the constant vertical density region since $(r_0/2)T+g(T)\le5/128+25/65536<1$, using $\sqrt{1+v}-1\le v/2$. Hence $\Delta_0(T)=r_0g(T)\ge g(T)>\delta/3072=2\eta/3$, where the strict bound follows from $c_\eta\ge1/4$ and $\sqrt{1+c_\eta T^2}+1<3$. This pays more than the maximal coarse deficit $\eta/2$ for every remaining upward orientation.

A downward orientation reflected in $y$ adds another $2\delta$ to its upward comparison margin. If the upward parameters were minimizing, that extra shift increases the proper own capture strictly; otherwise the upward mass already exceeds $Q_\eta$. A horizontal orientation with positive unit horizontal coefficient has shift $\delta\sqrt{c_\eta}\le5/16<r_0/2$ and increases own area by exactly $2\delta\sqrt{c_\eta}\ge\delta>\eta/2$. Its unshifted mass is at least $Q_\eta-\eta/2$. The closed pure-base caps through zero have masses $6+16\eta$ and $6+18\eta$, both above $Q_\eta$. Outward relaxation increases mass weakly, and strictly at the unique minimizing threshold because its own trace is proper. The minimizing parameters $t=0,a=-1/2$ now give the claimed facets and the proper remaining sections directly.
