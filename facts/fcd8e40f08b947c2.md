## Statement

For any integer $k\ge8$ put $N=3k+1$ and $C=\{(z,x):0\le z\le N,\ 0\le x\le N-z\}$. Let $\mu$ be the original mixed length, $M=N(N+1)/2$, and $H=\{z\ge k\}$. The mixed centroid has base $k$ and belongs to $H$. Every nonnegative measure $\nu$ supported on the centroid fiber of total mass $M-(2k+1)$, in particular every noncentral torque target, satisfies
$$
\nu(H)-2\mu(H)=(k^2-7k-4)/2>0.
$$
Adding the original centroid-fiber length also violates the factor-two bound. For $k=8$ the centroid is $(8,17/2)$ and $(M,\mu(H),\nu(H))=(325,153,308)$.

## Proof

The integer section at $z$ is $[0,N-z]$ for $z=0,\ldots,N$. The terminal singleton has zero length. Summing lengths and their base moments gives $M=N(N+1)/2$ and $\sum_z z(N-z)=N(N-1)(N+1)/6$, so the centroid base is $(N-1)/3=k$. Summing the continuous first moments gives $\frac12\sum_{r=1}^N r^2$, hence continuous centroid coordinate $(2N+1)/6$. At $k=8$ this is $17/2$ and is in the interval of length $17$. More generally the length there is $N-k=2k+1$ and $(2N+1)/6=(2k+1)/2$.

The closed pure-base cap has length $\sum_{r=1}^{2k+1}r=(2k+1)(k+1)$ and contains the entire centroid fiber. Thus $\nu(H)=M-(2k+1)$ for every target specified, and subtraction gives the displayed quadratic. Its numerator at $8$ is $4$ and increases for integer $k\ge8$. Adding the centroid-fiber measure increases $\nu(H)$ by its full length. The stated values at $8$ follow by substitution.
