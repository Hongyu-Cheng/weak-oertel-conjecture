## Statement

Let $d\ge1$. Let $P\subset\mathbb R^n$ be finite and nonempty, let each $K_z\subset\mathbb R^d$ for $z\in P$ be a convex body, and assume the sampled anchored Minkowski inclusions. Put $C=\operatorname{conv}\bigcup_{z\in P}(\{z\}\times K_z)$. Fix $p\in P$ and $y\in\operatorname{int}K_p$, and let $a_y$ be the optimal parameter for $E_{K_p}(y)=\min_a\int_{K_p}[1+a\cdot(x-y)]^{-d-1}\,dx$ over positive denominators. There exists a fixed base vector $b$ for which
$$
L(z,x)=1+a_y\cdot(x-y)+b\cdot(z-p),\qquad
\min_C L=\min_{x\in K_p}[1+a_y\cdot(x-y)]\ge\frac1{d+1}.
$$
For every threshold $0<\delta<1/(d+1)$, a tangent continuation of $t^{-d}$ below $\delta$ is inactive on a neighborhood of $C$ when evaluated at $L$.

If $P=\operatorname{conv}(P)\cap\mathbb Z^n$, choose any continuous anchored support majorant $\ell(u,z)=h_{K_p}(u)+A_p(u)\cdot(z-p)\ge h_{K_z}(u)$ on $P$ for each unit $u$. Then the pure inverse-power cuts
$$
C_\varepsilon=C\cap\bigcap_{u\in\mathbb S^{d-1}}
\{(z,x):u\cdot x+\varepsilon h_{K_p-y}(u)L(z,x)^{-d}\le \ell(u,z)\}
$$
give a nested compact convex deformation for all sufficiently small $\varepsilon\ge0$, in $\operatorname{aff}(P)\times\mathbb R^d$, with projection $\operatorname{conv}(P)$ and positive integer fibers exactly at $P$. On the source fiber the cuts are exactly the prescribed single-source cuts with denominator $1+a_y\cdot(x-y)$. At a regular old boundary point $x\in\partial K_z$ with normal $\nu$, the inward first speed is $h_{K_p-y}(\nu)L(z,x)^{-d}$ if $\ell(\nu,z)=h_{K_z}(\nu)$, and zero otherwise.

## Proof

If $a_y=0$, take $b=0$. Otherwise use the accepted anchored support slope lemma 155691fc3768c06f in direction $u=-a_y/\|a_y\|$ and take $b=\|a_y\| A_p(u)$. Multiplying its support inequality by $\|a_y\|$ gives $-a_y\cdot x\le h_{K_p}(-a_y)+b\cdot(z-p)$ throughout $C$. It follows that $L\ge 1-a_y\cdot y-h_{K_p}(-a_y)$ on $C$, and equality is attained on the corresponding support face in $K_p$ at $z=p$. This establishes the identity of minima. The optimal denominator floor then gives the lower bound. The open halfspace $\{L>\delta\}$ contains $C$. The function agreeing with $t^{-d}$ for $t\ge\delta$ and with its tangent at $\delta$ below $\delta$ is positive, finite and convex, with the two branches matching in value and derivative. Its composition with $L$ consequently equals $L^{-d}$ on that neighborhood.

For the cuts in the lattice-complete case, the same accepted slope lemma supplies a bounded continuous choice $A_p(u)$ if desired. The baseline $\ell$ is continuous in $u$, affine in $z$, and majorizes every sampled support. Because $y$ is interior to $K_p$, $h_{K_p-y}(u)>0$ for each unit $u$. The field $h_{K_p-y}(u)L^{-d}$ is jointly continuous and nonnegative on the normal sphere times $C$. For fixed $u$ it is convex in $(z,x)$ on $C$ by positivity and affinity of $L$ and convexity of $t^{-d}$ on $t>0$. These are exactly the hypotheses of the one-action specialization of the accepted finite global-cut lemma 84cd8a85417b0d7a. It gives the actual convex deformation, positive integer fibers, and displayed contact speed. At $z=p$ the baseline is $h_{K_p}(u)$ and the base term of $L$ vanishes, giving exactly the indicated source cuts.
