## Statement

Assume the hypotheses and the bound $L_*$ of the same-force row-tail lemma, and $\int_S\log L\,dm=0$. Suppose all sampled fibers lie in $RB_d$. For any point $(p,y)$ with $B(y,r)\subset K_p$, $r>0$, one has
$$
L(p,y)\ge\frac{r}{r+2R}\sup_{K_p}L(p,\cdot)
\ge \frac{r}{r+2R}L_*^{-M_{\max}/v}.
$$
In particular this holds uniformly at source marks $(p_i,y_i)$ with a common own inball radius and with $L_i=L(p_i,y_i)$. If their $E_i$ are the marked projective energies, and $a_*\le\Delta/\varepsilon\le a^*<\infty$, then $\sigma_i$ and $\kappa_i$ are comparable at every source by positive constants depending only on these uniform bounds, $N$, and $d$.

## Proof

For $X\in K_p$ distinct from $y$ and $0<r'<r$, write $y$ as a combination of $X$ and $y-r'(X-y)/\|X-y\|$ in the inball. The weight of $X$ is $r'/(r'+\|X-y\|)\ge r'/(r'+2R)$. Concavity and nonnegativity give the comparison also for $X=y$. Let $r'\uparrow r$ and take the supremum over $X$. With $V_p=|K_p|$ the normalization and the upper bound give
$$
0\le V_p\log\sup_{K_p} L+(M-V_p)\log L_*.
$$
Since normalization implies $L_*\ge1$, the supremum is at least $L_*^{-(M-V_p)/V_p}\ge L_*^{-M_{\max}/v}$.

For the energy lower bound every admissible denominator on $K_p$ is admissible on the contained ball. Its infimal energy at the ball center is its volume by d90ba05577427854, so $E_i\ge\kappa_d r^d$ for the common source radius $r$, with $\kappa_d=|B_d|$. Thus $(L_iE_i/M)^{1/N}$ has a positive uniform lower bound from $M\le M_{\max}$ and a uniform upper bound from $L_i\le L_*$ and $E_i\le M$. Divide these bounds by the two bounds on $\Delta/\varepsilon$ in the same-force relation.
