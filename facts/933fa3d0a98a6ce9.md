## Statement

Fix a real number \(\eta>0\), independent of \(n,d\), and \(C\). Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and put
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),
 \qquad
 W:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}a_z>0.
\tag{1}
\]
For a closed halfspace \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\), write
\(H_z:=\{x:(z,x)\in H\}\), and define
\[
 h_{\max}(C):=
 \sup_{p\in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)}
 \inf_{\substack{H\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+d}\text{ closed halfspace}\\p\in H}}
 \sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z\cap H_z).
\tag{2}
\]
Use the exact canonical normalizations
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^nh_{\max}(C),
 \qquad
 G:=\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{core}}(C),
\tag{3}
\]
where \(G\) is the global complete metronoid-core capacity of accepted fact `3d1f63abf09b2abb`.

In the case \(G<W/4\), form exactly the half-strength canonical product reservoir \(r=(r_e)\) of accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70`. Let
\[
 Q:=\sum_er_e>0,
 \qquad
 R:=R_C(r),
\tag{4}
\]
where \(R\) is its affinely half-metronoid-realizable submatching capacity, and retain exactly the canonical capacities \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\), \(M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)\), and \(R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)\) from that fact. Define the literal six-term terminal ledger
\[
 \mathcal A(C):=
 R+G+R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)+M_{\mathrm{off}}(C)
 +R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C)+D.
\tag{5}
\]
The only additional hypothesis in this small-core case is
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal A(C)\geq\eta Q.}
\tag{AGG\(_\eta\)}
\]
In the complementary case \(G\geq W/4\), no reservoir object is formed and no aggregate hypothesis is imposed. No assertion that `(AGG\(_\eta\))` holds in the small-core case is part of this theorem.

Then
\[
 \boxed{
 D\geq
 \frac{3\eta\gamma_d}{128\gamma_d+64+12\eta}\,W
 >
 \frac{3\eta}{128+e(64+12\eta)}\,W.}
\tag{6}
\]
Consequently, with
\[
 \boxed{
 c_0(\eta):=\frac{3\eta}{128+e(64+12\eta)},}
\tag{7}
\]
one has
\[
 \boxed{
 h_{\max}(C)>c_0(\eta)2^{-n}W,}
 \qquad
 0<c_0(\eta)<\frac1{4e}<\frac1e.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus, if one fixed absolute \(\eta>0\) makes `(AGG\(_\eta\))` true for every positive-mass mixed convex body in its small-core case \(G<W/4\), then the locked weak Oertel conjecture follows with the absolute constant \(c_0(\eta)\). For zero mixed-integer mass the locked inequality is automatic, so this implication covers the full quantifier range of the locked problem.

At \(\eta=1/100\), the exact dimension-dependent and dimension-free coefficients in (6) become
\[
 \frac{3\gamma_d}{12800\gamma_d+6412}
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 \frac3{12800+6412e},
\tag{9}
\]
respectively. These are exactly the coefficients in accepted fact `5fb5c39736d2b4bf`. That accepted fact proves `(AGG\(_{1/100}\))` from its additional split-retention premise; the present theorem isolates and generalizes only the subsequent scalar aggregate-to-target closure.

## Proof

We first record the exact accepted inequalities for the objects in the statement. Accepted fact `6b09aae189415c70` gives, whenever \(G<W/4\),
\[
 Q\geq\frac3{32}(W-4G)>0
\tag{10}
\]
and, in the normalizations of (5),
\[
 D\geq R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C),
 \qquad
 D\geq M_{\mathrm{off}}(C),
 \qquad
 D\geq R_{\mathrm{cloud}}(C).
\tag{11}
\]
The complete-core depth conclusion of accepted fact `3d1f63abf09b2abb`, with the exact normalization already audited in accepted fact `5fb5c39736d2b4bf`, gives
\[
 D\geq\gamma_dG.
\tag{12}
\]
Every affinely realizable submatching counted by \(R=R_C(r)\) is admissible for the global affine matching capacity of accepted fact `8c92d1d2b1a1b7f5`. Its affine matching certificate therefore gives
\[
 D\geq\gamma_dR.
\tag{13}
\]
Combining (11)-(13) term by term in the literal sum (5), including its final summand \(D\), yields
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal A(C)\leq
 \left(4+\frac2{\gamma_d}\right)D.}
\tag{14}
\]
Indeed, \(R\) and \(G\) each contribute at most \(D/\gamma_d\), the three capacities in (11) each contribute at most \(D\), and the last ledger term is \(D\). This is a scalar comparison of capacities and does not add distinct depth witnesses.

We now prove (6) in both core branches. If \(G\geq W/4\), (12) gives
\[
 D\geq\frac{\gamma_d}{4}W.
\tag{15}
\]
For every \(\eta>0\) and \(\gamma_d>0\),
\[
 \frac{3\eta\gamma_d}{128\gamma_d+64+12\eta}
 <\frac{\gamma_d}{4},
\tag{16}
\]
because, after cancelling \(\gamma_d>0\) and cross-multiplying positive quantities, (16) is the strict inequality
\(12\eta<128\gamma_d+64+12\eta\). Hence (15) proves the first inequality of (6) in the large-core branch.

Suppose now that \(G<W/4\). The premise `(AGG\(_\eta\))`, (10), and (14) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D
 &\geq\frac{\eta Q}{4+2/\gamma_d}\\
 &=\frac{\eta\gamma_d}{2(2\gamma_d+1)}Q\\
 &\geq
 \frac{3\eta\gamma_d}{64(2\gamma_d+1)}(W-4G).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]
Independently, (12) gives \(D\geq\gamma_dG\). Put
\[
 g:=\frac GW\in\left[0,\frac14\right),
 \qquad
 B:=\frac{3\eta}{64(2\gamma_d+1)}>0.
\tag{18}
\]
Dividing the two lower bounds by \(W\) gives
\[
 \frac DW\geq
 \max\{\gamma_dg,\ \gamma_dB(1-4g)\}.
\tag{19}
\]
The first entry in (19) is increasing in \(g\), while the second is decreasing. Their unique intersection is
\[
 g_*:=\frac{B}{1+4B},
\tag{20}
\]
which lies strictly between \(0\) and \(1/4\). If \(g\leq g_*\), the second entry in (19) is at least its value at \(g_*\); if \(g\geq g_*\), the first entry is at least its value there. Therefore
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \frac DW
 &\geq\frac{\gamma_dB}{1+4B}\\
 &=\frac{3\eta\gamma_d}{128\gamma_d+64+12\eta}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{21}
\]
Together with the large-core argument, this proves the first inequality in (6) for every \(C\) satisfying the stated conditional premise.

It remains to derive a dimension-free strict constant and check its full parameter range. For fixed \(\eta>0\), define
\[
 F_\eta(t):=\frac{3\eta t}{128t+64+12\eta}
 \qquad(t>0).
\tag{22}
\]
Its derivative is
\[
 F_\eta'(t)
 =\frac{3\eta(64+12\eta)}{(128t+64+12\eta)^2}>0.
\tag{23}
\]
Thus \(F_\eta\) is strictly increasing. Moreover,
\[
 \gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}>\frac1e,
\tag{24}
\]
because \(\log(1+x)<x\) for \(x>0\) gives
\(d\log(1+1/d)<1\). Equations (21)-(24), and (16) in the large-core branch, imply
\[
 \frac DW\geq F_\eta(\gamma_d)
 >F_\eta(1/e)
 =\frac{3\eta}{128+e(64+12\eta)},
\tag{25}
\]
which is (6). Since \(D=2^nh_{\max}(C)\), equation (8) follows.

The constant in (7) is positive. Also
\[
 128+e(64+12\eta)>12e\eta,
\]
so
\[
 c_0(\eta)<\frac{3\eta}{12e\eta}=\frac1{4e}<\frac1e.
\tag{26}
\]
Hence it is an admissible absolute constant whenever \(\eta\) is absolute. If the same \(\eta\) supplies `(AGG\(_\eta\))` for every positive-mass \(C\) with \(G<W/4\), (8), together with the unconditional large-core branch, proves the locked positive-mass assertion; when the mixed-integer mass is zero, any point of the assumed nonempty mixed-integer section has depth at least zero, proving the remaining vacuous case.

Finally, substituting \(\eta=1/100\) into (21) and (7), then multiplying numerator and denominator by \(100\), gives exactly (9). Accepted fact `5fb5c39736d2b4bf` additionally derives \(\mathcal A(C)\geq Q/100\) from its split-retention premise before applying this scalar closure. Thus the present result is its arbitrary-\(\eta\) aggregate closure, without asserting either that split-retention premise or any instance of `(AGG\(_\eta\))`.
