## Statement

Let $F=[\alpha,\beta]$, $\alpha<\beta$, and $f$ be a bounded piecewise constant function on $F$. Put
$
U(t)=\int_\alpha^\beta
\frac{(\min(t,s)-\alpha)(\beta-\max(t,s))}{\beta-\alpha} f(s)\,ds.
$
Then $U\in C^1(F)$, $U(\alpha)=U(\beta)=0$, and $-U''=f$ almost everywhere. For the piecewise affine tent $T_t$ of height one at $t\in(\alpha,\beta)$ and zero at the endpoints,
$
\int_F T_t f=\frac{(\beta-\alpha)U(t)}{(t-\alpha)(\beta-t)}.
$
Consequently nonnegative costs for all interior peaks are equivalent to $U\ge0$, and then $U'(\alpha)\ge0\ge U'(\beta)$.

For $\alpha\le\alpha_0<c\le d<\beta_0\le\beta$, define $H(s)=\min\{1,(s-\alpha_0)/(c-\alpha_0),(\beta_0-s)/(\beta_0-d)\}$. Then
$
\int_F H f
=\frac{U(c)-(\alpha_0-\alpha)U'(\alpha)}{c-\alpha_0}
+\frac{U(d)+(\beta-\beta_0)U'(\beta)}{\beta_0-d}.
$
In particular $U\ge0$ implies nonnegative cost for a trapezoid based on the whole face, including a tent with $c=d$. With a positive outer trimming length, $U\ge0$ alone does not fix the sign of the signed cost. For an already justified face variation with signed normal speed $H/2$, the profile term is one half this integral.

## Proof

Split the kernel integral at $s=t$. The two terms are $(\beta-t)\int_\alpha^t(s-\alpha)f(s)\,ds/(\beta-\alpha)$ and $(t-\alpha)\int_t^\beta(\beta-s)f(s)\,ds/(\beta-\alpha)$. Differentiating cancels the moving boundary terms and gives the stated regularity, endpoint values and $-U''=f$. As a function of $s$ the kernel is the unit tent multiplied by $(t-\alpha)(\beta-t)/(\beta-\alpha)>0$. This proves the cost and sign equivalence; the derivative signs follow from the endpoint values.

Integrate $-H U''$ by parts on the affine pieces. Since $U$ vanishes at both endpoints, the boundary contribution is $H(\alpha)U'(\alpha)-H(\beta)U'(\beta)$, and the interior contribution is minus the sum of $U$ times the jumps of $H'$. The two jumps are $-1/(c-\alpha_0)$ at $c$ and $-1/(\beta_0-d)$ at $d$; when $c=d$ they add at one point. The two endpoint values of $H$ are $-(\alpha_0-\alpha)/(c-\alpha_0)$ and $-(\beta-\beta_0)/(\beta_0-d)$. This gives the formula. In the whole-face case the endpoint contributions vanish, and the nonnegative interior terms also exhibit this trapezoid as a nonnegative linear combination of the two normalized tents.

If $\alpha_0>\alpha$, for example, choose $h>0$ with $\alpha+2h<c$, and prescribe $U(\alpha+x)=4hx-3x^2$ for $0\le x\le h$, $(2h-x)^2$ for $h\le x\le2h$, and zero beyond. This $C^1$ nonnegative $U$ is the stated potential of $f=-U''$, by uniqueness of the zero-endpoint solution, and gives a negative signed cost. Reflect the construction for right-hand trimming alone. Taking $f$ to be a nonzero nonnegative step supported where $H>0$ instead gives $U\ge0$ and positive cost. The identity requires no assertion that a signed shape path exists for arbitrary $H$.
