## Statement

In the affine case of the surface logarithmic reserve of 442c4c99600062dc, take $\kappa>0$, let $\Xi\,dS$ be the actual signed boundary measure there, and use $\ell_{\max}=\max_z\ell_z$. The reserve $R=-\int\Xi B\,dS$ is unchanged if $B=-\kappa\log\ell$ is augmented by an affine function of the base. If $R=0$, every physical martingale between the Jordan marginals preserves $\ell$ on its edges. On each sampled $\ell$-level the restrictions of those marginals have equal mass and full first moment and are in convex order. With $\ell$ injective on the sampled bases this holds separately on every fiber. In dimension $d=2$, injectivity and $R=0$ imply that $\Xi$ vanishes arclength-almost everywhere off the relative interiors of maximal line facets of each fiber; on each facet it has zero mass, zero first tangent moment and nonpositive hinge tests. If distinct sampled $\ell$ values have positive minimum gap $g$, the cross-level transport mass and the absolute Jordan mass imbalance on any union of entire levels are at most $2\ell_{\max}^2 R/(\kappa g^2)$.

## Proof

Affine tests annihilate the added affine term. The bound in 442c4c99600062dc gives $\int(\ell(Z')-\ell(Z))^2d\pi\le 2\ell_{\max}^2R/\kappa$ for every such coupling. This yields zero cross-level mass when $R=0$, and the stated quantitative bound since every unequal edge contributes at least $g^2$ per unit mass. For a union of levels, the Jordan mass imbalance is the difference between its outgoing and incoming crossing masses, whose absolute difference is at most total cross-level mass. When edges stay in their levels, restriction yields a physical martingale per level, proving the affine moments and convex order there by conditional Jensen. Injectivity identifies these with the individual fibers.

In a single planar fiber, an extreme point has only an identity row in any martingale supported on that fiber. The two Jordan measures are mutually singular, so a diagonal row carries zero source mass. Every other boundary point lies in the relative interior of a maximal nondegenerate line facet. Its supporting line confines its row to that facet by zero affine slack. The countably many facet endpoints have zero arclength measure, so every facet transports within itself, and neither marginal can charge the remainder off facets. Each facet's restricted row mean gives zero mass and first tangent moment of the signed measure, and Jensen for the tangent-coordinate hinges gives the nonpositive tests.
