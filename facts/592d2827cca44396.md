## Statement

Retain one common canonical small-defect intact instance of accepted facts 0636caea9cc33c59, 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, and 6bfe50fed9a5540b.  Use all notation of 0636caea9cc33c59, and abbreviate
\[
 P:=P_{\mathrm{all}},
 \qquad
 P':=\sum_\sigma\eta_\sigma',
 \qquad
 \alpha:=\alpha_{\mathrm{all}}=\frac{P}{\Omega_b}.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus
\[
 0<\alpha\leq\frac14,
 \qquad
 P>\frac Q{24},
 \qquad
 P'>\frac{93}{97}P,
\tag{2}
\]
and the full proportional fresh KKT field and its circuit-attached physical edge use are
\[
 \overline v_k:=\alpha v_k^b,
 \qquad
 c_k:=\alpha\omega_k^b,
 \qquad
 \zeta_k':=
 \sum_{\sigma,g,h}x_{\sigma,g,h}'
 \left(\mathbf1_{\{g=k\}}+\mathbf1_{\{h=k\}}\right).
\tag{3}
\]
They satisfy
\[
 \sum_kc_k=P,
 \qquad
 0\leq\zeta_k'\leq c_k,
 \qquad
 \sum_k\zeta_k'=P',
\tag{4}
\]
\[
 \sum_k\overline v_k=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_k\overline v_km_k^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 h_{D_k}(\overline v_k)=c_k.
\tag{5}
\]
Put
\[
 U:=P-P'=\sum_k(c_k-\zeta_k').
\tag{6}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{0\leq U<\frac4{97}P.}
\tag{7}
\]
For every positive fresh edge \(k=\{u,v\}\), retain
\[
 \overline c_k:=\frac{c_u+c_v}{2},
 \qquad
 L_k:=\overline c_k+D_k
 =\frac12(\mathcal K_u+\mathcal K_v),
\tag{8}
\]
where the symbols \(c_u,c_v\) on the right of (8) are the canonical fiber centroids, not the proportional edge loads \(c_k\) in (3).  Also retain
\[
 \ell_k^b:=\frac{p_u^b+p_v^b}{2}\in L_k.
\tag{9}
\]

Choose the particular support-minimal positive-dependence decomposition of the same load-minimal fresh KKT optimizer supplied by accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d.  After discarding zero components, index it by \(r=1,\ldots,N\):
\[
 v_k^b=\sum_{r=1}^Nv_k^{(r)},
 \qquad
 v_k^{(r)}=\omega_k^{(r)}a_k^\circ,
 \qquad
 a_k^\circ:=\frac{v_k^b}{\omega_k^b}
 \quad(\omega_k^b>0),
\tag{10}
\]
\[
 \sum_r\omega_k^{(r)}=\omega_k^b,
 \qquad
 h_{D_k}(a_k^\circ)=1,
\tag{11}
\]
\[
 \sum_kv_k^{(r)}=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_kv_k^{(r)}m_k^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 |\operatorname{supp}\omega^{(r)}|
 \leq d(n+1)+1,
\tag{12}
\]
\[
 \sum_kh_{L_k}(v_k^{(r)})<0.
\tag{13}
\]
Define the proportional packet loads and stresses
\[
 c_k^{(r)}:=\alpha\omega_k^{(r)},
 \qquad
 w_k^{(r)}:=\alpha v_k^{(r)}=c_k^{(r)}a_k^\circ,
\tag{14}
\]
\[
 C_r:=\sum_kc_k^{(r)},
 \qquad
 \delta_r:=-\sum_kh_{L_k}(w_k^{(r)})>0.
\tag{15}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\sum_rC_r=P,}
\tag{16}
\]
and, with
\[
 \delta_0:=\frac{95}{97}
 \left(\frac{3\log(4/3)}{\log2}-1\right),
\tag{17}
\]
the full packet margin satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\sum_r\delta_r>\delta_0P.}
\tag{18}
\]

Define the attachment fraction and its lossless packet marking by
\[
 \theta_k:=
 \begin{cases}
 \zeta_k'/c_k,&c_k>0,\\
 0,&c_k=0,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 \kappa_k^{(r)}:=\theta_kc_k^{(r)},
 \qquad
 K_r:=\sum_k\kappa_k^{(r)}.
\tag{19}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 0\leq\kappa_k^{(r)}\leq c_k^{(r)},
 \qquad
 \sum_r\kappa_k^{(r)}=\zeta_k',}
\tag{20}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 0\leq K_r\leq C_r,
 \qquad
 \sum_rK_r=P',
 \qquad
 \sum_r(C_r-K_r)=U.}
\tag{21}
\]

This marking can be made at the level of the genuine attached four-point circuits, not merely at the scalar edge marginals.  For \(c_k>0\), put
\[
 \pi_{r\mid k}:=\frac{c_k^{(r)}}{c_k}.
\tag{22}
\]
Every positive coupled triple \((\sigma,g,h)\) in accepted fact 0636caea9cc33c59 has \(c_g,c_h>0\).  Define
\[
 x_{\sigma,g,h}^{r,s}
 :=x_{\sigma,g,h}'\pi_{r\mid g}\pi_{s\mid h}.
\tag{23}
\]
For every such subpacket retain the same four actual points
\[
 K_g,K_h,J_{\sigma,g,h}^0,J_{\sigma,g,h}^1
 \in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)
\tag{24}
\]
and the same exact circuit identity
\[
 \boxed{K_g+K_h=J_{\sigma,g,h}^0+J_{\sigma,g,h}^1.}
\tag{25}
\]
The weights in (23) form a finite weighted multigraph \(\mathcal G\) on the KKT packet vertices \(\{1,\ldots,N\}\), allowing loops and parallel edges.  Its edge corresponding to \((\sigma,g,h,r,s)\) has weight \(x_{\sigma,g,h}^{r,s}\), and a loop contributes twice to weighted degree.  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{r,s}x_{\sigma,g,h}^{r,s}
 =x_{\sigma,g,h}',
 \qquad
 |E(\mathcal G)|
 :=\sum_{\sigma,g,h,r,s}x_{\sigma,g,h}^{r,s}
 =\frac{P'}2,}
\tag{26}
\]
and the weighted degree of packet vertex \(r\) is exactly
\[
 \boxed{\deg_{\mathcal G}(r)=K_r.}
\tag{27}
\]
All source and target collisions remain safe: the family (23)--(25) is a nonnegative splitting of the already collision-safe physical family in accepted fact 0636caea9cc33c59, and summing it recovers that family exactly.

Call a packet good if
\[
 K_r\geq\frac{C_r}{2},
\tag{28}
\]
and write \(\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}\) and \(\mathcal J_{\mathrm b}\) for the good and bad packet sets.  Put
\[
 C_{\mathrm g}:=\sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}}C_r,
 \qquad
 K_{\mathrm g}:=\sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}}K_r.
\tag{29}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 C_{\mathrm g}\geq P-2U>\frac{89}{97}P,
 \qquad
 K_{\mathrm g}\geq P-2U>\frac{89}{97}P.}
\tag{30}
\]
Let \(E_{\mathrm{gg}}\) be the total weight in \(\mathcal G\) of circuit subpackets whose two packet endpoints are good, with a good loop included in \(E_{\mathrm{gg}}\).  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 E_{\mathrm{gg}}\geq
 \frac{P-3U}{2}>
 \frac{85}{194}P.}
\tag{31}
\]
Thus (31) is one macroscopic, collision-safe family of genuine attached four-point circuits joining balanced KKT packets, not a sum of numerical rank screens.

Finally, each packet is individually balanced and has two exact affine-defect inequalities.  For every affine map \(a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_kc_k^{(r)}
 \|\ell_k^b-a(m_k)\|_{D_k}
 \geq\delta_r,}
\tag{32}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_kc_k^{(r)}
 \|\overline c_k-a(m_k)\|_{D_k}
 \geq C_r+\delta_r.}
\tag{33}
\]
The same conclusions aggregate over the good packet set.  Namely, with
\[
 c_k^{\mathrm g}:=
 \sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}}c_k^{(r)},
 \qquad
 w_k^{\mathrm g}:=
 \sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}}w_k^{(r)},
 \qquad
 \delta_{\mathrm g}:=
 \sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}}\delta_r>0,
\tag{34}
\]
one has
\[
 \sum_kw_k^{\mathrm g}=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_kw_k^{\mathrm g}m_k^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 h_{D_k}(w_k^{\mathrm g})=c_k^{\mathrm g},
\tag{35}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 \inf_a\sum_kc_k^{\mathrm g}
 \|\ell_k^b-a(m_k)\|_{D_k}
 \geq\delta_{\mathrm g}>0,}
\tag{36}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 \inf_a\sum_kc_k^{\mathrm g}
 \|\overline c_k-a(m_k)\|_{D_k}
 \geq C_{\mathrm g}+\delta_{\mathrm g}
 >\frac{89}{97}P.}
\tag{37}
\]

No assertion is made that the marked restriction \((\kappa_k^{(r)})_k\), the full attached restriction \(\zeta'\), or the good-good circuit subfamily is balanced.  The balance belongs to each whole KKT packet \((c_k^{(r)})_k\) and to their good aggregate.  No assertion is made that \(\delta_{\mathrm g}\) is an absolute multiple of \(P\), that terminal scores from different packets may be added, or that (30)--(37) alone close the high-rank branch.  The remaining bridge is the following strictly smaller stability problem: absorb a collision-safe four-point circuit multigraph of weight greater than \(85P/194\) whose incident packet vertices are individually balanced, strictly KKT-frustrated, at least half circuit-marked, and whose total packet load exceeds \(89P/97\).

## Proof

Equations (1)--(9) are direct aliases and conclusions of accepted fact 0636caea9cc33c59.  In particular, its equations defining the small-defect recoupling give (3)--(7), and its retained fresh endpoint points give \(p_u^b\in\mathcal K_u\), \(p_v^b\in\mathcal K_v\).  Hence (9) follows from (8).

Apply the decomposition clause of accepted fact 6496cb3b0fd44f9d to the same fresh gauge-load-minimal KKT optimizer \(v^b\).  It gives (10)--(13), including the fact that every nonzero component has strictly negative support objective.  Positive scaling by \(\alpha\) gives (14)--(16) and \(\delta_r>0\).

The optimizer \(v^b\) is dual optimal for the fresh matching capacity \(b\), so
\[
 -\sum_kh_{L_k}(v_k^b)=\Delta_C(b).
\tag{38}
\]
Since \(0\leq\omega^b\leq b\), monotonicity of the weighted affine defect in its matching weights gives
\[
 \Delta_C(b)\geq\Delta_C(\omega^b).
\tag{39}
\]
Accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b gives
\[
 \Delta_C(\omega^b)>\delta_0\Omega_b.
\tag{40}
\]
Summing (15), then using (38)--(40) and \(\alpha\Omega_b=P\), proves
\[
 \sum_r\delta_r
 =-\alpha\sum_kh_{L_k}(v_k^b)
 =\alpha\Delta_C(b)
 >\delta_0P,
\]
which is (18).

For (19)--(21), note first that \(c_k=0\) forces \(\zeta_k'=0\), by (4), so \(\theta_k\) is well defined and belongs to \([0,1]\).  Equations (11), (14), and (19) give, coordinatewise,
\[
 \sum_r\kappa_k^{(r)}
 =\theta_k\sum_rc_k^{(r)}
 =\theta_kc_k
 =\zeta_k'.
\tag{41}
\]
All other inequalities in (20) are immediate from \(0\leq\theta_k\leq1\).  Summing (41) first in \(k\) and then reversing the finite sums proves every identity in (21), using (4) and (6).

We now split the physical circuits.  A positive \(x_{\sigma,g,h}'\) contributes to both \(\zeta_g'\) and \(\zeta_h'\), with multiplicity when \(g=h\).  Thus (4) gives \(c_g,c_h>0\), and (22)--(23) are defined.  Equations (11) and (14) give
\[
 \sum_r\pi_{r\mid k}=1
 \qquad(c_k>0).
\tag{42}
\]
Equation (42) proves the first identity in (26).  Summing the retained label identity
\[
 \sum_{g,h}x_{\sigma,g,h}'=\frac{\eta_\sigma'}2
\]
from accepted fact 0636caea9cc33c59 over \(\sigma\) proves the second identity in (26).

Count the two edge slots of every subpacket in (23), retaining multiplicity.  The contribution of an occurrence of edge \(k\) to packet vertex \(r\) is its original occurrence weight multiplied by \(\pi_{r\mid k}\).  Summing all occurrences therefore gives
\[
 \deg_{\mathcal G}(r)
 =\sum_k\pi_{r\mid k}\zeta_k'
 =\sum_k\frac{c_k^{(r)}}{c_k}\zeta_k'
 =\sum_k\theta_kc_k^{(r)}
 =K_r.
\tag{43}
\]
When \(g=h\) and \(r=s\), the subpacket is a graph loop and both occurrences appear in (43), exactly as required.  This proves (27).  Every subpacket in (23) is obtained by multiplying the original representing densities by a number in \([0,1]\), and the multipliers sum to one over \((r,s)\).  Therefore all actual-point, integrality, circuit, source-ledger, and target-ledger conclusions of the original collision-safe family remain valid and recover the original family after summation.  This proves (24)--(27), including the collision assertion.

Let
\[
 C_{\mathrm b}:=\sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm b}}C_r,
 \qquad
 K_{\mathrm b}:=\sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm b}}K_r.
\]
For every bad packet, \(K_r<C_r/2\), so
\[
 C_r<2(C_r-K_r),
 \qquad
 K_r<C_r-K_r.
\tag{44}
\]
If the bad set is nonempty, summing (44) gives strict versions of the following estimates; if it is empty, both left sides vanish.  Thus in every case equations (21) and (44) give the boundary-safe bounds
\[
 C_{\mathrm b}\leq2U,
 \qquad
 K_{\mathrm b}\leq U.
\tag{45}
\]
Consequently
\[
 C_{\mathrm g}=P-C_{\mathrm b}\geq P-2U,
\]
and
\[
 K_{\mathrm g}=P'-K_{\mathrm b}
 =P-U-K_{\mathrm b}\geq P-2U.
\]
Combining these non-strict structural inequalities with the strict bound \(U<4P/97\) in (7) proves both strict numerical inequalities in (30), including the boundary case \(U=0\).

Let \(E_{\mathrm{gb}}\) and \(E_{\mathrm{bb}}\) denote the total weights of good-bad and bad-bad circuit subpackets in \(\mathcal G\), with loops counted in the same endpoint class.  Weighted degree counting gives
\[
 K_{\mathrm g}=2E_{\mathrm{gg}}+E_{\mathrm{gb}},
 \qquad
 K_{\mathrm b}=2E_{\mathrm{bb}}+E_{\mathrm{gb}}.
\tag{46}
\]
Hence (45) gives \(E_{\mathrm{gb}}\leq K_{\mathrm b}\leq U\).  Equations (30) and (46), using the non-strict first comparison in (30), now imply
\[
 2E_{\mathrm{gg}}
 =K_{\mathrm g}-E_{\mathrm{gb}}
 \geq P-3U
 >\frac{85}{97}P.
\]
This proves both the boundary-safe first comparison and the strict numerical consequence in (31).

It remains to prove the defect claims.  Equations (12) and (14) give, for every affine \(a\),
\[
 \sum_k\langle w_k^{(r)},a(m_k)\rangle=0.
\tag{47}
\]
By (9), \(\ell_k^b\in L_k\).  Hence
\[
 \langle w_k^{(r)},\ell_k^b\rangle
 \leq h_{L_k}(w_k^{(r)}).
\tag{48}
\]
Since \(D_k\) is origin-symmetric and
\(h_{D_k}(w_k^{(r)})=c_k^{(r)}\), polar Hölder gives
\[
 c_k^{(r)}\|y\|_{D_k}
 \geq\langle-w_k^{(r)},y\rangle.
\tag{49}
\]
Apply (49) to \(y=\ell_k^b-a(m_k)\), sum, and use (47)--(48) and (15).  This gives (32).

For the centroid field, (8), (11), and (14) give the exact support identity
\[
 h_{L_k}(w_k^{(r)})
 =\langle w_k^{(r)},\overline c_k\rangle
 +c_k^{(r)}.
\tag{50}
\]
Apply (49) to \(y=\overline c_k-a(m_k)\), sum, and use (47), (50), and (15):
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_kc_k^{(r)}
 \|\overline c_k-a(m_k)\|_{D_k}
 &\geq-\sum_k
 \langle w_k^{(r)},\overline c_k-a(m_k)\rangle\\
 &=-\sum_kh_{L_k}(w_k^{(r)})+\sum_kc_k^{(r)}\\
 &=\delta_r+C_r.
 \end{aligned}
\]
This proves (33).

The good set is nonempty by the strict numerical part of (30).  Summing its balanced packet stresses proves the two cancellations in (35).  On each fixed edge all packet stresses are nonnegative multiples of the same normalized direction \(a_k^\circ\), so positive homogeneity gives the last identity in (35).  Summing (32)--(33) over good packets, then taking the infimum over \(a\), proves (36)--(37).  The final scope paragraph records exactly what was and was not used: the whole KKT packets are balanced, the circuit marks are lossless but generally unbalanced restrictions, and no packetwise terminal score has been summed.
