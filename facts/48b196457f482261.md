## Statement

Let $G=\operatorname{conv}\{v_1,\ldots,v_k\}$ be a compact polytope, and let $w=\sum_i\lambda_i v_i$ with all $\lambda_i>0$ and $\sum_i\lambda_i=1$. Put $\lambda_*=\min_i\lambda_i$. Every real concave function $\rho$ on $G$ with $\rho(v_i)\ge-L$ and $\rho(w)\le U$ satisfies
$-L\le\rho(x)\le(U+L)/\lambda_*-L$ for all $x\in G$.

For a family of upper semicontinuous concave roofs $r_l$ and selectors $\operatorname{clamp}(1+\alpha_l r_l+\ell_l,0,1)$ with $\alpha_l\ge\alpha_*>0$ and $\ell_l$ affine, unsaturation at every $v_i$ and at $w$ yields such a uniform bound in the common gauge $\rho_l=r_l+\ell_l/\alpha_l$. If each further selector has the form $\operatorname{clamp}(1+\alpha_{j,l}\rho_l+\ell_{j,l},0,1)$ with a common positive lower bound and finite upper bound on its $\alpha_{j,l}$ and bounded $\ell_{j,l}$ at a fixed affine-spanning anchor set, finitely many such selectors have a common bounded convex hypograph realization by affine slabs, with slab length normalized by $\alpha_{j,l}$.

## Proof

The lower bound follows by writing $x$ as a convex combination of the vertices. Given $x=\sum_i\mu_i v_i$, let $\theta=\min_{\mu_i>0}\lambda_i/\mu_i$. Then $\lambda_*\le\theta\le1$ and $w=\theta x+(1-\theta)y$ for some $y\in G$ if $\theta<1$. Concavity gives $U+L\ge\theta(\rho(x)+L)$. If $\theta=1$ then $x=w$ and the same bound holds. Since $U+L\ge0$, the uniform upper bound follows.

Unsaturation means $-1/\alpha_l<\rho_l<0$ at the specified anchors, so use $L=1/\alpha_*$ and $U=0$. For every additional selector its affine correction is bounded throughout $G$ by its values on a fixed affine basis and bounded affine coordinates on the compact polytope. The slab endpoints $-(1+\ell_{j,l})/\alpha_{j,l}$ and $-\ell_{j,l}/\alpha_{j,l}$ are therefore uniformly bounded. Choose one constant lower floor below all slab floors and below all roofs. Its upper-semicontinuous hypograph truncation is compact and convex. At $x$, the vertical intersection with each slab has length $\operatorname{clamp}(1+\alpha_{j,l}\rho_l(x)+\ell_{j,l}(x),0,1)/\alpha_{j,l}$. For the anchor selector in this gauge the affine correction is zero and the same conclusion holds under the assumed lower bound on $\alpha_l$.
