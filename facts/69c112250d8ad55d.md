## Statement

The following finite scalar conditions in dimension three do not imply $Q\ge\Gamma(a+b+k)/2$. Put $\Gamma=27/64$, $\kappa=10/27$ and assume $a,b,k,Q>0$, $0<U_1\le\cdots\le U_4<a$, $q_i=Q-U_i>0$, $P_i>0$, $H_i=4P_i/(3b)$, $t_i=U_i/a$, and $X=\sum_iP_i-3b$. The conditions are
$$
\begin{gathered}
2\max(a,b,k)<a+b+k,\qquad
k\le Q/\Gamma-U_1-U_2-\kappa U_3,\qquad
a\le U_4+U_3+\kappa U_2,\\
8Q\ge9U_i+P_i+\sqrt{3U_iP_i}\quad(1\le i\le4),\qquad
P_3+P_4>3b,\qquad \sum_{j\ne i}P_j>3b\quad(1\le i\le4),\\
\sum_iP_iU_i>4ba,\qquad
\sum_i H_i(1+t_i/2-(3/4)t_i^{-1/3})\ge4,\qquad
X>0,\quad X^3\ge27b^2a,\qquad
8k\ge a+b+X+\sqrt{3aX}.
\end{gathered}
$$
The counterexample also has nonnegative abstract masses indexed by incidence patterns $I\subseteq\{1,2,3,4\}$, with total $a$, cap marginals $U_i$, and $\sum_{i\in I}P_i>3b$ at every positive atom. These are formal constraints on numbers and abstract masses.

## Proof

Set
$$
\begin{gathered}
b=1,\quad a=1/80,\quad k=99/100,\quad Q=4223/10000,\\
(t_1,t_2,t_3,t_4)=((2/5)^3,(3/4)^3,(7/8)^3,(8/9)^3),\\
(U_i,q_i,H_i)=(at_i,Q-at_i,4P_i/3),\qquad
(P_1,P_2,P_3,P_4)=(3/10,309/100,601/200,601/200).
\end{gathered}
$$
All the positivity and ordering conditions follow from these fractions, and $a+b+k-2\max(a,b,k)=1/400$. The two profile slacks, in order, have numerators $6839694,24657875$ over $3732480000$. For the local facet inequalities let $Z_i=8Q-9U_i-P_i$. The numerators of $Z_i$, over $414720000$, are $(1273688064,99922248,123600573,122088448)$. The numerators of $Z_i^2-3U_iP_i$, over $171992678400000000$, are $(1622157449647620096,1576633872573504,2293061105128329,1293394933448704)$. Thus squaring gives the claimed strict local tests.

We have $P_3+P_4-3=301/100$, $\min_i\sum_{j\ne i}P_j-3=331/100$, and $\sum_iP_iU_i-4a=2699360383/149299200000$. Using the given rational cube roots, the residual-moment slack is $9393445081/19595520000$. Also $X=32/5$ satisfies $X^3>27a$. The global radical test follows from $8k-a-b-X=203/400>0$ and $(203/400)^2-3aX=2809/160000>0$. The target gap is $\Gamma(a+b+k)/2-Q=131/1280000>0$. In particular the two additional bounds $k\le a+b-2(U_1+U_2+\kappa U_3)$ and $U_1+U_2+\kappa U_3\le a$ follow from the profile, the strict target gap, and $b<a+k$, and the summed local inequality follows from the individual ones and $P_3+P_4>3$.

For the incidence masses put $w=2a-\sum_iU_i$ on $\{4\}$, put $U_1$ on $\{1,4\}$, and set $d_2=U_2$, $d_3=U_3$, $d_4=U_4-w-U_1$. Put $(d_i+d_j-d_\ell)/2$ on $\{i,j\}\subset\{2,3,4\}$, with $\ell$ the remaining index. In the order $\{4\},\{1,4\},\{2,3\},\{2,4\},\{3,4\}$ these masses are
$$
\frac{6619141}{3732480000},\qquad \frac1{1250},\qquad
\frac{217}{58320},\qquad \frac{1159}{746496},\qquad
\frac{138943}{29859840}.
$$
They are positive, sum to $a$, and the construction gives the required marginals. Every pattern uses at least one of indices $2,3,4$, and each corresponding $P_i$ exceeds $3b$. The example therefore passes the named scalar and incidence tests with a strictly smaller $Q$.
