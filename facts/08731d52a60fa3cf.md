## Proof

Equations (1)--(9) are the common aliases and conclusions of accepted facts 0636caea9cc33c59, 592d2827cca44396, 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, and 6bfe50fed9a5540b.  In particular, 592d2827cca44396 applies the support-minimal decomposition of 6496cb3b0fd44f9d to the same fresh optimizer of 6bfe50fed9a5540b and proves
\[
 \sum_k\omega_k^{(r)}a_k^\circ=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_k\omega_k^{(r)}a_k^\circ m_k^{\mathsf T}=0,
\tag{37}
\]
as well as (7).  Equations (12)--(13) follow by summing a finite product measure in either coordinate.  Using (37), for every fixed \((r,k)\),
\[
 \sum_iB_{i,k}^{(r)}a_i^\circ
 =\frac{\omega_k^{(r)}}{\Omega_r}
   \sum_i\omega_i^{(r)}a_i^\circ
 =0.
\tag{38}
\]
This proves (15), and summing (38), or multiplying it first by \(m_k^{\mathsf T}\), proves (16).  Equations (9) and (13) prove the exact physical ledger (14).

We prove (18) in the same single-ledger form audited in accepted fact 624b3258dee4faea, now using all packets.  By (8) and (10), for every positive triple choose a representing density on \(C_{m_k}\):
\[
 0\leq F_{i,k}^{(r)}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int_{C_{m_k}}F_{i,k}^{(r)}=\vartheta_k,
 \qquad
 \frac1{\vartheta_k}\int_{C_{m_k}}xF_{i,k}^{(r)}(x)\,dx
 =p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}.
\tag{39}
\]
Fix a closed ambient halfspace \(H\).  At one physical target \(m\), retain only the summands whose atom belongs to \(H\), and put
\[
 \mathcal F_{m,H}(x)
 :=\sum_{\substack{r,i,k:\,m_k=m\\
 (m,p_{i\mid k}^{(r)})\in H}}
 \frac{B_{i,k}^{(r)}}{\vartheta_k}F_{i,k}^{(r)}(x).
\tag{40}
\]
Equations (13)--(14) and \(F_{i,k}^{(r)}\leq1\) give
\[
 0\leq\mathcal F_{m,H}\leq1.
\tag{41}
\]
Let \(t_m:=\int\mathcal F_{m,H}\).  If \(t_m>0\), its normalized barycenter is the \(B\)-weighted average of the selected points \(p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}\).  It therefore belongs both to the convex fiber halfspace
\[
 H_m:=\{x:(m,x)\in H\}
\tag{42}
\]
and, by (40)--(41), to \(\mathsf M_{t_m}(C_m)\).  Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_m\cap H_m)\geq\gamma_dt_m.
\tag{43}
\]
The empty and whole-space fiber-halfspace cases are immediate.  Summing (43) over the distinct integer targets gives
\[
 \mu_C(H)\geq\gamma_d\sum_mt_m=\nu_{\mathrm{full}}(H),
\]
which proves (18).  Equations (12)--(13) and (5)--(6) give
\[
 |\nu_{\mathrm{full}}|
 =\gamma_d\sum_r\Omega_r
 =\gamma_d\Omega_b,
\]
and (2) proves (19).

We next prove the lossless work identity.  From (10)--(11),
\[
 \mathcal T_{\mathrm{full}}
 =\sum_{r,i,k}B_{i,k}^{(r)}
 \bigl(h_{L_k}(a_i^\circ)-h_{L_i}(a_i^\circ)\bigr).
\tag{44}
\]
Write \(L_k=\overline c_k+D_k\).  For fixed \((r,k)\), equation (38) cancels the translation term:
\[
 \sum_iB_{i,k}^{(r)}
 \langle a_i^\circ,\overline c_k\rangle=0.
\tag{45}
\]
Hence the first support-function sum in (44) is \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{full}}\).  It is nonnegative because every \(D_k\) contains the origin.  The row marginal in (13) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{r,i,k}B_{i,k}^{(r)}h_{L_i}(a_i^\circ)
 &=\sum_{r,i}\omega_i^{(r)}h_{L_i}(a_i^\circ)\\
 &=\sum_i h_{L_i}(v_i^b).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{46}
\]
The last equality uses that all packet components on edge \(i\) lie on the same ray \(a_i^\circ\).  The exact KKT dual identity of 6496cb3b0fd44f9d, in the fresh specialization recorded explicitly in 592d2827cca44396, is
\[
 -\sum_i h_{L_i}(v_i^b)=\Delta_C(b).
\tag{47}
\]
Equations (44)--(47) prove the equality in (22).  Finally, (7) and \(P=\alpha\Omega_b\) give
\[
 \Delta_C(b)>\delta_0\Omega_b,
\tag{48}
\]
which proves the strict part of (22).  Multiplication by \(\alpha>0\) proves (23)--(24); halfspace domination of the scaled measure also follows from \(0<\alpha\leq1\).

We prove the scaled mass decomposition.  Equations (25)--(26) are exactly the lossless packet marking of 592d2827cca44396.  Substituting
\[
 c_k^{(r)}=\kappa_k^{(r)}+\varepsilon_k^{(r)}
\]
in (23) proves (27)--(28).  Its exact marking identities give
\[
 \sum_r\kappa_k^{(r)}=\zeta_k',
 \qquad
 \sum_{r,k}\kappa_k^{(r)}=P',
 \qquad
 \sum_{r,k}\varepsilon_k^{(r)}=P-P'.
\tag{49}
\]
Since \(\sum_i c_i^{(r)}/C_r=1\), equation (49) proves (29)--(30), including the strict constants from 0636caea9cc33c59.  Packet force balance gives
\[
 \sum_iB_{i,k}^{\mathrm{att},(r)}a_i^\circ
 =\frac{\kappa_k^{(r)}}{C_r}
   \sum_ic_i^{(r)}a_i^\circ=0,
\tag{50}
\]
and the same calculation with \(\varepsilon_k^{(r)}\) proves (31).  The attached physical target ledger follows from (30) and the accepted inequality
\[
 \sum_{k:m_k=m}\frac{\zeta_k'}{\vartheta_k}\leq1.
\tag{51}
\]
The combined scaled ledger has edge marginal \(c_k=\alpha\omega_k^b\), so it too is a subledger of (9).

It remains to verify the ancestry with exact slot multiplicity.  For the full multigraph \(\mathcal G\), define
\[
 d_{r,k}:=
 \sum_{\substack{e\in E(\mathcal G)\\
 \text{endpoint slots equal to }(r,k)}}x_e,
\tag{52}
\]
where both slots are counted and a loop with two equal slots contributes twice.  In the graph splitting of 592d2827cca44396, an occurrence of physical edge \(k\) is assigned to packet \(r\) with factor
\[
 \pi_{r\mid k}=\frac{c_k^{(r)}}{c_k}.
\tag{53}
\]
When \(c_k=0\), accepted fact 592d2827cca44396 gives \(\zeta_k'=0\), so no graph slot uses \(k\) and both \(d_{r,k}\) and \(\kappa_k^{(r)}\) vanish.  Thus it remains only to consider \(c_k>0\), when (53) is defined.
The full slot incidence at \(k\) is \(\zeta_k'\).  Therefore, with multiplicity retained,
\[
 d_{r,k}
 =\pi_{r\mid k}\zeta_k'
 =\theta_kc_k^{(r)}
 =\kappa_k^{(r)}.
\tag{54}
\]
This is the all-packet analogue of the corrected combined first-slot plus second-slot accounting in accepted fact 624b3258dee4faea; neither orientation alone is claimed to have the combined marginal.

Equation (35) follows by summing the two product probabilities in (34).  Fix \((r,k,i)\).  Summing first over the opposite packet index on all decorated edges whose first slot is \((r,k)\), and then over the opposite packet index on all decorated edges whose second slot is \((r,k)\), gives
\[
 \frac{c_i^{(r)}}{C_r}d_{r,k}
 =\frac{c_i^{(r)}}{C_r}\kappa_k^{(r)}
 =B_{i,k}^{\mathrm{att},(r)}.
\tag{55}
\]
For a loop with two equal slots, its two appearances in (55) are distinct and both are required.  This proves (36).  Also 592d2827cca44396 gives
\[
 \sum_{e\in E(\mathcal G)}x_e=\frac{P'}2.
\tag{56}
\]
Thus the two slot atoms decorated by each \(y_{e,i,j}\) have total mass
\[
 2\sum_{e,i,j}y_{e,i,j}=P',
\]
exactly the attached mass in (29).  Summing out \(i,j\) recovers each \(x_e\) and hence the original circuit (33), while summing by slots recovers the attached cross-contact family by (55).  This proves all ancestry and collision statements.  The slot-by-slot formulation also makes clear why no cross-contact point is asserted to equal any of the four circuit corners, and why the ancestry is a marking rather than an additional physical demand.  All claimed conclusions are now proved.
