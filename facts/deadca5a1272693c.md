## Statement

In $\mathbb R^2$ put $v_1=(1,0)$, $v_2=(0,1)$, $v_3=(-1,-1)$ and $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$. Let $K$ be a triangle, and $A,B$ convex bodies with $B\subseteq T$ and $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$. Suppose for one $y$ all three lines $v_i\cdot x=2v_i\cdot y-1$ meet $\operatorname{int}A$. Write $a=|A|$, $k=|K|$, and $b=|B|$. Assume $h_K(-v_i)=1$ for all $i$, $y\in\operatorname{int}K$, $a<b/32$ and $k\le a+b$. If each middle cap at $v_i\cdot y$ has area at most $(128/279)k$, then $K$ contains a vertex of $-T$.

## Proof

If $K$ has no outer vertex, the near-corner contact theorem gives distinct contacts and some off-diagonal barycentric entry greater than $62/63$. Permute the outer coordinates and the corresponding contact labels to write the three vertices in the outer coordinates as
$$
P_1=(0,p,1-p),\quad P_2=(r,0,1-r),\quad P_3=(s,1-s,0),
\qquad 0<p,s<1,\quad 62/63<r<1.
$$
The matched placement bound is the case $t_i=1,\tau=3$ of the unequal-offset theorem. It gives $\lambda_i(y)>(41/100)\max_K\lambda_i$. For each coordinate its three values are zero and the two remaining nonnegative entries $x,y'$ in that row. The affine quantile minorant for the specified cap ceiling gives a second bound with $A_0=241/800$, $B_0=347/800$. Set $T_0=41/100$ and
$$
G(x,y')=\max\{T_0\max(x,y'),\ A_0\max(x,y')+B_0\min(x,y')\}.
$$
Summing the two lower estimates in the form of their coordinatewise maximum gives $1\ge G(r,s)+G(p,1-s)+G(1-p,1-r)$. The two defining expressions have slopes in $[0,B_0]$ in either coordinate separately, as do their pointwise maxima in the sense of monotonicity and the $B_0$ Lipschitz bound. Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
G(r,s)&\ge A_0+B_0s-B_0(1-r),\\
G(p,1-s)&\ge A_0+B_0(1-s)-B_0(1-p),\\
G(1-p,1-r)&\ge T_0(1-p).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sum is at least $2A_0+T_0-B_0(1-r)$, because $0<1-p<1$ and $B_0>T_0$. It exceeds $2A_0+T_0-B_0/63=1+283/50400$, a contradiction.
