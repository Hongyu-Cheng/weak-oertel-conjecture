## Statement

For a bounded Borel set $A\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ define $M_t(A)=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}|A_{z+t}|_d$ for $t\in[0,1)^n$, using counting for $d=0$. Then $\int M_t(A)\,dt=|A|_{n+d}$. If $C$ is a convex body and $H$ a closed halfspace, set $p_t(H)=M_t(C\cap H)/M_t(C)$ when $M_t(C)>0$ and zero otherwise. Then the $M_t(C)$-weighted average of $p_t(H)$ is $|C\cap H|_{n+d}/|C|_{n+d}$. If $M_0(C)>0$, every finite real combination satisfies
$$
\int M_t(C)\sum_j c_j[p_0(H_j)-p_t(H_j)]\,dt
=|C|_{n+d}\sum_j c_jp_0(H_j)-\sum_j c_j|C\cap H_j|_{n+d}.
$$

## Proof

Tonelli's theorem on the nonnegative fiber indicators and the disjoint partition by $z+[0,1)^n$ identify the integral of $M_t(A)$ with the ambient integral of the indicator of $A$. This works also in dimension $d=0$. If $M_t(C)=0$, then $M_t(C\cap H)=0$, so $M_t(C)p_t(H)=M_t(C\cap H)$ holds at every phase. Integrate this equality and divide by the positive ambient volume of $C$ for the weighted average. Subtraction and linearity give the finite signed identity.
