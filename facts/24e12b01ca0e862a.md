## Statement

There is an actual two-fiber triangle family and a single group of three fixed ambient caps covering both whole fiber planes, every proper trace canonical, with first cap derivative of rank $3$ on the direct sum of the two canonical positive velocity spaces. Thus the complete-block bound $dh$ cannot be inferred from the assumption that every proper trace is canonical alone.

## Proof

At bases $z=0,1$ take identical triangles with affine barycentric coordinates $\lambda_0+\lambda_1+\lambda_2=1$. Their convex hull has exactly these integer fibers, and arbitrary small independent variations of the two endpoint triangles remain actual. Use thresholds $\lambda_0\ge1/3-4z/3$, $\lambda_1\ge1/3-4z/3$, $\lambda_2\ge-1+4z/3$, with budgets equal to their masses. At zero the first two traces are canonical and the last strictly full; at one the first two are strictly full and the last canonical. The sum of the three thresholds is less than $1$ at both bases, so the caps cover both whole planes, including outside the triangles.

The zero-sum column-sum lifts of e42e4ad8eaa4f705 have trace zero and full row vectors $-dm c/(dm-1)$ with $\sum_i c_i=0$, hence exhaust all zero-sum row vectors independently in each fiber. A canonical trace's derivative is $\gamma V$ times its row coordinate and a full trace has zero derivative in these directions. At zero the first two rows are arbitrary independent derivatives and the last is zero; at one the last row is arbitrary and the first two are zero. The map has rank $3>2=dh$.
