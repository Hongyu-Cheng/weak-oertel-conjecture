## Statement

For the actual rectangle $C=[-1,1]\times[-1,1]$ with first coordinate integer, put $M=6$, $\Sigma=1/3$, $E(y)=2(1-y^2)$ and $f(y)=\sqrt{E(y)/M}$. At the fixed mark $Y=(0,s)$ with $0<s<1/2$, use the invariant buffer $\varepsilon f(s)\sqrt{u^2\Sigma}$ added to the through-mark margin, where $\varepsilon=3s/\sqrt{1-s^2}$. The raw buffered infimum is $3$. It is attained by $x+z\ge0$, with inside support at $(1,-1)$, outside support at $(-1,1)$ and proper own trace at $(0,0)$. Its displaced inclusion of inside and outside moment sets holds with equality.

## Proof

The total mass is three times two and the uniform variance in each interval is $1/3$. The energy calculation in the proof of 8cc17f76507d258f gives $E(y)=2(1-y^2)$ on the same interval. For a unit continuous coefficient, the given physical buffer is $s$. Every upward cap is therefore $x+az\ge0$, of mass $3$ by central symmetry and the zero one-dimensional mass of the boundary traces. Every downward cap is $x\le az+2s$. Its own contribution is $1+2s$, and at the two endpoint fibers the sum is at least $2$ since the thresholds $\pm a$ split the reflected intervals into contributions adding to $2$, and increasing both by $2s$ can only increase the mass. Pure-base through-mark caps have mass $4$. Any outward threshold shift increases mass weakly. At $a=1$ in the upward family the three traces are as stated. The displaced mark is the origin; the inside and outside section points are opposite, their counting coareas are both one, and the proper section point is the origin. Hence the translated moment inclusion is equality.
