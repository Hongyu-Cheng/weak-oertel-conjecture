## Statement

In the ambient simplex with vertices $(0,0),(4,0),(2-1/10,e_1),(2+1/10,e_2),(2,e_3),(2,e_4),(2,e_5)$, the true mixed centroid has base $2$. Its continuous coordinates sum to $q=310337/387828$. Write $a=25/798$ and $W=20/21$ for the relative endpoint and middle fiber volumes in units of $|T_5|$. The closed cylindrical cap $\sum_i x_i\le q$ through this centroid has normalized mass $(q^5+2a)/(W+2a)<\Gamma_5=(5/6)^5$. The centroid of the base-$2$ fiber has normalized mixed depth at least $\Gamma_5 W/(W+2a)=59375/157464>\Gamma_5/2$.

## Proof

The base reflection $z\mapsto4-z$ combined with swapping $x_1,x_2$ preserves the simplex and mixed measure. Thus the mixed centroid has base $2$, and by convexity it is in the actual middle fiber. Its sum of continuous coordinates is computed from the three measured fibers of the non-log-concave simplex example. For general $d,\varepsilon$ in that example, a uniform point in an endpoint simplex has expected coordinate sum $(d/2+\varepsilon^2/(4-\varepsilon^2))/(d+1)$, since its centroid is the average of its vertices. The middle fiber is $T_d$ minus the two disjoint vertex caps, up to their null boundaries. Each removed cap has one vertex whose coordinate sum is $W=2/(2+\varepsilon)$ and $d$ vertices whose sum is one. The expected sum in the middle fiber is therefore
$$
\frac{d-(1-W)(d+W)}{(d+1)W}=\frac{d-1+W}{d+1}.
$$
Weighting these expected sums by $a,W,a$ gives
$$
q=\frac{W(d-1+W)+2a(d/2+\varepsilon^2/(4-\varepsilon^2))}
{(d+1)(W+2a)}.
$$
For $d=5$ and $\varepsilon=1/10$, the values of $a,W$ in the statement give $W+2a=135/133$ and $q=310337/387828$. One has $10/19<q<20/21$. The largest endpoint coordinate sum is $10/19$, so the cap takes both endpoints completely. At base two its trace is exactly $qT_5$, because $2\sum_i x_i+\varepsilon|x_1-x_2|\le(2+\varepsilon)q<2$ for $x\in qT_5$. Thus its mass fraction is $(q^5+2a)/(W+2a)$. Using $q<161/200$ gives a strict gap since
$$
\left(\frac56\right)^5\frac{135}{133}
-\left[\left(\frac{161}{200}\right)^5+\frac{25}{399}\right]
=\frac{2763786689203}{383040000000000}>0.
$$
For the second point, each ambient closed halfspace containing the own centroid of the base-$2$ fiber cuts that fiber by a halfspace containing its centroid, or contains it entirely. The ordinary centroid inequality, as stated in the proof of 03bc771bddd39a8a, gives at least $\Gamma_5 W|T_5|$. Divide by $(W+2a)|T_5|$ to obtain the displayed bound, which is strictly greater than $\Gamma_5/2$ since $W>2a$.
