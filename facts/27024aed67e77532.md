## Statement

Retain one common canonical hard instance of accepted facts 036a276c54ec5e66, 6bfe50fed9a5540b, 73982b467964f46a, and d01de728d8b5e33c. In particular, the half-strength product reservoir is \(r=(r_e)\),

\[
 Q:=\sum_e r_e>0,
 \qquad
 d_y(r):=\sum_{e\ni y}r_e,
 \qquad
 \sum_y d_y(r)=2Q.
\tag{1}
\]

Fix the same \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\) as in accepted facts 73982b467964f46a and d01de728d8b5e33c, and write

\[
 Y:=\widehat Y(v),
 \qquad
 w_y:=\widehat\tau_y(v)>0\quad(y\in Y),
 \qquad
 \widehat P:=\sum_\sigma\widehat\theta_\sigma(v).
\tag{2}
\]

Extend \(w_y\) by zero to every positive-fiber base outside \(Y\). Every retained label \(\sigma\) has two distinct physical opposite-source endpoints \(a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\), and physical collision aggregation gives

\[
 w_y
 =\sum_{\sigma:\,y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}
 \widehat\theta_\sigma(v),
 \qquad
 \sum_yw_y=2\widehat P>\frac{7Q}{100}.
\tag{3}
\]

Assume the registered proper-subset antecedent

\[
 2^{n-\rho_A}\frac{\sum_{y\in A}w_y}{r_A^c+1}
 <\frac{Q}{400\gamma_d}
 \quad
 \text{for every nonempty proper }A\subsetneq Y,
\tag{4}
\]

with \(\rho_A,r_A^c,\gamma_d\) in the canonical notation of accepted fact 73982b467964f46a.

Assume additionally the zero-fresh hypothesis of accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b, and retain from its completed-midpoint restart the submatching \(b\le r\), its fresh load-minimal KKT loads

\[
 0\le\omega_e^b\le b_e\le r_e,
 \qquad
 \Omega_b:=\sum_e\omega_e^b>\frac{97Q}{100},
\tag{5}
\]

and the physical restarted endpoint loads

\[
 \beta_y^b:=\sum_{e\ni y}\omega_e^b.
\tag{6}
\]

Then

\[
 0\le w_y\le d_y(r),
 \qquad
 0\le\beta_y^b\le d_y(r),
 \qquad
 \sum_y\beta_y^b=2\Omega_b,
\tag{7}
\]

and the exact physical endpoint overlap obeys

\[
 \boxed{
 U:=\sum_y\min\{w_y,\beta_y^b\}>\frac Q{100}.}
\tag{8}
\]

For \(y\in Y\), define the completion-to-atlas ratio and overlap load

\[
 q_y:=\min\left\{1,\frac{\beta_y^b}{w_y}\right\},
 \qquad
 u_y:=q_yw_y=\min\{w_y,\beta_y^b\}.
\tag{9}
\]

Define the fused internal-label mass and fused cut mass by

\[
 P_\cap
 :=\sum_\sigma\widehat\theta_\sigma(v)
 \min\{q_{a(\sigma)},q_{b(\sigma)}\},
\tag{10}
\]

\[
 I_\cap
 :=\sum_\sigma\widehat\theta_\sigma(v)
 \left|q_{a(\sigma)}-q_{b(\sigma)}\right|.
\tag{11}
\]

Then the collision-aggregated whole-label conservation law is

\[
 \boxed{U=2P_\cap+I_\cap.}
\tag{12}
\]

For \(0<s\le1\), put

\[
 S_s:=\{y\in Y:q_y\ge s\},
 \qquad
 \Gamma_s:=\{\sigma:a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\in S_s\},
\tag{13}
\]

and let

\[
 M_s:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma_s}\widehat\theta_\sigma(v),
\tag{14}
\]

\[
 C_s:=
 \sum_{\sigma:
 |\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}\cap S_s|=1}
 \widehat\theta_\sigma(v).
\tag{15}
\]

The exact layer-cake identities are

\[
 \boxed{P_\cap=\int_0^1M_s\,ds,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{I_\cap=\int_0^1C_s\,ds.}
\tag{16}
\]

Consequently at least one of the following two alternatives holds.

1. There is \(s\in(0,1]\) for which \(\Gamma_s\) is an intact source-feasible whole-label subfamily and

\[
 M_s\ge\frac Q{800}.
\tag{17}
\]

With its physical selected endpoint loads

\[
 u_y^{(s)}
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma_s\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \widehat\theta_\sigma(v),
\tag{18}
\]

one has

\[
 \boxed{\sum_yu_y^{(s)}=2M_s\ge\frac Q{400}.}
\tag{19}
\]

Thus (19) is exactly the scalar condition of the registered positive-simplex alternative at \(q=n\); only containment in one greedy simplex and its positive root inequalities remain to be proved.

2. There is \(s\in(0,1]\) for which

\[
 \boxed{C_s>\frac{3Q}{400}.}
\tag{20}
\]

Both \(S_s\) and \(Y\setminus S_s\) are then nonempty proper endpoint subsets, and

\[
 \sum_{y\in S_s}w_y\ge C_s,
 \qquad
 \sum_{y\in Y\setminus S_s}w_y\ge C_s.
\tag{21}
\]

Moreover, this is a literal KKT-to-atlas ratio cut:

\[
 \beta_y^b\ge sw_y\quad(y\in S_s),
 \qquad
 \beta_y^b<sw_y\quad(y\in Y\setminus S_s).
\tag{22}
\]

Writing \(A=S_s\) or \(A=Y\setminus S_s\), the registered antecedent (4) and (20)--(21) force on both sides

\[
 \boxed{
 r_A^c+1
 >3\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_A}.}
\tag{23}
\]

No assertion is made that Alternative 1 already has positive barycentric containment, or that Alternative 2 is already a complete-core profile. Equations (19) and (20)--(23) isolate the two remaining completed-midpoint bridges without replacing either by signed affine closure or by a sum of local simplex scores.

## Proof

Accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b gives (5), and therefore

\[
 \beta_y^b
 =\sum_{e\ni y}\omega_e^b
 \le\sum_{e\ni y}r_e
 =d_y(r).
\tag{24}
\]

Every retained synchronized label is a thinning of one positive transverse-atlas label of accepted fact 036a276c54ec5e66. Fix a physical source \(y\). At its occurrence in a label, \(y\) is the opposite endpoint of one original reservoir edge \(e\ni y\). For a fixed \(e\), the transverse-pairing constraint bounds the sum of all original label weights using \(e\) by its original load, which is at most \(r_e\). Thinning only decreases every label weight. Summing first over labels using a fixed edge and then over the original edges incident with \(y\) gives

\[
 w_y
 =\sum_{\sigma\ni y}\widehat\theta_\sigma(v)
 \le\sum_{e\ni y}r_e
 =d_y(r).
\tag{25}
\]

This proves the two coordinatewise inequalities in (7). Each reservoir edge and each restarted edge load has two distinct physical endpoints, so

\[
 \sum_yd_y(r)=2\sum_er_e=2Q,
 \qquad
 \sum_y\beta_y^b=2\sum_e\omega_e^b=2\Omega_b.
\tag{26}
\]

For three numbers \(0\le x,z\le c\), one has

\[
 \min\{x,z\}\ge x+z-c.
\tag{27}
\]

Apply (27) at every physical base with

\[
 x=w_y,
 \qquad z=\beta_y^b,
 \qquad c=d_y(r),
\]

and sum. Accepted fact 73982b467964f46a gives the strict strengthened synchronized mass in (3), while accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b gives (5). Thus (3), (5), and (26) yield

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 U
 &\ge\sum_yw_y+\sum_y\beta_y^b-\sum_yd_y(r)\\
 &>\frac{7Q}{100}+\frac{97Q}{50}-2Q
 =\frac Q{100}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{28}
\]

This proves (8). Notice that (28) is a physical-base overlap taken only after all endpoint collisions in both ledgers have been aggregated.

By (3), every label has exactly the two displayed source occurrences. Hence

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 U
 &=\sum_{y\in Y}w_yq_y\\
 &=\sum_\sigma\widehat\theta_\sigma(v)
 \bigl(q_{a(\sigma)}+q_{b(\sigma)}\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{29}
\]

For \(r,t\in[0,1]\),

\[
 r+t=2\min\{r,t\}+|r-t|.
\tag{30}
\]

Apply (30) label by label in (29) and use (10)--(11). This proves (12), with every original retained label charged once.

Also, for \(r,t\in[0,1]\),

\[
 \min\{r,t\}
 =\int_0^1\mathbf1_{\{r\ge s\}}\mathbf1_{\{t\ge s\}}\,ds,
\tag{31}
\]

\[
 |r-t|
 =\int_0^1
 \left|\mathbf1_{\{r\ge s\}}-\mathbf1_{\{t\ge s\}}\right|\,ds.
\tag{32}
\]

Substitute (31)--(32) in (10)--(11), interchange the finite sums with the integrals, and use (13)--(15). This proves (16). Physical midpoint or target-atom collisions do not enter these identities, and physical source collisions were already aggregated in (3).

Suppose first that \(P_\cap\ge Q/800\). The finite step function \(M_s\) on \((0,1]\) has integral \(P_\cap\), so it attains a value at least \(P_\cap\). Choose such an \(s\). This proves (17). The source-feasibility inequality for every whole-label subfamily is inherited from accepted fact d01de728d8b5e33c. Summing (18) over physical endpoints counts each selected label at its two distinct source occurrences, proving (19).

Suppose instead that \(P_\cap<Q/800\). Equations (8) and (12) give

\[
 I_\cap=U-2P_\cap
 >\frac Q{100}-\frac Q{400}
 =\frac{3Q}{400}.
\tag{33}
\]

The finite step function \(C_s\) has integral \(I_\cap\), so some \(s\in(0,1]\) satisfies (20). A positive cut contains a label with one endpoint on each side, proving that both sides are nonempty and proper. Every cut label contributes its full weight once to the physical degree sum on each side. Noncut labels contribute nonnegative weights. This proves (21).

If \(y\in S_s\), then \(q_y\ge s\). By (9), this implies \(\beta_y^b/w_y\ge s\). If \(y\notin S_s\), then \(q_y<s\le1\), so the minimum in (9) is attained by \(\beta_y^b/w_y\), which is strictly smaller than \(s\). This proves (22).

Finally let \(A\) be either side of the cut. It is a nonempty proper subset, so (4) applies. Rearranging (4), then using (20)--(21), gives

\[
 r_A^c+1
 >400\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_A}
 \frac{\sum_{y\in A}w_y}{Q}
 >3\gamma_d\,2^{n-\rho_A}.
\tag{34}
\]

This is (23) for both sides and completes the proof.
