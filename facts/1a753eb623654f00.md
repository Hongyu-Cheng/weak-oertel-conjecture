## Statement

Let $S$ be a finite union of sampled convex $d$-fibers with $d$-dimensional volume at least $v>0$ in each fiber, summed measure $dm$ and $M\le M_{\max}$. Let $L>0$ on sampled interiors be nonnegative, upper semicontinuous and concave on each fiber. For finitely many source values $L_i>0$ assume probabilities $\rho_i,\bar\rho_i$ and weights $\sigma_i$ satisfy
$$
\frac{dm}{M}=\sum_i\sigma_i\bar\rho_i,\quad
d\bar\rho_i=(L/L_i)\,d\rho_i,\quad L/L_i\le A\ \rho_i\text{-a.e.},\qquad
\sum_i\sigma_i\log L_i\le0.
$$
Assume $N>0$, $0\le E_i\le M$, $\kappa_i\ge0$, $\sum_i\kappa_i\le K_*$, and
$$
\sigma_i=\frac{\kappa_i (L_i E_i/M)^{1/N}}{\Delta/\varepsilon},
\qquad \Delta/\varepsilon\ge a_*>0.
$$
Put $B_*=K_*N/(e a_*)$. Then for $T>1$,
$$
m\{L>AT\}/M\le B_*/\log T,\qquad
\sup_S L\le 2A\exp(2^dM_{\max}B_*/v)=:L_*.
$$

## Proof

For $0<l\le1$, $l^{1/N}(-\log l)\le N/e$ by maximizing $x\exp(-x/N)$ for $x\ge0$. Thus the same-force formula and $E_i\le M$ imply $\sum_i\sigma_i(-\log L_i)_+\le B_*$. The nonpositive logarithmic mean of the source values gives $\sum_i\sigma_i(\log L_i)_+\le B_*$. Hence $\sum_{L_i>T}\sigma_i\le B_*/\log T$. For each $L_i\le T$ the likelihood bound and absolute continuity give $\bar\rho_i\{L>AT\}=0$. The target mixture proves the tail estimate.

The likelihood bound and the probability normalization give $A\ge1$. Take any $X_0$ in a fiber $K_z$. Concavity and nonnegativity give $L\ge L(X_0)/2$ on $(X_0+K_z)/2$, of volume at least $2^{-d}v$. If $L(X_0)>2A$, apply the tail estimate for every $1<T<L(X_0)/(2A)$ and let $T\uparrow L(X_0)/(2A)$. It follows that $\log(L(X_0)/(2A))\le 2^dM B_*/v$. The bound holds also if $L(X_0)\le2A$, and taking the supremum proves the result.
