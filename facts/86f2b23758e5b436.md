## Statement

In the standard tetrahedron $S$, let $0<\varepsilon\le1/100$ and
$$
Q_\varepsilon=S\cap\{\varepsilon(\lambda_0+\lambda_1-2\lambda_2)\le\lambda_3\},\qquad R_\varepsilon=S\setminus Q_\varepsilon.
$$
The four facet centroids belong to $Q_\varepsilon$, and the Hausdorff distance to $S$ in the barycentric sup norm is at most $\varepsilon$. Nevertheless $(9/25,9/25,7/25,0)$ belongs to $R_\varepsilon$ and to three canonical centroid caps, outside the tube $\{\lambda_2+\lambda_3\le1/4\}$. For $H_i=\{\lambda_i\ge1/4\}$, the exact fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R_\varepsilon|/|S|&=\frac{\varepsilon(5\varepsilon+8)}{9(1+\varepsilon)^2},\\
|R_\varepsilon\cap H_0|/|S|=|R_\varepsilon\cap H_1|/|S|
&=\frac{\varepsilon(11\varepsilon+23)}{36(1+\varepsilon)^2},\\
|R_\varepsilon\cap H_2|/|S|&=\frac{\varepsilon(23\varepsilon+26)}{576(1+\varepsilon)^2},\qquad
|R_\varepsilon\cap H_3|=0 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The summed-loss ratio to $|R_\varepsilon|$ is $3(125\varepsilon+254)/[64(5\varepsilon+8)]<3/2$, with limit $381/256$. In particular arbitrarily small volume loss and Hausdorff distance, even with all facet centroids retained, do not imply the thin-edge tube condition.

## Proof

The affine margin $h=\varepsilon(\lambda_0+\lambda_1-2\lambda_2)-\lambda_3$ has vertex values $(\varepsilon,\varepsilon,-2\varepsilon,-1)$. At each facet centroid the margin is the average of the corresponding three vertex values, which is nonpositive; it is zero at the centroid of the facet opposite vertex 3. At any excluded point $x$, $0<h(x)\le\varepsilon$, and $h(v_3)=-1$. The point $(x+h(x)v_3)/(1+h(x))$ has margin zero and is within $\varepsilon$ in barycentric sup norm of $x$. This proves the Hausdorff bound. At the displayed rational point, the margin is $4\varepsilon/25>0$, and its first three coordinates are above $1/4$, with $\lambda_2+\lambda_3=7/25>1/4$.

For positive $a,r,w$, the raw strict-quadrilateral cap formula of 1462fa9ce6ced54e at heights $(a,a,-r,-w)$ simplifies to
$$
P(a,r,w)=\frac{a^2[a^2+2a(r+w)+3rw]}{(a+r)^2(a+w)^2}.
$$
The open and closed versions have the same ordinary volume. Each $H_i\cap S$ is the affine copy $v_i/4+(3/4)S$, of volume fraction $27/64$, and its vertex margins are $(h(v_i)+3h(v_j))/4$. Rescale all four margins by 4 when evaluating its captured fraction. The whole loss is $P(\varepsilon,2\varepsilon,1)$; the $H_0,H_1$ losses are $(27/64)P(4\varepsilon,5\varepsilon,3-\varepsilon)$; the $H_2$ loss is $(27/64)P(\varepsilon,8\varepsilon,3+2\varepsilon)$. All these inputs are positive in the stated range. The $H_3$ transformed heights are strictly negative, so its loss is zero. Substitution gives the three displayed rational fractions. Dividing the summed fractions by the whole loss gives the stated ratio; its gap below $3/2$ is $3(35\varepsilon+2)/[64(5\varepsilon+8)]>0$.
