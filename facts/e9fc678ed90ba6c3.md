## Statement

Let $dm$ be finite positive mixed volume of mass $M$ on a compact sampled set $S$. Let $L$ be continuous and strictly positive on its compact convex hull with $\int\log L\,dm/M=0$. Let $\rho_i$ be finitely many probability rows on $S$ with $L_i=\int L\,d\rho_i$, and assume $\sigma_i>0$ with $\sum_i\sigma_i=1$ and $\sum_i(\sigma_i/L_i)\rho_i=dm/(ML)$. Set $d\bar\rho_i=(L/L_i)d\rho_i$, $\ell_i=\operatorname*{ess\,inf}_{\rho_i}L$, $u_i=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\rho_i}L$, and $D_i=D(\bar\rho_i\|\rho_i)$. Then
$$
D_i=\int\log(L/L_i)\,d\bar\rho_i,\qquad
\frac{\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_i}(L)}{2L_i u_i}\le D_i\le
\frac{\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_i}(L)}{2L_i\ell_i},\qquad
\sum_i\sigma_i D_i=-\sum_i\sigma_i\log L_i\ge0.
$$
The last inequality is equality exactly when each row has $L=L_i$ almost everywhere. If in a positive row $L$ agrees almost everywhere with an affine function whose continuous gradient is nonzero, and $dm$ sums Lebesgue $d$-volume on finitely many positive fibers with $d\ge1$, then that row has $D_i>0$ and the total reserve is strictly positive.

## Proof

The tilted laws $\bar\rho_i$ are probabilities whose weighted sum is $dm/M$. Their likelihood ratios $s=L/L_i$ have $\rho_i$-mean one. Thus $D_i=\int s\log s\,d\rho_i$ is finite. Since $(s\log s)''=1/s$, Taylor bounds at one give
$$
(s-1)+\frac{L_i}{2u_i}(s-1)^2
\le s\log s\le
(s-1)+\frac{L_i}{2\ell_i}(s-1)^2
$$
on the essential range interval $[\ell_i/L_i,u_i/L_i]$, which contains one. Integration proves the variance bounds and their zero cases. The mixture and normalization give
$$
\sum_i\sigma_i D_i=\int\log L\,dm/M-\sum_i\sigma_i\log L_i.
$$
If a positive row with the stated affine contact had $D_i=0$, it would be carried by a fixed level of the contacting affine function. Its nonzero continuous slope makes that level null in each sampled fiber, contrary to its probability mass and its absolute continuity, which follows from the inverse-density mixture.
