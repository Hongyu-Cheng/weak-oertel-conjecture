## Statement

Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^2$ be any union of triangles $\{(u,v):u,v\ge0,\ u/a+v/b<1\}$, where $0<a\le1/100$ and $0<b\le B=501/1000$. Then, with $s=309/1000$ and $r=64/167=1-s/B$,
$$
|U\cap\{v\ge s\}|\le r^2 |U|,
$$
where $r^2<3/20$. The factor $r^2$ is attained for one constituent triangle with $b=B$.

## Proof

The union is relatively open in the closed first quadrant, hence Borel, and it is bounded by the intercept limits. On $v\ge s$, use the injective affine map $(u,v)\mapsto(u/r,(v-s)/r)$. A point in one constituent with $v\ge s$ has $b>s$; its image has nonnegative coordinates and normalized sum
$$
\frac{u/a+v/b-s/b}{r}<\frac{1-s/b}{r}\le1 .
$$
Thus the map takes the tail into the same constituent and hence the whole union into itself on that tail, without splitting the overlaps. Its area Jacobian is $r^{-2}$, which gives the bound. The inverse maps the entire single constituent with $b=B$ onto its own tail because then $r+s/b=1$. This proves sharpness; $20\cdot64^2<3\cdot167^2$ gives the numerical bound.
