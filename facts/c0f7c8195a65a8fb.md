## Statement

The attained hinge value for whole-space covers is right-continuous at every feasible budget, in the fixed-count framed or unframed class with the full-dimensional compact convex source fibers of the whole-space attainment theorem. In the framed polytopal setting, for interior feasible $a<b$,
$$
w(a)-w(b)=M\int_a^b \sum_i r_i(t)\,dt+\sum_{a<t\le b}\big[w(t-)-w(t)\big],
$$
where the integral uses the normal stress budget multipliers at regular budgets through their unique total $-w'(t)/M$. The jump sum has finitely many nonzero terms.

## Proof

Let feasible budgets $\tau_j$ decrease to a feasible $\tau$, and take their attained minimizing covers. Their objectives are bounded by the finite value at $\tau$. The minimizing-sequence compactification in the whole-space attainment theorem applies to this bounded sequence: it preserves every finite threshold, gives convergence of source cap masses and hinges, and retains whole-space coverage by the limiting balanced weights. The limiting cap masses obey $c_i\le\tau M$. The limit objective is therefore at least the value at $\tau$, and monotonicity gives the reverse bound. This proves right continuity in each stated class.

In the framed polytopal class, partition $[a,b]$ at the finitely many nonregular interior budgets, including any at the right endpoint as needed. On the open intervals the value is $C^1$ with $-w'=M\sum_i r_i$. Its monotonicity and finiteness on $[a,b]$ ensure finite one-sided limits. Apply the fundamental theorem of calculus first on closed subintervals of each open interval and take one-sided limits. The integrand is nonnegative, so these improper integrals exist and telescope to the total value drop after adding the downward jumps. Right continuity makes the initial value the right limit at $a$ and gives the stated right-hand convention on all jumps. The total budget multiplier is the derivative at every regular point, so no selection of individual multiplier functions is needed.
