## Statement

Let $v_1,\ldots,v_{d+1}$ span $\mathbb R^d$ and sum to zero, $d\ge1$, and set $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$ and $\kappa_d=|T\cap(-T)|/|T|$. Suppose $A,K,B$ are full-dimensional convex bodies, $B\subseteq T$, $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$, and each $v_i\cdot x=-1$ meets $\operatorname{int}A$. Let $K_{\rm out}=\{x:v_i\cdot x\ge-h_K(-v_i)\ \forall i\}$, and write $a=|A|$, $b=|B|$, $k=|K|$. Then $b<\kappa_d|K_{\rm out}|$. If $a<b/32$ and $k\le a+b$, then
$
\frac{k}{|K_{\rm out}|}<\frac{33}{32}\kappa_d .
$
For $d=2$ this is the strict bound $11/16$.

## Proof

The set $K_{\rm out}$ contains $K$ and hence the midpoint body, so it has nonempty interior. It is bounded because any recession vector has all $v_i\cdot x\ge0$, which by balance and spanning forces it to be zero. Its $d+1$ halfspaces give a positive aligned simplex: their offsets can be equalized by a translation since the evaluations of the $v_i$ have image the zero-sum subspace. The equalized scale is positive by full dimension, and each outward support is the defining value. Thus the aligned-simplex-midpoint-volume-credit theorem applies with the same $A,B$ and the same proper $A$ cuts, giving the first strict inequality. Under the two mass hypotheses $k\le a+b<(33/32)b$ gives the second. For $d=2$, the erosion formula with $A=K=-T$ and $B=T\cap(-T)$ at the equal bounds $c_i=2/3$ yields $\kappa_2=1-3(1/3)^2=2/3$. Multiplying by $33/32$ gives $11/16$.
