## Statement

Let $P\subset\mathbb R^n$ be finite nonempty and let $K_z\subset\mathbb R^d$ be nonempty compact convex sets such that $C=\operatorname{conv}\bigcup_{z\in P}(\{z\}\times K_z)$ has exactly the sampled fibers $K_z$ at $z\in P$. Fix a unit normal $u$ and write $h_z=h_{K_z}(u)$. There is a globally finite concave piecewise affine function $\widetilde h$ on $\mathbb R^n$ with $\widetilde h(z)=h_z$ on $P$ and $u\cdot X\le\widetilde h(z)$ for all $(z,X)\in C$. If $\rho_{\rm old}\preceq_{\rm cx}\rho$ are measures supported on $C$ with bases in $P$, and the old law is supported on $\{(z,X):z\in P,\ u\cdot X=h_z\}$, the new law has the same support property.

## Proof

Consider the downward polyhedron $\operatorname{conv}\{(z,h_z):z\in P\}+\{(0,-s):s\ge0\}$ in $\operatorname{aff}(P)\times\mathbb R$. Its highest ordinate over $z\in\operatorname{conv}P$ is the maximum of $\sum_j\theta_j h_{z_j}$ over convex representations $z=\sum_j\theta_j z_j$. At a sampled site this is exactly $h_z$. Indeed a convex combination of maximizing points of the $K_{z_j}$ belongs to the exact sampled fiber, and the one-site representation gives equality. Over the whole projection this upper boundary majorizes $u\cdot X$ on $C$. The polyhedron is full-dimensional in its relative base times the ordinate line, with finitely many facets. Vertical facets restrict its projected domain. Every other facet is an upper bound by an affine function, since the polyhedron is invariant under downward translation. The minimum of these finitely many affine functions equals the roof on $\operatorname{conv}P$. Extend each affinely to the full base space and take their minimum $\widetilde h$. This argument includes a one-point projection, when the roof is constant there.

The function $\Phi(z,X)=u\cdot X-\widetilde h(z)$ is finite convex, nonpositive on $C$, and zero on the old support. Convex order gives $0=\int\Phi\,d\rho_{\rm old}\le\int\Phi\,d\rho\le0$. Thus $\Phi=0$ $\rho$-almost everywhere, and on each sampled base this is the actual support face.
