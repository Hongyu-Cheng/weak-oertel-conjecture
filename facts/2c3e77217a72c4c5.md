## Statement

Let $n,d\ge1$, let $K\subset\mathbb R^d$ be a $d$-simplex of volume $v$, and put $S=\{0,1\}^n\times K$ with summed fiber volume $\mu$ and mass $M=2^n v$. Write $H_j$ for the altitude from vertex $v_j$, $\lambda_j$ for its barycentric coordinate, $g$ for the centroid, $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$, and $N_0=2^n(d+1)$. Suppose $d\ge2$ and fix any full cover at count $N_0$ by continuously unit-normal caps with each cap of mass at most $\gamma_d v$. The actual-fiber joint Fritz–John stress system has a solution with objective multiplier $\alpha_0>0$ if and only if $K$ is regular. If $K$ is regular, one may choose $\alpha_0=1$ and all cap-budget multipliers zero.

In the system, $(c_i,P_i,m_i)$ are the cap mass and base and continuous first moments, $(S_i,P_i^s,m_i^s)$ are the corresponding effective section moments with one common density per section and density one on proper sections, $\Psi_i=\int_S(L_i)_+\,d\mu$, and $\lambda_p$ are optimal probability minimax weights at base $p$. The multipliers $\alpha_0,r_i,\eta_p$ are nonnegative with complementarity for budget and cover. At each $\eta_p>0$ the system has a genuine contact $x_p\in K$ with every margin nonpositive and equality on positively weighted caps. Its moment balances are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_0c_i+r_i S_i&=\sum_p\eta_p\lambda_{pi},\\
\alpha_0P_i+r_i P_i^s&=\sum_p\eta_p\lambda_{pi}p,\\
\alpha_0m_i+r_i m_i^s&=\sum_p\eta_p\lambda_{pi}x_p+\alpha_0\Psi_i u_i .
\end{aligned}
$$

## Proof

By the exact-count product theorem every cap is assigned to one binary fiber $p$, where it is a canonical centroid cap with unit normal $u_i=H_j\nabla\lambda_j$ for some $j$, and it has zero mass on every other fiber. Every cap assigned elsewhere has strictly negative margin at $(p,g)$: otherwise it would contain this interior point and have positive volume there. At $g$ the pointwise maximum margin is zero, so the full cover makes the minimax value zero. An optimal probability vector has weighted minimum zero and hence average zero at $g$. Its weights on off-fiber caps vanish. Since $g$ is interior and minimizes this affine weighted margin, its resultant normal is zero. The positive relation among the own-fiber normals is unique up to scaling: the gradients of the barycentric coordinates have rank $d$ and sum to zero, so the weights are proportional to $1/H_j$ and every local weight is positive. A genuine contact is necessarily $g$ because all own-fiber margins nonpositive means every barycentric coordinate is at most $1/(d+1)$.

Fix cap $i$ assigned to $p$. Its cap base moment is $c_ip$, and it can receive contact weight only at $p$. Subtracting $p$ times the mass balance from the base balance gives $r_i(P_i^s-pS_i)=0$. If $s_{iz}\ge0$ is the effective section mass at $z$, then $P_i^s-pS_i=\sum_z s_{iz}(z-p)$. Pair with the linear part of the affine binary distance $q_p(z)=\sum_k[(1-p_k)z_k+p_k(1-z_k)]$. This yields $r_i\sum_z s_{iz}q_p(z)=0$, so the multiplied section measure has no off-fiber mass. The proper own section at $\lambda_j=1/(d+1)$ has centroid $g$: it is a homothetic image of the opposite facet whose vertices are $v_j/(d+1)+d v_k/(d+1)$ for $k\ne j$. Thus $r_im_i^s=r_iS_i g$.

The own cap centroid is $g+(v_j-g)/(d+1)$ by homothety about $v_j$, and the product hinge formula for a single forced cap gives $\Psi_i/c_i=dH_j/(d+1)^2$. In the continuous moment balance use these two facts, the contact $g$, and the mass balance. If $\alpha_0>0$, the resulting identity is
$$
v_j-g=\frac{dH_j}{d+1}u_i .
$$
Consequently every $w_j=v_j-g$ is perpendicular to the corresponding opposite facet. For fixed $j$ the inner product $w_j\cdot w_k$ is constant over $k\ne j$. Symmetry shows all off-diagonal values equal a common $c$. Because $\sum_j w_j=0$, all squared lengths equal $-dc$, hence all squared edge lengths equal $-2(d+1)c>0$. This is regularity.

Conversely, in a regular simplex all altitudes agree, $v_j-g=(dH_j/(d+1))u_i$, and the local optimal weights just derived are uniform. At each binary $p$ choose the contact $g$ and multiplier $\eta_p=(d+1)\gamma_d v$, with $\alpha_0=1$ and all $r_i=0$. The averaged local margin vanishes identically, and $g$ has zero local margins and strictly negative off-fiber margins. Thus contact, normal-cone and complementarity conditions hold. The mass and base balances follow from $c_i=\gamma_d v$, $P_i=c_ip$ and $\eta_p\lambda_{pi}=c_i$. The cap centroid and the displayed altitude identity give the continuous balance. These same data lift to the joint chart stationarity of c343c9f3dc96957e: each inactive simplex coordinate has multiplier $-\eta_pL_i(p,g)$, and the sphere multiplier for cap $i$ is $-\Psi_i$ in the convention of that proof. Thus the stress is normal. The accepted joint theorem supplies existence of some stress at each such cover, because every exact-count cover minimizes the Euclidean hinge.
