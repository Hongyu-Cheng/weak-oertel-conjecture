## Statement

Retain the complete canonical hard-branch hypotheses, objects, and notation of accepted facts `2fe46f47d19112ea`, `d01de728d8b5e33c`, and `e0dcff96f9b90a96`.  Thus \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) is a convex body,
\[
 C_y:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(y,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 a_y:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_y),
 \qquad
 c_y:=\frac1{a_y}\int_{C_y}x\,dx,
\tag{1}
\]
for every positive-volume integer fiber, and
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<\frac Q{100},
 \qquad
 n\ge2,
 \qquad
 \gamma_d>\frac4{11}.
\tag{2}
\]
The rescaled internal atlas is the finite whole-label family \(\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\).  Every \(\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\) has positive weight \(\eta_\sigma\), two distinct opposite endpoints
\[
 a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\in S_{\mathrm{miss}},
\tag{3}
\]
original occurrence contacts
\[
 x_{\sigma,y}\in C_y
 \qquad
 \bigl(y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}\bigr),
\tag{4}
\]
and rescaled occurrence densities \(\widetilde g_{\sigma,y}\) of mass \(\eta_\sigma\) and barycenter \(x_{\sigma,y}\).  At every physical source \(y\in S_{\mathrm{miss}}\),
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \widetilde g_{\sigma,y}
 \leq\frac12
 \quad\text{pointwise on }C_y,
\tag{5}
\]
and
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma
 \leq t_{e_2(y)}
 \leq\frac{a_y}{4}.
\tag{6}
\]
The total internal mass satisfies
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 :=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}}\eta_\sigma
 >\frac7{400}Q.
\tag{7}
\]

Let \(\Gamma\subseteq\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\) be any whole-label subfamily and put
\[
 H_\Gamma:=\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\eta_\sigma,
\tag{8}
\]
\[
 R_y^\Gamma
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}\eta_\sigma,
 \qquad
 S_\Gamma:=\{y\in S_{\mathrm{miss}}:R_y^\Gamma>0\}.
\tag{9}
\]
For \(y\in S_\Gamma\), define the retained occurrence density, its recomputed complement, and the complement mass by
\[
 F_y^\Gamma
 :=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \widetilde g_{\sigma,y},
 \qquad
 B_y^\Gamma:=\frac12-F_y^\Gamma,
 \qquad
 b_y^\Gamma:=\int_{C_y}B_y^\Gamma.
\tag{10}
\]
Then
\[
 b_y^\Gamma=\frac{a_y}{2}-R_y^\Gamma
 \geq\frac{a_y}{4}>0,
\tag{11}
\]
so the recomputed complement barycenter
\[
 \bar q_y^\Gamma
 :=\frac1{b_y^\Gamma}
 \int_{C_y}xB_y^\Gamma(x)\,dx
\tag{12}
\]
is always defined.

Assume that there are one affine map
\[
 s:\operatorname{aff}(S_\Gamma)\longrightarrow\mathbb R^d
\tag{13}
\]
and one linear subspace \(W\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) with \(\dim W\le3\) such that
\[
 x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\in W
\tag{14}
\]
for every \(\sigma\in\Gamma\) and every \(y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}\), and
\[
 \bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\in W
 \qquad(y\in S_\Gamma).
\tag{15}
\]

For \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\), retain the synchronized endpoint set and weights of accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c`,
\[
 \widehat Y(v):=\{y:\widehat\tau_y(v)>0\}.
\tag{16}
\]
For every nonempty \(A\subseteq\widehat Y(v)\), put
\[
 \rho_A:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(A),
\tag{17}
\]
\[
 r_A^c
 :=\min_{s_A:\operatorname{aff}(A)\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}\{c_y-s_A(y):y\in A\},
\tag{18}
\]
and
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)
 :=2^{n-\rho_A}
 \frac{\sum_{y\in A}\widehat\tau_y(v)}{r_A^c+1}.
\tag{19}
\]

If
\[
 \boxed{H_\Gamma>\frac{3}{500\gamma_d}Q,}
\tag{20}
\]
then, for every \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\), there is a nonempty proper subset
\[
 A\subsetneq\widehat Y(v)
\]
such that
\[
 \boxed{
 \Lambda_v(A)
 \geq\frac5{12}H_\Gamma
 >\frac{Q}{400\gamma_d}.}
\tag{21}
\]
More precisely:

1. If \(S_\Gamma\subsetneq\widehat Y(v)\), one may choose \(A=S_\Gamma\), and then
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)\geq\frac12H_\Gamma.
\tag{22}
\]

2. If \(S_\Gamma=\widehat Y(v)\), one may delete an endpoint of minimum \(\widehat\tau_y(v)\)-weight, and the resulting proper set satisfies the first inequality in (21).

Consequently, equation (38) of accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c` gives
\[
 \boxed{R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)>\frac Q{100},}
\tag{23}
\]
contradicting the hard inequality in (2).

In particular, let
\[
 \Delta:=\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\Gamma.
\]
If
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Delta}\eta_\sigma
 \leq\frac Q{1000},}
\tag{24}
\]
then the chart hypotheses (13)-(15), with the complements recomputed from \(\Gamma\) as in (10)-(12), already imply (20)-(23).  No assertion is made that such a retained bulk chart always exists.

## Proof

Fix a whole-label subfamily \(\Gamma\).  Equation (5) and nonnegativity of the densities give
\[
 0\leq F_y^\Gamma
 \leq
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \widetilde g_{\sigma,y}
 \leq\frac12
\tag{25}
\]
pointwise on every source fiber used by \(\Gamma\).  Hence \(B_y^\Gamma\ge0\).  Every retained occurrence density has mass \(\eta_\sigma\), so
\[
 \int_{C_y}F_y^\Gamma=R_y^\Gamma.
\tag{26}
\]
Equations (6), (9), and (26) imply
\[
 b_y^\Gamma
 =\frac{a_y}{2}-R_y^\Gamma
 \geq
 \frac{a_y}{2}-t_{e_2(y)}
 \geq\frac{a_y}{4}>0.
\tag{27}
\]
This proves (11) and verifies that no zero denominator is hidden in (12).

For \(y\in S_\Gamma\), the retained occurrence first moment is
\[
 \int_{C_y}xF_y^\Gamma(x)\,dx
 =
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}.
\tag{28}
\]
The identity \(F_y^\Gamma+B_y^\Gamma=1/2\), together with (1), (12), and (28), gives
\[
 \frac{a_y}{2}c_y
 =
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma x_{\sigma,y}
 +b_y^\Gamma\bar q_y^\Gamma.
\tag{29}
\]
The scalar coefficients on the right have the exact sum
\[
 R_y^\Gamma+b_y^\Gamma=\frac{a_y}{2}.
\tag{30}
\]
Subtracting \((a_y/2)s(y)\) from (29) and using (30) yields
\[
 \frac{a_y}{2}\bigl(c_y-s(y)\bigr)
 =
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Gamma\\
 y\in\{a(\sigma),b(\sigma)\}}}
 \eta_\sigma\bigl(x_{\sigma,y}-s(y)\bigr)
 +b_y^\Gamma\bigl(\bar q_y^\Gamma-s(y)\bigr).
\tag{31}
\]
Every term on the right belongs to \(W\) by (14)-(15).  Since \(a_y>0\) and \(W\) is linear,
\[
 c_y-s(y)\in W
 \qquad(y\in S_\Gamma).
\tag{32}
\]
Restricting the same affine map \(s\) to any nonempty \(A\subseteq S_\Gamma\) proves
\[
 r_A^c\leq\dim W\leq3.
\tag{33}
\]

Now fix an arbitrary \(v\in\mathcal V_{\min}(t)\).  Accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c` retains every internal whole label with its exact rescaled weight:
\[
 \widehat\theta_\sigma(v)=\eta_\sigma
 \qquad(\sigma\in\mathscr A_{\mathrm{int}}).
\tag{34}
\]
Therefore, for every \(y\in S_\Gamma\),
\[
 \widehat\tau_y(v)\geq R_y^\Gamma>0.
\tag{35}
\]
In particular,
\[
 S_\Gamma\subseteq\widehat Y(v).
\tag{36}
\]
Summing (35) over physical endpoints and then using the fact that every whole label has exactly two distinct opposite endpoints gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{y\in S_\Gamma}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 &\geq\sum_{y\in S_\Gamma}R_y^\Gamma\\
 &=2\sum_{\sigma\in\Gamma}\eta_\sigma
 =2H_\Gamma.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{37}
\]
This is exact occurrence double-counting.  Repeated labels, repeated contacts, and target collisions do not change it, and no label is split between its endpoints.

Suppose first that \(S_\Gamma\subsetneq\widehat Y(v)\), and choose
\[
 A:=S_\Gamma.
\]
Condition (20) makes \(H_\Gamma>0\), so \(A\) is nonempty; it is proper by the present case.  Since \(A\subseteq\mathbb R^n\), one has \(\rho_A\leq n\), and hence \(2^{n-\rho_A}\geq1\).  Equations (19), (33), and (37) give
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)
 \geq\frac14
 \sum_{y\in S_\Gamma}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 \geq\frac12H_\Gamma.
\tag{38}
\]
This proves (22).

It remains to treat \(S_\Gamma=\widehat Y(v)\).  Accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c` gives
\[
 |\widehat Y(v)|\geq n+4\geq6.
\tag{39}
\]
Put \(k:=|S_\Gamma|\), choose \(y_0\in S_\Gamma\) minimizing \(\widehat\tau_y(v)\), and set
\[
 A:=S_\Gamma\setminus\{y_0\}.
\]
Then \(A\) is nonempty and proper in \(\widehat Y(v)\), and minimality gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{y\in A}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 &\geq
 \left(1-\frac1k\right)
 \sum_{y\in S_\Gamma}\widehat\tau_y(v)\\
 &\geq\frac56
 \sum_{y\in S_\Gamma}\widehat\tau_y(v)\\
 &\geq\frac53H_\Gamma.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{40}
\]
Again \(\rho_A\leq n\), while (33) applies to this subset.  Hence
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)
 \geq\frac14\sum_{y\in A}\widehat\tau_y(v)
 \geq\frac5{12}H_\Gamma.
\tag{41}
\]
Together, (38) and (41) give the first inequality in (21) in both cases, since \(H_\Gamma/2\geq5H_\Gamma/12\).

The threshold comparison is exact:
\[
 \frac5{12}\cdot\frac{3}{500\gamma_d}
 =\frac{15}{6000\gamma_d}
 =\frac1{400\gamma_d}.
\tag{42}
\]
The strict hypothesis (20) therefore turns (21) into
\[
 \Lambda_v(A)>\frac Q{400\gamma_d}.
\tag{43}
\]
Equation (38) of accepted fact `d01de728d8b5e33c` now yields
\[
 R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
 \geq4\gamma_d\Lambda_v(A)
 >\frac Q{100}.
\tag{44}
\]
This proves (23) for the arbitrary load-minimal optimizer \(v\).

Finally, assume (24).  Whole-label partitioning gives the exact mass identity
\[
 H_\Gamma
 =H_{\mathrm{int}}
 -\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta}\eta_\sigma.
\tag{45}
\]
Using the strict internal-mass bound (7),
\[
 H_\Gamma
 >\left(\frac7{400}-\frac1{1000}\right)Q
 =\frac{33}{2000}Q.
\tag{46}
\]
Accepted fact `2fe46f47d19112ea` gives \(\gamma_d>4/11\), so
\[
 \frac{3}{500\gamma_d}
 <\frac3{500}\cdot\frac{11}{4}
 =\frac{33}{2000}.
\tag{47}
\]
Equations (46)-(47) prove (20), with strictness preserved even when the discarded mass equals \(Q/1000\).  The already proved argument then gives (21)-(23).  This completes the deletion-stable corollary.
