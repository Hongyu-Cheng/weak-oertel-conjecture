## Statement

Let $D=\operatorname{conv}\{(2,-1),(-1,2),(-1,-1)\}$, $a=(1/4,0)$ and $K=\{\xi/(1-a\cdot\xi):\xi\in D\}$. At the mark $y=0$, the attained energy denominator is $P=1+X_1/4$, with $E=9/2$ and $V=144/25$. For the three through-zero halfspaces $H_0=\{X_1\ge0\}$, $H_1=\{X_2\ge0\}$, $H_2=\{-X_1-X_2\ge0\}$, the projective cone, energy cone, ordinary cone and H height probabilities are respectively
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &H_0&H_1&H_2\\\hline
\nu_C&4/9&4/9&4/9\\
\nu_E&1/3&1/2&1/2\\
\nu_V&25/36&8/27&8/27\\
\nu_H&49/108&35/81&35/81
\end{array}
$$
Thus the individual source-height bound $\nu_H(H)\ge\Gamma_2$ can fail without the buffer factor. The three cuts do not have a common original raw volume budget.

## Proof

The chart is positive on $D$, and its inverse maps the vertices to $(4,-2),(-4/5,8/5),(-4/5,-4/5)$. These give the stated $V$ by a determinant. The forward chart with $P=1+X_1/4$ has image $D$ of area $9/2$ and centroid zero. Its Jacobian converts the energy gradient to the first moment of $D$, so the gradient vanishes. Strict convexity of the energy in its parameter follows by differentiating twice: each nonzero parameter direction gives a strictly positive integral of the square of its scalar product with $X$ times a positive power weight. Thus the exhibited denominator attains the minimum. The ratio $c=E/V$ equals $25/32$.

Use the exact projective H laws with $L=1-\xi_1/4$. The ordinary cone probability of $D$ is $dt/3$ along every edge in its unit affine parameter, because the three triangles from zero to the edges have equal area. The halfspaces through zero have the same signs in the chart. Write $A,B,C$ for the vertices of $D$ in the displayed order. On each of $AB$ and $AC$ use $t$ measured from the non-$A$ vertex to $A$, so $L=5/4-3t/4$. On $BC$ it is $5/4$. For $H_0$ the captured edges are the portions $1/3\le t\le1$ of $AB$ and $AC$, giving cone mass $4/9$, energy mass $(2/3)\int_{1/3}^1L\,dt=1/3$, and ordinary cone mass $(2c/3)\int_{1/3}^1L^{-2}\,dt=(2c/3)(4/3)=25/36$.

For $H_1$ the captured $AB$ portion is $0\le t\le2/3$, and the captured $BC$ portion has parameter length $2/3$. Thus the cone mass is $4/9$, energy mass $(2/3+5/6)/3=1/2$, and ordinary cone mass $(c/3)(32/45+32/75)=8/27$, by integrating the inverse square of the affine $L$ on those two portions. Interchanging $B,C$ preserves $L$, the cone measure and $H_0$, and interchanges $H_1,H_2$. The formula $\nu_H=(2\nu_E+\nu_V)/3$ gives the last row, with $35/81<4/9$. Since through-mark ordinary cone mass equals original cap-volume fraction, the third row verifies the unequal original raw volumes.
