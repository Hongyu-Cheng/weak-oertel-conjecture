## Statement

For $B=[-1,2]\times[-1,1]$, $P=B\cap\mathbb Z^2$, $q(x,y)=2x+y-1$, $\ell=5-|q|$ and $J=\{(0,0),(1,0)\}$ take the canonical centered interval body with roof
$$
V(x,y)=\min\{270+67q,270-67q,270+268x,538-268x\}/1230 .
$$
Its twelve positive lengths in lexicographic order are $(2,2,2,136,203,270,270,203,136,2,2,2)/1230$, with total mixed mass one. There is an $\ell$-tight martingale from $(\delta_{(0,0)}+\delta_{(1,0)})/8$ to $\sum_{z\in P}(V(z)/\ell(z))\delta_z$. All 704 nonempty proper closed minors $T=P\cap\operatorname{conv}T$ obey $\sum_{z\in T}V(z)<k(T)/4$, with minimal slacks $(150,24,166,8)/4920$ for $k(T)=1,2,3,4$, respectively. Nevertheless any cover by closed minors each contained in the projection of one source's minimal upper-roof face costs at least eight occupied parities in total, and eight is attained. The original base occupies four parities.

## Proof

Apply e0da699db6f5c6fd with its identical interval roof, twelve lengths, two lifted marks with continuous coordinate zero, and full-space tilted law $dm/\ell$. Its canonical hull and exact quota loop give the stated masses, minor count, and four integer minima. Base projection of its full-space martingale takes the source masses $1/8$ to the two displayed atoms and takes each interval's $dm/\ell$ to the base atom of mass $V(z)/\ell(z)$. Projection commutes with barycenters and preserves $\ell$ because $\ell$ depends only on the base, so row $\ell$ equality is retained.

The source projections and the roof data are exactly those of the two-facet cover lemma, including both distinct minimal upper-roof faces. The cost computation there is independent of the selected martingale. In particular each parity requires two cells to cover its strict-side representatives, and the two full closed facet minors give the upper bound eight. This proves the cover obstruction under all strict quotas.
