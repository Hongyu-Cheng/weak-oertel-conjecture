## Statement

Let $C$ be the convex hull of finitely many sampled positive $d$-dimensional convex fibers $K_z=C_z$ over a base set $P$, $d\ge1$. Let $B$ be the convex hull of some sampled bases, with $\operatorname{aff}B=\operatorname{aff}P$. Suppose $C_z=y(z)+\ell(z)D$ for every $z\in B$, where $D$ is a convex body containing $0$ in its interior and $y,\ell$ are affine on $B$, with $\ell>0$ there. Their affine extensions satisfy $\min_{z\in\operatorname{conv}P}\ell(z)>0$ and $C_z\subseteq y(z)+\ell(z)D$ throughout $\operatorname{conv}P$. For each $\xi\in D$, the set of sampled bases with $\xi\in(K_z-y(z))/\ell(z)$ is the sample of a convex base set containing $B$.

## Proof

Choose $q\in\operatorname{relint}B$. For a nonzero continuous direction $u$, $H(z)=h_{C_z}(u)$ is concave on the real base hull by convexity of $C$. On $B$ it equals the affine function $G(z)=u\cdot y(z)+\ell(z)h_D(u)$. For any $z$ in the base hull choose $r\in B$ on the line from $z$ through and just beyond $q$, so that $q=\theta z+(1-\theta)r$ with $0<\theta<1$ (the case $z=q$ is immediate). The choice is possible by full relative rank. Concavity gives $G(q)=H(q)\ge\theta H(z)+(1-\theta)G(r)$, hence $H(z)\le G(z)$. Apply this to $u$ and $-u$. Their sum bounds the width of $C_z$ by $\ell(z)$ times that of $D$. Every real fiber contains a convex combination of positive sampled fibers, so its width is positive. This proves positivity of $\ell$, and compactness gives a positive minimum. The support inequalities give the inclusion. The footprint in the real base hull is the inverse image of $C$ under the affine map $z\mapsto(z,y(z)+\ell(z)\xi)$. It is convex and contains $B$ by the equality of its fibers there.
