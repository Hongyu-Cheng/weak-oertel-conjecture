## Statement

Use $T=\operatorname{conv}\lbrace(2,-1),(-1,2),(-1,-1)\rbrace$, $K_\varepsilon=\lbrace(x,t):|t|\le1,\ x\in(1+\varepsilon t)T\rbrace$ with $0<\varepsilon\le10^{-9}$, and $t_*=\varepsilon/3$. Put $g_0=1/19{,}876{,}428-8\cdot10^{-9}$. If $t_0=t_*+h$ and $|h|<\min(1/48,g_0/12)$, the normalized ordinary depth at $(0,t_0)$ is
$$
\frac49-\frac{4h^2}{9(1+\varepsilon^2/3)(1/3+t_0^2)}.
$$
It is realized at any of the three canonical horizontal normals with vertical coefficient $\beta=2h/(1/3+t_0^2)$ in the same scaled normal units.

## Proof

Use the triangle scaling, height law, and exterior gap from triangle-frustum-actual-depth-and-active-menu. Moving the mark from $t_*$ to $t_0$ changes each cap fraction by at most $6|h|$. For $n\ne0$ and $|\beta|\le4$ condition on $t$: the density of $r(t)n\cdot X$ is at most $2/[3r(t)]\le4/3$, and the threshold moves by $|\beta h|$. If $|\beta|\ge4$, condition on $X$: the height coefficient is $\beta+\varepsilon n\cdot X$ with magnitude at least $|\beta|-2\ge|\beta|/2$, since $\varepsilon\le1/2$ and $|n\cdot X|\le4$. The height density is at most $9/8$, so the interval moved has probability at most $(9/4)|h|$. Pure vertical caps have the same bound by directly moving the height threshold. Thus every exterior direction remains above $\gamma+g_0/2$.

Inside the local region, $|h|<1/48$ gives physical threshold magnitude at most $(1+1/6+1/48)/4<3/8$, preserving the slice incidence. The exact numerator of the local fraction is $4I_2-8\beta h+2\beta^2(1/3+t_0^2)$, since $\int_{-1}^1r(t)(t-t_0)dt=-2h$ and $\int_{-1}^1(t-t_0)^2dt=2(1/3+t_0^2)$. The denominator is $(9-s^2)I_2$. The numerator is positive, so minimize by $s=0$ and then by completing the square in $\beta$. The resulting $\beta$ has magnitude at most $6|h|<1/4$. The displayed value is at most $\gamma$, hence it is the global cap minimum by the exterior gap.
