## Statement

Let $p\ge2$, $d=p+1$, and $T_p=\operatorname{conv}(v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_p)$ with $v_0=(-1,\ldots,-1)$ and $v_i=v_0+(p+1)e_i$. Set $V_p=(p+1)^p/p!$, $\delta=(p/(p+1))^p$, and let
$$
K_p=\{(x,t): |t|\le1,\ x\in(1-|t|)T_p\},
\qquad V=2V_p/(p+1).
$$
Write $u_i=e_i$ for $1\le i\le p$ and $u_0=-\sum_{i=1}^p e_i$.

Let $B\subset\mathbb R^n$ be a compact convex body (a singleton for $n=0$), with $P=B\cap\mathbb Z^n$ centrally symmetric about and containing zero. Let $r^0:B\to(0,\infty)$ be continuous and concave with even sampled values. The actual body has fibers $r^0(z)K_p$ over $B$; mixed measure is summed $d$-volume at $P$. Put $M_0=V\sum_{z\in P}(r_z^0)^d$ and $Q_0=\delta M_0$.

For each sufficiently small nonzero $a\in\operatorname{span}P$, the family $r_a(z)=r^0(z)+a\cdot z$ is realized by a convex body on $B$. All canonical caps $u_i\cdot x-pa\cdot z\ge0$ have mass
$$
Q(a)=Q_0-Vp^{d-1}\sum_{z\in P}(-a\cdot z)_+^d<Q_0,
\qquad M(a)=V\sum_{z\in P}(r_z^0+a\cdot z)^d>M_0.
$$
They cover the middle plane and can be completed by fixed slack groups at the other original bases. The actual global raw depth is at most $Q(a)$, and the middle centroid is an actual maximizer. Its normalized depth is strictly below $\delta$. A continuous homothety by $(Q_0/Q(a))^{1/d}$ gives strictly greater volume than $M_0$ under the same numerical raw-depth budget $Q_0$.

For $p=2$, $P=\{-1,0,1\}$ and baseline radii one on $B=[-1,1]$, the statements hold throughout $0<s=|a|\le1/5$. Here $M_0=9$, $Q_0=4$, $M(a)=9+18a^2$ and $Q(a)=4-12s^3$. The endpoint maxima are $(4/3)(1\pm a)^3$; the central centroid has depth at least $(4/3)[1+2(1-s)^3]$ and is globally deepest. The three moving central caps plus two groups of three endpoint caps give a nine-cap cover. At $s=1/5$, the normalized depth is between $1012/3645$ and $488/1215$, inclusive.
