## Statement

There is a strict quadrilateral cut of a tetrahedron with a noncentral actual section centroid, a positive middle-pair coefficient $c=4/(12-1/\lambda_j-1/\lambda_k)$, and $F-cs>37/64$. Here $F$ is the captured volume fraction and $s$ is the section area divided by tetrahedral volume times the projection of the ordinary centroid minus the section centroid on the oriented unit normal.

## Proof

Use the strict cut for diagonal coordinates $(16,29,5,8)/58$ with the first two vertices captured. The admitted quadrilateral section calculus (1462fa9ce6ced54e) gives
$$
\lambda=(78/203,715/1887,3095/31059,1325/9657),\quad
F=5465/39627,\quad s=-15341/52836.
$$
The coordinate order is $\lambda_2<\lambda_3<\lambda_1<\lambda_0$, with $\lambda_0,\lambda_1>1/3$. Substitution of the middle pair $\{1,3\}$ gives $c=378950/196347>0$ and $F-cs=172480645/247004526>37/64$. The positive raw diagonal coordinates ensure a strict section and make its centroid and fractions actual geometric data.
