## Statement

Let $d,n\ge2$, let $T$ be a volume-one $d$-simplex with vertex $0$, barycentric coordinates $\lambda_i$, and centroid $g$. Set $b=1/(d+1)$, $\Gamma=(1-b)^d$ and $c=(1-\Gamma)^{1/d}<n/(n+1)$. Place $T$ at all binary cube vertices and $A=\varepsilon T$ at $v_-=-\mathbf 1$ in their actual lifted convex hull. Assume $\varepsilon>0$ and $a=\varepsilon^d<\rho_d$, the reflected simplex profile minimum. Then $(0,g)$ is the unique deepest mixed point with raw depth $Q=\Gamma+a$. The closed ambient halfspaces containing it and attaining that depth are exactly
$$
F_{i,\beta}=\lbrace(z,x):\lambda_i(x)+\beta\cdot z\ge b\rbrace,\qquad
0\le i\le d,\quad \beta_j\le-(1-b)\quad(1\le j\le n),
$$
up to positive scaling. At a supporting threshold a nonzero cube trace can be a vertex of zero $d$-volume.

For the finite cover and exact KKT row in the finite simplex cap-cover counterexample, every $F_{i,\beta}$ fails unchanged residual-root Jensen on that same row by $[n-(n+1)c]/(n+2)>0$. Adding any $F_{i,\beta}$ or replacing the original source cap of the same barycentric direction preserves the cover, residual obstacles, and KKT row. All fibers have mass less than $M/k$ for total $M=2^n+a$ and occupied parity count $k=2^n$. The sharp-threshold excess in raw depth is $Q-\Gamma M/k=a(1-\Gamma/2^n)>0$.

## Proof

The profile bound 53fe12b742a7608d gives $\varepsilon<1$, so $A$ is compact convex and contained in $T$. Apply accepted fact a0223f71231de506 with the binary pair $q=0$, $v=\mathbf1$, and volume $V=1$. This is the exact assigned lifted hull, with $v_-=2q-v$. Its mass hypothesis holds and it gives the stated unique point and exact depth. The finite simplex cap-cover counterexample applies because $c<n/(n+1)$ and $0<\varepsilon<1$.

Restrict any attaining ambient halfspace to the diagonal line of bases $z=t\mathbf1$ times $\mathbb R^d$. Over the three integer bases $v_-,0,\mathbf1$ the sections are exactly $A,T,T$. The restricted region contains the middle centroid. If the restriction is the entire slice, its mass on these three bases is $2+a>Q$. Otherwise it is a halfspace and has mass at least $Q$ on the three bases by their exact-depth theorem, as retained in the full critical menu for the tiny simplex endpoint. Consequently it attains equality there and has zero volume on every other cube base. That three-fiber menu, applied in parameter $t$, forces a nonzero continuous normal along some $\nabla\lambda_i$ and a boundary through $(0,g)$. Thus the full equation can be written $\lambda_i(x)+\beta\cdot z\ge b$. Since $n\ge2$, each unit vector $u_j$ is a cube base outside the diagonal triple. Zero cap volume at $u_j$ is equivalent to $b-\beta_j\ge1$, giving the individual coefficient bounds.

Conversely those bounds give threshold at least one at every nonzero cube vertex, so every such trace has zero volume. The threshold at $v_-$ is $b+\sum_j\beta_j\le b-n(1-b)<0$, so the cap contains $A$. Its source trace is canonical with mass $\Gamma$. It therefore attains $Q$. Equality $b-\beta_j=1$ leaves the corresponding vertex in the trace at $u_j$.

All these caps have the same trace volumes as the original source caps. In particular they have roots $(0,c,1,\ldots,1)$ on the same row, with source root equal to the obstacle there. Adding one preserves the original cover and obstacles. Replacing one source cap in the same direction keeps $A$ covered, preserves the middle canonical cover, and leaves the separate caps covering all nonzero cube bases. The obstacle values and KKT data persist. The strict row gap and nonheaviness now follow from the finite-cover result. The raw excess follows by subtraction and tends to zero as $a\downarrow0$.

The dimension condition is achievable with $n=3d$, since $(n/(n+1))^d>1-d/(n+1)>2/3$ and $\Gamma>1/3$ from $(1+1/d)^d<e<3$. For a rational-coordinate example take $d=2,n=3$, $T=\operatorname{conv}\lbrace (0,0),(1,0),(0,2)\rbrace$ and $\varepsilon=1/10$. Then $c=\sqrt5/3<3/4$ by $80<81$. The strict profile inequality follows from $(3+9/200)^2<10$ and the exact planar profile formula in 53fe12b742a7608d. The masses are $Q=409/900$, $M=801/100$, $k=8$, the excess is $17/1800$, the residual objective is $9c^2=5$, and the gap is $(9-4\sqrt5)/15>0$. The original finite cap cover uses rational coefficients.
