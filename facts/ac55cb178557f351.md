## Proof

The base projection of the compact body \(C\) meets \(\mathbb Z^n\) in finitely many points, so every edge family and sum below is finite. We first derive the two global scalar estimates that force collisions. In one good parity class \(\xi\), direct summation of (4a) gives
\[
 \sum_{\substack{u<v\\u,v\equiv\xi}}r_{\{u,v\}}
 =\frac{A_\xi^2-\sum_{z\equiv\xi}a_z^2}{8A_\xi}
 \leq\frac{A_\xi}{8}.
\tag{52}
\]
Summing over good classes gives \(Q\leq W/8\). Hence (9) implies \(G<W/2\), so accepted fact c81f02314cb34f8b applies to the actual reservoir and gives
\[
 Q\geq\frac3{32}(W-4G).
\tag{53}
\]
Rearranging (53) and using (9),
\[
 W\leq\frac{32}{3}Q+4G
 <\left(\frac{32}{3}+\frac1{25}\right)Q
 =\frac{803}{75}Q.
\tag{54}
\]

If \(e\notin\mathcal H\) and \(r_e=0\), then \(\omega_e=0\) and
\(r_e=2(r_e-\omega_e)=0\). If \(e\notin\mathcal H\) and \(r_e>0\), then \(\omega_e<r_e/2\), so \(r_e<2(r_e-\omega_e)\). Therefore, including the boundary case \(\Omega=Q\),
\[
 \sum_{e\notin\mathcal H}r_e
 \leq2\sum_e(r_e-\omega_e)
 =2(Q-\Omega)
 <\frac Q{50}.
\tag{55}
\]
Subtracting (55) from \(Q\) proves (16).

Fix a positive reservoir edge \(e=\{u,v\}\) in the good class \(\xi\), and put \(m=m_e\). Accepted fact 32aedd8da549416a gives
\[
 a_m\geq b_e^{\mathrm{BM}}
 =\left(\frac{a_u^{1/d}+a_v^{1/d}}2\right)^d
 \geq\sqrt{a_ua_v}.
\tag{56}
\]
Goodness gives \(a_u,a_v\leq A_\xi/4\), and hence
\[
 \sqrt{a_ua_v}\leq\frac{A_\xi}{4}.
\tag{57}
\]
Using (4a), (56), and (57),
\[
 16r_e
 =\frac{4a_ua_v}{A_\xi}
 =4\sqrt{a_ua_v}\frac{\sqrt{a_ua_v}}{A_\xi}
 \leq\sqrt{a_ua_v}
 \leq a_m.
\tag{58}
\]
For each physical target with \(\mathcal H_m\ne\varnothing\), choose an edge attaining \(r_m^{\max}\). The chosen targets are distinct physical fibers. Summing (58) and then using (54) gives
\[
 16\sum_{m:\mathcal H_m\ne\varnothing}r_m^{\max}
 \leq\sum_{m:\mathcal H_m\ne\varnothing}a_m
 \leq W
 <\frac{803}{75}Q.
\tag{59}
\]
This proves (17). Since
\(\sum_mR_m^{\mathcal H}=\sum_{e\in\mathcal H}r_e\), subtracting (17) from (16) proves (18).

\tag{60}
\]
For nonempty \(\mathcal H_m\), the exact off-diagonal fractional degree criterion used in accepted fact c81f02314cb34f8b gives an off-diagonal pairing of edge occurrences with mass

 \frac{C_m^{\mathcal H}}2,

\tag{61}

 :=\frac{\lambda_L}{2b_e^{\mathrm{BM}}}\psi_e
 +\frac{\lambda_L}{2b_f^{\mathrm{BM}}}\psi_f.
\tag{76}
\]
It has mass \(\lambda_L\) and normalized first moment \((\bar c_e+\bar c_f)/2\). Pointwise, equations (20) and (72) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{L:m_L=m}F_L^c
 &\leq\frac12
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal H_m}
 \frac{d_e(\lambda)}{b_e^{\mathrm{BM}}}\\
 &\leq\frac1{16}
 \sum_{e\in\mathcal H_m}\frac{r_e}{b_e^{\mathrm{BM}}}
 \leq\frac1{16}\,2^n\frac18
 =2^{n-7}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{77}
\]
Thus
\[
 F_m^c:=\alpha_c\sum_{L:m_L=m}F_L^c
\tag{78}
\]
is one legal density on \(C_m\), of mass \(\alpha_c\Pi_m\) and normalized first moment \(y_m^c\). This proves the second membership in (45). In (75) and (78), every component at one physical target is aggregated before the metronoid membership is invoked, so repeated target fibers and repeated continuous atoms cause no reuse.
