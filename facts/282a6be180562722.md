## Statement

Take the independent reflected guard body of w31-axis-guard-active-menus with Euclidean regular $T$, $\sigma=1/4$, and $0<w\le1/100$. For the balanced canonical menu that captures all positive matching guards, let the inward field be $\Phi(z,x)=\sum_{i,r}z_{i,r}$. Write $D=\int\Phi\,dS$ and, for every continuous direction $u$, $J_u=\sum_z\int_{\partial K_z}[u\cdot(x-g_z)]^2\Phi\,dS$, where $g_z$ is the old fiber centroid. Then $D=J_u=0$ for all $u$, and the captured boundary integral is $LP_D>0$. The full unnormalized improvement response, including width and total mass corrections, is $LP_D$. Replacing $\Phi$ by $\Phi+\varepsilon$ gives positive total boundary cost and an unbounded full-response-to-cost ratio as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$.

## Proof

The canonical positive-guard menu exists by w31-axis-guard-active-menus. Its field is zero on $T$ and takes the values $+1,-1$ on two copies of the identical guard $D_i$. These have identical centroids and boundary measures, so the paired contributions to $D$ and every $J_u$ cancel. The selected profile is $1/3$ on each of the $3L$ positive guards and zero on each negative guard. It follows that $\int W\Phi\,dS=LP_D$.

For completeness, let an inward speed $\varphi$ be constant on each fiber as in $\Phi$ or $\Phi+\varepsilon$, realized by changing each homothety scale by $-t\varphi/r_T$ about its fixed center. The old boundary speed is $\varphi$ on every edge since all homothetic triangles share the same edge normals. These are compatible for small signed $t$: the paired average changes its scale by $-t\varepsilon/r_T$ and stays in the central triangle, whose support values on the same three normals each change by the same $-t\varepsilon$. The exact outer bases and central-fiber argument are unchanged. First differentiation of the area gives $\dot M=-D$. Differentiating the summed centered covariance numerator in direction $u$ gives $-J_u$ by the boundary area formula; centroid derivatives multiply zero centered first moments. Thus for a cap normalized by the old $\Gamma(u)=1$, $\dot\Gamma(u)=(D-J_u)/(2M)$. At fixed cap margin $m$, put $q=m/h$ and $A=\int q\Psi'(q)\,d\mu$ in this normalization. The raw first loss is $\int\Psi(q)\varphi\,dS+A\dot\Gamma(u)$: the boundary term is the loss from moving the edges and the width derivative uses $\dot q=-q\dot\Gamma(u)$. The kink planes are area-null, so this differentiation is valid. The normalized loss times $M$ subtracts $\beta D$, with $\beta$ the old normalized maximum. The complete menu response is therefore the weighted average of those raw losses minus $\beta D$. Its two correction terms are zero for $\Phi$, leaving $LP_D$. The response is linear in the field for this fixed old menu, so the response to $\Phi+\varepsilon$ tends to $LP_D$, while its cost is $\varepsilon\int dS>0$. This proves the divergence of the ratio.
