## Statement

Retain one common canonical small-defect intact instance of accepted facts 0636caea9cc33c59, 592d2827cca44396, 6769574fc1bd9f8d, and 6bfe50fed9a5540b.  Use all notation of 592d2827cca44396.  In particular, \(P>0\), the good KKT packet set is \(\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}\), and the good-good part of the genuine circuit multigraph \(\mathcal G\) has total edge weight
\[
 E_{\mathrm{gg}}>\frac{85}{194}P.
\tag{1}
\]
Every good-good graph edge \(e\) is one subpacket
\[
 e=(\sigma,g,h,r,s),
 \qquad r,s\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g},
 \qquad x_e:=x_{\sigma,g,h}^{r,s}>0,
\tag{2}
\]
with two endpoint slots \((r,g)\) and \((s,h)\).  A graph loop has two slots, including when the two physical edges also coincide.  It retains the four actual points
\[
 K_g,K_h,J_e^0,J_e^1
 \in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d),
 \qquad
 K_g+K_h=J_e^0+J_e^1.
\tag{3}
\]

For \(r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}\) and a positive fresh physical edge \(k\), define the good-good incidence weight
\[
 d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}
 :=\sum_{\substack{e\text{ good-good}\text{ endpoint slots equal to }(r,k)}}x_e,
 \qquad
 D_r^{\mathrm{gg}}:=\sum_kd_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}.
\tag{4}
\]
Multiplicity in (4) is literal.  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 0\leq D_r^{\mathrm{gg}}\leq K_r,
 \qquad
 \sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}}D_r^{\mathrm{gg}}
 =2E_{\mathrm{gg}}>\frac{85}{97}P,}
\tag{5}
\]
and, for every physical edge \(k\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}}d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}
 \leq\zeta_k'\leq c_k.}
\tag{6}
\]

For every \(r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}\), every packet edge \(i\) with \(c_i^{(r)}>0\), and every \(k\) with \(d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}>0\), choose a cross-contact point
\[
 p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}\in F_{L_k}(a_i^\circ),
 \qquad
 F_{L_k}(a_i^\circ)
 :=\{p\in L_k:\langle a_i^\circ,p\rangle
 =h_{L_k}(a_i^\circ)\}.
\tag{7}
\]
This is an actual physical target point:
\[
 p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}\in L_k
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{\vartheta_k}(C_{m_k}),
 \qquad
 (m_k,p_{i\mid k}^{(r)})
 \in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d).
\tag{8}
\]
Define the product-recoupled weights
\[
 b_{i,k}^{(r)}
 :=\frac{c_i^{(r)}}{C_r}d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}.
\tag{9}
\]
They have the exact row and column marginals
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_i b_{i,k}^{(r)}=d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}},
 \qquad
 \sum_k b_{i,k}^{(r)}
 =\frac{D_r^{\mathrm{gg}}}{C_r}c_i^{(r)}.}
\tag{10}
\]
Most importantly, the cross-contact normal bundle is balanced pointwise in every packet-target block:
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_i b_{i,k}^{(r)}a_i^\circ=0
 \qquad(r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g},\ k).}
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently it has both global affine-annihilation equations when its vectors are placed at their actual target bases:
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{r,i,k}b_{i,k}^{(r)}a_i^\circ=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{r,i,k}b_{i,k}^{(r)}a_i^\circ m_k^{\mathsf T}=0.}
\tag{12}
\]

Put
\[
 \nu_\times
 :=\gamma_d\sum_{r\in\mathcal J_{\mathrm g}}
 \sum_{i,k}b_{i,k}^{(r)}
 \delta_{(m_k,p_{i\mid k}^{(r)})}.
\tag{13}
\]
This is one collision-pooled physical measure, not a sum of separately feasible terminal witnesses.  Every closed ambient halfspace \(H\) satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\nu_\times(H)\leq\mu_C(H),}
\tag{14}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 |\nu_\times|
 =2\gamma_dE_{\mathrm{gg}}
 >\frac{85\gamma_d}{97}P
 >\frac{340}{1067}P.}
\tag{15}
\]

The product recoupling can be retained jointly with every actual circuit, rather than only at its scalar incidence marginals.  For an edge \(e=(\sigma,g,h,r,s)\) in (2), and packet indices \(i,j\) with \(c_i^{(r)},c_j^{(s)}>0\), put
\[
 y_{e,i,j}
 :=x_e\frac{c_i^{(r)}}{C_r}
          \frac{c_j^{(s)}}{C_s}.
\tag{16}
\]
For a loop, the two factors in (16) refer to its two separately counted endpoint slots.  Then
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{i,j}y_{e,i,j}=x_e,}
\tag{17}
\]
for every \(r,k,i\), the sum of the contributions from first slots equal to \((r,k)\) and second slots equal to \((r,k)\) is exactly \(b_{i,k}^{(r)}\), with a loop contributing through both of its endpoint slots.  Each orientation separately has only its corresponding oriented incidence marginal.  Every positive decorated subpacket in (16) retains the unchanged four points in (3) and the unchanged identity
\[
 K_g+K_h=J_e^0+J_e^1.
\tag{18}
\]
Thus summing out \(i,j\) recovers the original good-good circuit subfamily exactly, and every original source and target collision budget remains a positive subledger of the accepted one.

Finally define the cross-gauge energy and the incidence-alignment mass
\[
 \mathcal E_\times
 :=\sum_{r,i,k}b_{i,k}^{(r)}h_{D_k}(a_i^\circ),
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_\times

 \frac{d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}c_k^{(r)}}{C_r}.
\tag{19}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal E_\times\geq\mathcal A_\times>0,}
\tag{20}

and, for every affine map \(a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\),
\[
 \boxed{

 \geq\mathcal E_\times.}
\tag{21}
\]
Thus (21) is an exact normalized affine-spread inequality for the same pointwise-balanced cloud: the weights on its left sum to \(\mathcal E_\times\).

No assertion is made that \(\mathcal A_\times\) or \(\mathcal E_\times\) is an absolute fraction of \(P\), that their gauge weights obey the physical mass ledger \(b_{i,k}^{(r)}\), or that (14)--(21) alone remove the intrinsic residual-rank denominator.  The remaining interface is strictly narrower than balance of the marked restriction: absorb the single macroscopic measure (13), together with its pointwise zero-force cross-normal bundle (11), or prove a terminal alignment/cross-gauge dichotomy for (19).

## Proof

Equation (4) only groups the endpoint slots of the good-good restriction of the weighted multigraph in accepted fact 592d2827cca44396.  Weighted degree counting, with a loop counted twice, gives
\[
 D_r^{\mathrm{gg}}\leq\deg_{\mathcal G}(r)=K_r.
\tag{22}
\]
Every good-good graph edge contributes its weight once at each of its two slots, so
\[
 \sum_rD_r^{\mathrm{gg}}=2E_{\mathrm{gg}}.
\tag{23}
\]
Equation (1) now proves (5).  If one fixes a physical edge \(k\) and sums (4) over all good packets, one obtains a restriction of the full graph incidence at \(k\).  Equations (20), (23), and (27) of accepted fact 592d2827cca44396 identify that full incidence with
\[
 \sum_r\kappa_k^{(r)}=\zeta_k'.
\tag{24}
\]
Its equation (4) gives \(\zeta_k'\leq c_k\), proving (6).

For \(c_i^{(r)}>0\), the normalized packet direction \(a_i^\circ\) is nonzero because \(h_{D_i}(a_i^\circ)=1\).  Hence the exposed face in (7) is nonempty.  The metronoid inclusion \(L_k\subseteq\mathsf M_{\vartheta_k}(C_{m_k})\) is the unchanged fresh-edge inclusion of accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b, and \(m_k\in\mathbb Z^n\); this proves (8).

Every packet has \(C_r>0\).  Summing (9) first in \(i\), using \(\sum_i c_i^{(r)}=C_r\), and then in \(k\) proves (10).  Packet balance in accepted fact 592d2827cca44396 is
\[
 \sum_ic_i^{(r)}a_i^\circ=0.
\tag{25}
\]
Therefore, for every fixed pair \((r,k)\),
\[
 \sum_i b_{i,k}^{(r)}a_i^\circ
 =\frac{d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}}{C_r}
  \sum_ic_i^{(r)}a_i^\circ=0.
\tag{26}
\]
This proves (11), and summing (26), or multiplying it first by \(m_k^{\mathsf T}\), proves both equations in (12).  Notice that no balance property of \(d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}\), \(\kappa_k^{(r)}\), or \(\zeta_k'\) was used.

We prove the physical domination in one ledger.  For every point in (7), choose a density \(F_{i,k}^{(r)}\) on \(C_{m_k}\) representing its membership in (8):
\[
 0\leq F_{i,k}^{(r)}\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int F_{i,k}^{(r)}=\vartheta_k,
 \qquad
 \frac1{\vartheta_k}\int xF_{i,k}^{(r)}(x)\,dx
 =p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}.
\tag{27}
\]
At one physical target \(m\), pool all these densities before making any halfspace assertion:
\[
 \mathcal F_m
 :=\sum_{\substack{r,i,k:m_k=m}}
 \frac{b_{i,k}^{(r)}}{\vartheta_k}F_{i,k}^{(r)}.
\tag{28}
\]
Equations (6), (10), and the accepted target ledger in 0636caea9cc33c59 give the pointwise estimate
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 0\leq\mathcal F_m
 &\leq
 \sum_{k:m_k=m}\frac1{\vartheta_k}
 \sum_rd_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}\\
 &\leq
 \sum_{k:m_k=m}\frac{\zeta_k'}{\vartheta_k}
 \leq1.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{29}
\]
Fix a closed ambient halfspace \(H\), and in (28) retain only the summands whose atom \((m_k,p_{i\mid k}^{(r)})\) lies in \(H\).  Their density is still at most one.  If its mass is \(t_m>0\), its barycenter is a convex combination of selected points in the convex fiber section \(H_m\), and (27)--(29) put that barycenter in
\(\mathsf M_{t_m}(C_m)\cap H_m\).  Accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_d(C_m\cap H_m)\geq\gamma_dt_m.
\tag{30}
\]
The same estimate is immediate for an improper whole-space fiber section.  Summing (30) over the distinct physical targets proves (14).  Equations (5), (9), and (10) give
\[
 |\nu_\times|
 =\gamma_d\sum_{r,i,k}b_{i,k}^{(r)}
 =\gamma_d\sum_rD_r^{\mathrm{gg}}
 =2\gamma_dE_{\mathrm{gg}},
\tag{31}
\]
which proves the first strict inequality in (15).  The elementary estimates
\[
 \gamma_d=\left(1+\frac1d\right)^{-d}>\frac1{\mathrm e}>\frac4{11}
\tag{32}
\]
give its last inequality.

For (16)--(18), sum the product probabilities at one graph edge:
\[
 \sum_{i,j}
 \frac{c_i^{(r)}}{C_r}\frac{c_j^{(s)}}{C_s}=1.
\tag{33}
\]
This proves (17).  Fix \(r,k,i\).  Sum first over the opposite packet index on every decorated edge whose first slot is \((r,k)\), and then over the opposite packet index on every decorated edge whose second slot is \((r,k)\).  The combined result is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\frac{c_i^{(r)}}{C_r}
 \left(
 \sum_{\substack{e\text{ good-good}\\
                  \text{first slot }(r,k)}}x_e
 +
 \sum_{\substack{e\text{ good-good}\\
                  \text{second slot }(r,k)}}x_e
 \right)\\
 &\qquad=
 \frac{c_i^{(r)}}{C_r}d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}
 =b_{i,k}^{(r)}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{34}
\]
A loop whose two slots both equal \((r,k)\) occurs once in each displayed sum and hence twice, exactly as in definition (4).  Neither oriented sum alone is asserted to equal \(b_{i,k}^{(r)}\).  The four actual points in (3) do not depend on \(i,j\), so every positive product subpacket retains (18), and (33) recovers the original edge weight exactly.  Nonnegative splitting of a collision-safe family preserves every source and target ledger and proves the final assertion following (18).

It remains to prove the energy identities.  For fixed \(r,k\), equations (7), (9), and (26) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_i b_{i,k}^{(r)}
 \langle a_i^\circ,p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}\rangle
 &=\sum_i b_{i,k}^{(r)}h_{L_k}(a_i^\circ)\\
 &=\sum_i b_{i,k}^{(r)}
 \left(\langle a_i^\circ,\overline c_k\rangle
       +h_{D_k}(a_i^\circ)\right)\\
 &=\sum_i b_{i,k}^{(r)}h_{D_k}(a_i^\circ).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{35}
\]
All terms in the last line are nonnegative.  If \(d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}>0\), the definition of the graph splitting in 592d2827cca44396 forces \(c_k^{(r)}>0\).  The term \(i=k\) is therefore present in (35), and
\[
 h_{D_k}(a_k^\circ)=1.
\tag{36}
\]
Keeping only these diagonal terms and summing proves
\[
 \mathcal E_\times
 \geq\sum_{r,k}b_{k,k}^{(r)}
 =\sum_{r,k}
 \frac{d_{r,k}^{\mathrm{gg}}c_k^{(r)}}{C_r}
 =\mathcal A_\times.
\tag{37}
\]
The right side is positive because (5) gives a positive incidence and every positive incidence has \(c_k^{(r)}>0\).  This proves (20).

Finally fix an affine map \(a\).  Sum (35), subtract the affine values, and use (11):
\[
 \sum_{r,i,k}b_{i,k}^{(r)}
 \left\langle a_i^\circ,
 p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}-a(m_k)\right\rangle
 =\mathcal E_\times.
\tag{38}
\]
Polar Hölder for the \(D_k\)-norm gives, term by term,
\[
 \left\langle a_i^\circ,
 p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}-a(m_k)\right\rangle
 \leq h_{D_k}(a_i^\circ)
 \bigl\|p_{i\mid k}^{(r)}-a(m_k)\bigr\|_{D_k}.
\tag{39}
\]
Comparing (38) and the sum of (39) proves (21).  The scope paragraph distinguishes the physical masses \(b_{i,k}^{(r)}\) from the cross-gauge weights \(b_{i,k}^{(r)}h_{D_k}(a_i^\circ)\); no unproved comparison between them, no balance of the marked restriction, and no addition of separate terminal scores has been used.
