## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be a convex body with $n,d\ge1$, and suppose the integer anchor $p$ has full-dimensional fiber $K_0$ of volume $k$ and centroid $g_0$. Suppose there is a finite nonempty collection of disjoint reflected pairs of integer bases $p\pm v_j$, $v_j\ne0$, whose two fibers at pair $j$ equal the same convex body $B_j$ of volume $b_j>0$ and centroid $g_j$. Assume there is a nonzero $w$ with $g_j=g_0+s_jw$ and $0<s_1\le s_j$ for every $j$. For $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$, the actual point $Y=(p,g_1)$ has raw mixed depth at least
$$
\gamma_d\min\{k+2b_1,\ b_1+2\sum_j b_j\}.
$$

## Proof

Taking the same continuous point at both endpoints of pair $j$ and using convexity shows $B_j\subset K_0$, so $Y$ belongs to the anchor fiber. Every closed containing halfspace can be shifted parallel until its boundary passes through $Y$, reducing its captured mass. Write such a through-halfspace as $a\cdot(z-p)+u\cdot(x-g_1)\ge0$. For $u\ne0$, the two thresholds on $u\cdot(x-g_j)$ are $u\cdot(g_1-g_j)\pm a\cdot v_j$. If $u\cdot w\ge0$, their common term is nonpositive for every $j$. The accepted paired-centroid inequality on the uniform law on $B_j$ at opposite thresholds, followed by monotonicity under decreasing both thresholds, gives $2\gamma_d b_j$ per pair. At the anchor the cap through $g_1$ captures at least $\gamma_d b_1$ in $B_1\subset K_0$, by the same accepted inequality at zero shift.

If $u\cdot w\le0$, the anchor cap contains $g_0$, so the zero-shift bound on $K_0$ gives $\gamma_d k$. Pair $1$ has opposite thresholds and contributes at least $2\gamma_d b_1$. Both arguments are valid when $u\cdot w=0$. For $u=0$ the closed cap contains the anchor and at least one endpoint of every pair, giving $k+\sum_j b_j$. This exceeds or equals both required alternatives: $\gamma_d\le1/2$ since $(1+1/d)^d\ge2$, and $b_1\le k$ by containment. This proves the minimum bound; any remaining fibers only add mass.

For the seven-fiber calibration, the actual chain in accepted fact 905d9f7f7b8c6d4f has fibers $(1-|z|/4)T+(|z|/4)P$ at $z=-3,\ldots,3$, where $T$ is an area-one triangle of centroid zero and $P$ one of its vertices. At the central anchor the three pairs have areas $9/16,1/4,1/16$ and centroids $P/4,P/2,3P/4$, by the homothety ratios and centroid transformation. Thus $w=P$, $k=1$, $\sum_j b_j=7/8$, and the bound at $(0,P/4)$ is $(4/9)\min(17/8,37/16)=17/18$. In any binary padding $[0,1]^{n-1}$, restrict a halfspace to the block containing the chosen point. A proper trace is a containing chain halfspace and the entire-block trace captures its full mass. All other blocks add nonnegative mass. The raw target for the padded body is $\gamma_2(2^{n-1}11/4)/2^n=11/18$, so this calibration gives a raw surplus of at least $1/3$.
