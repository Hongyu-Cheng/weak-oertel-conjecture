## Statement

Lemma (score-aware capacity transport). Let \(n,d\ge 1\), \(m=n+d\), let \(D\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a nonempty compact convex set, and put \(K=\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbb R^n}D\). For \(\mathbf y\in\mathbb R^n\), write
\[
D_{\mathbf y}:=\{\mathbf x\in\mathbb R^d:(\mathbf y,\mathbf x)\in D\},
\]
with \(D_{\mathbf y}=\varnothing\) when \(\mathbf y\notin K\). Let \(\ell:\mathbb R^{n+d}\to\mathbb R\) be a continuous affine functional, let \(H=\{\ell\ge0\}\), and define
\[
\Phi(\mathbf p):=\max_{\mathbf x\in D_{\mathbf p}}\ell(\mathbf p,\mathbf x)
\qquad(\mathbf p\in K).
\]
Then every maximum is attained, \(\Phi\) is upper semicontinuous and hence Borel measurable, and \(\Phi\) is concave.

Fix \(0<\eta<1\) and \(\alpha=\eta/(1-\eta)\). Let \(U\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) be Lebesgue measurable and let
\[
U=\bigsqcup_{\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n}U_{\mathbf z}
\]
be a Lebesgue-measurable partition such that
\[
\lambda_n(U_{\mathbf z})\le (1-\eta)^{-n}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y):=
\frac{\mathbf z-(1-\eta)\mathbf y}{\eta}\in K
\quad
(\mathbf y\in U_{\mathbf z}).
\]
Define
\[
a_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y):=
\lambda_d\!\left\{\mathbf x\in D_{\mathbf y}:
\ell(\mathbf y,\mathbf x)\ge
-\alpha\Phi(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y))\right\}.
\]
The functions \(a_{\mathbf z}\) are measurable, and, for the mixed-integer measure
\[
M_\Lambda(A):=\sum_{\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\lambda_d\{\mathbf x:(\mathbf z,\mathbf x)\in A\},
\]
one has
\[
M_\Lambda(D\cap H)
\ge
(1-\eta)^{n+d}
\sum_{\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\int_{U_{\mathbf z}}a_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)\,d\mathbf y.
\tag{A}
\]

For \(t\in\mathbb R\), let \(G_t=\{\mathbf p\in K:\Phi(\mathbf p)\ge t\}\). If the same partition satisfies \(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)\in G_t\) for every \(\mathbf y\in U_{\mathbf z}\), then
\[
M_\Lambda(D\cap H)
\ge
(1-\eta)^m
\lambda_m\!\left\{(\mathbf y,\mathbf x)\in D:
\mathbf y\in U,\ \ell(\mathbf y,\mathbf x)\ge-\alpha t\right\}.
\tag{B}
\]

In particular, suppose \(\rho>1/2\), set
\[
\eta=\frac1{2\rho}\in(0,1),
\qquad
\alpha=\frac1{2\rho-1},
\qquad
Q=[-1/2,1/2)^n,
\]
and assume \(\mathbf a+\rho[-1,1]^n\subseteq K\). The expanded cells
\[
R_{\mathbf z}:=
\mathbf a+\frac{\mathbf z+Q-\mathbf a}{1-\eta}
\qquad(\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n)
\]
form a measurable partition of \(\mathbb R^n\), satisfy
\(\lambda_n(R_{\mathbf z})=(1-\eta)^{-n}\), and, for the unique
\(\mathbf q\in Q\) determined by
\[
\mathbf y=\mathbf a+
\frac{\mathbf z+\mathbf q-\mathbf a}{1-\eta},
\]
satisfy
\[
\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)
=\mathbf a-\frac{\mathbf q}{\eta}
\in\mathbf a+\rho[-1,1]^n.
\]
Consequently (A), with \(U=\mathbb R^n\) and \(U_{\mathbf z}=R_{\mathbf z}\), is precisely the varying-score expanded-cell bound.

Finally, existence of a fixed-score partition as in (B) necessarily implies, for every measurable \(E\subseteq U\) of finite measure,
\[
\#\!\left(
\mathbb Z^n\cap\bigl((1-\eta)E+\eta G_t\bigr)
\right)
\ge
(1-\eta)^n\lambda_n(E).
\tag{C}
\]
No converse to (C) is asserted.

## Proof

For \(\mathbf p\in K\), the fiber \(\{\mathbf p\}\times D_{\mathbf p}\) is a nonempty compact subset of \(D\), so continuity of \(\ell\) gives a maximizing lift
\[
\mathbf P_{\mathbf p}=(\mathbf p,\mathbf x_{\mathbf p})\in D,
\qquad
\ell(\mathbf P_{\mathbf p})=\Phi(\mathbf p).
\]
If \(\mathbf p_j\to\mathbf p\) in \(K\), choose maximizing lifts \((\mathbf p_j,\mathbf x_j)\). Compactness of \(D\) gives, along a subsequence realizing the limsup, convergence to some \((\mathbf p,\mathbf x)\in D\). Hence
\[
\limsup_j\Phi(\mathbf p_j)
=\ell(\mathbf p,\mathbf x)
\le\Phi(\mathbf p),
\]
which proves upper semicontinuity and Borel measurability. Convexity of \(D\) and affinity of \(\ell\), applied to maximizing lifts at two base points, prove concavity of \(\Phi\).

For each \(\mathbf z\), the affine map \(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}\) is continuous on \(U_{\mathbf z}\), so \(\Phi\circ\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}\) is measurable. Therefore
\[
\left\{(\mathbf y,\mathbf x):
\mathbf y\in U_{\mathbf z},\
(\mathbf y,\mathbf x)\in D,\
\ell(\mathbf y,\mathbf x)+
\alpha\Phi(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y))\ge0\right\}
\]
is Lebesgue measurable. Tonelli's theorem shows that its section-volume function \(a_{\mathbf z}\) is measurable. Compactness of \(D\) makes all relevant volumes finite.

Fix \(\mathbf z\) and \(\mathbf y\in U_{\mathbf z}\). Choose any maximizing lift
\[
\mathbf P=(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y),\mathbf x_{\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)}).
\]
No jointly measurable choice of these lifts is needed, because the following argument is pointwise in \(\mathbf y\) and no union of transported images is integrated. For every \(\mathbf x\) counted by \(a_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)\), set
\[
\mathbf x'=(1-\eta)\mathbf x+
\eta\mathbf x_{\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)}.
\]
The base coordinate of the corresponding convex combination is
\[
(1-\eta)\mathbf y+
\eta\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)=\mathbf z.
\]
Thus convexity of \(D\) gives \((\mathbf z,\mathbf x')\in D\), while
\[
\ell(\mathbf z,\mathbf x')
=(1-\eta)\ell(\mathbf y,\mathbf x)
+\eta\Phi(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y))
\ge
-(1-\eta)\alpha\Phi(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y))
+\eta\Phi(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y))
=0.
\]
Hence this image lies in the single target fiber
\[
(D\cap H)_{\mathbf z}
:=\{\mathbf x:(\mathbf z,\mathbf x)\in D\cap H\}.
\]
On the \(d\) continuous coordinates, the map
\(\mathbf x\mapsto(1-\eta)\mathbf x+\eta\mathbf x_{\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)}\)
has Jacobian determinant \((1-\eta)^d\). Writing
\[
b_{\mathbf z}:=\lambda_d((D\cap H)_{\mathbf z}),
\]
we therefore have, for every \(\mathbf y\in U_{\mathbf z}\),
\[
b_{\mathbf z}\ge(1-\eta)^d a_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y).
\tag{D}
\]

Images arising from different \(\mathbf y\in U_{\mathbf z}\) may overlap completely, so their volumes cannot be added. Instead, (D) gives the required maximum-versus-average step
\[
b_{\mathbf z}
\ge
(1-\eta)^d
\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\mathbf y\in U_{\mathbf z}}
a_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y).
\]
If \(\lambda_n(U_{\mathbf z})>0\), then
\[
\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{U_{\mathbf z}}a_{\mathbf z}
\ge
\frac{1}{\lambda_n(U_{\mathbf z})}
\int_{U_{\mathbf z}}a_{\mathbf z}
\ge
(1-\eta)^n\int_{U_{\mathbf z}}a_{\mathbf z}.
\]
If \(\lambda_n(U_{\mathbf z})=0\), the last integral is zero and the same conclusion is automatic. Thus
\[
b_{\mathbf z}\ge
(1-\eta)^{d}(1-\eta)^n
\int_{U_{\mathbf z}}a_{\mathbf z}
=(1-\eta)^m\int_{U_{\mathbf z}}a_{\mathbf z}.
\tag{E}
\]
The factor \((1-\eta)^d\) is the fiber homothety Jacobian; the factor \((1-\eta)^n\) is the reciprocal partition capacity after the \(n\) base dimensions have collapsed to \(\mathbf z\). Summing (E) over distinct lattice fibers, which are disjoint in the definition of \(M_\Lambda\), proves (A). No disjointness of images within one target fiber was used.

If \(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)\in G_t\), then
\[
-\alpha\Phi(\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y))
\le-\alpha t.
\]
Therefore every \(\mathbf x\in D_{\mathbf y}\) with
\(\ell(\mathbf y,\mathbf x)\ge-\alpha t\) is counted by
\(a_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)\). Applying (A) and then Tonelli proves (B).

For the expanded cells, the half-open translates \(\mathbf z+Q\) partition \(\mathbb R^n\). Applying the affine map
\[
\mathbf r\longmapsto
\mathbf a+\frac{\mathbf r-\mathbf a}{1-\eta}
\]
preserves the partition and has \(n\)-dimensional Jacobian determinant
\((1-\eta)^{-n}\), proving the claimed cell capacity. Direct substitution gives
\[
(1-\eta)\mathbf y+
\eta\left(\mathbf a-\frac{\mathbf q}{\eta}\right)
=\mathbf z.
\]
Since \(\mathbf q\in[-1/2,1/2)^n\) and
\((2\eta)^{-1}=\rho\), the displayed anchor lies in
\(\mathbf a+\rho[-1,1]^n\subseteq K\). This proves the expanded-cell corollary and accounts for its base-cell Jacobian.

It remains to prove the necessary condition (C). For measurable
\(E\subseteq U\), let
\[
N(E):=\{\mathbf z\in\mathbb Z^n:
\lambda_n(E\cap U_{\mathbf z})>0\}.
\]
The partition and its capacities imply
\[
\lambda_n(E)
=\sum_{\mathbf z\in N(E)}
\lambda_n(E\cap U_{\mathbf z})
\le(1-\eta)^{-n}\#N(E).
\]
For every \(\mathbf z\in N(E)\), choose
\(\mathbf y\in E\cap U_{\mathbf z}\). The fixed-score compatibility gives
\[
\mathbf z=(1-\eta)\mathbf y+
\eta\mathbf p_{\mathbf z}(\mathbf y)
\in(1-\eta)E+\eta G_t.
\]
Thus
\[
N(E)\subseteq
\mathbb Z^n\cap\bigl((1-\eta)E+\eta G_t\bigr),
\]
and (C) follows. This argument proves necessity only.
