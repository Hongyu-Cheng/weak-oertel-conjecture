## Statement

Let $T=\operatorname{conv}\{0,e_1,e_2\}$ and $Q=[0,1]^2$. The convex hull of $T$ at integer base $0$ and $Q$ at base $3$ has exactly four integer fibers $K_j=[0,1]^2\cap\{x_1+x_2\le1+j/3\}$, $j=0,1,2,3$. Their areas are $(1/2,7/9,17/18,1)$, of total $29/9$, and the two centroid coordinates in each fiber are equal, with values $(1/3,53/126,73/153,1/2)$. The two midpoint centroid defects in each coordinate are $11/714$ and $2/119$. The body midpoint identities are $K_1=(K_0+K_2)/2$ and $K_2=(K_1+K_3)/2$. If a compact convex superset replaces any single $K_j$ in a convex realization with all other integer fibers held fixed, the replacement equals $K_j$. Taking products with $[0,1]^{d-2}$ in the continuous coordinates and $[0,1]^{n-1}$ in the base extends the obstruction to every $d\ge2$, $n\ge1$, with $2^n$ occupied parities and each fiber volume at most $1<29/18$, where $29/18$ is the total mixed volume divided by $2^n$.

## Proof

Put $K(t)=(1-t)T+tQ$. The lifted hull has only the integer bases $0,1,2,3$, with fiber $K(j/3)$ by grouping a convex combination into its two endpoint weights. The inequalities of $[0,1]^2\cap\{x_1+x_2\le1+t\}$ hold throughout $K(t)$. Conversely the vertices $(0,0),(1,0),(1,t),(t,1),(0,1)$ of that intersection, allowing coincident vertices at $t=0,1$, are Minkowski combinations of vertices of $T,Q$ with weights $1-t,t$. This proves equality and gives the five possible outward facet normals $-e_1,-e_2,e_1,e_2,e_1+e_2$, all present at $j=1,2$.

The removed corner triangle has area $(1-t)^2/2$ and both centroid coordinates $(2+t)/3$. The remaining area is $v(t)=1-(1-t)^2/2$ and each remaining centroid coordinate is $c(t)=[1/2-((1-t)^2/2)(2+t)/3]/v(t)$. Substitution yields the four asserted volumes and centroids, $c(1/3)-(c(0)+c(2/3))/2=11/714$, and $c(2/3)-(c(1/3)+c(1))/2=2/119$.

Support functions are affine in $t$, so $K_1=(K_0+K_2)/2$ and $K_2=(K_1+K_3)/2$. Fix a site $p$ and select a row in which it is an input, with other input $r$ and target $q$. Any compact convex replacement $L_p\supseteq K_p$ feasible with all other fibers fixed satisfies $(L_p+K_r)/2\subseteq K_q$ by convexity of the proposed realization. In every direction $u$, $h_{L_p}(u)\le 2h_{K_q}(u)-h_{K_r}(u)=h_{K_p}(u)$, hence $L_p=K_p$.

A product with the indicated continuous unit cube preserves volume, appends $1/2$ to the centroid in each added coordinate, and preserves both Minkowski identities. The integer-coordinate unit cube yields $2^{n-1}$ copies of the chain, exactly $2^n$ occupied parities, and total volume $2^{n-1}(29/9)$. The same two within-copy constraints still lock each site, and every fiber volume is at most $1<29/18$.
