## Statement

Let $d\ge1$ be an integer and let $\ell,u$ be real functions on a compact interval with $\ell$ convex, $u$ concave, and $\ell\le u$. Assume
$$
C=\{(z,x):z\text{ is in the interval},\ x_1\ge\ell(z),\ x_j\ge0\ (2\le j\le d),\ \textstyle\sum_j x_j\le u(z)\}
$$
is a compact convex body with positive mixed volume. Let $S=C\cap(\mathbb Z\times\mathbb R^d)$ and let $\mu$ be the sum of $d$-dimensional volume on its integer fibers. On the $N$ positive-volume integer fibers write $r_i=u(i)-\ell(i)$, $m=\max_i r_i$, $M=\sum_i r_i^d$ and $Q=\sum_i r_i^{d+1}$. Let $H=\{x_1+az\ge b\}$ have boundary through the true centroid of $\mu$ and suppose $\ell(i)+ai-b\le0$ on each positive-volume fiber. With $U_i=u(i)+ai-b$, $v_+=\max\{v,0\}$ and $\Gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$, the captured fraction satisfies
$$
\frac{\mu(H)}{\mu(S)}=\frac{\sum_i(U_i)_+^d}{M}
\ge\Gamma_d\frac{Q}{mM}
\ge\Gamma_d\left(\frac{d+1}{d+2}+\frac{m^d}{(d+2)NM}\right)
>\frac{\Gamma_d}{2}.
$$
The centroid's base coordinate need not be an integer. Lower-endpoint equality $\ell(i)+ai-b=0$ is allowed.

## Proof

The shear $(z,x)\mapsto(z,x_1+az-b,x_2,\ldots,x_d)$ preserves every integer fiber's volume and maps $H$ to $\{x_1\ge0\}$. It maps the mixed centroid to a point with first continuous coordinate zero. In fiber $i$ the transformed simplex is a translate of $r_i\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_d)$ and has first centroid coordinate $U_i-dr_i/(d+1)$. Zero-volume fibers have no contribution to either the mass or the centroid. Therefore
$$
\sum_i r_i^d U_i=\frac d{d+1}Q .
$$
The transformed lower endpoint is nonpositive. The positive-side cap is empty or volume-null if $U_i\le0$, and it is the simplex of radius $U_i$ if $U_i>0$. This includes the whole fiber when the transformed lower endpoint is zero. The factors $1/d!$ in the captured and total masses cancel, giving the first equality with $F=\sum_i(U_i)_+^d$.

If $d=1$, the centroid identity gives $Q/2\le\sum_i r_i(U_i)_+\le mF$. If $d>1$, Hölder gives
$$
\frac d{d+1}Q\le \sum_i r_i^d(U_i)_+
\le \left(\sum_i r_i^{d^2/(d-1)}\right)^{(d-1)/d} F^{1/d}
\le m^{1/d}Q^{(d-1)/d}F^{1/d}.
$$
The last step uses $d^2/(d-1)=d+1+1/(d-1)$ and $r_i\le m$. In either case $F\ge\Gamma_d Q/m$.

The positive widths are on consecutive integer indices because $u-\ell$ is nonnegative and concave. Its sampled positive widths form a concave sequence, so accepted fact 73234f91aeecda16 with $p=d$ gives $(d+2)Q-(d+1)mM\ge m^{d+1}/N$. Substitute this into the captured fraction. The correction is positive, and $(d+1)/(d+2)>1/2$ for $d\ge1$.
