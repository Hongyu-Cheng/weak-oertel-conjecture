## Statement

In the tied four-fiber family and at its triangle source, use its balanced response probabilities. There is a nonnegative field on the fiber boundary normal bundle, continuous in the outward unit normal and convex in the ambient point for each normal, whose support in boundary measure is exactly the facet $F=\{(x,y)\in T:x+y=1\}$ at base zero. Its response-weighted capture efficiency, the field-weighted smoothed captured surface mass divided by total field-weighted surface mass, is strictly greater than $4/9$ at all sufficiently small $h>0$ for fixed $\sigma>0$. At zero width it is
$$
\frac{4t^2}{6t-1}=\frac49+\frac{4(3t-1)^2}{9(6t-1)}>\frac49 ,
\qquad t=(1+\sqrt{1-3\sigma^2})/6.
$$
The positive-width cutoff may depend on $\sigma$.

## Proof

Multiply a nonnegative continuous normal bump positive at $(1,1)/\sqrt2$ and vanishing on all other fiber facet normals by the convex physical hinge $[x+y+2-3\sigma]_+$. The two squares have no facet with the selected normal. The parallel facet of $D$ lies on $x+y=-2+3\sigma$, where the hinge is zero. The hinge is constant $3-3\sigma>0$ on $F$, so the field weights only the full facet, up to vertices of surface measure zero.

The zero-width $A$ cap misses $F$. On $F$ the zero-width $B$ cap takes the portion from $B$ up to fraction $2t$ of the edge, and the $C$ cap takes the portion of the same length fraction from the other end. These are the proper intercepts from the triangle-source theorem. Using the response-weight theorem, the weighted length fraction is $2t(\theta_B+\theta_C)=4t^2/(6t-1)$. The displayed rearrangement proves strict excess because $\sigma>0$ implies $t\ne1/3$. At positive width the normal bump continues to isolate the same fixed facet and hinge weight. The kernel traces there converge away from the finitely many limiting cut points, with the branch $A$ trace identically zero in the limit, and each integrand is bounded by one. The own cap directions, thresholds and aggregate weights have uniform limits on the compact $L$ interval, so the corresponding fractions converge also along $L_h$. The strict excess at fixed $\sigma$ persists for sufficiently small $h$.
