## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and set

\[
C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},\qquad
a_z:=\operatorname{vol}_d(C_z),\qquad
E:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^n:a_z>0\},\qquad
W:=\sum_{z\in E}a_z>0.
\]

Fix \(m\in\mathbb Z^n\cap\operatorname{conv}E\). For \(z\in E\), define

\[
D_z:=\mathsf M_{a_z/2}(C_z)
=\left\{\frac{2}{a_z}\int_{C_z}x f(x)\,dx:
0\leq f\leq1,\ \int_{C_z}f(x)\,dx=\frac{a_z}{2}\right\},
\]

\[
h_z(v):=\max_{q\in D_z}\langle v,q\rangle,
\]

and

\[
\Lambda_m(E):=
\left\{\lambda\in\mathbb R_+^E:
\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z=1,
\ \sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z z=m\right\},
\qquad
\phi(v):=\min_{\lambda\in\Lambda_m(E)}
\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z h_z(v).
\]

Let

\[
(v_i,\theta_i,\ell_i)_{i=1}^r
\]

be a support-minimal balanced-cap circuit supplied by accepted fact
\(51ff186e1aaeaa85\). Explicitly, the \(v_i\in\mathbb R^d\setminus\{0\}\),
the \(\theta_i>0\), and the globally affine functions
\(\ell_i:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R\) satisfy

\[
\sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i=1,
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i v_i=0,
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^r\theta_i\phi(v_i)<0,
\tag{1}
\]

\[
\ell_i(z)\leq h_z(v_i)\quad(z\in E),
\qquad
\ell_i(m)=\phi(v_i),
\tag{2}
\]

and \(r\) is the minimum support cardinality among all families satisfying
the three conditions in (1). Put

\[
G_i:=\{(z,x):\langle v_i,x\rangle\geq\ell_i(z)\},
\qquad
A_i:=C\cap G_i.
\tag{3}
\]

Then the following assertions hold.

1. For every strictly positive probability vector
\(\boldsymbol\vartheta=(\vartheta_1,\ldots,\vartheta_r)\), define

\[
v_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta}:=
\frac{\theta_i}{\vartheta_i}v_i,
\qquad
\ell_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta}:=
\frac{\theta_i}{\vartheta_i}\ell_i.
\tag{4}
\]

The family
\((v_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta},\vartheta_i,
\ell_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta})_{i=1}^r\)
is again a support-minimal balanced negative family with exact affine
minorants. Its cap halfspaces and cap bodies are exactly \(G_i\) and \(A_i\).
Its legal balanced-cap descendant \(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta}\) has fibers

\[
(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})_z
=\sum_{i=1}^r\vartheta_i(A_i)_z.
\tag{5}
\]

2. Fix \(i\in\{1,\ldots,r\}\). As
\(\boldsymbol\vartheta\to e_i\) through strictly positive probability
vectors,

\[
(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})_z
\longrightarrow (A_i)_z
\quad\text{in Hausdorff distance for every }z\in E,
\tag{6}
\]

and

\[
W(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})
:=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\operatorname{vol}_d((B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})_z)
\longrightarrow
W(A_i):=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\operatorname{vol}_d((A_i)_z).
\tag{7}
\]

In particular,

\[
\inf_{\substack{\vartheta_j>0\\\sum_j\vartheta_j=1}}
\frac{W(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})}{W}
\leq \frac{W(A_i)}{W}.
\tag{8}
\]

3. Put \(\rho:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E)\). For every \(i\), there is a
nonempty set \(T_i\subseteq E\) with

\[
1\leq |T_i|\leq\rho+1\leq n+1
\tag{9}
\]

such that

\[
\ell_i(z)=h_z(v_i)
\quad(z\in T_i)
\tag{10}
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{vol}_d((A_i)_z)\leq\frac{a_z}{2}
\quad(z\in T_i).
\tag{11}
\]

Consequently,

\[
W-W(A_i)\geq\frac12\sum_{z\in T_i}a_z,
\tag{12}
\]

and combining (8) and (12) gives

\[
1-
\inf_{\substack{\vartheta_j>0\\\sum_j\vartheta_j=1}}
\frac{W(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})}{W}
\geq
\frac{1}{2W}\sum_{z\in T_i}a_z.
\tag{13}
\]

In particular, if

\[
W(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})\geq(1-\varepsilon)W
\]

for every strictly positive probability vector \(\boldsymbol\vartheta\),
then every cap \(A_i\) in the fixed circuit has a contact set \(T_i\) as
above such that

\[
\sum_{z\in T_i}a_z\leq2\varepsilon W.
\tag{14}
\]

Consequently, if the same mass lower bound holds for every legal
balanced-cap descendant arising from every legal support-minimal circuit,
then (14) holds for every exact-minorant cap in every such circuit.

## Proof

Positive homogeneity of a support function gives

\[
h_z(cv)=c h_z(v)\qquad(c>0).
\]

Taking the minimum over \(\Lambda_m(E)\) therefore also gives

\[
\phi(cv)=c\phi(v)\qquad(c>0).
\tag{15}
\]

For (4), equations (1) and (15) imply

\[
\sum_i\vartheta_i v_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta}
=\sum_i\theta_i v_i=0,
\]

and

\[
\sum_i\vartheta_i\phi(v_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta})
=\sum_i\theta_i\phi(v_i)<0.
\]

The rescaled weights are positive and sum to one. Moreover, (2) and (15)
give

\[
\ell_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta}(z)
\leq h_z(v_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta})\quad(z\in E),
\qquad
\ell_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta}(m)
=\phi(v_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta}),
\]

so the rescaled functions are again exact affine minorants. The support
cardinality is still \(r\). Since \(r\) was globally minimum among negative
balanced families, the rescaled family is support-minimal as well. Finally,
because \(\theta_i/\vartheta_i>0\),

\[
\langle v_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta},x\rangle
\geq\ell_i^{\boldsymbol\vartheta}(z)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\langle v_i,x\rangle\geq\ell_i(z).
\tag{16}
\]

Thus every cap is unchanged. Applying the same-base balanced-cap construction
of accepted fact \(51ff186e1aaeaa85\) to this rescaled support-minimal family
shows that \(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta}\) is a legal compact full-dimensional
convex descendant contained in \(C\), and gives exactly the fiber formula
(5).

Fix \(i\) and \(z\in E\). Accepted fact \(51ff186e1aaeaa85\) gives positive
\(d\)-volume, hence nonemptiness, of every compact convex cap fiber
\((A_j)_z\). For compact convex sets, the support function of (5) is

\[
h_{(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})_z}(u)
=\sum_{j=1}^r\vartheta_j h_{(A_j)_z}(u).
\]

This converges uniformly for \(u\) on the unit sphere to
\(h_{(A_i)_z}(u)\) as \(\boldsymbol\vartheta\to e_i\), because there are
only finitely many fixed compact summands. Uniform convergence of support
functions is equivalent to Hausdorff convergence of nonempty compact convex
sets, which proves (6). Volume is continuous under Hausdorff convergence of
compact convex sets, so

\[
\operatorname{vol}_d((B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta})_z)
\longrightarrow\operatorname{vol}_d((A_i)_z)
\quad(z\in E).
\tag{17}
\]

The compact base projection of \(C\) contains only finitely many lattice
points. If \(z\notin E\), every fiber of every \(A_j\subseteq C\), and every
fiber of \(B_{\boldsymbol\vartheta}\subseteq C\), has \(d\)-volume zero.
Summing (17) over the finite set \(E\) therefore proves (7), and (8) follows.

It remains to prove the contact assertion. The polytope
\(\Lambda_m(E)\) is nonempty and compact, so the linear program defining
\(\phi(v_i)\) has a vertex optimizer \(\lambda^i\). Let

\[
T_i:=\{z\in E:\lambda_z^i>0\}.
\]

This set is nonempty. Its points are affinely independent. Indeed, if they
were affinely dependent, there would be a nonzero vector \(\delta\) supported
on \(T_i\) such that

\[
\sum_z\delta_z=0,
\qquad
\sum_z\delta_z z=0.
\]

For all sufficiently small \(t>0\), both
\(\lambda^i+t\delta\) and \(\lambda^i-t\delta\) would remain nonnegative,
belong to \(\Lambda_m(E)\), and be distinct, while their midpoint would be
\(\lambda^i\). This contradicts that \(\lambda^i\) is a vertex. Hence
\(|T_i|\leq\rho+1\), proving (9).

Since \(\ell_i\) is affine and \(\lambda^i\) has barycenter \(m\), exactness
in (2) and optimality of \(\lambda^i\) give

\[
0
=\phi(v_i)-\ell_i(m)
=\sum_{z\in E}\lambda_z^i
\bigl(h_z(v_i)-\ell_i(z)\bigr).
\tag{18}
\]

Every summand in (18) is nonnegative by (2). Therefore it vanishes on the
positive support \(T_i\), which proves (10).

Fix \(z\in T_i\). By (3) and (10),

\[
(A_i)_z
=C_z\cap\{x:\langle v_i,x\rangle\geq h_z(v_i)\}.
\tag{19}
\]

Suppose for contradiction that this set has volume \(s>a_z/2\). Since
\(v_i\neq0\), its boundary hyperplane has \(d\)-dimensional Lebesgue measure
zero. Hence

\[
U:=\{x\in C_z:\langle v_i,x\rangle>h_z(v_i)\}
\]

also has volume \(s>a_z/2\). The density

\[
f:=\frac{a_z}{2s}\mathbf 1_U
\]

satisfies \(0\leq f\leq1\) and has mass \(a_z/2\). Its normalized barycenter

\[
q:=\frac{2}{a_z}\int_{C_z}x f(x)\,dx
\]

therefore belongs to \(D_z\), while

\[
\langle v_i,q\rangle
=\frac{2}{a_z}\int_U\langle v_i,x\rangle f(x)\,dx
>h_z(v_i).
\]

This contradicts the definition of \(h_z(v_i)\) as the maximum of
\(\langle v_i,\cdot\rangle\) on \(D_z\). Thus (11) holds. Since
\(A_i\subseteq C\), all fiberwise mass losses are nonnegative, and summing
the losses on \(T_i\) gives

\[
W-W(A_i)
=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}
\left(a_z-\operatorname{vol}_d((A_i)_z)\right)
\geq\frac12\sum_{z\in T_i}a_z,
\]

which is (12). Equations (8) and (12) imply (13). Under the displayed
near-identity hypothesis, the infimum in (13) is at least
\(1-\varepsilon\). Equation (13) consequently yields (14). Applying this
argument to each legal support-minimal circuit proves the final consequence.
