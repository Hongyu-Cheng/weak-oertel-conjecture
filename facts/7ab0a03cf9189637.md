## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body of positive mixed volume, and write
\[
 C_z:=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:(z,x)\in C\},
 \qquad
 \mu_C(H):=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z^n}
 \operatorname{vol}_d\{x\in C_z:(z,x)\in H\},
\]
\[
 h_{\max}(C):=
 \sup_{y\in C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)}
 \inf_{\substack{H\ni y\\H\ \mathrm{closed\ halfspace}}}\mu_C(H).
\]
For a positive-volume convex body \(K\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) and
\(0<t\leq\operatorname{vol}_d(K)\), use
\[
 \mathsf M_t(K):=
 \left\{
 \frac1t\int_Kxg(x)\,dx:
 0\leq g\leq1\ \text{a.e.},\quad \int_Kg(x)\,dx=t
 \right\}.
\]
Put
\[
 \gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d,
 \qquad
 D:=2^n h_{\max}(C).
\]
Retain one common canonical zero-fresh hard instance of accepted facts
6339b0d39775c51c, 1d61837127904315, and 6bfe50fed9a5540b.  Thus \(Q>0\),
\[
 D<\frac{Q}{100},
\tag{1}
\]
and there are target loads \((\lambda_m)\), physical target-fiber volumes \(a_m\), and an exact minimum-unmatched off-diagonal same-parity target matching \(s^{\mathrm{tar}}\).  Write
\[
 d_m:=d_m(s^{\mathrm{tar}}),
 \qquad
 \Lambda:=\sum_m\lambda_m,
 \qquad
 P_{\mathrm{tar}}:=|s^{\mathrm{tar}}|.
\]
They satisfy
\[
 0\leq d_m\leq\lambda_m\leq\frac{a_m}{2},
 \qquad
 P_{\mathrm{tar}}>\frac{381}{800}Q,
 \qquad
 \Lambda\leq Q.
\tag{2}
\]
For every positive-volume physical target fiber, let
\[
 c_m:=\frac1{a_m}\int_{C_m}x\,dx,
 \qquad
 \mathcal K_m:=\mathsf M_{a_m/2}(C_m),
 \qquad
 D_m:=\mathcal K_m-c_m.
\tag{3}
\]

For each parity class \(\xi\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\), put
\[
 L_\xi:=\sum_{m\equiv\xi\pmod2}d_m.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(L_\xi=0\), put \(q_{\{m,m'\}}=0\) for all pairs in that class.  If \(L_\xi>0\), define, only for distinct \(m,m'\) in the class,
\[
 q_{\{m,m'\}}:=\frac{d_md_{m'}}{L_\xi}.
\tag{5}
\]
Let \(F:=\{f:q_f>0\}\), and write
\[
 d_m(q):=\sum_{f\ni m}q_f,
 \qquad
 |q|:=\sum_{f\in F}q_f.
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 d_m(q)=d_m\left(1-\frac{d_m}{L_\xi}\right)
 \leq d_m\leq\lambda_m\leq\frac{a_m}{2}}
\tag{6}
\]
whenever \(L_\xi>0\), while all quantities are zero when \(L_\xi=0\), and
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{381}{1600}Q<|q|\leq P_{\mathrm{tar}}\leq\frac Q2.}
\tag{7}
\]

For \(f=\{m,m'\}\in F\), put
\[
 \widehat m_f:=\frac{m+m'}2\in\mathbb Z^n,
 \qquad
 \bar c_f:=\frac{c_m+c_{m'}}2,
 \qquad
 \bar D_f:=\frac12(D_m+D_{m'}),
 \qquad
 L_f:=\bar c_f+\bar D_f.
\tag{8}
\]
Every \(\bar D_f\) is a full-dimensional compact origin-symmetric convex body.  Define
\[
 \Delta_{\mathrm{prod}}
 :=\min_{a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \sum_{f\in F}q_f
 \left(
  \|a(\widehat m_f)-\bar c_f\|_{\bar D_f}-1
 \right)_+.
\tag{9}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\Delta_{\mathrm{prod}}>\frac{293}{8000}Q.}
\tag{10}
\]

For \(f=\{m,m'\}\in F\), define its positive-root mass
\[
 \vartheta_f
 :=2^{-(d+1)}
 \left(a_m^{1/d}+a_{m'}^{1/d}\right)^d.
\tag{11}
\]
Then
\[
 L_f\subseteq\mathsf M_{\vartheta_f}(C_{\widehat m_f}),
 \qquad
 \vartheta_f\geq\frac12\sqrt{a_ma_{m'}}
 \geq\sqrt{d_md_{m'}}.
\tag{12}
\]
Fix \(\xi\) with \(L_\xi>0\) and \(t\in\mathbb Z^n\), and put
\[
 F_{\xi,t}:=
 \{f=\{m,m'\}\in F:m\equiv m'\equiv\xi\pmod2,
 \ \widehat m_f=t\},
 \qquad
 q_{\xi,t}:=\sum_{f\in F_{\xi,t}}q_f.
\tag{13}
\]
If \(F_{\xi,t}=\varnothing\), then \(q_{\xi,t}=0\) and no normalized body is defined.  If \(F_{\xi,t}\neq\varnothing\), then
\[
 \sum_{f\in F_{\xi,t}}\frac{q_f}{\vartheta_f}\leq\frac12,
\tag{14}
\]
and the actual weighted Minkowski body
\[
 \mathcal P_{\xi,t}
 :=\frac1{q_{\xi,t}}
 \sum_{f\in F_{\xi,t}}q_fL_f
\tag{15}
\]
satisfies the lossless same-color same-target inclusion
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal P_{\xi,t}
 \subseteq\mathsf M_{2q_{\xi,t}}(C_t).}
\tag{16}
\]

Put \(\eta_f:=q_f/2\) and define the threshold-one-half defect
\[
 \Delta_{1/2}
 :=\min_{a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \sum_{f\in F}\eta_f
 \left(
  \|a(\widehat m_f)-\bar c_f\|_{\bar D_f}-\frac12
 \right)_+.
\tag{17}
\]
There are vectors \(\theta_f\in\mathbb R^d\) such that
\[
 h_{\bar D_f}(\theta_f)\leq\eta_f,
 \qquad
 \sum_f\theta_f=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_f\theta_f\widehat m_f^{\mathsf T}=0,
\tag{18}
\]
and
\[
 \Delta_{1/2}
 =\sum_f
 \left(
  \langle\theta_f,\bar c_f\rangle
  -\frac12h_{\bar D_f}(\theta_f)
 \right)
 >\frac{293}{16000}Q.
\tag{19}
\]
For each \(f\in F\), choose \(d_f^*\in\bar D_f\) exposing \(\bar D_f\) in direction \(\theta_f\), and put
\[
 p_f:=\bar c_f-\frac12d_f^*,
 \qquad
 \mathcal Q_f:=p_f+\frac1{40}\bar D_f.
\tag{20}
\]
Then every \(\mathcal Q_f\) is a full-dimensional convex body and
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal Q_f\subseteq L_f.}
\tag{21}
\]

For every simultaneous selection \(y=(y_f)_{f\in F}\) with
\(y_f\in\mathcal Q_f\), define
\[
 \sigma_y
 :=\gamma_d\sum_{f\in F}q_f
 \delta_{(\widehat m_f,y_f)}.
\tag{22}
\]
Then \(\sigma_y\) is carried by
\(C\cap(\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d)\), and, for every closed ambient halfspace,
\[
 \boxed{\sigma_y\preceq_{\mathrm{hs}}\mu_C,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{|\sigma_y|=\gamma_d|q|>
 \frac{381\gamma_d}{1600}Q.}
\tag{23}
\]
The same bodies obey the all-selection score bound
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{f\in F}\langle\theta_f,y_f-a(\widehat m_f)\rangle
 >\frac{193}{16000}Q}
\tag{24}
\]
for every simultaneous \(y_f\in\mathcal Q_f\) and every affine
\(a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\).  Equivalently, with
\[
 \operatorname{dist}_{\bar D_f}(x,\mathcal Q_f)
 :=\inf_{y\in\mathcal Q_f}\|x-y\|_{\bar D_f},
\]
one has the actual-body affine-distance defect
\[
 \boxed{
 \min_{a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \sum_{f\in F}\eta_f
 \operatorname{dist}_{\bar D_f}
 \bigl(a(\widehat m_f),\mathcal Q_f\bigr)
 >\frac{193}{16000}Q.}
\tag{25}
\]

Finally, put
\[
 \rho:=\dim\operatorname{aff}\{\widehat m_f:f\in F\},
\]
and, for a simultaneous selection \(y_f\in\mathcal Q_f\), define
\[
 r(y):=\min_{a:\operatorname{aff}\{\widehat m_f:f\in F\}
 \to\mathbb R^d\ \mathrm{affine}}
 \dim\operatorname{span}
 \{y_f-a(\widehat m_f):f\in F\}.
\tag{26}
\]
Then every simultaneous selection satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 r(y)+1>
 \frac{381\gamma_d}{16}\,2^{n-\rho}.}
\tag{27}
\]
No assertion is made about pooling different parity classes at a common target, collapsing the product edges to an admissible endpoint object, bounding the residual rank from above, or proving the locked theorem.

## Proof

Accepted fact 6339b0d39775c51c gives (1), the strict lower bound for
\(P_{\mathrm{tar}}\) in (2), and the same canonical target system.  Accepted fact 1d61837127904315 gives
\(0\leq d_m\leq\lambda_m\leq a_m/2\).  In the notation of accepted fact 6bfe50fed9a5540b, one also has
\(\lambda_m\leq t_m\),
\(\sum_mt_m=T\), and
\(T=\sum_e\alpha_e\leq\sum_er_e=Q\).  Therefore
\(\Lambda\leq Q\), completing (2).

We first verify the product matching, including empty classes.  If
\(L_\xi=0\), every \(d_m\) in that class is zero, so the stipulated zero matching has every asserted property.  Suppose \(L_\xi>0\).  Since
\(s^{\mathrm{tar}}\) is off-diagonal, the total degree at any one vertex is at most the total degree at all other vertices:
\[
 d_m\leq L_\xi-d_m,
 \qquad\text{hence}\qquad
 d_m\leq\frac{L_\xi}{2}.
\tag{28}
\]
Summing (5) over \(m'\neq m\) gives
\[
 d_m(q)=\frac{d_m}{L_\xi}(L_\xi-d_m),
\]
which proves (6).  Moreover,
\[
 |q|_\xi
 :=\sum_{\substack{f\in F\\f\subseteq\xi}}q_f
 =\frac1{2L_\xi}
 \left(L_\xi^2-\sum_{m\equiv\xi}d_m^2\right).
\tag{29}
\]
By (28),
\[
 \sum_{m\equiv\xi}d_m^2
 \leq\left(\max_{m\equiv\xi}d_m\right)L_\xi
 \leq\frac{L_\xi^2}{2},
\]
so \(|q|_\xi\geq L_\xi/4\).  Also (29) gives
\(|q|_\xi\leq L_\xi/2\).  Since
\[
 \sum_\xi L_\xi=\sum_md_m=2P_{\mathrm{tar}},
\]
we obtain
\[
 |q|\geq\frac12P_{\mathrm{tar}}
 >\frac{381}{1600}Q,
 \qquad
 |q|\leq P_{\mathrm{tar}}.
\tag{30}
\]
The exact minimum-unmatched identity in 1d61837127904315 gives
\(P_{\mathrm{tar}}=(\Lambda-U)/2\leq\Lambda/2\leq Q/2\).
This proves (7), including the upper bound used below.

Apply the in-particular affine-defect estimate of accepted fact
11e347c295e9430b to the matching
\[
 \widetilde q_f:=2\gamma_dq_f.
\]
By (6),
\[
 d_m(\widetilde q)=2\gamma_dd_m(q)
 \leq\gamma_da_m,
\]
so its capacity hypothesis holds.  Under the alias map from that fact's
\((z,z',m_e,\bar c_e,D_e,q_e)\) to the present
\((m,m',\widehat m_f,\bar c_f,\bar D_f,\widetilde q_f)\), its defect and mass scale by \(2\gamma_d\).  Thus
\[
 D\geq\frac{\gamma_d}{2}
 \left(|q|-5\Delta_{\mathrm{prod}}\right)_+.
\tag{31}
\]
Whether or not the argument of the positive part is positive, (1) and
(31) imply
\[
 |q|-5\Delta_{\mathrm{prod}}
 <\frac{Q}{50\gamma_d}.
\tag{32}
\]
Accepted fact 6339b0d39775c51c also gives
\(1/(100\gamma_d)<11/400\), hence
\(1/(50\gamma_d)<11/200\).  Combining this with (7) and (32),
\[
 \Delta_{\mathrm{prod}}
 >\frac15
 \left(\frac{381}{1600}-\frac{11}{200}\right)Q
 =\frac{293}{8000}Q.
\]
This proves (10).

We next prove (12) directly from the general root-lift convexity of accepted fact dfd25e72c1f45a4e, because the present product edges need not be edges of the first-generation canonical reservoir.  If
\(x\in\mathcal K_m\) and \(x'\in\mathcal K_{m'}\), their root-lift parameters are
\((a_m/2)^{1/d}\) and \((a_{m'}/2)^{1/d}\).  Midpoint convexity gives
\[
 \frac{x+x'}2\in
 \mathsf M_{\left(\frac{(a_m/2)^{1/d}+(a_{m'}/2)^{1/d}}2\right)^d}
 (C_{(m+m')/2})
 =\mathsf M_{\vartheta_f}(C_{\widehat m_f}).
\]
Varying \(x,x'\) proves the first inclusion in (12).  The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives
\[
 \vartheta_f
 \geq\frac12\sqrt{a_ma_{m'}}.
\tag{33}
\]
Since \(a_m\geq2d_m\) and \(a_{m'}\geq2d_{m'}\), (33) gives the last inequality in (12).

Fix a nonempty cell \(F_{\xi,t}\).  The reflection
\(m\mapsto2t-m\) is an involution.  After excluding its fixed point, which would create a forbidden diagonal pair, its unordered two-cycles are endpoint-disjoint.  Therefore, using (5), (12), and arithmetic-geometric mean,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{f\in F_{\xi,t}}\frac{q_f}{\vartheta_f}
 &\leq\frac1{L_\xi}
 \sum_{\{m,m'\}\in F_{\xi,t}}\sqrt{d_md_{m'}}\\
 &\leq\frac1{2L_\xi}
 \sum_{\{m,m'\}\in F_{\xi,t}}(d_m+d_{m'})
 \leq\frac12.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{34}
\]
This proves (14).  To prove (16), choose arbitrary
\(x_f\in L_f\) for all \(f\in F_{\xi,t}\).  By (12), choose a representing metronoid density \(g_f\) on \(C_t\) with
\[
 0\leq g_f\leq1,
 \qquad
 \int g_f=\vartheta_f,
 \qquad
 \int xg_f(x)\,dx=\vartheta_fx_f.
\]
Then
\[
 g:=\sum_{f\in F_{\xi,t}}\frac{q_f}{\vartheta_f}g_f
\]
satisfies \(0\leq g\leq1/2\), has mass \(q_{\xi,t}\), and has barycenter
\(q_{\xi,t}^{-1}\sum_fq_fx_f\).  Hence \(2g\leq1\) represents the same barycenter with mass \(2q_{\xi,t}\).  Since the choices \(x_f\) were arbitrary, the whole Minkowski body in (15) lies in
\(\mathsf M_{2q_{\xi,t}}(C_t)\).  Empty cells were excluded before every division, so (16) is proved without a zero-mass metronoid convention.

We now construct the thick edge bodies.  Pointwise,
\[
 (u-1/2)_+\geq(u-1)_+.
\]
Since \(\eta_f=q_f/2\), minimization over the same affine maps gives
\[
 \Delta_{1/2}\geq\frac12\Delta_{\mathrm{prod}}
 >\frac{293}{16000}Q.
\tag{35}
\]
The endpoint occurrence load of the label family with weights \(\eta_f\) is
\[
 \ell_m:=\sum_{f\ni m}\eta_f
 =\frac12d_m(q)\leq\frac{a_m}{4}.
\tag{36}
\]
Apply accepted fact f025d631bd2f3444 with quotient subspace
\(W=\{0\}\) and the exact alias map
\[
 (\sigma,\eta_\sigma,a_\sigma,b_\sigma,m_\sigma,
 q_\sigma,B_\sigma)
 \longleftrightarrow
 (f,\eta_f,m,m',\widehat m_f,\bar c_f,\bar D_f).
\tag{37}
\]
Here the predecessor's center symbol \(q_\sigma\) maps to \(\bar c_f\) and is unrelated to the present matching-weight symbol \(q_f\).  An affine map on the affine hull of the midpoints extends to \(\mathbb R^n\), so its defect is exactly (17).  The accepted KKT conclusion gives (18), the equality in (19), and hence the strict inequality there by (35).

Choose \(d_f^*\) as in (20), including an arbitrary point of
\(\bar D_f\) when \(\theta_f=0\).  Since \(\bar D_f\) is origin-symmetric and convex,
\[
 -\frac12d_f^*+\frac1{40}\bar D_f
 \subseteq\frac12\bar D_f+\frac1{40}\bar D_f
 =\frac{21}{40}\bar D_f
 \subseteq\bar D_f.
\]
Thus \(\mathcal Q_f\subseteq\bar c_f+\bar D_f=L_f\).  The body
\(\bar D_f\), and hence \(\mathcal Q_f\), is full-dimensional by the half-metronoid structure recorded in accepted fact 11e347c295e9430b.  This proves (21) and the positive-radius assertion.

For arbitrary simultaneous \(y_f\in\mathcal Q_f\), (36), (21), and accepted whole-label domination fact ff5bc0ba02cc86cd give
\[
 2\gamma_d\sum_f\eta_f
 \delta_{(\widehat m_f,y_f)}
 \preceq_{\mathrm{hs}}\mu_C.
\]
The left side is exactly \(\sigma_y\).  Its total mass and the strict lower bound in (23) follow from (7).  The same accepted fact also supplies the support assertion.

For every \(y_f\in\mathcal Q_f\), the exposure choice in (20) gives
\[
 \langle\theta_f,y_f\rangle
 \geq
 \langle\theta_f,\bar c_f\rangle
 -\frac12h_{\bar D_f}(\theta_f)
 -\frac1{40}h_{\bar D_f}(\theta_f).
\tag{38}
\]
Summing (38), using (18)-(19), and then (7),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_f\langle\theta_f,y_f\rangle
 &\geq\Delta_{1/2}
 -\frac1{40}\sum_fh_{\bar D_f}(\theta_f)\\
 &\geq\Delta_{1/2}-\frac1{40}\sum_f\eta_f\\
 &=\Delta_{1/2}-\frac{|q|}{80}\\
 &>\left(\frac{293}{16000}-\frac1{160}\right)Q
 =\frac{193}{16000}Q.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{39}
\]
For every affine \(a(x)=Ax+b\), the two equilibrium identities in (18) give
\[
 \sum_f\langle\theta_f,a(\widehat m_f)\rangle=0.
\tag{40}
\]
Equations (39)-(40) prove (24).

For completeness, (25) follows by direct weak duality and does not require an unlisted distance theorem.  For every affine \(a\) and every
\(z\in\mathcal Q_f\), origin symmetry and (18) give
\[
 \langle\theta_f,z-a(\widehat m_f)\rangle
 \leq h_{\bar D_f}(\theta_f)
 \|z-a(\widehat m_f)\|_{\bar D_f}
 \leq\eta_f
 \|z-a(\widehat m_f)\|_{\bar D_f}.
\]
Taking the infimum over \(z\in\mathcal Q_f\), summing over \(f\), and using (38)-(40) proves that every affine \(a\) has objective strictly larger than \(193Q/16000\).  Taking the minimum proves (25).

Finally fix any simultaneous selection.  The base projection of
\(\operatorname{supp}\sigma_y\) is exactly
\(\{\widehat m_f:f\in F\}\), because all weights are positive and repeated atoms only add.  Its base rank is therefore \(\rho\), and its continuous residual rank in accepted fact be359f8a6c628483 is exactly \(r(y)\).  Apply that accepted dominated-measure residual theorem to (23):
\[
 h_{\max}(C)
 \geq\frac{\gamma_d|q|}{2^\rho(r(y)+1)}.
\]
Multiplication by \(2^n\), followed by (1) and (7), yields
\[
 \frac{Q}{100}
 >D
 \geq\frac{2^{n-\rho}\gamma_d|q|}{r(y)+1}
 >\frac{381\gamma_d}{1600}
 \frac{2^{n-\rho}Q}{r(y)+1}.
\]
Rearrangement proves (27).  All constructions were paritywise up to the explicitly stated dominated cloud; no step pools different parity classes at a common target or produces the downstream endpoint collapse.
