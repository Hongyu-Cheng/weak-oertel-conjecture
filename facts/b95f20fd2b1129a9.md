## Statement

Let $d\ge3$. For independent unit exponentials $E_0,\ldots,E_d$, if $a_0=-1$, $a_i\in[-1,1]$ and $\sum_i a_i=0$, then
$$
\Pr\Bigl(\sum_i a_iE_i\ge0\Bigr)>\frac76\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d.
$$
Equivalently, for $T_d=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,\ldots,e_d)$ and any $u\in[0,\infty)^d$ with $\sum_i u_i=1$ and $\max_i u_i\le2/(d+1)$, the cap $T_d\cap\{x:u\cdot x\ge1/(d+1)\}$ has relative volume strictly greater than $\tfrac76(d/(d+1))^d$.

## Proof

For $3\le d\le9$ apply the exact finite capped-minimum theorem. For $d\ge10$, set $c=9/20$ in the accepted capped escape theorem 65d374efd09b8c3d. The accepted fractional gamma theorem 870ad0085472647d gives the strict scalar premise for every positive integer shape and every $p\in[0,1]$. The resulting sign probability is strictly greater than $9/20$. The sequence $\Gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$ is decreasing because the derivative of $x\log(x/(x+1))$ for $x>0$ is $-\log(1+1/x)+1/(x+1)<0$. The sign follows from $\log(1+t)>t/(1+t)$ for $t>0$, by differentiating the difference. The exact comparison is
$$
\frac9{20}-\frac76\left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^{10}
=\frac{310464227}{1556245476060}>0.
$$

For the geometric equivalence let $G=\sum_{i=0}^d E_i$. The change to $G$ and the barycentric ratios has Jacobian $G^d$, and the joint density factors into a gamma density and a constant simplex density. Thus $(E_1/G,\ldots,E_d/G)$ is uniform on $T_d$. Set $a_0=-1$ and $a_i=(d+1)u_i-1$ for $i\ge1$. The coefficient and normal constraints correspond in both directions, and the cap condition becomes $\sum_i a_i E_i\ge0$ after multiplying by the positive $G(d+1)$. Its boundary has zero simplex volume, and the signed sum has no atom at zero since the fixed negative unit phase has a continuous law.
