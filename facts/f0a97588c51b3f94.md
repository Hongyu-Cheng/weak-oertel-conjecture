## Statement

For each $d\ge1$, even with $n=1$ and $f(z,x)=z+x_1$, there is no $c_d>0$ such that $G((s+t)/2)\ge c_d\Phi_d(G(s),G(t))$ for every compact convex source and $s<t$, where $G$ sums all actual integer-fiber $d$-volumes in the closed cap and $\Phi_d(A,B)=2^{-d}(A^{1/d}+B^{1/d})^d$. This holds with both endpoint cuts proper on their respective active fibers.

## Proof

For $A\ge2$ take the convex hull of the two fibers $[-2A-2,-A-2]\times[0,1]^{d-1}$ at base $0$ and $[0,1]^d$ at base $1$. These are its only integer bases and its exact fibers, since they are the two exposed endpoint bases. Set $s=-2A-1$, $t=3/2$, $h=-A+1/4$. The $s$-tail retains volume $A-1$ at base $0$ and all of base $1$, so $G(s)=A$. The $t$-tail is the proper half cube at base $1$, so $G(t)=1/2$. The $h$-threshold is strictly between the two support intervals of $f$, so $G(h)=1$. Therefore $G(h)/\Phi_d(G(s),G(t))<2^d/A\to0$. The proper $s$-cut is on the large fiber, whose parity has no surviving $t$-trace.
