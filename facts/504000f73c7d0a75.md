## Statement

Let $T=\operatorname{conv}\lbrace(2,-1),(-1,2),(-1,-1)\rbrace$, $K_\varepsilon=\lbrace(x,t):|t|\le1,\ x\in(1+\varepsilon t)T\rbrace$, $0<\varepsilon\le10^{-9}$, and $Y=(0,\varepsilon/3)$. For the ordinary projective energy $E(y)=\min_a\int_{K_\varepsilon}[1+a\cdot(W-y)]^{-4}\,dW$ over denominators strictly positive on $K_\varepsilon$, the unique minimizer at $Y$ points in the positive vertical direction. The gradients of $E$ and $E^{1/3}$ at $Y$ lie outside the span of the active ordinary halfspace normals, although $Y$ maximizes both on the deepest set.

For the actual body $[0,1]\times K_\varepsilon$ with the first coordinate integer, the two global deepest marks are $(0,Y)$ and $(1,Y)$ and their normalized mixed depth is $2/9$. Every critical ambient cap there has zero mass in the other fiber and one of the three active horizontal continuous normals, up to positive scale. The same secondary-gradient obstruction holds on either own fiber.

## Proof

By triangle-frustum-actual-depth-and-active-menu, $Y$ is the unique deepest point and all active oriented normals are horizontal. The affine triangle vertex permutations fix the centroid zero and hence are linear in the plane. They act on $K_\varepsilon$ fixing height and $Y$, and preserve ordinary volume since their absolute determinants are one. They act transitively on the vertices, so any invariant horizontal covector is constant on all three vertex values and, by their sum zero and spanning, is zero. The unique minimizing parameter from d90ba05577427854 is invariant under these permutations by change of variables, so it is vertical.

The frustum centroid has zero horizontal component and height $2\varepsilon/[3(1+\varepsilon^2/3)]>\varepsilon/3$, by integrating against the height density proportional to $(1+\varepsilon t)^2$. The derivative of the parameter objective at zero in the vertical direction is therefore $-4|K_\varepsilon|(g_t-\varepsilon/3)<0$, where $g_t$ is that centroid height. Strict convexity on its admissible interval and attainment force its minimizer to have positive vertical component. The accepted envelope formulas a56f8711406a3870, in continuous dimension three at the interior mark, give the nonzero vertical spatial gradients of $E$ and $E^{1/3}$. The deepest set is a singleton, so both are maximized there on that set. The horizontal active normals cannot represent either gradient even with signed coefficients.

The product has exactly the integer bases $0,1$, with identical fibers. Any ambient cap through or containing a mark includes its own trace, so its raw mass is at least the ordinary own-fiber depth. Conversely, any ordinary own cap is realized with zero mass in the other fiber by a sufficiently large finite base tilt of the appropriate sign, by compactness. Thus the normalized mixed depth at each mark is half its normalized own depth. At either maximizing mark a critical ambient cap must have own mass exactly $(4/9)|K_\varepsilon|$ and zero other mass. A pure-base cap takes the whole own fiber and cannot be critical. Applying the ordinary active-menu statement to the own trace proves the classification and the same continuous-gradient failure.
