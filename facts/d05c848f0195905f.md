## Statement

In the standard tetrahedron fix an interior barycentric point $\lambda$ with $\lambda_i<1/3$ for all $i$. Set $\sigma=\sum_i\lambda_i^2$, $D=1-3\sigma$, and $R=27\prod_i\lambda_i/D$. For vertex $i$, orient the centroidal vertex-isolating cap toward $i$, denote its relative volume by $f_i=27\prod_j\lambda_j/\lambda_i$, and write $q_i$ for its oriented section area vector divided by the tetrahedron volume. Then
$$
f_i=R+v\cdot q_i,\qquad v_k=\frac{\lambda_k(\sigma-\lambda_k)}{D}\quad(k=1,2,3).
$$
There is a unique section-balanced probability vector on the four caps, with weights $\lambda_i(1-3\lambda_i)/D$, and its mean is $R$. For a strict quadrilateral cap with centroid $\lambda$, raw signed heights $t_i$, normalized coarea $S$, and fraction $F$, the condition that its fraction is at least $R+v\cdot(S\nabla m)$ for $m=\sum_i t_i\xi_i$ is
$$
DF+S\sum_i\lambda_i^2t_i-27\prod_i\lambda_i\ge0.
$$
For the complementary cap this condition is $D(1-F)-S\sum_i\lambda_i^2t_i-27\prod_i\lambda_i\ge0$.

## Proof

The vertex cap at $i$ meets the edge from $i$ to $j$ at parameter $3\lambda_j\in(0,1)$. The average of its three edge points is $\lambda$ and the determinant gives fraction $f_i$. Any vertex-isolating cut with that centroid must have the same edge parameters, as each centroid coordinate at $j\ne i$ is one third of that parameter. Its raw margin is $m_i=3-\sum_{j\ne i}\xi_j/\lambda_j$. The cap is a pyramid of height $3/\lVert\nabla m_i\rVert$ and base equal to the section, so $q_i=f_i\nabla m_i$. In a relation $\sum_i b_i\nabla m_i=0$, set $B=\sum_i b_i$. The only relation among the four barycentric gradients is their sum, so $(B-b_j)/\lambda_j$ is constant in $j$. Summing shows that this constant is $3B$. Thus $b_j=B(1-3\lambda_j)$, and the relation holds for these coefficients. Setting $b_i=\theta_i f_i$ and requiring $\sum_i\theta_i=1$ gives the stated probability weights. They are positive because each $\lambda_i<1/3$ and $D=\sum_i\lambda_i(1-3\lambda_i)>0$.

For a margin $m$ through $\lambda$, standard coordinates give $(\nabla m)_k=t_k-t_0$. Since $\sum_i\lambda_i(\sigma-\lambda_i)=0$ and $\sum_i\lambda_i t_i=0$, one has
$$
D\,v\cdot\nabla m=\sum_i\lambda_i(\sigma-\lambda_i)t_i=-\sum_i\lambda_i^2t_i.
$$
For the vertex margin $m_i$, the last sum before negation is $3\sigma-(1-\lambda_i)=\lambda_i-D$. Therefore $v\cdot q_i=f_i-f_i\lambda_i/D=f_i-R$. The balanced mean follows by summing this identity. For the quadrilateral margin multiply the identity for $v\cdot\nabla m$ by $S$ and rearrange the lower affine bound. The complementary cap replaces $(t,F)$ by $(-t,1-F)$ and leaves $S$ unchanged.
