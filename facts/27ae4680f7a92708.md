## Statement

Let $3\le d\le9$, and let $E_0,\ldots,E_d$ be independent unit exponentials. Over all $a\in[-1,1]^{d+1}$ with $a_0=-1$ and $\sum_i a_i=0$, the minimum of $\Pr(\sum_i a_iE_i\ge0)$ for each $d$ is given by
$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrr}
d&3&4&5&6&7&8&9\\ \hline
\text{minimum}&1/2&13/27&243/512&1472/3125&21875/46656&384912/823543&15647317/33554432 .
\end{array}
$$
In every listed dimension the minimum is realized by two positive unit coefficients, the fixed negative unit coefficient, and $d-2$ negative coefficients of magnitude $1/(d-2)$. Each minimum is strictly greater than $\tfrac76(d/(d+1))^d$.

## Proof

The accepted capped minimizer classification 38821acd2e746146 gives an attaining vector and the three possible minimizing strata. First, if all nonzero negative coefficients are units, its probability is at least $1/2$.

If all positive coefficients are units, let $k$ be their number. There are at most $q=d-k$ negative coefficients besides the fixed unit, with magnitudes of sum $k-1$ and each at most one. In particular $1\le k\le\lfloor(d+1)/2\rfloor$. Pad the residual list with zeros to length $q$. By accepted fixed-count equalization ad017d8380c3bf8d, the probability is at least
$$
B_{d,k}=\Pr\left\{X_k\ge E+\frac{k-1}{q}Y_q\right\},
$$
where $X_k,Y_q,E$ are independent unit-rate gamma variables of shapes $k,q,1$. This also holds at $k=1$ with all the residual magnitudes zero.

In the third stratum, the positive list consists of $k$ units and one $p\in(0,1)$, and the negative magnitudes are the fixed unit and $m$ copies of $(k+p-1)/m\in(0,1)$, where $m\ge k$ and $k+m=d-1$. If the probability is below $1/2$, accepted stationary exclusion 18d3a5cf0b729f6d gives $m>k$. Thus $d\le9$ forces $k\le3$. For each $k=1,2,3$ the corresponding reviewed endpoint theorem bounds this probability below by the smaller of its two values at $p=0$ and $p=1$. At $p=0$ there are $k$ positive units and a zero fractional coordinate, so the residual negative magnitudes can be padded to $d-k=m+1$. At $p=1$ there are $k+1$ positive units and $m$ residual magnitudes. Both endpoint lists obey the coefficient bounds because $m\ge k$. They have probability at least $B_{d,k}$ and $B_{d,k+1}$ respectively by the same equalization. All three strata therefore give a probability at least the smaller of $1/2$ and the minimum of the admissible $B_{d,k}$.

For the exact arithmetic put $z=(k-1)/(q+k-1)$. The integer-shape gamma survival is $e^{-x}\sum_{\ell=0}^{k-1}x^\ell/\ell!$ for $x\ge0$. Tilting the negative sum at rate one gives the Laplace factor $\tfrac12(q/(q+k-1))^q$, and changes the exponential means to $1/2$ and $z$ repeated $q$ times. The divided moment of order $\ell$ is the sum of monomials of total degree $\ell$ in those means, obtained by multiplying the geometric series of the moment-generating functions. Hence
$$
B_{d,k}=\frac12\left(\frac{q}{q+k-1}\right)^q
\sum_{\ell=0}^{k-1}\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}
\binom{q+j-1}{j}z^j 2^{-(\ell-j)} .
$$
The degree-zero power is one, including at $z=0$. Substitution in this finite sum gives $\min_k B_{d,k}=B_{d,2}$ in each listed dimension, with value at most $1/2$ and the exact values in the statement. The margins over $\tfrac76(d/(d+1))^d$ in increasing $d$ are
$$
\frac1{128},\quad
\frac{61}{16875},\quad
\frac{2147}{373248},\quad
\frac{439904}{52521875},\quad
\frac{32753357}{3057647616},\quad
\frac{1348942633648}{106352477257509},\quad
\frac{939511987337}{65536000000000}.
$$
The [rational verifier](../assets/pk-nov6/computations/verify_capped_angular_low_dimension_constants.py) evaluates every admissible $B_{d,k}$ and these margins. The list with two positive units and equal residual negative magnitudes realizes $B_{d,2}$, which proves sharpness.
