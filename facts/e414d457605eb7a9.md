## Statement

Let \(n,d\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), let
\(C\subseteq\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d\) be a convex body, and use the
canonical hard \(I\)-branch objects of accepted facts 6b09aae189415c70 and
7ec63df101c30343. Thus, for every retained parity label \((\xi,c)\),
\[
S_{\xi,c}:=
\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi\\m_e=c}}s_e,
\qquad
q_{\xi,c}^{\circ}+\frac12D_*
\subseteq
\mathsf M_{2S_{\xi,c}}(C_c),
\tag{1}
\]
where, for \(0<m\le\operatorname{vol}_d(K)\),
\[
\mathsf M_m(K):=
\left\{
\frac1m\int_Kx f(x)\,dx:
0\le f\le1\ {\rm a.e.},\quad \int_Kf=m
\right\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Let \(t_e\) be the actual opposite-edge masses of the transverse triangle
construction in accepted fact 7ec63df101c30343, so
\[
0\le t_e\le\frac12s_e.
\tag{3}
\]
Let \(\mathcal E_I\) be its canonical set of \(I\)-bad opposite edges. Define
\[
\Theta_{\xi,c}:=
\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi,\ m_e=c\\e\in\mathcal E_I}}t_e,
\qquad
\Theta_I(E):=
\sum_{c\in E}\sum_\xi\Theta_{\xi,c}
\quad(E\subseteq\mathbb Z^n).
\tag{4}
\]
In the hard \(I\)-branch, accepted fact 7ec63df101c30343 gives
\[
\Theta_I(\mathbb Z^n)>\frac{3Q}{100}.
\tag{5}
\]

For each physical target \(c\) satisfying
\(\Theta_I(\{c\})>0\), choose a retained label \(\xi(c)\) for which
\(\Theta_{\xi(c),c}\) is maximal, and put
\[
q_c:=q_{\xi(c),c}^{\circ}.
\tag{6}
\]
Then
\[
\Theta_{\xi(c),c}\ge2^{-n}\Theta_I(\{c\}),
\tag{7}
\]
and the selected point is an actual metronoid point satisfying
\[
q_c\in
\mathsf M_{4\Theta_{\xi(c),c}}(C_c).
\tag{8}
\]
With
\[
\gamma_d:=\left(\frac d{d+1}\right)^d
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
\delta_C(c,q_c):=
\inf\left\{
\operatorname{vol}_d(C_c\cap H):
H\subseteq\mathbb R^d
\text{ is a proper closed halfspace and }q_c\in H
\right\},
\tag{10}
\]
one has
\[
\delta_C(c,q_c)\ge
4\gamma_d\Theta_{\xi(c),c}.
\tag{11}
\]

Let \(E\) be any nonempty set of physical targets with
\(\Theta_I(\{c\})>0\) for every \(c\in E\). Define
\[
\rho_E:=\dim\operatorname{aff}(E)
\tag{12}
\]
and the continuous residual rank of the fixed selected points by
\[
r_E:=
\min_{a:\operatorname{aff}(E)\to\mathbb R^d\ {\rm affine}}
\dim\operatorname{span}\{q_c-a(c):c\in E\}.
\tag{13}
\]
Then the residual-selector capacity in the exact normalization of accepted
fact be359f8a6c628483 satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\Theta_I(E)
\le
\frac{2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1)}
{4\gamma_d}\,R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C).
}
\tag{14}
\]
In particular, if \(\Theta_I(E)\ge\eta Q\) for some \(\eta>0\) and the hard
bound \(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<Q/100\) holds, then
\[
2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1)>400\gamma_d\eta.
\tag{15}
\]
No conclusion about an integral packet rank follows from (13)--(15).

## Proof

Fix a physical target \(c\) with \(\Theta_I(\{c\})>0\). There are at most
\(2^n\) parity labels at \(c\), so the maximizing choice in (6) gives (7).
Equations (3)--(4) give
\[
\Theta_{\xi(c),c}
\le
\frac12
\sum_{\substack{e\subseteq\xi(c)\\m_e=c}}s_e
=
\frac12S_{\xi(c),c}.
\tag{16}
\]
In particular \(S_{\xi(c),c}>0\).

By the actual target inclusion (1), which is supplied in the exact ordered-pair
normalization by accepted fact 6b09aae189415c70, there is an admissible density
\(f_c\) on \(C_c\) such that
\[
0\le f_c\le1,\qquad
\int_{C_c}f_c=2S_{\xi(c),c},\qquad
q_c=
\frac1{2S_{\xi(c),c}}
\int_{C_c}x f_c(x)\,dx.
\tag{17}
\]
Set
\[
g_c:=
\frac{2\Theta_{\xi(c),c}}{S_{\xi(c),c}}\,f_c.
\tag{18}
\]
Equation (16) makes the scalar in (18) at most one. Hence
\(0\le g_c\le1\), and direct calculation gives
\[
\int_{C_c}g_c=4\Theta_{\xi(c),c},
\qquad
\frac1{4\Theta_{\xi(c),c}}
\int_{C_c}x g_c(x)\,dx=q_c.
\tag{19}
\]
This proves the metronoid membership (8). It uses one density in one actual
target fiber; densities belonging to different parity labels or different
targets are never pooled.

Apply accepted fact 6769574fc1bd9f8d to (8), with
\(K=C_c\) and \(m=4\Theta_{\xi(c),c}\). It gives
\(q_c\in C_c\) and says that every proper closed halfspace of
\(\mathbb R^d\) containing \(q_c\) cuts from \(C_c\) volume at least
\(4\gamma_d\Theta_{\xi(c),c}\). This is exactly (11), including boundary
halfspaces.

Now fix a nonempty \(E\) as in the statement, and choose an affine map
\(a:\operatorname{aff}(E)\to\mathbb R^d\) attaining the minimum in (13).
For every \(c\in E\), put
\[
w_c:=4\gamma_d\Theta_{\xi(c),c}.
\tag{20}
\]
Equations (11) and (20) give
\[
0<w_c\le\delta_C(c,q_c).
\tag{21}
\]
Thus \(E\), the actual points \(q_c\in C_c\), the weights \(w_c\), and the
chosen affine map \(a\) are an admissible test family in the definition of
\(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)\) from accepted fact be359f8a6c628483. Its exact
normalization yields
\[
R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)
\ge
2^{n-\rho_E}
\frac{
4\gamma_d
\sum_{c\in E}\Theta_{\xi(c),c}
}{r_E+1}.
\tag{22}
\]
Summing (7) over \(c\in E\) gives
\[
\sum_{c\in E}\Theta_{\xi(c),c}
\ge2^{-n}\Theta_I(E).
\tag{23}
\]
Substituting (23) into (22) and rearranging proves (14). Finally, if
\(\Theta_I(E)\ge\eta Q\) while
\(R_{\mathrm{sel}}(C)<Q/100\), equation (14) gives
\[
\eta Q
<
\frac{2^{\rho_E}(r_E+1)}{400\gamma_d}Q,
\]
which is (15). The factor \(r_E+1\) remains the continuous residual
denominator from the selector capacity throughout.
