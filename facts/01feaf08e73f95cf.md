## Statement

Let $d\ge2$, $m=d+1$, and let $v_0,\ldots,v_d$ span $\mathbb R^d$ with sole dependence generated by $\sum_i v_i=0$. Put $T=\{x:v_i\cdot x\le1\ \forall i\}$. Let $A\subset\mathbb R^d$ be a convex body and let $J$ consist of $k$ distinct indices, $2\le k\le m$, for which $R_j=A\cap\bigcap_{i\ne j}\{v_i\cdot x<-1\}$ has positive $d$-volume. Choose any $P_j\in R_j$, put $P=\operatorname{conv}\{P_j:j\in J\}$, and set $r=k-1$. Then $\dim P=r$. If $F$ is the linear space parallel to $\operatorname{aff}P$, every nonempty section $T\cap(z+F)$ lies in a translate of $-sP$ for some $0\le s\le1$, and has $r$-dimensional Euclidean volume strictly less than $|P|_r$.

## Proof

The affine coordinates $\lambda_i(x)=(1+v_i\cdot x)/m$ identify $\mathbb R^d$ with $\sum_i\lambda_i=1$ by the rank hypothesis. In these coordinates $T$ is given by $\lambda_i\le2/m$. Let $C$ have columns $\lambda(P_j)$. Its diagonal entries $C_{jj}$ are greater than one, the other entries are negative, and every column sum is one.

The square row restriction $C_J$ has positive column sums. Write it as $D-N$ with $D$ its positive diagonal and $N\ge0$ entrywise with zero diagonal. Since the sum of each column of $N$ is strictly less than the corresponding entry of $D$, $\|ND^{-1}\|_1<1$. Consequently $C_J^{-1}=D^{-1}\sum_{q\ge0}(ND^{-1})^q$ exists and is nonnegative. Invertibility gives affine independence of the selected points.

In any translate of $F$, points have coordinates $c+C\theta$ for a fixed $c$ with $\sum_i c_i=0$ and parameters $\sum_j\theta_j=1$. For instance translate the parametrization of $\operatorname{aff}P$ to a point in the translate. Membership in $T$ is $C\theta\le h$, where $h_i=2/m-c_i$ and $\sum_i h_i=2$. Put $M=C_J^{-1}h_J$ and $U=\sum_jM_j$. Every feasible parameter has $\theta\le M$, so $U\ge1$. On rows outside $J$, negativity implies $C_{\mathrm{out}}\theta\ge C_{\mathrm{out}}M$ and hence $h_{\mathrm{out}}\ge C_{\mathrm{out}}M$. Since $C_JM=h_J$, summing all rows and using the unit column sums gives $U\le2$.

The set $\{\theta:\theta\le M,\ \sum_j\theta_j=1\}$ is $M-(U-1)\Delta$, with $\Delta$ the standard probability simplex on $J$. Under the affine parametrization it is a translate of $-(U-1)P$. For $U=1$ it is a singleton. Otherwise each vertex $M-(U-1)e_j$ violates any selected row $i\ne j$, because $C_{ij}<0$. The feasible section is closed and omits a relative neighborhood of each such vertex, of positive $r$-volume inside the containing simplex. It therefore has volume strictly less than $(U-1)^r|P|_r\le|P|_r$. The affine parametrization on each parallel plane has the same Euclidean $r$-volume Jacobian.
