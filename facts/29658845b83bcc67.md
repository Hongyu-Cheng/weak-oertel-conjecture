## Statement

Fix $n,d\ge1$ and let $C=[0,1]^n\times K$ for a full-dimensional $d$-simplex $K$ of volume $v$. Its mixed source has mass $M=2^n v$. Write $\lambda_0,\ldots,\lambda_d$ for the barycentric coordinates and $\gamma_d=(d/(d+1))^d$. For $0<t<1/(d+1)$, $A>1$, $p\in\{0,1\}^n$, and $0\le j\le d$, use the cap with unscaled margin $\lambda_j(x)-t-Aq_p(z)$, where $q_p(z)=\sum_{\ell=1}^n[(1-p_\ell)z_\ell+p_\ell(1-z_\ell)]$. The $2^n(d+1)$ cap interiors cover every point of the mixed source. Each cap has mass $v(1-t)^d$ and, after normalization of its full ambient normal to length one, hinge integral $v(1-t)^{d+1}/((d+1)\sqrt{nA^2+\lVert\nabla\lambda_j\rVert^2})$. Consequently for any $\tau>2^{-n}\gamma_d$ there are such covers with individual cap masses strictly below $\tau M$ and total normalized hinge tending to zero. The limiting closed vertical cap at each $(p,j)$ has mass $v$.

## Proof

For any binary $z\ne p$, $q_p(z)\ge1$, while $\lambda_j(x)\le1$ on $K$. Thus only the fiber over $p$ is met. On that fiber the cap is $\{\lambda_j\ge t\}$, and every point satisfies $\lambda_j\ge1/(d+1)>t$ for some $j$, since the barycentric coordinates sum to one. For $0\le s\le1$, $\{\lambda_j\ge s\}\cap K$ is the homothet of $K$ about vertex $j$ of ratio $1-s$, so its volume is $v(1-s)^d$. Integrating this tail from $t$ to $1$ gives the unscaled hinge $v(1-t)^{d+1}/(d+1)$. The $n$ coefficients of $q_p$ are $\pm1$, so the ambient norm is $\sqrt{nA^2+\lVert\nabla\lambda_j\rVert^2}$. Choose $t$ sufficiently close to $1/(d+1)$ from below for the strict budget, then let $A\to\infty$. The normalized margins tend to $-q_p(z)/\sqrt n$, whose nonnegative set meets the source in the entire fiber at $p$. In the range $2^{-n}\gamma_d<\tau<2^{-n}$ this also gives nonattainment of the zero infimum among ambient-unit-normalized feasible covers, even if vertical caps are permitted. Any vertical cap meeting the source has mass at least $v>\tau M$, and any nonvertical cap containing an inball center has strictly positive hinge on that ball.
