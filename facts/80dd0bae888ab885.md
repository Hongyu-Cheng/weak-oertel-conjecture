## Statement

Let $C\subset\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^d$ be an ambient convex body, $\Lambda=\mathbb Z^n\times\mathbb R^d$, $S=C\cap\Lambda$, $q=\dim_H S>0$, and $M=\mathcal H_q(S)$. For every $0<t\le M$, some actual $y\in S$ has $\mathcal H_q(S\cap(2y-S))\ge t$ if and only if there is an ambient convex body $D\subset C$ with $\mathcal H_q(D\cap\Lambda)\ge t$ whose full mixed centroid has integer base and normalized local closed-halfspace depth at least $1/2$ for its own intrinsic law.

## Proof

Given the retained intersection, exact contained ambient thickening produces an ambient body $D\subset C$ with its exact mixed trace. Its centroid is the original reflection center and its local normalized depth is $1/2$.

Conversely, suppose $D$ satisfies the conditions. Since $D\subset C$ and $\mathcal H_q(D\cap\Lambda)>0$, the largest affine dimension of its integer fibers is $q$. The full mixed centroid $y$ belongs to $D$ by convexity and compactness, and its integer base ensures $y\in D\cap\Lambda$. Intrinsic half-depth rigidity makes the $\mathcal H_q$-law of $D\cap\Lambda$ symmetric about $y$. Consequently $D\cap\Lambda$ lies up to a null set in its own reflected intersection, which is contained in $S\cap(2y-S)$. The original retention at $y$ is therefore at least $\mathcal H_q(D\cap\Lambda)\ge t$.
