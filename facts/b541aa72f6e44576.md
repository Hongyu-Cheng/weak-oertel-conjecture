## Statement

Let $B=[-1,2]\times[-1,1]$, $P=B\cap\mathbb Z^2$, $q(x,y)=2x+y-1$, $\ell=5-|q|$ and $J=\{p_0=(0,0),p_1=(1,0)\}$. The roof is positive and concave, $J=P\cap\operatorname{conv}J$, and every anchored representation in $J$ has $\ell$ equality. There is an $\ell$-tight martingale, meaning a martingale preserving the row mean of $\ell$, from the unit atom at each source to a target with mass $7/12$ at each crease site $(0,1),(1,-1)$ and $1/12$ at all other sites. For any finite cover of $P$ by closed minors $T=P\cap\operatorname{conv}T$ each lying in the projection of a minimal upper-roof face containing a lifted source, the sum of their occupied parity counts is at least $8$, attained by the two facet minors. The original count is $k(P)=4$.

## Proof

The roof lies between $1$ and $5$ because $|q|\le4$ on the rectangle. Its two upper faces project onto $B_\pm=B\cap\{\pm q\ge0\}$. The sources are relatively interior to different faces and their base segment is primitive, so each representation anchored inside $J$ is trivial. In the ordered targets
$$
(-1,-1),\,(-1,0),\,(-1,1),\,(0,-1),\,(0,0),\,(0,1),\,(1,-1)
$$
the first row has masses $(1,1,1,1,1,4,3)/12$. They sum to one, and their coordinate moments have numerators $-3+3=0$ and $-1+1-1+4-3=0$. The row stays on $B_-$, where $\ell=5+q$ is affine. Reflection $(x,y)\mapsto(1-x,-y)$ gives the second row with barycenter $p_1$ and interchanges the two crease targets. The row sums at the crease are both $7/12$ and elsewhere are $1/12$.

For parities $(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)$ respectively, representatives in the strict negative side are $(0,0),(0,-1),(-1,0),(-1,1)$, and representatives in the strict positive side are $(2,0),(2,-1),(1,0),(1,1)$. A permitted cell lies wholly on one side, so each parity is counted in at least two cells. The negative facet polygon has vertices $(-1,-1),(-1,1),(0,1),(1,-1)$, all in $P$, and the positive polygon is its reflection. Thus their full traces are closed minors and attain cost eight. This count depends only on $B,P,\ell,J$ and continues to hold with any other transport realizing those data.
