## Statement

For the convex body $C=\{(z,x):1\le z\le3,\ 0\le x\le z\}$, the naive twofold shared-base fiberwise join
$$
J=\{(z,u,\xi_1,\xi_2):1\le z\le3,\ 0\le u\le1,\ 0\le\xi_1\le uz,\ 0\le\xi_2\le(1-u)z\}
$$
is not convex, even though the midpoint of the displayed witnessing bases $1$ and $3$ is integer.

## Proof

The point $(1,0,0,1)$ belongs to $J$ and so does $(3,1,3,0)$. Their midpoint is $(2,1/2,3/2,1/2)$, with $\xi_1=3/2>uz=1$. It does not belong to $J$.
