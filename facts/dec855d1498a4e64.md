## Statement

Let $P$ be finite and nonempty in a real affine space, let $K_z\subset\mathbb R^d$ be compact with $d\ge1$, and let $\mu$ be a finite nonnegative Borel measure supported on $S=\bigcup_{z\in P}(\{z\}\times K_z)$. For any nonempty face $F$ of $\operatorname{conv}(P)$, set $S_F=S\cap(F\times\mathbb R^d)$ and $\mu_F=\mu|_{S_F}$. At every $Y\in S_F$, the infimum of $\mu(H)$ over closed ambient halfspaces $H$ containing $Y$ equals the corresponding infimum of $\mu_F(H)$. Every such closed trace on $S_F$ is realized by a containing ambient halfspace avoiding $S\setminus S_F$. The same depth results if the restricted calculation uses the relative base affine hull of $F$ and permits the whole-space trace.

## Proof

The full face is immediate for the ambient identity. Otherwise choose an affine base exposer $\ell$ with $\ell=0$ on $F$ and $\ell<0$ at every sampled base outside $F$. Given a containing ambient cap $\{A\ge b\}$ for an affine functional $A$, the cap $\{A+t\ell\ge b\}$ keeps the identical closed trace on $S_F$ and still contains $Y$. Compactness of every sampled fiber and finiteness of $P$ ensure that all other traces are empty for a sufficiently large finite $t$. Such a $t$ can be chosen with nonzero normal, since at most one $t$ can cancel it. Taking the infimum gives the inequality opposite to the immediate inequality $\mu_F\le\mu$. Any relative cap functional extends affinely to the ambient space, and restriction of an ambient cap containing $Y$ produces a relative cap or the whole trace. The whole trace has mass $\mu_F(S_F)$ and can be admitted without lowering the infimum. For a proper face it can also be realized by the exposing cap $\{\ell\ge0\}$.
