## Statement

Let \(I\) be a finite nonempty set. For every \(i\in I\), let \(\sigma_i>0\), let \(\mathbf m_i\in\mathbb R^r\), let \(K_i\subset\mathbb R^d\) be a nonempty compact convex set, let \(\mathbf v_i\in\mathbb R^d\), and let
\[
 \mathbf q_i\in F_{K_i}(\mathbf v_i)
 :=\{\mathbf x\in K_i:\langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf x\rangle=h_{K_i}(\mathbf v_i)\}.
\]
Assume
\[
 \sum_{i\in I}\sigma_i\mathbf v_i=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{i\in I}\sigma_i\mathbf v_i\mathbf m_i^{\mathsf T}=0,
 \qquad
 \Omega:=-\sum_{i\in I}\sigma_i h_{K_i}(\mathbf v_i)>0.
\]
Put
\[
 \Sigma:=\sum_i\sigma_i,
 \qquad \pi_i:=\frac{\sigma_i}{\Sigma},
 \qquad
 \Psi:=\sum_j\sigma_j\sum_i\pi_i h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i).
\]
Then \(\Psi\ge 0\), so
\[
 t:=\frac{\Omega}{\Omega+\Psi}
\]
is well-defined and belongs to \((0,1]\). Define
\[
 a_{ij}:=(1-t)\sigma_i\mathbf 1_{\{i=j\}}+t\sigma_i\pi_j
 \qquad(i,j\in I).
\]
The numbers \(a_{ij}\) are nonnegative and obey all of the following identities:
\[
 \sum_j a_{ij}=\sigma_i,
 \qquad
 \sum_i a_{ij}=\sigma_j,
 \qquad
 \sum_{i,j}a_{ij}\mathbf v_i=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{i,j}a_{ij}\mathbf v_i\mathbf m_j^{\mathsf T}=0,
\]
and
\[
 \sum_{i,j}a_{ij}h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)=0.
\]
Define
\[
 \mathcal O:=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}
 \bigl(h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)-\langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_j\rangle\bigr)
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal T:=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}
 \langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_j-\mathbf q_i\rangle.
\]
Then
\[
 \mathcal O=(1-t)\Omega=t\Psi\ge0,
 \qquad
 \mathcal T=t\Omega>0,
 \qquad
 \Omega=\mathcal O+\mathcal T.
\]
In particular, \(\max\{\mathcal O,\mathcal T\}\ge\Omega/2\).

More precisely, for each target \(j\), put \(\lambda_{ij}:=a_{ij}/\sigma_j\) and let \(\mathcal O_j\) denote its contribution to \(\mathcal O\). Then
\[
 \lambda_{ij}=(1-t)\mathbf 1_{\{i=j\}}+t\pi_i,
 \qquad
 \sum_i\lambda_{ij}=1,
 \qquad
 \sum_i\lambda_{ij}\mathbf v_i=(1-t)\mathbf v_j,
\]
and \(\mathcal O_j\) is exactly the support-function Jensen gap
\[
 \mathcal O_j
 =\sigma_j\left(
   \sum_i\lambda_{ij}h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)
   -h_{K_j}\left(\sum_i\lambda_{ij}\mathbf v_i\right)
 \right)
 =t\sigma_j\sum_i\pi_i h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i).
\]
For arbitrary choices \(\mathbf p_{ij}\in F_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)\), define ordered-pair masses
\[
 b_{ik}^{(j)}:=\frac{t\sigma_j}{2}\pi_i\pi_k.
\]
Then the Jensen term has the exact nonnegative pairization
\[
 \mathcal O_j
 =\sum_{i,k}b_{ik}^{(j)}
 \langle\mathbf v_i-\mathbf v_k,\mathbf p_{ij}-\mathbf p_{kj}\rangle,
\]
every displayed pair integrand is nonnegative, and the use of contact \(\mathbf p_{ij}\) across the two ordered-pair endpoints is exactly
\[
 \sum_k b_{ik}^{(j)}+\sum_k b_{ki}^{(j)}
 =t\sigma_j\pi_i.
\]
Consequently the total endpoint use in target \(j\) is exactly \(t\sigma_j\le\sigma_j\), with no hidden multiplicity. All statements remain valid in the boundary case \(t=1\).

## Proof

Because \(\sum_i\pi_i\mathbf v_i=0\), convexity and positive homogeneity of each support function give, for every \(j\),
\[
 \sum_i\pi_i h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)
 \ge h_{K_j}\left(\sum_i\pi_i\mathbf v_i\right)
 =h_{K_j}(0)=0.
\]
Multiplying by \(\sigma_j>0\) and summing proves \(\Psi\ge0\). Since \(\Omega>0\), the denominator \(\Omega+\Psi\) is positive and \(0<t\le1\). Thus \(1-t\ge0\), and every \(a_{ij}\) is nonnegative.

For each source \(i\),
\[
 \sum_j a_{ij}
 =(1-t)\sigma_i+t\sigma_i\sum_j\pi_j
 =\sigma_i.
\]
For each target \(j\), using \(\pi_j\Sigma=\sigma_j\),
\[
 \sum_i a_{ij}
 =(1-t)\sigma_j+t\pi_j\sum_i\sigma_i
 =\sigma_j.
\]
The row marginal and the assumed zero-force identity yield
\[
 \sum_{i,j}a_{ij}\mathbf v_i
 =\sum_i\sigma_i\mathbf v_i=0.
\]
The transported affine moment is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{i,j}a_{ij}\mathbf v_i\mathbf m_j^{\mathsf T}
 &=(1-t)\sum_i\sigma_i\mathbf v_i\mathbf m_i^{\mathsf T}
   +t\sum_{i,j}\sigma_i\pi_j\mathbf v_i\mathbf m_j^{\mathsf T}\\
 &=(1-t)\sum_i\sigma_i\mathbf v_i\mathbf m_i^{\mathsf T}
   +t\left(\sum_i\sigma_i\mathbf v_i\right)
       \left(\sum_j\pi_j\mathbf m_j\right)^{\mathsf T}
 =0.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Thus the transport preserves both exact capacities and affine balance.

Its support score is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{i,j}a_{ij}h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)
 &=(1-t)\sum_i\sigma_i h_{K_i}(\mathbf v_i)
   +t\sum_{i,j}\sigma_i\pi_jh_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)\\
 &=-(1-t)\Omega+t\Psi.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The definition of \(t\) gives \(t\Psi=(1-t)\Omega\), including when \(\Psi=0\) and \(t=1\). Hence the transported support score is zero.

Each summand defining \(\mathcal O\) is nonnegative because \(\mathbf q_j\in K_j\), so \(\langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_j\rangle\le h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)\). Furthermore,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{i,j}a_{ij}\langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_j\rangle
 &=(1-t)\sum_i\sigma_i\langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_i\rangle
   +t\sum_j\pi_j\left\langle\sum_i\sigma_i\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_j\right\rangle\\
 &=-(1-t)\Omega.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Subtracting this from the zero transported support score proves
\[
 \mathcal O=(1-t)\Omega=t\Psi\ge0.
\]
For \(\mathcal T\), the diagonal part of \(a_{ij}\) contributes zero, while the product part gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal T
 &=t\sum_{i,j}\sigma_i\pi_j
   \langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_j-\mathbf q_i\rangle\\
 &=t\sum_j\pi_j\left\langle\sum_i\sigma_i\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_j\right\rangle
   -t\sum_i\sigma_i\langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_i\rangle\sum_j\pi_j\\
 &=t\Omega>0.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Therefore \(\mathcal O+\mathcal T=\Omega\), and at least one of the two nonnegative terms is at least \(\Omega/2\).

Fix a target \(j\). Since \(\pi_j=\sigma_j/\Sigma\), division of \(a_{ij}\) by \(\sigma_j\) gives
\[
 \lambda_{ij}=(1-t)\mathbf 1_{\{i=j\}}+t\pi_i.
\]
It follows that \(\sum_i\lambda_{ij}=1\) and
\[
 \sum_i\lambda_{ij}\mathbf v_i
 =(1-t)\mathbf v_j+t\sum_i\pi_i\mathbf v_i
 =(1-t)\mathbf v_j.
\]
Positive homogeneity of \(h_{K_j}\), also at the scale \(1-t=0\), gives
\[
 h_{K_j}\left(\sum_i\lambda_{ij}\mathbf v_i\right)
 =(1-t)h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_j)
 =\left\langle\sum_i\lambda_{ij}\mathbf v_i,\mathbf q_j\right\rangle.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal O_j
 &=\sigma_j\left(
   \sum_i\lambda_{ij}h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)
   -h_{K_j}\left(\sum_i\lambda_{ij}\mathbf v_i\right)
 \right)\\
 &=t\sigma_j\sum_i\pi_i h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i).
 \end{aligned}
\]
This proves both the claimed Jensen-gap description and its exact normalization.

For the pairization, compactness of \(K_j\) ensures that every face \(F_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)\) is nonempty, so the stated contacts \(\mathbf p_{ij}\) exist. Put \(\overline{\mathbf p}_j:=\sum_i\pi_i\mathbf p_{ij}\). Expanding the symmetric double sum and using \(\sum_i\pi_i\mathbf v_i=0\) gives the covariance identity
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \frac12\sum_{i,k}\pi_i\pi_k
 \langle\mathbf v_i-\mathbf v_k,\mathbf p_{ij}-\mathbf p_{kj}\rangle
 &=\sum_i\pi_i\langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf p_{ij}\rangle
   -\left\langle\sum_i\pi_i\mathbf v_i,\overline{\mathbf p}_j\right\rangle\\
 &=\sum_i\pi_i h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i).
 \end{aligned}
\]
Multiplication by \(t\sigma_j\) is exactly the asserted formula for \(\mathcal O_j\). Each integrand is nonnegative, since
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \langle\mathbf v_i-\mathbf v_k,\mathbf p_{ij}-\mathbf p_{kj}\rangle
 &=h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)-\langle\mathbf v_i,\mathbf p_{kj}\rangle\\
 &\quad+h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_k)-\langle\mathbf v_k,\mathbf p_{ij}\rangle
 \ge0.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Finally,
\[
 \sum_k b_{ik}^{(j)}+\sum_k b_{ki}^{(j)}
 =\frac{t\sigma_j}{2}\pi_i\sum_k\pi_k
  +\frac{t\sigma_j}{2}\pi_i\sum_k\pi_k
 =t\sigma_j\pi_i.
\]
Summing over \(i\) gives total endpoint use \(t\sigma_j\le\sigma_j\). This count includes the harmless zero-integrand diagonal ordered pairs \(i=k\), so it hides no omitted pair mass.

If \(t=1\), then \(\Psi=0\). Since every target quantity \(\sum_i\pi_i h_{K_j}(\mathbf v_i)\) is nonnegative and every \(\sigma_j\) is positive, each of them is zero. Thus \(a_{ij}=\sigma_i\pi_j\), \(\mathcal O_j=0\), all nonnegative pair integrands have zero weighted sum, \(\mathcal O=0\), and \(\mathcal T=\Omega\). No displayed formula divides by \(1-t\), so every marginal, balance, score, pairization, and use identity remains valid at this boundary.
