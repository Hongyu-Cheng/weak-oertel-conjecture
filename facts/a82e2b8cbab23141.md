## Statement

Let $A,K,B$ be full-dimensional planar convex bodies with $(A+B)/2\subseteq K$, let $v_i+v_j+v_k=0$ be nonzero spanning normals, and let $B\subseteq\bigcap_l\{v_l\cdot x\le1\}$. Write $R_l(A)=A\cap\bigcap_{h\ne l}\{v_h\cdot x<-1\}$, $U_l=|A\cap\{v_l\cdot x\ge-1\}|$, $a=|A|$, $b=|B|$, and $k_0=|K|$. Suppose $R_i(A)=\varnothing$ and choose $P_j\in R_j(A)$ and $P_k\in R_k(A)$. Put $P=[P_j,P_k]$, $w=P_k-P_j$, $G=\{v_j\cdot x<0\}\cup\{v_k\cdot x<0\}$ and
$$
\eta_j=-\max_{x\in P_j+B}v_k\cdot x>0,\quad
\delta=\min(1/2,\eta_j/(2v_k\cdot w))>0,\quad
J_B(w)=\|w\|\mathcal H_1(\operatorname{proj}_{w^\perp}B)>0.
$$
Then $|(P+B)\cap G|\ge2b+\delta J_B(w)$ and $4|K\cap G|\ge2b+\delta J_B(w)+2a-U_j-U_k$. If $A,K,B$ are the actual fibers at $-1,0,1$ and all the caps $v_l\cdot x-z\ge0$ have raw area $Q$ summed over these three fibers, the middle residual $r_i^K=|K\cap\{v_j\cdot x<0,\ v_k\cdot x<0\}|$ satisfies
$$4r_i^K\le8k_0-8Q+5(U_j+U_k)-2a-2b-\delta J_B(w).$$

## Proof

The sets in $E=A\cap(\{v_j\cdot x<-1\}\cup\{v_k\cdot x<-1\})$ are disjoint because $R_i(A)$ is empty, so $|E|=2a-U_j-U_k$. Every translation of $E$ by a point of $B$ lies in $G$. Put $B_j=P_j+B$ and $B_k=P_k+B$. The defining strict source inequalities and the upper bounds on $B$ give $v_i\cdot x,v_k\cdot x<0$ on $B_j$, and $v_i\cdot x,v_j\cdot x<0$ on $B_k$. In particular $v_k$ is strictly negative on $B_j$ and strictly positive on $B_k$. The compactness of $B_j$ gives $\eta_j>0$, and $v_k\cdot w>0$ since $v_k\cdot P_j<-1$ and $v_k\cdot P_k>2$. The short sweep $B_j+[0,\delta]w$ lies in $P+B$ and stays in $v_k<0$ by the definition of $\delta$. It is therefore disjoint from $B_k$ and both are in $G$. In every nonempty section parallel to $w$ the sweep lengthens the compact interval by $\delta\|w\|$ and leaves the perpendicular projection fixed. Integration gives its area $b+\delta J_B(w)$. Since $B$ has interior, its perpendicular projection has positive length. Adding the other translate gives the stated baseline estimate.

Apply the simultaneous profile inequality to the OR of the $j,k$ misses with this single $P$. It yields $4|K\cap G|\ge|(P+B)\cap G|+|E|$, proving the joint inequality. In the three-fiber equal-cap sum, $B$ contributes zero area and the two corresponding middle captured areas are $Q-U_j,Q-U_k$. Thus inclusion–exclusion gives $|K\cap G|=2k_0-2Q+U_j+U_k-r_i^K$. Substituting this identity yields the stated bound, strictly stronger than dropping the positive sweep term.
