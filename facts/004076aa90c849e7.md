## Statement

Let $a\ge3$ and $0\le\delta\le1/4$. At integer bases $-1,0,1,2$ take centered intervals of widths $(a+2,3a/2,a,1)$ and their convex hull. Its mass is $M_a=(7a+6)/2$, its true centroid is $(0,0)$, and its normalized local depth there is $\beta_a=(7a+4)/[2(7a+6)]$. Form the bulge by raising just the upper endpoint at base $1$ by $\delta$, then erode each fiber by radius $\delta 2^z/19$. The positive-fiber hull of this correction lies inside the original enlargement at $\delta=1/4$. It has mass $m=M_a+4\delta/19$ and centroid $(0,c)$ with $c=\delta(19a+15\delta)/(133a+8\delta+114)$. Write $P=133a+8\delta+114$. Its absolute local depth at the true centroid is $\beta_a m$ plus the minimum of the following four deficits:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{\delta[1197a^2+4750a+3192-\delta(1771a+1486)]}{19(7a+6)P},\\
&\frac{\delta[4921a^2-3496a-6612+\delta(11354a+9796)]}{38(7a+6)P},\\
&\frac{\delta[6251a^2+9082a+3192+\delta(2219a+1934)]}{19(7a+6)P},\\
&\frac{\delta[9975a^2+31160a+19380-\delta(10458a+8900)]}{38(7a+6)P}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For every $\delta>0$ these are strictly positive. Their right derivatives at zero are $(9a+28)/[19(7a+6)]$, $(37a-58)/[38(7a+6)]$, $(47a+28)/[19(7a+6)]$ and $5(15a+34)/[38(7a+6)]$. All are at least $53/1026$ as $\beta_a$ increases to $1/2$.

## Proof

The enlarged upper slopes in increasing base order are $a/4-1,-a/4+\delta,1/2-a/2-\delta$, which decrease for $a\ge3$, $\delta\le1/4$. The unchanged lower slopes increase, so the enlarged fibers are the exact fibers of their hull. For the correction use the convex real-base radius $\delta 2^z/19$ and the convex erosion lemma, then take the positive-fiber hull. The removed masses are $\delta(1,2,4,8)/19$ in base order, totaling $15\delta/19$ with first base moment $\delta$. This exactly matches the added base moment, and the old base moment is $-(a+2)+a+2=0$. Let $w_z^0$ be the old widths and $l_z,r_z$ the new endpoints. Then
$$
 l_z=-w_z^0/2+\delta 2^z/19,\qquad
 r_z=w_z^0/2+\delta\mathbf1_{\{z=1\}}-\delta 2^z/19.
$$
All widths $w_z=r_z-l_z$ stay positive. The midpoint is zero except at base one, where it is $\delta/2$ and the new width is $a+15\delta/19$. This yields the asserted mass and continuous centroid, with $0\le c\le\delta/2$.

For any real slope $h$ the cap $x+hz\ge c$ has mass $\sum_z\min\{w_z,\max\{0,r_z+hz-c\}\}$. The base-zero cut is proper throughout since $c\le1/8$ and $r_0=3a/4-\delta/19>1/8$, $l_0<0$. The nonconstant breakpoints in increasing order are
$$
 l_{-1}-c,\quad c-r_1,\quad (c-r_2)/2,\quad (c-l_2)/2,\quad c-l_1,\quad r_{-1}-c .
$$
Direct subtraction gives their consecutive differences with denominators $38P,4P,38,4P,38P$ and numerators
$$
\begin{aligned}
 &5054a+4332-\delta(3211a+3686)+860\delta^2,\\
 &266a^2+95a-114+510a\delta+2\delta^2+448\delta,\qquad 19-8\delta,\\
 &266a^2+95a-114+(54a-8)\delta+30\delta^2,\\
 &5054a+4332-1045a\delta+646\delta-1116\delta^2 .
\end{aligned}
$$
All are positive on the stated ranges, by using $\delta\le1/4$ on the negative terms and $a\ge3$. In the open slope intervals the proper indices other than zero are successively $\varnothing,\{-1\},\{-1,1\},\{-1,1,2\},\{-1,1\},\{-1\},\varnothing$, so the slopes of the cap mass are $0,-1,0,2,0,-1,0$. The two nonvertical orientations complement each other in mixed mass. The minimum of their masses over all slopes is therefore the minimum of the following four values:
$$
 w_1+w_2+r_0-c,\qquad
 m-(r_{-1}+r_0+r_1+w_2-3c),\qquad
 m-(w_{-1}+r_0-c),\qquad
 r_{-1}+r_0+r_1-3c .
$$
The first and third come from the constant tails; the second and fourth from the two constant interior pieces. The two closed pure-base caps contain the entire base-zero interval in place of the partial interval in the corresponding tilted limit, so neither lowers the depth. Translating any cap containing the centroid to the centroid can only reduce its mass. Thus these four values account for all closed caps. At $\delta=0$ each is $(7a+4)/4$, giving the old exact normalized depth.

Substituting the endpoints and $c$ into the four displayed values and subtracting $\beta_a m$ gives the stated four rational deficits. For the first and fourth, bounding the negative $\delta$ terms by their values at $1/4$ leaves polynomials with positive coefficients in $a$. The third is positive termwise. The second has positive $\delta$ coefficient and the quadratic $4921a^2-3496a-6612$ is positive at $3$ and increasing for $a\ge3$. At $\delta=0$ the ratios of the numerators to their denominators reduce to the four stated derivatives. The first decreases toward $9/133$, the second increases from $53/1026$ at $a=3$, and the third and fourth dominate the first and second respectively. This proves the absolute uniform derivative bound. The member $a=3$ has old normalized depth $25/54$ and derivatives $55/513,53/1026,169/513,395/1026$.
