## Statement

Let $p\ge2$, $d=p+1$, and $T_p=\operatorname{conv}(v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_p)$ with $v_0=(-1,\ldots,-1)$ and $v_i=v_0+(p+1)e_i$. Set $V_p=(p+1)^p/p!$, $\delta=(p/(p+1))^p$, and let
$$
K_p=\{(x,t): |t|\le1,\ x\in(1-|t|)T_p\},
\qquad V=2V_p/(p+1).
$$
Write $u_i=e_i$ for $1\le i\le p$ and $u_0=-\sum_{i=1}^p e_i$.

For $r>0$ and any $u_i$, the closed cap of $rK_p$ at level $u_i\cdot x\ge h$ has volume
$$
\delta V(r-h/p)^d-\frac Vp(-h)_+^d\qquad(-r\le h\le pr).
$$
The volume is full for $h<-r$ and zero for $h>pr$.

## Proof

The slice at height $t$ has radius $\rho=r-|t|$, and $u_i\cdot x$ on that slice has one vertex level $p\rho$ and all other vertex levels $-\rho$. Its full volume is $V_p\rho^p$. For $-\rho\le h\le p\rho$, the vertex cap is homothetic to the slice and its volume is $\delta V_p(\rho-h/p)^p$. If $h\ge0$, integrating over $h/p\le\rho\le r$ with the two reflected height contributions gives $\delta V(r-h/p)^d$. For $h=-q$ with $0\le q\le r$, the slices with $0\le\rho\le q$ are full. Integration gives
$$
2V_p\int_0^q \rho^p\,d\rho
+2\delta V_p\int_q^r(\rho+q/p)^p\,d\rho
=\delta V(r+q/p)^d+Vq^d[1-\delta(1+1/p)^d].
$$
Since $d=p+1$, the bracket is $-1/p$. The support endpoints are $-r,pr$, so the remaining levels have the asserted full or zero volumes. The formula agrees with those volumes at both endpoints.
