## Statement

For $L>1$ let $A=[-L,L]\times[-1,1]$, $B=[-2,-1]\times[-L,L]$, and $C=\operatorname{conv}((\{0\}\times A)\cup(\{1\}\times B))$. The global normalized mixed depth is $1/3$, attained at the origin. The two closed oblique caps $x+z\ge0$ and $-x-2z\ge0$ each minimize mixed mass there, with mixed fraction $1/3$ and ambient fraction $L(L+2)/(2L^2+6L+1)>1/3$. Their continuous normals balance. At $L=2$ the ambient fraction is $8/21$.

## Proof

The only integer fibers are $A$ and $B$, both exposed. By 2acc8bc803acf1c0 their raw point depths equal the corresponding ordinary depths. Each rectangle has maximum raw depth half its area, attained at its center by central symmetry. The larger maximum is $2L$ on $A$, while the total mixed mass is $6L$. Each given cap captures half of $A$ and only a zero-area edge of $B$, so minimizes at the origin.

At $0\le z\le1$ the Minkowski interpolant has horizontal interval $[-L(1-z)-2z,L(1-z)-z]$ and vertical interval $[-(1-z)-Lz,(1-z)+Lz]$. Either cap retains horizontal width $L(1-z)$. Integration gives ambient volume $(2L^2+6L+1)/3$ and either ambient cap volume $L(L+2)/3$. Subtracting $1/3$ from the ratio gives $(L^2-1)/(3(2L^2+6L+1))>0$. The normals in the continuous coordinates are opposite.
